Can I lose my salvation?

Question
Can I lose my salvation?
Short Answer
Scripture assures those truly united to Christ that nothing can separate them from God's love and that Jesus keeps His sheep securely. At the same time, the Bible warns against falling away, calling every professing believer to persevere in faith.
Detailed Answer

Can I lose my salvation? This question often comes from a tender place, a fear that God might let go of us. The Bible offers strong assurance to those in Christ. Jesus said His sheep hear His voice, He gives them eternal life, and no one can snatch them from His Father's hand. Paul declares nothing in all creation can separate us from the love of God in Christ. Salvation is God's work from start to finish, and He completes what He begins. Scripture also contains sober warnings against drifting, hardening the heart, and abandoning the faith, which we should receive humbly rather than explain away. These warnings function as one of the very means by which God keeps His people persevering. The pastoral takeaway is this: do not rest your assurance on a past prayer alone or on your emotional stability. Rest it on Christ's finished work, and express it in ongoing trust, repentance, and obedience. If you have wandered, return today. The Father's arms are open, His promises stand, and the Good Shepherd who sought you first is faithful to keep you to the end.
