Who is God according to the Bible?

Question
Who is God according to the Bible?
Short Answer
The Bible reveals one God who is eternal, holy, loving, and faithful, Creator of all things and Father who saves. He is both transcendent in majesty and near to His people in mercy and grace.
Detailed Answer

Who is God? The Bible does not begin with an argument for His existence. It begins with His action: In the beginning, God created. From Genesis to Revelation, God reveals Himself as eternal and unchanging, holy and just, yet also merciful, compassionate, and faithful. He is one God who exists as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. He is Creator and Sustainer, King and Shepherd, Judge and Redeemer. What makes the biblical portrait so hopeful is that this majestic God chooses to make Himself known. He speaks, He promises, He rescues, and He dwells with His people. In Exodus, He tells Moses His name and describes Himself as gracious and abounding in steadfast love. In Jesus, we see God's character most clearly, full of grace and truth. Knowing God is not about mastering information. It is about trusting the One who has revealed Himself and learning to rest in who He says He is. If you feel distant from God today, start where Scripture starts: He is near, He is good, and He invites you to know Him personally through Christ.
