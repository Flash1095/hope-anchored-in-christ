Why did Jesus have to die for our sins?

Question
Why did Jesus have to die for our sins?
Short Answer
Jesus died because sin carries a real penalty, and only a perfect substitute could pay it. God's justice requires sin to be dealt with, and God's love provided the One who would take our place — so that forgiveness could be both merciful and just.
Detailed Answer
The Bible holds two truths together that can feel hard to reconcile: God is love, and God is just. Sin is not a small mistake; it is rebellion that breaks our relationship with God, and Scripture says its wages are death (Romans 6:23). For God simply to ignore sin would deny His justice. Instead, Isaiah pictured a servant who would be 'pierced for our transgressions' and crushed for our iniquities (Isaiah 53:5-6), and Jesus fulfilled that picture: on the cross He bore the penalty our sins deserved, so that we could go free (1 Peter 3:18). Hebrews adds that without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness (Hebrews 9:22). The cross is not divine child abuse or an angry God taking it out on an innocent third party — Jesus went willingly, as God the Son, laying down His own life (John 10:18). That is why Christians call it good news: at the cross, God's love and God's justice meet, and sinners can be forgiven without God pretending sin does not matter.
