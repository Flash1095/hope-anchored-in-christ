What is true worship according to the Bible?

Question
What is true worship according to the Bible?
Short Answer
True worship is honoring God with your whole life, in spirit and in truth. Jesus said the Father seeks worshipers who worship 'in the Spirit and in truth' (John 4:23) — heartfelt, honest, and shaped by who God really is.
Detailed Answer

Worship in the Bible is far bigger than singing on Sunday, though singing is part of it (Psalm 95:6; Colossians 3:16). Jesus' conversation with the Samaritan woman redefined worship: it is not about the right mountain or temple, but about the right heart — 'in the Spirit and in truth' (John 4:23-24). 'In truth' means worship shaped by who God actually is, not by our feelings or preferences; 'in Spirit' means worship empowered by the Holy Spirit, not mere ritual. Paul expands it to all of life: 'offer your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and pleasing to God — this is your true and proper worship' (Romans 12:1). That means worship happens at work, in parenting, in how you spend money — anywhere you honor God. Hebrews adds the attitude: worship 'with reverence and awe' (Hebrews 12:28), because God is both our Father and a consuming fire. True worship, then, is not a performance to impress God or others; it is a life turned toward God in love, expressed in singing, prayer, obedience, and service — every day, not just on Sundays.
