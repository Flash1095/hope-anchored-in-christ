What does it mean that God is holy?

Question
What does it mean that God is holy?
Short Answer
To say God is holy means He is completely set apart — perfectly pure, majestic, and unlike anything else. In heaven, the angels never stop crying, 'Holy, holy, holy is the LORD Almighty' (Isaiah 6:3).
Detailed Answer

Holiness is the Bible's master word for who God is. When Isaiah saw the Lord on His throne, the seraphim covered their faces and cried, 'Holy, holy, holy' — the threefold repetition means supremely, utterly holy (Isaiah 6:3). Holiness means separation: God is set apart from sin the way light is separate from darkness, and from creation the way the Creator is separate from what He made. It also means moral perfection: God cannot lie, cannot do evil, cannot tolerate sin — which is why sin is so serious, and why the cross was necessary. But God's holiness is not cold distance. The holy God says, 'Be holy, because I am holy' (Leviticus 19:2; 1 Peter 1:15-16) — He invites His people to share His character. And in Jesus, holiness came close: the Holy One touched lepers, ate with sinners, and bore our sin so that unholy people could be made holy (Hebrews 10:10). God's holiness is why we worship with reverence, why we confess our sin honestly, and why grace is so amazing: the holy God has made a way for sinners to stand before Him, clean.
