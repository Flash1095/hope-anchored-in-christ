Which prophecies did Jesus fulfill?

Which prophecies did Jesus fulfill?

Short Answer

Jesus fulfilled prophecies across the whole Old Testament: born in Bethlehem, born of a virgin, from David's line, a suffering servant who would be pierced for our sins, and a king who would enter Jerusalem humbly. Luke says He explained 'what was said in all the Scriptures concerning himself' (Luke 24:27).

Detailed Answer

The Gospel writers constantly point back: Matthew alone cites fulfillment after fulfillment — born of a virgin (Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:22-23), born in Bethlehem (Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:5-6), called out of Egypt (Hosea 11:1; Matthew 2:15). Isaiah 53 is the centerpiece: a servant 'pierced for our transgressions,' 'led like a lamb to the slaughter,' and buried 'with the rich' — written centuries before crucifixion existed as a method of execution. Psalm 22 describes hands and feet pierced and garments divided, fulfilled at the cross (John 19:23-24). Zechariah 9:9 pictures a king entering Jerusalem 'gentle and riding on a donkey,' fulfilled on Palm Sunday (Matthew 21:4-5). Genesis 3:15 promised a seed of the woman who would crush the serpent — the first gospel promise, fulfilled at the cross and empty tomb. Some fulfillments are direct predictions; others are patterns (like Hosea's 'out of Egypt I called my son') that Jesus recapitulates as Israel's true representative. Either way, the sheer range — birthplace, birth, ministry, betrayal, death, burial, resurrection — is why the apostles preached that Jesus died and rose 'according to the Scriptures' (1 Corinthians 15:3-4).
