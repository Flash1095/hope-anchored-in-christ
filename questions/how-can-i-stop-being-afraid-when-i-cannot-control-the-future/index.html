How can I stop being afraid when I cannot control the future?

Question
How can I stop being afraid when I cannot control the future?
Short Answer
You cannot control the future, but you can trust the God who holds it — and Scripture's practical answer to fear is to name it honestly, pray specifically, remember God's past faithfulness, and take the next small step of obedience.
Detailed Answer

Fear of the future is usually fear of losing control, and the honest truth is that control was never really ours. The psalmist's strategy was simple: 'When I am afraid, I put my trust in you' (Psalm 56:3) — not 'when I stop being afraid,' but when fear is present. Paul's prescription is equally practical: instead of nursing anxiety, present your requests to God, and 'the peace of God, which transcends all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds' (Philippians 4:6-7). Jesus adds a daily discipline: do not borrow tomorrow's trouble today, because 'each day has enough trouble of its own' (Matthew 6:34). And Peter says to cast all your anxiety on God 'because he cares for you' (1 Peter 5:7) — fear shrinks when it is carried to Someone who cares. Practically, that looks like: writing down the specific fear and praying about it by name, recalling times God carried you before, limiting the news and noise that feed dread, and talking with a trusted believer. If fear is ruling your days, a counselor or doctor can help too — that is wisdom, not weakness. You may not feel brave, but courage is not the absence of fear; it is trusting God and taking the next step anyway.
