Is it a sin to doubt God?

Question
Is it a sin to doubt God?
Short Answer
Honest doubt is not the same as rebellious unbelief, and God is patient with honest questions. The Psalms, the prophets, and even the apostles brought their doubts to God — and He met them with answers, not rejection.
Detailed Answer

Scripture draws a line between two kinds of doubt. One is the honest struggle of a believer who wants to trust God but is wrestling: the psalmist crying 'How long, LORD?' (Psalm 13:1), Habakkuk asking why God seems silent, Thomas asking to see the risen Jesus — and Jesus answered him, wounds and all (John 20:27-29). The other is a settled refusal to trust, a heart that turns away from God (Hebrews 3:12). James warns that the person who doubts God's character and will not trust Him is 'double-minded' (James 1:6-8) — but that is about refusing to rely on God, not about asking hard questions. God is not fragile; He can handle your hardest questions, and He often uses them to deepen faith rather than destroy it. If you are doubting, bring it to Him honestly, keep reading Scripture, talk with a trusted believer, and remember that faith is not the absence of questions — it is trusting God in the middle of them.
