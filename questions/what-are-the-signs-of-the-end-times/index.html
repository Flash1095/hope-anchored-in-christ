What are the signs of the end times?

Question
What are the signs of the end times?
Short Answer
Jesus described signs including wars, earthquakes, persecution, false teachers, and the gospel reaching all nations, while warning that no one knows the day or hour. These signs call believers to watchfulness and endurance, not date-setting or fear.
Detailed Answer

What are the signs of the end times? Jesus addressed this directly on the Mount of Olives, and His answer balances sobriety with calm. He described an age marked by wars and rumors of wars, famines and earthquakes, persecution of His followers, the rise of false messiahs and prophets, growing lawlessness and cooling love, and, shining through it all, the gospel of the kingdom preached to all nations. He called these birth pains, real and intensifying, but He immediately forbade panic and date speculation: the end is not yet, no one knows the day or hour, not even the angels. Two thousand years of headlines tempt every generation to claim final certainty, and two thousand years of failed predictions counsel humility. The signs function less like a coded timetable and more like a standing summons: stay awake, endure hardship without losing love, refuse deception by knowing Scripture, and keep proclaiming Christ to the nations, since that mission milestone matters more than any headline. For the believer, end-times teaching should produce hope, holiness, and courage, never fear-mongering. Our future is not secured by decoding events but by a returning Person. Keep your eyes on Him, your lamp trimmed with oil, and your heart anchored in His certain promise: I am with you always, to the very end of the age.
