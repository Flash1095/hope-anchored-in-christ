What will happen when Jesus comes back?

Short Answer

When Jesus comes back, He will raise the dead, judge the world with perfect justice, and make all things new: a new heaven and a new earth where God dwells with His people forever.

Detailed Answer

The New Testament describes Christ's return as public, powerful, and final. Jesus said He will come 'on the clouds of heaven, with power and great glory' (Matthew 24:30), and angels told the disciples He will return 'in the same way you have seen him go into heaven' (Acts 1:11) — visibly, not secretly. Paul describes the sequence: the Lord will descend with a shout, the dead in Christ will rise first, and living believers will be caught up together with them (1 Thessalonians 4:16-17). Then comes judgment: Jesus will separate people as a shepherd separates sheep from goats (Matthew 25:31-46), judging with perfect justice — no mistakes, no favoritism, no overlooked wrongs. Finally comes renewal: Peter says the present heavens and earth will give way to 'a new heaven and a new earth, where righteousness dwells' (2 Peter 3:13), and Revelation pictures God wiping every tear away, with no more death, mourning, or pain (Revelation 21:1-5). For believers, His return is not something to fear but to long for: it is the day faith becomes sight, justice is done, and home is restored. That is why the Bible's last prayer is, 'Come, Lord Jesus' (Revelation 22:20).
