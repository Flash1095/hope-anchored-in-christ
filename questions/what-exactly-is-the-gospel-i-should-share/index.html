What exactly is the gospel I should share?

Question
What exactly is the gospel I should share?
Short Answer
The gospel is the good news that Jesus Christ, God's Son, died for our sins and rose again, so that everyone who trusts in Him is forgiven and given eternal life. Paul summarizes it in one breath: Christ died for our sins, was buried, and was raised on the third day (1 Corinthians 15:3-4).
Detailed Answer

The word 'gospel' means good news, and the news has four parts. First, God: He made us, loves us, and rules over all. Second, sin: we have all turned from God, and our sin separates us from Him (Romans 3:23). Third, Christ: Jesus, fully God and fully man, lived the life we could not, died the death we deserved, and rose again, defeating sin and death (Mark 1:15; 1 Corinthians 15:3-4). Fourth, response: everyone who repents and trusts in Jesus — turning from sin and relying on Him — is forgiven, adopted into God's family, and given eternal life (John 3:16; Romans 10:9-10). That is the whole message, and it is wonderfully simple — but it is also deep enough to spend a lifetime exploring. When you share it, you do not need fancy arguments: tell people who Jesus is, what He did, and what He offers, and invite them to trust Him. The power is in the message itself, because, as Paul says, the gospel 'is the power of God that brings salvation to everyone who believes' (Romans 1:16).
