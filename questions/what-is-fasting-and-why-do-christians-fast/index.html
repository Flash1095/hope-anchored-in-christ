What is fasting and why do Christians fast?

Question
What is fasting and why do Christians fast?
Short Answer
Fasting is voluntarily abstaining from food, or another good thing, for a time to seek God with focused prayer and humility. Christians fast to deepen dependence on God, sharpen spiritual hunger, and align their hearts with His purposes.
Detailed Answer

Fasting appears throughout Scripture, from Moses and Esther to Jesus and the early church. Simply put, it is choosing to set aside food, or for a season another comfort like media or sweets, in order to feast more deliberately on God through prayer. Jesus assumed His followers would fast, saying when you fast, not if, and He warned only against doing it for public applause. Fasting exposes what controls us, makes space to hear God, and embodies the prayer that we need Him more than our appetites. The church in Antioch fasted while seeking the Spirit's direction and launched Paul and Barnabas into mission. Fasting does not earn God's favor or twist His arm. It positions our hearts in humility and urgency. If you try it, start wisely: choose a meal or a day, drink water, check with a doctor if you have health concerns, and fill the freed time with Scripture and prayer. Break the fast gently and without pride. Used this way, fasting can refresh a stalled prayer life and remind your body and soul alike that man does not live by bread alone.
