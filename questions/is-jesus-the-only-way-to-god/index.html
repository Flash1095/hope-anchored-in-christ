Is Jesus the only way to God?

Is Jesus the only way to God?

Short Answer

According to Jesus' own words, He is the way, the truth, and the life, and no one comes to the Father except through Him. Christians proclaim this not from arrogance but from gratitude for the unique rescue God provided in Christ.

Detailed Answer

Few of Jesus' sayings are quoted more, or questioned more, than I am the way and the truth and the life. Spoken to His disciples on the night before the cross, this claim rests on who Jesus is and what He alone has done. No other founder died for the sins of the world and rose again. Salvation in the Bible is not a ladder of religions reaching up to God. It is God reaching down in the person of His Son. Acts declares there is salvation in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven by which we must be saved. This exclusivity is paired in Scripture with a wide-open invitation. Whoever believes, everyone who calls on the name of the Lord, and whosoever will may come. The narrowness is in the Savior, the wideness is in the welcome. If this claim troubles you, consider Jesus Himself, His character, His cross, His empty tomb, before dismissing it. And if you follow Him, hold this truth with humility and love, sharing the good news as an invitation to life, not a weapon in an argument.
