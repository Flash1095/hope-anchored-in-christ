Do people become angels when they die?

Question
Do people become angels when they die?
Short Answer
No. People do not become angels when they die. Angels and humans are different kinds of creatures, and the Bible promises believers something better than becoming angels: resurrection life as fully human, fully restored people in God's new creation.
Detailed Answer
It is a comforting thought, and you will hear it at funerals, but Scripture does not teach it. Angels are spiritual beings God created to serve Him (Hebrews 1:14), while humans are made in God's image from dust and breath, body and soul together. Jesus said that in the resurrection people will be 'like the angels' in one specific way — they will not marry (Luke 20:36) — but 'like' is not 'become.' The Christian hope is not that we turn into angels but that we are raised: Paul describes a resurrection body, imperishable and glorious, in 1 Corinthians 15. Your loved ones in Christ are not floating spirits with wings; they are people, known and loved by God, awaiting the day when He makes all things new and they live with Him in resurrected life. That hope is more personal, and more wonderful, than becoming an angel.
