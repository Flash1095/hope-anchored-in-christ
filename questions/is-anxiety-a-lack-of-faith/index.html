Is anxiety a lack of faith?

Question
Is anxiety a lack of faith?
Short Answer
No. Anxiety is not simply a lack of faith, and feeling anxious does not mean you have failed God. Faith and anxiety often live side by side: many faithful people in Scripture were anxious, and God's answer to them was comfort and presence, not shame.
Detailed Answer
It is easy to read 'do not be anxious' (Philippians 4:6) as a scolding, but Paul wrote those words as an invitation, not an accusation: bring everything to God in prayer, and His peace will guard your heart. The Psalms are full of anxious prayers (Psalm 42:5), Jesus Himself was sorrowful and troubled in Gethsemane (Matthew 26:38), and Paul admitted to daily pressure and anxiety for the churches (2 Corinthians 11:28). Anxiety can be a signal worth listening to — sometimes it points to something that needs prayer, rest, wise counsel, or professional help, and seeking help is not unfaithful. Faith does not mean never feeling afraid; it means bringing your fear to a God who is bigger than it, again and again, and trusting Him even while your heart is still racing. If anxiety is overwhelming your daily life, talking with a trusted pastor, counselor, or doctor is a wise and faithful step.
