How do I share my faith without being pushy?

Question
How do I share my faith without being pushy?
Short Answer
Share your faith the way Peter describes: with gentleness and respect, ready to give a reason for your hope (1 Peter 3:15). Listening well, telling your own story, and letting the other person set the pace is not compromise — it is love.
Detailed Answer
Pushy evangelism usually comes from pressure: feeling that you must close the deal. The New Testament picture is different. Peter says to be prepared to explain your hope 'with gentleness and respect' (1 Peter 3:15) — the manner matters as much as the message. Paul says to let your conversation be 'full of grace, seasoned with salt' (Colossians 4:6), and to make the most of every opportunity, not manufacture one. Practically, that means: listen before you speak, and ask what the other person actually believes; share your own story — what Jesus has done for you — rather than arguing them into a corner; answer honest questions honestly, and admit what you do not know; invite, don't corner ('Would you like to read one of the Gospels with me sometime?'); and trust the Holy Spirit with the outcome. You are responsible for being faithful and kind; God is responsible for opening hearts. People can tell the difference between someone who wants to win an argument and someone who genuinely cares about them.
