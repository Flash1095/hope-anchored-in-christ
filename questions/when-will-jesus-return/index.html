When will Jesus return?

Question
When will Jesus return?
Short Answer
No one knows — and Jesus said so plainly: 'about that day or hour no one knows, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, but only the Father' (Matthew 24:36). Anyone who claims to know the date is contradicting Jesus Himself.
Detailed Answer

Jesus gave signs of His return — wars, famines, earthquakes, persecution, and the gospel preached to all nations (Matthew 24:6-14) — but He refused to give a date, and He warned against those who would: 'See that no one leads you astray' (Matthew 24:4). History is littered with failed predictions, from ancient sects to modern bestsellers, and every one of them has damaged people's faith. Peter explains the delay with mercy: 'The Lord is not slow in keeping his promise... instead he is patient with you, not wanting anyone to perish, but everyone to come to repentance' (2 Peter 3:9). Acts adds the right focus: the disciples asked about timing, and Jesus answered, 'It is not for you to know the times or dates... but you will receive power... and you will be my witnesses' (Acts 1:7-8). The Christian posture toward the return is readiness, not calculation: live faithfully, share the gospel urgently, and keep watch (Matthew 25:13). You do not need to know when He is coming; you need to be ready whenever He comes — and readiness looks like ordinary faithfulness today.
