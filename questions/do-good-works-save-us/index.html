Do good works save us?

Question
Do good works save us?
Short Answer
No, good works do not save us; we are justified by grace through faith in Christ alone. Good works matter deeply as the fruit and evidence of salvation, the grateful response of a life God has already rescued.
Detailed Answer
Do good works save us? The Bible's answer is both humbling and liberating: no. Even our best deeds cannot erase sin or earn heaven, because God's standard is perfection and all have fallen short. Salvation is a gift received by faith in Christ, who did for us what we could never do for ourselves. Titus says plainly He saved us, not because of works done by us in righteousness, but according to His mercy. Yet the same Bible insists good works truly matter. Ephesians follows its famous grace passage by calling us God's workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works prepared beforehand. Works are the fruit, not the root; the evidence, not the entrance fee. A living faith will love neighbors, pursue justice, give generously, and obey Jesus. If you find yourself trying to earn God's approval, rest in the finished work of Christ today. Then let gratitude fuel a lifetime of good works done not to be loved, but because you already are. That order, grace first, works following, is what keeps our hope anchored and our service joyful.
