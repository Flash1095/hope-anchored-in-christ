What is the Trinity?

Question
What is the Trinity?
Short Answer
The Trinity means God is one God in three Persons, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, each fully God and eternally distinct. Christians worship one God, not three gods, united in essence, love, and purpose.
Detailed Answer

The Trinity is one of Christianity's most beautiful and mysterious truths. The Bible teaches there is one God, yet the Father is God, the Son is God, and the Holy Spirit is God. At Jesus' baptism, all three Persons appear together: the Son in the water, the Spirit descending like a dove, and the Father speaking from heaven. This is not three gods working together, nor one God wearing three masks. It is one God in eternal relationship, love, and unity. Why does this matter for our hope? Because salvation is the work of the whole Trinity. The Father plans and sends, the Son accomplishes redemption on the cross, and the Spirit applies that work to our hearts and assures us we belong. The Trinity also shows us God was never lonely or needy. Love is at the very heart of who He is. When you pray, worship, or feel alone, remember you are known and loved by this triune God who invites you into fellowship with Himself, a hope that holds steady in every season.
