Can we earn God's grace?

Can we earn God's grace?

Short Answer

No. Grace, by definition, cannot be earned — if it could be, it would not be grace. Paul is blunt: if salvation comes by grace, 'it cannot be based on works; if it were, grace would no longer be grace' (Romans 11:6).

Detailed Answer

Every religion except Christianity offers some version of earning God's favor: be good enough, pray enough, give enough. The gospel says the opposite: 'it is by grace you have been saved, through faith — and this is not from yourselves, it is the gift of God — not by works, so that no one can boast' (Ephesians 2:8-9). Paul learned this the hard way: as a Pharisee he had the finest religious resume of his day, and he counted it all as loss compared to knowing Christ (Philippians 3:7-9). That does not make good works unimportant — Paul immediately adds that we are 'created in Christ Jesus to do good works' (Ephesians 2:10). But the order matters forever: works are the fruit of grace, never the root of it. Trying to earn grace actually rejects it, because it says Christ's work was not enough. The Christian life begins by receiving what you could never deserve, and then grows by living gratefully in what you have freely received.
