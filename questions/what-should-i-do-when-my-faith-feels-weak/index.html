What should I do when my faith feels weak?

What should I do when my faith feels weak?

Short Answer

When faith feels weak, do what the father of the demon-possessed boy did: pray honestly, 'I do believe; help me overcome my unbelief!' (Mark 9:24). Weak faith is still faith, and God strengthens it through His Word, His people, and ordinary faithfulness.

Detailed Answer

Feeling weak in faith is a normal part of the Christian life, not proof that you have lost it. Start with honesty: tell God exactly where you are, the way the psalmists did (Psalm 13). Then feed your faith: Paul says faith comes by hearing the message about Christ (Romans 10:17), so keep reading Scripture even when it feels dry — feelings often follow obedience, not the other way around. Stay close to God's people: Hebrews urges us not to give up meeting together, because encouragement is a lifeline when your own grip feels weak (Hebrews 10:24-25). Keep doing the next faithful thing — pray short prayers, serve someone, confess sin quickly — and be patient with yourself: Jude urges believers to build themselves up in the faith and pray in the Holy Spirit (Jude 20-21). And remember the object of faith matters more than its size: Jesus said faith as small as a mustard seed is enough, because it rests on a great God (Matthew 17:20). Weak faith in a strong Savior is still saving faith.
