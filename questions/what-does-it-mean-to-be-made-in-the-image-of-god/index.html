What does it mean to be made in the image of God?

Question
What does it mean to be made in the image of God?
Short Answer
Being made in God's image means every person reflects God in dignity, reason, creativity, morality, and relationship, and possesses immeasurable worth. It is the foundation for human value, purpose, and how we must treat one another.
Detailed Answer
Few truths carry more hope and weight than this one: you are made in the image of God. Genesis introduces humanity not as an afterthought but as creation's crown, formed to reflect God's character into the world. The image includes our capacity to reason, create, choose, love, and relate, to God, to each other, and to the earth we are called to steward. Above all, it confers dignity. Every person, regardless of age, ability, background, or stage of life, bears God's image and therefore possesses inviolable worth. This is why Scripture forbids murder and cursing others with the same breath used for praise, and why Jesus measures greatness by serving the least. Sin has marred the image but not erased it, like a cracked mirror that still reflects. And in Christ, the perfect image of God, believers are being renewed into that likeness day by day. Let this truth shape how you see yourself on hard days and how you treat everyone you meet. The image of God in others is a reason for reverence, and in Christ, the image in you is being restored to its original radiance.
