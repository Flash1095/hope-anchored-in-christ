Is the Bible reliable and true?

Question
Is the Bible reliable and true?
Short Answer
Christians believe the Bible is God's inspired and trustworthy Word, faithfully preserved through thousands of manuscripts. Its historical accuracy, fulfilled prophecy, internal unity, and life-changing power support confidence that it is reliable and true.
Detailed Answer

Is the Bible reliable? Consider how unusually well it has been preserved. The New Testament survives in thousands of Greek manuscripts, far more than any other ancient work, with copies remarkably close to the events they describe. Discoveries like the Dead Sea Scrolls confirmed the Old Testament text was transmitted with extraordinary care over a thousand years. The Bible also demonstrates a striking unity: dozens of authors across fifteen centuries and multiple continents telling one coherent story of creation, fall, redemption, and restoration, centered on Jesus. Archaeology has repeatedly corroborated its people, places, and customs, and hundreds of prophecies, especially concerning Christ, found precise fulfillment. Ultimately, Christians trust Scripture because Jesus did. He quoted it as God's Word, staked His mission on it, and rose from the dead vindicating His claims. Reliability questions deserve honest, unhurried investigation, and this blog exists partly for that exploration. Start by reading a Gospel with fresh eyes and testing its words in your own life. Generations have found that the Bible reads us even as we read it, and that its promises hold firm enough to anchor a whole life upon.
