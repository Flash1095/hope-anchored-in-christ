What is biblical wisdom and how do I get it?

Question
What is biblical wisdom and how do I get it?
Short Answer
Biblical wisdom is skill for living God's way: seeing life truthfully and choosing what is right. It begins with reverence for God — 'The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom' (Proverbs 9:10) — and it is received by asking God for it.
Detailed Answer
Wisdom in the Bible is not mainly IQ or information; it is applied truth — knowing the right thing and doing it at the right time. Proverbs is the textbook: wisdom calls out in the streets (Proverbs 1:20), it is more precious than rubies (Proverbs 8:11), and it shows up in ordinary choices about money, words, work, and relationships. How do you get it? First, by fearing the Lord — taking God seriously as the source of truth (Proverbs 9:10). Second, by asking: 'If any of you lacks wisdom, you should ask God, who gives generously to all without finding fault' (James 1:5). Third, by digging in Scripture: 'The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge' (Proverbs 1:7), and Proverbs 2 promises that if you seek wisdom like silver, 'then you will understand the fear of the LORD' (Proverbs 2:1-6). Fourth, by listening to wise counsel (Proverbs 15:22) and learning from others' mistakes. And supremely, wisdom is a person: Paul says Christ 'has become for us wisdom from God' (1 Corinthians 1:30). To grow wise is to grow closer to Jesus, whose life shows what wisdom looks like in flesh and blood.
