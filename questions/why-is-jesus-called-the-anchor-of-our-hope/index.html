Why is Jesus called the Anchor of our hope?

Why is Jesus called the Anchor of our hope?

Short Answer

Hebrews 6:19 calls our hope in Christ an anchor for the soul, firm and secure. An anchor holds a ship steady in a storm, and Jesus holds us steady because His promises and His finished work do not drift.

Detailed Answer

This blog takes its name from Hebrews 6:19, and for good reason. We have this hope as an anchor for the soul, firm and secure. In the ancient world, an anchor kept a ship from drifting onto the rocks when winds and currents pulled hard. The writer of Hebrews says our hope in Jesus works the same way for us. This hope is not wishful thinking or optimism that circumstances will improve. It is confidence anchored in something outside the storm itself: Jesus, who has entered heaven on our behalf and whose promises are backed by God's unchanging oath. Anchors work precisely when the sea is rough. You do not need one on calm days. If your life feels unsettled today, that does not mean your hope has failed. It means your Anchor is doing His work. Hold fast to Christ through prayer, Scripture, and community. He will not drag, slip, or let go, and neither will the hope that is secured in Him.
