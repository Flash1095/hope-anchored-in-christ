What will heaven be like?

Question
What will heaven be like?
Short Answer
Heaven, in the Bible's fullest picture, is not floating on clouds but life with God in a renewed creation: no more death, tears, or pain, in resurrected bodies, on a new earth where God dwells with His people forever (Revelation 21:1-5).
Detailed Answer

Popular pictures of heaven — harps, clouds, endless boredom — owe more to cartoons than to Scripture. The Bible's final vision is far richer: 'a new heaven and a new earth' (Revelation 21:1), where the holy city comes down to us and God Himself lives with His people. There will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain (Revelation 21:4). Jesus promised His followers a prepared place: 'My Father's house has many rooms... I am going there to prepare a place for you' (John 14:2-3). And our life there will be embodied, not ghostly: Paul says our lowly bodies will be transformed to be like Christ's glorious body (Philippians 3:20-21), and the resurrection body he describes is imperishable, glorious, and powerful (1 Corinthians 15:42-44). What will we do there? Worship (Revelation 22:3), serve, feast (Revelation 19:9), and enjoy God's presence face to face (1 Corinthians 13:12) — the best parts of this life, healed and completed, without the sin and sorrow that mar them now. Heaven is not an escape from creation; it is creation restored, with God at the center.
