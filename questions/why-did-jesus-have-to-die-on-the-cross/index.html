Why did Jesus have to die on the cross?

Question
Why did Jesus have to die on the cross?
Short Answer
Jesus died on the cross to pay the penalty for our sin and reconcile us to a holy God. His death was substitutionary and loving, He took our place, so that everyone who trusts in Him receives forgiveness and life.
Detailed Answer

Why the cross? The Bible holds two truths together: God is holy and just, so sin cannot simply be ignored, and God is loving, so He Himself provides the way of rescue. On the cross, Jesus, who knew no sin, bore our sin in His body so that we might become the righteousness of God. This is substitution at the heart of the gospel. He took our place, endured the judgment we deserved, and cried, It is finished, because the debt was fully paid. The cross also displays God's love more clearly than anywhere else. While we were still sinners, Christ died for us. That means your forgiveness does not depend on cleaning yourself up first. It depends on receiving what Jesus has already accomplished. The resurrection confirms the cross worked. Death could not hold Him, and it will not have the final word over those who belong to Him. When guilt or shame feels heavy, look to the cross. There you see both how seriously God takes sin and how deeply He loves sinners, a love strong enough to anchor your hope forever.
