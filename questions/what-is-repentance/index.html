What is repentance?

Question
What is repentance?
Short Answer
Repentance is turning away from sin and turning toward God with a changed heart and mind. It involves honest sorrow for sin, confession, and a new direction empowered by grace, not just feeling sorry.
Detailed Answer

Repentance sounds like a heavy word, but in the Bible it is actually a door to joy and freedom. To repent means to change your mind about sin and self-rule, agree with God about what is true, and turn in a new direction. It is more than guilt or embarrassment at being caught. David's great prayer in Psalm 51 shows real repentance: honest confession, a plea for mercy, and a desire for a clean heart only God can create. Jesus began His preaching with the call to repent and believe the good news, pairing turning and trusting together. Repentance is also not a one-time event for Christians. We return to it daily, bringing fresh failures to the cross and receiving fresh mercy. Genuine repentance bears fruit, restored relationships, changed habits, generosity where there was greed, honesty where there was deceit. If you need to repent today, do not delay or despair. God promises that if we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive and cleanse us. Grace meets the turning heart with open arms, every single time.
