What is the church supposed to do?

Question
What is the church supposed to do?
Short Answer
The church exists to worship God, grow disciples, love one another, and carry the gospel to the world. The earliest church in Acts shows the pattern: devoted to teaching, fellowship, prayer, and sharing life together — and God added to their number daily.
Detailed Answer
Jesus gave the church its mission in the Great Commission: make disciples of all nations, baptizing them and teaching them to obey everything He commanded (Matthew 28:19-20). Acts 2 shows what that looked like in practice: the believers devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching, to fellowship, to the breaking of bread, and to prayer (Acts 2:42), sharing their possessions with anyone in need. Paul adds that Christ gave the church leaders and gifts 'to equip his people for works of service, so that the body of Christ may be built up' (Ephesians 4:11-12). So a healthy church does several things at once: it gathers to worship, it teaches the Word, it cares for its members like a family, it serves its neighbors, and it sends people out with the gospel (Acts 1:8). No single program covers all of that, and no church does it perfectly — but wherever God's people worship, grow, love, and go, the church is doing what it was made to do.
