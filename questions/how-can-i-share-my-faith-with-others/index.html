How can I share my faith with others?

Question
How can I share my faith with others?
Short Answer
Share your faith by living a credible, loving life, praying for people, and gently telling what Jesus has done for you, using Scripture's gospel clearly. The results belong to God; our calling is faithful, respectful witness.
Detailed Answer
Sharing your faith does not require a theology degree or a confrontational personality. The New Testament pattern is refreshingly human: ordinary people telling what Jesus did for them. The healed demoniac was sent home to declare how much God had done. The Samaritan woman simply said, Come, see a man who told me everything, and a town came to listen. Start there. Pray regularly for a few specific people, invest in genuine friendship without hidden agendas, and serve practical needs. When God opens natural moments, perhaps in conversations about hope, struggle, or purpose, share your story briefly: life before Christ, how you met Him, and the difference He makes now. Learn to explain the gospel itself simply: God, our sin, Christ's cross and resurrection, and the call to repent and believe. Peter advises doing this with gentleness and respect, ready to give a reason for the hope within you. That hope is your best credential. Invite people to church, offer to read a Gospel together, and leave outcomes to God, who alone opens hearts. Some plant, some water, but God gives the growth, and He delights to use willing, loving, ordinary witnesses like you.
