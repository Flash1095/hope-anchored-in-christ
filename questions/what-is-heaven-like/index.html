What is heaven like?

Question
What is heaven like?
Short Answer
Heaven is God's renewed creation where His people dwell with Him forever, free from sin, sorrow, death, and tears, in resurrected bodies and joyful community. The Bible pictures it as a garden-city, a marriage feast, and home.
Detailed Answer

What is heaven really like? Scripture answers with images rather than blueprints, and the images are glorious. Revelation's finale shows not disembodied spirits on clouds but a new heaven and new earth, with God's city, the New Jerusalem, descending like a bride: garden and city combined, river and tree of life restored from Eden, and God Himself dwelling with His people. Every curse is reversed there. No more death, mourning, crying, or pain. Tears are wiped away by God's own hand. Believers receive resurrection bodies like Christ's, real, recognizable, and incorruptible, suited for an embodied eternity of worship, work, feasting, and fellowship without sin's shadow. Jesus called it His Father's house with many rooms and paradise itself. Importantly, heaven's center is not golden streets but a Person. The Lamb is its light, and seeing God face to face is the joy from which all other joys flow. This hope is meant to work in the present. It steadies martyrs, comforts the grieving, and loosens our grip on what moth and rust destroy. If you belong to Christ, your future is not vague optimism. It is a prepared place, a prepared people, and a King who says, Behold, I am making all things new.
