Why does God want our worship?

Question
Why does God want our worship?
Short Answer
God does not need our worship — He is complete without it. He wants it because worship is good for us and glorious for Him: it aligns our hearts with reality, declaring that the God who made and saved us is worthy.
Detailed Answer

Paul told the Athenians that God 'is not served by human hands, as if he needed anything' (Acts 17:25). God was perfectly happy before creation, in the eternal fellowship of Father, Son, and Spirit. So why does He seek worshipers? Jesus said, 'a time is coming and has now come when the true worshipers will worship the Father in the Spirit and in truth, for they are the kind of worshipers the Father seeks' (John 4:23). He seeks worshipers not out of need but out of love: worship is how creatures rightly relate to their Creator — acknowledging truth (He is God, we are not), receiving grace, and finding joy. Psalm 100 commands joyful worship because 'the LORD is good and his love endures forever' — worship is the fitting response to goodness. And Revelation shows worship as heaven's eternal activity (Revelation 4:11): 'You are worthy, our Lord and God, to receive glory and honor and power.' When we worship, we join that reality, we re-center our hearts away from idols, and we become more like what we adore. God wants our worship because He wants us — and worship is how we draw near to Him.
