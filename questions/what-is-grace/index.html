What is grace?

Question
What is grace?
Short Answer
Grace is God's unearned favor and kindness toward sinners, freely given in Christ, not earned by good works. It is God giving us what we do not deserve, forgiveness, new life, and daily help, because of His love.
Detailed Answer

Grace may be the most hope-filled word in the Bible. It means God gives generously to people who have not earned it and cannot repay it. Ephesians says we are saved by grace through faith, and that it is the gift of God, not a result of works. That overturns the exhausting idea that we must be good enough for God to love us. In Christ, God moves toward us first. Grace pardons our sin through the cross, but it does not stop there. The same grace teaches us, sustains us in weakness, and meets us in daily need. Paul heard Jesus say, My grace is sufficient for you, when his prayer for relief was not answered as he wished. Grace is therefore both rescue and strength. If you feel unworthy today, grace has good news for you. Your standing with God rests on Christ's finished work, not your performance. Receive it with open hands, thank Him for it, and let it reshape how you treat others, freely giving the kindness you have freely received.
