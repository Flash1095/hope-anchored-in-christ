What are spiritual gifts?

What are spiritual gifts?

Short Answer

Spiritual gifts are abilities given by the Holy Spirit to every believer for serving others and building up the church. They include gifts like teaching, mercy, hospitality, leadership, encouragement, and giving, all to be used in love.

Detailed Answer

Every believer in Jesus has been given at least one spiritual gift, a Spirit-empowered capacity to serve others for the common good. Paul compares the church to a body: different members, different functions, one Spirit, all needed. The New Testament lists gifts such as teaching, prophecy, serving, encouragement, giving, leadership, mercy, wisdom, faith, healing, and hospitality, and the lists vary, suggesting they are samples rather than an exhaustive catalog. Gifts are not trophies of spiritual status or tools for self-promotion. They are stewardships, given so that others are strengthened, comforted, and built up in faith. Discovering your gifts usually happens in community: serve where there is need, notice where God brings fruit and joy, and listen to what other believers affirm in you. If you feel you have nothing to offer, Scripture gently disagrees. The quiet gift of mercy at a hospital bedside matters as much as the public gift of teaching. Ask the Spirit to show you your place, step out in humble service, and watch how God uses ordinary people with surrendered gifts to anchor others in hope.
