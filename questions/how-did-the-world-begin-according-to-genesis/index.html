How did the world begin according to Genesis?

Question
How did the world begin according to Genesis?
Short Answer
Genesis teaches God created everything from nothing by His word in the beginning, forming a good, ordered world and crowning it with humanity in His image. The central claim is who created, God, and that His creation is good and purposeful.
Detailed Answer
Genesis opens with some of the most famous words ever written: In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth. Before debating timelines and mechanisms, notice what the text most wants us to know. Creation has an Author. The universe is not an accident, God is eternal and self-existent, and everything He made was declared good, finally very good. By His word alone, light pierces darkness, seas gather, life flourishes, and humanity is formed in God's own image, male and female, with dignity, purpose, and a mandate to cultivate the earth. Genesis 1 and 2 give complementary portraits, one sweeping and ordered, one intimate, showing God both as transcendent Creator and as the Lord who walks with His creatures. Christians discuss the age of the earth and the length of the days in good faith, but they unite on the anchor truths: God made all things, He made them well, and He made us for Himself. That foundation steadies everything else. Your life is not random. You were crafted by a wise and loving Maker, in a world that still bears His fingerprints, and the story that starts in a garden ends in a restored creation where He dwells with His people forever.
