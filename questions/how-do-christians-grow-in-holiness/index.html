How do Christians grow in holiness?

Question
How do Christians grow in holiness?
Short Answer
Christians grow in holiness by the Spirit's power through ordinary means: God's Word, prayer, worship, community, and obedience — over a lifetime, not overnight. Paul calls it working out what God is working in (Philippians 2:12-13).
Detailed Answer

Holiness is not achieved by trying harder in your own strength; it is the Spirit's work, and our part is to cooperate with it. Peter lists the pattern: add to faith goodness, knowledge, self-control, perseverance, godliness, mutual affection, and love (2 Peter 1:5-8) — growth is gradual and cumulative. Paul describes the engine: 'be transformed by the renewing of your mind' (Romans 12:2), which happens as Scripture reshapes what you believe and want. Galatians describes the fruit: as we 'walk by the Spirit,' love, joy, peace, patience, and self-control grow (Galatians 5:16-25). Practically, growth comes through rhythms: reading Scripture daily, even briefly; praying honestly; worshiping with God's people; confessing sin quickly rather than hiding it; and obeying in small things — the conversation you handle graciously, the money you give generously, the apology you make promptly. Hebrews adds community: 'spur one another on toward love and good deeds' (Hebrews 10:24). Expect slow progress with setbacks; sanctification is a direction, not a destination reached in this life. The question is not 'Are you perfect?' but 'Are you growing?' — and growth, however slow, is evidence of grace at work.
