How can I know I am truly saved?

Question
How can I know I am truly saved?
Short Answer
You can know you are saved by resting on God's promises in Christ, confirmed by the Spirit's witness and a transformed life. Assurance grows as you trust Jesus' finished work, not your fluctuating feelings.
Detailed Answer

Many sincere believers wrestle with the question, Am I really saved? John wrote his first letter precisely so that those who believe in the Son of God may know they have eternal life. That knowledge rests first on God's promise, not our performance. Jesus said whoever hears His word and believes has eternal life and will not come into judgment. Assurance also has confirming signs: the Holy Spirit testifies with our spirit that we are God's children, we grow in love for God and fellow believers, we grieve over sin and return in repentance, and we keep trusting Christ over time. None of these signs will be perfect this side of heaven, and feelings rise and fall with health, seasons, and circumstances. That is why we anchor assurance in the cross. On doubtful days, preach the gospel to yourself: Christ died for sinners, rose again, and promises never to cast out anyone who comes to Him. Bring your doubts into the light with a trusted believer or pastor, stay close to Scripture and church, and keep coming to Jesus. He delights to assure the hearts of His children.
