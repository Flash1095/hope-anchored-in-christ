Does God answer prayer?

Question
Does God answer prayer?
Short Answer
Yes, God hears and answers prayer according to His wisdom, timing, and good purposes, sometimes with yes, sometimes with wait, and sometimes with a loving no. Prayer truly matters and changes things because God works through it.
Detailed Answer

Does God really answer prayer? Scripture answers with a confident yes, while also treating us as adults about timing and outcomes. God invites us to ask, seek, and knock, promising the Father gives good gifts to His children. James says the prayer of a righteous person has great power. Yet every honest believer knows the ache of prayers that seem unanswered, needs that persist, and heavens that feel silent. The Bible does not hide this tension. Paul prayed three times for his thorn to be removed and received grace to endure instead of removal. Jesus in Gethsemane prayed for the cup to pass and surrendered to the Father's will. God's answers, yes, wait, and no, all flow from the same love and wisdom. What we can be certain of is that no prayer is wasted, no tear is unseen, and our Father is never indifferent or incapable. Keep praying with both boldness and surrender. Ask largely, trust deeply, and anchor your hope not in getting the exact outcome you envisioned, but in the character of the God who hears, who is good, and who will do what is ultimately best.
