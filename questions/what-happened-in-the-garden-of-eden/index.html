What happened in the Garden of Eden?

Question
What happened in the Garden of Eden?
Short Answer
In Eden, Adam and Eve lived in perfect fellowship with God until the serpent tempted them to eat the forbidden fruit. Their disobedience, the Fall, brought sin, shame, and death into the world, yet God immediately promised a Redeemer.
Detailed Answer
The Garden of Eden was humanity's first home, a place of beauty, abundance, meaningful work, and unbroken fellowship with God. Adam and Eve lacked nothing, yet the serpent sowed a subtle doubt: Did God really say? Tempted to seize wisdom and autonomy on their own terms, they ate from the one tree God had forbidden, the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. Immediately, shame replaced innocence. They hid from the God they once walked with, blamed each other, and learned that sin brings death, fractured relationships, painful toil, and exile from the garden. Every human sorrow traces back to this rupture, and every honest heart recognizes its pattern in their own choices. But listen for the grace woven into the story. God comes seeking, calling, Where are you? He clothes their shame, disciplines in mercy, and speaks the Bible's first gospel promise: the offspring of the woman will crush the serpent's head. Eden ends with cherubim guarding the way, but the story moves toward Jesus, the second Adam, who obeyed where Adam fell and who reopens paradise to all who trust Him. Even at the Fall, hope was already anchored in a promise.
