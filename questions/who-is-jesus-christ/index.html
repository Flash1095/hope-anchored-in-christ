Who is Jesus Christ?

Who is Jesus Christ?

Short Answer

Jesus is the Son of God, fully God and fully man, who lived a sinless life, died on the cross for our sins, and rose again. He is Savior, Lord, and the center of the Christian faith and hope.

Detailed Answer

Jesus Christ stands at the very center of the Bible's story. The Old Testament promises a coming Messiah, and the New Testament announces that Jesus is that promised One. He is fully God, the eternal Word who was with God and was God, and He is fully man, born of Mary, who knew hunger, sorrow, temptation, and suffering without sin. His life revealed God's kingdom in teaching, compassion, and miracles that healed the sick and welcomed the outcast. His death on the cross was not an accident. It was God's plan to bear our sin and reconcile us to Himself. His resurrection three days later proved His victory over sin and death and launched the early church with unshakable hope. To know Jesus is more than knowing facts about Him. It is trusting Him as Savior and following Him as Lord. Whatever questions you bring today, start with Him. In Jesus, God is not distant. He has come near, He has made a way home, and He invites everyone who believes to receive new and eternal life in His name.
