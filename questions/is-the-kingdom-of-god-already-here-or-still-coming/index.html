Is the kingdom of God already here or still coming?

Question
Is the kingdom of God already here or still coming?
Short Answer
Both. The kingdom of God has already arrived in Jesus, and it is still coming in its fullness. Theologians call this the 'already and not yet': the King has come, but His reign is not yet complete.
Detailed Answer

Jesus said both things, and both are true. On one hand, He told the Pharisees, 'The kingdom of God is in your midst' (Luke 17:21), and after casting out a demon He said, 'If I drive out demons by the Spirit of God, then the kingdom of God has come upon you' (Matthew 12:28). The King had arrived, and His reign was breaking in through healing, forgiveness, and deliverance. On the other hand, Jesus taught us to pray for the kingdom to come (Matthew 6:10), and Paul says Christ must reign 'until he has put all his enemies under his feet,' with death as the last enemy (1 Corinthians 15:24-28). Sickness, death, and evil still exist — so the kingdom is clearly not yet complete. This tension explains the Christian life: we experience real foretastes of the kingdom now — forgiveness, the Spirit's power, reconciled relationships — while still groaning for the day when God will wipe every tear away (Revelation 21:1-5). Living in the 'already and not yet' means neither despair (the King has come) nor triumphalism (the kingdom is not yet finished) — but faithful hope in between.
