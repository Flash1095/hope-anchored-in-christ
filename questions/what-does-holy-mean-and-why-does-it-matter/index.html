What does holy mean and why does it matter?

Short Answer

Holy means set apart, pure, and wholly other, and it describes God's perfect character above all. It matters because this holy God calls His people to holiness and, in Christ, makes us holy by His Spirit.

Detailed Answer

When the Bible calls God holy, it means He is set apart in absolute purity, majesty, and goodness, unlike anything else in creation. Isaiah saw the Lord enthroned while angels cried, Holy, holy, holy, and he became painfully aware of his own sin. Holiness matters first because it tells the truth about God. He is not a slightly better version of us. He is perfect in every way, and His holiness is why sin is serious and why the cross was necessary. Yet the story does not end in distance. The holy God draws near to make His people holy. Through Christ's sacrifice we are set apart for God, and by the Spirit we grow into lives that reflect His character in love, integrity, and compassion. Holiness is not about earning approval or looking religious. It is about belonging to a holy God and becoming like Him day by day. That journey gives hope, because the God who calls you holy is also the God who empowers and completes the work He began in you.
