What is baptism and should I be baptized?

Question
What is baptism and should I be baptized?
Short Answer
Baptism is a public act of obedience symbolizing a believer's union with Christ in His death and resurrection and washing away of sins. Jesus commanded it, and every believer should be baptized as a step of following Him.
Detailed Answer

Baptism is one of the first joyful steps of obedience for a new believer. In the New Testament, those who believed in Jesus were baptized in water, publicly identifying with Him and His people. Paul pictures it beautifully: buried with Christ in baptism and raised to walk in newness of life, just as Christ was raised from the dead. Baptism does not save us by its own power. It testifies to the salvation God has already given by grace through faith, like a wedding ring declares a marriage it did not create. Jesus Himself was baptized and He commissioned His disciples to baptize others, so this is not an optional extra for committed followers. Churches differ on mode and timing for infants, and Christians discuss these with charity, but the call to be baptized as a believer is clear. If you have trusted Christ and have not been baptized, talk to a pastor or mature believer about taking that step. It is a powerful moment to declare before heaven and earth: I belong to Jesus, my old life is gone, and my hope is anchored in the risen Lord.
