Did Jesus really rise from the dead?

Question
Did Jesus really rise from the dead?
Short Answer
Yes, Christians confess Jesus rose bodily from the dead on the third day, seen by many witnesses including over 500 people. The empty tomb, transformed disciples, and birth of the church all point to a real resurrection.
Detailed Answer

The resurrection is not a decorative add-on to the Christian faith. Paul says if Christ has not been raised, our faith is futile. That is why the evidence matters and why the New Testament names witnesses. Jesus appeared to Mary Magdalene, to the disciples, to two travelers on the Emmaus road, and at one time to more than five hundred people, most of whom were still alive when Paul wrote. The tomb was empty, guarded, and sealed, yet no body was ever produced. Cowardly disciples who fled at the arrest became bold witnesses willing to suffer and die, a transformation hard to explain if they had invented the story. The resurrection also changes everything for us personally. Because Jesus lives, death is not the end, our sins are forgiven, and we have a living Savior who intercedes for us today. This is hope with a foundation in history, not just feeling. If you are exploring the faith, investigate the resurrection honestly. If you already believe, let Easter truth steady you every ordinary day: your Redeemer lives, and so will all who are united to Him.
