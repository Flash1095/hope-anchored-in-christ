What miracles did Jesus perform and why?

Question
What miracles did Jesus perform and why?
Short Answer
Jesus healed the sick, gave sight to the blind, calmed storms, fed thousands, and raised the dead. His miracles were signs of God's kingdom, revealing His compassion and authority and pointing to who He truly is.
Detailed Answer
Jesus' miracles fill the Gospels, and each one tells us something about Him. He healed lepers who were outcasts, opened blind eyes, stilled a raging storm with a word, fed a hungry crowd from a boy's small lunch, walked on water, and raised Lazarus from the tomb. John calls these miracles signs, meaning they point beyond themselves. They show that God's kingdom has come near, that Jesus has authority over disease, nature, demons, and death itself, and that He cares deeply about ordinary human suffering. The miracles were never mere spectacle. Jesus often responded to faith, touched the untouchable, and restored people to community. Yet the Gospels also note that miracles alone did not force belief. They invite a response of trust. For us today, these accounts anchor hope when we pray for healing or help. They remind us our Savior is both powerful and compassionate, able to act and willing to draw near. And they point forward to the day when He will wipe away every tear and make all things new, completing what His miracles first previewed.
