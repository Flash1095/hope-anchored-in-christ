What is the main message of the Bible?

What is the main message of the Bible?

Short Answer

The Bible's main message is the gospel: God created us, we fell into sin, and God redeems us through Jesus Christ's life, death, and resurrection. It is one story of a holy, loving God rescuing His people and restoring creation.

Detailed Answer

If you could summarize sixty-six books in one storyline, it would be this: creation, fall, redemption, and restoration. God creates a very good world and people made in His image for relationship with Him. Humanity rebels, and sin fractures everything, our bond with God, with each other, and with creation itself. But God immediately begins a rescue. He promises a Redeemer in Genesis, chooses Abraham, delivers Israel from Egypt, gives the law, raises up kings, and speaks through prophets, all pointing forward. The center of the story is Jesus: God Himself enters His world, lives the life we failed to live, dies the death we deserved, and rises to launch God's new creation. The rest of the New Testament spreads this good news, the gospel, to the nations through the church. And the story ends not with escape from the world but with its healing: a new heaven and earth where God dwells with His people and wipes away every tear. Every passage you read finds its place in this arc. Read the Bible asking where you are in the story and how it points to Jesus, and its many parts will lock together into the greatest hope the world has ever known.
