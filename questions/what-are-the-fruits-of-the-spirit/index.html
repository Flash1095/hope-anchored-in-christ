What are the fruits of the Spirit?

Question
What are the fruits of the Spirit?
Short Answer
The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control. These qualities grow in believers as the Holy Spirit transforms our character to become more like Jesus.
Detailed Answer

Paul's list of the fruit of the Spirit in Galatians 5 is one of the most loved passages in Scripture, and for good reason. Love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control describe exactly the kind of person most of us long to become and the kind of community the world desperately needs. Notice Paul says fruit, singular, not fruits we pick and choose. It is one harvest the Spirit grows as a whole. Notice also that fruit grows, slowly and organically, rather than being manufactured by sheer effort. Our part is to keep in step with the Spirit, abide in Christ, confess sin quickly, and practice these virtues in daily choices. God's part is the deep transformation no self-help program can produce. If you feel discouraged by slow growth, remember that gardeners do not shout at seeds. They water, tend, and wait. Stay close to Jesus through prayer and the Word, remain planted in Christian community, and trust the Spirit's patient work. The same God who began growing this fruit in you will bring it to fullness for His glory and others' good.
