Who is the Holy Spirit?

Short Answer

The Holy Spirit is God, the third Person of the Trinity, who convicts, regenerates, indwells, and empowers believers. He is not a force or feeling but a divine Person who guides us into truth and comforts us as Helper.

Detailed Answer

Who is the Holy Spirit? He is not an impersonal energy or a vague sense of inspiration. The Bible presents the Spirit as a divine Person who speaks, teaches, grieves, intercedes, and can be obeyed or resisted, and who is fully God alongside the Father and the Son. In creation He hovered over the waters. Through the Old Testament He empowered prophets, judges, and kings for specific tasks. At Pentecost He came in a new and permanent way to indwell every believer. Jesus called Him the Helper, the Spirit of truth, who would be with us forever, convict the world of sin, guide us into all truth, and glorify Christ. This means God is not only above us and for us. By His Spirit He is within us, present in every moment, including the lonely and ordinary ones. If you belong to Christ, you are never without God's personal presence. Take time to thank the Spirit for His nearness today, ask for His filling and guidance, and listen for His voice through Scripture. The Helper Jesus promised has come, and He will never leave you.
