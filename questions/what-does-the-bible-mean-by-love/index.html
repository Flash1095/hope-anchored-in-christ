What does the Bible mean by love?

Question
What does the Bible mean by love?
Short Answer
Biblical love is not mainly a feeling but a committed action: seeking another person's good at cost to yourself. Its definition is Jesus: 'God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son' (John 3:16).
Detailed Answer

Our culture often defines love as a feeling that comes and goes, but the Bible defines it by what it does. Paul's great chapter says love is patient, kind, not envious or boastful, not easily angered, and it 'always protects, always trusts, always hopes, always perseveres' (1 Corinthians 13:4-7). John roots it deeper: 'God is love' (1 John 4:8), and love is known by this — Jesus laid down His life for us (1 John 3:16). That means love is both a command and a gift: Jesus commands us to love God with all our heart and our neighbor as ourselves (Mark 12:30-31), and He gives us the power to do it by first loving us. Biblical love includes affection, but it is not limited to it: it feeds the hungry, forgives the offender, tells the truth kindly, and stays faithful when feelings fade. If you want to know what love means, do not look at romance novels or greeting cards — look at the cross, where love gave everything for people who had done nothing to deserve it.
