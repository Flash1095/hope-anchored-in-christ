What makes a good leader according to the Bible?

Question
What makes a good leader according to the Bible?
Short Answer
A good leader, in the Bible's picture, is a servant: someone of proven character who leads by example, puts others' good first, and follows Jesus, the Good Shepherd. Biblical leadership is measured by service, not by power.
Detailed Answer

Jesus redefined leadership completely: 'Whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant' (Mark 10:42-45). The world's leaders use power for themselves; Jesus' leaders use it for others, following His own example of giving His life as a ransom. Paul's qualifications for church leaders focus on character, not charisma: above reproach, self-controlled, hospitable, able to teach, not a lover of money (1 Timothy 3:1-7; Titus 1:7-9). The Old Testament adds the same pattern: Moses learned to share leadership rather than carry it alone (Exodus 18:21), Nehemiah refused to exploit his people even when he had the right to (Nehemiah 5:14-19), and David, for all his failures, was a man after God's own heart. Good leaders also tell the truth, admit mistakes, protect the vulnerable, and develop others rather than hoarding influence. The test is simple: does this leader look like the Good Shepherd, who lays down His life for the sheep (John 10:11) — or like a hired hand, who runs when trouble comes? Follow the shepherds; be one.
