Has Noah's ark been found?

Question
Has Noah's ark been found?
Short Answer
No verified discovery of Noah's ark has been confirmed, despite many claims and expeditions on Mount Ararat and elsewhere. Christians trust the flood account on Scripture's testimony, while remaining honest that the ark itself has not been found.
Detailed Answer

Has Noah's ark been found? The honest answer is no, not in any way verified by credible investigation, and a hope-anchored faith can afford honesty. Over the decades, expeditions to Mount Ararat in Turkey and sites in Iran have announced wood samples, shapes, and photographs, yet none has withstood scientific scrutiny or produced examinable evidence accepted by archaeologists. Wood rarely survives five thousand years of ice, weather, and volcanic activity on an exposed peak, so absence of remains is unsurprising and proves little either way. Genesis itself does not give Ararat as a single peak but the mountains of Ararat, a region. Some believers also point to widespread flood traditions and geological features as supporting a great ancient deluge, though interpretations differ. Our confidence in the flood rests where Jesus placed His: He spoke of Noah and the flood as real, and Scripture's reliability does not rise or fall with a single expedition headline. Be wary of sensational claims, whether they promise proof or disproof. The ark's greater significance endures regardless: God judges sin, God saves by grace, and in Christ He has provided the ultimate refuge into which all are invited to enter by faith today.
