What does it mean that God is sovereign?

Question
What does it mean that God is sovereign?
Short Answer
God's sovereignty means He rules over all creation with wisdom, power, and purpose, and nothing is outside His control. It does not mean we understand everything, but it does mean we can trust Him in everything.
Detailed Answer
To say God is sovereign is to say He is King over all, and there is no corner of creation or moment of your life outside His wise and loving rule. Scripture shows this from Joseph's betrayal to the cross itself, where human evil and divine purpose meet without God ever being the author of evil. Sovereignty is not cold fate. In the Bible, God's rule is always joined to His goodness, so His control becomes a comfort rather than a fear. Romans 8 promises He works all things together for good for those who love Him, not that all things are good in themselves. That distinction matters when we suffer or wait. We may not see how the pieces fit, and Scripture gives us room to lament and ask why. Yet we are never left to conclude that life is random or meaningless. The same God who holds the universe holds your story. Resting in His sovereignty means surrendering the need to control every outcome and trusting the character of the One who does, whose purposes for His people will not fail.
