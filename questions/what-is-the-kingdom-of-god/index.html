What is the kingdom of God?

Short Answer

The kingdom of God is God's reign — His rule breaking into the world through Jesus. It is not mainly a place, but a King ruling: where Jesus is obeyed and His will is done, the kingdom is present.

Detailed Answer

When Jesus began His ministry, His first message was, 'The time has come. The kingdom of God has come near. Repent and believe the good news!' (Mark 1:14-15). The kingdom is the central theme of Jesus' teaching: His parables describe it, His miracles display it, and His Sermon on the Mount explains life under it. Daniel had promised that God would set up a kingdom that would never be destroyed (Daniel 2:44), and Jesus announced that kingdom had arrived in Him — yet it is also still coming in fullness, when every knee will bow (Revelation 11:15). That is why Jesus taught us to pray, 'Your kingdom come, your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven' (Matthew 6:10): the kingdom is present wherever people submit to King Jesus, and future in the sense that His reign is not yet complete. Entering the kingdom means repenting, trusting Jesus, and living under His rule — in your home, your work, and your relationships — as a citizen of a kingdom that will never end.
