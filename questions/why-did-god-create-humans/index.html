Why did God create humans?

Why did God create humans?

Short Answer

God created humans out of His love and for His glory, to know Him, reflect His image, care for creation, and enjoy fellowship with Him forever. Our purpose is found not in ourselves but in relationship with our Creator.

Detailed Answer

Why are we here? Genesis answers with dignity rather than accident. God, who needs nothing, freely created humanity out of overflowing love, to share His life and goodness. He made us in His image to know Him personally, to reflect His character in relationships and work, and to steward His world as His representatives, cultivating gardens, building families and cultures, and caring for creatures. The Westminster tradition captures it well: our chief end is to glorify God and enjoy Him forever. Glory and joy are not competitors. We glorify God most as we find our deepest satisfaction in Him. Sin distorted this purpose, turning us inward, but it did not delete it. That is why achievements without God still leave an ache, and why Augustine prayed that our hearts are restless until they rest in Him. In Christ, our original purpose is restored. We are reconciled to God, renewed in His image, and sent back into the world as His ambassadors. If you are searching for meaning today, start here: you were made by Love, for love, to know your Maker and make Him known. In that purpose, your life has eternal significance.
