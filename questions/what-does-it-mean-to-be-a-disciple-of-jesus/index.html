What does it mean to be a disciple of Jesus?

Question
What does it mean to be a disciple of Jesus?
Short Answer
A disciple is a learner and follower of Jesus: someone who trusts Him, learns His teaching, and shapes their whole life around obeying Him. Discipleship is not a class you complete; it is a life you live.
Detailed Answer

In Jesus' day, a disciple was someone who attached themselves to a teacher, lived with them, and learned to live like them. Jesus used the same picture: 'Whoever wants to be my disciple must deny themselves and take up their cross daily and follow me' (Luke 9:23). That means discipleship touches everything — how you spend money, treat enemies, raise children, and spend Sundays. John adds a test: 'If you hold to my teaching, you are really my disciples' (John 8:31). Notice the order: discipleship starts with grace — Jesus calls people before they are ready — and grows through obedience, not perfection. Disciples stumble (Peter did, publicly), repent, and keep following. It is also never solitary: Jesus gave His disciples to one another, to learn together in community. To be a disciple is simply to say, 'Jesus, You are my Teacher and my Lord, and I am learning to live Your way, for the rest of my life.'
