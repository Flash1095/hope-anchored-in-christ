How can I hear God's voice?

Question
How can I hear God's voice?
Short Answer
God speaks primarily through Scripture, and also guides through His Spirit, prayer, wise counsel, and circumstances. Hearing God grows as we slow down, open the Bible expectantly, obey what we know, and test impressions against His Word.
Detailed Answer

How can I hear God's voice? Start with the voice He has already given: Scripture. The Bible is God's breathed-out Word, and the Spirit who inspired it delights to illuminate it, convict us, comfort us, and direct our steps as we read with an obedient heart. Most guidance comes not through dramatic signs but through this steady diet of truth shaping our desires and decisions. God also guides through prayerful impressions from His Spirit, the wise counsel of mature believers, and providential circumstances, but these are always tested against Scripture, never above it. God will never contradict His written Word. Practically, hearing grows in quiet. Jesus often withdrew to desolate places to pray, and we need similar unhurried space away from noise. Try reading a short passage slowly, asking what it reveals about God and what response it requires, then sit in silence and journal what comes. And remember, God is a Father, not a reluctant whisperer trying to frustrate you. If you sincerely seek Him, walk in the light you have, and stay close to His people, you can trust Him to guide you in His timing.
