What does it mean to be filled with the Spirit?

What does it mean to be filled with the Spirit?

Short Answer

Being filled with the Spirit means being continually controlled, empowered, and refreshed by the Holy Spirit's presence. It shows in worship, gratitude, humble relationships, and bold, loving witness to Jesus.

Detailed Answer

To be filled with the Spirit is to live under the Holy Spirit's gracious influence, the way a sail is filled with wind and moved forward. Paul contrasts being filled with the Spirit with drunkenness, which controls a person for harm, to show that the Spirit's filling brings clarity, joy, and self-control instead. In Ephesians, a Spirit-filled life sings with gratitude, submits to one another in love, and overflows in healthy homes and relationships. In Acts, Spirit-filled believers speak God's word with boldness and endure hardship with surprising joy. Being filled is not a one-time achievement for an elite class of Christians. The grammar of Paul's command suggests an ongoing reality: keep on being filled. We pursue it the normal biblical way, through prayer, Scripture, repentance, obedience, and worshiping community, asking the Father who loves to give the Spirit to those who ask Him. If you feel spiritually dry today, do not strive in shame. Come thirsty to Jesus, confess what has crowded out His voice, and ask afresh for the Spirit's filling. He delights to renew His people day by day.
