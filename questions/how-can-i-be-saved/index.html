How can I be saved?

Question
How can I be saved?
Short Answer
You are saved by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, trusting His death and resurrection for the forgiveness of your sins. Confess Him as Lord, believe in your heart, repent, and receive the new life He freely gives.
Detailed Answer

How can I be saved? The Bible's answer is wonderfully clear and deeply personal. We are saved by grace through faith, not by our works, rituals, or moral record. Salvation begins with honest need: all have sinned and fall short of God's glory. Then comes the good news: Christ died for our sins and rose again. Our response is to repent, turning from sin toward God, and to believe, entrusting ourselves to Jesus as Savior and Lord. Romans says everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved, and that if you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved. Water baptism, church life, and good works follow as the fruit and obedience of new life, not its price. If you are ready, you can pray today in your own words: acknowledge your sin, thank Jesus for the cross, and surrender your life to Him. Then tell another Christian, get a Bible, and connect with a local church. The God who saved you will also keep you, and your hope will rest on His promise, not your perfection.
