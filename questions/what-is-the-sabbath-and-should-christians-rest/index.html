What is the Sabbath and should Christians rest?

What is the Sabbath and should Christians rest?

Short Answer

The Sabbath was God's gift of rest to Israel, rooted in creation, pointing to our need for rhythmic rest and trust. Christians honor its principle by regular rest and worship, celebrating the finished work of Christ, our ultimate Sabbath rest.

Detailed Answer

Rest is not a reward for the finished. In God's design, it is the starting rhythm of creation itself. God rested on the seventh day, blessed it, and later commanded Israel to remember the Sabbath as a sign of two liberations: creation and the Exodus. A people who had been slaves, valued only for output, were ordered to stop, delight, and trust that God provides even when they do not produce. That remains a radical, merciful word in an exhausted age. Christians relate to the Sabbath day in different ways, most gathering for worship on Sunday, the Lord's Day of resurrection, while agreeing on the abiding principle: regular, protected rest for body and soul, worship, mercy, and delight. Jesus declared Himself Lord of the Sabbath and invited the weary to find rest in Him, and Hebrews says a Sabbath rest still awaits God's people in the world to come. Practically, build a weekly rhythm that stops: unhurried worship, a table with people you love, a walk, a nap, screens down, work truly laid aside. Rest is an act of faith that declares God runs the world without your exhaustion. Receive it as the gift it is, and let it anchor your week in grace.
