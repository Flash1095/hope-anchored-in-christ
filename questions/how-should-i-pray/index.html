How should I pray?

How should I pray?

Short Answer

Pray honestly and simply, as Jesus taught in the Lord's Prayer: honor God, seek His kingdom, ask for daily needs, confess sin, and forgive others. God hears heartfelt prayer, not perfect wording or long speeches.

Detailed Answer

Many people avoid prayer because they fear doing it wrong. Jesus removes that fear with a simple, beautiful pattern we call the Lord's Prayer. Begin with relationship and reverence: Our Father in heaven, hallowed be Your name. Prayer starts by remembering who God is and that we come as His beloved children. Then align your heart with His purposes, Your kingdom come, before bringing your needs: daily bread for today, forgiveness for sin as we forgive others, and protection from temptation and evil. Jesus also taught us to pray in secret without showy repetition, to persist like a friend knocking at midnight, and to ask in His name according to God's will. There is no sacred formula or required posture. You can pray aloud or silently, kneeling or walking, in written words or in tears. What matters is honesty, dependence, and regularity. If you are starting out, try five unhurried minutes: praise God for one attribute, thank Him for one gift, confess one sin, and ask for one need. Prayer is not performing for God. It is communing with a Father who already knows, already cares, and delights to hear your voice.
