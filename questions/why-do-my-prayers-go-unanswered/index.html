Why do my prayers go unanswered?

Question
Why do my prayers go unanswered?
Short Answer
Prayers may seem unanswered because of God's better timing, a wiser no, unseen spiritual realities, or our need to persevere, and sometimes the reason remains hidden. Scripture assures us God hears every prayer in Christ and answers according to perfect love and wisdom.
Detailed Answer

Few experiences test faith like praying earnestly for something good, healing, a child, a restored marriage, a opened door, and hearing heaven's silence. You are in towering company. David asked How long? in psalm after psalm. Paul pleaded three times about his thorn and was denied removal. Jesus Himself asked for the cup to pass in Gethsemane. Unanswered prayer is not proof of unheard prayer. Several biblical realities can be at work: God's timing often runs longer than our urgency, His no can be protection or redirection toward a better yes, and Scripture portrays perseverance itself as part of prayer's purpose, shaping us as the friend at midnight kept knocking. Sometimes, too, the reason stays hidden this side of eternity, and faith must rest on God's character rather than His explanations. What unanswered prayer never means is that God is indifferent. The cross settles that question permanently. Keep praying honestly, using the Psalms of lament as your vocabulary. Pray with others who can believe alongside you. Hold your request with open hands, trusting the Father who gave His Son will give what is truly good. And anchor your hope in the one prayer God always answers with yes: the cry for His presence, mercy, and grace in time of need.
