Do angels still appear today?

Question
Do angels still appear today?
Short Answer
The Bible shows angels appearing throughout history, and nothing in Scripture says they have stopped. Hebrews tells us some people have entertained angels without knowing it, so God may still send them — but every genuine angelic message agrees with Scripture and points to God, never away from Him.
Detailed Answer

Angels appear all through the Bible: to Abraham, to Mary, to the shepherds, to Peter in prison. Hebrews 13:2 adds a striking note for today, that some people have shown hospitality to strangers and entertained angels without knowing it. That suggests angelic activity did not end with the apostles. At the same time, Scripture urges discernment. Angels never contradict God's Word, never draw worship to themselves — when John tried to worship an angel in Revelation, the angel refused — and never deliver a 'new gospel' (Galatians 1:8). So if someone reports an angelic encounter, the wise response is humble openness paired with testing: does it agree with Scripture, does it glorify Christ, and does it bear good fruit? We should not chase supernatural experiences, but we need not fear them either. God remains free to send His messengers, and our job is to trust Him, test everything, and keep our eyes on Jesus rather than on angels.
