How do I forgive someone who hurt me?

Question
How do I forgive someone who hurt me?
Short Answer
Forgiveness means releasing the debt and desire for revenge to God, even while feelings heal slowly and trust may need rebuilding. We forgive because Christ forgave us, and the Spirit empowers what feels humanly impossible.
Detailed Answer

Forgiveness may be the hardest obedience Jesus asks of us, especially when the wound is deep and the apology never comes. Begin by understanding what forgiveness is and is not. It is a decision, often renewed daily, to release the offender's debt to God's just hands and refuse revenge. It is not pretending the harm was fine, forgetting what happened, removing consequences, or automatically restoring trust, which must be rebuilt with evidence of change and sometimes should not be restored where safety is at stake. Our motivation is not that the other person deserves release, but that Christ forgave us a debt we could never repay, and unforgiveness chains us to the injury. Colossians urges us to forgive as the Lord forgave us. Practically, name the hurt honestly before God, pray David's raw prayers, ask the Spirit for help you do not feel, and consider support from a pastor or counselor for serious wounds. Feelings usually follow obedience slowly, like healing after surgery. If you cannot yet say the words with warmth, start with willingness: Lord, I am willing to be made willing. The God who forgave you at the cross will walk you through forgiving, one surrendered step at a time.
