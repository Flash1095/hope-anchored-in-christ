Will Jesus come back?

Question
Will Jesus come back?
Short Answer
Yes, Jesus promised He will return visibly and gloriously to judge the living and the dead, defeat evil, and make all things new. His return is the blessed hope of the church, certain even though its timing is unknown.
Detailed Answer
Will Jesus come back? His own promise is unambiguous: I will come back and take you to be with me. The angels at His ascension echoed it: this same Jesus will come back in the same way you have seen him go into heaven. The early church lived tilted toward this hope, praying Maranatha, Come, Lord Jesus, and Paul calls Christ's appearing our blessed hope. His return will be personal, visible, and glorious, not a metaphor for progress or private spirituality. He will raise the dead, judge every person with perfect justice, finally defeat Satan and evil, and inaugurate the new heaven and new earth where righteousness dwells. The timing, however, is deliberately hidden. Jesus said no one knows the day or hour, a humility check for every generation tempted to set dates. The proper response to an unknown date is not speculation but readiness: watchfulness, holiness, perseverance in hardship, and urgent, loving witness. For suffering believers, the return is pure comfort. The story does not end with graves and empires. It ends with the King on a white horse who makes all things new. Anchor your hope there: He came once in humility to save, and He will come again in glory to finish what He started.
