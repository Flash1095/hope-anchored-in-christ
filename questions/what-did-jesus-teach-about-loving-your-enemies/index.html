What did Jesus teach about loving your enemies?

Question
What did Jesus teach about loving your enemies?
Short Answer
Jesus taught His followers to love their enemies and pray for those who persecute them, reflecting the Father's generous kindness. This radical love does not deny justice but refuses revenge and seeks the good of others.
Detailed Answer

Love your enemies may be Jesus' most challenging command. In the Sermon on the Mount, He pushes beyond the instinct to love only those who love us back. He calls us to pray for persecutors, do good to those who hurt us, and reflect our heavenly Father, who sends sun and rain on the just and unjust alike. This is not a call to pretend harm did not happen, excuse abuse, or abandon wise boundaries and justice. It is a call to refuse hatred and revenge as the ruling forces in our hearts, and to entrust final judgment to God. Jesus embodied His own teaching. On the cross He prayed, Father, forgive them, for those crucifying Him. Following Him here is impossible in our own strength, which is why we need His Spirit daily. Start small and honestly: pray by name for the person who wounded you, ask God to soften your heart, and take one concrete step of kindness if it is safe and wise to do so. Enemy-love is slow, costly work, but it frees us from bitterness and makes our hope visible in a divided world.
