How do I teach my children about God at home?

Question
How do I teach my children about God at home?
Short Answer
Teach your children about God the Deuteronomy 6 way: weave Him into ordinary life — talk about Him at meals, at bedtime, on walks, and when life gets hard — and let them see your own faith lived honestly, not just heard.
Detailed Answer
Deuteronomy 6:6-9 gives the pattern: God's words should be on your heart first, then impressed on your children 'when you sit at home and when you walk along the road, when you lie down and when you get up.' That means teaching happens less in lectures and more in rhythms: a short Bible story or verse at breakfast or bedtime, prayer together before meals and sleep, honest answers to their questions (including 'I don't know — let's find out'), and talking about God when life happens — thanking Him for good news, praying together when someone is sick, apologizing when you lose your temper. Psalm 78 says the point is telling 'the next generation the praiseworthy deeds of the LORD' (Psalm 78:4) — God's story, not just God's rules. Proverbs pictures a father passing wisdom to his son (Proverbs 1:8), and Ephesians says to bring children up 'in the training and instruction of the Lord' (Ephesians 6:4) — training implies practice, not just information. Two cautions: keep it age-appropriate and joyful rather than forced, and remember your example preaches louder than your lessons — children believe what they see you believe. You do not need to be a theologian; you need to be a faithful parent who keeps pointing, day after day, to Jesus.
