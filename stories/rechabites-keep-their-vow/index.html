The Rechabites Keep Their Vow

Story
The Rechabites Keep Their Vow
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Jeremiah 35
Retelling
In the days of Jehoiakim, the word came to Jeremiah: Go unto the house of the Rechabites, and speak unto them, and bring them into the house of the LORD, into one of the chambers, and give them wine to drink. Jeremiah took the whole house of the Rechabites and set pots full of wine and cups before them, saying, Drink ye wine. But they said, We will drink no wine, for Jonadab the son of Rechab our father commanded us, saying, Ye shall drink no wine, neither ye, nor your sons for ever. Neither shall ye build house, nor sow seed, nor plant vineyard, nor have any, but all your days ye shall dwell in tents, that ye may live many days in the land where ye be strangers. And we have obeyed the voice of Jonadab the son of Rechab our father in all that he hath charged us. Then the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah: Go and tell the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, Will ye not receive instruction to hearken to My words? The sons of Jonadab have performed the commandment of their father which he commanded them, but I have spoken unto you, rising early and speaking, and ye hearkened not unto Me. Therefore, because the Rechabites obeyed their father, Jonadab the son of Rechab shall not want a man to stand before Me for ever.
Discussion
A nomad family's three-hundred-year-old vow shamed a whole nation. Reflect: 1) Why does obedience to a human father convict Judah more than any sermon? 2) What small, unfashionable obedience has your family or church kept that the world has forgotten? 3) How does God honour quiet faithfulness that never makes a headline?
Significance
Steadfast obedience to a father's command exposes a nation's disobedience to God — and God promises the faithful family a standing before Him for ever.
