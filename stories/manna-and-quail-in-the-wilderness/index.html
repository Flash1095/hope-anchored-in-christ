Manna and Quail in the Wilderness

Manna and Quail in the Wilderness

Retelling

In the second month after leaving Egypt, the whole congregation murmured against Moses and Aaron in the wilderness of Sin, saying they had sat by fleshpots in Egypt and been brought out to die of hunger. God told Moses he would rain bread from heaven, with each day's portion gathered daily, and a double portion on the sixth day, so that Israel would know whether they would walk in his law. In the evening quails came up and covered the camp, and in the morning a small round thing, like hoar frost, lay on the ground. Israel called it manna, and Moses said, This is the bread which the LORD has given you to eat. Some kept it overnight against the command and it bred worms, and on the Sabbath none was found, just as God had said. An omer of manna was kept in a pot before the testimony, as a memorial for generations to come.

Discussion

Manna teaches daily dependence: enough for today, double before rest, nothing to hoard, nothing on the Sabbath. God feeds a complaining people and trains them at the same time. Reflect: 1) Where are you hoarding what God meant for today? 2) How does the Sabbath portion of manna challenge your work? 3) What does it mean that God fed Israel before they deserved it?

Significance

Manna is God's forty-year school of daily trust, providing bread from heaven on God's terms and marking Israel as a people fed by his word as well as by his hand.
