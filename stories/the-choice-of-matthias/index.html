The Choice of Matthias

Story
The Choice of Matthias
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Acts 1:15-26
Retelling
After the ascension, about a hundred and twenty believers waited in Jerusalem as Jesus had commanded. Peter stood up and said that Scripture had to be fulfilled concerning Judas, who had guided those who arrested Jesus and then bought a field with the reward of his betrayal and died there. Since Judas's office must be filled, the believers proposed two men who had accompanied them throughout Jesus' ministry, from John's baptism to the ascension: Joseph called Barsabbas, also called Justus, and Matthias. They prayed, asking the Lord who knows all hearts to show which of the two he had chosen, then cast lots, and the lot fell to Matthias, who was numbered with the eleven apostles.
Discussion
The first decision of the waiting church combined qualification, prayer, and trust in God's choice. The apostolic circle was restored before the Spirit fell, not after. Reflect: How do you make decisions when both candidates are qualified? Do you pray before you vote, or baptise a decision already made? What 'office' in your community is waiting to be filled by a faithful, overlooked person?
Significance
Matthias's choice restores the Twelve before Pentecost, showing the church ordering itself under Scripture and prayer while waiting for the Spirit.
