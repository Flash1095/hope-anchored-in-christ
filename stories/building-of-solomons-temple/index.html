Building of Solomon's Temple

Story
Building of Solomon's Temple
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
1 Kings 5-8; 2 Chronicles 3-7
Summary
Solomon built the Temple on Mount Moriah in Jerusalem over seven years, overlaying it with gold and furnishing it according to God's pattern. At its dedication the ark was brought in, God's glory filled the house like a cloud, and Solomon prayed for all who would ever turn toward that place in prayer.
Significance
The Temple was God's gracious promise to dwell among His people. Christians read it with hope fulfilled: in Christ, God has come to dwell with us — and now in us — forever.
