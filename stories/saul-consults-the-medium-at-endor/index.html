Saul Consults the Medium at Endor

Story
Saul Consults the Medium at Endor
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
1 Samuel 28
Retelling
Samuel was dead, the Philistines were massing at Shunem, and Saul was terrified. He asked the LORD for guidance, but God did not answer him, by dreams, by Urim, or by prophets. In desperation Saul turned to what he himself had outlawed: he disguised himself, travelled by night to Endor, and asked a woman with a familiar spirit to bring up Samuel. She cried out when Samuel appeared, and Saul bowed with his face to the ground. The message he heard was only judgment: because he had disobeyed God, the kingdom would be torn from him and given to David, and tomorrow he and his sons would fall to the Philistines. Saul collapsed in terror and had eaten nothing all day and night, until the woman and his servants persuaded him to eat a little before he went out to his doom.
Discussion
Saul's last night is a warning about what happens when a person keeps refusing God's word and then demands an answer on his own terms. God had spoken clearly enough; Saul wanted a different answer. Reflect: 1) How do you respond when God seems silent? 2) What forbidden shortcuts tempt you when obedience feels slow? 3) Why is it dangerous to seek spiritual guidance outside God's own ways?
Significance
At Endor, Saul's disobedience came full circle: the king who had banned mediums ended by consulting one, and heard only the judgment he had refused to heed.
