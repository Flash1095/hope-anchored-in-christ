The Last Supper

The Last Supper

Retelling

On the night before He died, Jesus gathered His twelve disciples in an upper room in Jerusalem to eat the Passover meal. As they reclined at table, He took bread, blessed and broke it, and said, 'This is my body, given for you; do this in remembrance of me.' Then He took the cup and said, 'This cup is the new covenant in my blood, poured out for many for the forgiveness of sins.' He told them one of them would betray Him, and though they all protested, Judas left into the night. Then Jesus led them out, singing a hymn, toward the garden of Gethsemane.

Themes

Covenant; Sacrifice; Remembrance

Discussion

At the table, Jesus gave His friends a meal to remember Him by — bread and cup that still anchor Christian worship today. Every communion is a return to that upper room and to the cross it anticipated. Reflect: 1) What does it mean to you that Jesus shared this meal knowing betrayal was coming? 2) How does the Lord's Supper strengthen your faith? 3) What does the new covenant promise you personally?

Significance

At a table of betrayal and failure, Jesus gave gifts — His body, His blood, His love to the end. Every Communion table since anchors hope in that night: we proclaim His death until He comes.
