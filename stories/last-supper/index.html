Last Supper

Story
Last Supper
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 26:17-30; John 13; 1 Corinthians 11:23-26
Summary
In an upper room in Jerusalem, Jesus washed His disciples' feet, shared the Passover meal, and gave it new meaning: the bread was His body, the cup the new covenant in His blood. He foretold Judas's betrayal and Peter's denial, promised the Holy Spirit, and prayed for all His followers.
Significance
At a table of betrayal and failure, Jesus gave gifts — His body, His blood, His love to the end. Every Communion table since anchors hope in that night: we proclaim His death until He comes.
