Jesus Appears to Thomas

Story
Jesus Appears to Thomas
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
John 20:24-29
Retelling
Thomas, one of the Twelve, was not with the disciples when Jesus first appeared to them, and when they told him, We have seen the Lord! he said, Unless I see the nail marks in his hands and put my finger where the nails were, and put my hand into his side, I will not believe. A week later the disciples were again together, and Thomas was with them. Though the doors were locked, Jesus came and stood among them, said Peace be with you, and turned to Thomas: Put your finger here; see my hands. Reach out your hand and put it into my side. Stop doubting and believe. Thomas answered him, My Lord and my God! Jesus said, Because you have seen me, you have believed; blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed.
Discussion
Thomas asked for the evidence the others had already received, and Jesus gave it without scolding, then blessed those who would believe without touching: doubt honestly brought to Jesus ends in worship. Reflect: 1) What evidence are you honestly asking Jesus for? 2) How does Thomas' confession, My Lord and my God, go beyond the other disciples' report? 3) What blessing belongs to believers who have not seen?
Significance
Thomas, who demanded to touch the wounds, became the first to confess the risen Jesus as My Lord and my God, and Jesus blessed all who believe without seeing.
