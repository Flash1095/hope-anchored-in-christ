Conversion of Saul (Paul)

Story
Conversion of Saul (Paul)
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Acts 9; Acts 22; Galatians 1:13-24
Summary
Saul of Tarsus, breathing threats against the church, was confronted on the Damascus road by the risen Jesus in blinding light: 'Saul, Saul, why do you persecute me?' Blinded for three days, he was healed and baptised through Ananias's brave obedience and immediately began preaching the Christ he had tried to destroy.
Significance
If grace could turn the church's fiercest enemy into its greatest missionary, no one you love is too far gone. Paul's conversion is hope's proof that Jesus transforms persecutors into proclaimers.
