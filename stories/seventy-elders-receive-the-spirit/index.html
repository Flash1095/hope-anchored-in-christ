Seventy Elders Receive the Spirit

Story
Seventy Elders Receive the Spirit
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Numbers 11:16-30
Retelling
When Moses said he could not bear the people alone, God told him to gather seventy men of the elders of Israel, known as elders and officers, and bring them to the tent of meeting. God said he would take of the Spirit that was upon Moses and put it upon them, so that they would bear the burden of the people with him. When the elders stood around the tent, the LORD came down in a cloud, took of the Spirit upon Moses, and put it upon the seventy, and they prophesied, and did not cease. But two men, Eldad and Medad, had remained in the camp, and the Spirit rested on them also, and they prophesied in the camp. Joshua urged Moses to forbid them, but Moses answered, Are you jealous for my sake? Would that all the LORD's people were prophets, and that the LORD would put his Spirit upon them.
Discussion
Shared burden becomes shared Spirit: God does not merely lighten Moses' load, he multiplies the Spirit that carried it. Moses refuses to guard spiritual gifts jealously. Reflect: 1) How does God share leadership without diminishing the leader? 2) Why does Moses celebrate Eldad and Medad instead of correcting them? 3) What would it mean for all God's people to carry his Spirit?
Significance
The seventy elders show God's answer to a burdened leader: the same Spirit, shared, so that the people are carried together and prophecy is not confined to one man.
