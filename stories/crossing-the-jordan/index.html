Crossing the Jordan

Story
Crossing the Jordan
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Joshua 3-5; Psalm 114
Summary
After Moses' death, Joshua led Israel to the flooded Jordan, where the waters stopped as the priests carrying the ark stepped in. The nation crossed on dry ground, set up twelve memorial stones at Gilgal, and celebrated Passover in the land at last.
Significance
God who parted the sea for one generation parted the river for the next — He does not run out of miracles. Memorial stones remind us to tell the next generation what God has done.
