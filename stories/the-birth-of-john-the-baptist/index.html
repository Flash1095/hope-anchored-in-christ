The Birth of John the Baptist

Story
The Birth of John the Baptist
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Luke 1:5-25; Luke 1:57-80
Retelling
Zechariah, an aged priest, and his wife Elizabeth had no children. While Zechariah was serving in the temple, Gabriel appeared and promised them a son, to be named John, who would be great before the Lord, turn many in Israel back to God, and prepare the way for him in the spirit of Elijah. Zechariah doubted because of their age and was struck silent until the promise came true. When Elizabeth's time came she bore a son, and at his circumcision, when relatives wanted to name him after his father, Elizabeth insisted on John, and Zechariah, writing the name on a tablet, regained his speech. He praised God in prophecy over the child who would prepare the Lord's way, and the boy grew strong in spirit in the wilderness.
Discussion
God's promise survived Zechariah's doubt, but doubt still had a cost: months of silence. Elizabeth's neighbours saw mercy, not just a miracle. Reflect: What promise of God are you tempted to treat as too late? How has doubt cost you a voice you could have used sooner? What does Zechariah's restored praise teach you about second chances?
Significance
John's birth opens Luke's Gospel by fulfilling Malachi's promise of a forerunner, preparing Israel for the Messiah who follows him.
