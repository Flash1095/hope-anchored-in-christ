Solomon's Wise Judgment

Story
Solomon's Wise Judgment
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
1 Kings 3:16-28
Retelling
Early in Solomon's reign, two prostitutes who lived in the same house came before him. Each had given birth to a son, and one child had died in the night. Both women claimed the living child was hers, and there were no witnesses to settle it. Solomon called for a sword and ordered the living child to be cut in two, giving half to each woman. At once the true mother cried out that the child should be given to the other woman alive rather than killed, while the other woman agreed to the division. Solomon saw the truth in that cry: the woman who would give up her own claim to save the child's life was its mother. He gave the child to her, and all Israel heard of the judgment and feared the king, seeing that the wisdom of God was in him to do justice.
Discussion
Solomon's wisdom was not cleverness for its own sake; it was the ability to see a mother's heart when evidence ran out. True justice protects the vulnerable, even when the case is messy. Reflect: 1) What does the true mother's sacrifice teach about love? 2) How do you judge when both sides sound convincing? 3) Where do you need God's wisdom for a decision with no clear evidence?
Significance
The judgment of the two mothers became the signature proof of Solomon's God-given wisdom, showing a king who could discern truth and protect life.
