Jesus and the Samaritan Woman

Story
Jesus and the Samaritan Woman
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
John 4:1-42
Retelling
Travelling through Samaria, Jesus sat weary by Jacob's well near Sychar while his disciples went for food. A Samaritan woman came to draw water, and Jesus, breaking custom by speaking to her and asking for a drink, offered her living water that would become a spring welling up to eternal life. When he showed he knew her life — five husbands, and the man she now lived with not her husband — she recognised him as a prophet and raised the old dispute about the right mountain for worship. Jesus answered that true worshippers would worship the Father in spirit and in truth, and when she spoke of the coming Messiah, he said plainly, I who speak to you am he. Leaving her jar, she ran to town saying, Come, see a man who told me everything I ever did, and many Samaritans believed.
Discussion
Jesus crossed ethnic, moral, and gender barriers in a single conversation, and the outsider became the town's evangelist. Her witness began with her own story, not a polished argument. Reflect: Who is the person you would naturally avoid at the well? What part of your story are you hiding that could actually point others to Jesus? How does worship 'in spirit and in truth' free you from defending your own mountain?
Significance
The well conversation reveals Jesus as Messiah to a Samaritan outsider first in John's Gospel, and models evangelism that starts with honest personal testimony.
