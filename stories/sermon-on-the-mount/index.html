Sermon on the Mount

Story
Sermon on the Mount
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 5-7; Luke 6:20-49
Summary
On a Galilean hillside Jesus taught His most famous sermon, opening with the Beatitudes — blessings on the poor in spirit, the mourning, the meek, and the persecuted. He taught on prayer, forgiveness, treasure in heaven, and the wise builder who puts His words into practice.
Significance
The Sermon is the constitution of Christ's upside-down kingdom, where the lowly are blessed. Building on these words, Jesus promises, is building a life that stands when the storms come.
