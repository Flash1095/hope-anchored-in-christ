Fall of Jerusalem and the Exile

Fall of Jerusalem and the Exile

Summary

After a long siege, Nebuchadnezzar's Babylonian army breached Jerusalem in 586 BC, burned the Temple and palace, tore down the walls, and carried Judah's people into exile in Babylon. Jeremiah, who had warned of this day for forty years, wept over the city in Lamentations.

Significance

Even here — especially here — Jeremiah could write that God's compassions never fail and great is His faithfulness. Exile was not abandonment; God went with His people and promised to bring them home.
