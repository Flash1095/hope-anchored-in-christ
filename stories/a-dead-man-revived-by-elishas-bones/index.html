A Dead Man Revived by Elisha's Bones

A Dead Man Revived by Elisha's Bones

Retelling

Elisha died and was buried. The bands of the Moabites invaded the land at the coming in of the year, and as men were burying a man, they saw a band of raiders coming and cast the body hastily into Elisha's sepulchre. When the man touched the bones of Elisha, he revived and stood up on his feet. The text records nothing more about him: not his name, his family, or what he did after standing up.

Discussion

Elisha's last miracle happened after his death, without a word, a prayer, or a prophet present: contact with his bones revived a corpse. It is the book's final testimony that God's power in Elisha outlived Elisha himself. Reflect: 1) How does God's power continue through people after they are gone? 2) What legacy of faith are you leaving that might revive someone else? 3) Why does the text leave the revived man unnamed and his later life untold?

Significance

A dead man revived on touching Elisha's bones, the prophet's final miracle after his death. Doubt: the account is two verses with no name, word, or sequel for the revived man, so it is filed as borderline rather than as a fully narrated miracle.
