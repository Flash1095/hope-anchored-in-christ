Jesus Sends Out the Twelve

Story
Jesus Sends Out the Twelve
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 10:1-42; Mark 6:7-13; Luke 9:1-6
Retelling
Jesus called his twelve apostles, gave them authority over unclean spirits and disease, and sent them out in pairs to the towns of Israel, to preach that the kingdom of heaven had come near and to heal the sick, cleanse lepers, raise the dead, and cast out demons. They were to travel light — no spare money, bag, or extra coat — and to depend on the hospitality of those who welcomed them, shaking the dust from their feet where they were refused. Jesus warned them plainly: they would face courts, floggings, and family division, and would be like sheep among wolves, yet they need not fear, since not even a sparrow falls without the Father, and whoever acknowledged him before others, he would acknowledge before his Father.
Discussion
Mission came with authority and with warning in the same breath: power to heal, no promise of safety. Travelling light forced dependence on God and on strangers' welcome. Reflect: What extra baggage are you carrying into the work God has given you? How do you respond when welcome turns to refusal? Where do you need courage to speak when silence would be safer?
Significance
The sending of the Twelve extends Jesus' own ministry through his apostles and establishes the pattern of dependent, costly mission.
