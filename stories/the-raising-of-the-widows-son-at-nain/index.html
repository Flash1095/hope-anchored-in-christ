The Raising of the Widow's Son at Nain

Story
The Raising of the Widow's Son at Nain
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Luke 7:11-17
Retelling
The day after healing the centurion's servant, Jesus arrived at the town of Nain with his disciples and a large crowd, just as a funeral procession was leaving the gate. A widow was burying her only son, and with him went her last support and security. When Jesus saw her, his heart went out to her, and he said, Do not weep. He stepped forward and touched the bier, and the bearers stood still. Then he said, Young man, I say to you, arise. The dead man sat up and began to speak, and Jesus gave him back to his mother. Fear seized everyone, and they glorified God, saying a great prophet had risen among them, and the news spread through all Judea and the surrounding country.
Discussion
Jesus interrupted a funeral uninvited, moved by compassion before anyone asked. The miracle restored a son and a widow's future in one act. Reflect: Where has grief left you with no one left to ask for help? Do you believe Jesus sees you before you call on him? What does it mean that his compassion, not just his power, drives his miracles?
Significance
The raising at Nain reveals Jesus as Lord over death and as the compassionate prophet who restores the most vulnerable.
