Story of Ruth

Story
Story of Ruth
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Ruth 1-4
Summary
Widowed in Moab, Ruth the Moabite refused to leave her mother-in-law Naomi, pledging that Naomi's people and God would be hers. Gleaning in Bethlehem's fields, she met Boaz, a kind kinsman-redeemer who married her, and their great-grandson was King David.
Significance
In the dark days of the judges, quiet loyalty and kindness carried the line of the Messiah. Ruth anchors hope that God weaves ordinary faithfulness — and outsiders welcomed in — into His greatest stories.
