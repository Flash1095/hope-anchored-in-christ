The Baptism of Jesus

The Baptism of Jesus

Retelling

John the Baptist was preaching repentance at the Jordan River when Jesus came from Galilee to be baptized by him. John protested — he needed Jesus to baptize him, not the other way around — but Jesus said it was fitting to fulfill all righteousness. As Jesus came up from the water, the heavens opened, the Spirit of God descended like a dove and rested on Him, and a voice from heaven declared, 'This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased.' The Trinity appeared together at the river, and Jesus' public ministry began.

Themes

Identity; Trinity; Obedience

Discussion

Before Jesus did a single miracle, the Father declared who He was: beloved Son. Your identity is not earned by performance; in Christ, it is declared before you do anything. Reflect: 1) How does Jesus' baptism shape your understanding of your own baptism? 2) What does the Father's declaration over Jesus say about your identity in Him? 3) How does obedience open the door to ministry?

Significance

At the Jordan all three Persons of the Trinity appear together as Jesus steps into our place. His baptism anchors hope that in Him we too are called God's beloved children.
