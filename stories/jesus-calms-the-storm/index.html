Jesus Calms the Storm

Story
Jesus Calms the Storm
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 8:23-27; Mark 4:35-41; Luke 8:22-25
Summary
Crossing the Sea of Galilee, a furious storm swamped the boat while Jesus slept, and the terrified disciples woke Him crying that they were perishing. He rebuked the wind and the waves, a great calm followed, and the disciples marvelled: 'Who is this? Even the wind and the waves obey him!'
Significance
The One in the boat was greater than the storm around it. When waves feel higher than your faith, this story anchors hope: Jesus is with you in the boat, and His word stills storms.
