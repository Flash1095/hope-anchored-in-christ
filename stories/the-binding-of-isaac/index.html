The Binding of Isaac

Story
The Binding of Isaac
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Genesis 22
Retelling
God tested Abraham by asking him to take his beloved son Isaac — the son of the promise — up Mount Moriah and offer him as a sacrifice. Abraham rose early, gathered wood, and walked with his son for three days, answering Isaac's question about the lamb with the words, 'God Himself will provide.' At the very last moment, as Abraham raised the knife, the angel of the Lord stopped him, and Abraham saw a ram caught by its horns in a thicket. He offered the ram instead and named the place 'The Lord Will Provide.'
Key People
Abraham; Isaac
Themes
Trust; Sacrifice; God's Provision
Discussion
This is one of the hardest stories in Scripture, and it only makes sense in light of what God did later: He did not spare His own Son. Abraham's trust teaches us that God can be trusted with what we love most — even when we cannot see how. Reflect: 1) What is hardest for you to entrust to God? 2) How does God's provision of the ram point to Jesus? 3) What does it mean to worship God when you do not understand His command?
