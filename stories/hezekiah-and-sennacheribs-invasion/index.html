Hezekiah and Sennacherib's Invasion

Story
Hezekiah and Sennacherib's Invasion
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
2 Kings 18-19; Isaiah 36-37; 2 Chronicles 32
Summary
Sennacherib of Assyria crushed Judah's fortified cities and demanded Jerusalem's surrender, mocking Hezekiah's trust in God. Hezekiah spread the threatening letter before the LORD in prayer, Isaiah brought God's answer, and in one night the angel of the LORD struck down 185,000 Assyrian soldiers.
Significance
Hezekiah dug his famous tunnel for water and then did something greater — he prayed. This deliverance anchors hope for anyone outmatched: bring the threat into God's presence and let Him answer.
