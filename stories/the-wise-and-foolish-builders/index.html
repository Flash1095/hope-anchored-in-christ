The Wise and Foolish Builders

Story
The Wise and Foolish Builders
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 7:24-27; Luke 6:46-49
Retelling
Closing the Sermon on the Mount, Jesus said that everyone who hears his words and does them is like a wise man who built his house on the rock. The rain fell, the floods came, and the winds blew and beat on the house, but it did not fall, because it was founded on rock. But everyone who hears his words and does not do them is like a foolish man who built his house on sand. The rain fell, the floods came, and the winds beat on the house, and it fell, and great was its fall. Luke's version adds that the wise builder dug deep and laid the foundation on rock, while the foolish builder built on the ground with no foundation, and the house collapsed at once.
Discussion
Both builders heard the same sermon; only one did it. The storm did not choose the house — the foundation did. Hearing without doing is sand, however orthodox the hearing sounds. Reflect: What teaching of Jesus have you heard often and never done? Where is your foundation sand — appearance, habit, reputation? What would digging deep look like in one concrete area?
Significance
The builders close the Sermon on the Mount by making obedience, not hearing, the foundation that survives the storm.
