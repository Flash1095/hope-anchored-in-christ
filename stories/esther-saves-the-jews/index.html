Esther Saves the Jews

Story
Esther Saves the Jews
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Esther 4-9
Summary
In Susa, the wicked Haman plotted to annihilate all Jews in the Persian Empire on a single day. Queen Esther, a Jewish orphan raised by Mordecai, risked her life to approach the king uninvited — 'if I perish, I perish' — exposed Haman's plot, and saw her people saved and the feast of Purim established.
Significance
God is never named in Esther, yet His fingerprints are on every page. For seasons when heaven seems silent, Esther anchors hope that God is working behind the scenes for His people's good.
