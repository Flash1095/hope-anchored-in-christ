Triumphal Entry

Story
Triumphal Entry
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 21:1-11; Zechariah 9:9; John 12:12-19
Summary
Riding into Jerusalem on a young donkey, as Zechariah foretold, Jesus was welcomed by crowds waving palm branches and shouting 'Hosanna to the Son of David!' Days later many of the same city would call for His crucifixion, yet He wept over Jerusalem rather than turning away.
Significance
Hosanna means 'save us, please' — and the humble King on the donkey came to do exactly that, though not as crowds expected. Hope rides lowly and keeps coming toward us.
