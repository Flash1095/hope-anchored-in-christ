Joseph Rises to Power in Egypt

Story
Joseph Rises to Power in Egypt
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Genesis 41-50; Genesis 50:20
Summary
Interpreting Pharaoh's dreams of feast and famine, Joseph was raised from prison to second-in-command of Egypt and stored grain that saved nations — including his own family. When his brothers came begging for food, Joseph forgave them, saying what they meant for evil, God meant for good.
Significance
Joseph's forgiveness is one of Scripture's clearest pictures of God's providence turning harm into salvation. It gives hope that God can redeem even family wounds and wasted years for the saving of many lives.
