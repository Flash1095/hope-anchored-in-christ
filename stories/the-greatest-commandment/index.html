The Greatest Commandment

The Greatest Commandment

Retelling

After Jesus had silenced the Sadducees, a lawyer — in Mark's account, a scribe who had heard the debate and thought Jesus answered well — asked which commandment was the greatest. Jesus answered from the Law itself: You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul, mind, and strength. And the second is like it: You shall love your neighbour as yourself. On these two commandments, he said, hang all the Law and the Prophets. The scribe agreed, adding that loving God and neighbour is more than all burnt offerings and sacrifices, and Jesus told him he was not far from the kingdom of God. After that, no one dared ask him any more questions.

Discussion

Jesus reduced 613 commandments to two loves, and made love of neighbour the test of love of God. The scribe saw that love outranks sacrifice — and was told he was near, not yet in, the kingdom. Reflect: Which love is weaker in your life right now — love of God or love of neighbour? Do you use religious activity as a substitute for either? What would 'not far' need to become 'in' for you?

Significance

The greatest commandment centres the whole Law on love of God and neighbour, and makes neighbour-love the visible proof of devotion.
