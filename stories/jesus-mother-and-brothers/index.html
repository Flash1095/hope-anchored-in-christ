Jesus' Mother and Brothers

Story
Jesus' Mother and Brothers
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 12:46-50; Mark 3:31-35; Luke 8:19-21
Retelling
While Jesus was speaking to the crowds, his mother and brothers arrived and, unable to reach him through the crowd, sent word asking to speak with him. Someone told him, Your mother and brothers are outside, wanting to speak to you. Jesus replied, Who is my mother, and who are my brothers? Then, pointing to his disciples, he said, Here are my mother and my brothers. For whoever does the will of my Father in heaven is my brother and sister and mother. He did not reject his family, but he redefined belonging around doing the Father's will.
Discussion
Jesus widened the family circle without breaking it: belonging to him is marked by doing the Father's will, not by blood alone. Reflect: 1) How does this redefinition comfort those whose families do not share their faith? 2) Where might family loyalty compete with obedience to God in your life? 3) What does it mean practically to treat fellow disciples as brother and sister?
Significance
Jesus taught that the truest kinship is obedience to the Father, making the community of disciples a family bound by God's will.
