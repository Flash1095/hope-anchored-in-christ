The Kingdom Divides

Story
The Kingdom Divides
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
1 Kings 12; 2 Chronicles 10
Summary
After Solomon's death, his son Rehoboam refused the people's plea for lighter burdens and answered harshly, and ten tribes seceded under Jeroboam. The kingdom split into Israel in the north and Judah in the south, and Jeroboam set up golden calves at Bethel and Dan to keep his people from Jerusalem.
Significance
Pride and harshness can split in a day what generations built. Yet even through centuries of divided kings, God never abandoned His covenant — a hope for fractured families and churches today.
