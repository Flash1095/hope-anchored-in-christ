Ananias and Sapphira

Story
Ananias and Sapphira
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Acts 5:1-11
Retelling
In the early Jerusalem church, believers were selling land and laying the proceeds at the apostles' feet for distribution to the needy. Ananias and his wife Sapphira sold a piece of property, but secretly kept back part of the price while presenting the rest as the whole gift. When Ananias laid the money before Peter, Peter asked why Satan had filled his heart to lie to the Holy Spirit: the land was his to keep or sell, and the money was his to control — the sin was not in keeping part, but in pretending the part was the whole. Hearing this, Ananias fell down dead, and young men carried him out and buried him. Three hours later Sapphira came in, unaware, repeated the same lie, and likewise fell dead at Peter's feet. Great fear seized the whole church and all who heard.
Discussion
Ananias and Sapphira died not for stinginess but for staged generosity: a lie told to God and to the church. The early church learned that holiness is not optional decoration. Reflect: Where is your generosity staged — presented as whole when part is held back? Do you treat lying to God as a small sin? How does holy fear differ from mere fright?
Significance
Ananias and Sapphira establish that the Spirit-filled church takes truth seriously, and that hypocrisy at its heart meets God's judgment.
