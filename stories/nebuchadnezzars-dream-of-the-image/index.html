Nebuchadnezzar's Dream of the Image

Story
Nebuchadnezzar's Dream of the Image
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Daniel 2
Retelling
King Nebuchadnezzar dreamed dreams that troubled him, and demanded that his wise men tell him both the dream and its meaning, on pain of death. None could, until Daniel, after seeking God in prayer with his friends, received the secret in a night vision. He told the king of a great image with a head of gold, chest and arms of silver, belly and thighs of bronze, legs of iron, and feet of iron mixed with clay, smashed by a stone cut out without hands that became a great mountain filling the whole earth. Daniel explained that the parts were successive kingdoms, but the God of heaven would set up a kingdom that would never be destroyed. The king honoured Daniel and set him over Babylon.
Discussion
When human wisdom runs out, prayer is not a last resort but the first move. Daniel credited God, not himself, with the answer. Reflect: 1) What problem are you trying to solve with only human wisdom? 2) How does Daniel's humility shape the way you talk about your own gifts? 3) What does it mean for you that God's kingdom outlasts every empire?
Significance
God alone reveals hidden things, and His kingdom — not any human empire — is the one that endures for ever.
