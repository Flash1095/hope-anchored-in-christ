Jesus Rejected at Nazareth

Story
Jesus Rejected at Nazareth
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 13:53-58; Mark 6:1-6; Luke 4:16-30
Retelling
Jesus returned to Nazareth, where he had grown up, and taught in the synagogue. At first the people were amazed at his wisdom and mighty works, but then they stumbled over his familiarity: was this not the carpenter's son, whose family they knew? Their amazement curdled into offence. Jesus told them a prophet is not without honour except in his own country and among his own kin, and he did few mighty works there because of their unbelief. In Luke's account the confrontation sharpened: when Jesus said God's favour reached outsiders like the widow of Zarephath and Naaman the Syrian, the crowd drove him out of town and tried to throw him from a cliff, but he passed through their midst and went on his way.
Discussion
Familiarity blinded Nazareth to Jesus: they could not receive a prophet they thought they already knew. Unbelief, not inability, limited what happened there. Reflect: Where has familiarity dulled your sense of who Jesus is? Do you resist a word from God because of who delivers it? What would it mean to let Jesus surprise you again?
Significance
Nazareth's rejection previews the wider pattern of Jesus' ministry: honour from outsiders alongside refusal from those closest to home.
