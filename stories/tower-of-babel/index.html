Tower of Babel

Story
Tower of Babel
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Genesis 11:1-9; Acts 2:5-11
Summary
Speaking one language, people settled at Shinar and began building a city and a tower to reach the heavens and make a name for themselves. God confused their language and scattered them over the earth, halting the proud project.
Significance
Babel shows what human pride builds, and Pentecost will one day show what God's Spirit gathers. Our hope is not in making our own name great, but in the God who brings scattered people together in Christ.
