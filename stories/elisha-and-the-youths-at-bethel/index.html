Elisha and the Youths at Bethel

Story
Elisha and the Youths at Bethel
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
2 Kings 2:23-25
Retelling
As Elisha went up from Jericho to Bethel, little children came out of the city and mocked him, saying, 'Go up, thou bald head; go up, thou bald head.' Elisha turned back, looked on them, and cursed them in the name of the LORD. Two she bears came out of the wood and tore forty-two of the children. Elisha went on from there to Mount Carmel, and from there he returned to Samaria. The text gives no further explanation of the mockery, the curse, or the bears.
Discussion
This is the hardest of Elisha's miracles to read: a curse answered by bears against mocking youths at Bethel, the city of Jeroboam's calf. The mockery of 'go up' may have scorned Elijah's ascent and Elisha's succession, and Bethel was a centre of idolatry, but the passage itself explains nothing, and any retelling must say so. Reflect: 1) How do you read a passage whose moral logic the text does not explain? 2) What does contempt for God's messengers reveal about a city like Bethel? 3) Why is it important to admit what a passage does not say?
Significance
At Bethel, mockery of Elisha was answered by bears, in a brief and unexplained judgment at the heart of calf-worship. Doubt: the passage is two verses with no explanation of the mockery, the curse, or the bears' scale, so it is filed as borderline rather than as a fully narrated miracle.
