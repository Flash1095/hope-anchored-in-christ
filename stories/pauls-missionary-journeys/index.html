Paul's Missionary Journeys

Paul's Missionary Journeys

Summary

Across three great journeys from Antioch, Paul and his companions planted churches through Cyprus, Asia Minor, and Greece — preaching in synagogues and marketplaces, enduring riots, prison, and shipwreck, and appointing elders in every church. Lydia, the Philippian jailer, and countless others came to faith as the gospel leapt from city to city.

Significance

Paul carried one message through beatings and open doors alike: Jesus is risen and grace is for everyone. His journeys map hope spreading — and remind us no hardship on mission is wasted.
