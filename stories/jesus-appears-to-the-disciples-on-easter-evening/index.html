Jesus Appears to the Disciples on Easter Evening

Story
Jesus Appears to the Disciples on Easter Evening
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Luke 24:36-49; John 20:19-23; Mark 16:14
Retelling
On Easter evening, while the disciples were together behind locked doors for fear, Jesus himself stood among them and said, Peace be with you. They were startled and frightened, thinking they saw a ghost, so he showed them his hands and his side, and invited them to touch him, saying a ghost does not have flesh and bones as he had. Still disbelieving for joy, they watched as he ate a piece of broiled fish before them. Then he opened their minds to understand the Scriptures, that the Messiah would suffer and rise on the third day, and that repentance for the forgiveness of sins would be preached in his name to all nations, beginning at Jerusalem. He told them to stay in the city until clothed with power from on high, and he breathed on them, saying, Receive the Holy Spirit.
Discussion
The risen Jesus did not appear as a ghost or a vision but as flesh that could be touched and fed, and he turned frightened fugitives into witnesses to all nations. Reflect: 1) Why does Jesus prove his resurrection with hands, side, and fish rather than argument alone? 2) How does Peace be with you answer locked doors and fear? 3) What does it mean to wait for power before going out as witnesses?
Significance
On Easter evening the risen Christ stood bodily among his frightened disciples, proved his resurrection, opened the Scriptures, and commissioned them as witnesses to all nations.
