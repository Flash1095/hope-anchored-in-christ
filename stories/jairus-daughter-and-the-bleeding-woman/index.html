Jairus' Daughter and the Bleeding Woman

Story
Jairus' Daughter and the Bleeding Woman
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 9:18-26; Mark 5:21-43; Luke 8:40-56
Retelling
Jairus, a ruler of the synagogue, fell at Jesus' feet begging him to come, because his only daughter, about twelve years old, was dying. On the way, a woman who had suffered bleeding for twelve years and spent everything on doctors pressed through the crowd and touched the fringe of Jesus' cloak, believing even that would heal her. At once her bleeding stopped. Jesus stopped and asked who had touched him; trembling, she told him the whole truth, and he called her daughter and sent her away in peace, her faith having healed her. By then messengers said Jairus's daughter was dead, but Jesus told him not to fear, only to believe. At the house he took the girl by the hand and said, Little girl, arise, and she stood up at once.
Discussion
Two twelve-year stories intertwine: a desperate father and an untouchable woman both find that faith reaches Jesus even in delay and death. Jesus honoured the interruption without abandoning the first need. Reflect: What delay makes you fear Jesus has arrived too late? Where are you reaching for him quietly, hoping no one notices? How does his word 'do not fear, only believe' speak to your impossible situation?
Significance
The paired healings show Jesus' power over chronic suffering and death itself, and his personal care for the overlooked alongside the prominent.
