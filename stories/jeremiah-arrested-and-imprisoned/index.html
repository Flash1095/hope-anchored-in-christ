Jeremiah Arrested and Imprisoned

Jeremiah Arrested and Imprisoned

Retelling

Zedekiah the son of Josiah reigned instead of Coniah, and neither he, nor his servants, nor the people of the land, listened to the words of the LORD spoken by Jeremiah. When Pharaoh's army came out of Egypt, the Chaldeans raised the siege of Jerusalem, and Jeremiah warned that Pharaoh's army would return to Egypt and the Chaldeans would come again and burn the city. As Jeremiah was going out of Jerusalem into the land of Benjamin, to separate himself there, Irijah the son of Shelemiah, captain of the ward at the gate of Benjamin, seized him, saying, Thou fallest away to the Chaldeans. Jeremiah said, It is false; I fall not away to the Chaldeans. But Irijah would not hear him, took him to the princes, who were angry with him, beat him, and put him in prison in the house of Jonathan the scribe, for they had made that the prison. Jeremiah remained there many days. Then Zedekiah sent and took him out, and asked him secretly in his house, Is there any word from the LORD? Jeremiah said, There is: for thou shalt be delivered into the hand of the king of Babylon. Jeremiah asked why he was imprisoned when the prophets who told Zedekiah the king of Babylon would not come had been proved false, and begged not to be sent back to Jonathan's house to die there. Zedekiah commanded that Jeremiah be committed into the court of the prison, with a piece of bread daily out of the bakers' street, until all the bread in the city was spent.

Discussion

Jeremiah was arrested for desertion while telling the truth about the siege. Reflect: 1) How do you keep telling the truth when it gets you labelled a traitor? 2) Why did Zedekiah ask Jeremiah in secret what he would not hear in public? 3) What does Jeremiah's plea — do not send me back to die — teach about honest prayer under unjust imprisonment?

Significance

Truth-telling under siege looks like treason to frightened rulers; Jeremiah was beaten and jailed for a prophecy that was already coming true.
