Mary Visits Elizabeth

Mary Visits Elizabeth

Retelling

After Gabriel's announcement, Mary hurried into the hill country of Judah to visit her relative Elizabeth, who was herself miraculously pregnant. As soon as Elizabeth heard Mary's greeting, the baby in her womb leaped, and Elizabeth, filled with the Holy Spirit, blessed Mary as the mother of her Lord and praised her for believing what God had promised. Mary answered with a song of praise, magnifying the Lord who had looked on her lowly state, done great things for her, scattered the proud, lifted the humble, filled the hungry, and remembered his mercy to Abraham's descendants. She stayed with Elizabeth about three months before returning home.

Discussion

Two unlikely mothers meet, and the younger one's faith is confirmed by the elder's Spirit-given greeting. Joy grows when promises are shared, not carried alone. Reflect: Who confirms God's work in your life the way Elizabeth confirmed Mary's? What promise are you carrying that needs speaking aloud to a trusted believer? How does Mary's song challenge your picture of what God values?

Significance

The visitation links the forerunner and the Messiah before either is born, and Mary's song roots Jesus' coming in God's long mercy to Israel.

Themes

Joy; Faith; God's mercy; Humility
