Destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah

Summary

Abraham pleaded with God for the wicked cities of Sodom and Gomorrah, and God sent angels to rescue Lot and his family before judgment fell. Fire rained down on the cities, and Lot's wife was turned to a pillar of salt as she looked back.

Significance

This sobering story shows God is both just and merciful — He heard Abraham's prayers and rescued the righteous. It urges us to pray boldly for others and to keep moving toward God's rescue without looking back.
