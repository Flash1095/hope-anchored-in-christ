Ezra Reads the Law

Story
Ezra Reads the Law
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Ezra 7; Nehemiah 8
Summary
Ezra the scribe, a skilled teacher of God's Law, led a second return from Babylon and called the people back to covenant faithfulness. Standing on a wooden platform at the Water Gate, he read the Book of the Law aloud from dawn to noon while the people wept, repented, and then celebrated with great joy.
Significance
A community rebuilt around the open Word of God found both conviction and joy in the same morning. Ezra's platform still models revival: hear the Word, weep, worship — and feast, for the joy of the LORD is strength.
