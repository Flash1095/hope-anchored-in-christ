David Anointed by Samuel

Story
David Anointed by Samuel
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
1 Samuel 16
Retelling
After God rejected Saul as king, he sent Samuel to Bethlehem, to the house of Jesse, saying that he had chosen a king among Jesse's sons. Jesse brought seven sons before Samuel, each impressive in turn, beginning with Eliab, tall and kingly. But the LORD told Samuel not to look on outward appearance, for people look at the face, while the LORD looks at the heart. When none of the seven was chosen, Samuel asked if there were another son. The youngest, David, was out keeping the sheep. They sent for him, a ruddy youth with fine eyes and a good appearance, and as he stood before them the LORD said, 'Arise, anoint him: for this is he.' Samuel poured oil on his head, and the Spirit of the LORD came upon David from that day forward, while the Spirit departed from Saul.
Discussion
Anointing did not mean immediate coronation. God's choice of David overturns every human shortlist. He passes over the impressive older brothers and chooses the shepherd boy nobody thought to invite. Reflect: 1) Where are you judging yourself or others by appearance? 2) What does it mean that God looks at the heart? 3) How does David's long wait after anointing speak to your own waiting?
Significance
David's secret anointing begins the true kingship in Israel: a king chosen by God's heart, not by human height or status.
