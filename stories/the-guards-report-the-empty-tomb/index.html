The Guards Report the Empty Tomb

The Guards Report the Empty Tomb

Retelling

While the women were on their way from the tomb, some of the guards went into the city and reported to the chief priests everything that had happened. The chief priests met with the elders, devised a plan, and gave the soldiers a large sum of money, telling them to say, His disciples came during the night and stole him away while we were asleep. If this report reached the governor, they promised, they would satisfy him and keep the soldiers out of trouble. So the soldiers took the money and did as they were instructed, and Matthew notes that this story has been widely circulated among the Jews to this very day.

Discussion

The first official response to the resurrection was a bribe: a story bought to explain away an empty tomb, admitting the tomb was empty while denying the risen Lord. Reflect: 1) Why does the bribed story itself confirm the tomb was empty? 2) How do money and fear still shape explanations of Jesus today? 3) What does it cost to tell the truth when an easier story is on offer?

Significance

The guards' own report, bought and retold, unintentionally testifies that the tomb was empty on the third morning, while the priests chose a purchased lie over the risen Christ.
