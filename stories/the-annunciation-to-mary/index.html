The Annunciation to Mary

The Annunciation to Mary

Retelling

In Nazareth, the angel Gabriel came to Mary, a young woman engaged to Joseph, and greeted her as one highly favoured by God. He told her she would conceive and bear a son, to be named Jesus, who would be great and called the Son of the Most High, and would reign over the house of Jacob for ever. Mary asked how this could be, since she was a virgin. Gabriel answered that the Holy Spirit would come upon her and the power of the Most High would overshadow her, so the child would be called holy, the Son of God. He added that her relative Elizabeth, long childless, was already six months pregnant, for nothing is impossible with God. Mary answered that she was the Lord's servant and asked that it happen as Gabriel had said.

Discussion

Mary's response is trust without full understanding: she asks an honest question, hears an impossible promise, and yields. Her yes made room for God's plan at personal cost. Reflect: What honest question do you need to ask God before you can trust him? Where is obedience costing you comfort or reputation? How does Mary's example reshape what surrender looks like for you?

Significance

The Annunciation announces the incarnation: Jesus' conception by the Holy Spirit, foretold before his birth, is the foundation of the Christian claim that God himself entered human life.
