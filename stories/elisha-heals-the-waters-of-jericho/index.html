Elisha Heals the Waters of Jericho

Story
Elisha Heals the Waters of Jericho
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
2 Kings 2:19-22
Retelling
After Elijah was taken up, the men of Jericho came to Elisha and said the situation of the city was pleasant, but the water was bad and the ground barren, so that it caused death and miscarriage. Elisha said, 'Bring me a new cruse, and put salt therein.' They brought it, and Elisha went to the spring of the waters, cast the salt in, and said, 'Thus saith the LORD, I have healed these waters; there shall not be from thence any more death or barren land.' So the waters were healed to this day, according to the saying that Elisha spoke. The spring at Jericho became a source of life for the city instead of a source of death.
Discussion
Elisha's first miracle after succeeding Elijah was not a spectacle but a healing of a city's water supply: ordinary life, crops, and children depended on it. A new cruse and a handful of salt carried God's word into a daily need. Reflect: 1) What 'bad water' in your community needs healing rather than attention? 2) How does God work through small, ordinary means? 3) What does it mean for a prophet's first act to be practical provision?
Significance
Elisha's healing of Jericho's waters opened his ministry with practical grace, turning a spring of death into a spring of life for the city.
