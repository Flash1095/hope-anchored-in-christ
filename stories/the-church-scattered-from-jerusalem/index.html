The Church Scattered from Jerusalem

The Church Scattered from Jerusalem

Retelling

Saul was consenting to Stephen's death. At that time there was a great persecution against the church which was at Jerusalem, and they were all scattered abroad throughout the regions of Judaea and Samaria, except the apostles. Devout men carried Stephen to his burial and made great lamentation over him. As for Saul, he made havoc of the church, entering into every house, and haling men and women and committing them to prison. But they that were scattered abroad went everywhere preaching the word.

Discussion

Persecution does what comfort could not: it moves the gospel out of Jerusalem into Judaea and Samaria, exactly the widening circle Jesus had named. Saul's havoc and the scattered believers' preaching stand side by side in the same verses. Reflect: 1) How has hardship ever carried the gospel further than comfort did? 2) What does it mean that ordinary scattered believers, not only apostles, preached the word? 3) How do you honour both grief for Stephen and hope in the scattering?

Significance

The scattering turns Stephen's martyrdom into mission: the church leaves Jerusalem not by a plan but by persecution, and the word spreads through unnamed believers who preach wherever they land.
