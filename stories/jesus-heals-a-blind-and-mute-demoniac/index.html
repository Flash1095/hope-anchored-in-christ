Jesus Heals a Blind and Mute Demoniac

Story
Jesus Heals a Blind and Mute Demoniac
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 12:22-24
Retelling
Then they brought Jesus a demon-possessed man who was blind and mute, and Jesus healed him, so that he could both talk and see. All the people were astonished and said, Could this be the Son of David? But when the Pharisees heard this, they said, It is only by Beelzebul, the prince of demons, that this fellow drives out demons. This is a separate narration from the earlier healing of a mute demoniac: here the man is both blind and mute, the healing restores both sight and speech, and the crowd's astonishment turns explicitly to the question of whether Jesus is the Son of David, provoking the full Beelzebul controversy that follows.
Discussion
Sight and speech restored together made the crowd ask the right question, Could this be the Son of David? while the Pharisees answered it with the worst explanation. Reflect: 1) How does restored sight and speech picture what Jesus does for the spiritually blind and mute? 2) What makes the crowd's question better than the Pharisees' answer? 3) Where are you tempted to attribute God's work to an evil source?
Significance
Jesus healed a demon-possessed man who was both blind and mute, restoring sight and speech together, and the crowd wondered whether he was the Son of David.
