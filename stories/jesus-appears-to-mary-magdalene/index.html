Jesus Appears to Mary Magdalene

Story
Jesus Appears to Mary Magdalene
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
John 20:11-18; Matthew 28:9-10; Mark 16:9
Retelling
Early on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene came to the tomb and found the stone removed, and after Peter and John had come and gone, she stood outside weeping. As she wept she looked into the tomb and saw two angels in white, who asked why she was crying, and she answered, They have taken my Lord away, and I don't know where they have put him. Turning around, she saw Jesus standing there but did not recognise him, supposing him to be the gardener, until he said her name, Mary. She turned and cried out, Rabboni! Jesus told her not to hold on to him, for he had not yet ascended to the Father, but to go to his brothers and say he was ascending to his Father and their Father, to his God and their God. Mary went and announced to the disciples, I have seen the Lord! Matthew records that as she and the other Mary left the tomb, Jesus met them and they clasped his feet and worshipped him.
Discussion
The risen Lord first revealed himself to a weeping woman outside the tomb, and she became the first witness of the resurrection to the apostles. Reflect: 1) Why does Jesus reveal himself by speaking Mary's name? 2) How does Mary's journey from weeping to announcing model witness? 3) What does it mean that the first resurrection preacher was a woman whose testimony the world discounted?
Significance
Mary Magdalene, first at the tomb and first to see the risen Lord, carried the first resurrection announcement to the disciples: I have seen the Lord.
