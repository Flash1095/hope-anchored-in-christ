Calling of Samuel

Calling of Samuel

Summary

Hannah, long barren, prayed at Shiloh for a son and dedicated young Samuel to serve in God's house under Eli the priest. One night God called the boy by name — 'Samuel! Samuel!' — and Samuel answered, 'Speak, for your servant is listening,' beginning a lifetime of prophetic ministry.

Significance

God still calls the young, the overlooked, and the prayed-for. Samuel's simple answer is a model for every reader: a listening heart is where a life of purpose begins.
