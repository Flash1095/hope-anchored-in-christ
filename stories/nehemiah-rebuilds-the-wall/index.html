Nehemiah Rebuilds the Wall

Nehemiah Rebuilds the Wall

Summary

Nehemiah, cupbearer to Persia's king, wept over Jerusalem's broken walls, prayed, and was sent with royal authority to rebuild them. Despite mockery, threats, and plots from Sanballat and Tobiah, the wall was finished in just fifty-two days, with workers building with one hand and holding a weapon with the other.

Significance

Nehemiah prayed first and worked hard second — and enemies themselves admitted the work was done with God's help. Broken walls in a life or a city are not the end when God stirs someone to rebuild.
