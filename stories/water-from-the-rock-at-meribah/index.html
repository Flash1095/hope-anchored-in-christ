Water from the Rock at Meribah

Story
Water from the Rock at Meribah
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Exodus 17:1-7; Numbers 20:1-13; 1 Corinthians 10:4
Retelling
At Rephidim, with no water, Israel quarrelled with Moses and tested God, asking whether the Lord was among them. God told Moses to strike the rock at Horeb, and water flowed for the people and their animals. Years later, at Kadesh, after Miriam's death, the people quarrelled again. God told Moses to speak to the rock, but in anger Moses struck it twice and claimed the miracle as his own work. Water still flowed abundantly, yet God told Moses and Aaron that, because they had not trusted and honoured him, they would not lead Israel into the promised land. The same God who gave water in mercy also held his leaders to account.
Discussion
God meets real need, but complaining that doubts his presence turns provision into a test, and leaders bear a heavier trust. Trust is tested most when the need is ordinary, not only when it is dramatic. Reflect: 1) How do you speak to God when a need feels urgent? 2) Where does frustration tempt you to take credit or control? 3) What does God's mercy alongside discipline teach you about him?
Significance
Twice God gave water from rock in a desert, proving his presence with a quarrelling people, while the second occasion shows that even faithful leaders face consequences when they dishonour him.
