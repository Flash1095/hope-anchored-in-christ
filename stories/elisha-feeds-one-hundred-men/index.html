Elisha Feeds One Hundred Men

Story
Elisha Feeds One Hundred Men
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
2 Kings 4:42-44
Retelling
A man from Baal-shalisha brought Elisha, the man of God, bread of the firstfruits, twenty loaves of barley, and full ears of corn in his husk. Elisha said, 'Give unto the people, that they may eat.' His servant objected, 'What, should I set this before an hundred men?' Elisha answered, 'Give the people, that they may eat: for thus saith the LORD, They shall eat, and shall leave thereof.' So the servant set it before them, and they ate, and left thereof, according to the word of the LORD.
Discussion
Twenty barley loaves fed one hundred men with food left over, because God's word, not the servant's arithmetic, set the portion. The servant's objection is the honest one, and the leftovers are the answer. Reflect: 1) What provision in front of you looks too small for the need? 2) How does firstfruits giving, the gift itself, become the means of feeding others? 3) Where have you seen leftovers after obedience, not before it?
Significance
Elisha's feeding of one hundred men with twenty barley loaves showed God's provision outrunning human arithmetic, with food left over.
