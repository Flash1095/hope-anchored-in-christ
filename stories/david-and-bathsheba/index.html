David and Bathsheba

Story
David and Bathsheba
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
2 Samuel 11-12; Psalm 51
Summary
King David took Bathsheba, wife of his loyal soldier Uriah, and arranged Uriah's death to cover the sin. The prophet Nathan confronted him with a parable, David confessed 'I have sinned against the LORD,' and though consequences followed, God forgave him and David poured out his repentance in Psalm 51.
Significance
No fall is hidden from God — and no repentant heart is turned away. David's story anchors hope for anyone carrying guilt: a broken and contrite heart, God will not despise.
