Elon, Judge of Israel

Story
Elon, Judge of Israel
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Judges 12:11-12
Retelling
After Ibzan, Elon, a Zebulonite, judged Israel for ten years. Then he died and was buried at Aijalon in the land of Zebulun. No enemy, battle, or specific deed is recorded in his account, and no other detail of his life is given. He follows Ibzan and precedes Abdon in the closing list of judges before Samson, and his ten years are the longest of those three brief judgeships. His burial at Aijalon in Zebulun is the only place the text attaches to his name.
Discussion
Elon's whole biblical record is two verses: a name, a tribe, ten years, and a burial place. Yet he is counted among the judges, which means God counts leadership that leaves no story behind. Reflect: 1) Does a life need a dramatic story to have mattered to God? 2) What does ten years of unnamed faithfulness look like in your own setting? 3) How do you honour leaders whose work you will never read about?
Significance
Elon's ten years of judgeship are preserved as a bare notice with no deed recorded. Doubt: the text records no enemy, battle, or specific deed, so this row preserves a notice of judgeship rather than a narrated deliverance.
