The Flight into Egypt

Story
The Flight into Egypt
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 2:13-23
Retelling
After the wise men left, an angel warned Joseph in a dream to take the child and his mother and flee to Egypt, because Herod was about to search for the child to kill him. Joseph rose by night and escaped with them to Egypt, where they stayed until Herod's death, fulfilling the word, Out of Egypt I called my son. When Herod realised he had been outwitted, he ordered the killing of all boys in Bethlehem and its vicinity aged two and under, matching the time he had learned from the wise men. After Herod died, an angel told Joseph in another dream to return, but hearing that Archelaus reigned in Judea, Joseph was warned again and withdrew to Galilee, settling in Nazareth, fulfilling that Jesus would be called a Nazarene.
Discussion
Joseph obeyed three dreams in a row, each one uprooting his family, and God preserved the child through a refugee's flight. Reflect: 1) How do you obey when God's guidance disrupts your plans? 2) Where do you see God protecting his purposes through ordinary, frightened people? 3) What does Jesus' own experience as a refugee child mean for how you see displaced people?
Significance
The infant Messiah was hunted, hidden, and brought home again, so that even his childhood retraced Israel's story: called out of Egypt and raised in obscurity in Nazareth.
