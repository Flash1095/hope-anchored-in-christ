The Good Samaritan

Story
The Good Samaritan
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Luke 10:25-37
Retelling
A lawyer asked Jesus, 'Who is my neighbor?' and Jesus told a story. A man traveling from Jerusalem to Jericho was robbed, beaten, and left half dead on the roadside. A priest passed by on the other side. A Levite did the same. Then a Samaritan — a member of a people the lawyer's people despised — stopped, bandaged the man's wounds, set him on his own animal, brought him to an inn, and paid for his care, promising to cover whatever more was needed. Jesus asked, 'Which of these three was a neighbor to the man?' The lawyer answered, 'The one who had mercy on him.' Jesus said, 'Go and do likewise.'
Key People
The Samaritan; The Wounded Man; The Priest; The Levite
Themes
Compassion; Neighbor; Mercy
Discussion
The parable refuses to let us define 'neighbor' by tribe, religion, or convenience — the neighbor is the one who shows mercy, even across enemy lines. And it quietly points to Jesus, the true Samaritan who stopped for us when we were left for dead. Reflect: 1) Who is the 'Samaritan' in your life — someone you overlook or avoid? 2) What does mercy cost, and are you willing to pay it? 3) How has Jesus been a neighbor to you?
