The Healing of the Ten Lepers

The Healing of the Ten Lepers

Retelling

On his way to Jerusalem, travelling along the border between Samaria and Galilee, Jesus met ten lepers who stood at a distance, as the law required, and called out, Jesus, Master, have mercy on us. He told them to go and show themselves to the priests. As they went, they were cleansed. One of them, a Samaritan, when he saw he was healed, turned back, praising God loudly, fell at Jesus' feet, and thanked him. Jesus asked where the other nine were: had no one returned to give glory to God except this foreigner? Then he told the Samaritan his faith had made him well.

Discussion

All ten were healed; only one returned. Obedience on the road brought cleansing, but gratitude brought the deeper word of wholeness. The outsider modelled what the insiders missed. Reflect: What answered prayer have you walked on from without turning back to thank God? Does gratitude come naturally to you, or does it need a deliberate turn? How do you honour God publicly, not just privately?

Significance

The ten lepers teach that gratitude completes healing, and that faith is sometimes found most clearly outside expected circles.

Gratitude; Healing; Faith; Outsiders
