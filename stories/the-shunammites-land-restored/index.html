The Shunammite's Land Restored

Story
The Shunammite's Land Restored
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
2 Kings 8:1-6
Retelling
Elisha had warned the Shunammite woman, whose son he had raised, that the LORD had called for a famine of seven years, and told her to go and sojourn wherever she could. She went with her household and sojourned seven years in the land of the Philistines. When the seven years ended, she returned and cried to the king for her house and her land. At that very moment Gehazi, Elisha's servant, was telling the king about the great things Elisha had done, and had just reached the story of how Elisha restored a dead child to life, when the woman herself came in crying for her house and land. Gehazi said, 'My lord, O king, this is the woman, and this is her son, whom Elisha restored to life.' The king asked her, and she told him, and he appointed an officer to restore all that was hers, with all the fruits of the field since the day she left the land until now.
Discussion
The Shunammite's restoration turns on timing: she arrived at the king's court at the exact moment Gehazi was telling her own story. Seven years of exile ended in a single audience, with interest. Reflect: 1) What has a long exile cost you that restoration might repay? 2) How do you recognise God's timing in an ordinary coincidence? 3) What does full restoration, house, land, and seven years' produce, say about God's justice?
Significance
The Shunammite's land was restored in full, with seven years' produce, when she reached the king at the very moment Gehazi told her story.
