Giving of the Law at Sinai

Story
Giving of the Law at Sinai
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Exodus 19-20; Exodus 24; Deuteronomy 5
Summary
Three months after leaving Egypt, Israel camped before Mount Sinai, where God descended in fire, thunder, and trumpet blast. He gave Moses the Ten Commandments and the Law, and the people entered covenant with God, sealed with blood and a shared meal.
Significance
The Law was a gift to a rescued people — God saved them first, then showed them how to live. It anchors hope by revealing God's holy character and pointing us to the Saviour who fulfils it.
