Jesus Turns Water into Wine at Cana

Story
Jesus Turns Water into Wine at Cana
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
John 2:1-11
Summary
At a wedding in Cana, when the wine ran out, Jesus turned six large jars of water into the finest wine, sparing the hosts deep embarrassment. John calls this the first of Jesus' signs, through which He revealed His glory and His disciples believed in Him.
Significance
Jesus' first miracle blessed an ordinary celebration and an unnamed family's crisis. He still delights to bring joy out of empty jars for people who simply do what He says.
