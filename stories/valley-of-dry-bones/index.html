The Valley of Dry Bones

Story
The Valley of Dry Bones
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Ezekiel 37
Retelling
The hand of the LORD carried Ezekiel in the Spirit and set him down in a valley full of very dry bones, and asked him whether these bones could live. He answered that only the Lord God knew. Told to prophesy to the bones, Ezekiel watched as they came together, sinews and flesh covered them, but there was no breath in them. Then he prophesied to the breath, and breath entered them; they lived and stood on their feet, an exceedingly great army. The LORD explained the vision: the bones were the whole house of Israel, saying their hope was lost and they were cut off, but God would open their graves, bring them back to their own land, and put His Spirit in them so they would live.
Discussion
When a situation looks dead beyond reviving, this vision says God is not finished. Israel's hope was not in its own strength but in God's breath. Reflect: 1) What in your life looks like a valley of dry bones right now? 2) How does God's promise of His Spirit change hopelessness into hope? 3) Where is God calling you to speak His word over a dead-looking situation?
Significance
God gives life where only death remains, and His Spirit is the breath of restoration for His people. Doubt: this is a visionary episode, not an ordinary historical event, so it is drafted as borderline despite its famous narrative frame.
