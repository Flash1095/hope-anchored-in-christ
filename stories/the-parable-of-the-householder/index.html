The Parable of the Householder

Story
The Parable of the Householder
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 13:52
Retelling
After telling the parables of the kingdom, Jesus asked his disciples whether they had understood all these things, and they answered, Yes. He said to them, Therefore every teacher of the law who has become a disciple in the kingdom of heaven is like the owner of a house who brings out of his storeroom new treasures as well as old. The trained scribe, made a disciple, does not discard the old treasure of Scripture, nor cling to it alone, but brings out both old and new for the household. The parable closes the chapter of kingdom parables, turning the hearer into a steward of treasures.
Discussion
A disciple-scribe brings out old and new together: the kingdom does not cancel the old treasure, and the old treasure is not enough without the new. Reflect: 1) What old treasure from Scripture are you neglecting, and what new treasure are you resisting? 2) How does this parable shape teaching that serves a household rather than showing off? 3) What does it mean to be a steward, not an owner, of these treasures?
Significance
Every teacher of the law become a disciple of the kingdom is like a householder bringing new treasures out of the storeroom along with the old.
