The Believers Pray for Boldness

Story
The Believers Pray for Boldness
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Acts 4:23-31
Retelling
Being let go, Peter and John went to their own company and reported all that the chief priests and elders had said to them. When the company heard it, they lifted up their voice to God with one accord and prayed, Lord, thou art God, which hast made heaven, and earth, and the sea, and all that in them is. They recalled the words of David: Why did the heathen rage, and the people imagine vain things? The kings of the earth stood up, and the rulers were gathered together against the Lord, and against his Christ. For truly, they said, Herod and Pontius Pilate, with the Gentiles and the people of Israel, had gathered against God's holy child Jesus, whom God had anointed, to do whatever God's hand and counsel had determined before to be done. And now, Lord, they prayed, behold their threatenings, and grant unto thy servants that with all boldness they may speak thy word, by stretching forth thine hand to heal, and that signs and wonders may be done by the name of thy holy child Jesus. When they had prayed, the place was shaken where they were assembled together, and they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and they spake the word of God with boldness.
Discussion
The church's first response to a threat is not a strategy meeting but a prayer that names God's sovereignty first and asks for boldness, not safety. They do not pray for the threats to vanish; they pray to speak through them. Reflect: 1) What do you ask God for first when opposition comes? 2) How does remembering that God determined the outcome change your fear? 3) What would boldness, rather than safety, look like in your situation?
Significance
Persecution drives the young church to prayer, and prayer to boldness: the shaken room and the fresh filling show that courage is a gift God gives his people when they ask, not a temperament some possess.
