The Plague of Gnats

The Plague of Gnats

Retelling

God told Moses to have Aaron stretch out his rod and strike the dust of the land, so that it would become gnats throughout Egypt. Aaron did so, and gnats came upon man and beast; all the dust of the land became gnats. This time the magicians tried to bring forth gnats with their enchantments and could not. They said to Pharaoh, This is the finger of God. But Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he did not listen.

Discussion

The gnats are the first plague Egypt's magicians cannot copy, and their own confession names the finger of God. Power that could be imitated reaches its limit. Reflect: 1) What is the difference between a trick and the finger of God? 2) Why does even an honest confession from others not soften Pharaoh? 3) Where have you seen God's power pass beyond human imitation?

Significance

The gnats mark the point where Egypt's wisdom admits defeat, yet Pharaoh still refuses, showing that evidence alone does not create obedience.

Themes

God's Power; Judgment; Pride
