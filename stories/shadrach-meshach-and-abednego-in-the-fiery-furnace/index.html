Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego in the Fiery Furnace

Story
Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego in the Fiery Furnace
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Daniel 3; Hebrews 11:34
Summary
Ordered to bow to Nebuchadnezzar's golden image, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego refused, declaring God was able to save them — but even if He did not, they would not bow. Thrown into a furnace heated seven times hotter, they were seen walking unbound with a fourth figure, and emerged without even the smell of fire.
Significance
Their 'but even if He does not' is faith at its bravest. Whether God delivers us from the fire or in it, He walks with His people there — and that is our anchor.
