The New Jerusalem

Story
The New Jerusalem
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Revelation 21:9-22:5
Retelling
In a vision, there came unto John one of the seven angels which had the seven vials full of the seven last plagues, and talked with him, saying, Come hither, I will shew thee the bride, the Lamb's wife. And he carried John away in the spirit to a great and high mountain, and shewed him that great city, the holy Jerusalem, descending out of heaven from God, having the glory of God, and her light was like unto a stone most precious, even like a jasper stone, clear as crystal. And it had a wall great and high, and had twelve gates, and at the gates twelve angels, and names written thereon, which are the names of the twelve tribes of the children of Israel: on the east three gates, on the north three gates, on the south three gates, and on the west three gates. And the wall of the city had twelve foundations, and in them the names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb. And he that talked with John had a golden reed to measure the city, and the gates thereof, and the wall thereof. And the city lieth foursquare, and the length is as large as the breadth, and he measured the city with the reed, twelve thousand furlongs. The length and the breadth and the height of it are equal. And he measured the wall thereof, an hundred and forty and four cubits, according to the measure of a man, that is, of the angel. And the building of the wall of it was of jasper, and the city was pure gold, like unto clear glass. And the foundations of the wall of the city were garnished with all manner of precious stones. The first foundation was jasper, the second, sapphire, the third, a chalcedony, the fourth, an emerald, the fifth, sardonyx, the sixth, sardius, the seventh, chrysolite, the eighth, beryl, the ninth, a topaz, the tenth, a chrysoprasus, the eleventh, a jacinth, the twelfth, an amethyst. And the twelve gates were twelve pearls, every several gate was of one pearl, and the street of the city was pure gold, as it were transparent glass. And John saw no temple therein, for the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are the temple of it. And the city had no need of the sun, neither of the moon, to shine in it, for the glory of God did lighten it, and the Lamb is the light thereof. And the nations of them which are saved shall walk in the light of it, and the kings of the earth do bring their glory and honour into it. And the gates of it shall not be shut at all by day, for there shall be no night there. And they shall bring the glory and honour of the nations into it. And there shall in no wise enter into it any thing that defileth, neither whatsoever worketh abomination, or maketh a lie, but they which are written in the Lamb's book of life. And he shewed John a pure river of water of life, clear as crystal, proceeding out of the throne of God and of the Lamb. In the midst of the street of it, and on either side of the river, was there the tree of life, which bare twelve manner of fruits, and yielded her fruit every month, and the leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations. And there shall be no more curse, but the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in it, and his servants shall serve him, and they shall see his face, and his name shall be in their foreheads. And there shall be no night there, and they need no candle, neither light of the sun, for the Lord God giveth them light, and they shall reign for ever and ever.
Discussion
This is a vision of a city that is also a bride: walls of jasper, a city of pure gold like clear glass, twelve gates of pearl bearing the tribes' names, twelve foundations bearing the apostles' names, and measurements that make it a perfect cube. There is no temple, because God and the Lamb are the temple, no sun or moon, because God's glory lightens it, and no night, because the Lord gives light. A river of life flows from the throne, and the tree of life bears twelve fruits with leaves for the healing of the nations. Reflect: 1) Why does the vision join the tribes and the apostles in one city's walls? 2) What does a city with no temple, no sun, and no night say about God's presence? 3) How do the tree's leaves for the healing of the nations complete a book that began with persecuted churches?
Significance
The New Jerusalem vision is Revelation's last picture: a bride-city descending from God, measured and jewelled and open-gated, where God and the Lamb are temple and light, the river of life flows, the tree of life heals the nations, and the servants see his face and reign for ever and ever.
