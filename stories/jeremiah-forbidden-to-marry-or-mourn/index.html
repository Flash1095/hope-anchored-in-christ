Jeremiah Forbidden to Marry or Mourn

Story
Jeremiah Forbidden to Marry or Mourn
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Jeremiah 16:1-9
Retelling
The word of the LORD came unto Jeremiah, saying, Thou shalt not take thee a wife, neither shalt thou have sons or daughters in this place. For thus saith the LORD concerning the sons and concerning the daughters that are born in this place, and concerning their mothers that bare them, and concerning their fathers that begat them in this land: They shall die of grievous deaths; they shall not be lamented, neither shall they be buried; but they shall be as dung upon the face of the earth, and they shall be consumed by the sword, and by famine, and their carcasses shall be meat for the fowls of heaven, and for the beasts of the earth. Thou shalt not go into the house of mourning, neither go to lament nor bemoan them, for I have taken away My peace from this people, saith the LORD, even lovingkindness and mercies. Neither shalt thou go into the house of feasting, to sit with them to eat and to drink. For thus saith the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: Behold, I will cause to cease out of this place in your eyes, and in your days, the voice of mirth, and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride.
Discussion
Jeremiah's whole life — no wedding, no funeral, no feast — became the sermon. Reflect: 1) What does it cost to let your private life preach a public warning? 2) Why would God take away mourning itself as part of judgment? 3) How do you live faithfully in a community whose celebrations God says will cease?
Significance
When a people's peace is taken away, the prophet's celibacy, absence from funerals, and absence from feasts preach what words alone could not carry. Doubt: this is a prophetic sign in the form of a commanded way of life rather than a single enacted event.
