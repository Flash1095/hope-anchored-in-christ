The Calling of Matthew

The Calling of Matthew

Retelling

As Jesus passed through Capernaum he saw Matthew sitting at the tax collector's booth and said simply, Follow me. Matthew got up and followed. That evening Jesus ate at Matthew's house with many tax collectors and others regarded as sinners, and the Pharisees asked his disciples why their teacher ate with such people. Jesus answered that it is not the healthy who need a doctor but the sick, and that he had come to call sinners, not the righteous. Mark and Luke call the tax collector Levi, the same man under his other name. Matthew's old trade had made him rich and despised; his new calling made him the host of a table where outcasts sat with Jesus.

Discussion

Jesus called a collaborator with Rome before he reformed him, and then defended eating with the very people religion avoided. Grace seeks before it scolds. Reflect: Who would you consider too compromised for Jesus to call? Do you keep a cleaner table than Jesus kept? What would following him cost you in your current work?

Significance

Matthew's call shows Jesus' mission to the despised and explains why his table fellowship scandalised the religious establishment.
