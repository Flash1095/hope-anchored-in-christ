Jesus Heals Peter's Mother-in-Law

Story
Jesus Heals Peter's Mother-in-Law
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 8:14-15; Mark 1:29-31; Luke 4:38-39
Retelling
When Jesus entered Simon Peter's house in Capernaum, he found Peter's mother-in-law lying in bed with a fever. Mark and Luke record that they told Jesus about her at once. Jesus went to her, took her by the hand and helped her up, and rebuked the fever, and the fever left her. Immediately she got up and began to wait on them, serving the household and its guests. The healing took place on the same Sabbath as the synagogue exorcism, at the start of Jesus' Galilean ministry, in an ordinary home.
Discussion
The first healing in Peter's own house was quiet, domestic, and instantly fruitful: she was raised up, and she served. Reflect: 1) How does this small household miracle dignify care given at home? 2) What does her immediate service say about gratitude that turns at once to serving others? 3) Where is Jesus present in your own house's fevers and sickrooms?
Significance
Jesus healed Peter's mother-in-law of a fever with a touch and a word, and her restored strength turned at once to serving him and his disciples.
