Peter Denies Jesus

Story
Peter Denies Jesus
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 26:69-75; Mark 14:66-72; Luke 22:54-62; John 18:15-27
Retelling
While Jesus was on trial inside, Peter sat outside in the courtyard warming himself by a fire. A servant girl said he had been with Jesus of Galilee, and Peter denied it before them all. A second time, as he went out to the gateway, another person recognised him and Peter denied it with an oath. A little later the bystanders said his accent gave him away, and Peter began to call down curses and swear, I don't know the man! Immediately a rooster crowed, and Peter remembered Jesus' words, Before the rooster crows, you will disown me three times. Luke adds that the Lord turned and looked straight at Peter. Peter went outside and wept bitterly.
Discussion
Peter's denials escalated from evasion to curses in one courtyard, yet the rooster and the Lord's look broke him into tears: failure named honestly is the doorway to restoration. Reflect: 1) Where does fear make you deny what you know is true? 2) How does the Lord's look, rather than a lecture, bring Peter to tears? 3) What does Peter's bitter weeping, without yet a resolution, teach about repentance?
Significance
The boldest disciple denied his Lord three times in a single night, exactly as Jesus had foretold, and his tears mark the beginning, not the end, of his story.
