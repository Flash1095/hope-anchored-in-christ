Jerusalem Council

Story
Jerusalem Council
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Acts 15; Galatians 2:1-10
Summary
When some insisted Gentile believers must be circumcised and keep the Law to be saved, apostles and elders gathered in Jerusalem to decide. After Peter's testimony and debate, James concluded from Scripture that Gentiles should not be burdened beyond essentials, and a letter of fellowship went out to the churches.
Significance
The Council protected the heart of the gospel: people are saved by grace through faith in Jesus, not by added requirements. That decision still anchors hope — we come to God as we are, by grace alone.
