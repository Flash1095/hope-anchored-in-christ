The Parable of the Growing Seed

The Parable of the Growing Seed

Retelling

Jesus said, This is what the kingdom of God is like: a man scatters seed on the ground, and night and day, whether he sleeps or gets up, the seed sprouts and grows, though he does not know how. All by itself the soil produces grain, first the stalk, then the head, then the full kernel in the head, and as soon as the grain is ripe, he puts the sickle to it, because the harvest has come. The farmer's part is to scatter and to harvest; the growth itself belongs to the earth and to God, and it proceeds whether the farmer watches or sleeps.

Discussion

The kingdom grows while the farmer sleeps: sowing and harvest are ours, but growth is God's secret work. Reflect: 1) Where are you anxiously watching for growth only God can give? 2) How does this parable free you from measuring every seed's progress? 3) What does readiness for harvest look like when the growth has been God's?

Significance

The kingdom grows like seed scattered on the ground: the farmer sows and reaps, but the sprouting, stalk, head, and full kernel come by God's own power, day and night.
