David and Abigail

Story
David and Abigail
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
1 Samuel 25
Retelling
While David was sheltering in the wilderness, he asked Nabal, a wealthy but surly sheep-master at Carmel, for provisions for his men, who had protected Nabal's shepherds. Nabal answered with insults and refused. David, furious, set out with four hundred armed men to destroy Nabal's household. But Abigail, Nabal's wise and beautiful wife, heard what had happened, loaded donkeys with bread, wine, sheep, grain, and fruit, and rode out to meet David. Falling at his feet, she took the blame on herself, urged David not to stain his hands with needless bloodshed, and reminded him that God would make him king without this vengeance. David blessed her, turned back, and accepted her gifts. Ten days later, after hearing what had nearly happened, Nabal died, and David took Abigail as his wife.
Discussion
Abigail stopped a massacre with food, humility, and a timely word, and David later admitted she had kept him from bloodguilt. Godly counsel often arrives as an interruption. Reflect: 1) Who has been an Abigail to you, stopping you from a rash act? 2) How do you receive correction when you are angry? 3) What vengeance are you being urged to leave to God?
Significance
Abigail's intervention saved David from avenging himself, showing that wisdom and peacemaking can turn aside a king's wrath.
