Elisha Purifies the Poisoned Stew

Story
Elisha Purifies the Poisoned Stew
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
2 Kings 4:38-41
Retelling
When Elisha returned to Gilgal during a dearth in the land, the sons of the prophets were sitting before him, and he told his servant to set on the great pot and boil stew for them. One of them went into the field to gather herbs, found a wild vine, gathered wild gourds, filled his lap, and shred them into the pot, not knowing what they were. When the stew was poured out for the men to eat, they cried out, 'O thou man of God, there is death in the pot,' and they could not eat of it. Elisha said, 'Then bring meal,' and he cast the meal into the pot and said, 'Pour out for the people, that they may eat.' And there was no harm in the pot.
Discussion
A well-meaning gatherer nearly poisoned a whole company of prophets with wild gourds he did not recognise. Elisha's remedy was as ordinary as the danger: meal cast into the pot, and the meal was safe. Reflect: 1) What good intentions in your life have carried hidden harm? 2) How do you respond when a community meal, project, or plan turns out to be dangerous? 3) What ordinary remedies has God used in your own crises?
Significance
Elisha's purification of the poisoned stew at Gilgal turned a famine meal that carried death into safe food for the sons of the prophets.
