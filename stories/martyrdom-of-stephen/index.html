Martyrdom of Stephen

Story
Martyrdom of Stephen
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Acts 6-7; Acts 7:54-60
Summary
Stephen, a Spirit-filled servant chosen to care for widows, performed wonders and preached Christ boldly before the Sanhedrin, tracing God's faithfulness through Israel's whole story. Enraged, they stoned him, while Stephen — seeing Jesus standing at God's right hand — prayed for his killers' forgiveness as he died, and a young man named Saul watched approvingly.
Significance
The church's first martyr died as his Master did — forgiving. Stephen's vision anchors hope for persecuted believers everywhere: Jesus does not sit distant from suffering, He stands to receive His own.
