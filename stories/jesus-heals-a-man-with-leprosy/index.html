Jesus Heals a Man with Leprosy

Story
Jesus Heals a Man with Leprosy
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 8:1-4; Mark 1:40-45; Luke 5:12-16
Retelling
As Jesus came down from the mountain, a man covered with leprosy knelt before him and said, Lord, if you are willing, you can make me clean. Jesus stretched out his hand, touched him — the touch itself breaking the rules of separation — and said, I am willing; be clean. At once the leprosy left him. Jesus told him to say nothing to anyone yet, but to show himself to the priest and offer the gift Moses commanded, as a testimony. Instead the man spread the news so widely that Jesus could no longer enter a town openly, and crowds came to him from every quarter, while he withdrew to lonely places to pray.
Discussion
The leper asked about willingness before ability, and Jesus answered both with a touch. Cleansing restored him to community, not just to health. Reflect: What shame or condition makes you keep your distance from Jesus? Do you believe he is willing, or only able? Who around you needs a touch that restores them to community?
Significance
The cleansing shows Jesus' authority over ritual uncleanness and his compassion that restores outcasts to worship and community.
