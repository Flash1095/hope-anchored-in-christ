Jonah and the Great Fish

Story
Jonah and the Great Fish
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Jonah 1-3; Matthew 12:40-41
Summary
Called to preach to enemy Nineveh, Jonah fled by ship toward Tarshish, was thrown into the sea in a storm, and was swallowed by a great fish for three days. Praying from the deep, Jonah was spat onto dry land, preached his reluctant message — and the whole city, from king to cattle, repented.
Significance
God's mercy reached a runaway prophet and a wicked city alike. Jonah anchors hope that no one — not the fleeing, not the far enemy — is beyond the reach of God's compassion.
