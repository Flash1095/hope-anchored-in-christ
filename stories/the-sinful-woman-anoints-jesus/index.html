The Sinful Woman Anoints Jesus

Story
The Sinful Woman Anoints Jesus
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Luke 7:36-50
Retelling
A Pharisee named Simon invited Jesus to eat at his house. While they reclined, a woman of the town known as a sinner learned Jesus was there, brought an alabaster flask of ointment, and stood weeping behind him. She wet his feet with her tears, wiped them with her hair, kissed them, and anointed them with the ointment. Simon thought to himself that if Jesus were a prophet he would know what kind of woman this was. Jesus answered his unspoken thought with a parable of two debtors, one owing much and one little, both forgiven, and asked which would love more. Simon answered, the one forgiven more. Jesus turned to the woman and told Simon that she had loved much because she had been forgiven much, while Simon had offered no water, kiss, or oil. He told her, Your faith has saved you; go in peace.
Discussion
Simon measured the woman by her past; Jesus measured her by her love. Forgiveness, not respectability, produced the devotion Simon's hospitality lacked. Reflect: Whose presence at the table makes you uncomfortable, and why? Do you love much because you know how much you have been forgiven, or little because you think you owe little? What would extravagant gratitude look like in your worship?
Significance
The sinful woman's anointing teaches that great forgiveness produces great love, and it is a distinct event from the later anointing at Bethany.
