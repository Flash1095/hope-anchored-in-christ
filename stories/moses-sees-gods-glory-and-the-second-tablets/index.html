Moses Sees God's Glory and the Second Tablets

Story
Moses Sees God's Glory and the Second Tablets
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Exodus 33-34
Retelling
After the golden calf, Moses interceded for a guilty people and asked God to go with them and to show him his glory. God hid Moses in a cleft of the rock, covered him with his hand, and passed by, proclaiming his own name, merciful and gracious, slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness. Moses then cut two new stone tablets to replace those he had shattered, and God renewed the covenant on Sinai. When Moses came down, his face shone from speaking with God, so brightly that Israel was afraid to approach, and he wore a veil whenever he was not speaking with God or delivering God's words.
Discussion
After failure, God does not merely forgive from a distance; he reveals his character and renews the covenant, and glory leaves a visible mark. Reflect: 1) What does God's self-proclamation teach you about his character after sin? 2) How does Moses' shining face picture time spent with God? 3) Where do you need covenant renewal rather than a fresh start alone?
Significance
On Sinai after the calf, God passed by Moses and proclaimed his merciful character, renewed the broken covenant, and left Moses' face shining. Doubt: this unit is the sequel to The Golden Calf and The Ten Commandments, so whether it stands alone as a separate row is debatable.
