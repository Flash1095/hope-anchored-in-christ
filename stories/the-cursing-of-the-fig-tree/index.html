The Cursing of the Fig Tree

Story
The Cursing of the Fig Tree
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 21:18-22; Mark 11:12-25
Retelling
On the morning after the triumphal entry, Jesus left Bethany hungry and saw a fig tree in leaf by the road. Going to it for fruit, he found nothing but leaves, for it was not the season for figs. He said to it, May no one ever eat fruit from you again, and the tree withered. In Mark's account the disciples noticed it withered by the next morning, and Peter pointed it out in amazement. Jesus used the moment to teach about faith: whoever does not doubt in his heart but believes that what he says will happen will have it, and whatever they ask in prayer, believing, they will receive — with the warning that when they stand praying they must forgive, so that their Father may forgive them.
Discussion
A leafy tree with no fruit became a acted parable of promise without performance, aimed at a temple system in full leaf the same week. The lesson attached is prayer with forgiving faith. Reflect: Where is your life in leaf but without fruit? Do you pray with expectation, or with hedging? What unforgiveness is blocking the prayer you keep praying?
Significance
The fig tree is a prophetic sign against fruitless religion and a teaching on prayer that trusts God and forgives others.
