Paul Shipwrecked on Malta

Story
Paul Shipwrecked on Malta
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Acts 27-28:10
Summary
Sailing to Rome as a prisoner, Paul's ship was battered by a fourteen-day storm until it wrecked on Malta — yet all 276 aboard survived, just as God had promised Paul. On Malta Paul healed the chief official's father and many sick islanders, and the islanders honoured the prisoners greatly.
Significance
Even a shipwreck could not sink a promise. Paul's calm in the storm anchors hope for travellers in crisis: God can bring every soul safely through, even if the ship itself is lost.
