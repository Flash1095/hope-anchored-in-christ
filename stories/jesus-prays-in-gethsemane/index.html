Jesus Prays in Gethsemane

Summary

In the Garden of Gethsemane, Jesus prayed in anguish as He faced the cross, asking if the cup might pass yet submitting: 'Not my will, but yours be done.' His sweat fell like drops of blood, His friends slept instead of watching, and Judas arrived with a crowd to arrest Him with a kiss.

Significance

Gethsemane shows that surrender can be prayed through tears, not around them. Because Jesus submitted there, we can bring our hardest 'let this pass' prayers to a Saviour who understands.
