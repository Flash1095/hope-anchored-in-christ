The Two Sons

Story
The Two Sons
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 21:28-32
Retelling
A man had two sons. He told the first, Son, go and work in the vineyard today. The son answered, I will not, but afterwards he changed his mind and went. The father told the second the same, and he answered, I go, sir, but he did not go. Jesus asked the chief priests and elders which of the two did his father's will. They answered, the first. Jesus told them that tax collectors and prostitutes were entering the kingdom ahead of them, because John the Baptist had come in the way of righteousness and they had not believed him, while the outcasts had — and even after seeing that, the religious leaders had not changed their minds and believed.
Discussion
The parable judges deeds, not declarations: the son who said no and went obeyed; the son who said yes and stayed disobeyed. Religious profession without repentance counts for nothing. Reflect: Where is your obedience a polite 'I go, sir' with no going? What first 'no' in your life needs changing into action? Do you resent outcasts entering ahead of you?
Significance
The two sons expose profession without obedience and show repentant outcasts entering the kingdom ahead of the religious establishment.
