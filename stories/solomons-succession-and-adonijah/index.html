Solomon's Succession and Adonijah

Story
Solomon's Succession and Adonijah
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
1 Kings 1-2
Retelling
When King David was old and stricken in years, Adonijah, his son by Haggith, exalted himself, saying, 'I will be king.' He prepared chariots, horsemen, and fifty men to run before him, and won the support of Joab, captain of the host, and Abiathar the priest. His father had never displeased him at any time by asking, 'Why hast thou done so?' But Zadok the priest, Benaiah, Nathan the prophet, and David's mighty men did not follow Adonijah. While Adonijah feasted with his supporters at En-rogel, Nathan warned Bathsheba that David had sworn Solomon would reign after him, and Bathsheba reminded David of his oath before Nathan confirmed it. David swore again that Solomon would sit on his throne that very day, and ordered Zadok and Nathan to take Solomon to Gihon, anoint him, blow the trumpet, and proclaim, 'God save King Solomon.' The sound of the city rejoicing reached Adonijah's feast, and his guests scattered in fear. Adonijah fled to the horns of the altar, and Solomon spared him on condition of good behaviour. Before he died, David charged Solomon to walk in God's ways, and Solomon then dealt with the old scores: Adonijah was executed when he asked for Abishag as wife, a renewed claim on the throne; Abiathar was banished to Anathoth; Joab, who had fled to the altar, was executed for the murders of Abner and Amasa; and Shimei, who had cursed David, was confined to Jerusalem and later executed for leaving it. So the kingdom was established in Solomon's hand.
Discussion
Succession is where character shows: Adonijah crowned himself at a feast while David still lived, and Solomon's throne was secured by oath, anointing, and hard judgments on old crimes. David's charge to walk in God's ways sits beside his instructions to settle scores, and the two sit uneasily together. Reflect: 1) How do you tell ambition from legitimate succession? 2) What does David's failure ever to ask Adonijah 'Why?' suggest about parenting and leadership? 3) When does settling old scores become justice, and when revenge?
Significance
Solomon's succession turned a feast-day coup into a settled throne, through David's oath, Solomon's anointing at Gihon, and the removal of Adonijah, Joab, and Shimei.
