Israel Demands a King

Israel Demands a King

Retelling

When Samuel was old, he made his sons judges over Israel, but his sons walked not in his ways: they turned aside after lucre, took bribes, and perverted judgment. The elders of Israel gathered at Ramah and said to Samuel, 'Behold, thou art old, and thy sons walk not in thy ways: now make us a king to judge us like all the nations.' The thing displeased Samuel, and he prayed to the LORD. The LORD answered that they had not rejected Samuel but had rejected him, that he should no longer reign over them, as they had done since he brought them out of Egypt. Yet Samuel was to warn them solemnly of the manner of the king who would reign over them: he would take their sons for his chariots and armies, their daughters for his kitchens, their fields, vineyards, and flocks for his servants, and they themselves would become his servants. The people refused to listen and said, 'Nay; but we will have a king over us, that we also may be like all the nations.'

Discussion

Israel wanted a king 'like all the nations' and got exactly what it asked for, with the price stated in advance. The demand was not for leadership but for sameness, and it was a rejection of God's own kingship. Reflect: 1) When have you wanted to be like everyone else more than you wanted to obey God? 2) How do you respond when God grants a request and names its cost? 3) What does it mean for God to remain king even under a human king?

Significance

Israel's demand for a king rejected God's direct reign and accepted, with eyes open, the taxation and conscription Samuel warned would follow.
