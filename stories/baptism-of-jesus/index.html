Baptism of Jesus

Story
Baptism of Jesus
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 3; Mark 1:9-11; John 1:29-34
Summary
Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan to be baptised by John, who called Him the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world. As Jesus came up from the water, the heavens opened, the Spirit descended like a dove, and the Father declared, 'This is my beloved Son.'
Significance
At the Jordan all three Persons of the Trinity appear together as Jesus steps into our place. His baptism anchors hope that in Him we too are called God's beloved children.
