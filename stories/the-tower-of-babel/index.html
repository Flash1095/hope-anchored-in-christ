The Tower of Babel

Story
The Tower of Babel
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Genesis 11:1-9; Acts 2:5-11
Retelling
After the flood, humanity shared one language and settled together on a plain in Shinar. There they decided to build a city and a tower reaching to the heavens, so they could make a name for themselves and avoid being scattered. God saw their pride and confused their language, so they could no longer understand one another, and the building stopped. The people scattered across the earth, and the unfinished city was called Babel, because there the Lord confused the language of the whole world.
Key People
The builders of Babel; Nimrod
Themes
Pride; Language; God's Sovereignty
Discussion
Babel is what happens when human ambition tries to reach heaven on its own terms — impressive, united, and entirely God-less. God scatters what pride builds, but He does not abandon the nations; at Pentecost He begins gathering them back. Reflect: 1) Where do you see 'make a name for ourselves' ambition in your own goals? 2) How does God humble proud projects today? 3) What does Babel teach about unity without God?

Significance
Babel shows what human pride builds, and Pentecost will one day show what God's Spirit gathers. Our hope is not in making our own name great, but in the God who brings scattered people together in Christ.
