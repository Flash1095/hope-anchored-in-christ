Peter Heals the Lame Man

Story
Peter Heals the Lame Man
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Acts 3:1-10
Retelling
Peter and John went up to the temple at the hour of prayer, three in the afternoon, and found a man lame from birth being carried daily to the gate called Beautiful, to beg from those entering. Seeing Peter and John about to go in, he asked for alms. Peter looked at him intently and said, Look at us. Expecting money, the man gave them his attention. Peter said, Silver and gold I do not have, but what I do have I give you: in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, rise up and walk. Taking him by the right hand, Peter raised him, and at once his feet and ankles were made strong. He leaped up, stood, and went into the temple with them, walking, leaping, and praising God, and all the people recognised him as the beggar from the gate and were filled with wonder.
Discussion
The man wanted alms; Peter gave him legs. What the church has to offer is not always what is asked for, but it is always Jesus' name. Reflect: Do you give people what they ask for, or what they actually need? Where has God given you something better than silver and gold to offer? How does praise follow healing in your own story?
Significance
The lame man's healing is the first miracle of Acts, showing the risen Jesus working through his apostles at the temple gate.
