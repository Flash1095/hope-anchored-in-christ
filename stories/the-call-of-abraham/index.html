The Call of Abraham

The Call of Abraham

Retelling

God spoke to Abram in a land far from home and told him to leave his country, his people, and his father's household and go to a land God would show him. God promised to make him a great nation, to bless him, and to bless all the families of the earth through him. Abram was seventy-five years old, with no child and no map, but he went — taking Sarai, his nephew Lot, and everything he owned. He journeyed to Canaan, built altars to the Lord wherever he camped, and walked by faith in a promise he would not see fulfilled in his lifetime.

Themes

Faith; Promise; Obedience

Discussion

Abraham's story begins with a call to leave the familiar before he knew the destination. Faith is not certainty about the road; it is trust in the One who calls. Reflect: 1) What 'familiar' thing might God be asking you to leave behind? 2) How do you trust a promise you cannot yet see fulfilled? 3) How does Abraham's story point forward to blessing for all nations?

Significance

Abraham's journey is the heartbeat of this blog's hope: God keeps covenant promises across generations. When God calls you into the unknown, the same faithful God goes with you.
