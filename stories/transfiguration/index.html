Transfiguration

Transfiguration

Summary

Jesus took Peter, James, and John up a high mountain, where He was transfigured before them — His face shining like the sun and His clothes dazzling white. Moses and Elijah appeared speaking with Him, and the Father's voice declared, 'This is my beloved Son; listen to him.'

Significance

For a moment the disciples saw Jesus as He truly is, glory shining through humanity. That glimpse anchors hope for suffering believers: the Christ who would suffer is the King who will be glorified — and so will His people.
