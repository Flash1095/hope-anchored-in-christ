The Angel Appears to Joseph

The Angel Appears to Joseph

Retelling

When Joseph learned that Mary, to whom he was betrothed, was with child, he planned to divorce her quietly rather than expose her to shame. But an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream and told him not to fear taking Mary as his wife, because the child conceived in her was from the Holy Spirit. The angel said the son would be named Jesus, because he would save his people from their sins, and that this fulfilled the prophet's word that a virgin would bear a son called Immanuel, God with us. Joseph woke, obeyed, took Mary as his wife, and when the child was born, named him Jesus.

Discussion

Joseph's obedience came before he understood everything: he trusted God's word in a dream over the shame and confusion around him. Reflect: 1) Where does obedience require trusting God before you see the outcome? 2) How does Joseph's quiet protection of Mary shape the way you treat people under suspicion? 3) What does the name Jesus, saviour from sins, mean for your own need?

Significance

God announced the incarnation to Joseph as well as to Mary, and Joseph's obedience gave Jesus a lawful home in David's line. The Saviour's mission was named before he was born: he would save his people from their sins.

Obedience; God's Providence; Salvation
