Joash Preserved and Athaliah Overthrown

Story
Joash Preserved and Athaliah Overthrown
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
2 Kings 11; 2 Chronicles 23
Retelling
When King Ahaziah of Judah died, his mother Athaliah seized the throne and destroyed all the royal seed, so that no heir of David would remain. But Jehosheba, Ahaziah's sister and wife of the high priest Jehoiada, stole away the infant Joash, Ahaziah's son, and hid him and his nurse in a bedroom, in the temple for six years while Athaliah ruled. In the seventh year Jehoiada made a covenant with the captains, brought out the seven-year-old Joash, crowned and anointed him king, while the people clapped and shouted, 'God save the king.' Athaliah heard the noise, rushed into the temple crying treason, and was led out and put to death outside. Jehoiada then made a covenant between the LORD, the king, and the people, that they would be the LORD's people, and the people broke down the house of Baal. The child king was set on the throne of David, and the city was quiet.
Discussion
One woman's courage preserved the line of David when a queen tried to end it, and one priest's patient plan waited six years for the right day. God's promises can survive in a hidden bedroom. Reflect: 1) What promise of God feels hidden or endangered in your life? 2) How does Jehosheba's quiet courage compare with loud heroism? 3) What does Jehoiada's covenant say about the link between king, people, and God?
Significance
Joash's preservation kept David's line alive through Athaliah's massacre, and his crowning renewed Judah's covenant after six years of usurpation.
