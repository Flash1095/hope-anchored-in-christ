The Shepherds Visit the Newborn Jesus

Story
The Shepherds Visit the Newborn Jesus
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Luke 2:8-20
Retelling
On the night Jesus was born, shepherds were keeping watch over their flocks near Bethlehem when an angel of the Lord appeared, the glory of the Lord shone around them, and they were terrified. The angel told them not to fear: he brought good news of great joy for all people, that in David's city a Saviour had been born, Christ the Lord, and the sign would be a baby wrapped in cloths and lying in a manger. Suddenly a multitude of angels praised God, saying glory to God in the highest and peace to those on whom his favour rests. The shepherds hurried to Bethlehem, found Mary, Joseph, and the baby in the manger exactly as told, spread the word about what they had heard, and returned glorifying and praising God.
Discussion
The first evangelists were night-shift shepherds, not priests or kings: God announced his Son to ordinary workers and they told everyone. Reflect: 1) Who in your life would least expect to hear good news from you? 2) How does the shepherds' hurry challenge slow, cautious faith? 3) What does it mean that this joy is for all people?
Significance
The gospel was first preached by angels to shepherds and then by shepherds to their neighbours, showing from the first night that Christ came for the lowly as well as for all people.
