The Queen of Sheba Visits Solomon

Story
The Queen of Sheba Visits Solomon
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
1 Kings 10:1-13
Retelling
When the queen of Sheba heard of Solomon's fame concerning the name of the LORD, she came to prove him with hard questions. She arrived at Jerusalem with a very great train, with camels bearing spices, very much gold, and precious stones, and she communed with Solomon about all that was in her heart. Solomon answered all her questions, and there was not anything hidden from the king which he told her not. When the queen saw all Solomon's wisdom, the house he had built, the meat of his table, the seating of his servants, the attendance of his ministers and their apparel, his cupbearers, and his ascent to the house of the LORD, there was no more spirit in her. She told the king that the report she had heard in her own land was true, and behold, the half had not been told her: his wisdom and prosperity exceeded the fame she had heard. Blessed be the LORD thy God, she said, who delighted in thee to set thee on the throne of Israel, because the LORD loved Israel for ever and made thee king to do judgment and justice. She gave the king one hundred and twenty talents of gold, a great abundance of spices, and precious stones, and Solomon gave her all her desire, whatever she asked, besides what he gave out of his royal bounty. Then she returned to her own land with her servants.
Discussion
The queen of Sheba came to test Solomon and left blessing his God. Her visit shows wisdom as witness: a foreign ruler recognised the LORD through the order, justice, and splendour of Solomon's court. Reflect: 1) What would a visitor conclude about your God from the way your household or work is ordered? 2) How do hard questions strengthen faith rather than threaten it? 3) Why does the queen praise God, not Solomon, at the end of her visit?
Significance
The queen of Sheba's visit turned Solomon's wisdom into a witness, as a foreign ruler blessed the LORD who had set him on Israel's throne.
