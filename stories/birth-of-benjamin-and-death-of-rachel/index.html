The Birth of Benjamin and the Death of Rachel

The Birth of Benjamin and the Death of Rachel

Retelling

Leaving Bethel, Jacob's household journeyed toward Ephrath, and Rachel went into hard labour. The midwife comforted her, saying she would have this son also, but as her soul departed she named him Ben-oni, son of my sorrow. His father renamed him Benjamin, son of the right hand. Rachel died and was buried on the way to Ephrath, which is Bethlehem, and Jacob set up a pillar on her grave, the pillar of Rachel's grave, which stood there long after.

Discussion

Joy and grief arrive together in this short episode: a longed-for son is born, and the mother who bore him dies. Jacob's renaming refuses to let sorrow have the last word over the child's identity. Reflect: 1) How have joy and grief arrived together in your own life? 2) What does Jacob's pillar teach about remembering the dead honestly? 3) Why does the renaming of Ben-oni to Benjamin matter?

Significance

This brief journey episode completes Rachel's story and gives Benjamin, the youngest son of Jacob and the brother who will matter so much in the Joseph story, his birth and his name, while marking Rachel's grave as a lasting place of memory.
