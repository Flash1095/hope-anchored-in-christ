Raising of Lazarus

Story
Raising of Lazarus
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
John 11
Summary
Jesus' friend Lazarus died, and by the time Jesus reached Bethany he had been in the tomb four days, while his sisters Martha and Mary grieved. After declaring 'I am the resurrection and the life' and weeping with them, Jesus cried, 'Lazarus, come out!' — and the dead man walked out alive.
Significance
Jesus wept at a grave He was about to empty — He shares our grief even when He knows the ending. This sign anchors the Christian's deepest hope: death does not have the final word over those who belong to Him.
