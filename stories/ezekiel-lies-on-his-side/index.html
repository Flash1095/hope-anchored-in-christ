Ezekiel Lies on His Side

Story
Ezekiel Lies on His Side
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Ezekiel 4:4-8
Retelling
The LORD told Ezekiel, Lie thou also upon thy left side, and lay the iniquity of the house of Israel upon it: according to the number of the days that thou shalt lie upon it thou shalt bear their iniquity. For I have laid upon thee the years of their iniquity, according to the number of the days, three hundred and ninety days: so shalt thou bear the iniquity of the house of Israel. And when thou hast accomplished them, lie again on thy right side, and thou shalt bear the iniquity of the house of Judah forty days: I have appointed thee each day for a year. Therefore thou shalt set thy face toward the siege of Jerusalem, and thine arm shall be uncovered, and thou shalt prophesy against it. And behold, I will lay bands upon thee, and thou shalt not turn thee from one side to another, till thou hast ended the days of thy siege.
Discussion
Ezekiel lay bound on one side for over a year, carrying in his body the years of Israel's sin. Reflect: 1) What does it mean for a prophet to bear a people's sin in his own body? 2) Why a day for a year — what does slow, counted time teach about judgment? 3) How do you stay faithful to an obedience that takes months and earns only stares?
Significance
A prophet lying bound for 390 days and then 40 more made Israel's long years of iniquity visible in a single suffering body. Doubt: this is a prophetic sign-act, a staged picture or lived sign, rather than an ordinary historical episode.
