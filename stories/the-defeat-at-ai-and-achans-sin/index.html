The Defeat at Ai and Achan's Sin

Story
The Defeat at Ai and Achan's Sin
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Joshua 7-8
Retelling
Fresh from the victory at Jericho, Israel attacked the small town of Ai and was driven back in defeat. Joshua fell on his face before God, who told him that Israel had taken devoted spoil from Jericho against his command. Lots were cast, and the guilt fell on Achan, who admitted hiding a robe, silver, and gold in his tent. The stolen goods were removed and Achan was judged, and the ban on Jericho's spoil was cleared. Then God told Joshua to attack Ai again, this time with an ambush behind the town. The men of Ai chased the main force, the ambush took the town, and Ai fell. Joshua built an altar and read the Law to all Israel, blessing and curse together.
Discussion
One hidden compromise can stall a whole community, and God will not bless a victory built on disobedience. Yet the story also shows a way back: confession, removal of the sin, and a fresh start. Reflect: 1) What 'hidden spoil' might you be keeping that God has told you to let go? 2) How does Achan's confession challenge the way you handle your own failures? 3) Where do you need a fresh start after a defeat?
Significance
Israel learned that victory depends on obedience, not on the size of the enemy, and that hidden sin must be faced honestly before God can give success.
