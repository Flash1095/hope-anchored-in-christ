The Parable of the Leaven

Story
The Parable of the Leaven
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 13:33; Luke 13:20-21
Retelling
Jesus told another parable: The kingdom of heaven is like yeast that a woman took and mixed into three measures of meal until the whole was leavened. The leaven is tiny beside the flour, hidden once mixed, and yet it transforms the whole batch from within, quietly and completely. Luke records the same parable beside the mustard seed, pairing small beginnings with inward, pervasive growth. Nothing in the story explains itself further; the point lies in the hidden, thorough work of the kingdom.
Discussion
The kingdom does not arrive by spectacle but works like yeast: hidden, small, and thorough. Reflect: 1) Where is God's kingdom working hiddenly in your life, like yeast in dough? 2) How does this parable correct the expectation that God's work must be visible to be real? 3) What small, faithful mixture, prayer, word, or kindness, might God be using to leaven a whole household?
Significance
The kingdom of heaven works like leaven hidden in a large measure of flour: small, unseen, and transforming everything it touches.
