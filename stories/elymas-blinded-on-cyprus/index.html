Elymas Blinded on Cyprus

Story
Elymas Blinded on Cyprus
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Acts 13:4-12
Retelling
Barnabas and Saul, sent forth by the Holy Ghost, departed unto Seleucia, and from thence sailed to Cyprus. When they were at Salamis, they preached the word of God in the synagogues of the Jews, and they had also John to their minister. When they had gone through the isle unto Paphos, they found a certain sorcerer, a false prophet, a Jew, whose name was Barjesus, which was with the deputy of the country, Sergius Paulus, a prudent man, who called for Barnabas and Saul and desired to hear the word of God. But Elymas the sorcerer, for so is his name by interpretation, withstood them, seeking to turn away the deputy from the faith. Then Saul, who also is called Paul, filled with the Holy Ghost, set his eyes on him and said, O full of all subtilty and all mischief, thou child of the devil, thou enemy of all righteousness, wilt thou not cease to pervert the right ways of the Lord? And now, behold, the hand of the Lord is upon thee, and thou shalt be blind, not seeing the sun for a season. And immediately there fell on him a mist and a darkness, and he went about seeking some to lead him by the hand. Then the deputy, when he saw what was done, believed, being astonished at the doctrine of the Lord.
Discussion
The first missionary encounter is a contest over one man's hearing: a sorcerer tries to turn the deputy from the faith, and Paul names the opposition plainly and answers it with a sign. Sergius Paulus believes not because of the marvel alone but because he is astonished at the doctrine of the Lord. Reflect: 1) How do you tell opposition that is spiritual from opposition that is merely human? 2) Why does Luke pair the blinding with the deputy's belief in the teaching? 3) When is plain speech about evil necessary, and when is it merely harsh?
Significance
On Cyprus the gospel wins its first Gentile ruler, and Paul steps forward as the mission's spokesman: the word, defended and demonstrated, overcomes a court sorcerer's opposition and opens the Gentile world.
