Zacchaeus Meets Jesus

Story
Zacchaeus Meets Jesus
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Luke 19:1-10
Retelling
Zacchaeus, chief tax collector in Jericho and rich, wanted to see who Jesus was, but he was short, and the crowd blocked his view. So he ran ahead and climbed a sycamore tree along the road Jesus would pass. When Jesus reached the spot, he looked up, called Zacchaeus by name, and told him to come down at once, because he must stay at his house that day. Zacchaeus hurried down and welcomed him joyfully, while the crowd grumbled that Jesus had gone to be the guest of a sinner. Standing up, Zacchaeus pledged to give half his goods to the poor and to repay fourfold anyone he had cheated. Jesus declared that salvation had come to that house, for the Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost.
Discussion
Zacchaeus climbed a tree to see Jesus and found Jesus already looking for him. His repentance was not a feeling but a ledger: money returned, wrongs repaid. Reflect: What tree are you willing to climb — what embarrassment will you risk — to see Jesus? Does your repentance change your money? Who in your town does everyone grumble about, whom Jesus might be seeking?
Significance
Zacchaeus embodies Luke's theme that salvation reaches the lost and proves itself in restitution and generosity.
