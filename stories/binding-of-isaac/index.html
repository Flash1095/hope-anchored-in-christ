Binding of Isaac

Story
Binding of Isaac
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Genesis 22; Hebrews 11:17-19
Summary
God tested Abraham by asking him to offer his beloved son Isaac as a burnt offering on Mount Moriah. At the last moment God stopped him and provided a ram caught in a thicket, and Abraham named the place 'The LORD Will Provide.'
Significance
On the very mountain where Jerusalem's Temple would stand, God showed He Himself provides the sacrifice. This story anchors our hope in a Father who did not spare His own Son — and can be trusted with what we love most.
