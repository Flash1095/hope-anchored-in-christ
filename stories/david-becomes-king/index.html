David Becomes King

Story
David Becomes King
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
2 Samuel 2-5; 1 Chronicles 11
Summary
After Saul's death, Judah anointed David king at Hebron, and seven years later all Israel came to make him king over the united kingdom. David captured Jerusalem from the Jebusites, made it his capital — the City of David — and brought the ark of God toward it with rejoicing.
Significance
God kept His promise to the shepherd of Bethlehem through years of waiting and civil war. David's throne reminds us God's timing is rarely fast, but His anointing never fails.
