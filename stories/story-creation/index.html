Creation

Story
Creation
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Genesis 1:1-2:3; Genesis 1-2; Psalm 19:1
Retelling
In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth. Over six days He spoke light into darkness, separated sky from sea, filled the land with plants and animals, and set the sun, moon, and stars in their courses. On the sixth day He formed human beings in His own image, male and female, and gave them stewardship over the world He had made. Then He looked at everything He had made and called it very good, and on the seventh day He rested, blessing it as holy.
Discussion
If God made everything and called it good, then your life has purpose and your body, your work, and your world have dignity. Creation is not an accident, and neither are you. Reflect: 1) What part of creation most reminds you of God's power and goodness? 2) How does knowing you bear God's image change the way you see yourself? 3) How does it change the way you treat others?
Significance
Everything begins with a good Creator who made people on purpose and for fellowship with Him. When life feels chaotic, Creation reminds us our hope rests in the One who was there first and still sustains all He made.
