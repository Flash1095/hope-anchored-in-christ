Isaiah's Vision and Call

Story
Isaiah's Vision and Call
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Isaiah 6
Retelling
In the year King Uzziah died, Isaiah saw the Lord seated on a throne, high and lifted up, His robe filling the Temple, while seraphim cried to one another, Holy, holy, holy, is the LORD of hosts, and the whole earth is full of His glory. Overwhelmed by his own unclean lips and those of his people, Isaiah cried out in despair, but a seraph touched his mouth with a live coal from the altar and told him his sin was purged. Then he heard the voice of the Lord asking whom He should send, and answered, Here am I; send me. The Lord sent him to a people who would hear without understanding, a hard commission Isaiah accepted from a cleansed heart.
Discussion
God's call begins with seeing Him as He is, and that sight exposes us before it sends us. Isaiah could only go once his guilt was dealt with. Reflect: 1) How does God's holiness change the way you see your own words? 2) What excuse keeps you from answering, Here am I? 3) Are you willing to serve faithfully even when the response is hard?
Significance
True service flows from cleansed lips and a willing heart, not from confidence in our own fitness. Doubt: this is a visionary call account, not an ordinary historical episode, so its narrative frame is visionary in character.
