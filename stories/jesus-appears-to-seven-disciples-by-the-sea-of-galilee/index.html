Jesus Appears to Seven Disciples by the Sea of Galilee

Story
Jesus Appears to Seven Disciples by the Sea of Galilee
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
John 21:1-23
Retelling
Afterwards Jesus appeared again to his disciples by the Sea of Galilee, where Simon Peter, Thomas, Nathanael, James and John, and two others were together. Peter said, I'm going out to fish, and the others went with him, but that night they caught nothing. At dawn Jesus stood on the shore, though they did not recognise him, and called out to cast the net on the right side of the boat. When they did, they could not haul in the net for the great number of fish, one hundred and fifty-three, yet the net was not torn, and John said to Peter, It is the Lord! Peter jumped into the water and swam to shore, where Jesus had a charcoal fire with fish and bread ready, and invited them, Come and have breakfast. After breakfast Jesus asked Peter three times, Simon son of John, do you love me? and each time restored him with the charge to feed his lambs and sheep, foretelling that Peter would one day be led where he did not want to go. Jesus then told Peter to follow him.
Discussion
Three denials by a charcoal fire were answered by three questions beside another charcoal fire, and a failed night's fishing by a net-breaking catch: restoration, not replacement, is how Jesus reinstates his own. Reflect: 1) How does Jesus' threefold question heal Peter's threefold denial? 2) What does breakfast on the beach say about the risen Lord's care for ordinary work? 3) What does Follow me mean for you after failure?
Significance
By the Sea of Galilee the risen Jesus gave a miraculous catch, shared breakfast, and restored Peter with threefold love and commission, closing the Gospel with Follow me.
