The Presentation of Jesus in the Temple

Story
The Presentation of Jesus in the Temple
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Luke 2:22-38
Retelling
When the time came for their purification under the Law of Moses, Mary and Joseph brought the infant Jesus to Jerusalem to present him to the Lord, offering the sacrifice of a pair of doves or two young pigeons, the offering of the poor. In the temple was Simeon, a righteous and devout man waiting for the consolation of Israel, to whom the Holy Spirit had promised he would not die before seeing the Lord's Messiah. Guided by the Spirit, Simeon took the child in his arms, praised God, and said he could now depart in peace, for his eyes had seen God's salvation, a light for the Gentiles and glory for Israel. He blessed them and told Mary that this child was destined for the falling and rising of many in Israel and for a sign spoken against, and that a sword would pierce her own soul. The prophetess Anna, who had worshipped in the temple for decades, also came up, gave thanks to God, and spoke about the child to all who were waiting for Jerusalem's redemption.
Discussion
Simeon and Anna had waited a lifetime, and they recognised the Saviour as a baby in arms: long faithfulness sharpens sight. Reflect: 1) What are you waiting for God to show you? 2) How does Simeon's warning to Mary prepare you for a Saviour who divides responses? 3) What does Anna's lifelong worship say about faithfulness without a platform?
Significance
At his presentation Jesus was publicly recognised as God's salvation for Israel and the Gentiles, and Mary was warned that his mission would pierce her own heart.
