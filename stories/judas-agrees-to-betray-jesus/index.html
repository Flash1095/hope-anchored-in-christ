Judas Agrees to Betray Jesus

Story
Judas Agrees to Betray Jesus
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 26:14-16; Mark 14:10-11; Luke 22:3-6
Retelling
Then Judas Iscariot, one of the Twelve, went to the chief priests and asked, What are you willing to give me if I deliver him over to you? They counted out thirty pieces of silver for him, and from that time Judas watched for an opportunity to hand Jesus over when no crowd was present. Luke records that Satan entered Judas, and that he conferred with the chief priests and the officers of the temple guard about how he might betray Jesus. The bargain was struck before the Passover meal, and the betrayal price was fixed in advance of the arrest.
Discussion
Judas sold his Master for the price of a slave's compensation, and the chief priests bought a quiet arrest: betrayal often starts as a bargain, not a blow. Reflect: 1) What small bargains have you made that traded loyalty for gain or safety? 2) How does the chief priests' willingness to pay for secrecy expose fear of the crowds? 3) Where do you need to name a compromise before it becomes a betrayal?
Significance
The betrayal was arranged in advance for thirty pieces of silver, showing that Jesus' arrest was not an accident of the crowd but a purchased, deliberate act.
