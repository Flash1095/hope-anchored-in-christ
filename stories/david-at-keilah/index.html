David at Keilah

Story
David at Keilah
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
1 Samuel 23:1-14
Retelling
David was told that the Philistines were fighting against Keilah and robbing the threshingfloors. He inquired of the LORD, who said, 'Go and smite the Philistines, and save Keilah.' His men feared to go, for they were already afraid in Judah, so David inquired again, and the LORD said, 'Arise, go down to Keilah; for I will deliver the Philistines into thine hand.' David went, fought the Philistines with a great slaughter, took their cattle, and saved the inhabitants of Keilah. Then Saul heard David was at Keilah and said God had delivered him into his hand, for he was shut in by gates and bars. David, learning Saul meant to besiege the city, asked Abiathar to bring the ephod and inquired of the LORD whether the men of Keilah would deliver him up to Saul. The LORD answered that they would, so David and his six hundred men departed and stayed in the wilderness of Ziph, while Saul sought him every day and God delivered him not into his hand.
Discussion
Keilah shows a deliverer betrayed by the very people he saved: David rescued the city, then learned its men would hand him to Saul. Twice he inquired of God before acting, and twice God answered plainly. Reflect: 1) How do you respond when gratitude turns into betrayal? 2) What does inquiring of God twice, not once, teach about guidance? 3) When should you leave a place you have served rather than defend your right to stay?
Significance
At Keilah David saved a city that would have surrendered him, and learned to seek God's guidance before trusting even those he had rescued.
