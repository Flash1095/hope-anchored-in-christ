The Thief on the Cross

Story
The Thief on the Cross
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Luke 23:39-43
Retelling
Two criminals were crucified with Jesus, one on either side. One of them hurled insults at him: Aren't you the Messiah? Save yourself and us! But the other rebuked him, saying, Don't you fear God, since you are under the same sentence? We are punished justly, for we are getting what our deeds deserve. But this man has done nothing wrong. Then he said, Jesus, remember me when you come into your kingdom. Jesus answered him, Truly I tell you, today you will be with me in paradise.
Discussion
A dying criminal with no time for good works, baptism, or a better life was promised paradise on the strength of one plea: remember me. Reflect: 1) What does this promise say about salvation by grace rather than by record? 2) How does the second thief's confession, justly punished, innocent Jesus, model honest repentance? 3) What does today mean for someone who fears it is too late?
Significance
Beside the cross, a condemned criminal confessed Jesus' innocence and kingship and received the promise of paradise that very day, showing that grace reaches even the final hour.
