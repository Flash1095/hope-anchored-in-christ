The Temptation in the Wilderness

Story
The Temptation in the Wilderness
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 4:1-11; Luke 4:1-13; Hebrews 4:15
Retelling
Fresh from His baptism, Jesus was led by the Spirit into the wilderness, where He fasted forty days and nights. There the tempter came with three offers: turn stones into bread, throw Himself from the temple and let angels catch Him, and bow down in exchange for all the kingdoms of the world. Each time Jesus answered not with argument but with Scripture — 'Man shall not live by bread alone,' 'You shall not put the Lord your God to the test,' 'Worship the Lord your God and serve Him only.' The devil left Him, and angels came and ministered to Him. Where Adam fell in a garden of abundance, Jesus stood firm in a desert of hunger.
Key People
Jesus; Satan
Themes
Temptation; Scripture; Victory
Discussion
Jesus fought temptation the way you can: with God's Word, wielded precisely, in the moment of weakness. Temptation itself is not sin — even Jesus was tempted — but it must be answered. Reflect: 1) Which of the three temptations — appetite, spectacle, or power — tempts you most? 2) What Scripture do you have ready for your weakest moments? 3) How does Jesus' victory give you hope in your own struggles?

Significance
Where Adam fell and Israel grumbled, Jesus stood firm in the same wilderness. Because He was tempted in every way yet did not sin, He understands our struggles and helps us in ours.
