Jesus Heals a Blind Man at Bethsaida

Story
Jesus Heals a Blind Man at Bethsaida
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Mark 8:22-26
Retelling
When Jesus and his disciples came to Bethsaida, some people brought a blind man and begged Jesus to touch him. Jesus took the blind man by the hand and led him outside the village, spat on his eyes and put his hands on him, and asked, Do you see anything? The man looked up and said, I see people; they look like trees walking around. Once more Jesus put his hands on his eyes, and then his eyes were opened, his sight was restored, and he saw everything clearly. Jesus sent him home, telling him not to go into the village. This two-stage healing is unique in the Gospels, and it stands in Mark's narrative between the feeding of the four thousand and Peter's confession.
Discussion
Sight came in stages: first trees walking, then everything clearly, and Jesus led the man outside the village to heal him personally. Reflect: 1) Where is your sight still partial, like trees walking? 2) What does Jesus' willingness to touch a second time say about gradual healing? 3) How does being led by the hand picture guidance when you cannot see the way?
Significance
At Bethsaida Jesus healed a blind man in two stages, first partial sight and then clear sight, after leading him by the hand outside the village.
