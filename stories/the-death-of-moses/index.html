The Death of Moses

Story
The Death of Moses
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Deuteronomy 34; Numbers 27:12-23
Retelling
After forty years of leading Israel, Moses climbed Mount Nebo at God's command and looked across the Jordan at the land he had longed to enter: Gilead, Judah, and the Negev as far as Zoar. God showed him the land promised to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, but because of his disobedience at Meribah, Moses would see it only with his eyes. There in Moab, Moses, the servant of the Lord, died at 120, still vigorous, and God himself buried him in a valley no one has ever found. Israel wept for thirty days, and Joshua, filled with the spirit of wisdom, took up leadership, though no prophet ever arose like Moses, whom the Lord knew face to face.
Discussion
Moses finished faithful without finishing in the land, and God honoured him in death as in life. A life's work can be complete even when you do not see its end. Reflect: 1) How do you handle a promise you see but do not enter? 2) What does God's burial of Moses say about his care for his servants? 3) How are you preparing the next leader, as Moses prepared Joshua?
Significance
Moses died within sight of the promise, buried by God himself, closing the wilderness generation and handing leadership to Joshua, while remaining the unmatched prophet whom God knew face to face.
