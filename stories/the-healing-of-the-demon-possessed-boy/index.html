The Healing of the Demon-Possessed Boy

Story
The Healing of the Demon-Possessed Boy
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 17:14-21; Mark 9:14-29; Luke 9:37-43
Retelling
Coming down from the mountain of transfiguration, Jesus found a crowd arguing with his disciples. A father knelt before him: his only son was seized by a spirit that threw him down, made him foam and grind his teeth, and had nearly destroyed him since childhood. The disciples had tried and failed to cast it out. Jesus rebuked the faithless generation, had the boy brought to him, and as the spirit convulsed the boy, the father cried, I believe; help my unbelief. Jesus rebuked the spirit and commanded it out, and the boy, who looked dead, rose to his feet. Alone later, the disciples asked why they had failed, and Jesus answered that this kind comes out only by prayer.
Discussion
The father's prayer is the most honest in the Gospels: belief and unbelief in one breath. The disciples' failure came after their greatest moment, a warning that yesterday's mountain does not guarantee today's power. Reflect: Can you pray 'I believe; help my unbelief' without editing it? Where has ministry failure humbled you into prayer? What impossible case are you bringing to Jesus after others have failed?
Significance
The boy's deliverance links faith, prayer, and Jesus' authority, and humbles a discipleship that trusts method over dependence on God.
