The Healing of Bartimaeus

Story
The Healing of Bartimaeus
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 20:29-34; Mark 10:46-52; Luke 18:35-43
Retelling
As Jesus left Jericho with a great crowd, Bartimaeus, a blind beggar sitting by the roadside, heard that Jesus of Nazareth was passing and began to shout, Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me. The crowd told him to be quiet, but he shouted all the louder. Jesus stopped, had him called, and asked what he wanted. Bartimaeus answered, Lord, that I might receive my sight. Jesus said his faith had healed him, and immediately he received his sight and followed Jesus along the road. Matthew's account mentions two blind men healed at the same place; Mark and Luke name Bartimaeus, the one whose persistent cry the crowd tried to silence.
Discussion
Bartimaeus would not let the crowd manage his access to Jesus, and Jesus stopped a whole procession for one shouting beggar. His request was specific, and his healing ended in following. Reflect: What voices are telling you to quiet down before Jesus? Is your prayer specific, or safely vague? After Jesus answers, do you follow, or walk away?
Significance
Bartimaeus's healing, on the road to Jerusalem, models persistent, specific faith and shows mercy reaching the last person the crowd would have chosen.
