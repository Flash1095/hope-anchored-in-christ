Barnabas and Saul Sent Out from Antioch

Barnabas and Saul Sent Out from Antioch

Retelling

Now there were in the church that was at Antioch certain prophets and teachers: Barnabas, and Simeon that was called Niger, and Lucius of Cyrene, and Manaen, which had been brought up with Herod the tetrarch, and Saul. As they ministered to the Lord, and fasted, the Holy Ghost said, Separate me Barnabas and Saul for the work whereunto I have called them. And when they had fasted and prayed, and laid their hands on them, they sent them away.

Discussion

The missionary journeys begin not with a strategy but with worship and fasting, and the church sends its two best teachers rather than keeping them. The call is heard together and confirmed by laying on hands. Reflect: 1) How does your church discern a call: by vote, by voice, or by worship and fasting together? 2) What does it cost a church to send its best people away? 3) How do you tell a genuine call from a good idea?

Significance

Antioch's sending is the hinge of Acts: from here the gospel moves deliberately to the Gentile world, and the church learns that mission begins in worship and is confirmed by the whole body, not by individuals alone.
