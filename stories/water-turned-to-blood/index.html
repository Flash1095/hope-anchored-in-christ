Water Turned to Blood

Story
Water Turned to Blood
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Exodus 7:14-25
Retelling
Moses and Aaron went to Pharaoh at the river in the morning, as God commanded. Aaron stretched out his rod over the waters of Egypt, and all the water in the Nile, its streams, canals, ponds, and pools of water became blood. The fish died, the river stank, and the Egyptians could not drink its water. Yet Pharaoh's magicians did the same with their enchantments, and Pharaoh's heart was hardened; he turned into his house and did not take even this to heart. Seven days passed while the river remained struck.
Discussion
The first plague strikes at Egypt's lifeline: the river that watered, fed, and carried its life becomes undrinkable. Imitation again hardens rather than humbles Pharaoh. Reflect: 1) What 'river' in your life would leave you helpless if God struck it? 2) Why does seeing a wonder not always lead to repentance? 3) How does this plague show that God's judgment touches daily life, not only religion?
Significance
The Nile turned to blood announces that the LORD, not Egypt's river, gives life, and that Pharaoh's refusal will now cost Egypt itself.
