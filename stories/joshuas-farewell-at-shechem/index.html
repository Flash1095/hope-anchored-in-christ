Joshua's Farewell at Shechem

Story
Joshua's Farewell at Shechem
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Joshua 23-24
Retelling
Old and advanced in years, Joshua gathered all Israel at Shechem, the place where God had first promised the land to Abraham. He retold their story: God had called Abraham, brought Israel out of Egypt, given them the land, and driven out their enemies. Then he set a choice before them in plain words: put away the gods your ancestors served, and serve the LORD. He declared his own decision for all to hear: as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD. The people answered that they too would serve him. Joshua made a covenant with them that day, wrote the words in the book of the Law, and set up a great stone under an oak as a witness against them if they broke their word. Then he sent the people away, each to his inheritance.
Discussion
Every generation must choose for itself whom it will serve; a parent's faith cannot be inherited like land. Joshua's farewell is both a warning and an invitation. Reflect: 1) What 'other gods' compete for your loyalty today? 2) How would you finish Joshua's sentence, 'As for me and my house...'? 3) What witness or habit could help your household keep its promise?
Significance
Joshua's farewell closes the conquest with a covenant choice: the land is given, but loyalty to the LORD must be renewed by each generation.
