Peter and Cornelius

Peter and Cornelius

Summary

God gave Peter a rooftop vision in Joppa while preparing Cornelius, a God-fearing Roman centurion in Caesarea, to send for him. Peter entered a Gentile home — unthinkable for him before — preached Jesus, and the Holy Spirit fell on all the Gentile hearers, who were then baptised.

Significance

At Caesarea the gospel crossed its widest cultural border yet, and God made clear He shows no partiality. This anchors hope for every outsider: the door of faith is open to all who believe.
