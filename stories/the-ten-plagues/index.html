The Ten Plagues

Story
The Ten Plagues
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Exodus 7-11; Psalm 78:43-51
Summary
When Pharaoh refused to let Israel go, God sent ten plagues on Egypt — water to blood, frogs, gnats, flies, livestock disease, boils, hail, locusts, darkness, and finally the death of the firstborn. Each plague confronted Egypt's gods and Pharaoh's hard heart.
Significance
The plagues show that no power on earth can outlast the LORD who hears His people groan. For anyone facing a seemingly immovable Pharaoh, this story says God is able to break every chain.
