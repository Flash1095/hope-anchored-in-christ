Elijah on Mount Carmel

Story
Elijah on Mount Carmel
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
1 Kings 18; James 5:17-18
Retelling
Israel had turned to Baal under King Ahab and Queen Jezebel, and Elijah — God's lone prophet — challenged the 450 prophets of Baal to a contest on Mount Carmel. Each side would build an altar and call on their god to send fire; the god who answered would be the true God. Baal's prophets shouted and cut themselves from morning until evening with no answer. Then Elijah rebuilt the Lord's altar, soaked it with water three times, and prayed a simple prayer — and fire fell from heaven, consuming the sacrifice, the wood, the stones, and even the water. The people fell on their faces crying, 'The Lord — He is God!'
Discussion
Elijah's prayer was short and plain, and God answered with fire — because the issue was never the prophet's eloquence but God's reality. False gods always demand more and deliver nothing; the true God acts. Reflect: 1) What false gods demand your time, money, or devotion? 2) How does Elijah's simple prayer encourage your own prayer life? 3) Where do you need God to show Himself as the true God?
Significance
When faith feels outnumbered 450 to one, Carmel still stands: the LORD is God, and He answers prayer. One faithful witness, trusting Him, is never truly alone.
