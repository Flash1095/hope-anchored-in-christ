The Magi Visit Jesus

Story
The Magi Visit Jesus
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 2:1-12
Retelling
After Jesus was born in Bethlehem, wise men from the east arrived in Jerusalem asking where the king of the Jews had been born, because they had seen his star and come to worship him. King Herod was troubled, gathered the chief priests and scribes, and learned from them that the Messiah was to be born in Bethlehem, as the prophet Micah had written. Herod sent the wise men to Bethlehem, asking them to report back so that he too might worship, though he meant to kill the child. The star went ahead of them until it stopped over the house, and entering, they saw the child with Mary his mother, fell down and worshipped him, and opened their treasures, offering gold, frankincense, and myrrh. Warned in a dream not to return to Herod, they went home by another way.
Discussion
The wise men followed a star across borders to worship a child in a house, while the king in Jerusalem plotted murder: the same news divides hearts. Reflect: 1) What has God used to draw you toward Jesus? 2) How do you tell true worship from Herod's false worship? 3) What costly gift does worship ask of you?
Significance
Gentile seekers recognised and worshipped Israel's newborn king before his own ruler did, and their gifts honoured him as king, priest, and sacrifice to come.
