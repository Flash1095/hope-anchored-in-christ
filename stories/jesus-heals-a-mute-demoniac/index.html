Jesus Heals a Mute Demoniac

Story
Jesus Heals a Mute Demoniac
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 9:32-34
Retelling
While the two blind men were going out, a man who was demon-possessed and could not talk was brought to Jesus. When the demon was driven out, the man who had been mute spoke, and the crowd was amazed, saying, Nothing like this has ever been seen in Israel! But the Pharisees said, It is by the prince of demons that he drives out demons. The healing belongs to the early Galilean ministry, after the raising of Jairus' daughter, and it is narrated separately from the later healing of a blind and mute demoniac: here the man's affliction is muteness alone, and the charge it provokes is recorded in brief.
Discussion
A voice restored became a voice of amazement for the crowd and of accusation for the Pharisees: the same miracle divides its witnesses. Reflect: 1) What silenced voice in your life or community needs Jesus' freeing word? 2) How does amazement differ from the Pharisees' explanation? 3) Where do you explain away God's work rather than wonder at it?
Significance
Jesus freed a demon-possessed man who could not speak, and the crowd marvelled at what had never been seen in Israel, while the Pharisees attributed the work to the prince of demons.
