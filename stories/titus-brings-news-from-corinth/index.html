Titus Brings News from Corinth

Titus Brings News from Corinth

Retelling

Paul writes that when he was come into Macedonia, his flesh had no rest, but he was troubled on every side: without were fightings, within were fears. Nevertheless God, that comforteth those that are cast down, comforted him by the coming of Titus, and not by his coming only, but by the consolation wherewith he was comforted in the Corinthians, when he told Paul of their earnest desire, their mourning, and their fervent mind toward him, so that Paul rejoiced the more. Paul had made them sorry with a letter, and though he had repented of it at first, he now rejoiced, not that they were made sorry, but that they sorrowed to repentance, for godly sorrow worketh repentance to salvation not to be repented of, but the sorrow of the world worketh death. He lists what their godly sorrow had wrought: carefulness, clearing of themselves, indignation, fear, vehement desire, zeal, revenge. He writes that he did not write for the cause of him that had done the wrong, nor for his sake that suffered wrong, but that their care for Paul might appear unto them in the sight of God. Therefore they were comforted in their comfort, yea, and exceedingly the more joyed they for the joy of Titus, because his spirit was refreshed by them all. For if Paul had boasted any thing to Titus of the Corinthians, he was not ashamed, but as he spake all things in truth, even so their boasting, which he made before Titus, was found a truth. And Titus's inward affection was more abundant toward them, whilst he remembered the obedience of them all, how with fear and trembling they received him. Paul closes, I rejoice therefore that I have confidence in you in all things.

Discussion

This is a reconciliation told by letter: a severe letter, a painful visit, and Titus returning with news of repentance that comforts a troubled apostle. Paul distinguishes godly sorrow, which leads to repentance, from the sorrow of the world, which leads to death. Reflect: 1) How do you tell godly sorrow from mere regret? 2) Why does Titus's refreshment matter as much as the Corinthians' repentance? 3) What does it take for a wounded relationship between a church and its founder to be restored?

Significance

Titus's news turns Paul's trouble in Macedonia into joy: the Corinthian church's repentance proves the severe letter's purpose, refreshes Titus, and restores Paul's confidence in a church he had feared losing.
