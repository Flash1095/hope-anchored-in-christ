Jeremiah's Call

Story
Jeremiah's Call
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Jeremiah 1
Retelling
The word of the LORD came to Jeremiah in the days of Josiah, king of Judah, telling him that before he was formed in the womb God knew him, and before he was born God had set him apart as a prophet to the nations. Jeremiah objected that he could not speak, for he was only a youth, but the LORD told him not to say so, touched his mouth, and put His words in it, appointing him over nations and kingdoms to uproot and tear down, to build and to plant. Two signs followed: a branch of an almond tree, showing God watching over His word to perform it, and a boiling pot tilted from the north, picturing the disaster coming on Judah.
Discussion
God calls the unwilling and the young, and His answer to I cannot is I am with you. Jeremiah's authority came from God's words, not his age or eloquence. Reflect: 1) What inadequacy do you use to excuse yourself from obeying God? 2) How does it change things to know God knew and set you apart before you could speak? 3) What hard word might God be asking you to carry faithfully?
Significance
A prophet's authority rests on God's call and God's words, not on age, experience, or confidence.
