The Death of Ezekiel's Wife

Story
The Death of Ezekiel's Wife
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Ezekiel 24:15-27
Retelling
The word of the LORD came to Ezekiel: Son of man, behold, I take away from thee the desire of thine eyes with a stroke: yet neither shalt thou mourn nor weep, neither shall thy tears run down. Forbear to cry, make no mourning for the dead, bind the tire of thine head upon thee, and put on thy shoes upon thy feet, and cover not thy lips, and eat not the bread of men. So Ezekiel spoke unto the people in the morning, and at even his wife died, and in the morning he did as he was commanded. The people said unto him, Wilt thou not tell us what these things are to us, that thou doest so? Ezekiel answered, The word of the LORD came unto me, saying, Speak unto the house of Israel: Thus saith the Lord GOD: Behold, I will profane My sanctuary, the excellency of your strength, the desire of your eyes, and that which your soul pitieth; and your sons and your daughters whom ye have left shall fall by the sword. And ye shall do as I have done: ye shall not cover your lips, nor eat the bread of men. And your tires shall be upon your heads, and your shoes upon your feet: ye shall not mourn nor weep; but ye shall pine away for your iniquities, and mourn one toward another. Thus Ezekiel is unto you a sign: according to all that he hath done shall ye do: and when this cometh, ye shall know that I am the Lord GOD. And thou, son of man, in the day when I take from them their strength, the joy of their glory, the desire of their eyes, and that whereupon they set their minds, their sons and their daughters — in that day he that escapeth shall come unto thee, to cause thee to hear it with thine ears. In that day shall thy mouth be opened to him which is escaped, and thou shalt speak, and be no more dumb: and thou shalt be a sign unto them; and they shall know that I am the LORD.
Discussion
Ezekiel lost his wife and was forbidden to mourn her — so that his grief would preach Jerusalem's grief. Reflect: 1) What does it cost a prophet when his own sorrow becomes the sermon? 2) Why would God forbid mourning for the very loss He inflicted? 3) How do you grieve faithfully when God says the normal comforts are not yours to take?
Significance
A prophet forbidden to weep for his wife became the sign of a people who would lose their sanctuary and their children and have no mourning left to mourn with. Doubt: this is a prophetic sign-act, a staged picture or lived sign, rather than an ordinary historical episode.
