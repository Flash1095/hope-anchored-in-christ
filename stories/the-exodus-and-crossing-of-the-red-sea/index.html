The Exodus and Crossing of the Red Sea

Story
The Exodus and Crossing of the Red Sea
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Exodus 12-14; Psalm 78:13
Summary
Pharaoh finally released Israel, then pursued them with his army to the shore of the Red Sea, where the people cried out in terror. God parted the waters through Moses, Israel crossed on dry ground, and the sea closed over Pharaoh's army, and Miriam led the women in a song of praise.
Significance
The Exodus is Israel's defining rescue and a pattern of all God's saving work. When there is water in front and an army behind, the God who makes a way through the sea can be trusted.
