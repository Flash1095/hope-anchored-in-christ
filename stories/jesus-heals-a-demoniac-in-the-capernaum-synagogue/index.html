Jesus Heals a Demoniac in the Capernaum Synagogue

Story
Jesus Heals a Demoniac in the Capernaum Synagogue
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Mark 1:21-28; Luke 4:31-37
Retelling
On the Sabbath in Capernaum, Jesus entered the synagogue and taught with authority, unlike the scribes, and the people were amazed. A man possessed by an impure spirit cried out, What do you want with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are, the Holy One of God! Jesus rebuked him, Be quiet! and Come out of him! The impure spirit shook the man violently and came out with a shriek, without injuring him. The people were astonished and asked what this new teaching with authority was, that even impure spirits obeyed him, and news about Jesus spread throughout Galilee.
Discussion
The first miracle Mark records is not a healing of the body but a confrontation in worship: authority over evil, exercised by a word, in the synagogue itself. Reflect: 1) How does the demon's accurate confession challenge faith that knows the right words without obeying? 2) What does Jesus' silence command say about testimony from evil? 3) Where do you need Jesus' authoritative word rather than more discussion?
Significance
In the Capernaum synagogue Jesus' authoritative teaching was confirmed by an authoritative word that drove out an impure spirit, and his fame spread through Galilee.
