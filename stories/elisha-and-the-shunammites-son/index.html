Elisha and the Shunammite's Son

Story
Elisha and the Shunammite's Son
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
2 Kings 4:8-37
Retelling
A wealthy woman of Shunem persuaded her husband to build a small chamber on their roof for Elisha, so the prophet would have a place to stay. In return, Elisha promised that within a year she would embrace a son, though she protested that he should not mislead her. The child was born, grew, and one day, working with his father in the harvest, cried out about pain in his head, and died on his mother's knees at noon. She laid him on Elisha's bed, shut the door, and rode to find Elisha at Carmel. She seized his feet and would not let go until he came. Elisha sent Gehazi ahead with his staff, but the boy did not stir. Then Elisha himself went in, shut the door, prayed, and stretched himself upon the child until the boy sneezed seven times and opened his eyes. Elisha called the mother in, and she took up her son and went out.
Discussion
The Shunammite's hospitality made room for God in her house, and when grief struck, she went straight to the prophet instead of accepting death as final. Her steady refusal to turn back is a model of persevering faith. Reflect: 1) How does your home make room for God's servants and God's work? 2) What grief have you carried straight to God without stopping? 3) How does this mother's persistence encourage you to keep praying?
Significance
The Shunammite's son is Elisha's great resurrection miracle, rewarding quiet hospitality with life restored to a household that had asked for nothing.
