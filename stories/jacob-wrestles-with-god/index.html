Jacob Wrestles with God

Story
Jacob Wrestles with God
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Genesis 32:22-32; Hosea 12:3-5
Summary
Returning home in fear of his brother Esau, Jacob wrestled through the night with a mysterious man — God Himself — at the ford of the Jabbok. Jacob clung on saying he would not let go without a blessing, and he left limping with a new name: Israel.
Significance
God meets us in the night seasons, and even our limps can become marks of blessing. Jacob's clinging faith is a picture of prayer that holds on to God until He blesses — and He does.
