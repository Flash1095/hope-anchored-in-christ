Wilderness Wanderings

Wilderness Wanderings

Summary

For forty years Israel wandered in the wilderness, sustained by manna, quail, and water from the rock, yet often grumbling and rebelling. God disciplined the generation that left Egypt, raised up a new one, gave the Law again through Moses, and brought them at last to the plains of Moab.

Significance

The wilderness was not wasted years — God fed, led, and formed His people there day by day. If you are in a long in-between season, His daily manna is still enough, and He has not stopped leading.
