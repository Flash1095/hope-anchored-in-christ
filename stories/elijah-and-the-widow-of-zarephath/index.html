Elijah and the Widow of Zarephath

Story
Elijah and the Widow of Zarephath
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
1 Kings 17:8-24
Retelling
God sent Elijah to Zarephath, where a widow would feed him. When he arrived he found her gathering sticks, and asked for water and a morsel of bread. She answered that she had only a handful of meal in a barrel and a little oil in a cruse, and she was gathering sticks to bake a last meal for herself and her son . Elijah told her not to fear: make him a little cake first, for God had said the barrel of meal would not waste, nor the cruse of oil fail, until rain came. She obeyed, and the meal and oil lasted daily. Later her son fell sick and died, and she blamed Elijah. Elijah took the boy stretched himself upon him three times, prayed, and the child's life returned to him. The widow then said, now I know you are a man of God and the word of the LORD in your mouth is truth.
Discussion
God provided for a foreign widow through a prophet she had no reason to trust, and her small act of giving first opened the door to daily provision. Provision and resurrection meet in one household. Reflect: 1) What 'handful of meal' are you afraid to share? 2) How do you pray when provision is followed by fresh grief? 3) What does this widow's story say about God's care crossing national borders?
Significance
At Zarephath, God sustained a Gentile widow through famine and raised her son, showing that his provision and power reach beyond Israel.
