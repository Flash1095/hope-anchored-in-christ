Aaron's Staff Becomes a Serpent

Story
Aaron's Staff Becomes a Serpent
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Exodus 7:8-13
Retelling
Before the plagues began, God told Moses and Aaron what to do when Pharaoh demanded a sign. Aaron cast down his staff before Pharaoh and his servants, and it became a serpent. Pharaoh called in the wise men, sorcerers, and magicians of Egypt, who did the same with their rods, each becoming a serpent. But Aaron's rod swallowed up their rods. Pharaoh's heart was hardened, and he would not listen, just as the LORD had said.
Discussion
The sign is a preview of the whole contest: Egypt can imitate the form of God's power, but it cannot match it, and imitation is finally swallowed up. Pharaoh sees the sign and still refuses. Reflect: 1) Why is imitation of God's power not the same as God's power? 2) How do you respond when evidence is clear but obedience is costly? 3) What does Pharaoh's hardening warn you about repeated refusal?
Significance
The serpent sign establishes the pattern of the Exodus contest: God's power shown openly, Egypt's magicians outmatched, and Pharaoh's heart hardening in spite of what he has seen.
