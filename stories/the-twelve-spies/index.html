The Twelve Spies and Israel's Unbelief

Story
The Twelve Spies and Israel's Unbelief
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Numbers 13-14; Hebrews 3:16-19
Summary
Twelve spies explored Canaan for forty days and returned with magnificent fruit — but ten spread a fearful report about giants that melted the people's hearts. Only Joshua and Caleb trusted God's promise, and the unbelieving generation was sentenced to wander until a new generation could enter.
Significance
Fear and faith saw the same land and reached opposite conclusions. Caleb's story anchors hope for anyone outnumbered by doubters: God honours those who follow Him wholeheartedly, however long the wait.
