The Birth of Isaac

Isaac's birth came at the set time God had spoken, after long waiting, when Abraham was a hundred years old. God's promise did not depend on human timing or strength. Reflect: 1) Where are you waiting for a promise God has spoken, and how does Isaac's birth shape your waiting? 2) What does it mean that God acted exactly when he said he would? 3) How does Sarah's laughter — surprise turned to joy — speak to your own trust in God?

Retelling

The LORD visited Sarah as he had said, and did unto Sarah as he had spoken. Sarah conceived, and bare Abraham a son in his old age, at the set time of which God had spoken to him. Abraham called the name of his son that was born unto him, whom Sarah bare to him, Isaac. Abraham circumcised his son Isaac being eight days old, as God had commanded him. Abraham was an hundred years old when his son Isaac was born unto him. And Sarah said, God hath made me to laugh, so that all that hear will laugh with me. And she said, Who would have said unto Abraham, that Sarah should have given children suck? For I have born him a son in his old age.

Significance

Isaac's birth shows that God keeps his covenant promises at his appointed time, even when fulfilment seems humanly impossible.
