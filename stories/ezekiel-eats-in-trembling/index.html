Ezekiel Eats in Trembling

Story
Ezekiel Eats in Trembling
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Ezekiel 12:17-20
Retelling
The word of the LORD came to Ezekiel: Son of man, eat thy bread with quaking, and drink thy water with trembling and with carefulness. And say unto the people of the land, Thus saith the Lord GOD of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and of the land of Israel: They shall eat their bread with carefulness, and drink their water with astonishment, that her land may be desolate from all that is therein, because of the violence of all them that dwell therein. And the cities that are inhabited shall be laid waste, and the land shall be desolate; and ye shall know that I am the LORD.
Discussion
Ezekiel ate and drank as a man under siege — trembling, careful, astonished — though no army was at his door. Reflect: 1) Why does God make an exile in Babylon eat like a man in besieged Jerusalem? 2) What does eating 'with carefulness' teach about taking daily bread for granted? 3) How does a prophet's trembling meal preach that judgment is not far away, but already at the table?
Significance
A trembling meal in Babylon preached a desolate land in Judah: the siege would reach every table, and the people would know the LORD in their want. Doubt: this is a prophetic sign-act, a staged picture or lived sign, rather than an ordinary historical episode.
