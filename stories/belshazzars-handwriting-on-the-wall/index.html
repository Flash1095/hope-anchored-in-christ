Belshazzar's Handwriting on the Wall

Story
Belshazzar's Handwriting on the Wall
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Daniel 5
Retelling
While Babylon was under threat, King Belshazzar held a great feast for a thousand lords and, in a drunken boast, ordered the gold and silver vessels taken from the Temple in Jerusalem to be brought so his guests could drink from them, praising gods of gold, silver, bronze, iron, wood, and stone. Suddenly fingers of a man's hand appeared and wrote on the plaster of the palace wall. Terrified, the king summoned his wise men, but none could read the writing until Daniel was called. Daniel read MENE, MENE, TEKEL, UPHARSIN: God had numbered Belshazzar's kingdom and finished it, weighed him and found him wanting, and divided his kingdom to the Medes and Persians. That very night Belshazzar was slain.
Discussion
Sacred things are not props for a party, and a kingdom that mocks God is already counted. Belshazzar knew his father's story and ignored it. Reflect: 1) What warnings from others' lives are you tempted to ignore? 2) Where do you treat holy things casually? 3) How does knowing that God weighs our lives change the way you spend today?
Significance
God numbers kingdoms and weighs kings; no feast, army, or wall can postpone His judgment when it comes.
