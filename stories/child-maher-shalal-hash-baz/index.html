The Child Maher-shalal-hash-baz

Story
The Child Maher-shalal-hash-baz
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Isaiah 8:1-4
Retelling
The LORD told Isaiah to take a great roll and write in it with a man's pen concerning Maher-shalal-hash-baz, and Isaiah took faithful witnesses, Uriah the priest and Zechariah the son of Jeberechiah. Then Isaiah went to the prophetess, and she conceived and bore a son. The LORD told him to call the child's name Maher-shalal-hash-baz, for before the child would have knowledge to cry, My father, and my mother, the riches of Damascus and the spoil of Samaria would be taken away before the king of Assyria. The child's name itself — speed to the spoil, haste to the prey — was the message: Assyria was coming, and the powers Judah feared would fall first.
Discussion
Isaiah's own child carried a sermon in his name. Reflect: 1) What does it cost a family when a parent's obedience becomes public? 2) Why does God sometimes write His warning in a life, not just in words? 3) How does this sign warn Judah that its feared enemies are not the real danger?
Significance
God's warnings are written into ordinary lives as well as spoken words; this child's name announced that Assyria would strip the very powers Judah feared. Doubt: this is a prophetic sign-act, a staged picture or lived sign, rather than an ordinary historical episode.
