The Healing of the Official's Son

The Healing of the Official's Son

Retelling

When Jesus returned to Cana, where he had turned water into wine, a royal official came from Capernaum begging him to come down and heal his son, who was at the point of death. Jesus answered that unless people saw signs and wonders they would not believe. The official pressed on: Sir, come down before my child dies. Jesus told him to go; his son would live. The man believed the word Jesus had spoken and went on his way. While he was still travelling, his servants met him with the news that his son was recovering, and on comparing the hour, he found it was the very hour Jesus had said, Your son will live. He himself believed, and his whole household with him.

Discussion

The official's faith moved in stages: he came for a visit, settled for a word, and discovered the healing had happened at a distance. Belief preceded evidence. Reflect: Are you demanding Jesus come down to your terms before you trust him? What word of his are you being asked to walk home on? How has one person's faith spread to a whole household in your experience?

Significance

The official's son is John's second sign, showing that Jesus' word heals at a distance and that faith can rest on his word alone.
