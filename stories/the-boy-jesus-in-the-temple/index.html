The Boy Jesus in the Temple

Story
The Boy Jesus in the Temple
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Luke 2:41-52
Retelling
Every year Jesus' parents went to Jerusalem for Passover, and when he was twelve they took him with them. After the feast, as they travelled home, Mary and Joseph realised Jesus was not among the caravan, and after searching a day they returned anxiously to Jerusalem. After three days they found him in the temple courts, sitting among the teachers, listening to them and asking them questions, and everyone who heard him was amazed at his understanding and answers. His mother asked why he had treated them so, saying she and his father had been anxiously searching, and Jesus answered, Didn't you know I had to be in my Father's house? They did not understand what he meant. He returned with them to Nazareth and was obedient to them, and he grew in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and men.
Discussion
At twelve, Jesus already knew whose Son he was, yet he went home and lived obediently in obscurity for years: identity did not skip ordinary faithfulness. Reflect: 1) How does Jesus' question about his Father's house shape your own sense of belonging to God? 2) What does his return to Nazareth teach about honouring parents while following God? 3) Where is God growing you quietly, without an audience?
Significance
The only recorded episode from Jesus' childhood shows him already conscious of his Father's business, yet choosing years of obedient obscurity before his public ministry.
