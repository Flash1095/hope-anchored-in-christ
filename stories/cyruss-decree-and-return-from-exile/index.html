Cyrus's Decree and Return from Exile

Story
Cyrus's Decree and Return from Exile
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Ezra 1; Isaiah 44:28-45:1; 2 Chronicles 36:22-23
Summary
In his first year, Cyrus of Persia — named in Isaiah's prophecy long before — decreed that the exiles could return to Jerusalem and rebuild God's house, even returning the Temple treasures Nebuchadnezzar had taken. Some 42,000 exiles journeyed home under Sheshbazzar and Zerubbabel.
Significance
God moved the heart of a pagan emperor to fulfil His promise of homecoming, right on Jeremiah's schedule. No exile — of place, habit, or heart — is permanent when God says it is time to come home.
