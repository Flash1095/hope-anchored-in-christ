Jesus Heals a Man with Dropsy

Jesus Heals a Man with Dropsy

Retelling

One Sabbath Jesus went to eat in the house of a prominent Pharisee, where he was being carefully watched, and in front of him was a man suffering from abnormal swelling of the body, called dropsy. Jesus asked the Pharisees and experts in the Law, Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath or not? They remained silent, so he took hold of the man, healed him, and sent him on his way. Then he asked them, If one of you has an ass or an ox fallen into a pit on the Sabbath day, will you not immediately pull it out? They had nothing to say. The healing took place before the meal and the parable of the wedding feast that followed it.

Discussion

Jesus asked the question before he healed, and silence answered him: no one could defend leaving a suffering man unhealed on the Sabbath. Reflect: 1) Where does careful watching replace compassion in your gatherings? 2) How does Jesus' well-side argument expose inconsistent mercy? 3) What suffering person is in front of you while you debate the rules?

Significance

At a Pharisee's Sabbath meal Jesus healed a man with dropsy after his critics fell silent, showing that mercy to the suffering cannot wait for a more convenient day.
