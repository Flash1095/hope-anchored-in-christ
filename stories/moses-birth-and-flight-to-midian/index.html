Moses' Birth and Flight to Midian

Story
Moses' Birth and Flight to Midian
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Exodus 1-2; Acts 7:20-29; Hebrews 11:23-27
Retelling
Pharaoh, fearing Israel's growth, ordered every Hebrew baby boy thrown into the Nile. A Levite mother hid her son for three months, then set him in a basket among the reeds, where Pharaoh's daughter found him, took pity, and raised him as her own, with his own mother hired as his nurse. Grown, Moses saw an Egyptian beating a Hebrew and killed him, burying the body in the sand. When the killing became known and Pharaoh sought his life, Moses fled to Midian, where he sat by a well, defended Jethro's daughters, married Zipporah, and settled as a shepherd, far from the palace and the people he had tried to defend.
Discussion
God preserved Moses through a mother's courage, a princess's pity, and years of obscurity in Midian before he was ready to lead. Rescue often looks slow and hidden. Reflect: 1) Where has God used an unlikely protector in your life? 2) How do you respond when trying to help goes wrong? 3) What might God be forming in you during a waiting season?
Significance
Before Moses led Israel out, God first rescued Moses himself and humbled him in Midian, showing that deliverance begins with God's preservation, not human strength.
