Anointing of Saul as King

Story
Anointing of Saul as King
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
1 Samuel 9-10; 1 Samuel 15
Summary
Israel demanded a king like the other nations, and God directed Samuel to anoint Saul, a tall Benjamite searching for his father's lost donkeys. Saul was empowered by God's Spirit and acclaimed king at Mizpah, though Samuel warned the people of the cost of kingship.
Significance
Saul's rise shows God working through Israel's flawed request — and his later failure shows why no human king is enough. Our hope finally rests in the King God would raise from David's line.
