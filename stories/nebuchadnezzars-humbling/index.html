Nebuchadnezzar's Humbling

Story
Nebuchadnezzar's Humbling
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Daniel 4
Retelling
Nebuchadnezzar dreamed of a great tree reaching to heaven, cut down at God's command but with its stump left bound in the field. Daniel explained the dream with grief: the tree was the king himself, who would be driven from men to live like a beast and eat grass, until he learned that the Most High rules over the kingdom of men. A year later, as the king boasted over Babylon as his own great work, the word came true at once. After seven times had passed, he lifted his eyes to heaven, his understanding returned, and he praised the King of heaven, declaring that those who walk in pride God is able to abase.
Discussion
Pride turns blessing into boasting, and God has a way of teaching what success cannot. Nebuchadnezzar's restoration began when he looked up. Reflect: 1) Where are you tempted to call God's gifts your own achievement? 2) What would it look like to acknowledge God's rule before a crisis forces it? 3) How does this story change the way you pray for proud or powerful people?
Significance
Pride goes before a fall, but humble acknowledgment of God's rule opens the way back to restoration.
