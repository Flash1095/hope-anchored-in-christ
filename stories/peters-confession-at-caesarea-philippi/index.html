Peter's Confession at Caesarea Philippi

Peter's Confession at Caesarea Philippi

Retelling

At Caesarea Philippi, Jesus asked his disciples who people said the Son of Man was. They reported the guesses: John the Baptist, Elijah, Jeremiah, or one of the prophets. Then he asked the sharper question, But what about you? Who do you say I am? Simon Peter answered, You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God. Jesus blessed him, saying flesh and blood had not revealed this but his Father in heaven, and declared that on this rock he would build his church, and the gates of Hades would not overcome it, giving Peter the keys of the kingdom. Then he ordered the disciples not to tell anyone that he was the Messiah.

Discussion

Peter's confession came not from crowd opinion but from the Father's revelation, and it became the foundation-stone of the church's identity. Reflect: 1) Whose opinion of Jesus most shapes yours: the crowd's or the Father's? 2) What does it mean to confess Jesus as Messiah and Son of the living God today? 3) Why would Jesus order silence about a true confession at that moment?

Significance

The disciples' confession that Jesus is the Messiah, the Son of the living God, marks the turning point of the Gospels, and on it Jesus promised to build his church.
