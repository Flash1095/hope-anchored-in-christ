Samson and the Philistines

Story
Samson and the Philistines
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Judges 13-16; Hebrews 11:32
Summary
Set apart as a Nazirite from birth, Samson fought the Philistines with supernatural strength, yet was undone by compromise and betrayed by Delilah, who learned the secret of his hair. Blinded and enslaved, Samson prayed one last time and pulled down the temple of Dagon on Israel's enemies — and on himself.
Significance
Samson's wasted gifts are a warning, but his final prayer is pure hope: God heard a broken man in the dark. It is never too late to cry out to the God who restores.
