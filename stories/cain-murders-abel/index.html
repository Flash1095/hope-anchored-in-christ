Cain Murders Abel

Cain Murders Abel

Summary

Cain and Abel, sons of Adam and Eve, both brought offerings to God, and God looked with favour on Abel's offering. Jealous and angry, Cain lured his brother into a field and killed him, becoming the first murderer and a wanderer, though God still marked him for protection.

Significance

Abel's story shows that faithful worship matters and that God sees those who suffer for righteousness. Even in this dark first family tragedy, God's mercy toward Cain points to a God who restrains evil and preserves hope.
