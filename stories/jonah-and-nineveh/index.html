Jonah and Nineveh

Story
Jonah and Nineveh
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Jonah
Retelling
God told Jonah to preach to Nineveh, the brutal capital of Assyria, and Jonah ran the other way — boarding a ship to the far end of the world. God sent a storm, the sailors threw Jonah overboard, and a great fish swallowed him. Three days in the belly of the fish, Jonah prayed, and the fish vomited him onto dry land. He went to Nineveh and preached the shortest sermon in the Bible — 'Forty more days and Nineveh will be overthrown' — and the whole city, from king to cattle, repented in sackcloth. God relented, and Jonah was furious: he had wanted judgment for his enemies, but God wanted mercy.
Key People
Jonah
Themes
Mercy; Repentance; Obedience
Discussion
Jonah is the prophet who hated his own message succeeding. God's mercy is wider than our comfort — it extends even to people we consider enemies. Reflect: 1) Who are you tempted to believe is beyond God's mercy? 2) What are you running from that God has asked you to do? 3) How does Jonah's story confront your own desire for judgment over grace?
