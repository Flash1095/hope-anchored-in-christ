Israel Leaves Sinai

Story
Israel Leaves Sinai
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Numbers 10:11-36
Retelling
On the twentieth day of the second month of the second year, the cloud lifted from the tabernacle of testimony, and Israel set out from the wilderness of Sinai for the first time in marching order. The tribes moved in their divisions, the ark went before them to seek a resting place, and the tabernacle was carried in the midst. Moses invited Hobab, son of Reuel the Midianite and Moses' brother-in-law, to come with them, saying they would do him good, for the LORD had spoken good concerning Israel, and Hobab would be as eyes to them in the wilderness. Whenever the ark set forward, Moses said, Rise up, LORD, and let your enemies be scattered; and when it rested, he said, Return, O LORD, to the many thousands of Israel.
Discussion
Leaving Sinai is leaving the mountain of law for the road of trust: the cloud, not a map, decides when to move and when to rest. Moses' prayer turns every march into worship. Reflect: 1) How do you discern when God is lifting the cloud in your life? 2) Why does Moses invite Hobab, an outsider guide, to travel with Israel? 3) What does it mean to pray at both setting out and settling down?
Significance
Israel's departure from Sinai closes the year at the mountain and opens the wilderness march, with the ark and the cloud leading a people who must now follow day by day.
