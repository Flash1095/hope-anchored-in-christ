The Prodigal Son

Story
The Prodigal Son
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Luke 15:11-32
Retelling
A younger son demanded his inheritance early — in effect wishing his father dead — and squandered it all in wild living until a famine left him feeding pigs and longing for their pods. Coming to his senses, he rehearsed a speech of repentance and headed home, expecting at best to be hired as a servant. But while he was still far off, his father saw him, ran to him, embraced him, and threw a feast, saying, 'This son of mine was dead and is alive again; he was lost and is found.' The older brother, who had never left, stood outside angry — and the father went out to him too, pleading, 'All that is mine is yours.'
Key People
The Father; The Younger Son; The Older Son
Themes
Grace; Repentance; Forgiveness
Discussion
This is the gospel in one story: a Father who runs toward rebels and pleads with the self-righteous alike. Whether you are the younger son or the older brother, the feast is open and the Father is waiting. Reflect: 1) Which brother do you relate to more, and why? 2) What keeps you from 'coming home' to God? 3) How does the Father's welcome change the way you welcome others?
