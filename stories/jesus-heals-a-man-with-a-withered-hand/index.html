Jesus Heals a Man with a Withered Hand

Jesus Heals a Man with a Withered Hand

Retelling

On another Sabbath Jesus entered the synagogue, where a man with a shrivelled hand was present, and the Pharisees watched to see whether he would heal on the Sabbath so they could accuse him. Jesus asked them whether it was lawful on the Sabbath to do good or to do evil, to save life or to kill, and told the man to stand up in front of everyone. Then he said to the man, Stretch out your hand. The man stretched it out, and his hand was completely restored, as sound as the other. The Pharisees went out and plotted with the Herodians how they might kill Jesus, while the healed man stood whole among them.

Discussion

Healing on the Sabbath forced the question into the open: is the Sabbath for doing good or for watching a man suffer? Jesus answered by healing in full view. Reflect: 1) Where do you watch suffering while debating rules? 2) How does Stretch out your hand call for trust before the healing is seen? 3) What does the plot to kill Jesus after a healing reveal about hardened hearts?

Significance

On the Sabbath Jesus restored a shrivelled hand in full view of his critics, teaching that doing good is lawful on the Sabbath, and the healing hardened his opponents toward murder.
