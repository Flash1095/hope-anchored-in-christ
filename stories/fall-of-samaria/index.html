Fall of Samaria

Story
Fall of Samaria
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
2 Kings 17; 2 Kings 18:9-12
Summary
After centuries of idolatry and ignored prophetic warnings, the northern kingdom of Israel fell when Assyria besieged and captured its capital, Samaria, in 722 BC. Thousands were deported across the Assyrian empire, and foreign peoples were settled in the land, ending the northern kingdom.
Significance
Samaria's fall is a sober warning that God keeps His word in judgment as in mercy. Yet the surviving southern kingdom — and God's promises through it — carried hope forward toward the Messiah.
