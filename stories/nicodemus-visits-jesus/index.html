Nicodemus Visits Jesus

Story
Nicodemus Visits Jesus
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
John 3:1-21
Retelling
Nicodemus, a Pharisee and member of the Jewish council, came to Jesus by night. He admitted that Jesus' signs showed God was with him, but Jesus answered that no one can see the kingdom of God unless he is born again. Nicodemus, thinking in literal terms, asked how an old man could re-enter his mother's womb. Jesus explained the new birth as being born of water and the Spirit, as mysterious as wind whose sound is heard but whose origin cannot be traced. When Nicodemus still struggled, Jesus pressed further: as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so the Son of Man must be lifted up, so that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life, for God so loved the world that he gave his only Son.
Discussion
Nicodemus came with respect and left with a riddle: everything he knew as a teacher could not explain the new birth. Jesus moved him from signs to surrender. Reflect: What part of your faith is still lived at night — private, cautious, uncommitted? Where are you trying to understand the Spirit instead of receiving him? What does being 'born again' demand of your old certainties?
Significance
The night-time conversation gives the Gospel its most quoted summary of salvation and frames the new birth as the entry into God's kingdom.
