Jesus Heals the Gerasene Demoniac

Story
Jesus Heals the Gerasene Demoniac
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 8:28-34; Mark 5:1-20; Luke 8:26-39
Retelling
Across the Sea of Galilee, in the country of the Gerasenes, Jesus met a man possessed by many demons who lived among the tombs, naked, uncontrollable, and crying out night and day. No chains could hold him. Seeing Jesus from afar, he ran and worshipped him, and the demons, calling themselves Legion, begged not to be tormented, but to be sent into a nearby herd of pigs. Jesus permitted it, and the herd rushed down the steep bank into the lake and drowned. The townspeople, finding the man sitting clothed and in his right mind, were afraid — and begged Jesus to leave their region. The healed man begged to go with Jesus, but Jesus sent him home instead, telling him to declare how much God had done for him, and he proclaimed it through the whole Decapolis.
Discussion
The region feared the healer more than the horror he had removed, because restoration had a price: the pigs. The healed man became the first missionary to the Decapolis. Reflect: What loss makes you ask Jesus to leave, even after seeing his power? Do you value comfort over someone else's restoration? How has God used your own deliverance as a witness at home?
Significance
The Gerasene demoniac displays Jesus' authority over the demonic and his mission to Gentile territory, where the healed man preaches before any apostle.
