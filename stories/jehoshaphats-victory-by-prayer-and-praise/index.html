Jehoshaphat's Victory by Prayer and Praise

Story
Jehoshaphat's Victory by Prayer and Praise
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
2 Chronicles 20
Retelling
Moabite and Ammonite armies came against Judah, and King Jehoshaphat was afraid. He set himself to seek the LORD, proclaimed a fast throughout Judah, and gathered the people in Jerusalem to pray. He recalled God's promises and confessed, 'We have no might against this great company that cometh against us; neither know we what to do: but our eyes are upon thee.' Then Jahaziel told them not to be afraid: the battle was not theirs, but God's, and they should go out and stand still and see the salvation of the LORD. The next morning Jehoshaphat put singers in front of the army, praising God as they marched. As they began to sing, the LORD set ambushes against the invaders, and they destroyed one another. When Judah reached the watchtower, they saw only dead bodies, and gathered spoil for three days. They returned to Jerusalem with joy, and the fear of God fell on the surrounding kingdoms.
Discussion
Jehoshaphat's victory was won before the battle, in prayer, and then in praise on the march. When we do not know what to do, the first move is still to look to God. Reflect: 1) What great company feels too large for you right now? 2) How does public, honest prayer change a crisis? 3) What would it mean to put praise ahead of your own plans this week?
Significance
Jehoshaphat's victory, told only in Chronicles, is the Old Testament's clearest picture of a battle won by prayer and praise rather than by military strength.
