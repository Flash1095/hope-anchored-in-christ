Jesus Walks on Water

Jesus Walks on Water

Retelling

After feeding the five thousand, Jesus sent his disciples ahead by boat across the Sea of Galilee while he went up a mountain to pray. By night a strong wind rose, the waves battered the boat, and the disciples were straining at the oars far from land. In the darkest hours Jesus came to them, walking on the water. They cried out in terror, thinking he was a ghost, but he answered, Take courage, it is I, do not be afraid. In Matthew's account Peter asked to come to him on the water; he walked a few steps, then saw the wind, grew afraid, and began to sink, crying, Lord, save me. Jesus caught him at once and asked why he had doubted. When they climbed into the boat the wind died, and the disciples worshipped him, saying, Truly you are the Son of God.

Discussion

Jesus came to the disciples in the storm rather than removing the storm first, and Peter's sinking began when the wind mattered more than the word. Doubt did not disqualify Peter; it drove him to cry out. Reflect: What wind are you watching instead of Jesus right now? When you sink, do you cry out or try to swim? How does worship grow out of rescue in your own story?

Significance

Walking on water reveals Jesus' mastery over chaos and his identity as the Son of God, confessed by the disciples in the boat.

Themes

Faith; Fear; Doubt; Divine identity
