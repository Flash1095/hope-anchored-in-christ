Jesus Heals Two Blind Men

Jesus Heals Two Blind Men

Retelling

As Jesus went on from healing Jairus' daughter, two blind men followed him, calling out, Have mercy on us, Son of David! When he had gone indoors, the blind men came to him, and Jesus asked, Do you believe that I am able to do this? They answered, Yes, Lord. Then he touched their eyes and said, According to your faith let it be done to you, and their sight was restored. Jesus warned them sternly not to tell anyone, but they went out and spread the news about him all over that region. This healing is distinct from the later healing of Bartimaeus and his companion at Jericho: it takes place in Galilee, in a house, and follows the raising of Jairus' daughter.

Discussion

Two blind men saw what sighted critics missed, calling Jesus Son of David, and Jesus tied the healing to their faith in his power. Reflect: 1) What does their persistent following, crying for mercy, teach about prayer? 2) How does Do you believe I am able to do this make faith personal? 3) Why would Jesus warn them to silence, and why could they not keep it?

Significance

In Galilee Jesus restored sight to two blind men who confessed him as Son of David, a healing distinct from the later Jericho healing of Bartimaeus.
