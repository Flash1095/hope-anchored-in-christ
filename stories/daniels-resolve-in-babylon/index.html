Daniel's Resolve in Babylon

Story
Daniel's Resolve in Babylon
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Daniel 1
Retelling
When Nebuchadnezzar besieged Jerusalem, Daniel and other young men from Judah were taken to Babylon and trained for service in the king's court. Daniel resolved not to defile himself with the king's food and wine, and asked the official in charge to test him and his three friends on vegetables and water for ten days. At the end of the test they looked healthier than the young men who had eaten the royal food, so they were allowed to keep to their diet. God gave all four knowledge and skill, and Daniel understanding in visions and dreams, and they entered the king's service, found ten times better than all the magicians and astrologers in the kingdom.
Discussion
Small faithfulness in exile still matters. Daniel did not wait for a grand stage to honour God; he started with his daily meals. Reflect: 1) Where is your quiet, everyday test of faithfulness right now? 2) How do you decide what you will not compromise on, before pressure arrives? 3) Who could you stand with, as Daniel stood with his friends, rather than standing alone?
Significance
Faithfulness in small, costly choices prepares a person for larger ones, and God honours those who honour Him even far from home.
