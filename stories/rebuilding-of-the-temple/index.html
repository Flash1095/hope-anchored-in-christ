Rebuilding of the Temple

Story
Rebuilding of the Temple
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Ezra 3-6; Haggai 1-2; Zechariah 4
Summary
The returned exiles laid the Temple's foundation with shouts and tears, then stalled for years under opposition until prophets Haggai and Zechariah stirred them to resume. In 515 BC the second Temple was completed and dedicated with joy, and Passover was celebrated by the restored community.
Significance
The second Temple looked small beside Solomon's, yet God promised its glory would be greater. Do not despise small, faithful rebuilding — God measures by His presence, not our comparisons.
