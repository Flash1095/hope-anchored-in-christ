Edom Refuses Israel Passage

Story
Edom Refuses Israel Passage
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Numbers 20:14-21
Retelling
From Kadesh, Moses sent messengers to the king of Edom, saying, Thus says your brother Israel: you know all the travail that has befallen us, how our fathers went down into Egypt, and we dwelt there a long time, and the Egyptians afflicted us, and we cried to the LORD, who sent an angel and brought us out. Let us pass, we pray, through your country; we will not pass through field or vineyard, nor drink water from wells; we will go by the king's highway and not turn aside until we pass your borders. Edom answered, You shall not pass by me, lest I come out against you with the sword. Israel offered again to keep to the highway and pay for any water drunk, but Edom refused and came out against Israel with much people and a strong hand. So Israel turned away from him.
Discussion
Brotherhood does not guarantee passage: Edom, descended from Esau, meets Jacob's descendants with a sword at the border. Israel, told not to contend with Edom, turns away rather than fight. Reflect: 1) How do old family divisions shape present refusals? 2) What does Israel's turning away teach about restraint? 3) How do you respond when a reasonable request is met with a threat?
Significance
Edom's refusal forces Israel to turn aside and lengthens the wilderness road, showing that not every closed border is to be forced, even by a people on God's errand.
