The Golden Calf

Story
The Golden Calf
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Exodus 32; Psalm 106:19-23
Summary
While Moses was on the mountain with God, the people grew impatient and persuaded Aaron to make a golden calf, which they worshipped as the god who brought them from Egypt. Moses interceded for them, broke the tablets in anger, ground the calf to powder, and pleaded with God not to destroy the nation.
Significance
Even a fresh-from-deliverance people can fall quickly — and God still listens to intercession. This story warns against substitute gods while anchoring hope in a merciful God who forgives repentant people.
