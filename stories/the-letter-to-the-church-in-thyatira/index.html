The Letter to the Church in Thyatira

Story
The Letter to the Church in Thyatira
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Revelation 2:18-29
Retelling
In a vision, John was told to write unto the angel of the church in Thyatira: These things saith the Son of God, who hath his eyes like unto a flame of fire, and his feet are like fine brass. I know thy works, and charity, and service, and faith, and thy patience, and thy works, and the last to be more than the first. Notwithstanding I have a few things against thee, because thou sufferest that woman Jezebel, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants to commit fornication, and to eat things sacrificed unto idols. And I gave her space to repent of her fornication, and she repented not. Behold, I will cast her into a bed, and them that commit adultery with her into great tribulation, except they repent of their deeds. And I will kill her children with death, and all the churches shall know that I am he which searcheth the reins and hearts, and I will give unto every one of you according to your works. But unto you I say, and unto the rest in Thyatira, as many as have not this doctrine, and which have not known the depths of Satan, as they speak, I will put upon you none other burden. But that which ye have already hold fast till I come. And he that overcometh, and keepeth my works unto the end, to him will I give power over the nations, and he shall rule them with a rod of iron, as the vessels of a potter shall they be broken to shivers, even as I received of my Father. And I will give him the morning star. He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches.
Discussion
This is a vision-message, the longest of the seven: Thyatira's love, service, faith, and patience are growing, yet it suffers a self-styled prophetess, called Jezebel, to seduce servants into fornication and idol food. Space to repent was given and refused, and judgment is pronounced, while the faithful are told simply to hold fast what they have. Reflect: 1) How does a growing church come to tolerate a destructive teacher? 2) What does space to repent mean for how long judgment waits? 3) Why is hold fast, rather than a new programme, the command to the faithful?
Significance
The Thyatira letter shows growth and toleration side by side: love and service increase, yet a false prophetess is suffered to teach, and Christ, who searches hearts, promises both judgment on the seducers and the morning star to those who hold fast.
