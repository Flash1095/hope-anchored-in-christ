The Ascension

Story
The Ascension
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Luke 24:50-53; Acts 1:3-11
Summary
For forty days the risen Jesus taught His disciples about the kingdom, then on the Mount of Olives He blessed them and was taken up into heaven as they watched. Two angels promised that this same Jesus, who was taken from them, would return in the same way He went.
Significance
Jesus did not vanish — He reigns, intercedes for us, and will return. The Ascension anchors hope upward: our Saviour is not absent, He is enthroned and coming again.
