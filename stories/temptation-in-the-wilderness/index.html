Temptation in the Wilderness

Story
Temptation in the Wilderness
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 4:1-11; Luke 4:1-13; Hebrews 4:15
Summary
Led by the Spirit into the wilderness, Jesus fasted forty days and faced Satan's temptations — to turn stones to bread, to seize glory, and to test the Father. Each time He answered with Scripture, 'It is written,' and the devil departed and angels ministered to Him.
Significance
Where Adam fell and Israel grumbled, Jesus stood firm in the same wilderness. Because He was tempted in every way yet did not sin, He understands our struggles and helps us in ours.
