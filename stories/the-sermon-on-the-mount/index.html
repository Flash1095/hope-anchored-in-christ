The Sermon on the Mount

The Sermon on the Mount

Retelling

Jesus went up on a mountainside, sat down as a teacher, and opened His mouth to describe life in the kingdom of God. Blessed are the poor in spirit, the meek, the merciful, the peacemakers. Love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you. Do not store up treasures on earth; seek first the kingdom. Do not judge, do not worry, ask and it will be given. He warned against building on sand and ended by saying that everyone who hears His words and puts them into practice is like a wise man who built his house on the rock. The crowds were amazed, because He taught with authority.

Themes

Kingdom Living; Righteousness; Mercy

Discussion

The Sermon is not a list of rules to earn God's favor; it is a portrait of life under a new King, lived by grace. It is meant to be practiced, not just admired. Reflect: 1) Which teaching in the Sermon most challenges you right now? 2) What would 'seek first the kingdom' look like in your week? 3) Are you building on rock or sand — and how can you tell?

Significance

The Sermon is the constitution of Christ's upside-down kingdom, where the lowly are blessed. Building on these words, Jesus promises, is building a life that stands when the storms come.
