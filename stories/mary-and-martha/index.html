Mary and Martha

Story
Mary and Martha
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Luke 10:38-42
Retelling
As Jesus and his disciples travelled, they came to a village where a woman named Martha opened her home to him. Her sister Mary sat at the Lord's feet listening to what he said, while Martha was distracted by all the preparations. She came to Jesus and asked, Lord, don't you care that my sister has left me to do the work by myself? Tell her to help me! Jesus answered, Martha, Martha, you are worried and upset about many things, but few things are needed, or indeed only one. Mary has chosen what is better, and it will not be taken away from her. Service was not condemned, but listening to Jesus was named the one necessary thing.
Discussion
Martha's serving was good and needed, yet it became anxious distraction, while Mary's listening was called better: service must not crowd out the Lord himself. Reflect: 1) Where has good work made you worried and upset rather than attentive? 2) What would choosing the better part look like in your busiest week? 3) How do you honour both serving and sitting at Jesus' feet without pitting sisters against each other?
Significance
Jesus honoured hospitality while insisting that listening to his word is the one necessary thing, and that it cannot be taken from the one who chooses it.
