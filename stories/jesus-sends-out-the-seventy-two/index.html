Jesus Sends Out the Seventy-Two

Story
Jesus Sends Out the Seventy-Two
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Luke 10:1-24
Retelling
Later, Jesus appointed seventy-two others and sent them ahead of him in pairs into every town he intended to visit, telling them the harvest was plentiful but the workers few, and to pray for more workers. They were to carry no purse, bag, or sandals, to greet no one on the road, to eat what was set before them, and to heal the sick and announce that the kingdom of God had come near. When they returned rejoicing that even demons submitted in Jesus' name, he told them not to rejoice in power over spirits but that their names were written in heaven. He also pronounced woe on towns like Chorazin and Bethsaida that had seen his works and not repented, saying they would fare worse than Tyre and Sidon.
Discussion
The wider mission shows the work was never meant for twelve alone: ordinary followers carried it town by town. Their joy in results needed redirecting toward the deeper joy of belonging to God. Reflect: Do you measure your service by visible results or by your standing with God? What town, workplace, or street is your 'ahead of him' assignment? How do you pray for more workers rather than just working harder?
Significance
The sending of the Seventy-Two widens mission beyond the apostles and previews the church's task of going ahead of Christ into every place.
