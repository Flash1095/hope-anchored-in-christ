The Lost Coin

The Lost Coin

Retelling

Continuing his answer to the grumbling Pharisees, Jesus told of a woman who had ten silver coins and lost one. She lit a lamp, swept the house, and searched carefully until she found it. Then she called her friends and neighbours and said, Rejoice with me, for I have found the coin that I had lost. Jesus concluded that in the same way there is joy before the angels of God over one sinner who repents. The coin, unlike the sheep, could not wander back or cry out; it had to be searched for, diligently, in the dark corners of the house, until the searcher found what she valued.

Discussion

The lost coin could do nothing to be found — the searching did it all. God's pursuit is thorough: lamp lit, house swept, no corner skipped. Reflect: Who in your life is lost in a way they cannot fix themselves? How diligent is your searching — lamp, broom, and patience, or a quick glance? Do you celebrate repentance the way this woman celebrated a coin?

Significance

The lost coin, middle of Luke's trio of lost things, shows God's diligent search for the helplessly lost and heaven's joy at their recovery.

Themes

Seeking God; Repentance; Joy; God's love
