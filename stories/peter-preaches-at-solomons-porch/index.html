Peter Preaches at Solomon's Porch

Peter Preaches at Solomon's Porch

Retelling

After the lame man was healed, the people ran together to Peter and John in Solomon's porch. Peter told them not to marvel as though the healing had come by their own power or holiness: it was faith in the name of Jesus, whom they had delivered up and denied before Pilate, that had made the man strong. He said they had acted in ignorance, as their rulers had, but God had fulfilled what the prophets had foretold, that Christ should suffer. He called them to repent and be converted, that their sins might be blotted out and times of refreshing might come from the Lord. He reminded them that Moses had promised a prophet like himself, that all the prophets from Samuel onward had foretold these days, and that they were children of the covenant God made with Abraham, in whom all kindreds of the earth would be blessed. God had raised up Jesus and sent him first to them, to bless them by turning each one away from his iniquities.

Discussion

Peter turns a miracle into a message: the healing points to Jesus, and the crowd's guilt is met not with despair but with a call to repent. Notice how he is both blunt about what they did and gentle about ignorance and the way back. Reflect: 1) When God works through you, how do you keep the attention on Jesus rather than yourself? 2) What does repentance look like as a turning, not just a feeling? 3) How does the promise to Abraham shape who the gospel is for?

Significance

The first sermon after Pentecost's sequel shows the apostolic pattern: a sign, a plain account of Jesus, a call to repentance, and a promise of refreshing for all who turn.
