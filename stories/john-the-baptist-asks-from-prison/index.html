John the Baptist Asks from Prison

Story
John the Baptist Asks from Prison
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 11:2-6; Luke 7:18-23
Retelling
From prison, John the Baptist heard about the deeds of the Messiah and sent his disciples to ask Jesus, Are you the one who is to come, or should we expect someone else? Jesus replied by pointing to what was happening: the blind receive sight, the lame walk, those with leprosy are cleansed, the deaf hear, the dead are raised, and the good news is proclaimed to the poor. He closed with a blessing on anyone who does not stumble on account of him. John, who had announced the coming one, now asked from a cell whether he had been right, and Jesus answered with evidence rather than rebuke.
Discussion
Even the forerunner asked his question from prison, and Jesus answered with deeds, not scolding: doubt brought honestly to Jesus is met with evidence. Reflect: 1) What do you do with faith when circumstances contradict what you expected? 2) How does Jesus' list of healings and good news for the poor define his Messiahship? 3) Where might you be stumbling over Jesus because he does not match your expectations?
Significance
John's question and Jesus' answer together define the Messiah by his works: healing, raising, and good news for the poor, in fulfilment of Isaiah's hope.
