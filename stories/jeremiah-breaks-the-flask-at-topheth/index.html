Jeremiah Breaks the Flask at Topheth

Story
Jeremiah Breaks the Flask at Topheth
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Jeremiah 19
Retelling
The LORD told Jeremiah to get a potter's earthen bottle, take some of the elders of the people and the elders of the priests, and go to the valley of the son of Hinnom, by the entry of the east gate, and proclaim there the words God would tell him. He proclaimed judgment on Jerusalem for forsaking God, burning incense to other gods, and filling the place with the blood of innocents — for building the high places of Baal to burn their sons in the fire, which God had not commanded, nor spoken, nor come into His mind. He declared that the place would no longer be called Tophet, nor the valley of the son of Hinnom, but the valley of slaughter, where the people would fall by the sword and their carcasses become meat for the fowls of heaven. Then, in the sight of the men who went with him, Jeremiah broke the bottle, saying, Even so will I break this people and this city, as one breaketh a potter's vessel, that cannot be made whole again. He came back and stood in the court of the LORD's house and repeated the word to all the people.
Discussion
A smashed flask cannot be unbroken — and Jeremiah made Jerusalem watch it happen. Reflect: 1) Why does God sometimes give a warning that cannot be softened? 2) What in your life is being treated as unbreakable that God says will break? 3) How do you speak a hard word in the very place where it will be heard?
Significance
Some judgments, once the vessel is broken, cannot be mended on human terms; the flask at Topheth made Jerusalem's coming ruin visible before it arrived. Doubt: this is a prophetic sign-act, a staged picture or lived sign, rather than an ordinary historical episode.
