The Passover

Story
The Passover
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
Exodus 12; 1 Corinthians 5:7
Summary
On the night of the final plague, Israelite families sacrificed a lamb without defect, painted its blood on their doorframes, and ate the first Passover meal in readiness to leave. The destroyer passed over homes marked by the blood, and Israel was to celebrate this feast every year in remembrance.
Significance
Passover is the Old Testament's great picture of salvation by the blood of a lamb — fulfilled when Christ, our Passover Lamb, was sacrificed. Hope lives under that same covering today.
