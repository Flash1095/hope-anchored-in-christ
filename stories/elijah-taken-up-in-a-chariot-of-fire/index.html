Elijah Taken Up in a Chariot of Fire

Story
Elijah Taken Up in a Chariot of Fire
Testament
Old Testament
Scripture
2 Kings 2:1-14
Retelling
When the LORD was about to take Elijah up to heaven by a whirlwind, Elijah and Elisha travelled together from Gilgal through Bethel and Jericho to the Jordan, while prophets at each stop warned Elisha his master would be taken that day. Elisha refused to leave him. At the Jordan, Elijah struck the water with his rolled mantle, and they crossed on dry ground. Elijah asked what he could do for Elisha before he was taken, and Elisha asked for a double portion of his spirit. Elijah said it was a hard thing, but if Elisha saw him when he was taken, it would be so. A chariot of fire and horses of fire appeared and parted them, and Elijah went up by a whirlwind into heaven. Elisha cried out, 'My father, my father, the chariot of Israel, and the horsemen thereof,' picked up Elijah's fallen mantle, struck the Jordan with it, and the waters parted for him too.
Discussion
Elijah did not die; he was taken, and his work passed to a successor who had stayed close enough to see it happen. Faithfulness often looks like refusing to leave when others say goodbye. Reflect: 1) What does Elisha's refusal to leave Elijah teach about loyalty? 2) How do you handle the passing of a mentor or leader? 3) What 'mantle' has God placed in your hands to carry forward?
Significance
Elijah's fiery departure closes his ministry without a grave and opens Elisha's: the same God who parted the Jordan for Elijah parts it for his successor.
