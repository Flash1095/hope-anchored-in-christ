Jesus Blesses Little Children

Story
Jesus Blesses Little Children
Testament
New Testament
Scripture
Matthew 19:13-15; Mark 10:13-16; Luke 18:15-17
Retelling
People brought little children to Jesus so that he might place his hands on them and pray for them, but the disciples rebuked those who brought them. When Jesus saw this he was indignant and said, Let the little children come to me, and do not hinder them, for the kingdom of God belongs to such as these. He added that whoever will not receive the kingdom of God like a little child will never enter it. Then he took the children in his arms, placed his hands on them, and blessed them.
Discussion
The disciples saw children as interruptions; Jesus saw them as the pattern of kingdom entrance: small, dependent, and welcomed by touch. Reflect: 1) Who do you treat as an interruption to real ministry? 2) What does receiving the kingdom like a child mean for an adult used to earning? 3) How does Jesus' embrace of children shape a church's welcome?
Significance
Jesus welcomed children as full members of the kingdom's welcome, making childlike dependence, not achievement, the way into God's reign.
