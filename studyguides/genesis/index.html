Genesis

Guide
Genesis
Category
Book Study
Summary
Genesis tells the beginning of everything: creation, the fall, the flood, and God's covenant with Abraham and his family. Every later story in the Bible grows out of these chapters. This guide follows Genesis from the garden to Egypt, tracing the promises God makes and keeps, so you can see your own story inside the story God has been telling from the start.
Scripture
Genesis 1:1-2:3; Genesis 12:1-3
Steps
  1. Read Genesis in large sections first (chapters 1-11, 12-25, 26-36, and 37-50), so you grasp the story's shape
  2. Track the promises God makes — to Adam, to Noah, to Abraham — and note how each one is repeated and expanded
  3. Study the fall (Genesis 3) carefully, asking what broke and what God promised to restore
  4. Follow one family line through the book, noting how God's purposes survive human failure
  5. Compare the creation accounts with the flood account, watching for the pattern of sin, judgment, and grace
  6. End by reading Joseph's story (Genesis 37-50) as a whole, and ask what it teaches about God's hidden providence
Questions
  • What does Genesis 1-2 teach about who you are — and how does that change the way you see your work and relationships?
  • Why does God keep choosing unlikely, flawed people to carry his promises?
  • How does the story of the fall explain the world you actually live in — its beauty and its brokenness?
  • Where in Genesis do you see God's grace most clearly, even before the law or the cross?
  • What does Joseph's story teach about trusting God when you cannot see what he is doing?
Application
Write down one promise of God from Genesis that speaks to your present situation, and put it where you will see it every day this week.
