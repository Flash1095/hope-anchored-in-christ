Faith

Guide
Faith
Category
Topical Study
Summary
Faith is how we receive everything God gives — not a feeling, not a performance, but trust in a person: Jesus Christ. Hebrews 11 shows faith lived out across generations, in people as different as Abel and Rahab. This topical study explores what faith is, how it grows, and what it does when life tests it.
Scripture
Hebrews 11:1-12; Romans 4; James 2:14-26
Steps
  1. Read Hebrews 11 in full, noting what each person believed and what each person did because of it
  2. Study Abraham's faith in Romans 4, asking how trust and promise work together
  3. Compare Hebrews 11 with James 2, asking how faith and works belong together
  4. Trace your own faith story: when did you first trust Christ, and how has that trust been tested since?
  5. Identify one area where you say you trust God but live as if you do not — money, health, the future — and name it honestly
  6. Take one step of obedience this week that only makes sense if God is trustworthy
Questions
  • What is faith, exactly — and how is it different from optimism, willpower, or certainty?
  • Why does Hebrews praise people whose stories ended badly by human standards?
  • How do you tell the difference between trusting God and testing him?
  • What do you do when you believe and yet doubt at the same time — is that failure, or honesty?
  • How has your faith grown most: through teaching, through suffering, through other people, or through obedience?
Application
Take one concrete step this week that requires trusting God — a hard conversation, a generous gift, a surrendered plan — and do it because he is faithful, not because you feel ready.
