Ruth

Guide
Ruth
Category
Book Study
Summary
Ruth is a short, beautiful story about loyalty, loss, and the quiet providence of God. A widowed foreigner chooses to stay with her mother-in-law, gleans in a stranger's field, and becomes part of the family line of David — and of Jesus. This guide reads Ruth slowly, chapter by chapter, so you can see how God works through ordinary faithfulness.
Scripture
Ruth 1; Ruth 4
Steps
  1. Read the whole book aloud in one sitting — it takes about twenty minutes — and note where you see God's hand
  2. Study one chapter per session, asking what each character chooses and what each choice costs
  3. Trace the theme of hesed — loyal, covenant love — in Ruth, Naomi, and Boaz
  4. Compare Ruth's story with the judges-era darkness around it, and ask what faithfulness looks like in dark times
  5. Read Ruth 4 with Matthew 1:5 in view, and trace how this small story reaches the story of Jesus
  6. End by naming one act of ordinary faithfulness God may be asking of you right now
Questions
  • What does Ruth's decision in chapter 1 cost her — and what does it show about loyalty?
  • Where is God in this story? He speaks little, yet his hand seems everywhere — how do you recognize that kind of work in your own life?
  • What does Boaz's kindness teach about how God's people should treat outsiders and the vulnerable?
  • How does a story about two widows and a farmer end up in the genealogy of Jesus — and what does that say about who God uses?
  • What would it look like for you to practice hesed — loyal love — toward someone this week?
Application
Do one concrete act of loyal kindness this week for someone who cannot repay you, and do it quietly, without telling anyone.
