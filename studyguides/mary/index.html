Mary

Guide
Mary
Category
Character Study
Summary
Mary, the mother of Jesus, is the Bible's picture of quiet, costly obedience. A young woman in a small town, she said yes to God's impossible plan, treasured his works in her heart, and stood by the cross. This character study follows Mary from the angel's greeting to the empty tomb's aftermath, so you can learn what it means to say yes to God and keep saying it.
Scripture
Luke 1:26-56; Luke 2; John 19:25-27
Steps
  1. Read Mary's story in sequence (Luke 1-2, Matthew 1-2, John 2, 19:25-27, Acts 1:14), noting what she says and what she treasures silently
  2. Study the Magnificat (Luke 1:46-55) as Mary's theology: what does she believe about God?
  3. Follow Mary through the sword of Simeon's prophecy (Luke 2:35) to the cross, asking what obedience cost her
  4. Compare Mary's "let it be to me" (Luke 1:38) with Zechariah's doubt, and ask what surrender looks like
  5. Notice Mary's presence in Acts 1:14, praying with the church she helped begin
  6. End by writing your own short prayer of surrender, in Mary's spirit
Questions
  • What does Mary's yes cost her — reputation, plans, safety — and what does it gain?
  • Why does Luke emphasize that Mary "treasured up" and "pondered" things — what does that teach about faith?
  • How does Mary respond when God's plan hurts — at the cross, most of all?
  • What does Mary's life teach about greatness in God's kingdom: is it visibility, or faithfulness?
  • Where is God asking for your yes right now, even though you cannot see how it will work?
Application
Pray Mary's words — "let it be to me according to your word" — over one situation you cannot control, and mean it for a week.
