The Sermon on the Mount

Guide
The Sermon on the Mount
Category
Passage Study
Summary
The Sermon on the Mount (Matthew 5-7) is Jesus' most famous teaching: the Beatitudes, the Lord's Prayer, and a vision of life in God's kingdom that turns the world's values upside down. It is beautiful, demanding, and impossible to live by your own strength — which is exactly the point. This passage study works through the sermon section by section, so its demands drive you to grace and to obedience.
Scripture
Matthew 5-7
Steps
  1. Read the whole sermon aloud in one sitting, noting which teaching surprises or disturbs you most
  2. Study the Beatitudes (Matthew 5:3-12) one by one, asking what kind of person Jesus calls blessed
  3. Work through the antitheses (5:21-48) slowly, asking what Jesus means by fulfilling the law rather than abolishing it
  4. Pray through the Lord's Prayer (6:9-13) phrase by phrase, as Jesus taught his disciples to pray
  5. Study the closing warnings (7:13-27), and ask which foundation your life is actually built on
  6. End by choosing one teaching of the sermon to practice this week — and one to keep wrestling with
Questions
  • Why does Jesus begin with blessing the poor in spirit, the mourning, and the meek — the people the world overlooks?
  • What does it mean to be salt and light — and where is your salt losing its flavor?
  • How do Jesus' commands about anger, lust, and love of enemies go deeper than the law — and what do they expose in you?
  • Why does Jesus warn so strongly about hypocrisy in giving, praying, and fasting?
  • What does it mean to build your house on the rock — practically, this week?
Application
Choose one Beatitude to live out this week — mercy, purity of heart, peacemaking — and let it shape one real interaction each day.
