Joseph

Guide
Joseph
Category
Character Study
Summary
Joseph, son of Jacob, is the Bible's great story of providence: betrayed by his brothers, sold into slavery, falsely accused, forgotten in prison — and raised to save the very family that sold him. His life asks the hardest question of faith: can God be good when life is not? This character study follows Joseph from the coat to the throne, so you can trust God's hidden hand in your own story.
Scripture
Genesis 37; Genesis 39-45
Steps
  1. Read Joseph's story in full (Genesis 37
  2. 39-50), noting each reversal and each moment God is mentioned — or not
  3. Study Joseph's dreams and his brothers' hatred, asking how pride and favoritism poisoned the family
  4. Follow Joseph through slavery and prison, noting his faithfulness in small, hidden places
  5. Read Genesis 45 and 50:20 together, asking what Joseph understood about God's purposes
  6. Compare Joseph's forgiveness with his brothers' fear, and ask what made forgiveness possible
  7. End by writing your own reflection on Genesis 50:20 — how God meant for good what others meant for evil
Questions
  • Where is God in Joseph's story? He speaks rarely — yet Joseph insists God was at work. How do you recognize that?
  • What kept Joseph faithful in prison, when no one was watching and nothing was improving?
  • How could Joseph forgive brothers who never fully repented — and what does that teach about forgiveness?
  • What does Joseph's story say about suffering: does God cause it, use it, or both?
  • What chapter of your life looks like a prison right now — and what would it mean to trust God's purpose in it?
Application
Name one hard chapter of your life, and pray Joseph's confession over it: God meant it for good — even if you cannot yet see how.
