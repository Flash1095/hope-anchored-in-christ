1 Corinthians 13

Guide
1 Corinthians 13
Category
Passage Study
Summary
First Corinthians 13 is the Bible's great hymn to love, written to a church that had gifts but no grace. Paul insists that without love, even the most spectacular spirituality is noise. This passage study works through the chapter's three movements — love's necessity, love's character, love's permanence — so you can measure your own life by love's standard.
Scripture
1 Corinthians 13:1-13
Steps
  1. Read the chapter in context first (1 Corinthians 12-14), so you see why Paul wrote about love to this church
  2. Study verses 1-3, asking what impressive things are worthless without love — and where you chase them
  3. Work through the qualities in verses 4-7 one by one, testing yourself against each
  4. Compare the chapter's picture of love with your closest relationship, honestly
  5. Study verses 8-13, asking why love remains when gifts and knowledge pass away
  6. End by rewriting verses 4-7 with your own name, and praying it as a confession
Questions
  • Why does Paul place this chapter between two chapters about spiritual gifts — what problem was he solving?
  • Which quality of love in verses 4-7 is hardest for you — patience, kindness, humility, forgiveness?
  • What is the difference between love as a feeling and love as Paul describes it?
  • How does this chapter challenge the way you use your own gifts and strengths?
  • What would your church, family, or workplace look like if it were marked by this kind of love?
Application
Pick the one quality of love you most lack, and practice it deliberately toward one person every day this week.
