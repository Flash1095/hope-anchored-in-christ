Summary
John's Gospel was written so readers may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and find life in his name. Unlike the other Gospels, John lingers on long conversations and seven miraculous signs, each revealing who Jesus is. This guide walks through the Gospel sign by sign, so you can meet Jesus as John presents him: the Word made flesh, the light of the world, and the good shepherd.
Scripture
John 1:1-18; John 20:30-31
Steps
  1. Read the whole Gospel once at a steady pace, marking every "I am" statement and every sign
  2. Study one sign per session, asking what it reveals about Jesus and what response it calls for
  3. Trace the word "believe" through the Gospel, noting who believes, who doubts, and why
  4. Compare John's account of an event with the same event in Matthew, Mark, or Luke
  5. Read the farewell discourse (John 13-17) slowly, treating it as Jesus' own summary of the Christian life
  6. End each session by writing one sentence about who Jesus is, based only on the passage you read
Questions
  • Which of the seven signs speaks most directly to something you are facing right now, and why?
  • Why do you think John chose these particular signs and conversations, rather than a full biography?
  • What does it mean to "believe" in John's Gospel — is it agreement, trust, or something more?
  • How does John's picture of Jesus challenge the way you usually think about him?
  • Where in your life do you most need to hear Jesus say "I am" — and which "I am" is it?
Application
Pick one "I am" statement to carry through your week, and let it shape one concrete decision — how you speak, spend, forgive, or rest.
