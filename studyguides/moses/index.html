Moses

Guide
Moses
Category
Character Study
Summary
Moses is the reluctant deliverer: a murderer turned shepherd, called by a burning bush to lead a nation out of slavery. He argued with God, parted a sea, received the law, and never entered the promised land — yet Scripture calls him faithful in all God's house. This character study follows Moses from the Nile to Nebo, so you can learn leadership, obedience, and humility from a man who felt unqualified.
Scripture
Exodus 3-4; Exodus 14; Deuteronomy 34
Steps
  1. Read Moses' story in sequence (Exodus 1-20, 32-34, Numbers 11-14, Deuteronomy 31-34), noting his growth and his failures
  2. Study the burning bush (Exodus 3-4) closely, listing Moses' excuses and God's answers to each
  3. Follow the exodus itself (Exodus 5-15), asking what it teaches about God's power and human fear
  4. Study the golden calf (Exodus 32) and Moses' intercession, asking what it means to stand between God and people
  5. End with Deuteronomy 34, reflecting on a life that finished faithful without finishing the journey
  6. Throughout, note Moses' humility (Numbers 12:3) and ask what humble leadership looks like for you
Questions
  • Why does God choose a reluctant, flawed man to deliver Israel — and what does that say about who he uses?
  • What do Moses' excuses reveal about your own excuses when God calls you to something hard?
  • How did Moses handle the people's complaints — and what does that teach about leading people who grumble?
  • Why did Moses not enter the promised land, and what does that ending teach about faithfulness versus success?
  • Where is God calling you to step forward despite feeling unqualified?
Application
Name one thing God has been nudging you toward that you have avoided, and take the smallest possible first step this week.
