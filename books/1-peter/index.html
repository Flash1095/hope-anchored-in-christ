1 Peter

Book
1 Peter
Testament
New Testament
Category
Epistles
Traditional Author
Peter
Date Approx
c. AD 62-64
Timeline Era
Early Church
Key Theme
Hope and holiness amid suffering
Summary
Peter encourages Christians suffering as strangers in the world to rejoice in their living hope through Christ's resurrection. He calls them to holiness, submission, and humble trust as God's chosen people.
Key Verses
1 Peter 1:3-4; 1 Peter 2:9; 1 Peter 3:15; 1 Peter 5:7
Key People
Peter; Silas; Mark
Themes
Living hope; Suffering; Holiness; Identity in Christ
Purpose
To strengthen persecuted believers in Asia Minor to stand firm in grace while living honorably among unbelievers.
Outline
Living hope and holiness (1-2); Submission in relationships (3); Suffering and shepherding (4-5)
Historical Context
Written around AD 62-64, likely from Rome, to churches across Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia in modern Turkey.
