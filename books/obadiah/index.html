Summary
Obadiah, the shortest Old Testament book, pronounces judgment on Edom for rejoicing over and looting Jerusalem in its disaster. It promises the Day of the Lord for all nations and Israel's eventual restoration.
Key Verses
Obadiah 1:15; Obadiah 1:17
Key People
Obadiah
Themes
Judgment on pride; Day of the Lord; Israel's restoration
Purpose
To assure Judah that God will judge Edom's pride and violence and restore His people.
Outline
Judgment on Edom (1-14); Day of the Lord (15-16); Israel restored (17-21)
Historical Context
Issued after an attack on Jerusalem in which Edom participated, commonly linked to the Babylonian destruction in 586 BC.
Related Places
Jerusalem
