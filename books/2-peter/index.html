Key Theme

Summary
Peter urges believers to grow in godly character and knowledge of Christ, warns against false teachers, and answers scoffers about Christ's return. He closes by pointing to the new heavens and new earth.
Key Verses
2 Peter 1:3; 2 Peter 3:9; 2 Peter 3:18
Key People
Peter; Noah; Lot
Themes
Growth; Knowledge of Christ; False teachers; Christ's return
Purpose
To spur spiritual growth, expose false teaching, and confirm confidence in the Lord's promised return.
Outline
Grow in grace (1); False teachers judged (2); The Day of the Lord (3)
Historical Context
Peter's farewell letter, written around AD 67-68 shortly before his martyrdom under Nero.
