2 Peter

Peter urges believers to grow in godly character and knowledge of Christ, warns against false teachers, and answers scoffers about Christ's return. He closes by pointing to the new heavens and new earth.

Themes

Growth; Knowledge of Christ; False teachers; Christ's return

Purpose

To spur spiritual growth, expose false teaching, and confirm confidence in the Lord's promised return.

Outline

Grow in grace (1); False teachers judged (2); The Day of the Lord (3)

Historical Context

Peter's farewell letter, written around AD 67-68 shortly before his martyrdom under Nero.

Life Application

Second Peter urges you to grow deliberately in godliness, because spiritual stagnation leaves you vulnerable to false teaching and doubt. Add virtue, knowledge, self-control, and love to your faith step by step, and anchor your hope in Christ's promised return rather than in scoffers who claim He has forgotten.

Cultural Background

Second Peter confronts teachers who denied the Lord's return and used grace as cover for immorality, a problem that grew as the apostles died and the first generation passed. Its appeal to eyewitness testimony and to prophecy reflects a church learning to stand on Scripture after the eyewitnesses were gone.
