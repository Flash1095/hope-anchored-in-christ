1 John

Book
1 John
Testament
New Testament
Category
Epistles
Traditional Author
John the Apostle
Date Approx
c. AD 85-95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Key Theme
Fellowship with God, love, and assurance of salvation
Summary
John writes to assure believers that they have eternal life, testing true faith by belief in Jesus, obedience, and love for one another. He declares that God is light and God is love, and that perfect love casts out fear.
Key Verses
1 John 1:9; 1 John 3:16; 1 John 4:8; 1 John 5:13
Key People
John the Apostle; Jesus
Themes
Fellowship; Love; Assurance; Truth; Light
Purpose
To give believers assurance of salvation and to counter false teaching denying that Jesus came in the flesh.
Outline
Walk in the light (1-2); Children of God love one another (3-4); Faith and assurance (5)
Historical Context
Written late in the first century AD, likely from Ephesus, to churches in Asia Minor troubled by early Gnostic-like teaching.
Related Places
Ephesus
