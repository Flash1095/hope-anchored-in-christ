Habakkuk

Book
Habakkuk
Testament
Old Testament
Category
Minor Prophets
Traditional Author
Habakkuk
Date Approx
c. 612-605 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Key Theme
Trusting God when evil seems to prevail
Summary
Habakkuk questions God about violence in Judah and God's plan to use wicked Babylon as judgment. God answers that the righteous shall live by faith, and the prophet ends rejoicing in God despite coming hardship.
Key Verses
Habakkuk 2:4; Habakkuk 3:17-19; Habakkuk 1:5
Key People
Habakkuk
Themes
Faith; God's justice; Waiting on God; Joy in hardship
Purpose
To show how a believer can wrestle honestly with God and learn to trust Him when evil seems to triumph.
Outline
First complaint and answer (1); Second complaint and woe oracles (2); Habakkuk's prayer of praise (3)
Historical Context
Prophesied in Judah around 612-605 BC, as Babylon rose and threatened Jerusalem.
Related Places
Jerusalem; Babylon
Related Events
Fall of Jerusalem and the Exile
