Key Theme
Jesus as King and fulfillment of Old Testament prophecy
Summary
Matthew presents Jesus as Israel's promised Messiah and King, tracing His genealogy to David and Abraham and quoting Old Testament prophecy throughout. It includes the Sermon on the Mount and ends with the Great Commission.
Key Verses
Matthew 1:21; Matthew 11:28-30; Matthew 28:19-20; Matthew 6:33
Key People
Jesus; Joseph; Mary; John the Baptist; The twelve disciples; Herod
Themes
Messiah and King; Kingdom of heaven; Fulfillment; Discipleship
Purpose
Written mainly for Jewish readers to prove Jesus is the promised Messiah and to teach His followers to make disciples of all nations.
Outline
Birth and beginnings (1-4); Sermon on the Mount (5-7); Miracles and mission (8-18); Journey to Jerusalem (19-25); Passion and resurrection (26-28)
Historical Context
Written in the first century AD for Jewish-Christian readers, drawing on Jesus' ministry in Galilee and Judea around AD 27-30.
Related Places
Bethlehem; Nazareth; Galilee; Capernaum; Jerusalem; Bethany; Golgotha; Jordan River
