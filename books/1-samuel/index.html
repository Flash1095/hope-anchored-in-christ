Book

Testament
Old Testament
Category
History
Traditional Author
Samuel (traditional)
Date Approx
c. 1000-950 BC
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Key Theme
Samuel, Saul, and the rise of David
Summary
1 Samuel traces the transition from judges to kingship through Samuel, Saul, and David. Samuel anoints Saul as first king, but Saul's disobedience leads God to choose David, a man after His own heart.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 15:22; 1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Samuel 2:2
Key People
Samuel; Hannah; Saul; David; Jonathan; Eli
Themes
Kingship; Obedience; God's choice; Prayer; Rise of David
Purpose
To explain the origin of Israel's monarchy and why God chose David's line over Saul's.
Outline
Samuel's ministry (1-7); Saul as king (8-15); David's rise and Saul's decline (16-31)
Historical Context
Set in the 11th century BC as Philistine pressure pushed Israel's tribes toward a united monarchy.
Related Places
Shiloh; Ramah; Mizpah; Gibeah; Bethlehem; Gilgal
Related Events
Calling of Samuel; Anointing of Saul as King; David and Goliath
