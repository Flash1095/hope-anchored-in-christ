Malachi

Book
Malachi
Testament
Old Testament
Category
Minor Prophets
Traditional Author
Malachi
Date Approx
c. 440-400 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Key Theme
Call to faithfulness and the coming messenger
Summary
Malachi, the last Old Testament prophet, confronts a discouraged post-exile people and their priests for half-hearted worship and broken covenants. He promises God will send a messenger before the great Day of the Lord.
Key Verses
Malachi 3:10; Malachi 4:2; Malachi 3:6
Key People
Malachi; The coming messenger (Elijah)
Themes
Faithfulness; Worship; Tithing; Marriage covenant; Day of the Lord
Purpose
To call a complacent generation back to wholehearted devotion and to prepare them for God's coming messenger.
Outline
God's love affirmed (1); Priests and people rebuked (2); Robbing God and the Book of Remembrance (3); Day of the Lord (4)
Historical Context
Addressed to Jerusalem in the mid-5th century BC, a generation after the Temple was rebuilt, under Persian rule.
Related Places
Jerusalem
