Zechariah

Book
Zechariah
Testament
Old Testament
Category
Minor Prophets
Traditional Author
Zechariah
Date Approx
c. 520-480 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Key Theme
Visions of restoration and the coming King
Summary
Zechariah encourages the Temple builders with eight night visions of restoration and prophesies the coming King who rides in humbly and is pierced. Its later chapters look to God's final victory and the Shepherd-King.
Key Verses
Zechariah 4:6; Zechariah 9:9; Zechariah 12:10
Key People
Zechariah; Zerubbabel; Joshua the high priest
Themes
Visions; Restoration; Coming King; Holy Spirit; Repentance
Purpose
To encourage the returned exiles to rebuild and to reveal God's messianic plans for Jerusalem and the world.
Outline
Night visions (1-6); Fasting question (7-8); Oracles about the coming King (9-14)
Historical Context
Began in Jerusalem in 520 BC alongside Haggai, during Darius's reign, as the Second Temple was being rebuilt.
Related Places
Jerusalem
