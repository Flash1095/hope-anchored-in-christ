John

Book
John
Testament
New Testament
Category
Gospels
Traditional Author
John the Apostle
Date Approx
c. AD 85-95
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Key Theme
Jesus as the Son of God, the Word made flesh
Summary
John presents Jesus as the eternal Word made flesh and the Son of God, through seven signs and deep discourses like the Good Shepherd and the Vine. It was written so readers may believe in Jesus and have life in His name.
Key Verses
John 1:1; John 1:14; John 3:16; John 14:6; John 20:31
Key People
Jesus; John the Baptist; Mary; Nicodemus; Samaritan woman; Lazarus; Thomas
Themes
Son of God; Belief; Eternal Life; Light; Love
Purpose
Stated in John 20:31 - so that readers may believe Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and have life in His name.
Outline
Prologue (1); Book of Signs (2-12); Upper Room and Farewell Discourse (13-17); Passion and resurrection (18-21)
Historical Context
Written late in the first century AD, traditionally at Ephesus, decades after the Synoptic Gospels.
Related Places
Nazareth; Galilee; Capernaum; Jerusalem; Bethany; Golgotha; Jordan River
