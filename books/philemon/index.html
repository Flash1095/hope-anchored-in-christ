Philemon

Book
Philemon
Testament
New Testament
Category
Epistles
Traditional Author
Paul
Date Approx
c. AD 60-62
Timeline Era
Early Church
Key Theme
Forgiveness and brotherhood in Christ
Summary
In this brief personal letter Paul appeals to Philemon to receive back his runaway slave Onesimus, now a Christian, no longer as a slave but as a beloved brother. It beautifully applies the gospel to reconciliation and forgiveness.
Key Verses
Philemon 1:16; Philemon 1:18
Key People
Paul; Philemon; Onesimus; Apphia; Archippus
Themes
Forgiveness; Reconciliation; Brotherhood; Gospel in relationships
Purpose
To persuade Philemon to forgive and welcome Onesimus, modeling Christlike love and costly intercession.
Outline
Thanksgiving (1-7); Paul's appeal for Onesimus (8-21); Closing greetings (22-25)
Historical Context
Written from Paul's Roman imprisonment around AD 60-62 and sent to Colossae with the Colossian letter.
Related Places
Colossae; Rome
