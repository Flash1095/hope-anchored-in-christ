Galatians

Book
Galatians
Testament
New Testament
Category
Epistles
Traditional Author
Paul
Date Approx
c. AD 48-55
Timeline Era
Early Church
Key Theme
Freedom in Christ, not under the Law
Summary
Paul passionately defends the gospel that sinners are justified by faith in Christ alone, not by works of the Law. He warns the Galatians against returning to slavery and calls them to live by the Spirit.
Key Verses
Galatians 2:20; Galatians 5:1; Galatians 5:22-23; Galatians 6:14
Key People
Paul; Peter (Cephas); Barnabas; Abraham
Themes
Justification by faith; Freedom; Fruit of the Spirit; Gospel purity
Purpose
To refute teachers adding circumcision and Law-keeping to faith, and to secure the Galatians' freedom in Christ.
Outline
Personal defense (1-2); Doctrinal argument from Abraham (3-4); Freedom and life by the Spirit (5-6)
Historical Context
Written to churches in Galatia (in modern Turkey) Paul founded on his journeys, likely AD 48-55, as Judaizers followed behind him.
Related Places
Jerusalem; Antioch
Related Events
Jerusalem Council
