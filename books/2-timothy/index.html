2 Timothy

Paul's final letter, written from prison awaiting execution, charges Timothy to guard the gospel, preach the Word, and endure suffering without shame. It is a moving passing of the torch to the next generation.

Themes

Faithfulness; Scripture; Endurance; Finishing well

Purpose

To summon Timothy to Rome and to steadfast, unashamed ministry as Paul faces death.

Outline

Be unashamed (1); Be strong and entrust the gospel (2); Godlessness and Scripture (3); Preach the Word; Paul's farewell (4)

Historical Context

Written from a Roman prison around AD 66-67, during Nero's persecution, shortly before Paul's traditional execution.

Life Application

Second Timothy is a charge to pass the faith on before it is too late: guard the gospel, preach the Word, and endure hardship without shame. If you have been given truth by faithful people, entrust it to others who will teach it too, because the gospel advances one generation at a time through ordinary faithfulness.

Cultural Background

Paul wrote from a Roman prison under Nero's persecution, when being associated with him was dangerous and former companions had deserted. His plea for Timothy to come quickly and bring his cloak and scrolls reflects the harsh reality of a prisoner facing winter, trial, and likely execution.
