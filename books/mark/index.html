Life of Christ
Key Theme

Summary
Mark is the shortest and fastest-paced Gospel, presenting Jesus as the powerful yet suffering Son of God and Servant. It emphasizes Jesus' actions - miracles, exorcisms, and ultimately His death and empty tomb.
Key Verses
Mark 1:15; Mark 10:45; Mark 8:34-35
Key People
Jesus; John the Baptist; Peter; The twelve disciples
Themes
Son of God; Servant; Discipleship; Suffering; Urgency
Purpose
To proclaim Jesus as the suffering Servant-Son and call readers to follow Him in costly discipleship.
Outline
Ministry in Galilee (1-8); Journey to Jerusalem (9-10); Passion and resurrection (11-16)
Historical Context
Traditionally written in Rome from Peter's preaching, for Gentile readers facing persecution in the AD 50s-60s.
Related Places
Nazareth; Galilee; Capernaum; Jerusalem; Bethany; Golgotha; Jordan River
