2 John

Book
2 John
Testament
New Testament
Category
Epistles
Traditional Author
John the Apostle
Date Approx
c. AD 85-95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Key Theme
Walk in truth and love, beware deceivers
Summary
In a short letter to the elect lady and her children, John rejoices that they walk in truth and love. He warns them not to welcome deceivers who deny Christ's coming in the flesh.
Key Verses
2 John 1:6; 2 John 1:7
Key People
John the Apostle; The elect lady and her children
Themes
Truth; Love; Discernment; Hospitality guarded
Purpose
To commend walking in truth and love while warning against supporting false teachers.
Outline
Greeting and walking in truth (1-6); Warning against deceivers (7-11); Closing (12-13)
Historical Context
Written late in the first century AD from Asia Minor, in the same setting as 1 and 3 John.
