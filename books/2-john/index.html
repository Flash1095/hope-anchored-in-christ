2 John

In a short letter to the elect lady and her children, John rejoices that they walk in truth and love. He warns them not to welcome deceivers who deny Christ's coming in the flesh.

Themes

Truth; Love; Discernment; Hospitality guarded

Purpose

To commend walking in truth and love while warning against supporting false teachers.

Outline

Greeting and walking in truth (1-6); Warning against deceivers (7-11); Closing (12-13)

Historical Context

Written late in the first century AD from Asia Minor, in the same setting as 1 and 3 John.

Life Application

Second John balances love with discernment: welcome fellow believers warmly, but do not lend your home or platform to teachers who deny Christ. Loving well includes protecting the truth, and refusing to support false teaching is not unkindness but faithfulness to the gospel you received.

Cultural Background

In the ancient world, traveling teachers depended on the hospitality of local believers for food and lodging, which gave hosts real influence over what teaching spread. John's warning not to receive deceivers into the house addresses that system directly, where hospitality was a form of endorsement.
