Summary
Haggai urges the returned exiles, who had stalled for years, to resume rebuilding the Temple and put God first. His brief dated messages promise God's presence and greater future glory for the house.
Key Verses
Haggai 1:8; Haggai 2:9; Haggai 1:13
Key People
Haggai; Zerubbabel; Joshua the high priest
Themes
Priorities; Temple rebuilding; God's presence; Encouragement
Purpose
To stir the post-exile community to finish the Temple and trust God's promised blessing.
Outline
Call to build (1); Greater glory and blessings (2)
Historical Context
Delivered in Jerusalem over four months in 520 BC, eighteen years after the first exiles returned under Cyrus.
