Book

Testament
Old Testament

History
Traditional Author
Jeremiah (traditional)
Date Approx

Summary
2 Kings continues the story of Israel and Judah through the ministry of Elisha to the fall of both kingdoms. Israel falls to Assyria and Judah to Babylon, fulfilling prophetic warnings against persistent idolatry.
Key Verses
2 Kings 17:7-8; 2 Kings 22:19
Key People
Elisha; Hezekiah; King Josiah; Naaman; Manasseh; King Nebuchadnezzar
Themes
Judgment; Exile; Prophets; Repentance; Covenant unfaithfulness
Purpose
To explain why the exile happened - Israel and Judah broke God's covenant despite prophetic warning.
Outline
Elisha and the divided kingdoms (1-13); Fall of Israel (14-17); Judah alone to exile (18-25)
Historical Context
Covers the 9th to 6th centuries BC, ending with Jerusalem's destruction by Babylon in 586 BC.
Related Places
Samaria; Jerusalem; Babylon; Mount Carmel
Related Events
Fall of Samaria; Fall of Jerusalem and the Exile; Hezekiah and Sennacherib's Invasion
