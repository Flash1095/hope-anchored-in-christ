Isaiah

Book
Isaiah
Testament
Old Testament
Category
Major Prophets
Traditional Author
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 740-680 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Key Theme
Judgment and the coming Messiah, the Suffering Servant
Summary
Isaiah prophesies judgment on Judah and the nations for sin, but also glorious hope: a coming Messiah, the Suffering Servant who bears sin, and a renewed creation. It spans from Isaiah's own day to promises of restoration after exile.
Key Verses
Isaiah 7:14; Isaiah 9:6; Isaiah 40:31; Isaiah 53:5; Isaiah 55:8-9
Key People
Isaiah; Hezekiah; Ahaz; The Suffering Servant
Themes
Judgment; Salvation; Messiah; Holiness; Comfort
Purpose
To call Judah to trust God alone amid Assyrian threat and to announce God's worldwide salvation through the Servant.
Outline
Judgment on Judah and nations (1-35); Hezekiah's day (36-39); Comfort and Servant songs (40-55); Future glory (56-66)
Historical Context
Prophesied in Jerusalem during the reigns of Uzziah through Hezekiah, as Assyria rose to power in the 8th century BC.
Related Places
Jerusalem; Babylon
Related Events
Fall of Jerusalem and the Exile; Hezekiah and Sennacherib's Invasion
