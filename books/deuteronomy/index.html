Deuteronomy

Book
Deuteronomy
Testament
Old Testament
Category
Law
Traditional Author
Moses
Date Approx
c. 1400 BC
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Key Theme
Moses renews the covenant before entering Canaan
Summary
Deuteronomy is Moses' farewell sermons on the plains of Moab, restating the Law for the new generation about to enter Canaan. It calls Israel to love God with all the heart and to choose life by obeying His covenant.
Key Verses
Deuteronomy 6:4-5; Deuteronomy 30:19-20; Deuteronomy 31:8
Key People
Moses; Joshua
Themes
Covenant renewal; Obedience; Love for God; Blessing and curse; Remembering
Purpose
To renew the covenant and prepare Israel to live faithfully in the Promised Land after Moses' death.
Outline
First sermon - history reviewed (1-4); Second sermon - Law restated (5-26); Third sermon - blessings and curses; Moses' death (27-34)
Historical Context
Spoken in Moab just east of the Jordan River at the end of the forty wilderness years, before the conquest under Joshua.
Related Places
Plains of Moab; Jordan River
Related Events
Giving of the Law at Sinai
