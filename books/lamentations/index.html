Summary
Lamentations is a collection of five funeral poems mourning Jerusalem's destruction by Babylon. Amid grief it affirms God's faithfulness and mercy, giving voice to sorrow that still hopes in the Lord.
Key Verses
Lamentations 3:22-23; Lamentations 3:25-26
Key People
Jeremiah (traditional)
Themes
Grief; Judgment; God's faithfulness; Hope in suffering
Purpose
To mourn the fall of Jerusalem, confess sin, and teach God's people to hope in His mercy even in disaster.
Outline
Five laments - Jerusalem's misery (1); God's wrath (2); Hope in affliction (3); Siege recalled (4); Prayer for restoration (5)
Historical Context
Written shortly after Babylon destroyed Jerusalem and the Temple in 586 BC.
Related Places
Jerusalem; Babylon
Related Events
Fall of Jerusalem and the Exile
