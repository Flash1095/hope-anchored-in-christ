Daniel

Book
Daniel
Testament
Old Testament
Category
Major Prophets
Traditional Author
Daniel
Date Approx
c. 605-530 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Key Theme
Faithfulness in exile and prophecy of coming kingdoms
Summary
Daniel and his friends remain faithful to God while serving in Babylonian and Persian courts, and God delivers them from the fiery furnace and lions' den. Daniel's visions reveal God's sovereign plan for successive kingdoms and the coming Son of Man.
Key Verses
Daniel 2:44; Daniel 6:22; Daniel 7:13-14; Daniel 12:3
Key People
Daniel; Shadrach; Meshach; Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego; King Nebuchadnezzar; Belshazzar; Darius
Themes
Sovereignty; Faithfulness in exile; Prophecy; Kingdoms; Son of Man
Purpose
To encourage God's people that He rules over empires and will establish His everlasting kingdom.
Outline
Stories of faithfulness (1-6); Daniel's visions (7-12)
Historical Context
Set in Babylon and Persia during the exile, from Daniel's deportation in 605 BC through the early Persian period.
Related Places
Babylon; Susa
