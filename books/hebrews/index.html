Epistles

Key Theme
Christ superior to angels, Moses, and the old covenant
Summary
Hebrews proclaims Jesus as greater than angels, Moses, and the Levitical priesthood, and His sacrifice as the perfect, once-for-all fulfillment of the old covenant. It urges wavering believers to hold fast and run with endurance.
Key Verses
Hebrews 4:15-16; Hebrews 11:1; Hebrews 12:1-2; Hebrews 13:8
Key People
Jesus; Moses; Abraham; Melchizedek; Heroes of faith in ch. 11
Themes
Superiority of Christ; Faith; Perseverance; New covenant; Rest
Purpose
To keep Jewish Christians from drifting back to Judaism under pressure by showing Christ fulfills and surpasses the old covenant.
Outline
Christ greater than angels and Moses (1-4); Greater priesthood and covenant (5-10); Faith and endurance (11-13)
Historical Context
Written before the Temple's destruction in AD 70, likely AD 64-68, to Jewish Christians facing persecution; its author is unknown.
Related Places
Jerusalem
