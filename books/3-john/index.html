3 John

Book
3 John
Testament
New Testament
Category
Epistles
Traditional Author
John the Apostle
Date Approx
c. AD 85-95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Key Theme
Hospitality and faithfulness to the truth
Summary
John commends Gaius for generously hosting traveling missionaries and rebukes Diotrephes, who loves to be first and refuses hospitality. It models supporting gospel workers worthily of God.
Key Verses
3 John 1:11; 3 John 1:4
Key People
John the Apostle; Gaius; Diotrephes; Demetrius
Themes
Hospitality; Support for ministry; Humility; Truth
Purpose
To encourage Gaius's hospitality, commend Demetrius, and address Diotrephes's pride and obstruction.
Outline
Commendation of Gaius (1-8); Condemnation of Diotrephes (9-10); Commendation of Demetrius and closing (11-14)
Historical Context
Written late in the first century AD, reflecting traveling teachers dependent on local believers' hospitality in Asia Minor.
