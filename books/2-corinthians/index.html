Summary
In his most personal letter, Paul defends his apostleship, shares his sufferings, and urges reconciliation and generous giving. He shows that God's power is made perfect in weakness.
Key Verses
2 Corinthians 5:17; 2 Corinthians 5:21; 2 Corinthians 12:9; 2 Corinthians 4:16-18

Themes
Ministry; Suffering; Reconciliation; Generosity; Strength in weakness
Purpose
To reconcile with the Corinthians, defend Paul's ministry against critics, and prepare the collection for Jerusalem.
Outline
Paul's ministry explained (1-7); Generous giving (8-9); Defense against false apostles (10-13)
Historical Context
Written from Macedonia around AD 56, after a painful visit and a severe letter to Corinth.
