Jesus Did Not Die on the Cross

Objection
Jesus Did Not Die on the Cross
Worldview
Islam
Objection Summary
Jesus was too great a prophet to suffer such a shameful death; God would not allow it. Another was made to resemble him, or he was taken up alive.
Response
This is a sincere difference, and Christians should state it plainly rather than paper over it. Yet the crucifixion is among the best-attested facts of ancient history: all four Gospels, Paul's letters, and non-Christian writers like Tacitus and Josephus agree Jesus was executed under Pilate. The resurrection, not an escape from death, is the heart of the Christian message - God defeating death by going through it.
Scripture
Matthew 27:32-56; Mark 15:21-41; 1 Corinthians 15:3-4
