The Church's Violent History

Objection
The Church's Violent History
Worldview
Skepticism
Objection Summary
Crusades, inquisitions, religious wars - the church's own history is stained with blood. How can it claim moral authority?
Response
Honesty requires admitting it: Christians have done terrible things in Christ's name, and the church must repent of them, not excuse them. But those acts contradict Christ's teaching, which is precisely why they are condemned - by Christians first. The same church also built hospitals, ended the slave trade, and fed the poor. Judge the faith by its Founder and its fruits, not only by its failures.
Scripture
Matthew 5:44; Luke 9:54-55; James 1:27
