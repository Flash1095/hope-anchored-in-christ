Jesus Is Not the Messiah

Objection
Jesus Is Not the Messiah
Worldview
Judaism
Objection Summary
The Messiah was to bring world peace, rebuild the temple, and gather Israel. Jesus did none of these, so he cannot be the promised one.
Response
Jewish expectation deserves respect, and Christians agree the story is not finished - the New Testament itself expects a second coming when peace is completed. But the Hebrew prophets also described a suffering servant who would be pierced for others' transgressions before glory came (Isaiah 53; Zechariah 12:10). Christians read the promises as fulfilled in two stages: the servant first, the reigning king at His return.
Scripture
Isaiah 53:5-6; Zechariah 12:10; Micah 5:2
