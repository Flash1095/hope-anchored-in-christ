God Has No Partners: Tawhid vs Trinity

Objection
God Has No Partners: Tawhid vs Trinity
Worldview
Islam
Objection Summary
Tawhid - God's absolute oneness - is Islam's central truth. The Trinity associates partners with God, which the Qur'an calls the gravest sin.
Response
Muslims are right to guard God's oneness, and Christians share that zeal: the Trinity is not three gods but one God in three persons. Christians did not arrive at this doctrine lightly; it came from wrestling with Jesus' own claims and the resurrection. The honest disagreement is not about whether God is one - both faiths agree - but about how God has revealed Himself in Jesus.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 6:4; John 10:30; Matthew 28:19
Related Topics
Trinity; Islam; God
Related People
Jesus; Abraham
Related Events
The Baptism of Jesus
