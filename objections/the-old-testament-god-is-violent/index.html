The Old Testament God Is Violent

Objection
The Old Testament God Is Violent
Worldview
Skepticism
Objection Summary
The God who commands conquest and judgment in the Old Testament looks nothing like the loving Jesus of the New Testament.
Response
This tension is real and Christians should not flatten it. The conquest narratives are historically bounded judgments on deeply corrupt cultures, described with ancient war rhetoric, and they sit within a larger story of God's patience - centuries of warning first. The same God judges evil and provides mercy, and the cross is where both meet: judgment borne by God Himself so mercy can reach us.
Scripture
Exodus 34:6-7; Jonah 4:2; Romans 3:25-26
