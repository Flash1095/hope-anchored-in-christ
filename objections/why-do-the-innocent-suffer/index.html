Why Do the Innocent Suffer?

Objection
Why Do the Innocent Suffer?
Worldview
Skepticism
Objection Summary
It is one thing for wrongdoers to suffer; it is another for children to get cancer or villages to drown in floods. A just God would not allow innocent suffering.
Response
This objection deserves tears before arguments, and Christians should never offer glib answers to grieving people. Scripture itself wrestles with it - Job, the Psalms, Lamentations - and offers no tidy formula. What it does offer is a God who entered innocent suffering Himself on the cross, who weeps with us, and who promises a day when every tear is wiped away and justice is complete.
Scripture
Job 1:20-22; Psalm 34:18; Revelation 21:4
