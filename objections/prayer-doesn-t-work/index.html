Prayer Doesn't Work

Objection
Prayer Doesn't Work
Worldview
Atheism
Objection Summary
Studies show prayer has no measurable effect, and prayers for healing routinely go unanswered. Prayer is talking to an empty room.
Response
Prayer is not a vending machine or a laboratory variable; it is relationship. Christians pray for healing and sometimes see remarkable answers, and sometimes grieve unanswered prayers - Scripture itself records both (Paul's thorn, Hezekiah's extension). The deepest evidence for prayer is not statistics but the transformed lives of people who pray, and a God who listens even when His answer is 'not yet' or 'no.'
Scripture
James 5:16; 2 Corinthians 12:8-9; Psalm 34:17
