God Is One, Not Three

Objection
God Is One, Not Three
Worldview
Judaism
Objection Summary
The Shema declares God is one. The Trinity divides God into three and compromises the pure monotheism at Judaism's heart.
Response
Christians affirm the Shema wholeheartedly: there is one God, not three gods. The Trinity is the claim that this one God exists eternally as Father, Son, and Spirit - a mystery, not a contradiction, and one the New Testament presents as the outworking of God's own self-revelation. The doctrine was hammered out precisely to protect monotheism while honoring everything Jesus claimed and did.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 6:4; Matthew 28:19; John 1:1
