Karma Explains Suffering Better

Objection
Karma Explains Suffering Better
Worldview
Hinduism
Objection Summary
Suffering is explained by karma: present pain results from past actions, across lifetimes. This is juster than a God who allows innocent suffering.
Response
Karma offers an explanation, but at a steep price: it can make compassion seem pointless (suffering is deserved) and offers no forgiveness, only endless balancing. Christianity agrees actions have consequences, but adds grace: God does not leave us to pay an infinite debt across lifetimes. In Christ, suffering is met with presence, forgiveness, and a final end to pain.
Scripture
Romans 3:23-24; Revelation 21:4; Galatians 6:7
Related Topics
Karma; Grace; Evil and Suffering
