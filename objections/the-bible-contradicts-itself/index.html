The Bible Contradicts Itself

Skepticism

Objection Summary

The Bible disagrees with itself - different genealogies, different resurrection accounts, different numbers. A book that contradicts itself cannot be God's word.

Response

Most alleged contradictions dissolve on closer reading: different authors emphasizing different details, rounding numbers, or writing from different viewpoints - exactly what honest eyewitness testimony looks like. Serious scholars, including skeptics, acknowledge the text's overall coherence. Christians invite doubters to examine specific passages rather than settle for lists, and to distinguish between genuine difficulties and manufactured ones.
