All Religions Lead to God

Other

Objection Summary

Every religion is a path up the same mountain. It is arrogant to claim Christianity alone is true.

Response

This sounds humble but actually claims to know more than every religion claims for itself - most religions make exclusive truth claims. Christianity's exclusive claim is not arrogance but the logic of its center: if Jesus truly rose from the dead, then His words carry unique authority. Christians should hold this conviction with humility and love, never as a weapon, and listen seriously to neighbors of other faiths.
