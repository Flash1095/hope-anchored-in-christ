The Qur'an Corrects the Bible

Objection
The Qur'an Corrects the Bible
Worldview
Islam
Objection Summary
The Qur'an is God's final revelation, preserved perfectly, and it corrects the errors that crept into earlier scriptures.
Response
Muslims and Christians both care deeply about preserving God's word, and that shared concern is a good starting point. Christians note two things with respect: the biblical manuscript evidence shows remarkable preservation, and the Qur'an's own affirmations of the Torah and Gospel sit uneasily with the claim that those books were lost. The fairest path is to read both texts carefully and weigh the historical claims - especially about Jesus - on their own merits.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 4:2; Matthew 24:35; John 17:17
