Science Has Disproved God

Objection
Science Has Disproved God
Worldview
Atheism
Objection Summary
Science explains lightning, disease, and the origin of species without God. As explanations advance, God retreats, and a thinking person no longer needs the 'God hypothesis.'
Response
Science describes how the world works; it does not by itself say why there is a world, why it is orderly, or why minds can understand it. Many of history's greatest scientists were Christians who saw science as exploring God's creation. God is not a gap in our knowledge but the ground of the knowledge itself - the reason science works at all.
Scripture
Psalm 19:1; Romans 1:20; Colossians 1:16-17
