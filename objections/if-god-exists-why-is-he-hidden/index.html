If God Exists, Why Is He Hidden?

Objection
If God Exists, Why Is He Hidden?
Worldview
Skepticism
Objection Summary
If God wanted a relationship with us, He would make Himself obvious. His hiddenness suggests He is not there.
Response
Hiddenness is a real struggle, and Scripture voices it (Psalm 10:1; Job). Yet Christianity claims God has revealed Himself - in creation, in conscience, in Israel's story, and decisively in Jesus - while respecting human freedom: overwhelming proof would compel assent but not love. God reveals enough to seek Him and hides enough that seeking is real. Many who sought honestly report they found Him.

Psalm 19:1-4; Romans 1:19-20; Jeremiah 29:13
