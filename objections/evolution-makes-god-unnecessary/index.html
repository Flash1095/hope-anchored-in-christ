Evolution Makes God Unnecessary

Objection
Evolution Makes God Unnecessary
Worldview
Atheism
Objection Summary
Evolution explains life's diversity through natural selection. There is no need for a designer.
Response
Evolution, even fully granted, describes a process; it does not explain why there is a process, why it produces information-rich life, or why the universe is fine-tuned for it. Many Christians accept an old earth and evolutionary mechanisms while seeing God's hand in the whole. The real question is not mechanism but meaning: does unguided process explain everything, including mind, morality, and beauty?
Scripture
Genesis 1:27; Psalm 139:13; Romans 1:20
