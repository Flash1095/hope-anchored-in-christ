Jesus Never Claimed to Be God

Islam

Objection Summary

Jesus called himself a prophet and a servant of God. The claim that he is divine was invented later by the church.

Response

Muslim friends raise this sincerely, and it deserves a textual answer. In the Gospels, Jesus forgives sins, receives worship, calls Himself 'I AM,' and claims authority over the Sabbath and judgment - claims His Jewish hearers understood as divine, which is why they accused Him of blasphemy. The early church did not invent His divinity; it struggled to make sense of what He said and did.
