Muhammad Is the Final Prophet

Objection
Muhammad Is the Final Prophet
Worldview
Islam
Objection Summary
Muhammad is the seal of the prophets, and the Qur'an is God's final, perfect revelation, superseding earlier scriptures.
Response
Christians honor Muhammad's sincerity and the moral seriousness of Islam while respectfully disagreeing. The Christian claim rests on Jesus Himself: not merely a prophet bringing a message, but the Son who is the message, whose death and resurrection were foretold and historically attested. If Jesus rose from the dead, His claim to be the final Word stands; that historical question is where the conversation should focus.
Scripture
John 1:1-14; Hebrews 1:1-3; Matthew 28:18

Related People
Jesus; Moses
