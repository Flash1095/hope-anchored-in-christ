If God Is Good, Why Is There Evil?

Objection
If God Is Good, Why Is There Evil?
Worldview
Atheism
Objection Summary
A good, all-powerful God would prevent suffering, yet the world is full of pain, cruelty, and natural disaster. Either God cannot stop evil, or He will not - so the God Christians describe cannot exist.
Response
Christianity does not minimize evil; it takes it more seriously than any worldview, calling it real, wrong, and an intruder. God gave creatures genuine freedom, which makes love possible and evil possible; and He did not stay distant - in Christ He entered suffering and defeated it, promising a world where evil is finally undone. The question is not whether evil is real, but whether any worldview offers both an explanation and a remedy. Christianity offers both.
Scripture
Genesis 3:17-19; Romans 8:18-23; Revelation 21:4
