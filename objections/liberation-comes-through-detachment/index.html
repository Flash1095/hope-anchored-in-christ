Liberation Comes Through Detachment

Objection
Liberation Comes Through Detachment
Worldview
Buddhism
Objection Summary
Suffering comes from craving and attachment. Freedom comes from letting go, not from a savior.
Response
Buddhism rightly sees that craving causes much suffering, and Christians can learn from its honesty about desire. But Christianity says the deepest problem is not attachment itself - love and longing are good - but disordered loves and a broken relationship with God. Detachment alone cannot forgive guilt or defeat death. Christ offers not escape from the world but redemption of it.
Scripture
Romans 7:18-25; 1 John 2:15-17; John 10:10

Desire; Salvation; Buddhism
Related People
