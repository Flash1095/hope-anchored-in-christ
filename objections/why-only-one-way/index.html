Why Only One Way?

Objection
Why Only One Way?
Worldview
Other
Objection Summary
If God loves everyone, why would He provide only one way to be saved? That seems unfair to billions.
Response
Christians feel the weight of this question - it should never be answered lightly. The answer is not that God is stingy but that He is generous: He came Himself, at infinite cost, to open the way. And Scripture leaves room for mystery about those who never hear, urging us to trust God's justice and mercy while making the message known. The narrowness is in the door; the invitation is to all.
Scripture
John 3:16; Romans 10:14-15; 1 Timothy 2:3-4
