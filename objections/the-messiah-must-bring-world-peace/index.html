The Messiah Must Bring World Peace

Objection
The Messiah Must Bring World Peace
Worldview
Judaism
Objection Summary
The prophets promise the Messiah will usher in universal peace and rebuild the temple. The world is still at war, so Jesus cannot be the Messiah.
Response
This expectation is taken seriously by Christians, who agree peace has not yet fully come. The New Testament's answer is that the Messiah's work unfolds in two comings: first as the suffering servant who deals with sin (Isaiah 53), then as the reigning king who brings final peace (Isaiah 9:7; Revelation 21). The first coming is historically anchored; the second is the church's living hope.
Scripture
Isaiah 9:6-7; Isaiah 53:5; Revelation 21:1-4
