Who Created God?

Objection
Who Created God?
Worldview
Atheism
Objection Summary
If everything needs a cause, then God needs one too - and if God can be uncaused, the universe could be as well.
Response
The argument is not 'everything has a cause' but 'everything that begins to exist has a cause.' God, by definition, did not begin; He is eternal and self-existent. The universe, by contrast, shows evidence of a beginning. Asking who made the Unmade One misunderstands the claim, like asking what is north of the North Pole.
Scripture
Psalm 90:2; Exodus 3:14; Revelation 1:8
