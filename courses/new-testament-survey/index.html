Description
Build on the Gospels by exploring how the good news travelled and took root. You will survey Acts, Paul's letters, the general letters and Revelation in their historical setting, seeing how each book answers real questions real churches faced. Expect clarity, context and plenty of encouragement.
Key Topics
Church; Evangelism; Resurrection; Second Coming; Grace
