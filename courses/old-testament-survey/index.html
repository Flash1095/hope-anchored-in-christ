Description
Go deeper into the first three-quarters of your Bible with confidence. This survey traces God's covenant story through creation, exodus, kingdom, exile and return, helping you read the Law, the Prophets and the Writings as one unfolding promise. You will finish with a map you can trust.
Key Topics
Covenant; Exodus; Exile; Prophecy; Holiness
Related Books
Genesis; Exodus; Joshua; 1 Samuel; Psalms; Isaiah; Daniel
