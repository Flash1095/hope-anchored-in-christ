Women of the Bible

Course
Women of the Bible
Level
Beginner
Duration
6 weeks
Lessons
  • Eve and Sarah: Promise and Waiting
  • Ruth and Naomi: Loyalty and Redemption
  • Hannah and Esther: Courage in Prayer and Action
  • Mary, Mother of Jesus
  • Mary Magdalene and the Women at the Tomb
  • Lydia, Priscilla and the Early Church
Description
Meet women whose faith, courage and faithfulness shaped the whole biblical story. From Sarah and Ruth to Esther, Mary and Priscilla, their lives show that God sees, calls and uses women in powerful ways. Their stories will encourage yours.
Key Topics
Faith; Prayer; Courage; Redemption
