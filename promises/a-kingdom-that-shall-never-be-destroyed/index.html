Title
A Kingdom That Shall Never Be Destroyed
Promise
In the days of earthly kingdoms, the God of heaven would set up a kingdom that would never be destroyed nor left to another people, that would break in pieces all other kingdoms and stand forever.
Fulfillment
Revelation presents the kingdom of the world becoming the kingdom of the Lord and of his Christ.
