Solomon, a Man of Rest and Temple Builder

Title
Solomon, a Man of Rest and Temple Builder
Promise
A son would be born to David who would be a man of rest, with rest from all his enemies, named Solomon, bringing peace and quietness to Israel in his days; he would build a house for God's name, be God's son, and have his kingdom's throne established over Israel for ever.
Fulfillment
Solomon sat on the throne of David and his kingdom was established greatly, as 1 Kings 2:12 records, and he built the house and finished it, as 1 Kings 6:14 records.
