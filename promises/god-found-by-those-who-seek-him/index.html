Title
God Found by Those Who Seek Him
Promise
God would stay with Asa and Judah while they stayed with him, and would be found by them when they sought him.
Condition
Being with the LORD and seeking him.
Fulfillment
Asa sought the LORD, and 2 Chronicles 15:15 states the LORD gave them rest round about.
