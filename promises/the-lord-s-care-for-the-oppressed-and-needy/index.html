Title
The LORD's Care for the Oppressed and Needy
Promise
The LORD executes judgment for the oppressed, feeds the hungry, frees prisoners, opens blind eyes, lifts the bowed down, loves the righteous, preserves strangers, and relieves the fatherless and widow. Doubt: Present-tense descriptions of God's character and habitual action, not future commitments; partly overlaps the clear Psalms 12:5 entry.
