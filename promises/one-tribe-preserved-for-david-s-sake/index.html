Title
One Tribe Preserved for David's Sake
Promise
For David's sake and Jerusalem's sake, God would not tear the whole kingdom away, but would leave one tribe to Solomon's son.
Fulfillment
1 Kings 12:20 records that none followed the house of David but the tribe of Judah only.
