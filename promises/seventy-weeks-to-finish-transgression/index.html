Title
Seventy Weeks to Finish Transgression
Promise
Seventy weeks were determined for Daniel's people and city to finish transgression, end sins, make reconciliation for iniquity, bring in everlasting righteousness, seal up vision and prophecy, and anoint the most Holy. Doubt: A prophetic timetable and revelation, not a simple divine commitment; its dating and the identity of its fulfilment events are heavily debated, so it does not cleanly fit the definition.
