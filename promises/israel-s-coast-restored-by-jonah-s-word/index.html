Israel's Coast Restored by Jonah's Word

Title
Israel's Coast Restored by Jonah's Word
Promise
Israel's territory would be restored from the Hamath frontier to the lowland sea, as the LORD had spoken through Jonah. Doubt: Promise content is reported, not quoted: the text says Jeroboam restored the coast 'according to the word of the LORD... by Jonah' without quoting the word itself, so the promise wording cannot be verified from this book.
Fulfillment
The restoration itself is recorded in the same verse.
