Title
An Everlasting Priest After Melchizedek
Promise
By an unchangeable oath, the LORD appointed the one addressed to a permanent priesthood patterned on Melchizedek's. Doubt: A genuine divine oath, but addressed to the Messiah rather than a promise made to believers generally; prophetic in character. Secondary covenant: New Covenant.
