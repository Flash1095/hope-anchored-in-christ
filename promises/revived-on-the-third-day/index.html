Revived on the Third Day

Title
Revived on the Third Day
Promise
After two days his people would be revived, and on the third day raised up, so they would live in his sight, as surely as the morning and the latter rain come. Doubt: The words are the people's resolve and hopeful expectation ('let us return'), not God speaking a promise; the third-day language is also debated and should not be forced into a resurrection promise.
Condition
Returning to the LORD, as Hosea 6:1 urges.
Key Scriptures
Hosea 6:2; Hosea 6:3
