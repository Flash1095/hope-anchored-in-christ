Title
Signs Following Believers
Promise
believers will be accompanied by signs, including speaking in new tongues, taking up serpents unharmed, and healing the sick by laying on of hands. Doubt: stands in the longer ending of Mark, whose textual status is debated; the promise of signs (new tongues, serpents, healing) is otherwise unattested in the NT.
