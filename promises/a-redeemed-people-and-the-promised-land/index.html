A Redeemed People and the Promised Land

Title
A Redeemed People and the Promised Land
Promise
God pledged to bring Israel out from under Egyptian burdens, free them from bondage, redeem them with a stretched-out arm and great judgments, take them as his own people and be their God, and bring them into the land sworn to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob and give it to them as a heritage.
Fulfillment
Exodus 14:30 records the LORD saving Israel that day from the Egyptians, and Joshua 21:43 records the LORD giving Israel all the land he swore to give their fathers.
