A Heart to Know the LORD

Title
A Heart to Know the LORD
Promise
God would set his eyes on the exiles for good, bring them back to the land, build them up rather than pull them down, and give them a heart to know him, so that they would be his people and he would be their God, as they return to him wholeheartedly.
Condition
Returning to the LORD with the whole heart.
Fulfillment
The return to the land is recorded in Ezra.
Key Scriptures
Jeremiah 24:6; Jeremiah 24:7
Fulfillment Scriptures
Ezra 1:1
Related Books
