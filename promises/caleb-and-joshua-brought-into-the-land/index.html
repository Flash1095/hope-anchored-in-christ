Caleb and Joshua Brought Into the Land

Title
Caleb and Joshua Brought Into the Land
Promise
God pledged that Caleb, who had another spirit and wholly followed him, would be brought into the land he had searched, with his seed possessing it, and that Caleb and Joshua alone of their generation would come into the land sworn to their fathers.
Condition
Caleb wholly following the LORD, as the passage itself states.
Fulfillment
Joshua 14:13 records Joshua blessing Caleb and giving him Hebron for an inheritance, and Joshua 14:14 records that Hebron became Caleb's inheritance because he wholly followed the LORD.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 14:24; Numbers 14:30

Joshua 14:13; Joshua 14:14
