Cleansing by Washing in the Jordan

Title
Cleansing by Washing in the Jordan
Promise
If Naaman washed seven times in the Jordan, his flesh would come again like a little child's and he would be clean. Doubt: Healing instruction with a promised outcome, spoken by Elisha without 'thus saith the LORD' framing; closer to a prophetic prescription than a divine promise speech.
Condition
Washing seven times in the Jordan.
Fulfillment
2 Kings 5:14 records that Naaman dipped himself seven times and his flesh came again like a little child's, and he was clean.
