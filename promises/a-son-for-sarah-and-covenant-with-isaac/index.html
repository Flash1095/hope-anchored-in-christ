Title
A Son for Sarah and Covenant With Isaac
Promise
God assured Abraham that Sarah would bear him a son at the set time in the following year, that the child would be called Isaac, and that God would establish his covenant with Isaac and his seed after him.
Fulfillment
Genesis 21:2 records that Sarah bore Abraham a son in his old age, at the set time of which God had spoken.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 17:19; Genesis 17:21; Genesis 18:10
