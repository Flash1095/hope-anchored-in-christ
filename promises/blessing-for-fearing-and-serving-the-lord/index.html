Title
Blessing for Fearing and Serving the LORD
Promise
If Israel feared the LORD, served him, obeyed his voice, and did not rebel, then both they and their king would continue following the LORD their God. Doubt: General covenant conditional blessing in Samuel's farewell address rather than a distinct divine commitment to a named recipient or occasion; verse 15 is the warning half of the same conditional.
Condition
Fearing the LORD, serving him, obeying his voice, and not rebelling against his commandment.
Key Scriptures
1 Samuel 12:14; 1 Samuel 12:15
