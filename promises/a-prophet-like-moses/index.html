A Prophet Like Moses

Title
A Prophet Like Moses
Promise
God pledged to raise up a prophet from among Israel's brethren, like Moses, and to put his words in that prophet's mouth, so that the prophet would speak all that God commanded, with God requiring it of anyone who would not listen to his words.
Fulfillment
Acts 3:22 records Peter quoting Moses' words about a prophet like him, and Acts 3:23 records that every soul who would not hear that prophet would be destroyed from among the people.
Key Scriptures
