Title
The Messenger Preparing the Way
Promise
God would send his messenger to prepare the way before him, and the Lord whom his people seek would suddenly come to his temple, even the messenger of the covenant in whom they delight.
Fulfillment
Matthew's Gospel and Mark's Gospel quote the messenger preparing the way for John the Baptist.
