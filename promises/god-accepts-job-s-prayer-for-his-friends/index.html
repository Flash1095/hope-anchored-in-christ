God Accepts Job's Prayer for His Friends

Title
God Accepts Job's Prayer for His Friends
Promise
God told Eliphaz and his two friends to offer a burnt offering and have Job pray for them, committing himself to accept Job's prayer on their behalf rather than dealing with them for their folly.
Condition
Offering the burnt offering as commanded, with Job praying for them.
Fulfillment
Job 42:9 records that the friends did as the LORD commanded them and that the LORD accepted Job.
