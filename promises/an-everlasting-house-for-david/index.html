Title
An Everlasting House for David
Promise
God would raise up David's seed after him, establish his kingdom, and build him a house; his throne would be established for ever.
Fulfillment
Luke 1:32-33 states the Lord God would give Jesus the throne of his father David and that his kingdom would have no end.
