A Child Named Josiah Born to David's House

Title
A Child Named Josiah Born to David's House
Promise
A future descendant of David's line, named Josiah, would sacrifice the high-place priests upon the Bethel altar and burn human bones on it. Doubt: Predictive prophecy naming a future king, fulfilled centuries later, but framed as foretelling rather than a commitment made to a recipient; its main clause is judgment on the altar.
Fulfillment
2 Kings 23:16 records Josiah taking bones from the sepulchres and burning them upon the altar at Bethel.
