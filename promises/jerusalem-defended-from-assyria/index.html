Title
Jerusalem Defended From Assyria
Promise
The king of Assyria would not enter Jerusalem, shoot an arrow there, or cast a bank against it, but would return by the way he came, for God would defend the city to save it, for his own sake and for David's sake.
Fulfillment
2 Kings 19:35 records that the angel of the LORD went out and smote one hundred and eighty-five thousand in the camp of the Assyrians that night.
