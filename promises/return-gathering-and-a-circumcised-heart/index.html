Return, Gathering, and a Circumcised Heart

Title
Return, Gathering, and a Circumcised Heart
Promise
God pledged that when Israel returned to the LORD and obeyed his voice with all their heart and soul, he would turn their captivity, have compassion on them, gather them from all the nations where he had scattered them, bring them into the land their fathers possessed so they would possess it and be multiplied above their fathers, and circumcise their hearts and the hearts of their seed so that they would love the LORD with all their heart and soul and live.
Condition
Returning to the LORD and obeying his voice with all heart and soul.
Key Scriptures
Deuteronomy 30:3; Deuteronomy 30:5; Deuteronomy 30:6
