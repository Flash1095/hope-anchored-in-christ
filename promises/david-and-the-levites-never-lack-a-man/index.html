David and the Levites Never Lack a Man

Title
David and the Levites Never Lack a Man
Promise
David would never lack a man to sit on Israel's throne, nor would the Levitical priests lack a man to offer sacrifices, secured by the same fixed covenant as day and night. Doubt: The Davidic half is a genuine promise, but the Levitical offering perpetuity sits in tension with Hebrews' teaching that sacrifices have ceased; drafting both halves as one fulfilled promise would overstate the case.
Key Scriptures
Jeremiah 33:17; Jeremiah 33:18; Jeremiah 33:21
