Spirit Upon the Seventy Elders

Title
Spirit Upon the Seventy Elders
Promise
God pledged to share the empowering spirit resting on Moses with seventy Israelite elders, so that they would help carry the people's load and Moses would not carry it by himself.
Fulfillment
Numbers 11:25 records the LORD coming down, taking of the spirit upon Moses, and giving it to the seventy elders, who prophesied.
