Elijah Before the Great Day

Title
Elijah Before the Great Day
Promise
Before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the LORD, God would send Elijah the prophet, who would turn the heart of the fathers to the children and the heart of the children to their fathers, so the land would not be smitten with a curse.
Fulfillment
Jesus teaches that Elijah has already come, and the disciples understand him to mean John the Baptist.
Key Scriptures
Malachi 4:5; Malachi 4:6
