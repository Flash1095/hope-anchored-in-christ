Passover Protection for Marked Houses

Title
Passover Protection for Marked Houses
Promise
God assured Israel that the blood on the doorposts would be a token, and that when he saw the blood he would pass over the house, so that the plague would not destroy them when he struck Egypt.
Condition
Blood of the lamb applied to the doorposts, as the passage itself requires.
