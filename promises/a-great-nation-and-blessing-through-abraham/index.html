A Great Nation and Blessing Through Abraham

Title
A Great Nation and Blessing Through Abraham
Promise
Through Abraham God would make a great nation, bless him, and bless all families of the earth in him.
Fulfillment
Galatians 3:8 states the gospel was preached beforehand to Abraham in this promise, and Galatians 3:16 identifies the Seed as Christ.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 12:2; Genesis 12:3
Fulfillment Scriptures
Galatians 3:8; Galatians 3:16
