Title
Meal and Oil That Will Not Fail
Promise
The barrel of meal would not run out and the cruse of oil would not fail until the day God sent rain on the earth.
Fulfillment
1 Kings 17:16 records that the barrel of meal wasted not and the cruse of oil did not fail, according to the word of the LORD spoken by Elijah.
