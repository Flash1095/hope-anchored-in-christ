Title
Near to Those Who Call, Fulfilling Their Desire
Promise
The LORD is near to all who call on him in truth, fulfils the desire of those who fear him, hears their cry, saves them, and preserves all who love him. Doubt: Overlaps 'Prayer According to His Will' (call/answer) and 'Refuge and Protection' (preservation).
Condition
Calling on him in truth; fearing him; loving him.
Key Scriptures
Psalms 145:18; Psalms 145:19; Psalms 145:20
