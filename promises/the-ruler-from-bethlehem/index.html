Title
The Ruler From Bethlehem
Promise
From Bethlehem Ephratah, small among Judah's thousands, would come forth a ruler for Israel whose goings forth are from of old, from everlasting, and he would stand and feed his flock in the strength of the LORD.
Fulfillment
Matthew's Gospel quotes this passage for the birth of Jesus in Bethlehem.
