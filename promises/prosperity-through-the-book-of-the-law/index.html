Prosperity Through the Book of the Law

Title
Prosperity Through the Book of the Law
Promise
If Joshua meditated on the book of the law day and night and observed all that was written in it, his way would prosper and he would have good success. Doubt: Part of the same Joshua 1 commissioning speech as the clear presence entry; listing it separately would split one speech, but its condition (meditation on the law) differs from the unconditional presence promise.
Condition
Meditating on the book of the law day and night and observing all that is written in it.
