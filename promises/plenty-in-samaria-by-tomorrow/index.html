Title
Plenty in Samaria by Tomorrow
Promise
By the next day at about that time, fine flour and barley would be selling cheaply in the gate of Samaria.
Fulfillment
2 Kings 7:18 records that it came to pass as the man of God had spoken, with flour and barley at those prices in the gate of Samaria.
