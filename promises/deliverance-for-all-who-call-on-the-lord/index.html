Title
Deliverance for All Who Call on the LORD
Promise
Whoever calls on the name of the LORD would be delivered, for in Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there would be deliverance, among the remnant whom the LORD calls.
Condition
Calling on the name of the LORD.
Fulfillment
Peter quotes this promise on the day of Pentecost, and Paul quotes it for all who call on the Lord.
