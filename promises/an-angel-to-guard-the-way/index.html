An Angel to Guard the Way and Give the Land

Title
An Angel to Guard the Way and Give the Land
Promise
God pledged to send an angel before Israel to keep them on the journey and bring them to the prepared place, to oppose their enemies and fight their adversaries, to cut off the peoples of the land, to put dread before them, to clear out the inhabitants gradually rather than in a single year, and to fix their territory stretching between the southern sea and the Philistine coast, and between the wilderness and the great river, handing the inhabitants over to them.
Condition
Obeying the angel's voice and doing all that God spoke.
Key Scriptures
Exodus 23:20; Exodus 23:22; Exodus 23:23; Exodus 23:27; Exodus 23:30; Exodus 23:31
