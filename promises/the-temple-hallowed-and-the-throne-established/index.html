The Temple Hallowed and the Throne Established

Title
The Temple Hallowed and the Throne Established
Promise
God had heard Solomon's prayer and hallowed the temple he built, so that God's name would be there for ever, with his eyes and heart there perpetually; and if Solomon walked as David had, God would establish his kingdom's throne over Israel for ever.
Condition
Walking before God as David did, in integrity of heart and uprightness, keeping his statutes and judgments.
Fulfillment
The temple stood as God's house, but the everlasting throne depended on walking as David did, and 1 Kings 11:11 records the kingdom rent from Solomon's son for his disobedience.
Key Scriptures
1 Kings 9:3; 1 Kings 9:4; 1 Kings 9:5
