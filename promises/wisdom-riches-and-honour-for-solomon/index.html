Wisdom, Riches, and Honour for Solomon

Title
Wisdom, Riches, and Honour for Solomon
Promise
God had given Solomon a wise and understanding heart, unmatched before or after, and had also given him riches and honour beyond any king of his days.
Condition
Lengthened days depended on walking in God's ways and keeping his statutes and commandments, as David did.
Fulfillment
1 Kings 4:29 states God gave Solomon wisdom, and 1 Kings 10:23 states he exceeded all kings in riches and wisdom; the lengthened days depended on obedience, and Solomon later turned aside, as 1 Kings 11:4 records.
Key Scriptures
1 Kings 3:12; 1 Kings 3:13; 1 Kings 3:14
Fulfillment Scriptures
