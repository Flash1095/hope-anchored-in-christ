Ishmael Multiplied and Made a Great Nation

Title
Ishmael Multiplied and Made a Great Nation
Promise
God assured Abraham and Hagar that Ishmael's seed would be multiplied exceedingly, that twelve princes would come from him, and that he would be made a great nation, because he was Abraham's seed.
Fulfillment
Genesis 25:16 records twelve princes descended from Ishmael, named by their towns and castles.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 16:10; Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:18
