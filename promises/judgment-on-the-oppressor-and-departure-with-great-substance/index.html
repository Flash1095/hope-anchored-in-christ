Judgment on the Oppressor and Departure With Great Substance
Promise
God assured Abraham that the nation which afflicted his seed would be judged, and that afterwards his seed would come out with great substance.
Fulfillment
Exodus 12:36 records that Israel asked for and received articles of silver, gold, and raiment from the Egyptians, so that they spoiled Egypt.
