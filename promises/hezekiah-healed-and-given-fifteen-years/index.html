Title
Hezekiah Healed and Given Fifteen Years
Promise
God had heard Hezekiah's prayer and seen his tears, and would heal him so that he would go up to the house of the LORD on the third day, add fifteen years to his life, and deliver him and Jerusalem from the king of Assyria, defending the city for God's own sake and for David's sake.
Fulfillment
2 Kings 20:7 records that the boil was treated and Hezekiah recovered.
