The Suffering Servant

Title
The Suffering Servant
Promise
A servant would bear the griefs and sorrows of others, suffer in their place for their wrongdoing, and through that suffering bring them wholeness and peace. Doubt: Prophetic portrait of atonement, not phrased as a divine commitment to a recipient; its force as a promise rests on later application to Christ rather than on a stated 'I will' commitment in the passage itself.
Fulfillment
The Gospels and epistles apply the servant's suffering to Jesus.
Key Scriptures
Isaiah 53:5; Isaiah 53:6
Fulfillment Scriptures
1 Peter 2:24
