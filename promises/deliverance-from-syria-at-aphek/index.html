Title
Deliverance From Syria at Aphek
Promise
Because the Syrians claimed the LORD was only God of the hills, God would deliver their great army into the king of Israel's hand.
Fulfillment
1 Kings 20:29 records that in the seventh day the battle was joined and the children of Israel slew one hundred thousand Syrian footmen in one day.
