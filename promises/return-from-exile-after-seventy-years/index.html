Title
Return From Exile After Seventy Years
Promise
After seventy years in Babylon, God would visit his people and bring them back to Jerusalem.
Fulfillment
Ezra 1:1-3 records Cyrus' decree for the return and the rebuilding of the house of the LORD at Jerusalem.
