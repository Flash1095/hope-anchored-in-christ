A Light to the Gentiles

Title
A Light to the Gentiles
Promise
God's servant would be more than Israel's restorer: he would be a light to the nations, so that God's salvation would reach to the ends of the earth.
Fulfillment
Simeon calls Jesus a light to the Gentiles, and Paul and Barnabas quote this passage for their mission to the Gentiles.
Key Scriptures
Isaiah 49:6
