Title
David the Shepherd and the Covenant of Peace
Promise
God would set one shepherd over his people, David his servant, who would feed them and be their prince, and God would make an everlasting covenant of peace with them, set his sanctuary among them forever, and be their God while they are his people.
Fulfillment
Luke's Gospel presents Jesus as the heir who will sit on David's throne.
