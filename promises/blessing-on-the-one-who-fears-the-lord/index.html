Title
Blessing on the One Who Fears the LORD
Promise
Everyone who fears the LORD and walks in his ways is blessed, eating the labour of their hands, happy and well. Doubt: Wisdom-pattern blessedness (prosperity follows godliness) rather than a divine commitment.
Condition
Fearing the LORD and walking in his ways.
