Title
Seed Multiplied and Blessing Confirmed by Oath
Promise
After Abraham obeyed in offering Isaac, God swore by himself that he would greatly bless Abraham, multiply his seed as the stars of heaven and the sand on the seashore, give his seed possession of the gate of their enemies, and bless all nations of the earth in his seed.
Condition
Abraham's obedience in the offering of Isaac, as stated in the passage itself.
