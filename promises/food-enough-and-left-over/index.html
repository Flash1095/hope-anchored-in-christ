Food Enough and Left Over

Title
Food Enough and Left Over
Promise
The hundred men would eat and leave food over, according to the word of the LORD. Doubt: Genuine 'thus saith the LORD' wording, but a single-meal provision announcement, minor in scope beside the sustained provision entry from 1 Kings 17:14.
Fulfillment
2 Kings 4:44 records that they ate and left thereof, according to the word of the LORD.
