Good Tidings of Peace for Judah

Title
Good Tidings of Peace for Judah
Promise
Good tidings of peace would be brought to Judah, her feasts kept and vows performed, for the wicked would no longer pass through her, being utterly cut off. Doubt: Judah's deliverance here is an implied consequence of Nineveh's fall, not a standalone commitment addressed to Judah; the feast-keeping is a command rather than a promise condition, so the entry would rest on implication.
