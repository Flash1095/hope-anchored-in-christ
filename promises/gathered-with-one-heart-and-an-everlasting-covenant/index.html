Gathered With One Heart and an Everlasting Covenant

God would gather his people from all the lands where he had driven them, bring them back to their place, give them one heart and one way so they would fear him always, make an everlasting covenant with them to do them good and never turn away, and plant them in the land in faithfulness.

Fulfillment

The gathering is recorded in the return from exile, and the everlasting covenant is instituted by Jesus at the Last Supper.
