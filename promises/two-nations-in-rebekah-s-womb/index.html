Two Nations in Rebekah's Womb

Title
Two Nations in Rebekah's Womb
Promise
God revealed to Rebekah that her twins would found two distinct peoples, that the two groups would diverge from birth, that one would prove stronger than the other, and that the older child's line would end up serving the younger's. Doubt: This reads as a prophetic oracle about the future of two nations, not a divine commitment of benefit to Rebekah herself; no 'I will' promise is addressed to her, and the elder-serving-younger outcome is national history rather than a promised blessing.
