Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
A town Gad built in the Transjordan settlement, later on Gideon's pursuit route when he chased the Midianite kings eastward past Nobah and Jogbehah.
Biblical Significance
Jogbehah links two eras: a town built in the settlement, then a landmark on the road of Gideon's victory.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 32:35; Judges 8:11
