Tyre

Name
Tyre
Modern Location
Southern Lebanon
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
The wealthy Phoenician port whose king Hiram supplied cedar and craftsmen for Solomon's temple - and whose princess Jezebel married Ahab, bringing Baal worship into Israel.
Biblical Significance
Tyre embodies the double edge of the nations: partner in building God's house, then pipeline for idolatry. Jesus later praised the faith He found in Tyre's region above Israel's unbelief.
Key Scriptures
1 Kings 5:1-6; 1 Kings 16:31; Matthew 15:21-28
