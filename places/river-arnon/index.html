River Arnon

Name
River Arnon
Modern Location
Wadi Mujib, Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The deep gorge river forming Moab's northern border, crossed by Israel after defeating Sihon.
Biblical Significance
Arnon is the border river Israel crossed into the Amorite kingdom, and the line of Moab's lament.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 21:13; Deuteronomy 2:24; Isaiah 16:2
Related Books
Numbers; Deuteronomy; Isaiah
Related People
Moses
