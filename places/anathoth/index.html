Description
A Levitical town in Benjamin, home of Jeremiah and of Abiathar, where Jeremiah bought a field as a sign of return.
Biblical Significance
Anathoth is Jeremiah's hometown, whose men plotted against him, and where he bought land in a besieged city as hope of return.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 21:18; Jeremiah 1:1; Jeremiah 32:7
