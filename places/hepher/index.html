Hepher

Name
Hepher
Modern Location
Tell el Muhaffar
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A Canaanite royal city whose king Joshua defeated, later the centre of one of Solomon's supply districts under Ben-hesed, covering Socoh and the land of Hepher.
Biblical Significance
Hepher moved from defeated enemy capital to a working district of Solomon's kingdom, one of the towns that fed the king's table.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 12:17; 1 Kings 4:10
Related Books
Joshua; 1 Kings
Related People
Joshua; Ben-Hesed, Officer of Solomon
