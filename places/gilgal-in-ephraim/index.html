Gilgal in Ephraim

View on map →

The Gilgal where Elijah and Elisha stopped on the way to Bethel and Jericho before Elijah was taken up, and where Elisha later returned to the sons of the prophets during a dearth in the land — a northern Gilgal, distinct from the camp near Jericho.

Biblical Significance

This Gilgal belongs to the Elijah-Elisha story: the last stopping place of the two prophets together, and a home of the prophetic community Elisha fed in famine.
