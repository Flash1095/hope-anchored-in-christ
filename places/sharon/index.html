Sharon

View on map →

Name
Sharon
Modern Location
Coastal plain north of Joppa, Israel
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
The fertile coastal plain stretching north from Joppa toward Carmel, famed for its flowers and pastures. Called Saron in the New Testament.
Biblical Significance
Sharon's beauty became a picture of restoration: Isaiah promises the wilderness will blossom like Sharon.
Key Scriptures
1 Chronicles 27:29; Song of Solomon 2:1; Isaiah 35:2; Acts 9:35
Related Books
1 Chronicles; Song of Solomon; Isaiah; Acts
Related People
