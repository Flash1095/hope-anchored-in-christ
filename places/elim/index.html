Name

Modern Location
Unknown; in the Sinai wilderness
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
One of the stations on Israel's wilderness journey, named in the Numbers 33 itinerary. The oasis of twelve wells and seventy palm trees where Israel camped after Marah. No further detail is recorded beyond the journey account.
Biblical Significance
The wilderness stations trace the route God led Israel from Egypt to the Plains of Moab.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 33:9; Exodus 15:27
Related Books
Numbers; Exodus
