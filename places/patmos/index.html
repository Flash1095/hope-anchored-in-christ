Patmos

Name
Patmos
Modern Location
Greece (Aegean island)
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Description
The small rocky island where the aged apostle John was exiled 'for the word of God' - and where he received the Revelation, the Bible's final book.
Biblical Significance
Patmos turns exile into vision. Banished and alone, John saw the throne, the Lamb, and the new heaven and earth. Our bleakest places can become our clearest views of Christ.
Key Scriptures
Revelation 1:9-11; Revelation 21:1-4
