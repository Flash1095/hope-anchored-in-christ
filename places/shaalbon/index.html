Description
Shaalbon was the home of Eliahba the Shaalbonite, one of David's mighty men.
Biblical Significance
Shaalbon is known only as the home of one of David's warriors, a name preserved in the list of the mighty men.
Key Scriptures
2 Samuel 23:32; 1 Chronicles 11:33
