Territory of Zebulun

View on map →

The tribe takes its name from Zebulun, son of Jacob and Leah (Genesis 30:20; 49:13). The allotment of the tribe Zebulun, whose people profile is the Zebulun entry in Ancient Civilizations, in the Lower Galilee of the north, as described in general terms in Joshua 19:10-16. Its territory lay between Asher to the west, Naphtali to the north and Issachar to the south-east, reaching toward Mount Tabor; Joshua gives its boundary by neighbouring territories and named towns, not by a precise surveyed line, and no precise border is claimed here. None of the towns named in its Joshua allotment yet has a separate place entry on the site, so no key city is claimed here rather than naming one that is not an existing entry. In Judges, Zebulun is praised for risking its life in the battle against Sisera.

Biblical Significance

Zebulun's small Galilean allotment, tucked between larger neighbours, is remembered not for size but for courage: its people jeoparded their lives unto death in Deborah's battle.
