Armenia

Name
Armenia
Modern Location
Eastern Turkey and Armenia
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
The highland land to which Sennacherib's murderers fled, and a kingdom summoned against Babylon in Jeremiah.
Biblical Significance
Armenia is the refuge beyond the mountains, where even a fleeing king's sons disappear from the story.
Key Scriptures
2 Kings 19:37; Isaiah 37:38; Jeremiah 51:27
Related Books
2 Kings; Isaiah; Jeremiah
