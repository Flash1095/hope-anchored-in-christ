Name
River of Egypt
Modern Location
Wadi el-Arish, Egypt
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The southwestern boundary stream of the promised land, named in the covenant with Abram and in the borders of Numbers and Kings.
Biblical Significance
The River of Egypt is the promised land's southern edge.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 15:18; Numbers 34:5; 1 Kings 8:65
Related Books
