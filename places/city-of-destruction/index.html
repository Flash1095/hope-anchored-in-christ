City of Destruction

View on map →

One of the five cities in the land of Egypt that shall speak the language of Canaan and swear to the LORD of hosts in Isaiah's oracle, called the city of destruction, and in other translations the city of the sun.

Biblical Significance

The City of Destruction is Isaiah's startling promise for Egypt: a city of idols renamed by allegiance, speaking Canaan's tongue and swearing to the LORD.
