Dead Sea

View on map →

The salt sea at the lowest place on earth, also called the Salt Sea, bordering the cities of the plain and the wilderness of Judah.

Biblical Significance

The Dead Sea is the sea of judgment over the plain, and the border of the promised land's eastern side.
