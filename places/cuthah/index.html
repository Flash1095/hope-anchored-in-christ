Cuthah

Name
Cuthah
Modern Location
Tell Ibrahim, Iraq
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A Mesopotamian city whose people were settled in Samaria after Israel's deportation, and from whom the Samaritans were later called Cuthites by tradition.
Biblical Significance
Cuthah is the city whose settlers became Samaria's new people.
Key Scriptures
2 Kings 17:24; 2 Kings 17:30
Related Books
