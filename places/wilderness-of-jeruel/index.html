Wilderness of Jeruel

View on map →

The wilderness where Jehoshaphat's army was told to go down against the Ammonites and Moabites, at the cliff of Ziz, at the end of the brook before Jeruel.

Biblical Significance

Jeruel's wilderness is where Judah was told to stand still and see the salvation of the LORD.
