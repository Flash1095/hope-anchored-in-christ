Gibeah

Name
Gibeah
Modern Location
Near Jerusalem, West Bank (Tell el-Ful)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
King Saul's hometown and capital. It was also the scene of the horrific crime in Judges 19 that nearly wiped out the tribe of Benjamin.
Biblical Significance
Gibeah shows how far a people can fall - and that God still raises leaders from broken places. Saul's story began with such promise here.
Key Scriptures
Judges 19:22-30; 1 Samuel 10:26; 1 Samuel 11:4
Related Books
Judges; 1 Samuel
Related People
