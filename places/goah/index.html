Description
A landmark in Jeremiah's vision of Jerusalem's restored boundary, where the measuring line shall go forth over the hill Gareb and compass to Goah.
Biblical Significance
Goah is a boundary stone of the city to come: a small name at the far end of Jeremiah's measuring line for a restored Jerusalem.
