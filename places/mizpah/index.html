Mizpah

Name
Mizpah
Modern Location
Near Jerusalem, West Bank (Tell en-Nasbeh)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The assembly place where Samuel called Israel to repentance, put away their idols, and watched God thunder against the Philistines. Samuel set up the Ebenezer stone here: 'Thus far the Lord has helped us.'
Biblical Significance
Mizpah is the geography of return - repentance, prayer, and a stone of remembrance. 'Thus far' looks back with gratitude and forward with hope.
Key Scriptures
1 Samuel 7:5-12; Judges 20:1
Related Books
Judges; 1 Samuel
Related People
