Name
Fish Gate
Modern Location
Jerusalem
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A gate of Jerusalem built by the sons of Hassenaah in Nehemiah's wall, named in Manasseh's wall-building and in Zephaniah's cry, when the fish gate shall sound with howling.
Biblical Significance
The Fish Gate is a market gate turned to warning: built for trade, and named by Zephaniah as a place where judgment's cry shall be heard.
Key Scriptures
2 Chronicles 33:14; Nehemiah 3:3; Nehemiah 12:39; Zephaniah 1:10
Related Books
