En Gedi

Name
En Gedi
Modern Location
Ein Gedi, western Dead Sea shore, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
An oasis town above the Dead Sea where David cut Saul's robe in a cave and spared his life.
Biblical Significance
En Gedi is the desert spring where David proved his innocence, cutting a robe instead of a throat.
Key Scriptures
1 Samuel 23:29; 1 Samuel 24:1; Song of Solomon 1:14
Related Books
1 Samuel; Song of Solomon
Related People
King David; King Saul
