Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A boundary marker on Naphtali's coast, named in the border running 'from Allon to Zaanannim' toward the Jordan.
Biblical Significance
Allon is a border stone in words: one of the small names that fixed where Naphtali's inheritance began and ended.
