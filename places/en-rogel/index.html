En-rogel

View on map →

Name
En-rogel
Modern Location
Unknown; near Jerusalem, in the Kidron valley
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A spring near Jerusalem, on the Judah-Benjamin border, where Jonathan and Ahimaaz waited during Absalom's revolt, and where Adonijah later held his feast.
Biblical Significance
En-rogel is the spring of David's messengers in Absalom's revolt and of Adonijah's rival feast.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 15:7; 2 Samuel 17:17
Related Books
Joshua; 2 Samuel
Related People
Jonathan
