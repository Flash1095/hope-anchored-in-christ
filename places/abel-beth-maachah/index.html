Abel-beth-maachah

Name
Abel-beth-maachah
Modern Location
Tell Abil el-Qamh, northern Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A northern border city, called a mother in Israel, where the wise woman saved the city from Joab by delivering Sheba's head.
Biblical Significance
Abel-beth-maachah is where one wise woman's words stopped Joab's siege and saved a city.
Key Scriptures
2 Samuel 20:14; 1 Kings 15:20
Related Books
2 Samuel; 1 Kings
Related People
King David
