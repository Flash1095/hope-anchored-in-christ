Name
Senaah
Modern Location
Unknown; candidates include Khirbet el Aujah el Foqa and Khirbat al Beiyudat
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Description
Senaah, also called Hassenaah, was a town whose people are counted among those who returned from exile, and whose sons built the Fish Gate in Nehemiah's wall.
Biblical Significance
Senaah is remembered both in the return census and on the wall: a town that came back and helped rebuild Jerusalem.
Key Scriptures
Ezra 2:35; Nehemiah 3:3; Nehemiah 7:38
Related Books
Ezra; Nehemiah
