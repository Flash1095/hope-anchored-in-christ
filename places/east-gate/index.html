Name
East Gate
Modern Location
Jerusalem
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Description
A gate of Jerusalem named in Ezekiel's visions, where the glory of the God of Israel stood and departed, and in Jeremiah's and Nehemiah's accounts of the city wall.
Biblical Significance
The East Gate is the gate of glory's departure and return: the threshold where Ezekiel watched God's presence leave, and toward which it shall come back.
Key Scriptures
Ezekiel 10:19; Ezekiel 11:1; Ezekiel 40:44; Jeremiah 19:2; Nehemiah 3:29
Related Books
Ezekiel; Jeremiah; Nehemiah
