Arimathaea

View on map →

The hometown of Joseph, the wealthy disciple who asked Pilate for Jesus' body and laid it in his own new tomb.

Biblical Significance

Arimathaea gave the church the tomb: a rich man's grave, fulfilling prophecy, emptied on the third day.
