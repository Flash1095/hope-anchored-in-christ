Laishah

View on map →

A town in the path of the Assyrian advance on Jerusalem in Isaiah's oracle, called to cry out as the invader marched village by village toward the city.

Biblical Significance

Laishah is one of the frightened villages in Isaiah's map of invasion, a name cried out as an army came down the road.
