Edrei

Name
Edrei
Modern Location
Der'a, Bashan, Syria
Testament
Old

Exodus and Wilderness
Description
A royal city of Og king of Bashan, where Israel defeated him in battle before taking the land.
Biblical Significance
Edrei is where Og's last stand fell, and Bashan passed to Israel.

Numbers 21:33; Deuteronomy 3:1
