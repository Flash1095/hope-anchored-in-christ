Kadesh Barnea

Name
Kadesh Barnea
Modern Location
Northern Sinai / Negev border
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The wilderness camp where the twelve spies returned from Canaan. Ten brought fear, two brought faith - and Israel's unbelief turned an eleven-day journey into forty years of wandering.
Biblical Significance
Kadesh Barnea is the geography of almost. A whole generation stood at the edge of promise and turned back. Hope anchored in Christ refuses to let fear veto God's promise.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 13:26-33; Numbers 14:22-23; Hebrews 3:16-19
