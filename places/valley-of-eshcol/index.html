Valley of Eshcol

View on map →

The valley where the spies cut down a cluster of grapes so large that two men carried it on a staff, and from which the valley took its name, Eshcol, meaning cluster.

Biblical Significance

Eshcol is the valley of the giant grape cluster, the spies' proof that the land was fruitful.
