Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The desert around Mount Sinai where Israel camped after leaving Rephidim, where the LORD gave the Law, and where the people were numbered and ordered for the march to Canaan. This entry is distinct from the existing Mount Sinai place entry: the wilderness is the desert region of the encampment, not the mountain itself. No modern location is given here, because the location of Sinai itself is debated among scholars, so the wilderness around it cannot be fixed with confidence either.
Biblical Significance
The Wilderness of Sinai is where Israel stopped wandering and became a covenant nation. At Sinai they heard the Ten Commandments, received the pattern of the tabernacle, failed with the golden calf, and were forgiven and reordered. The census in Numbers was taken here, and from here the cloud lifted and the march began. Sinai is less a dot on a map than the place where a rescued people were given God's law and identity.
