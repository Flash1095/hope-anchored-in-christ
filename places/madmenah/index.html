Description
A town in the path of the Assyrian advance on Jerusalem in Isaiah's oracle, said to be 'removed' as the invader marched village by village toward the city.
Biblical Significance
Madmenah is one of the frightened villages in Isaiah's map of invasion, a place emptied before an army's approach.
