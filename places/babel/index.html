Babel

View on map →

The plain in Shinar where humanity built a city and a tower reaching to the heavens, defying God's command to fill the earth. God confused their language and scattered them across the world.

Biblical Significance

Babel shows what happens when people chase greatness without God: confusion and scattering. At Pentecost God reversed Babel, uniting every language in one gospel.
