Libya

View on map →

The North African land west of Egypt, called Lubim in Nahum and Chronicles. Libyans marched with Egypt and with Gog, and are named among the nations judged.

Biblical Significance

Libya marks the western edge of Egypt's world, and one more nation in the reach of judgment.
