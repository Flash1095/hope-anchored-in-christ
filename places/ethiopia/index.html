Ethiopia

Name
Ethiopia
Modern Location
Ethiopia and Sudan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
The African kingdom south of Egypt, called Cush in the genealogies. It appears in prophecies of judgment and of distant peoples coming to know God, and its borders marked the edge of the Persian empire.
Biblical Significance
Ethiopia reminds us that God's purposes reach to the ends of the earth, beyond every familiar border.
Key Scriptures
Isaiah 18:1; Ezekiel 29:10; Esther 1:1
Related Books
Isaiah; Ezekiel; Esther
