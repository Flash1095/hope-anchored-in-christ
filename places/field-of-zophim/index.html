Field of Zophim

View on map →

Name
Field of Zophim
Modern Location
Unknown; on Mount Pisgah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
A field on Mount Pisgah to which Balak brought Balaam, so that Balaam might see only part of Israel and curse them. From there Balaam spoke his second oracle instead.
Biblical Significance
Zophim's field is where Balaam's second blessing was spoken instead of a curse.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 23:14
