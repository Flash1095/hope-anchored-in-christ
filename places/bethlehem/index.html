Bethlehem

Name
Bethlehem
Modern Location
West Bank
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Description
The 'little town' of Ruth and Boaz, birthplace of David, and - as Micah foretold - birthplace of Jesus. Shepherds heard the angels here; wise men brought gifts here.
Biblical Significance
Bethlehem is God's pattern of choosing the small and overlooked. The Savior of the world arrived not in Rome but in a village - hope rarely looks the way we expect.
Key Scriptures
Ruth 4:11; Micah 5:2; Matthew 2:1-6; Luke 2:4-7
