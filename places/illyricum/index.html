Illyricum

Name
Illyricum
Modern Location
Western Balkans (Albania/Croatia region)
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Description
The province on the Adriatic's eastern shore, the far edge of Paul's preaching from Jerusalem round about.
Biblical Significance
Illyricum marks how far Paul's gospel had run by the time he wrote to Rome.
