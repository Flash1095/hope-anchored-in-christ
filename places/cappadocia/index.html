Cappadocia

Name
Cappadocia
Modern Location
Central Turkey
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Description
The high plateau province of central Asia Minor, whose people were at Pentecost and to whom Peter later wrote.
Biblical Significance
Cappadocia heard the gospel at Pentecost and received it again in Peter's letter.
Key Scriptures
Acts 2:9; 1 Peter 1:1
Related Books
Acts; 1 Peter
