Galilee

Name
Galilee
Modern Location
Northern Israel
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
The northern region - 'Galilee of the Gentiles' - where Jesus grew up, called His disciples, preached the Sermon on the Mount, and walked on water. After the resurrection He met them here again.
Biblical Significance
Galilee was the despised north, far from Jerusalem's power - exactly where Jesus chose to begin. God still starts His greatest works in overlooked places.
