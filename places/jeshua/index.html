Return and Second Temple
Description
A village where children of Judah dwelt after the return from exile, listed among the resettled towns from Kirjath-arba to the valley of Hinnom.
Biblical Significance
Jeshua is one of the small places that made the return real: not just Jerusalem rebuilt, but Judah's villages lived in again.
