Shinar

Name
Shinar
Modern Location
Southern Iraq
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The plain of southern Mesopotamia, the land of Babel and of Nimrod's first cities. Here men built the tower of Babel, and here later Judah's treasures were carried in exile.
Biblical Significance
Shinar is where human pride first organised itself against heaven, and where God scattered it.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:10; Genesis 11:2; Daniel 1:2
