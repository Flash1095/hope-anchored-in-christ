Name
Socoh in the Hill Country
Modern Location
Khirbet Shuweikah, in the hill country of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A town in Judah's hill country named in Joshua's allotment, distinct from the Socoh of the Elah Valley and the Socoh of Solomon's districts.
Biblical Significance
Socoh in the Hill Country is the hill-country namesake of the better-known Elah Valley town.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 15:48
