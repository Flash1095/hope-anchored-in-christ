Description
A town spoiled by Shalman in the day of battle, when the mother was dashed in pieces upon her children, which Hosea holds up as a warning of what Israel's own day would bring.
Biblical Significance
Beth-arbel is Hosea's dreadful example: a town's destruction remembered so that Israel might see its own danger and turn.
Key Scriptures
Hosea 10:14
