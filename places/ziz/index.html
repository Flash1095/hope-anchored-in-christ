Ziz

View on map →

The Ascent of Ziz, the Pass of Ziz, was the climb by which the armies of Moab, Ammon, and Mount Seir came up against Jehoshaphat, and which Jehoshaphat's singers met with praise.

Biblical Significance

The ascent is the approach road of Jehoshaphat's victory by praise: the climb the enemy took toward the wilderness of Tekoa.
