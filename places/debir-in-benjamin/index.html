Name
Debir in Benjamin
Modern Location
Disputed; Khan el Hatrur area, near Jericho
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A place named on Judah's northern border in Joshua 15:7, on the ascent from the Jordan valley near Gilgal.
Biblical Significance
It marks a border point between Judah and Benjamin, distinct from the Debir of the southern hill country.
