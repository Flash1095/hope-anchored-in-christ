Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A town in the valley of Lebanon under Mount Hermon, named as the northern limit of Joshua's conquests from the smooth mountain by Seir.
Biblical Significance
Baal-gad is the far north of Joshua's map: from the southern hills to Hermon's valley, the land was taken town by town.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 11:17; Joshua 12:7; Joshua 13:5
