Hazor in Benjamin

View on map →

A town named among the settlements of Benjamin where the returned exiles lived, listed with Ramah and Gittaim after the return from Babylon — distinct from the northern city of Hazor.

Biblical Significance

Nehemiah 11's list shows Hazor lived on as a Benjaminite village in the restoration period, not only as the ruined Canaanite capital.
