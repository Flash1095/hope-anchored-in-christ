Mamre

View on map →

The oaks and plains of Mamre near Hebron, where Abram settled, built an altar, and was visited by three men who announced Isaac's birth, and where he pleaded for Sodom.

Biblical Significance

Mamre is Abram's home in the land, the place of the visitors' promise and of his prayer for Sodom.
