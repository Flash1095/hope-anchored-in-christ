Aphek in Bashan

View on map →

The Aphek east of the Sea of Galilee where Israel fought Aram: Ben-hadad's army was defeated there under Ahab, and Joash later struck the ground there before defeating Aram.

Biblical Significance

It is the battlefield of two Aramean wars, showing God's deliverance of Israel from a stronger Aramean army.
