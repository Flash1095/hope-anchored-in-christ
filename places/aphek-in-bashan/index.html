Aphek in Bashan

View on map →

Name
Aphek in Bashan
Modern Location
Disputed; Tel Ein Gev or Fiq, Golan Heights
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
The Aphek east of the Sea of Galilee where Israel fought Aram: Ben-hadad's army was defeated there under Ahab, and Joash later struck the ground there before defeating Aram.
Biblical Significance
It is the battlefield of two Aramean wars, showing God's deliverance of Israel from a stronger Aramean army.
Key Scriptures
1 Kings 20:26; 1 Kings 20:30; 2 Kings 13:17
Related Books
1 Kings; 2 Kings
Related People
King Ahab
