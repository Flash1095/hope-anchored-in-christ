Beth-le-aphrah

View on map →

The 'house of Aphrah' in Micah's lament, told to roll in the dust, in a wordplay on Aphrah and dust.

Biblical Significance

Beth-le-aphrah is Micah's wordplay town: a house of dust told to roll in dust as judgment comes.
