Name
Mearah
Modern Location
Disputed; proposed sites include Jezzine and Khirbet 'Ara
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A place beside the Sidonians named among the land still unconquered in Joshua's old age, from the south, all the land of the Canaanites, and Mearah by Sidon.
Biblical Significance
Mearah marks the unfinished edge of the conquest: land promised but not yet taken when Joshua was old.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 13:4
