Shittim

Name
Shittim
Modern Location
Unknown; traditionally associated with the Tell el-Hammam area, Jordan Valley, Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The last camp of Israel east of the Jordan, where Israel sinned with Moab and from which Joshua sent the spies to Jericho.
Biblical Significance
Shittim is the camp of the last temptation before the crossing, and the launching place of the conquest.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 25:1; Joshua 2:1; Joshua 3:1
Related Books
Numbers; Joshua
Related People
Moses; Joshua
