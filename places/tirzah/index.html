Tirzah

Name
Tirzah
Modern Location
Tell el-Farah, Samaria hills, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
An early capital of the northern kingdom, praised for beauty in the Song of Solomon, seat of kings from Jeroboam to Omri.
Biblical Significance
Tirzah was Israel's first royal city after the division, beautiful and brief, before Omri moved the capital to Samaria.
Key Scriptures
1 Kings 14:17; 1 Kings 16:8; Song of Solomon 6:4
Related Books
1 Kings; Song of Solomon
