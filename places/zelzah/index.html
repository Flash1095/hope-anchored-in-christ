Zelzah

View on map →

Zelzah was a place on the border of Benjamin, at Rachel's tomb, where Samuel told Saul he would find two men who would tell him the donkeys were found.

Biblical Significance

Zelzah is one of Samuel's signs to Saul: the second sign on the road to his anointing, at Rachel's sepulchre.
