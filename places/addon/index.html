Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Description
A place in Babylonia named among the returnees who came up with Zerubbabel but could not prove their father's house or their descent from Israel.
Biblical Significance
Addon is a reminder that return from exile was tested by record: those who could not show their ancestry were kept from the priesthood until a priest with Urim and Thummim should arise.
Key Scriptures
Ezra 2:59; Nehemiah 7:61
Related Books
Ezra; Nehemiah
