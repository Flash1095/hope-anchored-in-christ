Description
The field near Gibeon where twelve of Abner's men and twelve of Joab's men fought in single combat at the pool, a contest that broke into a bitter battle; the place was called Helkath-hazzurim, the Field of Swords.
Biblical Significance
Helkath-hazzurim is where Israel's civil war turned personal: a champion contest meant to settle the kingdom became a day of hard fighting instead.
