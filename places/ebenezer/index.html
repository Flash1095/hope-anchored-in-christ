Name
Ebenezer
Modern Location
Izbet Sartah, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The place where Israel camped against the Philistines before the battle in which the ark was captured, in 1 Samuel 4:1, and past which the Philistines' route ran in 1 Samuel 5:1.
Biblical Significance
It is the campsite of Israel's defeat and the ark's capture, the disaster that the later Ebenezer stone would answer with remembrance.
Key Scriptures
1 Samuel 4:1; 1 Samuel 5:1
