Testament

Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The hill beside Timnath-heres, on whose north side Joshua was buried, and whose brooks gave their name to warriors among David's mighty men.
Biblical Significance
Gaash is Joshua's burial hill: the mountain beside his inheritance town, and the brooks that named his later soldiers.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 24:30; Judges 2:9; 2 Samuel 23:30; 1 Chronicles 11:32
