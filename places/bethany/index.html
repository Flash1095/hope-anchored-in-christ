Name
Bethany
Modern Location

Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
The village home of Mary, Martha, and Lazarus - Jesus's retreat two miles from Jerusalem. Here He raised Lazarus, was anointed for burial, and ascended from nearby.
Biblical Significance
Bethany was where Jesus was loved as a friend, not just followed as a teacher. Sitting at His feet, like Mary, is still the one necessary thing.
Key Scriptures
