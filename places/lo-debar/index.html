Lo-debar

View on map →

Name
Lo-debar
Modern Location
Disputed; proposed sites include Tel Dover and Umm ed Debar
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
The town in Gilead where Mephibosheth, Jonathan's lame son, lived in the house of Machir until David sent for him and restored Saul's land to him; Machir of Lo-debar later supplied David in his flight from Absalom.
Biblical Significance
Lo-debar is where David's kindness reached: a forgotten prince in an out-of-the-way town, brought to eat at the king's table continually.
Key Scriptures
2 Samuel 9:4-5; 2 Samuel 17:27
Related Books
2 Samuel
Related People
Mephibosheth, Son of Jonathan; King David
