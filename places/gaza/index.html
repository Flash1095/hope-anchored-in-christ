Gaza

Name
Gaza
Modern Location
Gaza, Gaza Strip
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A Philistine royal city on the coastal road to Egypt, where Samson carried off the city gates and later died pulling down Dagon's temple.
Biblical Significance
Gaza is Samson's city of triumph and death, and the southern gate of Canaan on the way to Egypt.
Key Scriptures
Judges 1:18; Judges 16:1; Acts 8:26
Related Books
Judges; Acts
Related People
Samson; Philip
