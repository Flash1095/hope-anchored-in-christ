Shaphir

View on map →

Shaphir was a town named in Micah's lament over the towns of the Shephelah, told that its inhabitants would go out in nakedness and shame.

Biblical Significance

Shaphir's name, meaning fair or beautiful, is turned against it in Micah's wordplay: the fair town would be exposed.
