Name
Garden of Eden
Modern Location
Unknown; traditionally associated with Mesopotamia
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The garden God planted as humanity's first home, where Adam and Eve walked with Him. Four rivers watered it, and at its center stood the tree of life and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.
Biblical Significance
Eden is where humanity's story begins - and where it broke. Yet even in the curse, God promised a coming offspring who would crush the serpent: the first whisper of the hope this blog is named for.
