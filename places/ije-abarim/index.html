Ije-abarim

View on map →

One of the stations on Israel's wilderness journey, named in the Numbers 33 itinerary. The ruins of Abarim on Moab's border, where Israel camped approaching the promised land. No further detail is recorded beyond the journey account.

Biblical Significance

The wilderness stations trace the route God led Israel from Egypt to the Plains of Moab.
