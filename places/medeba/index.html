Medeba

Name
Medeba
Modern Location
Madaba, Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A Moabite city on the plateau, named in the conquest lists and in the oracles against Moab, and the scene of Ammon's muster against David.
Biblical Significance
Medeba is the plateau city where Ammon mustered against David.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 21:30; Joshua 13:9; Isaiah 15:2; 1 Chronicles 19:7
Related Books
Numbers; Joshua; Isaiah; 1 Chronicles
Related People
King David
