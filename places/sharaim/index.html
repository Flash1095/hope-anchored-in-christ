Description
Sharaim was a town in Judah's lowland (Shephelah), named in the town list of Joshua 15. No further story is attached to it in Scripture, and its exact site is unidentified.
Biblical Significance
Its inclusion in the allotment list marks it as one of the towns assigned to Judah.
