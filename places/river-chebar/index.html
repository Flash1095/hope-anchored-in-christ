River Chebar

Name
River Chebar
Modern Location
Southern Iraq
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Description
The canal in Babylonia where the exiles settled - and where Ezekiel, among the captives, saw the heavens opened and the glory of God in visions.
Biblical Significance
By the Chebar, God proved He was not confined to Jerusalem's temple. His glory travels with His people, even into exile.
Key Scriptures
Ezekiel 1:1-3; Ezekiel 3:15; Psalm 137:1
