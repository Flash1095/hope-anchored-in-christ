Baal-zephon

View on map →

Name
Baal-zephon
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The Exodus station named as the landmark opposite which Israel camped before Pi-hahiroth, between Migdol and the sea, before the Red Sea crossing. The Egyptians overtook Israel encamped there by the sea. The location of Baal-zephon is unknown, and no modern site is claimed for it here.
Biblical Significance
Baal-zephon marks the place where Israel, camped with the sea before them and Pharaoh's army behind them, waited for the deliverance only God could bring.

Exodus 14:2; Exodus 14:9; Numbers 33:7
