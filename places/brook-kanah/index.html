Brook Kanah

Conquest and Judges
Description
The brook that formed part of the boundary between the tribes of Ephraim and Manasseh in the division of the land west of the Jordan. Joshua 16 describes it on Ephraim's border, and Joshua 17 describes it again on the border between Ephraim to the south and Manasseh to the north. Wadi Qana is the standard identification of the brook, stated here as such.
Biblical Significance
Kanah matters because boundaries mattered. In a land given by lot and inheritance, a brook line between brother tribes kept each family's portion clear and kept peace between Ephraim and Manasseh, the two sons of Joseph. The brook is a reminder that the conquest ended not only in battles won but in an ordered, shared inheritance.
