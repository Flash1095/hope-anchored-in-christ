Conquest and Judges
Description
The tribe takes its name from Simeon, son of Jacob and Leah (Genesis 29:33; 49:5-7). The allotment of the tribe Simeon, whose people profile is the Simeon entry in Ancient Civilizations, lying inside Judah's larger allotment in the south, as described in general terms in Joshua 19:1-9. Joshua states expressly that Simeon's inheritance was taken from within Judah's portion, because Judah's share was too large for it; no precise border is claimed here, since Joshua gives the allotment by named towns within Judah's territory. Its key city among existing place entries is Beersheba, in the southern Negev. In Judges, Simeon joined with Judah in the early fighting to take possession of the land.
Biblical Significance
Simeon is the tribe without a territory of its own apart from Judah: its inheritance inside another tribe's land foreshadows its gradual absorption into Judah in later history.
