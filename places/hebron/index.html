Description
One of the oldest continuously inhabited cities on earth, where Abraham bought the cave of Machpelah as a burial place for Sarah - his first owned parcel of the promised land. David was anointed king here before taking Jerusalem.
Biblical Significance
Hebron holds the tombs of the patriarchs: a city of burial that speaks of promise. Abraham owned only a grave there, yet believed his descendants would own it all.
