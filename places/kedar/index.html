Kedar

Name
Kedar
Modern Location
Northern Arabia
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A nomadic Arabian land named for Ishmael's son, known for its flocks, tents of black goat hair, and archers. Isaiah foretells the fall of Kedar's glory within a year.
Biblical Significance
Kedar stands for the desert peoples who live beyond the reach of cities, yet within the reach of prophecy.
Key Scriptures
Isaiah 21:16; Isaiah 42:11; Jeremiah 49:28
Related Books
Isaiah; Jeremiah
