Migdol

Name
Migdol
Modern Location
Unknown; on the west shore of the Red Sea, Egypt
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
One of the stations on Israel's wilderness journey, named in the Numbers 33 itinerary. A landmark named beside Pi-hahiroth in the crossing account; the same name later marks an Egyptian frontier town. No further detail is recorded beyond the journey account.
Biblical Significance
The wilderness stations trace the route God led Israel from Egypt to the Plains of Moab.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 33:7; Exodus 14:2
Related Books
Numbers; Exodus
Related People
Moses
