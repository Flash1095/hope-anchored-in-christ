Description
Sibraim was a town on the northern boundary of the land in Ezekiel's vision of the restored allotment, named between the border of Damascus and the border of Hamath.
Biblical Significance
Sibraim belongs to the ideal boundary of the restored land: a northern marker in Ezekiel's vision of Israel re-divided.
Key Scriptures
Ezekiel 47:16
