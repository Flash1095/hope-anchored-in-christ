Gebal

Name
Gebal
Modern Location
Byblos, Lebanon
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
The ancient Phoenician port, called Byblos by the Greeks, whose skilled caulkers worked on Tyre's ships and whose land belonged to the unconquered coast.
Biblical Significance
Gebal is the old port whose craftsmen served the ships of Tyre.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 13:5; Psalms 83:7; Ezekiel 27:9
Related Books
Joshua; Psalms; Ezekiel
