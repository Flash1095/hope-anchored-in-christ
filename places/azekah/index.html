Azekah

Name
Azekah
Modern Location
Tel Azekah, Elah Valley, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A Judahite fortress town in the Elah Valley, on the Philistine border, besieged by Nebuchadnezzar among Judah's last strongholds.
Biblical Significance
Azekah guarded the Elah Valley road, and its name appears in Jeremiah's account of Judah's final days.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 15:35; 1 Samuel 17:1; Jeremiah 34:7
Related Books
Joshua; 1 Samuel; Jeremiah
Related People
King David; Jeremiah
