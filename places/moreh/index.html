Moreh

View on map →

The plain and hill of Moreh near Shechem, where Abram first camped in the land and built an altar, and where Gideon later camped by the well of Harod opposite the Midianites.

Biblical Significance

Moreh spans the patriarchs and the judges: Abram's first altar in the land, and Gideon's camp before the Midianite victory.
