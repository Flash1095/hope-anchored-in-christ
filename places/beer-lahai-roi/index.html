Beer Lahai Roi

View on map →

Name
Beer Lahai Roi
Modern Location
Unknown; in the Negev between Kadesh and Bered
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The well where the angel of the Lord found Hagar, named "the well of him that liveth and seeth me," later Isaac's dwelling place.
Biblical Significance
Beer Lahai Roi is where God saw Hagar in her affliction, the first well named for God seeing the unseen.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 16:14; Genesis 25:11
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Isaac
