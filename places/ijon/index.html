Ijon

View on map →

A city in Naphtali that Ben-hadad of Syria struck at Asa's request, and that Tiglath-pileser of Assyria later captured and carried into exile with the rest of Naphtali.

Biblical Significance

Ijon is a northern city struck by Syria and then carried off by Assyria.
