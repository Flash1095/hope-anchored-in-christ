Name
Gethsemane
Modern Location

Testament

Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
The olive grove across the Kidron where Jesus prayed in agony on the night of His betrayal.
Biblical Significance
Gethsemane is where Jesus submitted to the Father's will, sweating as it were great drops of blood.
