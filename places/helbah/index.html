Conquest and Judges
Description
A town in Asher's allotment that Asher failed to take, listed among the unconquered coastal towns alongside Ahlab, Accho, Zidon and Achzib. The same site has also been proposed for Ahlab, so this identification is not certain.
Biblical Significance
Helbah is one of the places where Israel's conquest stopped short, a reminder that the allotment on paper and the land in hand were not yet the same.
