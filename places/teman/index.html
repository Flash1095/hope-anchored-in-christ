Teman

Name
Teman
Modern Location
Southern Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A region and city of Edom famed for wisdom, named in the oracles against Edom and as the place from which God comes in Habakkuk.
Biblical Significance
Teman is Edom's wise region, whose wisdom could not save it.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 36:11; Jeremiah 49:7; Habakkuk 3:3
Related Books
Genesis; Jeremiah; Habakkuk
