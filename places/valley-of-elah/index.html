Valley of Elah

Name
Valley of Elah
Modern Location
Wadi es-Sunt, Shephelah, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
The valley between Socoh and Azekah where the armies of Israel and Philistia faced each other and David slew Goliath.
Biblical Significance
Elah is the valley of the giant's challenge, where a shepherd boy's stone ended forty days of defiance.
Key Scriptures
1 Samuel 17:2; 1 Samuel 17:49
