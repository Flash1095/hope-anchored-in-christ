Elam

View on map →

Name
Elam
Modern Location
Southwestern Iran
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Description
An ancient kingdom east of Babylon, with its capital at Susa. Elamites were among the peoples settled in Samaria, Elam is judged in Jeremiah, and Daniel saw his vision of the ram and the goat by the river Ulai in Elam.
Biblical Significance
Elam stands among the nations God judges and yet names among those who will one day be gathered.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 14:1; Isaiah 21:2; Jeremiah 49:34; Daniel 8:2
Related Books
Genesis; Isaiah; Jeremiah; Daniel
