Bethsaida

Name
Bethsaida
Modern Location
El-Araj or et-Tell, northern shore of the Sea of Galilee (debated)
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
A fishing town on the Sea of Galilee, home of Philip, Andrew, and Peter, where Jesus healed a blind man and fed the five thousand nearby.
Biblical Significance
Bethsaida heard much of Jesus' teaching and saw His miracles, yet He pronounced woe on it for unbelief.
Key Scriptures
Matthew 11:21; Mark 8:22; Luke 9:10
Related Books
Matthew; Mark; Luke
