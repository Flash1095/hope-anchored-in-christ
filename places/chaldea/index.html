Chaldea

View on map →

The land of the Chaldeans, the ruling people of Neo-Babylon under Nebuchadnezzar. Its armies destroyed Jerusalem, and its learning and astrology framed Daniel's exile.

Biblical Significance

Chaldea is the power God used to chasten Judah, and the power whose pride He then brought down.
