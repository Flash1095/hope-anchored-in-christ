Land of Nod

Name
Land of Nod
Modern Location
Unknown; east of Eden
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The land east of Eden to which Cain went after murdering Abel, where he built a city and his line began.
Biblical Significance
Nod is the land of wandering: the first exile, carrying the mark of Cain and the beginning of city-building apart from God.

Genesis 4:16
