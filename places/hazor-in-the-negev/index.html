Hazor in the Negev

View on map →

Name
Hazor in the Negev
Modern Location
El Jebariyeh, Negev, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A town named in Judah's southern town list, between Kedesh and Ithnan, in the Negev allotment — a different Hazor from the great northern city Joshua burned.
Biblical Significance
Its only appearance is the allotment list of Joshua 15; it shows how the same town name recurs in Judah's south, far from the northern Hazor of the conquest wars.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 15:23
