Caphtor

Name
Caphtor
Modern Location
Unknown; traditionally Crete
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The homeland from which the Philistines came, named in Deuteronomy, Jeremiah, and Amos. Traditionally identified with Crete.
Biblical Significance
Caphtor reminds us that even Israel's fiercest neighbours had an origin and a history God knew.
Key Scriptures
Deuteronomy 2:23; Jeremiah 47:4; Amos 9:7
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Jeremiah; Amos
