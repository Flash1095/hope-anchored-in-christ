Pool of Siloam

Name
Pool of Siloam
Modern Location
Jerusalem, south end of the City of David
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
The pool fed by Hezekiah's tunnel, called Shiloah in Isaiah and Nehemiah; Jesus sent the man born blind to wash there, and he came back seeing.
Biblical Significance
Siloam links Hezekiah's waterworks to Jesus' sign: the sent one gives sight to the blind.
Key Scriptures
John 9:7; John 9:11
Related Books
John
Related People
Jesus
