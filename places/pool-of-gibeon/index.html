Modern Location

Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
The pool at Gibeon where the servants of David under Joab and the servants of Ish-bosheth under Abner met, and where twelve champions from each side fought and fell together, beginning the long war between the houses of Saul and David. A finds entry also covers the excavated pool; this is the geographic place.
Biblical Significance
Gibeon's pool is where the war between Saul's house and David's house began in single combat.
Key Scriptures
2 Samuel 2:13; Jeremiah 41:12
Related Books
2 Samuel; Jeremiah
Related People
Joab, Son of Zeruiah; Abner, Son of Ner; Asahel, Son of Zeruiah
