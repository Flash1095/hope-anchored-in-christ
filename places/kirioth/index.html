Description
A Moabite city named in the oracles of judgment against Moab: Jeremiah lists it among the cities of Moab, far and near, declares that Kerioth is taken, and Amos warns that fire will devour its palaces. The KJV spells the name Kerioth in these passages. Its location is uncertain, and no modern site is claimed for it here. It is distinct from Kerioth in Judah (Joshua 15:25), a minor town in the south of Judah that is not the Moabite city of these oracles.
Biblical Significance
Kirioth stands among the proud cities of Moab that the prophets warned would fall, a reminder that no city is beyond God's judgment.
Key Scriptures
Jeremiah 48:24; Jeremiah 48:41; Amos 2:2
Related Books
