Description
Rogelim in Gilead was the home of Barzillai, who brought supplies to David when David fled from Absalom and was crossing the Jordan.
Biblical Significance
Rogelim is remembered through Barzillai's loyalty: food, beds, and vessels for a fugitive king and his people in their need.
Key Scriptures
2 Samuel 17:27; 2 Samuel 19:31
