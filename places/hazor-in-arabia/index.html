Hazor in Arabia

View on map →

The kingdoms of Hazor named in Jeremiah's oracle against Kedar and the men of the east, whom Nebuchadnezzar would smite — a desert Hazor of nomad kingdoms, not the city in the north.

Biblical Significance

Jeremiah pairs Hazor with Kedar as the settled and nomad east facing Babylon's judgment; its kingdoms and unwalled villages mark it as Arabian, far from Galilee.
