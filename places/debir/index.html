Debir

Name
Debir
Modern Location
Khirbet Rabud, Judean hills, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A Canaanite royal city, also called Kirjath-sepher, taken by Othniel, who won Caleb's daughter Achsah for it.
Biblical Significance
Debir is the city of the book, taken by Othniel for love of Achsah, and a Levitical city in Judah.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 10:38; Joshua 15:15; Judges 1:11
Related Books
Joshua; Judges
