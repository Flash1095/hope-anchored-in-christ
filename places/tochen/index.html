Description
Tochen was a town in Simeon's allotment, named in the town list of 1 Chronicles 4 with Ain, Rimmon, Ether, and Ashan.
Biblical Significance
Its inclusion in the Simeonite list marks it as one of the towns assigned within Judah's larger territory to Simeon.
