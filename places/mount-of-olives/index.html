Mount of Olives

Name
Mount of Olives
Modern Location
East of Jerusalem
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
The ridge east of Jerusalem, named in David's flight and in Zechariah's prophecy, where Jesus taught, prayed, and ascended.
Biblical Significance
The Mount of Olives frames the end of Jesus' ministry: His teaching, His agony, and His ascension to heaven.
Key Scriptures
Matthew 24:3; Matthew 26:30; Acts 1:12; Zechariah 14:4
Related Books
Matthew; Acts; Zechariah
Related People
Jesus
Related Events
The Ascension; Triumphal Entry; Jesus Prays in Gethsemane
