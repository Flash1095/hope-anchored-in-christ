Description
The hill north of Gideon's camp by the well of Harod, where the Midianite host lay in the valley below — the landmark of Gideon's night battle, distinct from the oak of Moreh at Shechem where Abram camped.
Biblical Significance
The hill of Moreh sets the scene of Gideon's victory: Israel camped by the spring, Midian by the hill, and the LORD's reduction of the army happened between them.
