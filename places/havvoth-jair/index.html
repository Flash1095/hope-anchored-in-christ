Havvoth-jair

View on map →

Name
Havvoth-jair
Modern Location
Region of Qamm, Bashan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The towns of Jair: the villages Jair son of Manasseh took in Gilead and Bashan and called after his own name, later counted among the towns of the judge Jair the Gileadite and listed in Solomon's districts.
Biblical Significance
Havvoth-jair shows a family name becoming geography: one man's conquests turned into a lasting region on Israel's eastern frontier.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 32:41; Deuteronomy 3:14; Joshua 13:30; Judges 10:4; 1 Kings 4:13; 1 Chronicles 2:23

Related People
