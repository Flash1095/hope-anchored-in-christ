Tahpanhes

Name
Tahpanhes
Modern Location
Tell Defenneh, Egypt
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
The Egyptian border fortress where Jeremiah was carried after Jerusalem's fall, and where he buried stones and foretold Nebuchadnezzar's throne set over Egypt.
Biblical Significance
Tahpanhes is where the prophet was dragged in disobedience, and still prophesied.
Key Scriptures
Jeremiah 43:7; Jeremiah 44:1; Ezekiel 30:18
Related Books
Jeremiah; Ezekiel
Related People
Jeremiah
