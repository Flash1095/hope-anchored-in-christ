Ephes-dammim

View on map →

Name

Modern Location
Khirbet Damun
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
The place where the Philistines gathered between Shochoh and Azekah, and Saul and Israel pitched by the valley of Elah, where David met Goliath; called Pas-dammim in Chronicles, where Eleazar stood against the Philistines.
Biblical Significance
Ephes-dammim is the battlefield of David and Goliath: two armies on two hills, and a shepherd boy walking into the valley between them.
Key Scriptures
1 Samuel 17:1; 1 Chronicles 11:13
