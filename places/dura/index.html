Description
The plain in Babylon where Nebuchadnezzar set up his golden image, and where Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego refused to bow and walked from the furnace unharmed.
Biblical Significance
Dura is the plain of the golden image, and of the fourth Man in the fire.
