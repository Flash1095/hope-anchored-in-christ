Lower Pool

View on map →

A pool in Jerusalem named in Isaiah's oracle about the city's siege preparations, when Hezekiah's people repaired the wall and gathered the waters of the lower pool.

Biblical Significance

The Lower Pool is one of the waters Jerusalem counted on when an army came: a reservoir made part of the city's defence.
