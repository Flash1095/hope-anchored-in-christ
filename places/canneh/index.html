Canneh

View on map →

A trading partner named with Haran and Eden among the merchants of Tyre, who traded choice wares, blue clothes and rich apparel bound with cords.

Biblical Significance

Canneh is a merchant name in Tyre's market list: a distant trading city whose wealth flowed into Tyre before Tyre fell.
