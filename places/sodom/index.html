Sodom

Name
Sodom
Modern Location
Unknown; traditionally associated with the southern Dead Sea region
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The chief city of the plain where Lot settled, notorious for its wickedness, destroyed by fire from heaven.
Biblical Significance
Sodom is the Bible's standing warning of judgment, and the backdrop of Abraham's intercession for the righteous.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 13:10; Genesis 18:20; Genesis 19:24; Matthew 10:15; Matthew 11:23
Related Books
Genesis; Matthew
Related People
Abraham; Lot; Jesus
Related Events
Destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah
