Libnah in the Wilderness

View on map →

Name
Libnah in the Wilderness
Modern Location
Unknown; a wilderness station, possibly along Wadi el Beidha
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
A station on Israel's wilderness journey, pitched after Rimmon-parez and before Rissah, on the numbered itinerary of Numbers 33 — a different Libnah from the Levitical city in Judah's Shephelah.
Biblical Significance
Libnah's place in the itinerary marks one night's camp of the wilderness generation; its name is shared with a later Judahite city, but its setting is the desert march.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 33:20; Numbers 33:21
