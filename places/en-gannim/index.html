En-gannim

View on map →

The name belongs to two towns: one in Judah's lowland and one in Issachar's allotment. Both appear only in the allotment lists, and neither site is identified.

Biblical Significance

En-gannim, spring of gardens, is shared by a Judah town and an Issachar town.
