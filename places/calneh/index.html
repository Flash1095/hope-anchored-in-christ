Timeline Era

Description
One of Nimrod's first cities in Shinar, later named by Amos among the great cities that fell.
Biblical Significance
Calneh, great in Nimrod's day and a warning in Amos's, shows that great cities fall.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:10; Amos 6:2
