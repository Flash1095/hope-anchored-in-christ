Name

Modern Location
Mount of Megiddo, Jezreel Valley
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Description
The place called in the Hebrew tongue Armageddon, where the kings of the earth are gathered for the battle of the great day of God Almighty; commonly identified with Megiddo, though the passage also carries a symbolic reading.
Biblical Significance
Armageddon is the gathering place of the last battle in Revelation: every earthly power assembled, and the outcome already declared by the One who comes as a thief.
Key Scriptures
Revelation 16:16
