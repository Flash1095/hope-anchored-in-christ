Description
Topheth in the Valley of Hinnom was the place where children were passed through the fire to Molech, which Josiah defiled so it could no longer be used, and which Jeremiah proclaimed would become a valley of slaughter.
Biblical Significance
Topheth is Scripture's picture of child sacrifice and judgment: defiled by Josiah, and renamed in prophecy as the valley of slaughter.
