Conquest and Judges
Description
A Canaanite royal city whose king joined the alliance against Gibeon and was hanged by Joshua at Makkedah.
Biblical Significance
Eglon is one of the five Amorite kings defeated at Gibeon, whose defeat opened the south to Israel.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 10:5; Joshua 10:26
