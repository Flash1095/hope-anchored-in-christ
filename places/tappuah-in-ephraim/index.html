Tappuah in Ephraim

View on map →

Name
Tappuah in Ephraim
Modern Location
Tell Abu Zarad, on the Ephraim-Manasseh border
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A town on the border between Ephraim and Manasseh, named in Joshua's allotment and in the list of kings defeated by Israel; distinct from the Tappuah of Judah's lowland.
Biblical Significance
Tappuah in Ephraim stood on the tribal border, a former Canaanite royal city taken into the allotments.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 12:17; Joshua 16:8; Joshua 17:8
Related Books
Joshua
