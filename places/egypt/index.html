Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The Nile civilization where Joseph rose from prisoner to prime minister, where Israel multiplied into a nation - and where Pharaoh enslaved them for 400 years.
Biblical Significance
Egypt is Scripture's great picture of bondage - and of deliverance. The Exodus became Israel's defining story: our God hears, He comes down, and He brings out. Every redemption since echoes it.
