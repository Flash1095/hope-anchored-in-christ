Nebo

View on map →

A Moabite city named in the conquest lists and in the oracles against Moab. It is distinct from Mount Nebo, from which Moses viewed Canaan.

Biblical Significance

Nebo the city must not be confused with Nebo the mountain: Scripture names both.
