Description
The mountain land north of Israel, famed for its cedars. Its timber built Solomon's temple and palace, and its heights are a byword for majesty in the prophets and the Song.
Biblical Significance
Lebanon's cedars became God's house, a picture of glory cut down and raised for His dwelling.
Key Scriptures
Deuteronomy 3:25; 1 Kings 5:6; Song of Solomon 4:8
