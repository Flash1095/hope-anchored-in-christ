Name
Forest of Ephraim
Modern Location
Ajloun Forest Reserve area, Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
The forest east of the Jordan where Absalom's army was defeated in 2 Samuel 18:6, and where Absalom himself was caught and killed.
Biblical Significance
The forest itself devoured more men than the sword, and it became the place where Absalom's rebellion ended.
