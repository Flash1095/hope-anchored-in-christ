Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
Rumah was the home of Pedaiah, whose daughter Zebudah was the mother of King Jehoiakim of Judah.
Biblical Significance
Rumah appears only as a family home in the royal genealogy, a reminder that the kings of Judah came from named towns and families.
Key Scriptures
2 Kings 23:36
