Wilderness of Paran

View on map →

Name
Wilderness of Paran
Modern Location
Desert south of Canaan, modern Negev-Sinai region
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The great desert where Ishmael grew up and where Israel camped after leaving Sinai, sending the spies into Canaan from its edge.
Biblical Significance
Paran is the wilderness of waiting and failure: from here the spies went up, and here the people refused to enter the land.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 21:21; Numbers 10:12; Numbers 13:3
Related Books
Genesis; Numbers
Related People
Moses
