Ashdod

Name
Ashdod
Modern Location
Ashdod, coastal Israel
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A Philistine royal city where the captured ark toppled Dagon, and a port city denounced by the prophets. Called Azotus in the New Testament.
Biblical Significance
Ashdod is where Dagon fell face down before the ark, and where the ark's presence broke the city's idol.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 11:22; 1 Samuel 5:1; Amos 1:8; Acts 8:40
Related Books
Joshua; 1 Samuel; Amos; Acts
Related People
Samuel; Philip
