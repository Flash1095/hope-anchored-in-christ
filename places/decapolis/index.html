Decapolis

View on map →

Name
Decapolis
Modern Location
East of the Jordan and the Sea of Galilee, Jordan/Syria
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
A league of ten largely Gentile cities east of Galilee, where Jesus healed the demoniac and the deaf man.
Biblical Significance
The Decapolis shows Jesus' ministry reaching beyond Jewish territory, and its people spread His fame abroad.
Key Scriptures
Matthew 4:25; Mark 5:20; Mark 7:31
Related Books
Matthew; Mark
Related People
