Description
A town in Solomon's district under the son of Hesed, to whom pertained Sochoh and all the land of Hepher.
Biblical Significance
Arubboth is one of the twelve districts that fed Solomon's household month by month: ordinary towns carrying the weight of a kingdom's table.
