Sidon

Name
Sidon
Modern Location
Sidon, Lebanon
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
The ancient Phoenician seaport, called Zidon in the King James Version. Mother city of Tyre's trade, home of Jezebel, and a city named in the prophets' oracles and in Jesus' teaching.
Biblical Significance
Sidon, older than Tyre and judged beside it, heard Jesus say that Tyre and Sidon would have repented at His works.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:15; Judges 1:31; 1 Kings 5:6; Ezekiel 27:8; Matthew 15:21; Acts 27:3
