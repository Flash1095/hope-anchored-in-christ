Testament

Description
A river of Egypt named as a border of the promised land and as the waters of Egypt in Jeremiah's rebuke.
Biblical Significance
Sihor is Egypt's dark water, named at the border and in rebuke.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 13:3; Isaiah 23:3; Jeremiah 2:18
