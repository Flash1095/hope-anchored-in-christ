Pool of Bethesda

Name
Pool of Bethesda
Modern Location
Jerusalem, near the Sheep Gate
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
A pool in Jerusalem surrounded by five porches, where the sick waited for the stirring of the water; Jesus healed a man there who had been infirm thirty-eight years.
Biblical Significance
Bethesda shows Jesus healing on the Sabbath and claiming authority over the law's own holy day.
Key Scriptures
John 5:2
