Name

Modern Location
Al-Karak, Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A chief city of Moab, also called Kir-haresh and Kirheres, besieged by Israel and Judah in 2 Kings 3 and lamented in Isaiah and Jeremiah.
Biblical Significance
Kir-hareseth is Moab's fortress city, besieged and lamented.
Key Scriptures
2 Kings 3:25; Isaiah 16:7; Jeremiah 48:31
Related Books
2 Kings; Isaiah; Jeremiah
