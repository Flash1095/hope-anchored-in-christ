Seleucia

View on map →

Name
Seleucia
Modern Location
Seleucia Pieria, near Samandağ, Turkey
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Description
Seleucia was the port from which Paul and Barnabas sailed to Cyprus at the start of the first missionary journey, sent out by the church at Antioch.
Biblical Significance
Seleucia is the departure point of the church's first sent mission: the harbour where the journey to Cyprus, and to the Gentiles, began.
Key Scriptures
Acts 13:4
