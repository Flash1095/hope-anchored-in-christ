Valley of Jezreel
Modern Location
Jezreel Valley, northern Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The broad fertile valley between the hills of Samaria and Galilee, Israel's breadbasket and battlefield.
Biblical Significance
Jezreel is the valley of harvest and of war, from Gideon's Midianites to the blood of Naboth's vineyard.
Key Scriptures
Judges 6:33; Hosea 1:4
Related Books
Judges; Hosea
