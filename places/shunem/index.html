Shunem

Name
Shunem
Modern Location
Sulam, Jezreel Valley, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
An Issachar town where the Philistines camped before Gilboa, and home of the Shunammite woman who hosted Elisha.
Biblical Significance
Shunem is where Elisha raised the Shunammite's son, and where the Philistine army gathered before Saul's last battle.
Key Scriptures
1 Samuel 28:4; 2 Kings 4:8
Related Books
1 Samuel; 2 Kings
Related People
King Saul; Elisha
