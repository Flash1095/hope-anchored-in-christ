Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A Levitical town in Benjamin, given with its suburbs among the thirteen cities of the Aaronites' families.
Biblical Significance
Alemeth shows the Levites settled town by town among the tribes, so that teaching and sacrifice were never far from any family.
