Description
The later Nabataean rock city in Jordan, traditionally identified with the Sela of 2 Kings 14:7, which Amaziah took by war and called Joktheel, and of Isaiah 16:1, where the lamb is sent to the ruler of the land from Sela. That identification of Petra with biblical Sela is debated, and the existing Sela entry likewise presents it only as traditional; this entry therefore carries the identification as traditional, not settled. Petra itself is not named in Scripture.
Biblical Significance
Petra, if it is biblical Sela, is the rock city taken by Amaziah and named in Isaiah's message to Moab; as a traditional identification, it is presented here with that debate stated openly.
Key Scriptures
