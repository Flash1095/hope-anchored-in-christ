Name

Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
The land of Zuph was the district Saul and his servant reached while searching for the lost donkeys, where Saul proposed to turn back and his servant proposed to consult the man of God.
Biblical Significance
Zuph is the district of Saul's decision to seek Samuel: the search that ended in anointing.

1 Samuel 9:5
