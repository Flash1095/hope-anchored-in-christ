Canaan

Name
Canaan
Modern Location
Israel, Palestine, Lebanon, and western Jordan and Syria
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The land God promised to Abraham and his descendants - 'a land flowing with milk and honey.' Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob lived there as nomads, dwelling in tents on the strength of a promise.
Biblical Significance
Canaan taught the patriarchs to live as strangers holding a promise. Hebrews says they were looking for a better country - a heavenly one. Our anchor, like theirs, rests in a homeland we have not reached yet.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 12:7; Genesis 17:8; Hebrews 11:9-10
