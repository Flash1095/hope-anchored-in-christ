Name

Testament

Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
A small tetrarchy northeast of Galilee, ruled by Philip the tetrarch when John the Baptist began preaching.
Biblical Significance
Ituraea dates the start of John's ministry, anchoring the gospel story in real rulers and real places.
