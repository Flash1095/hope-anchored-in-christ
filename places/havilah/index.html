Havilah

View on map →

A gold-bearing land named in the description of Eden's rivers and again in the Table of Nations and Ishmael's territory.

Biblical Significance

Havilah, rich in gold yet unlocated, reminds us how much of the earliest world Scripture sketches rather than maps.
