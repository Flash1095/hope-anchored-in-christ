Sea of Chinnereth

Name
Sea of Chinnereth
Modern Location
Sea of Galilee, Israel
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The freshwater lake of the north, called Chinnereth in the Old Testament, bordering Naphtali and the tribal lands. Called the Sea of Galilee in the New Testament.
Biblical Significance
Chinnereth is the northern sea of the tribal allotments, the lake that would later hear the preaching of Jesus.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 34:11; Joshua 12:3; Matthew 4:18; Mark 1:16; John 21:1
