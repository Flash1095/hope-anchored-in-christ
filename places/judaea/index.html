Name

Modern Location
Southern Israel/Palestine, around Jerusalem
Testament

Timeline Era

Description
The Roman province covering southern Israel, home of Jerusalem, Bethlehem, and the temple; the KJV spelling of Judea.
Biblical Significance
Judaea is where Jesus was born, where He taught and was crucified, and where the church was born at Pentecost.
Key Scriptures
Matthew 2:1; Matthew 3:1; Acts 2:9
Related Books
Matthew; Acts
Related Events
Pentecost; The Temptation in the Wilderness
