Jeshimon

View on map →

Name
Jeshimon
Modern Location
Wilderness region near Zahrat al Kula
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The desert waste that the wilderness journeys and David's wanderings both look toward: Pisgah's height faces it, Balak brought Balaam to a height overlooking it, and the hill of Hachilah stood on its southern edge.
Biblical Significance
Jeshimon is Scripture's word for the empty land itself, the backdrop against which the wilderness years and David's fugitive years both unfold.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 21:20; Numbers 23:28; 1 Samuel 23:19; 1 Samuel 23:24; 1 Samuel 26:1; 1 Samuel 26:3
Related Books
Numbers; 1 Samuel
Related People
Balaam; Balak
