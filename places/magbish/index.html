Name
Magbish
Modern Location
Disputed; proposed sites include Khirbet el Makhbiyeh and Khirbet Qanan Mugheimis
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Description
A place whose 156 returning children are counted in Ezra's list of the exiles who came back with Zerubbabel.
Biblical Significance
Magbish survives as a number in a homecoming list: 156 people whose town is otherwise lost to us.
Key Scriptures
Ezra 2:30
