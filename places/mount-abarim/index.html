Mount Abarim

View on map →

Name
Mount Abarim
Modern Location
Unknown; east of the Jordan, opposite Jericho
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The range of mountains east of the Jordan from which Moses viewed the promised land before his death. Mount Nebo, where Moses died, is part of this range.
Biblical Significance
Abarim marks the end of the wilderness journey: from its heights Moses saw the land he would not enter.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 27:12; Numbers 33:47
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
