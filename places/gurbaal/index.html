Name
Gurbaal
Modern Location
Buseira, Edom
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A place of the Arabians and Mehunims against whom Uzziah warred, when God helped him against the Philistines and the Arabians that dwelt in Gurbaal.
Biblical Significance
Gurbaal is one of Uzziah's victories: a desert town whose people fell before a king God helped.
