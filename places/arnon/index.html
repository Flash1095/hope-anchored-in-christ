Arnon

Name
Arnon
Modern Location
Wadi Mujib, Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The gorge river that was the border of Moab and of Sihon's Amorite kingdom, crossed by Israel on the way to Canaan.
Biblical Significance
The Arnon is the border Israel crossed into its inheritance.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 21:13; Deuteronomy 2:24; Judges 11:13
Related Books
Numbers; Deuteronomy; Judges
Related People
Moses; Sihon
