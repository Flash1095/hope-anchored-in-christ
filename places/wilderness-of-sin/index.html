Wilderness of Sin

View on map →

The wilderness Israel crossed after Elim, where God first gave manna and where the people grumbled about bread.

Biblical Significance

Sin is where daily bread from heaven began: manna fell here, teaching Israel to trust God one day at a time.

Details
