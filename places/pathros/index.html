Pathros

Name
Pathros
Modern Location
Southern Egypt
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
Upper Egypt, the southern land of the Nile, named as a place of both judgment and promised return for scattered Israel.
Biblical Significance
Pathros shows God gathering His people from the far south as surely as from Babylon.
Key Scriptures
Isaiah 11:11; Jeremiah 44:1; Ezekiel 29:14
