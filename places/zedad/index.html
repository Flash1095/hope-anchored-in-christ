Name

Modern Location
Unknown; candidates include Sadad and Khirbet Serada
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
Zedad was a town on the northern boundary of the land, named in the boundary list of Numbers 34 and again in Ezekiel's vision of the restored allotment.
Biblical Significance
Zedad is a boundary name: a northern marker of the promised land in both Moses' list and Ezekiel's vision.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 34:8; Ezekiel 47:15
Related Books
Numbers; Ezekiel
