Description
The oak of the diviners near Shechem, by which Gaal's company was seen coming down from the middle of the land during Abimelech's war.
Biblical Significance
The Diviners' Oak is a landmark in Abimelech's war: a named tree by which an army was spotted coming down to Shechem.
