Gadara

Name
Gadara
Modern Location
Umm Qais, northern Jordan
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
The city behind the KJV's 'country of the Gadarenes' (Mark, Luke) and 'country of the Gergesenes' (Matthew), where Jesus cast out the legion of demons.
Biblical Significance
Gadara is where Gentile territory first saw Jesus' power over the demonic, and the healed man became its first witness.
Key Scriptures
Matthew 8:28; Mark 5:1; Luke 8:26
Related Books
Matthew; Mark; Luke
Related People
Jesus
