Ezion-geber

Name
Ezion-geber
Modern Location
Near Aqaba, Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
The Red Sea port where Solomon built ships for the voyage to Ophir, at the edge of Edom.
Biblical Significance
Ezion-geber is Solomon's navy yard, the port of the gold ships.
Key Scriptures
Deuteronomy 2:8; 1 Kings 9:26; 1 Kings 22:48
