Libnah

Name
Libnah
Modern Location
Unknown; in the Shephelah, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A Levitical city of Judah that revolted from Jehoram, besieged by Sennacherib after Lachish. The same name also belongs to a station on the wilderness journey (Numbers 33:20), a distinct place sharing the name.
Biblical Significance
Libnah is the city that broke from Judah's apostate king, and the next stop on Sennacherib's campaign after Lachish.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 10:29; 2 Kings 8:22; 2 Kings 19:8
Related Books
Joshua; 2 Kings
