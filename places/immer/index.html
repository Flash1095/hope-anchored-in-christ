Name
Immer
Modern Location
Unknown; in Babylonia, exact site not stated
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Description
A place in Babylonia from which exiles returned with Zerubbabel, listed alongside Tel-melah, Tel-harsa, Cherub and Addan; those from Immer could not prove their ancestry and so were kept from the priesthood until a priest could consult Urim and Thummim.
Biblical Significance
Immer marks the honest edge of the return: a community that came home but could not prove its line, and waited for God's answer rather than forcing one.
