Name
Ashkelon
Modern Location
Ashkelon, coastal Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A Philistine royal city on the coast, taken briefly by Judah, later a byword for Philistine pride under judgment.
Biblical Significance
Ashkelon is named in Samson's riddle-vengeance and in the prophets' oracles against Philistia.
Key Scriptures
Judges 1:18; Judges 14:19; Zephaniah 2:4
Related Books
Judges; Zephaniah
Related People
