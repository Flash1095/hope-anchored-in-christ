Solomon's Porch

Name
Solomon's Porch
Modern Location
Jerusalem, eastern side of the temple precinct
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Description
The covered colonnade on the temple's east side where Jesus walked and where the early church gathered daily.
Biblical Significance
Solomon's Porch became the early church's meeting place, where signs and wonders drew all Jerusalem's attention.
Key Scriptures
John 10:23; Acts 5:12
Related Books
John; Acts
Related People
Jesus; Simon Peter
