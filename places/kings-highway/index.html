King's Highway

View on map →

Name
King's Highway
Modern Location
Transjordan (the ancient highway through Edom and Moab)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The great north-south road through Edom and Moab along which Israel asked leave to pass, promising to keep to the road, drink no well water, and turn neither right nor left; Edom refused, and Israel went around.
Biblical Significance
The King's Highway is where Israel's politeness met Edom's refusal, and the long way round became part of the wilderness story. The KJV spells it 'king's high way.'
Key Scriptures
Numbers 20:17; Numbers 21:22
