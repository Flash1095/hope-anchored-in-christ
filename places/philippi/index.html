Philippi

Name
Philippi
Modern Location
Northern Greece
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Description
The Roman colony where Paul's European mission began - Lydia's conversion by the river, the jailer's midnight baptism after an earthquake, and the joyful letter Paul later wrote from prison.
Biblical Significance
Philippi is where the gospel first touched European soil - through a businesswoman, a slave girl, and a jailer. God builds His church from the most unlikely people.
Key Scriptures
Acts 16:11-15; Acts 16:25-34; Philippians 1:3-6
