Ephesus

View on map →

The great Asian port where Paul taught for over two years, 'so that all who lived in Asia heard the word.' Home of the Artemis temple, the silversmiths' riot, and the church that lost its first love.

Biblical Significance

Ephesus had sound doctrine and lost its heart - Jesus's warning to return to first love still speaks to every busy, correct, cooling church.
