Tel Gezer, Shephelah, Israel

Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A strategic Canaanite city on the road from the coast to Jerusalem, given to Solomon by Pharaoh as a wedding gift and rebuilt by him.
Biblical Significance
Gezer guarded the approach to Jerusalem and became a Solomonic fortress city with its famous gate.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 16:10; 1 Kings 9:16
