Mount Paran

View on map →

Name
Mount Paran
Modern Location
Unknown; in the wilderness of Paran
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
A mountain associated with the wilderness of Paran, named in Moses' blessing when the LORD is pictured as shining forth from Paran, and again in Habakkuk's vision of God coming from Teman and Paran.
Biblical Significance
Paran is where God's appearing is pictured in poetry, alongside Sinai and Seir.
Key Scriptures
Deuteronomy 33:2; Habakkuk 3:3
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Habakkuk
Related People
Moses
