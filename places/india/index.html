India

Name
India
Modern Location
India (Indus region)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Description
The eastern edge of Ahasuerus' empire, named when Esther's story opens with a kingdom stretching 'from India unto Ethiopia' and again when Mordecai's decree went out to all 127 provinces.
Biblical Significance
India sets the scale of the Persian world Esther lived in: a kingdom so wide that one decree had to travel to its farthest edge to save a people.
Key Scriptures
Esther 1:1; Esther 8:9
