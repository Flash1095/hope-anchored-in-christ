Nineveh

Name
Nineveh
Modern Location
Mosul, Iraq
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
The great Assyrian capital - 'the great city' of 120,000 people - to which Jonah reluctantly preached. To his dismay, they repented, and God spared them.
Biblical Significance
Nineveh proves God's mercy has no borders. The prophet wanted judgment; God wanted repentance. His compassion for the lost rebukes every narrow heart.
Key Scriptures
Jonah 3:1-10; Jonah 4:10-11; Matthew 12:41
