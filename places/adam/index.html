Modern Location
Tell ed Damiyeh, Jordan Valley
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A city beside Zaretan on the Jordan, very far from which the waters coming down stood up in a heap when Israel crossed into Canaan.
Biblical Significance
Adam is where the crossing became possible: the river held back upstream so that Israel could pass over on dry ground toward Jericho.
