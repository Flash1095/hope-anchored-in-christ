Mortar

View on map →

A district of Jerusalem named in Zephaniah's oracle, where the merchant people lived and silver was weighed; the KJV calls it Maktesh, which means mortar, the hollow where grain and trade were ground.

Biblical Significance

Mortar is the market quarter under judgment: the place of merchants and silver, told to howl because its trade would be cut off.
