Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
Zarethan, also called Zartanah, was a town in the Jordan valley, named as the point to which the Jordan's waters stood back at the crossing, and as the place in whose plain Solomon cast the temple's bronze vessels.
Biblical Significance
Zarethan is a Jordan-valley name of both crossing and casting: the waters' limit at the crossing, and the foundry plain of the temple.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 3:16; 1 Kings 4:12; 1 Kings 7:46
