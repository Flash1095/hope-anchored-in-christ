View on map →

Name
Beth-diblathaim
Modern Location
Duleilat esh Sharqiyeh, Moab
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A town of Moab named in Jeremiah's judgment upon Moab, among Dibon, Nebo, Kiriathaim and the cities of the plain and of the high places.
Biblical Significance
Beth-diblathaim is one of Moab's judged towns: a name in a long oracle showing that no Moabite city stood outside the LORD's reckoning.
Key Scriptures
Jeremiah 48:22
