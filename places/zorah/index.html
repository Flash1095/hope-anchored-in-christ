Zorah

Name
Zorah
Modern Location
Tel Zor'a, Shephelah, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A Danite town in the Shephelah, home of Manoah and birthplace of Samson, near Eshtaol.
Biblical Significance
Zorah is Samson's hometown, where the angel appeared to Manoah and his wife.
Key Scriptures
Judges 13:2; Judges 13:25
