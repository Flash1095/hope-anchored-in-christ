Zair

View on map →

Zair was the place to which Joram, king of Israel, went with his chariots against Edom, and where, surrounded by Edomites, he broke through by night to escape.

Biblical Significance

Zair is the scene of Joram's narrow escape in the Edomite revolt: a night breakthrough when the army had fled to its tents.
