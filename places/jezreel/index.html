Jezreel

Name
Jezreel
Modern Location
Zerin, Jezreel Valley, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
An Israelite royal city with a palace of Ahab, beside Naboth's vineyard, where Jehu's purge began.
Biblical Significance
Jezreel is where Naboth was murdered for his vineyard and where Jehu judged the house of Ahab.
Key Scriptures
1 Kings 18:45; 1 Kings 21:1; 2 Kings 9:16
Related Books
1 Kings; 2 Kings
