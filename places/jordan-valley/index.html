Description
The well-watered plain of the Jordan, which Lot chose when he and Abram parted, pitching his tent toward Sodom; the KJV calls it 'the plain of Jordan.'
Biblical Significance
The Jordan Valley is where Abram and Lot's parting became a choice about the future: Lot took the green plain, and Abram was left with the promise.

Genesis 13:10-11
