Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A town Abijah king of Judah took from Jeroboam king of Israel, along with Bethel and Ephrain, in the war that followed the kingdom's division.
Biblical Significance
Jeshanah changed hands in the brothers' war, one of the border towns fought over when one kingdom became two.
