Timeline Era

Description
The home town of Micah the Morasthite, whose prophecy Jeremiah's elders quoted a century later to save Jeremiah's life, and the place Micah told to give parting gifts in his lament over Judah's towns.
Biblical Significance
Moresheth-gath gave Israel a prophet from the countryside, whose words outlived him long enough to rescue another prophet.
Key Scriptures
Jeremiah 26:18; Micah 1:1; Micah 1:14
Related Books
