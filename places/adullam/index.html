Adullam

Name
Adullam
Modern Location
Khirbet esh-Sheikh Madkur, Shephelah, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
A Canaanite royal city near which David hid in the cave of Adullam, gathering the distressed and discontented around him.
Biblical Significance
Adullam's cave is where David's mighty men first gathered: debtors and malcontents who became his army.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 12:15; 1 Samuel 22:1

Joshua; 1 Samuel
