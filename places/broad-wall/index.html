Broad Wall

View on map →

Name
Broad Wall
Modern Location
Jerusalem
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Description
A broad section of Jerusalem's wall named in Nehemiah's repair account and in the dedication procession, when half the people went upon the wall toward the broad wall.
Biblical Significance
The Broad Wall is Nehemiah's rebuilt strength: a wall wide enough to walk in procession, celebrating a city enclosed again.
Key Scriptures
Nehemiah 3:8; Nehemiah 12:38
Related Books
Nehemiah
