Aram

Name
Aram
Modern Location
Syria
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
The land of the Arameans, north and east of Israel, with Damascus as its great city. Aram warred with David, allied and fought with Israel's kings for centuries, and is also called Syria in the King James Version. Called Syria in the New Testament.
Biblical Significance
Aram shows how a near neighbour can be enemy, ally, and instrument of God in turn, and how God's word runs even to Damascus.
