Pelusium

View on map →

The fortress city at Egypt's eastern edge, called Sin in the KJV, on which Ezekiel says God would pour out fury as the strength of Egypt, and whose multitudes would be cut off.

Biblical Significance

Pelusium, Sin, is Egypt's door: the fortress whose fall would open the land, named in Ezekiel's oracle against the nation.
