Description
One of the fortified cities of Judah listed under Rehoboam, named alongside Lachish and Azekah.
Biblical Significance
Adoraim belongs to Rehoboam's hurried fortifying of Judah after the kingdom divided: a town made strong for a kingdom suddenly smaller.
