Samaria

View on map →

Name
Samaria
Modern Location
Near Nablus, West Bank (Sebastia)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
Capital of the northern kingdom of Israel, built by Omri. Its kings led the nation into idolatry until Assyria carried it away. Jesus later crossed its prejudices at Jacob's well.
Biblical Significance
Samaria fell because its kings forgot God - yet grace kept reaching there: Elisha's miracles, Philip's revival, and a Samaritan woman who became an evangelist.
Key Scriptures
1 Kings 16:24; 2 Kings 17:6; John 4:4-26; Acts 8:5-8
