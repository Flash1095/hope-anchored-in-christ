Description
Shebarim was a place near Ai to which the men of Ai chased Israel after the first, failed attack on Ai, striking down about thirty-six men before the pursuit broke Israel's heart.
Biblical Significance
Shebarim marks the cost of Achan's sin: the chase from Ai that exposed Israel's defeat before the second attack succeeded.
