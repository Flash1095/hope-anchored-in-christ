Humtah

View on map →

Humtah was a town in Judah's hill country, named in the town list of Joshua 15. No further story is attached to it in Scripture, and its exact site is unidentified.

Biblical Significance

Its inclusion in the allotment list marks it as one of the towns assigned to Judah.
