Riblah

Name
Riblah
Modern Location
Near Ribleh, Syria
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
The Syrian town where Pharaoh Necho imprisoned Jehoahaz, and where Nebuchadnezzar blinded Zedekiah after slaying his sons before his eyes.
Biblical Significance
Riblah is where Judah's kingship ended in chains and darkness.
Key Scriptures
2 Kings 23:33; 2 Kings 25:6; Jeremiah 39:5
Related Books
2 Kings; Jeremiah
Related People
Jeremiah
