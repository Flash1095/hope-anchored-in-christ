Shephelah

View on map →

Name
Shephelah
Modern Location
Low foothills between the coastal plain and the Judean hills, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The rolling foothills west of the Judean mountains, a contested borderland between Israel and Philistia where many battles were fought.
Biblical Significance
Border country: the Shephelah saw the battles of Aijalon, Elah, and the Philistine wars of Saul and David.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 15:33; 1 Samuel 17:1; Jeremiah 32:44
Related Books
Joshua; 1 Samuel; Jeremiah
