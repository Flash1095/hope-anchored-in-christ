Name

Modern Location

Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
The settlement near Ramah where Samuel's company of prophets lived, to which David fled from Saul, and where Saul's messengers, and then Saul himself, fell to prophesying instead of arresting him.
Biblical Significance
Naioth is where pursuit turned into prophecy: three bands of messengers and a king himself stopped to prophesy among the prophets.
Key Scriptures
1 Samuel 19:18-19; 1 Samuel 19:22-23; 1 Samuel 20:1
Related Books
