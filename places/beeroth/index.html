Beeroth

Name
Beeroth
Modern Location
El-Bireh area, central Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
One of the four Gibeonite cities that made peace with Joshua by cunning, later a Benjamite town.
Biblical Significance
Beeroth is one of the cities whose treaty trick bound Israel to an oath, and whose men later murdered Ish-bosheth.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 9:17; 2 Samuel 4:2
Related Books
Joshua; 2 Samuel
Related People
