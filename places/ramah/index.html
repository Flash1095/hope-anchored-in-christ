Ramah

Name
Ramah
Modern Location
Near Jerusalem, West Bank
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
Samuel's hometown, where he judged Israel and where the elders demanded a king. Deborah held court between Ramah and Bethel.
Biblical Significance
Ramah witnessed Israel's fateful demand - 'give us a king' - trading God's direct rule for a human one. Even our rejections become part of His redemptive plan.
Key Scriptures
1 Samuel 7:17; 1 Samuel 8:4-7; Judges 4:5
