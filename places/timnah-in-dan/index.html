Conquest and Judges
Description
The Timnah of Samson's story, where he saw a Philistine woman and where the men of Judah later bound him; named in both Judah's and Dan's allotment lists on their shared border.
Biblical Significance
Timnah in Dan is Samson's town, where his riddle and his first confrontations with the Philistines unfolded.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 15:10; Joshua 19:43; Judges 14:1-2; Judges 14:5; Judges 15:6; 2 Chronicles 28:18
