Eden

Name
Eden
Modern Location
Unknown; traditionally northern Mesopotamia
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A Mesopotamian trading place named with Haran and Canneh among Tyre's merchants, and as a conquered people in the Assyrian boast. It is distinct from the Garden of Eden.
Biblical Significance
This Eden is a market town, not a paradise: a reminder that Scripture reuses names and the context must decide.
Key Scriptures
2 Kings 19:12; Isaiah 37:12; Ezekiel 27:23
Related Books
2 Kings; Isaiah; Ezekiel
