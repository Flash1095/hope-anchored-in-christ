Ezion-gaber

View on map →

One of the stations on Israel's wilderness journey, named in the Numbers 33 itinerary. The Red Sea port on the journey's edge, later Solomon's fleet harbour. No further detail is recorded beyond the journey account.

Biblical Significance

The wilderness stations trace the route God led Israel from Egypt to the Plains of Moab.
