Name
Washer’s Field
Modern Location
Jerusalem area, near En-rogel and the Gihon
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
The Washer's Field, the fuller's field in the King James Version, was a field by the conduit of the upper pool, where Isaiah met Ahaz and where the Assyrian officers later stood to taunt Jerusalem.
Biblical Significance
The field is the place of two confrontations outside the wall: Isaiah's word to Ahaz, and Rabshakeh's taunt to Hezekiah's people.
Key Scriptures
2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 7:3; Isaiah 36:2
