Atroth-shophan

View on map →

Name
Atroth-shophan
Modern Location
Rujm Ataruz, east of the Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
A town in Gad's building list east of the Jordan, named with Atroth, Jaazer and Jogbehah among the cities Gad built for their flocks and families.
Biblical Significance
Atroth-shophan is a list-only town: Gad's settlement east of the Jordan, built for cattle and children before the conquest west of the river was finished.
