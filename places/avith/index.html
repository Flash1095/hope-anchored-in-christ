Name
Avith
Modern Location
Rujum al Juththa, Edom
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The city of Hadad son of Bedad, who smote Midian in the field of Moab and reigned over Edom after Husham died.
Biblical Significance
Avith is one of Edom's king-cities: a reminder that Edom had kings long before Israel asked for one.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 36:35; 1 Chronicles 1:46
Related Books
Genesis; 1 Chronicles
