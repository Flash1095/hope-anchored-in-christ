Ashteroth-karnaim

View on map →

Name
Ashteroth-karnaim
Modern Location
Tell Ashtara
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The place where Chedorlaomer and the kings with him smote the Rephaim in the fourteenth year, on the campaign that swept down to Sodom and took Lot captive.
Biblical Significance
Ashteroth-karnaim is the first battlefield of Genesis 14: giants smitten in the east before Abram ever took up arms to rescue Lot.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 14:5
