Kiriathaim

Name
Kiriathaim
Modern Location
Near Amman, Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A Moabite city named in the war of the four kings, in the conquest lists, and in the oracles against Moab.
Biblical Significance
Kiriathaim is the ancient city of the Emim, named from Abram's day to Jeremiah's.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 14:5; Numbers 32:37; Jeremiah 48:1; Isaiah 15:2
Related Books
Genesis; Numbers; Jeremiah; Isaiah
Related People
Abraham; Moses
