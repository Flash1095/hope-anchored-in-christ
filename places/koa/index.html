Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Description
A people named in Ezekiel's allegory of Jerusalem's unfaithfulness, listed among the Babylonians, Chaldeans, Pekod and Shoa who came against her, and identified with Gutium in the Zagros.
Biblical Significance
Koa is one of the far names in Ezekiel's charge sheet, a people from the eastern mountains drawn into Babylon's orbit.
Key Scriptures
Ezekiel 23:23
