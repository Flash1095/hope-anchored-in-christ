Idumaea

Name
Idumaea
Modern Location
Southern Judea and the northern Negev
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
The Greek name for Edom, south of Judea; crowds from Idumaea came to hear Jesus.
Biblical Significance
Idumaea shows how far Jesus' fame spread beyond Israel's old borders, drawing even Edom's descendants to Him.
Key Scriptures
Mark 3:8
