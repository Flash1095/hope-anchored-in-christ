Rehoboth by the River

View on map →

Name
Rehoboth by the River
Modern Location
Unknown; proposed sites include Ras Rihab near Wadi Ghuwayr
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
A city named as the home of Saul, a king of Edom, described as Rehoboth by the river; distinct from the Assyrian Rehoboth and from Isaac's well.
Biblical Significance
Rehoboth by the River belongs to the early king list of Edom before Israel had a king.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 36:37; 1 Chronicles 1:48
Related Books
Genesis; 1 Chronicles
