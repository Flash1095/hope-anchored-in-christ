Valley of Gerar

View on map →

The valley where Isaac settled and dug wells again, reopening the wells his father Abraham had dug, after the Philistines had stopped them.

Biblical Significance

Gerar's valley is where Isaac re-dug Abraham's wells and contended for water in the Negev.
