Description
A river or canal in Babylonia where Ezra gathered the returning exiles on their way back to Jerusalem. There Ezra proclaimed a fast, that the company might humble themselves before God and seek a safe journey for themselves, their children, and their goods. The exact canal is unidentified, and this entry does not guess at its course or modern course.
Biblical Significance
Ahava is where the return from exile paused for prayer before it moved. Ezra would not ask the king for soldiers and horsemen; instead the exiles fasted and sought God's protection for the road, and God answered. The gathering at Ahava also exposed a practical problem for the journey: no Levites were found among those assembled, and Ezra sent for ministers for the house of God before setting out.
