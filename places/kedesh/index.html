Kedesh

Name
Kedesh
Modern Location
Tel Qedesh, northern Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A city of refuge in Naphtali, home of Barak, where Deborah sent for him to gather the tribes against Sisera.
Biblical Significance
Kedesh is the refuge city that sent Barak to battle, and the town of the judge who freed Israel.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 20:7; Judges 4:6
Related Books
Joshua; Judges
Related People
Joshua; Deborah; Barak
