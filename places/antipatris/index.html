Antipatris

View on map →

A town on the road from Jerusalem to Caesarea, built by Herod and named for his father, where soldiers escorted Paul by night.

Biblical Significance

Antipatris is a night-stop on Paul's hurried journey from a Jerusalem plot to a Caesarean trial.
