Description
The Upper Pool was a pool at Jerusalem, by the conduit of the upper pool on the highway of the fuller's field, where Isaiah met Ahaz and where the Assyrian envoys later stood.
Biblical Significance
The pool is a meeting place of crisis: Isaiah's sign to Ahaz, and later the Assyrian taunt outside the wall.
Key Scriptures
2 Kings 18:17; Isaiah 7:3; Isaiah 36:2
