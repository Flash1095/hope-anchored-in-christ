Nebo in Judah

View on map →

Name
Nebo in Judah
Modern Location
Unknown; possibly Nuba, near Hebron, in Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Description
A town named in the lists of those who returned from exile with Zerubbabel, distinct from the Moabite town of Nebo east of the Jordan.
Biblical Significance
Nebo in Judah marks a home to which exiles returned, a small name in the record of restoration.
Key Scriptures
Ezra 2:29; Nehemiah 7:33
Related Books
Ezra; Nehemiah
