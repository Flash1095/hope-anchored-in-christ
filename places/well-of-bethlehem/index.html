Well of Bethlehem

View on map →

The well by the gate of Bethlehem whose water David longed for while the Philistines held the town, and which three of his mighty men fetched at the risk of their lives. David poured it out before the LORD rather than drink it.

Biblical Significance

Bethlehem's well is the water David would not drink, because it was fetched at the risk of men's lives.
