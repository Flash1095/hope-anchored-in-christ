Derbe

Name
Derbe
Modern Location
Near Karaman, Turkey
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Description
A frontier town of Lycaonia where Paul and Barnabas preached and made many disciples before turning back.
Biblical Significance
Derbe is the turnaround point of the first journey, where the missionaries doubled back to strengthen new churches.
Key Scriptures
Acts 14:6; Acts 14:20; Acts 16:1
