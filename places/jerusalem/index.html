Jerusalem

Name
Jerusalem
Modern Location
Israel
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
David's capital, Solomon's temple city, the place of Jesus's death and resurrection, and the birthplace of the church at Pentecost. No city looms larger in Scripture.
Biblical Significance
Jerusalem is where God's story concentrates - temple, cross, empty tomb, Spirit. What happened there anchors every hope we have.
