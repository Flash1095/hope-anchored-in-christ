Arpad

Name
Arpad
Modern Location
Tell Rifa'at, Syria
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A Syrian city conquered by Assyria, named in the taunt against Jerusalem and in the oracle against Damascus.
Biblical Significance
Arpad is the fallen neighbour held up to frighten Jerusalem.
Key Scriptures
2 Kings 18:34; Isaiah 10:9; Jeremiah 49:23
Related Books
2 Kings; Isaiah; Jeremiah
