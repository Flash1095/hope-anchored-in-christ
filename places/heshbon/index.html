Heshbon

Name
Heshbon
Modern Location
Tell Hesban, Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The Amorite royal city of Sihon, taken by Israel and given to Reuben, later a Moabite city lamented by the prophets.
Biblical Significance
Heshbon is Sihon's capital, whose fall opened Transjordan to Israel, and whose ruins Isaiah mourns.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 21:25; Joshua 13:17; Isaiah 15:4; Numbers 21:26; Deuteronomy 2:24
Related Books
Numbers; Joshua; Isaiah; Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses; Sihon
