Wilderness of Judah

View on map →

Name
Wilderness of Judah
Modern Location
Barren hills east of Bethlehem toward the Dead Sea, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
The rugged desert east of the Judean hills where David hid from Saul and where John the Baptist would later preach.
Biblical Significance
The wilderness of Judah is David's refuge and the psalmist's thirsty land: "my soul thirsteth for thee in a dry land."
Key Scriptures
1 Samuel 23:14; Psalm 63:1
Related Books
1 Samuel; Psalms
Related People
King David
