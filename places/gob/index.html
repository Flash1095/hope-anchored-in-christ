Name
Gob
Modern Location
Tel Gezer (Gezer identification)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
A place where Israel fought the Philistines again, where Sibbechai slew Saph of the giants, and where Elhanan slew the brother of Goliath.
Biblical Significance
Gob is a giants' battlefield: the place where David's men cut down the last of Goliath's kin.
Key Scriptures
2 Samuel 21:18; 2 Samuel 21:19
Related Books
2 Samuel
Related People
King David; Sibbechai the Hushathite
