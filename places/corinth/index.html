Description
The wealthy, immoral Greek port where Paul planted a church and wrote two of his most famous letters - on love, resurrection, and grace for a messy congregation.
Biblical Significance
Corinth shows that no church is too broken for God to love and no people too messed up to become saints. Grace met Corinth's chaos and made it holy.
