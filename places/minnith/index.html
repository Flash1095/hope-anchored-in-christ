Name
Minnith
Modern Location
Umm el Hanafish
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A place east of Jordan named as the limit of Jephthah's slaughter of the Ammonites, 'even unto Minnith,' and later famous for wheat traded to Tyre.
Biblical Significance
Minnith is both a battle's boundary and a market's pride: where a victory stopped, and whose wheat a trading empire bought.
Key Scriptures
Judges 11:33; Ezekiel 27:17
Related Books
Judges; Ezekiel
Related People
Jephthah, Judge of Israel
