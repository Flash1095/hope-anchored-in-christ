Modern Location
Seneh cliff, Michmash pass
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
Seneh was one of the two sharp rocks, with Bozez, between which Jonathan and his armourbearer climbed up to the Philistine garrison at Michmash.
Biblical Significance
Seneh is half of the narrow pass of Jonathan's daring climb, where two men trusted God against a garrison.
