Chittim

View on map →

Name
Chittim
Modern Location
Unknown; traditionally Cyprus
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The "isles of Chittim" named among Javan's sons, and later the ships of Chittim that come against kings in Daniel. Traditionally identified with Cyprus and the western isles.
Biblical Significance
Chittim marks the western horizon of the biblical world, the isles beyond the sea.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:4; Numbers 24:24; Isaiah 23:1
Related Books
Genesis; Numbers; Isaiah
