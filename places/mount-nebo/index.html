Mount Nebo

Name
Mount Nebo
Modern Location
Mount Nebo, Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The mountain in Moab from which Moses saw the Promised Land before he died, and where he was buried in the valley below.
Biblical Significance
Nebo is where Moses saw the land he would not enter, and where his eyes stayed undimmed to the end.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 27:12; Deuteronomy 34:1
Related Books
Numbers; Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
