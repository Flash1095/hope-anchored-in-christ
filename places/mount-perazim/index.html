Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A mountain named by Isaiah when he says the LORD will rise up as in Mount Perazim, recalling David's victory over the Philistines at Baal-perazim, where the LORD broke through his enemies like the breaking forth of waters.
Biblical Significance
Perazim is Isaiah's picture of God breaking through against his enemies, as he did for David.

Isaiah 28:21
