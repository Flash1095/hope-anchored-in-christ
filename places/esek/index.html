Esek

View on map →

The first well Isaac's servants dug in the valley of Gerar, named Esek, 'contention', because the herdmen of Gerar strove with Isaac's herdmen for it.

Biblical Significance

Esek is contention over water: the first of Isaac's wells claimed by others, and the name kept the quarrel in memory.
