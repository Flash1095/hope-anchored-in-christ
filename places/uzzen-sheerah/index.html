Uzzen-sheerah

View on map →

Uzzen-sheerah was a town built by Sheerah, daughter of Ephraim, who also built Lower and Upper Beth-horon.

Biblical Significance

Uzzen-sheerah is one of the three towns Scripture credits to Sheerah, a woman builder in Ephraim's genealogy.
