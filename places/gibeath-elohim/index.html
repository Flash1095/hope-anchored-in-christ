Gibeath-elohim

View on map →

Name
Gibeath-elohim
Modern Location
Tel el Ful (Gibeah area)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
The hill of God where Saul, newly anointed, was to meet a company of prophets with psaltery, tabret, pipe and harp, and where a Philistine garrison stood.
Biblical Significance
Gibeath-elohim is where Saul met the prophets: music, prophesying, and a changed heart at the hill of God.
Key Scriptures
1 Samuel 10:5
Related Books

Related People
