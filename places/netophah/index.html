Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
A village near Bethlehem that gave David two of his mighty men, Maharai and Heleb the Netophathites, and whose sons returned from exile with Zerubbabel and are counted in Ezra and Nehemiah.
Biblical Significance
Netophah is a village of soldiers and returnees: its men fought for David and its children came home from Babylon.
Key Scriptures
2 Samuel 23:28-29; 1 Chronicles 9:16; 1 Chronicles 11:30; 1 Chronicles 27:13; 1 Chronicles 27:15; Ezra 2:22; Nehemiah 7:26
