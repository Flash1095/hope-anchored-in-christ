Karkor

View on map →

The place in the desert east of Jordan where Zebah and Zalmunna, kings of Midian, rested with their remaining fifteen thousand men before Gideon fell on them and took them.

Biblical Significance

Karkor is where Gideon's long pursuit ended: the Midianite kings thought the desert had hidden them, and it had not.
