Hanes

View on map →

Name
Hanes
Modern Location
Disputed; proposed identifications include Heracleopolis Magna (Ihnasiyyah al Madinah) and Tahpanhes (Tell Defenneh)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
An Egyptian city named in Isaiah's oracle against trusting Egypt: Judah's princes were at Zoan and her ambassadors had come to Hanes, yet Egypt's help would prove worthless.
Biblical Significance
Hanes stands in Isaiah's warning that Egypt, however impressive, could not save Judah; trust placed there would end in shame.
Key Scriptures
Isaiah 30:4
