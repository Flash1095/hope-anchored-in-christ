Description
The Rocks of the Wild Goats were the crags near En-gedi where David hid from Saul, and where Saul went to seek him, entering the cave in which David cut off the skirt of his robe.
Biblical Significance
The rocks are David's hiding place in the En-gedi wilderness: the crags above the cave where he spared Saul.
