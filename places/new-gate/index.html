New Gate

View on map →

A gate of the LORD's house in Jerusalem, where the princes sat to hear Jeremiah's temple sermon, and where Baruch read Jeremiah's scroll in Gemariah's chamber.

Biblical Significance

The New Gate is where Jeremiah's words were heard and read in public: a gate that became a courtroom and a reading desk.
