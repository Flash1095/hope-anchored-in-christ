Midian

Name
Midian
Modern Location
Northwestern Saudi Arabia
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The desert wilderness east of the Red Sea where Moses fled after killing an Egyptian. He shepherded Jethro's flocks here for 40 years until God met him in the burning bush.
Biblical Significance
Midian was Moses's school of obscurity - 40 years of silence before the call. God often anchors hope in us during the years nobody sees.
Key Scriptures
Exodus 2:15-22; Exodus 3:1-6; Acts 7:29-30
