Timeline Era

Description
The habitation of Chimham near Bethlehem, where Johanan and the captains stayed on their way to enter Egypt after Gedaliah's murder, fearing the Chaldeans.
Biblical Significance
Geruth Chimham is a stopping place on the road to exile: a lodging near Bethlehem where frightened leaders paused before fleeing to Egypt.
Key Scriptures
Jeremiah 41:17
Related Books
