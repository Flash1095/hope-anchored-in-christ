Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
A town Nobah took in the Transjordan settlement and renamed after himself, later counted among the towns of Jair in Gilead and Bashan.
Biblical Significance
Kenath is a town that changed its name with its conqueror, then kept its place in the lists of Jair's country.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 32:42; 1 Chronicles 2:23
Related Books
Numbers; 1 Chronicles
Related People
