On

Name
On
Modern Location
Heliopolis (near Cairo), Egypt
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The Egyptian city of the sun-god, home of Joseph's father-in-law the priest Potiphera, and named by Ezekiel among the cities judged.
Biblical Significance
On is Egypt's priestly city, joined to Joseph's family by marriage.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 41:45; Genesis 46:20; Ezekiel 30:17
Related Books
Genesis; Ezekiel
Related People
Joseph, Son of Jacob
