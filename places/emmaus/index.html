Emmaus

Name
Emmaus
Modern Location
Unknown; traditionally associated with Emmaus-Nicopolis or Qubeibeh (debated)
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
A village about sixty furlongs from Jerusalem, where the risen Jesus walked with two disciples and broke bread with them.
Biblical Significance
Emmaus is where downcast disciples learned to see the whole Scripture pointing to Christ.
