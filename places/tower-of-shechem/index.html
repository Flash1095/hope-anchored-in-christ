Name

Modern Location
Shechem (Tell Balatah)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The Tower of Shechem was the stronghold to which the men of Shechem fled when Abimelech attacked the city, and which Abimelech burned with about a thousand people inside.
Biblical Significance
The tower is the end of Shechem's revolt against Abimelech: a refuge that became a pyre.
Key Scriptures
Judges 9:46; Judges 9:47; Judges 9:49
Related Books
Judges
Related People
