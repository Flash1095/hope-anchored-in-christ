Name
Land of Israel
Modern Location
Israel and the Palestinian territories (general)
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
The land of the united and divided kingdoms as a whole, whose people profile is the Israel entry in Ancient Civilizations, from Dan in the north to Beersheba in the south. The overlap is stated honestly: Canaan, already a place entry, is the same land under its pre-conquest name, and Judaea and Galilee, already place entries, are its later parts, in the south and the north respectively; this entry is the kingdom-era whole, not a replacement for any of them. Samuel's phrase from Dan even to Beersheba became the standard way of describing the whole land, and Solomon's reign is described as all Israel dwelling safely from Dan even to Beersheba.
Biblical Significance
The Land of Israel as a whole is the stage of the monarchy: one kingdom under Saul, David and Solomon, then divided, yet still spoken of as a single land from Dan to Beersheba.
