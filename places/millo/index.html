Millo

View on map →

Name
Millo
Modern Location
Jerusalem (City of David area)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
The great terrace or fill in Jerusalem that David built up when he took the city, that Solomon raised levies to repair, and that Hezekiah strengthened when Sennacherib came; the stepped stone structure in the City of David is proposed as its remains.
Biblical Significance
Millo was Jerusalem's engineering: the fill that made the steep city buildable, repaired by every king who took the city's defence seriously.
Key Scriptures
2 Samuel 5:9; 1 Kings 9:15; 1 Kings 9:24; 1 Kings 11:27; 1 Chronicles 11:8; 2 Chronicles 32:5
