Mount Halak

View on map →

Name
Mount Halak
Modern Location
Unknown; in the south, toward Seir
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The mountain that marked the southern end of Joshua's conquests, on the way up to Seir. Joshua's campaigns are measured from Baal-gad in the north to Mount Halak in the south.
Biblical Significance
Halak bounds the land Joshua took, from the Lebanon valley to the edge of Seir.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 11:17; Joshua 12:7
