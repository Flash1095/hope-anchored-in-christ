Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A gate in Jerusalem's wall, named in the account of Athaliah's execution, in Jeremiah's promise of the city's restoration, and in Nehemiah's wall repairs, where the priests rebuilt above it.
Biblical Significance
The Horse Gate frames three moments of Jerusalem's story: a queen's fall, a prophet's promise, and a wall rebuilt stone by stone.
Key Scriptures
