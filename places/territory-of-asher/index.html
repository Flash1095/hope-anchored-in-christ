Territory of Asher

Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The tribe takes its name from Asher, son of Jacob and Zilpah, Leah's servant (Genesis 30:13; 49:20). The coastal allotment of the tribe Asher, whose people profile is the Asher entry in Ancient Civilizations, in the far north-west of the land, as described in general terms in Joshua 19:24-31. Its border ran along the Mediterranean coast, reaching toward Sidon in the north and touching the territories of Zebulun and Naphtali inland; Joshua gives its boundary by neighbouring territories and named towns, not by a precise surveyed line, and no precise border is claimed here. Its key cities among existing place entries include the coastal cities Sidon and Tyre. The tribe did not drive out all the inhabitants of its allotment, and in Judges Asher is noted as dwelling among the Canaanites.
Biblical Significance
Asher's allotment shows the conquest as inheritance with unfinished work: land given by lot, yet neighbours left in place, setting up the compromises Judges describes.
