Ur

View on map →

A wealthy Sumerian city of moon-god worship where Abraham was born. God called him out of Ur to a land He would show him - the beginning of the journey of faith.

Biblical Significance

Ur represents everything Abraham had to leave: security, family gods, the known. Hope anchored in Christ often starts the same way - leaving the familiar at God's word.
