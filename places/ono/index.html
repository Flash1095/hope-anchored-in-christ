Ono

Name
Ono
Modern Location
Kafr Ana, central Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Description
A Benjamite town in the plain where Sanballat and Geshem tried to lure Nehemiah to a meeting to do him harm.
Biblical Significance
Ono is the plain of the trap Nehemiah refused: "I am doing a great work, so that I cannot come down."
Key Scriptures
Nehemiah 6:2; Nehemiah 11:35

Nehemiah
