Bozrah

Name
Bozrah
Modern Location
Buseirah, Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
The fortified capital region of Edom, denounced by the prophets as the city of the winepress of God's judgment.
Biblical Significance
Bozrah is Edom's stronghold, named in Isaiah's vision of the Lord coming with garments stained red.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 36:33; Isaiah 34:6; Isaiah 63:1; Jeremiah 49:13
Related Books
Genesis; Isaiah; Jeremiah
