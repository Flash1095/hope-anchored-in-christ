Javan

View on map →

Name
Javan
Modern Location
Greece and western Turkey (Ionia)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The Greek world, named among Japheth's sons and later among the nations trading with Tyre and hearing of God's glory.
Biblical Significance
Javan is the western world entering the biblical story, long before the gospel reached Greece.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:2; Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 27:13
Related Books
Genesis; Isaiah; Ezekiel
