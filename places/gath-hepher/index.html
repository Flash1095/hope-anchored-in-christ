Gath-hepher

View on map →

A town in Zebulun, on the border described in Joshua (where the KJV spells it Gittah-hepher), and the hometown of Jonah son of Amittai, the prophet whose word Jeroboam II's restoration of Israel's borders fulfilled. Its modern site is traditionally identified with el-Meshhed in Israel.

Biblical Significance

Gath-hepher is remembered as Jonah's hometown, tying the prophet of Nineveh to a small Galilean town and to God's word about Israel's borders.
