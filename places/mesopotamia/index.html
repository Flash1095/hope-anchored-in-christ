Mesopotamia

Name
Mesopotamia
Modern Location
Iraq, between the Tigris and Euphrates
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The land between the rivers, Abraham's homeland, whose people were present at Pentecost.
Biblical Significance
Mesopotamia is where the patriarchs began, and whose sons heard the gospel in their own tongue at Pentecost.
Key Scriptures
Acts 2:9; Acts 7:2
