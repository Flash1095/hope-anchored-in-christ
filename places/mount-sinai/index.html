Mount Sinai

Name
Mount Sinai
Modern Location
Sinai Peninsula, Egypt (traditionally Jebel Musa)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The mountain of God, wrapped in thunder and smoke, where Moses received the Ten Commandments and Israel became a covenant nation.
Biblical Significance
Sinai reveals God's holiness - the mountain no one could touch. But the law given there was a tutor leading to Christ, showing us our need for the grace that would come.
Key Scriptures
Exodus 19:16-20; Exodus 20:1-17; Galatians 3:24
Related Books
Exodus; Deuteronomy; Galatians
Related People
Moses; Aaron
Related Events
The Ten Commandments; The Golden Calf; The Burning Bush
Related Places
Egypt; Midian; Kadesh Barnea
