Description
Shaveh-kiriathaim was the plain or valley of Kiriathaim where Chedorlaomer and his allied kings defeated the Emim in the days of Abraham.
Biblical Significance
The place belongs to the campaign that led to Lot's capture and Abraham's rescue: the first great battle named in Genesis.
