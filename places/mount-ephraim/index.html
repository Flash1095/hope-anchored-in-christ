Mount Ephraim

Name
Mount Ephraim
Modern Location
Hill country of Ephraim, central Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The central hill country settled by Ephraim, home of Joshua at Timnath-serah and of Micah's idols and the Levite's concubine.
Biblical Significance
Mount Ephraim is the heartland of the central tribes, the hill country of Joshua's inheritance.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 17:15; Judges 2:9; Judges 17:1
Related Books
Joshua; Judges
