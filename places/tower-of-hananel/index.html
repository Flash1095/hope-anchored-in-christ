Tower of Hananel

View on map →

The Tower of Hananel was a tower on Jerusalem's northern wall, named in Nehemiah's wall-building account and in the prophecies of Jerusalem's restoration.

Biblical Significance

The tower is a landmark of the rebuilt wall: a point on the line Nehemiah's builders restored and the prophets said would be rebuilt.

Details

Name: Tower of Hananel
Modern Location: Jerusalem, northern wall area
Testament: Old
Timeline Era: Return and Second Temple
Key Scriptures: Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 12:39; Jeremiah 31:38; Zechariah 14:10
Related Books: Nehemiah; Jeremiah; Zechariah
