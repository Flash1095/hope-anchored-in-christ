River Jabbok

View on map →

Name
River Jabbok
Modern Location
Wadi Zarqa, Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The tributary of the Jordan that Jacob crossed after wrestling with God, marking the boundary of Gilead and Ammon.
Biblical Significance
Jabbok is the ford of the wrestling night, where Jacob became Israel.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 32:22; Numbers 21:24; Deuteronomy 3:16
Related Books
Genesis; Numbers; Deuteronomy
Related People
Jacob; Moses
