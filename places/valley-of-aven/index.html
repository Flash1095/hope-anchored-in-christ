Name

Modern Location

Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
The Valley of Aven, the 'valley of wickedness', was named in Amos's oracle against Damascus, whose people would go into captivity and whose king's house would fall.
Biblical Significance
The valley is an oracle name: a place of idolatry in Amos's judgment on Syria, not a valley elsewhere described in the narrative.
Key Scriptures
Amos 1:5
