Ebron

View on map →

Ebron was a town in Asher's allotment, named in the town list of Joshua 19. No further story is attached to it in Scripture, and its exact site is unidentified.

Biblical Significance

Its inclusion in the allotment list marks it as one of the towns assigned to Asher.
