Description
The place where David smote the Philistines after inquiring of the LORD, and named it Baal-perazim, 'the place of breakings', because the LORD had broken forth upon his enemies as waters break forth.
Biblical Significance
Baal-perazim is David's breakthrough: prayer first, then a breach like floodwaters, and the enemy's images left behind to be burned.
Key Scriptures
2 Samuel 5:20; 1 Chronicles 14:11
