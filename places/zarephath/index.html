Zarephath

Name
Zarephath
Modern Location
Sarafand, Lebanon
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
The Phoenician town between Tyre and Sidon where Elijah was fed by a widow whose oil and meal did not run out, and which Obadiah names as a place of returning exiles. Called Sarepta in the New Testament.
Biblical Significance
Zarephath is where a Gentile widow fed the prophet, and the barrel did not fail.
Key Scriptures
1 Kings 17:9; Obadiah 1:20; Luke 4:26
Related Books
1 Kings; Obadiah; Luke
Related People
Elijah; Jesus
