Jordan River

Name
Jordan River
Modern Location
Israel / Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The river Israel crossed on dry ground to enter the promised land, its waters piled up as the priests' feet touched them. Centuries later John baptized in the Jordan, and Jesus Himself was baptized here.
Biblical Significance
The Jordan is Scripture's river of transition - from wilderness to promise, from old life to new. Every crossing says the same thing: God makes a way through.
