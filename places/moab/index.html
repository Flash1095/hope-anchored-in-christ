Moab

View on map →

The kingdom east of the Dead Sea, descended from Lot. Moab opposed Israel in the wilderness, oppressed Israel in the days of the judges, and is judged at length by Isaiah and Jeremiah; yet Ruth the Moabitess became the ancestress of David.

Biblical Significance

Moab holds both warning and grace: a nation under judgment, and a Moabite woman grafted into the line of the Messiah.
