Serpent’s Stone

View on map →

The Serpent's Stone, the stone of Zoheleth, stood by En-rogel, where Adonijah sacrificed sheep, oxen, and fat cattle and invited his brothers and the king's servants while claiming the throne.

Biblical Significance

The stone marks Adonijah's rival feast on the edge of Jerusalem, held while David still lived and Solomon's anointing was yet to come.
