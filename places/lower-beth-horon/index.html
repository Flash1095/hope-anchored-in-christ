Lower Beth-horon

View on map →

Name
Lower Beth-horon
Modern Location
Beit Ur al Tahta
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The lower of the two Beth-horons, on Ephraim's border and the road down from the hills, built by Sherah daughter of Ephraim and later fortified by Solomon with walls, gates and bars.
Biblical Significance
Lower Beth-horon guarded the descent from the hill country to the coastal plain, a town built by a woman and fortified by a king.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 16:3; Joshua 18:13; 1 Kings 9:17; 1 Chronicles 7:24; 2 Chronicles 8:5
Related Books
Joshua; 1 Kings; 1 Chronicles; 2 Chronicles
Related People
King Solomon; Sherah
