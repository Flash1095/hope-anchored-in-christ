Description
The hill before Giah by the way of the wilderness of Gibeon, reached at sunset as Joab and Abishai pursued Abner after the battle at Gibeon.
Biblical Significance
Ammah is a sunset marker in a civil war: the pursuit stopped there as darkness fell, and the fighting paused for the night.

2 Samuel 2:24
