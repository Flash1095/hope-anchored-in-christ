Zererah

View on map →

Zererah, also called Zererath, was a place toward which the Midianite army fled after Gideon's night attack, on the route toward Beth-shittah and Abel-meholah.

Biblical Significance

Zererah is a name on the Midianites' flight route, marking the pursuit after Gideon's victory.
