View on map →

Name
Maareh-geba
Modern Location
Near Gibeah (Tel el Ful area)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The open ground near Gibeah, called 'the meadows of Gibeah' in the KJV, from which Israel's ambush broke out in the war against Benjamin at Baal-tamar.
Biblical Significance
Maareh-geba is where the ambush waited: the meadow that hid the men who turned the battle for Benjamin.
Key Scriptures
Judges 20:33
