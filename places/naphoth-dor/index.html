Name
Naphoth-dor
Modern Location
Region around Tel Dor
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The heights and coastland district around Dor, named in Joshua's conquest lists and in Solomon's districts, where Ben-abinadab held all the region of Dor.
Biblical Significance
Naphoth-dor is Dor's hinterland: not the port city itself, but the heights and coast around it that made it worth holding.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 11:2; Joshua 12:23; 1 Kings 4:11
Related Books
Joshua; 1 Kings
Related People
Joshua; Ben-Abinadab, Officer of Solomon
