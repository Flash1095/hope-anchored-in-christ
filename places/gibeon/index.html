Gibeon

View on map →

Name
Gibeon
Modern Location
Near Jerusalem, West Bank (el-Jib)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The Hivite city that tricked Israel into a treaty, then was defended by Joshua when the sun stood still. Later Solomon worshipped here, and God appeared to him in a dream offering anything he asked.
Biblical Significance
Gibeon reminds us that even our mistakes become God's instruments - Israel's foolish treaty became the stage for the longest day in history.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 9:14-15; Joshua 10:12-13; 1 Kings 3:5-9
