Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The stone Samuel set up between Mizpah and Shen after Israel's victory over the Philistines, naming it Ebenezer, 'hitherto hath the LORD helped us,' in 1 Samuel 7:12.
Biblical Significance
It is a memorial of God's help, not a town: the stone that gave its name to remembrance after the earlier defeat at Ebenezer.
