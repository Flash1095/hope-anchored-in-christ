Mareshah

Name
Mareshah
Modern Location
Tel Maresha, Shephelah, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A fortified city of Judah where Asa defeated Zerah the Ethiopian's vast army in the Valley of Zephathah.
Biblical Significance
Mareshah is where Asa's prayer met an army of a million, and God gave the victory.
Key Scriptures
2 Chronicles 11:8; 2 Chronicles 14:9
