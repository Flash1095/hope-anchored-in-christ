Life of Christ
Description
The Galilean hill town where Jesus grew up - 'Can anything good come from Nazareth?' His neighbors knew Him as the carpenter's son and rejected Him.
Biblical Significance
Nazareth reminds us that God hides glory in obscurity. Thirty hidden years in a despised town preceded three years that changed the world.
Key Scriptures
Matthew 2:23; Luke 4:16-24; John 1:46
