Beth-haccherem

View on map →

Name
Beth-haccherem
Modern Location
Ramat Rahel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Description
A district whose ruler Malchiah repaired part of Jerusalem's wall, and where Jeremiah tells Benjamin to blow the trumpet and set up a sign of fire, for evil appears out of the north.
Biblical Significance
Beth-haccherem is both a warning post and a repair crew: a signal hill for invasion, and a district whose men rebuilt the wall.
Key Scriptures
Nehemiah 3:14; Jeremiah 6:1
Related Books
Nehemiah; Jeremiah
Related People
