Aijalon

View on map →

Name
Aijalon
Modern Location
Yalo, Aijalon Valley, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A Levitical city in Dan's territory, in the valley where Joshua commanded the sun to stand still and where Saul pursued the Philistines.
Biblical Significance
Aijalon's valley is where the sun stood still over Gibeon and the moon over Aijalon, in Israel's longest day.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 10:12; Joshua 21:24; 1 Samuel 14:31
Related Books
Joshua; 1 Samuel
Related People
