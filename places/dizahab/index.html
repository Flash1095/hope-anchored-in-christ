Dizahab

View on map →

A place named in the opening geography of Deuteronomy, among the wilderness landmarks opposite the Red Sea, between Paran and Tophel, Laban, Hazeroth and Dizahab.

Biblical Significance

Dizahab is a wilderness signpost in Deuteronomy's first verse: one of the small names fixing where Moses spoke his second law.
