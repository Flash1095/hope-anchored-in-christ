Nicopolis

Name
Nicopolis
Modern Location
Nicopolis, Epirus
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Description
The city in Epirus where Paul planned to spend the winter and told Titus to come to him, after leaving Titus in Crete to set the churches in order.
Biblical Significance
Nicopolis is a winter plan in a letter: the place Paul meant to wait out the cold, and where he hoped to see Titus again.
Key Scriptures
Titus 3:12
Related Books
Titus
Related People
