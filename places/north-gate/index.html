Name
North Gate
Modern Location
Jerusalem (temple vision)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Description
The north gate of the temple in Ezekiel's vision, measured and described among the gates of the new temple, where the glory of the LORD entered and where the prince would eat bread.
Biblical Significance
The North Gate belongs to Ezekiel's vision of a restored house: measured, guarded, and open to the glory's return.
Key Scriptures
Ezekiel 40:35; Ezekiel 40:40; Ezekiel 40:44; Ezekiel 44:4; Ezekiel 46:9
