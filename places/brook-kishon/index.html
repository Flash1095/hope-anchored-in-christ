Description
The seasonal stream of the Jezreel Valley that swept away Sisera's army and beside which Elijah slew Baal's prophets.
Biblical Significance
Kishon is the ancient river that swept the Canaanite host away, and where Baal's prophets fell.
Key Scriptures
Judges 4:7; Judges 5:21; 1 Kings 18:40
