Philistia

Name
Philistia
Modern Location
Southern coastal plain of Canaan, modern Gaza Strip and southern Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The coastal territory of the Philistines and their five cities, Israel's near-constant rival from the judges through David.
Biblical Significance
Philistia is the stage of the Samson stories, the capture of the ark, and David's long wars before he subdued it.
Key Scriptures
Exodus 13:17; Judges 14:1; 1 Samuel 17:1
Related Books
Exodus; Judges; 1 Samuel
Related People
Samson; King David
