Description
Upper Beth-horon was the upper of the two Beth-horons, a border town of Ephraim and a fortified city rebuilt by Solomon, on the ascent where God threw down hailstones on the Amorites.
Biblical Significance
Upper Beth-horon, with its lower town, guards the famous ascent: the road of Joshua's victory and Solomon's fortification.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 16:5; 2 Chronicles 8:5
