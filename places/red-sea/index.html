Red Sea

Name
Red Sea
Modern Location
Red Sea, between Egypt and Arabia
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The sea God parted for Israel's escape from Egypt and closed over Pharaoh's army, also called the Sea of Reeds.
Biblical Significance
The Red Sea is the water of deliverance, where Israel walked dry-shod and sang the song of Moses.
Key Scriptures
Exodus 14:21; Exodus 15:1; Numbers 33:10; Exodus 15:4; 1 Kings 9:26; Acts 7:36; Hebrews 11:29
Related Books
