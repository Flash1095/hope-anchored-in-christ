Description
The oak under Bethel beneath which Deborah, Rebekah's nurse, died and was buried, and which was called Allon-bachuth, 'the oak of weeping'.
Biblical Significance
Allon-bacuth is grief on the road home: Jacob buried a faithful old servant under an oak, and the tree kept her memory in its name.
