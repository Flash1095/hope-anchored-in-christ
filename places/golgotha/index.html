Golgotha

Name
Golgotha
Modern Location
Jerusalem
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
'The Place of the Skull' - the hill outside Jerusalem's walls where Jesus was crucified between two criminals, with a nearby tomb where He was buried and rose.
Biblical Significance
Golgotha is the anchor point of the Christian hope - the place where justice and mercy met, sin was paid for, and death was defeated. Everything this blog proclaims flows from this hill.
