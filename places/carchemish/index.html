Carchemish

View on map →

The Euphrates fortress city where Pharaoh Necho was defeated by Nebuchadnezzar in 605 BC, the battle that made Babylon master of the Near East and set the course of Judah's exile.

Biblical Significance

Carchemish is the hinge of empires: Egypt fell there, and Judah's fate was sealed.
