Description
The King's Valley where the king of Sodom met Abram after Abram had rescued Lot and defeated the kings who had taken him. Abram came home from the battle with captives and goods recovered, and the meeting in the valley set the stage for his encounter with Melchizedek, king of Salem. The valley's location is uncertain and is left blank here; it is traditionally associated with the area near Salem, or Jerusalem, but that tradition is not asserted as certain.
Biblical Significance
Shaveh is the valley of homecoming after rescue. Abram had risked everything for his nephew Lot, and the king of Sodom came out to meet the victor in the King's Valley, offering a bargain over persons and goods. What follows in the chapter matters more than the bargain: Abram meets Melchizedek, is blessed, and refuses to be enriched by Sodom, so that no one could say a king of Sodom had made Abram rich.
