Description
The Sheep Gate was a gate of Jerusalem's wall, built by Eliashib the high priest and his fellow priests, and named in the dedication procession of Nehemiah's wall.
Biblical Significance
The gate is the priests' gate: built and sanctified by the priests at the start of Nehemiah's wall, beside the sheep market.
Key Scriptures
Nehemiah 3:1; Nehemiah 3:32; Nehemiah 12:39
