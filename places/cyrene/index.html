Shahhat, Libya

Life of Christ
Description
The great Greek city of North Africa, home of Simon who carried Jesus' cross, and of men who preached to Greeks at Antioch.
Biblical Significance
Cyrene gave the cross a bearer and Antioch some of its first Greek preachers.

Matthew 27:32; Acts 11:20; Acts 13:1
