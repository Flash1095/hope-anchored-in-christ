Hill Country of Judah

View on map →

Name
Hill Country of Judah
Modern Location
The hill country south of Jerusalem
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
The upland region of Judah, including the country around Hebron and the villages south of Jerusalem. Mary went into this hill country to visit Elisabeth, and the news of John's birth spread through all of it.
Biblical Significance
The hill country of Judah is where the births of John the Baptist and Jesus were first spoken of among neighbors.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 20:7; Luke 1:39
Related Books
Joshua; Luke
