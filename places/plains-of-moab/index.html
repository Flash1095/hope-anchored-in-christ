Plains of Moab

Name
Plains of Moab
Modern Location
Jordan, east of the Dead Sea
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The final camp of the wilderness generation, across the Jordan from Jericho. Here Moses preached Deuteronomy, viewed the promised land from Mount Nebo, and died.
Biblical Significance
The plains of Moab are where one generation ended and another began - Moses's farewell sermons handing the promise to those who would actually enter it.
Key Scriptures
Deuteronomy 1:1-5; Deuteronomy 34:1-5; Numbers 22:1
Related Books
Numbers; Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses; Joshua
Related Places
Jordan River; Jericho
