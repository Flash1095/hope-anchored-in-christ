Meshech

Name
Meshech
Modern Location
Unknown; traditionally Asia Minor or the Black Sea region
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
A northern people and land, joined with Tubal in the Table of Nations, in Tyre's trade, and in Ezekiel's oracle.
Biblical Significance
Meshech, named beside Tubal, belongs to the far north that prophecy gathers against God's people.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:2; Ezekiel 27:13; Ezekiel 38:2
Related Books
Genesis; Ezekiel
