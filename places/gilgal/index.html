Gilgal

View on map →

Israel's first camp inside the promised land, where twelve stones from the Jordan were set up as a memorial, the manna ceased, and the nation celebrated its first Passover in Canaan.

Biblical Significance

Gilgal was Israel's memorial ground - stones of remembrance for a generation prone to forget. Remembering what God has done anchors hope for what He will do.
