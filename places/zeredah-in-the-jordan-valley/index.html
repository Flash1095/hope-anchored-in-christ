Zeredah in the Jordan Valley

View on map →

Name
Zeredah in the Jordan Valley
Modern Location
Unknown; in the plain of Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
A place in the plain of Jordan named with Succoth, between which Solomon's temple vessels were cast in bronze; identified with Zarethan, distinct from Jeroboam's Zeredah.
Biblical Significance
Zeredah in the Jordan Valley belongs to the foundry ground of Solomon's temple work.
