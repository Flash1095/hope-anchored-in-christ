Beth-jeshimoth

View on map →

A Moabite town on the plains of Moab by the Jordan, where Israel made its last camp east of the Jordan, pitching from Beth-jeshimoth to Abel-shittim before crossing into the promised land. It later appears among the towns allotted to Reuben and among the frontier cities of Moab named in Ezekiel's oracle. Its modern site is commonly proposed as Tell el-Azeimeh in Jordan.

Biblical Significance

Beth-jeshimoth marks the threshold of the promised land: Israel's last camp before the Jordan crossing, and later a frontier town on Moab's border.
