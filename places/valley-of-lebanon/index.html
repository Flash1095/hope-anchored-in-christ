Valley of Lebanon

View on map →

Name
Valley of Lebanon
Modern Location
The Beqaa valley between the Lebanon and Anti-Lebanon ranges
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The valley under Mount Hermon, between the Lebanon ranges, named as the northern end of Joshua's conquests. It is commonly identified with the Beqaa valley. The plain of Aven in Amos may be the same valley, but that identification is not certain, so it is noted here rather than given a separate entry.
Biblical Significance
The valley of Lebanon bounds Joshua's land to the north, under Hermon.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 11:17; Joshua 12:7
