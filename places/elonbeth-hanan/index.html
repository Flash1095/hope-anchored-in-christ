Elonbeth-hanan

View on map →

A town in Solomon's district under Ben-deker, named with Makaz, Shaalbim, Beth-shemesh and Elon-beth-hanan.

Biblical Significance

Elonbeth-hanan is one of Solomon's provision towns: an ordinary settlement carrying a month of the king's table.
