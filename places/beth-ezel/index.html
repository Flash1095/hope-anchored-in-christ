Description
A town in Micah's lament over the towns of the lowland, which Micah says shall receive of Israel her standing when the invader comes.
Biblical Significance
Beth-ezel is a town in Micah's mourning roll: each town's name carrying its own sorrow as judgment marched through Judah.
