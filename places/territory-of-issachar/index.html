Description
The tribe takes its name from Issachar, son of Jacob and Leah (Genesis 30:18; 49:14-15). The allotment of the tribe Issachar, whose people profile is the Issachar entry in Ancient Civilizations, in the fertile Jezreel Valley region of the north, as described in general terms in Joshua 19:17-23. Its territory lay between Zebulun to the west, Naphtali to the north and Manasseh to the south, with the Jordan valley to its east; Joshua gives its boundary by neighbouring territories and named towns, not by a precise surveyed line, and no precise border is claimed here. Its key city among existing place entries is Jezreel, in the valley that bears its name. In Judges, the princes of Issachar stood with Deborah and Barak against Sisera.
Biblical Significance
Issachar's valley allotment put the tribe on a great east-west route through the land, and its leaders are remembered in Deborah's song as standing with Israel in battle.
