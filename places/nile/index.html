Nile

Name
Nile
Modern Location
Egypt
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The river of Egypt, turned to blood in the first plague, named in the prophets' oracles against Egypt and in the story of Moses' basket.
Biblical Significance
The Nile is Egypt's life and Egypt's judgment, bloodied and then restored.
Key Scriptures
Exodus 7:17; Isaiah 19:5; Ezekiel 29:3
