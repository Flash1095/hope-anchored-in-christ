Description
The small city that humiliated Israel after the triumph at Jericho - defeated because of Achan's hidden sin, then taken after the sin was judged.
Biblical Significance
Ai teaches that hidden sin stops a whole people. The defeat was devastating, but confession and cleansing turned it into victory. God restores what sin forfeits.
