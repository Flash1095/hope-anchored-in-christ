Mount Seir

Name
Mount Seir
Modern Location
Southern Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The mountain of Seir, home of the Horites and later of Esau's descendants, the land of Edom. Abram's war reached its slopes.
Biblical Significance
Seir is Esau's mountain, the land of the brother who sold his birthright.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 14:6; Genesis 36:8; Deuteronomy 2:1; Deuteronomy 2:5
Related Books
Genesis; Deuteronomy
Related People
Abraham; Jacob
