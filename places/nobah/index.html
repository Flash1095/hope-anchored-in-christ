Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The town Nobah captured and renamed after himself, east of the Jordan; the gazetteer identifies it with Kenath.
Biblical Significance
Nobah shows a conqueror renaming a captured town, a name carried into the record of the east-Jordan settlement.
