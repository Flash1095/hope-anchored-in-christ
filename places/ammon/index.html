Ammon

Name
Ammon
Modern Location
Jordan (Amman region)
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
The kingdom east of the Jordan, descended from Lot's younger son. Ammon warred with Israel from the judges to the exile, and its capital Rabbah fell to David.
Biblical Significance
Ammon is a standing reminder that old hostilities run deep, and that God judges nations as surely as individuals.
Key Scriptures
Deuteronomy 2:19; Judges 11:12; Jeremiah 49:1; Judges 11:4; 2 Samuel 11:1; 1 Kings 11:7
