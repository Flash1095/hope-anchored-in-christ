Gebal in Edom

View on map →

Name
Gebal in Edom
Modern Location
Between the Dead Sea and Petra, Jordan (region; exact site unidentified)
Testament
Old
Description
A Gebal named among the neighbours conspiring against Israel in Psalm 83, listed with Edom, Moab, Ammon and others of the southern lands — a different Gebal from the Phoenician port of Byblos in the north.
Biblical Significance
Psalm 83 names this southern Gebal among the peoples whose counsel against Israel the psalmist asks God to defeat; it shows the name belonged to more than one place, and the southern one must not be confused with Byblos.
Key Scriptures
Psalms 83:7
