Dan

Name
Dan
Modern Location
Tel Dan, northern Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
The northernmost city of Israel, settled by the tribe of Dan after they took Laish and renamed it, marking the proverbial border "from Dan to Beersheba."
Biblical Significance
Dan marks Israel's northern edge, and also its idolatry: Jeroboam set up a golden calf here.
Key Scriptures
Judges 18:29; 1 Kings 12:29
Related Books
Judges; 1 Kings
