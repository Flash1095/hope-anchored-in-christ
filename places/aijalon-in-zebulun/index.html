Aijalon in Zebulun

View on map →

Name
Aijalon in Zebulun
Modern Location
Disputed; Tel el Butmeh area, in Zebulun's allotment
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A town in Zebulun's allotment where the judge Elon was buried, named only in the burial notice of Judges 12:12.
Biblical Significance
Its single mention marks the burial place of Elon, one of the minor judges, in the northern tribal allotment, distinct from the Aijalon of the Aijalon Valley in the south.
Key Scriptures
Judges 12:12
