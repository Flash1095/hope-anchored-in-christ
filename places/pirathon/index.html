Conquest and Judges
Description
A town in Ephraim's hill country where Abdon son of Hillel, who judged Israel eight years, was buried, and the home of Benaiah the Pirathonite, one of David's mighty men and a commander of his monthly divisions.
Biblical Significance
Pirathon gave Israel a judge's grave and a king's commander: a hill town remembered through the men it raised.
Key Scriptures
Judges 12:13; Judges 12:15; 2 Samuel 23:30; 1 Chronicles 11:31; 1 Chronicles 27:14
