Zoar

Name
Zoar
Modern Location
Unknown; traditionally associated with the southeastern Dead Sea region
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The small city of the plain, also called Bela, to which Lot fled and which was spared at his plea.
Biblical Significance
Zoar is the city spared for one man's sake: Lot's refuge when Sodom burned, and the ancestor-city of Moab and Ammon's line.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 19:20; Genesis 19:30; Genesis 13:10; Genesis 19:22; Deuteronomy 34:3
Related Books
Genesis; Deuteronomy
Related People
Lot; Abraham
