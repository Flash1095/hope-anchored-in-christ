Name

Modern Location

Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A town on Ephraim's border, named as the border passed eastward to Taanath-shiloh and on to Janohah — an Ephraimite border town, distinct from the northern Janoah taken by Tiglath-pileser.
Biblical Significance
Janoah appears only in the boundary description of Joshua 16, fixing Ephraim's northern edge; it is the southern Janoah, not the Naphtali town of 2 Kings 15.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 16:6; Joshua 16:7
