Description
A hill on the north side of Timnath-serah, where Joshua was buried in the land of his inheritance.
Biblical Significance
Gaash marks the burial place of Joshua, in the hill country of Ephraim he was given.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 24:30; Judges 2:9
