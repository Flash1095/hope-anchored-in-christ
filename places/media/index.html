Timeline Era

Description
The kingdom of the Medes, joined with Persia under Cyrus. The "law of the Medes and Persians" could not be changed, and it was under a Median king that Daniel was thrown to the lions and delivered.
Biblical Significance
Media shows that no unchangeable human law can stand against God when He purposes to deliver His servant.
Key Scriptures
Daniel 5:28; Daniel 6:8; Esther 1:19
