Paddan Aram

View on map →

The "plain of Aram" where Abraham's kin lived, around Haran. Jacob fled here to Laban, married Leah and Rachel, and served twenty years before returning to Canaan.

Biblical Significance

Paddan Aram is the place of waiting and wrestling: God formed Jacob's family far from home before bringing them back.
