Modern Location
Ophel, Jerusalem
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
The steep hill in Jerusalem south of the temple, built up by Jotham, walled by Manasseh, repaired by the Nethinim under Nehemiah, and lived in by the temple servants after the return.
Biblical Significance
Ophel is Jerusalem's working hill: fortified by kings, repaired by servants, and lived in by the men who kept the temple running.
Key Scriptures
2 Chronicles 27:3; 2 Chronicles 33:14; Nehemiah 3:26-27; Nehemiah 11:21
