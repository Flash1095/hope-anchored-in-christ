Description
A town in Judah's hill country, listed in Joshua's allotment as 'Kerioth, and Hezron, which is Hazor,' and identified with the site at Khirbet el Qaryatein.
Biblical Significance
Kerioth-hezron is one of Judah's southern towns, named in the allotment that turned promise into addresses.
