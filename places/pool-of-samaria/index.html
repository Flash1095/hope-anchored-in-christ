Description
A pool in Samaria where Ahab's chariot was washed after his death at Ramoth-gilead, and the dogs licked up his blood, as the word of the LORD had said.
Biblical Significance
Samaria's pool is where Ahab's blood was washed from his chariot, fulfilling the word against him.
