Tekoa

Name
Tekoa
Modern Location
Teqoa, Judean hills, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A Judahite town south of Bethlehem, home of the prophet Amos and of the wise woman who pleaded for Absalom.
Biblical Significance
Tekoa is Amos's home among the herdsmen, and the town whose wise woman spoke truth to David.
Key Scriptures
2 Samuel 14:2; Amos 1:1; 2 Chronicles 11:6
Related Books
2 Samuel; Amos; 2 Chronicles
Related People
King David
