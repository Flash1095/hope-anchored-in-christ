Mount Hor

Name
Mount Hor
Modern Location
Jebel Harun, Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The mountain on Edom's border where Aaron died and Eleazar took his priestly garments, a station on the wilderness journey.
Biblical Significance
Hor is where the first high priest died in sight of the land, and the priesthood passed to his son.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 20:22; Numbers 33:37
Related Books

Related People
Aaron; Moses
