Dibon

Name
Dibon
Modern Location
Dhiban, Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
A Moabite city rebuilt by Gad, later famous for the Mesha Stele, the Moabite Stone, found there.
Biblical Significance
Dibon is where Mesha king of Moab recorded his revolt against Israel, a stone that still speaks.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 21:30; Numbers 32:34; Isaiah 15:2; Jeremiah 48:18
Related Books
Numbers; Isaiah; Jeremiah
Related People
