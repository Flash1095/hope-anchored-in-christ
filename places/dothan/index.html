Dothan

Name
Dothan
Modern Location
Tell Dothan, Samaria hills, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
A town on the road to Egypt where Joseph's brothers sold him, and where Elisha's servant saw the hills full of horses and chariots of fire.
Biblical Significance
Dothan is where Joseph was cast into a pit and where Elisha opened blind eyes to an army of fire.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 37:17; 2 Kings 6:13
Related Books
Genesis; 2 Kings
Related People
Joseph, Son of Jacob; Elisha
