Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
Ziphron was a town on the northern boundary of the land, named in the boundary list of Numbers 34, after which the border went out to Hazar-enan.
Biblical Significance
Ziphron is a boundary name: a northern marker in the list that drew the promised land's edge.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 34:9
