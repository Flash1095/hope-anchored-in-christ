Description
A city named in Ezekiel's prophecy of the cleansing of the land after the defeat of Gog: 'the name of the city shall be Hamonah.' It stands in the valley of Hamon-gog, the burial place of Gog's host.
Biblical Significance
Hamonah's name is part of the prophecy's picture of the land made clean after judgment, a city whose very name marks the scale of the burial.
