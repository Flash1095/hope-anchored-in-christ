Description
The place where Israel set themselves in array against Benjamin and lay in wait round about Gibeah, in the war over the outrage at Gibeah.
Biblical Significance
Baal-tamar is the battlefield of a civil war: Israel against Benjamin, with an ambush waiting beyond the town.
