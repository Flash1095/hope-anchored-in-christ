Bath-rabbim

View on map →

Name
Bath-rabbim
Modern Location
Heshbon
Testament
Old
Description
The gate of Heshbon by whose fishpools the beloved's eyes are compared in the Song: eyes like the pools in Heshbon, by the gate of Bath-rabbim.
Biblical Significance
Bath-rabbim is a gate turned to poetry: Heshbon's pools giving the Song its picture of clear, deep eyes.
Key Scriptures
Song of Solomon 7:4
Related Books
Song of Solomon
