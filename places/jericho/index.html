Jericho

View on map →

The fortified Canaanite city whose walls fell after Israel marched around them for seven days - the first conquest of the promised land. Later, Jesus healed blind Bartimaeus and called Zacchaeus down from his tree here.

Biblical Significance

Jericho proves that God's battle plans rarely look like ours. Marching, trumpets, and a shout - and the walls fell. Victory belongs to the Lord, not to conventional wisdom.
