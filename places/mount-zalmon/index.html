Mount Zalmon

View on map →

A mountain near Shechem from which Abimelech cut a bough and told his men to do as he had done, before setting fire to the tower of Shechem.

Biblical Significance

Zalmon is the mountain in Abimelech's attack on Shechem, a dark episode of Israel's judges.
