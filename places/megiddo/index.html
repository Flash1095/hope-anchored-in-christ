Megiddo

View on map →

Name
Megiddo
Modern Location
Tel Megiddo, Jezreel Valley, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A fortress city guarding the Jezreel pass, allotted to Manasseh, fortified by Solomon, where kings Ahaziah and Josiah died.
Biblical Significance
Megiddo is the crossroads city of armies, from Deborah's battle to Josiah's death, and the namesake of Armageddon.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 17:11; Judges 5:19; 2 Kings 23:29
Related Books
Joshua; Judges; 2 Kings
Related People
Deborah; Barak; King Solomon
