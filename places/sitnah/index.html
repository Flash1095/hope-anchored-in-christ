Name

Modern Location

Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
Sitnah, 'contention', was the second well Isaac's servants dug in the Gerar valley, over which the herdsmen of Gerar also strove, so Isaac named it for the strife and moved on.
Biblical Significance
Sitnah is the second of Isaac's disputed wells, between Esek and Rehoboth: contention before room was found.
