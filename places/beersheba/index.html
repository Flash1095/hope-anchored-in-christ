Beersheba

Name

Modern Location
Southern Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
The southernmost city of settled Israel - 'from Dan to Beersheba' marked the nation's full extent. Abraham planted a tamarisk tree here and called on the name of the Lord, the Eternal God.
Biblical Significance
Beersheba means 'well of the oath.' The patriarchs kept digging wells and making covenants here, learning that God's promises hold even at the desert's edge.
