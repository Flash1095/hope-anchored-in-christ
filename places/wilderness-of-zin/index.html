Wilderness of Zin

Name
Wilderness of Zin
Modern Location
Desert south of Canaan around Kadesh, modern Negev
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The desert wilderness bordering Edom where Israel camped at Kadesh, and from which the spies entered the land.
Biblical Significance
Zin is the wilderness of Kadesh: Miriam died here, Moses struck the rock here, and the spies set out from here.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 13:21; Numbers 20:1; Numbers 27:14
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Moses; Aaron
