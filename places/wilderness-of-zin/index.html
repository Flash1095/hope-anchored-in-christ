Wilderness of Zin

View on map →

The desert wilderness bordering Edom where Israel camped at Kadesh, and from which the spies entered the land.

Biblical Significance

Zin is the wilderness of Kadesh: Miriam died here, Moses struck the rock here, and the spies set out from here.

Details
