Hazar-enan

View on map →

Name
Hazar-enan
Modern Location
Al Qaryatayn
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
A station on the northern border of the promised land, named in the wilderness boundary list and again in Ezekiel's vision of the restored land's borders.
Biblical Significance
Hazar-enan anchors the north edge of the land in both the wilderness promise and the exile's vision of return.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 34:9-10; Ezekiel 47:17; Ezekiel 48:1
Related Books
Numbers; Ezekiel
Related People
Moses; Ezekiel
