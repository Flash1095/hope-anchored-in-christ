Ascent of Adummim

View on map →

A pass on the border between Judah and Benjamin, on the way up from the Jordan valley toward Jerusalem. It is traditionally the Jerusalem-Jericho road, but that identification is a tradition, not a certainty.

Biblical Significance

Adummim's ascent is a border pass on the road between Jericho and Jerusalem.
