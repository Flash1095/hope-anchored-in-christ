Amana

View on map →

Name
Amana
Modern Location
Jebel Zabadani, near Lebanon
Testament
Old
Description
A mountain top named with Shenir and Hermon, from which the bride in the Song is called to look, among lions' dens and mountains of leopards.
Biblical Significance
Amana belongs to the Song's high places: a peak of Lebanon from which love calls the beloved down into safety.
Key Scriptures
Song of Solomon 4:8
Related Books
Song of Solomon
