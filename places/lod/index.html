Lod

Name
Lod
Modern Location
Lod, central Israel
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Description
A Benjamite town rebuilt by returned exiles, on the road between Joppa and Jerusalem. Called Lydda in the New Testament.
Biblical Significance
Lod is one of the towns the exiles rebuilt, a waypoint on the road from the coast to Jerusalem.
Key Scriptures
Ezra 2:33; Nehemiah 11:35; Acts 9:32; Acts 9:35
Related Books
Ezra; Nehemiah; Acts
Related People
Ezra; Nehemiah; Simon Peter
