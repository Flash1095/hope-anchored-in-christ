Goshen

Name
Goshen
Modern Location
Eastern Nile Delta, Egypt
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The fertile delta region Pharaoh gave to Joseph's family - the best of the land. Israel lived here for generations, growing from a family into a people, until Goshen's plenty became Egypt's prison.
Biblical Significance
Goshen shows how blessing can curdle into bondage when we settle for less than God's promise. Comfort in Egypt is still Egypt.
