Rabbah

Name
Rabbah
Modern Location
Amman, Jordan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Description
The capital of Ammon, where David's army besieged the city while he stayed in Jerusalem, and where Og's great iron bedstead was kept.
Biblical Significance
Rabbah is Ammon's capital, the city of David's siege and his great sin.
Key Scriptures
Deuteronomy 3:11; 2 Samuel 11:1; Jeremiah 49:2; 2 Samuel 12:26
Related Books
Deuteronomy; 2 Samuel; Jeremiah
Related People
King David; Og; Bathsheba
