Arabah

View on map →

Name
Arabah
Modern Location
The Jordan Rift Valley from the Sea of Galilee to the Gulf of Aqaba
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The long rift valley running south from the Dead Sea, crossed by Israel on the wilderness journey and marking the eastern edge of Judah.
Biblical Significance
The Arabah frames the final approach to the Promised Land and the setting of Moses' farewell speeches.
Key Scriptures
Deuteronomy 1:1; Deuteronomy 3:17; Joshua 18:18
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Joshua
Related People
Moses
