Babylon

Name
Babylon
Modern Location
Near Hillah, Iraq
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Description
The glittering empire that destroyed Jerusalem and carried Judah into exile - and the city where Daniel served kings, his friends survived the furnace, and God's people learned to sing the Lord's song in a strange land.
Biblical Significance
Babylon is Scripture's symbol of worldly power opposed to God - yet God ruled there too, humbling Nebuchadnezzar and raising Daniel. No exile is beyond His reach.
Key Scriptures
2 Kings 25:8-11; Daniel 2:47; Daniel 3:28-29
