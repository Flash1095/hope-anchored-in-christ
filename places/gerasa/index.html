Gerasa

Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
A city of the Decapolis east of the Jordan, in the region where Jesus cast out the legion of demons and sent them into a herd of swine. The naming of this region varies in the Gospel manuscripts: the on-site KJV reads 'the country of the Gadarenes' in Mark 5:1 and 'the country of the Gergesenes' in Matthew 8:28, while Gerasa is the Decapolis city named in the variant tradition for the same region and event. The healed man was told to tell his friends what the Lord had done for him, and he published it throughout Decapolis.
Biblical Significance
Gerasa is where Jesus' power over the demonic was displayed in Gentile territory, and where a healed outcast became the region's first witness.
