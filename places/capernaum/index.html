Capernaum

View on map →

Name
Capernaum
Modern Location
Northern Israel (Sea of Galilee)
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
The fishing town Jesus made His home base - 'His own town.' Peter's house was here; the synagogue where He taught stands in ruins still. More miracles happened here than anywhere else.
Biblical Significance
Capernaum received the most light - and Jesus warned it faced the greatest accountability. Privilege without repentance becomes judgment.
Key Scriptures
Matthew 4:13; Matthew 9:1; Matthew 11:23
