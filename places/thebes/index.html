Thebes

View on map →

Called No in the King James Version, the great southern capital of Egypt and centre of Amun worship, named by Jeremiah, Ezekiel, and Nahum.

Biblical Significance

Thebes, no less than Nineveh, is held up as a city whose greatness could not save it.
