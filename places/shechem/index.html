Shechem

View on map →

Name
Shechem
Modern Location
Near Nablus, West Bank
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Description
One of the first places Abraham camped in Canaan, where God appeared and promised the land to his offspring. Centuries later Joshua renewed the covenant here, and Joseph's bones were finally buried at Shechem.
Biblical Significance
Shechem was a place of altars and choices: Abraham's altar, Jacob's purging of idols, Joshua's 'choose this day whom you will serve.'
Key Scriptures
Genesis 12:6-7; Genesis 35:2-4; Joshua 24:14-15
