Hormah

Name
Hormah
Modern Location
Unknown; in the Negev, Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
The place in the Negev where Israel, after the spies' failure, was beaten back, and later destroyed the Canaanites in fulfillment of a vow.
Biblical Significance
Hormah is the place of both defeat and vow: Israel's presumption failed there, and its later obedience destroyed the cities.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 14:45; Numbers 21:3; Judges 1:17
Related Books
Numbers; Judges
Related People
Moses
