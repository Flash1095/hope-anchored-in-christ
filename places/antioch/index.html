Antioch

Name
Antioch
Modern Location
Antakya, Turkey
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Description
The Syrian metropolis where believers were first called 'Christians.' From here Paul and Barnabas were sent out, and the gospel crossed decisively into the Gentile world.
Biblical Significance
Antioch proves the church was always meant to go - not a Jerusalem sect but a world mission. The name 'Christian' was born where the gospel crossed cultures.
Key Scriptures
Acts 11:25-26; Acts 13:1-3
