Waters of Nephtoah

View on map →

Name
Waters of Nephtoah
Modern Location
Unknown; near Jerusalem, on the Judah-Benjamin border
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A spring on the border between Judah and Benjamin, named in the boundary descriptions of both tribes. It is traditionally associated with Lifta, west of Jerusalem, but that identification is not certain.
Biblical Significance
Nephtoah's waters mark the border spring between Judah and Benjamin.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 15:9; Joshua 18:15
