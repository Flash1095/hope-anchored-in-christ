Dor

View on map →

Name
Dor
Modern Location
Unknown; on the coast south of Mount Carmel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Description
A coastal town in Manasseh's portion, whose Canaanite king joined Jabin's alliance against Joshua, and which Manasseh failed to take. It remained a Canaanite town on the coast.
Biblical Significance
Dor is a coastal town Manasseh could not take, left among the unconquered cities.
Key Scriptures
Joshua 17:11; Judges 1:27
Related Books
Joshua; Judges
