Description
Rimmono was a Levitical town in Zebulun's allotment, given to the Merarites, named in the same list-form as Rimmon in Joshua.
Biblical Significance
Its inclusion in the Levitical allotment marks it as one of the towns set apart for the Levites' dwelling and pasturelands.
