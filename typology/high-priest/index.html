The High Priest

Type
The High Priest
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Exodus 28:1; Leviticus 16:32-34; Psalm 110:4
Fulfillment
Aaron and his sons entered God's presence on Israel's behalf, bearing the people's names over their hearts, yet they had to offer sacrifices for their own sins first and repeat them year after year. Jesus is our great High Priest forever: sinless, He offered Himself once for all and now always lives to intercede for us.
Fulfillment Passage
Hebrews 4:14-16; Hebrews 7:23-28; Hebrews 9:24-28
Explanation
The high priest showed that sinners need a mediator who is both set apart for God and able to sympathize with human weakness. Jesus fulfills both perfectly, so we can approach God's throne of grace with confidence, exactly the anchored hope Hebrews describes.
