The Red Sea Crossing

Type
The Red Sea Crossing
Category
Event
Old Testament Passage
Exodus 14:13-31
Fulfillment
Israel passed through the sea, rescued from Pharaoh's army, on their way from slavery to the promised land, and Paul calls this experience a kind of baptism into Moses. In Christ, believers pass through the waters of baptism into a greater exodus, freed from sin's tyranny and raised to walk in newness of life.
Fulfillment Passage
Romans 6:3-4; 1 Corinthians 10:1-2; Colossians 2:12
Explanation
The sea that saved Israel destroyed the power that enslaved them; God made a way where there was no way, through water no one could cross alone. Baptism proclaims the same pattern: behind us, a defeated enemy and a buried old life; ahead of us, resurrection life with Christ.
