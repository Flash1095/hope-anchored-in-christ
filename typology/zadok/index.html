Zadok

Type
Zadok
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
1 Samuel 2:35; 1 Kings 1:32-39; 1 Kings 2:35; 1 Kings 4:4
Fulfillment
When Abiathar sided with Adonijah, Zadok stayed loyal to David, anointed Solomon, and took the priesthood the LORD had promised to 'a faithful priest.' If he pictures Christ at all, it is in the faithful-priest office — but Zadok's story is mostly loyalty to a king, and his priestly work itself is barely narrated.
Fulfillment Passage
Hebrews 2:17; Hebrews 7:26
Explanation
BORDERLINE — doubt: the faithful-priest correspondence is real in outline — 1 Samuel 2:35 promises a priest who 'shall walk before mine anointed for ever,' and Zadok fills that seat — but Zadok's own narrative is that of a loyal supporter, not a priest whose ministry prefigures Christ's sacrifice or intercession in any detail. The office maps; the story barely does. TRADITIONAL reading (doubtful): the priest who stayed faithful when the other priest chose the usurper.
