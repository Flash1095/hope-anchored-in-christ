The Feast of Weeks

Type
The Feast of Weeks
Category
Institution
Old Testament Passage
Leviticus 23:15-22; Deuteronomy 16:9-12
Fulfillment
Fifty days after the first sheaf was waved, Israel kept the Feast of Weeks: a harvest feast with two wave loaves, a freewill offering, and a command to leave gleanings for the poor and the stranger. On that very feast day in Jerusalem, the Spirit fell, the apostles preached in many tongues, and three thousand souls were added — the firstfruits of a worldwide harvest.
Fulfillment Passage
Acts 2:1-4; Acts 2:41
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: the New Testament never calls the Feast of Weeks a type, but the fulfillment lands on the feast's own day — Pentecost is the Greek name for Weeks — and the harvest pattern fits: Firstfruits (already a live entry, Christ risen) is followed fifty days later by a gathered harvest, now the church. The two loaves, offered with leaven unlike any other offering, are traditionally read as Jew and Gentile gathered into one harvest. This entry is distinct from the Feast of Firstfruits entry (the sheaf, Christ's resurrection): this is the fiftieth-day harvest feast.
