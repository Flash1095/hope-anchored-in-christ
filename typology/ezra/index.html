Ezra

Type
Ezra
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Ezra 7:6-10; Ezra 9:5-15; Nehemiah 8:1-8
Fulfillment
Ezra prepared his heart to seek the Law, to do it, and to teach it; he led exiles home from captivity and stood between a sinful people and God in prayer. Jesus is the Teacher who fulfills the Law He teaches, leads His people out of a deeper captivity, and ever intercedes for them.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 5:17; Luke 24:27; Hebrews 7:25
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: Ezra is never called a type in the New Testament, but the pattern is close — priest and 'ready scribe in the law of Moses' (Ezra 7:6) who sought, did, and taught the Law (Ezra 7:10), brought the captives home, and confessed the people's sin as his own (Ezra 9:6). The scribe who teaches with authority and the priest who intercedes meet in Christ, who is both.
