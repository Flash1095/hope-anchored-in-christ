The Branch

Type
The Branch
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Isaiah 11:1; Jeremiah 23:5-6; Zechariah 3:8; Zechariah 6:12-13
Fulfillment
When David's line looked cut down to a stump, the prophets promised a Branch from Jesse's roots — a righteous shoot who would grow up, build the temple, bear the glory, and sit and rule on His throne as priest and king at once. Paul quotes the Root of Jesse as the one in whom the Gentiles hope, and Revelation names Jesus the Root and the Offspring of David.
Fulfillment Passage
Romans 15:12; Revelation 22:16; Luke 1:32-33
Explanation
EXPLICIT warrant for the figure: Romans 15:12 quotes Isaiah 11:10 ('the Root of Jesse') of Christ, and Revelation 22:16 names Him the Root and Offspring of David. The Branch and the Root of Jesse are the same Jesse-tree image, so they are one entry, not two; the temple-building and priest-king details come from Zechariah's Branch passages.
