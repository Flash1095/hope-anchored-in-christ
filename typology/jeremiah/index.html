Jeremiah

Type
Jeremiah
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Jeremiah 1:4-10; Jeremiah 20:7-9
Fulfillment
Jeremiah, called before he was born to speak God's word to a people who would not listen, was mocked, beaten, and imprisoned for it, and wept over the city he could not save. Jesus, the prophet rejected by His own, wept over Jerusalem in almost the same words and was likewise rejected for telling the truth.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 23:37-39; Luke 19:41-44
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: the 'weeping prophet' and the weeping Saviour stand in the same posture toward the same city, both sent to a people who would not be gathered. Jeremiah is never called a type in the New Testament, so this is stated as traditional.
