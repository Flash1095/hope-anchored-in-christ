The Feast of Unleavened Bread

Type
The Feast of Unleavened Bread
Category
Institution
Old Testament Passage
Exodus 12:15-20; Exodus 13:6-7; Leviticus 23:6-8
Fulfillment
For seven days after Passover, Israel ate bread without leaven and cleared every trace of leaven from their houses — a week of clean bread for a people just delivered. Paul takes the feast up directly: Christ our passover is sacrificed for us, therefore let us keep the feast with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.
Fulfillment Passage
1 Corinthians 5:7-8; Luke 22:19
Explanation
EXPLICIT warrant for the feast-connection (1 Corinthians 5:7-8 ties the feast to Christ's sacrifice); the further reading — leaven as sin, unleavened bread as Christ's sinlessness — is traditional and stated as such. This is distinct from the Passover Lamb entry (the lamb) because the bread is the feast's own element, kept for seven days.
