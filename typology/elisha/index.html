Elisha

Type
Elisha
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
2 Kings 4:1-37; 2 Kings 5:1-14; 2 Kings 6:1-7; 2 Kings 13:20-21
Fulfillment
Elisha, given a double portion of Elijah's spirit, multiplied a widow's oil, raised a dead boy, healed a Gentile leper, fed a hundred men from twenty loaves, and even after his own death a corpse thrown into his grave came back to life. His ministry reads like a rehearsal of the miracles of Jesus — provision, healing, raising the dead — down to the feeding of a multitude from too little bread.
Fulfillment Passage
Luke 4:27; Matthew 11:4-5; Luke 7:22
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: Jesus Himself pointed to Naaman's healing when He spoke of a prophet unwelcome at home, and His answer to John's disciples — the blind see, the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, the dead are raised — matches Elisha's wonders point for point. Elisha is never called a type in the New Testament, so the reading is stated as traditional.
