Solomon's Temple

Type
Solomon's Temple
Category
Institution
Old Testament Passage
1 Kings 6:1; 1 Kings 8:27-30; 2 Chronicles 3:1
Fulfillment
Jesus spoke of His own body as the temple that would be destroyed and raised in three days, and He claimed something greater than the temple was here. Through His resurrection, God's dwelling is no longer a building in Jerusalem but Christ Himself and, in Him, His people built together as a living temple.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 12:6; John 2:19-22; 1 Corinthians 3:16; Ephesians 2:21-22; 1 Peter 2:4-5
Explanation
Built on Mount Moriah, where Abraham once offered Isaac, the temple was the meeting place of heaven and earth, filled with God's glory at its dedication. Christ fulfills it by being the true meeting place, and one day the whole new creation becomes God's temple, with no building needed at all.
