Isaac

Type
Isaac
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Genesis 21:1-7; Genesis 22:1-19
Fulfillment
Isaac, the beloved only son of promise, who carried the wood for his own sacrifice up Mount Moriah and was received back as from the dead, prefigures Jesus, the beloved Son given by the Father, who carried His cross and rose again.
Fulfillment Passage
Hebrews 11:17-19; Galatians 4:28; John 3:16
Explanation
Abraham offered his only son Isaac on Mount Moriah, and Isaac carried the wood for the offering (Genesis 22:6) as Jesus carried His cross. Hebrews says Abraham received Isaac back 'in a figure' of resurrection (Hebrews 11:19), and Paul calls believers children of promise 'like Isaac' (Galatians 4:28). Isaac is the son of promise and heir through whom the covenant line runs.
