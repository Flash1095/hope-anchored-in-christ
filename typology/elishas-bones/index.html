Elisha's Bones

Type
Elisha's Bones
Category
Object
Old Testament Passage
2 Kings 13:20-21
Fulfillment
After Elisha died and was buried, a dead man cast hastily into his grave touched the prophet's bones and came back to life, standing on his feet. Life came through contact with the dead — from a grave, unexpectedly, to a man who had no hope of it.
Fulfillment Passage
John 11:25; Romans 6:5
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading, stated as such: a single verse with no New Testament citation, offered as a pattern parallel only — life through contact with death, matching the claim that believers are planted together with Christ in the likeness of His death and raised in the likeness of His resurrection. The bones are not a redeemer; the parallel is in the mechanism, life from death.
Related Books
2 Kings; John; Romans
Related People
Elisha; Jesus
