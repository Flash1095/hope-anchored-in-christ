Bezaleel

Type
Bezaleel
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Exodus 31:1-11; Exodus 35:30-35
Fulfillment
Bezaleel, filled with the Spirit of God to build the tabernacle, God's dwelling among His people, faintly prefigures Christ, who builds the true temple — His body and His church — as God's dwelling among us.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 16:18; John 2:19-21
Explanation
DOUBT: the correspondence is a single point — a Spirit-filled builder of God's dwelling — and Bezaleel's work is already covered in substance by the live 'Tabernacle' entry. TRADITIONAL reading: the Spirit's endowment for building (Exodus 31:3) is sometimes linked to Christ the builder (Matthew 16:18; John 2:19-21), but the New Testament never draws that parallel.
