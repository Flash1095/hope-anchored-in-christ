The Son Called Out of Egypt

Type
The Son Called Out of Egypt
Category
Event
Old Testament Passage
Hosea 11:1; Exodus 4:22-23
Fulfillment
God called Israel, His son, out of Egypt at the Exodus, and Matthew says Jesus retraced that path: taken down into Egypt as a child to escape Herod, then called back out, 'that it might be fulfilled which was spoken of the Lord by the prophet, saying, Out of Egypt have I called my son.' Israel's sonship and exodus were the pattern; Christ, the true Son, recapitulates them.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 2:13-15
Explanation
NT-explicit warrant: Matthew 2:15 quotes Hosea 11:1 of Jesus directly. Hosea's words were originally historical, about Israel ('When Israel was a child, then I loved him, and called my son out of Egypt'), which is what makes this typology rather than a forward prediction: the historical pattern of God's son called out of Egypt is fulfilled again, and finally, in Christ. Distinct from the live Red Sea Crossing and Passover Lamb rows, which cover the deliverance itself; this row covers the sonship pattern and the Egypt-to-return movement.
