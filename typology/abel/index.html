Abel

Abel offered the first acceptable sacrifice and became the first righteous sufferer, murdered by his own brother for doing right. His blood, Scripture says, cried out from the ground — and Hebrews sets that cry beside the blood of Jesus, which speaks a better word: not vengeance, but forgiveness.

Explanation

TRADITIONAL reading, anchored in Hebrews 12:24: Abel's blood speaks of a wrong that demands justice, while the blood of Jesus speaks of a wrong forgiven. The parallel is in the pattern — an innocent man slain, whose blood speaks after his death — not in Abel's life as a model. Abel offered in faith; Jesus is both the offering and the one who offers.
