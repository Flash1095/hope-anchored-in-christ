The Prophet Like Moses

Type
The Prophet Like Moses
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Deuteronomy 18:15-19
Fulfillment
Moses promised that God would raise up a prophet like him from among Israel, putting God's words in his mouth, and that anyone who would not listen would be held accountable. Jesus is that prophet: He speaks only what the Father gives Him, He is authenticated by signs like Moses, and He mediates a new covenant. Peter preached this promise as fulfilled in Jesus, and Stephen repeated it before his martyrdom.
Fulfillment Passage
John 5:46; John 6:14; John 7:40; John 12:49; Acts 3:22-23; Acts 7:37; Hebrews 3:5-6
Explanation
Like Moses, Jesus speaks God's words directly (Deuteronomy 18:18; John 12:49), stands between God and the people, and is authenticated by signs. But Jesus is greater: Moses was a faithful servant in God's house, while Jesus is the Son over the house (Hebrews 3:5-6). The crowds recognized it themselves, saying after the feeding of the five thousand, "This is indeed the Prophet who is to come into the world" (John 6:14). See the Moses entry for the full set of parallels between their lives.
