Jacob

Type
Jacob
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Genesis 28:10-22; Genesis 32:24-32; Genesis 48:8-20
Fulfillment
Jacob's life — exile and return, wrestling and blessing, the father of the twelve tribes — is sometimes read as a shadow of Christ, who leaves His Father's house, wrestles for His people, and gathers the twelvefold people of God.
Fulfillment Passage
John 1:51
Explanation
DOUBT: no single passage or act of Jacob maps cleanly onto Christ; the parallels are diffuse. The ladder at Bethel is already covered by the live 'Jacob's Ladder' entry; the crossed-hands blessing of Ephraim and Manasseh (Genesis 48) is sometimes read as a cross-shape, but that is a visual resemblance rather than a stated correspondence; and the 'man' Jacob wrestled (Genesis 32) is an appearance of God, not a Jacob-type. TRADITIONAL reading: at most, Jacob the chosen younger son and father of Israel shadows Christ as head of the true Israel — but the case is suggestive, not strong.
