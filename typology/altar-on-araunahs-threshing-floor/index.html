The Altar on Araunah's Threshing Floor

Type
The Altar on Araunah's Threshing Floor
Category
Event
Old Testament Passage
2 Samuel 24:18-25; 1 Chronicles 21:18-27; 2 Chronicles 3:1
Fulfillment
When plague falls on Israel for David's census, the LORD stays the destroying angel at the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite, and David insists on paying the full price, refusing an offering that costs him nothing. The altar he builds there becomes the site of Solomon's temple. In Christ, the costly sacrifice that turns away wrath and the temple where God meets His people are one.
Fulfillment Passage
Romans 3:25; Hebrews 9:26; John 2:19-21
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: the pattern is a sacrifice, paid for at full price (2 Samuel 24:24), that stops a plague, on ground that then becomes the temple site (2 Chronicles 3:1, Mount Moriah). It is distinct from the live 'Solomon's Temple' entry, which covers the building: this row covers the atoning altar that precedes it. The New Testament never names Araunah's altar a type, so the reading is stated as traditional.
