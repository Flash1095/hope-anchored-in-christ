Boaz the Kinsman-Redeemer

Type
Boaz the Kinsman-Redeemer
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Ruth 2:20; Ruth 3:9-13; Ruth 4:9-10
Fulfillment
Boaz was a near relative who had both the right and the means to redeem Ruth and Naomi, and he gladly paid the price at the city gate to make Ruth his bride. Jesus is our kinsman-redeemer: truly our brother in the flesh, He paid the full price at the cross to redeem a people for Himself.
Fulfillment Passage
Leviticus 25:47-49; Titus 2:14; 1 Peter 1:18-19; Revelation 5:9
Explanation
The Hebrew word goel, redeemer, required kinship, ability, and willingness, and Boaz shows all three with kindness and integrity. Ruth, a foreign widow with nothing, pictures us: welcomed by grace into the family line that would lead, through her own great-grandson David, to Christ.
