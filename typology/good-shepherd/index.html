The Good Shepherd

Type
The Good Shepherd
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Psalm 23:1-6; Ezekiel 34:11-16; Ezekiel 34:23; Zechariah 13:7
Fulfillment
David sang of the LORD as his shepherd, and Ezekiel promised that God Himself would shepherd His scattered flock and raise up one shepherd, His servant David, over them. Jesus claimed the office outright — 'I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep' — and Peter calls Him the chief Shepherd who appears with an unfading crown.
Fulfillment Passage
John 10:11-16; Hebrews 13:20; 1 Peter 5:4
Explanation
EXPLICIT warrant: John 10:11 is Jesus' own claim, and Hebrews 13:20 and 1 Peter 5:4 keep the shepherd titles for Him. This is distinct from the King David entry, which covers David's kingship; the shepherd office here is Ezekiel's promised shepherd, claimed by Jesus Himself.
