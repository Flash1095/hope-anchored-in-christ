Benjamin

Type
Benjamin
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Genesis 35:16-18; Genesis 43:29-34; Genesis 44:18-34
Fulfillment
Benjamin, the 'son of the right hand,' born in sorrow and renamed in favor, is sometimes read as a faint shadow of Christ, the Son at the Father's right hand who was born to sorrow and raised to honor.
Fulfillment Passage
Hebrews 1:3
Explanation
DOUBT: the parallel rests almost entirely on the meaning of the name ('son of the right hand') plus a birth-in-sorrow motif; Benjamin's own life — favored youngest brother, the cup in his sack — has no act that maps onto Christ's work, and the substitution in Genesis 44 is Judah's type, already covered by the live 'Judah' entry. TRADITIONAL reading: at most a name-based hint toward Christ seated at the right hand (Hebrews 1:3).
