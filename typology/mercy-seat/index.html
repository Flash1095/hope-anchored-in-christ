The Mercy Seat

Type
The Mercy Seat
Category
Object
Old Testament Passage
Exodus 25:17-22; Leviticus 16:14-16
Fulfillment
The mercy seat, sprinkled with blood on the Day of Atonement, was the very place where God met His people and forgave them. Paul uses this same word for Jesus: God presented Christ as a mercy seat, a sacrifice of atonement, received by faith, demonstrating His righteousness and mercy together.
Fulfillment Passage
Romans 3:25; Hebrews 9:5; 1 John 2:2
Explanation
Between the cherubim above the ark, judgment and mercy met: the law inside the ark condemned, yet the blood above it covered. In Christ, God's justice and love meet fully at the cross, so sinners can draw near without fear.
