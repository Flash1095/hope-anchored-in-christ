Gideon

Type
Gideon
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Judges 6:11-16; Judges 7:2-7; Judges 7:19-22
Fulfillment
As Gideon defeated Midian not with an army but with three hundred men carrying trumpets, pitchers, and torches — so that Israel could not boast in its own strength — Jesus wins His victory through what the world counts as weakness: a cross, and a small band of witnesses. Isaiah ties the breaking of Midian's yoke to the coming Child whose government will have no end.
Fulfillment Passage
Isaiah 9:4-7; 1 Corinthians 1:27-29; 2 Corinthians 12:9
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: Gideon is never called a type in the New Testament, but the shape matches — God raises a deliverer, strips away human strength so the victory is plainly the LORD's (Judges 7:2), and breaks the oppressor's yoke, the very image Isaiah 9:4 uses for the Messiah's work. Gideon first tears down Baal's altar, builds an altar called 'The LORD Is Peace,' and then delivers a people who could not deliver themselves.
