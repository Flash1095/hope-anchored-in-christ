Job

Type
Job
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Job 1:20-22; Job 19:25-27; Job 42:10-12
Fulfillment
Job, blameless and upright, lost everything and still would not curse God; in the middle of his suffering he declared, 'I know that my redeemer liveth.' He is the righteous sufferer who intercedes for his friends and is restored double at the end — a sketch of the innocent one who suffers, prays for His tormentors, and is vindicated.
Fulfillment Passage
James 5:11; 1 Peter 2:21-24
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: James holds up 'the patience of Job' as the model of endurance, and Peter describes Christ suffering without retaliation, bearing our sins, by whose stripes we are healed. Job's redeemer confession points forward to a living Redeemer; Job himself is never called a type in the New Testament, so this is stated as traditional.
Related Books
Job; James; 1 Peter
