Cyrus

Type
Cyrus
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Isaiah 44:28; Isaiah 45:1-4; Ezra 1:1-4
Fulfillment
Cyrus, a pagan king who did not know God, was nevertheless called by name a century and a half before his birth, titled God's shepherd and His anointed, and used to end the exile and send God's people home to rebuild. He is the anointed deliverer who sets captives free by decree — a sketch, in a foreign king, of the Anointed One who sets captives free in truth.
Fulfillment Passage
Luke 4:18-21
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: Isaiah's use of 'anointed' for Cyrus is the Old Testament's own language, and Jesus opened His ministry by reading Isaiah's anointing passage as fulfilled in Himself. Cyrus is never called a type in the New Testament, so this is stated as traditional. Note: no Cyrus people profile exists yet, so RelatedPeople omits him rather than linking a name with no page.
