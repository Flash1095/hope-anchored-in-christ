The Cornerstone

Type
The Cornerstone
Category
Object
Old Testament Passage
Psalm 118:22-23; Isaiah 28:16
Fulfillment
The stone the builders rejected became the head of the corner, and Isaiah promised a tried stone, a precious corner stone, a sure foundation, laid in Zion. Jesus quoted the psalm against the leaders who rejected Him, and Peter applies both passages to Christ: the rejected stone is the foundation stone of God's house.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 21:42; 1 Peter 2:6-7; Ephesians 2:20
Explanation
EXPLICIT warrant: Matthew 21:42 is Jesus quoting Psalm 118:22 of Himself, and 1 Peter 2:6-7 quotes Isaiah 28:16 and Psalm 118:22 of Christ, while Ephesians 2:20 names Jesus Christ Himself the chief corner stone. This is distinct from the Rock in the Wilderness entry (water from the rock) and from the Stone Cut Without Hands entry (the kingdom stone).
