Enoch

Type
Enoch
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Genesis 5:21-24
Fulfillment
Enoch, who walked with God and was taken without seeing death, faintly prefigures Christ's ascension — the man who passes from earthly life into God's presence alive.
Fulfillment Passage
Hebrews 11:5; Acts 1:9
Explanation
DOUBT: the correspondence is a single point — translation without death — with no broader life-parallel; Enoch's story is four verses long. TRADITIONAL reading: Enoch's being taken (Genesis 5:24; Hebrews 11:5) is sometimes read as a shadow of Christ's ascension (Acts 1:9), but the New Testament never draws that parallel; Hebrews commends Enoch's faith, not a typology.
