The Day of Atonement

Type
The Day of Atonement
Category
Institution
Old Testament Passage
Leviticus 16:1-34
Fulfillment
Once a year the high priest alone entered the Holy of Holies with blood, first for himself and then for the people, to make atonement for the sins of the whole nation. Hebrews presents Christ as the high priest who entered once for all, not with the blood of goats and calves but with His own blood, into the true Holy Place, having obtained eternal redemption.
Fulfillment Passage
Hebrews 9:7-12; Hebrews 9:24-28
Explanation
EXPLICIT warrant: Hebrews 9 draws the parallel directly, contrasting the yearly entrance with blood not his own against Christ's single entrance with His own blood, and the repeated sacrifices against His once-for-all offering. This entry is distinct from its parts, which already have entries: the Mercy Seat (the object sprinkled), the Scapegoat (the goat sent away), and the Sacrificial System (the system in general). This entry covers the day as a whole — the yearly entrance pattern that Hebrews says Christ fulfills by entering heaven itself for us.
