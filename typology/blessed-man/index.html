The Blessed Man

Type
The Blessed Man
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Psalm 1:1-6
Fulfillment
The blessed man of Psalm 1 delights in the law of the LORD and meditates on it day and night, standing like a tree planted by streams of water, fruitful in season and unwithering. Jesus is the only man who perfectly delighted in God's law and did His Father's will, the one who fulfills the Law rather than merely keeping it, and in whom believers abide and bear fruit.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 5:17; Hebrews 10:5-7; John 15:5
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: the blessed man is read as a portrait of Christ, the perfectly righteous man who delights in the law, prospers like the tree by the water, and stands when the ungodly are driven away like chaff. DOUBT: the figure is an unnamed, generic wisdom ideal, not an individual person with a life, office, or action of his own; the correspondence rests on ideal righteousness rather than a specific narrative parallel, and Psalm 1 functions as a wisdom prologue to the Psalter rather than a personal history. This may be wisdom portraiture rather than a true type, so it is held as borderline, not clear.
