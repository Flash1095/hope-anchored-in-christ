Judah

Type
Judah
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Genesis 44:18-34
Fulfillment
When Joseph demanded Benjamin as a slave, Judah stepped forward and offered himself in his brother's place: let me bear the punishment so he can go free. It is the clearest picture in Genesis of substitution — one man volunteering to take another's sentence — and it points to the Substitute who takes ours.
Fulfillment Passage
Mark 10:45; 1 Peter 3:18
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: Judah's offer is not called a type in the New Testament, but the shape matches exactly — a willing substitute, a guilty brother set free, a sentence transferred from the one who deserved it to the one who offered. Judah could only offer himself for one brother for a time; Jesus offers Himself for many, once and for all.
