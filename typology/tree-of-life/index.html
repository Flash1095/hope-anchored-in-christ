The Tree of Life

Type
The Tree of Life
Category
Object
Old Testament Passage
Genesis 2:9; Genesis 3:22-24; Proverbs 3:18
Fulfillment
In Eden the tree of life stood at the garden's center, and after the fall its way was guarded so fallen humanity could not live forever in sin. At the cross, Jesus hung on a tree to bear the curse for us, and in the new Jerusalem the tree of life stands open again, its leaves for the healing of the nations.
Fulfillment Passage
Galatians 3:13; Revelation 2:7; Revelation 22:1-2; Revelation 22:14
Explanation
The Bible begins and ends around this tree, with exile in between, and Christ's cross, itself called a tree, stands at the turning point. What Adam lost by reaching for forbidden fruit, Jesus restores by His obedience, so all who wash their robes in His blood regain the right to eat and live.
