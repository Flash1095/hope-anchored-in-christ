Noah

Type
Noah
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Genesis 6:8-22; Genesis 7:1-24; Genesis 8:15-22
Fulfillment
Noah, the righteous man who found grace and was saved through the waters of judgment, prefigures Christ, through whom a new humanity is saved through the waters of baptism and the judgment of the cross. As Noah's ark carried his household safely through the flood, so Christ carries His people safely through death into new life.
Fulfillment Passage
1 Peter 3:20-21; Hebrews 11:7; 2 Peter 2:5
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: Noah is the righteous head of a new humanity after judgment — the man through whom God preserves a remnant and begins again. The New Testament explicitly connects the flood and the ark with baptism (1 Peter 3:20-21) and commends Noah's faith (Hebrews 11:7); the person-typology is the traditional reading built on that explicit link. Noah is called a preacher of righteousness (2 Peter 2:5), and like Christ he stands between a condemned world and the family God saves.
