Solomon

Type
Solomon
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
1 Kings 3:5-14; 1 Kings 4:29-34; 1 Kings 10:1-9
Fulfillment
Solomon, son of David, reigned in peace, built the house of God, and was given wisdom wider than any king before him, so that rulers came from afar to hear him. Jesus Himself said that one greater than Solomon is here — greater in wisdom, greater in peace, and the true builder of God's house.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 12:42; Luke 11:31
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading, distinct from the Solomon's Temple entry (which covers the building): Solomon the person foreshadows Christ the King — son of David, prince of peace, possessor of wisdom, builder of the house. Where Solomon's wisdom was a gift and his peace was bounded by his lifetime, Jesus is wisdom itself and His peace has no end.

1 Kings; Matthew; Luke
