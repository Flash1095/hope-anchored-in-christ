The Key of David

God took the key of the house of David from an unfaithful steward and laid it on Eliakim's shoulder: he shall open, and none shall shut; and he shall shut, and none shall open. The key is authority over the house — who comes in, and who does not.

Explanation

TRADITIONAL reading, with a direct New Testament echo: Revelation gives the key of David to Christ Himself, who opens and no man shuts. The type is carried by the key, not by Eliakim — he was a flawed steward whose own peg was later removed (Isaiah 22:25), so he is named in the entry but not made the type.
