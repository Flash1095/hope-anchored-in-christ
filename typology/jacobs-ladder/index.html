Jacob's Ladder

Type
Jacob's Ladder
Category
Event
Old Testament Passage
Genesis 28:10-22
Fulfillment
Alone in the wilderness, Jacob dreamed of a stairway reaching from earth to heaven, with angels ascending and descending, and God renewed the covenant promise above it. Jesus claimed that vision for Himself, telling Nathanael, 'You will see heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending on the Son of Man.'
Fulfillment Passage
John 1:51
Explanation
Bethel, 'the house of God,' was an ordinary patch of ground until God revealed that heaven was nearer than Jacob knew. Jesus is the true ladder and the true Bethel: in Him, heaven and earth meet, God speaks His promises, and a runaway sinner finds the way home open.
