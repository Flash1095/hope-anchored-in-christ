The Ascent on High

Type
The Ascent on High
Category
Event
Old Testament Passage
Psalm 68:18; Psalm 68:24
Fulfillment
Psalm 68 sings of God ascending on high in triumphal procession, leading captivity captive and receiving gifts among men, His goings seen in the sanctuary. Paul quotes the ascent verse directly of Christ: when He ascended up on high He led captivity captive and gave gifts unto men, the same One who first descended and then ascended far above all heavens.
Fulfillment Passage
Ephesians 4:8-10
Explanation
EXPLICIT warrant: Ephesians 4:8 quotes Psalm 68:18 and applies it to Christ's ascension, so this is Scripture's own parallel, not an imposed one. The pattern is a victor's ascent after triumph, with captives led in train and gifts distributed — the shape of the ascension after the victory of the cross and resurrection. Honest note: the psalm says the ascender 'received gifts for men' while Ephesians says He 'gave gifts unto men'; Paul reads the ascended victor as the giver, and the entry rests on the ascent, captives, and gifts together, not on smoothing over that change of direction. No live or round-2 entry covers the ascension.
