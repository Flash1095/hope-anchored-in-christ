The Sacrificial System

Type
The Sacrificial System
Category
Institution
Old Testament Passage
Leviticus 1:1-4; Leviticus 4:20; Leviticus 17:11
Fulfillment
The daily and yearly sacrifices taught that sin costs life, yet they could never finally take sin away, so they were offered again and again. Jesus, the perfect Lamb without blemish, offered Himself once for all, and by that single offering He has perfected forever those who are being made holy.
Fulfillment Passage
Hebrews 10:1-14; 1 Peter 1:18-19; 1 John 1:7
Explanation
Burnt, sin, guilt, and fellowship offerings each highlighted a facet of atonement: substitution, cleansing, forgiveness, and restored fellowship. Christ gathers them all into Himself; where the old system was a shadow that pointed forward, His sacrifice is the substance that settles the debt.
