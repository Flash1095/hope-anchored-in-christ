The Man Hanged on a Tree

Type
The Man Hanged on a Tree
Category
Institution
Old Testament Passage
Deuteronomy 21:22-23
Fulfillment
The law commanded that a man executed for a capital crime and hanged on a tree must not remain overnight, 'for he that is hanged is accursed of God.' Paul applies that curse directly to Christ: He redeemed us from the curse of the law by becoming a curse for us, 'for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree.'
Fulfillment Passage
Galatians 3:13
Explanation
EXPLICIT warrant: Galatians 3:13 quotes Deuteronomy 21:23 of Christ's crucifixion. The parallel is in the curse-bearing, not in the criminal: Christ was no lawbreaker, but He bore the law's curse on a tree so that the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles. This entry is distinct from the live Sacrificial System entry (offerings in general): this is the specific tree-curse provision and Paul's explicit application of it.
