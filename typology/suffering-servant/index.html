The Suffering Servant

Type
The Suffering Servant
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Isaiah 52:13-53:12; Isaiah 50:6; Isaiah 61:1-2
Fulfillment
Isaiah's Servant was despised and rejected, pierced for our transgressions and crushed for our iniquities, silent before His accusers like a lamb to the slaughter. The Gospels show Jesus fulfilling this chapter phrase by phrase, and the risen Christ Himself explained that the Scriptures spoke of His necessary suffering.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 8:17; Luke 22:37; Acts 8:32-35; 1 Peter 2:22-25
Explanation
Long before Isaiah wrote, Joseph's rejection by his brothers and unjust suffering before his exaltation sketched the same pattern of the righteous one suffering to save others. Isaiah gives that pattern its clearest voice, and in Jesus the Servant's wounds become, as Peter writes, the very means by which we are healed.
