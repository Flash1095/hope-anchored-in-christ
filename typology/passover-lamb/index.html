The Passover Lamb

Type
The Passover Lamb
Category
Object
Old Testament Passage
Exodus 12:1-13; Exodus 12:21-27; Exodus 12:46
Fulfillment
John the Baptist announced Jesus as 'the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world,' and Paul calls Christ our Passover lamb, sacrificed for us. At the cross, not one of Jesus' bones was broken, just as the Passover lamb's bones were to remain unbroken.
Fulfillment Passage
John 1:29; John 19:33-36; 1 Corinthians 5:7
Explanation
The blood on the doorposts turned God's judgment aside and set a captive people free to journey toward the promised land. Christ's blood does far more: it cleanses the conscience, frees us from slavery to sin, and marks us as God's own for the journey home.
