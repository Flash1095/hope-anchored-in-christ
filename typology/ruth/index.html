Ruth

Type
Ruth
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Ruth 1:16-17; Ruth 2:1-13; Ruth 4:13-17
Fulfillment
Ruth, a Moabite widow with no claim on Israel, clung to Naomi, gleaned in a stranger's field, and was welcomed by Boaz the redeemer into the family and the line of David. She is the Gentile outsider brought near by grace alone — and she stands, by name, in the genealogy of Jesus.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 1:5; Ephesians 2:12-13
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: Ruth's welcome pictures the welcome of the nations — those once far off, with no citizenship in Israel, brought near. Ruth is never called a type in the New Testament, so this is stated as traditional; it is distinct from the Boaz entry, which covers the redeemer rather than the one redeemed.
