The Willing Bondservant

Type
The Willing Bondservant
Category
Institution
Old Testament Passage
Exodus 21:5-6
Fulfillment
If a servant, offered his freedom, says, 'I love my master... I will not go out free,' his master brings him to the doorpost and bores his ear with an awl, and he serves forever — not driven, but willing, marked by love.
Fulfillment Passage
Psalms 40:6-8; Hebrews 10:5-7; Philippians 2:7-8
Explanation
SPLIT warrant: the willing-servant words are explicit — Psalm 40's 'mine ears hast thou opened... I delight to do thy will' is applied to Christ in Hebrews 10 — but the link from that psalm back to the bored-ear law of Exodus 21 is traditional inference, stated as such. The servant's mark is love, not coercion; Christ took the servant's form willingly.
