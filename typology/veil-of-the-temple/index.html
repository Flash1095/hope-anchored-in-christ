The Veil of the Temple

Type
The Veil of the Temple
Category
Object
Old Testament Passage
Exodus 26:31-33; Leviticus 16:2; 2 Chronicles 3:14
Fulfillment
The heavy veil barred the way into the Holy of Holies, entered only once a year by one man with blood, but at the moment Jesus died the veil was torn in two from top to bottom. Through His flesh, Hebrews says, He opened a new and living way into God's presence for all who believe.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 27:50-51; Hebrews 6:19-20; Hebrews 10:19-22
Explanation
The torn veil is God Himself removing the barrier, top to bottom, heaven to earth, not human hands reaching up. It stands behind the blog's anchor verse: our hope enters the inner sanctuary behind the curtain, where Jesus has gone before us and holds it secure.
