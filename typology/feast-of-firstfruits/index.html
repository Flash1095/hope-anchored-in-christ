The Feast of Firstfruits

Type
The Feast of Firstfruits
Category
Institution
Old Testament Passage
Leviticus 23:9-14; Exodus 23:19
Fulfillment
On the day after the sabbath in Passover week, Israel waved the first sheaf of the harvest before the LORD — the first and the best, offered while the rest of the harvest still stood in the fields. Paul names the fulfillment directly: Christ is risen from the dead and become the firstfruits of them that slept.
Fulfillment Passage
1 Corinthians 15:20-23
Explanation
EXPLICIT warrant: 1 Corinthians 15:20 states it — 'now is Christ risen from the dead, and become the firstfruits of them that slept.' The first sheaf guarantees the harvest that follows; the risen Christ guarantees the resurrection of His people. This is distinct from the Passover Lamb entry (the sacrifice) because Firstfruits is the resurrection offering.
