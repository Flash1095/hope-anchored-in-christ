Caleb

Type
Caleb
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Numbers 13:30; Numbers 14:6-9; Numbers 14:24; Joshua 14:6-15
Fulfillment
Caleb, who followed the Lord fully when the majority feared, and who at eighty-five still claimed the promised inheritance, faintly prefigures Christ, the faithful witness who trusts the Father's promise and leads God's people into their inheritance.
Fulfillment Passage
Hebrews 4:8-11; 1 Peter 1:3-5
Explanation
DOUBT: the parallel is generic faithfulness — any faithful believer parallels it — with no act of Caleb's that distinctively maps onto Christ's person or work; Caleb is a model of perseverance, and Joshua (already a live entry) carries the leader-into-the-land typology. TRADITIONAL reading: Caleb's 'different spirit' (Numbers 14:24) and his wholehearted following are sometimes read as a shadow of Christ's perfect trust, but the case is suggestive, not strong.
