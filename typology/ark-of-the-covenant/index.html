The Ark of the Covenant

Type
The Ark of the Covenant
Category
Object
Old Testament Passage
Exodus 25:10-22; Exodus 37:1-9; Joshua 3:14-17
Fulfillment
The ark, overlaid with gold and overshadowed by cherubim, carried the law, the manna, and Aaron's rod, and God's presence rested above it between the cherubim. In Christ, all that the ark held and signified comes together: He is God's presence with us, the law fulfilled, the true bread from heaven, and our resurrected High Priest.
Fulfillment Passage
John 1:14; Romans 3:25; Hebrews 9:4-5; Revelation 11:19
Explanation
Israel carried the ark into battle, across the Jordan, and finally into the temple, confessing that the Lord Himself went before them. The ark pointed to a day when God would not merely go before His people in a box of gold-covered wood, but dwell with them in a person, Jesus Christ.
