The True Vine

Isaiah sang of a vineyard planted by God that yielded only wild grapes, and the psalmist recalled a vine brought out of Egypt that spread across the land and was then broken down. Israel was the vine that failed — so Jesus declares Himself the vine that does not fail: 'I am the true vine, and my Father is the husbandman.'

Explanation

EXPLICIT warrant: John 15:1 is Jesus' own claim, set against Isaiah's failed vineyard and the psalm's broken vine. This is distinct from the Tree of Life entry (Eden's tree and the cross): the vine is Israel's covenant plant, replaced by a true vine whose branches bear fruit by abiding.
