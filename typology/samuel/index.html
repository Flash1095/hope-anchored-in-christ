Samuel

Type
Samuel
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
1 Samuel 1:20-28; 1 Samuel 2:26; 1 Samuel 3:19-21; 1 Samuel 7:15-17
Fulfillment
Samuel served the LORD as prophet, priest, and judge, and never ceased interceding for Israel. Jesus holds all three offices perfectly — Prophet who speaks God's word, Priest who offers Himself, and King who judges justly — and He ever lives to intercede for His people.
Fulfillment Passage
Luke 2:52; Hebrews 7:25; Acts 3:22-23
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: Samuel is never called a type in the New Testament, but the correspondences are close: a child given in answer to prayer and lent to the LORD, who 'grew on, and was in favour both with the LORD, and also with men' (1 Samuel 2:26) — the very pattern Luke 2:52 uses for Jesus — and who combined the work of prophet, priest, and judge while interceding for a sinful people (1 Samuel 12:23).
