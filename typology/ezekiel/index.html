Ezekiel

Type
Ezekiel
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Ezekiel 3:17; Ezekiel 4:4-6; Ezekiel 12:11; Ezekiel 24:24
Fulfillment
Ezekiel was commanded to lie on his side and bear the iniquity of Israel and Judah as a living sign, and God twice calls him a sign to the people. Jesus does not merely picture bearing sin but truly bears it: He was made sin for us, bore our sins in His own body on the tree, and was once offered to bear the sins of many. As watchman, Ezekiel warned of judgment to come; Christ warns and rescues.
Fulfillment Passage
2 Corinthians 5:21; 1 Peter 2:24; Hebrews 9:28
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: Ezekiel's sign-acts — bearing the people's iniquity on his side for 390 and 40 days, and standing before the exiles as a sign — correspond to Christ, who truly bears the sins of His people rather than picturing it. His watchman office, warning the people of judgment, echoes Christ, who warns and rescues. The New Testament never calls Ezekiel a type, so this is stated as traditional.
