Naboth

Type
Naboth
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
1 Kings 21:1-16
Fulfillment
Naboth would not sell his ancestral vineyard, so Jezebel had false witnesses swear against him, and he was stoned to death outside the city so the king could seize his vineyard. An innocent man, condemned by false testimony, killed outside the walls so another could take what was his.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 26:59-60; Matthew 21:33-39; Hebrews 13:12
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: no New Testament writer calls Naboth a type, but three details converge — false witnesses, death outside the city, and a vineyard seized by killing the rightful heir, matching the vineyard parable where the tenants kill the son to seize the inheritance. Naboth's death did not atone; the parallel is in the pattern of innocent suffering, not in the efficacy.
