Hosea

Type
Hosea
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Hosea 1:2-3; Hosea 3:1-3
Fulfillment
God told Hosea to marry an unfaithful wife, and then, after she had left him, to love her again and buy her back — paying the price to redeem the wife who had sold herself away. It is the story of a husband who will not let an unfaithful bride go, and it points to the Bridegroom who pays to redeem an unfaithful people and make them His own.
Fulfillment Passage
Ephesians 5:25-32
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: Hosea is never called a type in the New Testament, but the shape matches — a husband redeeming an unfaithful bride at his own cost, set beside Paul's picture of Christ loving the church and giving Himself for her. Hosea's wife stands for unfaithful Israel; the church, not Christ, is the bride in the fulfillment. Hosea's own marriage is the picture, not the person redeemed.
