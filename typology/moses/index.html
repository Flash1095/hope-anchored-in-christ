Moses

Type
Moses
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Exodus 1:22; Exodus 2:1-10; Exodus 3:1-10; Exodus 16; Exodus 32:32; Numbers 12:3; Deuteronomy 18:15-18; Deuteronomy 34:10-12
Fulfillment
God promised a prophet like Moses, and Jesus is that prophet, yet greater. Moses led Israel out of slavery in Egypt and mediated the old covenant; Jesus leads all who trust Him out of slavery to sin and mediates a new and better covenant in His blood.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 2:13-15; Matthew 4:2; Matthew 11:29; Matthew 17:1-5; John 1:11; John 6:14; John 6:35; John 7:40; Acts 3:22-23; Acts 7:37; Hebrews 3:1-6
Explanation
Moses and Jesus mirror each other at nearly every turn: both were born under a king's order to kill Hebrew baby boys and were miraculously preserved (Exodus 1-2; Matthew 2); both spent time in Egypt and came out of it (Hosea 11:1; Matthew 2:15); both fasted forty days (Exodus 34:28; Matthew 4:2); both mediated a covenant between God and His people (Exodus 24; Luke 22:20); both interceded for a guilty people, Moses even offering himself for them (Exodus 32:32), while Jesus actually gave Himself (Romans 5:8); both were meek (Numbers 12:3; Matthew 11:29); both taught God's law from a mountain, Sinai and the Sermon on the Mount (Exodus 20; Matthew 5-7); both were rejected by their own people (Exodus 32; John 1:11); both provided bread in the wilderness, manna and the Bread of Life (Exodus 16; John 6:35); both led God's people out of bondage, from Egypt and from sin. Where Moses could only lead Israel to the border of the promised land, Jesus brings His people all the way home. And Moses himself foretold a Prophet like him (Deuteronomy 18:15), a promise Peter and Stephen applied directly to Jesus (Acts 3:22-23; Acts 7:37).
