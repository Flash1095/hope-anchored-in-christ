Release at the High Priest's Death

Type
Release at the High Priest's Death
Category
Institution
Old Testament Passage
Numbers 35:25; Numbers 35:28
Fulfillment
A man who killed unintentionally fled to a city of refuge and stayed there — until the death of the high priest. After the high priest's death, the slayer could return home free. Freedom came, by law, only through the high priest's death.
Fulfillment Passage
Romans 6:7; Hebrews 9:15
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: the mechanism — release through the high priest's death — matches the claim that death frees from sin and that Christ's death redeems from transgressions, but no New Testament writer cites Numbers 35 as a type, so this is stated as traditional. It is distinct from the Cities of Refuge entry (the refuge itself): this entry is the release mechanism, the death that sets free.
Related Books
Numbers; Romans; Hebrews
Related People
Moses; Jesus
