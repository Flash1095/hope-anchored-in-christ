Deborah

Type
Deborah
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Judges 4:4-9; Judges 5:1-7
Fulfillment
Deborah judged Israel and sent Barak into battle against Sisera, and the victory song celebrates the LORD who routed the enemy. If she pictures Christ at all, it is only in the general office of judge and deliverer — every judge delivered Israel, and nothing in her story maps to His priestly or atoning work.
Fulfillment Passage
Hebrews 11:32-33
Explanation
BORDERLINE — doubt: Deborah's deliverance of Israel is genuine, but the judge-pattern is generic — Othniel, Ehud, and Gideon all delivered Israel too — and her story offers no specific correspondence to Christ's person or work beyond 'a leader who won a battle.' Her prophetic office does not map to Christ's offices in any particular way. TRADITIONAL reading (doubtful): a judge raised up to save a people who cried out.
