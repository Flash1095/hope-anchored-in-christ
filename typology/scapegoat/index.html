The Scapegoat

Type
The Scapegoat
Category
Object
Old Testament Passage
Leviticus 16:7-10; Leviticus 16:20-22
Fulfillment
On the Day of Atonement, Aaron laid both hands on the scapegoat, confessed all Israel's sins over it, and sent it into the wilderness bearing their guilt away, never to return. Jesus bore our sins in His body on the cross, and God removes them from us as far as the east is from the west.
Fulfillment Passage
Isaiah 53:6; Psalm 103:12; Hebrews 9:26-28; 1 Peter 2:24
Explanation
The two goats together paint one complete picture: one died for sin, the other carried sin away, showing both forgiveness and removal. In Christ we have both, a sacrifice that pays for guilt and a Savior who takes it so far away it can never be charged to us again.
