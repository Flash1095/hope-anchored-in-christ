Manna from Heaven

Type
Manna from Heaven
Category
Object
Old Testament Passage
Exodus 16:4-18; Exodus 16:31-35; Psalm 78:24-25
Fulfillment
When the crowds asked for a sign like the manna, Jesus declared that He is the true bread from heaven the Father gives. Whoever ate manna still died, but whoever feeds on Christ by faith receives life that never ends.
Fulfillment Passage
John 6:31-35; John 6:48-51
Explanation
Manna taught Israel daily dependence: God fed His people morning by morning with bread they did not earn or bake. Jesus feeds our souls the same way, not once but daily, as we come to Him in His word and prayer and find Him enough for today.
Related Books
Exodus; Psalms; John
Related People
Moses; Aaron; Jesus
