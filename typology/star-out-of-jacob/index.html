The Star out of Jacob

Type
The Star out of Jacob
Category
Object
Old Testament Passage
Numbers 24:17-19
Fulfillment
Balaam, a reluctant pagan seer, foresaw 'a star will come out of Jacob; a scepter will rise out of Israel,' a king who would crush God's enemies. Wise men from the east followed a star to Bethlehem to worship the newborn King, and Jesus closes Scripture calling Himself 'the bright Morning Star.'
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 2:1-10; Revelation 22:16
Explanation
The oracle is striking because God spoke it through a hired prophet who came to curse Israel and could only bless. The star promises a royal deliverer rising from Jacob's line in God's timing, a promise the Magi's journey confirms and Revelation seals, as Christ's reign dawns over all nations.
