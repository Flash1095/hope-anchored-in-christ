Elihu

Type
Elihu
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Job 33:23-24
Fulfillment
Elihu told Job that if there is a messenger with a sufferer, an interpreter, one among a thousand, to show him what is right — then God is gracious, and says, 'Deliver him from going down to the pit: I have found a ransom.' A mediator who declares what is right, and a ransom found, so the sufferer need not die.
Fulfillment Passage
1 Timothy 2:5-6
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: Elihu's words are the Old Testament's clearest picture of a mediator and a ransom together, and Paul names Christ as the one mediator who gave Himself a ransom for all. Elihu himself is not called a type in the New Testament, and he is a young friend speaking in a debate, not a redeemer — the parallel is in his words about mediation and ransom, not in his person as a whole.
