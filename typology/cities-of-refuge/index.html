The Cities of Refuge

Type
The Cities of Refuge
Category
Institution
Old Testament Passage
Numbers 35:9-15; Joshua 20:1-9
Fulfillment
Israel set apart cities where someone who had killed unintentionally could flee from the avenger and find safety until a fair trial, remaining until the death of the high priest. Hebrews speaks of believers who have 'fled to take hold of the hope set before us,' finding in Christ a refuge no accuser can breach.
Fulfillment Passage
Joshua 20:3; Hebrews 6:18-20
Explanation
God built mercy into Israel's justice system, marking refuge cities with open gates and clear roads for desperate people running for their lives. Christ is our city of refuge in full: we flee to Him not because we are innocent, but because He has borne our judgment and His protection never expires.

Numbers; Deuteronomy; Joshua; Hebrews
Related People
