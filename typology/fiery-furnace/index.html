The Fiery Furnace

Type
The Fiery Furnace
Category
Event
Old Testament Passage
Daniel 3:1-30
Fulfillment
Three men who would not bow to Nebuchadnezzar's golden image were bound and cast into a furnace heated seven times hotter, yet they walked loose in the midst of the fire unharmed, the king saw a fourth figure with them, and they came out with not a hair singed and their clothes untouched. The king then blessed their God and decreed that no one speak against Him. Bound, cast into a place of death, preserved alive in it, and brought out unharmed while their persecutor confessed the God who delivered them, the furnace sketches Christ's passage through death and His presence with His people in suffering.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 28:20; 1 Peter 4:12-14
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: no New Testament passage calls the furnace a type of Christ, so this is stated as traditional, not explicit. The pattern is in the details, not in a single word: innocent men condemned for refusing false worship, bound and cast into death, a divine companion seen with them in the fire, deliverance without even the smell of fire on their clothes, and a royal confession afterward. It is distinct from the live Daniel entry, which covers the sealed lions' den; this is the companion deliverance, by fire rather than by beasts. The fourth figure Nebuchadnezzar saw is reported as part of the narrative and is not made into a separate Christophany entry.
