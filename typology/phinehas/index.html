Phinehas

Type
Phinehas
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Numbers 25:7-13
Fulfillment
When Israel's sin brought a plague on the camp, Phinehas the priest acted with zeal, and the plague stopped. God said he had turned away His wrath, and gave him a covenant of peace and an everlasting priesthood — wrath averted by a priest's act, and a priesthood that would not end.
Fulfillment Passage
John 2:17; Romans 3:25; Hebrews 7:24
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: Phinehas is never called a type in the New Testament, but the shape is there — zeal for God's honor (echoed in Jesus cleansing the temple), wrath turned away by a priestly act, and an everlasting priesthood, which Hebrews assigns to Christ alone. Phinehas's act stopped a plague for a generation; Christ's priesthood is unchangeable and His atonement is once for all.
Related Books
Numbers; John; Romans; Hebrews
Related People
Phinehas; Zimri, Son of Salu; Cozbi; Jesus
