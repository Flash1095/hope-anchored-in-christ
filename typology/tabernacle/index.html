The Tabernacle

Type
The Tabernacle
Category
Institution
Old Testament Passage
Exodus 25:8-9; Exodus 26:30; Exodus 40:34-38
Fulfillment
John writes that the Word became flesh and 'tabernacled' among us, full of grace and truth. In Jesus, God no longer dwelt behind a curtain in a tent but walked among His people in person, and through Him God's presence now fills His church and one day all creation.
Fulfillment Passage
John 1:14; Colossians 2:9; Hebrews 9:11-12; Revelation 21:3
Explanation
Every part of the tabernacle whispered of Christ: the one door, the altar, the lampstand, the bread, and the veil guarding the Holy of Holies. It taught a wandering people that a holy God longs to dwell in their midst, a longing fulfilled when Emmanuel came.
