Nehemiah

Type
Nehemiah
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Nehemiah 1:4-11; Nehemiah 2:17-18; Nehemiah 6:15-16; Nehemiah 13:7-9
Fulfillment
Nehemiah left the king's court when he heard Jerusalem lay in ruins, wept and prayed for his people, and rebuilt the wall in fifty-two days against mockery and threat — then cleansed God's house of what defiled it. Jesus left His Father's glory for a ruined people, wept over Jerusalem, finished His work against every opposition, and cleanses the temple of His people.
Fulfillment Passage
Luke 19:41-44; John 2:13-17; Hebrews 12:2
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: Nehemiah is never called a type in the New Testament, but the shape matches — the king's cupbearer who gives up comfort for a broken city, intercedes with fasting and confession (Nehemiah 1:4-11), rallies the people with 'the God of heaven, he will prosper us' (Nehemiah 2:20), completes the wall despite Sanballat's threats, and casts the defiling goods out of the temple chambers (Nehemiah 13:8), as Jesus would cleanse the temple in His day.
