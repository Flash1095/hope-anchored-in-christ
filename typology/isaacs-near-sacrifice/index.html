Isaac's Near-Sacrifice

On Mount Moriah Abraham was ready to offer his beloved son, but God stopped him and provided a substitute. Centuries later, on that same mountain region, God did not spare His own Son: Jesus, the beloved Son of promise, was offered so that we might live.

Explanation

Isaac carried the wood up the mountain just as Jesus carried His cross, and Abraham's words, 'God himself will provide the lamb,' found their full answer in Jesus. The scene teaches both the cost of sin and the wonder of a God who provides the sacrifice Himself.
