Zerubbabel

Type
Zerubbabel
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Haggai 2:21-23; Zechariah 4:6-10; Ezra 3:8-13
Fulfillment
Zerubbabel, governor of the returned exiles and descendant of David, laid the foundation of the second temple when the work seemed hopeless, and God called him His signet ring — the chosen seal of His authority. He stands in the genealogy of Jesus, the governor who rebuilds what was ruined and the signet by which God seals His work.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 1:12-13; John 2:19-21
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: Zerubbabel's two roles — Davidic governor and temple-builder — both land on Christ, who is of David's line and who builds the true temple, His own body raised in three days. The signet-ring promise is never applied to Jesus in the New Testament, so this is stated as traditional.
