Mephibosheth

Type
Mephibosheth
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
2 Samuel 9:1-13
Fulfillment
David seeks out a lame, hidden grandson of his enemy Saul — not to punish him but to show him 'the kindness of God' for Jonathan's sake, restoring his grandfather's land and seating him at the king's table continually. So God seeks out the undeserving, restores what was lost, and seats the lame and the outcast at His own table by grace alone.
Fulfillment Passage
Ephesians 2:4-7; Luke 14:21-23
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: the pattern is the king's seeking kindness to one who cannot walk to him and has no claim — Mephibosheth hides in Lo-debar and calls himself a 'dead dog' (2 Samuel 9:8), and eats at the king's table 'continually' (2 Samuel 9:13). This is the recipient side of grace, distinct from the live 'King David' entry which covers David himself, and parallel to the live 'Ruth' entry, which likewise covers the one welcomed rather than the redeemer.
