The Wilderness Testing

Type
The Wilderness Testing
Category
Event
Old Testament Passage
Numbers 14:20-35; Deuteronomy 8:2-3; Exodus 16:1-36; Exodus 17:1-7
Fulfillment
Israel, tested forty years in the wilderness, failed at every turn — grumbling for bread, water, and gods they could see. Jesus, tested forty days in the same wilderness, answered every temptation from Deuteronomy and did not fail once. Where the old people broke, the new head of the people held.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 4:1-11; 1 Corinthians 10:1-11
Explanation
EXPLICIT warrant: 1 Corinthians 10:6 says these things 'were our examples' — the Greek word is typoi, types — and Paul lists the cloud, the sea, the manna, and the rock as written for our admonition. The parts (Red Sea, Manna, Rock) already have entries; this entry covers the testing pattern itself, which has none: forty years of failure set against forty days of victory.
