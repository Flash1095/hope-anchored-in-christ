The Son of Man

Type
The Son of Man
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Daniel 7:13-14
Fulfillment
Daniel saw one like a son of man coming with the clouds of heaven, brought before the Ancient of Days, and given dominion, glory, and a kingdom that all peoples would serve and that would never be destroyed. Jesus took this figure as His own name for Himself more than any other, and before the council He declared that they would see the Son of Man seated at the right hand of Power and coming on the clouds.
Fulfillment Passage
Mark 14:61-62; Matthew 24:30; Revelation 1:13
Explanation
EXPLICIT warrant: Jesus Himself applies Daniel's figure to Himself, and the New Testament keeps the title — in the Gospels, in Stephen's vision, and in Revelation's description of one like a son of man among the lampstands. This is not a traditional reading but the New Testament's own identification.
Related Books
Daniel; Mark; Matthew; Revelation
Related People
