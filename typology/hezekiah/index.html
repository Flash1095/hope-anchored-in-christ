Hezekiah

Type
Hezekiah
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
2 Kings 18:5-7; 2 Kings 19:14-19; 2 Kings 20:1-6; Isaiah 38:1-5
Fulfillment
Hezekiah trusted the LORD when Assyria threatened, prayed as Jerusalem was surrounded, and was delivered from a sickness unto death — his life extended and his tears seen. Jesus is the King who trusts perfectly, bears the siege of sin and death itself, and rises, His life not merely extended but endless.
Fulfillment Passage
Hebrews 2:14-15; Matthew 27:43; 2 Corinthians 1:9-10
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: Hezekiah is never called a type in the New Testament, but the shape matches — a king who 'trusted in the LORD God of Israel' (2 Kings 18:5), cleansed the house of God, prayed when death and an army stood at the gate, and was heard: 'I have heard thy prayer, I have seen thy tears' (Isaiah 38:5). His recovery from sickness unto death pictures, in a lesser key, the King who passes through death and comes out alive.
