Joash

Type
Joash
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
2 Kings 11:2-3; 2 Kings 11:12; 2 Chronicles 23:11
Fulfillment
When Athaliah seized the throne and murdered the royal seed, the infant Joash was hidden in the temple for six years, kept alive in secret while a usurper reigned — then brought out, crowned, and acclaimed king, and a covenant was made between the LORD, the king, and the people.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 2:13-18
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: the preserved royal child hidden from a murderous ruler matches the infancy pattern — a king's seed kept alive in secret and then revealed — but no New Testament writer calls Joash a type, so this is stated as traditional. Joash's reign later failed when his protector died; the pattern is in the preservation and revealing, not in Joash's whole life.
