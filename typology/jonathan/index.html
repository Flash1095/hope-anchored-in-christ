Jonathan

Type
Jonathan
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
1 Samuel 18:1-4; 1 Samuel 19:4-5; 1 Samuel 20:16-17; 1 Samuel 23:16-18
Fulfillment
Jonathan loved David as his own soul, stripped off his robe and gave it with his sword and bow, pleaded for David's life before Saul, and yielded his own claim to the throne. If he pictures Christ at all, it is in self-emptying covenant love — but Jonathan dies without delivering anyone, and the love runs toward David the king rather than from the King to His people.
Fulfillment Passage
John 15:13; Philippians 2:5-8
Explanation
BORDERLINE — doubt: Jonathan's covenant love is beautiful, but the direction of the picture runs the wrong way for a type of Christ: Jonathan is the friend who gives everything to David, whereas Christ is the King who gives everything for His friends. He lays down no life for David's sake in any atoning sense, and his story ends in death on Mount Gilboa, not deliverance. TRADITIONAL reading (doubtful): the prince who surrendered his throne-right to the anointed king.
