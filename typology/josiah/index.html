Josiah

Type
Josiah
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
2 Kings 22:11-20; 2 Kings 23:1-3; 2 Kings 23:21-23
Fulfillment
Josiah tore his clothes at the rediscovered Law, renewed the covenant, purged the idols, and kept a Passover like none since Samuel's day — then died young at Megiddo. If he pictures Christ at all, it is in the reforming king who restores God's word and keeps the feast — but his death does not atone, and every reforming king cleansed the land.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 5:17; Luke 22:15-16
Explanation
BORDERLINE — doubt: Josiah's reform is the fullest in Kings, but 'a good king who restored worship' is the generic pattern of Asa, Jehoshaphat, and Hezekiah, and Josiah adds no distinctive correspondence to Christ's person or work — his Passover keeps the feast, it does not become it, and his death at Megiddo is a political disaster, not a sacrifice. TRADITIONAL reading (doubtful): the boy-king whose heart was tender at the word of God.
