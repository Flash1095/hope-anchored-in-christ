King David

Type
King David
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
1 Samuel 16:1-13; 2 Samuel 7:12-16; Psalm 22
Fulfillment
God promised David a descendant whose kingdom would endure forever, and Jesus is that Son of David, born in Bethlehem and raised to David's throne by His resurrection. Even David's psalms reach beyond himself: Psalm 22 describes the cross, and Psalm 16 celebrates a Holy One who would not see decay.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 1:1; Luke 1:32-33; Acts 2:25-36; Romans 1:3-4
Explanation
David was shepherd, singer, and king, a man after God's own heart, yet he also fell grievously and needed mercy. Jesus is David perfected: the Good Shepherd who lays down His life, the righteous King who never fails, whose reign brings the justice and peace David only glimpsed.
