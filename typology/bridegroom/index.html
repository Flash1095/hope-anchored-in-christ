The Bridegroom

Type
The Bridegroom
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Song of Solomon 3:11; Hosea 2:19-20
Fulfillment
The Song sings of a bridegroom and his bride, and Hosea promises a betrothal that will last forever — 'I will betroth thee unto me for ever.' John the Baptist called himself the friend of the Bridegroom, and Paul says the marriage mystery is really about Christ and the church, consummated at the marriage supper of the Lamb.
Fulfillment Passage
John 3:29; Ephesians 5:25-32; Revelation 19:7-9
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading for the Song's bridegroom himself, with an explicit anchor for the pattern: Ephesians 5:32 says the marriage union is 'a great mystery: but I speak concerning Christ and the church.' The Song is never quoted as a type in the New Testament, so the bridegroom identification is stated as traditional.
Related Books
Song of Solomon; Hosea; John; Ephesians; Revelation
Related People
Jesus; John the Baptist
