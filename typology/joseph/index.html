Joseph

Type
Joseph
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Genesis 37:18-28; Genesis 39:20; Genesis 50:20
Fulfillment
Joseph was betrayed by his brothers for pieces of silver, falsely accused, and lowered into prison, yet God raised him to the right hand of power to save many lives. Jesus was likewise betrayed, rejected by His own people, and humbled to death, then exalted to the Father's right hand to save the world.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 26:14-16; Acts 7:9-14; Philippians 2:8-11
Explanation
Joseph's story whispers of Jesus at every turn: the beloved son, the unjust suffering, and the forgiving heart that says, 'You intended to harm me, but God intended it for good.' Joseph fed the hungry with bread in famine; Jesus gives Himself as the bread of life forever.
