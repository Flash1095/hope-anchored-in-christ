Amos

Type
Amos
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Amos 7:2-3; Amos 7:10-17
Fulfillment
Amos, a herdsman taken from following the flock, interceded for Israel until judgment was turned aside, and was rejected by Amaziah the priest for speaking God's word. Jesus, taken from lowly obscurity, ever lives to intercede for His people and was rejected by the chief priests for telling the truth.
Fulfillment Passage
Hebrews 7:25; John 1:11
Explanation
BORDERLINE: TRADITIONAL reading — the parallels are real: a lowly call, intercession that turns away judgment, rejection by the religious establishment. The doubt: each element is common to the prophets — Moses and Jeremiah both interceded and were rejected — and no passage of Scripture connects Amos with Christ. The correspondence is a shared prophetic pattern rather than a distinctive type, so this entry is held as borderline.
