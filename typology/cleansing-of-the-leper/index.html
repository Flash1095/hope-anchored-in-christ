The Cleansing of the Leper

Type
The Cleansing of the Leper
Category
Institution
Old Testament Passage
Leviticus 14:1-7
Fulfillment
When a leper was cleansed, the priest took two birds: one was slain over running water, and the other, dipped in its blood with cedar, scarlet, and hyssop, was released alive into the open field. One bird dies, one bird flies free — death and release in a single rite, performed for the outcast who could not enter the camp until he was clean.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 8:2-4; Luke 5:12-14
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading: the slain bird and the living bird carry the same twofold pattern as the two goats of the Day of Atonement — death borne and life set free — applied here to the cleansing of the untouchable. Jesus cleansed lepers and sent them to the priests with Moses' own rite, but the rite itself is never called a type in the New Testament, so this is stated as traditional. This is a specific rite, distinct from the general Sacrificial System entry.
Related Books
Leviticus; Matthew; Luke
Related People
Moses; Jesus
