The Sabbath

Type
The Sabbath
Category
Institution
Old Testament Passage
Genesis 2:2-3; Exodus 20:8-11; Deuteronomy 5:12-15
Fulfillment
God rested on the seventh day and made it holy, and Israel was commanded to keep it as a sign of creation and of deliverance from Egypt. Paul writes that sabbaths are a shadow of things to come, and Hebrews says a rest remains for the people of God — a rest entered by ceasing from one's own works, as God did from His.
Fulfillment Passage
Colossians 2:16-17; Hebrews 4:9-10; Mark 2:27-28
Explanation
EXPLICIT warrant: Colossians 2:17 calls sabbaths 'a shadow of things to come; but the body is of Christ,' and Hebrews 4:9-10 names the remaining rest. Jesus, Lord of the sabbath, is both the giver of the rest and the rest itself. This is distinct from the Year of Jubilee entry (a fiftieth-year release): the Sabbath is the weekly sign of rest.
