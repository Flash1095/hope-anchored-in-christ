The Bronze Serpent

Type
The Bronze Serpent
Category
Object
Old Testament Passage
Numbers 21:4-9
Fulfillment
Jesus explained His own cross with this story: 'Just as Moses lifted up the snake in the wilderness, so the Son of Man must be lifted up, that everyone who believes may have eternal life in him.' Those who simply looked in faith were healed, and all who look to the crucified Christ are saved.
Fulfillment Passage
John 3:14-15; John 12:32-33
Explanation
The cure mirrored the curse: the image of the very thing that was killing Israel became, lifted up, the means of life. At the cross, Jesus, made sin for us though He knew no sin, becomes the one we look to for healing from sin's deadly bite.

Numbers; John
Related People
Moses; Jesus; Nicodemus
