The Marriage of Adam and Eve

Type
The Marriage of Adam and Eve
Category
Institution
Old Testament Passage
Genesis 2:18-25
Fulfillment
God formed Eve from Adam's side and joined them as one flesh, the first marriage — and Paul quotes that very union as the pattern of Christ and the church: 'This is a great mystery: but I speak concerning Christ and the church.'
Fulfillment Passage
Ephesians 5:31-32
Explanation
EXPLICIT warrant: Ephesians 5:31 quotes Genesis 2:24 ('they two shall be one flesh') and verse 32 applies it directly to Christ and the church. The first marriage is therefore not merely an illustration Paul borrows but a union whose one-flesh pattern finds its full meaning in Christ's union with His people. This entry is distinct from the live Bridegroom entry (the Song/Hosea bridegroom figure): this is the Genesis institution of marriage itself.
