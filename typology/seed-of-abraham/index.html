The Seed of Abraham

Type
The Seed of Abraham
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Genesis 12:3; Genesis 22:18
Fulfillment
God promised Abraham that in his seed all the nations of the earth would be blessed — a promise repeated at the very mountain where Abraham offered his son and received him back. Paul insists the promise was spoken to a seed, singular, and that seed is Christ: the one descendant through whom the blessing reaches every nation.
Fulfillment Passage
Galatians 3:16; Galatians 3:8-9
Explanation
EXPLICIT warrant: Galatians 3:16 states it directly — 'he saith not, And to seeds, as of many; but as of one, And to thy seed, which is Christ.' This is distinct from the seed of Genesis 3:15 (covered in the Adam entry): that is the woman's seed promised at the Fall; this is Abraham's seed promised at Moriah.
Related Books
Genesis; Galatians
Related People
Abraham; Isaac; Jesus
