The Red Heifer

Type
The Red Heifer
Category
Institution
Old Testament Passage
Numbers 19:1-10
Fulfillment
A red heifer without blemish, never yoked, was slain outside the camp and burned whole, and its ashes mixed with water purified anyone defiled by contact with death. It is the one sacrifice whose whole point is cleansing from death itself — and Hebrews notes that the bodies of such sacrifices were burned without the camp, as Jesus suffered without the gate.
Fulfillment Passage
Hebrews 13:11-13; Hebrews 9:13-14
Explanation
TRADITIONAL reading with a strong Hebrews anchor: Hebrews 13:11-13 draws the outside-the-camp parallel explicitly for the Day of Atonement sacrifices, and 9:13-14 reasons from ashes that purify the flesh to blood that purges the conscience. The red heifer itself is never named in the New Testament, so the identification is stated as traditional. This is a specific rite, distinct from the general Sacrificial System entry.
