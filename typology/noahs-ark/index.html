Noah's Ark

Type
Noah's Ark
Category
Object
Old Testament Passage
Genesis 6:13-22; Genesis 7:1-16; Genesis 8:1
Fulfillment
God provided one ark, entered by one door, through which Noah and his family were saved from judgment on a sinful world. In the same way, God provides one Savior: all who are in Christ are kept safe through the waters of judgment and brought into new life.
Fulfillment Passage
Matthew 24:37-39; Hebrews 11:7; 1 Peter 3:20-21
Explanation
The ark pictures salvation in Christ: judgment is real, but God Himself designs the refuge and invites people inside before the storm comes. Peter links the floodwaters to baptism, pointing to a conscience cleansed and a life rescued by Christ's resurrection.
