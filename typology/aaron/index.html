Aaron

Type
Aaron
Category
Person
Old Testament Passage
Exodus 28:1-43; Exodus 32:1-6; Leviticus 8:1-36
Fulfillment
Aaron, the first high priest, who bore the names of Israel on his breastplate and made atonement once a year, prefigures Christ, the great High Priest who bears His people before God and made atonement once for all.
Fulfillment Passage
Hebrews 5:4; Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 9:11-12
Explanation
DOUBT: this entry substantially overlaps the live 'The High Priest' entry, which already covers the office, and Aaron's personal record breaks the parallel — he made the golden calf (Exodus 32) and was barred with Moses from the land (Numbers 20:12). TRADITIONAL reading: Aaron's priestly mediation — bearing names over his heart (Exodus 28:29) and entering the holy place with blood (Leviticus 16) — shadows Christ's perpetual intercession (Hebrews 7:25; Hebrews 9:11-12), but the office, not Aaron's life, carries the type.
