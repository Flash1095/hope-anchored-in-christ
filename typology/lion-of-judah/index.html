The Lion of Judah

Type
The Lion of Judah
Category
Object
Old Testament Passage
Genesis 49:9-10
Fulfillment
Dying Jacob blessed Judah as a lion's whelp who crouches and rises, and said the scepter would not depart from Judah until tribute came to him. John, weeping because no one could open the sealed book, was told to stop weeping: the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has prevailed to open it.
Fulfillment Passage
Revelation 5:5; Hebrews 7:14
Explanation
EXPLICIT warrant: Revelation 5:5 names the Lion of the tribe of Judah directly, and Hebrews 7:14 notes that our Lord sprang out of Judah. This entry covers the lion image only; the scepter-and-Shiloh oracle of Genesis 49:10 as a whole is treated in the report as a title-oracle and is not given a separate entry.
Related Books
Genesis; Revelation; Hebrews
Related People
