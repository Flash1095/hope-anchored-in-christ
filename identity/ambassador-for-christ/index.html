Ambassador for Christ

Title
Ambassador for Christ
Meaning
Believers are ambassadors for Christ, as though God did beseech men by them, entrusted with the ministry and word of reconciliation.
Practical Change
You represent Christ where you live and work, speaking and acting on his behalf.
Key Scriptures
2 Corinthians 5:20; Ephesians 6:20
