Saint (Holy One)

Title
Saint (Holy One)
Meaning
Believers are called saints — holy ones set apart to God — not because they are sinless, but because God has sanctified them in Christ.
Practical Change
Holiness is your identity before it is your achievement, which is why pursuing it is not pretending.
Key Scriptures
Romans 1:7; 1 Corinthians 1:2; Ephesians 1:1; Philippians 1:1
