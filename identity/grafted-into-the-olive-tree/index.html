Grafted Into the Olive Tree

Title
Grafted Into the Olive Tree
Meaning
Gentile believers, a wild olive tree, were grafted in among the natural branches to share the root and fatness of God's olive tree.
Practical Change
You stand in a story you did not start, supported by a root you did not plant.
Key Scriptures
Romans 11:17; Romans 11:24
