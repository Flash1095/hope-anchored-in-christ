Kings and Priests

Title
Kings and Priests
Meaning
Christ washed believers from their sins and made them kings and priests unto God, to reign on the earth, and those who receive the gift of righteousness reign in life by him.
Practical Change
You serve God as a priest and you live with a king's dignity, because Christ gave you both.
Key Scriptures
Revelation 1:6; Revelation 5:10; Romans 5:17
