Servant of Christ

Title
Servant of Christ
Meaning
Believers are servants of Jesus Christ, bought and owned by him, whose service is freedom rather than bondage.
Practical Change
Your work, whatever it is, is done for Christ as your master, not for men's approval.
Key Scriptures
Romans 1:1; 1 Corinthians 7:22; Galatians 1:10
