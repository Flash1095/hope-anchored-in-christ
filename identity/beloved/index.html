Title
Beloved
Meaning
Believers are beloved of God, accepted in the Beloved, and loved with the love the Father has for the Son.
Practical Change
You are loved before you perform and after you fail; that is the ground of obedience, not its reward.
Key Scriptures
Romans 1:7; Colossians 3:12; Ephesians 1:6
