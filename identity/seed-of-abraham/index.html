Title
Seed of Abraham
Meaning
Those who are of faith are Abraham's children, and all who belong to Christ are Abraham's seed and heirs according to the promise.
Practical Change
You belong to a promise older than your own story, and you inherit it by faith, not by ancestry.
Key Scriptures
Galatians 3:29; Galatians 3:7
