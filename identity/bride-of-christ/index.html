Bride of Christ

Title
Bride of Christ
Meaning
The church is presented to Christ as a bride, espoused to one husband and made ready for the marriage of the Lamb.
Practical Change
You belong to Christ with the closeness and faithfulness of a marriage, not a casual arrangement.
Key Scriptures
Ephesians 5:27; 2 Corinthians 11:2; Revelation 19:7
