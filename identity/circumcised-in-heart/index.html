Circumcised in Heart

Title
Circumcised in Heart
Meaning
Believers are circumcised with the circumcision made without hands, in putting off the body of the sins of the flesh, and they worship God in the spirit.
Practical Change
Your heart, not just your outward life, has been cut away from the old flesh.
Key Scriptures
Colossians 2:11; Philippians 3:3; Romans 2:29
