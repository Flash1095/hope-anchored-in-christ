Clothed With Christ

Title
Clothed With Christ
Meaning
As many as have been baptized into Christ have put on Christ, wearing him the way a garment is worn, so that he covers and identifies them.
Practical Change
You present yourself to the world in Christ's name and character, not in your old reputation.
Key Scriptures
Galatians 3:27; Romans 13:14
