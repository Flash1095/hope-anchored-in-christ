Title
Sealed With the Spirit
Meaning
Believers are sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise, the earnest of their inheritance until the redemption of the purchased possession.
Practical Change
God has marked you as his own, and the Spirit in you is the down payment of what is to come.
Key Scriptures
Ephesians 1:13; 2 Corinthians 1:22; Ephesians 4:30
