Holy Nation and Peculiar People

Title
Holy Nation and Peculiar People
Meaning
Believers together are God's own people, purchased and set apart to be his, distinct from the world in allegiance and conduct.
Practical Change
You belong to a people before you belong to any tribe, party, or nation.
Key Scriptures
1 Peter 2:9; Titus 2:14
