Title
Branch in the Vine
Meaning
Christ is the true vine and believers are the branches; abiding in him, they bear fruit, and apart from him they can do nothing.
Practical Change
Fruitfulness comes from staying close to Christ, not from straining harder on your own.
Key Scriptures
John 15:1; John 15:5
