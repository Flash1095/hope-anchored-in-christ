Christ in You

Title
Christ in You
Meaning
Christ in believers is the hope of glory; Christ lives in them, and the Spirit who lives in them is greater than he that is in the world. Doubt: Overlaps Temple of the Holy Spirit (indwelling) and Crucified With Christ.
Practical Change
You carry Christ within you, and his presence is your hope.
Key Scriptures
Colossians 1:27; Galatians 2:20; 1 John 4:4
