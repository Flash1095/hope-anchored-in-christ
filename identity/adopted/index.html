Title
Adopted
Meaning
Believers have received the Spirit of adoption, whereby they cry Abba, Father, and are no longer servants but sons.
Practical Change
You have a permanent place in God's family, with the rights and access of a son or daughter.
Key Scriptures
Romans 8:15; Galatians 4:5; Galatians 4:6; Galatians 4:7; Ephesians 1:5
