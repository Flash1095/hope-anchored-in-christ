Title
Child of God
Meaning
Through faith in Christ, believers receive power to become sons and daughters of God, with the Spirit bearing witness that they are his children.
Practical Change
You approach God as a Father, not a stranger, and you belong to his household.
Key Scriptures
John 1:12; Romans 8:16; 1 John 3:1
