Title
Free
Meaning
Believers are made free by the truth and by the Son, freed from the law of sin and death, and called to stand fast in that liberty.
Practical Change
Freedom means serving in love, not returning to bondage or using liberty as a cover for sin.
Key Scriptures
John 8:32; John 8:36; Galatians 5:1; Romans 8:2
