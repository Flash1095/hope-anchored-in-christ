Under No Condemnation

Title
Under No Condemnation
Meaning
There is no condemnation for those in Christ Jesus, and believers will not come into condemnation, because judgment for their sin fell on Christ.
Practical Change
You stop living under a verdict God has already lifted, and you face failures with repentance instead of dread.
Key Scriptures
Romans 8:1; John 5:24
