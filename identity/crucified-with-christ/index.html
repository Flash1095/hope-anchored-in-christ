Crucified With Christ

Title
Crucified With Christ
Meaning
Believers were crucified with Christ: the old self was put to death with him, so that sin no longer rules them and Christ lives in them.
Practical Change
You treat old patterns as dead, not as masters, and you live by faith in the Son of God.
Key Scriptures
Galatians 2:20; Romans 6:6; Romans 6:11
