Title
White Stone and a New Name
Meaning
To him that overcometh Christ will give a white stone, and in the stone a new name written, which no man knows saving he that receives it. Doubt: Future reward, not present identity.
Practical Change
You will receive a new name from Christ himself, known only to you and him.
Key Scriptures
Revelation 2:17; Revelation 3:12
