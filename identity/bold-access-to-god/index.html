Bold Access to God

Title
Bold Access to God
Meaning
Believers have boldness and access with confidence by the faith of Christ, and they may come boldly unto the throne of grace. Doubt: Privilege/access rather than identity title; overlaps Reconciled and Free.
Practical Change
You approach God freely and confidently, not timidly.
Key Scriptures
Ephesians 3:12; Hebrews 4:16; Hebrews 10:19
