Title
Sheep of the Good Shepherd
Meaning
Believers are the sheep of the good shepherd, who laid down his life for them; they hear his voice and follow him, and they shall never perish.
Practical Change
You follow one voice above the noise, trusting the shepherd who already died for you.
Key Scriptures
John 10:14; John 10:27; 1 Peter 2:25; Luke 12:32
