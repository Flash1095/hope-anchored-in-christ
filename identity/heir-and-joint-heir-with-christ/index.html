Heir and Joint-Heir With Christ

Title
Heir and Joint-Heir With Christ
Meaning
As children of God, believers are heirs of God and joint-heirs with Christ, sharing in his inheritance and, if they suffer with him, in his glory.
Practical Change
Your future is an inheritance kept for you, not wages you must earn.
Key Scriptures
Romans 8:17; Galatians 4:7; Titus 3:7
