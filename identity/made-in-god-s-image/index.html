Title
Made in God's Image
Meaning
God created man in his own image, and in Christ that image is renewed in knowledge after the image of him that created him.
Practical Change
Your worth was stamped on you at creation and is being renewed in Christ.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 1:27; Colossians 3:10
