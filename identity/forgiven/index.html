Forgiven

Title
Forgiven
Meaning
In Christ believers have forgiveness of sins according to the riches of God's grace, and are commanded to forgive one another as God forgave them.
Practical Change
Guilt is answered, not carried; and forgiveness received becomes forgiveness extended.
Key Scriptures
Ephesians 4:32; Colossians 2:13; 1 John 2:12
