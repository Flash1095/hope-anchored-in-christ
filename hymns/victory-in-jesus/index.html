Victory in Jesus

Eugene Bartlett, a gospel songwriter and music publisher, wrote this song in 1939 near the end of his life. It tells of redemption from sin and the believer's victory through Christ, and it became a standard of gospel singing, still sung at revivals and funerals generations later.
