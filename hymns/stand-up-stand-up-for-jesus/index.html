Stand Up, Stand Up for Jesus

George Duffield Jr. wrote this hymn in 1858 following the death of his friend and fellow minister Dudley Tyng, whose dying exhortation to stand for Christ inspired it. It was first sung at a Young Men's Christian Association meeting in Philadelphia. George Webb's tune Webb, composed earlier in the century, became its standard setting, and the hymn became a rallying song for Christian courage.

Themes

Courage; Stand Firm; Faith
