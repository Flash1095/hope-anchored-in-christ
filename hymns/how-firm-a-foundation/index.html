Scripture

Story
This hymn appeared in John Rippon's Selection of Hymns in 1787, signed only with the letter K, so its author remains unknown. Each verse speaks a promise of God directly to the fearful believer: fear not, I am with you; when you pass through the waters, I will be with you. It was a favorite of American presidents and soldiers alike.
