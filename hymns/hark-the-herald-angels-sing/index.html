Hark! The Herald Angels Sing

Hymn
Hark! The Herald Angels Sing
Author
Charles Wesley
Year
1739
Tune
MENDELSSOHN
Story
Charles Wesley wrote this Christmas hymn in 1739, a year after his conversion, and it was first published in Hymns and Sacred Poems. It announces the birth of Christ as good news of peace between God and humanity, drawing on the angels' announcement to the shepherds. The familiar tune MENDELSSOHN, adapted from Felix Mendelssohn, became attached to the text in the mid-nineteenth century.
