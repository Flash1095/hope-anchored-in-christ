Glorious Things of Thee Are Spoken

Hymn
Glorious Things of Thee Are Spoken
Author
John Newton
Year
1779
Tune
AUSTRIAN HYMN
Story
John Newton published this hymn in the Olney Hymns of 1779, written on Isaiah 33:20-21 with echoes of Psalm 87. It celebrates Zion, the city of God, as a picture of the church: founded on Christ, fed by streams of grace, and kept safe by God's presence. It is most commonly sung to the tune AUSTRIAN HYMN by Joseph Haydn.
