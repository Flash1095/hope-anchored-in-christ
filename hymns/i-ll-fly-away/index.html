Hymn

Author

Year
1929
Tune
I'LL FLY AWAY
Scripture
1 Thessalonians 4:17
Story
Albert Brumley wrote this joyful song about heaven in 1929 while picking cotton on his family's farm in Oklahoma. It pictures the believer's release from earthly trouble into glory. It became one of the most recorded songs in gospel music and a staple of funerals, revivals, and family singings across America.
