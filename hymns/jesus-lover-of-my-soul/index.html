Jesus, Lover of My Soul

Hymn
Jesus, Lover of My Soul
Author
Charles Wesley
Year
1740
Tune
ABERYSTWYTH
Story
Charles Wesley wrote this hymn in 1740, and it appeared in Hymns and Sacred Poems that same year. It pictures the believer fleeing to Christ for refuge and shelter, like a traveler seeking cover in a storm. It is often counted among Wesley's finest hymns and is widely sung to the Welsh tune ABERYSTWYTH.
