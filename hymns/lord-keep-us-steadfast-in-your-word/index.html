Lord, Keep Us Steadfast in Your Word

Author
Martin Luther
Hymn

Related Books

Related Topics
Prayer; Faith; Perseverance
Scripture
John 8:31; Psalm 119:105
Story
Martin Luther wrote this short prayer hymn in 1543, asking God to keep his people steadfast in the Word and to restrain the enemies of the gospel. It was written for children to sing as a prayer for the church under threat, and it became a regular prayer hymn in Lutheran worship. Its plain petition still fits any congregation asking to hold fast to Scripture.
