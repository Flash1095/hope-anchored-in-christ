The Church's One Foundation

Hymn
The Church's One Foundation
Author
Samuel John Stone
Year
1866
Tune
AURELIA
Story
Samuel John Stone wrote this hymn in 1866 during controversy in the Church of England over the authority of Scripture, as part of a set of hymns on the Apostles' Creed. Samuel Sebastian Wesley composed the tune Aurelia for it in 1864, before the text was written. The hymn confesses one church founded on Christ, united across divisions and sustained through trial.
Scripture
Ephesians 2:20; Matthew 16:18
