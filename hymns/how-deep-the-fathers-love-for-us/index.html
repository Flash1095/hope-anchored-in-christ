Story
Stuart Townend wrote this hymn in England, with a stated copyright date of 1995; its first recorded appearance came on a live Stoneleigh Bible Week album in 1996. Townend aimed for a modern hymn with the humble gratitude of an older hymn, dwelling on the Father giving his only Son to make sinners his treasure.
