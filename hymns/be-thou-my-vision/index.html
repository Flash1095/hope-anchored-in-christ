Scripture

Story
This prayer comes from early medieval Ireland, traditionally linked to the poet Dallan Forgaill in the 8th century. It asks God to be the believer's sight, wisdom, and treasure above all else. Mary Byrne translated the old Irish text into English in 1905, and Eleanor Hull versified it in 1912, giving the church one of its most beloved prayers of devotion.
