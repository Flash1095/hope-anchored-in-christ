What a Friend We Have in Jesus

Joseph Scriven, an Irish immigrant to Canada, wrote this hymn in 1855 to comfort his sick mother back in Ireland. Scriven had lost two fiancees to death, gave away his wealth, and spent his life serving the poor. He never meant the poem for publication, but it became one of the most beloved hymns of prayer ever written.
