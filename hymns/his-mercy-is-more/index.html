His Mercy Is More

Author

Hymn

Related Books

Related Topics

Scripture
Psalm 103:11; Lamentations 3:22
Story
Matt Boswell and Matt Papa wrote this modern hymn in 2016, setting God's mercy above the weight of human sin. Drawing on Psalm 103's picture of mercy as high as the heavens are above the earth, it has become a staple of contemporary congregational singing about forgiveness.
