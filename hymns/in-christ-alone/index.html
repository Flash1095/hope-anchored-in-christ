In Christ Alone

Hymn
In Christ Alone
Author
Keith Getty and Stuart Townend
Year
2001
Tune
IN CHRIST ALONE
Scripture
Romans 8:1
Story
Keith Getty and Stuart Townend wrote this modern hymn in 2001 with a deliberate aim: to confess the whole gospel in a single song, from incarnation to resurrection, so a congregation could sing its faith complete. It spread rapidly through churches worldwide and became one of the defining hymns of the twenty-first century church.
