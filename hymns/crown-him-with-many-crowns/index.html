Author

Year

Tune

Scripture
Revelation 19:12
Story
Matthew Bridges, an English writer who converted from Anglicanism to Catholicism, wrote this hymn of Christ's many crowns in 1851. Godfrey Thring later added verses, and together the hymn crowns Jesus as Lord of life, love, and heaven itself. Its tune, Diademata, was written by George Elvey for a missionary anniversary.
