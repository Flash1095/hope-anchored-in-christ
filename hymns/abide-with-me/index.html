Abide with Me

Henry Lyte, a pastor in the fishing town of Brixham, England, wrote this hymn as tuberculosis was ending his life. On his final Sunday in the pulpit, knowing he would soon leave for Italy in a last hope of recovery, he gave his congregation this evening prayer. He died weeks later in Nice, France. It has been sung at bedsides, funerals, and farewells ever since.
