Nearer, My God, to Thee

Hymn
Nearer, My God, to Thee
Author
Sarah Flower Adams
Year
1841
Tune
BETHANY
Scripture
Genesis 28:12
Story
Sarah Flower Adams wrote this hymn in 1841 for her brother-in-law, a minister preaching on Jacob's dream of a ladder reaching to heaven. Its prayer to be drawn nearer to God through every circumstance made it beloved at funerals and farewells. Tradition holds that the band of the Titanic played it as the ship went down.
