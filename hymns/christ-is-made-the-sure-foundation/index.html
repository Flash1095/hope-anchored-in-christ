Story
This hymn comes from a seventh-century Latin hymn, Urbs beata Jerusalem, sung at the dedication of a church, picturing the church as a city built on Christ the cornerstone. John Mason Neale translated it into English in the nineteenth century. It is still sung at church dedications and anniversaries, and whenever the church confesses Christ as its foundation.
