Love Divine, All Loves Excelling

Charles Wesley wrote this great hymn of divine love in 1747. It asks for the love of God to dwell fully in the believer's heart, making the Christian life complete. Set to the tune Beecher by John Zundel, it has been sung at weddings, revivals, and state occasions, and is often called Wesley's finest hymn.
