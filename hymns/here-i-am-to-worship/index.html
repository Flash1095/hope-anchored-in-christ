Related Topics

Scripture

Story
Tim Hughes, a young worship leader in England, wrote this song in 2000, also known by its opening phrase Light of the World. It moves from Christ's humble coming to the worship he deserves, and it became one of the most widely used contemporary worship songs of the 2000s.
