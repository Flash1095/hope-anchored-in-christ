Take My Life and Let It Be

Hymn
Take My Life and Let It Be
Author
Frances Ridley Havergal
Year
1874
Tune
HENDON
Scripture
Romans 12:1
Story
Frances Ridley Havergal wrote this hymn of consecration in 1874 during a visit to friends, when she felt freshly convicted to give every part of her life to God. Verse by verse it offers hands, feet, voice, silver, gold, will, and heart. It remains the church's classic prayer of total surrender.
