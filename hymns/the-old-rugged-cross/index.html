The Old Rugged Cross

George Bennard, a Methodist evangelist, wrote this hymn in 1913 during revival meetings in Albion, Michigan. He had been teased about the meaning of the cross and spent weeks meditating on it before the song came. It became the most popular gospel song of its generation and is still sung wherever the cross is preached.
