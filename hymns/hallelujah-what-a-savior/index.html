Hallelujah, What a Savior

Hymn
Hallelujah, What a Savior
Author
Philip P. Bliss
Year
1875
Tune
HALLELUJAH WHAT A SAVIOR
Story
This hymn is also known under the title Man of Sorrows. Philip Bliss wrote both words and tune, and it was first published in 1875 in the International Lessons Monthly under the title Redemption, then included in Gospel Hymns No. 2 in 1876. It follows Isaiah's suffering servant through Christ's atoning death to his exaltation, with each stanza closing in praise of so great a Saviour.
