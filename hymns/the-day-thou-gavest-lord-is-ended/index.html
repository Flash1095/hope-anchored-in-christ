The Day Thou Gavest, Lord, Is Ended

Hymn
The Day Thou Gavest, Lord, Is Ended
Author
John Ellerton
Year
1870
Tune
ST CLEMENT
Story
John Ellerton wrote this evening hymn in 1870 for a missionary service, and it was first published in a collection for a choral festival. Clement Scholefield composed the tune St Clement for it in 1874. As evening falls in one land and dawn breaks in another, the hymn pictures unceasing praise circling the earth, and it is widely sung at evening services and at the close of the day.
