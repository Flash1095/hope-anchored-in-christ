Author

Year

Tune
FAITHFULNESS
Scripture
Lamentations 3:22-23
Story
Thomas Chisholm lived a quiet life of frail health and modest means, never wealthy and often unwell. Out of that ordinary life he wrote this hymn on Lamentations 3: new mercies every morning, faithfulness without end. William Runyan set it to music in 1923, and it became beloved through the Moody Bible Institute and Billy Graham's crusades.
