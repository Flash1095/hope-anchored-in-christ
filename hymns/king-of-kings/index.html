Story
Brooke Ligertwood, Scott Ligertwood, and Jason Ingram wrote this hymn for Hillsong Worship in 2019, releasing it on the album Awake. In four verses it traces the whole gospel story, from creation and the fall through the incarnation, cross, and resurrection to the church and the return of Christ.
