Jesus Shall Reign Where'er the Sun

Hymn
Jesus Shall Reign Where'er the Sun
Author
Isaac Watts
Year
1719
Tune
DUKE STREET
Story
Isaac Watts published this hymn in 1719 in his Psalms of David Imitated, as a paraphrase of Psalm 72. It looks forward to the worldwide reign of Christ, with people of every nation blessing his name. It is commonly sung to the tune DUKE STREET and became a favorite missionary hymn.
Scripture
Psalm 72:8; Revelation 11:15
