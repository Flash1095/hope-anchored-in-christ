And Can It Be That I Should Gain

Hymn
And Can It Be That I Should Gain
Author
Charles Wesley
Year
1738
Tune
SAGINA
Story
Charles Wesley wrote this hymn in May 1738, shortly after his evangelical conversion, and it first appeared in Hymns and Sacred Poems in 1739. It marvels that Christ would die for one who had opposed him, and it has become one of the most loved testimonies of personal redemption in English hymnody. Its best-known tune is SAGINA, composed by Thomas Campbell.
