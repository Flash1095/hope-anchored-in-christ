Savior of the Nations, Come

Author
Ambrose of Milan
Hymn
Savior of the Nations, Come
Related Books
Matthew; Luke
Related Topics
Hope; Salvation
Scripture
Matthew 1:23; Luke 1:35
Story
This Advent hymn began as the Latin Veni, redemptor gentium by Ambrose, bishop of Milan in the fourth century, confessing Christ's miraculous birth as true God and true man. Martin Luther made a German version of it in 1524, which is why it is closely tied to the Reformation as well as to the early church. It is sung at Advent as a call for the promised Savior to come.
