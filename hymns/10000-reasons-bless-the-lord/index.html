Scripture

Story
Matt Redman co-wrote this song with Jonas Myrin in 2011, basing it on Psalm 103's call to bless the Lord and forget none of his benefits. Also known under the alternate title Bless the Lord, O My Soul, it won the Grammy Award for Best Contemporary Christian Music Song in 2013 and became a staple of congregational praise.
