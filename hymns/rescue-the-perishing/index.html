Rescue the Perishing

Hymn
Rescue the Perishing
Author
Fanny J. Crosby
Year
1869
Tune
RESCUE
Story
Composer William Howard Doane gave Fanny Crosby the subject of rescuing the perishing, and she wrote the text in 1869 after speaking at a New York City mission in the Bowery. That evening a young man who had drifted from his mother's teaching came forward for prayer and professed faith. Crosby completed the hymn that night, and Doane set it to music; it later became a staple of Moody and Sankey evangelistic meetings.
