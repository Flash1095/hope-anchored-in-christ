Author

Year

Tune

Scripture
Psalm 119:171
Story
Charles Wesley wrote this hymn in 1739 on the first anniversary of his conversion, when his heart was, in his own words, strangely warmed. Originally eighteen verses, it pours out praise for the grace that saved him. It has opened Methodist worship and revival meetings for nearly three centuries.
