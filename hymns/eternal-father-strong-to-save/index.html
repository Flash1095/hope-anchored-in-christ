Eternal Father, Strong to Save

Hymn
Eternal Father, Strong to Save
Author
William Whiting
Year
1860
Tune
MELITA
Story
William Whiting, headmaster of the Winchester College Choristers' School, wrote this hymn in 1860 to comfort a pupil who was anxious about an approaching sea voyage. It was first published, with revisions, in Hymns Ancient and Modern in 1861. John Bacchus Dykes composed the tune Melita in 1861, named for Malta, where Paul was shipwrecked, and the hymn became widely known as the Navy Hymn.
