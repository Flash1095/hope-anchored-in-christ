Trust and Obey

Hymn
Trust and Obey
Author
John Sammis
Year
1887
Tune
TRUST AND OBEY
Scripture
1 Samuel 15:22
Story
John Sammis wrote this hymn in 1887 after hearing Dwight Moody preach on obedience, and Daniel Towner composed its tune. Its simple message, that there is no other way to be happy in Jesus but to trust and obey, made it a favorite of Sunday schools, camps, and missionary gatherings.
