O Sacred Head, Now Wounded

Author

Hymn

Related Books

Related Topics

Scripture
Matthew 27:29; Isaiah 53:5
Story
This Passion hymn began as a Latin poem on Christ's suffering, traditionally attributed to Arnulf of Louvain in the thirteenth century, though that attribution is not certain. Paul Gerhardt turned it into German in 1656, and James Waddell Alexander's English translation made it widely known. It meditates verse by verse on the wounded head of Christ on the cross, and is sung above all in Holy Week.
