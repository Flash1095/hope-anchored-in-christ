How Sweet the Name of Jesus Sounds

Hymn
How Sweet the Name of Jesus Sounds
Author
John Newton
Year
1779
Tune
ST PETER
Story
John Newton published this hymn in the Olney Hymns of 1779. It praises the name of Jesus as comfort to the sorrowful, rest to the weary, and the believer's rock and shield. It is often sung to the tune ST PETER by Alexander Reinagle.
Scripture
Acts 4:12; Philippians 2:10
Themes
Name of Jesus; Comfort; Worship
