Story
Joachim Neander, a young Reformed pastor and teacher in seventeenth-century Germany, wrote this paraphrase of Psalm 103 in 1680. Neander loved to walk and worship in the valley near Düsseldorf that later bore his name, the Neanderthal, and his hymns breathe that delight in God's creation and care. Catherine Winkworth's translation made it one of the great English praise hymns.
