O for a Closer Walk with God

Hymn
O for a Closer Walk with God
Author
William Cowper
Year
1772
Tune
CAITHNESS
Story
William Cowper wrote this hymn in 1772, and it appeared in the Olney Hymns of 1779, the collection he produced with John Newton. It laments the loss of the closeness with God the believer once knew and prays for the return of the Holy Spirit's restoring presence. It is commonly sung to the tune CAITHNESS.
Scripture
Genesis 5:24; Micah 6:8
Themes
Devotion; Repentance; Presence of God
