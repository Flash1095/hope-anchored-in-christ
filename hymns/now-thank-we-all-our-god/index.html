Now Thank We All Our God

Author
Martin Rinkart
Hymn

Related Books

Related Topics

Scripture
Psalm 100:4; 1 Thessalonians 5:18
Story
Martin Rinkart, pastor at Eilenburg during the Thirty Years' War, wrote this thanksgiving hymn around 1636, a year when plague and war had devastated his town and he had buried thousands, including members of his own family. That it is a song of thanks written in such suffering is what has made it endure. It is sung at harvest, at year's end, and in national thanksgivings.
