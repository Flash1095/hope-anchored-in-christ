Scripture

Story
Keith Getty, Kristyn Getty, and Stuart Townend wrote this hymn in 2009, copyrighted that year by Thankyou Music. Following the pattern of Hebrews 11, it moves from creation and the patriarchs through the prophets to the church's mission, calling believers to walk by faith and not by sight.
