Scripture

Story
Jean Tisserand, a fifteenth-century French Franciscan, wrote this Easter hymn retelling the resurrection appearances, from the empty tomb to the disciples' joy and Thomas's confession. Its repeated alleluias carry the Easter proclamation from verse to verse. John Mason Neale's English translation brought it into English Easter worship.
