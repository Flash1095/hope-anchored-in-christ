There Is a Higher Throne

Keith and Kristyn Getty wrote this hymn in 2002, and it appeared on their album In Christ Alone. It pictures the worship of heaven, where the Lamb becomes the Shepherd King, tears are wiped away, and the redeemed sing before God's throne.
