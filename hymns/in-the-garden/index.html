In the Garden

Author

Hymn

Related Books

Related Topics

Scripture
John 20:16; Luke 24:32
Story
C. Austin Miles, a pharmacist turned hymnwriter, wrote both the words and music of this gospel song in 1912. It pictures Mary Magdalene meeting the risen Christ in the garden on Easter morning, and it became one of the most beloved gospel songs of the twentieth century.
