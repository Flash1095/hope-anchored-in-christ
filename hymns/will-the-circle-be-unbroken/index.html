Will the Circle Be Unbroken

Hymn
Will the Circle Be Unbroken
Author
Ada R. Habershon; music by Charles H. Gabriel
Year
1907
Tune
CIRCLE
Scripture
Revelation 21:4
Story
Ada Habershon wrote this funeral hymn in 1907 about the hope that death does not break the family circle, because heaven reunites those who die in the Lord. Charles Gabriel set it to music, and it later became a beloved standard of gospel and country music, sung at countless funerals and family gatherings.
