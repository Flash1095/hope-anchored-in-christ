God Moves in a Mysterious Way

Hymn
God Moves in a Mysterious Way
Author
William Cowper
Year
1774
Tune
DUNDEE
Story
William Cowper wrote this hymn in 1773, and it was first published in 1774 in John Newton's Twenty-six Letters on Religious Subjects, before appearing in the Olney Hymns of 1779. Written during a period of severe depression, it confesses trust in God's wise providence even when his purposes cannot be understood. It is most frequently sung to the tune DUNDEE. The popular story linking it to a prevented suicide attempt is traditional and not firmly documented, so it is not stated here as fact.

Psalm 77:19; Isaiah 55:8
