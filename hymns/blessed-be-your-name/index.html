Blessed Be Your Name

Author
Matt Redman and Beth Redman
Hymn

Related Books

Related Topics

Scripture
Job 1:21; Psalm 113:2
Story
Matt and Beth Redman wrote this song in 2002, releasing it on the album Where Angels Fear to Tread. Following Job's example, it blesses God's name in times of plenty and in times of want, and it became one of the defining worship songs of the early 2000s.
