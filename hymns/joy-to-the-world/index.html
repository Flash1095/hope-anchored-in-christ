Joy to the World

Isaac Watts published this hymn in 1719 in his Psalms of David Imitated, as a paraphrase of Psalm 98 looking forward to the reign of Christ. Although now sung at Christmas, it celebrates Christ's kingship over all the earth rather than his birth alone. The tune ANTIOCH, adapted by Lowell Mason, became its standard setting in the nineteenth century.
