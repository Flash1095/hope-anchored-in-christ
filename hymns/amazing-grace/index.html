Scripture

Story
John Newton once captained slave ships. In a violent storm off Ireland in 1748 he cried out to God, and his slow conversion began. Years later, as a pastor in Olney, England, he wrote this testimony of grace that found a wretch and made him new. Near the end of his life he worked alongside William Wilberforce for the abolition of the slave trade he had once served.
