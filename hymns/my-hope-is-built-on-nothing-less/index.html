My Hope Is Built on Nothing Less

Hymn
My Hope Is Built on Nothing Less
Author
Edward Mote
Year
1834
Tune
SOLID ROCK
Scripture
Matthew 7:24-27
Story
Edward Mote, a London cabinetmaker who became a Baptist pastor, wrote the refrain of this hymn first as his daily motto: on Christ the solid Rock I stand. The full hymn grew from it, contrasting every sinking sand of human hope with the one foundation that holds. It is still sung wherever believers need to remember what their hope rests on.
