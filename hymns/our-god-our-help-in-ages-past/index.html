Our God, Our Help in Ages Past

Isaac Watts published this hymn in 1719 in his Psalms of David Imitated, as a paraphrase of Psalm 90. It contrasts human frailty and the brevity of life with God's everlasting faithfulness across generations. It is traditionally sung to the tune ST ANNE and is often chosen at funerals, remembrances, and national occasions.

Themes

Refuge; Eternity; Trust
