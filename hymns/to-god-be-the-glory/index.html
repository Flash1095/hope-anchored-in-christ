To God Be the Glory

Hymn
To God Be the Glory
Author
Fanny J. Crosby
Year
1875
Tune
TO GOD BE THE GLORY
Story
Fanny Crosby wrote this gospel song with a tune by William Howard Doane, and it was first published in 1875 in Lowry and Doane's collection Brightest and Best, under the title Praise for Redemption. Ira Sankey introduced it in Britain during the Moody campaigns of 1873-1874, where it became popular long before it was widely sung in the United States. Its wider American use followed its revival at the 1954 Billy Graham crusade in London.
