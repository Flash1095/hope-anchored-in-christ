Blest Be the Tie That Binds

Hymn
Blest Be the Tie That Binds
Author
John Fawcett
Year
1782
Tune
DENNIS
Story
John Fawcett published this hymn in 1782 under the title Brotherly Love. It celebrates the bond of Christian fellowship that unites believers in shared sorrows, prayers, and hopes. The widely repeated story that Fawcett wrote it when deciding to stay with his poor congregation rather than accept a London pulpit is traditional and lacks direct evidence, so it is not stated here as fact. It is commonly sung to the tune DENNIS.
Scripture
Acts 2:42; Colossians 3:14
Themes
Fellowship; Unity; Love
