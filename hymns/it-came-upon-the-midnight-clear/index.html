It Came Upon the Midnight Clear

Hymn
It Came Upon the Midnight Clear
Author
Edmund Hamilton Sears
Year
1849
Tune
CAROL
Story
Edmund Hamilton Sears, a Unitarian minister in Massachusetts, wrote this carol in 1849, in a period of social tension in America and unrest in Europe. Richard Storrs Willis composed the tune Carol for it in 1850. Unlike many carols, it dwells on the angels' message of peace in a weary world and on the hope that the message will one day be fully heard.
