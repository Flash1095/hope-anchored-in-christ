O Little Town of Bethlehem

Hymn
O Little Town of Bethlehem
Author
Phillips Brooks
Year
1868
Tune
ST LOUIS
Story
Phillips Brooks wrote this carol in 1868 for the children of his Philadelphia Sunday school, three years after he had visited Bethlehem on Christmas Eve. His organist, Lewis Redner, composed the tune St Louis for it the same year. The carol contrasts the quiet of the small town with the birth of Christ within it, and it remains one of the most sung American Christmas hymns.
Scripture
Micah 5:2; Luke 2:11
