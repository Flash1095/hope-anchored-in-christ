Turn Your Eyes upon Jesus

Author
Helen H. Lemmel
Hymn

Related Books

Related Topics

Scripture
Hebrews 12:2; 2 Corinthians 3:18
Story
Helen Lemmel, an English hymnwriter who was blind herself, wrote this hymn in 1922 after reading a missionary tract urging readers to turn their eyes upon Jesus. It became widely used in evangelistic meetings, including the Billy Graham crusades, where its call to look to Christ above earthly troubles struck a broad chord.
