Author

Year
1873
Tune
ASSURANCE
Scripture
Hebrews 10:22
Story
Fanny Crosby, blind from infancy, wrote more than 8,000 hymns. In 1873 her friend Phoebe Knapp played a new tune for her and asked what it said. Crosby answered at once with the words of this hymn: a song of quiet certainty that Jesus is ours and heaven is ahead. It remains one of the most sung testimonies of assurance in the church.
