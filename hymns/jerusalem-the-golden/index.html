Author

Scripture
Revelation 21:2; Hebrews 11:10
Story
Bernard, a twelfth-century monk of Cluny, wrote a long Latin poem contrasting the sorrows of this world with the glory of the heavenly Jerusalem. John Mason Neale translated the heavenward portion in the nineteenth century, and this hymn is drawn from that translation. It sings of the city where sorrow ends and God's people see him face to face.
