Author
Isaac Watts
Year

Tune
HAMBURG
Scripture
Galatians 6:14
Story
Isaac Watts, often called the father of English hymnody, wrote this hymn in 1707 when he was 33. Breaking from strict psalm paraphrases, he dared to write freely of Christ's cross, and the church has counted it among the greatest hymns in the language ever since. It turns the gaze from human pride to the crucified Savior.
