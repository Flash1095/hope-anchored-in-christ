Rock of Ages

Augustus Toplady wrote this hymn in 1776, drawing on the picture of Moses sheltered in the cleft of the rock in Exodus 33. Tradition says Toplady himself sheltered from a storm in a cleft rock at Burrington Combe in England, though the story is debated. Either way, the hymn's prayer to hide in Christ's wounds has comforted believers for nearly 250 years.
