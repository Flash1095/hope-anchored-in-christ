Onward, Christian Soldiers

Hymn
Onward, Christian Soldiers
Author
Sabine Baring-Gould
Year
1865
Tune
ST GERTRUDE
Story
Sabine Baring-Gould, an Anglican clergyman, wrote this hymn in 1865 for a children's procession between two Yorkshire villages, originally as a marching song for young people. Arthur Sullivan composed the tune St Gertrude for it in 1871, and the pairing made it one of the best-known Victorian marching hymns. Its military imagery describes the church advancing in spiritual conflict, not earthly warfare.
