Story
This hymn is drawn from a long Latin poem on the name of Jesus traditionally attributed to Bernard of Clairvaux, the twelfth-century Cistercian abbot, though the full poem's authorship is not certain. Edward Caswall's nineteenth-century translation gives the best-known English verses. It meditates on the sweetness of Jesus' name and presence to the believing heart.
