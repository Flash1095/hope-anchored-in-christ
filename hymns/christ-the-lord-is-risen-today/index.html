Christ the Lord Is Risen Today

Hymn
Christ the Lord Is Risen Today
Author
Charles Wesley
Year
1739
Tune
EASTER HYMN
Story
Charles Wesley wrote this Easter hymn in 1739 for use at the first service in the Foundry, the early Methodist meeting place in London. Its repeated alleluias celebrate Christ's resurrection as the defeat of death and the ground of Christian hope. It is traditionally sung to the tune EASTER HYMN.

Matthew 28:6; 1 Corinthians 15:20
