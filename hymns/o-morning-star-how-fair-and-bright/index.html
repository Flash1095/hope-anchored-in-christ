O Morning Star, How Fair and Bright

Philipp Nicolai wrote this hymn in 1599, the same year as Wake, Awake, for Night Is Flying, as a song of love and praise to Christ the Morning Star. Written amid the same plague year in Unna, it turns from suffering to adoration of the Bridegroom who brings light and life. It is often called the queen of chorales, paired with Nicolai's king of chorales.
