Am I a Soldier of the Cross

Hymn
Am I a Soldier of the Cross
Author
Isaac Watts
Year
1724
Tune
ARLINGTON
Story
Isaac Watts published this hymn in 1724 in Hymns and Spiritual Songs. It asks whether the believer will shrink from the hardships of following Christ or follow him boldly, even at cost. It is commonly sung to the tune ARLINGTON.
Scripture
2 Timothy 2:3; Matthew 16:24
Themes
Discipleship; Perseverance; Spiritual Warfare
Related Books
2 Timothy; Matthew
Related Topics
Perseverance; Spiritual Warfare; Obedience
