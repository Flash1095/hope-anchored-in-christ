All the Way My Savior Leads Me

Fanny Crosby wrote this hymn in 1875, and it was her first text set to music by Robert Lowry, who composed the tune specially for the words. It was first published in 1875 in Brightest and Best, edited by Lowry and William Howard Doane. Crosby later linked the hymn to an answered prayer for a small sum of money she urgently needed, which she took as an instance of God's guidance.

Themes

Guidance; Providence; Trust
