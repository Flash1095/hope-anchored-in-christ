Christ Lay in Death's Bonds

Author

Hymn

Related Books

Related Topics
Resurrection; Easter
Scripture
1 Corinthians 15:20; Romans 6:9
Story
Martin Luther wrote this Easter hymn in 1524, reworking an older German Easter song into a Reformation confession of Christ's victory over death and sin. It pictures Christ as the Passover Lamb whose death breaks death's bonds and opens life to believers. It has remained a classic Lutheran Easter hymn ever since.
