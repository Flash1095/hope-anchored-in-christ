Wake, Awake, for Night Is Flying

Philipp Nicolai, a Lutheran pastor, wrote this hymn in 1599 during a plague outbreak in Unna, Germany, where he buried many of his parishioners. Cast as the parable of the wise virgins awaiting the bridegroom, it looks to Christ's return and the wedding feast of the Lamb. It is often called the king of chorales and is sung in Advent and at the end of the church year.
