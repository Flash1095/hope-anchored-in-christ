Soldiers of Christ, Arise

Hymn
Soldiers of Christ, Arise
Author
Charles Wesley
Year
1749
Tune
DIADEMATA
Story
Charles Wesley first published this hymn in Hymns and Sacred Poems in 1749 under the title The Whole Armour of God, expanding Paul's exhortation in Ephesians 6 into verse. It was written while the Wesleys faced physical opposition to their preaching, and it became known as a call to spiritual battle. The tune DIADEMATA is its most familiar modern setting.
Scripture
Ephesians 6:11; Ephesians 6:13
