Scripture

Story
Jonny Robinson, Rich Thompson, and Michael Farren wrote this hymn for CityAlight, a church music ministry in Sydney, Australia, releasing it in 2018. Built on Paul's words in Galatians 2, it traces the Christian life from the gift of faith to final rest, confessing that every good is through Christ, not self.
