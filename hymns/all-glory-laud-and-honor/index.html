All Glory, Laud and Honor

Author
Theodulph of Orléans
Hymn
All Glory, Laud and Honor
Related Books

Related Topics
Worship; Kingdom of God
Scripture
Matthew 21:9; John 12:13
Story
Theodulph, bishop of Orléans, is traditionally said to have written this Palm Sunday hymn around 820, possibly while imprisoned after falling from political favor; that imprisonment story is tradition, not certain fact. Its refrain echoes the crowd's welcome of Jesus into Jerusalem. Catherine Winkworth's English translation brought it into wide English use in the 1850s.
