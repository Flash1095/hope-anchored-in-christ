Holy, Holy, Holy

Hymn
Holy, Holy, Holy
Author
Reginald Heber
Year
1826
Tune
NICAEA
Scripture
Revelation 4:8
Story
Reginald Heber, later Bishop of Calcutta, wrote this hymn for Trinity Sunday in 1826. It takes its words from the worship of heaven in Revelation 4 and Isaiah 6, where angels cry holy to the three-in-one God. The tune Nicaea, named for the council that defended the Trinity, was written by John Bacchus Dykes.
