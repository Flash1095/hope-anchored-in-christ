Come, Ye Sinners, Poor and Needy

Hymn
Come, Ye Sinners, Poor and Needy
Author
Joseph Hart
Year
1759
Tune
RESTORATION
Story
Joseph Hart first published this hymn in 1759, originally under the title Come, and Welcome to Jesus Christ. Written after Hart's conversion under George Whitefield's preaching, it invites sinners, the weary, and the heavy-laden to come to Jesus just as they are. It is commonly sung to the tune RESTORATION, an American folk melody.
Scripture
Matthew 11:28; Luke 15:20
