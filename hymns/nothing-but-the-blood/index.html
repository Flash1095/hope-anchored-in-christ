Nothing But the Blood

Robert Lowry wrote both words and tune in 1876, asking in each stanza what can wash away sin and answering with the blood of Christ alone. It was published in gospel song collections of the Moody and Sankey era and became a standard evangelistic hymn. The hymn draws directly on the New Testament teaching that forgiveness comes through Christ's shed blood.
