Story
Brooke Ligertwood and Ben Fielding of Hillsong Worship wrote this song in 2016, releasing it on the album let there be light. It celebrates the name of Jesus above every other name, from incarnation to resurrection, and won the 2018 Grammy Award for Best Contemporary Christian Music Performance/Song.
