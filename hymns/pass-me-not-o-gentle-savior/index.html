Pass Me Not, O Gentle Savior

Hymn
Pass Me Not, O Gentle Savior
Author
Fanny Crosby
Year
1868
Tune
PASS ME NOT
Scripture
Luke 18:35-43
Story
Fanny Crosby wrote this hymn in 1868 after speaking at a prison service, where a young inmate cried out, 'Good Lord, do not pass me by.' The plea of the blind beggar Bartimaeus in Luke 18 stands behind it. William Doane set it to music, and it became one of the great invitation hymns of the revival era.
