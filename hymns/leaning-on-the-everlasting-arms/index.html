Leaning on the Everlasting Arms

Hymn
Leaning on the Everlasting Arms
Author
Elisha A. Hoffman
Year
1887
Tune
SHOWALTER
Story
Elisha Hoffman wrote the words in 1887, and Anthony Showalter composed the tune Showalter the same year. The hymn grew out of a correspondence between the two men about the support believers find in God, and it was published in a gospel song collection of the period. Its refrain image of leaning on everlasting arms has made it a favourite at funerals, revivals, and times of weakness.

Deuteronomy 33:27; Proverbs 3:5
