Wonderful Words of Life

Hymn
Wonderful Words of Life
Author
Philip P. Bliss
Year
1874
Tune
WORDS OF LIFE
Story
Philip Bliss wrote both words and tune of this gospel song in 1874, and it became a favourite in evangelistic meetings after he and his wife sang it as a duet during a campaign in New Haven, Connecticut. Bliss, a close associate of Dwight Moody, wrote many of the earliest American gospel songs in the brief period from 1864 until his death in 1876. The song invites hearers to attend to the words of Scripture as words of life.
