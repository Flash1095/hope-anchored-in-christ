Rejoice, the Lord Is King

Hymn
Rejoice, the Lord Is King
Author
Charles Wesley
Year
1744
Tune
DARWALL
Story
Charles Wesley wrote this hymn in 1744 and published it in Hymns for Our Lord's Resurrection in 1746. It calls God's people to rejoice because Christ reigns as King, even while his kingdom is not yet fully seen. The tune DARWALL, by John Darwall, is its most common setting.
Scripture
Psalm 97:1; Revelation 19:16
