Guide Me, O Thou Great Jehovah

William Williams, the great Welsh evangelist, wrote this hymn in Welsh in 1745 as a pilgrim's prayer for the wilderness journey. Peter Williams translated it into English, and the towering Welsh tune Cwm Rhondda carried it around the world. It pictures the Christian life as Israel's journey: led by cloud and fire, fed with heavenly bread, brought safely home.
