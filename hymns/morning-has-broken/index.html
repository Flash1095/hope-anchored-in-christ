Author

Scripture

Story
Eleanor Farjeon wrote these words in 1931 for the Scottish Gaelic melody Bunessan, praising God for the new morning as part of the fresh creation. It reached a vast new audience when Cat Stevens recorded it in 1971, and it has remained a beloved morning hymn ever since.
