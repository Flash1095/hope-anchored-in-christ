My Jesus, I Love Thee

Hymn
My Jesus, I Love Thee
Author
William R. Featherston
Year
1864
Tune
GORDON
Story
William Featherston, a teenager in Montreal, wrote this poem about love for Christ, dated by some sources to 1862; it was first published anonymously in the London Hymn Book in 1864. The author's identity was only established decades later, after his early death. Adoniram Judson Gordon, dissatisfied with the earlier setting, composed the tune Gordon for it, published in 1876, and that pairing became standard.

1 John 4:19; Deuteronomy 6:5
