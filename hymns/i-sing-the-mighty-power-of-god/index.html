I Sing the Mighty Power of God

Hymn
I Sing the Mighty Power of God
Author
Isaac Watts
Year
1715
Tune
ELLACOMBE
Story
Isaac Watts published this hymn in 1715 in his Divine Songs for children, though it has long been sung by adults as well. It praises God's power and wisdom displayed in creation, from the seas and mountains to the smallest creatures. The tune ELLACOMBE is its most common setting.
Scripture
Psalm 19:1; Genesis 1:1
Themes
Creation; Worship; Power of God
