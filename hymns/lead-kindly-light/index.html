Lead, Kindly Light

Hymn
Lead, Kindly Light
Author
John Henry Newman
Year
1833
Tune
LUX BENIGNA
Story
John Henry Newman wrote this poem, originally titled The Pillar of the Cloud, in 1833 while becalmed in the Straits of Bonifacio on his way home from Italy, after illness had delayed his journey. It was first published in the British Magazine in 1834. John Bacchus Dykes later composed the tune Lux Benigna for it in 1865, though it is also sung to Sandon.
Scripture
Exodus 13:21; Psalm 119:105
