Alas! and Did My Saviour Bleed

Hymn
Alas! and Did My Saviour Bleed
Author
Isaac Watts
Year
1707
Tune
MARTYRDOM
Story
Isaac Watts published this hymn in 1707 in Hymns and Spiritual Songs. It meditates with astonishment that Christ would suffer and die for sinners, and it closes with the believer's resolve to give all to him in return. It is often sung to the tune MARTYRDOM.
Scripture
Isaiah 53:5; Galatians 6:14
Themes
Atonement; Cross; Surrender
