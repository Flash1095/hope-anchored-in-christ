Come, We That Love the Lord

Hymn
Come, We That Love the Lord
Author
Isaac Watts
Year
1707
Tune
MARCHING TO ZION
Story
Isaac Watts published this hymn in 1707 in Hymns and Spiritual Songs. It invites all who love the Lord to join in joyful praise as pilgrims journeying together toward heaven, and it is also known by the alternate title We're Marching to Zion, taken from its later refrain. It is commonly sung to the tune MARCHING TO ZION.
Scripture
Psalm 95:1; Psalm 100:1
