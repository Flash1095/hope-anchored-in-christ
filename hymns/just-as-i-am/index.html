Just As I Am

Hymn
Just As I Am
Author
Charlotte Elliott
Year
1835
Tune
WOODWORTH
Scripture
John 6:37
Story
Charlotte Elliott, an invalid sister of a London clergyman, was distressed that her illness kept her from doing anything useful for God. A visiting minister told her to come to Christ just as she was. Years later, on the anniversary of that conversation, she wrote this invitation hymn. Billy Graham's crusades made it the most famous altar-call hymn in the world.
