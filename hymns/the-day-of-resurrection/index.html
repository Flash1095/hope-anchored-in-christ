Scripture
Matthew 28:6; 1 Corinthians 15:20
Story
John of Damascus, an eighth-century monk and theologian, wrote the Greek Easter canon from which this hymn is drawn, celebrating Christ's rising as the Passover from death to life. John Mason Neale translated it into English in the nineteenth century. It is sung on Easter as a call to rejoice in the empty tomb.
