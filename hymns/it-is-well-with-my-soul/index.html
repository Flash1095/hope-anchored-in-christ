It Is Well with My Soul

Hymn
It Is Well with My Soul
Author
Horatio Spafford
Year
1873
Tune
VILLE DU HAVRE
Scripture
Romans 8:28
Story
Horatio Spafford, a Chicago lawyer, lost his fortune in the Great Chicago Fire of 1871. Two years later he sent his wife and four daughters ahead to Europe by ship; the vessel collided mid-Atlantic and all four girls drowned. Sailing to join his grieving wife, Spafford passed over the spot where they were lost and wrote this hymn of peace that does not depend on circumstances.
