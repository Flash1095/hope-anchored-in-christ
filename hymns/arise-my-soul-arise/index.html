Arise, My Soul, Arise

Hymn
Arise, My Soul, Arise
Author
Charles Wesley
Year
1742
Tune
LENOX
Story
Charles Wesley first published this hymn in Hymns and Sacred Poems in 1742 under the title Behold the Man. It presents Christ as the believer's surety before God's throne, interceding on the basis of his wounds, and it urges the guilty soul to shake off fear. It is widely sung to the tune LENOX by Lewis Edson.
Scripture
Romans 8:34; Hebrews 7:25
