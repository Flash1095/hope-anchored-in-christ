Author
Dan Schutte
Hymn

Related Books

Related Topics
Mission; Obedience
Scripture
Isaiah 6:8; 1 Samuel 3:10
Story
Dan Schutte, a Jesuit composer, wrote this song in 1981 while at Saint Louis University, basing it on Isaiah's call in Isaiah 6 and Samuel's answer in 1 Samuel 3. Written for ordinations and commissioning services, it spread far beyond its Catholic origins and is now sung across denominations.
