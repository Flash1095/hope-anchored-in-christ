There Is a Fountain Filled with Blood

Hymn
There Is a Fountain Filled with Blood
Author
William Cowper
Year
1772
Tune
CLEANSING FOUNTAIN
Story
William Cowper's hymn was first published in 1772 in Richard Conyers's Collection of Psalms and Hymns and republished in the Olney Hymns of 1779. Based on Zechariah 13:1, it presents the blood of Christ as a fountain that cleanses sinners, including the dying thief beside Jesus. It is commonly sung to the American folk tune CLEANSING FOUNTAIN.
Scripture
Zechariah 13:1; 1 John 1:7
