O Come, O Come, Emmanuel

This Advent hymn grew out of the ancient Latin O Antiphons, short chants sung in the days before Christmas that address Christ by Old Testament titles such as Emmanuel and Dayspring. The exact date and author of the hymn form are unknown, and the Latin verses were later combined into one hymn. John Mason Neale's 1851 English translation made it a standard Advent hymn in English.

Advent; Longing; Incarnation
