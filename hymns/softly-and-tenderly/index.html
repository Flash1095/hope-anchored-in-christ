Softly and Tenderly

Hymn
Softly and Tenderly
Author
Will L. Thompson
Year
1880
Tune
THOMPSON
Scripture
Matthew 11:28
Story
Will Thompson, an Ohio music publisher and evangelist, wrote this invitation hymn in 1880. Its gentle call to come home became a favorite of Dwight Moody and Ira Sankey's great evangelistic campaigns. It is still sung at altar calls and funerals, inviting the weary to answer Jesus.
