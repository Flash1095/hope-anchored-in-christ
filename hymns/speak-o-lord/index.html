Speak, O Lord

Author
Keith Getty and Stuart Townend
Hymn

Related Books

Related Topics

Scripture
1 Samuel 3:10; Psalm 119:105
Story
Keith Getty and Stuart Townend wrote this modern hymn in 2005 as a hymn for the preaching of the Word, asking God to speak through Scripture and shape his people by it. It appeared in the New Irish Hymns series and quickly became a standard opening hymn for services built around Bible teaching.
