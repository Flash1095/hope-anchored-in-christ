A Mighty Fortress Is Our God

Martin Luther wrote this battle hymn of the Reformation while plague, persecution, and political pressure closed in on the young Protestant movement. Built on Psalm 46, it confesses God himself as the fortress no enemy can take. It became the anthem of the Reformation and is still sung wherever believers face opposition.

Themes

Strength; Spiritual Warfare; Refuge
