Story
Martin Luther's 1524 paraphrase of Psalm 130 turns the psalmist's cry from the depths into a Reformation prayer: sinners plead for mercy, confess that no works can earn forgiveness, and wait on God's gracious word. It was one of the earliest Lutheran psalm hymns and was sung at funerals as well as in ordinary worship. Its tune, Aus tiefer Not, is one of Luther's own chorale melodies.
