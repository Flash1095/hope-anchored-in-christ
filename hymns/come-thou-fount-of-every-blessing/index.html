Come Thou Fount of Every Blessing

Hymn
Come Thou Fount of Every Blessing
Author
Robert Robinson
Year
1758
Tune
NETTLETON
Scripture
1 Samuel 7:12
Story
Robert Robinson was converted at age 22 under George Whitefield's preaching and wrote this hymn three years later. Its image of the Ebenezer stone comes from 1 Samuel 7, where Samuel raised a stone and said, 'Thus far the Lord has helped us.' The honest line about a heart prone to wander has kept the hymn close to believers who know their own need of grace.
Themes
