Precious Lord, Take My Hand

Author
Thomas A. Dorsey

Related Books

Related Topics
Comfort; Prayer
Scripture
Psalm 23:4; Isaiah 41:10
Story
Thomas A. Dorsey, known as the father of gospel music, wrote this song in 1932 in grief after his wife and newborn son died. A prayer for God to lead through darkness, it became an anthem of comfort and was sung at the funeral of Martin Luther King Jr. in 1968.
