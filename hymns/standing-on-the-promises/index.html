Standing on the Promises

Russell Carter, a Pennsylvania preacher and teacher, wrote this hymn in 1886 while recovering from a serious heart condition. Unable to continue his usual work, he rested his faith on God's promises and turned that experience into a marching song of assurance. It became a staple of Sunday schools and revivals.
