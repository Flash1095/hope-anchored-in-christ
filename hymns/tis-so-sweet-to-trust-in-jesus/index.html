'Tis So Sweet to Trust in Jesus

Louisa Stead wrote this hymn in 1882 during a season of hardship following her husband's death, when she and her young daughter faced poverty. William James Kirkpatrick composed the tune Trust in Jesus for it the same year. The hymn testifies to simple, daily trust in Christ through sorrow and uncertainty, and it has remained a standard gospel song of assurance.
