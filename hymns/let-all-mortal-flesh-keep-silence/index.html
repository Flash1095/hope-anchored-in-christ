Story
This hymn comes from the Liturgy of St. James, one of the oldest Christian liturgies, where it was sung as the bread and wine were brought forward. Its call to reverent silence before Christ's presence reflects the awe of that moment in worship. Gerard Moultrie's 1864 English translation, set to the French carol tune Picardy, brought it into English hymnals.
