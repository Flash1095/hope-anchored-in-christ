Praise, My Soul, the King of Heaven

Hymn
Praise, My Soul, the King of Heaven
Author
Henry Francis Lyte
Year
1834
Tune
LAUDA ANIMA
Story
Henry Francis Lyte wrote this paraphrase of Psalm 103 in 1834 for his congregation at Lower Brixham in Devon, where he ministered to a fishing community. It was published that year in his Spirit of the Psalms. John Goss composed the tune Lauda Anima for it in 1869, and the hymn became one of the great English hymns of praise.

Psalm 103:1; Psalm 103:8
