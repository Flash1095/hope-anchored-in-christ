Dear Christians, One and All, Rejoice

Author
Martin Luther
Hymn

Related Books

Related Topics
Salvation; Grace
Scripture
Romans 3:24; Ephesians 2:8
Story
Martin Luther wrote this hymn in 1523, one of his earliest hymns, telling in song how God in mercy sent his Son to rescue lost sinners who could not save themselves. It became a kind of sung summary of justification by faith for the young Reformation churches. Its tune, Nun freut euch, was one of the first Lutheran chorale tunes.
