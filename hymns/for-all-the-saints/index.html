For All the Saints

Hymn
For All the Saints
Author
William Walsham How
Year
1864
Tune
SINE NOMINE
Story
William Walsham How wrote the words in 1864 for All Saints' Day, celebrating the faithful of every generation. The best-known tune, Sine Nomine, was not composed until 1906, when Ralph Vaughan Williams wrote it for the English Hymnal. The hymn moves from the saints who have finished their course to believers still running, and ends with the vision of the heavenly multitude.
