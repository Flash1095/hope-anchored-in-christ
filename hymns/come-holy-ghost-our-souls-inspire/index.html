Story
This hymn is the English form of the ninth-century Latin Veni Creator Spiritus, traditionally attributed to Rabanus Maurus, though that attribution is not certain. It was sung at ordinations, the opening of councils, and other solemn occasions as a prayer for the Spirit's guidance. John Cosin's 1627 translation is the best-known English version; another well-known translation begins Come, Holy Ghost, Creator Blest.
