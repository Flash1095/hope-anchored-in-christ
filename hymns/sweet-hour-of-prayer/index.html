Sweet Hour of Prayer

Hymn
Sweet Hour of Prayer
Author
William W. Walford
Year
1845
Tune
SWEET HOUR
Story
William Walford, an English preacher, is credited with this hymn, which was first published in the New York Observer in 1845. William Bradbury later composed the tune Sweet Hour for it in 1861. The hymn presents prayer as the believer's regular refuge in trouble and as the place where burdens are carried to God.
Scripture
Philippians 4:6; Psalm 55:17
