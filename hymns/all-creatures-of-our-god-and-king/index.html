All Creatures of Our God and King

Francis of Assisi wrote the Canticle of the Sun in 1225, near the end of his life, praising God through sun, moon, wind, water, fire, and earth. William Henry Draper turned it into this English hymn in 1919, keeping its call for all creation to praise its Maker. It remains one of the most widely sung hymns of creation praise.
