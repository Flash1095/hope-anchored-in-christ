A Charge to Keep I Have

Hymn
A Charge to Keep I Have
Author
Charles Wesley
Year
1762
Tune
BOYLSTON
Story
Charles Wesley first published this hymn in 1762 in his Short Hymns on Select Passages of the Holy Scriptures, where it was based on Leviticus 8:35. It treats the Christian life as a stewardship to be kept faithfully from youth to old age, with an account to give at the end. It is most commonly sung to the tune BOYLSTON by Lowell Mason.
Scripture
Leviticus 8:35; 1 Corinthians 4:2
