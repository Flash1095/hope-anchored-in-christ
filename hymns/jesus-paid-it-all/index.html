Jesus Paid It All

Elvina Hall, a member of a Methodist church in Baltimore, wrote these words in 1865 during a Sunday service while the choir sang another hymn. She scribbled the lines in her hymnal, and the church organist, John Grape, realized he had a tune written years earlier that fit them exactly. The pairing has carried this song of full atonement ever since.
