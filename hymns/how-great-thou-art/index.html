How Great Thou Art

Swedish pastor Carl Boberg wrote the original poem in 1885 after a thunderstorm broke over the Swedish countryside and church bells rang out across the valley. Missionary Stuart Hine later adapted it into English, adding verses shaped by his own years in Ukraine. Billy Graham's crusades carried it around the world, and it became one of the most sung hymns of the twentieth century.
