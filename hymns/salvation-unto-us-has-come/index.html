Scripture

Story
Paul Speratus, a supporter of Luther, wrote this hymn in 1523, the same year as Luther's earliest hymns, setting out the Reformation teaching that salvation comes by grace through faith, not by works of the law. It was published in the first Lutheran hymnbooks and became one of the Reformation's defining songs. Its plain, doctrinal verses made it a teaching hymn for new congregations.
