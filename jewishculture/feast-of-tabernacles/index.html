Feast of Tabernacles

Topic
Feast of Tabernacles
Category
Feast
Summary
Tabernacles, or Sukkot, remembers Israel's forty years in the wilderness, when families lived in fragile booths and God sheltered them.
Jewish Background
For seven days, families eat and sometimes sleep in a sukkah, a booth roofed with branches, and wave the lulav of palm, willow, myrtle, and citron. In Second Temple times the feast included a water-pouring ceremony and great lamps lighting the Temple courts.
Points To Christ
John places two of Jesus' great claims at this feast: during the water ceremony he cries out that whoever thirsts should come to him for living water, and he declares himself the light of the world. And John opens his Gospel saying the Word became flesh and tabernacled among us.
