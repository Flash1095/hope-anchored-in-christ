Passover and Messianic Hope

Topic
Passover and Messianic Hope
Category
Messianic Reading
Summary
Passover looks forward as well as back: the Seder's cup for Elijah and the cry 'Next year in Jerusalem' carry a hope that final redemption will come at Passover as the first did.
Jewish Background
The Passover story ends with a future tense: the meal remembers the Exodus and anticipates the redemption to come. A fifth cup is poured for Elijah, the herald of the Messiah, and the Seder closes with the hope of Jerusalem rebuilt. Tradition holds that the final redemption, like the first, will arrive at Passover time. This entry is the future-hope thread of Passover; the sacrifice itself is covered in The Passover Lamb and the meal's order in Passover Seder.
Points To Christ
The site's reading is that Jesus took this forward-looking meal and filled it: at the Last Supper he spoke of eating it anew in the kingdom of God, and the church still waits at the table for the marriage supper of the Lamb. The hope Passover rehearses every year is, for Christians, already begun and not yet finished.
