The Star Prophecy (Numbers 24:17)

Topic
The Star Prophecy (Numbers 24:17)
Category
Messianic Reading
Summary
'There shall come a Star out of Jacob, and a Sceptre shall rise out of Israel' — Balaam's oracle of a coming ruler, read messianically in Second Temple Judaism.
Jewish Background
Balaam, hired to curse Israel, instead blessed it and spoke of a star and sceptre from Jacob. The Targum of Onkelos renders the verse of a king and a Messiah, and in the second century Simon bar Kokhba took his name — 'son of the star' — from this very verse, showing how firmly it was read as a messianic promise. The oracle's star and sceptre join heavenly sign to royal rule.
Points To Christ
The site's reading is that Matthew's star over Bethlehem answers Balaam's star, and that Revelation names Jesus himself 'the bright and morning star.' Where bar Kokhba's star set in defeat, Christians hold that Jesus' star still rises.
