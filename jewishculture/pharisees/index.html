Pharisees

Topic
Pharisees
Category
History
Summary
The Pharisees were teachers of the law who shaped popular Judaism through study, synagogue teaching, and an oral tradition alongside the written Torah.
Jewish Background
Pharisees believed in resurrection, angels, and divine providence, and they brought Torah into daily life through detailed application. They were the most influential teachers of ordinary Jews, and their debates with Jesus fill the Gospels.
Points To Christ
The Pharisees must not be caricatured: Nicodemus sought Jesus by night and defended him, Gamaliel urged restraint toward the apostles, and Paul proudly called himself a Pharisee. Jesus debated them fiercely precisely because they were serious about God — and because he claimed an authority greater than their tradition.
