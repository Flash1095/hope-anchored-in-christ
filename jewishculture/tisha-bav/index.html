Tisha B'Av

Topic
Tisha B'Av
Category
Custom
Summary
Tisha B'Av, the fast of the ninth of Av, mourns the destruction of Jerusalem and its Temple, an observance rooted in the exile fasts named by Zechariah.
Jewish Background
Zechariah names a fast of the fifth month, kept because Jerusalem had fallen, and later Jewish tradition fixed its observance on 9 Av, the date tradition assigns to the burning of the Temple. Jeremiah 52 and 2 Kings 25 describe that burning in the fifth month of the Babylonian conquest, and Lamentations gives voice to the grief that the fast preserves. Because the Second Temple was also destroyed in the summer month of Av, the same fast came to carry both destructions, and in time other calamities in Jewish history were remembered on the same day. The fast is a full day of abstinence from food and drink, kept with readings of Lamentations and the mourning prayers of the community.
Points To Christ
