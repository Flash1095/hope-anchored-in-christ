The Scapegoat

On the Day of Atonement, two goats stood before the Lord: one sacrificed, one sent alive into the wilderness bearing the sins of the people.

Jewish Background

The high priest laid both hands on the live goat, confessed over it all the iniquities of Israel, and sent it away by a fit man into the wilderness, carrying sins to a land that is not inhabited. The two goats together pictured atonement: death for sin, and sin carried away.

Points To Christ

Messianic readers see both goats fulfilled in Jesus: he died as the sacrifice for sin, and he carried our sins away — outside the camp, as Hebrews puts it, bearing our shame. The scapegoat's journey into the wilderness is the picture of sins remembered no more.
