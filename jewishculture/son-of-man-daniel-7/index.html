The Son of Man (Daniel 7)

Topic
The Son of Man (Daniel 7)
Category
Messianic Reading
Summary
Daniel saw 'one like the Son of man' coming with the clouds of heaven to the Ancient of Days, receiving dominion, glory, and a kingdom that will never pass away.
Jewish Background
In Daniel 7, the Son of Man is a heavenly figure, distinct from the four beasts that represent earthly empires. In Second Temple literature, notably the Parables of Enoch, a Son of Man figure appears as a preexistent heavenly judge. Rabbinic reading varied: some identified the figure with Israel itself, others read it messianically. The title therefore carried both human and heavenly associations in first-century Judaism, which is why it could serve as a claim to more than ordinary humanity.
Points To Christ
The site's reading is that Jesus deliberately took this title as his own — it is his most frequent self-designation in the Gospels — and that at his trial he joined Daniel 7 to Psalm 110, telling the high priest they would see the Son of Man seated at the right hand of power and coming on the clouds (Matthew 26:64). The Christian claim is that the heavenly figure of Daniel's vision is Jesus himself.
