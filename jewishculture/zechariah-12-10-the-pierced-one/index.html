Zechariah 12:10: The Pierced One

Topic
Zechariah 12:10: The Pierced One
Category
Messianic Reading
Summary
Zechariah promises a day when God will pour out grace and the people will look on the one they have pierced, and mourn for him as for an only child.
Jewish Background
Spoken to Jerusalem in a prophecy of siege and deliverance, the verse pictures a pierced figure whose wounding brings national repentance. Jewish readings of the pierced one have varied across the centuries, and the passage remains one of the most discussed in the prophets.
Points To Christ
The New Testament applies the verse directly to Jesus: John records the soldier piercing his side and cites Zechariah, and Revelation pictures every eye seeing him, even those who pierced him. For Messianic readers, the mourning Zechariah foresaw begins at the cross and continues until the day of his return.
