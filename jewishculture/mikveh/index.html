Mikveh

Topic
Mikveh
Category
Custom
Summary
The mikveh is a ritual bath of living water used for purification, conversion, and preparation for holy moments.
Jewish Background
Immersion in a mikveh marks transitions: after impurity, before marriage, and at conversion to Judaism, when the convert emerges as a new person. The bath must hold gathered rainwater or a flowing source, and the whole body goes under.
Points To Christ
John the Baptist's immersions in the Jordan drew on this world, and Christian baptism carries the same shape deeper: buried with Christ and raised to new life. The mikveh's picture of washing and new beginning is fulfilled in the baptism of believers into Jesus.
