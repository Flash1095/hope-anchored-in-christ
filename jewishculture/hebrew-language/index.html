Hebrew Language

Topic
Hebrew Language
Category
Language
Summary
Hebrew is the language of the Old Testament, a tongue whose words carry layers of meaning that translation can only partly convey.
Jewish Background
Biblical Hebrew's root system lets single words branch into families of meaning: shalom means peace, wholeness, and welfare; hesed means steadfast love, mercy, and loyalty. Wordplay runs through the text, from Adam formed from adamah, the ground, to names that tell a person's story.
Points To Christ
The New Testament preserves the Hebrew world even in Greek: Jesus is Yeshua, a name meaning salvation, and Matthew ties that name to his mission, for he will save his people from their sins. Reading the Old Testament with Hebrew ears lets the Messiah's story sound in its original key.
