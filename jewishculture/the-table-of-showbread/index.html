The Table of Showbread

Topic
The Table of Showbread
Category
Custom
Summary
The table of showbread held twelve loaves, renewed every Sabbath, as a continual offering representing Israel's tribes before the Lord.
Jewish Background
Standing in the Holy Place opposite the lampstand, the gold-covered acacia table carried twelve loaves arranged in two rows, one for each tribe, with frankincense set beside them. Fresh loaves replaced them every Sabbath, and the loaves removed were eaten by the priests in a holy place, since only priests might eat them. The bread was called the bread of the Presence because it stood continually before the Lord, a sign that Israel lived by his provision and in his presence.
Points To Christ
The explicit New Testament link is Jesus' own appeal to this table: when challenged about his hungry disciples, he recalled how David ate the showbread at Nob, bread lawful only for priests, and used it to defend mercy over ritual restriction. TRADITIONAL reading: Messianic readers also hear an echo in Jesus' words, 'I am the bread of life,' the true bread from heaven set before God's people.
