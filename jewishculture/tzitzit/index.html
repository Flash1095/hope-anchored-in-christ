Tzitzit

Topic
Tzitzit
Category
Custom
Summary
Tzitzit are the fringes worn on the four corners of a garment, with a cord of blue, as a visible reminder to remember and keep God's commandments.
Jewish Background
Numbers commands Israel to make tassels on the corners of their garments, with a cord of blue in each tassel, so that seeing them they will remember all the Lord's commandments and be holy. Deuteronomy repeats the command for the four-cornered cloak. The blue thread, tekhelet, was dyed from a sea snail, and its cost and rarity made the fringe a deliberate, daily mark of covenant identity, worn in the market and the field, not only in worship. The fringe's purpose is stated plainly in the command itself: sight leading to remembrance leading to obedience.
Points To Christ
EXPLICIT New Testament link: the woman who had suffered bleeding for twelve years came behind Jesus and touched the hem of his garment (Matthew 9:20-22), the hem being the fringe itself, and she was healed. Jesus also criticised leaders who lengthened their tassels to be noticed (Matthew 23:5), which again assumes the tassels were worn as a matter of course.
