Malachi's Elijah Forerunner

Topic
Malachi's Elijah Forerunner
Category
Messianic Reading
Summary
'Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the LORD' — the last word of the Old Testament prophets.
Jewish Background
Malachi closes the prophetic books with a promise: Elijah will return before the day of the Lord, turning the hearts of fathers to children. Jewish expectation kept Elijah alive in the tradition — a cup is poured for him at the Passover Seder, and he is expected to resolve hard questions and herald the Messiah. Elijah never died, so his return seemed fitting for a herald's role.
Points To Christ
The site's reading is that John the Baptist came in the spirit and power of Elijah, as Gabriel announced before his birth, and that Jesus identified John as the Elijah who was to come. The Gospels hold the tension honestly: John himself said he was not Elijah, yet Jesus said the prophecy was fulfilled in his ministry of calling Israel to repentance.
