The Menorah

Topic
The Menorah
Category
Custom
Summary
The menorah is the seven-branched golden lampstand that burned continually in the tabernacle and the Temple.
Jewish Background
Crafted from a single talent of pure gold with almond-blossom cups, the menorah's seven lamps were tended daily by the priests and burned from evening to morning. Its light symbolized God's presence and Israel's calling to be a light to the nations.
Points To Christ
Jesus declared himself the light of the world, and Revelation pictures the risen Christ standing among seven golden lampstands, the churches. The Temple lamp that never went out points to the one whose light darkness cannot overcome.
