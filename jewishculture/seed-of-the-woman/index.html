The Seed of the Woman (Genesis 3:15)

Topic
The Seed of the Woman (Genesis 3:15)
Category
Messianic Reading
Summary
The first promise after the fall: enmity between the serpent and the woman's seed, who will bruise the serpent's head.
Jewish Background
Genesis 3:15, spoken to the serpent, promises ongoing enmity between the serpent's seed and the woman's seed, with a decisive head-wound to come. Mainstream Jewish reading centers on the human struggle with evil, while later Targumic tradition gives the verse a messianic coloring, looking to a coming deliverer. The verse is brief and its fuller meaning unfolds only across the rest of Scripture, which is why it is called the first gospel promise rather than a full one.
Points To Christ
The site's reading is that the seed of the woman is Christ, born of a woman, who crushed the serpent's head at the cross — the wound to his heel being the crucifixion itself. Paul's promise that God will bruise Satan under the church's feet and Revelation's woman and dragon carry the same thread to its end.
