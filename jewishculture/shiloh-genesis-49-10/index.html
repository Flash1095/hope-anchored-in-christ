Shiloh (Genesis 49:10)

Topic
Shiloh (Genesis 49:10)
Category
Messianic Reading
Summary
'The sceptre shall not depart from Judah... until Shiloh come; and unto him shall the gathering of the people be.'
Jewish Background
In Jacob's blessing on Judah, the promise of enduring kingship runs 'until Shiloh come.' Targum Onkelos renders the verse explicitly: 'until the Messiah comes, to whom the kingdom belongs.' Rabbinic tradition has largely read Shiloh messianically, whatever the word's exact etymology (a place, a title, or 'the one to whom it belongs'). The verse ties the Messiah to Judah's line, not to any other tribe.
Points To Christ
The site's reading is that the sceptre stayed with Judah until Jesus, of Judah's tribe, came as the one to whom the peoples gather. Hebrews notes that our Lord sprang from Judah, and Revelation names him the Lion of the tribe of Judah — the gathering of the nations to Shiloh fulfilled in the church drawn from every people.
