Isaiah 7:14: The Virgin and the Young Woman

Topic
Isaiah 7:14: The Virgin and the Young Woman
Category
Messianic Reading
Summary
'Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel' — a sign given to King Ahaz, read very differently in Jewish and Christian tradition.
Jewish Background
The Hebrew word is 'almah, meaning a young woman of marriageable age; it does not by itself mean 'virgin.' Jewish reading commonly places the sign in Ahaz's own day, often identifying the child with Hezekiah. But the ancient Greek Septuagint, translated by Jewish scholars centuries before Jesus, rendered 'almah as 'parthenos,' meaning virgin — and that Greek text was the Bible of much of the early church. Both facts are stated here, since the debate turns on them.
Points To Christ
The site's reading follows Matthew, who quotes the Septuagint rendering and applies it to Jesus' birth: the child is Immanuel, 'God with us,' born of a virgin. Christians hold that the sign given to Ahaz carried a fuller meaning that only the birth of Christ unpacked.
