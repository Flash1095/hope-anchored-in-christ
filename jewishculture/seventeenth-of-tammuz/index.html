Seventeenth of Tammuz

Topic
Seventeenth of Tammuz
Category
Custom
Summary
The Seventeenth of Tammuz fasts for the breaching of Jerusalem's walls, the fast of the fourth month named by Zechariah.
Jewish Background
Zechariah names a fast of the fourth month among the fasts kept after the exile, and later tradition fixed it on 17 Tammuz, the day tradition assigns to the breaching of Jerusalem's walls in the Babylonian siege. The biblical narrative itself dates the breach to the ninth day of the fourth month (Jeremiah 52:6-7; 2 Kings 25:3-4), so the later fixed day differs from the narrative's date; the fast's anchor is Zechariah's fourth-month fast, not a single agreed calendar date in the biblical text. The fast opens the mourning period that runs to Tisha B'Av, and tradition also associates the day with the breaking of the first tablets, though that association is traditional, not stated in the breach accounts.
Points To Christ
No direct New Testament link to the Seventeenth of Tammuz is drawn. No New Testament passage names this fast, and none is cited here as if it did.
