The Branch

Topic
The Branch
Category
Messianic Reading
Summary
A shoot from the stump of Jesse, a righteous Branch from David's line — the prophets' image of a king who rises after the royal tree has been cut down.
Jewish Background
Isaiah 11 pictures a shoot growing from Jesse's stump after judgment has felled the tree, and Jeremiah 23 names the coming king 'a righteous Branch.' Zechariah calls God's servant 'the Branch' and pictures him building the Temple. The Targums make the messianic reading explicit in places, rendering the Branch of Isaiah 11 as the Messiah. The image holds judgment and hope together: the old kingdom falls, but the root survives.
Points To Christ
The site's reading is that Jesus is the shoot from Jesse's line — Luke traces his genealogy to David and to Jesse — and the righteous Branch who reigns in justice where the old kings failed. The Branch's Temple-building in Zechariah is read of the church, the living temple Christ builds.
Scripture
Isaiah 11:1-5; Jeremiah 23:5-6; Zechariah 6:12-13; Luke 1:32-33
