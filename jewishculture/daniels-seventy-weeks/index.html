Daniel's Seventy Weeks

Topic
Daniel's Seventy Weeks
Category
Messianic Reading
Summary
Seventy weeks of years decreed for Israel — a timetable ending with an anointed one cut off, one of the most debated passages in Scripture.
Jewish Background
Daniel 9 promises that seventy 'weeks' (weeks of years) will run from the decree to rebuild Jerusalem until the finishing of transgression and the anointing of the most holy. Jewish readings differ sharply on the details: the 'anointed one' cut off has been identified with various figures across the centuries, and the final week is read in several ways. The passage's difficulty is stated plainly here — confident timetables built on it should be treated with caution.
Points To Christ
The site's reading is that many Christian interpreters see the seventy weeks pointing to Jesus: the anointed one cut off after sixty-nine weeks, in the fullness of time, with the final week still debated among Christians themselves. The passage is presented as a genuine messianic pointer whose details Christians continue to discuss, not as a settled calculation.
