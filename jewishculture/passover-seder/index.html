Passover Seder

Topic
Passover Seder
Category
Custom
Summary
The Seder is the ordered Passover meal in which the story of the Exodus is retold, tasted, and passed to children.
Jewish Background
The Seder follows a set order of fifteen steps, including four cups of wine — traditionally linked to sanctification, deliverance, redemption, and praise — bitter herbs, unleavened bread, and the afikoman: the middle matzah, broken, hidden, and later ransomed back by the children.
Points To Christ
Messianic readers see the Seder's symbols fulfilled at the Last Supper: Jesus took bread, broke it, and called it his body, and took the cup after supper, the cup of redemption, as the new covenant in his blood. The hidden afikoman, broken and brought back, pictures the buried and risen Messiah — a Messianic reading, not the mainstream Jewish understanding of the custom.
