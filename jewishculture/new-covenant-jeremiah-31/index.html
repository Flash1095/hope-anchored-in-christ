The New Covenant (Jeremiah 31)

Topic
The New Covenant (Jeremiah 31)
Category
Messianic Reading
Summary
'I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel, and with the house of Judah... I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts.'
Jewish Background
Jeremiah spoke to a people in exile whose covenant lay broken. His promise was of a covenant unlike the one broken at Sinai: the law internalised, sins remembered no more, all knowing the Lord. Rabbinic tradition has debated whether this is a renewal of the old covenant or something new, and most readings place its fulfilment in the messianic age to come. The promise is addressed to Israel and Judah together, a reunited people.
Points To Christ
The site's reading is that Jesus inaugurated this covenant at the Last Supper — 'this cup is the new testament in my blood' — and that Hebrews quotes Jeremiah 31 at length to show the old covenant giving way to the new. The law written on hearts is the Spirit's work in believers, and the forgiveness promised is the cross's work.
