Synagogue

Topic
Synagogue
Category
Custom
Summary
The synagogue is the local house of prayer, study, and assembly that became the center of Jewish life after the exile.
Jewish Background
With no sacrifices offered there, the synagogue's life centers on prayer, the reading of Torah and Prophets, teaching, and community. By Jesus' day, synagogues stood in towns across the land, and visiting teachers were invited to read and speak.
Points To Christ
Jesus' own ministry moved through synagogues: he taught in them, read Isaiah in Nazareth's synagogue and announced its fulfillment, and his followers carried the same pattern to synagogues across the Roman world, where the message of the Messiah was first preached.
