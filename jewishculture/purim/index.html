Purim

Topic
Purim
Category
Feast
Summary
Purim celebrates the deliverance of the Jews of Persia from Haman's plot to destroy them, as told in the Book of Esther.
Jewish Background
On the 14th of Adar, Jews read the Megillah, the scroll of Esther, aloud, feast, give gifts of food, and give to the poor. Notably, God's name never appears in Esther — yet his providence is everywhere in the story's reversals.
Points To Christ
Purim's hidden God who overturns gallows and thrones points to the same providence that preserved the people from whom the Messiah would come. The feast reminds believers that God is at work even when he is not named, and that deliverance often arrives through unlikely hands.
