Tenth of Tevet

Topic
Tenth of Tevet
Category
Custom
Summary
The Tenth of Tevet fasts for the day Nebuchadnezzar's army began the siege of Jerusalem, the first step toward the city's fall.
Jewish Background
On the tenth day of the tenth month, in the ninth year of Zedekiah, Nebuchadnezzar came against Jerusalem and built siege works around it, as 2 Kings 25, Jeremiah 52, and Ezekiel 24 all record, with Ezekiel dating his parable of the boiling pot to that very day. Zechariah names the fast of the tenth month among the exile fasts; later tradition fixed it on 10 Tevet. The fast therefore marks a beginning rather than an ending: the day the siege closed around the city, months before the walls were breached and the Temple burned.
Points To Christ
No direct New Testament link to the Tenth of Tevet is drawn. No New Testament passage names this fast, and none is cited here as if it did.
