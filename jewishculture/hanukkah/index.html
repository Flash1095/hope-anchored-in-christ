Hanukkah

Topic
Hanukkah
Category
Feast
Summary
Hanukkah, the Festival of Lights, celebrates the rededication of the Temple in 164 BC after its desecration, and God's provision of oil for the menorah.
Jewish Background
When Judah Maccabee's forces retook Jerusalem, they cleansed the Temple and relit the menorah. Tradition holds that one day's supply of consecrated oil burned for eight days, and the feast is kept for eight nights with the lighting of the hanukkiah, games, and gifts.
Points To Christ
John's Gospel notes that Jesus himself was in Jerusalem for the Feast of Dedication, walking in the Temple courts. The feast of light points naturally to the one who called himself the light of the world, rededicating not a building of stone but his own people.
Scripture
