Second Passover (Pesach Sheni)

Topic
Second Passover (Pesach Sheni)
Category
Feast
Summary
Second Passover allowed anyone unclean from contact with the dead, or away on a journey, to keep Passover a month late, on 14 Iyyar, rather than miss it altogether.
Jewish Background
Numbers 9 records the provision directly: when men defiled by a dead body asked why they should be kept back from offering the Lord's offering at its appointed time, God answered that the unclean and the traveller should keep Passover in the second month, on the fourteenth day at twilight, with unleavened bread and bitter herbs, leaving none of it until morning and breaking no bone. The one condition was that it still be kept: a person who was clean and at home and neglected Passover was cut off. A later second-month Passover under Hezekiah (2 Chronicles 30) shows the same principle reused for a whole nation that could not keep the feast at its usual time, though that occasion arose from unconsecrated priests and an ungathered people rather than from the Numbers 9 provision itself.
Points To Christ
No direct New Testament link to Second Passover is drawn. No New Testament passage names Pesach Sheni, and none is cited here as if it did.
