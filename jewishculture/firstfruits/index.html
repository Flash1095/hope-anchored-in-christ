Firstfruits

Firstfruits offered the first sheaf of the barley harvest to God, a sign that the whole harvest belonged to him and that more was coming.

Jewish Background

On the day after the Sabbath during Unleavened Bread, a priest waved the first sheaf of barley before the Lord. No one ate of the new harvest until this offering was made, making the firstfruits both a gift and a pledge of the full harvest to come.

Points To Christ

Paul calls the risen Christ the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep: his resurrection is the first sheaf, guaranteeing the resurrection of all who belong to him. The feast's timing, in Passover week, matches the weekend of his death and resurrection.
