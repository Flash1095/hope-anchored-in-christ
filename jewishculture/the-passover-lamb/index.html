The Passover Lamb

Topic
The Passover Lamb
Category
Messianic Reading
Summary
The Passover lamb was unblemished, slain at twilight, its blood marking the doorposts of every house that would be spared.
Jewish Background
Exodus commands a lamb without defect, kept from the tenth day and slain on the fourteenth, with none of its bones broken, its blood painted on the lintel and doorposts. The lamb's death stood between Israel and judgment, and its flesh fed the people on their journey out.
Points To Christ
John the Baptist pointed at Jesus and said, Behold, the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world. John notes that Jesus' legs were not broken on the cross, like the Passover lamb's bones, and Paul states it plainly: Christ, our Passover lamb, has been sacrificed.

Exodus 12:1-13; Exodus 12:46; John 1:29; John 19:31-36; 1 Corinthians 5:7
