Psalm 22

Topic
Psalm 22
Category
Messianic Reading
Summary
Psalm 22 opens with a cry of abandonment and moves, verse by verse, through suffering that ends in praise and a kingdom proclaimed to future generations.
Jewish Background
David's psalm describes mockery, pierced hands and feet, garments divided by lot, and bones out of joint — suffering that David knew, yet described in terms that reach beyond his own life. Jewish tradition reads it as David's prayer, and later as the voice of Israel in exile.
Points To Christ
On the cross, Jesus quoted its first line, and the Gospel writers let its details tell the story: the mockery, the lots cast for his clothing, the thirst. The psalm does not end at the cross — it ends with all the ends of the earth turning to the Lord, which is exactly where the Gospel goes.
