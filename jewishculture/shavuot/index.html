Shavuot

Shavuot, the Feast of Weeks, falls fifty days after Firstfruits and celebrates the wheat harvest — and, by tradition, the giving of the Torah at Mount Sinai.

Jewish Background

Shavuot is one of the three pilgrimage feasts, when Israelites brought two loaves of wheat bread to the Temple. Later Jewish tradition ties the day to Sinai and reads the Book of Ruth in the synagogue.

Points To Christ

On Shavuot the Holy Spirit fell on the first believers in Jerusalem, and about three thousand were added to the church in one day. What began at Sinai with law written on stone continued at Pentecost with the Spirit writing on hearts.
