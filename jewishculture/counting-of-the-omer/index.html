Counting of the Omer

Topic
Counting of the Omer
Category
Custom
Summary
The Counting of the Omer counts forty-nine days from the offering of the first sheaf to Shavuot, binding the barley harvest to the wheat harvest and Passover to Sinai.
Jewish Background
Leviticus 23 commands Israel to count seven full weeks from the day after the Sabbath on which the first sheaf was waved, bringing a new grain offering on the fiftieth day; Deuteronomy 16 gives the same count in the form of seven weeks from the first sickle in the standing grain. Each evening the count was spoken aloud — 'today is day one of the Omer,' and so on — turning the seven weeks into a deliberate period of anticipation rather than empty waiting. The count is distinct from Firstfruits itself, which is the single offering that starts it, and from Shavuot, which is the feast that ends it.
Points To Christ
No New Testament verse names the Omer, so no explicit fulfilment is claimed. The link is calendrical only: the count ends at Shavuot, and Acts 2:1 places the outpouring of the Holy Spirit at Pentecost, the fiftieth day.
