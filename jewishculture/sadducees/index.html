Sadducees

Topic
Sadducees
Category
History
Summary
The Sadducees were the priestly aristocracy of the Second Temple, guardians of the Temple and its sacrifices.
Jewish Background
Drawn largely from wealthy priestly families, Sadducees held authority in the Temple and the Sanhedrin. They accepted only the written Torah, denied the resurrection, angels, and spirits, and were skeptical of the oral tradition the Pharisees prized.
Points To Christ
The Sadducees questioned Jesus with a riddle about resurrection, and he answered from the Torah itself: God is the God of the living. Their power faded with the Temple's destruction in AD 70, but their question remains the question at the heart of the Gospel — is there life beyond death, and has God answered it?
