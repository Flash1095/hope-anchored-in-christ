Day of Atonement

Topic
Day of Atonement
Category
Feast
Summary
The Day of Atonement was the one day each year when the high priest entered the Most Holy Place to make atonement for Israel's sins.
Jewish Background
On the 10th of Tishri, Israel fasted and the high priest, after sacrifices for himself and the people, entered the Holy of Holies with blood, then sent a goat bearing the people's sins into the wilderness. It was the most solemn day of the Jewish year.
Points To Christ
The letter to the Hebrews reads this day as a shadow of Christ: he entered the true Holy Place once for all, not with the blood of goats but with his own blood, securing an eternal redemption that yearly sacrifices could only picture.
