Fast of Esther

Topic
Fast of Esther
Category

Summary
The Fast of Esther, kept on 13 Adar, recalls the three-day fast Esther called before she risked her life to plead for her people.
Jewish Background
Esther 4 records that when Haman's decree threatened every Jew in Persia, Esther asked Mordecai to gather all the Jews in Susa and fast for her for three days, night and day, before she went to the king uninvited — an act that could have cost her life. Esther 9 later notes fasting and lamentation among the commemorations tied to Purim, and the fast on the day before Purim, 13 Adar, grew from that account: the day of the intended battle became a day of fasting before the day of feasting. It is a communal fast, not a private discipline, remembering a moment when a whole people turned to God together before acting.
