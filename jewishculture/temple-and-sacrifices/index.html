Temple and Sacrifices
Category
Custom
Summary
The Temple in Jerusalem was the place of God's dwelling, where priests offered daily sacrifices for atonement, thanksgiving, and fellowship.
Jewish Background
From the tabernacle to Solomon's Temple and the Second Temple, Israel's worship centered on sacrifice: burnt offerings, grain offerings, peace offerings, sin offerings, and guilt offerings, with the high priest mediating on the Day of Atonement. The Temple was destroyed by Rome in AD 70.
Points To Christ
Jesus called his own body the temple and presented himself as the final sacrifice: the Lamb who takes away sin, the great high priest who mediates a new covenant. With his death, the sacrificial system found its completion, and his people became the temple of the living God.
