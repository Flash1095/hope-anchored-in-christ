Zechariah 9:9: The Humble King

Topic
Zechariah 9:9: The Humble King
Category
Messianic Reading
Summary
'Behold, thy King cometh unto thee... lowly, and riding upon an ass' — the king of peace, arriving without war-horse or chariot.
Jewish Background
Zechariah pictures a king who comes in humility, and the next verse banishes the battle bow and speaks peace to the nations. Rabbinic tradition includes a messianic reading of this king, though the passage's peaceful character sat uneasily beside hopes for a warrior who would throw off foreign rule. The donkey, not the war-horse, is the point: this king conquers by peace.
Points To Christ
The site's reading is that Jesus staged this prophecy deliberately on Palm Sunday, entering Jerusalem on a donkey while the crowds cried for salvation. The crowd wanted the warrior; the king who came was the humble one Zechariah described, and his peace would come through the cross later that week.
