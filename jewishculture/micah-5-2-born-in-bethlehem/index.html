Micah 5:2: Born in Bethlehem

Micah promised that from Bethlehem, small among the clans of Judah, would come a ruler whose origins are from of old, from ancient days.

Jewish Background

Bethlehem was David's town, and the promise tied the coming ruler to David's line. When Herod asked where the Messiah was to be born, the chief priests and scribes answered from this very verse: in Bethlehem of Judea.

Points To Christ

Matthew records Jesus' birth in Bethlehem as the fulfillment of Micah's words, and John records the debate it caused — some dismissed Jesus because they thought he came from Galilee, missing the Bethlehem birth. The small town's small promise carries the weight of eternity: a ruler from ancient days, born in time.
