Second Temple Judaism

Topic
Second Temple Judaism
Category
History
Summary
Second Temple Judaism is the world of Jesus: Jewish life from the rebuilding of the Temple after exile until its destruction by Rome in AD 70.
Jewish Background
After the exile, Jewish life reorganized around Torah, synagogue, and Temple, while living under Persian, Greek, and Roman rule. Sects formed — Pharisees, Sadducees, Essenes, Zealots — and expectation of a Messiah ran high, shaped by prophets and by suffering under foreign powers.
Points To Christ
The Gospels cannot be understood apart from this world: its festivals set Jesus' calendar, its synagogues hosted his teaching, its Temple framed his death, and its Messianic hopes explain both the crowds that hailed him and the leaders who rejected him. He came, Paul writes, in the fullness of time.
