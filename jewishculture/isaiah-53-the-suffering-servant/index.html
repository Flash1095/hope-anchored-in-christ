Isaiah 53: The Suffering Servant

Topic
Isaiah 53: The Suffering Servant
Category
Messianic Reading
Summary
Isaiah 53 pictures a servant who is pierced for our transgressions, crushed for our iniquities, and led like a lamb to the slaughter — yet whose suffering brings healing.
Jewish Background
In mainstream Jewish tradition, the servant is commonly understood as Israel itself, or the righteous remnant suffering for the nations. The passage sits within Isaiah's servant songs, written to exiles longing for redemption.
Points To Christ
Messianic readers hear the servant as the Messiah: despised and rejected, wounded for transgressions, silent before shearers, assigned a grave with the rich — every detail fulfilled in Jesus' crucifixion. Philip used this very chapter to lead an Ethiopian official to faith in Jesus.
