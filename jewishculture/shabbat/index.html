Category

Summary
Shabbat, the Sabbath, is the weekly day of rest commanded from Creation itself, a holy pause from work for worship, family, and delight in God.
Jewish Background
From Friday sunset to Saturday sunset, observant Jews cease creative work, light candles, bless bread and wine, share festive meals, and gather for prayer and Torah study. The Sabbath remembers both Creation and the Exodus: God rested, and God freed slaves who could not rest.
Points To Christ
Jesus called himself Lord of the Sabbath and taught that the Sabbath was made for people, not people for the Sabbath. Hebrews then holds out a deeper rest: the Sabbath rest that remains for God's people is found in Christ, in whom striving ceases.
