Election and the Chosen People

Topic
Election and the Chosen People
Category
History
Summary
Election is the belief that God freely chose Israel, not for its size or merit, but to be his treasured people and a light to the nations.
Jewish Background
Deuteronomy is explicit that Israel was chosen because God loved her and kept his oath to the patriarchs, not because she was the greatest nation. Election carries a mission, not a privilege alone: Israel is to keep Torah, display God's justice, and be the people through whom the nations come to know him. The prophets wrestle with the tension this creates — a chosen people who fail, judged, exiled, and yet never finally cast off.
Points To Christ
Paul takes up election directly in Romans 9-11, insisting that God's gifts and calling to Israel are without repentance, while the church is grafted into the same story. Peter applies election language to believers in Christ: 'ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood' (1 Peter 2:9). The entry states the Jewish belief on its own terms; the Christian extension is Paul's and Peter's argument, not a replacement of Israel's election.
