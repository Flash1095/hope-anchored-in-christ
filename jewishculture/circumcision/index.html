Circumcision

Topic
Circumcision
Category
Custom
Summary
Circumcision is the sign of God's covenant with Abraham, performed on Jewish boys on the eighth day after birth.
Jewish Background
Commanded in Genesis 17, circumcision marks every male child of the covenant people. It is performed at a brit milah, a covenant ceremony, and remains a defining practice of Jewish identity to this day.
Points To Christ
The apostles wrestled with what circumcision meant for Gentile believers, and the Jerusalem council decided Gentiles need not be circumcised. Paul then points to the deeper reality: true circumcision is of the heart, by the Spirit, a mark every believer in Christ carries.
