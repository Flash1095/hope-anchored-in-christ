Mezuzah

Topic
Mezuzah
Category
Custom
Summary
The mezuzah is a small case fixed to the doorposts of a Jewish home, holding a parchment inscribed with the Shema.
Jewish Background
Deuteronomy commands God's words to be written on the doorposts of houses and gates. The parchment inside a mezuzah carries the Shema and the passage on teaching children, so that crossing a threshold becomes a reminder of the covenant.
Points To Christ
For Messianic readers, the mezuzah's Word on the doorway echoes the Word who became flesh and dwelt among us — the one who stands at the door and knocks. The practice turns an ordinary doorway into a daily confession of whose house this is.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 6:4-9; Deuteronomy 11:20; John 1:14
