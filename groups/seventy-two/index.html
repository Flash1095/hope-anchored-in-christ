The Seventy-Two

Group
The Seventy-Two
Also Called
The Seventy
Period
During the ministry of Jesus
Origin
During his journey toward Jerusalem, Jesus appointed seventy-two others beyond the Twelve and sent them ahead in pairs into the towns he would visit. Some manuscripts read seventy, echoing the seventy elders of Moses' day and the nations of the world in Genesis.
Beliefs
They believed the kingdom of God had come near in Jesus and carried that announcement as his advance party, depending on the hospitality of those who received them.
Practices
They went in pairs, carrying no purse, bag, or sandals, eating what was set before them, healing the sick, announcing the kingdom, and shaking the dust off where they were rejected. They returned rejoicing that even demons submitted in Jesus' name.
Role
The Seventy-Two show that Jesus' mission was never limited to the Twelve. They were ordinary disciples trusted with real authority, and their mission previews the church's wider sending to every town and nation.
Relationship To Jesus
Jesus appointed them, instructed them, and received their report — redirecting their joy from power over demons to the deeper joy that their names were written in heaven. Their mission is the pattern for every later sending of disciples beyond the inner circle.
Scripture
Luke 10:1-24
Summary
The Seventy-Two were the wider mission party Jesus sent ahead in pairs to announce the kingdom — ordinary disciples trusted with real authority, previewing the church's mission to every town.
