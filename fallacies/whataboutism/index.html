Whataboutism

Category
Relevance
Definition
Answering a criticism by pointing at someone else's fault instead of addressing the criticism. A form of deflection closely related to tu quoque, aimed at changing the subject rather than answering.
Example
Someone argues, 'Our factory pollutes — but what about the factory across town?'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'Yes, Christians have failed here — but what about what atheists did?'
Fallacy
Whataboutism
How To Respond
Acknowledge both, then stay on topic: 'The other failure may be real, but it doesn't answer this one — let's deal with each honestly.'

Logic; Apologetics; Respectful Dialogue
