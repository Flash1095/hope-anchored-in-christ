Appeal to Consequences

Category
Relevance
Definition
Rejecting a claim because its consequences would be unpleasant, or accepting one because its consequences are welcome. How we feel about a conclusion does not decide whether it is true.
Example
Someone argues, 'Free will cannot be an illusion — that would make morality meaningless.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'Hell cannot be real — that would be too terrible.'
Fallacy
Appeal to Consequences
How To Respond
Separate feeling from fact: 'How we feel about a conclusion doesn't decide whether it's true — let's look at the grounds.'
