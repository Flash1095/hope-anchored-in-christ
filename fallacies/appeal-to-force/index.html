Appeal to Force

Category
Relevance
Definition
Trying to win an argument by threat rather than evidence: agree, or face a penalty. A threat may be real, but it is not a reason the claim is true.
Example
Someone argues, 'Support this policy or you will lose your job.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'Accept my interpretation, or I will tell everyone you are a heretic.'
Fallacy
Appeal to Force
How To Respond
Name it kindly and return to reasons: 'A threat isn't evidence — could we discuss the reasons themselves?'
