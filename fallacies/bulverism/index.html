Bulverism

Category
Relevance
Definition
Assuming someone is wrong, then explaining psychologically why they believe it, instead of showing the error. Motives do not settle truth, even when the motives are correctly described.
Example
Someone argues, 'You only oppose this policy because you fear change.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'You only believe in God because you need a crutch.'
Fallacy
Bulverism
How To Respond
Return to reasons: 'Even if motives were as claimed, motives don't settle truth — let's examine the reasons.'
