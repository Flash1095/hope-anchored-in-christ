Single Cause Fallacy

Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Reducing a complex outcome to a single cause. Most big outcomes have many causes, and naming one hides the rest.
Example
Someone argues, 'The war happened because of one assassination.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'The church declined because of one worship change.'
Fallacy
Single Cause Fallacy
How To Respond
Name the rest: 'Most big outcomes have many causes — let's name them.'
