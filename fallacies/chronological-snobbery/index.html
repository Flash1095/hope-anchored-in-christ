Chronological Snobbery

Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Dismissing ideas merely because they are old, or assuming the new is better simply for being new. Age alone, in either direction, does not settle truth.
Example
Someone argues, 'That book is from the 1800s — obviously outdated.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'Ancient creeds can't speak to us today — they're too old.'
Fallacy
Chronological Snobbery
How To Respond
Test the idea, not its date: "Age alone doesn't settle truth — old or new, let's test the idea."
