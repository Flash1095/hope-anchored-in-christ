Hasty Generalization

Drawing a broad conclusion from too small or unrepresentative a sample. A few examples are treated as proof about a whole group.

Example

After meeting two rude tourists, someone declares, 'Everyone from that country is rude.'

Faith Discussion Example

Someone meets two disinterested church members and concludes, 'Christians never really care about anyone outside their group,' based on a very narrow experience.

How To Respond

Validate the experience, then widen the picture: 'I am sorry that was your experience. Would you be open to meeting other examples before we settle on the whole group?'
