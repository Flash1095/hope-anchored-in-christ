Sunk Cost Fallacy

Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Continuing a course because of what has already been spent on it, rather than what it will serve. Past investment does not make a bad course good.
Example
Someone argues, 'We've spent millions on this project, so we can't stop now.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'We've always done it this way for decades, so we can't change now.'
Fallacy
Sunk Cost Fallacy
How To Respond
Look forward: "Past investment doesn't make a bad course good — what serves the future?"
