Strawman

Fallacy
Strawman
Category
Relevance
Definition
Misrepresenting someone's position to make it easier to attack, then arguing against the distorted version instead of their real view.
Example
Someone argues for better road safety and is accused of wanting to ban all cars, which was never what they said.
Faith Discussion Example
A Christian who says faith and reason work together is told, 'So you think we should just believe blindly without any evidence,' which misstates their position.
How To Respond
Restate your view calmly and check understanding: 'That is not quite what I meant — here is what I am actually saying. Does that version sound fair to discuss?'
Related Topics
Apologetics; Logic; Listening Well
