Appeal to Nature

Fallacy
Appeal to Nature
Category
Presumption
Definition
Assuming that because something is natural it must be good, or because something is unnatural it must be bad, without further moral reasoning.
Example
Someone claims, 'This supplement must be safe and better because it is all-natural,' though some natural substances are harmful.
Faith Discussion Example
In an ethics discussion someone says, 'That behavior occurs in nature, so it must be morally right for people,' skipping the step of asking what God intends for human flourishing.
How To Respond
Appreciate creation while asking the deeper question: 'Nature teaches us a lot, but could we also ask what is good and purposeful, not only what occurs naturally?'

Christian Ethics; Logic; Nature and Creation
