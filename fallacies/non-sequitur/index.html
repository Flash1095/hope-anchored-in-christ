Non Sequitur

Category
Relevance
Definition
A conclusion that simply does not follow from its premises — no stated connection, just a leap. Specific forms of this leap have their own entries elsewhere in this collection.
Example
Someone argues, 'She is a great singer, so she would make a great mayor.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'He knows Greek, so his theology must be right.'
Fallacy
Non Sequitur
How To Respond
Ask for the missing link: 'The conclusion may be true, but it needs its own support — what connects these?'
