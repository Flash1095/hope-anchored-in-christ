Genetic Fallacy

Judging a claim true or false based only on where it came from — its origin, history, or source — instead of its merits.

Example

A helpful idea is dismissed with, 'That suggestion came from the new employee, so it cannot be any good.'

Faith Discussion Example

Someone says, 'The Bible cannot be trusted because it was written by ancient people,' judging the message only by its age and authors rather than its content and evidence.

How To Respond

Affirm that origins can raise good questions, then return to content: 'Ancient does not automatically mean untrue — could we examine what it says and the evidence for it?'
