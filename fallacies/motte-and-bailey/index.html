Motte-and-Bailey

Category
Presumption
Definition
Retreating from a bold, controversial claim to a trivially true one when challenged, then returning to the bold claim once the pressure passes. The modest claim is the motte; the bold one is the bailey.
Example
Someone argues, 'Everyone should live off-grid' — challenged — 'Well, nature is nice' — and later returns to off-grid living for all.
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'All other churches are apostate' — challenged — 'Well, no church is perfect' — and later returns to calling them apostate.
Fallacy
Motte-and-Bailey
How To Respond
Pin down the claim: "Let's settle which claim we're defending — the strong one or the modest one?"
