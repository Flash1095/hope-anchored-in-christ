Kettle Logic

Category
Presumption
Definition
Offering several defenses that contradict each other, so that at least one always seems available. If the defenses cannot all be true, they cannot jointly support the claim.
Example
Someone argues, 'I never borrowed the pot; it was broken when I got it; I returned it undamaged.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'The text means this; also it means the opposite; also it means nothing at all.'
Fallacy
Kettle Logic
How To Respond
Pick one: "These defenses can't all be true — which one are we actually claiming?"
