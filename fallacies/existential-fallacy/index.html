Existential Fallacy

Category
Formal
Definition
Concluding that something exists merely because a universal claim is true. 'All A are B' does not by itself prove that any A exists.
Example
Someone argues, 'All unicorns have horns, so some unicorn has a horn.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'All flawless Bible translations agree, so a flawless translation exists' — the universal claim alone does not prove one exists.
Fallacy
Existential Fallacy
How To Respond
Ask for the extra step: 'A universal claim doesn't by itself prove something exists — what separate evidence shows it does?'
