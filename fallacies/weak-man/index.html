Weak Man

Category
Presumption
Definition
Attacking the weakest real version of a position instead of its strongest. Unlike a strawman, which distorts the view, the weak man cherry-picks its feeblest genuine defender.
Example
Someone argues, 'Some flat-earther said something silly, so all skeptics of the official story are silly.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'One prosperity preacher said something foolish, so all charismatics are foolish.'
Fallacy
Weak Man
How To Respond
Raise the standard: "Let's engage the strongest version of the view, not its weakest defender."
