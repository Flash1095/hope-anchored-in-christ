False Dichotomy

Fallacy
False Dichotomy
Category
Presumption
Definition
Presenting only two options as if they are the only possibilities, when other options exist. It is also called a false dilemma.
Example
A speaker says, 'You are either with our plan completely or you want the project to fail,' ignoring middle options and compromises.
Faith Discussion Example
Someone says, 'You must choose science or the Bible — you cannot have both,' as if thoughtful Christians have not held both together for centuries.
How To Respond
Name the missing options with kindness: 'I wonder if there might be a third way here that honors both concerns. Could we explore it together?'
