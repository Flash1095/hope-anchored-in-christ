Appeal to Novelty

Fallacy
Appeal to Novelty
Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Assuming something is better or truer simply because it is new, modern, or recent. Newness alone is not evidence.
Example
An advert claims, 'This is the newest model, so it must be the best choice for everyone,' without comparing features.
Faith Discussion Example
Someone dismisses historic Christian teaching with, 'That belief is centuries old — it is time we moved past it,' as if age alone proved it wrong.
How To Respond
Welcome fresh insight while testing it: 'New ideas can be a gift. Could we judge this one by its truth and fruit, rather than by its date?'
