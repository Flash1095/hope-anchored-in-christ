Guilt by Association

Category
Relevance
Definition
Discrediting a claim because of who else holds it, rather than engaging its reasons. A claim stands or falls on its own merits, not on its neighbors.
Example
Someone argues, 'That diet must be wrong — a celebrity criminal endorses it.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'That doctrine must be wrong — a cult also teaches something like it.'
Fallacy
Guilt by Association
How To Respond
Return to the claim itself: 'A claim stands or falls on its own reasons, not on its neighbors.'
