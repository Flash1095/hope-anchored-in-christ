Appeal to Ignorance

Claiming something is true because it has not been proved false, or false because it has not been proved true. Lack of evidence is treated as evidence itself.

Example

Someone says, 'No one has proved this remedy does not work, so it definitely works.'

Faith Discussion Example

A skeptic says, 'Since you cannot prove God exists in a laboratory, God cannot exist,' and a believer might reply in reverse, 'You cannot disprove God, so that proves He exists.' Neither move is fair on its own.

How To Respond

Agree that uncertainty is honest, then shift to what we do know: 'Not having a final proof either way invites us to weigh the evidence we actually have.'
