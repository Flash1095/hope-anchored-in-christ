Division

Fallacy
Division
Category
Ambiguity
Definition
Assuming that what is true of the whole must be true of each part. It is the reverse of composition, dividing a group quality down to every member.
Example
A famous university is assumed to mean every single student there must be brilliant in every subject.
Faith Discussion Example
Someone says, 'The early church was generous, so every individual early Christian must have been generous in every way,' flattening a general description into a claim about each person.
How To Respond
Keep the whole and the parts distinct: 'A true description of the group does not always describe each member — could we look at the individuals on their own evidence?'
