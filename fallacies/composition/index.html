Composition

Fallacy
Composition
Category
Ambiguity
Definition
Assuming that what is true of the parts must be true of the whole. A quality of individual pieces is transferred to the group without justification.
Example
Someone reasons, 'Every player on this team is excellent, so the team as a whole must play excellently together,' ignoring teamwork and chemistry.
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'Each verse in this chapter is encouraging, so the whole Bible must never contain anything challenging or hard,' moving from a part to the whole too quickly.
How To Respond
Distinguish parts and whole gently: 'That may be true of the parts — could we check whether it still holds when we consider the whole together?'
