Appeal to Authority

Fallacy
Appeal to Authority
Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Treating a claim as true simply because an authority figure said it, especially when that person is not an expert on the topic or experts disagree.
Example
An advertisement says a toothpaste must be best because a famous actor recommends it, though the actor is not a dentist.
Faith Discussion Example
A video claims, 'A famous scientist said the resurrection is impossible, so it is settled,' without examining the historical evidence itself.
How To Respond
Respect expertise while asking for reasons: 'I value what experts say — could we look at the evidence and arguments they are pointing to, not just the conclusion?'
