Accent

Category
Ambiguity
Definition
A change of meaning produced by emphasis. Stressing different words in the same sentence can smuggle in a meaning the words alone do not carry.
Example
Someone stresses 'We should NOT trust him' versus 'We SHOULD not trust him' — the emphasis shifts what is being denied.
Faith Discussion Example
Someone stresses 'all' in 'all have sinned' to mean every single act is sin, rather than that all people stand in need of grace.
Fallacy
Accent
How To Respond
Read it plainly: "Emphasis can smuggle in a meaning the words don't carry — let's read the whole sentence plainly."
