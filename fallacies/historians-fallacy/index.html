Historian's Fallacy

Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Judging people of the past as if they knew what we know now and had our options. Fair judgment starts by understanding their world.
Example
Someone argues, 'They should have known better in 1850,' without asking what was knowable then.
Faith Discussion Example
Someone judges Old Testament figures as if they had a modern conscience and modern options, without first understanding their world.
Fallacy
Historian's Fallacy
How To Respond
Understand first: "Let's understand their world first, then judge fairly."
