Anecdotal Fallacy

Fallacy
Anecdotal Fallacy
Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Using a single personal story or vivid example as proof for a broad claim, instead of wider evidence. Stories illuminate, but one story rarely settles a pattern.
Example
Someone says, 'My grandfather smoked every day and lived to 95, so smoking cannot be harmful.'
Faith Discussion Example
A person says, 'I prayed once and nothing happened, so prayer never works,' or, 'My friend had a powerful experience, so that proves every claim about that teaching.'
How To Respond
Honor the story, then place it in context: 'Your experience matters and I want to hear it. Could we also look at the wider picture and what Scripture shows over time?'
