No True Scotsman

Fallacy
No True Scotsman
Category
Presumption
Definition
Redefining a group on the spot to exclude a counterexample, so the original claim can never be challenged. The definition moves whenever evidence appears.
Example
After hearing of a kind Scotsman who disproves a stereotype, someone replies, 'Well, no true Scotsman would act that way.'
Faith Discussion Example
When a professing Christian behaves badly, someone says, 'No true Christian would ever do that,' instead of honestly acknowledging failure and the need for repentance.
How To Respond
Be honest about real examples: 'Christians, like everyone, can fail badly. Rather than redefine the word, let us look at what Christ actually calls His followers to be.'
