Appeal to Popularity

Fallacy
Appeal to Popularity
Category
Relevance
Definition
Treating widespread belief as evidence of truth — if most people believe it, it must be right. It is closely related to the bandwagon, but focuses on belief rather than behavior.
Example
A student argues, 'Most people in our class think this answer is right, so it must be the correct one.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone says, 'Millions of people no longer believe the Bible is relevant, so it clearly is not,' or the reverse, 'So many people believe it, that alone proves it true.' Both rely on numbers alone.
How To Respond
Respect people on all sides while returning to reasons: 'Numbers tell us what is common, not always what is true — could we weigh the actual claims together?'
