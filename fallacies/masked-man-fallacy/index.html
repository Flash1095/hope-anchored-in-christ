Masked Man Fallacy

Substituting co-referring terms inside a belief or knowledge context, where recognition matters. Not recognizing someone under one description does not change who they are.

Example

Someone argues, 'You know your father, but you do not know the masked man — so the masked man is not your father.'

Faith Discussion Example

Someone argues, 'The disciples did not recognize the risen Jesus at first, so the man with them was not Jesus' — yet recognition is not identity.

How To Respond

Separate knowing from being: 'Not recognizing someone under one description doesn't change who they are.'
