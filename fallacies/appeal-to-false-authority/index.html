Appeal to False Authority

Category
Presumption
Definition
Citing a genuine expert outside their field as if the expertise transferred. Authority counts only within the field it was earned in.
Example
Someone argues, 'A famous actor says this medicine works.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'A celebrity pastor's view on ancient Hebrew grammar settles it.'
Fallacy
Appeal to False Authority
How To Respond
Check the field: "Expertise in one field doesn't transfer — what do the relevant specialists say?"
