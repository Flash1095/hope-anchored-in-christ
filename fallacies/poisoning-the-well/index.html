Poisoning the Well

Category
Relevance
Definition
Preemptively discrediting a source so that nothing they say counts, before they have even made their case. Unlike ordinary ad hominem, it poisons the well in advance.
Example
Someone argues, 'Don't listen to him — he's just a paid shill,' before he has spoken a word.
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'Don't read that scholar — he's an atheist, so nothing he says about the Bible counts.'
Fallacy
Poisoning the Well
How To Respond
Insist on a hearing: "Let's hear the argument first and judge it on its merits."
