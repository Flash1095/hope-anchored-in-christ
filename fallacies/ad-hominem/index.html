Ad Hominem

Attacking the person making an argument instead of addressing the argument itself. It distracts from whether the claim is true.

Example

Someone says, 'You only think that diet works because you sell diet books,' instead of discussing the evidence for the diet.

Faith Discussion Example

In a discussion someone says, 'You only believe the Bible because you were raised in church,' instead of engaging with what the Bible actually says.

How To Respond

Thank the person for being honest, then gently separate the messenger from the message: 'My background is part of my story — could we look together at whether the claim itself holds up?'
