Hot Hand Fallacy

Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Believing a random streak must continue — that a run of successes makes the next success more likely. Streaks happen by chance too.
Example
Someone argues, 'She has made five shots, so she cannot miss now.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'This ministry grew three years running, so it will keep growing.'
Fallacy
Hot Hand Fallacy
How To Respond
Ask for the cause: 'Streaks happen by chance too — what's the underlying cause?'
