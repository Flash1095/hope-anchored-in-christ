Continuum Fallacy

Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Rejecting a distinction because no sharp line marks it. Vague boundaries do not erase real differences; clear cases still differ.
Example
Someone argues, 'There's no exact moment day becomes night, so day and night don't differ.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'No sharp line marks when doubt becomes unbelief, so doubt and unbelief are identical.'
Fallacy
Continuum Fallacy
How To Respond
Use clear cases: "Vague boundaries don't erase real differences — let's use clear cases."
