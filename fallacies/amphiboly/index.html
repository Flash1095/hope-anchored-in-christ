Amphiboly

Category
Ambiguity
Definition
Ambiguity arising from sentence structure rather than from a single word's meaning. The grammar itself allows two readings, and the argument leans on only one.
Example
Someone argues from 'I saw the man with the telescope' without saying who held the telescope.
Faith Discussion Example
Someone quotes 'Judge not' to mean never discern anything, leaning on one possible reading of the sentence rather than its context.
Fallacy
Amphiboly
How To Respond
Pause on the structure: 'When a sentence can be read two ways, let's check the context before building on one reading.'
