Illicit Conversion

Category
Formal
Definition
Illegitimately converting 'all A are B' into 'all B are A'. Conversion works for 'some' and 'no' statements, but not for universal affirmatives.
Example
Someone argues, 'All roses are flowers, so all flowers are roses.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'All prayers in the Bible are inspired, so all inspired words are prayers in the Bible.'
Fallacy
Illicit Conversion
How To Respond
Keep the direction straight: 'Conversion only works one way for all-statements — let's not flip it.'
