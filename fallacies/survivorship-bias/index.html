Survivorship Bias

Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Judging by the survivors while ignoring the failures. The visible winners are a biased sample, because the losers are no longer around to be counted.
Example
Someone argues, 'Every successful CEO wakes at 5am, so waking at 5am causes success' — ignoring the many who woke early and failed.
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'Every thriving church uses this method, so the method guarantees thriving' — ignoring the churches that used it and closed.
Fallacy
Survivorship Bias
How To Respond
Count the missing: "Let's also count the cases that didn't make it."
