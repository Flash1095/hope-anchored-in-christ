Illicit Minor

Category
Formal
Definition
An invalid syllogism: all A are B; all A are C; therefore all C are B. The minor term is distributed in the conclusion but not in the premises.
Example
Someone argues, 'All cats are mammals. All cats are four-legged, so all four-legged things are mammals.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'All apostles were Jews. All apostles were men, so all Jewish men are apostles.'
Fallacy
Illicit Minor
How To Respond
Flip it to expose the error: 'By that pattern, every shared trait would prove membership — let's check the direction.'
