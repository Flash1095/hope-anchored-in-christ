Fallacy of Exclusive Premises

Category
Formal
Definition
Drawing a conclusion from two negative premises. Two 'no' statements together prove nothing about the relationship between the remaining terms.
Example
Someone argues, 'No birds are fish. No penguins are fish, so penguins are not birds' — yet penguins are birds.
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'No angel is human. No demon is human, so demons are not angels' — yet demons are fallen angels.
Fallacy
Fallacy of Exclusive Premises
How To Respond
Note the shared gap: 'Two negatives leave the connection open — we'd need a positive premise to settle it.'
