Argument from Silence

Treating the absence of a mention as evidence that something did not happen or is not true. Silence is only weak evidence, and only when a mention would be expected.

Example

Someone argues, 'The report never mentions safety problems, so there were none.'

Faith Discussion Example

Someone argues, 'The Gospels never record Jesus laughing, so Jesus never laughed.'

How To Respond

Ask what silence can carry: "Silence isn't evidence unless we'd expect a mention — and even then it's weak."
