Argument from Incredulity

Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Rejecting a claim because you cannot imagine how it could be true. Our imagination is not the measure of reality.
Example
Someone argues, 'I can't see how evolution could produce an eye, so it couldn't.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'I can't understand the Trinity, so it can't be true.'
Fallacy
Argument from Incredulity
How To Respond
Return to evidence: "Our imagination isn't the measure — let's look at the evidence."
