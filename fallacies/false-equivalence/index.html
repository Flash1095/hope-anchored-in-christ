False Equivalence

Category
Presumption
Definition
Treating two unequal things as if they were equivalent, so that what holds for one is assumed to hold for the other. The comparison hides a difference in weight.
Example
Someone argues, 'Skipping a workout and skipping heart medicine are both just missing a dose.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'Doubting one doctrine and rejecting the whole faith are the same thing.'
Fallacy
False Equivalence
How To Respond
Compare like with like: 'These differ in weight — let's compare like with like.'
