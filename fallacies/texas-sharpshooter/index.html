Texas Sharpshooter

Finding a pattern in random or selected data after the fact, then treating that pattern as if it was the target all along — like painting the target around bullet holes.

Example

A gambler notices he won on three Tuesdays and declares Tuesdays are his lucky winning pattern, ignoring all the Tuesdays he lost.

Faith Discussion Example

Someone collects scattered numbers and dates from unrelated passages after an event and presents them as a precise prophecy of that event, having chosen only the fitting pieces afterward.

How To Respond

Ask what was predicted beforehand: 'That is an interesting pattern — was it identified before the event, and does it hold up when we include the pieces that do not fit?'
