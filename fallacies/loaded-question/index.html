Loaded Question

Fallacy
Loaded Question
Category
Presumption
Definition
Asking a question that smuggles in an unproved assumption, so any direct yes-or-no answer accepts that assumption.
Example
A reporter asks, 'Have you stopped wasting company money?' — answering either yes or no admits there was waste to begin with.
Faith Discussion Example
Someone asks a pastor, 'Why does your church keep brainwashing young people?' — the question assumes manipulation before any evidence is discussed.
How To Respond
Unpack the assumption politely before answering: 'There is an assumption tucked into that question — could we look at whether it is accurate first, then I will gladly answer?'
