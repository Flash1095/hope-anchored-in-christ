Proof by Assertion

Category
Presumption
Definition
Treating repetition as proof. Saying a claim again, louder or more often, adds no evidence for it.
Example
Someone argues, 'It's true, it's true, it's just true.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'This interpretation is obviously right — everyone knows it.'
Fallacy
Proof by Assertion
How To Respond
Ask for support, not volume: "Repetition isn't evidence — what supports it?"
