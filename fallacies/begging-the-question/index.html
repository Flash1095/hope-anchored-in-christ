Begging the Question

Fallacy
Begging the Question
Category
Presumption
Definition
Assuming the conclusion is already true inside the argument itself, so the reasoning goes in a circle without independent support. The claim is restated rather than proved.
Example
Someone argues, 'This book is the most reliable because it says so itself and everything it says is true.'
Faith Discussion Example
In conversation: 'The Bible is true because it is God's Word, and we know it is God's Word because the Bible says so,' offered as the whole argument without any other reasons.
How To Respond
Notice the circle gently and offer additional grounds: 'That states what I believe — let me also share historical and experiential reasons that support it.'
