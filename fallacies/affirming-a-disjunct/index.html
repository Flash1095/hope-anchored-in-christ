Affirming a Disjunct

Category
Formal
Definition
Arguing 'A or B; A; therefore not B' when the 'or' is not exclusive. Unless the options genuinely exclude each other, one being true does not rule out the other.
Example
Someone argues, 'You can have tea or coffee. You chose tea, so you cannot have coffee.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'Worship can be in spirit or in truth. This service worships in spirit, so it cannot be in truth' — yet John 4:24 joins both.
Fallacy
Affirming a Disjunct
How To Respond
Check the 'or': "'Or' often means 'and/or' — could both be true together here?"
