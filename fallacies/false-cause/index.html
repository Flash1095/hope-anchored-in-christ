False Cause

Fallacy
False Cause
Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Mistaking a connection between two things for proof that one caused the other, when they may be linked by coincidence or a third factor. It includes post hoc reasoning but is broader.
Example
Ice cream sales and sunburn both rise in summer, so someone wrongly concludes ice cream causes sunburn, missing the sun as the common cause.
Faith Discussion Example
A decline in church attendance and a social problem rise in the same decade, and someone blames one for the other without checking other causes or evidence.
How To Respond
Look for other explanations together: 'They do rise together — could there be a third factor, or a different connection, that explains both more fairly?'
