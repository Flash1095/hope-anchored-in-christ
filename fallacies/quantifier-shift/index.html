Quantifier Shift

Category
Formal
Definition
Illegitimately moving from 'every A has some B' to 'there is some B that fits every A'. Each person having something does not mean one thing serves everyone.
Example
Someone argues, 'Every student has a favorite teacher, so there is one teacher who is every student's favorite.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'Every believer has a spiritual gift, so there is one gift every believer has.'
Fallacy
Quantifier Shift
How To Respond
Separate the two claims: 'Each person having something doesn't mean one thing serves everyone.'
