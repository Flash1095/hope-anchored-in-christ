Appeal to Anonymous Authority

Category
Presumption
Definition
Citing unnamed experts — 'studies show,' 'scholars agree' — with no one named and no finding cited. Anonymity makes the authority impossible to check.
Example
Someone argues, 'Studies show this works,' without naming any study.
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'Scholars agree the Gospels were written centuries later,' without naming any scholar.
Fallacy
Appeal to Anonymous Authority
How To Respond
Ask for names: 'Which experts, and what did they actually conclude?'
