Slippery Slope

Fallacy
Slippery Slope
Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Claiming that one step will inevitably lead to an extreme outcome, without showing why each step must follow. A possible concern is treated as a certain chain reaction.
Example
A parent hears, 'If we let him stay up ten minutes later tonight, soon he will never follow any bedtime at all.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone warns, 'If you question one interpretation of a passage, soon you will not believe anything in the Bible,' without showing why careful study must lead there.
How To Respond
Acknowledge the underlying concern, then ask for the links in the chain: 'I share your desire to stay faithful — can we look step by step at what actually follows?'
