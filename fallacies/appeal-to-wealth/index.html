Appeal to Wealth

Category
Relevance
Definition
Treating wealth or success as proof of correctness. Success can come many ways, and it does not validate every opinion of the successful.
Example
Someone argues, 'He is a billionaire, so his health advice must be sound.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'That ministry is huge and wealthy, so its teaching must be correct.'
Fallacy
Appeal to Wealth
How To Respond
Test the teaching, not the treasury: 'Success can come many ways — let's test the teaching itself.'
