Appeal to Ridicule

Mocking a claim instead of refuting it. Laughter at a position is not an argument against it.

Example

Someone argues, 'Believing that is like believing the moon is cheese — absurd!'

Faith Discussion Example

Someone argues, 'You believe a virgin had a baby? Come on.'

How To Respond

Ask for the real objection: "Mockery isn't an argument — what's the actual objection?"
