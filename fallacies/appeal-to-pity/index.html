Appeal to Pity

Category
Relevance
Definition
Urging belief because of sad circumstances rather than evidence. Suffering can show sincerity, but sincerity does not settle truth.
Example
Someone argues, 'This product must be good — the inventor lost everything making it.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'This teaching must be true — the preacher suffered so much for it.'
Fallacy
Appeal to Pity
How To Respond
Honor the suffering, then weigh the claim: 'Suffering can show sincerity, but let's weigh the evidence too.'
