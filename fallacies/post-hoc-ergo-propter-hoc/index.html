Post Hoc Ergo Propter Hoc

Fallacy
Post Hoc Ergo Propter Hoc
Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Assuming that because one event followed another, the first must have caused the second. The Latin means 'after this, therefore because of this.'
Example
A player wears lucky socks, the team wins, and he concludes the socks caused the victory.
Faith Discussion Example
Someone prays for a job, receives an offer the next week, and while gratitude is fitting, a friend might too quickly claim prayer always works like a formula for getting what we want.
How To Respond
Celebrate good outcomes while staying careful about causes: 'I am thankful for how this turned out. Let us be thoughtful about what we can and cannot claim caused it.'

Logic; Prayer; Providence
