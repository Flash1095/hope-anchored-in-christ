Bandwagon

Fallacy
Bandwagon
Category
Relevance
Definition
Claiming something is true or good because many people are joining in or doing it right now. Popular momentum is treated as proof.
Example
An advert urges, 'Everyone is switching to this phone — do not be left behind,' without comparing the phones themselves.
Faith Discussion Example
Someone says, 'This new spiritual trend is everywhere online, so it must be true and good for you,' based on how fast it is spreading.
How To Respond
Slow the momentum kindly: 'It is understandable to notice what is trending. Could we ask whether it is true and wise, apart from how popular it feels right now?'
