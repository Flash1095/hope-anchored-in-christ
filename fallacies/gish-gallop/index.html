Gish Gallop

Category
Presumption
Definition
Overwhelming an opponent with a rapid flood of weak arguments, so no single one can be answered in the time available. Quantity is used to simulate strength.
Example
Someone fires fifty flimsy reasons in two minutes and declares victory when only three get answered.
Faith Discussion Example
A debate opponent fires twenty objections in two minutes, leaving no time to test any one properly.
Fallacy
Gish Gallop
How To Respond
Slow it down: "Let's take one argument at a time and test it properly."
