Cherry Picking

Selecting only the evidence that supports a preferred conclusion while ignoring evidence that points the other way. It is also called selective evidence.

Example

A review quotes one positive sentence from a mostly critical review and presents it as if the reviewer loved the product.

Faith Discussion Example

Someone quotes a single verse about judgment to prove God is only angry, ignoring the many passages about His mercy — or quotes only comfort verses and ignores calls to repentance.

How To Respond

Invite the whole picture: 'That passage is important — could we read it in context and alongside the other passages on this topic, so we hear the full counsel?'
