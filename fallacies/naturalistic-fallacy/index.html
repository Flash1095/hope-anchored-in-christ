Naturalistic Fallacy

Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Deriving an 'ought' from an 'is' — treating a description of nature as if it settled a moral question, or defining good in purely natural terms.
Example
Someone argues, 'Lions kill rivals, so killing rivals is natural and therefore acceptable.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'This behavior occurs in animals, so it must be morally fine.'
Fallacy
Naturalistic Fallacy
How To Respond
Ask for the missing step: "What is doesn't by itself tell us what ought to be — that step needs its own argument."
