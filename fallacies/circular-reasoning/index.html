Circular Reasoning

Fallacy
Circular Reasoning
Category
Presumption
Definition
A form of begging the question where the conclusion is used to support its own premise, so A is true because of B and B is true because of A.
Example
A student says, 'Our team is the best because we are better than everyone, and we know we are better because we are the best.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone says, 'I know this interpretation is right because my teacher taught it, and I know my teacher is always right because he taught this interpretation.'
How To Respond
Unpack the loop together without embarrassment: 'It sounds like we are using the same idea twice — is there an outside reason or passage we can test it against?'
