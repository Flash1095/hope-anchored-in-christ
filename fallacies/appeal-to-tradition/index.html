Appeal to Tradition

Fallacy
Appeal to Tradition
Category
Weak Induction
Definition
Assuming something is right or better simply because it has long been done or believed that way. Tradition can carry wisdom, but longevity alone is not proof.
Example
A workplace resists a helpful change with, 'We have always done it this way, so this way must be best.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone says, 'Our church has always interpreted this passage this way, so there is no need to ever re-examine it,' closing the door on careful study of the text itself.
How To Respond
Honor tradition as a starting point: 'Tradition often preserves real wisdom — could we also test our practice against Scripture together, as earlier generations did?'
