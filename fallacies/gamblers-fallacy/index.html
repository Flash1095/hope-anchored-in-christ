Gambler's Fallacy

Believing a random streak must reverse — that after several of one outcome, the opposite is 'due'. Independent events owe us no correction.

Example

Someone argues, 'Five reds in a row — black is due.'

Faith Discussion Example

Someone argues, 'We've had three bad years, so a revival must be next.'

How To Respond

Check independence: "Independent events don't owe us a correction."
