Appeal to Fear

Category
Relevance
Definition
Using fear as a substitute for evidence. Fear can warn us, but a scary outcome does not prove a claim true or false.
Example
Someone argues, 'If you don't buy this alarm, burglars will target your home.'
Faith Discussion Example
Someone argues, 'If you question this teaching, you will lose your salvation.'
Fallacy
Appeal to Fear
How To Respond
Separate warning from proof: 'Fear can warn us, but it can't prove a claim — what are the reasons?'
