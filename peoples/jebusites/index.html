Jebusites

The Jebusites were the pre-Israelite inhabitants of Jerusalem. Genesis 10:16 lists them among the sons of Canaan. In Judges 1:21 the Benjamites failed to drive them out of Jerusalem, and in Judges 19:10 the city is still called Jebus, a city of the Jebusites. David captured their stronghold, the strong hold of Zion, and made it his capital (2 Samuel 5:6-9). Later, Araunah the Jebusite sold David the threshingfloor on Mount Moriah where the altar was built (2 Samuel 24:18-25).

Culture

A fortified city people. Their stronghold on the hill later called Zion held out against Judah and Benjamin until David took it, and the city they built became the capital of Israel.

Religion

They shared the Canaanite religion of their neighbors. The Bible records no distinct Jebusite cult, only that their city and its threshingfloor passed into Israel's worship when David built an altar there.

Bible Connection

The Jebusites held the city that became Jerusalem. Their defeat gave David his capital, and Araunah's threshingfloor became the site of the future temple.
