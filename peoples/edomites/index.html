Edomites

People Group
Edomites
Time Period
c. 1300 BC-first century AD (Idumean phase)
Region
South of the Dead Sea, from the Arabah to the Red Sea, around Mount Seir
History
This entry covers the Edomites as a people; the Edomite kingdom is covered by the Edom entry in Civilizations. The Edomites descend from Esau, who is Edom (Genesis 36:1), named for the red stew for which he sold his birthright (Genesis 25:30). Edom refused Israel passage in the wilderness (Numbers 20:14), and David put garrisons throughout Edom (2 Samuel 8:14). Obadiah's whole oracle turns on brotherhood betrayed: Edom stood by while strangers carried away Jacob's wealth (Obadiah 1:1). In the later period the same people appear as the Idumeans, whose family produced Herod the Great.
Culture
Miners and traders of the Arabah, working copper and running the Red Sea port of Ezion-geber; in the later period the same people are called Idumeans.
Religion
Edomite inscriptions name Qaus as a national god; Scripture names no Edomite god directly, and the brother-rivalry, not religion, drives the biblical story.
Bible Connection
The Edomites are Esau's people, Israel's brother and rival: the refused passage, the wars of Judah's kings, and Obadiah's oracle against a brother who stood by.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 25:30; Genesis 36:1; Numbers 20:14; 2 Samuel 8:14; Obadiah 1:1
Related People
Esau; Jacob; King David; Herod the Great
Related Places
Edom; Mount Seir; Ezion-geber
Related Events
Esau Sells His Birthright; Jacob Meets Esau Again
