Magog

People Group
Magog
Time Period
Ancient (Genesis 10 era); Ezekiel's oracle and Revelation
Region
The far north (Ezekiel 38:2; 39:6); Magog is also named as a land, and a place entry covers the land sense
History
Magog, son of Japheth, is named in the Table of Nations (Genesis 10:2) and becomes the land of Gog's rule in Ezekiel 38-39: 'the land of Magog, the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal.' Ezekiel 39:6 sends fire on Magog itself. In Revelation 20:8 'Gog and Magog' gather for the final rebellion, an eschatological use of the names rather than a first-century nation. Gog is a ruler, not a people, and is not drafted here.
Culture
Nothing distinct is recorded of Magogite culture; in Ezekiel they appear as a great northern host.
Religion
Nothing distinct is recorded of Magogite religion.
Bible Connection
Magog moves from genealogy to prophecy: a Japhethite name, then the northern land of Ezekiel's invasion oracle, then the final rebellion's army in Revelation.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:2; Ezekiel 38:2; Ezekiel 39:6; Revelation 20:8
