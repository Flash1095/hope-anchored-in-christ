Hagarites

People Group
Hagarites
Time Period
Settlement era (1 Chronicles 5); Psalm 83 confederacy
Region
East of Gilead (1 Chronicles 5:10); the Reubenites, Gadites, and half-tribe of Manasseh took their land
History
The Hagarites are the Arabian people fought by Reuben, Gad, and the half-tribe of Manasseh east of Gilead (1 Chronicles 5:10). In the fuller war account they fight alongside Jetur, Nephish, and Nodab, and are defeated because their enemies 'cried to God in the battle' (1 Chronicles 5:19-20). Psalm 83:6 names the 'Hagarenes' in the confederacy against Israel. The name is traditionally taken from Hagar, but the text itself does not state the descent, and the tradition is noted as tradition. Modern works usually spell the name Hagrites.
Culture
An eastern people with tents and livestock, taken in war by the Transjordan tribes.
Religion
Nothing distinct is recorded of Hagarite religion.
Bible Connection
The Hagarites are the eastern enemy of the Transjordan tribes: defeated in a war the Chronicler credits to prayer, then named in Psalm 83's confederacy.
Key Scriptures
1 Chronicles 5:10; 1 Chronicles 5:19; Psalm 83:6
Related People
Hagar; Reuben; Gad
Related Places
