Kedar

People Group
Kedar
Time Period
From Genesis through the prophets (Arabian nomad horizon)
Region
The Arabian desert east of Israel, in tents and villages (Isaiah 42:11; Psalm 120:5)
History
Kedar, son of Ishmael, heads the Arabian nomads of the prophets. Psalm 120:5 makes 'the tents of Kedar' proverbial for distant dwelling, and the Song of Songs borrows the same image for dark tents (Song of Songs 1:5). Isaiah 21:16-17 gives Kedar's glory a single year to fail, Isaiah 42:11 calls the villages that Kedar inhabits to sing, Isaiah 60:7 gathers 'the flocks of Kedar' to the restored Zion, Jeremiah 49:28 sends Nebuchadnezzar against Kedar's men of the east, and Ezekiel 27:21 has Arabia and the princes of Kedar trading lambs, rams, and goats with Tyre.
Culture
Nomadic herders of the Arabian desert, living in tents and villages, trading flocks with Tyre.
Religion
Nothing distinct is recorded of Kedarite religion.
Bible Connection
Kedar is the desert neighbor in Israel's poetry and prophecy: the psalmist's distant tent, Isaiah's doomed glory and gathered flock, Jeremiah's target.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 25:13; Psalm 120:5; Isaiah 21:16; Isaiah 42:11; Isaiah 60:7; Jeremiah 49:28; Ezekiel 27:21
Related People
Ishmael; Abraham; Hagar
Related Places
Kedar; Arabia
