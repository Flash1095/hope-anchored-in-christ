Togarmah

People Group
Togarmah
Time Period
Ancient (Genesis 10 era); Ezekiel's oracles
Region
The far north, 'of the north quarters' (Ezekiel 38:6); later writers link the name with Armenia, a proposal, not a certainty
History
Togarmah, son of Gomer, is named in the Table of Nations (Genesis 10:3). Ezekiel 27:14 has the house of Togarmah trading horses, horsemen, and mules with Tyre, and Ezekiel 38:6 marches Togarmah in Gog's coalition from the north quarters.
Culture
Horse-breeders and traders, in Ezekiel's Tyre catalogue (Ezekiel 27:14).
Religion
Nothing distinct is recorded of Togarmahite religion.
Bible Connection
Togarmah is the Bible's horse-supplier from the north: Tyre's market partner, then one of Gog's bands.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:3; Ezekiel 27:14; Ezekiel 38:6
