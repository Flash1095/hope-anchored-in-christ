Moabites

People Group
Moabites
Time Period
c. 1300-400 BC
Region
Highlands east of the Dead Sea, between the Arnon and the Zered
History
This entry covers the Moabites as a people; the Moabite kingdom is covered by the Moab entry in Civilizations. The Moabites descend from Moab, born to Lot and his elder daughter (Genesis 19:37). Israel camped in the plains of Moab within sight of the Promised Land (Numbers 22:1), where Balak hired Balaam to curse them. Ruth the Moabitess married into Bethlehem (Ruth 1:4) and became the great-grandmother of David. Mesha's revolt against Israel is recorded both in 2 Kings 3:4 and on his own victory stele, and Isaiah's oracle over Moab (Isaiah 15:1) mourns a people, not only a kingdom.
Culture
Highland farmers and herders east of the Dead Sea, famed for sheep: Mesha's tribute to Israel was a hundred thousand lambs and a hundred thousand rams (2 Kings 3:4).
Religion
Chemosh, god of Moab, to whom Solomon built a high place (1 Kings 11:7) and whose people are called 'the people of Chemosh' (Numbers 21:29).
Bible Connection
The Moabites are Lot's descendants turned neighbor, enemy, and kin: Balak's people, Ruth's people, and the people of Mesha's stele.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 19:37; Numbers 22:1; Ruth 1:4; 2 Kings 3:4; Isaiah 15:1
