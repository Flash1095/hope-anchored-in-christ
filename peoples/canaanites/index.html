Canaanites

People Group
Canaanites
Time Period
Patriarchal era through the monarchy; the name still appears in the Gospels (Matthew 15:22)
Region
The land of Canaan, from Sidon to Gaza and inland to the Jordan (Genesis 10:19)
History
This entry covers the Canaanites as a people; the land and its city-states are covered by the Canaan entry in Civilizations. The Canaanites descend from Canaan, son of Ham (Genesis 10:15-19), whose sons give the coastal and inland peoples their names. The name is sometimes used broadly for all the pre-Israelite inhabitants: 'the Canaanite was then in the land' when Abram arrived (Genesis 12:6). Deuteronomy 7:1 counts the Canaanites among the seven nations Israel was to displace, and Joshua 3:10 names them among the peoples the living God would drive out. In Judges the pattern reverses: Israel 'dwelt among the Canaanites' and took their daughters in marriage (Judges 3:5), and the peoples and their gods became the standing test of the covenant.
Culture
City-dwellers and farmers of walled towns and open villages, trading along the coastal road and the Jordan Valley.
Religion
The gods of Canaan, Baal and Asherah and the gods of the city-states, whose worship Israel was warned against and repeatedly adopted.
Bible Connection
The Canaanites are the peoples of the Promised Land: the inhabitants Israel was told to displace, the neighbors Israel lived among, and the constant covenant test of Judges.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:15; Genesis 12:6; Deuteronomy 7:1; Judges 3:5; Joshua 3:10
Related People
Abraham; Isaac; Jacob; Joshua; Caleb
Related Places
Canaan; Sidon; Tyre; Gaza; Hebron
Related Events
The Twelve Spies and Israel's Unbelief; The Walls of Jericho
