Seba

People Group
Seba
Time Period
Ancient (Genesis 10 era) through Isaiah
Region
A Cushite kingdom, named beside Egypt and Ethiopia (Isaiah 43:3); the precise location is debated, with an upper Nile region proposed
History
Seba, son of Cush, is named in the Table of Nations (Genesis 10:7). Psalm 72:10 has the kings of Sheba and Seba offering gifts, treating them as two distinct kingdoms. Isaiah 43:3 gives 'Ethiopia and Seba' as Israel's ransom, and Isaiah 45:14 names 'the Sabeans, men of stature' among the peoples coming over to Israel. Seba is not Sheba: the two names sit side by side in Psalm 72:10 and belong to different lines, Seba to Cush and Sheba to Raamah and to Joktan.
Culture
A trading kingdom of the south, named for its merchandise in Isaiah 45:14.
Religion
Nothing distinct is recorded of Sebaite religion.
Bible Connection
Seba is the southern kingdom in Israel's ransom price and tribute vision: named with Egypt and Ethiopia, distinct from Sheba.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:7; Psalm 72:10; Isaiah 43:3; Isaiah 45:14
