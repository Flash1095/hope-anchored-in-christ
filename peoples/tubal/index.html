Tubal

Tubal, son of Japheth, is named in the Table of Nations (Genesis 10:2). Isaiah 66:19 sends messengers to Tubal and Javan among 'the isles afar off, that have not heard my fame.' Ezekiel 27:13 pairs Tubal with Meshech trading slaves and brass with Tyre, Ezekiel 32:26 counts Tubal among the slain nations, and Ezekiel 38:2 puts Tubal under Gog's rule.

Culture

Traders in persons and bronze-work, in Ezekiel's Tyre catalogue (Ezekiel 27:13).

Details

People Group: Tubal
Time Period: Ancient (Genesis 10 era) through Ezekiel's oracles
Region: The far north, paired with Meshech (Ezekiel 38:2); the precise location is debated, with an Anatolian and Black Sea region proposed
Religion: Nothing distinct is recorded of Tubalite religion.
Bible Connection: Tubal stands with Meshech throughout: a Japhethite name, a distant coastland in Isaiah's mission vision, and one of Gog's peoples.
Key Scriptures: Genesis 10:2; Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 27:13; Ezekiel 32:26; Ezekiel 38:2
Related Places: Tubal
