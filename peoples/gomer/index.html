Gomer

People Group
Gomer
Time Period
Ancient (Genesis 10 era); named again in Ezekiel 38
Region
The far north, in the company of Magog (Ezekiel 38:6); the precise location is debated
History
Gomer, son of Japheth, heads the first branch of the Table of Nations (Genesis 10:2). The name returns only in Ezekiel 38:6, where Gomer and his bands march in Gog's coalition from the north. Later writers commonly identify Gomer with the Cimmerians of the ancient Near East, but the text itself gives no location and no story, and that identification remains a proposal, not a certainty. Gomer the people is not Gomer the wife of Hosea, a different person.
Culture
Nothing distinct is recorded of Gomerite culture; in Ezekiel they appear only as a warrior host from the north.
Religion
Nothing distinct is recorded of Gomerite religion.
Bible Connection
Gomer brackets the Bible's north: a Japhethite name in the Table of Nations, then a northern invader in Ezekiel's oracle against Gog.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:2; Ezekiel 38:6
