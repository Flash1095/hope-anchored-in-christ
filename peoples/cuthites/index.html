Cuthites

The Cuthites were one of the Mesopotamian peoples Assyria resettled in Samaria after Israel's fall (2 Kings 17:24). The men of Cuth made Nergal their god (2 Kings 17:30). Later Jewish tradition called the Samaritans Cuthites or Cutheans after them, though the biblical text itself does not use that name for the Samaritans.

Culture

As a Mesopotamian people, the men of Cuth shared the urban, scribal, and idol-making culture of Babylon's world.

Bible Connection

The Cuthites' biblical footprint is the resettlement: a foreign people planted in Israel's empty cities, whose god Nergal joined the mixed worship of the new Samaria.

Details

People Group: Cuthites
Time Period: c. 700 BC (Assyrian resettlement)
Region: Mesopotamia (exact site debated)
Religion: The men of Cuth worshipped Nergal, god of the underworld and of plague, whom they set up in Samaria.
Key Scriptures: 2 Kings 17:24; 2 Kings 17:30
Related Places: Samaria
Related Events: Fall of Samaria
