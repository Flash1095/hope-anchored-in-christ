Ammonites

People Group
Ammonites
Time Period
c. 1300-500 BC
Region
East of the Jordan, around Rabbah
History
This entry covers the Ammonites as a people; the Ammonite state is covered by the Ammon entry in Civilizations. The Ammonites descend from Ben-Ammi, born to Lot and his younger daughter (Genesis 19:38). In the wilderness Israel was told not to take Ammonite land (Deuteronomy 2:19). Jephthah defeated them in the judges era (Judges 11:1), Saul's first victory as king broke Nahash's siege of Jabesh-gilead (1 Samuel 11:1), and David's war with Ammon (2 Samuel 10:1) frames the Bathsheba story. Solomon's many wives included Ammonites (1 Kings 11:1), and their god's high place stood outside Jerusalem until Josiah.
Culture
Farmers and warriors of the Jordan highlands around Rabbah, a walled capital city that held out against Israel's kings.
Religion
Milcom (Molech), the abomination of the Ammonites, to whom Solomon built a high place (1 Kings 11:5, 33).
Bible Connection
The Ammonites are Lot's younger line turned rival: Jephthah's enemy, Saul's first victory, and the war that frames David's fall.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 19:38; Deuteronomy 2:19; Judges 11:1; 1 Samuel 11:1; 2 Samuel 10:1
Related People
Jephthah, Judge of Israel; King Saul; King David; Nahash, King of Ammon; Lot
Related Places
Ammon; Rabbah
