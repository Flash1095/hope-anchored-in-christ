Dedan

People Group
Dedan
Time Period
From Genesis through Ezekiel
Region
Northwest Arabia, on the incense-trade routes; the precise site is debated, with the oasis of al-Ula the usual proposal
History
Two Dedans appear in Genesis and must not be merged silently: Dedan son of Raamah (Genesis 10:7) and Dedan son of Jokshan (Genesis 25:3). The Arabian traders of the prophets are Dedan without further distinction in the text. Jeremiah 25:23 names Dedan among the judged nations, Jeremiah 49:8 warns the inhabitants of Dedan to flee and dwell deep, Ezekiel 27:15 and 27:20 have Dedan trading ivory, ebony, and saddlecloths with Tyre, and Ezekiel 38:13 has Sheba and Dedan questioning Gog's spoil.
Culture
Caravan traders of northwest Arabia, dealing in ivory, ebony, and fine cloth.
Religion
Nothing distinct is recorded of Dedanite religion.
Bible Connection
Dedan is the Arabian merchant in Tyre's market, the warned inhabitant of Jeremiah's oracle, and the questioner of Gog's spoil.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:7; Genesis 25:3; Jeremiah 25:23; Jeremiah 49:8; Ezekiel 27:15; Ezekiel 27:20; Ezekiel 38:13
Related Places
Dedan; Arabia
