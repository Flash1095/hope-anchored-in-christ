Meshech

Meshech, son of Japheth, is named in the Table of Nations (Genesis 10:2). Psalm 120:5 makes Meshech proverbial for distant exile: 'Woe is me, that I sojourn in Mesech' (the Psalm spells the name Mesech). Ezekiel 27:13 has Meshech trading slaves and vessels of brass with Tyre, Ezekiel 32:26 counts Meshech among the slain nations gone down to the pit, and Ezekiel 38:2 puts Meshech under Gog's rule.

Culture

Traders in persons and bronze-work, in Ezekiel's Tyre catalogue (Ezekiel 27:13).

Details

People Group: Meshech
Time Period: Ancient (Genesis 10 era) through Ezekiel's oracles
Region: The far north, paired with Tubal (Ezekiel 38:2); the precise location is debated, with an Anatolian and Black Sea region proposed
Religion: Nothing distinct is recorded of Meshechite religion.
Bible Connection: Meshech is the Bible's far north made personal: the psalmist's distant sojourn, Tyre's trading partner, and one of Gog's peoples.
Key Scriptures: Genesis 10:2; Psalm 120:5; Ezekiel 27:13; Ezekiel 32:26; Ezekiel 38:2
Related Places: Meshech
