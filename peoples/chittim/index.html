Chittim

People Group
Chittim
Time Period
From Balaam's oracle through Daniel
Region
The western sea-peoples: 'ships of Chittim' (Numbers 24:24; Daniel 11:30); usually identified with Cyprus and the western Mediterranean islands, an identification, not a certainty
History
Chittim, son of Javan, is named in the Table of Nations (Genesis 10:4). Balaam's oracle warns that 'ships shall come from the coast of Chittim' and afflict both Asshur and Eber (Numbers 24:24). Isaiah 23:1 and 23:12 tie Chittim to Tyre's shipping news, Jeremiah 2:10 sends Israel to 'pass over the isles of Chittim' and see, Ezekiel 27:6 brings ivory 'out of the isles of Chittim,' and Daniel 11:30 has 'ships of Chittim' turning back the king of the north. Modern scholarship usually spells the name Kittim.
Culture
Seafarers and traders of the western isles, in Tyre's orbit.
Religion
Nothing distinct is recorded of Chittimite religion.
Bible Connection
Chittim is the Bible's west: the ships that come from beyond the sea in Balaam's oracle, Isaiah's Tyre lament, and Daniel's wars.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 24:24; Isaiah 23:1; Isaiah 23:12; Jeremiah 2:10; Ezekiel 27:6; Daniel 11:30
Related Places
Cyprus
