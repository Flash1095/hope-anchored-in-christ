Nebaioth

People Group
Nebaioth
Time Period
Patriarchal era (Genesis) and Isaiah's restoration vision
Region
Arabian, beside Kedar (Isaiah 60:7); the precise location is not stated
History
Nebaioth, firstborn of Ishmael, is named in the Ishmaelite list (Genesis 25:13). Esau married Mahalath, daughter of Ishmael and sister of Nebajoth (Genesis 28:9; 36:3 spells the name Nebajoth), tying Nebaioth's house to Esau's line. The name returns in Isaiah 60:7, where 'the rams of Nebaioth shall minister unto thee' at the restored Zion, beside the flocks of Kedar.
Culture
Herders of the Arabian desert, named for their rams in Isaiah's vision.
Religion
Nothing distinct is recorded of Nebaiothite religion.
Bible Connection
Nebaioth links Ishmael's firstborn to Esau's household and to Isaiah's gathered flocks.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 25:13; Genesis 28:9; Isaiah 60:7
Related People
Ishmael
