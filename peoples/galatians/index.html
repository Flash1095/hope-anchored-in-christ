Galatians

Celtic tribes that invaded Asia Minor settled in the central highlands and gave the region its name; under Rome it became the province of Galatia. Paul evangelised its churches on his missionary journeys and wrote them his sharpest letter.

Culture

Celtic heritage under Greco-Roman rule; by New Testament times the Galatians were Greek-speaking provincials, organised in the churches Paul founded.

Religion

Pagan Celtic and Greek cults before conversion; Paul's letter confronts Galatian believers who were turning back to law-observance as the ground of acceptance with God.

Bible Connection

Paul's letter to the Galatians defends justification by faith alone and is addressed to churches he had visited; the churches of Galatia also contributed to the collection for Jerusalem.
