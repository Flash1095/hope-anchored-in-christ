Put Off the Old Self and Put On the New

Command
Put Off the Old Self and Put On the New
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's renewal command: put off the old man corrupted by deceitful lusts, be renewed in the spirit of your mind, and put on the new man created in righteousness and true holiness.
Commandment
Put off concerning the former conversation the old man, which is corrupt according to the deceitful lusts; and be renewed in the spirit of your mind; and put on the new man.
Scripture
Ephesians 4:22-24; Colossians 3:9-10
Application
The old and new selves are not improved and un-improved versions of the same life but two garments: one is taken off because it belongs to a former conversation, the other put on because it is created after God's image.
Related Topics
Holiness; Sanctification; Sin
Related Books
Ephesians; Colossians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to make sanctification a daily change of clothing, not a single past event.
Duration
For all time
