Escape Sodom, Do Not Look Back

Command
Escape Sodom, Do Not Look Back
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
The angels' command to Lot as Sodom's judgment fell: escape for your life, do not look behind, do not stay in the plain, escape to the mountain.
Commandment
Escape for thy life; look not behind thee, neither stay thou in all the plain; escape to the mountain.
Scripture
Genesis 19:17
Application
One moment, one household, one road out. Lot's wife looked back and became a pillar of salt (Genesis 19:26) — the warning inside the command, fulfilled in the same chapter. Lot's plea for Zoar (Genesis 19:20) does not change the command's form.
Related Topics
Obedience; Judgment; Repentance
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Lot
Related Events
Destruction of Sodom and Gomorrah
Purpose
To rescue Lot's household alive from a judgment already falling, with no delay and no backward glance (Genesis 19:17).
