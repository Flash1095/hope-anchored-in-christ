Marry a captive woman only after the appointed month

Command
Marry a captive woman only after the appointed month
Category
Civil Law
Summary
One of the family laws of Deuteronomy 21–25: marry a captive woman only after the appointed month. These laws protected the weaker party at each family step.
Commandment
Marry a captive woman only after the appointed month
Scripture
Deuteronomy 21:11
Application
These civil provisions belonged to ancient Israel's family law and have passed with it; the protection of the weaker party they aimed at remains the lesson.
Related Topics
Marriage; Family; Justice
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Matthew
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To restrain the conqueror and protect the captive woman: a month of mourning and a settled household before any marriage, so she is not taken in the heat of war (Deuteronomy 21:11-13).
