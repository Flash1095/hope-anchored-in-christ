Do not eat the heave offering outside Jerusalem

The command: Do not eat the heave offering outside Jerusalem (Deuteronomy 12:17). Heave offerings belonged to the place God chose and might not be eaten in one's own towns.

Commandment

Do not eat the heave offering outside Jerusalem.

Application

It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.

Purpose

Scripture's stated ground: the heave offering was eaten before the LORD in the place he chose (Deuteronomy 12:17-18).

Function

Heave offerings belonged to the place God chose and might not be eaten in one's own towns — a ceremonial law of place. Its goal was to keep the lifted-up gifts where God put his name, the holy portions eaten before him in Jerusalem. The evil was treating the heave offering as ordinary food to be eaten at home, the sanctuary's claim ignored. It restrained that by the ban: the offering stayed with the place, and the eater came to it.
