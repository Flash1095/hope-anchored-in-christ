Circumcise every male child

Command
Circumcise every male child
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
God's covenant command to Abraham: every man child among you shall be circumcised on the eighth day, as the token of the covenant between God and Abraham's seed (Genesis 17:10-12). The sign was cut into the flesh of the household — sons, servants, and all — marking them as covenant people.
Commandment
Circumcise every male child, as the sign of God's covenant with Abraham.
Scripture
Genesis 17:10
Application
The sign marked Abraham's household as God's own, and the uncircumcised male was cut off from his people (Genesis 17:14). Debated: Jews continue circumcision as the covenant sign; the New Testament holds it is not required for Gentile believers (Galatians 5:2-6; Acts 15), with circumcision of the heart as the fulfilment. This row does not settle that difference.

Related Events
The Covenant of Circumcision
Purpose
To mark Abraham's seed with the sign of the everlasting covenant in their flesh (Genesis 17:11-13); the sign's ongoing force is the debated point, not its original purpose.
