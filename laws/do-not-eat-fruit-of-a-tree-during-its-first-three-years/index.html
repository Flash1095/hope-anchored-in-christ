Do not eat fruit of a tree during its first three years

Command
Do not eat fruit of a tree during its first three years
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Fruit of a newly planted tree is not to be eaten during its first three years; it is counted as uncircumcised, set apart and not for use.
Commandment
Do not eat fruit of a tree during its first three years.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:23
Application
The three-year wait taught patience and restraint: the planter does not snatch the first fruit but lets the tree establish itself. The rule belonged to Israel's land law; whether and how it binds outside the land is a matter of the land system's scope, and it is drafted here in its stated form.
Related Topics
Holiness; Obedience
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: no reason is stated in Scripture for the three-year bar; it is classed among the statutes whose wisdom is God's own, teaching restraint with firstfruits.
