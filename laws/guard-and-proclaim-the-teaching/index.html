Guard and Proclaim the Teaching

Command
Guard and Proclaim the Teaching
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
The stewardship command: hold fast the apostolic teaching, preach the word in season and out, and pass it to faithful people who will teach others.
Commandment
Preach the word in season and out of season, hold fast the pattern of sound words, and entrust it to faithful people who will teach others also.
Scripture
2 Timothy 4:2; 2 Thessalonians 2:15; Titus 2:1; 2 Timothy 2:2
Application
The teaching is a deposit to be guarded, not an invention to be improved. Each generation receives it, keeps it, and hands it on intact.
Related Topics
Discipleship; Truth; Church
Related Books
2 Timothy; 2 Thessalonians; Titus
Related People
Paul; Timothy; Titus
Purpose
The apostolic deposit to be guarded and handed on intact to faithful teachers (2 Timothy 2:2).
Duration
For all time
