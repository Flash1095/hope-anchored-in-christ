Burn a garment with spreading plague

Command
Burn a garment with spreading plague
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
A garment in which a fretting leprosy spreads, after washing and re-examination, must be burned in the fire; it is unclean and cannot be kept.
Commandment
Burn a garment in which the plague spreads and is not cleansed by washing.
Scripture
Leviticus 13:52
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Burning destroyed what washing could not cleanse, so the plague could not spread through the camp. Jesus' touch restored those the purity laws isolated (Mark 1:40-45).
Related Topics
Purity; Holiness
Related Books
Leviticus; Mark; Luke
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To order who could approach worship while ceremonially unclean and how they were restored. TRADITIONAL reading: with the temple order ended, these washings no longer bind believers; Jesus' touch restored those the laws isolated (Mark 1:40-45; Luke 8:43-48).
