Give the firstborn a double portion

Command
Give the firstborn a double portion
Category
Civil Law
Summary
The firstborn receives a double portion of all that his father has: the right of the firstborn is his, whatever the father's affections.
Commandment
Give the firstborn a double portion.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 21:17
Application
The double portion recognised the firstborn's standing and his future responsibility for the household. The inheritance system belonged to Israel's tribal order, but the principle — family provision by fixed right, not by favour — still rebukes partiality.
Related Topics
Family; Justice
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To keep family land within the tribe and provide for daughters where there was no son (Numbers 27; 36). Here that purpose takes the form of the firstborn's double portion.
