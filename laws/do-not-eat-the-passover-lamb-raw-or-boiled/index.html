Do not eat the Passover lamb raw or boiled

The Passover lamb may not be eaten raw or boiled in water; it must be roasted with fire.

Commandment

Do not eat the Passover lamb raw, nor boiled in water, but roast with fire.

Application

The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The roasting was part of the haste and wholeness of the meal on the night of deliverance. The ritual form belongs to Israel's covenant calendar, not to Christian observance.

Purpose

Scripture's stated context: the meal is eaten in haste on the night of deliverance from Egypt, ready to depart (Exodus 12:11).

Function

God prescribed exactly how the lamb was to be eaten, and that exactness is what makes this ceremonial: a rite kept in a set form. Its goal was to keep the meal whole and unhurried in its own way — roasted with fire, as the night of deliverance required. It did not address a particular evil; its work was remembrance, not restraint. The rule guarded the form of the memorial, so the meal stayed the meal God had appointed.
