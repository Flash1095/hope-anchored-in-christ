Give to Everyone Who Asks

Command
Give to Everyone Who Asks
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' open-handed command: give to the one who asks, and do not turn away the one who would borrow.
Commandment
Give to him that asketh thee, and from him that would borrow of thee turn not thou away.
Scripture
Matthew 5:42; Luke 6:30
Application
The command checks a closed fist. Wisdom still governs how a gift helps best, but the default posture is generosity, not refusal.
Related Topics
Generosity; Mercy; Love
Related Books
Matthew; Luke
Related People
Jesus
Related Events
The Sermon on the Mount
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to imitate a giving Father and to refuse the love of possessions that shuts out a neighbour in need.
Duration
For all time
