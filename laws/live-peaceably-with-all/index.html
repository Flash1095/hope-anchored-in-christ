Live Peaceably with All People

Command
Live Peaceably with All People
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's peace command: if it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men.
Commandment
If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men.
Scripture
Romans 12:18
Application
The qualifiers are part of the command, not an escape from it: 'if possible' admits that some will refuse peace, and 'as much as lieth in you' places the whole of your share on you.
Related Topics
Peace; Fellowship; Mercy
Related Books
Romans
Related People
Paul
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to pursue peace to the limit of one's own power, without guaranteeing the other's response.
Duration
For all time
