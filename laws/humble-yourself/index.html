Humble Yourself

Command
Humble Yourself
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' exaltation reversal: whoever exalts himself shall be abased, and he that humbles himself shall be exalted.
Commandment
Whosoever shall exalt himself shall be abased; and he that shall humble himself shall be exalted.
Scripture
Matthew 23:12; Luke 14:11; Luke 18:14
Application
The command runs through the banquet and the Pharisee's prayer alike: the seat you take for yourself will be taken from you, but the low place is where God lifts.
Related Topics
Humility; Obedience
Related Books
Matthew; Luke
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to make humility the posture of the kingdom, reversing the world's climb for honour.
Duration
For all time
