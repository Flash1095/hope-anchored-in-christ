Do Not Put God to the Test

Command
Do Not Put God to the Test
Category
Moral Law
Summary
The testing ban: do not test the LORD your God as Israel tested him at Massah, demanding proof before obedience. Doubt: Distinct imperative, but closely tied to the Shema passage and the Massah narrative; could fold into Love God Wholeheartedly rather than standing alone.
Commandment
Do not put the LORD your God to the test.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 6:16; Matthew 4:7
Application
Jesus quotes this against Satan's invitation to force God's hand. Testing God means demanding he prove himself on our terms before we trust him.

Faith; Obedience
