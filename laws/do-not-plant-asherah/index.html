Do Not Plant an Asherah by the Altar

No grove of trees — an Asherah, the wooden symbol of the goddess — is to be planted near the altar of the LORD. True worship must not be mixed with Canaanite symbols, even as decoration.

Commandment

Do not plant a grove of trees near the altar of the LORD your God.

Application

The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as moral. The altar belonged to the LORD alone; importing a neighbouring religion's symbol beside it was already a compromise. The New Testament parallel is not about trees but about mixture: worship is not to be blended with idolatry (2 Corinthians 6:14-16).

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: the grove was the visible sign of Asherah worship, and its presence beside the LORD's altar mixed two worships. Scripture states the prohibition without a separate reason (Deuteronomy 16:21).

Function

No grove of trees — an Asherah, the wooden symbol of the goddess. This law is moral in kind — it names what is right — though its setting was Israel's.
