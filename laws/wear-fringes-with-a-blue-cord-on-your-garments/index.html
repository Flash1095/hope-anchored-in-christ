Wear fringes with a blue cord on your garments

Command
Wear fringes with a blue cord on your garments
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Numbers 15:38 commands fringes on the borders of garments, with a ribband of blue upon the fringe of the borders. The blue cord marked the fringe as a reminder worn in daily sight.
Commandment
Wear fringes with a blue cord on the borders of your garments.
Scripture
Numbers 15:38
Application
Scripture's stated reason: that ye may look upon the fringe and remember all the commandments of the LORD and do them (Numbers 15:39). Debated: whether the fringe itself remains obligatory is a genuine difference between Jewish practice and Christian readings — this row does not settle it.
Related Topics
Holiness; Obedience; Remembrance
Related Books
Numbers; Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated reason: that ye may look upon the fringe and remember all the commandments of the LORD and do them, and not follow your own heart and eyes (Numbers 15:39).
