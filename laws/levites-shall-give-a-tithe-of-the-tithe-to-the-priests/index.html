Levites shall give a tithe of the tithe to the priests

Command
Levites shall give a tithe of the tithe to the priests
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: Levites shall give a tithe of the tithe to the priests (Numbers 18:26). From the tithe they received, the Levites lifted up a heave offering, a tenth of the tithe, to Aaron the priest.
Commandment
Levites shall give a tithe of the tithe to the priests, as prescribed in Numbers 18:26.
Scripture
Numbers 18:26
Application
This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah. The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The New Testament shifts the frame from a fixed percentage to cheerful, generous giving — God loves a cheerful giver — while keeping the duty to support ministry and the poor. Debated: some hold the fixed tenth continues as the giving standard; others hold the New Testament replaces the percentage with cheerful, generous giving (2 Corinthians 9:7) while keeping support for ministry and the poor.

Purpose
To support the Levites, the poor, and worship (Numbers 18; Deuteronomy 14). Debated: some hold the fixed tenth continues as the giving standard; others hold the New Testament replaces the percentage with cheerful, generous giving (2 Corinthians 9:7) while keeping support for ministry and the poor.
