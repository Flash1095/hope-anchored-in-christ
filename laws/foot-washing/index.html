Foot Washing

Command
Foot Washing
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
The basin and towel: Jesus washed his disciples' feet and told them to do as he had done to them. HELD ITEM, drafted on its merits: the borderline doubt is carried here, not resolved — some churches practice foot washing as an ordinance, most treat it as a model of humble service.
Commandment
Wash one another's feet, just as Christ has washed yours.
Scripture
John 13:12-17
Application
Jesus says 'ye also ought to wash one another's feet' and 'ye should do as I have done to you'. The example is certain; its standing as a required rite is the debate, and this row does not settle it.
