Separate the Army at the Water

Command
Separate the Army at the Water
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's command to Gideon: bring the people to the water and separate them by how they drink, keeping only the 300.
Commandment
Bring the people down unto the water, and I will try them for thee there; every one that lappeth of the water with his tongue, as a dog lappeth, him shalt thou set by himself.
Scripture
Judges 7:4-7
Application
A selection command, not a battle plan: the army was reduced from 32,000 to 10,000 to 300 by the water test (Judges 7:2-7), so that Israel could not claim its own hand had saved it. Kept as its own row because the imperative (bring them down to the water) is distinct from the go command; parent may fold it into the go row.
Related Topics
Faith; Obedience
Related Books
Judges
Related People
Gideon
Related Events
Gideon's Victory over Midian
Purpose
To reduce Gideon's army to 300 so Israel would know the LORD, not numbers, gave the victory (Judges 7:2, 7:7).
