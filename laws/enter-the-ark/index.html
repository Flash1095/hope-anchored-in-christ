Enter the Ark

Command
Enter the Ark
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's command to Noah when the ark was ready: come, with all his house, into the ark.
Commandment
Come thou and all thy house into the ark.
Scripture
Genesis 7:1
Application
A distinct imperative from building the ark: construction complete, the household is commanded inside, with the stated reason that Noah was seen righteous before God in his generation. Merge candidate: the parent may fold enter/leave into the build row as one ark episode; kept separate here under the one-row-per-distinct-command rule.
