Do not delay giving firstfruits

Command
Do not delay giving firstfruits
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: Do not delay giving firstfruits (Exodus 22:29). The first of the harvest was not to be held back while other produce was enjoyed.
Commandment
Do not delay giving firstfruits.
Scripture
Exodus 22:29
Application
This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah. The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as civil. Zacchaeus shows the spirit of restitution alive in the gospel: he repaid fourfold those he had defrauded. Justice restores, not merely punishes.

Purpose
The enduring duty behind this row is prompt giving: firstfruits were not to be held back. The firstfruits rite itself belonged to the land-and-temple system, while prompt, first-giving generosity endures.
