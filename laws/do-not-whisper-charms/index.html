Do not whisper charms

Command
Do not whisper charms
Category
Moral Law
Summary
One of the occult practices forbidden in Deuteronomy 18 and Leviticus 19: do not whisper charms. Israel was not to seek guidance from the dead or from hidden powers as the nations did.
Commandment
Do not whisper charms
Scripture
Deuteronomy 18:11
Application
Scripture's stated reason: for all that do these things are an abomination unto the LORD (Deuteronomy 18:12). God's people seek him alone for guidance, through his word and his prophets.
