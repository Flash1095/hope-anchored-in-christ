Examine the signs of fowl to distinguish clean from unclean

Command
Examine the signs of fowl to distinguish clean from unclean
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
Clean fowl are known by the signs Scripture gives: the unclean birds are listed by name, and all other fowl may be eaten.
Commandment
Examine the signs of fowl: eat only what is not among the unclean birds listed in Scripture.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 14:11
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Deuteronomy 14 repeats the Leviticus list for a new generation entering the land. The New Testament records these food distinctions set aside (Mark 7:19; Acts 10).
Related Topics
Purity; Holiness; Obedience
Related Books
Leviticus; Deuteronomy; Mark; Acts
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To mark Israel's table as distinct (Leviticus 11). The New Testament records the distinctions set aside: Jesus declares all foods clean (Mark 7:19) and Peter's vision confirms it (Acts 10).
