Repay five oxen for an ox stolen and slaughtered, four sheep for a sheep

Command
Repay five oxen for an ox stolen and slaughtered, four sheep for a sheep
Category
Civil Law
Summary
A thief who steals an ox and slaughters or sells it repays five oxen for the ox; for a sheep stolen and slaughtered or sold, four sheep.
Commandment
Repay five oxen for an ox stolen and slaughtered, and four sheep for a sheep.
Scripture
Exodus 22:1
Application
Theft that destroys the evidence or converts it to profit costs more than theft caught with the goods in hand. Zacchaeus shows restitution alive in the gospel (Luke 19:8): justice restores, and restoration is scaled to the wrong.
Related Topics
Justice; Money
Related Books
Exodus; Luke
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To make wrongs right: theft repaid, with a heavier scale where the stolen animal was slaughtered or sold.
Duration
For all time
