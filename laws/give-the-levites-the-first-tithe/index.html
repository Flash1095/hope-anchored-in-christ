Give the Levites the first tithe

The command: Give the Levites the first tithe (Numbers 18:21). The tithe of Israel's increase was given to the Levites for their service in the tent of meeting, in place of a land inheritance.

Commandment

Give the Levites the first tithe, as prescribed in Numbers 18:21.

Application

This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah. The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The New Testament shifts the frame from a fixed percentage to cheerful, generous giving — God loves a cheerful giver — while keeping the duty to support ministry and the poor. Debated: some hold the fixed tenth continues as the giving standard; others hold the New Testament replaces the percentage with cheerful, generous giving (2 Corinthians 9:7) while keeping support for ministry and the poor.

Purpose

To support the Levites, the poor, and worship (Numbers 18; Deuteronomy 14). Debated: some hold the fixed tenth continues as the giving standard; others hold the New Testament replaces the percentage with cheerful, generous giving (2 Corinthians 9:7) while keeping support for ministry and the poor. The site does not settle the percentage.

Function

The tithe of Israel's increase was given to the Levites for their service in the tent of meeting, in place of a land inheritance — a ceremonial law of support. Its goal was to sustain the tribe that had no fields of its own, so the men who served God could live by that service. It did not address a particular evil; its work was provision, not restraint. The tenth paid for the Levites' lives, and the people who gave it kept the worship of God staffed and fed.
