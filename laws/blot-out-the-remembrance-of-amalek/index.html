Blot out the remembrance of Amalek

Deuteronomy 25:19 commands blotting out Amalek's remembrance from under heaven, once Israel has rest in the land. The attacker of the weak was not to be left standing as a power.

Commandment

Blot out the remembrance of Amalek from under heaven.

Application

Scripture's stated context: Amalek met Israel by the way and smote the hindmost of thee (Deuteronomy 25:18). Debated: Amalek as a nation is gone, so how this command applies today is genuinely disputed — this row does not settle it.

Purpose

Scripture's stated context: Amalek met Israel by the way and smote the hindmost, even all that were feeble behind thee, when thou wast faint and weary; and he feared not God (Deuteronomy 25:18).

Function

This one belongs on the civil side of the Law: a rule for courts and neighbours, not for the altar.
