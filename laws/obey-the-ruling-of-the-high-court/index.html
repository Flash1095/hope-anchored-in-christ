Obey the ruling of the high court

Command
Obey the ruling of the high court
Category
Civil Law
Summary
When a matter is too hard for the local court, it goes up to the central court at the place God chooses, and its ruling is obeyed.
Commandment
Obey the ruling of the high court.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 17:10
Application
Hard cases had a final court, so that disputes did not fester or turn to feud. The central court belonged to Israel's polity; the enduring wisdom is that hard cases need a settled, lawful resolution rather than private force.
Related Topics
Justice; Authority
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: hard cases need a final, lawful resolution, so the central court's ruling binds — lest every dispute end in feud or private force.
