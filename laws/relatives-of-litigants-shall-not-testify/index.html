Relatives of litigants shall not testify

Command
Relatives of litigants shall not testify
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Fathers are not put to death for the children, nor children for the fathers: every man is put to death for his own sin, and no relative bears another's guilt in testimony or sentence.
Commandment
Relatives of litigants shall not testify.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 24:16
Application
The title follows the traditional mitzvah's form, which bars relatives from testifying in one another's cases; its ground is Deuteronomy 24:16 — guilt is individual, not inherited. A relative's testimony for or against kin is barred because affection and interest bend it, and because no man answers for another's sin.
Related Topics
Justice; Truth; Family
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: guilt is individual — every man for his own sin — so relatives, whose affection and interest bend testimony, do not testify in one another's cases (Deuteronomy 24:16).
