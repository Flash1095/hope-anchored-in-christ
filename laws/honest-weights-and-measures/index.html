Honest Weights and Measures

Command
Honest Weights and Measures
Category
Moral Law
Summary
The marketplace law: scales, weights, and measures must be true, because fraud in trade is theft disguised as business.
Commandment
Use honest scales, weights, and measures; do not keep a double standard, one for buying and one for selling.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:35-36; Deuteronomy 25:13-16; Proverbs 11:1
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as moral. Proverbs repeats the principle as wisdom: a false balance is an abomination to the LORD, but a just weight delights him.

Justice; Money; Truth
