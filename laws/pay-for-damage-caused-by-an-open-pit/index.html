Pay for damage caused by an open pit

Command
Pay for damage caused by an open pit
Category
Civil Law
Summary
If a man opens a pit or digs a pit and does not cover it, and an ox or ass falls in, the pit's owner pays for the animal, though the dead animal becomes his.
Commandment
Pay for damage caused by an open pit you have left uncovered.
Scripture
Exodus 21:33
Application
Negligence has a price: leaving a hazard open on your ground makes you liable for what falls into it. The rule still teaches that those who create a danger must bear the cost of it.
Related Topics
Justice; Money
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To make wrongs right: the one who left a hazard uncovered repays the loss it caused.
Duration
For all time
