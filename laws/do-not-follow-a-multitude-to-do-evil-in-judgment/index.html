Do not follow a multitude to do evil in judgment

Do not follow a multitude to do evil, and do not slant your testimony in a cause to follow a crowd and wrest judgment.

Commandment

Do not follow a multitude to do evil in judgment.

Application

A crowd is not evidence, and a cause is not decided by how many voices shout for it. The New Testament repeats the impartiality: show no partiality (James 2:9), and judge with right judgment, not by appearances (John 7:24).

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: the multitude can carry a court away from the truth, and the law guards the lone witness and the unpopular party against that pressure. Scripture states the prohibition without a separate reason (Exodus 23:2).

Function

Civil through and through: the Law governing a community here, not conducting worship.
