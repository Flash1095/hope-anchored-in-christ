Do not sow your field with mixed seed

Command
Do not sow your field with mixed seed
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Do not sow your field with mingled seed, nor your vineyard with divers seeds: each kind is sown by itself.
Commandment
Do not sow your field with mixed seed.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 22:9
Application
The agricultural mixture law draws a boundary in the field itself: kinds are kept distinct in sowing. These are chukim — statutes whose reason Scripture does not explain, and which tradition has long read as teaching that God may set boundaries whose wisdom is his own.
Related Topics
Holiness; Obedience
Related Books
Leviticus; Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: no reason is stated in Scripture; the law is classed among the chukim, statutes whose reason is not given. Deuteronomy 22:9 adds a consequence — the fruit is forfeited — but not a reason.
