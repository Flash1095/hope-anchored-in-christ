Do not retrieve the forgotten sheaf

When you reap your harvest and forget a sheaf in the field, do not go back to fetch it: the forgotten sheaf stays where it fell.

Commandment

Do not retrieve the forgotten sheaf.

Application

Forgetfulness becomes provision: the sheaf left behind by accident is not recovered by the owner but left for the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow. The law turns a mistake into charity without the owner having to decide to be generous.

Purpose

God's stated care for those without a defender — widow, orphan, stranger — carried by James into the church as pure religion (James 1:27). Here that care takes the form of a forgotten sheaf left in the field.

Function

When you reap your harvest and forget a sheaf in the field, do not go back to fetch it. A civil law: it governed life together in Israel — courts, property, neighbours — not worship.
