Compensate for loss and healing after injury

When one person injures another in a quarrel and the victim is confined but recovers, the one who struck him must pay for the lost time and for the healing.

Commandment

Compensate the injured person for his loss of time and for his healing.

Application

Justice here is restorative, not merely punitive: the offender bears the real costs his violence caused. The same duty lives on wherever injury is done — make the victim whole as far as payment can.

Purpose

To make wrongs right: the injured party is repaid for what the injury actually cost him, in lost time and in healing.

Function

When one person injures another in a quarrel and the victim is confined but recovers. This one belongs on the civil side of the Law: a rule for courts and neighbours, not for the altar.
