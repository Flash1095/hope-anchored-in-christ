Daughters inherit when there is no son

Command
Daughters inherit when there is no son
Category
Civil Law
Summary
If a man dies and has no son, his inheritance passes to his daughter: daughters inherit where there is no son.
Commandment
Daughters inherit when there is no son.
Scripture
Numbers 27:8
Application
The daughters of Zelophehad set the precedent: their appeal changed the law itself, showing God's law making room for justice within its own framework. Inheritance law protected the family's footing, and a daughter without brothers was not to be left landless.
Related Topics
Family; Justice
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To keep family land within the tribe and provide for daughters where there was no son (Numbers 27; 36). Here that purpose takes the form of daughters inheriting where there is no son.
