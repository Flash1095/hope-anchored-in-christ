Execute by stoning where the Law decrees stoning

Command
Execute by stoning where the Law decrees stoning
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Where the Law decrees death by stoning — as for the stubborn and rebellious son and other capital offences — the court was to carry out that sentence by stoning.
Commandment
Execute by stoning where the Law decrees stoning.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 21:22
Application
Stoning is one of the four modes of execution the Law assigns to the court, and the community itself took part in it. The mode belonged to Israel's court system, which no longer functions; the offences it marked still teach the gravity of rebellion against God's order.
Related Topics
Justice
Related Books

Related People

Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: each mode of execution matched the offence as the Law assigned it, so that punishment was measured rather than arbitrary.
