Fear your mother and father

Leviticus 19:3 opens the holiness chapter with two duties side by side: 'Ye shall fear every man his mother, and his father, and keep my sabbaths: I am the LORD.' Fear here is reverent honour, paired with keeping the Sabbath as the mark of a household ordered by God.

Commandment

Fear — that is, reverently honour — your mother and your father.

Application

Honour for parents is the first school of honour for God: a household where parents are feared in this reverent sense is a household learning to fear the LORD. The fifth commandment carries the same duty (Exodus 20:12).

Purpose

Scripture's stated context: the duty stands beside Sabbath-keeping under the seal 'I am the LORD' (Leviticus 19:3).

Function

Moral law, plain and simple: it binds wherever people live, in any age. Leviticus 19:3 opens the holiness chapter with two duties side by side. Scripture gives the context: the duty stands beside Sabbath-keeping under the seal 'I am the LORD'. This is how the line held. Honour for parents is the first school of honour for God.
