Go, But Speak Only My Word

God's command to Balaam: he may go with Balak's men, but only the word God speaks may he speak.

Commandment

If the men come to call thee, rise up, and go with them; but yet the word which I shall say unto thee, that shalt thou do.

Application

A conditional permission framed as command: Balaam might go, but only the word God gave him might he speak (repeated in Numbers 22:35). Balaam's later counsel against Israel (Numbers 31:16) shows the leash, not the license, was the point.

Purpose

To let Balaam go to Balak while keeping his mouth wholly under God's word, so blessing, not curse, came to Israel (Numbers 23:20).

Function

A conditional permission framed as command: Balaam might go, but only the word God gave him might he speak. A command for one moment only, so the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not apply. Its goal was to let Balaam go to Balak while keeping his mouth wholly under God's word, so blessing, not curse, came to Israel. The evil it addressed was a curse bought for hire. It restrained that evil by the leash itself: go, but speak only my word.
