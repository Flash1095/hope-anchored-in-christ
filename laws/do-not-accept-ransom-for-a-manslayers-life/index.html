Do not accept ransom for a manslayer's life

Command
Do not accept ransom for a manslayer's life
Category
Civil Law
Summary
No ransom may be taken for the life of a murderer who is guilty of death: he shall surely be put to death, and money cannot buy back a life deliberately taken.
Commandment
Do not accept ransom for a manslayer's life.
Scripture
Numbers 35:31
Application
Life deliberately taken is not a debt that money can settle. The ban on ransom marks murder as different in kind from property wrongs, where repayment can make things right.
Related Topics
Justice; Truth
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To balance life's sacredness with intent: the unintentional killer fled to a city and stood trial, safe from vengeance (Numbers 35). Here that balance forbids ransoming a murderer's life.
