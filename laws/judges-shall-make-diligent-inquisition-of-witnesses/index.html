Judges shall make diligent inquisition of witnesses

The judges must make diligent inquisition: when a witness is accused of falsehood, the court investigates carefully before it judges.

Commandment

Judges shall make diligent inquisition of witnesses.

Application

A court that takes testimony at face value invites false witnesses to prosper. Diligent inquisition — careful, sceptical investigation — is the court's duty, and it remains the standard of any fair hearing today.

Purpose

To secure impartial justice: no bribes, no partiality, no conviction on one witness. Here that purpose takes the form of diligent inquisition before judgment.

Function

This is community law, not temple law: it tells Israel how to live together justly.
