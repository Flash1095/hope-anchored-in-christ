The court shall count seven sabbaths of years

Command
The court shall count seven sabbaths of years
Category
Civil Law
Summary
The court counts seven sabbaths of years — seven times seven years, forty-nine years — to fix the Jubilee.
Commandment
The court shall count seven sabbaths of years.
Scripture
Leviticus 25:8
Application
Jubilee is not guessed at but counted: the court's count of seven sabbaths of years makes the fiftieth year a public, dated fact, proclaimed to all. Orderly counting keeps release from depending on anyone's memory or favour.
Related Topics
Justice; Obedience
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To prevent permanent destitution and permanent debt in Israel (Leviticus 25; Deuteronomy 15). The counting of seven sabbaths of years fixes the Jubilee as a public date, not a private guess.
