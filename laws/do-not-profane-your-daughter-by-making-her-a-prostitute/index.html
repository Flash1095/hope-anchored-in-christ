Do not profane your daughter by making her a prostitute

Command
Do not profane your daughter by making her a prostitute
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Leviticus 19:29 forbids a father from profaning his daughter by making her a harlot, 'lest the land fall to whoredom, and the land become full of wickedness.' The daughter's body is not her father's to trade.
Commandment
Do not profane your daughter by making her a prostitute.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:29
Application
The command protects daughters from being used by their own households, and warns that private exploitation corrupts the whole land. Parents guard their children's honour; they do not sell it.
