Do not eat flesh with its blood

Command
Do not eat flesh with its blood
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
Flesh may not be eaten with its blood in it; the blood must be drained, for the life is in the blood.
Commandment
Do not eat flesh with the blood.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:26
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Leviticus 19:26 forbids eating with the blood in the context of pagan practices, but the blood rule itself rests on the life in the blood (Leviticus 17:11) and is repeated for Gentile believers at Jerusalem (Acts 15:29).
Related Topics
Purity; Holiness; Obedience
Related Books
Leviticus; Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated reason: for the life of the flesh is in the blood, and it is given upon the altar to make atonement (Leviticus 17:11).
Duration
For all time
