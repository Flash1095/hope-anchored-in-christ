Do not eat or drink on the Day of Atonement

Command
Do not eat or drink on the Day of Atonement
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
On the Day of Atonement, no food or drink may be taken; the fast is the form of afflicting the soul.
Commandment
On the Day of Atonement, eat no food and drink nothing.
Scripture
Leviticus 23:29
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The fast made the day's humbling bodily and total, matching the solemnity of atonement. Debated: some see the commanded observance fulfilled as shadow (Colossians 2:16-17); others keep feasts voluntarily as memorial, not as binding command. The site does not settle whether observance continues.
Related Topics
Worship; Sacrifice; Covenant
Related Books
Leviticus; 1 Corinthians
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To rehearse God's saving acts at appointed seasons (Leviticus 23); Paul calls Christ our Passover (1 Corinthians 5:7).
