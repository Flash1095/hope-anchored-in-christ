The 613 Mitzvot: A Rabbinic Enumeration
Category
Overview
Summary
The tradition that the Torah contains 613 commandments — 248 positive and 365 negative — first systematized by medieval rabbis, not stated anywhere in Scripture itself.
Commandment
The Torah's commandments are traditionally counted as 613 in total.
Scripture
Exodus 20:1-17; Deuteronomy 6:1; Galatians 3:10-14
Application
Scripture never gives a count of its own commandments; the 613 figure comes from rabbinic tradition (traced to Rabbi Simlai and codified by Maimonides), and different rabbinic lists disagree on details. It is a study aid, not a biblical census. Christians also differ on how the Mosaic law applies today — fulfilled in Christ, yet its moral substance enduring — which is why this collection treats the traditional moral/ceremonial/civil division as traditional, not as Scripture's own. A full 613-entry draft is a separate future decision, not attempted here.
