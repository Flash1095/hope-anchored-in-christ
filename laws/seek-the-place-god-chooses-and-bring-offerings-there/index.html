Seek the place God chooses and bring offerings there

The command: Seek the place God chooses and bring offerings there (Deuteronomy 12:5). Israel was to seek the place where the LORD put his name and bring all offerings there, rather than worshipping on every high hill.

Commandment

Seek the place God chooses and bring offerings there, as prescribed in Deuteronomy 12:5.

Application

It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.

Purpose

Scripture's stated ground: the LORD chose one place to put his name, and there Israel was to bring all offerings (Deuteronomy 12:5-6).

Function

Israel was to seek the place where the LORD put his name and bring all offerings there, rather than worshipping on every high hill — a ceremonial law of one place. Its goal was to gather all worship to the one sanctuary God chose, so the offerings came to him and not to every hilltop. The evil was scattered worship, altars on every high place pulling the people apart. It restrained that by the seeking itself: one place, one altar, and every offering brought there.
