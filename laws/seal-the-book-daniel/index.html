Seal the Book

God's command to Daniel at the end of his visions: shut up the words and seal the book to the time of the end.

Commandment

Shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end.

Application

A closing command to the aged prophet: seal the book until the time of the end (Daniel 12:4, repeated in 12:9). Its time limit is part of the command itself — sealed, not suppressed forever, with knowledge to increase at the end.

Purpose

To preserve Daniel's vision sealed for the time of the end, when many shall run to and fro and knowledge shall be increased (Daniel 12:4).

Function

A closing command to the aged prophet: shut up the words and seal the book to the time of the end. A command for one moment only, so the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not apply. Its goal was to preserve Daniel's vision sealed for the time of the end, when many shall run to and fro and knowledge shall be increased. The evil or restraint angle does not apply here; this was preservation, not restraint. Sealed, not suppressed forever — with knowledge to increase at the end.
