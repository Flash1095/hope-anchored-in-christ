Slaughter animals for food as prescribed

Command
Slaughter animals for food as prescribed
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
Animals slaughtered for food away from the sanctuary must be killed as the LORD commanded, so that ordinary meat-eating stays within God's order.
Commandment
Slaughter animals for food as the LORD has commanded, when you eat meat away from the sanctuary.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 12:21
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Deuteronomy 12 allowed slaughter at home in the land, once Israel lived too far from the sanctuary for every meal to come from it — freedom within God's prescribed order, not lawlessness.
Related Topics
Purity; Holiness
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: the prescribed slaughter kept even ordinary meat-eating holy and distinct, with the blood duly dealt with (Deuteronomy 12:23-24).
