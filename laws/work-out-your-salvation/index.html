Work Out Your Salvation with Fear and Trembling

Command
Work Out Your Salvation with Fear and Trembling
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's working-out command: work out your own salvation with fear and trembling, for it is God who works in you both to will and to do.
Commandment
Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling. For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do of his good pleasure.
Scripture
Philippians 2:12-13
Application
The paradox is the point: human working-out rests on divine working-in. Fear and trembling are reverence before a holy God, not anxiety about whether grace will hold.
Related Topics
Faith; Obedience; Holiness
Related Books
Philippians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'for it is God which worketh in you' (Philippians 2:13).
