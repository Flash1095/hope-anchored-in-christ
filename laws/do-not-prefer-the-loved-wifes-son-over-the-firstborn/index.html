Do not prefer the loved wife's son over the firstborn

Command
Do not prefer the loved wife's son over the firstborn
Category
Civil Law
Summary
A father with two wives must not prefer the loved wife's son over the firstborn son of the hated wife when he divides the inheritance.
Commandment
Do not prefer the loved wife's son over the firstborn.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 21:16
Application
Favouritism in the household must not rewrite inheritance: birthright is not a prize for being loved. The rule protected the unloved wife's son from a father's affection becoming another child's disinheritance.
Related Topics
Family; Justice
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To keep family land within the tribe and provide for daughters where there was no son (Numbers 27; 36). Here that purpose takes the form of birthright protected from household favouritism.
