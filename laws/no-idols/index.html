No Idols

Command
No Idols
Category
Ten Commandments
Summary
The second commandment: God's people must not make or worship carved images or any likeness as a god.
Commandment
Do not make or worship an idol or any image as a god.
Scripture
Exodus 20:4-6; Deuteronomy 5:8-10
Application
God cannot be captured in an image made by human hands. The New Testament extends the warning inward: covetousness itself is idolatry (Colossians 3:5).
Related Topics
Idolatry; Worship
Related Books
Exodus; Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Related Events
The Ten Commandments
Purpose
Scripture states the reason in the command itself: God is a jealous God who shows mercy to those who love him and keep his commandments (Exodus 20:5-6). No image may stand in for him.
Duration
For all time
