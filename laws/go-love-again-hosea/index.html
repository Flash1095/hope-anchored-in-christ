Go, Love Again

Command
Go, Love Again
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's second marriage command to Hosea: go yet, love a woman beloved yet adulterous, as the LORD loves Israel.
Commandment
Go yet, love a woman beloved of her friend, yet an adulteress, according to the love of the LORD toward the children of Israel.
Scripture
Hosea 3:1
Application
The second Hosea marriage command (Hosea 3:1): love again a woman beloved yet adulterous, according to the LORD's love for Israel though they look to other gods. Same merge caveat as the chapter 1 row.
Related Topics
Prophecy; Covenant; Love
Related Books
Hosea
Related People
Hosea
Purpose
To enact the LORD's redeeming love for an unfaithful Israel, bought back and kept (Hosea 3:2-3).
