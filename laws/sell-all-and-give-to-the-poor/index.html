Sell All and Give to the Poor

Command
Sell All and Give to the Poor
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' word to one rich man: sell all you have, give to the poor, and come follow me. HELD ITEM, drafted on its merits: the borderline doubt is carried here, not resolved — spoken to one rich man in one conversation, whether it is a universal command or a personal call exposing that man's idol is debated.
Commandment
Sell all you have, give to the poor, and come follow Christ.
Scripture
Matthew 19:16-22; Mark 10:17-22; Luke 18:18-23
Application
The man went away sorrowful, for he had great possessions. The story exposes wealth as a rival god, and the call to follow as costlier than rule-keeping. As a held item it is published here as a person-specific word whose universal scope is debated (see Duration), not as a settled universal law.
Related Topics
Money; Discipleship; Obedience
Related Books
Matthew; Mark; Luke
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to expose the one idol that kept this man from following — though whether every believer must literally sell all is the debated point, and this row does not settle it.
