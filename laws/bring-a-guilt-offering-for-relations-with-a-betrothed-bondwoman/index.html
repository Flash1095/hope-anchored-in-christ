Bring a guilt offering for relations with a betrothed bondwoman

Command
Bring a guilt offering for relations with a betrothed bondwoman
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: Bring a guilt offering for relations with a betrothed bondwoman (Leviticus 19:22). A man who lay with a bondwoman betrothed to another brought a ram as a trespass offering, and the woman was scourged rather than put to death.
Commandment
Bring a guilt offering for relations with a betrothed bondwoman, as prescribed in Leviticus 19:22.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:22
Application
It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.
Related Topics
Sacrifice; Atonement; Forgiveness
Related Books
Leviticus; Hebrews
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: the offering made atonement where the death penalty did not apply, because the woman was not free; guilt still required blood and restitution.
