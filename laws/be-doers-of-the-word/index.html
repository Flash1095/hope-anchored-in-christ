Be Doers of the Word

Command
Be Doers of the Word
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
James's hearing command: be doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves.
Commandment
But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves.
Scripture
James 1:22
Application
The hearer-only is pictured as a man who looks in a mirror and forgets his face: hearing without doing is self-deception, not partial obedience.
Related Topics
Obedience; Faith; Wisdom
Related Books
James
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: the hearer-only 'deceiveth his own self' (James 1:22), like one who forgets his face (James 1:24).
Duration
For all time
