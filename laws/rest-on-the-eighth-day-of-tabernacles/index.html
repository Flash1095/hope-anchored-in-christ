Rest on the eighth day of Tabernacles

Command
Rest on the eighth day of Tabernacles
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The eighth day after Tabernacles is kept as a holy convocation of rest, a solemn assembly of its own.
Commandment
Keep the eighth day as a holy convocation and rest on it.
Scripture
Leviticus 23:36
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The closing rest gathered the whole feast season to its end in worship. Debated: some see the commanded observance fulfilled as shadow (Colossians 2:16-17); others keep feasts voluntarily as memorial, not as binding command. The site does not settle whether observance continues.
Related Topics
Worship; Sacrifice; Covenant
Related Books
Leviticus; 1 Corinthians
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To rehearse God's saving acts at appointed seasons (Leviticus 23); Paul calls Christ our Passover (1 Corinthians 5:7).
