Wait for the Promise of the Father

Command
Wait for the Promise of the Father
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
The risen Christ's one-time command to the apostles: tarry in Jerusalem until you are endued with power from on high. Drafted with its scope stated: a specific waiting, completed at Pentecost.
Commandment
Tarry ye in the city of Jerusalem, until ye be endued with power from on high.
Scripture
Luke 24:49; Acts 1:4
Application
The apostles obeyed and waited in Jerusalem; the promise came at Pentecost (live story). It is not a standing command to wait in a city, but the historical hinge between the resurrection and the church's mission.
Related Topics
Faith; Obedience
Related Books

Related People
Jesus
Related Events

Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you' (Acts 1:8, given with the command).
