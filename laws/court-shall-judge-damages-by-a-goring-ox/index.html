Court shall judge damages by a goring ox

Command
Court shall judge damages by a goring ox
Category
Civil Law
Summary
When an ox gores a man or woman to death, the ox is to be stoned and the owner judged according to the case — acquitted if the ox had not been known to gore, liable if it had.
Commandment
The court shall judge damages by a goring ox according to whether the animal was known to be dangerous.
Scripture
Exodus 21:28
Application
Liability follows knowledge: an owner who knew his animal was dangerous and did not restrain it bears a heavier guilt than one taken by surprise. The same rule of known risk still shapes fair judgments about negligence today.
Related Topics
Justice; Truth
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To make wrongs right: the owner answers for the damage his animal causes, in proportion to what he knew and failed to prevent.
Duration
For all time
