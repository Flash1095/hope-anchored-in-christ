Do not worship the sun, moon, or host of heaven

Command
Do not worship the sun, moon, or host of heaven
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 4:19 warns Israel, when it lifts its eyes to heaven, not to be driven to worship the sun, the moon, or the host of heaven, 'which the LORD thy God hath divided unto all nations under the whole heaven.' The heavenly bodies are creatures given to all peoples, not gods for any people.
Commandment
Do not worship the sun, the moon, or the host of heaven.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 4:19
Application
The lights of heaven declare God's glory; they do not receive his worship. Whatever beauty or order creation shows, praise passes through it to the Creator.
Related Topics
Idolatry; Worship
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated reason: the heavenly bodies are divided unto all nations as creatures, not given to Israel as gods (Deuteronomy 4:19).
Duration
For all time
