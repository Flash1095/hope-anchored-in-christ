Do not leave any of the thanksgiving offering until morning

The thanksgiving peace offering was a shared holy meal; unlike other peace offerings, its flesh had to be eaten the same day, with nothing kept overnight.

Commandment

The flesh of the thanksgiving offering shall be eaten on the day it is offered; none of it shall be left until morning.

Application

Thanksgiving was not to be deferred or drawn out: the meal of gratitude belonged to the day of the offering, and holy food left overnight was not to be treated as common.

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: thanksgiving was to be completed promptly, the holy meal eaten the same day rather than left to spoil or treated as common.

Function

The flesh of the thanksgiving offering had to be eaten on the day it was offered; none of it was left until morning — a ceremonial law of prompt thanks. Its goal, traditionally, was to complete thanksgiving promptly, the holy meal eaten the same day rather than left to spoil or treated as common. The evil was deferred gratitude, the thanksgiving meal stretched into leftovers and the thanks with it. It restrained that by the deadline: the meal belonged to the day of the offering.
