Listen to the Prophet Like Moses

Command
Listen to the Prophet Like Moses
Category
Moral Law
Summary
The LORD thy God will raise up unto thee a Prophet from the midst of thee, of thy brethren, like unto me; unto him ye shall hearken. And whosoever will not hearken unto my words which he shall speak in my name, I will require it of him. This is the true-prophet promise, distinct from the false-prophet test drafted next.
Commandment
Listen to the prophet whom the LORD raises up like Moses; whoever will not listen shall answer for it.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 18:15-19
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as moral. The New Testament applies the promise directly to Jesus: Moses said, 'A prophet shall the Lord your God raise up unto you of your brethren, like unto me; him shall ye hear' — and Peter and Stephen both quote it of Christ (Acts 3:22-23; 7:37). Listening to the prophet like Moses therefore continues, fulfilled in listening to Christ.
Related Topics

Related Books

Related People

Purpose
Scripture's stated context: God answers Israel's fear at Horeb — 'let me not hear the voice of the LORD my God any more' — by promising a mediator-prophet they can hear (Deuteronomy 18:15-19).
Duration
