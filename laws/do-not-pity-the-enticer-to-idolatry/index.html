Do not pity the enticer to idolatry

Command
Do not pity the enticer to idolatry
Category
Moral Law
Summary
One of the Deuteronomy 13 safeguards against idolatry: do not pity the enticer to idolatry. The chapter treats enticement to idolatry as a public danger to the whole community, not a private opinion.
Commandment
Do not pity the enticer to idolatry
Scripture
Deuteronomy 13:8
Application
Scripture's stated reason: for the LORD your God proveth you, to know whether ye love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul (Deuteronomy 13:3). These civil penalties belonged to ancient Israel's theocracy; the refusal to follow idolatrous leading endures.
