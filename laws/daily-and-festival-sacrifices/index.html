Daily and Festival Sacrifices

Command
Daily and Festival Sacrifices
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The sacrificial system: burnt, grain, peace, sin, and guilt offerings, daily, weekly, and at the appointed feasts.
Commandment
Bring the appointed offerings at the appointed times, as the Law prescribes, for atonement, thanksgiving, and fellowship with God.
Scripture
Leviticus 1:1-7:38; Numbers 28:1-29:40; Hebrews 10:1-14
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Hebrews teaches these sacrifices could never perfect the worshiper; they pointed forward to Christ's once-for-all offering, which is why they ceased with the new covenant.
