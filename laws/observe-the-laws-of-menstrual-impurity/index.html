Observe the laws of menstrual impurity

Command
Observe the laws of menstrual impurity
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
A woman in her monthly separation is unclean seven days, and whoever touches her or what she lies or sits upon is unclean until the evening and must wash.
Commandment
Observe the laws of menstrual impurity: seven days of separation, with washing for any contact.
Scripture
Leviticus 15:19
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The seven-day separation ordered household life around the rhythm of uncleanness and return. Jesus' touch restored those the purity laws isolated (Luke 8:43-48).
Related Topics
Purity; Holiness
Related Books
Leviticus; Mark; Luke
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To order who could approach worship while ceremonially unclean and how they were restored. TRADITIONAL reading: with the temple order ended, these washings no longer bind believers; Jesus' touch restored those the laws isolated (Mark 1:40-45; Luke 8:43-48).
