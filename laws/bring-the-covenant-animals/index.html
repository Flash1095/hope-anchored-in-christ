Bring the Covenant Animals

God's command to Abram in the covenant ceremony: take a heifer, she goat, ram, turtledove, and young pigeon.

Commandment

Take me an heifer of three years old, and a she goat of three years old, and a ram of three years old, and a turtledove, and a young pigeon.

Application

A one-time ritual act: Abram brought and divided the animals, and God passed between the pieces (Genesis 15:17). No story title matches this ceremony, so RelatedEvents is left empty rather than linked to a wrong story.

Purpose

To furnish the covenant-making rite by which God bound himself to give Abram's seed the land (Genesis 15:17-18).

Function

A one-time ritual act: Abram brought the animals, divided them, and God passed between the pieces. A command for one moment only, so the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not apply. Its goal was to furnish the covenant-making rite by which God bound himself to give Abram's seed the land. The evil or restraint angle does not apply here; this was a covenant ceremony, not a law restraining a recurring evil. No story title matches this ceremony, so it stands on its own.
