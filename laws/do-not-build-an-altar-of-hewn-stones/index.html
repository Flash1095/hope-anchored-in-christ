Do not build an altar of hewn stones

Command
Do not build an altar of hewn stones
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: Do not build an altar of hewn stones (Exodus 20:25). An altar of earth or unhewn stone kept human tool-work off the place of sacrifice.
Commandment
Do not build an altar of hewn stones.
Scripture
Exodus 20:25
Application
It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.
