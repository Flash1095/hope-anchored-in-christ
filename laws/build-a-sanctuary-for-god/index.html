Build a sanctuary for God

The command: Build a sanctuary for God (Exodus 25:8). Israel was to make a sanctuary patterned on what Moses was shown on the mountain, so that God might dwell among them.

Commandment

Build a sanctuary for God, as prescribed in Exodus 25:8.

Application

This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah. The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Hebrews teaches the earthly tabernacle was a copy of the heavenly one, and Christ now serves as high priest in the true tabernacle.

Purpose

To give Israel a mediated approach to God through the tabernacle pattern (Exodus 25:40). Hebrews teaches the earthly copy gave way to Christ's priesthood in the true tabernacle (Hebrews 8:1-6).

Function

God told Israel to build him a sanctuary so he might dwell among them, and that dwelling-place is what makes this ceremonial: a rite of approach. Its goal was a mediated way to God — a tabernacle patterned on what Moses was shown on the mountain, where a holy God could live in the midst of his people. It did not address a particular evil; its work was worship and presence, not restraint. The sanctuary said God was not far off; he had pitched his tent in the camp.
