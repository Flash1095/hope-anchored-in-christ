Do not eat leaven during Passover

Command
Do not eat leaven during Passover
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
No leavened bread may be eaten during the Passover feast; only unleavened bread is eaten.
Commandment
Eat no leavened bread during the feast; eat unleavened bread instead.
Scripture
Exodus 13:3
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The prohibition is the negative side of the unleavened-bread memorial of the hasty departure from Egypt. Debated: some see the commanded observance fulfilled as shadow (Colossians 2:16-17); others keep feasts voluntarily as memorial, not as binding command. The site does not settle whether observance continues.
