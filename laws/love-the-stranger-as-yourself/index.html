Love the stranger as yourself

Command
Love the stranger as yourself
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Leviticus 19:34 extends neighbour-love to the stranger dwelling among Israel: 'thou shalt love him as thyself; for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt: I am the LORD your God.' Israel's own memory of Egypt is the ground of the duty.
Commandment
Love the stranger who dwells with you as yourself.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:34
Application
The church hears the same ground: those who were once strangers and aliens have been brought near (Ephesians 2:19), and love for the stranger is neighbour-love in its widest honest form.
