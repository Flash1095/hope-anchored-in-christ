Quench Not the Spirit; Despise Not Prophesyings

Command
Quench Not the Spirit; Despise Not Prophesyings
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's Spirit command: quench not the Spirit, and despise not prophesyings — the counterweight to the testing command, which follows it.
Commandment
Quench not the Spirit. Despise not prophesyings.
Scripture
1 Thessalonians 5:19-20
Application
Testing (the next verses) must not become suppression: the Spirit's work through prophecy is not to be snuffed out because discernment is required. Both commands stand together.
Related Topics
Discernment; Worship; Faith
Related Books
1 Thessalonians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to keep the church open to the Spirit's gifts while still testing them.
Duration
For all time
