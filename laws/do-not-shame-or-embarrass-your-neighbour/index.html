Do not shame or embarrass your neighbour

Command
Do not shame or embarrass your neighbour
Category
Moral Law
Summary
One of the neighbour-laws of Leviticus 19:17-18: do not shame or embarrass your neighbour, part of the passage that ends in 'thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.'
Commandment
Do not shame or embarrass your neighbour
Scripture
Leviticus 19:17
Application
Jesus joins this neighbour rule to love of God as the hinge of the Law (Matthew 22:39-40); love of neighbour is the measure of love of God in practice.
