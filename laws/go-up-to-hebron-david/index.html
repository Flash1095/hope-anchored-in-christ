Go Up to Hebron

God's answer to David's enquiry after Saul's death: go up to Hebron in Judah.

Commandment

Go up into one of the cities of Judah — unto Hebron.

Application

David enquired and God answered with a single direction; David went up to Hebron and was anointed king over Judah there (2 Samuel 2:4). A small command with a large consequence: the first step of David's kingship.

Purpose

To move David from exile into Judah, where he was anointed king over the house of Judah (2 Samuel 2:4).

Function

David enquired and God answered with a single direction: go up to Hebron in Judah. A command for one moment only, so the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not apply. Its goal was to move David from exile into Judah, where he was anointed king over the house of Judah. The evil or restraint angle does not apply here; this was one step on the road to kingship, not a law against a recurring evil. A small command with a large consequence: the first step of David's kingship.
