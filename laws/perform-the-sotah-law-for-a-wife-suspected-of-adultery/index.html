Perform the sotah law for a wife suspected of adultery

Numbers 5:30 gives the law of jealousies: when the spirit of jealousy comes upon a husband, he shall set his wife before the LORD, and the priest shall execute upon her all this law. Where no witness existed, the matter was put before God.

Commandment

Perform the sotah law for a wife suspected of adultery, setting her before the LORD.

Application

TRADITIONAL reading: the procedure gave a suspected wife a public, God-decided vindication instead of a husband's private vengeance. The procedure belonged to the tabernacle system and has passed with it.

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: the procedure gave a suspected wife a public, God-decided vindication instead of a husband's private vengeance. Scripture states the procedure without a stated reason beyond the spirit of jealousy coming upon the husband (Numbers 5:14,30).

Function

This is classed as a moral law: it names a duty of character for Israel's life under the Law. Numbers 5:30 gives the law of jealousies: when the spirit of jealousy comes upon a husband. TRADITIONAL reading — and I hold with it: the procedure gave a suspected wife a public, God-decided vindication instead of a husband's private vengeance. The duty had teeth behind it.
