Be Swift to Hear, Slow to Speak, Slow to Wrath

Command
Be Swift to Hear, Slow to Speak, Slow to Wrath
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
James's temper command: let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath, for the wrath of man works not the righteousness of God.
Commandment
Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath: for the wrath of man worketh not the righteousness of God.
Scripture
James 1:19-20
Application
The order matters: listening first slows both tongue and temper. Human wrath, however righteous it feels, does not produce God's righteousness.
Related Topics
Speech; Patience; Peace
Related Books
James
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'the wrath of man worketh not the righteousness of God' (James 1:20).
Duration
For all time
