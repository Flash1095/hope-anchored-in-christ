The New Commandment: Love One Another

Command
The New Commandment: Love One Another
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
The command Jesus gave on the night before the cross: love one another as he has loved — a love measured by his own self-giving.
Commandment
Love one another just as Christ has loved you; let this love mark you as his disciples.
Scripture
John 13:34-35; John 15:12-13; 1 John 3:16
Application
The commandment is called new not because love was new, but because its measure is new: as I have loved you, even to laying down life for friends.
Related Topics
Love; Discipleship; Jesus Christ
Related Books
John; 1 John
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
Measured by Christ's own self-giving love (John 13:34); the mark of discipleship (John 13:35).
Duration
For all time
