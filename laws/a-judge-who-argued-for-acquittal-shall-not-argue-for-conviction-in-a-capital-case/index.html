A judge who argued for acquittal shall not argue for conviction in a capital case

Command
A judge who argued for acquittal shall not argue for conviction in a capital case
Category
Civil Law
Summary
In a capital case, a judge who argued for acquittal must not later turn and argue for conviction on the same case.
Commandment
A judge who argued for acquittal shall not argue for conviction in a capital case.
Scripture
Exodus 23:2
Application
The rule leans the court toward life: once a judge has seen a ground for acquittal, he may not reverse himself to seek a death sentence. In capital cases, the court is guarded against its own momentum toward condemnation.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy; Truth
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: in a capital case the court is guarded against turning toward death, so a judge who argued for acquittal may not argue for conviction. Scripture states the prohibition without a separate reason (Exodus 23:2).
Duration
For all time
