The Levites shall have no inheritance portion in the land

The command: The Levites shall have no inheritance portion in the land (Deuteronomy 18:2). The LORD himself was the Levites' inheritance; they received no portion of land among their brethren.

Commandment

The Levites shall have no inheritance portion in the land.

Application

It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.

Purpose

Scripture's stated reason: the LORD is their inheritance, as he said unto them (Deuteronomy 18:2).

Function

The LORD himself was the Levites' inheritance; they received no portion of land among their brethren — a ceremonial law of a different inheritance. Its goal was to keep the Levites free for God's service, sustained by the tithes rather than tied to fields of their own. It did not address a particular evil; its work was provision and calling, not restraint. The Levites' portion was the LORD himself, and the land they did not own was the price of the service they did.
