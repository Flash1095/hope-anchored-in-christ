Do not pervert justice for the poor

Do not wrest the judgment of the poor in his cause: poverty must not lose a man his case.

Commandment

Do not pervert justice for the poor.

Application

The poor man has no patron and no bribe to offer, which is exactly why the Law guards his cause expressly. A court that bends against the poor because they are poor has perverted judgment itself.

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: the poor are the most exposed to bent judgment, so the Law names their cause expressly; God is no respecter of persons in judgment.

Function

Government, not worship: the Law here is running a nation. Do not wrest the judgment of the poor in his cause: poverty must not lose a man his case.
