March Around Jericho

Command
March Around Jericho
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's command for Jericho: compass the city once a day for six days, seven times on the seventh, with trumpets and a shout.
Commandment
Ye shall compass the city, all ye men of war, and go round about the city once. Thus shalt thou do six days; and on the seventh day ye shall compass the city seven times.
Scripture
Joshua 6:3-5
Application
Six days of single circuits, seven on the seventh day, trumpets and a shout (Joshua 6:4-5). The walls fell flat (6:20). A tactical absurdity commanded as obedience, and therefore the classic one-time command of the conquest.
Related Topics
Faith; Obedience

Related Events
The Walls of Jericho
Purpose
To take Jericho by obedient procession and shout rather than by siege craft, showing the LORD gave the city (Joshua 6:2, 6:16).
