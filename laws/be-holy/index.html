Be Holy

Command
Be Holy
Category
Moral Law
Summary
The holiness call: be holy, for the LORD your God is holy — the summons that opens the holiness code. Doubt: Umbrella call rather than a distinct command: it introduces the whole holiness code and could fold into the moral laws as a group instead of standing alone.
Commandment
Be holy, for the LORD your God is holy.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:2; 1 Peter 1:15-16
Application
Peter carries the same call into the church: as obedient children, be holy in all your conduct. Holiness here is both a gift and a command.
Related Topics
Holiness; Obedience
Related Books
Leviticus; 1 Peter
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated reason: because the LORD your God is holy (Leviticus 19:2); Peter reissues the same call to the church (1 Peter 1:15-16).
Duration
For all time
