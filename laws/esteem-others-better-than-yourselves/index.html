Esteem Others Better Than Yourselves

Command
Esteem Others Better Than Yourselves
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's humility command: let nothing be done through strife or vainglory, but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves, with the mind that was in Christ Jesus.
Commandment
Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves. Let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus.
Scripture
Philippians 2:3-5
Application
The mind of Christ is then spelled out in self-emptying: form of God, form of servant, death on a cross. Esteeming others better is not flattery but the mind that took the lowest place.
Related Topics
Humility; Unity; Love
Related Books
Philippians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: Christ 'made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant' (Philippians 2:7).
Duration
For all time
