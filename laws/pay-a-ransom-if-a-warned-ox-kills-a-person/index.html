Pay a ransom if a warned ox kills a person

Command
Pay a ransom if a warned ox kills a person
Category
Civil Law
Summary
If an ox known to gore has been warned about and its owner failed to keep it in, and it kills a person, the owner pays a ransom for his life as the court lays upon him.
Commandment
Pay a ransom if a warned ox kills a person, as the court determines.
Scripture
Exodus 21:30
Application
Known danger ignored becomes personal responsibility: the owner who was warned and did nothing cannot plead accident. The ransom marks both the value of the life lost and the owner's culpable negligence.
Related Topics
Justice; Money
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To make wrongs right: a warned owner who failed to restrain a dangerous animal answers with a ransom for the life lost.
Duration
For all time
