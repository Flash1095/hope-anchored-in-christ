Let the Little Children Come to Me

Command
Let the Little Children Come to Me
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' welcome command: suffer the little children to come unto him and forbid them not, for of such is the kingdom of God.
Commandment
Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God.
Scripture
Mark 10:14-15; Matthew 19:14; Luke 18:16
Application
The disciples were rebuking the children as an interruption; Jesus rebukes the rebukers. Receiving the kingdom as a little child — trust without status — is the condition of entering it.
Related Topics
Humility; Faith; Kingdom of God
Related Books
Mark; Matthew; Luke
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'for of such is the kingdom of God' (Mark 10:14).
Duration
For all time
