Do not intermediate, guarantee, witness, or write a note for an interest loan

Command
Do not intermediate, guarantee, witness, or write a note for an interest loan
Category
Civil Law
Summary
No one may act as intermediary, guarantor, witness, or scribe of a note for a loan on interest between brothers — the whole apparatus of usury is forbidden, not only the lender's hand.
Commandment
Do not intermediate, guarantee, witness, or write a note for an interest loan between brothers.
Scripture
Exodus 22:25
Application
The ban reaches everyone who would make an unlawful loan work: the middleman, the guarantor, the witness, and the clerk. A wrong is not made right by doing only a small part of it.
Related Topics
Justice; Money; Truth
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To make wrongs right: the prohibition on interest to a brother binds every party who enables the loan, not only the lender.
Duration
For all time
