Anoint Hazael, Jehu, and Elisha

Command
Anoint Hazael, Jehu, and Elisha
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's command to Elijah at Horeb: return by the wilderness of Damascus and anoint Hazael, Jehu, and Elisha.
Commandment
Go, return on thy way to the wilderness of Damascus: and when thou comest, anoint Hazael to be king over Syria, and Jehu to be king over Israel, and Elisha to be prophet in thy room.
Scripture
1 Kings 19:15-16
Application
One commission, three anointings: Hazael over Syria, Jehu over Israel, Elisha as prophet in Elijah's room (1 Kings 19:16). Elijah anointed Elisha directly (19:19-21); Hazael and Jehu were anointed through Elisha's ministry (2 Kings 8:13; 9:3, 6). Rowed as one commission because it is one speech.
Related Topics
Prophecy; Obedience; Judgment
Related Books
1 Kings
Related People
Elijah; Elisha
Purpose
To raise the instruments of judgment and succession after Carmel and Horeb: kings who would cut down Baal's house, and a prophet to follow Elijah (1 Kings 19:17-18).
Duration
One-time
