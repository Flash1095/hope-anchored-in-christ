Walk Before God and Be Blameless

Command
Walk Before God and Be Blameless
Category
Pre-Mosaic Commands
Summary
The covenant walk command to Abram: walk before God and be perfect, given as the covenant of circumcision is established.
Commandment
Walk before me, and be thou perfect.
Scripture
Genesis 17:1
Application
A broad covenant call rather than a single act, and it should be read that way: it is the posture under which the circumcision covenant (rowed separately) was given. Kept as its own row because Genesis 17:1 states it as a distinct imperative and it is echoed across Scripture as the walk of faith, but the parent may fold it into the circumcision row if umbrella rows are cut.
Related Topics
Covenant; Holiness; Faith
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Abraham
Related Events
The Covenant of Circumcision
Purpose
To live the covenant life openly before God in integrity; Genesis 17:1 makes it the condition and character of the covenant that follows, not an optional extra.
Duration
For all time
