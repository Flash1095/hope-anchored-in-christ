Turn the Other Cheek and Go the Second Mile

Command
Turn the Other Cheek and Go the Second Mile
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' non-retaliation command: do not repay evil for evil; offer the other cheek, give the cloak as well, and walk the second mile.
Commandment
Do not resist evil with retaliation: if struck on one cheek, turn the other; if sued for your coat, give your cloak also; if compelled one mile, go two.
Scripture
Matthew 5:38-42; Luke 6:29-30
Application
Each example refuses the cycle of revenge while still naming the wrong. It is personal non-retaliation, not a ban on lawful justice or on protecting others.
Related Topics
Mercy; Justice; Love
Related Books
Matthew; Luke
Related People
Jesus
Related Events
The Sermon on the Mount
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to break the cycle of revenge and display the character of the kingdom rather than the honour code of the world.
Duration
For all time
