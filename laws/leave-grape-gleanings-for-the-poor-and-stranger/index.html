Leave grape gleanings for the poor and stranger

Command
Leave grape gleanings for the poor and stranger
Category
Civil Law
Summary
The gleanings of the vineyard are left for the poor and the stranger, who may gather them for themselves.
Commandment
Leave grape gleanings for the poor and stranger.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:10
Application
The positive form of the gleaning law: the vineyard's remainder is not waste but provision, deliberately left where the landless can gather it with dignity, by their own hands.
Related Topics
Mercy; Generosity
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
God's stated care for those without a defender — widow, orphan, stranger — carried by James into the church as pure religion (James 1:27). In the vineyard, that care takes the form of leaving the gleanings for the poor and stranger.
Duration
For all time
