Let Your Speech Be Seasoned with Salt

Command
Let Your Speech Be Seasoned with Salt
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's gracious-speech command: let your speech be always with grace, seasoned with salt, that you may know how you ought to answer every man.
Commandment
Let your speech be alway with grace, seasoned with salt, that ye may know how ye ought to answer every man.
Scripture
Colossians 4:6
Application
Grace is the flavour, salt the preservative and bite: speech to outsiders must be neither flattering nor caustic, but seasoned so that it preserves truth and answers each person fittingly.
Related Topics
Speech; Wisdom; Truth
Related Books
Colossians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'that ye may know how ye ought to answer every man' (Colossians 4:6).
