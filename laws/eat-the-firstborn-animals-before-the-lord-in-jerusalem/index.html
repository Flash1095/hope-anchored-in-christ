Eat the firstborn animals before the LORD in Jerusalem

Command
Eat the firstborn animals before the LORD in Jerusalem
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: Eat the firstborn animals before the LORD in Jerusalem (Deuteronomy 15:20). The firstling of herd and flock, if without blemish, was eaten year by year before the LORD in the place he chose.
Commandment
Eat the firstborn animals before the LORD in Jerusalem, as prescribed in Deuteronomy 15:20.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 15:20
Application
It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.
Related Topics
Temple; Redemption
