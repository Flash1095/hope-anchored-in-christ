Put On the Lord Jesus Christ

Paul's clothing command: put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make not provision for the flesh, to fulfil the lusts thereof.

Commandment

But put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make not provision for the flesh, to fulfil the lusts thereof.

Application

The night is far spent and the day at hand: the works of darkness are cast off, and Christ himself is put on like a garment. Making provision for the flesh — planning for indulgence — is the thing forbidden.

Purpose

Scripture's own reason: 'the night is far spent, the day is at hand' (Romans 13:12).

Function

The night is far spent, the day is at hand — and Christ himself is put on like a garment. An apostolic command, not a Mosaic one — the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not sort it. Its goal was a life clothed in Christ, with the works of darkness cast off. The evil it addressed was making provision for the flesh — planning for indulgence while professing the dawn. It restrained that evil by the clothing itself: put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make not provision for the flesh, to fulfil the lusts thereof.
