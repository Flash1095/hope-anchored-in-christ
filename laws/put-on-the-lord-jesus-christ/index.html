Put On the Lord Jesus Christ

Command
Put On the Lord Jesus Christ
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's clothing command: put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make not provision for the flesh, to fulfil the lusts thereof.
Commandment
But put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make not provision for the flesh, to fulfil the lusts thereof.
Scripture
Romans 13:14
Application
The night is far spent and the day at hand: the works of darkness are cast off, and Christ himself is put on like a garment. Making provision for the flesh — planning for indulgence — is the thing forbidden.
Related Topics
Holiness; Sanctification; Temptation
Related Books
Romans
Related People
Paul
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'the night is far spent, the day is at hand' (Romans 13:12).
Duration
