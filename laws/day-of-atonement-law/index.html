The Day of Atonement

Command
The Day of Atonement
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The annual fast and cleansing: on the tenth day of the seventh month the high priest entered the Most Holy Place with blood for the sins of the people.
Commandment
On the Day of Atonement, afflict yourselves, do no work, and let the high priest make atonement for the people.
Scripture
Leviticus 16:1-34; Hebrews 9:11-14
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Hebrews reads the Day as a shadow: Christ entered the true Most Holy Place with his own blood, securing an eternal redemption the yearly rite could only picture. (Distinct from the Jewish Cultural Backgrounds entry on the Day of Atonement as a feast; this entry covers the commanded rite as law.)
