Do not lie to one another

Do not lie to one another: speech between neighbours must be truthful.

Commandment

Do not lie to one another.

Application

Lying breaks the shared ground on which neighbours settle disputes, trade, and trust. The command is general — it binds every neighbourly word, not only testimony in court.

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: truthful speech is the ground of neighbourly trust; a community of lies cannot judge, trade, or live together justly.

Function

The Law as civil government: setting the terms for neighbours dealing with each other. Do not lie to one another: speech between neighbours must be truthful.
