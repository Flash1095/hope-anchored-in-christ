Come, Take My Yoke, and Learn from Me

Command
Come, Take My Yoke, and Learn from Me
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' invitation-command to the weary: come unto me, take my yoke upon you, and learn of me, for I am meek and lowly in heart.
Commandment
Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart.
Scripture
Matthew 11:28-30
Application
Distinct from the repent-and-believe row as a discipleship word: the yoke is not removed but exchanged — his yoke is easy, his burden light — and learning happens under it, from a meek Master.
Related Topics
Discipleship; Faith; Peace
Related Books
Matthew
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'ye shall find rest unto your souls. For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light' (Matthew 11:29-30).
