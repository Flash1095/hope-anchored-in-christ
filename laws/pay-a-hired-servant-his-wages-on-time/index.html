Pay a hired servant his wages on time

Command
Pay a hired servant his wages on time
Category
Civil Law
Summary
The wages of a hired servant are given him on his day, before the sun goes down: the day's pay is not held past the day it was earned.
Commandment
Pay a hired servant his wages on time.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 24:15
Application
Prompt pay is the positive form of the labour law: the wage arrives when it is needed, at the day's end, because the labourer is poor and sets his heart upon it. Employers today owe the same promptness, whatever the pay period's form.
Related Topics
Justice; Money; Mercy
Related Books
Leviticus; Deuteronomy; James
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Deuteronomy states the ground: the laborer depends on the day's pay (Deuteronomy 24:15); James warns withheld wages cry out (James 5:4). Here that ground requires paying wages on time.
Duration
For all time
