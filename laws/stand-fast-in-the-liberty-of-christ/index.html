Stand Fast in the Liberty of Christ

Command
Stand Fast in the Liberty of Christ
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's liberty command: stand fast in the liberty wherewith Christ has made us free, and be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage.
Commandment
Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free, and be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage.
Scripture
Galatians 5:1
Application
Liberty is not licence but freedom from law-keeping as the way of standing with God. Returning to circumcision as a requirement would entangle the Galatians again — Christ would profit them nothing on those terms.
Related Topics
Faith; Obedience; Doctrine
Related Books

Related People

Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage' (Galatians 5:1).
