Carry a paddle to cover excrement in the camp

Command
Carry a paddle to cover excrement in the camp
Category
Civil Law
Summary
One of the war-camp holiness laws of Deuteronomy 23: carry a paddle to cover excrement in the camp. The camp was to be kept clean because God walked in its midst.
Commandment
Carry a paddle to cover excrement in the camp
Scripture
Deuteronomy 23:13
Application
Scripture's stated reason: for the LORD thy God walketh in the midst of thy camp, to deliver thee (Deuteronomy 23:14). The camp itself has passed; the principle that God's presence calls for cleanliness and order remains.
Related Topics
Holiness; Purity
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated reason: for the LORD thy God walketh in the midst of thy camp, to deliver thee, and to give up thine enemies before thee; therefore shall thy camp be holy: that he see no unclean thing in thee, and turn away from thee (Deuteronomy 23:14).
