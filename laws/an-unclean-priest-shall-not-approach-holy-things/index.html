An unclean priest shall not approach holy things

Command
An unclean priest shall not approach holy things
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
A priest who touched uncleanness and approached the holy things in his uncleanness was to be cut off from before the LORD (Leviticus 22:3).
Commandment
An unclean priest shall not approach the holy things of the LORD in his uncleanness.
Scripture
Leviticus 22:3
Application
This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah. The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The New Testament replaces the whole system of approach to holy things: all who are in Christ are a holy priesthood (1 Peter 2:5,9), and access to God is through Christ, not through priestly approach to holy things. The boundary itself — holy things are not common things — is fulfilled, not continued.
Related Topics
Priesthood; Holiness
Related Books
Leviticus; Hebrews
Purpose
Scripture's stated ground: the holy things are the LORD's, and approaching them in uncleanness profaned his holy name (Leviticus 22:2-3).
