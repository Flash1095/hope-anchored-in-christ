Debt Release, Sabbatical, and Jubilee Years

Command
Debt Release, Sabbatical, and Jubilee Years
Category
Civil Law
Summary
The economic reset laws: every seventh year debts released and the land rested; every fiftieth year land returned and slaves freed.
Commandment
Every seventh year release debts and let the land rest; every fiftieth year proclaim liberty and return each family to its land.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 15:1-11; Leviticus 25:1-55; Luke 4:18-19
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as civil. Jesus opens his ministry proclaiming release to the captives, taking up Jubilee's language for his own mission. The laws guarded against permanent destitution and permanent debt.

Justice; Mercy; Money
Related Books
