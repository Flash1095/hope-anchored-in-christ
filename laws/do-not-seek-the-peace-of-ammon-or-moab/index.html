Do not seek the peace of Ammon or Moab

Command
Do not seek the peace of Ammon or Moab
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 23:6 forbids seeking the peace or prosperity of Ammon or Moab for ever, because they refused Israel bread and water and hired Balaam to curse them.
Commandment
Do not seek the peace or prosperity of Ammon or Moab.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 23:6
Application
This foreign-policy stance belonged to ancient Israel's relations with two specific nations, now gone as nations; it does not govern the church's relations with any people today. Its ground — remembered hostility to God's people on their journey — is the stated reason.

Purpose
Scripture's stated context: Ammon and Moab refused Israel bread and water on the way and hired Balaam to curse them (Deuteronomy 23:4-5).
