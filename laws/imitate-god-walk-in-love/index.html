Imitate God and Walk in Love

Command
Imitate God and Walk in Love
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's imitation command: be followers of God as dear children, and walk in love, as Christ has loved us and given himself for us.
Commandment
Be ye therefore followers of God, as dear children; and walk in love, as Christ also hath loved us, and hath given himself for us an offering and a sacrifice to God.
Scripture
Ephesians 5:1-2
Application
Imitation here is a child's copying of a Father, measured by Christ's self-giving. It frames the whole Ephesian ethic: love is not a feeling but a walk patterned on sacrifice.
Related Topics
Love; Discipleship; Holiness
Related Books
Ephesians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: Christ 'hath given himself for us an offering and a sacrifice to God' (Ephesians 5:2).
Duration
For all time
