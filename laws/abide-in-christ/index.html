Abide in Christ

Command
Abide in Christ
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
The vine command: remain in Christ as a branch remains in the vine, or you can do nothing.
Commandment
Abide in me as I abide in you; apart from me you can do nothing.
Scripture
John 15:4-10; 1 John 2:28
Application
Abiding is not a feeling but a staying: keep his words, keep his commands, and let his life flow through you into fruit.
Related Topics
Discipleship; Faith; Jesus Christ
Related Books
John; 1 John
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
Fruitfulness depends on remaining in Christ as branch in vine (John 15:4-5); apart from him, nothing.
Duration
For all time
