Do not leave sacrifices past the time allowed for eating them

The command: Do not leave sacrifices past the time allowed for eating them (Leviticus 22:30). Each offering had its appointed eating time; what remained beyond it was not to be eaten.

Commandment

Do not leave sacrifices past the time allowed for eating them.

Application

It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.

Purpose

Scripture's stated ground: an offering eaten beyond its appointed day was not accepted (Leviticus 22:30; Leviticus 19:7-8).

Function

Each offering had its appointed eating time; what remained beyond it was not to be eaten — a ceremonial law of the deadline. Its goal was to keep the offering inside its day: an offering eaten beyond its appointed day was not accepted. The evil was the late meal, holy food stretched past its time as if the appointment did not matter. It restrained that by the refusal: past the time, the sacrifice was not eaten, and the boundary of the day held.
