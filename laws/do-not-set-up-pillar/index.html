Do Not Set Up a Sacred Pillar

Command
Do Not Set Up a Sacred Pillar
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Israel shall not set up any image — in context, a standing stone pillar of the kind used in Canaanite worship — which the LORD hates. This is a distinct mitzvah from the Asherah verse beside it, and from the live No Idols row.
Commandment
Do not set up a sacred pillar, which the LORD your God hates.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 16:22
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as moral. The pillar was a rival worship object, not a mere monument: Jacob's pillar at Bethel (Genesis 28) was a memorial before this law, and the law draws the line at pillars used as objects of worship. The enduring principle is that worship objects borrowed from idolatry are refused, whatever their form.

Scripture's stated reason: the LORD thy God hateth it (Deuteronomy 16:22).
