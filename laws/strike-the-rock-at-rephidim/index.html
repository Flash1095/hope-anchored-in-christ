Strike the Rock at Rephidim

Command
Strike the Rock at Rephidim
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's command at Rephidim: smite the rock in Horeb, and water will come out for the people.
Commandment
Thou shalt smite the rock, and there shall come water out of it, that the people may drink.
Scripture
Exodus 17:6
Application
Distinct from the Meribah rock row: different place (Rephidim/Horeb, not Meribah), different verb (smite, not speak), different occasion. The two rocks must not be merged — Moses' later error was treating the second like the first.
Related Topics
Obedience; Faith
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To give water to a thirsting, murmuring people at Rephidim, proving God's presence among them (Exodus 17:7).
Duration
One-time
