Do not give your children to Molech

Command
Do not give your children to Molech
Category
Moral Law
Summary
One of the boundaries of Leviticus 18: do not give your children to Molech. The chapter forbids these unions as defiling, and warns that the land itself would spew out a people who practised them (Leviticus 18:24-28).
Commandment
Do not give your children to Molech
Scripture
Leviticus 18:21
Application
Leviticus 18 marks God's people as holy and distinct, and the New Testament reaffirms sexual purity, honouring the body as the temple of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 6:19-20; Hebrews 13:4).
Related Topics
Holiness; Marriage; Sin
Related Books
Leviticus; 1 Corinthians; Hebrews
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To mark God's people as holy and distinct (Leviticus 18) and, in the New Testament, to honor the body as the temple of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 6:19-20).
