Leave the Ark

God's command after the waters dried: go forth from the ark, with family and every living thing.

Commandment

Go forth of the ark, thou, and thy wife, and thy sons, and thy sons' wives with thee; bring forth every living thing with thee.

Application

Noah did not leave on his own timing: he waited for the spoken command even after the ground was dry. Distinct moment from entering; same merge caveat as the enter row.

Purpose

To repopulate the cleansed earth from the ark's household and creatures, opening the post-flood order (Genesis 8:17; 9:1).

Function

Noah did not leave on his own timing: he waited for the spoken command even after the ground was dry. This was a one-time command, given once for a specific moment, so the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not apply to it. Its goal was to repopulate the cleansed earth from the ark's household and creatures, opening the post-flood order. The evil or restraint angle does not apply here; this was a sending out, not a law against a recurring evil. Go forth from the ark, with family and every living thing.
