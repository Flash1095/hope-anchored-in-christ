Do Not Mention the Names of Other Gods

Command
Do Not Mention the Names of Other Gods
Category
Moral Law
Summary
In all that God has said, Israel is to be circumspect — and make no mention of the name of other gods, neither let it be heard out of their mouths. This is distinct from worshipping idols (live row No Idols): it governs speech and invocation.
Commandment
Do not invoke or make mention of the names of other gods.
Scripture
Exodus 23:13
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as moral. The New Testament carries the same instinct: flee idolatry (1 Corinthians 10:14), and let speech be governed by loyalty to Christ alone. The verse does not forbid historical discussion of other religions; it forbids invoking other gods as gods.
Related Topics
Idolatry; Worship; Speech
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: speech that invokes another god already gives it a place that belongs to the LORD alone. Scripture states the prohibition without a separate reason beyond the call to be circumspect in all God has said (Exodus 23:13).
Duration
For all time
