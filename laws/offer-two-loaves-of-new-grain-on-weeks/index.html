Offer two loaves of new grain on Weeks

Command
Offer two loaves of new grain on Weeks
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
On the Feast of Weeks, two wave loaves baked with leaven from the new grain are offered as firstfruits to the LORD.
Commandment
On the Feast of Weeks, offer two wave loaves of fine flour baked with leaven, the firstfruits unto the LORD.
Scripture
Leviticus 23:17
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The leavened loaves are the feast's one exception: the harvest offering itself, unlike the Passover bread, could be leavened. Debated: some see the commanded observance fulfilled as shadow (Colossians 2:16-17); others keep feasts voluntarily as memorial, not as binding command. The site does not settle whether observance continues.
Related Topics
Worship; Sacrifice; Covenant
Related Books
Leviticus; 1 Corinthians
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To rehearse God's saving acts at appointed seasons (Leviticus 23); Paul calls Christ our Passover (1 Corinthians 5:7).
