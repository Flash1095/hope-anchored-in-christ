Go in This Your Might

Command
Go in This Your Might
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
The LORD's commission to Gideon: go in this your might and save Israel from the Midianites.
Commandment
Go in this thy might, and thou shalt save Israel from the hand of the Midianites: have not I sent thee?
Scripture
Judges 6:14
Application
Gideon's commission, given at the winepress where he threshed wheat in hiding (Judges 6:11). The fleece episodes (6:36-40) are deliberately NOT rowed: they are Gideon's tests of God, not God's commands to Gideon.
Related Topics
Faith; Obedience
Related Books
Judges
Related People
Gideon
Related Events
Gideon's Victory over Midian
Purpose
To deliver Israel from seven years of Midianite oppression through a reluctant judge God had sent (Judges 6:1, 6:14).
Duration
One-time
