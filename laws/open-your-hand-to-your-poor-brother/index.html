Open your hand to your poor brother

Command
Open your hand to your poor brother
Category
Civil Law
Summary
To a poor brother you shall open your hand wide, and surely lend him sufficient for his need, in that which he wants.
Commandment
Open your hand to your poor brother.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 15:8
Application
Open-handed lending is measured by the need, not by the lender's comfort: 'sufficient for his need' is the standard. The sabbatical setting does not confine the duty — generous help for a brother in need endures in any age.
Related Topics
Mercy; Generosity; Love
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To prevent permanent destitution and permanent debt in Israel (Leviticus 25; Deuteronomy 15). Here that purpose takes the form of an open hand, sufficient for the brother's need.
Duration
Temporary
