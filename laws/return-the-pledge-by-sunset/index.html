Return the pledge by sunset

Command
Return the pledge by sunset
Category
Civil Law
Summary
In any case, you shall deliver the pledge again by sunset, that the debtor may sleep in his own raiment and bless you.
Commandment
Return the pledge by sunset.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 24:13
Application
Sunset is the deadline mercy sets: the pledge returns before night, so the borrower sleeps warm and the lender's righteousness is counted before the LORD. The procedure belonged to Israel's civil law; the dignity it protects is the same mercy that still binds a creditor today.
