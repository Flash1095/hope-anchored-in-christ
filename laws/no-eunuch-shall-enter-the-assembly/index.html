No eunuch shall enter the assembly

Command
No eunuch shall enter the assembly
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 23:1 excludes one wounded in the stones or with his privy member cut off from the assembly of the LORD. The assembly's wholeness rules marked Israel's worship community.
Commandment
No eunuch shall enter the assembly of the LORD.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 23:1
Application
Debated: how far these assembly exclusions carry into the church is genuinely disputed — this row does not settle it. The Ethiopian eunuch's welcome into the church (Acts 8:26-39) and Isaiah's promise to eunuchs (Isaiah 56:4-5) stand in the discussion.
