Comfort and Edify One Another

Command
Comfort and Edify One Another
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's comfort command: comfort one another with the hope of resurrection, and edify one another, even as you do.
Commandment
Wherefore comfort one another with these words. Wherefore comfort yourselves together, and edify one another, even as also ye do.
Scripture
1 Thessalonians 4:18; 1 Thessalonians 5:11
Application
Comfort here is doctrine applied: the return of Christ and the resurrection of the dead are not speculation but words for grieving believers to speak to each other.
Related Topics
Hope; Fellowship; Peace
Related Books
1 Thessalonians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'that ye sorrow not, even as others which have no hope' (1 Thessalonians 4:13, the passage's opening).
Duration
For all time
