Comfort and Edify One Another

Paul's comfort command: comfort one another with the hope of resurrection, and edify one another, even as you do.

Commandment

Wherefore comfort one another with these words. Wherefore comfort yourselves together, and edify one another, even as also ye do.

Application

Comfort here is doctrine applied: the return of Christ and the resurrection of the dead are not speculation but words for grieving believers to speak to each other.

Purpose

Scripture's own reason: 'that ye sorrow not, even as others which have no hope' (1 Thessalonians 4:13, the passage's opening).

Function

Comfort here is doctrine applied: the return of Christ and the resurrection of the dead are words for grieving believers to speak to each other. An apostolic command, not a Mosaic one — the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not sort it. Its goal was to keep hope from being private — comfort one another, and edify one another. The evil it addressed was sorrow without hope, grief borne as if the dead were lost. It restrained that evil with the reason Scripture gives: that ye sorrow not, even as others which have no hope.
