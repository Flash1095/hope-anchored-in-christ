The Public Reading Every Seventh Year

Command
The Public Reading Every Seventh Year
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
And Moses commanded them, saying, At the end of every seven years, in the solemnity of the year of release, in the feast of tabernacles, when all Israel is come to appear before the LORD thy God in the place which he shall choose, thou shalt read this law before all Israel in their hearing — gathering men, women, children, and the stranger within the gates. This Hakhel reading is in no live row.
Commandment
At the end of every seven years, at the Feast of Tabernacles in the year of release, read this law before all Israel, men, women, children, and strangers.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 31:10-13
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The seventh-year public reading kept the whole law audible to the whole people, including children and strangers — no one was left to learn the covenant by rumour. The New Testament keeps the practice in a new form: give attendance to reading (1 Timothy 4:13), so that the whole church hears the whole word, not only its favourite parts.

Purpose
Scripture's stated reason: that they may hear, and that they may learn, and fear the LORD your God, and observe to do all the words of this law — and that their children, which have not known any thing, may hear, and learn to fear the LORD (Deuteronomy 31:12-13).
