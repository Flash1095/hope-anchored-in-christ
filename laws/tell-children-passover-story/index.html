Tell Your Children the Passover Story

Command
Tell Your Children the Passover Story
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
When children ask what the Passover service means, parents are to tell them: it is the sacrifice of the LORD's Passover, who passed over Israel's houses in Egypt. This is the telling, distinct from the feast observance itself.
Commandment
When your children ask, tell them what the Passover means and what God did in Egypt.
Scripture
Exodus 12:26-27; Exodus 13:8; Exodus 13:14
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The Passover telling is the model for passing faith to children by story: Christians keep the same instinct — remembering deliverance — in the Lord's Supper (Luke 22:19; 1 Corinthians 11:26), without treating the Passover ritual itself as a Christian obligation.

Purpose
Scripture's stated reason: so that the next generation knows what God did — 'by strength of hand the LORD brought us out from Egypt' (Exodus 13:14).
