Keep the law of an unpaid bailee

Command
Keep the law of an unpaid bailee
Category
Civil Law
Summary
An unpaid bailee — one keeping a neighbour's money or goods for nothing — swears before God if the thing is stolen, and pays double only if he himself is found to be the thief.
Commandment
Keep the law of an unpaid bailee.
Scripture
Exodus 22:7
Application
Unpaid keeping is neighbourly help, so the keeper's liability is lighter than the paid keeper's: oath clears him where theft came from outside. But if the keeper himself stole, he pays double like any thief.
Related Topics
Justice; Money; Truth
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To make wrongs right: an unpaid keeper answers by oath, and pays double only if he himself took the thing.
Duration
For all time
