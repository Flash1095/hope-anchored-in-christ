Return every man to his possession in the Jubilee

Command
Return every man to his possession in the Jubilee
Category
Civil Law
Summary
In the Jubilee, every man returns to his possession: land sold under pressure reverts to its family, and the family's footing in the land is restored.
Commandment
Return every man to his possession in the Jubilee.
Scripture
Leviticus 25:13
Application
No family's loss was final. Jubilee capped the damage of debt and bad years by restoring the land itself — the one asset a farming family could not do without. The calendar system has ended, but the warning against permanent destitution still speaks.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy; Family
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To prevent permanent destitution and permanent debt in Israel (Leviticus 25; Deuteronomy 15). In the Jubilee, that purpose takes the form of every man returning to his possession.
