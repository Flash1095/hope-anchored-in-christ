Redeem a house in a walled city within a year

Command
Redeem a house in a walled city within a year
Category
Civil Law
Summary
A house sold in a walled city may be redeemed within a whole year after it is sold; after the year, if unredeemed, it passes to the buyer permanently.
Commandment
Redeem a house in a walled city within a year.
Scripture
Leviticus 25:29
Application
City houses followed a different rule from country land: the one-year window balanced the seller's chance to recover against the buyer's need for certainty. The distinction shows the Law's care for the different economics of town and country.
Related Topics
Justice; Family
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To prevent permanent destitution and permanent debt in Israel (Leviticus 25; Deuteronomy 15). For walled-city houses, that purpose takes the form of a one-year redemption window.
