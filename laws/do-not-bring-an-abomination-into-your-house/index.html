Do not bring an abomination into your house

Deuteronomy 7:26 forbids bringing an abomination into the house: 'Neither shalt thou bring an abomination into thine house, lest thou be a cursed thing like it: but thou shalt utterly detest it, and thou shalt utterly abhor it; for it is a cursed thing.' The household, like the land, was to be kept clear of idols.

Commandment

Do not bring an abomination into your house.

Application

What is kept in the home shapes the home's worship. Idols are not souvenirs, decorations, or investments; they are cursed things, to be detested and kept out.

Purpose

Scripture's stated reason: lest thou be a cursed thing like it (Deuteronomy 7:26).

Function

Moral law, plain and simple: it binds wherever people live, in any age. Deuteronomy 7:26 forbids bringing an abomination into the house. Scripture gives the reason: lest thou be a cursed thing like it. Here is how the line held. What is kept in the home shapes the home's worship.
