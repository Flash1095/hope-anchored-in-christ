Leave Your Country

Command
Leave Your Country
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's call command to Abram: get out of his country, kindred, and father's house to a land God would show him.
Commandment
Get thee out of thy country, and from thy kindred, and from thy father's house, unto a land that I will shew thee.
Scripture
Genesis 12:1
Application
The founding migration command of the patriarchal story: one man, one departure, with promises attached (a great nation, blessing to all families of the earth). Completed when Abram departed as commanded (Genesis 12:4); never reissued as a standing law.
Related Topics
Faith; Covenant; Obedience
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Abraham
Related Events
The Call of Abraham
Purpose
To separate Abram for the covenant people and the promised land from which blessing would reach all families of the earth (Genesis 12:2-3).
Duration
One-time
