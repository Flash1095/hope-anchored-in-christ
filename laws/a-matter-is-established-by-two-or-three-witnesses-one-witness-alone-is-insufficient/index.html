Command
A matter is established by two or three witnesses; one witness alone is insufficient
Category
Civil Law
Summary
At the mouth of two witnesses, or three, shall a matter be established; one witness alone is insufficient to convict.
Commandment
A matter is established by two or three witnesses; one witness alone is insufficient.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 19:15
Application
One voice, however confident, cannot carry a conviction: the two-witness rule guards against the single accuser, the mistaken memory, and the malicious charge. The rule carried into the church's own discipline (Matthew 18:16; 1 Timothy 5:19).
Related Topics
Justice; Truth
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Matthew
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To secure impartial justice: no bribes, no partiality, no conviction on one witness. Here that purpose takes the form of two or three witnesses establishing a matter.
Duration
For all time
