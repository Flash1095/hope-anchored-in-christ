Adorn with Modesty and Good Works

Command
Adorn with Modesty and Good Works
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's and Peter's adornment instruction: women should adorn themselves in modest apparel, with shamefacedness and sobriety, not with braided hair, gold, pearls, or costly array, but with good works. Whether the specific adornments are a transcultural ban or a first-century cultural expression of the enduring modesty command is debated, and this row does not settle it.
Commandment
In like manner also, that women adorn themselves in modest apparel, with shamefacedness and sobriety; not with broided hair, or gold, or pearls, or costly array; but with good works.
Scripture
1 Timothy 2:9-10; 1 Peter 3:3-4
Application
The positive half is the point both writers stress: the hidden person of the heart, a meek and quiet spirit, and good works as the true ornament. The specific items named are the debated cultural form; modesty itself is the enduring substance churches agree on.
Related Topics
Holiness; Family; Worship
Related Books
1 Timothy; 1 Peter
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to make character and good works, not display, the adornment — with the named items' literal scope debated.
