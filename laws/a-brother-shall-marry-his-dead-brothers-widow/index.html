A brother shall marry his dead brother's widow

Command
A brother shall marry his dead brother's widow
Category
Moral Law
Summary
One of the family laws of Deuteronomy 21–25: a brother shall marry his dead brother's widow. These laws protected the weaker party at each family step.
Commandment
A brother shall marry his dead brother's widow
Scripture
Deuteronomy 25:5
Application
These civil provisions belonged to ancient Israel's family law and have passed with it; the protection of the weaker party they aimed at remains the lesson.
Related Topics
Marriage; Family; Justice
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Matthew
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To preserve the dead brother's name and inheritance in Israel: the child of the union carries the dead man's name (Deuteronomy 25:5-6).
