A newly married man shall be free at home for a year

Command
A newly married man shall be free at home for a year
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 24:5 frees a newly married man from public business for a year: 'he shall be free at home one year, and shall cheer up his wife which he hath taken.' The new household takes precedence over the state's claims.
Commandment
A newly married man shall be free at home for one year, to cheer his wife.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 24:5
Application
Debated: how far these civil provisions carry into the church is genuinely disputed — this row does not settle it. What is not debated is the protection the law aimed at: a new marriage given its first year intact.
Related Topics
Marriage; Family; Justice
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Matthew
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To protect the weaker party at each family step (Deuteronomy 21-25). Debated: the divorce certificate is read by Jesus as concession to hardness of heart, not God's design (Matthew 19:8), and levirate marriage ended, while creation marriage itself is reaffirmed.
