Abstain from Blood, Strangled Things, and Fornication

Command
Abstain from Blood, Strangled Things, and Fornication
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
The Jerusalem decree: the council asks Gentile believers to abstain from meats offered to idols, from blood, from things strangled, and from fornication. Drafted beside the idol-food row because its blood and strangled clauses are distinct, and its scope is debated.
Commandment
Abstain from meats offered to idols, and from blood, and from things strangled, and from fornication.
Scripture
Acts 15:20; Acts 15:29; Acts 21:25
Application
The decree settled the Gentile question — no circumcision required — while asking for these abstentions for fellowship's sake. Fornication's ban is enduring moral law; whether the food clauses bind all churches for all time or eased Jewish-Gentile table fellowship is debated, and this row does not settle it.
