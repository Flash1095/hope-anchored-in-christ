Observe the law of a dedicated field

The command: Observe the law of a dedicated field (Leviticus 27:16). A field sanctified to the LORD was valued by the seed sown in it, fifty shekels of silver to a homer of barley seed, reckoned by the years to the jubile.

Commandment

Observe the law of a dedicated field, as prescribed in Leviticus 27:16.

Application

This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah. The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Valuations and redemptions priced devotion in a fixed, public way, so that a vow could not be quietly abandoned or quietly inflated. The whole system of dedicated things ended with the temple system; Christians give freely and cheerfully, not by valuation tables (2 Corinthians 9:7).

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: fixed valuations kept vows honest and public, preventing both evasion and extravagant self-pricing. Scripture states the scale without a separate reason (Leviticus 27:1-29).

Function

A field sanctified to the LORD was valued by the seed sown in it, reckoned by the years to the jubile — a ceremonial law of the dedicated land. Its goal, traditionally, was to price devotion in a fixed, public way, so a vow of land could not be quietly abandoned or quietly inflated. The evil was the vow played with, the field promised and then fudged. It restrained that by the fixed valuation: the seed measured the price, and the years to the jubile set it.
