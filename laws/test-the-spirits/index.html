Test the Spirits

Command
Test the Spirits
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
John's testing command: believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God, because many false prophets are gone out into the world.
Commandment
Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world.
Scripture
1 John 4:1-3
Application
The test is confessional: every spirit that confesses Jesus Christ come in the flesh is of God. Openness to spiritual claims without testing is credulity, not faith.
Related Topics
Discernment; Truth; Doctrine
Related Books
1 John
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'many false prophets are gone out into the world' (1 John 4:1).
Duration
For all time
