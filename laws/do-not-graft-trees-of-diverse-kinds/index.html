Do not graft trees of diverse kinds

Command
Do not graft trees of diverse kinds
Category
Civil Law
Summary
One of the mixture laws of Leviticus 19:19: do not graft trees of diverse kinds. The verse as written forbids mingled seed in the field; the tradition counts grafting of diverse trees as a distinct mixture law under the same verse. Doubt: whether grafting stands as a separate enumeration distinct from the field-seed mixture law is debated in the tradition, and the verse itself speaks of mingled seed rather than grafting by name.
Commandment
Do not graft trees of diverse kinds
Scripture
Leviticus 19:19
Application
TRADITIONAL reading: the mixture law is classed among the chukim, statutes whose reason Scripture does not state; like the other mixture laws of Leviticus 19:19 it belonged to Israel's land law under the Sinai covenant.
