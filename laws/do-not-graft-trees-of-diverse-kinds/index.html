Do not graft trees of diverse kinds

One of the mixture laws of Leviticus 19:19: do not graft trees of diverse kinds. The verse as written forbids mingled seed in the field; the tradition counts grafting of diverse trees as a distinct mixture law under the same verse. Doubt: whether grafting stands as a separate enumeration distinct from the field-seed mixture law is debated in the tradition, and the verse itself speaks of mingled seed rather than grafting by name.

Commandment

Do not graft trees of diverse kinds

Application

TRADITIONAL reading: the mixture law is classed among the chukim, statutes whose reason Scripture does not state; like the other mixture laws of Leviticus 19:19 it belonged to Israel's land law under the Sinai covenant.

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: no reason is stated in Scripture; the mixture law is classed among the chukim. Duration Temporary because it is a Mosaic land law bound to Israel under the Sinai covenant, of the same kind as the other mixture laws of Leviticus 19:19, which the collection files as Temporary.

Function

Israel's civil order: a rule to keep the common life honest and whole. One of the mixture laws of Leviticus 19:19: do not graft trees of diverse kinds. TRADITIONAL reading: no reason is stated in Scripture; the mixture law is classed among the chukim. It did not address a particular evil the way a civil law does; its work was keeping kinds distinct and marking God's people apart, not restraint. The prohibition comes without a stated reason, and I will not invent one.
