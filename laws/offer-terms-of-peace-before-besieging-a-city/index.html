Offer terms of peace before besieging a city

Command
Offer terms of peace before besieging a city
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 20:10 requires peace to be offered before a siege: 'When thou comest nigh unto a city to fight against it, then proclaim peace unto it.' Only a city that refuses peace is besieged.
Commandment
Offer terms of peace before besieging a city.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 20:10
Application
Even in war the Law set restraints — a striking limit for the ancient world, where siege warfare usually spared nothing. Peace offered first is the pattern; destruction was never the first word.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To restrain war even in conquest: offer peace, exempt the newly married and fearful, spare fruit trees (Deuteronomy 20). These laws bounded Israel's wars and do not govern any modern army as Mosaic law.
