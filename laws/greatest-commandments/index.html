The Greatest Commandments

Command
The Greatest Commandments
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' summary of the whole Law: love God with everything you are, and love your neighbor as yourself.
Commandment
Love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul, and mind, and love your neighbor as yourself.
Scripture
Matthew 22:37-40; Mark 12:29-31; Luke 10:25-28
Application
Jesus says all the Law and the Prophets hang on these two. Every other command in this collection is an unfolding of one of them.
