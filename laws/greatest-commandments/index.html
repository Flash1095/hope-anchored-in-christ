The Greatest Commandments

Jesus' summary of the whole Law: love God with everything you are, and love your neighbor as yourself.

Commandment

Love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul, and mind, and love your neighbor as yourself.

Application

Jesus says all the Law and the Prophets hang on these two. Every other command in this collection is an unfolding of one of them.

Purpose

Jesus' own summary: all the Law and the Prophets hang on these two (Matthew 22:40).

Function

Jesus' own summary of the whole Law — and every other command in this collection is an unfolding of one of them. A New Testament command, not a Mosaic one — the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not sort it. Its goal was to hang all the Law and the Prophets on two hooks: love God with everything you are, and love your neighbour as yourself. The evil it addressed was a law kept in fragments, its centre forgotten. It restrained that evil by naming the centre: on these two commandments hang all the Law and the Prophets.
