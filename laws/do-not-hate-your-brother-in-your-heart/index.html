Do not hate your brother in your heart

One of the neighbour-laws of Leviticus 19:17-18: do not hate your brother in your heart, part of the passage that ends in 'thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.'

Commandment

Do not hate your brother in your heart

Application

Jesus joins this neighbour rule to love of God as the hinge of the Law (Matthew 22:39-40); love of neighbour is the measure of love of God in practice.

Purpose

Leviticus 19:18 states the neighbor rule; Jesus joins it to love of God as the hinge of the Law (Matthew 22:39-40).
