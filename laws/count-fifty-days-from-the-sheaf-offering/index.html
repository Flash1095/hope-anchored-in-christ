Count fifty days from the sheaf offering

Command
Count fifty days from the sheaf offering
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
From the day the sheaf is waved, Israel counts seven Sabbaths complete — fifty days — to the Feast of Weeks.
Commandment
Count from the morrow after the Sabbath, from the day the sheaf is waved, seven Sabbaths complete, even fifty days.
Scripture
Leviticus 23:15
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The counting bound Weeks to Firstfruits and made the harvest season one long act of thanksgiving. Debated: some see the commanded observance fulfilled as shadow (Colossians 2:16-17); others keep feasts voluntarily as memorial, not as binding command. The site does not settle whether observance continues.
Related Topics
Worship; Sacrifice; Covenant
Related Books
Leviticus; 1 Corinthians
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To rehearse God's saving acts at appointed seasons (Leviticus 23); Paul calls Christ our Passover (1 Corinthians 5:7).
