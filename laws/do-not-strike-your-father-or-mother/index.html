Do not strike your father or mother

Command
Do not strike your father or mother
Category
Civil Law
Summary
A child who strikes his father or mother commits a capital offence under the Law of Moses, striking at the honour and authority God gave parents.
Commandment
Do not strike your father or mother.
Scripture
Exodus 21:15
Application
Honour for parents is not mere politeness; in Israel's law, violent assault on a parent was treated as an attack on God's own ordering of the family. The command still teaches that parental honour is owed in deed as well as word.
Related Topics
Justice; Family
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To guard the honour and authority of parents, whose place in the family God himself established; the scaled penalties were civil detail, while the duty to honour parents is the enduring substance.
Duration
For all time
