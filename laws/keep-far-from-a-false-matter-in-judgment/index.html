Keep far from a false matter in judgment

Command
Keep far from a false matter in judgment
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Keep far from a false matter: a judge must not lend his court to a charge he knows to be false, nor condemn the innocent or justify the wicked on false evidence.
Commandment
Keep far from a false matter in judgment.
Scripture
Exodus 23:7
Application
'Keep far' is stronger than 'do not do': the judge is to keep his distance from falsehood altogether, not merely avoid its worst forms. Innocent blood is the price when courts traffic in false matters.
Related Topics
Justice; Truth
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: falsehood in judgment sheds innocent blood, so the judge must keep far from a false matter, not merely refrain from its grossest form.

For all time
