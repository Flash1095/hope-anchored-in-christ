Do not destroy fruit trees in a siege

Command
Do not destroy fruit trees in a siege
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 20:19 forbids cutting down a city's fruit trees for siege works: 'for the tree of the field is man's life, to employ them in the siege?' Food-bearing trees were not weapons of war.
Commandment
Do not destroy fruit trees in a siege.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 20:19
Application
The restraint is memorable because it is so concrete: even besieging armies must leave the means of life standing. Wanton destruction, even of an enemy's livelihood, is refused.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To restrain war even in conquest: offer peace, exempt the newly married and fearful, spare fruit trees (Deuteronomy 20). These laws bounded Israel's wars and do not govern any modern army as Mosaic law.
