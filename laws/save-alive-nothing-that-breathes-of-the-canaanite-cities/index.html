Save alive nothing that breathes of the Canaanite cities

Command
Save alive nothing that breathes of the Canaanite cities
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 20:16 gives the conquest exception to the offer of peace: of the cities of the seven nations, 'thou shalt save alive nothing that breatheth.' The command is limited to those named cities in that campaign.
Commandment
Of the Canaanite cities, save alive nothing that breathes.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 20:16
Application
This is the hardest of the war laws, and it is also the most bounded: a specific campaign, named nations, a stated purpose — that they teach Israel not their abominations (Deuteronomy 20:18). It does not license any modern war, and it is not to be generalised beyond its stated bounds.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To restrain war even in conquest: offer peace, exempt the newly married and fearful, spare fruit trees (Deuteronomy 20). These laws bounded Israel's wars and do not govern any modern army as Mosaic law.
