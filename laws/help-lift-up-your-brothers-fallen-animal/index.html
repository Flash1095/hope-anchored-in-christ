Help lift up your brother's fallen animal

Command
Help lift up your brother's fallen animal
Category
Civil Law
Summary
If your brother's ass or ox is fallen down by the way, you shall surely help him lift it up again.
Commandment
Help lift up your brother's fallen animal.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 22:4
Application
The positive form of the fallen-animal law: help is not offered from a distance but given with one's own hands, lifting what the animal cannot lift alone. Even an enemy's animal is covered by the parallel account (Exodus 23:5).
Related Topics
Mercy; Compassion; Neighbor
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated ground: the animal is under a burden it cannot bear, and the owner's need — even an enemy owner's need — obliges help (Exodus 23:5; Deuteronomy 22:4).
Duration
For all time
