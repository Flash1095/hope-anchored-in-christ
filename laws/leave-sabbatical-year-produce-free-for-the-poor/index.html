Leave sabbatical-year produce free for the poor

Command
Leave sabbatical-year produce free for the poor
Category
Civil Law
Summary
In the sabbatical year, what the land produces of itself is left free: the poor of the people and the beasts of the field may eat of it.
Commandment
Leave sabbatical-year produce free for the poor.
Scripture
Exodus 23:11
Application
The sabbatical year taught dependence: for one year in seven the land was not worked, and its unaided produce belonged to whoever needed it. The calendar system belonged to Israel's land order, but the lesson — provision for the poor is built into the rhythm of work, not left to leftovers — endures.
Related Topics
Mercy; Generosity
Related Books
Exodus; Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: the sabbatical year rested the land and fed the poor from its unaided produce, so that need was met without the poor having to beg for it.
