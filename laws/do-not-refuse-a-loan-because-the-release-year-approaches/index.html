Do not refuse a loan because the release year approaches

In the sabbatical cycle, debts were released every seventh year (Deuteronomy 15:1-2); this law forbids letting the approaching release year harden the heart against lending to a poor brother.

Commandment

Do not refuse a loan to your poor brother because the seventh year, the year of release, is at hand.

Application

The release year was no excuse for a closed hand: the poor brother's need, not the calendar, was to govern lending, and the LORD promised blessing on open-handed giving.

Purpose

Lest the poor brother cry to the LORD and it be sin, and because the LORD blesses open-handed giving (Deuteronomy 15:9-10).

Function

A civil law: it governed life together in Israel — courts, property, neighbours — not worship. In the sabbatical cycle, debts were released every seventh year.
