Sanctify God's name

Command
Sanctify God's name
Category
Moral Law
Summary
One of the two sides of Leviticus 22:32: sanctify God's name. God's name is hallowed by his people's conduct, and profaned by it.
Commandment
Sanctify God's name
Scripture
Leviticus 22:32
Application
Scripture's stated reason: I am the LORD which hallow you, that brought you out of the land of Egypt, to be your God (Leviticus 22:32-33).
