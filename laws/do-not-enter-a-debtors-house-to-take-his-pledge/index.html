Do not enter a debtor's house to take his pledge

When you lend your brother anything, you shall not go into his house to fetch his pledge: you stand outside, and he brings the pledge out to you.

Commandment

Do not enter a debtor's house to take his pledge.

Application

Dignity is part of justice: the lender may not invade the borrower's house to seize the pledge. The threshold is a boundary the creditor does not cross, however good his claim.

Purpose

Scripture's stated reason: that he may sleep in his own raiment, and bless thee (Deuteronomy 24:13). Here that dignity forbids entering the debtor's house for the pledge.
