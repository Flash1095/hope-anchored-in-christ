Do not appear before the LORD empty-handed

Command
Do not appear before the LORD empty-handed
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
No one appears before the LORD at the pilgrimage feasts empty-handed; every man gives as he is able, according to the blessing God has given him.
Commandment
Do not appear before the LORD empty; every man shall give as he is able, according to the blessing of the LORD.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 16:16
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The gift measured gratitude, not wealth: each gave as he was able, according to the blessing he had received.
Related Topics
Worship; Sacrifice; Covenant
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated context: giving at the feasts answers the blessing God has given (Deuteronomy 16:17).
