Love Your Enemies and Pray for Your Persecutors

Command
Love Your Enemies and Pray for Your Persecutors
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' command to love beyond friends: love enemies, bless cursers, do good to haters, and pray for persecutors.
Commandment
Love your enemies, bless those who curse you, do good to those who hate you, and pray for those who persecute you.
Scripture
Matthew 5:43-48; Luke 6:27-28
Application
The command reverses the old slogan 'hate your enemy'. Its measure is God's own kindness to the evil and the good.
Related Topics
Love; Mercy; Forgiveness
Related Books
Matthew; Luke
Related People
Jesus
Related Events
The Sermon on the Mount
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'that ye may be the children of your Father which is in heaven' (Matthew 5:45).
Duration
For all time
