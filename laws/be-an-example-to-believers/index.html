Be an Example to Believers

Paul's example command to Timothy, drafted as a general charge: let no man despise your youth, but be an example of the believers in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.

Commandment

Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.

Application

Authority for the young leader is earned by example, not claimed by office: six spheres are named, from speech to purity. Addressed to Timothy, it is drafted because young believers in every generation face the same despising.

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: to make a life the credential where age cannot yet be one.

Function

Authority for the young leader is earned by example, not claimed by office. From the apostles rather than from Moses: the threefold division does not sort it. Its goal, on the TRADITIONAL reading, was to make a life the credential where age cannot yet be one. The evil it addressed was youth despised — a young believer's voice dismissed for his years. It restrained that evil by the example itself: be an example in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.
