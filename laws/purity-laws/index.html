Purity Laws: Skin Disease, Discharges, Childbirth

Command
Purity Laws: Skin Disease, Discharges, Childbirth
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The bodily purity laws: conditions that made a person ceremonially unclean and the washings and offerings that restored them.
Commandment
Follow the washings and offerings prescribed for skin disease, bodily discharges, and childbirth before returning to worship.
Scripture
Leviticus 12:1-15:33; Mark 1:40-45; Luke 8:43-48
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Jesus repeatedly touches those the purity laws isolated — the leper, the bleeding woman — and restores them, showing mercy reaching where ritual could only separate.

Purity; Holiness
