There shall be no cult prostitute among Israel

Command
There shall be no cult prostitute among Israel
Category
Moral Law
Summary
One of the Deuteronomy 23:17-18 laws: there shall be no cult prostitute among Israel. Fertility worship of the surrounding nations was to have no place in Israel, and its profits no place in God's house.
Commandment

Deuteronomy 23:17
