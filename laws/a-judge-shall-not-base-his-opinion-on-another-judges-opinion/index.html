A judge shall not base his opinion on another judge's opinion

Command
A judge shall not base his opinion on another judge's opinion
Category
Civil Law
Summary
A judge must not form his opinion merely by following another judge's opinion; each judge judges on the evidence before him.
Commandment
A judge shall not base his opinion on another judge's opinion.
Scripture
Exodus 23:2
Application
Deference is not judgment. A judge who merely echoes a colleague has not judged the case, and a court of echoes cannot be trusted to find the truth.
Related Topics
Justice; Truth; Integrity
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: the multitude can carry a court away from the truth, and the law guards the lone witness and the unpopular party against that pressure. Scripture states the prohibition without a separate reason (Exodus 23:2).
Duration
For all time
