Purge Out the Old Leaven; Put Away the Wicked Person

Command
Purge Out the Old Leaven; Put Away the Wicked Person
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's church-purity command: purge out the old leaven, that you may be a new lump, and put away from among yourselves that wicked person.
Commandment
Purge out therefore the old leaven, that ye may be a new lump, as ye are unleavened. Therefore put away from among yourselves that wicked person.
Scripture
1 Corinthians 5:7; 1 Corinthians 5:13
Application
Leaven spreads through the whole lump: tolerated sin in Corinth — a man with his father's wife — corrupts the feast, which must be kept in sincerity and truth. Discipline here is purging, not vengeance, and it belongs to the church, not outsiders.
Related Topics
Holiness; Church; Sin
Related Books
1 Corinthians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'a little leaven leaveneth the whole lump' (1 Corinthians 5:6).
Duration
For all time
