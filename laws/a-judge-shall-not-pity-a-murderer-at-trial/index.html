A judge shall not pity a murderer at trial

Command
A judge shall not pity a murderer at trial
Category
Civil Law
Summary
At the trial of a murderer, the judge's eye must not pity him so as to spare a guilty verdict: pity must not bend the finding of guilt.
Commandment
A judge shall not pity a murderer at trial.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 19:13
Application
Pity has its place, but not in the finding of fact: a judge who spares a murderer out of pity has perverted judgment and left innocent blood unanswered. The cities' court procedure belonged to Israel's land system; impartiality in judging guilt endures as justice wisdom.
Related Topics
Justice; Truth
Related Books
Numbers; Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To balance life's sacredness with intent: the unintentional killer fled to a city and stood trial, safe from vengeance (Numbers 35). Here that balance forbids pity bending a murder trial.
