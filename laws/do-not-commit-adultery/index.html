Do Not Commit Adultery

Command
Do Not Commit Adultery
Category
Ten Commandments
Summary
The seventh commandment: marriage is exclusive, and sexual faithfulness guards the covenant between husband and wife.
Commandment
Do not be unfaithful to your marriage; keep sexual union within marriage alone.
Scripture
Exodus 20:14; Deuteronomy 5:18; Matthew 5:27-28
Application
Jesus extends the commandment to the heart: lustful intent already breaks it. The positive side is faithfulness that protects both spouse and family.
Related Topics
Marriage; Sin; Holiness
Related Books
Exodus; Deuteronomy; Matthew
Related People
Moses
Related Events
The Ten Commandments
Purpose
To guard marriage exclusivity; Jesus roots marriage in creation: what God joined, man must not separate (Matthew 19:4-6).
Duration
For all time
