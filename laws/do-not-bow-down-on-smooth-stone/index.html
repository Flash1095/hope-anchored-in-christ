Do not bow down on smooth stone

Leviticus 26:1 forbids bowing down on a figured or smooth stone set up for worship: 'neither shall ye set up any image of stone in your land, to bow down unto it: for I am the LORD your God.' The stone need not be a statue to be an idol's furniture.

Commandment

Do not bow down on smooth or figured stone set up for worship.

Application

Worship is not made lawful by a humble object: a stone slab used as an idol's place is as forbidden as a golden calf. God alone receives bowing worship.

Purpose

Scripture's stated seal: for I am the LORD your God (Leviticus 26:1).
