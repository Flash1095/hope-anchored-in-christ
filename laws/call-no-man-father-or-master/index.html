Call No Man Father or Master

Command
Call No Man Father or Master
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' title command: be not called Rabbi, call no man your father on earth, neither be called masters, for one is your Master, even Christ, and all ye are brethren.
Commandment
Be not ye called Rabbi: for one is your Master, even Christ; and all ye are brethren. And call no man your father upon the earth: for one is your Father, which is in heaven.
Scripture
Matthew 23:8-10
Application
Spoken against titles used to claim spiritual superiority. How literally it forbids religious titles today is debated across traditions — some read it as an absolute ban on honorifics, others as a ban on the status-seeking it rebuked — and this row does not settle it.
Related Topics
Humility; Authority; Truth
Related Books
Matthew
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to forbid spiritual status-seeking, since all disciples are brethren under one Master and one Father.
