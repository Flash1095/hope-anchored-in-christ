Give firstborn clean animals to the priests

Command
Give firstborn clean animals to the priests
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: Give firstborn clean animals to the priests (Numbers 18:17). The flesh of the firstborn clean animals belonged to the priests, as the wave breast and heave shoulder.
Commandment
Give firstborn clean animals to the priests, as prescribed in Numbers 18:17.
Scripture
Numbers 18:17
Application
This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah. The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Redemption of the firstborn remembered that God spared Israel's firstborn in Egypt. The New Testament speaks of Jesus as the firstborn and of believers as redeemed, but does not continue the redemption payment as a Christian rite (Colossians 1:15-18; 1 Peter 1:18-19).
Related Topics
Priesthood; Redemption
Related Books
Numbers; Hebrews
Purpose
Scripture's stated reason: because the LORD slew all the firstborn in Egypt and spared Israel's, therefore the firstborn are his (Exodus 13:14-15).
