Carry out the laws of annulling vows

Command
Carry out the laws of annulling vows
Category
Civil Law
Summary
The laws of annulling vows are carried out as the Law sets them: a father may annul his young daughter's vow on the day he hears it, and a husband his wife's, with the stated consequences.
Commandment
Carry out the laws of annulling vows.
Scripture
Numbers 30:3
Application
Annulling a vow is not casual release but a household procedure: the father or husband who hears the vow may disallow it the same day, and the LORD forgives her. The procedure protected the household from rash vows while keeping every vow solemn.
Related Topics
Family; Truth; Obedience
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: vows bind, so release from a vow must itself follow a fixed procedure — the father's or husband's same-day annulment — rather than private disregard.
Duration
For all time
