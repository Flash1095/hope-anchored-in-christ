Do not breed cattle with a diverse kind

One of the mixture laws of Leviticus 19:19: do not breed cattle with a diverse kind. Scripture states the prohibition without a stated reason.

Commandment

Do not breed cattle with a diverse kind

Application

TRADITIONAL reading: the mixture law is classed among the chukim, statutes whose reason Scripture does not state; the priestly garments, which mixed materials by God's own command (Exodus 28), are the noted exception.

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: no reason is stated in Scripture; the mixture law is classed among the chukim. The one scriptural exception noted by tradition is the priestly garments themselves, which mixed materials by God's own command (Exodus 28).

Function

Classed with the moral laws: a duty of character as Israel knew it. TRADITIONAL reading — and I hold with it: no reason is stated in Scripture; the mixture law is classed among the chukim. It did not address a particular evil the way a civil law does; its work was keeping kinds distinct and marking God's people apart, not restraint. The prohibition comes without a stated reason, and I will not invent one.
