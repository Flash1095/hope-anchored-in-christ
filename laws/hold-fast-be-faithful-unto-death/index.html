Hold Fast and Be Faithful unto Death

Command
Hold Fast and Be Faithful unto Death
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
The risen Christ's command in the Revelation letters: hold fast what you have, be faithful unto death, and receive the crown of life.
Commandment
Be thou faithful unto death, and I will give thee a crown of life. Hold that fast which thou hast, that no man take thy crown.
Scripture
Revelation 2:10; Revelation 2:25; Revelation 3:11
Application
Spoken to Smyrna, Thyatira, and Philadelphia under pressure, merged here as one command because the wording and the charge are the same: endurance that does not let go under persecution.
Related Topics
Endurance; Courage; Faith
Related Books
Revelation
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'that no man take thy crown' (Revelation 3:11).
Duration
For all time
