Bring Every Thought into Captivity to Christ

Command
Bring Every Thought into Captivity to Christ
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's thought-warfare command: casting down imaginations and every high thing that exalts itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ.
Commandment
Casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ.
Scripture
2 Corinthians 10:5
Application
The warfare is not carnal but argumentative: strongholds are reasonings. Thoughts are not merely managed but captured — taken prisoner and brought under Christ's obedience.
Related Topics
Discernment; Truth; Obedience
Related Books
2 Corinthians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to treat rebellious reasoning as a stronghold to be demolished, not entertained.
Duration
For all time
