Do not touch the holy vessels

The command: Do not touch the holy vessels (Numbers 4:15). The Kohathites carried the holy vessels only after Aaron and his sons had covered them; touching them meant death.

Commandment

Do not touch the holy vessels.

Application

It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.

Purpose

Scripture's stated reason: touching the holy things meant death (Numbers 4:15).

Function

The Kohathites carried the holy vessels only after Aaron and his sons had covered them; touching them meant death — a ceremonial law of deadly holiness. Its goal was to keep the holiest objects untouched by unauthorised hands, the covering itself standing between the carriers and the vessels. The evil was presumptuous handling of holy things, reaching for what God had fenced off. It restrained that with the plainest warning in the law: touch and die.
