Do not harden your heart toward your poor brother

Command
Do not harden your heart toward your poor brother
Category
Civil Law
Summary
When a poor brother is among you, do not harden your heart or shut your hand against him.
Commandment
Do not harden your heart toward your poor brother.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 15:7
Application
Hardness of heart is the inward root of every refusal to help: the Law forbids the closing itself, not only the closed hand. Generosity begins before the purse opens, in a heart that stays soft toward a brother's need.
Related Topics
Mercy; Generosity; Love
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To prevent permanent destitution and permanent debt in Israel (Leviticus 25; Deuteronomy 15). Here that purpose takes the form of a heart not hardened toward a poor brother.
