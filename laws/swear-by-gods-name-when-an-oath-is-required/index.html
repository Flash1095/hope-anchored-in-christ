Swear by God's name when an oath is required

Command
Swear by God's name when an oath is required
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 6:13 permits swearing by God's name when an oath is required: 'and shalt swear by his name.' Oaths were not to be sworn by other gods, binding the swearer's word to the LORD alone.
Commandment
When an oath is required, swear by God's name alone.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 6:13
Application
The oath's weight comes from the name sworn by; swearing by other gods would bind the word to idols. The command guards truth-telling by anchoring oaths in the LORD.
Related Topics
Fear of God; Worship; Obedience
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated context: fear belongs with serving and cleaving to the God who brought Israel out of Egypt and keeps covenant (Deuteronomy 10:20-22).
