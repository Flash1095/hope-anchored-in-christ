Execute by burning where the Law decrees burning

Command
Execute by burning where the Law decrees burning
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Where the Law decrees death by burning — as for a man who marries both a woman and her mother — the court was to carry out that sentence by burning.
Commandment
Execute by burning where the Law decrees burning.
Scripture
Leviticus 20:14
Application
Burning is one of the four modes of execution the Law assigns to the court. The mode belonged to Israel's court system, which no longer functions; the offence it marked — sexual corruption within the family — still teaches how seriously the Law guarded the household.
Related Topics
Justice; Family
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: each mode of execution matched the offence as the Law assigned it, so that punishment was measured rather than arbitrary.
