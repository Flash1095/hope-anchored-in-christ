Fear the LORD your God

Command
Fear the LORD your God
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 6:13 commands fear of the LORD: 'Thou shalt fear the LORD thy God, and serve him.' Fear here is reverent obedience, joined to service and to swearing by his name alone.
Commandment
Fear the LORD your God, and serve him.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 6:13
Application
Fear belongs with serving and cleaving to the God who brought Israel out of Egypt and keeps covenant (Deuteronomy 10:20-22).
