Do All Things Decently and in Order

Command
Do All Things Decently and in Order
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's order command for worship: let all things be done decently and in order.
Commandment
Let all things be done decently and in order.
Scripture
1 Corinthians 14:40
Application
The closing rule of the long worship-order chapter: God is not the author of confusion but of peace. Decency and order are the frame within which gifts — tongues, prophecy, singing — are exercised, not a ban on them.
Related Topics
Worship; Church; Peace
Related Books
1 Corinthians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'For God is not the author of confusion, but of peace' (1 Corinthians 14:33).
Duration
For all time
