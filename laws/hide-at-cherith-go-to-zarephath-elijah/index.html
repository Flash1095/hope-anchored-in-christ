Hide at Cherith, Go to Zarephath

Command
Hide at Cherith, Go to Zarephath
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's drought-exile commands to Elijah: hide by the brook Cherith, then go to Zarephath and dwell there.
Commandment
Get thee hence, and turn thee eastward, and hide thyself by the brook Cherith; then arise, get thee to Zarephath, and dwell there.
Scripture
1 Kings 17:3; 1 Kings 17:9
Application
Merged row: two moves under one itinerant commission — hide by Cherith (1 Kings 17:3, ravens fed him) then go to Zarephath (17:9, the widow sustained him). Parent may split into two rows; kept merged because both are stages of the same drought exile, not two missions.
Related Topics
Faith; Obedience
Related Books
1 Kings
Related People
Elijah
Purpose
To hide and sustain Elijah through the drought God had announced — by ravens at Cherith, by a widow at Zarephath (1 Kings 17:4-6, 17:9).
Duration
One-time
