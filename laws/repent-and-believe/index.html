Repent and Believe the Gospel

Command
Repent and Believe the Gospel
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' opening proclamation: the kingdom of God is at hand, so turn from sin and trust the good news.
Commandment
Repent and believe the gospel, for the kingdom of God is at hand.
Scripture
Mark 1:14-15; Luke 24:47; Acts 2:38
Application
Peter preaches the same command at Pentecost: repent and be baptized for the forgiveness of sins. Repentance and faith are the door into the kingdom, not works added to it.
Related Topics
Repentance; Faith; Kingdom of God
Related Books
Mark; Luke; Acts
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
The door into the kingdom: Jesus' opening proclamation (Mark 1:15), preached at Pentecost (Acts 2:38).
