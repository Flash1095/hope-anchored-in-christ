Repent and Believe the Gospel

Jesus' opening proclamation: the kingdom of God is at hand, so turn from sin and trust the good news.

Commandment

Repent and believe the gospel, for the kingdom of God is at hand.

Application

Peter preaches the same command at Pentecost: repent and be baptized for the forgiveness of sins. Repentance and faith are the door into the kingdom, not works added to it.

Purpose

The door into the kingdom: Jesus' opening proclamation (Mark 1:15), preached at Pentecost (Acts 2:38).

Function

The door into the kingdom: turn from sin and trust the good news. This one is from the New Testament, not from Moses, so the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not apply. Its goal was entry itself — Jesus' opening proclamation that the kingdom of God is at hand, preached again at Pentecost. The evil it addressed was impenitence, the heart that will not turn. It restrained that evil by the call itself: repent, and believe the gospel — repentance and faith as the door, not works added to it.
