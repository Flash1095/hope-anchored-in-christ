Stretch Out Your Hand Over the Sea

Command
Stretch Out Your Hand Over the Sea
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's command at the Red Sea: speak that Israel go forward, lift the rod, and stretch out the hand over the sea to divide it.
Commandment
Lift thou up thy rod, and stretch out thine hand over the sea, and divide it, that the children of Israel shall go on dry ground through the midst of the sea.
Scripture
Exodus 14:15-16; Exodus 14:26-27
Application
Two utterances, one event: lift the rod and stretch out the hand (Exodus 14:16), then stretch it out again to close the sea (14:26). Moses is also told to speak to Israel that they go forward (14:15) — covered here as part of the same crossing command, not a separate row.
Related Topics
Obedience; Faith; Judgment
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Related Events
The Exodus and the Red Sea
Purpose
To divide the sea for Israel's escape and to close it on Pharaoh's army, showing that the LORD fought for Israel (Exodus 14:25, 14:30-31).
Duration
One-time
