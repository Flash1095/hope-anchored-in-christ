Do not offer an animal under eight days old

The command: Do not offer an animal under eight days old (Leviticus 22:27). A bullock, sheep, or goat had to remain seven days with its dam and might be offered only from the eighth day.

Commandment

Do not offer an animal under eight days old.

Application

It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: the newborn remained seven days with its dam before it could be given, honouring the natural order God made.

Function

A bullock, sheep, or goat stayed seven days with its dam before it could be offered — a ceremonial law that honoured the natural order. Its goal, traditionally, was to let the newborn remain with its mother before it was given, honouring the order God made. It did not address a particular evil; its work was reverence and order, not restraint. The eighth day said the offering was taken in God's time, not snatched in haste.
