Judges shall not hear one party in the absence of the other

A judge must not hear one party in the absence of the other; both sides are heard before judgment is given.

Commandment

Judges shall not hear one party in the absence of the other.

Application

Secret hearings breed suspicion and favouritism. A fair court hears both parties together, so that each can answer the other's claim before any judgment falls.

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: just judgment requires both parties to be heard; hearing one side alone prejudges the cause.

Function

A judge must not hear one party in the absence of the other. A civil law, and a practical one: it settled how neighbours lived side by side.
