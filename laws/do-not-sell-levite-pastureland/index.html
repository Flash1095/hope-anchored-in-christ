Do not sell Levite pastureland

The field of the suburbs of the Levite cities — their pastureland — must not be sold, for it is their perpetual possession.

Commandment

Do not sell Levite pastureland.

Application

Levite pastureland was common land for the cities' flocks, and its sale would have stripped the Levites' towns of their livelihood. The ban keeps a permanent possession permanent.

Purpose

To prevent permanent destitution and permanent debt in Israel (Leviticus 25; Deuteronomy 15). For Levite cities, that purpose takes the form of pastureland that may never be sold.

Function

This is community law, not temple law: it tells Israel how to live together justly. The field of the suburbs of the Levite cities — their pastureland — must not be sold.
