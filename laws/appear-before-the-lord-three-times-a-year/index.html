Appear before the LORD three times a year

Command
Appear before the LORD three times a year
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
Three times a year — at Unleavened Bread, at Weeks, and at Tabernacles — every male in Israel appears before the LORD in the place he chooses.
Commandment
Three times in a year shall all your males appear before the LORD your God in the place which he shall choose.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 16:16
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The thrice-yearly pilgrimage gathered the whole nation to one sanctuary and one calendar, so no tribe kept its own feasts alone.
Related Topics
Worship; Sacrifice; Covenant
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated context: the pilgrimage feasts gather Israel to the one place God chooses (Deuteronomy 16:16).
