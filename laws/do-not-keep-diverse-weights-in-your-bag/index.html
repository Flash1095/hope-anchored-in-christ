Do not keep diverse weights in your bag

Command
Do not keep diverse weights in your bag
Category
Civil Law
Summary
You shall not have in your bag diverse weights, a great and a small: one standard for buying and one for selling is fraud.
Commandment
Do not keep diverse weights in your bag.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 25:13
Application
Diverse weights are fraud kept ready: the heavy weight for buying, the light for selling, carried together in the same bag. The ban strikes at the instrument of fraud, not only at its use — the merchant must not even keep the means of cheating.
Related Topics
Justice; Money; Truth
Related Books
Leviticus; Deuteronomy; Proverbs
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Proverbs states the reason as God's own delight and abomination: a false balance is an abomination, but a just weight is his delight (Proverbs 11:1). Here that reason forbids even keeping diverse weights.
Duration
For all time
