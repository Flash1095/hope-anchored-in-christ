Court shall decide by majority

The court decides by majority: after deliberation, the verdict follows the greater number of judges.

Commandment

The court shall decide by majority.

Application

Deliberation without decision is paralysis, so the Law gives the court a way to conclude: the majority decides. Majority rule settles the verdict; it does not by itself make the verdict just, which is why the surrounding laws guard against following a multitude to do evil.

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: a court must be able to reach a verdict, and the majority decides — while the companion laws guard the majority itself against following a multitude to do evil (Exodus 23:2).

Function

A rule for life together: the Law here orders the common life of the people. The court decides by majority: after deliberation, the verdict follows the greater number of judges.
