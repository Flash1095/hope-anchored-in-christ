Do not press a debtor for payment when he cannot pay

Command
Do not press a debtor for payment when he cannot pay
Category
Civil Law
Summary
A creditor must not press a debtor for payment like an exactor when the debtor cannot pay; the law forbids treating a poor borrower as a thing to be squeezed.
Commandment
Do not press a debtor for payment when he cannot pay.
Scripture
Exodus 22:25
Application
Collection has a limit set by mercy: the creditor's right to be repaid does not licence harassment of a man who has nothing. Patience with the genuinely unable is part of justice, not a softening of it.
Related Topics
Justice; Money; Mercy
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To protect the poor debtor: the creditor's claim does not extend to pressing a man who cannot pay.
Duration
For all time
