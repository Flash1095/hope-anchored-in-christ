Care for the Poor, Widow, Orphan, and Stranger

Command
Care for the Poor, Widow, Orphan, and Stranger
Category
Moral Law
Summary
A cluster of laws protecting the vulnerable: gleaning left in the fields, prompt justice, and no oppression of those without a defender.
Commandment
Leave provision for the poor and the stranger, and do not oppress the widow, the orphan, or the foreigner.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:9-10; Exodus 22:21-24; Deuteronomy 24:17-21
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as moral. James carries the same duty into the church as pure religion: visiting orphans and widows in their affliction (James 1:27).
Related Topics
Mercy; Justice; Family
Related Books
Leviticus; Exodus; Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
