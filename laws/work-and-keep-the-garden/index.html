Work and Keep the Garden

Adam's Eden task: to dress and keep the garden God planted.

Commandment

Dress and keep the garden of Eden.

Application

Work precedes the Fall in this account: tending is part of humanity's original calling, not a curse. The specific garden task ended with the expulsion (Genesis 3:23-24); the work ethic it embodies is carried elsewhere, but this row covers the Eden task itself, not work in general.

Purpose

To tend the garden God planted as humanity's first appointed work; the task belongs to Eden and ended when Adam and Eve were sent out.

Function

Work precedes the Fall in this account: tending is part of humanity's original calling, not a curse. This command predates Sinai, so the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not sort it. Its goal was to tend the garden God planted — to dress and keep it, as humanity's first appointed work. The evil or restraint angle does not apply here; no particular evil is named, only a task given. The specific garden task ended with the expulsion, though the work ethic it embodies is carried elsewhere in Scripture.
