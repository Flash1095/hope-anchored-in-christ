Go and Sin No More

Command
Go and Sin No More
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' word to individuals — the man healed at Bethesda and the woman taken in adultery: go, and sin no more. Drafted on its merits with scope stated: spoken to individuals in the moment, and read by many as the general call that pardon carries a changed life.
Commandment
Go, and sin no more.
Scripture
John 5:14; John 8:11
Application
In both cases pardon or healing is joined to a command, not left bare: grace does not leave the life as it found it. Whether these two personal words form a standing law row or remain narrative pardons is debated, and this row does not settle it.
Related Topics
Repentance; Holiness; Sin
Related Books
John
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to join forgiveness to amendment of life, so that pardon is not taken as licence.
