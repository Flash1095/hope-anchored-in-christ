Do not remove the signs of skin disease

In a case of skin disease, Israel must take heed and observe diligently all that the Levitical priests teach, and not remove or tamper with the signs the priest must judge.

Commandment

Take heed in the plague of leprosy: observe diligently and do according to all that the priests the Levites teach you; do not remove the signs of the disease.

Application

The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Debated: the priestly examination procedure belonged to the temple order that has ended, while the underlying duty to heed God's word and to protect neighbors from disease is read by some as enduring wisdom. The site does not settle a single duration for this verse's procedure.

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: the signs were preserved so the priest could judge rightly whether the disease spread or healed, protecting both the afflicted and the camp.

Function

In a case of skin disease, Israel had to take heed and observe diligently all that the Levitical priests taught, and not remove the signs the priest must judge — a ceremonial law of honest examination. Its goal, traditionally, was to preserve the signs so the priest could judge rightly whether the disease spread or healed, protecting both the afflicted and the camp. The evil was hiding the disease, removing the marks so the judgment went the easy way. It restrained that by the command: take heed.
