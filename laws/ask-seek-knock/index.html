Ask, Seek, and Knock

Command
Ask, Seek, and Knock
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' persistence command in prayer: ask and it will be given, seek and you will find, knock and the door will be opened.
Commandment
Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you.
Scripture
Matthew 7:7-11; Luke 11:9-13
Application
Distinct from the Lord's Prayer pattern (live row), this is the manner of praying: persistent, childlike asking of a Father who gives good gifts, above all the Holy Spirit.
Related Topics
Prayer; Faith
Related Books
Matthew; Luke
Related People
Jesus
Related Events
The Sermon on the Mount
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: an earthly father gives bread, not a stone, to a child who asks — how much more the heavenly Father (Matthew 7:9-11).
Duration
For all time
