A non-priest shall not perform priestly service

Command
A non-priest shall not perform priestly service
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: A non-priest shall not perform priestly service (Numbers 18:7). The office of the priesthood belonged to Aaron and his sons alone; a stranger who came near was put to death.
Commandment
A non-priest shall not perform priestly service.
Scripture
Numbers 18:7
Application
It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.
Related Topics
Priesthood
Related Books
Numbers; Hebrews
Purpose
Scripture's stated reason: the priest's office was given as a gift of service to Aaron and his sons alone (Numbers 18:7).
