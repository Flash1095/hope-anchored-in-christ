A priest shall not serve with torn garments

Command
A priest shall not serve with torn garments
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: A priest shall not serve with torn garments (Leviticus 10:6). Aaron and his sons were not to tear their garments in mourning while the anointing oil was upon them.
Commandment
A priest shall not serve with torn garments.
Scripture
Leviticus 10:6
Application
This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah. The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Hebrews teaches the earthly tabernacle was a copy of the heavenly one, and Christ now serves as high priest in the true tabernacle.
Related Topics
Priesthood
Related Books
Leviticus; Hebrews
Purpose
To give Israel a mediated approach to God through the tabernacle pattern (Exodus 25:40). Hebrews teaches the earthly copy gave way to Christ's priesthood in the true tabernacle (Hebrews 8:1-6).
