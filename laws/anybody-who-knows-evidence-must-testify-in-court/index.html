Anybody who knows evidence must testify in court

Command
Anybody who knows evidence must testify in court
Category
Civil Law
Summary
If a person hears the voice of swearing — is called as a witness — and knows evidence but does not utter it, he bears his iniquity: one who knows evidence must testify.
Commandment
Anybody who knows evidence must testify in court.
Scripture
Leviticus 5:1
Application
Silence is not neutrality when a court needs the truth: the witness who withholds what he knows lets a false case stand. The duty to testify endures wherever justice depends on witnesses willing to speak.
Related Topics
Justice; Truth
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: justice depends on witnesses who speak what they know; withholding evidence lets a false case stand, so the witness who stays silent bears the guilt.
Duration
For all time
