Lend without interest to a poor brother

When a poor brother is in need, lend to him without interest, giving him what his need requires.

Commandment

Lend without interest to a poor brother.

Application

The positive side of the interest ban is open-handed lending: help is given freely, not sold. Generous lending to the needy remains a mark of a community that treats money as a means of mercy.

Purpose

To protect the poor: lending to a needy brother is help freely given, not an opportunity for gain.

Function
