Humble Yourselves, Draw Near to God, and Resist the Devil

Command
Humble Yourselves, Draw Near to God, and Resist the Devil
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
James's and Peter's joined command: humble yourselves under God's mighty hand, draw nigh to God and he will draw nigh to you, cleanse your hands, and resist the devil steadfast in the faith.
Commandment
Humble yourselves in the sight of the Lord, and he shall lift you up. Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.
Scripture
James 4:7-10; 1 Peter 5:6; 1 Peter 5:9
Application
James and Peter give the same sequence from two angles: humility first, nearness second, resistance third. Cleansed hands and purified hearts belong to the double-minded made single, not to a ritual.
Related Topics
Humility; Repentance; Temptation
Related Books
James; 1 Peter
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'he shall lift you up' and 'he will flee from you' (James 4:7, 10).
Duration
For all time
