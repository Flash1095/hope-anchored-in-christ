Cleave to God and walk in His ways

Deuteronomy 10:20 commands cleaving to God: 'thou shalt fear the LORD thy God; him shalt thou serve, and to him shalt thou cleave.' Cleaving is clinging loyalty, the opposite of drifting to other gods.

Commandment

Cleave to God, and walk in his ways.

Application

Fear belongs with serving and cleaving to the God who brought Israel out of Egypt and keeps covenant (Deuteronomy 10:20-22). Cleaving is daily, not occasional: service and loyalty held together.

Purpose

Scripture's stated context: fear belongs with serving and cleaving to the God who brought Israel out of Egypt and keeps covenant (Deuteronomy 10:20-22).

Function

A duty of character: the Law here names what is right, not merely what is punishable. Deuteronomy 10:20 commands cleaving to God: 'thou shalt fear the LORD thy God. Scripture gives the context: fear belongs with serving and cleaving to the God who brought Israel out of Egypt and keeps covenant. It did not address a particular evil the way a civil law does; its work was cleaving to God and walking in his ways, not restraint.
