Do not put a stumbling block before the blind

One half of the Leviticus 19:14 pair: do not put a stumbling block before the blind. The verse forbids cruelty that its victim cannot detect or avoid.

Commandment

Do not put a stumbling block before the blind

Application

Scripture's ground is the fear of God: he sees what the deaf cannot hear and the blind cannot see. The same duty covers every advantage taken over someone who cannot defend themselves.

Purpose

Scripture's stated ground: but shalt fear thy God (Leviticus 19:14) — God sees what the deaf cannot hear and the blind cannot see.
