Observe the laws of corpse impurity

Command
Observe the laws of corpse impurity
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
Whoever touches a dead body, a bone, a slain man, or a grave, or enters the tent of the dead, is unclean seven days and is purified with the water of separation on the third and seventh days.
Commandment
Whoever touches a dead body is unclean seven days, and shall purify himself with the water of separation on the third and seventh days.
Scripture
Numbers 19:14
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Death was the gravest defilement in the Law, which is why its cleansing took seven days and the strangest rite. The New Testament answers it directly: Christ touches the dead and they live (1 Corinthians 15:26).
Related Topics
Purity; Holiness
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Moses; Aaron
Purpose
Scripture's stated context: the law of the dead body defiles tent, vessels, and persons (Numbers 19:14-16); the water of separation is the appointed cleansing (Numbers 19:17-19).
