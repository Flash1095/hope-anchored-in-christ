Do not eat flesh left over from a sacrifice

Command
Do not eat flesh left over from a sacrifice
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: Do not eat flesh left over from a sacrifice (Leviticus 19:8). A peace offering eaten on the third day was an abomination and was not accepted; what remained was to be burned.
Commandment
Do not eat flesh left over from a sacrifice.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:8
Application
It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.
