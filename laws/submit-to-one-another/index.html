Submit to One Another

Command
Submit to One Another
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's mutual-submission command: submit yourselves one to another in the fear of God — the heading under which the household code itself is given.
Commandment
Submitting yourselves one to another in the fear of God.
Scripture
Ephesians 5:21
Application
Distinct from the household row (live), which spells out wives, husbands, children, parents, servants, and masters: this is the mutual posture that precedes and frames all of them, owed by every believer to every other.
Related Topics
Humility; Fellowship; Family
Related Books
Ephesians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to make deference mutual in the body, not a one-way duty of the weaker party.
Duration
For all time
