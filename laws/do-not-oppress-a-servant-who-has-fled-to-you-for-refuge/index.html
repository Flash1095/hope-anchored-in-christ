Do not oppress a servant who has fled to you for refuge

Command
Do not oppress a servant who has fled to you for refuge
Category
Civil Law
Summary
An escaped servant who dwells with you, in the place he chooses within your gates, must not be oppressed.
Commandment
Do not oppress a servant who has fled to you for refuge.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 23:16
Application
Refuge that oppresses is no refuge: the escaped servant dwells where it pleases him best, and is not vexed for having fled. Protection without oppression is the whole of the law's demand toward him.
Related Topics
Mercy; Justice; Compassion
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture states the command without a separate reason beyond the servant's refuge itself (Deuteronomy 23:15-16); TRADITIONAL reading: the escaped servant is treated as a person seeking protection, not property to be restored.
