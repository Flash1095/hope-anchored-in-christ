Rejoice before the LORD on the festivals

Command
Rejoice before the LORD on the festivals
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
At the appointed feasts, Israel rejoices before the LORD — sons, daughters, servants, Levites, strangers, fatherless, and widows together — in the place God chooses.
Commandment
Rejoice before the LORD your God at the appointed feasts, you and your household with the Levite, the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 16:14
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The feasts were not private fasts but shared joy, with the vulnerable deliberately included in the rejoicing. Debated: some see the commanded observance fulfilled as shadow (Colossians 2:16-17); others keep feasts voluntarily as memorial, not as binding command. The site does not settle whether observance continues.

Purpose
Scripture's stated context: the feasts rehearse God's saving acts and his gift of the harvest and the land (Deuteronomy 16:13-15).
