Let the land rest in the sabbatical year

Command
Let the land rest in the sabbatical year
Category
Civil Law
Summary
The land itself keeps a sabbath in the seventh year: a sabbath of rest unto the LORD, in which the ground is not worked as in other years.
Commandment
Let the land rest in the sabbatical year.
Scripture
Leviticus 25:4
Application
The land's sabbath mirrors the people's: one year in seven, work stops and the ground rests. The calendar system belonged to Israel's land order, but the pattern — rest built into the rhythm of labour — still instructs any people tempted to work without ceasing.
Related Topics
Obedience; Mercy
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To prevent permanent destitution and permanent debt in Israel (Leviticus 25; Deuteronomy 15). In the sabbatical year, that purpose takes the form of the land itself resting.
