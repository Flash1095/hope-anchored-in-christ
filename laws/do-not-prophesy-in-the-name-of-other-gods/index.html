Do not prophesy in the name of other gods

Deuteronomy 18:20 sentences the prophet who speaks in the name of other gods: 'even that prophet shall die.' Prophecy in another god's name imports another god's worship with it.

Commandment

Do not prophesy in the name of other gods.

Application

Scripture's stated test: when a prophet speaketh in the name of the LORD, if the thing follow not, nor come to pass, that is the thing which the LORD hath not spoken (Deuteronomy 18:22). A message from another god is false prophecy whatever its form.
