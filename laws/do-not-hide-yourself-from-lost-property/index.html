Do not hide yourself from lost property

Do not hide yourself from your brother's lost thing — his ox, sheep, ass, or garment gone astray: you must not look away and pass by.

Commandment

Do not hide yourself from lost property.

Application

Hiding oneself is the negative form of the return law: looking away is itself the offence. The Good Samaritan story makes the same point in the New Testament — passing by is not neutrality but neglect.

Purpose

Scripture's stated ground: the lost thing belongs to your brother, and you are its keeper until he comes for it (Deuteronomy 22:1-3).

Function

Do not hide yourself from your brother's lost thing — his ox, sheep, ass, or garment gone astray. A civil law, and a practical one: it settled how neighbours lived side by side.
