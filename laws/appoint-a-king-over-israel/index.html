Appoint a king over Israel

Command
Appoint a king over Israel
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 17:15 commands setting a king over Israel when the people ask: 'thou shalt in any wise set him king over thee, whom the LORD thy God shall choose.' The king is God's choice, from among Israel's brethren.
Commandment
Appoint as king over Israel the man whom the LORD your God chooses, from among your brethren.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 17:15
Application
Scripture's stated context: Israel's desire for a king like the nations is answered by God's choice, not by the people's preference alone (Deuteronomy 17:14-15). The Israelite monarchy has passed.
