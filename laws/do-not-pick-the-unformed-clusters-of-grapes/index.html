Do not pick the unformed clusters of grapes

The small, unformed clusters of grapes — the imperfect bunches left on the vine — are not to be picked clean by the owner.

Commandment

Do not pick the unformed clusters of grapes.

Application

Even the imperfect fruit counts as provision: the owner does not strip the vine bare but leaves the small clusters for those who come after the harvest. Generosity is measured by what is left, not only by what is given.

Purpose

God's stated care for those without a defender — widow, orphan, stranger — carried by James into the church as pure religion (James 1:27). In the vineyard, that care takes the form of leaving the unformed clusters unpicked.

Function

This is community law, not temple law: it tells Israel how to live together justly. The small, unformed clusters of grapes — the imperfect bunches left on the vine — stay for the poor. The care God states for those without a defender — widow, orphan, stranger — James carried into the church as pure religion. The evil it addressed was a vineyard stripped bare while the poor went without. It restrained that evil by leaving even the imperfect fruit as provision: what the owner did not want still fed someone.
