Pray Without Ceasing, Give Thanks in Everything

Command
Pray Without Ceasing, Give Thanks in Everything
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
The unceasing-prayer command: rejoice always, pray continually, and give thanks in every circumstance.
Commandment
Rejoice always, pray without ceasing, and give thanks in all circumstances, for this is God's will for you in Christ Jesus.
Scripture
1 Thessalonians 5:16-18; Philippians 4:6-7
Application
Thanksgiving in all circumstances is not denial of suffering but trust inside it — the peace of God guarding hearts that bring everything to him in prayer.
Related Topics
Prayer; Faith; Obedience
Related Books
1 Thessalonians; Philippians
Related People
Paul; Timothy
Purpose
God's stated will for believers in Christ Jesus (1 Thessalonians 5:18): rejoicing, continual prayer, thanks in all circumstances.
Duration
For all time
