Levites shall do the service of the Tent of Meeting

Command
Levites shall do the service of the Tent of Meeting
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: Levites shall do the service of the Tent of Meeting (Numbers 18:23). The Levites bore the iniquity of the sanctuary's service and did the work of the tent of meeting.
Commandment
Levites shall do the service of the Tent of Meeting, as prescribed in Numbers 18:23.
Scripture
Numbers 18:23
Application
This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah: the Levites did the work of the tent of meeting and bore the charge of the sanctuary. It cannot be practised as written without the standing tabernacle/temple; its enduring lesson is ordered, God-appointed service in worship.
Related Topics
Priesthood
Related Books
Numbers; Hebrews
Related People
Levi
Purpose
Adapted from the tithes parent: the Levites' service was sustained by the tithe because they had no land inheritance among Israel (Numbers 18:23-24); the service itself belonged to the tent of meeting.
