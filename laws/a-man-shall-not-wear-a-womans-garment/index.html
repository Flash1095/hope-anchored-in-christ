Commandment

A man shall not wear a woman's garment

Application

Scripture's stated ground: for all that do so are abomination unto the LORD thy God (Deuteronomy 22:5). Debated: whether the verse binds specific garments today or the broader distinction it expresses is a genuine difference of reading — this row does not settle it.

Purpose

Scripture's stated ground: for all that do so are abomination unto the LORD thy God (Deuteronomy 22:5).

Function

A moral law in its traditional classing: it addressed character and conduct within Israel. One half of the Deuteronomy 22:5 distinction: a man shall not wear a woman's garment. Scripture gives the ground: for all that do so are abomination unto the LORD thy God. No particular evil is named here; its work was keeping kinds distinct and marking God's people apart, not restraint.
