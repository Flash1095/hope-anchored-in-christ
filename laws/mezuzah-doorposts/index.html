Mezuzah: Write the Words on Your Doorposts

Command
Mezuzah: Write the Words on Your Doorposts
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
God's words are to be written upon the posts of the house and on the gates. Rabbinic tradition reads this literally as the mezuzah; the Christian reading has generally been that the household itself is to be marked by God's word. The obligation question is debated — see debated_mappings.
Commandment
Write God's words upon the doorposts of your house and upon your gates.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 6:9; Deuteronomy 11:20
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Views differ: Jewish observance affixes a mezuzah literally; many Christians read the verse as a picture of a home governed by God's word, and do not treat the object as a continuing Christian obligation. Either way, the point stands: God's word belongs at the threshold of daily life, not only in the place of worship.

Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: the home is marked as belonging to the LORD, so that God's word meets everyone entering and leaving. Scripture states the act, not a separate reason beyond the Shema context (Deuteronomy 6:4-9).
