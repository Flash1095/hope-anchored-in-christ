An heiress shall marry within her father's tribe

Command
An heiress shall marry within her father's tribe
Category
Civil Law
Summary
An heiress — a daughter who inherits because there is no son — marries within the family of her father's tribe, so that the inheritance stays in the tribe.
Commandment
An heiress shall marry within her father's tribe.
Scripture
Numbers 36:8
Application
The heiress's marriage rule is the complement of her inheritance right: she inherits, and her marriage keeps the inheritance where it belongs. The daughters of Zelophehad married within Manasseh, and the law was kept.
Related Topics
Family; Justice; Marriage
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To keep family land within the tribe and provide for daughters where there was no son (Numbers 27; 36). Here that purpose takes the form of an heiress marrying within her father's tribe.
