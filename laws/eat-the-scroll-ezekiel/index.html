Eat the Scroll

God's command to Ezekiel: eat the roll he is given, and go speak to the house of Israel.

Commandment

Son of man, eat that thou findest; eat this roll, and go speak unto the house of Israel.

Application

A sign-act command at Ezekiel's commission: eat the roll, then go speak to Israel (Ezekiel 3:1, 3:4). The eating is the distinctive commanded deed, which is why it is rowed while generic 'prophesy' commissions are not.

Purpose

To internalize God's word before speaking it — sweet as honey in the mouth (Ezekiel 3:3) — so the prophet speaks from within, not from outside.

Function

Eat the roll, then go speak to Israel — the word internalized before it is spoken. This was a one-time command, given once at Ezekiel's commission, so the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not apply to it. Its goal was to internalize God's word before speaking it — sweet as honey in the mouth — so the prophet speaks from within, not from outside. The evil or restraint angle does not apply here; this was a commissioning act, not a law against a recurring evil. The eating is the distinctive commanded deed, which is why it stands as its own entry.
