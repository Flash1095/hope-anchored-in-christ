Eat the Scroll

Command
Eat the Scroll
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's command to Ezekiel: eat the roll he is given, and go speak to the house of Israel.
Commandment
Son of man, eat that thou findest; eat this roll, and go speak unto the house of Israel.
Scripture
Ezekiel 3:1-3
Application
A sign-act command at Ezekiel's commission: eat the roll, then go speak to Israel (Ezekiel 3:1, 3:4). The eating is the distinctive commanded deed, which is why it is rowed while generic 'prophesy' commissions are not.
Related Topics
Prophecy; Obedience
Related Books
Ezekiel
Related People
Ezekiel
Purpose
To internalize God's word before speaking it — sweet as honey in the mouth (Ezekiel 3:3) — so the prophet speaks from within, not from outside.
