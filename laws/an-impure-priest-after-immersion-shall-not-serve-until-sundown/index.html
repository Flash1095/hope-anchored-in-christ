An impure priest after immersion shall not serve until sundown

Command
An impure priest after immersion shall not serve until sundown
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: An impure priest after immersion shall not serve until sundown (Leviticus 22:7). A priest who washed for uncleanness remained unclean until evening, and only then might eat of the holy things.
Commandment
An impure priest after immersion shall not serve until sundown.
Scripture
Leviticus 22:7
Application
This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah. The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The New Testament replaces the whole system of holy food: all who are in Christ are a holy priesthood (1 Peter 2:5,9), and the meal that marks God's people is the Lord's Supper, not the priestly portions. The boundary itself — holy things are not common things — is fulfilled, not continued.

Purpose
