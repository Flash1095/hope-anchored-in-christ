Release a Hebrew servant in the seventh year

Command
Release a Hebrew servant in the seventh year
Category
Civil Law
Summary
A Hebrew servant was to serve six years and go free in the seventh, so that servitude in Israel could never become permanent bondage for a fellow Hebrew.
Commandment
Release a Hebrew servant in the seventh year, after six years of service.
Scripture
Exodus 21:2
Application
The six-year limit restrained an ancient institution rather than endorsing it. Paul later points past regulation to brotherhood (Philemon 16); the institution these laws governed has ended, but the restraint they taught — no permanent grip on another person's liberty — remains instructive.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy; Family
Related Books
Exodus; Leviticus; Deuteronomy; Philemon
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To regulate and restrain an ancient institution: six-year release, provision, no ruthless rule (Exodus 21; Deuteronomy 15). The laws regulated servanthood, not endorsed it, and the institution they governed has ended.
