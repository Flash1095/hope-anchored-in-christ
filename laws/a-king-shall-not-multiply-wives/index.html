A king shall not multiply wives

Command
A king shall not multiply wives
Category
Civil Law
Summary
One of the restraints on Israel's king in Deuteronomy 17:16-17: a king shall not multiply wives. The king was to be kept from the usual machinery of ancient power.
Commandment
A king shall not multiply wives
Scripture
Deuteronomy 17:17
Application
Scripture's stated reasons: that his heart turn not away (wives, Deuteronomy 17:17), and that the people not be returned to Egypt (the horses restraint, Deuteronomy 17:16). Power was to rest on God's law, not on armies, wealth, or alliances.
