Write What You See

Command
Write What You See
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
The risen Christ's command to John on Patmos: write what you see in a book and send it to the seven churches.
Commandment
What thou seest, write in a book, and send it unto the seven churches which are in Asia.
Scripture
Revelation 1:11; Revelation 1:19
Application
The writing commission of Revelation: what John sees, he must write in a book and send to the seven churches (Revelation 1:11), and write the things which are, and which shall be hereafter (1:19). The seven letters (chapters 2-3) are applications of this one commission, not seven separate commands, and are not separately rowed.
Related Topics
Prophecy; Obedience
Related Books
Revelation
Related People
John the Apostle
Purpose
To preserve the revelation of Jesus Christ in written form for the seven churches and, through them, the church (Revelation 1:1-3).
Duration
One-time
