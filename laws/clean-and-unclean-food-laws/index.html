Clean and Unclean Food Laws

Command
Clean and Unclean Food Laws
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The dietary laws: which creatures may be eaten and which are unclean, marking Israel's table as distinct.
Commandment
Eat only what the Law calls clean; abstain from what it calls unclean.
Scripture
Leviticus 11:1-47; Deuteronomy 14:3-21; Mark 7:18-19; Acts 10:9-16
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The New Testament records these food distinctions set aside: Jesus declares all foods clean, and Peter's vision confirms that God has cleansed what was formerly called common.

Purity; Holiness; Obedience
Related Books
