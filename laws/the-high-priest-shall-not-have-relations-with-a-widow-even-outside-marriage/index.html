The high priest shall not have relations with a widow even outside marriage

Command
The high priest shall not have relations with a widow even outside marriage
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: The high priest shall not have relations with a widow even outside marriage (Leviticus 21:15). The prohibition reached beyond marriage itself: the high priest was not to profane his seed by relations with a widow.
Commandment
The high priest shall not have relations with a widow even outside marriage.
Scripture
Leviticus 21:15
Application
This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah. The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The marriage rules guarded the priesthood's public holiness in a world where priests were public figures; they are not Christian marriage law, which is governed by creation and by the New Testament household commands (Matthew 19:3-9; Ephesians 5:22-33). The high priest's stricter rule — a virgin of his own people — is included here as the second half of the same law.
