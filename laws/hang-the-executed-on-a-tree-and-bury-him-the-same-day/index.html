Hang the executed on a tree and bury him the same day

Command
Hang the executed on a tree and bury him the same day
Category
Civil Law
Summary
When a man has committed a sin worthy of death and is put to death, and is hanged on a tree, his body is taken down and buried the same day.
Commandment
Hang the executed on a tree and bury him the same day.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 21:22
Application
Hanging displayed the sentence, and same-day burial limited it: the body was not left as a lingering spectacle. The procedure belonged to Israel's court system; the dignity it guarded — even the executed are buried, not exposed — still speaks.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: the sentence is displayed and then ended by burial the same day, so that punishment does not become desecration.
