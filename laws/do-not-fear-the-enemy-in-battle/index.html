Do not fear the enemy in battle

Command
Do not fear the enemy in battle
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 20:3 addresses Israel's army before battle: 'Let not your hearts faint, fear not, and do not tremble, neither be ye terrified because of them.' The priest's charge grounds courage in God's presence with the army.
Commandment
Do not fear the enemy in battle; the LORD your God goes with you.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 20:3
Application
The battle setting belonged to ancient Israel's wars, which these laws bounded but do not govern today. The courage itself — fear answered by God's presence — is the enduring lesson for every fear God's people face.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To restrain war even in conquest: offer peace, exempt the newly married and fearful, spare fruit trees (Deuteronomy 20). These laws bounded Israel's wars and do not govern any modern army as Mosaic law.
