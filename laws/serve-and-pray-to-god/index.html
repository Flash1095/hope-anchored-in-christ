Serve and pray to God

Command
Serve and pray to God
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Exodus 23:25 promises blessing on a people who serve the LORD their God: 'ye shall serve the LORD your God, and he shall bless thy bread, and thy water.' Service here is worship in action — prayer, sacrifice, and obedience bound together.
Commandment
Serve the LORD your God, and pray to him alone.
Scripture
Exodus 23:25
Application
Service of God is not one duty among others but the shape of the whole life: bread and water blessed, sickness taken away, as Israel walked in his ways. The church serves the same Lord in prayer and obedience.
Related Topics
Worship; Obedience
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated context: service of God is set against service of the gods of the land, whose worship Israel was to refuse (Exodus 23:24-25).
Duration
For all time
