Present Your Bodies a Living Sacrifice

Command
Present Your Bodies a Living Sacrifice
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's presentation command: present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.
Commandment
I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.
Scripture
Romans 12:1
Application
With sacrifices ended, the body itself becomes the offering — living, not slain; holy, not blemished; daily, not annual. The mercies of God in Romans 1-11 are the ground: 'therefore' carries the whole gospel into the body.
Related Topics
Worship; Holiness; Obedience
Related Books
Romans
Related People
Paul
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'by the mercies of God' (Romans 12:1).
Duration
For all time
