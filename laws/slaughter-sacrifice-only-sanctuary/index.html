Slaughter and Sacrifice Only at the Sanctuary

Command
Slaughter and Sacrifice Only at the Sanctuary
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
What man soever killeth an ox, lamb, or goat in the camp and bringeth it not to the tabernacle door is cut off — and they shall no more offer their sacrifices unto devils. This Leviticus 17 centralization law is outside every live row's range (Sacrifices covers Leviticus 1-7; Priesthood covers Leviticus 8-10).
Commandment
Do not slaughter sacrificial animals in the open field or sacrifice to goat-demons; bring every offering to the tabernacle door.
Scripture
Leviticus 17:3-9
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The New Testament gives the reason the centralization ended: the one sacrifice of Christ replaced the many, and worship is no longer bound to one place (John 4:21-24; Hebrews 10:11-14). Sacrifices to goat-demons and private altars were the exact rivals this law excluded.
Related Topics
Sacrifice; Worship; Holiness
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses; Aaron
Purpose
Scripture's stated reason: that they may no more offer their sacrifices unto devils, after whom they have gone a whoring (Leviticus 17:7).
