Build the Ark

God's command to Noah: make an ark of gopher wood, with rooms, pitched within and without, to the dimensions and pattern given.

Commandment

Make thee an ark of gopher wood, with rooms, pitched within and without with pitch.

Application

The user's own example of a one-time command: temporary, completed, never repeated. Noah did according to all God commanded him (Genesis 6:22). Rowed separately from entering and leaving the ark, which are distinct imperatives at distinct moments.

Purpose

To preserve Noah, his family, and the living creatures through the flood judgment God had announced (Genesis 6:17-18).

Function

A one-time command, given once and never repeated: temporary and completed. It was given once, to Noah, for a specific moment — make an ark of gopher wood, to the dimensions and pattern given — so the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not apply to it at all. Its goal was to preserve Noah, his family, and the living creatures through the flood judgment God had announced. The evil or restraint angle does not apply here; this was rescue from judgment, not a law restraining a recurring evil. Noah did according to all God commanded him.
