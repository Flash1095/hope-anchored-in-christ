Support a poor brother or stranger living with you

Command
Support a poor brother or stranger living with you
Category
Civil Law
Summary
If a brother or a stranger living with you becomes poor and falls into decay, you shall relieve him, that he may live with you.
Commandment
Support a poor brother or stranger living with you.
Scripture
Leviticus 25:35
Application
Relief here is personal and practical: the poor man is kept living beside you, not shipped elsewhere or left to sink. The duty to sustain a needy neighbour outlives the land system it was first given in — it is pure religion in any age (James 1:27).
Related Topics
Mercy; Generosity; Love
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
God's stated care for those without a defender — widow, orphan, stranger — carried by James into the church as pure religion (James 1:27). Here that care takes the form of relieving a poor brother or stranger so he may live with you.
Duration
For all time
