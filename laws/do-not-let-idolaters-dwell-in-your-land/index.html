Do not let idolaters dwell in your land

Exodus 23:33 forbids letting the idolatrous nations remain in the land, 'lest they make thee sin against me: for if thou serve their gods, it will surely be a snare unto thee.' The danger named is not the neighbours as such but their worship, which would snare Israel.

Commandment

Do not let idolaters dwell in your land, lest their worship become a snare to you.

Application

This was a conquest-era land policy for ancient Israel, not a rule for the church's neighbours today. Its enduring lesson is the snare principle: tolerated idolatry spreads, and God's people guard their worship accordingly.

Purpose

Scripture's stated reason: lest they make thee sin against me, for if thou serve their gods, it will surely be a snare unto thee (Exodus 23:33).

Function

A moral law in its traditional classing: it addressed character and conduct within Israel. Exodus 23:33 forbids letting the idolatrous nations remain in the land, 'lest they make thee sin against me.
