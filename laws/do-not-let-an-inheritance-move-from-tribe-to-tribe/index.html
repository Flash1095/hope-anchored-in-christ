Do not let an inheritance move from tribe to tribe

The inheritance of the children of Israel must not move from tribe to tribe: each tribe keeps the inheritance of its fathers.

Commandment

Do not let an inheritance move from tribe to tribe.

Application

Tribal allotments were a permanent trust, not a market: marriage and inheritance law together kept each tribe's land within the tribe. The tribal land system ended with Israel's polity, but the care for a family's footing in the land is the law's lasting lesson.

Purpose

To keep family land within the tribe and provide for daughters where there was no son (Numbers 27; 36). Here that purpose takes the form of inheritance that does not move between tribes.

Function

The civil order shows here — a rule keeping the common life honest and whole. The inheritance of the children of Israel must not move from tribe to tribe.
