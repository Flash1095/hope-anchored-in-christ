Help your neighbour's fallen animal

Command
Help your neighbour's fallen animal
Category
Civil Law
Summary
If you see your neighbour's ass or ox fallen under its burden, do not hide yourself: help him lift it — and the same for the animal of one who hates you.
Commandment
Help your neighbour's fallen animal.
Scripture
Exodus 23:5
Application
Jesus appeals to the same instinct: which of you, having an ox fallen into a pit, will not straightway pull him out on the Sabbath day (Luke 14:5)? Mercy to a neighbour's animal, even an enemy's, is commanded help, not optional kindness.
Related Topics
Mercy; Compassion; Neighbor
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated ground: the animal is under a burden it cannot bear, and the owner's need — even an enemy owner's need — obliges help (Exodus 23:5; Deuteronomy 22:4).
Duration
For all time
