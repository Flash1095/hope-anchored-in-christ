Do Not Bear False Witness

Command
Do Not Bear False Witness
Category
Ten Commandments
Summary
The ninth commandment: truth must be told, especially where another person's name and standing are at stake.
Commandment
Do not testify falsely against another person.
Scripture
Exodus 20:16; Deuteronomy 5:20; John 8:44
Application
False witness destroys a neighbor's reputation and corrupts justice. Jesus contrasts truth-telling with the devil, whom he calls the father of lies.
Related Topics
Truth; Justice
Related Books
Exodus; Deuteronomy; John
Related People
Moses
Related Events
The Ten Commandments
Purpose
To protect a neighbor's name and the integrity of justice; false testimony corrupts both.
Duration
For all time
