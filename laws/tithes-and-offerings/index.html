Tithes and Offerings
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The giving laws: a tenth of produce and livestock for the Levites and the poor, plus firstfruits and freewill offerings.
Commandment
Give a tenth of your produce and livestock as the Law directs, and bring firstfruits and offerings to the LORD.
Scripture
Leviticus 27:30-33; Numbers 18:21-32; Deuteronomy 14:22-29; Malachi 3:8-10; 2 Corinthians 9:7
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The New Testament shifts the frame from a fixed percentage to cheerful, generous giving — God loves a cheerful giver — while keeping the duty to support ministry and the poor.
