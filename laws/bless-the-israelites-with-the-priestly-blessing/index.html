Bless the Israelites with the priestly blessing

The command: Bless the Israelites with the priestly blessing (Numbers 6:23). Aaron and his sons put the LORD's name upon Israel with the threefold blessing: the LORD bless thee, and keep thee.

Commandment

Bless the Israelites with the priestly blessing, as prescribed in Numbers 6:23.

Application

It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.

Purpose

Scripture's stated purpose: they shall put my name upon the children of Israel, and I will bless them (Numbers 6:27).

Function

Aaron and his sons put the LORD's name upon Israel with the threefold blessing — the LORD bless thee, and keep thee — a ceremonial law of spoken blessing. Its goal was to put God's name upon the children of Israel, and God himself promised: I will bless them. It did not address a particular evil; its work was blessing and belonging, not restraint. The blessing said the people carried God's name, and the name carried God's blessing with it.
