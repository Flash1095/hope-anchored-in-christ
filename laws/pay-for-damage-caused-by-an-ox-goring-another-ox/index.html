Pay for damage caused by an ox goring another ox

If one man's ox gores another man's ox so that it dies, the living ox is sold and the price divided, and the dead ox divided as well; if the ox was known to gore, the owner pays ox for ox.

Commandment

Pay for damage caused by an ox goring another ox, as the Law scales it to the owner's knowledge.

Application

Even animal against animal has its measure: shared loss where no fault was known, full repayment where danger was known and ignored. The distinction between accident and culpable negligence runs through the whole law of damages.

Purpose

To make wrongs right: damage between neighbours' animals is shared or repaid according to what the owner knew.

Function
