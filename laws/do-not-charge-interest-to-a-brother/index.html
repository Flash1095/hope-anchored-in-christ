Do not charge interest to a brother

A lender must not charge interest — usury — to a fellow Israelite, above all to the poor man who borrows out of need.

Commandment

Do not charge interest to a brother.

Application

Lending to a brother in need was help, not business: profit from a brother's poverty was forbidden. The enduring principle is that need must not be turned into a revenue stream, even where modern commercial lending raises questions the ancient law did not directly address.

Purpose

To make wrongs right and to protect the poor: a brother's need was not an occasion for profit.

Function

A lender must not charge interest — usury — to a fellow Israelite. Put this with the civil laws: it is about how people treat each other under one government.
