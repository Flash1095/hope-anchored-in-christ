Offer the additional Sabbath offering

The command: Offer the additional Sabbath offering (Numbers 28:9). On the Sabbath, besides the daily offering, two more lambs with their meal and drink offerings were offered.

Commandment

Offer the additional Sabbath offering, as prescribed in Numbers 28:9.

Application

This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah. The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Hebrews teaches these sacrifices could never perfect the worshiper; they pointed forward to Christ's once-for-all offering, which is why they ceased with the new covenant.

Purpose

To provide atonement, thanksgiving, and fellowship under the old covenant. Hebrews teaches these pointed forward to Christ's once-for-all offering (Hebrews 10:1-14), so the system ceased.

Function

On the Sabbath, besides the daily offering, two more lambs with their meal and drink offerings were added — a ceremonial law that marked the day with extra worship. Its goal was to make the Sabbath visibly richer at the altar, the day of rest carrying a fuller offering. It did not address a particular evil; its work was honouring holy time, not restraint. The extra lambs said the Sabbath was not an ordinary day with the ordinary offering; it was God's day, and the altar showed it.
