Do not make figures of human beings

Command
Do not make figures of human beings
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Exodus 20:23 forbids making gods of silver and gods of gold alongside the LORD — in the traditional reading, figures fashioned in human form for worship. The verse follows the ban on other gods and graven images, closing off the most dignified form of idolatry: an image that looks like a person.
Commandment
Do not make figures of human beings as gods, whether of silver or of gold, to set beside the LORD.
Scripture
Exodus 20:23
Application
Idolatry is not excused by fine materials or lifelike form. Worship belongs to the unseen God alone, who cannot be contained in any figure his creatures make.
Related Topics
Idolatry; Worship
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: the prohibition guards the second commandment — no image, however noble its form, may stand beside or represent the LORD (Exodus 20:4-5, 23).
Duration
For all time
