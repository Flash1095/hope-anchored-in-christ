Be fruitful and multiply

Command
Be fruitful and multiply
Category
Moral Law
Summary
The creation mandate, restated to Noah after the flood: humanity is to multiply and fill the earth.
Commandment
Be fruitful, multiply, and fill the earth.
Scripture
Genesis 1:28
Application
Given first in Eden and repeated as the first word of the post-flood world, this is a standing mandate, not a moment's task. The Genesis 1 and Genesis 9 utterances are one command restated, not two commands.
Related Topics
Creation; Family; Obedience
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Adam; Eve; Noah
Related Events
Creation
Purpose
To fill the earth with image-bearers who carry God's blessing outward; Genesis 1:28 joins it to dominion, and Genesis 9:1-7 restates it as the foundation of the renewed world.
Duration
For all time
