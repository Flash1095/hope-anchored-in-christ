Court shall carry out the laws of plaintiff, admitter, and denier

In a dispute over goods, where one claims a deposit and the other denies or admits part, the court carries out the laws of plaintiff, admitter, and denier — including oath and double payment where the Law assigns them.

Commandment

The court shall carry out the laws of plaintiff, admitter, and denier.

Application

Disputes are not settled by who shouts loudest but by the Law's procedures: claim, admission, denial, and oath each have their consequence. Orderly procedure protects both the claimant and the accused from arbitrary judgment.

Purpose

To make wrongs right: disputed deposits are settled by the Law's fixed procedures for plaintiff, admitter, and denier.

Function

In a dispute over goods, where one claims a deposit and the other denies or admits part. Not worship but government: the Law acting as Israel's civil code.
