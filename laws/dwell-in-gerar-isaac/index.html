Dwell in Gerar, Do Not Go to Egypt

Command
Dwell in Gerar, Do Not Go to Egypt
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's command to Isaac in famine: do not go down to Egypt; dwell in the land God would tell him of.
Commandment
Go not down into Egypt; dwell in the land which I shall tell thee of.
Scripture
Genesis 26:2-3
Application
A person-specific redirection: Isaac, unlike his father in an earlier famine, was told to stay. He dwelt in Gerar (Genesis 26:6). The covenant promises to Abraham were restated to him in the same speech.
Related Topics
Faith; Covenant; Obedience
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Isaac
Related Events
Isaac in Gerar
Purpose
To keep Isaac in the promised land through famine so the Abrahamic covenant would be established with him there (Genesis 26:3-4).
Duration
One-time
