The Twelve's Mission Charge

Command
The Twelve's Mission Charge
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' charge to the Twelve on their first mission: preach that the kingdom is at hand, heal the sick, cleanse lepers, raise the dead, cast out devils, freely give — wise as serpents, harmless as doves, shaking dust from unwelcoming towns.
Commandment
Go, preach, saying, The kingdom of heaven is at hand. Heal the sick, cleanse the lepers, raise the dead, cast out devils: freely ye have received, freely give.
Scripture
Matthew 10:5-15; Matthew 10:16; Mark 6:7-13; Luke 9:1-6
Application
Grouped as one mission charge because it was given as one sending. Whether its details (no bag, no staff, shaking dust) bind later missions or belonged to that sending is debated; its preaching, healing, and freely-giving heart is the enduring pattern churches draw from it.
