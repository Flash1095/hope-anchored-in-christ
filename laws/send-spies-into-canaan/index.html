Send Spies into Canaan

God's command to Moses in the wilderness of Paran: send men to search the land of Canaan.

Commandment

Send thou men, that they may search the land of Canaan, which I give unto the children of Israel.

Application

One sending, twelve men, one land. The spies' bad report and Israel's unbelief followed (Numbers 13:31-14:4), but the sending itself was commanded, not a human initiative — which is why it belongs here and not as mere narrative.

Purpose

To search the promised land before Israel's entry, as God gave it to them (Numbers 13:2).

Function

One sending, twelve men, one land. One-time and one-moment: the threefold division does not sort this one. Its goal was to search the promised land before Israel's entry, as God gave it to them. The evil or restraint angle does not apply here; the sending itself was commanded, not a human initiative, and the evil that followed — the spies' bad report and Israel's unbelief — was the people's response to it, not what the command was given to restrain.
