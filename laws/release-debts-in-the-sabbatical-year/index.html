Release debts in the sabbatical year

Command
Release debts in the sabbatical year
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Every seventh year is a year of release: the LORD's release is proclaimed, and debts among brothers are released.
Commandment
Release debts in the sabbatical year.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 15:2
Application
The sabbatical release stopped debt from becoming a life sentence: every seven years the slate among brothers was wiped. The calendar system belonged to Israel's land order, but the warning against permanent debt still speaks to any age of easy credit.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy; Money
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To prevent permanent destitution and permanent debt in Israel (Leviticus 25; Deuteronomy 15). Here that purpose takes the form of debts released every seventh year.
Duration
Temporary
