Do not demand payment of a released debt

Command
Do not demand payment of a released debt
Category
Civil Law
Summary
In the sabbatical year of release, a creditor must not demand payment of a debt from his neighbour or brother whose debt has been released.
Commandment
Do not demand payment of a released debt.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 15:2
Application
Release means release: once the sabbatical year has cancelled the debt, pursuing it again undoes the year's mercy. The creditor's loss is real, and the Law asks him to bear it as his share of the community's reset.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy; Money
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To prevent permanent destitution and permanent debt in Israel (Leviticus 25; Deuteronomy 15). Here that purpose takes the form of debts no longer demanded once released.
Duration
Temporary
