Do not demand payment of a released debt

In the sabbatical year of release, a creditor must not demand payment of a debt from his neighbour or brother whose debt has been released.

Commandment

Do not demand payment of a released debt.

Application

Release means release: once the sabbatical year has cancelled the debt, pursuing it again undoes the year's mercy. The creditor's loss is real, and the Law asks him to bear it as his share of the community's reset.

Purpose

To prevent permanent destitution and permanent debt in Israel (Leviticus 25; Deuteronomy 15). Here that purpose takes the form of debts no longer demanded once released.

Function

A civil law, and a practical one: it settled how neighbours lived side by side. In the sabbatical year of release, the creditor must not demand payment of a debt already released. What it wanted was to prevent permanent destitution and permanent debt in Israel (Leviticus 25; Deuteronomy 15). The evil it addressed was debt that never ended — a neighbour bound for life to what he owed. It restrained that evil by making the release real: the creditor's loss is genuine, and the Law asks him to bear it as his share of the community's reset.
