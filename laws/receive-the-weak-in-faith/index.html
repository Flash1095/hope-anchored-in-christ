Receive the Weak in Faith; Do Not Judge a Brother's Conscience

Command
Receive the Weak in Faith; Do Not Judge a Brother's Conscience
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's reception command: receive him that is weak in the faith, but not to doubtful disputations; let not him that eateth despise him that eateth not, and let every man be fully persuaded in his own mind.
Commandment
Him that is weak in the faith receive ye, but not to doubtful disputations.
Scripture
Romans 14:1; Romans 14:5; Romans 14:13
Application
The weak brother is received, not cross-examined: food days and holy days are conscience matters where each stands before his own Master. The strong bear, the weak are not despised, and judgment of a brother's servant is forbidden.
Related Topics
Fellowship; Discernment; Peace
Related Books
Romans
Related People
Paul
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to keep conscience questions from breaking fellowship, with each believer answerable to God.
Duration
For all time
