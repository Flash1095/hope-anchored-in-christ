Do not consult the dead

One of the occult practices forbidden in Deuteronomy 18 and Leviticus 19: do not consult the dead. Israel was not to seek guidance from the dead or from hidden powers as the nations did.

Commandment

Do not consult the dead

Application

Scripture's stated reason: for all that do these things are an abomination unto the LORD (Deuteronomy 18:12). God's people seek him alone for guidance, through his word and his prophets.
