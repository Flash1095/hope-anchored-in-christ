Household Commands

Command
Household Commands
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
The household code: wives and husbands, children and parents, servants and masters — each with duties shaped by Christ's own pattern.
Commandment
Wives, submit to your husbands as to the Lord; husbands, love your wives as Christ loved the church; children, obey your parents; parents, do not provoke your children.
Scripture
Ephesians 5:22-33; Ephesians 6:1-9; Colossians 3:18-25; 1 Peter 3:1-7
Application
The code's most radical stroke is its address to the powerful: husbands must love as Christ loved — giving himself up — and masters must remember they too have a Master in heaven.
Related Topics
Marriage; Family; Love
Related Books
Ephesians; Colossians; 1 Peter
Related People
Paul
Purpose
Household duties shaped by Christ's pattern (Ephesians 5-6). Debated: churches differ on how the wives/husbands, children/parents, servants/masters instructions apply today — as enduring order, or as a first-century household code whose Christ-shaped mutuality (husbands loving as Christ loved) governs application. The site does not settle this.
