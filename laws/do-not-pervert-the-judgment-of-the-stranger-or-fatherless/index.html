Do not pervert the judgment of the stranger or fatherless

Do not pervert the judgment of the stranger or of the fatherless: each one's cause is judged on its merits, without bending toward the stronger party.

Commandment

Do not pervert the judgment of the stranger or fatherless.

Application

This mitzvah names the stranger and the fatherless expressly, apart from the widow: two of the defenceless, whose causes a court is most tempted to bend toward a native or a patron. Their judgment is not to be wrested for any reason.

Purpose

God's stated care for those without a defender — widow, orphan, stranger — carried by James into the church as pure religion (James 1:27). Here that care forbids perverting the judgment of stranger or fatherless.

Function

Do not pervert the judgment of the stranger or of the fatherless: each one's cause is judged on its merits. Civil law, governing life together in Israel: courts, property, neighbours, not worship.
