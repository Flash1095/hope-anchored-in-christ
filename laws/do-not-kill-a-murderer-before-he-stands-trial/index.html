Do not kill a murderer before he stands trial

Command
Do not kill a murderer before he stands trial
Category
Civil Law
Summary
A manslayer must not be killed before he stands before the congregation in judgment: the avenger of blood may not strike before trial.
Commandment
Do not kill a murderer before he stands trial.
Scripture
Numbers 35:12
Application
Trial before punishment is the cities-of-refuge law's first guard: even the avenger waits for a hearing, because intent must be judged, not assumed. The cities belonged to Israel's land system and no longer function; the fair-trial principle they embodied endures as justice wisdom.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy
Related Books
Numbers; Deuteronomy; Joshua
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To balance life's sacredness with intent: the unintentional killer fled to a city and stood trial, safe from vengeance (Numbers 35). Here that balance forbids killing before trial.
