Remove the Unclean from the Camp

Command
Remove the Unclean from the Camp
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
Command the children of Israel, that they put out of the camp every leper, and every one that hath an issue, and whosoever is defiled by the dead — male and female alike. This Numbers 5 camp rule is distinct from the live Purity row, whose range is Leviticus 12-15.
Commandment
Put outside the camp every leper, everyone with a discharge, and everyone defiled by the dead, until cleansed.
Scripture
Numbers 5:1-4
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The camp was where God dwelt among his people, so uncleanness was kept outside it until cleansed. The New Testament reverses the direction: Jesus touches the leper and the unclean are brought in, not sent out (Mark 1:40-45; Luke 8:43-48, both cited on the live Purity row) — which is why this camp rule is temporary, not continuing.
