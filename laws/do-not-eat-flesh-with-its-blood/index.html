Do Not Eat Flesh with Its Blood

Command
Do Not Eat Flesh with Its Blood
Category
Pre-Mosaic Commands
Summary
The Noahic food prohibition: flesh with its lifeblood still in it must not be eaten.
Commandment
Do not eat flesh with the life thereof, which is the blood thereof.
Scripture
Genesis 9:4
Application
Given to Noah and his sons as part of the post-flood order, before Sinai and before any Jewish identity existed. Debated: some hold this Noahic prohibition still binds all humanity and note the Jerusalem council's abstain-from-blood instruction (Acts 15:29); others hold it belonged to the pre-Mosaic order and passed with the old covenant's food laws. The site does not settle this.

Related Events

Purpose
To honor life as residing in the blood; Genesis 9:4 states the prohibition without a stated expiry, which is why its duration is debated rather than settled here.
