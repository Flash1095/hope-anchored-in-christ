Go Down to Egypt Without Fear

God's command to Jacob at Beersheba: fear not to go down into Egypt, for God would make him a great nation there and bring him up again.

Commandment

Fear not to go down into Egypt; for I will there make of thee a great nation.

Application

Jacob was already on the road to Joseph when God spoke; the command removed the fear of leaving the promised land by promising both presence in Egypt and return. No story title matches Jacob's descent itself (Joseph and His Brothers is Joseph's story), so RelatedEvents is left empty.

Purpose

To authorize Jacob's descent to Joseph in Egypt with the promise of nationhood there and eventual return (Genesis 46:3-4).

Function

Jacob was already on the road to Joseph when God spoke; the command removed the fear of leaving the promised land. A command for one moment only, so the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not apply. Its goal was to authorize Jacob's descent to Joseph in Egypt with the promise of nationhood there and eventual return. The evil or restraint angle does not apply here; this was reassurance for one journey, not a law against a recurring evil.
