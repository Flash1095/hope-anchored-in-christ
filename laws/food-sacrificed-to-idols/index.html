Food Sacrificed to Idols

Command
Food Sacrificed to Idols
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
The idol-food instruction: do not eat food sacrificed to idols in a way that betrays conscience — your own or a brother's. HELD ITEM, drafted on its merits: the borderline doubt is carried here, not resolved — a situational instruction for a first-century problem, whose scope beyond idol-food settings is debated.
Commandment
Do not eat food sacrificed to idols where it would defile conscience or cause a brother to stumble.
Scripture
1 Corinthians 8:1-13; 1 Corinthians 10:14-33; Acts 15:29
Application
Paul balances knowledge (an idol is nothing) with love (knowledge puffs up, love builds up): liberty yields to a brother's conscience. Eating in an idol's temple as worship is condemned outright; eating marketplace meat is a conscience question.
Related Topics
Idolatry; Fellowship; Holiness
Related Books
1 Corinthians; Acts
Related People
Paul
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'if meat make my brother to offend, I will eat no flesh while the world standeth' (1 Corinthians 8:13).
