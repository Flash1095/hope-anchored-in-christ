Offer the Sacrifice of Praise Continually

Command
Offer the Sacrifice of Praise Continually
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Hebrews' praise command: by him let us offer the sacrifice of praise to God continually, the fruit of lips giving thanks to his name.
Commandment
By him therefore let us offer the sacrifice of praise to God continually, that is, the fruit of our lips giving thanks to his name.
Scripture
Hebrews 13:15
Application
With the temple sacrifices ended, praise itself becomes the continual offering — lips confessing his name — joined in the next verse to doing good and sharing, which are also sacrifices well pleasing to God.
Related Topics
Worship; Fellowship; Generosity
Related Books
Hebrews
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to replace ended animal sacrifice with continual praise as the church's offering.
Duration
For all time
