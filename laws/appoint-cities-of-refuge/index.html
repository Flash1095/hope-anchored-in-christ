Appoint cities of refuge

Command
Appoint cities of refuge
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Three cities of refuge are set apart in the land, with roads prepared, so that a manslayer may flee there and live.
Commandment
Appoint cities of refuge.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 19:2
Application
Refuge was prepared in advance, not improvised after a killing: cities set apart and roads maintained, so that flight was possible in the hour of danger. The cities belonged to Israel's land system and no longer function; the foresight they embody — protection prepared before it is needed — remains instructive.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy
Related Books
Numbers; Deuteronomy; Joshua
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To balance life's sacredness with intent: the unintentional killer fled to a city and stood trial, safe from vengeance (Numbers 35). Here that balance takes the form of cities appointed in advance.
