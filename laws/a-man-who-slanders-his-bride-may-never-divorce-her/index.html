A man who slanders his bride may never divorce her

Command
A man who slanders his bride may never divorce her
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 22:19 sentences a man who falsely accuses his bride of unchastity: fined, chastised, and forbidden ever to divorce her. The slander was an attempt to discard a wife on a lie, and the law closed that door permanently.
Commandment
A man who slanders his bride shall never divorce her.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 22:19
Application
Debated: how far these civil penalties carry into the church is genuinely disputed — this row does not settle it. What is not debated is the protection aimed at: a wife is not to be discarded on a false charge.
Related Topics
Marriage; Family; Justice
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Matthew
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To protect the weaker party at each family step (Deuteronomy 21-25). Debated: the divorce certificate is read by Jesus as concession to hardness of heart, not God's design (Matthew 19:8), and levirate marriage ended, while creation marriage itself is reaffirmed.
