A priest's daughter shall not profane herself by prostitution

Command
A priest's daughter shall not profane herself by prostitution
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Leviticus 21:9 addresses the priest's daughter who profanes herself by playing the harlot: she profanes her father, and the sentence is burning. The priest's household shared the priesthood's holiness.
Commandment
A priest's daughter shall not profane herself by prostitution.
Scripture
Leviticus 21:9
Application
Scripture's stated reason: for he is holy unto his God (Leviticus 21:7-8). The priestly system has passed; the call to a household above reproach, and the protection of daughters from exploitation, remains.
