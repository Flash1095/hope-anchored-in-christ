Judge your neighbour righteously

Command
Judge your neighbour righteously
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Judge your neighbour in righteousness: the verdict follows the truth of the cause, not the standing of the parties.
Commandment
Judge your neighbour righteously.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:15
Application
Righteous judgment is the positive side of impartiality — not merely refusing bribes and favour, but actively weighing the cause aright. A righteous verdict is the aim; impartiality is the guardrail that gets the judge there.
Related Topics
Justice; Truth
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: righteous judgment weighs the cause itself, with neither fear nor favour toward the parties.
Duration
For all time
