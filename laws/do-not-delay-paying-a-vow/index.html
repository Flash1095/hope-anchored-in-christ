Do not delay paying a vow

When you vow a vow to the LORD your God, do not be slack to pay it: the vow is paid promptly, not left to drift.

Commandment

Do not delay paying a vow.

Application

Delay turns a vow into a debt dodged: God requires what was promised, and slackness in paying is itself sin. Ecclesiastes agrees — do not be slow to keep what you promised God (Ecclesiastes 5:4-5).

Purpose

Scripture's stated reason: for the LORD thy God will surely require it of thee; and it would be sin in thee (Deuteronomy 23:21).

Function

A working rule the civil courts would have known: for judges and neighbours. When you vow a vow to the LORD your God, do not be slack to pay it.
