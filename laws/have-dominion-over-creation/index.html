Have Dominion Over Creation

Command
Have Dominion Over Creation
Category
Pre-Mosaic Commands
Summary
The creation mandate to subdue the earth and rule over its living creatures as God's stewards.
Commandment
Subdue the earth and have dominion over every living creature.
Scripture
Genesis 1:28
Application
Dominion here is stewardship under God, not exploitation: the same chapter that gives the mandate gives humanity plants for food and calls the world good. It is the standing counterpart to the multiply mandate in the same verse, rowed separately because it commands a different act (rule and care, not increase).
Related Topics
Creation; Obedience
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Adam; Eve
Related Events
Creation
Purpose
To order and care for the earth as God's appointed rulers within it, reflecting his own rule; Genesis 1:28 states the mandate without a stated expiry.
Duration
For all time
