Do not eat vow and freewill offerings outside Jerusalem

The command: Do not eat vow and freewill offerings outside Jerusalem (Deuteronomy 12:17). Vows and freewill offerings were brought to the place God chose and eaten there, not wherever one pleased.

Commandment

Do not eat vow and freewill offerings outside Jerusalem.

Application

It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.

Purpose

Scripture's stated ground: vows and freewill offerings were brought to the place the LORD chose (Deuteronomy 12:17-18).

Function

Vows and freewill offerings were brought to the place God chose and eaten there, not wherever one pleased — a ceremonial law of place. Its goal was to keep even voluntary gifts tied to the one sanctuary, so worship did not scatter into private altars. The evil was fragmented worship, each man eating his vow at home as if the place God chose did not matter. It restrained that by the ban: the vow was paid where God put his name, and the meal happened before him.
