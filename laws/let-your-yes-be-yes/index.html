Let Your Yes Be Yes

Jesus' truth-speaking command: do not swear oaths to shore up your word; let a plain yes be yes and a plain no be no.

Commandment

Do not swear at all; let your communication be Yes, yes and No, no, for whatever is more than these comes from evil.

Application

Oath-taking was being used to grade truthfulness. Jesus calls for speech so truthful that no oath is needed to believe it.

Purpose

Scripture's own reason: what goes beyond plain yes and no 'cometh of evil' (Matthew 5:37).

Function

Plain speech needs no oath to prop it up. Not Mosaic law but a New Testament command — the threefold division does not sort it. Its goal was truthful speech so steady that no one needs to swear to be believed. The evil it addressed was oath-taking used to grade truthfulness — as if some words were true and others only true when sworn. It restrained that evil by the rule Jesus gives: let your yes be yes and your no be no, for whatever goes beyond that comes from evil.
