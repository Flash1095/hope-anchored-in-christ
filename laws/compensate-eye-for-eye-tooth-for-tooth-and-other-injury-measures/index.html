Compensate eye for eye, tooth for tooth, and other injury measures

Injury was to be measured by injury: eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot, burning for burning, wound for wound, stripe for stripe.

Commandment

Compensate injury by the measures the Law sets: eye for eye, tooth for tooth, and the other measures likewise.

Application

This was a court's rule of proportion, capping retaliation at the harm done — never a licence for personal revenge. Jesus later forbids turning it into private vengeance (Matthew 5:38-39), while the principle of proportionate, court-administered justice endures.

Purpose

To make wrongs right in proportion: punishment must match the injury, neither falling short of it nor exceeding it.

Function

Neighbours, not priests: this law belongs to the ordering of the common life. Injury was to be measured by injury: eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.
