Do not work or sow the valley of the heifer

Command
Do not work or sow the valley of the heifer
Category
Civil Law
Summary
In the rite for an unsolved murder, the valley where the heifer's neck is broken is neither worked nor sown: no farming use is made of it.
Commandment
Do not work or sow the valley of the heifer.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 21:4
Application
The rough valley, neither eared nor sown, is set apart for the rite alone: ordinary use would profane the place where the community answers for innocent blood. The rite belonged to Israel's land system and no longer functions.
Related Topics
Justice; Atonement; Mercy
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated purpose: so shall the guilt of innocent blood be put away from among you (Deuteronomy 21:9, in the form of the elders' declaration and the atonement prayer, 21:7-8).
