Do not compel a Hebrew servant to do slave labour

Command
Do not compel a Hebrew servant to do slave labour
Category
Civil Law
Summary
A Hebrew who has sold himself to you because of poverty must not be compelled to do the labour of a slave; he serves as a hired servant and sojourner.
Commandment
Do not compel a Hebrew servant to do slave labour.
Scripture
Leviticus 25:39
Application
The distinction mattered: a Hebrew servant was a brother in hardship, not chattel, and his work was bounded accordingly. The laws regulated an ancient institution rather than endorsing it, and the institution has ended.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To regulate and restrain an ancient institution: six-year release, provision, no ruthless rule (Exodus 21; Deuteronomy 15). Here that restraint forbids slave labour for a Hebrew servant.
