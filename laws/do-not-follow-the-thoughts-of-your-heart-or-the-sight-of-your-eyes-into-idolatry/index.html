Do not follow the thoughts of your heart or the sight of your eyes into idolatry

Numbers 15:39 joins the fringe to its warning: Israel was not to seek after its own heart and its own eyes, after which it used to go a whoring. The wandering heart and eye are the road to idolatry.

Commandment

Do not follow the thoughts of your heart or the sight of your eyes into idolatry.

Application

The fringe reminded; the warning endures without the fringe. Hearts and eyes still lead astray, and God's word still answers them.

Purpose

Scripture's stated reason: that ye may look upon the fringe and remember all the commandments of the LORD and do them, and not follow your own heart and eyes (Numbers 15:39).

Function

Numbers 15:39 joins the fringe to its warning. This binds in every age: a duty of conduct, not a rule of Israel's civil code. Scripture gives the reason: that ye may look upon the fringe and remember all the commandments of the LORD and do them, and not follow your own heart and eyes. The Law answered practically. The fringe reminded; the warning endures without the fringe.
