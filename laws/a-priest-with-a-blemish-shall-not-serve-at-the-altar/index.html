A priest with a blemish shall not serve at the altar

Command
A priest with a blemish shall not serve at the altar
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: A priest with a blemish shall not serve at the altar (Leviticus 21:17). A priest with any blemish might not offer the bread of his God at the altar, though he might eat of the holy food.
Commandment
A priest with a blemish shall not serve at the altar.
Scripture
Leviticus 21:17
Application
This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah. The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The blemish did not make the priest unclean or unwelcome: he still ate the bread of his God, most holy and holy (Leviticus 21:22). He was barred only from approaching to offer — a visible picture that the offering itself must be unblemished, pointing to the unblemished Lamb (1 Peter 1:19).
