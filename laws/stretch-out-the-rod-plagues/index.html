Stretch Out the Rod for the Plagues

Command
Stretch Out the Rod for the Plagues
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's repeated command through the plagues: Moses (and Aaron with the rod) must stretch out hand and rod over Egypt for each sign.
Commandment
Take thy rod, and stretch out thine hand upon the waters of Egypt, and over the signs that follow.
Scripture
Exodus 7:19; Exodus 8:5; Exodus 8:16; Exodus 9:22; Exodus 10:12; Exodus 10:21
Application
A merged row under the one-row-per-distinct-command rule: the rod is stretched out for blood (Exodus 7:19), frogs (8:5), lice (8:16), hail (9:22), locusts (10:12), and darkness (10:21) — one repeated commanded act across the plague series, not six separate laws. The Red Sea stretching is rowed separately because it is a different act with a different outcome. Parent may split per plague if finer granularity is wanted.
Related Topics
Judgment; Obedience; Faith
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses; Aaron
Related Events
The Ten Plagues
Purpose
To bring each plague as a sign against Pharaoh and the gods of Egypt until Israel was let go (Exodus 7:17; 8:10).
Duration
One-time
