Do not eat green ears from the new crop before the sheaf offering

No green ears of the new grain harvest may be eaten until the sheaf of firstfruits has been waved before the LORD.

Commandment

Eat no green ears from the new crop until the day the sheaf offering is brought.

Application

The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The first sheaf went to God before Israel ate of the harvest: firstfruits first, then the table. The New Testament keeps the principle in a new key — Christ is the firstfruits of them that slept (1 Corinthians 15:20).

Purpose

Scripture's stated context: the confession itself gives the reason — 'a Syrian ready to perish was my father,' and God brought Israel out of Egypt and gave them this land (Deuteronomy 26:5-10).

Function

No green ears of the new grain harvest might be eaten until the sheaf of firstfruits had been waved before the LORD — a ceremonial law of the unripe ears. Its goal was to keep even the earliest grain behind the first sheaf, the green ears waiting their turn like the bread and the parched corn. It did not address a particular evil; its work was dedication and order, not restraint. The green ears said the harvest was God's from its first green shoot.
