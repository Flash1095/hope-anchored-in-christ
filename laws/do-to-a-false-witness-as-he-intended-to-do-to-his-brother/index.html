Do to a false witness as he intended to do to his brother

Command
Do to a false witness as he intended to do to his brother
Category
Civil Law
Summary
If a witness is found to be false, the court does to him as he intended to do to his brother: the penalty he sought becomes his own.
Commandment
Do to a false witness as he intended to do to his brother.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 19:19
Application
False witness is punished by its own design: the perjurer aimed a penalty at his brother, and that penalty falls on him. The symmetry deters the gravest courtroom crime, and the deterrent principle endures even where the penalty procedure belonged to Israel's courts.
Related Topics
Justice; Truth
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To secure impartial justice: no bribes, no partiality, no conviction on one witness. Here that purpose takes the form of the false witness bearing the penalty he sought.
