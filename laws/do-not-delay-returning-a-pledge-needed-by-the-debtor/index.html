Do not delay returning a pledge needed by the debtor

If the debtor is poor, you shall not sleep with his pledge: the garment taken in pledge is not kept overnight from a man who needs it.

Commandment

Do not delay returning a pledge needed by the debtor.

Application

A poor man's cloak is his blanket for the night: keeping it overnight takes his sleep, not only his garment. The delay itself is the offence, and the pledge must be back before dark.

Purpose

Scripture's stated reason: that he may sleep in his own raiment, and bless thee (Deuteronomy 24:13). Here that reason forbids sleeping with a poor man's pledge.

Function

If the debtor is poor, you shall not sleep with his pledge. Israel's civil order: a rule to keep the common life honest and whole. Scripture gives the reason: that he may sleep in his own raiment, and bless thee. The duty was placed where it could be kept.
