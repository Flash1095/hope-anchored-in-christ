Remember the Sabbath

Command
Remember the Sabbath
Category
Ten Commandments
Summary
The fourth commandment: one day in seven is set apart for rest and worship, rooted in creation and in Israel's deliverance from Egypt.
Commandment
Keep one day in seven holy: rest from ordinary work and set it apart for God.
Scripture
Exodus 20:8-11; Deuteronomy 5:12-15
Application
Jesus calls himself Lord of the Sabbath and teaches that the Sabbath was made for people, not people for the Sabbath (Mark 2:27-28). Christians differ on whether seventh-day observance continues literally or is fulfilled in the rest Christ gives (Hebrews 4:9-10); both readings are honestly held.

Purpose
Scripture gives two stated grounds: creation rest (Exodus 20:11) and deliverance from Egypt (Deuteronomy 5:15). Debated: some Christians keep a seventh-day Sabbath literally; others see the Sabbath fulfilled in the rest Christ gives (Hebrews 4:9-10). The site does not settle this.
