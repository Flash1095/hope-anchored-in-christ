Do Not Murder

The sixth commandment: human life, made in God's image, must not be taken unlawfully.

Commandment

Do not take a human life unlawfully.

Application

Jesus presses the commandment to the heart: contempt and hatred already break it in spirit. The commandment forbids murder, not lawful judgment or defense, which Scripture treats separately.

Purpose

To guard human life on the stated ground that man is made in God's image (Genesis 9:6).

Function

Life is not ours to take. This sixth of the Ten Words is foundational moral law, traditionally classed moral, binding in every age. Its goal was to guard human life on the ground Scripture states: man is made in God's image. The evil it addressed was murder — unlawful killing, and in Jesus' pressing of it, the contempt and hatred that lead there. It restrained that evil by drawing the line at life itself: no unlawful taking, while lawful judgment and defence are treated separately elsewhere in Scripture.
