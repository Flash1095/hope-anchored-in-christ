Arise, Go to Nineveh

Command
Arise, Go to Nineveh
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's command to Jonah, given twice: arise, go to Nineveh, and cry against it.
Commandment
Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city, and cry against it; for their wickedness is come up before me.
Scripture
Jonah 1:2; Jonah 3:2
Application
Parallels merged: the Jonah 1:2 commission and the Jonah 3:2 recommission are one command given twice (after the fish), not two missions. Jonah's flight is disobedience to this row, and his second going is obedience to it.
Related Topics
Repentance; Obedience; Evangelism
Related Books
Jonah
Related People
Jonah
Related Events
Jonah and Nineveh
Purpose
To warn Nineveh of judgment so the city might repent — and it did, from king to cattle (Jonah 3:5-10).
