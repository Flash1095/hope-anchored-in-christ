Do not desire silver or gold on idols

Command
Do not desire silver or gold on idols
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 7:25 forbids desiring the silver and gold on graven images: 'thou shalt not desire the silver or gold that is on them, nor take it unto thee, lest thou be snared therein: for it is an abomination to the LORD thy God.' The wealth on an idol is still the idol's wealth.
Commandment
Do not desire, or take for yourself, the silver or gold on idols.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 7:25
Application
Profit does not purify idolatry: taking an idol's ornaments home imports the snare with the silver. What God calls abomination is not made clean by being valuable.
Related Topics
Idolatry; Money
Related Books

Related People
