Anoint David as King

Command
Anoint David as King
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's command to Samuel: go to Jesse at Bethlehem, and anoint the son God shows him.
Commandment
Fill thine horn with oil, and go, I will send thee to Jesse the Bethlehemite: for I have provided me a king among his sons. Arise, anoint him: for this is he.
Scripture
1 Samuel 16:1; 1 Samuel 16:12
Application
Samuel was sent to Bethlehem while Saul still reigned, to a son of Jesse God had provided (1 Samuel 16:1). David was anointed in the midst of his brethren, and the Spirit came upon him from that day (16:13). RelatedEvents left empty: David Becomes King covers his later reign, not this secret anointing.
Related Topics
Obedience; Faith
Related Books
1 Samuel
Related People
Samuel; King David
Purpose
To provide Israel a king after God's own heart from Jesse's house while Saul was rejected (1 Samuel 16:1, 16:7).
