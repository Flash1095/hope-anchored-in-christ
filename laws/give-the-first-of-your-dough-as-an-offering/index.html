Give the first of your dough as an offering

The command: Give the first of your dough as an offering (Numbers 15:20). The first of the dough was lifted up as a heave offering to the LORD, as the firstfruits of the threshingfloor.

Commandment

Give the first of your dough as an offering, as prescribed in Numbers 15:20.

Application

It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.

Purpose

Scripture's stated ground: as the firstfruits of the threshingfloor, so the first of the dough was lifted up to the LORD (Numbers 15:20).

Function

The first of the dough was lifted up as a heave offering to the LORD, as the firstfruits of the threshingfloor — a ceremonial law of first giving in bread. Its goal was to claim the baking itself for God, the first dough lifted up before the loaves were eaten. It did not address a particular evil; its work was dedication, not restraint. The dough said the daily bread was God's before it was ours, and the first lump proved it.
