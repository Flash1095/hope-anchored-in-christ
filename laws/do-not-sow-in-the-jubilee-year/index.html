Do not sow in the Jubilee year

In the Jubilee year the field is not sown: the land rests, as in the sabbatical year, and the people live on what it gives of itself.

Commandment

Do not sow in the Jubilee year.

Application

Two fallow years could run together — sabbatical and Jubilee — and the people were promised provision enough to carry them (Leviticus 25:20-22). The command tests trust: release is practised, not merely proclaimed.

Purpose

To prevent permanent destitution and permanent debt in Israel (Leviticus 25; Deuteronomy 15). In the Jubilee year, that purpose takes the form of an unsown field.

Function

This is the Law governing, not the Law worshipping: a civil rule for a real community. In the Jubilee year the field is not sown: the land rests. What it aimed at was to prevent permanent destitution and permanent debt in Israel (Leviticus 25; Deuteronomy 15). No particular evil is named here; its work was rest, rhythm, and trust, not restraint.
