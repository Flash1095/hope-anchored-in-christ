Do not wear a garment of mixed wool and linen

Command
Do not wear a garment of mixed wool and linen
Category
Moral Law
Summary
One of the mixture laws of Leviticus 19:19: do not wear a garment of mixed wool and linen. Scripture states the prohibition without a stated reason.
Commandment
Do not wear a garment of mixed wool and linen
Scripture
Leviticus 19:19; Deuteronomy 22:11
Application
TRADITIONAL reading: the mixture law is classed among the chukim, statutes whose reason Scripture does not state; the priestly garments, which mixed materials by God's own command (Exodus 28), are the noted exception.
Related Topics
Holiness; Obedience
Related Books
Leviticus; Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: no reason is stated in Scripture; the mixture law is classed among the chukim. The one scriptural exception noted by tradition is the priestly garments themselves, which mixed materials by God's own command (Exodus 28).
