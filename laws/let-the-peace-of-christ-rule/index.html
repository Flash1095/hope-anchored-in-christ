Let the Peace of Christ Rule in Your Hearts

Command
Let the Peace of Christ Rule in Your Hearts
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's peace command: let the peace of God rule in your hearts, to which also you are called in one body, and be thankful.
Commandment
And let the peace of God rule in your hearts, to the which also ye are called in one body; and be ye thankful.
Scripture
Colossians 3:15
Application
Peace here is an umpire: where competing claims clash, Christ's peace arbitrates. It rules in hearts called into one body, so its decisions are corporate, not merely private calm.
Related Topics
Peace; Fellowship; Unity
Related Books
Colossians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to let Christ's peace arbitrate disputes rather than pride or fear.
Duration
For all time
