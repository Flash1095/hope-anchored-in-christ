Anoint Saul as King

Command
Anoint Saul as King
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's command to Samuel: anoint Saul to be captain over Israel against the Philistines.
Commandment
Thou shalt anoint Saul to be captain over my people Israel, that he may save my people out of the hand of the Philistines.
Scripture
1 Samuel 9:16; 1 Samuel 10:1
Application
Saul was anointed privately (1 Samuel 10:1) and then chosen publicly by lot (10:20-24). This row covers the command to anoint, not Saul's later reign or his Amalek disobedience (that command is the held Remember Amalek item, not duplicated here).
Related Topics
Obedience; Judgment
Related Books
1 Samuel
Related People
Samuel; King Saul
Related Events
Anointing of Saul as King
Purpose
To give Israel the king they demanded as a captain to save them from the Philistines, while God heard their cry (1 Samuel 9:16).
Duration
One-time
