Do not show mercy to idolaters

Command
Do not show mercy to idolaters
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 7:2, in the conquest commands, forbids showing mercy to the seven nations devoted to destruction: 'thou shalt smite them, and utterly destroy them; thou shalt make no covenant with them, nor shew mercy unto them.' The verse belongs to a specific, unrepeatable campaign against entrenched idolatry in the land.
Commandment
In the conquest campaign, show no mercy to the idolatrous nations devoted to destruction.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 7:2
Application
This command in its stated form governed ancient Israel's conquest, not the church's treatment of unbelievers today — the gospel commands mercy to all. Its enduring point is separation from idolatry itself, which is never to be spared a place in God's people's worship.
Related Topics
Idolatry; Justice
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: in the conquest setting, sparing the idolatrous nations would leave their idolatry standing in the land (Deuteronomy 7:4-5); the separation from idolatry is the enduring point, not mercy as such.
