Do not curse your father or mother

Command
Do not curse your father or mother
Category
Civil Law
Summary
A child who curses his father or his mother is not to do so: the Law treats cursing a parent as a grave offence against parental honour.
Commandment
Do not curse your father or mother.
Scripture
Leviticus 20:9
Application
Cursing is more than rudeness: it is the spoken rejection of the honour owed to parents. The death penalty belonged to Israel's court, but the prohibition itself — honour parents in word as well as deed — endures wherever the fifth commandment is honoured.
Related Topics
Family; Justice
Related Books
Leviticus; Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: parents bear God-given authority in the family, so cursing them strikes at the order God set; honour for parents is owed in speech as well as in deed.
Duration
For all time
