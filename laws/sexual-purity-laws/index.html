Sexual Purity Laws

Command
Sexual Purity Laws
Category
Moral Law
Summary
The holiness code's sexual laws: forbidden unions and practices that marked Israel as distinct from the surrounding nations.
Commandment
Keep sexual relations within the bounds God sets; do not practice the forbidden unions named in the Law.
Scripture
Leviticus 18:1-30; 1 Corinthians 6:18-20; Hebrews 13:4
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as moral. The New Testament reaffirms sexual purity for believers, grounding it in the body as the temple of the Holy Spirit and in marriage as honorable.

Holiness; Marriage; Sin
Related Books
Leviticus; 1 Corinthians; Hebrews
