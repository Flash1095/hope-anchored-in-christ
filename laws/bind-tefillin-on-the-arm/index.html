Bind tefillin on the arm

Deuteronomy 6:8 commands binding God's words as a sign upon the hand: 'thou shalt bind them for a sign upon thine hand.' The arm binding carries the words into action.

Commandment

Bind God's words as a sign upon your arm.

Application

Scripture's stated reason: that the LORD's law may be in your mouth, remembering the Exodus (Exodus 13:9). Debated: whether the binding remains a literal obligation is a genuine difference between Jewish practice and Christian readings — this row does not settle it.

Purpose

Scripture's stated reason: that the LORD's law may be in your mouth, remembering deliverance from Egypt by strength of hand (Exodus 13:9).

Function

A moral law in its traditional classing: it addressed character and conduct within Israel. Deuteronomy 6:8 commands binding God's words as a sign upon the hand. Scripture gives the reason: that the LORD's law may be in your mouth, remembering deliverance from Egypt by strength of hand. No particular evil is named here; its work was keeping God's law before the eyes and on the hand, in remembrance of the Exodus, not restraint.
