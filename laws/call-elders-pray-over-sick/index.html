Call the Elders and Pray Over the Sick

Command
Call the Elders and Pray Over the Sick
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
James's sickness command: if any is sick, let him call for the elders of the church, let them pray over him, anointing him with oil in the name of the Lord.
Commandment
Is any sick among you? let him call for the elders of the church; and let them pray over him, anointing him with oil in the name of the Lord.
Scripture
James 5:14-15
Application
The sick person takes the initiative, the elders pray, oil is the sign, and the Lord raises up: the prayer of faith is the instrument named, and forgiveness is joined to healing where sin is involved.
Related Topics
Healing; Prayer; Church
Related Books
James
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'the prayer of faith shall save the sick, and the Lord shall raise him up' (James 5:15).
Duration
For all time
