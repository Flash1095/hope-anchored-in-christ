Do not take the mother bird with her young

Command
Do not take the mother bird with her young
Category
Moral Law
Summary
One half of the mother-bird law of Deuteronomy 22:6-7: do not take the mother bird with her young. Even in taking from nature, mercy was to limit what could be taken.
Commandment
Do not take the mother bird with her young
Scripture
Deuteronomy 22:6
Application
Scripture's stated reason, in the form of a promise: that it may be well with thee, and that thou mayest prolong thy days (Deuteronomy 22:7). Mercy to the creature is joined to blessing on the keeper.
