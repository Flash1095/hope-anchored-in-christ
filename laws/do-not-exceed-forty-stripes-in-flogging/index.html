Do not exceed forty stripes in flogging

Command
Do not exceed forty stripes in flogging
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Forty stripes the judge may give him, and not exceed: forty is the cap, and the court must not go beyond it.
Commandment
Do not exceed forty stripes in flogging.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 25:3
Application
The cap exists because punishment without limit becomes cruelty: even a man judged worthy of stripes must remain a brother in the court's eyes, not a degraded thing. Forty stripes is the outer bound, and the court stops there.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated reason: lest, if thou shouldest exceed, and beat him above these with many stripes, then thy brother should seem vile unto thee (Deuteronomy 25:3).
