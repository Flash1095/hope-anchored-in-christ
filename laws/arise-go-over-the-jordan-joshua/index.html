Arise, Go Over the Jordan

God's commission to Joshua after Moses' death: arise, go over the Jordan with all the people into the promised land.

Commandment

Arise, go over this Jordan, thou, and all this people, unto the land which I do give to them.

Application

The commission that opens Joshua: Moses dead, Jordan ahead, land promised. Joshua obeyed within three days (Joshua 1:11; 3:1-2).

Purpose

To lead Israel across the Jordan into the land promised to their fathers (Joshua 1:2-4).

Function

Moses dead, Jordan ahead, land promised — the commission that opens Joshua. Given once, for one moment — a one-time command, so the threefold division does not apply to it. Its goal was to lead Israel across the Jordan into the land promised to their fathers. The evil or restraint angle does not apply here; this was a commission to a new leader, not a law against a recurring evil. Joshua obeyed within three days.
