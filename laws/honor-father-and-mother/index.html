Honor Father and Mother

The fifth commandment: parents are to be honored in word, care, and provision — the first commandment with a promise attached.

Commandment

Honor your father and mother in how you speak of them, care for them, and provide for them.

Application

Paul quotes this commandment to believers and repeats its promise: that it may go well with you and that you may live long. Honor does not end when a child leaves home; it grows into caring for aging parents.

Purpose

Scripture attaches the stated promise: that thy days may be long upon the land (Exodus 20:12), repeated by Paul to believers (Ephesians 6:2-3).

Function

The first commandment with a promise attached. This fifth of the Ten Words is foundational moral law, traditionally classed moral, and it binds in every age. Its goal was a household order where parents are honoured in word, care, and provision — honour that grows, Paul says, into caring for aging parents. The evil it addressed was contempt for parents, treating those who gave life as disposable. It restrained that evil by making honour a duty with a promise: that thy days may be long upon the land.
