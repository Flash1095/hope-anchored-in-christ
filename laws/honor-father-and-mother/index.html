Honor Father and Mother

Command
Honor Father and Mother
Category
Ten Commandments
Summary
The fifth commandment: parents are to be honored in word, care, and provision — the first commandment with a promise attached.
Commandment
Honor your father and mother in how you speak of them, care for them, and provide for them.
Scripture
Exodus 20:12; Deuteronomy 5:16; Ephesians 6:2-3
Application
Paul quotes this commandment to believers and repeats its promise: that it may go well with you and that you may live long. Honor does not end when a child leaves home; it grows into caring for aging parents.
