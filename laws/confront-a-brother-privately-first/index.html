Confront a Brother Privately First

Command
Confront a Brother Privately First
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' church-discipline command: if your brother trespasses against you, go and tell him his fault between you and him alone, before taking witnesses or telling the church.
Commandment
Moreover if thy brother shall trespass against thee, go and tell him his fault between thee and him alone: if he shall hear thee, thou hast gained thy brother.
Scripture
Matthew 18:15-20
Application
Distinct from the forgiveness command (live row), which governs the heart toward an offender, this governs the process: private first, then witnesses, then the church — gaining the brother, not winning the case.
Related Topics
Forgiveness; Church; Peace
Related Books

Related People

Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'if he shall hear thee, thou hast gained thy brother' (Matthew 18:15).
