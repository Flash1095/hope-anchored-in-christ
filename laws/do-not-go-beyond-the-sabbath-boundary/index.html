Do not go beyond the Sabbath boundary

Command
Do not go beyond the Sabbath boundary
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
On the Sabbath, each person is to remain in his place and not go out beyond the appointed boundary for gathering or travel.
Commandment
On the Sabbath day, abide in your place; do not go out beyond the Sabbath boundary.
Scripture
Exodus 16:29
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The manna rule taught Israel to rest in God's provision rather than go out gathering on the Sabbath. Debated: some Christians keep the Sabbath day as a binding command; others see its rest fulfilled in Christ (Hebrews 4; Colossians 2:16-17). The site does not settle the question.
Related Topics
Worship; Holiness; Obedience
Related Books
Exodus; Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated context: the Sabbath is the LORD's day of rest, kept holy in remembrance of creation and of deliverance from Egypt (Exodus 20:8-11; Deuteronomy 5:12-15).
