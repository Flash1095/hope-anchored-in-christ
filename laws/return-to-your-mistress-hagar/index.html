Return to Your Mistress

Command
Return to Your Mistress
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
The angel's command to the runaway Hagar: return to Sarai and submit under her hands.
Commandment
Return to thy mistress, and submit thyself under her hands.
Scripture
Genesis 16:9
Application
A genuine divine command to a person in flight, with a promise attached (a multiplied seed, Genesis 16:10). Small in scale but unambiguous in form, and therefore included rather than passed over as mere narrative.
Related Topics
Obedience; Humility; Family
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Hagar
Related Events
Hagar and Ishmael
Purpose
To send Hagar back under Sarai's hand with the promise of a multiplied seed through Ishmael (Genesis 16:10).
