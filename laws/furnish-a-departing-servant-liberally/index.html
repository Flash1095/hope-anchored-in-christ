Furnish a departing servant liberally

Command
Furnish a departing servant liberally
Category
Civil Law
Summary
A departing servant is furnished liberally out of the flock, the threshingfloor, and the winepress, as the LORD has blessed the master.
Commandment
Furnish a departing servant liberally.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 15:14
Application
Liberality is measured by the master's blessing: the servant shares in the prosperity his labour helped to make. Release with provision, not bare release, is the Law's pattern — and generosity at parting remains a mark of just dealing.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy; Generosity
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To regulate and restrain an ancient institution: six-year release, provision, no ruthless rule (Exodus 21; Deuteronomy 15). Here that restraint takes the form of liberal furnishing at departure.
