Do Not Go About as a Talebearer

Command
Do Not Go About as a Talebearer
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Do not go up and down as a talebearer among your people. This is the gossip prohibition of Leviticus 19:16a, distinct from the live Love Your Neighbor row, whose range (Leviticus 19:17-18) does not include verse 16.
Commandment
Do not spread tales about your neighbours among the people.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:16
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as moral. The New Testament lists gossip and whispering among the sins that mark a disordered community (Romans 1:29; 2 Corinthians 12:20), and James warns that the tongue, though small, sets great things on fire (James 3:5-6). Repeating what harms a neighbour, even if true, breaks the same love that forbids bearing false witness.
Related Topics
Speech; Love; Truth
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: tale-bearing destroys the trust on which neighbourly life depends, and the verse pairs it with standing against a neighbour's blood — speech that harms and silence that harms. Scripture states the prohibition without a separate reason (Leviticus 19:16).
Duration
For all time
