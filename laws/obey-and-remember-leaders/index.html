Obey and Remember Those Who Rule Over You

Command
Obey and Remember Those Who Rule Over You
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Hebrews' leadership command: remember those who have the rule over you and follow their faith, and obey them that have the rule over you and submit, for they watch for your souls.
Commandment
Remember them which have the rule over you, who have spoken unto you the word of God: whose faith follow, considering the end of their conversation. Obey them that have the rule over you, and submit yourselves: for they watch for your souls.
Scripture
Hebrews 13:7; Hebrews 13:17
Application
Distinct from submission to civil rulers (live row): this is submission to church leaders who teach the word and watch over souls, given that they must give account — with joy, not grief.
Related Topics
Leadership; Church; Obedience
Related Books
Hebrews
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'for they watch for your souls, as they that must give account' (Hebrews 13:17).
Duration
For all time
