Do Not Love the World

John's world command: love not the world, neither the things in the world; if any man love the world, the love of the Father is not in him.

Commandment

Love not the world, neither the things that are in the world. If any man love the world, the love of the Father is not in him.

Application

'The world' here is the ordered system of lust of the flesh, lust of the eyes, and pride of life — all passing away — set against doing the will of God, which abides forever.

Purpose

Scripture's own reason: 'the world passeth away, and the lust thereof: but he that doeth the will of God abideth for ever' (1 John 2:17).

Function

The world here is an ordered system — lust of the flesh, lust of the eyes, pride of life — and it is passing away. This one is apostolic, not Mosaic, so the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not apply. Its goal was to set doing the will of God, which abides forever, against loving what passes away. The evil it addressed was love of the world, which crowds out the love of the Father. It restrained that evil by the contrast itself: the world passeth away, but he that doeth the will of God abideth for ever.
