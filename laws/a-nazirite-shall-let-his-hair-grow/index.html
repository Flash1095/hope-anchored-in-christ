A Nazirite shall let his hair grow

Command
A Nazirite shall let his hair grow
Category
Moral Law
Summary
One component of the Nazirite vow of Numbers 6: a Nazirite shall let his hair grow. The Nazirite separated himself unto the LORD for a set time, marked by abstinence, uncut hair, and avoidance of corpses.
Commandment
A Nazirite shall let his hair grow
Scripture
Numbers 6:5
Application
The vow was voluntary and temporary by design, ending with offerings at the tabernacle (Numbers 6:13-21); its abstinences marked consecration, not a standing rule for all believers.
Related Topics
Holiness; Consecration; Worship
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated context: the Nazirite separates himself unto the LORD (Numbers 6:2,5-6); no further reason is given, so the components' purposes are traditional — wine abstained from as a mark of consecration, hair uncut as the visible sign, and corpse-avoidance as the priest-like holiness of the vow.
