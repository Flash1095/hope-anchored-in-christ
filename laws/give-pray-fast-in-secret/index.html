Give, Pray, and Fast in Secret

Command
Give, Pray, and Fast in Secret
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' secrecy command for devotion: do alms, prayer, and fasting before the Father in secret, not to be seen by people.
Commandment
When you give alms, pray, or fast, do it in secret before your Father, not sounding a trumpet or disfiguring your face to be seen by men.
Scripture
Matthew 6:1-18
Application
The acts themselves are assumed — giving, praying, fasting — but their audience is changed from the crowd to the Father, who sees in secret and rewards openly.

Prayer; Worship; Humility

Related People

Purpose
Scripture's own reason: the Father 'which seeth in secret shall reward thee openly' (Matthew 6:4, 6, 18).
