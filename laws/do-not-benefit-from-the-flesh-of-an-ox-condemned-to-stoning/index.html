Do not benefit from the flesh of an ox condemned to stoning

Command
Do not benefit from the flesh of an ox condemned to stoning
Category
Civil Law
Summary
The flesh of an ox that has been condemned to stoning for killing a person is not to be eaten or otherwise turned to profit.
Commandment
Do not benefit from the flesh of an ox condemned to stoning.
Scripture
Exodus 21:28
Application
A death that the Law treated as a judgment was not to become somebody's dinner or profit. The rule guards against turning a solemn sentence into gain.
Related Topics
Justice
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To keep a court sentence solemn: what the Law condemned was not to be converted into private benefit.
Duration
For all time
