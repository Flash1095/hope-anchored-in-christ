Put On the Whole Armour of God

Paul's armour command: put on the whole armour of God, that you may stand against the wiles of the devil, and having done all, stand.

Commandment

Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil.

Application

The armour is God's own, piece by piece — truth, righteousness, gospel peace, faith, salvation, the word — and the posture is standing, not advancing by human strength. Prayer on all occasions closes the list.

Purpose

Scripture's own reason: 'for we wrestle not against flesh and blood' (Ephesians 6:12).

Function

The armour is God's own, piece by piece — and the posture is standing, not advancing by human strength. An apostolic command, not a Mosaic one — the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not sort it. Its goal was to hold the line against the wiles of the devil, having done all, to stand. The evil it addressed was a spiritual enemy we do not wrestle against flesh and blood to defeat. It restrained that evil by the armour itself: truth, righteousness, gospel peace, faith, salvation, the word — and prayer on all occasions.
