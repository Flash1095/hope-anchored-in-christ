Put On the Whole Armour of God

Command
Put On the Whole Armour of God
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's armour command: put on the whole armour of God, that you may stand against the wiles of the devil, and having done all, stand.
Commandment
Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil.
Scripture
Ephesians 6:11-18
Application
The armour is God's own, piece by piece — truth, righteousness, gospel peace, faith, salvation, the word — and the posture is standing, not advancing by human strength. Prayer on all occasions closes the list.
Related Topics
Courage; Truth; Faith
Related Books
Ephesians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'for we wrestle not against flesh and blood' (Ephesians 6:12).
Duration
For all time
