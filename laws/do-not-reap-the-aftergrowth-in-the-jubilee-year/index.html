Do not reap the aftergrowth in the Jubilee year

Command
Do not reap the aftergrowth in the Jubilee year
Category
Civil Law
Summary
In the Jubilee year, the aftergrowth — what springs up of itself without sowing — is not to be reaped as a crop.
Commandment
Do not reap the aftergrowth in the Jubilee year.
Scripture
Leviticus 25:11
Application
Aftergrowth is the land's own gift, not the farmer's crop, and in Jubilee it stays free for whoever needs it. Reaping it as a harvest would turn release back into ordinary farming.
Related Topics
Mercy; Generosity
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To prevent permanent destitution and permanent debt in Israel (Leviticus 25; Deuteronomy 15). In the Jubilee year, that purpose takes the form of leaving the aftergrowth unreaped.
Duration
Temporary
