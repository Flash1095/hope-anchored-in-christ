Give the breast and thigh of peace offerings to the priests

The command: Give the breast and thigh of peace offerings to the priests (Leviticus 7:34). The wave breast and heave shoulder of the peace offerings belonged to Aaron and his sons by a perpetual statute.

Commandment

Give the breast and thigh of peace offerings to the priests, as prescribed in Leviticus 7:34.

Application

This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah. The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Hebrews teaches these sacrifices could never perfect the worshiper; they pointed forward to Christ's once-for-all offering, which is why they ceased with the new covenant.

Purpose

To provide atonement, thanksgiving, and fellowship under the old covenant. Hebrews teaches these pointed forward to Christ's once-for-all offering (Hebrews 10:1-14), so the system ceased.

Function

The wave breast and heave shoulder of the peace offerings belonged to Aaron and his sons by a perpetual statute — a ceremonial law of priestly provision. Its goal was to sustain the priests from the fellowship meals, so those who served at the altar ate from the altar. It did not address a particular evil; its work was provision and order, not restraint. The shared meal fed the priests too, and the people's peace offering became the priests' daily bread.
