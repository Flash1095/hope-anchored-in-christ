Give a bill of divorce when sending a wife away

Command
Give a bill of divorce when sending a wife away
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 24:1 requires a written bill of divorcement when a man sends his wife away: 'then let him write her a bill of divorcement, and give it in her hand, and send her out of his house.' The document protects the woman's standing to remarry.
Commandment
When sending a wife away, give her a bill of divorce in her hand.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 24:1
Application
Debated: how far these divorce provisions carry into the church is genuinely disputed, with Jesus' teaching on divorce (Matthew 19:3-9) central — this row does not settle it. The protection aimed at — a dismissed wife with a document, not a word — is plain.
Related Topics
Marriage; Family; Justice
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Matthew
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To protect the weaker party at each family step (Deuteronomy 21-25). Debated: the divorce certificate is read by Jesus as concession to hardness of heart, not God's design (Matthew 19:8), and levirate marriage ended, while creation marriage itself is reaffirmed. The site does not settle a single duration for this cluster because its parts do not share one fate.
