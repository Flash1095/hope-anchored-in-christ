Go to the Potter's House

Command
Go to the Potter's House
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's command to Jeremiah: arise, go down to the potter's house, and there hear God's words.
Commandment
Arise, and go down to the potter's house, and there I will cause thee to hear my words.
Scripture
Jeremiah 18:2
Application
A deed-commission, not a speech alone: Jeremiah must go to the place before he hears the word, and the potter's vessel becomes the sermon (Jeremiah 18:3-10). Generic prophetic 'speak to this people' commissions are not separately rowed (see notes); this one is included because the commanded journey and object lesson are a distinct act.
Related Topics
Prophecy; Obedience; Judgment
Related Books
Jeremiah
Related People
Jeremiah
Purpose
To show Jeremiah, in the potter's work, that Israel is clay in the LORD's hand — to be remade or ruined as he speaks (Jeremiah 18:6-10).
Duration
One-time
