Do not use second-tithe money for an unclean purpose

Command
Do not use second-tithe money for an unclean purpose
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: Do not use second-tithe money for an unclean purpose (Deuteronomy 26:14). The tithe confession declared that none of it had been taken away for any unclean use. This row covers spending tithe money for an unclean purpose; the companion row do-not-eat-the-tithe-in-mourning-in-uncleanness-or-for-the-dead covers the eating clauses of the same verse.
Commandment
Do not use second-tithe money for an unclean purpose
Scripture
Deuteronomy 26:14
Application
It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.
Related Topics
Temple; Tithing
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: holy gifts were not to be diverted to unclean purposes, keeping what was consecrated to the LORD distinct from impurity. Duration Debated: the scope of 'unclean use' in this verse — spending tithe money versus the eating clauses — is the debated point, so the collection files it Debated rather than settling it.
