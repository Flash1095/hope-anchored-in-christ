Watch and Pray; Be Ready

Command
Watch and Pray; Be Ready
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' watchfulness command: watch and pray lest you enter temptation, and be ready, for the Son of man comes at an hour you do not expect.
Commandment
Watch therefore: for ye know not what hour your Lord doth come. Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation.
Scripture
Matthew 24:42-44; Matthew 26:41; Mark 14:38; Luke 12:35-40; Luke 21:36
Application
Watchfulness is readiness, not date-setting: lamps burning, loins girded, prayer kept up when the flesh is weak. The command spans Gethsemane and the return of Christ.
Related Topics
Prayer; Faith; Kingdom of God
Related Books
Matthew; Mark; Luke
Related People
Jesus
Related Events
Jesus Prays in Gethsemane
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to keep disciples ready for trial and for the Master's return, since neither hour is known.
Duration
For all time
