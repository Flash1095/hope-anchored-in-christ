Do not abhor an Edomite

Command
Do not abhor an Edomite
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 23:7 forbids abhorring an Edomite: 'for he is thy brother.' Edom descends from Esau, Jacob's brother, and kinship limits enmity even after Edom's hostility.
Commandment
Do not abhor an Edomite, for he is your brother.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 23:7
Application
Kinship tempers enmity: even a hostile neighbour who is a brother is not to be abhorred. The admission rule that follows — their children of the third generation may enter the assembly — shows enmity with an end date. Debated: how far these assembly-admission provisions carry into the church is genuinely disputed — this row does not settle it.

Purpose
To protect the weaker party at each family step (Deuteronomy 21-25). Debated: the divorce certificate is read by Jesus as concession to hardness of heart, not God's design (Matthew 19:8), and levirate marriage ended, while creation marriage itself is reaffirmed.
