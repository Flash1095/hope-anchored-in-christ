Do Not Be Carried Away by Strange Teachings

Command
Do Not Be Carried Away by Strange Teachings
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Hebrews' doctrine command: be not carried about with divers and strange doctrines, for it is good that the heart be established with grace.
Commandment
Be not carried about with divers and strange doctrines: for it is a good thing that the heart be established with grace; not with meats, which have not profited them that have been occupied therein.
Scripture
Hebrews 13:9
Application
The strange doctrines in view are food-rules as a way of standing with God. The heart established by grace does not need dietary religion to be secure, and drifting teaching must not carry it away.

Doctrine; Discernment; Truth
