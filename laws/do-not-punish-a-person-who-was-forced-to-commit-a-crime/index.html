Do not punish a person who was forced to commit a crime

Command
Do not punish a person who was forced to commit a crime
Category
Civil Law
Summary
A person forced to commit a crime is not punished for it: where there was no willing act, there is no guilt to punish.
Commandment
Do not punish a person who was forced to commit a crime.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 22:26
Application
Guilt requires willingness: the betrothed damsel taken by force in the field is the Law's example — she cried out, none heard, and no guilt attaches to her. The principle endures wherever coercion is pleaded: the forced act is not the forced person's crime.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: guilt requires a willing act, so one forced to commit a crime bears no punishment for it; the Law's example is the damsel taken by force, to whom no guilt attaches.
Duration
For all time
