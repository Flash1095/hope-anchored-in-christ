Do not burn the fat of a blemished animal

Command
Do not burn the fat of a blemished animal
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: Do not burn the fat of a blemished animal (Leviticus 22:22). The fat of a blemished beast might not be burned on the altar as an offering made by fire.
Commandment
Do not burn the fat of a blemished animal.
Scripture
Leviticus 22:22
Application
It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.
Related Topics
Sacrifice; Holiness
Related Books
Leviticus; Hebrews
Purpose
Scripture's stated ground: the fat of a blemished beast was not an acceptable offering made by fire (Leviticus 22:22).
