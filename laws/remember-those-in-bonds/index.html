Remember Those in Bonds

Hebrews' prisoner command: remember them that are in bonds, as bound with them, and them which suffer adversity, as being yourselves also in the body.

Commandment

Remember them that are in bonds, as bound with them; and them which suffer adversity, as being yourselves also in the body.

Application

Remembrance here is identification: bound with them, as if in the same body. It is the prison-visiting command in its most inward form — sympathy that puts itself in the cell.

Purpose

Scripture's own reason: 'as bound with them' and 'as being yourselves also in the body' (Hebrews 13:3).

Function

Remembrance here is identification: bound with them, as if in the same body. This comes from the apostles, not from Sinai, so the old threefold division does not apply to it. Its goal was sympathy that puts itself in the cell — remember them that are in bonds, as bound with them. The evil it addressed was forgetting the imprisoned, leaving sufferers to bear adversity alone. It restrained that evil by the identification itself: as being yourselves also in the body.
