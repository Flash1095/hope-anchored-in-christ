Leave olive gleanings for the poor, stranger, fatherless, and widow

Command
Leave olive gleanings for the poor, stranger, fatherless, and widow
Category
Civil Law
Summary
When you beat your olive tree, do not go over the boughs again: the remaining olives are for the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow.
Commandment
Leave olive gleanings for the poor, stranger, fatherless, and widow.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 24:20
Application
One beating, not a second gleaning pass: the olives left after the first beating belong to those without trees of their own. The olive yard, like the field and the vineyard, carries provision for the defenceless in its remainder.
Related Topics
Mercy; Generosity
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
God's stated care for those without a defender — widow, orphan, stranger — carried by James into the church as pure religion (James 1:27). Here that care takes the form of olive gleanings left for the defenceless.
Duration
For all time
