Beware the Leaven of the Pharisees

Command
Beware the Leaven of the Pharisees
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' warning command: beware of the leaven of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy — the teaching that says one thing and lives another.
Commandment
Beware ye of the leaven of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy.
Scripture
Matthew 16:6; Mark 8:15; Luke 12:1
Application
Leaven spreads quietly through the whole lump; so does hypocrisy. The command guards disciples against a religion of outward performance hiding inward corruption.
Related Topics
Truth; Discernment; Doctrine
Related Books
Matthew; Mark; Luke
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
Scripture's own reason in Luke: 'for there is nothing covered, that shall not be revealed' (Luke 12:2).
Duration
For all time
