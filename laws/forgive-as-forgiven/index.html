Forgive as You Have Been Forgiven

Command
Forgive as You Have Been Forgiven
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
The forgiveness command: forgive those who wrong you, as freely as God in Christ has forgiven you.
Commandment
Forgive those who sin against you, as your Father has forgiven you.
Scripture
Matthew 6:14-15; Matthew 18:21-35; Colossians 3:13
Application
Jesus ties our forgiveness to God's with unsettling directness, and the unforgiving servant shows what happens when a forgiven person refuses to forgive.
Related Topics
Forgiveness; Mercy; Love
Related Books
Matthew; Colossians
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
Forgiveness received must become forgiveness given (Matthew 6:14-15; Colossians 3:13).
Duration
For all time
