Judge Righteously, Not Hypocritically

Command
Judge Righteously, Not Hypocritically
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
The judgment command: do not condemn others while blind to your own faults; judge with right judgment.
Commandment
Do not judge hypocritically; first take the plank from your own eye, then judge with right judgment.
Scripture
Matthew 7:1-5; John 7:24; Luke 6:37
Application
The command does not forbid all judgment — John 7:24 commands right judgment — but it forbids the hypocrite's judgment, which condemns in others what it excuses in itself.
Related Topics
Truth; Humility; Justice
Related Books
Matthew; John; Luke
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
To forbid hypocritical condemnation while commanding right judgment (John 7:24).
Duration
For all time
