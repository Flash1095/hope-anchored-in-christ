Do not bring a prostitute's wages into God's house

One of the Deuteronomy 23:17-18 laws: do not bring a prostitute's wages into God's house. Fertility worship of the surrounding nations was to have no place in Israel, and its profits no place in God's house.

Commandment

Do not bring a prostitute's wages into God's house

Application

Scripture's stated ground: for even both these are abomination unto the LORD thy God (Deuteronomy 23:18).

Purpose

Scripture's stated ground: for even both these are abomination unto the LORD thy God (Deuteronomy 23:18).

Function

One of the Deuteronomy 23:17-18 laws: do not bring a prostitute's wages into God's house. This binds in every age: a duty of conduct, not a rule of Israel's civil code. Scripture gives the ground: for even both these are abomination unto the LORD thy God. The restraint is the prohibition itself: do not bring a prostitute's wages into God's house.
