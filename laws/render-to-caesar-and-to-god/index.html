Render to Caesar and to God

Command
Render to Caesar and to God
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' tribute command: render to Caesar the things that are Caesar's, and to God the things that are God's.
Commandment
Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar's; and unto God the things which are God's.
Scripture
Matthew 22:21; Mark 12:17; Luke 20:25
Application
The trap — endorse Rome or defy it — is sidestepped by a division that still subordinates Caesar: coin bears Caesar's image, but people bear God's, and what bears God's image belongs to God.
Related Topics
Justice; Authority; Obedience
Related Books
Matthew; Mark; Luke
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to refuse both rebellion for its own sake and worship of the state, giving each realm its due.
Duration
For all time
