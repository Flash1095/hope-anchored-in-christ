Do to Others as You Would Have Them Do to You

Command
Do to Others as You Would Have Them Do to You
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
The Golden Rule: whatever you would want others to do to you, do the same to them — Jesus' summary of the Law and the Prophets in action.
Commandment
Therefore all things whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them: for this is the law and the prophets.
Scripture
Matthew 7:12; Luke 6:31
Application
Distinct from the love commands as a practical rule: put yourself in the other's place before you act, and treat them as you would wish to be treated.
Related Topics
Love; Mercy; Justice
Related Books
Matthew; Luke
Related People
Jesus
Related Events
The Sermon on the Mount
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'for this is the law and the prophets' (Matthew 7:12).
Duration
For all time
