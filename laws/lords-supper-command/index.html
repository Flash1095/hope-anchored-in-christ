The Lord's Supper: Do This in Remembrance

Command
The Lord's Supper: Do This in Remembrance
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
The meal Christ instituted on the night he was betrayed: bread as his body, cup as the new covenant in his blood, eaten in remembrance until he comes.
Commandment
Eat the bread and drink the cup in remembrance of Christ, proclaiming his death until he comes.
Scripture
Luke 22:19-20; 1 Corinthians 11:23-26
Application
Paul delivers the same institution to Corinth and adds its rhythm: as often as you eat and drink, you proclaim the Lord's death until he comes.
Related Topics
Worship; Covenant; Jesus Christ
Related Books
Luke; 1 Corinthians
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
Remembrance and proclamation until he comes (1 Corinthians 11:26): a standing meal, not a one-time act.
Duration
For all time
