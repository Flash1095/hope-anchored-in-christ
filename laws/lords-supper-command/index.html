The Lord's Supper: Do This in Remembrance

The meal Christ instituted on the night he was betrayed: bread as his body, cup as the new covenant in his blood, eaten in remembrance until he comes.

Commandment

Eat the bread and drink the cup in remembrance of Christ, proclaiming his death until he comes.

Application

Paul delivers the same institution to Corinth and adds its rhythm: as often as you eat and drink, you proclaim the Lord's death until he comes.

Purpose

Remembrance and proclamation until he comes (1 Corinthians 11:26): a standing meal, not a one-time act.

Function

The meal Christ instituted on the night he was betrayed: bread as his body, cup as the new covenant in his blood. A New Testament command, not a Mosaic one — the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not sort it. Its goal was remembrance and proclamation until he comes — a standing meal, not a one-time act. The evil it addressed was forgetting: a church that lets the cross fade from memory. It restrained that evil by the rhythm itself: as often as you eat and drink, you proclaim the Lord's death until he comes.
