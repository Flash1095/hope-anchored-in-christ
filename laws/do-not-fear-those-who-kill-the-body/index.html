Do Not Fear Those Who Kill the Body

Command
Do Not Fear Those Who Kill the Body
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' fear command: do not fear those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul; rather fear him who can destroy both soul and body in hell.
Commandment
Fear not them which kill the body, but are not able to kill the soul: but rather fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.
Scripture
Matthew 10:28; Luke 12:4-5
Application
The command reorders fear itself: persecutors have the lesser power. Right fear of God frees disciples from the fear of men.
Related Topics
Courage; Faith
Related Books
Matthew; Luke
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to free witnesses from the fear of persecutors by setting the greater judgment of God before them.
Duration
For all time
