Number the People

Command
Number the People
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's command to Moses, given twice for two generations: take the sum of the congregation of Israel.
Commandment
Take ye the sum of all the congregation of the children of Israel, by their families and fathers' houses.
Scripture
Numbers 1:2; Numbers 26:2
Application
Administrative, not moral — included because the brief is every distinct command, and these are explicit imperatives. Merged row: the Numbers 1 census (Sinai) and the Numbers 26 census (plains of Moab, a new generation) are two censuses under one command type; parent may split into two rows if each census is wanted separately.

Related People

Purpose
To count Israel by tribe and family for camp order, service, and war (Numbers 1:3; 26:2).
