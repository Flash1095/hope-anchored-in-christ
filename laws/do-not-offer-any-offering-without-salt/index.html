Do not offer any offering without salt

The command: Do not offer any offering without salt (Leviticus 2:13). No offering might be offered without salt; the salt of the covenant of God was never to be lacking.

Commandment

Do not offer any offering without salt.

Application

This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah. The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Hebrews teaches these sacrifices could never perfect the worshiper; they pointed forward to Christ's once-for-all offering, which is why they ceased with the new covenant.

Purpose

To provide atonement, thanksgiving, and fellowship under the old covenant. Hebrews teaches these pointed forward to Christ's once-for-all offering (Hebrews 10:1-14), so the system ceased.

Function

No offering might come to the altar without salt, and that flat prohibition is what makes this ceremonial: a rule guarding the covenant sign. Its goal was to keep the salt of the covenant from ever being lacking, so every sacrifice carried the mark of God's enduring covenant. The evil was careless worship — gifts brought stripped of the sign that bound them to God. It restrained that by making the salt non-negotiable: an offering without it was not an offering at all.
