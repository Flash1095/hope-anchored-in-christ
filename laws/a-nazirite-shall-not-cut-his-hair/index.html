A Nazirite shall not cut his hair

Command
A Nazirite shall not cut his hair
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
Throughout the vow, no razor comes upon the Nazirite's head; the locks of his hair grow until the days of separation are fulfilled.
Commandment
A Nazirite shall let no razor come upon his head for all the days of his separation.
Scripture
Numbers 6:5
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Acts 21:23-26 shows Paul joining men under a vow in Jerusalem, which shows the practice continued among Jewish believers, not that it is imposed on Christians.
Related Topics
Holiness; Consecration; Worship
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated context: the Nazirite separates himself unto the LORD (Numbers 6:2,5-6); no further reason is given, so the components' purposes are traditional — hair uncut as the visible sign of the vow.
