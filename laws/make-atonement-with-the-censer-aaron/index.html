Make Atonement with the Censer

Moses' command to Aaron in the plague: take a censer with altar fire and incense, go quickly, and make atonement for the congregation.

Commandment

Take a censer, and put fire therein from off the altar, and put on incense, and go quickly unto the congregation, and make an atonement for them.

Application

Source flag for the parent: the immediate speaker is Moses to Aaron (Numbers 16:46), acting on God's instruction in the plague crisis, not a direct divine speech formula. Kept because the act (censer atonement) stopped the plague (16:48) and is presented as commanded obedience, but drop it if the parent requires direct divine-speech commands only.

Purpose

To make atonement for the congregation and stop the plague after Korah's rebellion (Numbers 16:46-48).

Function

The plague was already falling when Moses sent Aaron with the censer. One-time and one-moment: the threefold division does not sort this one. Its goal was to make atonement for the congregation and stop the plague after Korah's rebellion. The evil it addressed was the plague itself, consuming the murmuring congregation. It restrained that evil by the atonement itself: take a censer with altar fire and incense, go quickly, and make atonement — and the plague was stayed.
