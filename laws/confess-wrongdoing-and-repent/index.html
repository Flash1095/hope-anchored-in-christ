Confess wrongdoing and repent

Command
Confess wrongdoing and repent
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Numbers 5:7 requires confession where wrong has been done: 'then they shall confess their sin which they have done: and he shall recompense his trespass with the principal thereof, and add unto it the fifth part thereof.' Confession here is joined to restitution, not offered instead of it.
Commandment
Confess the sin you have done, and make recompense for it.
Scripture
Numbers 5:7
Application
Confession without repayment is only half the command, and repayment without confession hides the wrong. The church keeps both together: confess, and put right what can be put right.
Related Topics
Sin; Justice
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated context: confession precedes recompense in the trespass procedure (Numbers 5:6-7).
Duration
For all time
