Sell Your Possessions and Give Alms

Command
Sell Your Possessions and Give Alms
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' general command to his disciples, distinct from the rich ruler's personal word: sell what you have, give alms, and provide treasure in heaven that does not fail.
Commandment
Sell that ye have, and give alms; provide yourselves bags which wax not old, a treasure in the heavens that faileth not.
Scripture
Luke 12:33
Application
Unlike the rich ruler's one-man word (separate row), this is spoken to disciples generally. How literally it requires selling possessions, versus requiring a generosity that loosens their grip, is debated, and this row does not settle it.
Related Topics
Generosity; Money; Kingdom of God
Related Books
Luke
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'for where your treasure is, there will your heart be also' (Luke 12:34).
