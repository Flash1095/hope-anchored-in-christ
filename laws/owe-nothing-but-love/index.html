Owe Nothing but Love

Command
Owe Nothing but Love
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's debt command: owe no man anything, but to love one another; for he that loveth another hath fulfilled the law.
Commandment
Owe no man any thing, but to love one another: for he that loveth another hath fulfilled the law.
Scripture
Romans 13:8
Application
Debts are to be cleared, not carried; the one debt that is never cleared is love, because it is due perpetually. Love here fulfils the law's neighbour-commands in a single obligation.
Related Topics
Love; Justice; Money
Related Books
Romans
Related People
Paul
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'for he that loveth another hath fulfilled the law' (Romans 13:8).
Duration
