The Great Commission

Command
The Great Commission
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
The risen Christ's charge to his church: go, make disciples of all nations, baptizing them and teaching them everything he commanded.
Commandment
Go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything Christ commanded.
Scripture
Matthew 28:18-20; Mark 16:15; Acts 1:8
Application
The commission carries Christ's own authority — all authority in heaven and on earth — and his own promise: I am with you always, to the end of the age.
Related Topics
Evangelism; Discipleship; Baptism
Related Books
Matthew; Mark; Acts
Related People
Jesus
Purpose
Given with all authority and the promise of Christ's presence to the end of the age (Matthew 28:18-20).
Duration
For all time
