Bridle the Tongue

Command
Bridle the Tongue
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
James's tongue command: if any man seems religious and bridles not his tongue, his religion is vain; the tongue is a fire that no man can tame.
Commandment
If any man among you seem to be religious, and bridleth not his tongue, but deceiveth his own heart, this man's religion is vain.
Scripture
James 1:26; James 3:2-10
Application
Distinct from the swift/slow row, which governs temper in listening: this governs the tongue's ongoing power to bless and curse from the same mouth, to set whole courses of life on fire.
Related Topics
Speech; Holiness; Truth
Related Books
James
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to expose unbridled speech as proof of vain religion, not a small fault.
Duration
For all time
