Do not set a foreigner as king over Israel

Deuteronomy 17:15 forbids setting a stranger as king: 'thou mayest not set a stranger over thee, which is not thy brother.' The king was to be one of the covenant people, sharing their history and law.

Commandment

Do not set a foreigner as king over Israel.

Application

Scripture's stated context: Israel's desire for a king like the nations is answered by God's choice, not by the people's preference alone (Deuteronomy 17:14-15). The king from outside the covenant would bring outside gods with him.
