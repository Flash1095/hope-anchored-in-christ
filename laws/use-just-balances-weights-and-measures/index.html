Use just balances, weights and measures

Command
Use just balances, weights and measures
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Keep just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin: every measure used in trade must be true.
Commandment
Use just balances, weights and measures.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:36
Application
The positive form of honest trade: the merchant's measures are themselves just, not merely free of proven fraud. Honest measures still mark honest trade today, wherever goods are weighed, counted, or priced.
Related Topics
Justice; Money; Truth
Related Books
Leviticus; Deuteronomy; Proverbs
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Proverbs states the reason as God's own delight and abomination: a false balance is an abomination, but a just weight is his delight (Proverbs 11:1).
Duration
For all time
