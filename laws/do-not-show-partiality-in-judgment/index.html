Do not show partiality in judgment

Command
Do not show partiality in judgment
Category
Civil Law
Summary
In judgment, do not respect persons: neither the great nor the small may bend the verdict by who they are.
Commandment
Do not show partiality in judgment.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:15
Application
Partiality is the judge's besetting sin, and it wears two faces — deference to the great and pity for the small. Both are forbidden, because judgment belongs to God, who is no respecter of persons.
Related Topics
Justice; Truth
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: judgment is God's, and he shows no partiality; a judge who respects persons has taken God's seat for his own favour.
Duration
For all time
