Speak to the Rock at Meribah

God's command at Meribah: speak to the rock before the assembly, and it will give forth water.

Commandment

Take the rod, gather the assembly, and speak ye unto the rock before their eyes, and it shall give forth his water.

Application

The command Moses broke: told to speak, he struck (Numbers 20:11), and for that he was barred from leading Israel into the land (20:12). Rowed separately from Rephidim precisely because the difference between the two commands is the point of the story.

Purpose

To give water to the congregation and their beasts at Meribah, and to sanctify God before Israel by exact obedience (Numbers 20:12).

Function

The command Moses broke: told to speak, he struck — and for that he was barred from leading Israel into the land. A command for one moment only, so the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not apply. Its goal was to give water to the congregation and their beasts at Meribah, and to sanctify God before Israel by exact obedience. The evil it addressed was the people's thirst, but the story's weight falls on Moses' disobedience. It restrained nothing by force; its work was obedience, and Moses' failure is the warning in it.
