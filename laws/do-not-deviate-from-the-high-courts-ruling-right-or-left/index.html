Do not deviate from the high court's ruling right or left

From the high court's sentence you shall not turn aside to the right hand or to the left: its ruling is followed as given.

Commandment

Do not deviate from the high court's ruling right or left.

Application

Deviation, even in a direction that looks wiser, unravels a common law: if every party follows only the rulings he likes, there is no court at all. The court system belonged to Israel's polity; respect for lawful judgment, rather than picking rulings à la carte, is the lasting principle.

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: a common law holds only if its final rulings are followed as given, not turned from to the right or to the left.

Function

Plain civil law: the Law laying down how a community handles this case. From the high court's sentence you shall not turn aside to the right hand or to the left.
