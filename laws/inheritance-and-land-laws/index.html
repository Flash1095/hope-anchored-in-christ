Inheritance and Land Laws

Command
Inheritance and Land Laws
Category
Civil Law
Summary
The land laws: inheritance passes within the family and tribe, daughters inherit when there is no son, and the land itself stays in the family.
Commandment
Keep inheritance within the tribe, let daughters inherit where there is no son, and do not sell the family's land permanently.
Scripture
Numbers 27:1-11; Numbers 36:1-13; Deuteronomy 21:15-17
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as civil. The daughters of Zelophehad set the precedent: their appeal changed the law itself, showing God's law making room for justice within its own framework.
Related Topics
Family; Justice
Related Books
Numbers; Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
