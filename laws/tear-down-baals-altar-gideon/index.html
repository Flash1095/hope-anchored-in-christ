Tear Down Baal's Altar

The LORD's night command to Gideon: throw down his father's Baal altar, cut down the grove, build an altar to the LORD, and offer the bullock.

Commandment

Throw down the altar of Baal that thy father hath, and cut down the grove that is by it; and build an altar unto the LORD thy God, and offer the second bullock.

Application

The night work before the campaign: tear down the family Baal altar, cut the grove, build an altar to the LORD, and offer the bullock (Judges 6:26). Gideon did it by night for fear (6:27) — obedience, not eagerness, is the note.

Purpose

To purge idolatry from Gideon's own household before God used him against Midian, putting the LORD's altar where Baal's had stood (Judges 6:25-26).

Function

The night work before the campaign: tear down the family Baal altar, cut the grove, build an altar to the LORD, and offer the bullock. Given once, for one moment — a one-time command, so the threefold division does not apply to it. Its goal was to purge idolatry from Gideon's own household before God used him against Midian. The evil it addressed was Baal worship in Gideon's own house. It restrained that evil by the replacement itself: the LORD's altar where Baal's had stood. Gideon did it by night for fear.
