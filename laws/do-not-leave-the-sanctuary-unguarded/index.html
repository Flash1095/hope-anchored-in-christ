Do not leave the sanctuary unguarded

Command
Do not leave the sanctuary unguarded
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: Do not leave the sanctuary unguarded (Numbers 18:5). Aaron and his sons kept the charge of the sanctuary and the altar, that wrath would not come upon Israel.
Commandment
Do not leave the sanctuary unguarded.
Scripture
Numbers 18:5
Application
It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.
Related Topics
Temple; Worship

Purpose
Scripture's stated reason: that there be no wrath upon the congregation of Israel (Numbers 18:5).
