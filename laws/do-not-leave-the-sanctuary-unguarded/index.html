Do not leave the sanctuary unguarded

The command: Do not leave the sanctuary unguarded (Numbers 18:5). Aaron and his sons kept the charge of the sanctuary and the altar, that wrath would not come upon Israel.

Commandment

Do not leave the sanctuary unguarded.

Application

It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.

Purpose

Scripture's stated reason: that there be no wrath upon the congregation of Israel (Numbers 18:5).

Function

Aaron and his sons kept the charge of the sanctuary and the altar, that wrath would not come upon Israel — a ceremonial law of the watch. Its goal was to keep the sanctuary guarded so that no wrath fell on the congregation. The evil was the unguarded holy place, an unwatched sanctuary open to unauthorised approach and the wrath that followed. It restrained that by the charge itself: the priests kept the watch, and the guard stood between the people and the holy things.
