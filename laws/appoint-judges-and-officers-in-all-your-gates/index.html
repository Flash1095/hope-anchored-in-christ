Appoint judges and officers in all your gates

Command
Appoint judges and officers in all your gates
Category
Civil Law
Summary
In all your gates you shall appoint judges and officers, by tribe, to judge the people with just judgment.
Commandment
Appoint judges and officers in all your gates.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 16:18
Application
Justice was local before it was national: every town had its judges, so that no one had to travel far or wait long for a hearing. The gate-court system belonged to Israel's polity, but the principle — justice accessible, local, and impartial — remains the measure of any court system.
Related Topics
Justice; Authority
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To secure impartial justice: no bribes, no partiality, no conviction on one witness. Here that purpose takes the form of judges and officers appointed in every town.
