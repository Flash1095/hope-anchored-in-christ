Do not add to the Law

Deuteronomy 12:32 guards the Law's wholeness from one side: 'What thing soever I command you, observe to do it: thou shalt not add thereto.' God's word is received whole, not enlarged by human addition.

Commandment

Do not add to the Law.

Application

Scripture's stated reason: that ye may keep the commandments of the LORD your God which I command you (Deuteronomy 4:2).

Purpose

Scripture's stated reason: that ye may keep the commandments of the LORD your God which I command you (Deuteronomy 4:2).

Function

I hold this as moral law: a duty written for every age, not for one nation's courts. Deuteronomy 12:32 guards the Law's wholeness from one side. Scripture gives the reason: that ye may keep the commandments of the LORD your God which I command you. The restraint is the prohibition itself: do not add to the Law.
