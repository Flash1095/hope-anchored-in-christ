Do not add to the Law

Command
Do not add to the Law
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 12:32 guards the Law's wholeness from one side: 'What thing soever I command you, observe to do it: thou shalt not add thereto.' God's word is received whole, not enlarged by human addition.
Commandment
Do not add to the Law.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 12:32
Application
Scripture's stated reason: that ye may keep the commandments of the LORD your God which I command you (Deuteronomy 4:2).
