Speak Truth; Let No Corrupt Talk Come Out

Command
Speak Truth; Let No Corrupt Talk Come Out
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's speech command: put away lying and speak truth to your neighbour, and let no corrupt communication proceed from your mouth, but only what is good for edifying.
Commandment
Putting away lying, speak every man truth with his neighbour: for we are members one of another. Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth, but that which is good to the use of edifying.
Scripture
Ephesians 4:25; Ephesians 4:29
Application
Truth-telling is grounded in membership: we are members of one another, so a lie wounds the body. Edifying speech is the positive half — words that build up and minister grace to hearers.
Related Topics
Truth; Speech; Fellowship
Related Books
Ephesians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'for we are members one of another' (Ephesians 4:25).
Duration
