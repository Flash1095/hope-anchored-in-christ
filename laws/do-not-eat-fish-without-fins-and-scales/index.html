Do not eat fish without fins and scales

Water creatures without fins and scales are unclean and may not be eaten; only fish with both fins and scales are clean.

Commandment

Do not eat any water creature that lacks fins and scales.

Application

The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The fins-and-scales test gave Israel a plain rule for the waters. The New Testament records these food distinctions set aside (Mark 7:19; Acts 10).

Purpose

To mark Israel's table as distinct (Leviticus 11). The New Testament records the distinctions set aside: Jesus declares all foods clean (Mark 7:19) and Peter's vision confirms it (Acts 10).

Function

Water creatures without fins and scales were unclean and might not be eaten — a ceremonial law of the waters. Its goal was to mark Israel's table as distinct, the fins-and-scales test giving a plain rule for what came out of the sea. The evil was the same as the land animals: a table with no difference from the nations around. It restrained that by a test anyone could apply at the market — fins and scales, or it stays in the water.
