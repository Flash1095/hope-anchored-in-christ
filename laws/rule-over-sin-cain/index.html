Rule Over Sin

Command
Rule Over Sin
Category
One-time Commands
Summary
God's word to Cain before the murder: sin lies at the door, and Cain must rule over it.
Commandment
Do thou rule over sin that lies at your door.
Scripture
Genesis 4:6-7
Application
A genuine imperative spoken to one man at one moment of temptation, not a general law text: Cain was warned before he acted and did not obey. Its principle is preached universally, but the command as given was Cain's, at that door, on that day — hence One-time here, with the universal principle noted rather than used to reclass it.
