Do not make anointing oil like God's holy oil

The command: Do not make anointing oil like God's holy oil (Exodus 30:32). No one was to compound oil after the same recipe for common use; it was holy to the LORD.

Commandment

Do not make anointing oil like God's holy oil.

Application

This row narrows the parent group command to this one mitzvah. The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Hebrews teaches the earthly tabernacle was a copy of the heavenly one, and Christ now serves as high priest in the true tabernacle.

Purpose

To give Israel a mediated approach to God through the tabernacle pattern (Exodus 25:40). Hebrews teaches the earthly copy gave way to Christ's priesthood in the true tabernacle (Hebrews 8:1-6).

Function

No one was to compound oil after the same recipe for common use; it was holy to the LORD — a ceremonial law guarding the holy oil. Its goal was to keep the anointing oil unique, the recipe itself set apart so the oil could not be copied for ordinary use. The evil was counterfeiting the sacred, making God's holy oil into a household perfume. It restrained that by forbidding the making itself: the recipe was holy, and the copy was forbidden.
