Flee Youthful Lusts; Pursue Righteousness

Command
Flee Youthful Lusts; Pursue Righteousness
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's fleeing command to Timothy, given as a general charge: flee youthful lusts, and follow righteousness, faith, charity, peace, with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart.
Commandment
Flee also youthful lusts: but follow righteousness, faith, charity, peace, with them that call on the Lord out of a pure heart.
Scripture
2 Timothy 2:22
Application
Fleeing is active, not passive: lusts are not debated with but fled from, and the pursuit that replaces them is corporate — 'with them that call on the Lord'. Addressed to Timothy, it is drafted because its wording is a general charge to the young in faith.
Related Topics
Purity; Faith; Peace
Related Books
2 Timothy
Related People
Paul; Timothy
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to replace forbidden desire with pursued righteousness in company, not alone.
Duration
For all time
