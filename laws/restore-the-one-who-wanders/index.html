Restore the One Who Wanders

Command
Restore the One Who Wanders
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
James's restoration command: if any err from the truth and one convert him, let him know that he who converts the sinner from the error of his way saves a soul from death.
Commandment
Brethren, if any of you do err from the truth, and one convert him; let him know, that he which converteth the sinner from the error of his way shall save a soul from death, and shall hide a multitude of sins.
Scripture
James 5:19-20
Application
Distinct from the burden-bearing row's gentle restoration (Galatians 6:1, noted there): this is the wandering believer pursued, not the fallen brother restored — the rescue of a soul from death, covering a multitude of sins.
Related Topics
Mercy; Fellowship; Repentance
Related Books
James
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: he 'shall save a soul from death, and shall hide a multitude of sins' (James 5:20).
Duration
For all time
