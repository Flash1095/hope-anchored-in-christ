Do not make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land

Exodus 34:12 warns Israel, at the renewal of the covenant after the golden calf, to make no covenant with the inhabitants of the land, 'lest it be for a snare in the midst of thee.' Treaties would have bound Israel to peoples whose altars and worship it was commanded to destroy.

Commandment

Make no covenant with the inhabitants of the land.

Application

The command belonged to the conquest generation; its principle endures wherever an alliance would bind God's people to idolatry. Agreements are weighed by whether they protect or endanger faithfulness.

Purpose

Scripture's stated reason: lest it be for a snare in the midst of thee (Exodus 34:12).
