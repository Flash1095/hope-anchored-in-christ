Keep the law of a paid bailee

Command
Keep the law of a paid bailee
Category
Civil Law
Summary
A paid bailee — one hired to keep another's animal or goods — answers by the Law's distinctions: he is liable where his own fault lost it, and cleared by oath where loss came without his fault.
Commandment
Keep the law of a paid bailee.
Scripture
Exodus 22:10
Application
Trusteeship is graded by reward: one paid to keep a thing bears more responsibility than one who keeps it for free. The rule still guides any paid custody — the keeper's duty matches the trust and payment he accepted.
Related Topics
Justice; Money; Truth
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
To make wrongs right: a paid keeper answers for goods entrusted to him, by the Law's distinctions between fault and misfortune.
Duration
For all time
