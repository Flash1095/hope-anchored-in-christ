Do not send a freed servant away empty-handed

When a Hebrew servant is sent out free in the seventh year, he must not be sent away empty-handed.

Commandment

Do not send a freed servant away empty-handed.

Application

Freedom without provision is a door into fresh destitution: the freed servant leaves with something to start on. The negative form guards the positive gift — liberation must not be hollow.

Purpose

To regulate and restrain an ancient institution: six-year release, provision, no ruthless rule (Exodus 21; Deuteronomy 15). Here that restraint forbids sending a freed servant away empty.
