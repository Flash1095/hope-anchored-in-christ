Do Not Move Your Neighbour's Landmark

Command
Do Not Move Your Neighbour's Landmark
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Thou shalt not remove thy neighbour's landmark, which they of old time have set in thine inheritance. This is the boundary-stone law, distinct from the live Inheritance row, whose Numbers and Deuteronomy 21 ranges do not include Deuteronomy 19:14.
Commandment
Do not remove your neighbour's ancient landmark in the inheritance God has given.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 19:14
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as civil. Boundaries are property made visible: moving one is theft that no one can see at once, because the field itself looks unchanged. The New Testament does not restate boundary stones, but it does restate the theft they guard against (Ephesians 4:28).
Related Topics
Justice; Money; Integrity
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: the landmark is the inherited boundary set by former generations, and moving it steals land by stealth. Scripture states the prohibition without a separate reason (Deuteronomy 19:14).
Duration
For all time
