Stand Fast, Watch, and Be Strong

Command
Stand Fast, Watch, and Be Strong
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's steadfastness command: watch, stand fast in the faith, quit yourselves like men, be strong, and let all your things be done with charity.
Commandment
Watch ye, stand fast in the faith, quit you like men, be strong.
Scripture
1 Corinthians 16:13; Philippians 4:1; 1 Thessalonians 3:8
Application
A soldier's closing charge, repeated across letters: watchfulness, unmoved faith, courage, strength. The Philippians and Thessalonians parallels show it as a standing apostolic charge, not a one-letter flourish.
Related Topics
Courage; Endurance; Faith
Related Books
1 Corinthians; Philippians; 1 Thessalonians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to hold the line of faith under pressure rather than drift with it.
Duration
For all time
