Keep the law of a borrower

A borrower who borrows an animal or article and it is hurt or dies while the owner is absent must make it good; if the owner was present, or it was hired, the terms differ.

Commandment

Keep the law of a borrower.

Application

Borrowing is not a gift: the borrower enjoys the use, so he bears the risk of loss while it is in his hands. The rule still teaches that borrowed things are held in trust and must be made good if lost.

Purpose

To make wrongs right: the borrower who had the use of a thing answers for its loss or hurt.

Function

A borrower who borrows an animal or article and it is hurt or dies while the owner is absent must make it good. Not worship but government: the Law acting as Israel's civil code. What it wanted was to make wrongs right: the borrower who had the use of a thing answers for its loss or hurt. The Law's answer was a working one.
