Do not make baldness for the dead

Deuteronomy 14:1 forbids making baldness between the eyes for the dead: 'Ye are the children of the LORD your God: ye shall not cut yourselves, nor make any baldness between your eyes for the dead.' Israel's grief was not to take the mourning rites of the nations.

Commandment

Do not make baldness for the dead.

Application

Grief is real and permitted; pagan mourning rites are not the shape it must take. God's children mourn as those who belong to a living Father, not as those who cut themselves for the dead. Debated: whether the prohibition covers all ritual mourning marks today, or only those tied to pagan rites for the dead, is a genuine difference of reading — this row does not settle it.

Purpose

Scripture's stated ground: ye are the children of the LORD your God (Deuteronomy 14:1).

Function

Deuteronomy 14:1 forbids making baldness between the eyes for the dead. This one speaks to character, not to courts, though its form belonged to Israel's day.
