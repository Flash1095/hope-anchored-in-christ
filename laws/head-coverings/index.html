Head Coverings

Command
Head Coverings
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's instruction on head coverings in worship: men uncovered, women covered, in the Corinthian assembly. HELD ITEM, drafted on its merits: the borderline doubt is carried here, not resolved — whether the covering is a transcultural command or a first-century cultural expression is disputed.
Commandment
In worship, let men pray uncovered and women pray covered, as a sign of the created order.
Scripture
1 Corinthians 11:2-16
Application
Paul grounds the practice in creation, nature, and the angels — yet its cultural expression varies widely across churches today, and this row does not settle which reading is right.
Related Topics
Worship; Family; Obedience
Related Books
1 Corinthians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to display the created order in worship — though whether the covering itself or only the order it signified endures is the debated point.
