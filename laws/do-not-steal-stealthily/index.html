Do not steal stealthily

Command
Do not steal stealthily
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Do not steal by stealth — quietly taking what is not yours, even where no force is used.
Commandment
Do not steal stealthily.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:11
Application
Stealth does not soften theft; it is theft's ordinary form, the quiet taking that hopes nobody will notice. The command names the secret taking alongside open robbery, so that no thief can plead that he took gently.
Related Topics
Justice; Truth; Money
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: taking by stealth is theft no less than taking by force, and is forbidden as such (Leviticus 19:11).
Duration
For all time
