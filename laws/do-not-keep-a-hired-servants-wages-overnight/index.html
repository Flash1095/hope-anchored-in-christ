Do not keep a hired servant's wages overnight

Command
Do not keep a hired servant's wages overnight
Category
Civil Law
Summary
The wages of a hired servant must not remain with the employer all night until morning; the day's pay is paid on the day.
Commandment
Do not keep a hired servant's wages overnight.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:13
Application
A day labourer lives on the day's wage: keeping it overnight is keeping his supper and his family's bread. Prompt pay is not generosity but justice, and delayed pay is a quiet form of oppression.
Related Topics
Justice; Money; Mercy
Related Books
Leviticus; Deuteronomy; James
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Deuteronomy states the ground: the laborer depends on the day's pay (Deuteronomy 24:15); James warns withheld wages cry out (James 5:4). Here that ground forbids keeping wages overnight.
Duration
For all time
