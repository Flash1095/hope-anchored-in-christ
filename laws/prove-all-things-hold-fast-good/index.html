Prove All Things; Hold Fast the Good

Command
Prove All Things; Hold Fast the Good
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's testing command: prove all things, hold fast that which is good, and abstain from all appearance of evil.
Commandment
Prove all things; hold fast that which is good. Abstain from all appearance of evil.
Scripture
1 Thessalonians 5:21-22
Application
Testing is commanded, not forbidden: claims — including prophetic ones — are weighed, the good kept, and even the appearance of evil avoided, because witness depends on it.
Related Topics
Discernment; Truth; Doctrine
Related Books
1 Thessalonians
Related People
Paul
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to combine open examination with firm retention of what proves true.
