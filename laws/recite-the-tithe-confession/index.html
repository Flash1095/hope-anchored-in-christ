Recite the tithe confession

The command: Recite the tithe confession (Deuteronomy 26:13). After giving the third-year tithe, Israel confessed before the LORD that the hallowed things had been brought out of the house and given as commanded.

Commandment

Recite the tithe confession, as prescribed in Deuteronomy 26:13.

Application

It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.

Purpose

TRADITIONAL reading: the confession made giving public and accountable before the LORD, closing the tithe cycle with an honest declaration.

Function

After giving the third-year tithe, Israel confessed before the LORD that the hallowed things had been brought out of the house and given as commanded — a ceremonial law of honest declaration. Its goal, traditionally, was to make giving public and accountable before God, closing the tithe cycle with an honest word. The evil was quiet dishonesty in giving, keeping back what was claimed as given. It restrained that by the confession itself: the giver said aloud what he had done, and the words held him to it.
