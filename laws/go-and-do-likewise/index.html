Go and Do Likewise

Command
Go and Do Likewise
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' closing command to the parable of the Good Samaritan, stated as a command and not merely a moral: go, and do thou likewise.
Commandment
Go, and do thou likewise.
Scripture
Luke 10:37
Application
The lawyer asked who his neighbour was; Jesus reverses the question into action. The Samaritan's mercy — binding wounds, paying the cost — becomes the hearer's assignment. This row is included because Jesus states it as a command, not because a parable's moral was assumed to be one.
Related Topics
Mercy; Love; Service
Related Books
Luke
Related People
Jesus
Related Events
The Good Samaritan
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to turn neighbour-love from a definition into costly action toward whoever lies wounded in the road.
Duration
For all time
