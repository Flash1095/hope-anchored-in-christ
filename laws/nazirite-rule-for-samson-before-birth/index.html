Keep the Nazirite Rule for Samson

The angel's command to Samson's mother before his birth: keep the Nazirite rule for the child from the womb.

Commandment

Beware, and drink not wine nor strong drink, and eat not any unclean thing; for the child shall be a Nazarite unto God from the womb.

Application

The command was given to Manoah's wife before Samson's birth (Judges 13:4-5, repeated 13:7): no wine or strong drink, no unclean thing, no razor on the child's head, because the child would be a Nazarite from the womb. Included because the imperative form is explicit and person-specific, even though its recipient is Samson's mother and its subject is Samson.

Purpose

To set Samson apart from the womb as a Nazarite who would begin to deliver Israel from the Philistines (Judges 13:5).

Function

The command was given to Manoah's wife before Samson's birth: no wine or strong drink, no unclean thing, no razor on the child's head. This was a one-time command, given once for one child, so the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not apply to it. Its goal was to set Samson apart from the womb as a Nazarite who would begin to deliver Israel from the Philistines. The evil it addressed was the Philistine oppression Samson would begin to break. It restrained that evil by the consecration itself: a child set apart from birth.
