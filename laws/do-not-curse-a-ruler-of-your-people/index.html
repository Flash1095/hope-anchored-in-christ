Do not curse a ruler of your people

Command
Do not curse a ruler of your people
Category
Civil Law
Summary
The ruler of the people — the one set in authority over them — is not to be cursed.
Commandment
Do not curse a ruler of your people.
Scripture
Exodus 22:28
Application
The New Testament repeats the respect: honour the king (1 Peter 2:17), and be subject to governing authorities (Romans 13:1-7). Respect does not mean silent approval of wrongdoing — the prophets rebuked kings — but it forbids contemptuous cursing of the ruler's person and office.
Related Topics
Authority; Justice; Speech
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: the ruler bears God-given authority, so cursing him dishonours the order God set. Scripture states the prohibition without a separate reason (Exodus 22:28).
Duration
For all time
