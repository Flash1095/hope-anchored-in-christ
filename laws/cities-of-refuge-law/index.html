Cities of Refuge

Command
Cities of Refuge
Category
Civil Law
Summary
The asylum law: six cities where a person who killed unintentionally could flee and stand trial, safe from the avenger of blood.
Commandment
Let the one who kills unintentionally flee to a city of refuge and stand trial before the congregation.
Scripture
Numbers 35:9-34; Deuteronomy 19:1-13; Joshua 20:1-9
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as civil. The cities balanced two truths: life is sacred, and intent matters. They protected the innocent from vengeance while still requiring a fair hearing.
Related Topics
Justice; Mercy
Related Books
Numbers; Deuteronomy; Joshua
Related People
Moses; Joshua
