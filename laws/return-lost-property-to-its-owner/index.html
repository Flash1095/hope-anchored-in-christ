Return lost property to its owner

Command
Return lost property to its owner
Category
Civil Law
Summary
If you meet your enemy's ox or ass going astray, you shall surely bring it back to him; lost property is returned to its owner, even an enemy's.
Commandment
Return lost property to its owner.
Scripture
Exodus 23:4
Application
Lost property is not finders-keepers; it belongs to its owner, and you are its keeper until he comes for it. The command pointedly includes an enemy's animal: neighbourliness is not reserved for friends.
Related Topics
Justice; Love; Neighbor
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Exodus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated ground: the lost thing belongs to your brother, and you are its keeper until he comes for it (Deuteronomy 22:1-3).
Duration
For all time
