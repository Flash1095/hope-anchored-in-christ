Women in Worship: Learn Quietly

Command
Women in Worship: Learn Quietly
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Paul's worship-order instruction: let women learn in silence with all subjection, and let women keep silence in the churches where the law's order is in view. Drafted neutrally and without settling the debate: some traditions read this as a transcultural order for worship, others as a first-century Corinthian and Ephesian correction whose application varies — this row does not settle it.
Commandment
Let the woman learn in silence with all subjection. Let your women keep silence in the churches: for it is not permitted unto them to speak; but they are commanded to be under obedience, as also saith the law.
Scripture
1 Timothy 2:11-12; 1 Corinthians 14:34-35
Application
The two passages are grouped as one row because both address women's speech in the gathered assembly, and both are debated together. Paul's stated ground is the created order (1 Timothy 2:13-14); the application churches draw from it differs widely, and this row reports the texts without adjudicating.
