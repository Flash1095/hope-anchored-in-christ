Do not leave the Passover fat until morning

Command
Do not leave the Passover fat until morning
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The fat of the Passover sacrifice may not be left until morning; the sacrifice must be completed within its appointed time.
Commandment
Do not leave the fat of the Passover sacrifice until the morning.
Scripture
Exodus 23:18
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Like the flesh, the fat belonged to the night's offering and could not be left over to the next day.

Purpose
Scripture's stated context: the sacrifice is offered at its appointed season, and its parts are dealt with within that time (Exodus 23:18).
