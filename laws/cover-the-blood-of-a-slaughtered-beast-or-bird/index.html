Cover the blood of a slaughtered beast or bird

Command
Cover the blood of a slaughtered beast or bird
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
When a beast or bird that may be eaten is hunted and killed, its blood must be poured out and covered with dust.
Commandment
When you hunt a beast or bird that may be eaten, pour out its blood and cover it with dust.
Scripture
Leviticus 17:13
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. Covering the blood honored the life it carried, even outside the sanctuary: the blood was not treated as common. The life-in-the-blood principle is repeated at Jerusalem for Gentile believers (Acts 15:29).
Related Topics
Purity; Holiness
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated reason: for the life of all flesh is the blood thereof (Leviticus 17:14).
