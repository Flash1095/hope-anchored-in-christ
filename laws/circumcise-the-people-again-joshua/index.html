Circumcise the People Again

God's command to Joshua at Gilgal: make sharp knives and circumcise the children of Israel the second time.

Commandment

Make thee sharp knives, and circumcise again the children of Israel the second time.

Application

A second circumcision because the wilderness generation had not been circumcised on the way (Joshua 5:4-7). Distinct from the Abrahamic covenant row: that row is the standing covenant sign; this is the one-time renewal act at Gilgal. RelatedEvents left empty: no story title matches the Gilgal circumcision, and Crossing the Jordan covers chapters 3-4, not this act.

Purpose

To renew the covenant sign in the new generation before they took the land, rolling away the reproach of Egypt (Joshua 5:9).

Function

A second circumcision, because the wilderness generation had not been circumcised on the way. This was a one-time command, given once at Gilgal for a specific moment, so the civil, ceremonial, and moral division does not apply to it. Its goal was to renew the covenant sign in the new generation before they took the land, rolling away the reproach of Egypt. The evil or restraint angle does not apply here; this was a renewal act, not a law against a recurring evil. Distinct from the Abrahamic covenant command, which is the standing sign itself.
