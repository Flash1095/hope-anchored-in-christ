Do not deal falsely with one another

Command
Do not deal falsely with one another
Category
Civil Law
Summary
Dealings between neighbours must be honest: no false dealing, no sharp practice dressed up as trade.
Commandment
Do not deal falsely with one another.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:11
Application
False dealing is fraud before it is theft — the rigged scale, the hidden defect, the misleading word. Honest trade is a moral duty, not merely a commercial convention.
Related Topics
Justice; Truth; Money
Related Books
Leviticus
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: false dealing corrupts the trust on which neighbourly life and trade depend, so it is forbidden alongside lying and stealing (Leviticus 19:11).
Duration
For all time
