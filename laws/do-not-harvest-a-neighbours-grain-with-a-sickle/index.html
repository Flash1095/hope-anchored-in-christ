Do not harvest a neighbour's grain with a sickle

Command
Do not harvest a neighbour's grain with a sickle
Category
Civil Law
Summary
When you go into your neighbour's standing corn, you may pluck ears with your hand, but you must not move a sickle over his grain: eating in passing is allowed, harvesting is not.
Commandment
Do not harvest a neighbour's grain with a sickle.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 23:25
Application
The traveller's hunger is met from the field's edge, but the sickle would turn a snack into a harvest. The line between eating and harvesting protected the neighbour's crop while feeding the passer-by — a distinction tied to Israel's agrarian field law.
Related Topics
Mercy; Justice; Neighbor
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: the neighbour's field feeds the passing traveller by hand, but the sickle marks a harvest, and a harvest belongs to the field's owner.
