Do not destroy objects associated with God's name

Command
Do not destroy objects associated with God's name
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Deuteronomy 12:4 forbids treating the LORD as the nations treated their gods: 'Ye shall not do so unto the LORD your God.' The nations destroyed and desecrated in worship; Israel was not to handle God's name, his sanctuary, or his worship with the same violence.
Commandment
Do not destroy or desecrate what is associated with God's name, as the nations did to their gods.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 12:4
Application
Reverence for God's name covers his sanctuary, his word, and his worship: what bears his name is handled with honour, not smashed, defaced, or treated as common.
Related Topics
Worship; Holiness
Related Books
Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated context: the verse answers the destruction of pagan altars in the preceding verses — that destruction is for idols, never for the LORD (Deuteronomy 12:2-4).
Duration
For all time
