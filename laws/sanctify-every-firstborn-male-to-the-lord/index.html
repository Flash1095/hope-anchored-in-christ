Sanctify every firstborn male to the LORD

Command
Sanctify every firstborn male to the LORD
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: Sanctify every firstborn male to the LORD (Exodus 13:2). The firstborn male, of both man and beast, belonged to the LORD from birth, set apart in memory of the Exodus.
Commandment
Sanctify every firstborn male to the LORD, as prescribed in Exodus 13:2.
Scripture
Exodus 13:2
Application
It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.
Related Topics
Temple; Redemption; Consecration
Related Books
Exodus
Related Events
The Passover
Purpose
Scripture's stated reason: because the LORD slew all the firstborn in Egypt and spared Israel's, therefore the firstborn are his (Exodus 13:14-15).
