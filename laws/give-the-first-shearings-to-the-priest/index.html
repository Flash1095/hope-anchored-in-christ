Give the first shearings to the priest

Command
Give the first shearings to the priest
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: Give the first shearings to the priest (Deuteronomy 18:4). The first of the fleece of the sheep was given to the priest for his clothing.
Commandment
Give the first shearings to the priest, as prescribed in Deuteronomy 18:4.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 18:4
Application
It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.
