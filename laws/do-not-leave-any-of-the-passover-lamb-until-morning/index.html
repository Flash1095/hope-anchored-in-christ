Do not leave any of the Passover lamb until morning

Command
Do not leave any of the Passover lamb until morning
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
No part of the Passover lamb may remain uneaten until morning; what is left must be burned with fire.
Commandment
Leave none of the Passover lamb until the morning; whatever remains until morning, burn with fire.
Scripture
Exodus 12:10
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as ceremonial. The New Testament reads the Passover lamb as fulfilled in Christ, our Passover sacrificed for us (1 Corinthians 5:7). The ritual meal form belongs to Israel's covenant calendar, not to Christian observance.
