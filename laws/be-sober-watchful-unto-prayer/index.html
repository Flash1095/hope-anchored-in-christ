Be Sober and Watchful unto Prayer

Command
Be Sober and Watchful unto Prayer
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Peter's sobriety command: the end of all things is at hand, so be sober and watch unto prayer; be sober, be vigilant, because your adversary the devil walks about seeking whom he may devour.
Commandment
But the end of all things is at hand: be ye therefore sober, and watch unto prayer. Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour.
Scripture
1 Peter 4:7; 1 Peter 5:8
Application
Sobriety here is clear-headedness, not merely abstinence: a clouded mind cannot pray or stand guard. The devil's prowl is the reason given for vigilance.
Related Topics
Prayer; Courage; Temptation
Related Books
1 Peter
Purpose
Scripture's own reason: 'your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour' (1 Peter 5:8).
Duration
For all time
