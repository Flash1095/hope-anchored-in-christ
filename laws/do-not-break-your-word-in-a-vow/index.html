Do not break your word in a vow

Command
Do not break your word in a vow
Category
Civil Law
Summary
If a man vows a vow to the LORD or swears an oath to bind his soul, he shall not break his word: he shall do according to all that proceeds from his mouth.
Commandment
Do not break your word in a vow.
Scripture
Numbers 30:2
Application
A vow is a word given to God, not a bargaining chip. The New Testament sharpens the duty further: let your Yes be Yes and your No be No (Matthew 5:37; James 5:12) — plain truthfulness that needs no oath to prop it up.
Related Topics
Truth; Obedience; Fear of God
Related Books
Numbers; Deuteronomy
Related People
Moses
Purpose
Scripture's stated reason: for the LORD thy God will surely require it of thee; and it would be sin in thee (Deuteronomy 23:21).
Duration
For all time
