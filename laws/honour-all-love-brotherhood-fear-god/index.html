Honour All People; Love the Brotherhood; Fear God

Command
Honour All People; Love the Brotherhood; Fear God
Category
Apostolic Commands
Summary
Peter's honour command: honour all men, love the brotherhood, fear God, honour the king.
Commandment
Honour all men. Love the brotherhood. Fear God. Honour the king.
Scripture
1 Peter 2:17
Application
Distinct from the submission-to-authorities row (live), which governs obedience to rulers: this governs honour itself — universal to all people, special to the brotherhood, reverent to God, and due even to the king.
Related Topics
Justice; Love; Fellowship
Related Books
1 Peter
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to order honour rightly — wide to all, deep to the church, highest to God.
Duration
For all time
