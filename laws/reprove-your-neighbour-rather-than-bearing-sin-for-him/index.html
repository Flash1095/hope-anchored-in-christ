Reprove your neighbour rather than bearing sin for him

Command
Reprove your neighbour rather than bearing sin for him
Category
Moral Law
Summary
One of the neighbour-laws of Leviticus 19:17-18: reprove your neighbour rather than bearing sin for him, part of the passage that ends in 'thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.'
Commandment
Reprove your neighbour rather than bearing sin for him
Scripture
Leviticus 19:17
Application
Jesus joins this neighbour rule to love of God as the hinge of the Law (Matthew 22:39-40); love of neighbour is the measure of love of God in practice.
