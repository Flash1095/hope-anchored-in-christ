Courts, Judges, and Witnesses

Command
Courts, Judges, and Witnesses
Category
Civil Law
Summary
The judicial laws: judges in every town, no bribes, no partiality, conviction only on two or three witnesses, and false witnesses bearing the penalty they sought.
Commandment
Appoint honest judges, take no bribe, show no partiality, and convict no one on a single witness.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 16:18-20; Deuteronomy 17:6-7; Deuteronomy 19:15-21
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as civil. The two-witness rule carried into the church's own discipline (Matthew 18:16; 1 Timothy 5:19), and the ban on bribes remains a standing test of any justice system.
