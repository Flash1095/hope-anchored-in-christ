Give the great heave offering to the priests

Command
Give the great heave offering to the priests
Category
Ceremonial Law
Summary
The command: Give the great heave offering to the priests (Numbers 18:8). The heave offerings of Israel, the best of oil, wine, wheat, and firstfruits, belonged to Aaron and his sons.
Commandment
Give the great heave offering to the priests, as prescribed in Numbers 18:8.
Scripture
Numbers 18:8
Application
It cannot be practised as written without the standing temple and Aaronic priesthood; its enduring lesson is God's holiness, the cost of atonement, and worship offered exactly as God appoints.

Purpose
Scripture's stated ground: the heave offerings were the priests' portion for their service at the altar (Numbers 18:8-19).
