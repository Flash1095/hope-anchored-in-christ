Do Not Intermarry with Idolatrous Nations

Command
Do Not Intermarry with Idolatrous Nations
Category
Moral Law
Summary
Israel shall not make marriages with the seven nations of the land — giving daughters to their sons or taking their daughters for sons. The verse itself gives the reason: they will turn your children away to serve other gods. The scope of this today is debated — see debated_mappings.
Commandment
Do not intermarry with the idolatrous nations of the land.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 7:3-4
Application
The moral/ceremonial/civil division is a traditional classification, not Scripture's own division; this law is traditionally classed as moral. Views differ today: some read the command by its stated reason — faith, not ethnicity — and point to Ruth and to Moses' own household, where foreigners who joined Israel's faith were welcomed; others read it as a standing ethnic separation for Israel. The New Testament parallel most often cited is not being unequally yoked with unbelievers (2 Corinthians 6:14), which is about shared faith, not ancestry.
