Enter by the Narrow Gate

Command
Enter by the Narrow Gate
Category
Commands of Jesus
Summary
Jesus' gate command: enter through the strait gate, for wide is the gate and broad the way that leads to destruction.
Commandment
Enter ye in at the strait gate: for wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life.
Scripture
Matthew 7:13-14; Luke 13:24
Application
The command warns against following the crowd. The narrow way is not narrow because God is stingy, but because it is the way of his commands rather than self-chosen religion.
Related Topics
Obedience; Kingdom of God
Related Books
Matthew; Luke
Related People
Jesus
Related Events
The Sermon on the Mount
Purpose
TRADITIONAL reading: to press hearers to choose the costly way of obedience over the easy way of the majority.
Duration
For all time
