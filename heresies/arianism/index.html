Arianism

Heresy
Arianism
Founder
Arius (c. AD 256-336)
Summary
Arianism taught in the early 4th century that the Son of God was created by the Father and therefore not eternal or fully God. It held that there was a time when the Son did not exist.
Beliefs
Jesus Christ was created by God the Father; The Son is not eternal; The Son is divine but not fully God; The Father alone is uncreated
Controversy
Condemned at the Council of Nicaea AD 325, which produced the Nicene Creed
Christian Response
The church affirmed that the Son is of one substance with the Father and eternally begotten, not made, citing John 1:1 and Colossians 1:15-17.
Related People
Arius; Athanasius; Constantine I
Related Doctrines
Trinity; Deity of Christ; Incarnation
