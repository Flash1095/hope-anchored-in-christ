Prosperity Gospel

Heresy
Prosperity Gospel
Founder
Teachers including Kenneth Hagin (1917-2003) and later Word of Faith teachers
Summary
The Prosperity Gospel, associated with the Word of Faith movement in the 20th and 21st centuries, teaches that faith, positive confession, and giving will reliably bring health and material wealth. Critics in historic churches view this as a distortion of biblical teaching on suffering and contentment.
Beliefs
Faith guarantees health and wealth in this life; Speaking positive confessions creates reality; Giving money leads to multiplied financial return; Suffering usually indicates lack of faith
Controversy
Addressed in statements and books by evangelical, Reformed, and Pentecostal leaders who affirm healing and provision while rejecting guaranteed wealth formulas
Christian Response
The church response affirms God's goodness and care while teaching contentment, generosity, and trust in suffering, citing Philippians 4, 1 Timothy 6, and the book of Job.
Related People
Kenneth Hagin; Kenneth Copeland
Related Doctrines
Faith; Suffering; Stewardship
