Montanism

Heresy
Montanism
Founder
Montanus (active c. AD 156-172)
Summary
Montanism, also called the New Prophecy, began in Phrygia in the 2nd century. It emphasized ecstatic prophecy, strict discipline, and an expectation that the New Jerusalem would soon appear in Phrygia.
Beliefs
New prophecies continue through Montanus and his followers; Ecstatic speech is a mark of the Spirit; Strict fasting and discipline are required; The end times would center on Pepuza in Phrygia
Controversy
Councils in Asia Minor in the late 2nd century rejected its prophecies; Tertullian later supported the movement
Christian Response
The church affirmed the work of the Holy Spirit while teaching that prophecy must be tested by Scripture and that Christ's return is not tied to one place, citing 1 Thessalonians 5:20-21.
Related People
Montanus; Priscilla; Maximilla; Tertullian
Related Doctrines
Holy Spirit; Prophecy; Eschatology
