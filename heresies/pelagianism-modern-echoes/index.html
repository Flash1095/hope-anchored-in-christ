Pelagianism (Modern Echoes)

Heresy
Pelagianism (Modern Echoes)
Founder
Modern teachers and movements emphasizing human ability
Summary
This entry describes modern ideas that echo Pelagian themes, such as the belief that people are basically good and can perfect themselves by effort alone. It is included as a comparison category, not as a single historic movement.
Beliefs
People are basically good by nature; Moral improvement comes mainly from personal effort; Talk of sin and grace is unnecessary or harmful; Examples and education are enough to save
Controversy
Discussed by theologians in response to self-help and moralistic teaching in churches and popular culture
Christian Response
The church response emphasizes both human responsibility and the need for grace and new birth, citing Jeremiah 17:9, John 3, and Titus 3:5.
Related People
Pelagius; Augustine of Hippo
Related Doctrines
Original Sin; Grace; Sanctification
