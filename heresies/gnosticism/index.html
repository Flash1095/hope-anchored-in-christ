Gnosticism

Heresy
Gnosticism
Founder
Various teachers including Valentinus (c. AD 100-160)
Summary
Gnosticism was a diverse movement in the 1st and 2nd centuries that taught salvation comes through secret knowledge. It often viewed the material world as evil and created by a lesser god.
Beliefs
Salvation comes through secret knowledge; The material world is evil; The God of the Old Testament is a lesser creator; Jesus brought hidden knowledge for the spiritual elite
Controversy
Opposed by early church writers such as Irenaeus in Against Heresies (c. AD 180) and by the formation of the New Testament canon
Christian Response
The church affirmed that creation is good, salvation is by grace through faith in Christ, and the gospel is openly proclaimed, citing Genesis 1 and John 1.
Related People
Valentinus; Marcion; Irenaeus
Related Doctrines
Creation; Salvation; Incarnation
