Marcionism

Marcionism taught in the 2nd century that the God of the Old Testament was different from and inferior to the God revealed by Jesus. Marcion rejected the Old Testament and edited the New Testament writings he accepted.

Beliefs

The Old Testament God is a separate and harsh creator; The God of Jesus is only love and mercy; The Old Testament should be rejected; Only parts of Luke and Paul's letters are Scripture

Controversy

Marcion was excommunicated in Rome AD 144; his teaching prompted the church to clarify the canon of Scripture

Details

Heresy: Marcionism
Founder: Marcion of Sinope (c. AD 85-160)
Christian Response: The church affirmed one God revealed in both Testaments and preserved the Old Testament as Scripture, citing Luke 24:27 and 2 Timothy 3:16.
Related People: Marcion; Tertullian; Irenaeus
Related Doctrines: Canon of Scripture; Nature of God; Old Testament
Related Places: Rome
