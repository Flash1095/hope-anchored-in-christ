Adoptionism

Heresy
Adoptionism
Founder
Theodotus of Byzantium (active late 2nd century)
Summary
Adoptionism taught in the 2nd and 8th centuries that Jesus was born a human and was adopted as God's Son at his baptism or resurrection because of his obedience. It denied his pre-existence as God the Son.
Beliefs
Jesus was adopted as Son at his baptism; Jesus did not pre-exist as God; Jesus earned sonship by obedience; Christ's deity is a conferred status, not his nature
Controversy
Condemned in Rome in the late 2nd century and again in the Spanish Adoptionist controversy addressed at Frankfurt AD 794
Christian Response
The church affirmed that Jesus is God's Son by nature from eternity, citing John 1:1-14 and Romans 1:3-4.
Related People
Theodotus of Byzantium; Elipandus; Alcuin
Related Doctrines
Deity of Christ; Incarnation; Sonship
Related Places
Rome
