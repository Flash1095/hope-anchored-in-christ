Donatism

Donatism arose in North Africa after persecution under Diocletian in the early 4th century. It taught that the church must be pure and that sacraments administered by clergy who had lapsed under persecution were invalid.

Beliefs

The church must consist only of the pure; Sacraments by unworthy clergy are invalid; Christians who lapsed under persecution must be rebaptized to rejoin

Controversy

Addressed at the Council of Arles AD 314 and in Augustine's writings; a conference at Carthage AD 411 ruled against the Donatists

Christian Response

The church taught that the validity of baptism and communion rests on Christ, not the moral worth of the minister, citing Matthew 13 on wheat and weeds growing together.

Details

Heresy: Donatism
Founder: Donatus Magnus (active 4th century)
Related People: Donatus Magnus; Augustine; Constantine I
Related Doctrines: Church; Baptism; Grace
