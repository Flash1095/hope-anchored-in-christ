Ebionism

Ebionism was held by some early Jewish-Christian groups who kept the Mosaic law as binding for all followers of Jesus. They typically viewed Jesus as a human prophet and Messiah rather than as pre-existent and divine.

Beliefs

Jesus was a human Messiah, not pre-existent God; Keeping the Mosaic law is required for salvation; Jesus was adopted as God's son because of his obedience; Paul's teaching was rejected by many Ebionites

Controversy

Opposed by writers such as Irenaeus and Origen; its views were inconsistent with the councils affirming Christ's deity

Christian Response

The church affirmed Jesus as Lord and God and taught justification by faith, while honoring the law's role in Israel's history, citing Acts 15 and Galatians 2-3.

Details

Heresy: Ebionism
Founder: Early Jewish-Christian groups (1st-2nd centuries)
Related People: Irenaeus; Origen
Related Doctrines: Deity of Christ; Justification; Law and Grace
