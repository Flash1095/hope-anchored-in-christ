Docetism

Heresy
Docetism
Founder
Early teachers in the late 1st and 2nd centuries
Summary
Docetism taught that Jesus only seemed to have a physical body and to suffer. Its name comes from the Greek word meaning to seem or appear.
Beliefs
Jesus only appeared to be human; Christ did not truly suffer or die in the flesh; The spiritual Christ separated from the man Jesus at the cross
Controversy
Opposed in the letters of Ignatius of Antioch (c. AD 110) and addressed in 1 John and 2 John
Christian Response
The church affirmed that Jesus came truly in the flesh and truly died and rose bodily, citing 1 John 4:2-3 and Luke 24:39.
Related People
Ignatius of Antioch; John the Apostle
Related Doctrines
Incarnation; Atonement; Resurrection
Related Events
The Crucifixion
