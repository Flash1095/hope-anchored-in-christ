Perizzites

Civilization
Perizzites
Time Period
Patriarchal era through the conquest, c. 2000-1200 BC
Region
The hill country and open country of Canaan, especially around Shechem and Bethel
History
The Perizzites appear in the land from Abram's time: in Genesis 13:7 the Canaanite and the Perizzite already dwelt in the land when Abram and Lot separated. They are named in the promise of Genesis 15:20, in the exodus promise of Exodus 3:8, and among the seven nations of Deuteronomy 7:1. In Joshua 17:15 Joshua tells the house of Joseph to clear land in the hill country of the Perizzites and the Rephaim, and in Judges 1:4-5 Judah defeated the Perizzites at Bezek.
Culture
Their name is often connected with unwalled villages and open country, in contrast to the fortified cities of other Canaanite peoples. They lived alongside the Canaanites in the hill country rather than in one great city.
Religion
The text gives no distinct Perizzite religion; they are counted among the peoples whose idolatry Israel was told to reject.
Bible Connection
Perizzites stand in the background of the whole story, from Abram's arrival to the conquest: one of the peoples already in the land when God promised it, and one of the peoples Israel was told to displace.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 13:7; Genesis 15:20; Exodus 3:8; Deuteronomy 7:1; Joshua 17:15; Judges 1:4-5
Related People
Abraham; Joshua
Related Places
Canaan; Shechem; Bethel
