Canaan

Civilization
Canaan
Time Period
c. 2000-1000 BC
Region
Eastern Mediterranean coast and hill country, between Egypt and Mesopotamia
History
Canaan was a land of independent city-states rather than one empire, fought over by Egypt and Mesopotamia. God promised it to Abraham's descendants, and under Joshua Israel conquered and settled it, then struggled for generations to hold it against its peoples.
Culture
Canaanites farmed, traded, and built walled cities, and wrote in an early alphabet that influenced later scripts. Their pottery, idols, and fortifications appear throughout the archaeology of Israel.
Religion
Canaanite religion centered on El and Baal, with Asherah and ritual practices Scripture repeatedly condemns, including child sacrifice. Israel's recurring turn to Baal worship is one of the Old Testament's persistent warnings.
Bible Connection
Canaan is the Promised Land of the patriarchs, the land of the conquest under Joshua, and the setting of Judges. Its peoples and gods are the constant test of Israel's faithfulness to the covenant.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 12:1-7; Joshua 1-12; Judges 2:11-23
