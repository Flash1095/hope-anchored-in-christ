Scythians

Civilization
Scythians
Time Period
1st century AD
Region
Steppe north of the Black Sea
History
Scythians were the nomadic steppe peoples north of the Black Sea, proverbial outsiders in the Greek world. Paul names them once, as the far edge of the distinctions abolished in Christ.
Culture
Nomadic horsemen and famed archers, outside the Greek and Jewish worlds alike.
Religion
Steppe gods and customs, far from temple or synagogue.
Bible Connection
Named once, in Paul's list of distinctions — Greek and Jew, circumcised and uncircumcised, Barbarian, Scythian — that do not divide those renewed in Christ.
Key Scriptures
Colossians 3:11
