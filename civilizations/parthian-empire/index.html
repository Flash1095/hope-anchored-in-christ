Parthian Empire

Civilization
Parthian Empire
Time Period
c. 247 BC-AD 224
Region
Iran plateau to Mesopotamia
History
Parthian horsemen from the steppe east of the Caspian took Persia from the Seleucids and built an empire Rome never conquered — Crassus died at Carrhae in 53 BC, and Augustus settled for a frontier on the Euphrates. Parthia ruled Mesopotamia through the whole New Testament period until the Sassanians replaced it in AD 224.
Culture
Parthians were famed mounted archers — the Parthian shot, firing backwards at full gallop — and their kings ruled through vassal lords rather than a centralized bureaucracy.
Religion
Parthians tolerated many faiths across their empire. Zoroastrian tradition was strong in Iran, while Mesopotamian and Greek cults continued in the west.
Bible Connection
Parthians were among the peoples at Pentecost (Acts 2:9), hearing the gospel in their own tongue. Some readers see Parthia behind Revelation's kings from the east (Revelation 16:12), though the text does not name them.
Key Scriptures
Acts 2:9; Revelation 16:12
