Dan

Civilization
Dan
Time Period
c. 1400–732 BC
Region
Originally the central coastal plain; later the far north at Laish (Dan)
History
Dan was Jacob's fifth son, by Rachel's servant Bilhah. Jacob's blessing pictured Dan as a serpent by the roadside (Genesis 49:17). The tribe's first allotment on the coastal plain proved too small and too exposed, so part of the tribe migrated north and captured Laish, renaming it Dan (Judges 18) — the town that became proverbial as Israel's northern border, 'from Dan to Beersheba'. Samson, Israel's most famous judge, was a Danite. Under Jeroboam, Dan became one of the two sites of a golden calf (1 Kings 12:28-30). The tribe was deported with the northern kingdom by Assyria. Dan is the one tribe absent from the sealing list in Revelation 7.
Culture
Danite culture combined coastal farming with the frontier life of a border tribe; its northern city guarded the road into Israel from Syria, and its southern settlements lived among the Philistines.
Religion
Dan shared Israel's worship of the LORD, but its history is marked by two corruptions of it: Micah's shrine with its Levite priest (Judges 17–18) and Jeroboam's golden calf at Dan, which the prophets condemned.
Bible Connection
Dan illustrates both halves of the judges period: a deliverer in Samson and, in the same tribe's story, the spread of idolatry from Micah's household shrine to a royal sanctuary.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 30:6; Genesis 49:16-17; Judges 13:2; Judges 18:1-2; Judges 18:27-29; 1 Kings 12:28-30
Related People
Dan; Jacob; Samson; Delilah; Manoah, Father of Samson; Aholiab
