Arabia

Civilization
Arabia
Time Period
c. 1000 BC-AD 100
Region
The Arabian Peninsula and the Syrian desert fringe
History
Arabians were the nomadic herders and caravan traders of the desert between Mesopotamia and Egypt. Kings of Arabia brought tribute to Solomon (1 Kings 10:15), Arabians raided Judah in Jehoram's reign (2 Chronicles 21:16) and joined the opposition to Nehemiah's wall under Geshem the Arabian (Nehemiah 2:19; 4:7), and Arabians were among the peoples who heard the gospel at Pentecost (Acts 2:11). Paul went away into Arabia after his conversion (Galatians 1:17).
Culture
Arabians lived by herding and by the caravan trade in incense, spices, and goods moving between Yemen, Mesopotamia, and the Mediterranean, organized in tribes under sheikhs.
Religion
Arabians worshipped pre-Islamic deities of the desert, including Al-Lat and Al-Uzza, alongside the astral gods of the caravan cities.
Bible Connection
Isaiah's oracle, the burden upon Arabia (Isaiah 21:13), and Jeremiah's list of Arabia's kings (Jeremiah 25:24) set Arabia among the nations judged with Judah's neighbors, while Pentecost and Paul's journey make Arabia part of the gospel's early geography.
Key Scriptures
1 Kings 10:15; Isaiah 21:13; Jeremiah 25:24; Ezekiel 27:21; Nehemiah 2:19; Acts 2:11; Galatians 1:17
