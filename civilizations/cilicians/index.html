Cilicians

Civilization
Cilicians
Time Period
1st century AD
Region
Southeastern Asia Minor (Cilicia)
History
Cilicia was the coastal region of southeastern Asia Minor, home of Tarsus. Men from the synagogue of the Cilicians disputed with Stephen, and Paul — a Cilician by birth — travelled through Cilicia strengthening the churches.
Culture
Greek-Roman cities; Tarsus was a noted centre of learning, and Paul calls himself a citizen of no mean city.
Religion
Pagan cults alongside a Jewish community strong enough to have its own synagogue in Jerusalem.
Bible Connection
Cilicians appear as Stephen's disputants and as Paul's home people; the letter of the Jerusalem Council was addressed to the brethren in Cilicia as well as Syria.
Key Scriptures
Acts 6:9; Acts 15:23; Acts 21:39
Related People
Paul
