Emim

Civilization
Emim
Time Period
Before the conquest (Genesis 14; Deuteronomy 2)
Region
The plain later held by Moab, east of the Dead Sea
History
The Emim were an ancient people of the Moabite plain, remembered as giants. Chedorlaomer defeated them at Shaveh Kiriathaim (Genesis 14:5), and Deuteronomy 2:10-11 explains that the Emim 'were a people great, and many, and tall, as the Anakims; which also were accounted giants, as the Anakims.' The Moabites, descended from Lot, inherited the land the Emim had held.
Culture
Known only as a great and tall people of the plain, counted among the Rephaim in Deuteronomy's reckoning.
Religion
Not described in the text.
Bible Connection
The Emim belong to Deuteronomy's lesson that the land's history is a history of displacements: giants held Moab's plain before Moab, just as the Anakim held Canaan before Israel.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 14:5; Deuteronomy 2:10-11
Related People
Abraham; Moses
Related Places
Plains of Moab; Canaan
