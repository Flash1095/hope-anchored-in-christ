Moab

Civilization
Moab
Time Period
c. 1300-400 BC
Region
Highlands east of the Dead Sea
History
Moab, descended from Lot according to Genesis, was a small kingdom east of the Dead Sea that alternated between war and uneasy peace with Israel. King Mesha's revolt against Israel is recorded both in 2 Kings 3 and on his own victory stele.
Culture
Moabites farmed the highlands and controlled the King's Highway trade route. Their language was closely related to Hebrew, and their royal inscriptions are among the closest parallels to biblical Hebrew.
Religion
Moab worshipped Chemosh, to whom kings offered sacrifices, including, at least once, a king's own son in wartime desperation. Ruth the Moabitess, who left Moab to follow Naomi's God, became an ancestor of David.
Bible Connection
Balak of Moab hired Balaam to curse Israel; Ruth came from Moab; and the plains of Moab are where Moses died within sight of the Promised Land.
Key Scriptures
