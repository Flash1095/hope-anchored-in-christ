Hivites

Civilization
Hivites
Time Period
Bronze Age, c. 2000-1200 BC
Region
Central and northern Canaan: Shechem, Gibeon, and the region toward Lebanon and Mount Hermon
History
The Hivites are listed among the sons of Canaan in Genesis 10:17. Hamor the Hivite was the prince of Shechem in the Dinah affair (Genesis 34:2). The Gibeonites, who tricked Israel into a covenant in Joshua 9, are identified as Hivites in Joshua 9:7; their cities Gibeon, Chephirah, Beeroth, and Kirjath-jearim were spared and made woodcutters and water drawers for the congregation. They are named among the seven nations in Deuteronomy 7:1 and among the peoples left to test Israel in Judges 3.
Culture
A city-based people of central Canaan. The Gibeonite cities were organized enough to send a delegation and negotiate a treaty, and their servitude in Israel's service lasted for generations.
Religion
They shared the Canaanite religion. The Gibeonites' treaty brought them under Israel's protection, and their service was attached to the sanctuary.
Bible Connection
Hivites appear at two turning points: the violence at Shechem in Jacob's day, and the Gibeonite treaty that taught Israel the cost of a hasty oath in Joshua's day.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:17; Genesis 34:2; Joshua 9:7; Joshua 11:19; Deuteronomy 7:1
Related People
Joshua; Jacob
Related Places
Shechem; Gibeon
