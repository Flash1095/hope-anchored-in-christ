Aram

Civilization
Aram
Time Period
c. 1200-700 BC
Region
Syria, centered on Damascus
History
Aramean kingdoms, chiefly Damascus, rose after the Bronze Age collapse and contested the land east and north of Israel. Aram fought Israel under Ben-Hadad and Hazael, allied with it at times, and finally fell to Assyria in 732 BC.
Culture
Aramaic, the language of the Arameans, became the lingua franca of the Near East and the everyday language of Jesus' world. Aramean trade and diplomacy connected Mesopotamia with the Levant.
Religion
Arameans worshipped Hadad (Rimmon), the storm god, among others. Naaman, commander of Aram's army, was healed of leprosy after washing in the Jordan at Elisha's word.
Bible Connection
Aram is both enemy and neighbor in Kings: wars with Ahab and Jehoram, the siege of Samaria, and Naaman's healing, which showed God's mercy reaching beyond Israel.
Key Scriptures
1 Kings 20; 2 Kings 5; 2 Kings 6:8-23
Related People
Naaman; Elisha; Laban
Related Places
Haran
