Elam

Civilization
Elam
Time Period
c. 2700-539 BC
Region
Southwestern Iran, east of Mesopotamia, around Susa
History
One of the ancient world's oldest kingdoms, Elam lay east of Babylon with its chief city at Susa. Chedorlaomer, king of Elam, led the coalition Abraham defeated to rescue Lot (Genesis 14). Elam later warred with Assyria, was absorbed into the Persian Empire, and Susa became a Persian royal capital. Elamites were among the peoples present at Pentecost.
Culture
Elamites spoke their own language, unrelated to Semitic or Indo-European tongues, built ziggurats and palaces at Susa, and traded across Mesopotamia and the Iranian plateau.
Religion
Elamites worshipped their own gods, chief among them Inshushinak, god of Susa, alongside a wider Mesopotamian pantheon.
Bible Connection
Beyond Chedorlaomer, Elam appears in judgment oracles (Isaiah 21:2; Jeremiah 49:34-39, which also promises a later restoration), in Ezekiel's lament over fallen nations, and in Daniel's vision, which takes place at Shushan in the province of Elam (Daniel 8:2).
Key Scriptures
Genesis 14:1; Isaiah 21:2; Jeremiah 49:34; Ezekiel 32:24; Daniel 8:2; Acts 2:9
Related People
Chedorlaomer; Daniel
Related Places
Susa; Babylon
Related Events
Abram Rescues Lot; Pentecost
