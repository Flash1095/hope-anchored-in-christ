Edom

Civilization
Edom
Time Period
c. 1300-100 BC
Region
South of the Dead Sea, from the Arabah to the Red Sea
History
Edom, descended from Esau, occupied the rugged land south of the Dead Sea and controlled the copper mines of the Arabah and the Red Sea port of Ezion-geber. Edom refused Israel passage in the wilderness, warred with Judah's kings, and was later displaced by the Nabataeans; its people, the Idumeans, included Herod the Great's family.
Culture
Edomites built their capital at Bozrah and the mountain stronghold of Petra's region, living by mining, herding, and tolls on the trade routes between Arabia and the Mediterranean.
Religion
Edom worshipped Qos as its chief god. The prophet Obadiah pronounced judgment on Edom for standing by, and even joining in, when Jerusalem fell.
Bible Connection
Edom is Esau's land, the brother-nation that became Israel's rival: the birthright story, the refused passage, and Obadiah's oracle all turn on that brotherhood.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 25:29-34; Genesis 36; Numbers 20:14-21; Obadiah 1
