Alexandrians

Civilization
Alexandrians
Time Period
1st century AD
Region
Alexandria, Egypt
History
Alexandria was the great Hellenistic city of Egypt, founded by Alexander. Men of the synagogue of the Alexandrians disputed with Stephen, and Apollos, eloquent in the Scriptures, came from Alexandria to Ephesus.
Culture
A centre of learning and philosophy, and home of Greek-speaking Judaism — the Septuagint, the Greek Old Testament, was produced there.
Religion
A large Jewish community alongside pagan temples; Alexandrian Judaism shaped the Greek-speaking church.
Bible Connection
Alexandrians appear as Stephen's disputants and as the home community of Apollos, whose preaching Paul and Priscilla and Aquila corrected and encouraged.
Key Scriptures
Acts 6:9; Acts 18:24
