Egypt

Civilization
Egypt
Time Period
c. 3100-30 BC
Region
Nile Valley, northeast Africa
History
Unified under the pharaohs around 3100 BC, Egypt endured through the Old, Middle, and New Kingdoms, building an empire that stretched into Canaan and Nubia. It later fell to Assyria, Persia, Greece, and Rome, but its monuments and records outlasted every conqueror.
Culture
Egyptians wrote in hieroglyphs, built pyramids and temples on a monumental scale, and advanced medicine, astronomy, and administration. The annual Nile flood made farming predictable and supported one of the ancient world's most stable states.
Religion
Egypt worshipped many gods, chief among them Ra, Osiris, Isis, and Horus, and treated the pharaoh as a divine king. Belief in judgment after death shaped its tombs, mummification, and funerary texts.
Bible Connection
Abraham visited Egypt in famine; Joseph rose to vizier there; Israel spent centuries in Egypt before the Exodus, the defining deliverance of the Old Testament. Later kings allied with Egypt, refugees fled there after Jerusalem's fall, and Joseph and Mary took the infant Jesus to Egypt to escape Herod.
