Philistia

Civilization
Philistia
Time Period
c. 1200-600 BC
Region
Southern coastal plain of Canaan: Gaza, Ashkelon, Ashdod, Ekron, Gath
History
The Philistines were part of the Sea Peoples who settled the coast around 1200 BC, organized in a league of five cities. They fought Israel from the days of the judges through David, captured the ark briefly, and were eventually absorbed by the empires that followed.
Culture
Philistine pottery, iron-working, and Aegean roots set them apart from their Canaanite neighbors. Their cities were wealthy trading ports ruled by lords called serens.
Religion
The Philistines worshipped Dagon, Ashtoreth, and Baal-zebub. The ark's capture and Dagon's fall before it (1 Samuel 5) became a vivid sign of God's supremacy over their gods.
Bible Connection
Philistia is the great rival of Israel's early history: Samson's enemy, the home of Goliath, and the power David fought before and after becoming king. The word Palestine derives from Philistia.
Key Scriptures
Judges 13-16; 1 Samuel 5; 1 Samuel 17
Related People
Samson; Delilah
Related Events
Samson and Delilah; David and Goliath
