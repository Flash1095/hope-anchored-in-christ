Cappadocians

Civilization
Cappadocians
Time Period
1st century AD
Region
Eastern Asia Minor (Cappadocia)
History
Cappadocia was an inland region of Asia Minor, a Roman province by New Testament times. Cappadocians are named among the hearers at Pentecost, and Peter addresses his first letter to believers scattered through Cappadocia.
Culture
A rural, volcanic plateau; Greek-speaking under Roman rule, with Persian-era roots in its older history.
Religion
Pagan cults before conversion; by the time of 1 Peter there were Christian communities scattered through the region.
Bible Connection
Named among the peoples who heard the apostles at Pentecost, and named again among the recipients of 1 Peter.
Key Scriptures
Acts 2:9; 1 Peter 1:1
