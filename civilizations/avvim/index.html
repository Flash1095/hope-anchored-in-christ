Avvim

Civilization
Avvim
Time Period
Before the conquest (Deuteronomy 2; Joshua 13)
Region
The southwestern coastal plain around Gaza, in unwalled settlements (Hazerim)
History
The Avvim dwelt in Hazerim, unwalled villages, on the coastal plain toward Gaza, until the Caphtorim came out of Caphtor, destroyed them, and dwelt in their place (Deuteronomy 2:23). Joshua 13:3 still counts the Avvim among the land yet to be taken, listed beside the five lords of the Philistines, a sign that a remnant remained into the conquest era.
Culture
Villagers of the coastal plain, living in unwalled settlements rather than fortified cities, which left them exposed to the Caphtorim who displaced them.
Religion
Not described in the text.
Bible Connection
The Avvim are a small, displaced people of the coast: an example in Deuteronomy 2 of how peoples rose and fell in the land before Israel ever arrived.
Key Scriptures
Deuteronomy 2:23; Joshua 13:3
Related People
Moses; Joshua
Related Places
Canaan
