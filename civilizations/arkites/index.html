Arkites

Civilization
Arkites
Time Period
Ancient (Genesis 10 era)
Region
Canaan (later Arka, on the Phoenician coast, is the common identification)
History
The Arkites are named in the Table of Nations as descendants of Canaan (Genesis 10:17; 1 Chronicles 1:15), with no further biblical history. The later Phoenician coastal town of Arka in northern Lebanon is commonly identified with them, but the text itself does not make that connection.
Culture
Nothing is recorded of Arkite culture beyond their place in the Canaanite family list; the later Arka was a Phoenician coastal town.
Religion
Nothing distinct is recorded of Arkite religion.
Bible Connection
The Arkites appear only in the Table of Nations, one branch of Canaan's family spread abroad after Babel.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:17; 1 Chronicles 1:15
