Rephaim

Civilization
Rephaim
Time Period
Bronze Age, from Genesis 14 to David's reign
Region
Bashan (Og's kingdom), the Valley of Rephaim near Jerusalem, and Transjordan
History
The Rephaim were a people of great stature, remembered as giants. Chedorlaomer defeated them at Ashteroth Karnaim (Genesis 14:5), and they are named in the promise of Genesis 15:20. Deuteronomy 2 explains that the Emim and the Zamzummim were also accounted Rephaim, and that Og king of Bashan was 'of the remnant of the giants' (Deuteronomy 3:11), his iron bedstead kept at Rabbath as a curiosity. Joshua 12:4 and 13:12 record Og's kingdom as the last Rephaim territory, and David fought Philistines in the Valley of Rephaim (2 Samuel 5:18-22). Later Philistine giants (2 Samuel 21:16-22) are described as sons of Rapha, linking them to this giant tradition.
Culture
Remembered above all for their stature: 'a people great, and many, and tall' (Deuteronomy 2:10). They held kingdoms and valleys that Israel inherited piece by piece.
Religion
The text describes no Rephaim religion. In later biblical usage the same word shades into 'the shades' of the dead, but the people of Bashan and the valley are a nation, not a ghost story.
Bible Connection
The Rephaim are the giants of the conquest story: the fear behind the spies' report, the kingdom of Og that fell before Israel, and the valley where David fought for Jerusalem.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 14:5; Genesis 15:20; Deuteronomy 2:10-11; Deuteronomy 3:11; Joshua 12:4; 2 Samuel 5:18-22
Related People
Og; Abraham; Moses; King David; Joshua
Related Places
Canaan; Jerusalem
