Horites

Civilization
Horites
Time Period
Patriarchal era, before Esau's descendants took Seir
Region
Mount Seir (later Edom) and its mountains
History
The Horites (also called Horim) were the earlier inhabitants of Seir. Chedorlaomer defeated them in Mount Seir in Genesis 14:6, and Genesis 36:20-30 gives the genealogy of Seir the Horite and his sons: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan, the dukes of the Horites. Deuteronomy 2:12 and 2:22 record that the children of Esau destroyed the Horites and dwelt in their stead, just as Israel would later do in Canaan.
Culture
Their name is traditionally connected with cave-dwelling in Seir's mountains. They were organized under dukes, a clan structure that Esau's line inherited along with the land.
Religion
No Horite religion is described in the text.
Bible Connection
The Horites show the pattern Deuteronomy repeats: one people displacing another in the land, so that Edom's story mirrors Israel's own conquest.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 14:6; Genesis 36:20-30; Deuteronomy 2:12; Deuteronomy 2:22
Related People
Esau; Seir; Abraham
Related Places
Canaan
