Naphtali

Civilization
Naphtali
Time Period
c. 1400–732 BC
Region
Upper Galilee, west of the Jordan's sources
History
Naphtali was Jacob's sixth son, by Rachel's servant Bilhah. Jacob blessed him as a hind let loose (Genesis 49:21). His tribe settled the hills of Upper Galilee, around Kedesh. Barak, who defeated Sisera with Deborah, mustered his army from Naphtali (Judges 4:6), and Naphtali's men are praised for risking their lives in the battle (Judges 5:18). The tribe was among the first deported by Tiglath-Pileser of Assyria (2 Kings 15:29). Isaiah's promise that a light would dawn on 'the land of Naphtali' (Isaiah 9:1-2) is applied to Jesus' ministry in Galilee in Matthew 4.
Culture
Naphtali's culture was that of the Galilean hills: farming, herding, and the fortified towns of the north, on the frontier with Syria and Phoenicia.
Religion
Naphtali shared Israel's worship of the LORD, with its religious life centred on the sanctuary and later the temple, and its towns included Kedesh, a city of refuge.
Bible Connection
Naphtali links Deborah's victory to Jesus' ministry: the same northern hills where Barak fought are where Isaiah promised light and where Jesus began preaching.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 30:8; Genesis 49:21; Deuteronomy 33:23; Judges 4:6; Judges 5:18; 2 Kings 15:29; Isaiah 9:1-2; Matthew 4:13-16
