Ishmaelites

Civilization
Ishmaelites
Time Period
Patriarchal era onward, c. 2000 BC and after
Region
The desert east of Egypt, from Havilah to Shur, and later the northern Arabian desert
History
The Ishmaelites descended from Ishmael, son of Abraham and Hagar (Genesis 16; 21). Genesis 25:12-18 lists his twelve sons, princes of their tribes, dwelling from Havilah to Shur, east of Egypt. An Ishmaelite caravan carrying spices to Egypt bought Joseph from his brothers (Genesis 37:25-28; the same passage also calls the traders Midianites, and the two names are used together there). Gideon asked for the gold earrings of the Ishmaelite traders after defeating Midian (Judges 8:24), and Psalm 83:6 counts the Ishmaelites among the confederates against Israel.
Culture
Nomadic desert traders, living in tents and moving goods between Arabia and Egypt. Ishmael himself 'grew, and dwelt in the wilderness, and became an archer' (Genesis 21:20).
Religion
The text ties Ishmael's line to Abraham's God through Hagar's encounter at the well (Genesis 16) and God's promise to make Ishmael a great nation (Genesis 17:20), but it describes no distinct Ishmaelite religion.
Bible Connection
Ishmaelites carry Joseph into Egypt, setting the exodus story in motion, and stand as the desert kin of Israel: Abraham's other son's line, blessed yet outside the covenant promise.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 16:11-12; Genesis 21:20-21; Genesis 25:12-18; Genesis 37:25-28; Judges 8:24; Psalm 83:6
Related People
Abraham; Hagar; Ishmael; Isaac; Jacob; Joseph, Son of Jacob
Related Places
Egypt; Canaan; Beersheba
Related Events
Joseph and His Brothers; Hagar and Ishmael
