Zebulun

Civilization
Zebulun
Time Period
c. 1400–732 BC
Region
Lower Galilee, between the Jezreel Valley and the coast
History
Zebulun was Jacob's tenth son, by Leah. Jacob blessed him as a haven for ships (Genesis 49:13), though his allotment in Lower Galilee never quite reached the sea. Zebulun's men fought with Deborah and Barak and are praised for risking their lives on the heights (Judges 5:18); the tribe also supplied Elon, one of the minor judges (Judges 12:11-12). Isaiah's promise of light to Zebulun and Naphtali (Isaiah 9:1) is applied to Jesus' Galilean ministry in Matthew 4. Zebulun was deported with the northern kingdom.
Culture
Zebulun's culture was that of the Galilean farming towns, trading toward the coast and the great north-south road, with a reputation for loyalty in battle disproportionate to its size.
Religion
Zebulun shared Israel's worship of the LORD, with its religious life centred on the sanctuary and later the temple.
Bible Connection
Zebulun links the judges' battlefield to the Gospels: the tribe praised for risking its life at the Kishon is named in the same Isaiah promise that Matthew applies to Jesus in Galilee.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 30:20; Genesis 49:13; Deuteronomy 33:18-19; Joshua 19:10-16; Judges 5:14; Isaiah 9:1
