Hamathites

Civilization
Hamathites
Time Period
Ancient (Genesis 10 era) onward
Region
Hamath on the Orontes, in Syria
History
The Hamathites, descendants of Canaan (Genesis 10:18; 1 Chronicles 1:16), were the people of Hamath, a Syrian kingdom on the Orontes. Hamath's people were among those Assyria resettled in Samaria (2 Kings 17:24), and the kings of Hamath were named by Assyria's king when he mocked the gods of the nations (2 Kings 18:34; 19:13).
Culture
Hamath was an Aramean city-state of farmers and traders on the Orontes, a stopping point between Mesopotamia and the Mediterranean.
Religion
The men of Hamath made Ashima their god (2 Kings 17:30), and carried that worship with them to Samaria.
Bible Connection
Hamath's story in the Bible runs from the Table of Nations to the Assyrian resettlement: a Canaanite people who became a Syrian kingdom and then exiles in Israel's land.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:18; 1 Chronicles 1:16; 2 Kings 17:24; 2 Kings 17:30; 2 Kings 18:34
