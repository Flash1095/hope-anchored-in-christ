Reuben

Civilization
Reuben
Time Period
c. 1400–732 BC
Region
East of the Jordan, from the Arnon to Heshbon
History
Reuben was Jacob's firstborn, by Leah, but he lost the birthright after sleeping with his father's concubine Bilhah (Genesis 35:22; 49:3-4). At the settlement the tribe asked Moses for the pasture land east of the Jordan, on condition its fighting men cross over to help conquer Canaan (Numbers 32). Reubenites Dathan and Abiram joined Korah's rebellion against Moses (Numbers 16). The tribe was among the first carried away when Assyria overran the east-Jordan territory under Tiglath-Pileser (1 Chronicles 5:26).
Culture
Reuben shared the pastoral, tribal culture of Israel east of the Jordan, raising flocks on the Transjordan plateau. Its towns included Heshbon, a former Amorite capital, and its territory bordered Moab.
Religion
Reuben shared Israel's worship of the LORD, with its religious life centred on the tabernacle and later the temple at Jerusalem, despite living across the Jordan from them.
Bible Connection
Reuben's story is a warning about the firstborn's lost privilege: Jacob's blessing says Reuben was 'unstable as water' and would not excel. The tribe's request for land east of the Jordan set the pattern for the two and a half Transjordan tribes.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 29:32; Genesis 35:22; Genesis 49:3-4; Numbers 32:1-5; Joshua 13:15-23; Numbers 16:1
