Issachar

Civilization
Issachar
Time Period
c. 1400–732 BC
Region
The Jezreel Valley, south of Naphtali
History
Issachar was Jacob's ninth son, by Leah. Jacob blessed him as a strong donkey crouching between sheepfolds, a tribe that would bear burdens (Genesis 49:14-15). His allotment lay in the fertile Jezreel Valley, including Jezreel and Shunem. Issachar's princes fought with Deborah and Barak (Judges 5:15), and the tribe's later fame was for wisdom: the men of Issachar 'had understanding of the times, to know what Israel ought to do' (1 Chronicles 12:32). Issachar was deported with the northern kingdom.
Culture
Issachar's culture was agricultural: the rich valley soil made it one of Israel's breadbaskets, and its strength lay in farming and in counsel rather than in kings or judges.
Religion
Issachar shared Israel's worship of the LORD, with its religious life centred on the sanctuary and later the temple at Jerusalem.
Bible Connection
Issachar is remembered for discernment rather than power: a tribe that knew its times and counselled the nation, in contrast to the strong-man history of its neighbours.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 30:18; Genesis 49:14-15; Deuteronomy 33:18-19; Joshua 19:17-23; 1 Chronicles 12:32
Related People
Issachar; Jacob; Moses; Joshua; Tola, Son of Puah; Baasha, King of Israel
