Assyria

Civilization
Assyria
Time Period
c. 900-612 BC
Region
Northern Mesopotamia, capital Nineveh
History
Assyria rose from a city-state on the Tigris to the Near East's first true empire, conquering the northern kingdom of Israel in 722 BC and deporting its people. Its siege of Jerusalem in 701 BC failed, and Nineveh itself fell to Babylon and Media in 612 BC.
Culture
Assyrians built vast palaces and libraries — Ashurbanipal's library at Nineveh preserved the Epic of Gilgamesh — and administered their empire with roads, officials, and mass deportations.
Religion
Assyria's chief god was Assur, with Ishtar of Nineveh and other gods of war and fertility. Its kings presented conquest as divine mandate, and their annals record cruelty as policy.
Bible Connection
Assyria is the rod of God's judgment in Isaiah and the repentant city of Jonah's preaching. Its fall, celebrated by Nahum, showed that even the mightiest empire answers to God.
