Syrophoenicians

Civilization
Syrophoenicians
Time Period
1st century AD
Region
Phoenician coast under Syria
History
Syrophoenicia was the Phoenician coast as part of the Roman province of Syria. A Syrophoenician woman sought Jesus for her demon-possessed daughter, and he praised her faith; Mark names her people explicitly, 'a Greek, a Syrophenician by nation.'
Culture
Phoenician cities, Greek-speaking under Roman Syria.
Religion
Pagan background; the woman's persistence and faith are the story's point.
Bible Connection
Mark is the only Gospel to name this people, and he does so precisely to mark the woman as an outsider to Israel whose faith Jesus honoured.
Key Scriptures
Mark 7:26
Related Places
Tyre
