Put

Civilization
Put
Time Period
c. 1000-500 BC
Region
North Africa, west of Egypt (the Libya region)
History
Put, son of Ham (Genesis 10:6), is a North African people closely linked with the Libyans. The King James Version spells the name Phut in Genesis and Ezekiel and Put in Nahum. Put's warriors served in Tyre's army (Ezekiel 27:10), stood with Egypt's allies (Ezekiel 30:5; Nahum 3:9), and are named among Gog's coalition (Ezekiel 38:5). Beyond this warrior role, little is recorded of them.
Culture
Like their Libyan neighbors, the men of Put were known as soldiers and mercenaries in the armies of Tyre, Egypt, and the great powers.
Religion
Nothing distinct is recorded of Put's religion; as a North African people they shared the religious world of Libya and Egypt.
Bible Connection
Put appears only as a military ally in the prophets — with Tyre, with Egypt, and with Gog — a small people named wherever the great powers mustered their armies.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:6; Ezekiel 27:10; Ezekiel 30:5; Ezekiel 38:5; Nahum 3:9
Related Places
Tyre; Egypt
