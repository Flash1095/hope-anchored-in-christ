Greece

Civilization
Greece
Time Period
c. 800-146 BC
Region
Greek mainland, Aegean islands, and Alexander's eastern empire
History
Greek city-states gave the West philosophy, drama, and democracy, and Alexander's conquests spread Greek language and culture across the East. After Alexander, his successors ruled the world of the Maccabees, and Greek became the language in which the New Testament was written.
Culture
Greece prized reason, rhetoric, athletics, and art, and its language became the common tongue of the eastern Mediterranean. The Septuagint, the Greek Old Testament, carried Scripture to the wider world.
Religion
Greeks worshipped Zeus, Athena, Apollo, and a host of gods on Olympus and in every city. Paul found Athens full of idols and an altar 'to an unknown god,' which became his opening to preach Christ.
Bible Connection
Daniel foresaw Greece as the goat that shatters Persia's ram; the New Testament was written in Greek; and Paul's missionary journeys carried the gospel through the Greek cities of Corinth, Athens, Philippi, and Ephesus.
Key Scriptures
Daniel 8; Acts 16-18; Acts 17
Related People
Paul; Timothy
Related Places
Corinth; Philippi; Ephesus
Related Events
Paul's Missionary Journeys; The Conversion of Paul
Related Finds
