Girgashites

Civilization
Girgashites
Time Period
Bronze Age, before and during the conquest
Region
Canaan (their exact territory is not stated in the text)
History
The Girgashites are listed among the sons of Canaan in Genesis 10:16, named in the land promise of Genesis 15:21, counted among the seven nations in Deuteronomy 7:1, and named among the peoples to be driven out before Israel in Joshua 3:10 and Joshua 24:11. Beyond these conquest lists, the Bible records nothing more about them: no king, city, or territory is named for them.
Culture
Unknown beyond their place among the Canaanite peoples of the conquest lists.
Religion
Unknown beyond their place among the peoples whose idolatry Israel was told to reject.
Bible Connection
The Girgashites are a reminder of how little the record preserves about some peoples: they are named in every great list of the land's nations, yet leave no story of their own.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:16; Genesis 15:21; Deuteronomy 7:1; Joshua 3:10; Joshua 24:11
Related People
Joshua
Related Places
Canaan; Jordan River
Related Events
Crossing the Jordan
