Zamzummim

Civilization
Zamzummim
Time Period
Before the conquest (Genesis 14; Deuteronomy 2)
Region
The land later held by Ammon, east of the Jordan
History
The Zamzummim were an ancient people of the Ammonite country, remembered as giants. Deuteronomy 2:20-21 says their land 'also was accounted a land of giants: giants dwelt therein in old time; and the Ammonites call them Zamzummim; a people great, and many, and tall, as the Anakims; but the LORD destroyed them before the Ammonites, and they succeeded them.' The Zuzim defeated at Ham in Genesis 14:5 are commonly identified with the Zamzummim, the same people under an older name.
Culture
Known only as a great and tall people, counted among the Rephaim, whose land passed to Ammon.
Religion
Not described in the text.
Bible Connection
Like the Emim, the Zamzummim teach Deuteronomy's point: God gave Ammon its land by displacing giants, just as He gave Israel Canaan.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 14:5; Deuteronomy 2:20-21
Related People
Abraham; Moses
Related Places
Canaan
