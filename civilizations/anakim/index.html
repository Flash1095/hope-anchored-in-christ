Anakim

Civilization
Anakim
Time Period
Wilderness and conquest era, c. 1400-1200 BC
Region
The hill country of Judah (Hebron, Debir, Anab) and, after the conquest, Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod
History
The Anakim descended from Anak (Numbers 13:33; Deuteronomy 9:2). The spies reported them as giants: 'we were in our own sight as grasshoppers, and so we were in their sight' (Numbers 13:33). Joshua cut them off from the hill country, leaving a remnant only in Gaza, Gath, and Ashdod (Joshua 11:21-22). Caleb drove out Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai, the children of Anak, from Hebron (Joshua 15:13-14), and Judges 1:20 records Caleb's taking of Hebron from the three sons of Anak.
Culture
A tall, formidable hill people whose walled towns and stature terrified the spies. Their cities in the hill country were the strongholds Caleb claimed by faith.
Religion
No distinct Anakim religion is described; they belong to the Canaanite world whose idolatry Israel was told to reject.
Bible Connection
The Anakim are the giants of the spies' report and of Caleb's faith: the very people Israel feared became the inheritance of the one spy who trusted God.
Key Scriptures
Numbers 13:28; Numbers 13:33; Deuteronomy 9:2; Joshua 11:21-22; Joshua 15:13-14; Judges 1:20
Related People
Caleb; Joshua; Moses; Anak
Related Places
