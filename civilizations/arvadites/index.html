Arvadites

Civilization
Arvadites
Time Period
Ancient (Genesis 10 era) onward
Region
The island city of Arvad, off the Phoenician coast
History
The Arvadites, descendants of Canaan (Genesis 10:18; 1 Chronicles 1:16), were the people of Arvad, an island city off the Phoenician coast. Ezekiel pictures Arvad's men as Tyre's mariners and as soldiers on Tyre's walls (Ezekiel 27:8, 11) — seafarers in the service of the great trading city.
Culture
Arvadites were seafarers and shipbuilders, manning Tyre's fleets and garrisoning its walls.
Religion
As a Phoenician city, Arvad shared the religion of the Phoenician coast; nothing distinct is recorded of Arvadite worship.
Bible Connection
Arvad's biblical footprint is maritime: the island people whose sailors and soldiers powered Tyre's trade and defense.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:18; 1 Chronicles 1:16; Ezekiel 27:8; Ezekiel 27:11
Related Places
Tyre
