Cypriots

Civilization
Cypriots
Time Period
1st century AD
Region
Island of Cyprus
History
Cyprus was a Mediterranean island under Roman rule. Barnabas was a Cypriot by birth; men of Cyprus were among the first to preach to Greeks at Antioch; and Paul's first missionary journey began on Cyprus.
Culture
Greek-speaking islanders; Cyprus was famed for copper, from which it takes its name.
Religion
Pagan temples alongside a Jewish community; early Christian preaching reached the island on the first journey.
Bible Connection
Barnabas's homeland; Cyprus was evangelised at the start of the first missionary journey, and Cypriot believers helped found the church at Antioch.
Key Scriptures
Acts 4:36; Acts 11:20; Acts 21:16
Related People
Barnabas
Related Events
Paul's Missionary Journeys
