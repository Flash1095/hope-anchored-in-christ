Samaritans

Civilization
Samaritans
Time Period
c. 722 BC – 1st century AD
Region
Samaria, between Judea and Galilee
History
After Assyria conquered the northern kingdom, the population of Samaria mixed Israelite remnants with settlers from elsewhere in the empire. By New Testament times the Samaritans were a distinct people with their own temple on Mount Gerizim and a long, bitter estrangement from the Jews.
Culture
Samaritans kept the Torah (in their own Samaritan Pentateuch), observed the Sabbath and the feasts, and worshipped on Mount Gerizim rather than in Jerusalem.
Religion
Samaritan religion centred on the God of Israel, the Torah, Mount Gerizim as the chosen place of worship, and the expectation of a prophet like Moses (John 4:25).
Bible Connection
Jesus made a Samaritan the hero of his parable of neighbour-love, spoke at length with a Samaritan woman at Jacob's well, praised a Samaritan leper's thanks, and sent Philip to preach in Samaria, where Samaritans believed and received the Holy Spirit.
Key Scriptures
Matthew 10:5; Luke 10:33; Luke 17:16; John 4:9; Acts 8:25; 2 Kings 17:29; John 4:39
Related People
Philip; Simon Peter
Related Places
