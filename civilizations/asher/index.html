Asher

Civilization
Asher
Time Period
c. 1400–732 BC
Region
Northern Mediterranean coast, from Carmel toward Tyre and Sidon
History
Asher was Jacob's eighth son, by Leah's servant Zilpah. His name means happy, and Jacob blessed him with rich food and royal delicacies (Genesis 49:20). Asher's allotment ran along the northern coast, but the tribe never drove out the Canaanites of its towns (Judges 1:31-32) and lived among them. Asher is the quietest tribe in the history: it appears in the census lists and in the blessing of Moses (Deuteronomy 33:24-25), and Anna the prophetess, who recognised the infant Jesus in the temple, was of the tribe of Asher (Luke 2:36). The tribe was deported with the northern kingdom.
Culture
Asher's culture was coastal: farming the fertile plain, trading with the Phoenician cities to its north, and living alongside Canaanite neighbours its armies never displaced.
Religion
Asher shared Israel's worship of the LORD, though living among Canaanite towns exposed it to the idolatry the conquest was meant to remove.
Bible Connection
Asher's story is one of quiet persistence: a tribe that never produced a judge or a king, yet is named among the sealed in Revelation 7 and gives the New Testament its aged prophetess Anna.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 30:13; Genesis 49:20; Deuteronomy 33:24-25; Joshua 19:24-31; Judges 1:31-32
Related People
Asher; Jacob; Moses; Joshua
