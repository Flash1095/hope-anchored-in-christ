Rome

Civilization
Rome
Time Period
c. 753 BC-AD 476
Region
Italian peninsula to an empire around the Mediterranean
History
Rome grew from a city-state to a republic and then an empire that ruled the whole Mediterranean world, including Judea from 63 BC. Its roads, laws, and peace — the Pax Romana — form the backdrop of the entire New Testament.
Culture
Rome built roads, aqueducts, and cities on a scale never seen before, spread Latin law and citizenship, and absorbed Greek art and learning. Its engineering and administration held a vast empire together for centuries.
Religion
Romans worshipped Jupiter, Mars, and the emperor himself, whose cult demanded loyalty from every subject. Christians' refusal to call Caesar lord was one reason for their persecution.
Bible Connection
Jesus was born under Caesar Augustus's census, crucified under Pontius Pilate, and Paul appealed to Caesar and was imprisoned in Rome itself. The empire that killed the apostles also carried their message to the ends of the earth.
