Kenizzites

Civilization
Kenizzites
Time Period
Patriarchal era through the conquest
Region
Southern Canaan and the Negev, associated with Caleb's family
History
The Kenizzites are named in the land promise of Genesis 15:19 and nowhere else as a nation. Their fame comes through one man: Caleb, son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite (Numbers 32:12; Joshua 14:6), who followed the LORD fully and received Hebron as his inheritance. Though called a Kenizzite, Caleb was counted with the tribe of Judah. Kenaz, named in 1 Chronicles 4:13-15, belongs to the same family line, and Othniel son of Kenaz took Debir and became a judge.
Culture
Little is recorded beyond their place in the Genesis 15 list and Caleb's line. They appear as a southern clan absorbed into Judah rather than as a separate kingdom.
Religion
No distinct Kenizzite religion is described. Caleb's faithfulness is the family's mark in the text.
Bible Connection
Through Caleb the Kenizzite, this obscure people of the promise list produced one of Israel's great heroes of faith, the spy who believed God could give the land.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 15:19; Numbers 32:12; Joshua 14:6; Joshua 14:14; 1 Chronicles 4:13-15
Related People
Caleb; Kenaz; Joshua
Related Places
Canaan; Hebron
