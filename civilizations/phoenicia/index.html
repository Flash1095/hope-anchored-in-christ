Phoenicia

Civilization
Phoenicia
Time Period
c. 1500-300 BC
Region
Northern Levantine coast: Tyre, Sidon, Byblos
History
Phoenician city-states dominated Mediterranean trade, founding colonies as far as Carthage. They were never a single empire, but their ships, timber, and purple dye made them indispensable partners of Israel's kings.
Culture
Phoenicians are credited with spreading the alphabet that became the ancestor of Greek and Latin scripts. Their cedar timber built temples and palaces across the region.
Religion
Phoenician religion honored Baal, Astarte, and Eshmun, with rites Scripture condemns. Jezebel, daughter of Sidon's king, brought Baal worship to Israel's court with devastating effect.
Bible Connection
Hiram of Tyre supplied cedar and craftsmen for Solomon's temple; Jezebel's marriage to Ahab imported Baal worship; Jesus visited the region of Tyre and Sidon, where a Syrophoenician woman's faith drew his praise.
Key Scriptures
1 Kings 5; 1 Kings 16:31-33; Matthew 15:21-28
