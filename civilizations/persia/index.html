Persia

Civilization
Persia
Time Period
c. 550-330 BC
Region
Iran plateau to the Mediterranean, capital Susa and Persepolis
History
Cyrus the Great founded the Persian Empire, the largest the ancient world had yet seen, stretching from India to Greece. His edict let the Jewish exiles return and rebuild the temple; later kings Darius, Xerxes, and Artaxerxes framed the world of Ezra, Nehemiah, and Esther.
Culture
Persia administered its empire through satrapies, royal roads, and a policy of tolerance toward local customs and gods. Its postal system and coinage knit together peoples from three continents.
Religion
Persian kings promoted Ahura Mazda, the wise lord of Zoroastrian teaching, while tolerating the religions of their subjects. The empire's tolerance is what made the return from exile possible.
Bible Connection
Persia is the setting of the return: Cyrus's decree, the temple's rebuilding, Esther in Susa, and Nehemiah's wall. Isaiah calls Cyrus God's anointed, the pagan king who serves God's purpose.
