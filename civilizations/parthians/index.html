Parthians

Civilization
Parthians
Time Period
c. 247 BC – AD 224 (Parthian Empire)
Region
Persia and Mesopotamia east of Rome
History
Parthia was the Iranian empire east of Rome, its great eastern rival. Parthians are named among the hearers at Pentecost; the New Testament says nothing further about them.
Culture
A horse-archer aristocracy ruling a Greek-influenced administration across Persia and Mesopotamia.
Religion
Zoroastrian tradition alongside other cults of the empire.
Bible Connection
Named only in the Acts 2 list of peoples who heard the apostles at Pentecost.
Key Scriptures
Acts 2:9
