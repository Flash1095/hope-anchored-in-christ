Nephilim

Civilization
Nephilim
Time Period
Before the Flood, and again in the wilderness generation (as reported)
Region
No territory is given for them
History
The Nephilim are named twice. Genesis 6:4 says, 'There were giants in the earth in those days; and also after that, when the sons of God came in unto the daughters of men, and they bare children to them, the same became mighty men which were of old, men of renown.' Numbers 13:33 reports the spies' words: 'we saw the giants, the sons of Anak, which come of the giants: and we were in our own sight as grasshoppers.' Whether the Nephilim of Genesis 6 are the same as the Anakim of Numbers 13 is debated; this entry records both passages without merging them into a settled identification.
Culture
The text gives no culture for them beyond great stature and renown, set against the wickedness of the age before the Flood.
Religion
None is described. Their story belongs to the corruption that preceded the Flood, not to any nation's worship.
Bible Connection
The Nephilim frame the Bible's two great judgments on giants: the wickedness before the Flood, and the fear that nearly turned Israel back at the edge of the promised land.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 6:4; Numbers 13:33
Related People
Moses; Anak; Noah
Related Places
Canaan
Related Events
