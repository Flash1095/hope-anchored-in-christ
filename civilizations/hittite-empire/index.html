Hittite Empire

Civilization
Hittite Empire
Time Period
c. 1600-1200 BC
Region
Anatolia (modern Turkey), with influence into Syria and Canaan
History
The Hittites built one of the Bronze Age's great empires from their capital Hattusa, fighting Egypt to a standstill at Kadesh and concluding the earliest surviving peace treaty. After the empire's fall around 1200 BC, smaller Neo-Hittite states survived in Syria for centuries.
Culture
Hittites pioneered iron-working, codified laws, and drew up detailed treaties and covenants. Their treaty forms — preamble, history, stipulations, blessings and curses — closely parallel the structure of the biblical covenants.
Religion
Hittite religion embraced a thousand gods, led by the storm god Tarhunt and the sun goddess Arinna. Their rituals and legal texts show a world of oaths, purity rules, and treaty obligations.
Bible Connection
Hittites appear in the patriarchal stories: Ephron the Hittite sold Abraham the cave of Machpelah, and Uriah the Hittite was the husband of Bathsheba. Hittite treaty patterns help explain the shape of God's covenants with Israel.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 23; 2 Samuel 11; 1 Kings 10:29
