Maacah

Maacah was a small kingdom northeast of Bashan. Like Geshur, it was not driven out in the conquest (Deuteronomy 3:14; Joshua 13:13), and Joshua 12:5 marks it on the border of Og's kingdom. In David's wars the king of Maacah sent a thousand men to fight with the Ammonites against Israel (2 Samuel 10:6, 8). Eliphelet, son of Ahasbai the Maachathite, served among David's mighty men (2 Samuel 23:34), and Jaazaniah the Maachathite was among the captains who came to Gedaliah after Jerusalem's fall (Jeremiah 40:8).

Culture

A small border kingdom, able to field troops for a coalition war yet small enough that its men served individually in David's and Gedaliah's companies.
