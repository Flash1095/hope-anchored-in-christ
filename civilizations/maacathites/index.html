Maacathites

Civilization
Maacathites
Time Period
Conquest era through the divided monarchy
Region
Maacah, northeast of Bashan, bordering Geshur
History
Maacah was a small kingdom northeast of Bashan. Like Geshur, it was not driven out in the conquest (Deuteronomy 3:14; Joshua 13:13), and Joshua 12:5 marks it on the border of Og's kingdom. In David's wars the king of Maacah sent a thousand men to fight with the Ammonites against Israel (2 Samuel 10:6, 8). Eliphelet, son of Ahasbai the Maachathite, served among David's mighty men (2 Samuel 23:34), and Jaazaniah the Maachathite was among the captains who came to Gedaliah after Jerusalem's fall (Jeremiah 40:8).
Culture
A small border people, able to field troops for a coalition war yet small enough that its men served individually in David's and Gedaliah's companies.
Religion
No distinct Maacathite religion is described.
Bible Connection
Maacah appears at both ends of the monarchy: fighting David with Ammon, then standing with Gedaliah's remnant after the exile began.
Key Scriptures
Deuteronomy 3:14; Joshua 12:5; Joshua 13:13; 2 Samuel 10:6; 2 Samuel 23:34; Jeremiah 40:8
Related People
King David; Og; Joshua
Related Places
Canaan
