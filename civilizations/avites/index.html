Avites

Civilization
Avites
Time Period
c. 700 BC (Assyrian resettlement)
Region
Ava, in Mesopotamia
History
The Avites were one of the Mesopotamian peoples Assyria resettled in Samaria after Israel's fall (2 Kings 17:24). They made Nibhaz and Tartak their gods (2 Kings 17:31). They are not the Avvim of Deuteronomy 2:23, a different people near Gaza displaced by the Caphtorim — the similar names cover two different groups.
Culture
As a Mesopotamian people, the Avites shared the urban culture of Babylon's world; nothing further is recorded of them.
Religion
The Avites worshipped Nibhaz and Tartak, gods they carried with them to Samaria.
Bible Connection
The Avites appear only in the resettlement list and its note on their gods — one more strand in the mixed population that became Samaria.
Key Scriptures
2 Kings 17:24; 2 Kings 17:31
Related Places
Samaria
Related Events
Fall of Samaria
