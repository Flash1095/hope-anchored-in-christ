Phrygians

Civilization
Phrygians
Time Period
1st century AD
Region
West-central Asia Minor (Phrygia)
History
Phrygia had been an ancient kingdom famed in legend for King Midas; by New Testament times it was part of the Roman province of Asia. Phrygians are named among the hearers at Pentecost, and Paul crossed Phrygia on his journeys.
Culture
Phrygian speech and the old mother-goddess cults were giving way to Greek language and Roman ways.
Religion
The mother-goddess cult before conversion; Christian communities grew along Paul's routes.
Bible Connection
Named among the Pentecost hearers; Paul passed through Phrygia and Galatia on his second journey and through Galatia and Phrygia on his third.
Key Scriptures
Acts 2:10; Acts 16:6; Acts 18:23
Related Events
Pentecost
