Libya

Civilization
Libya
Time Period
c. 1000-500 BC
Region
North Africa, west of Egypt
History
The Lubim, or Libyans, appear as warriors of the North African coast west of Egypt. They marched with Shishak against Rehoboam (2 Chronicles 12:3) and are named among Egypt's allies (2 Chronicles 16:8; Nahum 3:9). Ezekiel lists Libya among the peoples falling with Egypt (Ezekiel 30:5) and among Gog's allies (Ezekiel 38:5), and Libyans were present at Pentecost (Acts 2:10). The Lehabim of Genesis 10:13 are traditionally identified with these Libyans.
Culture
Libyans were semi-nomadic herders and warriors of the coastal tribes, serving as soldiers and charioteers in Egyptian and other Near Eastern armies.
Religion
Libyans shared much of Egypt's religious world, worshipping Libyan and Egyptian deities of the desert and the Nile valley.
Bible Connection
Libya's biblical role is chiefly as Egypt's western neighbor and ally, named in judgment oracles and, at Pentecost, among the nations hearing the gospel in their own tongue.
Key Scriptures
