Caphtorim

Civilization
Caphtorim
Time Period
Bronze Age (Genesis 10; Deuteronomy 2)
Region
Caphtor (commonly identified with Crete) and, after migration, the Gaza coast
History
The Caphtorim descended from Mizraim in the table of nations (Genesis 10:14). Deuteronomy 2:23 records their migration: 'the Caphtorims, which came forth out of Caphtor, destroyed the Avvims, which dwelt in Hazerim, even unto Azzah, and dwelt in their stead.' Jeremiah 47:4 calls the Philistines 'the remnant of the country of Caphtor,' and Amos 9:7 says God brought the Philistines from Caphtor as He brought Israel from Egypt. The Caphtorim are thus closely linked to Philistine origins, but they are distinct from the Philistines themselves in the Genesis 10 list, where the Philistim come from the Casluhim. Caphtor is usually identified with Crete.
Culture
A seafaring people of Caphtor who migrated to the Gaza coast and displaced its villagers, bringing Aegean connections to the coastal plain.
Religion
No distinct Caphtorim religion is described; their Philistine kin worshipped Dagon and other coastal gods.
Bible Connection
The Caphtorim explain how the Gaza coast changed hands before Israel arrived, and Amos uses their migration as a mirror of Israel's own exodus.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:14; Deuteronomy 2:23; Jeremiah 47:4; Amos 9:7; 1 Chronicles 1:12
Related People
Moses; Joshua
Related Places
Canaan; Egypt
