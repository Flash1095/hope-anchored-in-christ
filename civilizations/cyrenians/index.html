Cyrenians

Civilization
Cyrenians
Time Period
1st century AD
Region
Cyrenaica, North Africa (Cyrene)
History
Cyrene was a Greek city in North Africa with a large Jewish community. Cyrenians are named among the hearers at Pentecost; Simon of Cyrene was compelled to carry Jesus' cross; and men of Cyrene preached to Greeks at Antioch.
Culture
A Greek city with a strong Jewish community, closely tied to Alexandria and to Jerusalem.
Religion
A Jewish synagogue community; Cyrenian believers were among the founders of the Gentile church at Antioch.
Bible Connection
Simon of Cyrene's part in the crucifixion and the Cyrenians' preaching at Antioch are their main New Testament appearances.
Key Scriptures
Matthew 27:32; Mark 15:21; Acts 2:10; Acts 11:20
Related Events
The Crucifixion
