Lycaonians

Civilization
Lycaonians
Time Period
1st century AD
Region
Interior Asia Minor (Lycaonia)
History
Lycaonia was an inland district of Asia Minor. Paul preached at Lystra and Derbe in Lycaonia, where the crowds, hearing him in their own language, cried out that the gods had come down in human form and tried to sacrifice to him and Barnabas.
Culture
Lycaonian speech survived alongside Greek in a rural, pastoral district.
Religion
Local Zeus and Hermes cults — the crowd took Barnabas for Zeus and Paul for Hermes — before the gospel reached them.
Bible Connection
The Lycaonians' reaction at Lystra, and Paul's stoning there and return, are the Lycaonians' main New Testament appearance.
Key Scriptures
Acts 14:6; Acts 14:11
Related Events
Paul's Missionary Journeys
