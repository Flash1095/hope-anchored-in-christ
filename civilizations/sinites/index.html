Sinites

Civilization
Sinites
Time Period
Ancient (Genesis 10 era)
Region
Canaan (location uncertain)
History
The Sinites are named once in the Table of Nations as descendants of Canaan (Genesis 10:17; 1 Chronicles 1:15), with no further biblical history. Later writers proposed locations from the northern Levant to farther east, but the text itself gives no location and no story, and none of those identifications is settled.
Culture
Nothing is recorded of Sinite culture apart from their place in the Canaanite family list.
Religion
Nothing distinct is recorded of Sinite religion; as Canaanites they belonged to the wider Canaanite world.
Bible Connection
The Sinites matter here only as part of the Bible's own map of the peoples: one branch of Canaan's family, named and then never heard from again.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 10:17; 1 Chronicles 1:15
