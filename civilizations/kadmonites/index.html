Kadmonites

Civilization
Kadmonites
Time Period
Patriarchal era (mentioned once, Genesis 15)
Region
The east (the name means 'easterners'); no territory is stated in the text
History
The Kadmonites are named only once in the Bible, in the land promise to Abram (Genesis 15:19), among the peoples of the land: Kenites, Kenizzites, Kadmonites, Hittites, Perizzites, Rephaim, Amorites, Canaanites, Girgashites, and Jebusites. Nothing more is recorded about them: no king, city, territory, or later mention appears anywhere in the text.
Culture
Unknown. Their name points east, and they are sometimes grouped with the 'children of the east' of the desert fringe, but the text itself says no more.
Religion
Unknown.
Bible Connection
The Kadmonites appear only in the promise itself: part of the land God pledged to Abram's descendants, named once and never heard from again.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 15:19
Related People
Abraham
Related Places
Canaan
