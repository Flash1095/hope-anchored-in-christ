Ammon

Civilization
Ammon
Time Period
c. 1300-500 BC
Region
East of the Jordan, around modern Amman
History
The Ammonites, descended from Lot's son Ben-Ammi, held the land east of the Jordan around their capital Rabbah. They clashed with Israel from the judges through David, who captured Rabbah, and survived as a small state into the Persian period.
Culture
Ammon shared the language and material culture of its neighbors Moab and Israel, and its capital Rabbah was a fortified trade center on the King's Highway.
Religion
Ammon worshipped Molech (Milcom), whose rites included child sacrifice, which the Law of Moses strictly forbade. Solomon's Ammonite wives brought Molech's worship into Jerusalem itself.
Bible Connection
Jephthah defeated Ammon; Saul's first victory as king was against Ammon's siege of Jabesh-gilead; David's war with Ammon frames the Bathsheba story.
Key Scriptures
Judges 11; 1 Samuel 11; 2 Samuel 10
Related People
Lot
