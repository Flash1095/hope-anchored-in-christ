Babylon

Civilization
Babylon
Time Period
c. 1900-539 BC
Region
Southern Mesopotamia, capital Babylon
History
Babylon, heir of Sumer and Akkad, reached its height under Hammurabi and again under Nebuchadnezzar, who destroyed Jerusalem in 586 BC and carried Judah into exile. Cyrus of Persia took the city in 539 BC without a battle.
Culture
Babylon gave the world Hammurabi's law code, advanced astronomy and mathematics, the Hanging Gardens tradition, and the ziggurat — the stepped temple tower behind the Babel story.
Religion
Babylon worshipped Marduk above a large pantheon, with Ishtar, Shamash, and Nabu among the chief gods. Its priests read omens in the stars and livers, a practice Daniel's God repeatedly overruled.
Bible Connection
Babylon is both a place and a symbol in Scripture: the tower of Babel, the exile that reshaped Israel's faith, Daniel's court, and the 'Babylon' of Revelation standing for every proud power opposed to God.
