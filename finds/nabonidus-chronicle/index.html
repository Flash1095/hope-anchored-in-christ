Nabonidus Chronicle

Find Name
Nabonidus Chronicle
Date Discovered
1879
Artifact Date
c. 556–539 BC
Discovery Location
Babylon, Iraq
Current Location
British Museum, London
Timeline Era
Exile
Description
A Babylonian chronicle tablet recording the reign of Nabonidus, the last king of Babylon, down to the Persian conquest: the capture of Babylon by Cyrus's army in 539 BC, the death of the king's son, and the entry of Cyrus himself. It is the Babylonian side of the night Belshazzar's feast ended.
Biblical Significance
Daniel 5 tells of handwriting on the wall and a kingdom weighed and divided in a single night. The chronicle gives the same night from the empire's own records — Babylon fell, Cyrus entered, and the exile that had begun with Nebuchadnezzar ended with a Persian king's decree. God's timetable, kept by both prophet and chronicler.
