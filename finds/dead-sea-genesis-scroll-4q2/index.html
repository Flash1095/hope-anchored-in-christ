Dead Sea Genesis Scroll (4Q2)

Find Name
Dead Sea Genesis Scroll (4Q2)
Description
4Q2, also called 4QGen-b, is a Hebrew manuscript of Genesis from Qumran Cave 4, found in 1952. Written in a Roman-period hand (1st century AD), it survives only in fragments containing Genesis 1:1-27; 2:14-19; 4:2-4; and 5:13. Though small, it is one of many Genesis copies at Qumran showing the book's early circulation.
Historical Period
Second Temple period
Evidence Class
Direct
Interpretations
Its fragments cover the creation account, the garden, Cain and Abel, and the genealogy of Adam, giving a first-century witness to the opening chapters of Genesis. The readings align broadly with the Masoretic tradition, adding to the evidence that Genesis was transmitted stably. Its fragmentary state is a caution: it illuminates only the verses it preserves.
Alternative Names
4QGen-b; 4QGenb
Related Books
Genesis
Scripture Illuminated
Genesis 1:1-27; Genesis 2:14-19; Genesis 4:2-4; Genesis 5:13
Biblical Significance
It is a first-century witness to the opening chapters of Genesis, confirming that the creation and early genealogies were already fixed in essentially their present form.
