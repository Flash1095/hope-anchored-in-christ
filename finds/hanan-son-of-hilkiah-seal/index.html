Hanan Son of Hilkiah Seal

A seal ring published in 1980, bearing a three-line paleo-Hebrew inscription in reverse letters, as usual for a seal, reading '(Belonging) to Hanan, son (of) Hilkiah the priest'. Hilkiah was the high priest under King Josiah who found the Book of the Law in the Temple (2 Kings 22:8). Hanan himself is not named in Scripture; the seal attests a priestly son of the biblical high priest in the late 7th century BC. Unlike the Azariah bulla of 1982, this seal does not come from the stratified City of David hoard, so it carries less archaeological weight than that bulla.

Interpretations

The seal names the biblical high priest Hilkiah as the father of a priest, Hanan, giving a second extra-biblical witness to Hilkiah's family alongside the Azariah bulla, and the two seals appear to be the work of the same engraver. Because Hanan is not himself a biblical figure and the seal lacks the Azariah bulla's stratified context, the entry claims the Hilkiah connection, not a biblical identity for Hanan.

Scripture Illuminated

2 Kings 22:8

Biblical Significance

A priest's seal naming Hilkiah as his father corroborates the high priest of Josiah's reform from a second object, complementing the Azariah bulla found in Jerusalem.
