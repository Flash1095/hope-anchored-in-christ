Cyrus Cylinder

Find Name
Cyrus Cylinder
Date Discovered
1879
Artifact Date
c. 539 BC
Discovery Location
Babylon, Iraq
Current Location
British Museum, London
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Description
A barrel-shaped clay cylinder in Akkadian cuneiform recording Cyrus the Great's conquest of Babylon and his policy of restoring temples and returning displaced peoples to their homelands. It was buried as a foundation deposit in Babylon.
Biblical Significance
Though it does not name Judah, the cylinder confirms exactly the policy Ezra records: Cyrus decreed that exiled peoples could return and rebuild their sanctuaries. It shows the biblical return from exile fits real Persian royal practice, and reminds us God can move the heart of a pagan king to keep His promise.
