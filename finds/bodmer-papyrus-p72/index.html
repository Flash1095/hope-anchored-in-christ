Bodmer Papyrus P72

Find Name
Bodmer Papyrus P72
Description
Papyrus 72 designates the Jude and 1-2 Peter portions (P.Bodmer VII-VIII) of the Bodmer Miscellaneous Codex, copied by the same scribe. It is the earliest known manuscript of Jude and of 1 and 2 Peter in their entirety, with only a few verses of Jude also surviving in P78. The 1-2 Peter portion was donated by Martin Bodmer to the Vatican in 1969.
Historical Period
Roman
Evidence Class
Direct
Interpretations
Scholars agree P72 is the earliest complete copy of these three letters; its exact date within the third to fourth century and the reconstruction of the miscellaneous codex it belonged to are debated.
Alternative Names
Papyrus Bodmer VII-VIII

Biblical Significance
These short letters are thinly represented among the earliest papyri, so P72 is the closest surviving witness to how Jude and Peter's letters circulated in the third or fourth century. Its text of 1 Peter is strongly Alexandrian in character, while 2 Peter and Jude show a freer scribal hand, a reminder to weigh each book's evidence on its own merits.
