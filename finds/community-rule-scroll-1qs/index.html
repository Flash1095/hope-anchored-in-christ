Community Rule Scroll (1QS)

Find Name
Community Rule Scroll (1QS)
Description
The rule book for the life and organization of the yahad, the communal group at Qumran, and one of the original seven scrolls from Cave 1, found in 1946–1947. The most complete copy, 1QS, is dated paleographically to about 100–75 BC, with ten further copies from Cave 4 (4Q255–264a) and one from Cave 5 (5Q11). It covers admission, shared property, prayer, meals, study and rigorous purity, and includes the 'Treatise on the Two Spirits' (1QS iii.13–iv.26), a penal code, a covenant-renewal liturgy, and expectation of the Prophet and the Messiahs of Aaron and Israel.
Historical Period
Hellenistic–Roman
Evidence Class
Direct
Interpretations
The Community Rule is the primary source for the sect's dualism (Spirit of Light versus Spirit of Darkness), predestination and communal discipline, and it shows a Judaism organized as a separated, priestly community awaiting the end. Authorship by the Teacher of Righteousness is hypothesized but unproven, and the Rule describes the sect's own ideals, not mainstream Jewish practice.
Alternative Names
Manual of Discipline;Serekh ha-Yahad;Rule of the Community
Scripture Illuminated
Isaiah 40:3
Biblical Significance
It gives the clearest window into a disciplined Second Temple community's daily life and theology, and its 'prepare the way' self-understanding parallels the ministry of John the Baptist.
