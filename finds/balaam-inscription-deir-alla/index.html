Balaam Inscription (Deir Alla Inscription)

Find Name
Balaam Inscription (Deir Alla Inscription)
Date Discovered
1967
Artifact Date
c. 800 BC
Discovery Location
Deir Alla, Jordan
Current Location
The Jordan Museum, Amman, Jordan
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Description
Fragments of a plaster wall inscription in a local Aramaic-related script, telling of night visions of doom received by 'Balaam son of Beor, a seer of the gods.' It was found shattered by an earthquake in a building at Deir Alla in the Jordan Valley.
Biblical Significance
Outside the Bible, Balaam was remembered centuries later and in another country as a famous seer, exactly the reputation Numbers describes. The inscription does not retell the talking donkey, but it confirms Balaam son of Beor was a real figure of regional renown, remembered for words God gave him.
