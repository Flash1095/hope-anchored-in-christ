Sea of Galilee Boat

Find Name
Sea of Galilee Boat
Date Discovered
1986
Artifact Date
1st century AD
Discovery Location
Sea of Galilee, near Ginosar, Israel
Current Location
Yigal Allon Museum, Ginosar
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
A wooden fishing boat about twenty-seven feet long, exposed by a drought in 1986 and excavated from the mud of the Sea of Galilee by Shelley Wachsmann and Orna Cohen. Built of cedar, oak, and other local woods with mortise-and-tenon joints, it is the kind of boat Galilean fishermen used in the first century — the kind that carried thirteen people at most.
Biblical Significance
When Jesus calmed the storm or called fishermen from their nets, he did it from a boat like this one, on this lake, with men who mended nets for a living. The boat makes the Gospels' lake stories tangible: the same water, the same wood, the same sudden storms that still rise over Galilee.
