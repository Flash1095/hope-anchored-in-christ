Jehucal Son of Shelemiah Bulla

Find Name
Jehucal Son of Shelemiah Bulla
Description
A clay bulla found in 2005 by Eilat Mazar in the City of David, in palace ruins above the Stepped Stone Structure, reading 'Belonging to Jehucal, son of Shelemiah, son of Shovi'. Jehucal, also called Jucal, is the prince King Zedekiah sent to Jeremiah in Jeremiah 37:3, and one of the four princes who had Jeremiah thrown into the cistern in Jeremiah 38:1-6. The bulla was found a few yards from the Gedaliah bulla, in the same strata.
Historical Period
Iron Age II
Evidence Class
Direct
Interpretations
This is a direct match: the bulla names a man the Bible names, in the city and the final years of Judah where Jeremiah places him, alongside the bulla of his fellow prince Gedaliah. It puts a seal impression in the hand of one of the men who tried to silence Jeremiah, in the very generation of Jerusalem's fall.

Biblical Significance
The seal of one of Jeremiah's persecutors survives from the last days of Judah, confirming that the princes of Jeremiah 38 were real officials of Zedekiah's court.
