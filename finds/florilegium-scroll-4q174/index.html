Florilegium Scroll (4Q174)

Find Name
Florilegium Scroll (4Q174)
Description
An eschatological midrash from Qumran Cave 4, in Herodian script, that strings together biblical quotations with commentary: 2 Samuel 7:10–14, Exodus 15:17–18, Amos 9:11, Psalm 1:1, Psalm 2:1, Daniel 12:10 and others. Following Annette Steudel, it is now usually read as part of a larger eschatological midrash rather than a standalone anthology. It presents the sectarian doctrine identifying the Community with the Temple and announces two coming figures: the 'Branch of David' and the 'Interpreter of the Law', who will arise together in Zion in the last days.
Historical Period
Hellenistic–Roman
Evidence Class
Direct
Interpretations
The Florilegium is the clearest example of the sect's thematic interpretation: scattered promises are gathered to prove that the community itself is the true temple and that a Davidic messiah and a priestly interpreter will come together. Its exegesis is the sect's own reading, not the plain sense of the quoted passages, and its 'Branch of David' is the sect's royal messiah, not a named historical king.

Biblical Significance
It shows how Second Temple Jews read the prophets messianically, gathering Davidic and priestly promises into one end-times hope — the same two-messiah expectation behind the Community Rule.
