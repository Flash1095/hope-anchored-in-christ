Rule of the Blessings (1QSb)

Find Name
Rule of the Blessings (1QSb)
Description
The second appendix on the same Cave 1 scroll as the Community Rule (dated 100–75 BC), a series of blessings for the last days: one for the general assembly of eschatological Israel, one for the priestly Sons of Zadok who will act 'like angels' and lead Israel after the War, and one for the messianic meal blessing the 'Prince', the Davidic messiah who has come to deliver Israel. It is also designated 1Q28b, and a small fragment of it (MS 1909, 1st century BC, Hebrew on vellum) is in the Schøyen Collection.
Historical Period
Hellenistic–Roman
Evidence Class
Direct
Interpretations
The Rule of the Blessings shows the sect's end-times liturgy: blessing pronounced by the Master over the community, the high priest, the priests, the Sons of Zadok and the Prince of the congregation. Some scholars think it may be a collection of prayers for general daily use rather than only for the eschaton, so its setting should not be overstated. Its priestly blessings follow the pattern of the Aaronic blessing in Numbers 6.

Biblical Significance
It shows how Second Temple Jews turned the Aaronic blessing into an end-times liturgy, with priesthood and messiah side by side at the hoped-for renewal of Israel.
