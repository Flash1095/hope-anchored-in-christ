Seal of Isaiah

Find Name
Seal of Isaiah

Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Description
A small clay bulla found just metres from Hezekiah's seal impression, inscribed 'Belonging to Isaiah' and a second line that may read 'the prophet,' though the crucial final letter is missing where the seal is damaged. It was excavated in 2009 and announced in 2018.
Biblical Significance
If the damaged reading is correct, this could be the prophet Isaiah's own seal, found beside his king's, a thrilling pairing of the two men who stood together in Jerusalem's crisis. Scholars debate the reconstruction, so we present it honestly as possible rather than proven, while noting Isaiah's ministry itself is firmly rooted in Hezekiah's Jerusalem.
Evidence Class
Disputed
Interpretations
Scholars debate this bulla: the first line reads ‘Isaiah,’ but the second line’s ‘prophet’ reading is damaged and uncertain, and the bulla is unprovenanced — it may be the prophet’s seal, or another Isaiah’s.
