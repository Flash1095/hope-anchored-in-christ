Pool of Bethesda

Find Name
Pool of Bethesda
Date Discovered
1888
Artifact Date
c. 2nd century BC - AD 70
Discovery Location
Old City, Jerusalem, Israel
Current Location
In situ, Church of St. Anne, Jerusalem, Israel
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Description
A double pool complex north of the Temple Mount, excavated beside the Church of St. Anne, with five porticoes formed by colonnades around and between its two basins. In the Byzantine period a church was built over it to commemorate Jesus's healing there.
Biblical Significance
John 5 describes Bethesda as having five porticoes, a detail once doubted as symbolic until the five colonnades were uncovered. Archaeology here vindicates John's eyewitness eye for Jerusalem, and points us to the mercy of Jesus, who found a forgotten man at a crowded pool and made him whole.
