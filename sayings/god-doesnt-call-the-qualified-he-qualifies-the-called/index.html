God doesn't call the qualified, He qualifies the called

People take it to mean that God does not wait for the skilled, impressive or ready — He chooses ordinary, unlikely people and equips them for the work He gives them.

What The Bible Says

The pattern runs all through Scripture: "God hath chosen the foolish things of the world to confound the wise... and weak things of the world to confound the things which are mighty" (1 Corinthians 1:27-28), Moses protested that he was slow of speech (Exodus 4:10) and God answered, "I will be with thy mouth" (Exodus 4:12), and Jeremiah said, "I cannot speak: for I am a child" (Jeremiah 1:6). I hold that the sentence is true to Scripture's pattern even though the words themselves are not a verse — God really does call the unlikely and then equip them.
