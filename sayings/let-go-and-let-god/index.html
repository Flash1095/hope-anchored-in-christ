Let go and let God

Stop struggling, stop trying to fix it yourself, and simply hand the whole thing over — if you let go completely, God will take over and carry it for you.

What The Bible Says

Trust is commanded all through Scripture: Proverbs 3:5 tells us to trust in the LORD with all our heart and lean not on our own understanding, Psalm 46:10 says be still and know that I am God, and 1 Peter 5:7 tells us to cast all our care upon Him. But 'let go and let God' turns that trust into passivity, as if faith means doing nothing, while the Bible pairs trust with obedience and action — Philippians 4:6 tells us to pray rather than be anxious, and then to keep walking. I hold that the slogan gets the direction right and the method wrong: we do hand our burdens to God, but He never asks us to drop our hands from the work He has given us.

Details

Saying: Let go and let God
Closest Verse: Proverbs 3:5
Origin: The slogan grew out of the Keswick Convention and the Higher Life movement of the late 1800s, which taught a crisis of full surrender as the secret of the Christian life; it later spread through recovery programs such as Alcoholics Anonymous and into Christian self-help. No single author can be credited with coining the exact words.
Related Books: Proverbs; Psalms; 1 Peter
