The lion shall lie down with the lamb

People picture it as a promise of God's coming peace, when natural enemies will rest side by side and nothing will hurt or destroy.

What The Bible Says

The hope is real, but the pairing is wrong: Isaiah 11:6 says "The wolf also shall dwell with the lamb, and the leopard shall lie down with the kid," and the lion in that passage eats straw like the ox (Isaiah 11:7), while Isaiah 65:25 repeats the wolf and the lamb feeding together. No verse anywhere pairs the lion lying down with the lamb. I believe the promise of peace is true, and I also believe in getting the words right — the lion and the lamb never lie down together in Scripture.

Details

Saying: The lion shall lie down with the lamb
Closest Verse: Isaiah 11:6
Origin: No single author — a centuries-old misquotation of Isaiah 11:6, repeated in preaching, art and hymns until it felt like Scripture. The earliest manuscripts, including the Septuagint, the Dead Sea Scrolls and the 1611 King James Bible, all read wolf, not lion.
