God won't give you more than you can handle

People take it as a promise that God caps every hardship at what they can bear, so no suffering will ever be more than they can cope with.

What The Bible Says

The verse behind it, 1 Corinthians 10:13, is about temptation, not suffering: God "will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able" and makes a way of escape. About suffering Paul says the opposite: he and his companions were "pressed out of measure, above strength" (2 Corinthians 1:8), and it happened so they would trust God rather than themselves (2 Corinthians 1:9). I hold that the saying, meant as comfort, can wound: when life is genuinely more than someone can handle, it tells them God broke a promise He never made, instead of pointing them to the God who carries what we cannot.

Details

Saying: God won't give you more than you can handle
Closest Verse: 1 Corinthians 10:13
Origin: A modern folk paraphrase that stretches 1 Corinthians 10:13, swapping "temptation" for suffering in general. No single author or date is known for the wording.
