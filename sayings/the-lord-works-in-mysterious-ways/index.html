The Lord works in mysterious ways

People usually take it to mean that God has a hidden plan behind confusing or painful events, and that what looks senseless now will make sense in time.

What The Bible Says

Scripture does teach that God's ways are beyond ours: "For my thoughts are not your thoughts, neither are your ways my ways, saith the LORD" (Isaiah 55:8), and Romans 11:33 calls His judgments unsearchable and His ways past finding out, while Psalm 77:19 says His footsteps are not known. But the Bible never promises that every mystery will be explained to us in this life; it calls us to trust His character when we cannot trace His hand. I believe the thought behind the line is sound, but the words themselves are a hymn writer's, not Scripture's.
