Don't judge a book by its cover

Don't decide what someone is worth, or whether something is good, from how it looks on the outside — appearances mislead, and the real value only shows on closer acquaintance.

What The Bible Says

The Bible teaches the same caution in its own words: 1 Samuel 16:7 says man looketh on the outward appearance, but the LORD looketh on the heart, and John 7:24 commands us to judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgment. James 2:1 warns against having the faith of our Lord Jesus Christ with respect of persons. I believe the saying and the Scriptures agree here, but the proverb itself is not a Bible verse — it is a nineteenth-century saying that happens to point the same way the Bible does.

Details

Saying: Don't judge a book by its cover
Closest Verse: 1 Samuel 16:7
Origin: The exact wording appeared as a newspaper headline, 'Don't Judge a Book by its Cover,' in the Boston Daily Mail on 12 June 1849; a similar thought appears in George Eliot's The Mill on the Floss in 1860, where Mr. Tulliver concludes 'one mustn't judge by th' outside.' The sentiment is older, but the proverb is nineteenth-century, not biblical.
Related Books: 1 Samuel; John; James
Related Topics: Discernment; Judgment; Wisdom
