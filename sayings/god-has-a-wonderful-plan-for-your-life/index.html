God has a wonderful plan for your life

God has a specific, wonderful script written for your life — a good plan, made just for you, that you can step into and enjoy.

What The Bible Says

Jeremiah 29:11 is the verse always quoted for this — "For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the LORD, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end" — but that promise was spoken to the exiles in Babylon as a people, with a seventy-year horizon, not as a personal guarantee of a wonderful life for each believer. God does have purposes for His people — Ephesians 2:10 says we are created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath before ordained — yet the Bible never promises the plan will feel wonderful; Paul's own life was shipwrecks, beatings and prison. I believe God has good purposes for every believer, and I also believe the slogan promises more than the verse it leans on.

Details

Saying: God has a wonderful plan for your life
Closest Verse: Jeremiah 29:11
Origin: Bill Bright, founder of Campus Crusade for Christ (now Cru). He drafted a gospel presentation called "God's Plan for Your Life" in 1956, developed it into the Four Spiritual Laws talking points in 1957, and the booklet form was compiled in 1965, its first law reading: "God loves you and offers a wonderful plan for your life." The booklet went on to be printed in more than 2.5 billion copies in over 200 languages.
Related Books: Jeremiah; Ephesians; Romans
Related Topics: God's Providence; God's Sovereignty; Guidance; Hope
