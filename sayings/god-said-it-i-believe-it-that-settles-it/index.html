God said it, I believe it, that settles it

The speaker is declaring full confidence in Scripture — if God said it, that is the end of the argument, whatever anyone else thinks.

What The Bible Says

God's word is indeed settled: "For ever, O LORD, thy word is settled in heaven" (Psalm 119:89), and all Scripture is given by inspiration of God (2 Timothy 3:16). But notice the order in the slogan — God said it, I believe it, that settles it — as if God's word waits on my belief to become settled. My position is that the slogan gets the order wrong: God's word is settled whether I believe it or not, and my belief is the response, not the seal. The sentiment is sound; the wording puts me in the middle where I do not belong.

Details

Saying: God said it, I believe it, that settles it
Closest Verse: Psalm 119:89
Origin: No single author is documented. The phrase was popularised in the 1970s — the Heritage Singers published the song "God Said It, I Believe It" in 1975 and filmed a music video for it in 1976 — and it circulated widely as a bumper sticker. Billy Graham is sometimes associated with it, but there is no evidence he coined it.
Related Books: Psalms; 2 Timothy; Ecclesiastes
Related Topics: Faith; Trust; Truth
