The devil is in the details

A plan can look fine from a distance and still fall apart on the small print — the trouble hides in the particulars nobody bothered to check.

What The Bible Says

This one has no verse behind it at all, not even a close one. Scripture does value care and diligence — Colossians 3:23 tells us to do whatever we do heartily, as to the Lord, Luke 14:28 tells us to count the cost before building, and Proverbs 21:5 says the thoughts of the diligent tend only to plenteousness — but none of that is about a devil lurking in the fine print. I hold that the saying is sound practical advice dressed up in religious language, and it should be quoted as good sense, never as Scripture.

Details

Saying: The devil is in the details
Origin: It is a twentieth-century inversion of the older German saying 'God is in the detail,' traced to a seminar given by art historian Aby Warburg in Hamburg in November 1925 and often wrongly attributed to the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe. The devil version first appears in print in the early 1960s and only became common in the 1990s.
Related Books: Colossians; Luke; Proverbs
