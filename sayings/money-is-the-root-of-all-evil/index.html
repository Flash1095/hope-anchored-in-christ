Money is the root of all evil

People take it to mean money itself is evil, so having it, or wanting it, is the source of every wrong in the world.

What The Bible Says

Paul wrote something narrower and sharper: "the love of money is the root of all evil" (1 Timothy 6:10). One small word changes everything: the danger is loving money, not holding it. Jesus said no one can serve both God and mammon (Matthew 6:24), and Hebrews 13:5 tells believers to be content, yet Scripture also treats wealth as a tool to be stewarded and given generously. I believe the misquote does real harm: it lets greed hide behind a shrug at money itself, when the Bible's charge is aimed at the heart that trusts in riches.

Details

Saying: Money is the root of all evil
Closest Verse: 1 Timothy 6:10
Origin: A misquote of 1 Timothy 6:10. The popular form drops the word "love", turning Paul's warning about the love of money into a claim about money itself.
Related Books: 1 Timothy; Matthew; Hebrews
