Let your conscience be your guide

Your inner voice knows right from wrong, so the safest path through life is simply to follow what your conscience tells you.

What The Bible Says

The Bible does speak of conscience — Romans 2:15 says the conscience bears witness, accusing or else excusing — but it never tells us to follow it as a guide, and for good reason: conscience can be misled. "The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked" (Jeremiah 17:9), and Paul warns of consciences seared with a hot iron (1 Timothy 4:2). A clear conscience did not prove Paul right either — "I know nothing by myself; yet am I not hereby justified" (1 Corinthians 4:4). I hold that conscience is a witness, not a compass: it can tell you something is wrong, but only God's word can tell you what is right.

Details

Saying: Let your conscience be your guide
Closest Verse: Romans 2:15
Origin: Best known from Walt Disney's Pinocchio (1940), where the Blue Fairy tells Pinocchio to "always let your conscience be your guide," and Jiminy Cricket sings it in "Give a Little Whistle." An older origin is sometimes claimed — Caliph Umar ibn al-Khattab is said to have used the words in his address at Jabiah after the surrender of Jerusalem (c. 638) — but no primary source verifies the English wording as an accurate translation, so that attribution is reported, not certain.
Related Books: Romans; Jeremiah; 1 Timothy; 1 Corinthians; Proverbs
