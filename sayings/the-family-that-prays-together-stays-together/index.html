The family that prays together stays together

A family that prays together will hold together — shared prayer is what keeps a family from falling apart.

What The Bible Says

Prayer together is a good and biblical thing — Jesus said "where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them" (Matthew 18:20), and the early church continued steadfastly in prayer (Acts 2:42). And Joshua's resolve is real: "as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD" (Joshua 24:15). But that verse is about serving the Lord, not about prayer keeping a family together, and the Bible never promises that praying families never break apart. I hold that the slogan points at something true — prayer binds a household to God — while promising something Scripture never does.

Details

Saying: The family that prays together stays together
Closest Verse: Joshua 24:15
Origin: Written in 1947 by advertising copywriter Al Scalpone as the slogan for Fr. Patrick Peyton's Family Rosary Crusade and Family Theater radio dramas. Peyton (1909-1992), the Irish-American Holy Cross priest known as "the Rosary Priest," launched the Family Rosary Crusade in 1942; the slogan was heard weekly on the programme and on billboards.
Related Books: Joshua; Matthew; Acts
Related Topics: Prayer; Family; Parenting; Unity
