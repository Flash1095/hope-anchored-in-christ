Cleanliness is next to godliness

People take it to mean that being physically clean, washed, tidy and well-kept, is a mark of spiritual goodness, almost a spiritual duty in itself.

What The Bible Says

The Bible cares deeply about purity, but it is purity of heart, not of hands: "Create in me a clean heart, O God" (Psalm 51:10), and Jesus taught that what defiles a person comes from within, not from unwashed hands (Mark 7:15). God did give Israel washing laws (Leviticus 11:44), yet those pointed to holiness, not to soap as a virtue. My position is that a tidy house is a fine thing, but it is not godliness, and treating it as such shames the poor and confuses outward appearance with a clean heart.

Details

Saying: Cleanliness is next to godliness
Closest Verse: Psalm 51:10
Origin: First recorded in John Wesley's sermon "On Dress" (1778), where Wesley quotes it as an existing saying, "Cleanliness is, indeed, next to godliness", so the phrase is older than Wesley, though its earlier source is not known.
Related Books: Psalms; Mark; Leviticus
Related Topics: Purity; Gospel purity
