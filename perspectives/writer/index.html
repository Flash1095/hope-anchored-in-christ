Method
A writer's craft is imagination disciplined by truth: stories that make abstract ideas felt. Lewis used story, analogy, and plain reason to show how Christianity explains the world we actually live in - moral longing, joy, and the ache for something more.
Biblical Insight
Imagination is not the enemy of truth; it is one way truth reaches the heart. The Gospel is the true story every other story echoes.
