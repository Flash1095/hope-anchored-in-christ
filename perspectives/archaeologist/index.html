Exemplar
Yigael Yadin
Method
Archaeology reads the ground like a document: layers, inscriptions, and artifacts that either confirm or challenge a text's claims. Yadin's excavations at Masada and Hazor, and his work on the Dead Sea Scrolls, put the biblical world back on the map as real history.
Biblical Insight
The Bible's people and places keep turning up in the dirt. Archaeology does not prove faith, but it keeps showing the story is rooted in real ground.
