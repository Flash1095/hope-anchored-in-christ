Method
Great teachers ask questions, tell stories, and meet students where they are. Jesus, history's most influential teacher, taught this way constantly - parables that hid truth from the proud and revealed it to the humble, questions that exposed hearts.
Biblical Insight
Truth is not only stated; it is taught. Jesus' method shows that how we teach matters as much as what we teach.
