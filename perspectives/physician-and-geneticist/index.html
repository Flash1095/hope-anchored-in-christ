Exemplar

Method
Genetics reads the genome as a language - three billion letters of information that build a human being. Collins, who led the Human Genome Project, saw in that language both scientific wonder and a signature of a Creator, and found no conflict between rigorous science and Christian faith.
Biblical Insight
The human genome's elegance and the moral law within us both speak of a Mind behind nature. Science and faith answer different questions about the same reality.
