Detective

Profession
Detective
Exemplar
J. Warner Wallace
Method
Cold-case detectives work with incomplete evidence, test witness reliability, and use abductive reasoning - asking which explanation best fits all the facts. Wallace, a homicide detective and former atheist, applied the same method to the New Testament and concluded the Gospel writers were reliable witnesses.
Biblical Insight
Christianity's claims can be investigated like a case: the evidence for the resurrection, taken together, points to one conclusion - the risen Jesus.
