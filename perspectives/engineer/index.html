Engineer

Engineers recognize design: specified complexity, interlocking parts, and information that must be arranged to work. When they look at a cell's machinery or the universe's constants, many see the same hallmarks they use to identify design in their own work.

Biblical Insight

Design is recognized by its marks, not by its maker being visible. The world's machinery bears those marks.
