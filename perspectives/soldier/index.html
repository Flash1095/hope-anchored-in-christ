Method
Soldiers understand loyalty under pressure, sacrifice for others, and the reality of an enemy. Scripture speaks this language fluently: the armor of God, the good fight, the Captain of salvation. Service teaches what cheap faith forgets - that some things are worth cost.
Biblical Insight
Faith is not passive. It has an enemy, a cost, and a Commander worth following.
