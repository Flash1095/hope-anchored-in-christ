Cedar of Lebanon

Name
Cedar of Lebanon
Category
Plant
Description
Cedars of Lebanon grow massive and straight, their fragrant wood prized for temples and palaces. Solomon built the temple with it. A cedar's roots grip mountain rock, and its branches spread wide enough to shelter whole flocks of birds.
Scripture
Psalm 92:12
Creation Reflection
The righteous are not compared to a fast-growing weed but to a cedar: slow, strong, deeply rooted, and still green in old age.
Habitat
Mountain slopes of Lebanon and the eastern Mediterranean.
