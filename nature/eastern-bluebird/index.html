Eastern Bluebird

Name
Eastern Bluebird
Category
Bird
Description
A flash of sky-blue back and rust-red breast, the eastern bluebird was once in decline as old nest trees vanished, until thousands of people put up nest boxes and brought it back. It perches low over meadows, drops to catch insects, and returns to the same boxes year after year.
Scripture
Psalm 84:3
Creation Reflection
The bluebird's return each spring speaks of homecoming, and of a God who makes room for the small and overlooked right beside His altar.
Habitat
Meadows, orchards, and nest-box trails across eastern North America.
