Monarch Butterfly

Name
Monarch Butterfly
Category
Animal
Description
The monarch begins as a striped caterpillar eating milkweed, then dissolves inside a chrysalis and emerges utterly remade, orange and black. Each fall, monarchs migrate thousands of miles to a few mountain forests in Mexico, a journey no single butterfly has ever made before.
Scripture
2 Corinthians 5:17
Creation Reflection
Metamorphosis is the gospel in miniature: what goes into the chrysalis is not improved but transformed. In Christ, we are not merely better, we are new.
Habitat
Milkweed meadows and migration routes across North America.
