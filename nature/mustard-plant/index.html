Mustard Plant

Name
Mustard Plant
Category
Plant
Description
From a seed so small it is nearly dust, the mustard plant grows in one season into a shrub taller than a person, its branches sheltering birds. In Jesus' day it was a familiar sight in every Galilean field, the smallest seed in the garden, with the largest result.
Scripture
Matthew 13:31-32
Creation Reflection
The kingdom of God starts small, a seed, a prayer, a quiet act of faith, and grows beyond what anyone expected. Never despise a small beginning.
Habitat
Fields and roadsides across the Middle East and Mediterranean.
