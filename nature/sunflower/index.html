Sunflower

A sunflower can grow ten feet tall in a single summer, turning its young head to follow the sun across the sky, a habit called heliotropism. One flower head holds a thousand tiny florets, each becoming a seed that feeds birds, bees, and people alike.

Creation Reflection

The sunflower's whole posture is a picture of worship: face turned toward the light, all day, every day. We were made to orient our lives the same way, toward the Son.

Habitat

Fields and gardens in full sun, grown around the world.
