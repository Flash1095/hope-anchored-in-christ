Ruby-throated Hummingbird

Name
Ruby-throated Hummingbird
Category
Bird
Description
Weighing less than a nickel, the ruby-throated hummingbird beats its wings about fifty times a second and can hover, fly backward, and cross the entire Gulf of Mexico, some six hundred miles, without stopping. The male's throat flashes ruby only when the light catches it.
Scripture
2 Corinthians 12:9
Creation Reflection
The smallest bird on the continent makes the bravest journey. God delights in doing great things through small, weak creatures, including us.
Habitat
Gardens, feeders, and woodland edges across eastern North America in summer.
