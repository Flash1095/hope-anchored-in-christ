Northern Cardinal

Name
Northern Cardinal
Category
Bird
Description
The male northern cardinal is unmistakable: crested, bright red from beak to tail, with a black mask around his coral-red bill. Cardinals do not migrate, so their color and clear whistled song brighten backyards even in the depth of winter. Pairs stay together year-round, and both parents feed their young.
Scripture
Matthew 10:29
Creation Reflection
A cardinal in a snow-covered tree is a small sermon: color and song in the bleakest season. Hope does not wait for spring to sing. Like the anchor of Hebrews 6:19, it holds fast through winter.
Habitat
Gardens, thickets, and woodland edges across eastern North America, year-round.
