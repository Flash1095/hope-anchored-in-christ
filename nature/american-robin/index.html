American Robin

Name
American Robin
Category
Bird
Description
The robin is spring's first herald: gray-brown above, brick-red below, pulling earthworms from lawns at dawn and singing long before sunrise. Robins are among the first birds to sing in the morning and the last to stop at dusk, and many raise two or three broods a season.
Scripture
Ecclesiastes 3:1
Creation Reflection
Robins remind us that seasons turn. After every winter, God sends a song back to the garden, a small yearly picture of resurrection hope.
Habitat
Lawns, gardens, and parks across nearly all of North America.
