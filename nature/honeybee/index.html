Honeybee

Name
Honeybee
Category
Animal
Description
A single honeybee visits thousands of flowers a day, and a hive runs on remarkable order: one queen, thousands of workers, each with a task, communicating by dance. Their honey was the ancient world's sweetener, and their pollination feeds a third of what we eat.
Scripture
Psalm 19:10
Creation Reflection
Scripture calls God's words sweeter than honey. The bee's sweetness is a daily reminder that God's truth is not bitter medicine but nourishment and delight.
Habitat
Meadows, gardens, and hives wherever flowers bloom.
