Cattle Egret

Name
Cattle Egret
Category
Bird
Description
The cattle egret is a small white heron that walks beside grazing cattle, snapping up grasshoppers and insects stirred from the grass by their hooves. Originally from Africa, it crossed the Atlantic on its own and spread across the Americas, one of the great natural colonizations of the last century.
Scripture
Matthew 6:26
Creation Reflection
The egret does not farm its own field; it follows the herd and finds provision along the way. God often provides the same way, not through our own striving, but beside us on the ordinary path.
Habitat
Pastures, wetlands, and fields across the southern United States and around the world.
