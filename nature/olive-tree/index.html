Olive Tree

Name
Olive Tree
Category
Plant
Description
Olive trees live astonishingly long. The ancient tree at Vouves in Crete is thought to be thousands of years old and still bears fruit. Their oil lit lamps, healed wounds, and anointed kings, and the olive branch has meant peace ever since Noah's dove carried one home.
Scripture
Psalm 52:8
Creation Reflection
An olive tree is not in a hurry. It grows slowly, roots deeply, and keeps bearing fruit for generations, a picture of the steady, rooted life God grows in His people.
Habitat
Sunny groves around the Mediterranean and in warm climates worldwide.
