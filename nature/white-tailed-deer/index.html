White-tailed Deer

Name
White-tailed Deer
Category
Animal
Description
Graceful and alert, the white-tailed deer flashes the white underside of its tail as it bounds away, leaping fences and swimming rivers with ease. Does raise spotted fawns in spring, and deer have lived in North America's forests and fields since long before people farmed them.
Scripture
Psalm 42:1
Creation Reflection
The deer's thirst after water is the psalmist's picture of the soul's thirst for God, a longing built into us, meant to be satisfied only in Him.
Habitat
Forests, fields, and suburbs across North America.
