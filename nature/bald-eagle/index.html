Bald Eagle

Name
Bald Eagle
Category
Bird
Description
With a wingspan over six feet, the bald eagle soars on rising air with barely a wingbeat, scanning lakes and rivers for fish. Once nearly lost to pesticides, it has recovered dramatically, a conservation comeback that mirrors its name: a symbol of strength renewed.
Scripture
Isaiah 40:31
Creation Reflection
Eagles do not flap their way higher; they ride the wind. Waiting on the Lord is like that. Strength comes not from striving but from trusting the lift God provides.
Habitat
Lakes, rivers, and coasts across North America.
