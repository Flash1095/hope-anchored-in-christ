Barn Owl

Name
Barn Owl
Category
Bird
Description
The barn owl hunts in complete darkness, its heart-shaped face funneling sound to ears so sharp it can catch a mouse it cannot see. Its flight is nearly silent, thanks to soft-fringed feathers, and it has lived alongside farmers for centuries, nesting in barns and old towers.
Scripture
Psalm 102:6
Creation Reflection
Owls belong to the night watches, when worries grow loudest. The psalmist knew those hours too, and still prayed. God is present in the dark, not only in the daylight.
Habitat
Barns, open fields, and grasslands on every continent except Antarctica.
