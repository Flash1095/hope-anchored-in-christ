Blue Jay

Name
Blue Jay
Category
Bird
Description
Blue jays are loud, bold, and far smarter than their reputation suggests. They mimic the calls of hawks, remember thousands of hiding places, and — by burying acorns and forgetting some — plant whole oak forests. Their blue is not pigment at all but light scattered through feather structure.
Scripture
Job 12:7
Creation Reflection
The jay plants trees it will never sit beneath. Much of the Christian life is like that: sowing in faith, trusting God with a harvest we may never see.
Habitat
Oak woodlands, parks, and suburbs across eastern and central North America.
