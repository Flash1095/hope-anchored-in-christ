Red Fox

Name
Red Fox
Category
Animal
Description
Clever and adaptable, the red fox lives in forests, fields, and city suburbs alike, hunting mice with a pounce so precise it can hear them moving under snow. Its rust-red coat, white-tipped tail, and black legs make it one of the most recognizable animals in the world.
Scripture
Song of Solomon 2:15
Creation Reflection
It is the little foxes that spoil the vines, not the great disasters but the small compromises. The Christian life is guarded in the small, daily choices.
Habitat
Woodlands, fields, and suburbs across the Northern Hemisphere.
