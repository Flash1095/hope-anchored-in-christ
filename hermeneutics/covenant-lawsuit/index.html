Covenant Lawsuit

Summary

The covenant lawsuit (Hebrew rib) is a prophetic form modeled on an ancient treaty dispute: God summons his people, calls heaven and earth as witnesses, lays out the covenant breach, and announces judgment — often with an offer of restoration. It is a courtroom speech, not a poem about feelings.

How To Apply

Read lawsuit oracles as legal argument: identify the summons, the witnesses, the charges (which covenant stipulations are broken), and the verdict. The form explains why prophets sound like prosecutors and why their warnings carry the weight of a broken treaty.

Common Mistake

Reading lawsuit oracles as vague poetry and missing their covenant logic, or softening the charges into general complaints instead of specific breaches of a known agreement.

Details

Category: Genre
Scripture Example: Micah 6:1-8 — mountains are summoned as witnesses while the LORD states his case against Israel, then answers his own question about what he requires: to do justice, love mercy, and walk humbly with God. The passage only makes sense as a lawsuit that ends in an ethical demand.
Scripture: Micah 6:1-8; Isaiah 1:2-20; Deuteronomy 32:1-43
