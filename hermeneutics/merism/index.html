Merism

Principle
Merism
Category
Device
Summary
Merism names two opposites to mean the whole: 'heaven and earth' means all creation, 'day and night' means always, 'sitting and rising' means every posture of life. The pair is a figure for totality, not two separate categories.
How To Apply
When you meet an opposite pair, read it as a whole: ask what totality the two poles mark out. The figure is a compact way of saying 'everything of this kind, without exception.'
Scripture Example
Genesis 1:1 — 'In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth' means the whole created order, not merely sky plus soil. Psalm 139:2-3 works the same way: 'when I sit down and when I rise up' means God knows the psalmist at every moment.
Common Mistake
Literalizing each pole as a separate category and missing the whole — reading 'heaven and earth' as two items on a list instead of the totality it names.
Scripture
Genesis 1:1; Psalm 139:2-3; Deuteronomy 6:7
