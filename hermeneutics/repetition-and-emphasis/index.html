Repetition and Emphasis

Principle
Repetition and Emphasis
Category
Device
Summary
When biblical authors repeat a word, phrase, or idea, they are flagging it: repetition is emphasis. What gets said twice — or three times — is what the author most wants the reader to notice and remember.
How To Apply
Notice repeated words and phrases as you read, and ask why the author keeps returning to them. The repeated element is usually the passage's main point or its emotional center.
Scripture Example
Isaiah 6:3 — 'Holy, holy, holy is the Lord of hosts.' The threefold repetition is the Hebrew superlative: not merely holy, but holy beyond measure. The repetition itself is the theology.
Common Mistake
Skimming repeated material as filler or redundancy, and so walking past the very point the author was working hardest to make.
Scripture
Isaiah 6:3; Psalm 136:1-26; John 21:15-17
