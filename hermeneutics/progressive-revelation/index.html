Progressive Revelation

Progressive Revelation

Summary

Progressive revelation means God disclosed truth gradually across redemptive history: later revelation clarifies, deepens, and fulfills earlier revelation without contradicting it. Earlier texts are true as far as they go, but they do not yet say everything.

How To Apply

Read earlier texts in their own stage of the story, then in light of later fulfillment. Do not demand full New Testament clarity from Old Testament saints, and do not freeze God's people at an early stage once the story has moved on.

Common Mistake

Demanding that an early text answer a later question it never raised, or else treating an early stage as the final word and resisting the fulfillment that came after it.

Details

Category: Principle
Scripture Example: Hebrews 1:1-2 — God spoke 'in many times and in many ways' by the prophets, 'but in these last days he has spoken to us by his Son.' The shadow-to-substance pattern of Hebrews 10:1 shows the same logic: the sacrifices were real and God-given, yet provisional pointers to Christ.
Scripture: Hebrews 1:1-2; Hebrews 10:1; Galatians 3:23-25
