Law

Principle
Law
Category
Genre
Summary
The laws of the Torah were given to Israel under the Mosaic covenant, covering worship, justice, and daily life. Christians read them as God's revealed character and wisdom, fulfilled in Christ, not as a code to be re-imposed wholesale.
How To Apply
Ask what a law reveals about God's character, which covenant it belongs to, and how the New Testament handles it. Distinguish enduring moral principles from Israel-specific civil and ceremonial provisions.
Scripture Example
Leviticus 11 and Mark 7:19 — food laws that once marked Israel as set apart are declared clean in the New Testament, showing that the ceremonial boundary has served its purpose in Christ while the call to holiness remains.
Common Mistake
Cherry-picking laws — enforcing one (like a dress or diet rule) while ignoring its neighbors — or else imposing the whole Mosaic code on Christians as if the new covenant had not come.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:1-37; Mark 7:14-23; Acts 10:9-16; Matthew 5:17
