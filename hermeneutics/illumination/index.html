Illumination

Principle
Illumination
Category
Term
Summary
Illumination is the Holy Spirit's work of enabling believers to grasp Scripture's meaning. It is distinct from inspiration (the Spirit giving the text) and from new revelation: illumination opens eyes to what is already written; it does not add words to it.
How To Apply
Pray for illumination and study with diligence together: the Spirit ordinarily works through the text, its languages, its context, and the church's wisdom, not around them. Test any claimed insight by the text itself — the Spirit who inspired Scripture does not contradict it.
Scripture Example
Luke 24:45 — the risen Jesus 'opened their minds to understand the Scriptures.' The disciples already had the texts; what they needed was opened understanding, and that is illumination's pattern ever since.
Common Mistake
Claiming the Spirit's illumination for a meaning that contradicts the text, or the opposite error — bypassing study on the grounds that the Spirit will supply understanding without it.
Scripture
Luke 24:45; 1 Corinthians 2:12-14; Psalm 119:18
