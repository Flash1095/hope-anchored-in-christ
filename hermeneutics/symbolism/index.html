Symbolism

Principle
Symbolism
Category
Device
Summary
Symbols — a lamb, light, a vine, numbers like seven or forty — carry meaning beyond themselves, drawn from earlier Scripture and shared culture. A symbol's meaning comes from its biblical associations, not from a universal codebook.
How To Apply
Trace a symbol through Scripture: where has this image appeared before, and what did it mean there? Let earlier uses inform later ones, and hold interpretations loosely where Scripture itself does not define the symbol.
Scripture Example
Revelation 5:6 — the Lamb 'standing, as though it had been slain' draws on Passover and sacrifice: a victorious Lamb who conquers by being slain. The symbol only opens up when read against Exodus and Isaiah's Servant.
Common Mistake
Assigning fixed codebook meanings to every number or color everywhere ('seven always means perfection, so this verse about seven days means...'), or inventing symbolism Scripture never suggests.
Scripture
Revelation 5:6; Exodus 12:1-13; John 1:29
