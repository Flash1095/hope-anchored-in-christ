Epistle

Principle
Epistle
Category
Genre
Summary
Epistles are letters written to real churches and people facing real situations. Each letter carries an argument that unfolds from greeting to farewell, and its instructions make sense inside that occasion and argument.
How To Apply
Read the whole letter in one sitting before studying single verses. Track the argument: what problem is the author addressing, and how does each section serve the answer? Then carry the principle, not just the occasion, into today.
Scripture Example
Galatians 1:6-9 — Paul's astonishment and warning make sense only inside his argument: some were adding law-keeping to the gospel, and Paul insists that any other gospel is no gospel at all. The verse's force comes from the letter's whole case.
Common Mistake
Lifting a single verse out of a letter's argument — for example, quoting a corrective about one church's disorder as if it were a timeless rule for every church in every age.
Scripture
Galatians 1:6-9; Romans 12:1-21; Philippians 4:1-20
