Wisdom Literature

Principle
Wisdom Literature
Category
Genre
Summary
Proverbs, Job, and Ecclesiastes offer wisdom for living in a complicated world. Proverbs state general principles that usually hold true, not ironclad guarantees; Job and Ecclesiastes wrestle honestly with the exceptions.
How To Apply
Treat proverbs as wise probabilities, not promises: ask what pattern of life they commend. With Job and Ecclesiastes, let the struggle stand — the books refuse easy answers, and so should we.
Scripture Example
Proverbs 22:6 — 'Train up a child in the way he should go' is a principle about formative influence, not a promise that godly parenting always produces faithful children. Reading it as a guarantee burdens faithful parents whose children wander.
Common Mistake
Turning proverbs into guarantees and then blaming people when life does not follow the formula, or quoting Job's comforters as if their speeches were God's verdict.
Scripture
Proverbs 22:6; Job 1:1-2:13; Ecclesiastes 3:1-8
