Original Audience

Principle
Original Audience
Category
Context
Summary
Every biblical book was first addressed to particular people with particular situations, questions, and struggles. Knowing the original audience keeps us from assuming every instruction transfers to us in exactly the same form.
How To Apply
Ask who first received this book and what they were facing. Then distinguish the timeless principle from the historically specific form: the principle carries over, while the form (a garment, a greeting, a local custom) may not.
Scripture Example
1 Corinthians — Paul writes to a fractured church dealing with divisions, lawsuits, and disorder at the Lord's Supper. Reading the letter as pastoral counsel to that church explains its sharpness and its specific instructions far better than treating each line as a timeless rule.
Common Mistake
Assuming every command applies to us identically, so that a first-century church practice or a personal instruction to one individual gets imposed as a universal law.
Scripture
1 Corinthians 1:10-13; Romans 16:16; Revelation 2:1-3:22
