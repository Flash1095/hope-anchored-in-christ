Shepherd

Theme
Shepherd
Summary
God reveals Himself as a Shepherd who seeks, feeds, guides, and protects His sheep, most tenderly in Psalm 23. Israel's leaders were meant to shepherd the flock, and when they failed, God promised to shepherd His people Himself. Jesus fulfils that promise as the Good Shepherd who lays down His life for the sheep.
