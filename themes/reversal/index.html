Reversal

Theme
Reversal
Summary
God delights in reversals: the last become first, the proud are scattered, the humble lifted, the cross becomes a throne. Hannah sang it, Mary sang it, and Jesus lived it: the stone the builders rejected became the cornerstone. Reversal is the gospel's logic: God saves by turning the world's values upside down, so that no one can boast except in the Lord.
