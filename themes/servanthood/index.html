Servanthood

Theme
Servanthood
Summary
Greatness in God's kingdom is measured by service, a pattern set by Jesus who came not to be served but to serve and give His life as a ransom. Isaiah's Suffering Servant, the foot-washing in John 13, and Paul's teaching all call believers to humble, joyful service. The Servant King shows us that stooping to serve is the way up in His kingdom.
