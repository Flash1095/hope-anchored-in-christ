Hope in suffering

Theme
Hope in suffering
Summary
Hope in suffering is not denial but defiance: the conviction that present pain is not the whole story. Paul argues that suffering produces endurance, endurance character, and character hope — a hope that does not disappoint. The resurrection guarantees that suffering has an end and a purpose.
Key Passages
Romans 5:3-5; 1 Peter 1:6-7; Romans 8:18; Job 19:25-27
