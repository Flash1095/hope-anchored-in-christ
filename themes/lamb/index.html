Theme
Lamb
Summary
The Lamb is one of Scripture's richest pictures of Christ: the Passover lamb whose blood sheltered Israel, the lamb provided on Mount Moriah, and Isaiah's suffering servant led like a lamb to the slaughter. John the Baptist announced Jesus as the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world. Revelation crowns the Lamb who was slain as worthy of all worship.
