Peace

Theme
Peace
Summary
Peace — shalom — is wholeness with God, with others, and within ourselves, lost in the Fall and restored through Christ who is our peace. Jesus gives a peace the world cannot give, guarding hearts and minds even in trouble. One day the Prince of Peace will reign, and swords will become ploughshares.
