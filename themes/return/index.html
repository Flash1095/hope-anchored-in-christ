Return
Summary
Return is the Bible's word for repentance: turning back from the far country to the Father, from idols to the living God. The prodigal's return is the pattern: a long walk home met by a running father. God does not merely permit return; he pleads for it, promises to receive it, and rejoices over every sinner who turns.
Key Passages
Deuteronomy 30:2-3; Luke 15:20-24; James 4:8; Acts 3:19
