God's wrath and refuge

Theme
God's wrath and refuge
Summary
Scripture holds two truths together: God is wrathful toward evil, and He is a refuge for those who flee to Him. The flood, the Passover, and the cross all show the same pattern — judgment falls, and a shelter is provided. To take refuge in God is to be safe from the very wrath He pours out.
Key Passages
Psalm 46:1-3; Nahum 1:7; Romans 1:18; John 3:36
