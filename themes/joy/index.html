Theme
Joy
Summary
Joy in Scripture is deeper than happiness, rooted in who God is and what He has done, so it can endure even in sorrow and suffering. The Psalms sing it, Jesus promised His joy to His disciples, and joy is a fruit of the Spirit in every believer. The joy of the Lord is our strength.
