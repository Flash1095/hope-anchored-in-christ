Theme
Rebuilding
Summary
Rebuilding is God's repeated work among ruins: after judgment, after exile, after failure, he gathers what is broken and builds again. Nehemiah rebuilt a wall, Zerubbabel a temple, and God himself rebuilds ruined lives, raising them into a holy temple in the Lord. The Bible's rebuilding stories teach us not to despise small beginnings, for God delights to start again with what the world calls wreckage.
