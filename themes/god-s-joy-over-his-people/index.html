God's joy over His people

Theme
God's joy over His people
Summary
Zephaniah dares to say God rejoices over His people with singing. This is not a distant, reluctant love but a Father's delight in His restored children. Even after exile and failure, God celebrates His people's return — a joy the prodigal's father echoes when his lost son comes home.
