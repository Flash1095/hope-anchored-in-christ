Prayer
Summary
Prayer runs through Scripture as God's people cry out, intercede, give thanks, and listen — from Abraham pleading for Sodom to Hannah's tears to Daniel's open window. Jesus taught His disciples to pray to their Father with simple trust, and He Himself prayed through the night and on the cross. Because of Christ, we can approach God's throne of grace with confidence.
