Theme
Salvation
Summary
Salvation is God's rescue of sinners from sin, death, and judgment, accomplished by grace through faith in Jesus Christ. The Old Testament celebrates God as Saviour who delivers His people, and the New Testament proclaims that same salvation to all who believe. It is past, present, and future: we have been saved, are being saved, and will be saved.
