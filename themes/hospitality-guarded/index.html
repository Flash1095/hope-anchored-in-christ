Theme
Hospitality guarded
Summary
Hospitality must be guarded as well as generous: Scripture warns against welcoming deceivers and false teachers into the church's trust. Discernment is not the opposite of love but its protection — testing what is taught while keeping the door open to genuine need. Wise welcome loves both truth and neighbor.
