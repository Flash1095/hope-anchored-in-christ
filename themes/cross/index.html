Theme
Cross
Summary
The cross is the centre of the Christian faith, where Jesus bore our sin, defeated the powers of evil, and reconciled us to God. What looked like shame and defeat, God turned into wisdom, power, and salvation for all who believe. Believers take up their cross daily, boasting only in the cross of Christ.
