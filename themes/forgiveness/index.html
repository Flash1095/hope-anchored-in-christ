Theme
Forgiveness
Summary
Forgiveness is at the centre of God's dealings with us: Joseph forgave his brothers, David pleaded for pardon, and Jesus forgave sinners and prayed for His executioners. On the cross, God provided the basis on which sins can be fully and justly forgiven. Having been forgiven so much, we are commanded to forgive others from the heart.
