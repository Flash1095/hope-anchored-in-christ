Theme
Cycles of sin
Summary
Judges tells a grim cycle: sin, oppression, crying out, deliverance, and sin again. Each round shows that Israel's problem was not bad leaders but a wandering heart. The cycle only breaks when a true King comes who can change hearts from within.

Judges 2:16-19; Judges 21:25; Psalm 106:34-46; Romans 7:15-20
