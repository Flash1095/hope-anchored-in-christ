Theme
Messiah and King
Summary
Israel waited for a Messiah: an anointed king from David's line who would defeat God's enemies and reign in righteousness. Jesus is that King, though his crown came by a cross and his throne by resurrection. He reigns now over all things for the good of his church, and one day every knee will bow to him.
Key Passages
2 Samuel 7:12-16; Psalm 2:1-12; Isaiah 9:6-7; Philippians 2:9-11
