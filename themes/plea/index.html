Theme
Plea
Summary
A plea is prayer at its most honest: an urgent cry for mercy, help, or deliverance when we have nothing left to offer. The psalms are full of pleas: How long, O Lord? And Jesus honored the blind beggar's plea, Son of David, have mercy on me. God does not despise a pleading heart; he bends his ear to it, and often the plea itself is the beginning of faith.

Psalm 13:1-2; Psalm 130:1-2; Mark 10:46-52; Luke 18:1-8
