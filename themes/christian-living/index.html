Christian living
Summary
Christian living is ordinary life offered to God: work, family, neighbors, and rest all shaped by the gospel. Paul sums it up as presenting our bodies as living sacrifices and being transformed by renewed minds. It is not perfection but direction — a life increasingly conformed to Christ.
