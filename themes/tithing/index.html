Tithing

Theme
Tithing
Summary
Tithing is giving the first tenth to God, a practice that taught Israel to trust the Lord as provider and to fund the work of worship and the care of the poor. Abraham gave a tenth to Melchizedek and Jacob vowed one long before the law commanded it. Under the new covenant, the tenth remains a wise starting point, but the deeper principle is larger still: generous, cheerful, firstfruits giving from hearts that know everything belongs to God.
