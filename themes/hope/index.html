Hope
Summary
Biblical hope is not wishful thinking but a confident expectation anchored in God's promises and in Christ's resurrection. It held Abraham, sustained Israel in exile, and carries believers through suffering today. Hebrews calls this hope an anchor for the soul, firm and secure — the anchor at the heart of this blog.
