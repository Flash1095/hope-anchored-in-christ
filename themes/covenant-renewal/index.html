Theme
Covenant renewal
Summary
Again and again God's people returned to renew their covenant: at Shechem under Joshua, under Josiah after the Law was found, and under Nehemiah after exile. Renewal meant hearing God's word, confessing failure, and recommitting to obey. Every generation needs its own renewal, for faith is never inherited automatically.
