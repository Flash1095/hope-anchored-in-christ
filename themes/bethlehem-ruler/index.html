Bethlehem ruler

Theme
Bethlehem ruler
Summary
Long before Jesus was born, Micah promised that a ruler would come from little Bethlehem, one whose origins are from ancient days. Matthew sees that promise fulfilled when Jesus is born in David's town, the shepherd-king who would rule not by force but by laying down His life. The smallness of Bethlehem is the point: God's greatest King came in the humblest way.
