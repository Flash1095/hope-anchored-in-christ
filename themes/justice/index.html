Theme
Justice
Summary
God loves justice and hates oppression, commanding His people to defend the widow, the orphan, the foreigner, and the poor. The prophets thundered against injustice when Israel grew comfortable and careless. At the cross, God's justice and mercy meet, and His coming kingdom will set every wrong right at last.
