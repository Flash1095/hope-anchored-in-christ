Theme
Prophets
Summary
The prophets were men and women God called to speak his word into their times: Moses the lawgiver, Samuel the kingmaker, Elijah the firebrand, Isaiah the seer, and John the Baptist, the last and greatest of the old order. They comforted the afflicted and afflicted the comfortable, always pointing beyond themselves to the coming Messiah. Their books still preach, because the God who spoke through them has not stopped speaking.
