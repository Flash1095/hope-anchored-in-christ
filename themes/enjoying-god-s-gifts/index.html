Theme
Enjoying God's gifts
Summary
God's gifts are meant to be enjoyed, not merely endured: food, friendship, beauty, rest, and laughter are given by a generous Father. Scripture warns against both idolatry and ingratitude, teaching us to receive good things with thanks and open hands. Enjoyment with thanksgiving is itself a form of worship.
