Theme
Marriage covenant
Summary
Marriage in Scripture is a covenant, not merely a contract: a solemn, lifelong bond in which husband and wife become one flesh and mirror God's faithful love for his people. Genesis gives its pattern, the prophets use it to picture God's covenant with Israel, and Paul lifts it higher still as a picture of Christ and the church. Guarding marriage therefore guards a living sermon about God's keeping love.
Key Passages
Genesis 2:24; Malachi 2:14-15; Matthew 19:3-9; Ephesians 5:25-33
