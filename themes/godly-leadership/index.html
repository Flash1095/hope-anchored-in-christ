Godly leadership

Theme
Godly leadership
Summary
Godly leadership is shepherding, not dominating: feeding, guiding, and protecting those entrusted to your care. Scripture warns leaders against pride and greed, and holds up Moses, Nehemiah, and above all Christ, who led by serving. The measure of a leader is the flourishing of those led.
Key Passages
1 Peter 5:2-3; Nehemiah 5:14-19; Mark 10:42-45; Exodus 18:21
