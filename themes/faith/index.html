Theme
Faith
Summary
Faith is trusting God and taking Him at His word, exemplified by Abraham who believed God and it was credited to him as righteousness. Hebrews 11 celebrates ordinary men and women who lived, suffered, and died trusting promises they had not yet seen fulfilled. Faith is not wishful thinking; it rests on the faithful character of God Himself.
