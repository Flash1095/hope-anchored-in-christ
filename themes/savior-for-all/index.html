Savior for all

Theme
Savior for all
Summary
The good news is not for one tribe or nation: Simeon called Jesus a light for the Gentiles, the angels announced joy for all people, and Revelation pictures a multitude from every nation before the throne. God desires all people to be saved, and the church's mission flows from that breadth: a Savior for all means a gospel for all.
