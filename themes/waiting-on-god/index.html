Waiting on God

Theme
Waiting on God
Summary
Waiting on God is active trust, not passive resignation: the psalmist waits more than watchmen for the morning, Isaiah promises new strength to those who wait, and the church waited ten days in prayer before Pentecost. Waiting purifies our motives, deepens our dependence, and teaches us that God's timing is part of his goodness. Those who wait on the Lord will not be put to shame.
Key Passages
Psalm 27:14; Psalm 130:5-6; Isaiah 40:31; Acts 1:4-5
