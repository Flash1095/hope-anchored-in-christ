Theme
Apostasy
Summary
Apostasy is the sober reality of turning away from the faith once embraced. Scripture warns plainly that some will abandon the truth, exchanging it for error or for the world's approval, and it calls believers to hold fast and to watch over one another. The warnings are given not to frighten the faithful, but to keep them faithful.
