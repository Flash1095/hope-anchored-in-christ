Endurance
Summary
Endurance is staying faithful when faith is costly — under trial, persecution, or long waiting. Hebrews pictures believers surrounded by witnesses, laying aside weights and looking to Jesus, who endured the cross. Endurance is not grim stoicism but hope-fueled perseverance to the finish.
