Theme
Acceptance
Summary
To be accepted is to be welcomed as you are, and that is exactly how God receives those who come to Him through Christ. Jesus welcomed sinners, outcasts, and strangers, and Paul urges believers to accept one another just as Christ accepted them. Christian acceptance never means approving everything, but it does mean no one is beyond the reach of God's welcome.
