Theme
Knowledge of Christ
Summary
Knowledge of Christ is not mere information about Him but a living acquaintance that transforms. Paul counted everything loss beside knowing Christ, and Peter urges believers to grow in that knowledge. To know Him is eternal life, and it deepens through Scripture, prayer, and obedience.

Philippians 3:8-10; John 17:3; 2 Peter 1:2-3; Ephesians 3:18-19
