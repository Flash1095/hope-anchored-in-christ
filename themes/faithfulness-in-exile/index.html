Theme
Faithfulness in exile
Summary
Exile tests faith: far from home, temple, and familiar worship, God's people had to decide whether to compromise or remain faithful. Daniel, Esther, and Mordecai show different faces of that faithfulness — quiet integrity, courageous risk, and prayerful trust. Their stories teach believers living as strangers in a foreign culture how to stand firm without losing love.

Daniel 1:8-17; Daniel 6:10-23; Jeremiah 29:4-7; Esther 4:14-16
