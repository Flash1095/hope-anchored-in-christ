Visions
Summary
Visions are one way God has spoken: Jacob's ladder, Isaiah's throne room, Ezekiel's valley of dry bones, John's apocalypse. They come in pictures because some truths are too large for plain prose, and they always serve God's purposes: calling, comforting, or commissioning his people. Visions must be tested by Scripture and by their fruit, for not every dazzling sight is from God, but the true ones have steadied his people for millennia.
