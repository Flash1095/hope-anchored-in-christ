Theme
Sacrifice
Summary
From the first offerings in Genesis to the Passover lamb and the daily temple sacrifices, Scripture teaches that sin costs life and that God Himself provides the substitute. All those sacrifices point forward to Jesus, the Lamb of God who offered Himself once for all. Because His sacrifice is complete, we can draw near to God with confidence.
