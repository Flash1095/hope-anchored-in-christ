Theme
Mercy
Summary
Mercy is God's compassion toward those who deserve judgment, shown when He spared Israel again and again and supremely when He gave His Son. Jesus embodied mercy, welcoming sinners, healing the broken, and forgiving even those who crucified Him. Because we have received mercy, we are called to be merciful to others.
