Refuge

Theme
Refuge
Summary
When trouble comes, Scripture invites us to run to God as to a fortress, a shelter, a hiding place. The psalmists call him refuge in war, in grief, in guilt, and in old age: not a distant idea but a present help. To take refuge in God is to trust his character more than our circumstances, and to hide in him until the storm passes.
Key Passages
Psalm 46:1-3; Psalm 91:1-4; Proverbs 18:10; Hebrews 6:19-20
