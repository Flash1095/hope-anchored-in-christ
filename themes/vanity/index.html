Vanity

Theme
Vanity
Summary
Vanity is the emptiness Ecclesiastes names: chasing wealth, pleasure, wisdom, or fame apart from God leaves the hands full of wind. The Preacher tried everything and found it vapor: beautiful, brief, and unable to satisfy. Vanity is not a counsel of despair but a diagnosis: only when we stop expecting the world to give us meaning can we receive life as God's gift and enjoy it without worshipping it.
