Unfaithfulness

Theme
Unfaithfulness
Summary
Unfaithfulness is breaking trust with God: Israel's idolatry is pictured as adultery, and Hosea's marriage became a living parable of a people who kept wandering from their faithful Husband. Scripture names unfaithfulness plainly because it wounds real relationship. Yet it never ends there, for God remains faithful even when we are faithless, and calls his people back with cords of love.

Hosea 1:2; Hosea 2:19-20; Jeremiah 3:12-14; 2 Timothy 2:13
