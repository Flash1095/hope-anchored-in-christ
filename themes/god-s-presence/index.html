Theme
God's presence
Summary
God's presence is His nearness to His people: in the burning bush, in the tabernacle, in Christ Immanuel, and now by His Spirit. It is both comfort and challenge — the presence that guides, corrects, and never leaves. Heaven itself is described as God dwelling with His people forever.
Key Passages
Exodus 33:14-15; Psalm 139:7-10; Matthew 28:20; Revelation 21:3
