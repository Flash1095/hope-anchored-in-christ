Humble remnant
Summary
When the nation fell, God preserved a humble remnant — a small, faithful few who still trusted Him. Elijah thought he was alone; God reminded him of seven thousand who had not bowed. The remnant principle runs through Scripture: God often works through the small and the overlooked.
