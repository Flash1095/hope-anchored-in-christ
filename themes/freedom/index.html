Theme
Freedom
Summary
Freedom in Scripture begins with liberation: Israel freed from Egypt, sinners freed from sin's power. Christ sets people free not to live for themselves but to live for God and neighbor in love. True freedom is not doing whatever we want, but being free at last to do what is right.

John 8:31-36; Galatians 5:1; Romans 8:1-2; Exodus 14:13-14
