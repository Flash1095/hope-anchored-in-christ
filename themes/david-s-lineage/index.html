Theme
David's lineage
Summary
From Ruth and Boaz through Jesse to David, and from David to Jesus, Scripture traces a family line held together by God's promise. The lineage includes outsiders, failures, and kings, showing that God's plan runs through real human history. Matthew opens his Gospel with this genealogy to announce: the promised King has arrived.

Ruth 4:17-22; 2 Samuel 7:12-16; Matthew 1:1-17; Luke 3:23-38
