Kingdom of God

Theme
Kingdom of God
Summary
The Kingdom of God is God's righteous reign, promised in the Old Testament and announced by Jesus as near in His own ministry. It grows like a mustard seed, turns worldly values upside down, and will one day fill the earth when Christ returns. To seek first His kingdom is to live now by the values of the age to come.
