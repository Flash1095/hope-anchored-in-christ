Covenant
Summary
God binds Himself to His people with solemn promises, from Noah and Abraham through Moses and David to the new covenant in Christ's blood. Each covenant reveals more of His faithful character and His plan to bless the nations. In Jesus, every covenant promise finds its yes and its fulfilment.
