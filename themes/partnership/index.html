Theme
Partnership
Summary
God's work has always been shared work: Paul and Barnabas, Priscilla and Aquila, Paul and Timothy, partners in the gospel bound by affection, trust, and a common mission. Partnership is more than efficiency; it is the shape of the body of Christ, where gifts differ and burdens are carried together. Even the strongest leaders in Scripture leaned on companions, reminding us that ministry done alone is ministry done wrong.
