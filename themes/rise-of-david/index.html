Theme
Rise of David
Summary
David rose from shepherd boy to king: anointed in obscurity, tested in the wilderness, crowned after years of waiting and war. His rise shows God's pattern of choosing the overlooked and preparing leaders in hidden places. Yet David's story also warns us: the king after God's own heart fell grievously, and his greater Son would be the King who never falls.
