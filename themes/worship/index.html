Theme
Worship
Summary
Worship is God's people ascribing worth to Him in spirit and in truth, in song, prayer, obedience, and everyday life offered as a living sacrifice. From Abraham's altars to the Psalms to the heavenly throne room in Revelation, worship is heaven's activity breaking into earth. True worship centres on who God is and what He has done in Christ.
