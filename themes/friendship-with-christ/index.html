Friendship with Christ
Summary
Jesus startled His disciples by calling them friends, not merely servants. Friendship with Christ means knowing His mind, sharing His purposes, and enjoying His presence — a relationship of love, not just duty. He proved that friendship by laying down His life for His friends.
