Unity

Theme
Unity
Summary
Unity is the church's witness: Jesus prayed that his people would be one as he and the Father are one, so that the world would believe. Paul pleaded for one mind, one Spirit, one body: not uniformity, for gifts differ, but a deep oneness rooted in one Lord, one faith, one baptism. Unity is kept by humility, gentleness, and forgiveness, and it is broken most often by pride.
