Theme
Good works
Summary
Good works do not save, but the saved do good works: they are the fruit of faith, prepared by God for His people to walk in. From the Good Samaritan's mercy to simple daily kindness, good works make the gospel visible. Faith without works is dead; works without faith are empty.
