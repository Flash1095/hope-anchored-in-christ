Remembering

Theme
Remembering
Summary
Remembering is a spiritual discipline in Scripture, not mere nostalgia: Israel was commanded to remember the Exodus, to retell it to their children, and to set up stones so they would not forget. Forgetting God is the first step toward idolatry; remembering his deeds is the anchor of faith in every new crisis. The Lord's Supper is remembering made tangible: bread and cup that preach the gospel to our forgetful hearts.
