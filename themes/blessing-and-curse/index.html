Theme
Blessing and curse
Summary
At the threshold of the Promised Land, Moses set before Israel two paths: blessing for obedience and curse for rebellion. The choice was stark because the covenant was serious — God's people could not drift into faithfulness. Yet the story does not end with curse, for Christ bore the curse so that the blessing of Abraham might come to all who believe.
