Coming King
Summary
Israel waited for a King who would reign in righteousness, and the prophets pictured Him riding humbly into Jerusalem. Jesus entered the city to acclaim, yet His crown would be thorns before it was glory. He is the Coming King who will return to reign forever, and His kingdom is already breaking into the world.
