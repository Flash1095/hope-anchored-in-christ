John Bunyan

Name
John Bunyan
Type
Figure
Era
17th Century
Summary
English tinker turned Baptist preacher who spent twelve years in Bedford jail for preaching without a licence and wrote The Pilgrim's Progress there, following Christian from the City of Destruction to the Celestial City.
Contribution
Gave English-speaking Christians their most-read allegory of the Christian life, and a model of perseverance under persecution.
Key Works
The Pilgrim's Progress; Grace Abounding to the Chief of Sinners
Testimony
He testified that a poor, unlicensed preacher could trust Christ through prison and poverty, and wrote his own conversion story as proof.
Related Topics
Pilgrimage; Perseverance
