Summary
From Herrnhut, Count Zinzendorf's Moravians launched the modern missions movement, sending missionaries - some selling themselves into slavery to reach slaves - to the Caribbean, Greenland, and beyond.
Contribution
Pioneered Protestant world missions with sacrificial, prayer-soaked sending.
