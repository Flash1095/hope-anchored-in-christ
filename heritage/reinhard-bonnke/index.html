Reinhard Bonnke

Name
Reinhard Bonnke
Type
Figure
Era
20th-21st Century
Summary
German Pentecostal evangelist (1940-2019) who founded Christ for All Nations and held mass crusades across Africa for decades.
Contribution
His gospel crusades across Africa drew millions and helped shape African Pentecostal evangelism, making him one of the most influential evangelists on the continent.
