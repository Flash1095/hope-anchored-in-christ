John of the Cross

Name
John of the Cross
Type
Figure
Era
16th Century
Summary
Spanish Carmelite friar, priest, and poet (1542-1591) who, with Teresa of Avila, reformed the Carmelite order and wrote the classic poetry of the soul's journey to God.
Contribution
He helped found the Discalced Carmelites, endured imprisonment by opponents of the reform, and wrote poems and commentaries on the dark night of the soul that have guided Christian prayer and mysticism ever since.
Key Works
Dark Night of the Soul; Spiritual Canticle
Testimony
Imprisoned and beaten for his part in the reform, he wrote some of his greatest poetry in a cell, trusting God in darkness.
