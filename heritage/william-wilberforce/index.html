William Wilberforce

Name
William Wilberforce
Type
Figure
Era
18th-19th Century
Summary
British parliamentarian converted to evangelical faith who spent forty years fighting the slave trade. His faith turned politics into a calling, and in 1807 Parliament abolished the trade; slavery itself followed in 1833, days before his death.
Contribution
Led the abolition of the British slave trade and slavery, proving Christian conviction can change a nation's laws.
Key Works
A Practical View of Christianity
Testimony
His testimony was perseverance: a Christian conscience that refused to quit until an unjust system fell.
Scripture
Amos 5:24
Related Topics
Justice; Abolition; Compassion
Related Books
Amos; Isaiah
