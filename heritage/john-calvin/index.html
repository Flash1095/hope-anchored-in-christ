John Calvin

Name
John Calvin
Type
Figure
Era
16th Century
Summary
French reformer whose Institutes became Protestantism's most influential systematic theology. From Geneva he trained pastors, shaped Reformed worship, and stressed God's sovereignty and the authority of Scripture.
Contribution
Systematized Reformed theology, modeled disciplined church life, and spread the Reformation through trained ministers.
Key Works
Institutes of the Christian Religion; Commentaries on Scripture
Testimony
A shy scholar who never sought fame, Calvin's life testified to a single aim: that God be glorified in church, city, and home.
Scripture
Romans 11:36
Related Topics
Sovereignty; Reformation; Scripture
Related Books
Romans; Ephesians
