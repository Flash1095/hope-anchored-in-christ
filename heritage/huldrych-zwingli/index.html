Huldrych Zwingli

Name
Huldrych Zwingli
Type
Figure
Era
16th Century
Summary
Swiss priest and reformer (1484-1531) who led the Reformation in Zurich, preaching directly from Scripture and removing practices he found without biblical warrant.
Contribution
He gave the Swiss Reformation its shape: preaching through whole books of the Bible, reforming worship and church order in Zurich, and defending his reforms in public disputations. His theology of the Lord's Supper as memorial, differing from Luther's, shaped the Reformed tradition that followed.
Key Works
The Sixty-Seven Articles; Commentary on True and False Religion
Testimony
He died as a chaplain on the battlefield at Kappel, having tied his reforming work to the life of the city he served.
