Martyn Lloyd-Jones

Name
Martyn Lloyd-Jones
Type
Figure
Era
20th Century
Summary
Welsh preacher and physician (1899-1981) who ministered at Westminster Chapel in London for three decades.
Contribution
His long ministry at Westminster Chapel, London, and his expository preaching, especially on the Sermon on the Mount, shaped evangelical preaching across Britain and beyond.
Key Works
Studies in the Sermon on the Mount
