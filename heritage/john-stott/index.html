John Stott

English Anglican clergyman (1921-2011) known for clear biblical preaching and for leadership in global evangelicalism.

Contribution

As rector of All Souls, London, and a leader of the Lausanne movement, he shaped evangelical preaching, biblical exposition, and world mission, and his books remain standard introductions to Christian faith.
