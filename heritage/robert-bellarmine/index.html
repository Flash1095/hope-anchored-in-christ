Robert Bellarmine

Name
Robert Bellarmine
Type
Figure
Era
16th-17th Century
Summary
Italian Jesuit cardinal and theologian (1542-1621) who became the Catholic Church's foremost controversialist of the Counter-Reformation.
Contribution
His massive Disputationes answered Protestant arguments point by point and became the standard Catholic textbook of controversy, shaping Catholic teaching and seminary training for centuries. He also wrote a widely used catechism for ordinary believers.
Key Works
Disputationes de Controversiis Christianae Fidei
Testimony
Though a leading figure at Rome, he was known for a simple personal life and for pastoral writing aimed at ordinary Christians.
