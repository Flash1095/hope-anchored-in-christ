Ignatius of Loyola

Name
Ignatius of Loyola
Type
Figure
Era
16th Century
Summary
Spanish soldier turned priest (1491-1556) who founded the Society of Jesus, the Jesuits, after a conversion during a long recovery from battle wounds.
Contribution
He founded a new order devoted to education, missions, and service to the pope, and his Spiritual Exercises gave the church a structured method of prayer and discernment that spread far beyond his order.
Key Works
Spiritual Exercises
Testimony
After his conversion he gave up soldiering, lived as a pilgrim, studied for the priesthood late in life, and directed the Jesuits' first missions and schools.
