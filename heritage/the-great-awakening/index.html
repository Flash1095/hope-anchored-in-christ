The Great Awakening

The colonial American revival of the 1730s-40s, preached by Edwards, Whitefield, and the Wesleys, that awakened nominal churches, converted thousands, and seeded American evangelicalism.

Contribution

Revived personal conversion and heartfelt religion across the colonies.

Testimony

The awakened testified in tears and transformed lives: religion of the heart, not mere form.
