Council of Chalcedon

The council that defined Christ as one person in two natures, fully God and fully man, 'without confusion, without change, without division, without separation.' It remains the church's boundary for orthodox Christology.

Contribution

Guarded both Christ's divinity and humanity against one-sided errors.

Testimony

The council fathers' testimony: precision about Christ protects the Gospel itself.
