Fanny Crosby

Name
Fanny Crosby
Type
Figure
Era
19th-20th Century
Summary
American hymn writer, blind from infancy, who wrote more than eight thousand hymns, including 'Blessed Assurance', 'Pass Me Not, O Gentle Saviour', and 'To God Be the Glory', many set to tunes by her collaborators.
Contribution
Became the Salvation Army's first great hymn writer in song, writing thousands of hymns that carried the Gospel in plain, singable words.
Key Works
Hymns including 'Blessed Assurance' and 'To God Be the Glory'
Testimony
She testified that blindness was a gift, saying she would not exchange it, since it kept her eyes fixed on things unseen.

Worship; Faith
