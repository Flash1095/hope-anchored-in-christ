Name
Monica
Type
Figure
Era
4th Century
Summary
A fourth-century North African Christian woman, mother of Augustine of Hippo, known through her son's Confessions.
Contribution
Her patient prayers and counsel over many years accompanied Augustine's long journey from a wayward youth to Christian faith and baptism, making her an enduring example of persevering intercession for a child.
Testimony
Prayed for her son's conversion for years, wept over his wanderings, and lived to see him baptised before her death.
