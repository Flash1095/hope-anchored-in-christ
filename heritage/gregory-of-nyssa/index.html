Gregory of Nyssa

Name
Gregory of Nyssa
Type
Figure
Era
4th Century
Summary
Bishop of Nyssa in Cappadocia in the fourth century, younger brother of Basil of Caesarea and a profound mystical and doctrinal theologian.
Contribution
His writings on the Trinity, the Christian life as unending growth toward God, and the interpretation of Scripture deepened Eastern theology, and his account of his sister Macrina preserved one of the early church's most remarkable spiritual biographies.
Key Works
Life of Moses; On the Making of Man; Great Catechetical Oration
