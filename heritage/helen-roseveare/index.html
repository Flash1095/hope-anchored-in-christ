Helen Roseveare

Name
Helen Roseveare
Type
Figure
Era
20th-21st Century
Summary
English missionary doctor (1925-2016) who served with the Worldwide Evangelization Crusade in Congo, surviving capture and assault during the Simba rebellion.
Contribution
As a medical missionary in Congo she served through war and hardship, wrote honestly about suffering and God's faithfulness, and became a model of endurance for later missionaries.
