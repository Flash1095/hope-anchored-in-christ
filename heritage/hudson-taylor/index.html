Hudson Taylor

Name
Hudson Taylor
Type
Figure
Era
19th Century
Summary
English missionary who first sailed to China in 1853, founded the China Inland Mission in 1865, and sent hundreds of workers into China's interior provinces, wearing Chinese dress and living by faith support.
Contribution
Founded the China Inland Mission, pioneered inland mission work in China, and insisted on Chinese dress, faith support, and interdenominational sending.
Key Works
China's Spiritual Need and Claims; A Retrospect
Testimony
He testified that God's work done in God's way will never lack God's supply, and lived without guaranteed salary for decades.
Related Topics
Missions; Faith; Prayer
