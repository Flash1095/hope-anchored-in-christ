Hannah More

Name
Hannah More
Type
Figure
Era
18th-19th Century
Summary
English writer and philanthropist of the Clapham Sect who wrote bestselling moral tracts for the poor, founded schools in the Mendip Hills, and supported Wilberforce's abolition campaign.
Contribution
Used cheap tracts, Sunday schools, and education for poor girls to spread evangelical faith and morals, and campaigned against the slave trade.
Key Works
Cheap Repository Tracts; Strictures on the Modern System of Female Education
Testimony
She testified that a Christian woman's pen could serve the poor and the enslaved as surely as a pulpit could.
Related Topics
Abolition
