Bede

Name
Bede
Type
Figure
Era
7th-8th Century
Summary
Northumbrian monk of Wearmouth-Jarrow, scholar of Scripture, history and the reckoning of time.
Contribution
His Ecclesiastical History of the English People preserved the story of the early English church, and his biblical commentaries and work on the date of Easter shaped Christian learning for centuries.
Key Works
Ecclesiastical History of the English People
Testimony
He spent his whole adult life in his monastery in study, teaching and prayer, and said he delighted always in learning, teaching or writing.
