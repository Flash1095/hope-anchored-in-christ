J. I. Packer

Name
J. I. Packer
Type
Figure
Era
20th-21st Century
Summary
English-born Canadian theologian (1926-2020) who taught at Regent College, Vancouver, and wrote widely for ordinary readers as well as scholars.
Contribution
His book Knowing God became a classic introduction to Reformed evangelical theology, and his teaching on Scripture, the Puritans, and the Christian life shaped evangelical churches worldwide.
