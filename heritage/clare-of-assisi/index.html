Clare of Assisi

Name
Clare of Assisi
Type
Figure
Era
13th Century
Summary
Noblewoman of Assisi who followed Francis, founded the Poor Clares, and lived in enclosed poverty at San Damiano.
Contribution
She created a contemplative community for women on Franciscan principles and was the first woman to write a monastic rule, insisting on the privilege of poverty against pressure to accept property and income.
Key Works
Rule of Saint Clare
Testimony
She fled her family home to join Francis and held fast to a life of poverty and prayer to the end of her life.
