Hildegard of Bingen

Name
Hildegard of Bingen
Type
Figure
Era
12th Century
Summary
Benedictine abbess, visionary, composer and writer on nature and medicine in the Rhineland.
Contribution
Her recorded visions, her founding of the Rupertsberg community, her music and her letters of counsel to popes, emperors and bishops made her one of the most influential women voices of the medieval church.
Key Works
Scivias; Book of Divine Works
Testimony
She reported visions from childhood and, after long hesitation, obeyed what she understood as God's command to write them down.
