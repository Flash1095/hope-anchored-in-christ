Augustine of Canterbury

Name
Augustine of Canterbury
Type
Figure
Era
6th-7th Century
Summary
Italian monk sent by Gregory the Great in 597 to preach the gospel in the Anglo-Saxon kingdom of Kent.
Contribution
He baptised King Aethelberht, became the first Archbishop of Canterbury, and planted the Roman mission in England on which the later English church was built.
Testimony
He set out fearfully for a land he did not know and persisted with the mission after Gregory urged him not to turn back.
