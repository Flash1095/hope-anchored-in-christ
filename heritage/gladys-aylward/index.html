Gladys Aylward

Name
Gladys Aylward
Type
Figure
Era
20th Century
Summary
English missionary (1902-1970) who travelled alone to China, worked in Shanxi province, and later cared for orphans in Taiwan.
Contribution
As a missionary in inland China she led children to safety during the Japanese invasion, an act of courage that made her one of the best-known women missionaries of the twentieth century.
