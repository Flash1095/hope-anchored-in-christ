Francis Asbury

Name
Francis Asbury
Type
Figure
Era
18th-19th Century
Summary
English-born Methodist bishop who crossed the Atlantic in 1771 and rode an estimated 300,000 miles on horseback, preaching and organising the American frontier church until his death.
Contribution
Built American Methodism from a handful of societies into a continent-wide church through circuit riding and disciplined organisation.
Key Works
Journal of Francis Asbury
Testimony
He testified to a calling that kept him travelling, preaching, and planting churches until he could ride no more.
