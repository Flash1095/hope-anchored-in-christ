C. S. Lewis

Name
C. S. Lewis
Type
Figure
Era
20th Century
Summary
Oxford scholar and former atheist whose conversion, chronicled in Surprised by Joy, made him the twentieth century's most beloved Christian writer. Through Narnia and plain-spoken apologetics he led millions to faith.
Contribution
Defended the faith with reason and imagination, making Christianity credible and beautiful to ordinary readers.
Key Works
Mere Christianity; The Chronicles of Narnia; Surprised by Joy
Testimony
His testimony was joy itself: a longing nothing in this world satisfied, finally answered by the God who made him for Himself.
Scripture
Ecclesiastes 3:11
Related Topics
Imagination; Apologetics; Conversion
Related Books
Ecclesiastes; Romans
