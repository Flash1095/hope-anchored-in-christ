The Modern Missions Movement

Name
The Modern Missions Movement
Type
Movement
Era
18th-19th Century
Summary
Launched by William Carey's 1793 voyage to India, the movement sent thousands of missionaries worldwide, translated the Bible into hundreds of languages, and built schools and hospitals across continents.
Contribution
Carried the Gospel, literacy, and medicine to peoples who had never heard.
Key Works
Carey's Enquiry; Missionary Translations
Testimony
Carey's testimony - 'Expect great things from God; attempt great things for God' - became missions' motto.
Scripture
Matthew 28:19-20
Related Topics
Missions; Translation; Evangelism
Related Books
Matthew; Acts
