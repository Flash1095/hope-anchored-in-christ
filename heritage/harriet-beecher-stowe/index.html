Harriet Beecher Stowe

Name
Harriet Beecher Stowe
Type
Figure
Era
19th Century
Summary
American author and daughter of preacher Lyman Beecher whose 1852 novel, written from evangelical conviction that slavery was a sin, became the century's bestselling novel and a weapon of the abolition cause.
Contribution
Used the novel Uncle Tom's Cabin to turn American opinion against slavery, making Christian abolition a household cause.
Key Works
Uncle Tom's Cabin; A Key to Uncle Tom's Cabin
Testimony
She testified that she wrote the book as God gave it to her, believing slavery contradicted the Gospel she had been raised on.
Related Topics
Abolition; Justice; Compassion
