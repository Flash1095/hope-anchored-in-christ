Thomas Clarkson

Name
Thomas Clarkson
Type
Figure
Era
18th-19th Century
Summary
English abolitionist who gathered first-hand evidence of the slave trade, campaigned for fifty years, and worked alongside Wilberforce in Parliament while mobilising public opinion outside it.
Contribution
Supplied the abolition movement with its evidence, organising testimony and research that made the slave trade's horrors undeniable in Parliament.
Key Works
The History of the Rise, Progress, and Accomplishment of the Abolition of the African Slave-Trade
Testimony
He testified that Christian conviction demanded facts as well as feeling, and spent a lifetime collecting them.
Related Topics
Abolition; Justice
