Macrina the Younger

Name
Macrina the Younger
Type
Figure
Era
4th Century
Summary
A fourth-century Cappadocian woman, eldest sister of Basil of Caesarea and Gregory of Nyssa, who led her family's household into a life of communal prayer and simplicity.
Contribution
Her ascetic household became a model for Christian community life, and her teaching, preserved in her brother Gregory's writings, influenced Cappadocian thought on the soul, death, and resurrection.
Testimony
Turned her family home into a monastic community and faced her own death with calm Christian hope.
