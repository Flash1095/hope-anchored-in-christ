The Reformation

The movement sparked by Luther's 1517 theses that recovered justification by faith, Scripture's authority, and the priesthood of all believers. It reshaped Europe's churches, schools, and nations.

Contribution

Restored the Gospel of grace to the church's center and put the Bible in the people's language.

Testimony

The reformers' testimony was conscience captive to God's Word: here they stood, and could do no other.
