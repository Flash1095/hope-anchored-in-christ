Name

Type

Summary
American Reformed theologian and pastor (1939-2017) who founded Ligonier Ministries and taught at Reformed Theological Seminary.
Contribution
Through Ligonier Ministries, teaching, and writing on the holiness of God he renewed Reformed theology for a popular audience and trained a generation of pastors.
