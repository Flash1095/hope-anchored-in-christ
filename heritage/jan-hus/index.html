Jan Hus

Name
Jan Hus
Type
Figure
Era
14th-15th Century
Summary
Bohemian priest and preacher at Bethlehem Chapel in Prague who criticised indulgences, clerical corruption and abuses of church power; he was burned at the stake at the Council of Constance in 1415 despite a safe-conduct.
Contribution
His preaching and writing, influenced by Wycliffe, fuelled the Bohemian reform, promoted Czech preaching and Scripture, and gave rise to the Hussite movement that outlived him by centuries.
Key Works
De Ecclesia
Testimony
He refused to recant beliefs he held to be scriptural and went to the stake trusting in Christ, becoming a martyr of conscience for his followers.
