Thomas Aquinas

Name
Thomas Aquinas
Type
Figure
Era
13th Century
Summary
Dominican scholar who wedded Aristotle's philosophy to Christian theology in his monumental Summa. His 'five ways' argued for God's existence from motion, causation, and design, and his work still anchors Catholic theology.
Contribution
Showed faith and reason are allies, systematized doctrine, and gave the church a lasting framework for thinking about God.
Key Works
Summa Theologica; Summa Contra Gentiles
Testimony
A quiet, brilliant friar whose life testified that the deepest thinking leads not away from God but toward worship - he called his work straw beside the vision of Christ.
Scripture
Romans 1:20
Related Topics
Reason; Theology; Faith and Reason
Related Books
Romans; Psalms
