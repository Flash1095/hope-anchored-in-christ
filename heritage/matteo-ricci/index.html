Matteo Ricci

Name
Matteo Ricci
Type
Figure
Era
16th-17th Century
Summary
Italian Jesuit missionary (1552-1610) who spent nearly thirty years in China, learning its language and culture and winning a hearing at the imperial court.
Contribution
He presented Christianity in Chinese terms, translated works of science and philosophy in both directions, and opened a respectful path for the gospel in China that later missionaries followed. His approach of cultural accommodation became a model, and a controversy, for missions.
Key Works
The True Meaning of the Lord of Heaven
Testimony
He lived in China until his death in Beijing, dressed as a scholar, and was buried there by imperial favour.
