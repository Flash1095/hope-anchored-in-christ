Cyril

Name
Cyril
Type
Figure
Era
9th Century
Summary
Byzantine scholar and missionary who, with his brother Methodius, preached to the Slavs of Moravia.
Contribution
He helped create the Glagolitic alphabet and translate Scripture and the liturgy into Slavonic, and defended the right of Slavic peoples to worship in their own language.
Testimony
He gave his learning to a people whose language had no written Christian literature, so that they could hear the gospel in their own tongue.
