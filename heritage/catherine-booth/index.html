Catherine Booth

Name
Catherine Booth
Type
Figure
Era
19th Century
Summary
English co-founder of the Salvation Army whose pamphlet Female Teaching (1859) defended women's preaching from Scripture and who organised the Army's evangelism and social work in its early years.
Contribution
Preached women's right to ministry and built the Salvation Army's early evangelistic and social work alongside her husband.
Key Works
Female Teaching
Testimony
She testified that the Spirit falls on daughters as well as sons, and preached accordingly.
Related Topics
Preaching; Evangelism
