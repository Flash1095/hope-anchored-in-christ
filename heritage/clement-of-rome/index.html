Clement of Rome

Name
Clement of Rome
Type
Figure
Era
1st Century
Summary
Bishop of Rome in the late first century and one of the earliest Christian writers outside the New Testament, traditionally counted among the apostolic fathers.
Contribution
His letter to the church at Corinth urged unity, order, and humility in a divided congregation, and it became one of the most widely read early Christian letters, showing how the earliest churches appealed to apostolic teaching and mutual submission.
Key Works
First Epistle to the Corinthians (1 Clement)
