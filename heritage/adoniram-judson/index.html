Adoniram Judson

American Baptist missionary who sailed for Burma in 1812, spent nearly forty years there, and gave the Burmese church its Bible and dictionary despite war, prison, and the deaths of two wives.

Contribution

Brought the Gospel to Burma, translated the whole Bible into Burmese, and endured years of imprisonment and bereavement without abandoning the field.

Testimony

He testified that the future of the Burmese church belonged to God, and kept translating through suffering that would have ended a lesser work.
