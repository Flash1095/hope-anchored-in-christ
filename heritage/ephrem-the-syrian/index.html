Ephrem the Syrian

Name
Ephrem the Syrian
Type
Figure
Era
4th Century
Summary
A fourth-century Syriac-speaking deacon, poet, and teacher from Nisibis and later Edessa, one of the great hymn-writers of the early church.
Contribution
His hymns and poems taught the faith in memorable verse, defended orthodox belief against heresy, and meditated on creation, the incarnation, and paradise, making him a central voice of Syriac Christianity.
Key Works
Hymns on the Nativity; Hymns on Paradise
Testimony
Spent his life in teaching, hymn-writing, and service to the poor, including caring for plague victims in Edessa.
