David Livingstone

Name
David Livingstone
Type
Figure
Era
19th Century
Summary
Scottish missionary doctor and explorer who spent thirty years in Africa, combining evangelism, medicine, and exploration, and whose death in 1873, kneeling in prayer, became a legend that spurred a new wave of missionaries.
Contribution
Opened Central Africa to missions and commerce, mapped the Zambesi, and made the slave trade's horrors known to Britain.
Key Works
Missionary Travels and Researches in South Africa; Narrative of an Expedition to the Zambesi
Testimony
He testified that he would go anywhere, provided it be forward, trusting God with a life spent in Africa.
Related Topics
Missions; Abolition; Courage
