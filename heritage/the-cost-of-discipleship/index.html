The Cost of Discipleship

Bonhoeffer's 1937 classic contrasting 'cheap grace' - forgiveness without repentance - with the costly grace that demands obedience. Written as the Nazi church compromised, it remains a summons to serious faith.

Contribution

Recovered discipleship as obedience, not assent, for a compromising age.

Testimony

Bonhoeffer lived his thesis: grace that cost him everything, including his life.
