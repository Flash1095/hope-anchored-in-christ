Bonaventure

Name
Bonaventure
Type
Figure
Era
13th Century
Summary
Franciscan theologian and minister general of the order, known as the Seraphic Doctor.
Contribution
He systematised Franciscan theology, wrote the classic Life of Saint Francis that shaped Franciscan memory, defended the mendicant orders, and joined rigorous theology to mystical ascent to God.
Key Works
The Soul's Journey into God; Life of Saint Francis
Testimony
He sought God through contemplation as well as study, teaching that the soul journeys into God through prayer, humility and love.
