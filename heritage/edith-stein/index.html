Edith Stein

Name
Edith Stein
Type
Figure
Era
20th Century
Summary
German Jewish-born philosopher (1891-1942) who converted to Catholicism, became a Carmelite nun, and died at Auschwitz; she was later canonized.
Contribution
As a philosopher, Carmelite nun, and martyr at Auschwitz she joined rigorous thought to Christian faith and witness, becoming a symbol of faith under totalitarian persecution.
