Cyril of Jerusalem

Name
Cyril of Jerusalem
Type
Figure
Era
4th Century
Summary
Bishop of Jerusalem in the fourth century, best known as a teacher of new converts preparing for baptism.
Contribution
His Catechetical Lectures explained the creed, the sacraments, and the Christian life to catechumens in plain, pastoral language, and they remain a classic guide to what the fourth-century church believed and how it formed new believers.
