Charles Spurgeon

Name
Charles Spurgeon
Type
Figure
Era
19th Century
Summary
London's 'Prince of Preachers,' whose Metropolitan Tabernacle drew thousands weekly. Converted as a teenager in a snowstorm, he preached Christ-centered sermons, founded an orphanage and pastors' college, and printed sermons read worldwide.
Contribution
Modeled expository, Christ-centered preaching and combined evangelism with orphan care and ministry training.
Key Works
Spurgeon's Sermons; Morning and Evening
Testimony
His testimony was simple: a boy who looked to Christ and lived, then spent fifty years pointing others to the same look.
Scripture
Isaiah 45:22
Related Topics
Preaching; Evangelism; Grace
Related Books
Isaiah; Romans
