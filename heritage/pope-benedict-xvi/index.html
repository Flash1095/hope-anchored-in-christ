Pope Benedict XVI

Name
Pope Benedict XVI
Type
Figure
Era
20th-21st Century
Summary
German pope (1927-2022), born Joseph Ratzinger, who led the Catholic Church from 2005 until his resignation in 2013.
Contribution
As a theologian-pope he gave the church a deeply biblical and Augustinian teaching voice, and his resignation in 2013, the first in centuries, reshaped modern expectations of the papacy.
