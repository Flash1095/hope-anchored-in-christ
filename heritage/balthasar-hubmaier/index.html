Balthasar Hubmaier

Name
Balthasar Hubmaier
Type
Figure
Era
16th Century
Summary
German theologian (1480-1528) and former Catholic priest who became the most learned early defender of believer's baptism.
Contribution
He wrote extensively for believer's baptism, religious freedom, and a free church, and led Anabaptist congregations in Waldshut and Moravia. His writings gave the early Anabaptist movement theological weight beyond its Swiss origins.
Key Works
On the Christian Baptism of Believers
Testimony
He was burned at the stake in Vienna, and his wife was drowned in the Danube days later.
