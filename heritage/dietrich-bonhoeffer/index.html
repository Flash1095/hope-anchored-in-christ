Dietrich Bonhoeffer

Name
Dietrich Bonhoeffer
Type
Figure
Era
20th Century
Summary
German pastor and theologian who resisted the Nazi regime, helped Jews escape, and was executed at Flossenburg in 1945. His writings on costly grace and Christian community remain classics of faithful courage.
Contribution
Gave the church a theology of costly discipleship and a lived example of resistance to evil.
Key Works
The Cost of Discipleship; Life Together; Letters and Papers from Prison
Testimony
His testimony was costly grace: faith that obeys even to death, trusting that God meets us in suffering, not apart from it.
Scripture
Matthew 16:24-25
Related Topics
Discipleship; Courage; Suffering
Related Books
Matthew; Romans
