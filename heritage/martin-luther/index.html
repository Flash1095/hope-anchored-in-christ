Martin Luther

Name
Martin Luther
Type
Figure
Era
16th Century
Summary
German monk whose protest against indulgences in 1517 sparked the Reformation. Tormented by guilt until he grasped justification by faith alone, he translated the New Testament into German and put Scripture back in ordinary people's hands.
Contribution
Recovered justification by faith alone, Scripture alone, and grace alone; launched the Reformation and vernacular Bible translation.
Key Works
Ninety-five Theses; Luther's German New Testament; The Bondage of the Will
Testimony
His testimony was a conscience set free: 'The just shall live by faith' turned a terrified monk into a reformer who would not recant.
Scripture
Romans 1:17
Related Topics
Reformation; Justification; Grace
Related Books
Romans; Galatians
