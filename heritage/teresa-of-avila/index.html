Teresa of Avila

Name
Teresa of Avila
Type
Figure
Era
16th Century
Summary
Spanish Carmelite nun and mystic (1515-1582) who reformed her order and wrote some of the most influential works on prayer in Christian history.
Contribution
She founded the Discalced Carmelites, returning her order to poverty, enclosure, and deep prayer, and she wrote practical guides to the life of prayer that have formed Christians across traditions ever since.
Key Works
The Interior Castle; The Life of Teresa of Jesus
Testimony
Her writings describe prayer as friendship with God, pursued through long years of dryness, opposition within her own order, and ill health.
