Conrad Grebel

Name
Conrad Grebel
Type
Figure
Era
16th Century
Summary
Swiss scholar (1498-1526) who broke with Zwingli over infant baptism and became a founder of the Swiss Brethren, the first Anabaptist movement.
Contribution
He helped organise the first believer's baptisms in Zurich in 1525 and carried the new movement into other Swiss towns, insisting that the church be a voluntary community of baptised believers separate from the state church.
Testimony
He died young, probably of plague, while fleeing persecution after imprisonment for his baptising activity.
