Council of Nicaea

Name
Council of Nicaea
Type
Event
Era
325 AD
Summary
The first empire-wide council of bishops, convened at Nicaea to answer Arianism's claim that Christ was created. It affirmed the Son is 'of one substance' with the Father and produced the Nicene Creed.
Contribution
Defined orthodox Christology and gave the church its most-used creed.
Key Works
Nicene Creed
Testimony
The bishops' testimony under imperial pressure: truth about Christ was worth exile and division.
Scripture
John 1:1
