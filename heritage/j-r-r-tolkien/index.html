J. R. R. Tolkien

Name
J. R. R. Tolkien
Type
Figure
Era

Summary
English writer and Oxford professor (1892-1973), a devout Catholic whose Middle-earth writings carry a deeply Christian moral vision.
Contribution
His fiction, rooted in Catholic faith, gave the twentieth century its most influential Christian imaginative world, shaping how millions picture good, evil, sacrifice, and hope.
