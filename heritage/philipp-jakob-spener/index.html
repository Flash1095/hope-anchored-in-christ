Philipp Jakob Spener

Name
Philipp Jakob Spener
Type
Figure
Era
17th Century
Summary
German Lutheran pastor whose Pia Desideria (1675) called for small Bible-study groups, better preaching, and a faith shown in daily life, reforming a church he found formal and cold.
Contribution
Launched Pietism, the renewal movement that stressed personal conversion, Bible study, and holy living, and that later fed the Moravian and Methodist revivals.
Key Works
Pia Desideria
Testimony
He testified that true Christianity is a living faith that reforms both heart and household.
