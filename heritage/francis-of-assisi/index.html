Francis of Assisi

Name
Francis of Assisi
Type
Figure
Era
12th-13th Century
Summary
Italian merchant's son who renounced wealth and founded the Friars Minor, living in radical poverty and preaching to ordinary people.
Contribution
The Franciscan order carried his vision of poverty, humility, care for lepers and the poor, and love of creation across Europe, and renewed preaching and pastoral care in towns.
Key Works
Canticle of the Sun
Testimony
Hearing the gospel call to leave all and follow Christ, he stripped himself of wealth and lived as a poor brother among the poor.
Scripture
Matthew 19:21
