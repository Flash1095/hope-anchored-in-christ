Name

Type

Summary
Argentine-born evangelist (1934-2021) who moved to the United States and led large evangelistic festivals across the Spanish-speaking world.
Contribution
As an evangelist to the Spanish-speaking world he preached to millions across Latin America and the United States and founded a ministry that trained Hispanic evangelists.
