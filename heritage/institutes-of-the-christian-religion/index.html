Institutes of the Christian Religion

Calvin's systematic theology, revised across his life from a small handbook into four books on God, Christ, the Spirit, and the church. It shaped Reformed churches worldwide.

Contribution

Gave Protestantism a comprehensive, Scripture-saturated theology.

Testimony

Calvin's testimony was order from conviction: knowledge of God and of ourselves belong together.
