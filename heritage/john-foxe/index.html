John Foxe

Name
John Foxe
Type
Figure
Era
16th Century
Summary
English historian and martyrologist (1516-1587) whose great book preserved the stories of Christians who died for their faith.
Contribution
His Acts and Monuments, known as Foxe's Book of Martyrs, gathered accounts of persecuted Christians from the early church to his own day, including the Marian martyrs he knew personally. For centuries it shaped Protestant memory of persecution and steadfastness.
Key Works
Acts and Monuments (Foxe's Book of Martyrs)
Testimony
An exile himself under Mary I, he returned under Elizabeth I and used his writing to honour those who had not survived.
