John Cassian

Name
John Cassian
Type
Figure
Era
4th-5th Century
Summary
A monk and writer of the late fourth and early fifth centuries who carried Egyptian monastic wisdom to the West.
Contribution
His Institutes and Conferences recorded the teachings of the desert fathers on prayer, temptation, and the spiritual life, and they became foundational reading for Western monasticism, including the Rule of Benedict.
Key Works
Institutes; Conferences
