Jim Elliot

Name
Jim Elliot
Type
Figure
Era
20th Century
Summary
American missionary (1927-1956) killed, with four colleagues, on the Curaray River in Ecuador during Operation Auca.
Contribution
He was one of five missionaries killed in 1956 while seeking to reach the Huaorani people of Ecuador; his death, and his journal writings, inspired a generation of missionaries.
Testimony
He is remembered for the conviction that no sacrifice for Christ is wasted.
