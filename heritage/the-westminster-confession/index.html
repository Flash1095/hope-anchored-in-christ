The Westminster Confession

Name
The Westminster Confession
Type
Writing
Era
17th Century
Summary
The 1646 confession of the Westminster Assembly, summarizing Reformed doctrine on Scripture, God, salvation, and the church. It still guides Presbyterian churches worldwide.
Contribution
Gave Reformed churches a confessional standard of remarkable precision and pastoral warmth.
Key Works
Westminster Confession of Faith
Testimony
Its opening testimony - that humanity's chief end is to glorify God and enjoy Him forever - became the church's compass.
Scripture
Romans 11:36
