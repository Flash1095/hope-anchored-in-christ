Lesslie Newbigin

Name
Lesslie Newbigin
Type
Figure
Era
20th Century
Summary
British missionary and bishop (1909-1998) who served in India for decades and later wrote on mission in the secular West.
Contribution
As a missionary bishop in India and a leader of the World Council of Churches he shaped modern missiology, arguing that the gospel must be proclaimed afresh in both non-Western and secular Western cultures.
Key Works
The Gospel in a Pluralist Society
