Justin Martyr

Name
Justin Martyr
Type
Figure
Era
2nd Century
Summary
A second-century philosopher from Samaria who became a Christian and defended the faith in Rome, where he was eventually martyred.
Contribution
His apologies addressed to the Roman emperors argued that Christians were loyal, moral citizens and that Christ fulfilled the hopes of philosophy and prophecy, while his dialogue with a Jewish interlocutor defended Christian readings of the Old Testament.
Key Works
First Apology; Second Apology; Dialogue with Trypho
Testimony
Described his search through philosophy before finding in Christianity the true philosophy, and held to it through to his martyrdom.
