John Mbiti

Name
John Mbiti
Type
Figure
Era
20th-21st Century
Summary
Kenyan Anglican priest and theologian (1931-2019) who taught in East Africa and Switzerland and wrote widely on African religion and theology.
Contribution
His study of African traditional religion gave African theology academic standing and helped African Christians articulate faith in their own cultural terms rather than only in Western ones.
Key Works
