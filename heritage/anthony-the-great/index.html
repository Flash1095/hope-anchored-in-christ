Anthony the Great

Name
Anthony the Great
Type
Figure
Era
3rd-4th Century
Summary
An Egyptian Christian who left a comfortable inheritance in the late third century to live a life of prayer and simplicity in the desert, becoming the best-known of the desert fathers.
Contribution
His desert life inspired countless others to seek God in solitude and discipline, and the Life written about him spread the monastic ideal across the Christian world, making him a founding influence on Christian monasticism.
Testimony
Hearing the call to sell what he had and give to the poor, he gave away his possessions and devoted his long life to prayer in the desert.
Scripture
Matthew 19:21
