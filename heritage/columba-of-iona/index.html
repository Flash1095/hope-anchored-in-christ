Columba of Iona

Name
Columba of Iona
Type
Figure
Era
6th Century
Summary
Irish monk who founded the monastery on Iona in 563 and preached among the peoples of northern Scotland.
Contribution
Iona became a centre of mission, learning and manuscript work, and from it Christianity spread through Pictish lands and into Northumbria.
Testimony
He left Ireland and gave the rest of his life to preaching, founding communities and praying for the peoples he served.
