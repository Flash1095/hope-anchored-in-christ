Martin of Tours

Name
Martin of Tours
Type
Figure
Era
4th Century
Summary
A fourth-century soldier-turned-monk who became bishop of Tours in Gaul and a pioneer of monastic life and mission in the West.
Contribution
He founded one of the earliest monasteries in Gaul, evangelised rural districts that Christianity had barely reached, and modelled a bishop's life of simplicity and care for the poor.
Testimony
As a young soldier he famously shared his cloak with a beggar, and later left military service to serve Christ as a monk and bishop.
