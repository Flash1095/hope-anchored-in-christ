Francis de Sales

Name
Francis de Sales
Type
Figure
Era
16th-17th Century
Summary
French bishop of Geneva (1567-1622) who won back many to Catholic faith by preaching and writing, and who taught that holiness belongs to ordinary life.
Contribution
His Introduction to the Devout Life showed lay men and women how to pursue holiness in work, family, and daily duties, not only in monasteries, and his gentle approach to controversy influenced Catholic spirituality for centuries.
Key Works
Introduction to the Devout Life; Treatise on the Love of God
Testimony
He served a diocese centred on Calvinist Geneva, preaching patiently rather than by force, and corresponded widely as a spiritual director.
