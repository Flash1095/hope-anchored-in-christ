Watchman Nee

Name
Watchman Nee
Type
Figure
Era
20th Century
Summary
Chinese Christian leader (1903-1972) who preached and wrote across China and was imprisoned for twenty years under the Communist government, dying in prison.
Contribution
He founded the Little Flock movement in China, wrote on the deeper Christian life, and remained faithful through long imprisonment, influencing house churches in China and Christians worldwide.
Key Works
The Normal Christian Life
