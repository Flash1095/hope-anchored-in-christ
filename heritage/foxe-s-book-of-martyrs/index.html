Foxe's Book of Martyrs

Name
Foxe's Book of Martyrs
Type
Writing
Era
16th Century
Summary
John Foxe's vast history of Christian martyrs, from the early church to the Marian persecutions. For generations it stood beside the Bible in English homes, steeling Protestants for suffering.
Contribution
Preserved the memory of martyrs and taught the church that faithfulness can cost everything.
Key Works
Acts and Monuments (Foxe)
Testimony
The martyrs' testimonies - ordinary believers who would not recant - became the church's encouragement under fire.
Scripture
Revelation 12:11
Related Topics
Martyrdom; Persecution; Courage
Related Books
