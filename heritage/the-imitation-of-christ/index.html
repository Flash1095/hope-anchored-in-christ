The Imitation of Christ

Thomas a Kempis's devotional manual on following Jesus in humility, prayer, and inward devotion. For six centuries it has been, after the Bible, among the most widely read Christian books.

Contribution

Taught generations that following Christ is inward before it is outward - humility, prayer, and hidden obedience.

Testimony

Its author's testimony is its message: the quiet life of devotion matters more than reputation.
