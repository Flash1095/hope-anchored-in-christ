The Methodist Movement

Name
The Methodist Movement
Type
Movement
Era
18th Century
Summary
The societies Wesley organized for disciplined discipleship - classes, bands, and itinerant preachers - that grew into Methodism and spread evangelical faith across Britain and America.
Contribution
Wedded evangelism to accountable community and social compassion.
Key Works
Wesley's Sermons; Methodist Discipline
Testimony
Methodists testified that holiness is social as well as personal: grace pursued together.
Scripture
James 2:17
Related Topics
Methodism; Holiness; Revival
Related Books
