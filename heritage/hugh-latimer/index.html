Hugh Latimer

Name
Hugh Latimer
Type
Figure
Era
16th Century
Summary
English bishop and preacher (c. 1487-1555) whose plain, searching sermons made him one of the most loved voices of the English Reformation.
Contribution
As bishop of Worcester and court preacher under Edward VI, he preached against corruption and for reform in language ordinary people understood, and his sermons remain models of English preaching.
Key Works
Sermon on the Plough
Testimony
Under Mary I he was condemned and burned at the stake in Oxford alongside Nicholas Ridley, urging his fellow sufferer to be of good comfort.
