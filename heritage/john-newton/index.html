John Newton

Name
John Newton
Type
Figure
Era
18th Century
Summary
Former slave trader converted in a storm at sea, later an Anglican minister and mentor to Wilberforce. His hymn 'Amazing Grace' became the church's anthem of redemption.
Contribution
Embodied grace's power to transform a slaver into a saint, and mentored the abolition movement.
Key Works
Amazing Grace; Letters of John Newton
Testimony
His testimony - 'I once was lost, but now am found' - turned a wretch's story into the world's most sung hymn.
Scripture
1 Timothy 1:15
Related Topics
Grace; Redemption; Conversion
Related Books
1 Timothy; Romans
