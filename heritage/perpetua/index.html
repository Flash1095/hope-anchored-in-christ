Perpetua

Name
Perpetua
Type
Figure
Era
3rd Century
Summary
A young noblewoman of Carthage martyred in the arena in AD 203, whose prison diary is one of the earliest surviving writings by a Christian woman.
Contribution
Her diary of imprisonment, visions, and resolve, preserved in the Passion of Perpetua and Felicitas, gave the early church a first-hand martyr's voice and has inspired Christians facing persecution ever since.
Key Works
The Passion of Perpetua and Felicitas (prison diary portion attributed to Perpetua)
Testimony
Refused to renounce her faith despite imprisonment, separation from her infant son, and her father's pleas, and died in the arena professing Christ.
