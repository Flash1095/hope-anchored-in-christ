Elizabeth Fry

Name
Elizabeth Fry
Type
Figure
Era
18th-19th Century
Summary
English Quaker philanthropist who began visiting women in Newgate Prison in 1813, founded the Association for the Improvement of Female Prisoners, and carried prison reform across Europe.
Contribution
Reformed prisons in Britain and beyond, bringing Christian visitation, education, and dignity to women prisoners whom society had abandoned.
Testimony
She testified that Christ's love extended to the condemned, and acted on it inside prison walls.
Related Topics
Compassion; Justice
