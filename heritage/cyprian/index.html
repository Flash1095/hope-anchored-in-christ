Cyprian

Name
Cyprian
Type
Figure
Era
3rd Century
Summary
Bishop of Carthage in North Africa in the mid-third century, a leading voice of the church during and after the Decian persecution.
Contribution
His writings on the unity of the church and on how to receive back Christians who had lapsed under persecution guided church discipline, and his letters give a vivid picture of third-century church life in North Africa.
Key Works
On the Unity of the Church; letters
Testimony
Remained with his flock through plague and persecution and was beheaded for refusing to sacrifice to the Roman gods.
