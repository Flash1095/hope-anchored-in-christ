Martin Luther King Jr.

Name
Martin Luther King Jr.
Type
Figure
Era
20th Century
Summary
American Baptist minister and civil rights leader (1929-1968) who led the Southern Christian Leadership Conference and was assassinated in Memphis.
Contribution
As a Baptist minister he led the American civil rights movement with nonviolent, biblically grounded appeals to justice, and his preaching joined Christian faith to the dignity of every person.
Key Works
Strength to Love
Testimony
He rooted nonviolent resistance in Christian love of neighbour and enemy alike.
