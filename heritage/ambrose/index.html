Ambrose

Name
Ambrose
Type
Figure
Era
4th Century
Summary
Bishop of Milan in the late fourth century, a former Roman governor who was acclaimed bishop before he was even baptised.
Contribution
As bishop he defended Nicene faith against Arian influence at the imperial court, shaped the church's independence from state control, and introduced congregational hymn-singing to the West; he also baptised and influenced Augustine of Hippo.
Key Works
On the Duties of the Clergy; hymns; letters
Testimony
Gave up a high civil career for the bishopric and used his authority to call even the emperor to repentance.
