David Brainerd

American missionary to Native American peoples in New Jersey and Pennsylvania who died of tuberculosis at twenty-nine; Jonathan Edwards published his diary, which became a missions classic.

Contribution

Inspired generations of missionaries, including William Carey, through a short life of prayer, hardship, and devotion among Native American peoples.

Testimony

His diary testified to a life poured out in prayer and self-denial for people who had never heard the Gospel.
