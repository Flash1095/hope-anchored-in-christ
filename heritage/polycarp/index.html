Polycarp

Name
Polycarp
Type
Figure
Era
2nd Century
Summary
Bishop of Smyrna in Asia Minor, a respected elder of the second-century church who was martyred in old age.
Contribution
His letter to the Philippians commended steadfast faith and warned against false teaching, while the early account of his martyrdom became a model narrative of courageous witness that later Christians read and honoured.
Key Works
Letter to the Philippians
Testimony
At his trial he refused to deny Christ, saying he had served him for many decades and would not revile his King and Saviour.
Scripture
Revelation 2:10
