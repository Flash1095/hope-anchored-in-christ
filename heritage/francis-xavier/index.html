Francis Xavier

Name
Francis Xavier
Type
Figure
Era
16th Century
Summary
Spanish Jesuit missionary (1506-1552) and one of Ignatius of Loyola's first companions, who carried the gospel to India, Southeast Asia, and Japan.
Contribution
He preached and baptised across Goa, Malacca, and Japan, learned local languages, and wrote letters home that inspired a generation of missionaries. He is counted among the founders of the modern Catholic missionary movement.
Key Works
Letters of St Francis Xavier
Testimony
He died on an island off the coast of China, hoping to enter a country then closed to him, after a decade of travel and hardship.

Matthew 28:19
