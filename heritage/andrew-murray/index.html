Andrew Murray

South African Dutch Reformed pastor and writer whose devotional books, Keswick addresses, and missionary vision influenced prayer and holiness teaching across the English-speaking world.

Contribution

Gave the devotional life of the church its most widely translated classics on abiding in Christ and prayer.

Testimony

He testified that the Christian life is abiding, not striving: remaining in Christ as the branch remains in the vine.
