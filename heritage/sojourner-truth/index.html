Sojourner Truth

Name
Sojourner Truth
Type
Figure
Era
19th Century
Summary
African American abolitionist and itinerant preacher, born enslaved in New York, who renamed herself Sojourner Truth in 1843 and travelled preaching abolition and women's rights, famously asking 'Ain't I a woman?'
Contribution
Preached abolition and women's rights from her own experience of slavery, becoming a symbol of faith-fuelled freedom speaking.
Key Works
Narrative of Sojourner Truth
Testimony
She testified that God called her to travel and declare truth, and that her freedom was God's gift to proclaim.
Related Topics
Abolition; Freedom; Preaching
