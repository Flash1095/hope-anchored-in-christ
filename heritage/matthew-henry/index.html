Matthew Henry

Name
Matthew Henry
Type
Figure
Era
17th-18th Century
Summary
English Presbyterian minister whose warm, practical commentary on the whole Bible made Scripture plain to ordinary readers and remains in print today.
Contribution
Gave ordinary readers a verse-by-verse commentary that is still used in homes and pulpits more than three centuries later.
Key Works
Commentary on the Whole Bible
Testimony
His life testified to steady pastoral faithfulness: preaching, prayer, and family worship as the shape of a Christian life.

Related Books
