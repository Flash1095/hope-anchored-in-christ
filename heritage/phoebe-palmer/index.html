Phoebe Palmer

Name
Phoebe Palmer
Type
Figure
Era
19th Century
Summary
American Methodist evangelist whose Tuesday Meeting for the Promotion of Holiness drew thousands and whose teaching on entire sanctification and the altar shaped the holiness movement worldwide.
Contribution
Led the Methodist holiness movement and opened public evangelistic ministry to women through her Tuesday Meeting in New York.
Key Works
The Way of Holiness
Testimony
She testified to a definite experience of entire sanctification and taught others to claim it by faith at the altar.
Related Topics
Holiness; Sanctification; Preaching
