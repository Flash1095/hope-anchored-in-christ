John Sung

Name
John Sung
Type
Figure
Era
20th Century
Summary
Chinese evangelist (1901-1944) whose fiery preaching drew large crowds and founded many independent Chinese churches.
Contribution
His evangelistic campaigns helped spark revival across China and Southeast Asia, and his example shaped Chinese evangelical preaching for generations.
