Benedict of Nursia

Name
Benedict of Nursia
Type
Figure
Era
6th Century
Summary
Italian monk who founded the monastery at Monte Cassino and wrote a rule for communal monastic life.
Contribution
His Rule, built around prayer, work, reading and hospitality, became the foundation of Western monasticism, shaping monasteries that preserved learning, cared for travellers and the poor, and carried Christian life through the early Middle Ages.
Key Works
Rule of Saint Benedict
Testimony
He left a life of study in Rome to seek God first in solitude and then in a disciplined community, and his Rule calls monks to prefer nothing to Christ.
