George Muller

Name
George Muller
Type
Figure
Era
19th Century
Summary
German-born English pastor who founded the Ashley Down orphanage in Bristol, caring for more than ten thousand orphans in his lifetime and supporting missions worldwide, all funded, as he insisted, by prayer without solicitation.
Contribution
Showed a watching world that orphan care could be sustained by prayer alone, building five large orphan houses in Bristol without ever appealing for funds.
Key Works
A Narrative of Some of the Lord's Dealings with George Muller
Testimony
He testified that God answers prayer for daily bread, and kept a journal of answered prayers as his evidence.
Related Topics
Prayer; Faith; Compassion
