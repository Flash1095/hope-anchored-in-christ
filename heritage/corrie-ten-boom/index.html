Corrie ten Boom

Name
Corrie ten Boom
Type
Figure
Era
20th Century
Summary
Dutch watchmaker who hid Jews from the Nazis, survived Ravensbruck concentration camp, and spent the rest of her life preaching forgiveness. Her family paid with their lives; she answered with grace.
Contribution
Bore witness that forgiveness is possible even after horror, and carried that message to the world.
Key Works
The Hiding Place; Tramp for the Lord
Testimony
Her testimony was forgiveness in action: she forgave a former guard face to face, proving grace is stronger than hatred.
Scripture
Ephesians 4:32
Related Topics
Forgiveness; Suffering; Courage
Related Books
Ephesians; Romans
