Summary
Augustine's autobiography and prayer, tracing his journey from sin to sainthood. Part confession, part theology, it invented spiritual autobiography and still shapes how Christians tell their stories.
Contribution
Taught the church to read a life as God's story, grounding doctrine in lived experience.
