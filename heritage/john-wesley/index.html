John Wesley

Anglican preacher whose heart was 'strangely warmed' at Aldersgate in 1738. He rode 250,000 miles preaching grace, organized converts into accountable societies, and became a force for revival and social reform in England.

Contribution

Spread evangelical revival, founded Methodism, and tied personal holiness to social compassion.

Testimony

His testimony of assurance - trusting Christ alone for salvation - launched a movement that carried the Gospel to the working poor and the ends of the earth.
