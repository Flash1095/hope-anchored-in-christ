John Chrysostom

Name
John Chrysostom
Type
Figure
Era
4th-5th Century
Summary
Archbishop of Constantinople around the turn of the fifth century, called Chrysostom, meaning golden-mouthed, for his preaching.
Contribution
His plain, practical sermons expounded whole books of the Bible verse by verse and called rich and poor alike to repentance and generosity; his liturgy is still used in Eastern Orthodox worship today.
Key Works
Homilies on Matthew, John, and Romans; On the Priesthood
Testimony
Preached fearlessly against luxury and corruption, accepting exile twice rather than softening his message.
