Sadhu Sundar Singh

Name
Sadhu Sundar Singh
Type
Figure
Era
20th Century
Summary
Indian Christian sadhu (1889-1929) who, after converting from Sikhism, travelled as a barefoot evangelist wearing a saffron robe.
Contribution
As a wandering evangelist in India and beyond he preached a simple gospel of Christ, lived in voluntary poverty, and became a bridge figure between Indian culture and Christian faith.
