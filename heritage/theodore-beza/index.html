Theodore Beza

Name
Theodore Beza
Type
Figure
Era
16th Century
Summary
French reformer (1519-1605) who succeeded Calvin in Geneva and carried Reformed leadership into a second generation.
Contribution
He led Geneva's church and academy, defended Reformed doctrine in debate and print, supported persecuted French Protestants, and wrote on the right of resistance to tyranny. Under him Geneva remained the training ground for Reformed pastors across Europe.
Key Works
Du droit des magistrats (On the Right of Magistrates)
Testimony
He served Geneva for over forty years after Calvin's death, holding the church steady through war and persecution in France.
