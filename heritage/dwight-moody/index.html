Dwight Moody

Name
Dwight Moody
Type
Figure
Era
19th Century
Summary
American evangelist, a former shoe salesman with little formal schooling, whose campaigns drew enormous crowds in Chicago, London, and beyond, and whose schools and publishing work outlived him.
Contribution
Brought mass urban evangelism to America and Britain, founded Moody Bible Institute, and made the inquiry room a staple of evangelistic campaigns.
Key Works
Heaven
Testimony
He testified to a simple Gospel, summed in John 3:16, preached plainly to whoever would listen.
Scripture
John 3:16
