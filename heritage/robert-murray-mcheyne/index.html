Robert Murray M'Cheyne

Name
Robert Murray M'Cheyne
Type
Figure
Era
19th Century
Summary
Scottish minister of St Peter's, Dundee, who died at twenty-nine; his daily Bible-reading plan, taking readers through the Old Testament once and the New Testament and Psalms twice a year, is still widely used.
Contribution
Gave the church its most enduring Bible-reading plan and a model of youthful, prayerful pastoral ministry in Scotland.
Key Works
Memoir and Remains
Testimony
He testified that holiness and prayer, not mere activity, were the minister's first work.
Related Topics
Devotion; Prayer
Related Books
