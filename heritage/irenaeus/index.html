Irenaeus

Name
Irenaeus
Type
Figure
Era
2nd Century
Summary
Bishop of Lyons in Gaul in the late second century, a student of Polycarp's circle who became the church's leading opponent of Gnostic teaching.
Contribution
Against Heresies refuted Gnostic systems point by point and set out the church's rule of faith, the unity of the Old and New Testaments, and the goodness of creation, shaping orthodox theology for centuries.
Key Works
Against Heresies; Demonstration of the Apostolic Preaching
