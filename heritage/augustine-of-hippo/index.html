Augustine of Hippo

Name
Augustine of Hippo
Type
Figure
Era
4th-5th Century
Summary
North African bishop whose writings shaped Western Christianity for a thousand years. Converted from a life of ambition and sin after years of restless searching, he became the church's great teacher on grace, sin, and the city of God.
Contribution
Gave the church its classic doctrine of grace and original sin, defended the faith against Pelagianism and Donatism, and modeled honest confession.
Key Works
Confessions; The City of God; On Christian Doctrine
Testimony
His testimony - 'You have made us for yourself, O Lord, and our hearts are restless until they rest in you' - turned a sinner's story into the church's prayer book.
Scripture
Romans 7:24-25
Related Topics
Grace; Conversion; Sin
Related Books
Romans; Psalms
