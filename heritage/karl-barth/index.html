Karl Barth

Name
Karl Barth
Type
Figure
Era
20th Century
Summary
Swiss Reformed theologian (1886-1968) who led the renewal of Protestant theology after the First World War and helped draft the Barmen Declaration against Nazi control of the church.
Contribution
His Church Dogmatics became the most influential Protestant theology of the twentieth century, recentring theology on Jesus Christ and the Word of God and resisting both liberal reduction and Nazi ideology.
