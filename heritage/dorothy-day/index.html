Dorothy Day

Name
Dorothy Day
Type
Figure
Era
20th Century
Summary
American journalist and social activist (1897-1980) who converted to Catholicism and co-founded the Catholic Worker newspaper and houses of hospitality.
Contribution
She founded the Catholic Worker movement, combining houses of hospitality for the poor with pacifist witness and journalism, and her example shaped Catholic social action in America.
