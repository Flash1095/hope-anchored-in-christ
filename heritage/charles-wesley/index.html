Charles Wesley

Name
Charles Wesley
Type
Figure
Era
18th Century
Summary
English hymn writer and younger brother of John Wesley, whose more than six thousand hymns carried Methodist theology of grace, conversion, and holiness into the mouths of ordinary singers.
Contribution
Gave the church thousands of hymns that taught doctrine through song, including some of the most sung hymns in English.
Key Works
Hymns including 'Hark! The Herald Angels Sing' and 'Love Divine, All Loves Excelling'
Testimony
Converted days before his brother's Aldersgate experience, he testified in hymn after hymn to a Saviour who loved him and died for him.
