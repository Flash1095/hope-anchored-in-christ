Mary Slessor

Name
Mary Slessor
Type
Figure
Era
19th-20th Century
Summary
Scottish mill-worker turned missionary who arrived in Calabar in 1876, lived alone among the Okoyong people, rescued abandoned twins, and became a magistrate trusted by chiefs and colonial officials alike.
Contribution
Spent nearly forty years in Calabar, Nigeria, stopping twin-killing, negotiating peace between villages, and earning the trust of the Efik people as 'White Ma'.
Testimony
She testified that God had called a Dundee mill girl to Africa, and stayed when others left, through fever, loneliness, and danger.
Related Topics
Missions; Compassion; Courage
