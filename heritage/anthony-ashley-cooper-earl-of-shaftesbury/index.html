Anthony Ashley-Cooper, Earl of Shaftesbury

Name
Anthony Ashley-Cooper, Earl of Shaftesbury
Type
Figure
Era
19th Century
Summary
English evangelical peer, known as Lord Shaftesbury, who spent fifty years in Parliament reforming child labour, lunatic asylums, and working conditions, and supporting ragged schools and missions.
Contribution
Carried evangelical social reform into Parliament, securing factory laws, mine laws, and ragged schools for Britain's poorest children.
Testimony
He testified that a Christian lord's duty was to the defenceless, and legislated as if he believed it.
Related Topics
Justice; Compassion
