Elisabeth Elliot

Name
Elisabeth Elliot
Type
Figure
Era
20th-21st Century
Summary
American missionary and author (1926-2015) who served among the Huaorani people of Ecuador after her husband Jim Elliot was killed there in 1956, and who later lived and worked among them.
Contribution
After her husband's death she carried his missionary story to the world through writing and speaking, and her books on suffering, obedience, and singleness shaped evangelical thinking on Christian womanhood and endurance.
Key Works
Through Gates of Splendor
Testimony
She wrote that obedience to God, even through grief, is the ground of Christian peace.
