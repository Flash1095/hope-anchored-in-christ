The Pilgrim's Progress

Name
The Pilgrim's Progress
Type
Writing
Era
17th Century
Summary
John Bunyan's allegory, written in Bedford jail, follows Christian from the City of Destruction to the Celestial City. After the Bible, it became the best-selling book in English history.
Contribution
Put the Christian life into story form ordinary people could carry, and shaped evangelical imagination for centuries.
Key Works
The Pilgrim's Progress (Bunyan)
Testimony
Bunyan, a jailed tinker, testified that the Christian life is a journey - with sloughs, giants, and a shining gate at the end.
Scripture
Hebrews 11:13-16
