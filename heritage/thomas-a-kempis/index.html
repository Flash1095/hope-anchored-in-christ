Thomas à Kempis

Name
Thomas à Kempis
Type
Figure
Era
14th-15th Century
Summary
German-Dutch canon regular of the Windesheim congregation at Mount St Agnes near Zwolle, and devotional writer.
Contribution
The Imitation of Christ, long attributed to him, became one of the most widely read devotional books in Christian history, shaping inward, Christ-centred piety across Catholic and Protestant traditions alike.
Key Works
The Imitation of Christ
Testimony
He lived a quiet communal life of prayer and copying, urging readers to follow Christ inwardly rather than seek outward display.
