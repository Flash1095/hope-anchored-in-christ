Robert Morrison

Name
Robert Morrison
Type
Figure
Era
18th-19th Century
Summary
Scottish missionary who reached Canton in 1807 as the first Protestant missionary to China, working under East India Company cover and in defiance of Chinese bans on teaching the language to foreigners.
Contribution
Opened China to Protestant missions, translated the whole Bible into Chinese, and compiled the first major Chinese-English dictionary.
Key Works
A Dictionary of the Chinese Language; Chinese Bible translation
Testimony
He testified to patient, hidden labour: years of language study before a single convert, trusting God with the fruit.
Related Topics
Missions; Translation
