Desmond Tutu

Name
Desmond Tutu
Type
Figure
Era
20th-21st Century
Summary
South African Anglican archbishop (1931-2021) and Nobel Peace Prize laureate who led moral opposition to apartheid.
Contribution
As Anglican Archbishop of Cape Town he opposed apartheid with nonviolent moral courage, chaired the Truth and Reconciliation Commission, and became a global voice for forgiveness and human dignity.
