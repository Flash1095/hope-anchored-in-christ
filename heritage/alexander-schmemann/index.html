Alexander Schmemann

Name
Alexander Schmemann
Type
Figure
Era
20th Century
Summary
Russian-born Orthodox priest and theologian (1921-1983) who taught at St Vladimir's Seminary in New York and became dean there.
Contribution
His theology of liturgy and of the world as sacrament renewed Orthodox understanding of worship and mission, and his writings are now standard Orthodox introductions.
