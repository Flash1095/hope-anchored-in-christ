Blandina

Name
Blandina
Type
Figure
Era
2nd Century
Summary
An enslaved Christian woman martyred at Lyons in Gaul in AD 177 during a fierce local persecution.
Contribution
Her endurance under torture and her death in the arena, recorded in a letter from the churches of Lyons and Vienne, became one of the early church's most celebrated examples of witness, showing a supposedly weak slave outlasting her tormentors in faith.
Testimony
Endured repeated torture without denying Christ and died in the arena confessing her faith.
