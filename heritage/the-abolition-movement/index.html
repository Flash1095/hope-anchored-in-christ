The Abolition Movement

The Christian-led campaign, from Quakers to Wilberforce to American evangelicals, that ended the British slave trade (1807), British slavery (1833), and finally American slavery (1865).

Contribution

Showed Christian conviction dismantling an entrenched economic evil.

Testimony

Abolitionists testified that every person bears God's image - and no profit justifies chains.
