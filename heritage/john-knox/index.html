John Knox

Name
John Knox
Type
Figure
Era
16th Century
Summary
Scottish preacher (c. 1514-1572) who became the leading voice of the Scottish Reformation after years as a galley slave, exile, and pastor in Geneva.
Contribution
He preached against the Mass, helped draft the Scots Confession and the First Book of Discipline, and pressed for a national church ordered by Scripture, with preaching, discipline, and education for the people. His work made Scotland a Reformed nation.
Key Works
History of the Reformation in Scotland; The First Blast of the Trumpet Against the Monstrous Regiment of Women
Testimony
His preaching was famously uncompromising, and he endured imprisonment, exile, and confrontation with rulers rather than soften it.
