Criminal Justice and Restoration

Issue
Criminal Justice and Restoration
Summary
Punishment, policing, and prisons raise questions of justice and mercy.
Biblical Principles
Justice must punish wrong and restore brokenness; all have sinned; redemption is possible.
Biblical Response
Support just sentencing and honest policing; visit prisoners; champion restoration, victim care, and second chances.
Scripture
Micah 6:8; Hebrews 13:3; Luke 23:42-43
Pitfalls
Mercy without justice; justice without mercy; writing off either victims or offenders.
