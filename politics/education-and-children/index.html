Education and Children

Issue
Education and Children
Summary
Schools shape the next generation's view of truth, identity, and morality.
Biblical Principles
Children are a heritage; parents are first teachers; truth is not neutral.
Biblical Response
Invest in your children's formation; engage schools with grace and courage; support teachers; build alternatives where conscience requires.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 6:6-7; Proverbs 22:6; Matthew 19:14
Pitfalls
Abandoning public institutions entirely; trusting them entirely; fighting culture wars while neglecting your own children.
