Government Authority

Issue
Government Authority
Summary
How far does obedience to government go, and when must Christians disobey?
Biblical Principles
Authority is ordained by God; rulers answer to Him; conscience obeys God first.
Biblical Response
Honor and pray for leaders; obey just laws; disobey respectfully when law commands sin - accepting the cost, as Daniel and the apostles did.
Scripture
Romans 13:1-7; Acts 5:29; 1 Peter 2:13-17
Pitfalls
Treating every law as absolute; treating every disagreement as tyranny; confusing patriotism with worship.
