National Identity and Patriotism

Summary

Love of country can curdle into idolatry or be dismissed as bigotry.

Biblical Principles

Nations are God's provision, not gods; citizenship in heaven outranks earthly flags.

Biblical Response

Love your country gratefully, criticize it honestly, and never confuse it with the kingdom of God.

Pitfalls

Flag-worship; flag-burning contempt; equating a nation with God's chosen people.

Details

Scripture: Jeremiah 29:7; Philippians 3:20; 1 Peter 2:11
