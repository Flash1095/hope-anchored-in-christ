Work and Workers' Dignity

Issue
Work and Workers' Dignity
Summary
Labor conditions, wages, and the meaning of work affect millions daily.
Biblical Principles
Work is God's good gift; workers deserve just wages; rest is holy.
Biblical Response
Work excellently; pay fairly; honor rest; treat employees and employers as neighbors, not tools.
Scripture
Colossians 3:23; James 5:4; Exodus 20:9-10
Pitfalls
Idolizing career; despising manual work; exploiting workers while praising 'family values.'
