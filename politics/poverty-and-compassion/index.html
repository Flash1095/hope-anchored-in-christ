Poverty and Compassion

Issue
Poverty and Compassion
Summary
Millions lack food, housing, and opportunity while wealth concentrates.
Biblical Principles
God owns all wealth; the poor bear His image; generosity is worship.
Biblical Response
Give generously, serve locally, support wise policies that help the poor help themselves - and remember charity and justice belong together.
Scripture
Proverbs 19:17; Luke 10:30-37; 1 John 3:17-18
Pitfalls
Reducing poverty to a program or a slogan; giving without relationship; using the poor as political props.
