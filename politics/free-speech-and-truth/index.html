Free Speech and Truth

Issue
Free Speech and Truth
Summary
Truth is under pressure from censorship, misinformation, and the fear of speaking plainly.
Biblical Principles
Truth sets free; lying lips are an abomination; speech is stewardship.
Biblical Response
Speak truth without fear and without cruelty; refuse to spread what you have not checked; defend others' freedom to speak even when you disagree.
Scripture
John 8:32; Ephesians 4:15; Proverbs 12:22
Pitfalls
Confusing boldness with rudeness; sharing outrage before verifying; demanding silence from opponents.
