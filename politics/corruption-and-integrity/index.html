Corruption and Integrity

Issue
Corruption and Integrity
Summary
Bribery, self-dealing, and dishonest leadership corrode trust.
Biblical Principles
God hates unjust gain; leaders will give account; truth-telling is non-negotiable.
Biblical Response
Demand integrity from leaders of every stripe; practice it yourself; refuse bribes of money, status, or tribal loyalty.
Scripture
Proverbs 11:1; Luke 19:8; Micah 6:8
Pitfalls
Excusing corruption when 'our team' benefits; cynicism that expects nothing better; private virtue paired with public silence.
