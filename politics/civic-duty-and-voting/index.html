Civic Duty and Voting

Issue
Civic Duty and Voting
Summary
Elections force choices between imperfect candidates and platforms.
Biblical Principles
Stewardship includes the public square; no candidate is a savior; character and policy both matter.
Biblical Response
Vote prayerfully and informed; hold no party as the church's party; accept outcomes with grace; serve neighbors regardless of who wins.
Scripture
Proverbs 29:2; 1 Timothy 2:1-2; Jeremiah 29:7
Pitfalls
Making politics an idol; refusing to vote from purity or despair; demonizing fellow believers who vote differently.
