Immigration and the Stranger

Issue
Immigration and the Stranger
Summary
Borders, refugees, and neighbors from other lands stir fear and compassion in equal measure.
Biblical Principles
God commands love for the sojourner; Israel was once a stranger; nations have legitimate borders and duties.
Biblical Response
Welcome strangers personally and as churches; advocate humane, orderly policies; refuse both open-border utopianism and closed-heart fear.
Scripture
Leviticus 19:33-34; Matthew 25:35; Romans 13:1-7
Pitfalls
Treating prudence as hatred; treating compassion as naivety; letting party talking points replace Scripture.
