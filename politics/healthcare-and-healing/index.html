Healthcare and Healing

Issue
Healthcare and Healing
Summary
Access, cost, and conscience in medicine affect the sick and those who serve them.
Biblical Principles
Bodies matter to God; healing is His work through human hands; life is precious.
Biblical Response
Care for the sick personally and systemically; protect conscience rights of medical workers; support healing that honors life at every stage.
Scripture
Matthew 9:35; Luke 10:34; James 5:14-15
Pitfalls
Treating healthcare as a commodity only; treating it as a right without responsibility; ignoring the caregiver's conscience.
