Sanctity of Life

Issue
Sanctity of Life
Summary
Every human life - unborn, disabled, elderly - is at stake in laws and practices around abortion, euthanasia, and assisted suicide.
Biblical Principles
Humans bear God's image; life belongs to God; the shedding of innocent blood cries out to Him.
Biblical Response
Defend the vulnerable in word and deed: support mothers, adopt and foster, care for the dying, and advocate laws that protect life at every stage.
Scripture
Genesis 1:27; Psalm 139:13-16; Exodus 20:13
Pitfalls
Reducing the cause to one vote or one party; condemning without compassion for women in crisis; caring for the unborn while ignoring the born.
