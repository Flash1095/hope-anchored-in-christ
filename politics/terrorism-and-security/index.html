Terrorism and Security

Issue
Terrorism and Security
Summary
Attacks on the innocent demand both protection and moral clarity.
Biblical Principles
Life is sacred; evil must be restrained; vengeance belongs to God.
Biblical Response
Support just protection of the innocent; refuse hatred of whole peoples for the crimes of some; pray for enemies while restraining them.
Scripture
Romans 12:19-21; Romans 13:4; Matthew 5:44
Pitfalls
Answering terror with terror; answering terror with denial; abandoning justice for fear.
