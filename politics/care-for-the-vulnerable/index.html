Care for the Vulnerable

Issue
Care for the Vulnerable
Summary
Widows, orphans, the disabled, and the elderly are easily overlooked.
Biblical Principles
Pure religion cares for the fatherless and widow; the strong bear the weak.
Biblical Response
Visit, adopt, foster, employ, and include; build churches where the vulnerable belong, not merely receive.
Scripture
James 1:27; Psalm 82:3-4; Isaiah 1:17
Pitfalls
Pity without presence; programs without people; valuing the productive over the precious.
