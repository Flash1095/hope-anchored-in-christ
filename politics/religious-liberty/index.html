Religious Liberty

Issue
Religious Liberty
Summary
The freedom to worship, speak, and live according to conscience is under pressure in workplaces, schools, and public life.
Biblical Principles
Conscience is God's gift; the state is not lord of it; truth needs no coercion.
Biblical Response
Defend liberty for all faiths, not only our own; obey God over unjust mandates where conscience requires; bear witness graciously when the cost is real.
Scripture
Acts 5:29; Daniel 3:16-18; 1 Peter 2:13-17
Pitfalls
Claiming persecution over every inconvenience; using liberty as a cover for license; demanding rights while refusing duties.
