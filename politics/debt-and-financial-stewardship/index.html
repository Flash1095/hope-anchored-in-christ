Debt and Financial Stewardship

Issue
Debt and Financial Stewardship
Summary
Public and personal debt mortgage the future.
Biblical Principles
The borrower is servant to the lender; stewardship spans generations.
Biblical Response
Live within means; teach thrift; advocate budgets that do not steal from children yet unborn.
Scripture
Proverbs 22:7; Romans 13:8; Luke 14:28
Pitfalls
Debt-fueled living; austerity without compassion; pretending budgets are morally neutral.
