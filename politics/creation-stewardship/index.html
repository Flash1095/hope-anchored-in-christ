Creation Stewardship

Issue
Creation Stewardship
Summary
Climate, pollution, and resource use raise questions about our care for God's world.
Biblical Principles
Creation is God's, declared good; humanity is its steward, not its owner or its enemy.
Biblical Response
Tend the earth practically - waste less, care for land and creatures; support wise stewardship that also protects the poor from harm.
Scripture
Genesis 1:28; Genesis 2:15; Psalm 24:1
Pitfalls
Worshiping nature instead of its Maker; dismissing stewardship as politics; alarmism that harms the poor it claims to help.
