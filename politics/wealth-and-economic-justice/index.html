Wealth and Economic Justice

Issue
Wealth and Economic Justice
Summary
Inequality, wages, and economic systems raise questions of fairness and stewardship.
Biblical Principles
Wealth is a trust; the love of money is a root of evil; honest work deserves honest pay.
Biblical Response
Work diligently, pay fairly, give generously; support systems that reward work and protect the vulnerable - without envying or idolizing wealth.
Scripture
Proverbs 10:4; James 5:1-6; 1 Timothy 6:10
Pitfalls
Envy disguised as justice; greed disguised as freedom; pretending economics has no moral dimension.
