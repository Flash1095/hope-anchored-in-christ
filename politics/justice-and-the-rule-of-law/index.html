Justice and the Rule of Law

Issue
Justice and the Rule of Law
Summary
Courts, police, and laws either protect the weak or become tools of the powerful.
Biblical Principles
God loves justice; rulers are His servants for good; partiality is forbidden.
Biblical Response
Support just laws and honest institutions; hold power accountable; pray for judges and officers; refuse both lawlessness and idolatry of order.
Scripture
Micah 6:8; Romans 13:1-4; Leviticus 19:15
Pitfalls
Equating justice with one ideology; excusing corruption on 'our side'; cynicism that abandons institutions instead of reforming them.
