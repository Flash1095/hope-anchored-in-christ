Issue
Truth in Public Life
Summary
Misinformation, spin, and media distrust poison public conversation.
Biblical Principles
Truth is a moral absolute; bearing false witness destroys neighbors; light exposes darkness.
Biblical Response
Check before sharing; admit error quickly; reward honesty in leaders and media, even when it costs your side.
Scripture
Exodus 20:16; Proverbs 12:22; John 8:32
Pitfalls
Sharing what fits your tribe; treating disagreement as lying; cynicism that gives up on truth.
