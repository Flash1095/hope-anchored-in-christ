Race and Reconciliation

Issue
Race and Reconciliation
Summary
Racial division, historic injustice, and identity politics strain the body of Christ.
Biblical Principles
One blood, one image, one body; in Christ there is neither Jew nor Greek; reconciliation is the Gospel's fruit.
Biblical Response
Build real multiethnic fellowship; listen before lecturing; repent of partiality; pursue justice without adopting ideologies that deny grace.
Scripture
Galatians 3:28; Ephesians 2:14-16; James 2:9
Pitfalls
Denying real wounds; reducing reconciliation to slogans; letting secular frameworks replace biblical unity.
