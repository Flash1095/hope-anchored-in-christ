Providence

Doctrine
Providence
Summary
Providence is God's wise, powerful, and loving care by which He sustains, directs, and governs all creation and history toward His purposes. It gives Christians confidence that God is at work even in suffering and uncertainty, without making God the author of evil.
Biblical Support
Genesis 50:20; Psalm 103:19; Romans 8:28; Matthew 6:26; Acts 17:28
Historical Development
Taught throughout Scripture and developed by Augustine, Aquinas, and Calvin, providence received classic Reformed expression in the Westminster Confession (1646) and Heidelberg Catechism.
Interpretations
Christians agree God sovereignly cares for creation, while differing on how divine sovereignty and human responsibility relate, and on whether providence should be explained in more Calvinistic, Arminian, or open-theist terms.
