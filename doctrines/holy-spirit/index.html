Holy Spirit

Holy Spirit

Summary

The Holy Spirit is the third Person of the Trinity, fully God, who convicts of sin, regenerates believers, indwells God's people, and empowers them for holy living and witness. He glorifies Christ, applies salvation, and builds the church through His gifts and fruit.

Biblical Support

John 16:8-15; Acts 1:8; Romans 8:9; Galatians 5:22-23; 1 Corinthians 6:19

Historical Development

Confessed at Constantinople in AD 381 in the expanded Nicene Creed against those who denied the Spirit's deity, the Spirit's work was defended by Basil of Caesarea in On the Holy Spirit (c. AD 375).

Interpretations

Christians agree the Spirit is fully God and active today, while differing on the baptism in the Spirit, the continuation of miraculous gifts, and the language used for the Spirit's fullness and filling.
