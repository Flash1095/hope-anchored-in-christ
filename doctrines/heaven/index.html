Heaven

Doctrine
Heaven
Summary
Heaven is God's dwelling place and the future home of the redeemed, where God's people will live in His presence, free from sin, sorrow, and death, in a renewed creation. Christian hope looks not to escape the world forever, but to resurrection life in the new heaven and new earth.
Biblical Support
Revelation 21:3-4; John 14:2-3; Philippians 3:20; 2 Corinthians 5:8; Revelation 22:3-5
Historical Development
Confessed in historic creeds in the hope of resurrection and life everlasting, heaven was explored by Augustine in The City of God and by Aquinas and later writers on the beatific vision and eternal life.
Interpretations
Christians agree on eternal life with God, while differing on the intermediate state between death and resurrection and on how literally to read biblical images of heaven and the new creation.
Related Topics
Resurrection; Eternal Life; Second Coming; Hope
Related People
Augustine of Hippo; Thomas Aquinas; Jonathan Edwards; C. S. Lewis
