Angels

Doctrine
Angels
Summary
Angels are created spiritual beings who serve God as messengers, worshippers, and agents of His purposes, and who minister to God's people. Scripture presents them as real, personal, and powerful, while directing worship to God alone.
Biblical Support
Hebrews 1:14; Psalm 103:20; Luke 2:13-14; Matthew 18:10; Revelation 5:11
Historical Development
Taught throughout Scripture and early church writing, angelology was systematized by Pseudo-Dionysius (c. 500) and Aquinas in the Summa Theologiae, and affirmed in historic creeds and confessions.
Interpretations
Christians agree angels are real created servants of God, while differing on details such as ranks and orders of angels, guardian angels, and how much can be known beyond what Scripture states.

Creation; Heaven; Spiritual Warfare; Demons
Related People
