Resurrection

Doctrine
Resurrection
Summary
The Resurrection is the belief that Jesus rose bodily from the dead on the third day and that believers will also be raised to transformed, eternal life. It is central to Christian hope, confirming Jesus as Lord and guaranteeing the future renewal of creation.
Biblical Support
1 Corinthians 15:3-8; Luke 24:39; Romans 6:9; John 11:25; Revelation 21:4
Historical Development
Proclaimed from the earliest Christian preaching in Acts and confessed in the Apostles' and Nicene Creeds, the bodily resurrection was defended by early writers such as Irenaeus and Tertullian against Gnostic views.
Interpretations
Historic Christians agree on Christ's bodily resurrection and a future bodily resurrection, while differing on details of timing, the millennium, and the nature of the intermediate state.
