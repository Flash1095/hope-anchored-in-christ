Free Will

Free Will

Summary

Free Will concerns human ability to choose, and how that ability was affected by the Fall and restored by grace. Christians affirm real human responsibility for sin and for responding to God, while debating how free the fallen will is apart from grace.

Biblical Support

Deuteronomy 30:19; Joshua 24:15; John 7:17; Romans 6:16; Revelation 22:17

Historical Development

Debated by Augustine and Pelagius in the early 400s and by Erasmus and Luther in Bondage of the Will (1525), free will was addressed in the Canons of Dort and in Arminian and Wesleyan teaching on prevenient grace.

Interpretations

Reformed Christians teach the will is bound by sin until freed by effectual grace, Arminian and Wesleyan Christians teach grace enables a genuine response, and Molinist and other views seek middle positions.
