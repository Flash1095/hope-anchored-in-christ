Doctrine
Spiritual Gifts
Summary
Spiritual Gifts are abilities and ministries given by the Holy Spirit to each believer for the common good and the building up of the church. They include serving, teaching, mercy, leadership, generosity, prophecy, tongues, healing, and other gifts listed in Scripture.
Biblical Support
1 Corinthians 12:4-11; Romans 12:6-8; Ephesians 4:11-12; 1 Peter 4:10
Historical Development
Discussed by early church writers as the Spirit's work in the congregation, spiritual gifts received renewed attention with the Pentecostal movement beginning in the early 1900s and the later charismatic renewal.
Interpretations
Christians differ on whether certain miraculous gifts ceased with the apostolic age (cessationism) or continue today (continuationism), and on how gifts such as tongues and prophecy should be practiced and tested in the church.
