Virgin Birth

Doctrine
Virgin Birth
Summary
The Virgin Birth teaches that Jesus was conceived in Mary by the Holy Spirit, without a human father, so that He is truly the Son of God and truly human. It signals that salvation is God's gracious initiative and that Jesus is uniquely set apart from birth.
Biblical Support
Isaiah 7:14; Matthew 1:18-25; Luke 1:35; Galatians 4:4
Historical Development
Confessed in the Apostles' and Nicene Creeds as born of the Virgin Mary, the doctrine was defended by Ignatius, Irenaeus, and later councils as part of confessing the true identity of Christ.
Interpretations
Historic Christian traditions — Catholic, Orthodox, and Protestant — affirm the Virgin Birth, while Christians discuss the precise prophetic use of Isaiah 7:14 and the doctrine's relationship to teachings about Mary.
