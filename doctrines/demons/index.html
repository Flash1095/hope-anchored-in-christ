Demons

Doctrine
Demons
Summary
Demons are fallen angels who rebelled against God under Satan and now oppose God and His people, though they remain limited by God's sovereignty. Christ's life, death, and resurrection decisively defeated their power, and their final judgment is certain.
Biblical Support
Mark 1:24; James 2:19; Colossians 2:15; Revelation 12:9; Ephesians 6:12
Historical Development
Taught in Scripture and early church writings on spiritual warfare, the fall of angels was discussed by Augustine and Aquinas, who placed demons within a larger account of evil and God's providence.
Interpretations
Christians agree on the reality of evil spiritual powers and Christ's victory, while differing on explanations of demon possession, territorial spirits, and the methods emphasized in deliverance ministry.
Related Topics
Angels; Satan; Spiritual Warfare; Sin; Atonement
Related People
Augustine of Hippo; Thomas Aquinas; Martin Luther; C. S. Lewis
