Priesthood of Believers

Doctrine
Priesthood of Believers
Summary
The Priesthood of Believers teaches that through Christ every Christian has direct access to God and shares in the church's ministry of worship, prayer, witness, and service. It does not remove the need for church leaders, but affirms no human priest stands between the believer and God besides Christ.
Biblical Support
1 Peter 2:5; 1 Peter 2:9; Revelation 1:6; Hebrews 10:19-22; Romans 12:1
Historical Development
Grounded in the New Testament's teaching on Christ as High Priest, it was recovered with special force by Luther and the Reformers in the 1500s against a medieval system that emphasized a separate sacerdotal priesthood.
Interpretations
Protestants strongly emphasize this doctrine, while Roman Catholic and Orthodox traditions affirm a common priesthood of the baptized alongside a distinct ministerial priesthood, differing on how the two relate.

Related People
