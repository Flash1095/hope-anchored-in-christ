Total Depravity

Doctrine
Total Depravity
Summary
Total Depravity teaches that sin affects every part of human nature — mind, will, affections, and body — so no one can come to God apart from grace. It does not mean people are as evil as they could possibly be, but that no part is untouched by the Fall.
Biblical Support
Genesis 6:5; Jeremiah 17:9; Romans 3:10-18; Ephesians 2:1; Titus 1:15
Historical Development
Formulated in Reformed theology after Augustine's teaching on grace and given classic expression in the Canons of Dort (1618-1619) as the first of the Five Points of Calvinism.
Interpretations
Reformed Christians stress total inability apart from effectual grace, while Arminian and Wesleyan Christians teach depravity is real but can be responded to through prevenient grace given to all.
