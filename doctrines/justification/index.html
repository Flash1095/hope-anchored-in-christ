Justification

Justification

Summary

Justification is God's gracious act of declaring sinners righteous on the basis of Christ's righteousness, received through faith. It is a legal declaration of pardon and acceptance, distinct from the lifelong process of becoming holy.

Biblical Support

Romans 3:24; Romans 5:1; Galatians 2:16; Philippians 3:9; Romans 8:1

Historical Development

A central issue of the Reformation in the 1500s, it was defended by Luther and Calvin against late medieval views, and addressed by the Council of Trent (1545-1563), which gave the Roman Catholic response.

Interpretations

Protestants generally teach justification by faith alone as a declaration, while Roman Catholics and Eastern Orthodox understand it as also including inner renewal, though all agree it rests on God's grace in Christ.
