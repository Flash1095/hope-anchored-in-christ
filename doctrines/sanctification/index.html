Sanctification

Sanctification

Summary

Sanctification is God's ongoing work of making believers holy in character and conduct, by the Holy Spirit through faith. Christians are set apart at conversion and grow in likeness to Christ throughout their lives.

Biblical Support

1 Thessalonians 4:3; Hebrews 10:14; 2 Corinthians 3:18; Philippians 2:12-13; 1 Peter 1:15-16

Historical Development

Developed from biblical teaching on holiness by Augustine and the Reformers, it was given special emphasis in the Wesleyan and Holiness movements of the 1700s and 1800s and in later Keswick teaching.

Interpretations

Christians differ on whether a distinct second blessing or entire sanctification is taught in Scripture, and on how instantaneous and progressive aspects of holiness relate, while agreeing growth in holiness is essential.
