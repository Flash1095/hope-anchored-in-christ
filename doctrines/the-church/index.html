The Church

The Church

Summary

The Church is the people of God, called out from the world, united to Christ by the Spirit, and gathered in local congregations for worship, teaching, fellowship, the ordinances, and mission. Jesus is its Head, and every believer is a member of His body.

Biblical Support

Matthew 16:18; Acts 2:42; Ephesians 1:22-23; 1 Corinthians 12:27; 1 Peter 2:9

Historical Development

Described in the New Testament and confessed in the Nicene Creed as one, holy, catholic, and apostolic, the church's nature was debated at the Reformation over authority, sacraments, and marks of a true church.

Interpretations

Christians differ on church government — episcopal, presbyterian, or congregational — and on the relationship between the universal church and local churches and denominations.
