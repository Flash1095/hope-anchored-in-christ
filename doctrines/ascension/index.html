Ascension

Doctrine
Ascension
Summary
The Ascension is Christ's return to the Father in glory forty days after His resurrection, where He reigns at God's right hand and intercedes for His people. It marks the exaltation of Jesus as Lord and the sending of the Holy Spirit to the church.
Biblical Support
Acts 1:9-11; Luke 24:51; Ephesians 1:20-22; Hebrews 4:14; 1 Timothy 3:16
Historical Development
Celebrated in the early church and confessed in the Apostles' and Nicene Creeds — He ascended into heaven and is seated at the right hand of the Father — the Ascension was linked by early writers to Christ's kingship and priesthood.
Interpretations
Christians across traditions affirm the Ascension, while differing mainly in emphasis: some stress Christ's ongoing heavenly ministry and intercession, others His kingly reign and the church's mission until His return.
