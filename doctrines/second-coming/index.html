Second Coming

Doctrine
Second Coming
Summary
The Second Coming is the promised return of Jesus Christ in glory to judge the living and the dead, defeat evil, and establish God's kingdom in full. Christians live in hopeful readiness, working and praying until He comes.
Biblical Support
Acts 1:11; Matthew 24:30; 1 Thessalonians 4:16-17; Titus 2:13; Revelation 22:20
Historical Development
Confessed in the Apostles' and Nicene Creeds from the early centuries, expectation of Christ's return shaped early church hope, medieval theology, and Reformation and modern evangelical teaching.
Interpretations
Christians agree Christ will return personally and visibly, while differing on the timing of the rapture and tribulation and on millennial views, including premillennial, amillennial, and postmillennial readings.
Related Topics
Eschatology; Judgment; Millennial Views; Hope; Kingdom of God
Related People
Irenaeus; Augustine of Hippo; John Calvin; John Darby
