Perseverance of the Saints

Doctrine
Perseverance of the Saints
Summary
Perseverance of the Saints teaches that those truly united to Christ will be kept by God's power and will endure in faith to the end. It gives assurance not from human strength, but from God's faithfulness to complete His saving work.
Biblical Support
John 10:28-29; Philippians 1:6; Romans 8:38-39; Jude 24; 1 Peter 1:5
Historical Development
Rooted in Augustine's teaching on the gift of perseverance and affirmed by the Reformers, it was stated as the fifth point in the Canons of Dort (1618-1619).
Interpretations
Reformed Christians teach true believers cannot finally fall away, while Arminian, Wesleyan, and many other Christians warn that believers should heed Scripture's warnings and may teach the possibility of apostasy.
Related Topics
Assurance; Salvation; Eternal Security; Faith
Related People
Augustine of Hippo; John Calvin; Dort Synod; John Owen
