Original Sin

Doctrine
Original Sin
Summary
Original Sin is the doctrine that Adam's first sin affected all humanity, so every person inherits a fallen nature and a tendency toward sin. It explains both the universality of sin and the universal need for God's grace in Christ.
Biblical Support
Genesis 3; Psalm 51:5; Romans 5:12-19; 1 Corinthians 15:22; Ephesians 2:3
Historical Development
Developed most fully by Augustine in his debates with Pelagius in the early 400s and affirmed at the Council of Carthage in AD 418, it became a major theme for Aquinas, Luther, and Calvin.
Interpretations
Christians differ on how Adam's guilt and corruption are passed on, with Eastern Orthodoxy speaking more of ancestral sin and mortality, and Pelagian and some Arminian views placing greater stress on personal choice.
