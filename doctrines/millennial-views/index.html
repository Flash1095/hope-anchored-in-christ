Doctrine
Millennial Views
Summary
Millennial Views concern how Christians understand the thousand years of Revelation 20 and Christ's reign in relation to His return. The main historic positions are premillennialism, amillennialism, and postmillennialism, all seeking to honor the hope of Christ's kingdom.
Biblical Support
Revelation 20:1-6; 1 Corinthians 15:24-25; Matthew 24:30; Acts 1:6-7; Isaiah 11:6-9
Historical Development
Premillennial expectation appears in early writers such as Papias and Irenaeus, amillennial reading was shaped by Augustine in The City of God, and postmillennial hope rose in the Reformation and Great Awakening eras.
Interpretations
Premillennialists expect Christ to return before a literal millennium, amillennialists read the millennium as Christ's present reign, and postmillennialists expect gospel progress before Christ returns; Christians hold these as secondary views.
Related Topics
Second Coming; Eschatology; Kingdom of God; Resurrection
Related People
Irenaeus; Augustine of Hippo; Jonathan Edwards; John Darby
