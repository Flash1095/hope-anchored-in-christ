Inspiration of Scripture

Doctrine
Inspiration of Scripture
Summary
Inspiration teaches that Scripture is God-breathed: God worked through human authors, in their own styles and settings, so that what they wrote is His Word. Christians therefore receive the Bible as authoritative for faith and life.
Biblical Support
2 Timothy 3:16; 2 Peter 1:21; John 10:35; Hebrews 1:1; Psalm 119:105
Historical Development
Affirmed by early church fathers such as Irenaeus and Augustine as the church recognized the canon, inspiration was reaffirmed by the Reformers and discussed in modern statements such as the Chicago Statement (1978).
Interpretations
Christians agree Scripture is inspired, while differing on models of inspiration, the precise relationship between divine and human authorship, and how inspiration applies to translations and textual variants.

Related People
