Hell

Hell

Summary

Hell is the solemn doctrine of final judgment for persistent rebellion against God, described in Scripture with images of darkness, fire, and separation from God's gracious presence. It underscores both God's justice and the seriousness of human response to Christ.

Biblical Support

Matthew 25:41; Mark 9:48; Revelation 20:14-15; 2 Thessalonians 1:9; Luke 16:23

Historical Development

Taught by Jesus and the apostles and affirmed in early creeds and by Augustine and Aquinas, hell was a major topic in medieval theology and in Jonathan Edwards and other preachers on judgment.

Interpretations

Christians who affirm final judgment differ on the nature and duration of punishment, with traditional eternal conscious punishment, conditional immortality or annihilationism, and other views discussed, while universalism is rejected by historic confessions.
