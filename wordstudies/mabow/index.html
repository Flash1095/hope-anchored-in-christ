mâbôwʼ

an entrance (the place or the act); specifically sunset or the west; also (adverb with preposition) towards

Biblical Usage

The word names an entrance, the place of going in, and it is used for the going down of the sun, the west. The blessings and curses were set on the far side of Jordan, by the way where the sun goes down, and Joshua's border ran to the great sea toward the going down of the sun. Malachi says God's name shall be great from the rising of the sun even unto the going down of the same, and the psalm says the sun knows his going down.

Theological Significance

I believe the going down of the sun marks the edge of the day God made, and His name is great from its rising to its setting. I hold that the sun's faithful going down is a picture of God's own faithfulness, and the whole earth from east to west belongs to Him.
