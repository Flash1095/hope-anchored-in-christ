éxōthen

from G1854; external(-ly):--out(-side, -ward, - wardly), (from) without.

Biblical Usage

éxōthen means out(-side, -ward, - wardly), (from) without. In Matthew 23:25, the text says "hypocrites! for ye make clean the outside of the cup and of the platter." In Mark 7:15, the text says "There is nothing from without a man."

Theological Significance

éxōthen convinces me that purity is not prudishness; it is keeping ourselves for the God who made us. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
