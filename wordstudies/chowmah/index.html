chôwmâh

a wall of protection

Biblical Usage

A wall holds back the sea for Israel at the Red Sea, and it holds up Rahab's house on the edge of Jericho. The same word names the fortified cities of the land and the walls that fall when Israel shouts. Walls protect, and walls shut people out, and Scripture uses both sides of that truth.

Theological Significance

I believe walls in the Bible show where human safety ends and God's protection begins. The sea stood as a wall because God held it, and Jericho's wall fell because God brought it down. I hold that no wall is stronger than the God who commands it, and no wall is safer than the one He builds.
