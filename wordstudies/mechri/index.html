méchri

or μεχρίς from G3372; as far as, i.e. up to a certain point (as a preposition, of extent (denoting the terminus, whereas G891 refers especially to the space of time or place intervening) or conjunction):--till, (un-)to, until.

Biblical Usage

The word means as far as, until, and it marks the end of a road: Capernaum is told it shall be brought down to hell, though exalted to heaven, and death reigned from Adam until Moses. Paul says the gifts of the church will remain until we all come to the unity of the faith.

Theological Significance

I hold that until is the word of a limit: death reigned until Moses, judgment waits until the end, and the church's gifts last until unity comes — and every until in Scripture has a God who set it.
