bythízō

from G1037; to sink; by implication, to drown:--begin to sink, drown.

Biblical Usage

Here is bythízō, the word for to sink, to drown. It turns up in 1 Timothy, and the meaning is as plain as the word is short.

Theological Significance

I hold that bythízō is small but not slight: to sink, to drown is part of the picture too.
