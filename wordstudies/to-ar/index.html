tôʼar

outline, i.e. figure or appearance

Biblical Usage

Form and appearance are what this word measures, and the Bible measures them carefully. Adonijah was a very goodly man, and David's servant describes the son of Jesse as cunning in playing, a mighty valiant man, prudent in matters, and a comely person, with the Lord beside him.

Theological Significance

I believe the two descriptions warn me in both directions: good looks crowned a rebel in Adonijah, and the same good looks in David came with a heart the Lord had chosen.
