Chûwshay

Chushai, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Hushai is David's friend, the Archite who meets him on the Mount of Olives when Absalom has taken Jerusalem. David sends him back to defeat Ahithophel's counsel, and his advice saves David's life. He also appears as the father of Baana, one of Solomon's officers.

Theological Significance

I believe Hushai shows that loyalty in dark days is worth more than cleverness in easy ones. Ahithophel's counsel was better, and Hushai's was worse, yet God used the worse counsel to save David.
