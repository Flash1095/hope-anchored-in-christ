ʼallâh

{an oak or other strong tree}

Biblical Usage

Once, when Joshua sets up the stone of witness under the oak by the sanctuary of the LORD at Shechem, after the people have said, The LORD our God will we serve, and his voice will we obey. The word is the strong tree that stands over the stone, the witness to the promise.

Theological Significance

One word for an oak carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the tree it names, that the promise at Shechem was made under a tree that would outlast the men who made it.
