tiphʼârâh

ornament (abstractly or concretely, literally or figuratively)

Biblical Usage

Glory and beauty are the pair this word carries. Aaron's holy garments were made for glory and for beauty, the Lord Himself shall be a crown of glory and a diadem of beauty to the residue of His people, honour and majesty are before Him, and Esther's king showed the riches of his glorious kingdom for a hundred and eighty days.

Theological Significance

I hold that beauty is not an extra to God's glory but part of it: the garments, the crown, the sanctuary and the kingdom all shine because He does. I believe a faith that has no room for beauty has missed something the Bible keeps showing — that what belongs to God is glorious, and its glory is beautiful.
