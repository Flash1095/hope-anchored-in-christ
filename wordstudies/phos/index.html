phos

Word
φῶς
Language
Greek
Transliteration
phos
Strongs Number
G5457
Definition
Light — physical light and, figuratively, truth, purity and life; used of God, of Christ as Light of the world, and of His people.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
John 8:12; John 1:4-5; 1 John 1:5; Matthew 5:14-16
Biblical Usage
Phos opens John's Gospel: the life was the light of men, and the darkness has not overcome it. Jesus claims the title, then astonishingly gives it to His followers: 'You are the light of the world.'
Theological Significance
Phos is hope with a guarantee: darkness never wins. The Light has come, still shines, and now shines through ordinary people in ordinary places.
