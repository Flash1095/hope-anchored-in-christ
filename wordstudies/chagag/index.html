châgag

Word
חָגַג
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
châgag
Strongs Number
H2287
Definition
properly, to move in a circle, i.e. (specifically) to march in a sacred procession, to observe a festival; by implication, to be giddy
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to hold a feast, hold a festival, make pilgrimage, keep a pilgrim-feast, celebrate, dance, stagger; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to keep a pilgrim-feast; 1a2) to reel
