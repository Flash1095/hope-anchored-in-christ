Mᵉshêyzabʼêl

Meshezabel, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Meshezabeel is an Israelite of the return, a father whose sons built the wall and served the king. Nehemiah 3:4 says Meshullam the son of Berechiah, the son of Meshezabeel, repaired the wall, and Nehemiah 10:21 lists Meshezabeel among those who sealed the covenant. Nehemiah 11:24 says Pethahiah the son of Meshezabeel was at the king's hand in all matters concerning the people.

Theological Significance

I believe one family can span the whole return: a son on the wall, a name on the covenant, and a grandson at the king's hand.
