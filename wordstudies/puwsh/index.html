pûwsh

to spread; figuratively, act proudly

Biblical Usage

Push means to spread, to spread out, and figuratively to act proudly. Habakkuk says the Chaldeans' horses are swifter than the leopards, and their horsemen shall spread themselves, and Jeremiah says Babylon is grown fat as the heifer at grass, and belloweth as bulls. And Malachi says the righteous shall go forth, and grow up as calves of the stall.

Theological Significance

I hold that spreading is the picture of both pride and joy: the horsemen spread to conquer, Babylon spreads to gloat, and the righteous spread to run free in the sunshine.
