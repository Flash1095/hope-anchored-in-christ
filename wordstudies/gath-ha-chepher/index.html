Gath-ha-Chêpher

Gath-Chepher, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Gath-hepher is a town in Zebulun, known as the home of the prophet Jonah. Jeroboam restored Israel's coast according to the word the LORD spoke by Jonah, the son of Amittai, the prophet, which was of Gath-hepher, in 2 Kings 14:25. The border of Zebulun passes by Gittah-hepher in Joshua 19:13, the same town under a slightly different spelling.

Theological Significance

I believe a prophet's hometown matters, because God calls men from small places. Jonah came from Gath-hepher and was sent to Nineveh, and I hold that the God who sent a man from a small town to a great city still sends small people on great errands.
