Chaldaîos

probably of Hebrew or (H3778); a Chaldæan (i.e. Kasdi), or native or the region of the lower Euphrates:--Chaldæan.

Biblical Usage

Chaldaios is a Chaldean, a man of Chaldea — and it appears once, when Stephen says Abraham came out of the land of the Chaldeans and dwelt in Haran.

Theological Significance

I believe the Chaldeans are named once so we remember where Abraham started: God called a man out of Chaldea, and the calling never stopped.
