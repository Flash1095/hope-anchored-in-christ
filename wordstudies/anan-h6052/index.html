ʻÂnân

Anan, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Anan is named once, in the list of those who sealed the covenant in Nehemiah's day: 'And Ahijah, Hanan, Anan.' He was one of the men who put his name to the sealed promise that the returned exiles would keep God's law, and the Bible tells us nothing more about him.

Theological Significance

A name on a sealed covenant is all we have of Anan, and I believe it is enough: he put his name to God's law with the rest of the returned people, and the list remembers him for it.
