ʼâyôm

frightful

Biblical Usage

Ayom means frightful, terrible — the word for what makes the heart quail. Habakkuk says the Chaldeans are terrible and dreadful, and the Song says the beloved is terrible as an army with banners. The same terror, in a lover's face and in a conqueror's army.

Theological Significance

I hold that God's people are meant to be terrible to his enemies the way an army with banners is terrible — not by cruelty, but by the sheer weight of being his.
