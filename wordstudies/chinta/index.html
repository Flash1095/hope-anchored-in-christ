chinṭâʼ

wheat

Biblical Usage

Chinta is the Aramaic word for wheat, and it appears in the supplies ordered for the restored temple. The king's letter provides wheat for the priests and their offerings, and Ezra's commission lists a hundred measures of wheat among the gifts for the house of God.

Theological Significance

I believe the wheat in these lists is provision with a purpose, because God feeds His people so that they can worship.
