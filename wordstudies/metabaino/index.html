metabaínō

from G3326 and the base of G939; to change place:--depart, go, pass, remove.

Biblical Usage

Metabaino means to change place, to move on from where you were. John uses it for the greatest move of all, passing from death to life, and for Jesus departing out of this world to the Father. Matthew and Acts use it more plainly, of Jesus leaving a town and Paul leaving a synagogue.

Theological Significance

I believe this word draws the line between two kinds of leaving: the world asks Jesus to depart from its coasts, but the believer passes from death to life and never goes back. Jesus' own departing was not an escape; it was His hour, the way home to the Father.
