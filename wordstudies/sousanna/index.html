Sousánna

of Hebrew origin (H7799 feminine); lily; Susannah (i.e. Shoshannah), an Israelitess:--Susanna.

Biblical Usage

Susanna is named once, among the women who ministered unto Jesus of their substance as He went through every city and village preaching. Her name means lily, and she is remembered for giving what she had to the work.

Theological Significance

I believe Susanna shows what support looks like: she gave of her substance, and the gospel travelled on it.
