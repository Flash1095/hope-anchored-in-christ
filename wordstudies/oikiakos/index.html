oikiakós

from G3614; familiar, i.e. (as noun) relatives:--they (them) of (his own) household.

Biblical Usage

Oikiakos means of one's own household, the people under your own roof. Jesus warns in Matthew 10:25 that if they called the master of the house Beelzebub, they will call His household worse, and in Matthew 10:36 a man's foes shall be they of his own household.

Theological Significance

I believe the hardest opposition comes from inside the house, and following Jesus can divide the very roof you sleep under.
