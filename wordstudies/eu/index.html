eû

neuter of a primary εὖς (good); (adverbially) well:--good, well (done).

Biblical Usage

Eû means well — good, well done. The servant hears 'well done, thou good and faithful servant', and Paul says it shall be well with those who honour their parents. It is the word for God's approval, spoken plainly.

Theological Significance

I believe eû is the word I want to hear one day: well done. I hold that living well now is the way to hear it then.
