epistolḗ

from G1989; a written message:--"epistle," letter.

Biblical Usage

Epistole is a letter, an epistle — a written message that travels where the writer cannot. Saul desires letters to Damascus to bind the disciples; Paul says, ye are our epistle written in our hearts, known and read of all men; and Peter warns that in Paul's epistles are some things hard to be understood, which the unlearned wrestle to their destruction.

Theological Significance

A page carries the writer's heart across the miles, and a church becomes a letter others can read. I believe the letter is God's chosen way to speak across distance, and my life is the epistle my neighbours are reading.
