kermatistḗs

from a derivative of G2772; a handler of coins, i.e. money-broker:--changer of money.

Biblical Usage

Kermatistes means a money-changer, a handler of coins. John 2:14 says Jesus found in the temple those that sold oxen and sheep and doves, and the changers of money sitting.

Theological Significance

I believe the changers sat comfortably in God's house until Jesus stood up, and I want no seat where he would overturn the table.
