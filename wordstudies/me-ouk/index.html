mḕ ouk

may ook i.e. G3361 and G3756; as interrogative and negative, is it not that?:--neither (followed by no), + never, not. Compare G3364.

Biblical Usage

The word is two negatives together, not, and it is the question that expects no for an answer: Paul asks, have they not heard, and have we not power to eat and to drink, and to lead about a sister, a wife. The two words stand side by side in the Greek, and together they ask the question that answers itself.

Theological Significance

I believe the double negative is Paul at his most human: he asks the question and knows the answer, and the church is left to admit that the gospel's messengers have every right to live by the gospel.
