diairéō

from G1223 and G138; to separate, i.e. distribute:--divide.

Biblical Usage

To divide is to part a thing out, and the two places here could not be more different. The father in the parable divides his living between his sons, and the Spirit divides His gifts to every man severally as He wills.

Theological Significance

I hold that the Spirit's dividing is the opposite of the father's; one division wastes an inheritance on riotous living, and the other gives every believer exactly the gift the body needs, and both are left in God's hands.
