maʻămâd

(figuratively) a position

Biblical Usage

The word names a station or standing place, the post where a man stands to serve, and it is used for the queen of Sheba's wonder and for the priests of the temple. She saw the attendance of Solomon's ministers and their apparel and the cupbearers and their ascent, and there was no more spirit in her. Isaiah says God will drive Shebna from his station, and the Levites stood in their station to serve in the house of the LORD.

Theological Significance

I believe a station is the place God gives a man to stand, and faithfulness is standing there until He moves us. I hold that Shebna lost his station by pride and the Levites kept theirs by service, and the difference is everything.
