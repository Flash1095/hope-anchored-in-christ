Niçrôk

Nisrok, a Babylonian idol

Biblical Usage

Nisroch is the god Sennacherib is worshipping when his own sons kill him. The king who mocked the LORD in his letters goes into the house of his god, and Adrammelech and Sharezer strike him down with the sword in the very place he trusted.

Theological Significance

I hold that the end of Sennacherib is the answer to his boasting: the god he worshipped could not keep his own house safe.
