Midyânîy

a Midjanite or descendant (native) of Midjan

Biblical Usage

Midianite merchantmen bought Joseph out of the pit and sold him into Egypt, Hobab the Midianite was Moses' father-in-law and was asked to journey with Israel, and a Midianite woman was brought into the camp in the sight of Moses while the congregation wept. The word names the people of Midian, and their story runs from Joseph's sale to the plague at Baal-peor.

Theological Significance

I believe the Midianites show how a people can be both kin and snare: Hobab was family and guide, the merchants sold Joseph, and the women of Midian brought the plague. A people can be both kin and snare, and Israel learned it from Midian twice over.
