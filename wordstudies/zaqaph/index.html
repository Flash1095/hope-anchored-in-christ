zâqaph

to lift, i.e. (figuratively) comfort

Biblical Usage

Zaqaph means to lift up, to raise up, to uphold. In Psalms 145:14, "The LORD upholdeth all that fall, and raiseth up all those that be bowed down." Psalms 146:8 says the same again, "The LORD openeth the eyes of the blind: the LORD raiseth them that are bowed down," and the old gloss "to life" is a misprint for "to lift," which is what the verses show.

Theological Significance

I believe this word is one of the tenderest in the psalms, because the Lord's work is to raise up the bowed down, and anyone who feels bent low can take Psalms 145:14 as a promise, not just a picture.
