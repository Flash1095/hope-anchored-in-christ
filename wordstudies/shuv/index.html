shuv

Word
שׁוּב
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
shuv
Strongs Number
H7725
Definition
To return, turn back and repent — turning away from sin and returning home to God.
Part Of Speech
verb
Key Passages
Hosea 14:1; Joel 2:12-13; Zechariah 1:3; Deuteronomy 30:2-3
Biblical Usage
Shuv is the prophets' great call: 'Return to the Lord your God, for you have stumbled.' It pictures repentance as a homecoming journey, and God promises that when Israel returns, He restores.
Theological Significance
Shuv means no one has wandered too far to come home. Repentance is not grovelling — it is turning toward a Father who says, 'Return to Me, and I will return to you.'
