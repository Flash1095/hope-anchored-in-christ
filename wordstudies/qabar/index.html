qâbar

to inter

Biblical Usage

Burying is how the story marks the end of a life and the claim on the land: Abraham buried Sarah in the cave of Machpelah, Jacob was carried up and buried with his fathers, Samuel was buried at Ramah, David slept with his fathers and was buried in the city of David. Moses was buried by God Himself in a valley in Moab, where no man knows his sepulchre to this day.

Theological Significance

I believe burial in Scripture is never just disposal, it is a statement of hope, because the body is laid down where God can raise it. What moves me most is Moses: no human hands buried him, God did, and that tells me the dead are never out of His keeping.
