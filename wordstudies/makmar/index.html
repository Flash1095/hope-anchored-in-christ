makmâr

a (hunter's) net (as dark from concealment)

Biblical Usage

Makmar is the hunter's net, and both of its appearances are about being caught in one. Isaiah 51:20 has Jerusalem's sons lying at the head of the streets as a wild bull in a net, and Psalms 141:10 prays, let the wicked fall into their own nets, whilst that I withal escape.

Theological Significance

I hold that the net in Psalms 141:10 is justice itself: the trap set for the righteous catches the trapper.
