ménō

a primary verb; to stay (in a given place, state, relation or expectancy):--abide, continue, dwell, endure, be present, remain, stand, tarry (for), X thine own.

Biblical Usage

Meno means to abide, to stay, to remain where you were put, and it is the word John builds his gospel on. John 15:4 commands, Abide in me, and I in you, as the branch cannot bear fruit except it abide in the vine, and John 6:56 promises that he that eateth My flesh and drinketh My blood dwelleth in Me, and I in him. 1 John 4:16 says he that dwelleth in love dwelleth in God, and God in him, and 1 John 2:6 says he that saith he abideth in Him ought to walk even as He walked.

Theological Significance

I believe abiding is not a feeling but a staying: in the vine, in the love, in the walk. My position is that fruit comes from staying put, not from striving, and the one who abides in Christ has Christ abiding in him.
