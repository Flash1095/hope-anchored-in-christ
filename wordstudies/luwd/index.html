Definition
Lud, the name of two nations
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Lud or Lydia = "strife"; n pr m; 1) the 4th listed son of Shem and supposed progenitor of the Lydians; n patr; 2) descendants of Lud the son of Shem who settled in northern Africa
