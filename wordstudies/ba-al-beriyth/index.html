Baʻal Bᵉrîyth

Baal-Berith, a special deity of the Shechemites

Biblical Usage

Baal-berith was the god of the covenant at Shechem — the god Israel made after Gideon's death, and the god in whose temple Abimelech found seventy pieces of silver to hire vain and light persons to kill his brothers. The people forgot the Lord and served the god of the covenant they made with a lie.

Theological Significance

I believe Baal-berith is the clearest warning in Judges: a covenant with a false god pays for murder, and the silver from his temple bought seventy graves.
