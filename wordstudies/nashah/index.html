nâshâh

Word
נָשָׁה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
nâshâh
Strongs Number
H5382
Definition
to forget; figuratively, to neglect; causatively, to remit, remove
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to forget, deprive; 1a) (Qal) to forget; 1b) (Niphal) to be forgotten; 1c) (Piel) to cause to forget; 1d) (Hiphil) to cause to forget, allow to be forgotten
