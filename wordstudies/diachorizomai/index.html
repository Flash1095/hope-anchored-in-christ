diachōrízomai

from G1223 and the middle voice of G5563; to remove (oneself) wholly, i.e. retire:--depart.

Biblical Usage

This word means to depart or withdraw, and it stands only once, at the moment Moses and Elijah depart from Jesus on the mountain. Peter speaks up as they are leaving, not knowing what he says, because the glory is too much for him.

Theological Significance

I hold that Peter's words on the mountain are a comfort to me; he spoke in wonder and got it wrong, and Jesus did not rebuke him for it — He simply let the cloud speak instead.
