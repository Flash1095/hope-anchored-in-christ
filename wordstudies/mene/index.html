mᵉnêʼ

numbered

Biblical Usage

Mene is the word of the writing on the wall, numbered. Daniel 5:25 gives the writing itself, MENE, MENE, TEKEL, UPHARSIN, and Daniel 5:26 gives the reading: MENE, God hath numbered thy kingdom, and finished it.

Theological Significance

I believe God numbers kingdoms the way He numbers days, and when the count is finished the kingdom is finished too.
