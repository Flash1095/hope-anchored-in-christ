éntalma

from G1781; an injunction, i.e. religious precept:--commandment.

Biblical Usage

An entalma is a commandment — but in every New Testament use it is the commandments of men. Jesus says they worship in vain, teaching for doctrines the commandments of men, in Mark 7:7 and Matthew 15:9, and Paul says the things that perish with the using are after the commandments and doctrines of men in Colossians 2:22.

Theological Significance

I believe the difference between God's commandments and men's is the difference between life and vain worship — one frees, the other only burdens. I hold that any rule that stands on human authority alone, however old or pious, is not binding on my conscience.
