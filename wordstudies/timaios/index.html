Timaîos

probably of Chaldee origin (compare H2931); Timæus (i.e. Timay), an Israelite:--Timæus.

Biblical Usage

Timaeus is known to us only as a father. Mark tells us the blind beggar at Jericho was Bartimaeus, the son of Timaeus, and that is all the record gives: a name, a son, and a roadside where the son sat begging.

Theological Significance

I believe there is something tender in that: a man whose whole place in Scripture is to be named as the father of the one Jesus healed, and that is enough.
