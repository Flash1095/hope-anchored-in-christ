chăbôl

a pawn (as security for debt)

Biblical Usage

Chabol is a pledge, something held as security for a debt. Ezekiel draws the righteous man twice over: he has restored the debtor's pledge, given bread to the hungry, and spoiled none by violence, while the wicked has withheld the pledge and oppressed the poor. The repentant man proves his change by restoring the pledge and giving back what he robbed.

Theological Significance

I believe a pledge is a test of the heart: the righteous man gives it back, the wicked man keeps it, and the repentant man proves his repentance by returning it. God watches what we hold over the poor.
