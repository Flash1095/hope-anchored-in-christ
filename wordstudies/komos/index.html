kōmos

from G2749; a carousal (as if letting loose):--revelling, rioting.

Biblical Usage

Komos is a carousal, revelling, rioting. Romans 13:13 says, let us walk honestly, as in the day, not in rioting and drunkenness, not in chambering and wantonness, not in strife and envying, Galatians 5:21 lists revellings among the works of the flesh that shall not inherit the kingdom of God, and 1 Peter 4:3 says the time past of our life may suffice us to have wrought the will of the Gentiles, when we walked in revellings, banquetings, and abominable idolatries.

Theological Significance

I believe Peter's word is the key to the other two: the time past may suffice us, because the revels are behind us, not before us. I hold that the works of the flesh are named so plainly because God wants us to recognize them, not to argue about them.
