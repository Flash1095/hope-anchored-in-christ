ʻAmmôwn

Ammon, a son of Lot; also his posterity and their country

Biblical Usage

Ammon, the son of Lot by his younger daughter, and his people are Israel's neighbours and enemies for a thousand years. Ben-ammi, the son of my people, is the father of the children of Ammon unto this day; Moses is told not to distress them, for God has given their land to the children of Lot for a possession; and Jephthah sends messengers to the king of the children of Ammon, asking what he has to do with him, that he comes to fight in his land.

Theological Significance

The children of Lot are given their land by God and still come to fight for it, and that long neighbourhood is the whole story: Israel is told to leave them alone, and they will not leave Israel alone.
