Mibchâr

Mibchar, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Mibchar the son of Haggeri stands in the list of David's mighty men, and the King James spells his name Mibhar. He is named once, and his whole story is that he stood with David.

Theological Significance

I hold that a single verse can still honour a man: Mibchar is remembered among the mighty because he was there, and David's list keeps his name.
