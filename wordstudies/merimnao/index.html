merimnáō

from G3308; to be anxious about:--(be, have) care(-ful), take thought.

Biblical Usage

Merimnao is the verb, to be anxious, to take thought, to care. Matthew 6:25 commands, Take no thought for your life, what ye shall eat or drink, and Luke 10:41 has Jesus say to Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things. 1 Corinthians 7:32 says the unmarried care for the things of the Lord, and Philippians 2:20 praises Timothy, who will naturally care for the Philippians' state.

Theological Significance

I believe the word is neutral until you see what it is aimed at: care for the Lord's things is devotion, and care for tomorrow's bread is anxiety. My position is that Jesus does not forbid caring, He forbids the kind of caring that forgets who provides.
