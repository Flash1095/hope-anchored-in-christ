kopḗ

from G2875; cutting, i.e. carnage:--slaughter.

Biblical Usage

Kope is a cutting, a slaughter. Hebrews 7:1 tells how Melchisedec, king of Salem, priest of the most high God, met Abraham returning from the slaughter of the kings, and blessed him.

Theological Significance

I believe the slaughter is the setting of the blessing: Abraham comes back from the battle, and the priest of the most high God meets him with bread and wine.
