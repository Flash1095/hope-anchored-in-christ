skorpíos

Word
σκορπίος
Language
Greek
Transliteration
skorpíos
Strongs Number
G4651
Definition
probably from an obsolete σκέρπω (perhaps strengthened from the base of G4649 and meaning to pierce); a "scorpion" (from its sting):--scorpion.
