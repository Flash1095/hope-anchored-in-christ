Rᵉchabʻâm

Rechabam, an Israelite king

Biblical Usage

Rehoboam was Solomon's son, made king at Shechem, and when the people asked him to lighten their yoke he answered with the counsel of the young men and split the kingdom. Ten tribes went after Jeroboam, and Rehoboam reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem.

Theological Significance

I believe Rehoboam's folly is the warning of every leader: a harsh word at the wrong hour can split what took generations to build. God had foretold the division, and still the man's own pride did the work.
