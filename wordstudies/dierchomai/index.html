diérchomai

from G1223 and G2064; to traverse (literally):--come, depart, go (about, abroad, everywhere, over, through, throughout), pass (by, over, through, throughout), pierce through, travel, walk through.

Biblical Usage

To pass through is to go from place to place, and the word follows Jesus and the apostles across the whole New Testament. The shepherds pass through to Bethlehem, Peter says Jesus went about doing good, death passed upon all men, and all our fathers passed through the sea.

Theological Significance

I believe passing through is the missionary's life. Jesus and Paul never settled in one place when the next town still needed the gospel, and the church is still a people on the move.
