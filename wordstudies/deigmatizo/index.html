deigmatízō

from G1164; to exhibit:--make a shew.

Biblical Usage

To make a show of something is to expose it, and the word carries both a public display and a public shame. Christ spoiled principalities and powers, making a show of them openly and triumphing over them in the cross. Joseph, being a just man, was not willing to make Mary a public example, and chose to put her away privately.

Theological Significance

I believe the cross is the great public show: what was meant to shame Christ became the shame of His enemies.
