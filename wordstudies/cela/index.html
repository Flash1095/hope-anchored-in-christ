çelaʻ

a craggy rock, literally or figuratively (a fortress)

Biblical Usage

çelaʻ means a craggy rock, a fortress, and the Bible means it plainly. It occurs 53 times in the Old Testament, in 1 Kings and 1 Samuel and elsewhere, and none of those places bends the word out of shape. What it says in one place is what it says in all of them.

Theological Significance

I believe çelaʻ earns its place. A craggy rock, a fortress is the kind of thing God repeats because His people need to hear it more than once.
