entolḗ

from G1781; injunction, i.e. an authoritative prescription:--commandment, precept.

Biblical Usage

The commandment — God's authoritative prescription. Keeping the commandments of God is what matters in 1 Corinthians 7:19, we know that we know Him if we keep His commandments in 1 John 2:3, and Timothy is charged to keep the commandment without spot until Christ appears in 1 Timothy 6:14. John rejoiced to find children walking in truth as commanded in 2 John 1:4.

Theological Significance

I believe commandments are how love shows itself — keeping them is not earning God's favour but proving I know Him. I hold that the commandment is not a burden but a boundary, and inside it is where the freedom is.
