trymaliá

Word
τρυμαλιά
Language
Greek
Transliteration
trymaliá
Strongs Number
G5168
Definition
from a derivative of τρύω (to wear away; akin to the base of G5134, G5147 and G5176); an orifice, i.e. needle's eye:--eye. Compare G5169.
