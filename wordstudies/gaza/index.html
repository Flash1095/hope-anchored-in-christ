gáza

of foreign origin; a treasure:--treasure.

Biblical Usage

Gaza means a treasure, the wealth of a king kept in store. It appears once, in Acts 8:27, where the Ethiopian eunuch of great authority under Candace, queen of the Ethiopians, had the charge of all her treasure. He had come to Jerusalem to worship, and he carried the queen's riches with him.

Theological Significance

I believe the treasurer of Ethiopia shows that the gospel reaches the keepers of wealth as well as the poor; he had charge of all her treasure, yet he came to Jerusalem to worship, and I hold that the true treasure is the Christ he found on the road.
