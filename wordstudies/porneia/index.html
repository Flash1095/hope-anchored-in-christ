porneía

from G4203; harlotry (including adultery and incest); figuratively, idolatry:--fornication.

Biblical Usage

Porneia covers harlotry of every kind, including adultery and incest, and figuratively the idolatry that follows it. Jesus names it as the one ground of divorce, and the apostles tell Gentile believers to abstain from it. Paul is blunt: flee fornication, because every other sin is outside the body but this one sins against the body itself.

Theological Significance

I believe the body matters to God, and that is why this sin is never treated as small. Christ bought the body as well as the soul, so purity is not prudishness but ownership.
