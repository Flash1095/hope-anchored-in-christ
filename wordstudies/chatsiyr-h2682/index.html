châtsîyr

grass; also a leek (collectively)

Biblical Usage

Grass is the Bible's picture of how quickly flesh fades. Ahab sends Obadiah to find grass to save the horses in the drought, the enemies of Judah are like grass of the field and grass on the housetops, and Isaiah cries that all flesh is grass while the word of our God stands for ever.

Theological Significance

I believe the grass teaches me my place: I wither and fade, and only God's word stands. That is not a hard word to me, because the standing word is the one that saves.
