Chêlem

Chelem, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Helem is the same man as Heldai, named among those who bring gold from Babylon for the crown of Joshua, and the crowns are kept in the temple as a memorial to Helem and Tobijah and Jedaiah and Hen. His name is on the memorial.

Theological Significance

I hold that God keeps the givers' names on the crown: Helem's gift is remembered in the temple, and the memorial is still there.
