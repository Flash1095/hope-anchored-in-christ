pneuma

Word
πνεῦμα
Language
Greek
Transliteration
pneuma
Strongs Number
G4151
Definition
Spirit, breath and wind — the Holy Spirit, the third Person of the Trinity, and the unseen dimension of human life.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
John 3:8; Acts 2:1-4; Romans 8:26; Galatians 5:22-23
Biblical Usage
Pneuma echoes Hebrew ruach: the wind blows where it wishes, the Spirit fell at Pentecost with a sound like rushing wind, and He now intercedes, indwells and bears fruit in believers.
Theological Significance
Pneuma means believers are never left to manage the Christian life alone. The Spirit who raised Jesus lives in us — helper, seal and guarantee of what is coming.
