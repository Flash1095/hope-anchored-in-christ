qûwm

{to rise (in various applications, literal, figurative, intensive and causative)}

Biblical Usage

God changes the times and the seasons: he removes kings, and sets up kings. And Zerubbabel rose up with Jeshua and began to build the house of God, with the prophets of God helping them. The word is the rising itself.

Theological Significance

I believe God removes kings and sets up kings, and Zerubbabel rose because the prophets were with him.
