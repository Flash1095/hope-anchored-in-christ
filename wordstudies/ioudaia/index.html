Ioudaía

feminine of G2453 (with G1093 implied); the Judæan land (i.e. Judæa), a region of Palestine:--Judæa.

Biblical Usage

Judea is the land where the story happens, from Bethlehem to Jerusalem. In Matthew 2:1 Jesus is born in Bethlehem of Judea in the days of Herod, in Acts 1:8 the disciples are told they will be witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea, and in Samaria, and in Acts 9:31 the churches have rest throughout all Judea and Galilee and Samaria.

Theological Significance

I believe Judea is the ground the gospel walked on first: born there, preached there, and then sent out from there to the ends of the earth.
