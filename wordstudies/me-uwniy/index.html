Mᵉʻûwnîy

a Meunite, or inhabitant of Maon (only in plural)

Biblical Usage

The Meunites are a people of the desert, named where Judah fought them: God helped Uzziah against the Meunim, and the Simeonites smote their tents and destroyed them. The name also appears among the Nethinim who returned from exile.

Theological Significance

I hold that the Meunites are a reminder that God fights for His people against enemies history barely remembers, and that the victory belongs to Him whether the enemy's name survives or not.
