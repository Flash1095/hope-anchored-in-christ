ʻâgan

to debar, i.e. from marriage

Biblical Usage

Agan means to shut oneself off — to be debarred, in this case from marriage — and it appears only once, in Naomi's bitter question to her daughters-in-law: 'Would ye stay for them from having husbands?' She is telling Ruth and Orpah not to wait for sons she will never have, not to shut themselves away from marriage for her sake.

Theological Significance

On its own it says very little about God or about us; whatever it teaches, it teaches only inside the sentence where God placed it; in this case, the verse is Ruth 1:13.
