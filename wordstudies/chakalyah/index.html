Chăkalyâh

Chakaljah, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Hachaliah is named twice, both times as the father of Nehemiah — the words of Nehemiah the son of Hachaliah open the book, and his name stands among those who sealed the covenant. Two mentions, and both are about his son.

Theological Significance

I believe a father is remembered by his son's work, and Hachaliah's son rebuilt a wall and a people.
