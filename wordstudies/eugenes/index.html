eugenḗs

from G2095 and G1096; well born, i.e. (literally) high in rank, or (figuratively) generous:--more noble, nobleman.

Biblical Usage

eugenḗs is a plain word for more noble, nobleman. In Luke 19:12, the text says "A certain nobleman went into a far country to receive for himself a kingdom." In Acts 17:11, the text says "These were more noble than those in Thessalonica."

Theological Significance

I believe eugenḗs shows us that humility is not thinking less of ourselves; it is thinking of ourselves less, and of God more. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
