pniktós

from G4155; throttled, i.e. (neuter concretely) an animal choked to death (not bled):--strangled.

Biblical Usage

Pniktos means strangled, choked to death — an animal not properly bled. The Jerusalem council wrote to the Gentiles to abstain from things strangled in Acts 15:20, and repeated it in Acts 15:29 and Acts 21:25.

Theological Significance

I believe the council's care for a brother's conscience was worth more than a meal.
