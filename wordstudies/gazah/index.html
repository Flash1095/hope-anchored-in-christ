gâzâh

to cut off, i.e. portion out

Biblical Usage

Gazah means to cut off, to portion out — and in its one use it is God who does the cutting. The psalmist says in Psalms 71:6, By thee have I been holden up from the womb: thou art he that took me out of my mother's bowels. The word for took me out is the word for cutting the cord, the portioning of a child from its mother.

Theological Significance

I believe the psalmist's picture is tender and true: God cut the cord that held me, and He has held me up ever since; I hold that the God who brought me out of the womb will not let me go now.
