pâgaʻ

to impinge, by accident or violence, or (figuratively) by importunity

Biblical Usage

Pâga' is to meet, to light upon, to fall upon — and to intercede. Jacob lights upon a place and sleeps there, Ruth entreats Naomi not to leave her, the LORD lays on the Servant the iniquity of us all, and the Servant makes intercession for the transgressors.

Theological Significance

I believe this word carries the cross in its meeting. Our sin meets the Servant, the Servant meets our sin, and the meeting that should have crushed us becomes intercession for us.
