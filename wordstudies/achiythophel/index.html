ʼĂchîythôphel

Achithophel, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Ahithophel was David's counsellor, whose advice was as if a man had enquired at the oracle of God, until he went over to Absalom and, seeing his counsel rejected, went home and hanged himself.

Theological Significance

Wisdom without loyalty curdles into treason — that is what I hold his story to teach. The cleverest man in the kingdom could not save himself, because he had chosen the wrong king.
