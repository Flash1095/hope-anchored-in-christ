anéndektos

from G1 (as a negative particle) and a derivative of the same as G1735; unadmitted, i.e. (by implication) not supposable:--impossible.

Biblical Usage

Jesus says it is impossible but that offences will come, and then warns of the woe on the one through whom they come. The word means what cannot be admitted or allowed in, here the plain fact that stumbling blocks are unavoidable in this world.

Theological Significance

One word for what cannot be kept out carries no doctrine of its own; its sobriety is Jesus', that offences will come, and the woe falls on the one who causes them.
