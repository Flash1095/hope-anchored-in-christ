telṓnion

neuter of a presumed derivative of G5057; a tax-gatherer's place of business:--receipt of custom.

Biblical Usage

The word is the receipt of custom, the tax office where the collector sat. Jesus saw Matthew sitting at the receipt of custom and said, Follow Me; and he arose and followed Him, leaving all.

Theological Significance

I hold that the tax office is where Jesus found an apostle, and the call came in the middle of the day's work.
