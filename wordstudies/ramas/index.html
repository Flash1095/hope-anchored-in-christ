râmas

properly, to glide swiftly, i.e. to crawl or move with short steps; by analogy to swarm

Biblical Usage

Ramas is to glide swiftly, to crawl or move with short steps — and by analogy, to swarm. God created great whales, 'and every living creature that moveth, which the waters brought forth abundantly'; He gave man dominion 'over every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth'; and He blessed them and said, 'be fruitful, and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas, and let fowl multiply in the earth.'

Theological Significance

My conviction is that the creeping things are in the blessing too: God made them, God named them, and God gave man charge over them — nothing that moves is beneath His care.
