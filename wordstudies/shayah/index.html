shâyâh

to forget, be unmindful of

Biblical Usage

Israel forgot the God who formed her. Moses sings it plainly: of the Rock that begat thee thou art unmindful, and hast forgotten God that formed thee. The word is to forget, to be unmindful of, and the verse is the charge.

Theological Significance

I believe forgetting is the quiet sin behind every loud one, for Israel did not argue with God but forgot Him, and I hold that remembering Him is the first act of faithfulness.
