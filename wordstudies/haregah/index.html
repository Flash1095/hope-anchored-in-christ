hărêgâh

slaughter

Biblical Usage

Haregah is slaughter, the killing floor itself, and the prophets use it for the places where judgment falls. In Jeremiah 7:32 the valley of Tophet shall be called the valley of slaughter, in Jeremiah 12:3 the wicked are pulled out like sheep for the slaughter, in Jeremiah 19:6 the same valley is named again, and in Zechariah 11:4 the flock is called the flock of slaughter.

Theological Significance

I believe the prophets name the place plainly because the people needed to hear it plainly: a valley of slaughter is what a nation becomes when it burns its children to idols. The name is the warning.
