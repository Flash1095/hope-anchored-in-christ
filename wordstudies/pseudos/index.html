pseûdos

from G5574; a falsehood:--lie, lying.

Biblical Usage

Pseudos means a lie, a falsehood. Jesus told the Pharisees that the devil is a liar, and the father of it, and when he speaketh a lie, he speaketh of his own, and Paul says, putting away lying, speak every man truth with his neighbour. John says no lie is of the truth, and into the New Jerusalem there shall in no wise enter any thing that defileth, neither whatsoever worketh abomination, or maketh a lie.

Theological Significance

I believe every lie has one father, and he is the devil: the lie did not begin with me, it began with him, and every time I lie I speak his native language. I hold that the city of God has no room for lies, because the truth is the foundation it is built on.
