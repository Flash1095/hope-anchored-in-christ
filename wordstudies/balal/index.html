bâlal

to overflow (specifically with oil.); by implication, to mix; to fodder

Biblical Usage

Balal means to mix, and it runs from judgment to worship. At Babel God confounds the language of men by mixing it up, so the builders cannot understand one another, while in the law the grain offering is mixed with oil, and the daily offering is mixed with oil and wine before the Lord.

Theological Significance

I hold that the same mixing tells two stories. At Babel men mixed their plans and God scattered them; at the altar God commands the mixing, and the offering is accepted. The difference is who does the mixing and why.
