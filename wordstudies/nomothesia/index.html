nomothesía

from G3550; legislation (specially, the institution of the Mosaic code):--giving of the law.

Biblical Usage

The giving of the law is one of Israel's great possessions, and Paul lists it among them: the adoption, the glory, the covenants, the giving of the law, the service of God, and the promises all pertain to the Israelites.

Theological Significance

I hold that the giving of the law is a gift, not a burden: it belongs to Israel as surely as the promises do, and it points to the Christ who fulfils it.
