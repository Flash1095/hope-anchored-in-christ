dâmaʻ

to weep

Biblical Usage

Jeremiah uses the word once, for the tears that will not stop: his eyes run down with tears night and day, and will not cease, for the virgin daughter of his people is broken with a great breach. It is weeping as a ministry.

Theological Significance

I believe the verse gives the prophet's heart its voice: some sorrows are too deep for words, and tears are the prayer.
