dâlâh

properly, to dangle, i.e. to let down a bucket (for drawing out water); figuratively, to deliver

Biblical Usage

The word begins with drawing water and ends with deliverance. Moses' future wife draws water for her father's flock, and the daughters tell their father that an Egyptian delivered them and drew water enough for them. Proverbs pictures counsel as deep water that a man of understanding draws out, and the psalmist praises the Lord who has lifted him up and not let his foes rejoice over him.

Theological Significance

I believe drawing water is a picture of how God delivers: He reaches down into the deep and lifts us out, and what He draws up is life itself. The water we draw in prayer is the counsel God has already given, and the wise man draws it out.
