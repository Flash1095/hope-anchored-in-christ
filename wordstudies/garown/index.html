gârôwn

the throat (as roughened by swallowing)

Biblical Usage

Garon is the throat, the passage of breath and voice. God put a chain on Jerusalem's neck in Ezekiel 16:11, and the daughters of Zion walk with stretched forth necks in Isaiah 3:16. Jeremiah is told to withhold his throat from thirst in Jeremiah 2:25, and the idols have mouths but speak not through their throat in Psalms 115:7.

Theological Significance

I believe the throat shows what a man loves. The proud stretch their necks, the thirsty run after strangers, and the idols cannot speak at all. I hold that the throat God made for praise should not be spent on pride.
