Shâʼûwl

Shaul, the name of an Edomite and two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Shaul is a name three men carried, and two of them stand in these verses. One is Shaul of Rehoboth by the river, who reigned over Edom when Samlah was dead. The other is Saul the son of Kish, a choice young man, and a goodly, higher than any of the people from his shoulders upward, sent by his father to seek the lost asses.

Theological Significance

I believe the shared name is the point: an Edomite king and Israel's first king both answer to Shaul, and only one of them was chosen by God. The name does not make the king — the choosing does.
