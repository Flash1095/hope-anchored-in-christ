hyperbállō

from G5228 and G906; to throw beyod the usual mark, i.e. (figuratively) to surpass (only active participle supereminent):--exceeding, excel, pass.

Biblical Usage

hyperbállō means to throw beyond, to surpass, to excel. In 2 Corinthians 3:10, the text says "by reason of the glory that excelleth." In 2 Corinthians 9:14, the text says "which long after you for the exceeding grace of God in you."

Theological Significance

What hyperbállō teaches me is that grace is God giving what we could never earn, and never taking it back. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
