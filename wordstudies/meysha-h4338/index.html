Mêyshaʻ

Mesha, a Moabite

Biblical Usage

Mesha king of Moab was a sheepmaster who paid the king of Israel a hundred thousand lambs and a hundred thousand rams with the wool, until Ahab died and he rebelled. His rebellion brought the kings of Israel, Judah and Edom against Moab, and Elisha with them.

Theological Significance

I hold that Mesha's tribute is the measure of Moab's bondage: a hundred thousand lambs a year, until the yoke broke and the war came, and even then the prophet had the last word.
