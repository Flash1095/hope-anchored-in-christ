Têl ʼÂbîyb

Word
תֵּל אָבִיב
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Têl ʼÂbîyb
Strongs Number
H8512
Definition
Tel-Abib, a place in Chaldaea
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Tel-abib = "mound of the flood"; 1) a city in Babylon, the home of the prophet Ezekiel, located on the river Chebar which was probably a branch of the Euphrates
