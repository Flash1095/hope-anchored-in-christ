Nêr

Ner, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Ner is known almost entirely through his son: Abner the son of Ner, captain of Saul's army and Saul's uncle. He stands behind the throne through the whole reign, named when his son acts and named again when David remembers Abner's murder.

Theological Significance

I hold that a father can shape a story without ever standing in it: Ner never takes the stage himself, yet every telling of Abner's story begins with him.
