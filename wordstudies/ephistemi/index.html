ephístēmi

from G1909 and G2476; to stand upon, i.e. be present (in various applications, friendly or otherwise, usually literal); --assault, come (in, to, unto, upon), be at hand (instant), present, stand (before, by, over).

Biblical Usage

Ephistemi means to stand upon, to be present, to come suddenly. The angel of the Lord came upon the shepherds; Martha was cumbered about much serving and came to Jesus; the angel stood by Peter in prison; the Lord stood by Paul in the night; and Timothy is told to be instant in season and out of season.

Theological Significance

God stands by His people suddenly, and His people are to stand by their calling constantly. I believe the two truths belong together: the angel in the prison, the Lord in the night, and Timothy told to be instant in season and out of season.
