Yaʻăqôb

Word
יַעֲקֹב
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Yaʻăqôb
Strongs Number
H3290
Definition
Jaakob, the Israelitish patriarch
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Jacob = "heel holder" or "supplanter"; 1) son of Isaac, grandson of Abraham, and father of the 12 patriarchs of the tribes of Israel
