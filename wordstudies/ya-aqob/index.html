Yaʻăqôb

Jaakob, the Israelitish patriarch

Biblical Usage

After Esau came his brother out, and his hand took hold on Esau's heel, and his name was called Jacob. The names of the children of Israel who came into Egypt begin with Jacob's house, and God says, I will remember my covenant with Jacob.

Theological Significance

I believe Jacob's story is the story of a heel held and a blessing wrestled for: the boy who gripped his brother's heel became the father who gripped God through the night, and the covenant God made with him outlived him by a thousand years. I hold his name close because it is the name of a people, not only of a man.
