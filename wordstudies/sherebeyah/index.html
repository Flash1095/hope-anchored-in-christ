Shêrêbᵉyâh

Word
שֵׁרֵבְיָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Shêrêbᵉyâh
Strongs Number
H8274
Definition
Sherebjah, the name of two Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Sherebiah = "Jehovah has scorched"; 1) a Levite who assisted Ezra at the reading of the Law and who sealed the covenant with Nehemiah
