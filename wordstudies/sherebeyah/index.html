Shêrêbᵉyâh

Sherebjah, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Sherebjah is the Levite who teaches the law when Ezra reads it, who stands on the stairs and blesses the LORD, and who seals the covenant with Nehemiah. He is named among the men of understanding and the singers of the house.

Theological Significance

I believe the Levite who teaches the law is the hinge of the whole return from exile, because a people that hears the law can live again. I hold that Sherebjah's teaching is the work I most want done in my own house.
