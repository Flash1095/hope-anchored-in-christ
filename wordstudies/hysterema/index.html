hystérēma

from G5302; a deficit; specially, poverty:--that which is behind, (that which was) lack(-ing), penury, want.

Biblical Usage

hystérēma is a plain word for lack, need, poverty. In Luke 21:4, the text says "but she of her penury hath cast in all the living that she had." In 1 Corinthians 16:17, the text says "for that which was lacking on your part they have supplied."

Theological Significance

hystérēma convinces me that God provides, and His provision is enough — even when it does not look like we expected. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
