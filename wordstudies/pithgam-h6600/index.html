pithgâm

a word, answer, letter or decree

Biblical Usage

Pithgam is an Aramaic word for an answer, a decree, a matter decided. The three friends answered Nebuchadnezzar in Daniel 3:16, and the king's decree in Daniel 4:17 stands by the word of the holy ones. Darius made a decree in Ezra 6:11 that whoever alters this word shall be hanged.

Theological Significance

I believe a king's decree is only as sure as the King behind it — and God's word stands when every other decree falls.
