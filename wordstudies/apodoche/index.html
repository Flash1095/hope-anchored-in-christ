apodochḗ

from G588; acceptance:--acceptation.

Biblical Usage

Two uses, and in both Paul stamps a saying as worthy of all acceptation. The first is the heart of the gospel in 1 Timothy 1:15, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom Paul calls himself chief; the second, in 1 Timothy 4:9, is a faithful saying and worthy of all acceptation.

Theological Significance

Paul does not ask me to weigh these sayings; he asks me to receive them whole.
