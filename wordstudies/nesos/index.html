nēsos

probably from the base of G3491; an island:--island, isle.

Biblical Usage

Islands in Acts are the stepping stones of the gospel's journey by sea. Paul and Barnabas cross Cyprus and meet a sorcerer at Paphos, the storm drives Paul's ship toward an unknown island, and the survivors find it is Malta; John, years later, is on Patmos, an island, for the word of God.

Theological Significance

I believe the islands remind me that the gospel was never a landlocked message — it crossed water to reach Cyprus, Malta, and Patmos, and it still crosses water today. Paul's ship found Malta by storm, and John's Patmos found him by exile — the islands are where God meets His people.
