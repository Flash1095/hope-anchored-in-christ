Iákōbos

the same as G2384 Græcized; Jacobus, the name of three Israelites:--James.

Biblical Usage

James is the name of three men in the New Testament, and the Bible does not blur them together. Matthew 4:21 shows James the son of Zebedee mending nets with his father when Jesus calls him, Acts 12:2 tells how Herod killed that same James with the sword, and Galatians 1:19 names a different James, the Lord's brother, whom Paul met in Jerusalem.

Theological Significance

I believe the three men who share this name remind me that God calls ordinary working men, and that faithfulness can end in a sword as well as in a letter.
