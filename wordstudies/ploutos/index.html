ploûtos

from the base of G4130; wealth (as fulness), i.e. (literally) money, possessions, or (figuratively) abundance, richness, (specially), valuable bestowment:--riches.

Biblical Usage

Ploutos is riches, wealth, abundance. We have redemption through His blood, according to the riches of His grace, in Ephesians 1:7, and God will show the exceeding riches of His grace in Ephesians 2:7. Paul cries, O the depth of the riches both of the wisdom and knowledge of God, in Romans 11:33, and says Christ in you is the hope of glory, the riches of that mystery, in Colossians 1:27. My God shall supply all your need according to His riches in glory, says Philippians 4:19.

Theological Significance

I believe God's riches are not a vault but a river — grace, wisdom, glory, all flowing to me in Christ. That is why I hold that no need of mine is bigger than His riches.
