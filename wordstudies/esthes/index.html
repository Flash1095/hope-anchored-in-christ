esthḗs

from ἕννυμι (to clothe); dress:--apparel, clothing, raiment, robe.

Biblical Usage

Esthḗs is clothing — apparel, the robe, the garments a person wears. Herod dresses Jesus in a gorgeous robe to mock Him; two men stand by the disciples in white apparel at the Ascension; and James warns against judging a man by his fine clothes. It is the word for what covers a person, and what it says about them.

Theological Significance

I believe esthḗs is a word about judgment: God looks at the heart, but men look at the clothes, and the church must not. I hold that the fine robe and the vile raiment are both beside the point — the person is the point.
