ṭâʻam

to taste; figuratively, to perceive

Biblical Usage

David says, O taste and see that the Lord is good, blessed is the man that trusteth in Him. Jonathan says his eyes were enlightened because he tasted a little honey; and Job asks, does not the ear try words, as the mouth tastes meat? The word is tasting in the plain sense, and tasting as knowing: you find out by trying.

Theological Significance

I believe David's invitation is the simplest in the Bible — taste and see — because God is not proved by argument first but by tasting: try Him, and find out He is good.
