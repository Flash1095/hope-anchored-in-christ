zikrôwn

a memento (or memorable thing, day or writing)

Biblical Usage

A memorial, a reminder — the word is what God tells His people to keep. The Passover is a memorial forever, the stones of the Jordan stand as a memorial to the children of Israel, and God writes a book of remembrance for those who fear Him. The memorial does not help God remember; it helps His people never forget.

Theological Significance

I believe God commands memorials because He knows how quickly I forget. The stones, the feast, the book — all of them are His way of keeping His deeds in front of me. My position is that remembering is an act of faith: the God who acted then is the God who acts now, and the memorial says so.
