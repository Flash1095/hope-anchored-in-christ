shânîy

crimson, properly, the insect or its color, also stuff dyed with it

Biblical Usage

Scarlet runs through the tabernacle, the window, and the birth of twins. The tabernacle took blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine linen, Rahab bound the line of scarlet thread in her window, and the midwife bound a scarlet thread on the hand of Zarah. Isaiah carries the colour furthest, though your sins be as scarlet, they shall be as white as snow.

Theological Significance

I hold that scarlet is the colour of a thread that saves, from Rahab's window to the scarlet thread on a newborn hand. The tabernacle was dyed with it before the cross was raised, and Isaiah's promise is that scarlet sins can be washed white. My position is that the red thread never breaks, it runs from the window in Jericho to the blood of Christ.
