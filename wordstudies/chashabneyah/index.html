Chăshabnᵉyâh

Chashabnejah, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Hashabniah is a name carried by two men in Nehemiah's day. One is the father of Hattush, who repaired the wall beside Jedaiah, and the other is a Levite who stood on the stairs and called the people to bless the LORD their God for ever and ever.

Theological Significance

I believe the two Hashabniahs show the two works of the return: one man's son rebuilt the wall, and the other called the people to bless the God who brought them home.
