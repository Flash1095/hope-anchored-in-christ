çullâm

a stair-case

Biblical Usage

The ladder in Jacob's dream reaches from earth to heaven, and the angels of God ascend and descend on it. The word appears once, and it is the picture of the way between heaven and earth. Jacob wakes and says the LORD is in this place.

Theological Significance

I believe the ladder shows that heaven is not closed to earth, and God comes down to meet the man who is running away.
