lamprōs

adverb from G2986; brilliantly, i.e. figuratively, luxuriously:--sumptuously.

Biblical Usage

Sumptuously is the adverb for living in luxury, and it appears once, in the parable of the rich man in Luke 16:19: he "was clothed in purple and fine linen, and fared sumptuously every day."

Theological Significance

My position is that the one adverb carries the whole parable: he fared sumptuously every day, and Lazarus lay at his gate, and the luxury is the indictment.
