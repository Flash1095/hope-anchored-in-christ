oikourós

Word
οἰκουρός
Language
Greek
Transliteration
oikourós
Strongs Number
G3626
Definition
from G3624 and οὖρος (a guard; be "ware"); a stayer at home, i.e. domestically inclined (a "good housekeeper"):--keeper at home.
