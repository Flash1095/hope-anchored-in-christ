oikourós

from G3624 and οὖρος (a guard; be "ware"); a stayer at home, i.e. domestically inclined (a "good housekeeper"):--keeper at home.

Biblical Usage

Oikouros means a keeper at home, a stayer at home, domestically inclined. It occurs once, in Titus 2:5, where the aged women are to teach the young women to be discreet, chaste, keepers at home, good, and obedient to their own husbands.

Theological Significance

I believe keeping the home is a holy calling, and the young women taught to keep it well guard the word of God from reproach.
