íaspis

probably of foreign origin (see H3471); "jasper", a gem:--jasper.

Biblical Usage

Jasper is the stone John reaches for when he tries to describe the glory he saw. In Revelation 4:3 the One on the throne is to look upon like a jasper and a sardine stone, in Revelation 21:11 the New Jerusalem shines with a light like a jasper stone, clear as crystal, and in Revelation 21:18 the wall of the city is built of jasper.

Theological Significance

I believe John chose a stone, not a speech, because some glory can only be pointed at. The city God builds will shine like a jewel, and the foundation is his own.
