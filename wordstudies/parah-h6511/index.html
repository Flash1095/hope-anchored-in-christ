Pârâh

Parah, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Parah is a town in the wilderness of Benjamin. It is listed among the cities allotted to Benjamin's inheritance.

Theological Significance

I hold that even a town named once is part of the inheritance: Benjamin's portion is counted city by city, and Parah is one of them.
