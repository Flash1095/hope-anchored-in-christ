chrēmatízō

from G5536; to utter an oracle (compare the original sense of G5530), i.e. divinely intimate; by implication, (compare the secular sense of G5532) to constitute a firm for business, i.e. (generally) bear as a title:--be called, be admonished (warned) of God, reveal, speak.

Biblical Usage

To be warned by God is to receive His direction in a dream or a word, and the wise men are warned not to return to Herod. Simeon is told he will not die before he sees the Lord's Christ, and Cornelius is told by an angel to send for Peter. The same word names the woman who is called an adulteress while her husband lives.

Theological Significance

I believe God still directs His people, and the warnings in Scripture are always for protection, never for punishment. The wise men obeyed a dream and saved a child's life, and Simeon waited on a promise and saw it kept. I hold that hearing God is less about the method and more about the obedience that follows.
