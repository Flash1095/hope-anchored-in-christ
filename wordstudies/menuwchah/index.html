mᵉnûwchâh

repose or (adverbially) peacefully; figuratively, consolation (specifically, matrimony); hence (concretely) an abode

Biblical Usage

Menuwchah is rest, repose, the place where a weary people can settle. Deuteronomy 12:9 says Israel had not yet come to the rest and to the inheritance the LORD would give, and 1 Kings 8:56 blesses the LORD that He hath given rest unto His people Israel, according to all that He promised. 1 Chronicles 22:9 names Solomon a man of rest, and promises him peace and quietness, and 2 Samuel 14:17 has the wise woman of Tekoah call the king's word comfortable, as rest to her soul.

Theological Significance

I believe rest in Scripture is a place before it is a feeling: an inheritance to come to, a promise kept, a son named for peace. My position is that God gives rest the way He gives land, as a gift to be entered, not a mood to be chased.
