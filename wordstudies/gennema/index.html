génnēma

from G1080; offspring; by analogy, produce (literally or figuratively):--fruit, generation.

Biblical Usage

génnēma means fruit, generation. It occurs 4 times in all, and the passages I have picked — Matthew and Luke — show it in its plain sense. I read it as a word that does its work quietly, without dressing anything up.

Theological Significance

What génnēma tells me is that God works through generations, and no generation is forgotten by Him. I hold that reading it plainly keeps me honest about what God actually said.
