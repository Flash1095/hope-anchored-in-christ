Amots, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Amoz is named only as the father of the prophet Isaiah — 'the vision of Isaiah the son of Amoz' — and the phrase follows Isaiah through the book: when Sennacherib threatened Jerusalem, Isaiah the son of Amoz sent word to Hezekiah that God had heard his prayer, and the king and the prophet prayed together and cried to heaven. We know nothing else about Amoz himself, but his son's whole ministry carries his name.

Theological Significance

Amoz matters because of the son he raised: the prophet who stood before kings and spoke for God. I believe that is how most faithful lives work — the name is remembered because of what God did through the next generation.
