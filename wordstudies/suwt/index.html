sûwṭ

to detrude, i.e. (intransitively and figuratively) become derelict (wrongly practise; namely, idolatry)

Biblical Usage

1) to swerve, fall away; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to swerve, fall away; 1a2) those falling away (participle)
