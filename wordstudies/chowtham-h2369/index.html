Chôwthâm

Word
חוֹתָם
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Chôwthâm
Strongs Number
H2369
Definition
Chotham, the name of two Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Hotham = "seal"; 1) a man of Asher, son of Heber, of the family of Beriah; 2) father of two of David's mighty warriors
