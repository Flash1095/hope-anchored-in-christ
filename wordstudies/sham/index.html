Word

Language

Definition
there (transferring to time) then; often thither, or thence
Part Of Speech
adverb
Biblical Usage
1) there, thither; 1a) there; 1b) thither (after verbs of motion); 1c) from there, thence; 1d) then (as an adverb of time)
