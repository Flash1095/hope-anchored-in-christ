sarx

Flesh — the human body, human nature, and in Paul often humanity in its weakness and rebellion against God, apart from the Spirit.

Biblical Usage

Sarx spans neutral (the Word became flesh) to deeply negative (the mind set on the flesh). Paul contrasts life kata sarka — according to the flesh — with life according to the Spirit.

Theological Significance

Sarx explains the inner war every believer knows, and why victory is by the Spirit, not willpower. The Word became sarx to redeem sarx — flesh is not beyond His reach.
