shâmaṭ

to fling down; incipiently to jostle; figuratively, to let alone, desist, remit

Biblical Usage

To let go is the heart of this word, whether it is a debt, a field, or a queen. The law of release says every creditor shall release what his neighbour owes him, and in the seventh year the land shall rest and lie still so the poor may eat. Jehu cried, throw her down, and Jezebel was cast from the window, and the psalm says their judges are overthrown in stony places.

Theological Significance

I believe release is the gospel in one word, because God releases debts He is owed and rests land He owns. The same word that frees the debtor throws down the queen, and both are His justice. That is why I release what I am owed, because I have been released from more.
