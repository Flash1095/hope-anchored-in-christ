shâkab

to lie down (for rest, sexual connection, decease or any other purpose)

Biblical Usage

Shakab is to lie down, and Scripture uses it for sleep, for rest, for marriage, and for death. Jacob lay down on stones at Bethel and dreamed of a ladder reaching to heaven, Elijah lay and slept under a juniper tree until an angel woke him, and the psalmist says he laid down and slept and awoke because the LORD sustained him. The same word is the gentle way the patriarchs speak of dying, for Jacob asked to lie with his fathers and be buried in their burying place.

Theological Significance

I believe lying down in peace is one of the sweetest gifts God gives His people. The psalmist could sleep because the LORD sustained him, and Jacob could ask to lie with his fathers because death was only another lying down. That is why I trust God with my sleep and with my dying, because both are lying down in His hands.
