eirēnikós

from G1515; pacific; by implication, salutary:--peaceable.

Biblical Usage

This word means peaceable, and by extension wholesome, like fruit that does good to the one who eats it. Hebrews says chastening seems grievous at the time, but afterwards it yields the peaceable fruit of righteousness to those who are exercised by it, and James says the wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and full of mercy. Peace is the fruit, and wisdom is the tree.

Theological Significance

I believe God's discipline is peaceable in the end, because He chastens the children He loves to make them fruitful.
