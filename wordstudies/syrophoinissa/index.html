Syrophoínissa

Word
Συροφοίνισσα
Language
Greek
Transliteration
Syrophoínissa
Strongs Number
G4949
Definition
feminine of a compound of G4948 and the same as G5403; a Syro-phœnician woman, i.e. a female native of Phœnicia in Syria:--Syrophenician.
