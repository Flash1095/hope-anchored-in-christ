tíllō

perhaps akin to the alternate of G138, and thus to G4951; to pull off:--pluck.

Biblical Usage

Both tellings of the same Sabbath scene use this word. The disciples walk through the cornfields and pluck the ears of corn, rubbing them in their hands, and the Pharisees object — not to the plucking itself, but to the day on which it is done.

Theological Significance

I believe the smallness of the act is what makes the argument sharp: the disciples are hungry, the law allows gleaning, and the question is whether mercy or the Sabbath rule wins the day.
