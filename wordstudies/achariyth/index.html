ʼachărîyth

Word
אַחֲרִית
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼachărîyth
Strongs Number
H319
Definition
the last or end, hence, the future; also posterity
Part Of Speech
noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
1) after part, end; 1a) end, issue, event; 1b) latter time (prophetic for future time); 1c) posterity; 1d) last, hindermost
