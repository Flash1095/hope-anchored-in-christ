Definition

Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to distract, ring again, make a (great) noise, murmur, roar, discomfit, be moved; 1a) (Qal) to discomfit; 1b) (Niphal) to be in a stir; 1c) (Hiphil); 1c1) to murmur; 1c2) to show disquietude
