kremánnymi

a prolonged form of a primary verb; to hang:--hang.

Biblical Usage

To hang is the word for the cross in the apostles' preaching: "whom ye slew and hanged on a tree" in Acts 5:30 and again in Acts 10:39, and "Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree" in Galatians 3:13. It also carries the two great commandments, on which "hang all the law and the prophets" in Matthew 22:40, and the warning in Matthew 18:6 about the millstone hanged about the neck of whoever offends a little one.

Theological Significance

To my mind, the word holds the gospel together: Christ hung on a tree to bear the curse, and everything else in the law hangs on love of God and love of neighbour. The curse He bore is the reason I am free.
