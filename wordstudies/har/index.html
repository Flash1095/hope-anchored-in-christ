har

a mountain or range of hills (sometimes used figuratively)

Biblical Usage

A mountain is where God comes down and where his people look up. The Lord came down upon Mount Sinai in Exodus 19:20, the psalmist lifts his eyes to the hills in Psalms 121:1, Isaiah sees the mountain of the Lord's house established above the hills in Isaiah 2:2, and Moses is told to climb up and view the land in Deuteronomy 32:49.

Theological Significance

I believe the mountains in the Bible are never just scenery: they are where God speaks, where help comes from, and where the future is seen from. When I lift my eyes to the hills, I am looking toward the place God has always met his people.
