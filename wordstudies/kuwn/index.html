kûwn

properly, to be erect (i.e. stand perpendicular); hence (causatively) to set up, in a great variety of applications, whether literal (establish, fix, prepare, apply), or figurative (appoint, render sure, proper or prosperous)

Biblical Usage

Kuwn is one of the great establishing words of the Old Testament, used more than two hundred times for making firm, setting up, and preparing. David's throne was established in 1 Kings 2:12, Samuel told Israel to prepare their hearts unto the Lord in 1 Samuel 7:3, David perceived that the Lord had established him king in 2 Samuel 5:12, and the Kohathites were set over the shewbread "to prepare it every sabbath" in 1 Chronicles 9:32.

Theological Significance

I hold that the word's whole range is one promise: what God establishes stands, whether it is a throne, a heart turned to Him, or the bread set before Him every Sabbath. What God establishes stands, and I am standing on it.
