patriárchēs

from G3965 and G757; a progenitor ("patriarch"):--patriarch.

Biblical Usage

Patriarches is a patriarch, a progenitor, a father of a family. Peter calls David a patriarch in Acts 2:29, saying he is both dead and buried and his sepulchre is with us. Stephen says in Acts 7:9 that the patriarchs, moved with envy, sold Joseph into Egypt. Hebrews 7:4 says Abraham the patriarch gave a tenth of the spoils to Melchizedek.

Theological Significance

I believe the patriarchs are the fathers of the promise, and their failures are in the record beside their faith: they sold Joseph, and God used the selling. My position is that a patriarch is not a perfect man, he is a man God chose and kept.
