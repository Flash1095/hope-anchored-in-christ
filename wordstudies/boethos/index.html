boēthós

from G995 and theo (to run); a succorer:--helper.

Biblical Usage

A helper is the word for a succourer, and it appears once, in a confession of trust. In Hebrews 13:6 the writer says, so that we may boldly say, The Lord is my helper, and I will not fear what man shall do unto me. One word, for the Lord who stands beside.

Theological Significance

I believe the boldness in this verse comes from the Helper: I will not fear what man can do, because the Lord is my helper.
