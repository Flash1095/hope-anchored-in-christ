ekklesia

Church — the assembly or congregation of God's called-out people, gathered locally and universal across all time and place.

Biblical Usage

Ekklesia was a civic word for a summoned assembly that Scripture claims for God's people. Jesus promises to build His ekklesia, and not even the gates of Hades will overcome it.

Theological Significance

Ekklesia reminds lonely believers they belong to something Christ is building and loves — a people He gave Himself for, secure across every century and storm.
