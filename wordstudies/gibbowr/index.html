gibbôwr

powerful; by implication, warrior, tyrant

Biblical Usage

When Scripture says gibbôwr, it means strong, mighty. The word appears 152 times, and in Genesis, Deuteronomy, Joshua, and Judges it carries that same straightforward meaning. Nothing hidden sits behind it; the sense is the one you would expect from the word itself.

Theological Significance

gibbôwr matters to me because true strength belongs to the Lord, and He lends it to His people. My position is simple: I take God at His word here, and I build on that.
