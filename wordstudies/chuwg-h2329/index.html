chûwg

a circle

Biblical Usage

The circle is the vault of the heavens, and Isaiah says God sits upon the circle of the earth while men are as grasshoppers. Job's friend says God walks on the circle of heaven, beyond our sight. Wisdom says she was there when God set a compass upon the face of the deep.

Theological Significance

I believe the circle tells us two things at once: God is above it all, and He was there before it began. The earth is a circle under His feet, and wisdom was beside Him when He drew it. My position is that a God who can sit on the circle of the earth is a God big enough to hold my life.
