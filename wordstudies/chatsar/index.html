châtsar

to trumpet, i.e. blow on that instrument

Biblical Usage

To sound the trumpet is the work of the priests, and the word follows the ark and the temple through Israel's worship. Priests blow the trumpets before the ark as it comes to Jerusalem, a hundred and twenty priests sound at the dedication of Solomon's temple, and the trumpeters sound again when Hezekiah restores worship.

Theological Significance

I believe the trumpet is the sound of God's presence arriving among His people. It is blown at the ark, at the temple, and at the restoration, and each time it says the same thing: God is here, and worship has begun.
