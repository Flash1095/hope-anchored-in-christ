klásma

from G2806; a piece (bit):--broken, fragment.

Biblical Usage

Klasma is the fragment, the broken piece left over. Matthew 14:20 and Mark 6:43 and Luke 9:17 all count twelve baskets full of fragments after the five thousand ate and were filled, Matthew 15:37 and Mark 8:8 count seven baskets after the four thousand, and John 6:12 has Jesus saying, gather up the fragments that remain, that nothing be lost.

Theological Significance

I believe the fragments are the proof of abundance: twelve baskets after five thousand and seven after four thousand, because with Jesus there is always more left than there was to begin with. I hold that gather up the fragments is a command for every church, because nothing the Lord multiplies is meant to be wasted.
