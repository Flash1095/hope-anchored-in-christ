Môwreh

Moreh, a Canaanite; also a hill (perhaps named from him)

Biblical Usage

Moreh is the name of the oak and the hill where Abram first stops in Canaan. Abram passes through the land to the place of Sichem, to the plain of Moreh, and the hill of Moreh watches over Gideon's camp by the spring of Harod.

Theological Significance

I hold that Moreh marks the first footprint of faith in the land: Abram stands under its oak, and the promise is spoken there.
