Môwrashtîy

Word

Hebrew
Transliteration
Môwrashtîy

Definition

Part Of Speech
adjective
Biblical Usage
Morasthite see Moreshethgath = "possession of Gath"; 1) an inhabitant of Moresheth; 1a) the prophet Micah
