phōteinós

from G5457; lustrous, i.e. transparent or well-illuminated (figuratively):--bright, full of light.

Biblical Usage

Photeinos means full of light, lustrous, well-illuminated, and Jesus uses it for the body and the eye. The light of the body is the eye, He says, and if the eye is single the whole body shall be full of light, and if the whole body is full of light, having no part dark, it shall be wholly full of light. And at the transfiguration a bright cloud overshadows them, and a voice speaks from the cloud.

Theological Significance

I believe full of light is the state of the whole man when the eye is single: no part dark, no corner unlit, the body itself shining. The bright cloud on the mountain shows the same light from outside, the glory that falls on the Son. My position is that the eye decides the light: a single eye makes a luminous life, and a double eye leaves me in the dark.
