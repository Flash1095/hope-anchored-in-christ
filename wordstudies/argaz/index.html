ʼargâz

a box (as a pannier)

Biblical Usage

The word is a box or coffer, a pannier, and it appears only in the story of the ark's return. The Philistines put the golden jewels in a coffer by the side of the ark, the ark and the coffer are laid on the cart, and the Levites take down the ark and the coffer at Beth-shemesh.

Theological Significance

I believe the coffer beside the ark is the Philistines' apology in gold. It shows that even God's enemies know when they have trespassed and must pay.
