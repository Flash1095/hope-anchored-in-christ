ei kaí

from G1487 and G2532; if also (or even):--if (that), though.

Biblical Usage

This is another phrase, if also or even if, made of the little word if and the word and. A friend will rise and give bread because of importunity even if friendship alone would not move him, and Paul says that even if the outward man perishes, the inward man is renewed day by day. The phrase admits the hard case and then answers it.

Theological Significance

I believe even if is a word of faith, because it holds on to God when the outward case looks lost.
