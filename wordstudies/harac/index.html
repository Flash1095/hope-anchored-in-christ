hâraç

to pull down or in pieces, break, destroy

Biblical Usage

Haras means to pull down, to break to pieces, and it is used for enemies, for cities, and for foolish choices alike. In Exodus 15:7 God overthrows them that rise up against him, in Jeremiah 1:10 the prophet is set to root out and to pull down, in Proverbs 14:1 the foolish woman plucks down her house with her hands, and in Psalms 28:5 the wicked shall be destroyed and not built up.

Theological Significance

I believe pulling down is God's work and mine to fear in equal measure: he pulls down what rises against him, and my own hands can pull down what I was meant to build. The wise woman builds; the foolish one breaks, and I want to know which one I am being.
