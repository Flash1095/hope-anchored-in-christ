châbash

to wrap firmly (especially a turban, compress, or saddle); figuratively, to stop, to rule

Biblical Usage

Chabash means to bind on, to saddle, to gird — to get ready. Abraham saddled his ass for the journey to Moriah, Moses girded Aaron's sons with girdles for the priesthood, and Balaam saddled his ass and went with the princes of Moab. The same word is used for binding up wounds, so readiness and healing share one root.

Theological Significance

I believe God binds up what is broken and girds up what is sent. Abraham was saddled for obedience, Aaron was girded for service, and the same hands that bind the wound send the servant out.
