baḯon

a diminutive of a derivative probably of the base of G939; a palm twig (as going out far):--branch.

Biblical Usage

Baion is a palm branch — the word the crowd carried when Jesus entered Jerusalem. They took branches of palm trees and went forth to meet him, and cried, Hosanna, blessed is the King of Israel that cometh in the name of the Lord. The word is the same one the Greeks used for the palm twig, and it is the branch of the King's welcome.

Theological Significance

I believe the palm branches were the crowd's way of saying, this is our King — and I hold that I am still called to go out to meet him the same way, branch in hand, crying Hosanna.
