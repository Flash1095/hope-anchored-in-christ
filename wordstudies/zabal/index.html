zâbal

to reside

Biblical Usage

Zabal means to dwell with, to reside, and it occurs once, in the same verse as zabad. In Genesis 30:20 Leah says, "God hath endued me with a good dowry; now will my husband dwell with me, because I have born him six sons." The dwelling she hopes for is her husband's presence, won by the gift of sons.

Theological Significance

I believe this word holds a wife's honest hope, because Leah wanted more than sons, she wanted Jacob to dwell with her, and the longing for a husband's nearness is a good and God-given thing.
