Shêlâh

Shelah, the name of a postdiluvian patriarch and of an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Shelah was a son of Judah, born of the Canaanitess daughter of Shua, and his life runs through the story of Tamar. She waited for Shelah to grow, and Judah withheld him, and the family of the Shelanites is counted in the wilderness from him. His name means request.

Theological Significance

I believe Shelah's story is a warning about withheld promises. Judah promised and did not give, and Tamar acted, and I hold that a promise delayed can do as much harm as a promise broken.
