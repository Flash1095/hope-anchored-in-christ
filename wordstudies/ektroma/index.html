éktrōma

Word
ἔκτρωμα
Language
Greek
Transliteration
éktrōma
Strongs Number
G1626
Definition
from a compound of G1537 and τιτρώσκω (to wound); a miscarriage (abortion), i.e. (by analogy) untimely birth:--born out of due time.
