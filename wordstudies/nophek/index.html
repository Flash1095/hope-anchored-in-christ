nôphek

Word
נֹפֶךְ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
nôphek
Strongs Number
H5306
Definition
shining; a gem, probably the garnet
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) a precious stone in the high priest's breastplate; 1a) perhaps an emerald, turquoise, ruby, or carbuncle; 2) jewels imported from Tyre
