diastéllomai

middle voice from G1223 and G4724; to set (oneself) apart (figuratively, distinguish), i.e. (by implication) to enjoin:--charge, that which was (give) commanded(-ment).

Biblical Usage

To charge strictly is to command with an order not to be broken, and Jesus does it after every wonder. He charges the healed not to tell, He charges the disciples not to tell any man He is the Christ, and the apostles write that they gave no such commandment to trouble the Gentiles.

Theological Significance

I hold that the charge to keep silent was for a season, not forever. The wonders were not yet finished, and the time to speak would come when the cross had made the meaning plain.
