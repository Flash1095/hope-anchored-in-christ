pethach

an opening (literally), i.e. door (gate) or entrance way

Biblical Usage

Pethach is an opening, a door or gate or entrance, and it appears at the great doorways of Scripture. Abraham sits in the tent door when the LORD comes to him, Israel is told not to go out of the door of the house marked with blood, and the psalm calls the gates to lift up their heads for the King of glory to come in.

Theological Significance

I hold that doors in the Bible are never just wood: they are where God visits, where blood protects, and where the King enters.
