athetéō

from a compound of G1 (as a negative particle) and a derivative of G5087; to set aside, i.e. (by implication) to disesteem, neutralize or violate:--cast off, despise, disannul, frustrate, bring to nought, reject.

Biblical Usage

To set aside is to reject what God has given, and the word carries that weight in every place it falls. Paul says he does not frustrate the grace of God, the writer to the Hebrews says the man who despised Moses' law died without mercy, Jesus says the one who rejects Him rejects the One who sent Him, and the word He spoke will judge in the last day.

Theological Significance

I believe setting aside God's word is never a small thing, because the word set aside is the very word that will judge. Grace is not to be frustrated and Christ is not to be despised, and I hold that the safest response to His word is to receive it while there is time.
