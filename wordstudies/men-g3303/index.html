mén

a primary particle; properly, indicative of affirmation or concession (in fact); usually followed by a contrasted clause with G1161 (this one, the former, etc.):--even, indeed, so, some, truly, verily. Often compounded with other particles in an intensive or asseverative sense.

Biblical Usage

Men is the little particle that sets up a contrast, the 'indeed' that waits for its 'but'. 1 Corinthians 1:12 shows it at work in the divisions at Corinth, where every one saith, I am of Paul, and I of Apollos, and I of Cephas, and I of Christ, and 1 Peter 1:20 uses it of Christ, verily foreordained before the foundation of the world.

Theological Significance

I believe the smallest words carry the argument: Paul's 'indeed' divides the factions, and Peter's sets Christ before the world began.
