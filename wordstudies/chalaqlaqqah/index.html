chălaqlaqqâh

Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
chălaqlaqqâh
Strongs Number
H2519
Definition
properly, something very smooth; i.e. a treacherous spot; figuratively, blandishment
Part Of Speech
noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
1) flattery, slipperiness, fine promises, smoothness; 1a) slipperiness; 1b) fine promises
