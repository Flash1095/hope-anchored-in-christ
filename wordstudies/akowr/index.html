Akor, the name of a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Achor is the valley where Achan is stoned for taking the devoted spoil — the valley of trouble, named for what happened there. The place is covered in the Biblical Places collection — this study covers only how the name is used.

Theological Significance

I hold that Hosea's promise is the gospel in a place name. The valley where sin was judged becomes the valley where hope opens — and the God who named the trouble is the same God who turns it into a doorway.
