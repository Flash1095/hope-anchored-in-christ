ʻâçaç

to squeeze out juice; figuratively, to trample

Biblical Usage

This verb for treading down — pressing, crushing underfoot — occurs once, in Malachi 4:3: 'And ye shall tread down the wicked; for they shall be ashes under the soles of your feet in the day that I shall do this, saith the LORD of hosts.' It is the last promise of the Old Testament's last prophet.

Theological Significance

The Old Testament ends with the righteous treading the wicked to ashes — a hard promise, and a just one — and I believe it is the hope of everyone who has watched the wicked prosper.
