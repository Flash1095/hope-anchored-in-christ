ánemos

from the base of G109; wind; (plural) by implication, (the four) quarters (of the earth):--wind.

Biblical Usage

The wind is the Bible's picture of power no man controls and of teaching that blows men about. Jesus rebukes the winds on the sea and they obey Him, the sea rises with a great wind against the disciples, and Paul warns against being tossed to and fro by every wind of doctrine. The same wind that frightens the disciples is the one Christ commands.

Theological Significance

I believe the wind teaches me two things at once: my helplessness, because I cannot steer it, and Christ's authority, because He can. I hold that the winds of doctrine will keep blowing, and the only safety is staying anchored to the word He has spoken.
