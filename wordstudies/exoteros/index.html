exṓteros

comparative of G1854; exterior:--outer.

Biblical Usage

In the New Testament, exṓteros is the word for outer. In Matthew 8:12, the text says "But the children of the kingdom shall be cast out into outer darkness." In Matthew 22:13, the text says "and cast [him] into outer darkness."

Theological Significance

What exṓteros teaches me is that God's judgment is just, and it falls where it should — no one escapes it, and no one suffers it unjustly. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
