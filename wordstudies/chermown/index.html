Chermôwn

Chermon, a mount of Palestine

Biblical Usage

Hermon is the high mountain of the north, the snowy peak that marks the edge of the promised land. Moses names it among the borders Israel took from the Amorite kings, Joshua's conquests reach to its slopes, and the psalm says the dew of Hermon falls on the mountains of Zion.

Theological Significance

I believe the mountain that stood at the border reminds me that God's blessings run down from high places to His people below. I hold that Hermon is a picture of refreshment, because its dew falls where the Lord commands the blessing.
