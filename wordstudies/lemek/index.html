Lemek

Word
לֶמֶךְ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Lemek
Strongs Number
H3929
Definition
Lemek, the name of two antediluvian patriarchs
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Lamech = "powerful"; 1) the 5th lineal descendant from Cain, husband of Adah and Zillah, father of sons, Jabal, Jubal, and Tubal-cain, and daughter, Naamah; 2) father of Noah
