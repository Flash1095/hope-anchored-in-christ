Lemek

Lemek, the name of two antediluvian patriarchs

Biblical Usage

Lemek is the name of Lemek, the name of two antediluvian patriarchs. In Genesis 4:19, "Lamech took unto him two wives"; and in Genesis 5:26, "And Methuselah lived after he begat Lamech seven hundred eighty and two years". In Genesis 5:28, "And Lamech lived an hundred eighty and two years".

Theological Significance

I believe Lamech is the warning of what sin becomes when it grows up — Cain's descendant boasted of killing and multiplied vengeance seventy-sevenfold, while the other Lamech, Noah's father, is the quieter proof that God kept a line through the same dark world. My position is that I will not boast in my sin like the first Lamech; I will trust the God who preserved the second.
