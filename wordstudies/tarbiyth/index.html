tarbîyth

multiplication, i.e. percentage or bonus in addition to principal

Biblical Usage

The word is increase, interest, usury, the bonus charged on a loan. The law says to take no usury or increase from a poor brother, but fear God; Ezekiel says the righteous man hath not taken usury nor increase; and Proverbs says he that by usury increaseth his substance gathereth it for him that will pity the poor.

Theological Significance

I believe God forbids getting rich off a brother's need, and the gain of usury ends up with the merciful, not the lender.
