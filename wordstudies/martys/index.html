martys

Witness — one who testifies to what they have seen and heard, even at great cost; the root of the English word martyr.

Biblical Usage

Martys begins as courtroom language: witnesses testify to facts. Because so many Christian witnesses sealed their testimony with their lives — like Antipas in Revelation — the word came to mean martyr.

Theological Significance

Martys dignifies ordinary testimony: you are Christ's witness where you live. And we run surrounded by a great cloud of witnesses who prove God keeps His people.
