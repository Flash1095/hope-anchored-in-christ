kōlýō

from the base of G2849; to estop, i.e. prevent (by word or act):--forbid, hinder, keep from, let, not suffer, withstand.

Biblical Usage

Kolyo means to hinder, to forbid, to keep from. Matthew 19:14 has Jesus saying, suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come unto me, Mark 9:38 has John forbidding a man who cast out devils because he followeth not us, Luke 6:29 says him that taketh away thy cloke forbid not to take thy coat also, Acts 8:36 has the eunuch asking, what doth hinder me to be baptized, Romans 1:13 says Paul was let hitherto from coming to Rome, and 1 Corinthians 14:39 says, forbid not to speak with tongues.

Theological Significance

I believe kolyo is a warning against hindering: John hindered a man casting out devils, and the disciples hindered the children, and Jesus corrected them both. I hold that the eunuch's question is the right one to ask of every hindrance, because if water is there and faith is there, nothing may stand between.
