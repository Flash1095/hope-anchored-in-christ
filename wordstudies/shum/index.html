shum

{an appellation, as a mark or memorial of individuality; by implication honor, authority, character}

Biblical Usage

Shum is a name, and in Aramaic it carries honour, authority, and character. Daniel blesses the name of God for wisdom and might, Daniel's own name was Belteshazzar in Babylon, the builders of the temple were asked the names of the men making the building, and Darius decreed that the God whose name dwells in Jerusalem would destroy any king who touched His house.

Theological Significance

I believe a name is a person. Daniel's name was changed by Babylon but his God kept His own, and I hold that the name of God dwelling in His house is the reason the house stands.
