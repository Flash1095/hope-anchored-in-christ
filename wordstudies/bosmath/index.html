Bosmath

Word
בׇּשְׂמַת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Bosmath
Strongs Number
H1315
Definition
Bosmath, the name of a wife of Esau, and of a daughter of Solomon
Part Of Speech
proper noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
Bashemath or Basmath = "spice"; 1) Hittite wife of Esau, daughter of Ishmael, sister of Nebaioth; 2) daughter of Solomon, wife of Ahimaaz, one of Solomon's commissariat officers
