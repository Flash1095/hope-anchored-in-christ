zᵉbûwb

a fly (especially one of a stinging nature)

Biblical Usage

Zebuwb is a fly, and the Bible uses it twice to make a small thing do large damage. Ecclesiastes says dead flies spoil the ointment of the apothecary, so a little folly ruins a reputation for wisdom, and Isaiah warns that the LORD will whistle for the fly from Egypt as He calls up an army.

Theological Significance

I hold that small things are never as small as they look, because a fly in the ointment and a fly on the border both change everything.
