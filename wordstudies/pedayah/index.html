Pᵉdâyâh

Pedajah, the name of six Israelites

Biblical Usage

Six men bear the name Pedaiah, and the verses name three of them: the father of Zebudah, mother of King Jehoiakim; a son of Jeconiah in the royal line; and a son of Parosh who repaired the wall beside the king’s high house. The name means the LORD has redeemed, and each bearer served in a different place: the court, the royal line, and the wall.

Theological Significance

What I hold is that God keeps the names of men who served in quiet places — a grandfather of a king, a son in the royal line, a builder on the wall — and none of them is forgotten.
