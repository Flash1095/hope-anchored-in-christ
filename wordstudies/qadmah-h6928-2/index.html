qadmâh

former time

Biblical Usage

Aforetime is the Aramaic word for what was done before: Daniel kneeled and prayed and gave thanks before his God, as he did aforetime, and the builders of the house said it was built many years ago by a great king of Israel.

Theological Significance

I take Daniel's aforetime as the heart of his witness: the decree could not change what he did before, and he did it again.
