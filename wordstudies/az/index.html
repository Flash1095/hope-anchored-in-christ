ʼâz

at that time or place; also as a conjunction, therefore

Biblical Usage

Az is then — at that time, at that place. Then sang Moses and the children of Israel this song, then began men to call upon the name of the Lord, then Joshua spoke to the Lord in the day he delivered up the Amorites, and then the eyes of the blind shall be opened and the lame man shall leap as a hart. The word marks the turning point, the moment everything changes.

Theological Significance

I believe God writes then into history again and again: then the song, then the miracle, then the healing. My own life has its thens, and I hold that the next one is coming.
