pýrōsis

from G4448; ignition, i.e. (specially), smelting (figuratively, conflagration, calamity as a test):--burning, trial.

Biblical Usage

The burning here is twofold: the fiery trial Peter tells believers not to think strange, as though some strange thing happened to them. The burning of Babylon, whose smoke the kings of the earth watch from afar.

Theological Significance

I believe Peter's point is that the fire which tries us is not an accident, and the fire which judges Babylon is not an overreaction; both are God at work, and the first prepares us for the second. The trial is the fire that proves what we are made of, and I would rather be proved now than consumed then.
