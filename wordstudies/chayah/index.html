châyâh

to live, whether literally or figuratively; causatively, to revive

Biblical Usage

To live, to revive, to come alive again is the word for God's power over death. The widow's child revives at Elijah's prayer, a dead man revives when he touches Elisha's bones, the dry bones live and stand as an exceeding great army, and Jacob lives seventeen years in Egypt.

Theological Significance

I believe the God who revives the dead child and the dry bones is the same God who gives me life. Revival is not a metaphor here: breath returns, bones stand, and the dead get up.
