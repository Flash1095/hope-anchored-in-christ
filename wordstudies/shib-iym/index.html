shibʻîym

seventy

Biblical Usage

Seventy marks the generations from Cain's vengeance to Lamech's seventy and sevenfold, the seventy souls who went down into Egypt, the seventy elders who received the Spirit at the tabernacle, and the seventy years of the exile. It is the number of a family grown into a nation and a nation grown into exile.

Theological Significance

I believe seventy is the number of God's long patience with His people, from the seventy who entered Egypt to the seventy years that ended in Babylon. I hold that He counts the years of exile as carefully as He counts the souls of Israel, and He brings them home on time.
