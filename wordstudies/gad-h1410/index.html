Gâd

Gad, a son of Jacob, including his tribe and its territory; also a prophet

Biblical Usage

Gad is the seventh son of Jacob, the first by Zilpah, and Leah named him for the troop that came in Genesis 30:11. His sons are listed in Genesis 46:16 — Ziphion, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi, and Areli — when the family went down into Egypt. The tribe that grew from him took the east side of Jordan and fought for its brothers.

Theological Significance

I believe Gad's name is a promise kept. A troop came to Leah, and a tribe of warriors grew from her son, and I hold that God turns a mother's word of hope into a nation of fighters.
