pisteúō

from G4102; to have faith (in, upon, or with respect to, a person or thing), i.e. credit; by implication, to entrust (especially one's spiritual well-being to Christ):--believe(-r), commit (to trust), put in trust with.

Biblical Usage

Pisteuo means to believe, to trust, to put your weight on a person and His word. John 3:16 says whosoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life, and John 20:31 says these things are written that you might believe Jesus is the Christ. Paul says to believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead in Romans 10:9, and Abraham believed God and it was counted to him for righteousness in Galatians 3:6. The jailer heard it in one line in Acts 16:31: Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved.

Theological Significance

I believe believing is not agreeing with an idea — it is trusting a Person with everything. That is why I hold that faith is the hand that takes what God offers, and without it, Hebrews 11:6 says, it is impossible to please Him.
