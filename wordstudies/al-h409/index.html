ʼal

{not (the qualified negation, used as a deprecative); once (Job 24:25) as a noun, nothing}

Biblical Usage

The Aramaic word for not — the small word that turns a sentence around. Daniel tells the guard, Destroy not the wise men of Babylon; Nebuchadnezzar tells Daniel, no secret troubleth thee; and Job, in his last defence, says if his words are not true, who will make me a liar, and make my speech nothing worth?

Theological Significance

A negative carries no doctrine, and the word itself has none to give; its weight is how much of faith hangs on a not — destroy not the wise men, no secret troubles the dreamer.
