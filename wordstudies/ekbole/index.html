ekbolḗ

from G1544; ejection, i.e. (specially) a throwing overboard of the cargo:--+ lighten the ship.

Biblical Usage

This word is the throwing of cargo overboard to lighten a ship, and it occurs once, in the storm that carried Paul to Rome. The next day, being exceedingly tossed with a tempest, the sailors lighten the ship, throwing out what they had carried so far. The word is the sound of a crew choosing the ship over its cargo.

Theological Significance

I believe storms show what is worth keeping, because the sailors threw away their goods to save their lives, and God used the wreck to save them all.
