apolýtrōsis

from a compound of G575 and G3083; (the act) ransom in full, i.e. (figuratively) riddance, or (specially) Christian salvation:--deliverance, redemption.

Biblical Usage

Ransom in full — the buying back of what was lost. We are justified freely by His grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus in Romans 3:24, and in Him we have redemption through His blood, the forgiveness of sins, in Ephesians 1:7 and Colossians 1:14. Hebrews 9:15 adds the reach of it: by His death, for the redemption of the transgressions under the first testament, they which are called might receive the promise of eternal inheritance.

Theological Significance

I hold that redemption is the most personal word in the Bible, because a ransom is paid for someone, not something. Christ did not buy a principle; He bought me, and the price was His blood. The forgiveness is according to the riches of His grace — not the measure of my worth, but the measure of His.
