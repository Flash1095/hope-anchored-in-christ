skotia

Word
σκοτία
Language
Greek
Transliteration
skotia
Strongs Number
G4653
Definition
Darkness — physical darkness and, figuratively, ignorance, evil and separation from the light of God.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
John 1:5; John 12:46; Colossians 1:13; 1 John 2:8-11
Biblical Usage
Skotia describes the realm people love instead of the light — yet it is a realm God rescues us from, transferring us into His Son's kingdom. John insists the darkness is passing away because the true light is already shining.
Theological Significance
Skotia is real, but it is retreating. No darkness in your story is permanent for those in Christ: He came so that no one who believes need stay in the dark.
