ekpheúgō

from G1537 and G5343; to flee out:--escape, flee.

Biblical Usage

ekpheúgō turns up where the Bible says to escape, to flee out. In Luke 21:36, the text says "that ye may be accounted worthy to escape all these things that shall come to pass." In Acts 16:27, the text says "And the keeper of the prison awaking out of his sleep."

Theological Significance

ekpheúgō convinces me that God really does deliver; escape is not luck, it is His hand at work. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
