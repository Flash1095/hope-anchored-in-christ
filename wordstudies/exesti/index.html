éxesti

third person singular present indicative of a compound of G1537 and G1510; so also ἐξόν neuter present participle of the same (with or without some form of G1510 expressed); impersonally, it is right (through the figurative idea of being out in public):--be lawful, let, X may(-est).

Biblical Usage

The word éxesti carries the sense of it is lawful, it is allowed. In Matthew 12:2, the text says "thy disciples do that which is not lawful to do upon the sabbath day." In Matthew 12:10, the text says "Is it lawful to heal on the sabbath days? that they might accuse him."

Theological Significance

My position is simple: éxesti tells me that freedom in Christ is real freedom: not doing whatever we please, but being set free to live as God made us to live. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
