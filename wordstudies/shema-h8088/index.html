shêmaʻ

something heard, i.e. a sound, rumor, announcement; abstractly, audience

Biblical Usage

A report is what is heard, and what is heard travels. The queen of Sheba heard of the fame of Solomon and came to prove him, Habakkuk heard God's speech and was afraid, and the Chaldeans heard tidings of Pharaoh's army and departed from Jerusalem. Moses warns that if God kills all the people at once, the nations which have heard the fame of God will speak.

Theological Significance

I believe fame is a stewardship. Solomon's fame drew a queen, God's fame restrained His hand, and I hold that what men hear about God should lead them to Him.
