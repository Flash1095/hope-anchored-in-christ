ʼĂbîydân

Abidan, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Abidan the son of Gideoni is the prince of Benjamin in the wilderness — named in the census of Numbers 1, in the offerings of the princes in Numbers 7, and in the marching order of Numbers 10. He brings his offering on the ninth day and leads Benjamin’s host when the camp moves.

Theological Significance

A prince named four times, always in his place: counted, camped, offering, marching. I believe Abidan's story is the story of order — God counts His people, sets them in their places, and moves them together, and no tribe marches alone.
