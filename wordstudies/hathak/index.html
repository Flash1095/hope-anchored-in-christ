Hăthâk

Hathak, a Persian eunuch

Biblical Usage

Hathak was one of the king's chamberlains, and he becomes the go-between in the most urgent conversation in the book of Esther. In Esther 4:5 Esther sends him to Mordecai, in Esther 4:6 he carries her message to the square, and in Esther 4:9 he brings Mordecai's answer back.

Theological Significance

I believe Hathak matters because the whole rescue turns on messages carried faithfully: a servant who runs between a queen and a Jew in sackcloth, and does not add or take away a word.
