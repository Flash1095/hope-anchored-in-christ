kilyâh

Word
כִּלְיָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
kilyâh
Strongs Number
H3629
Definition
a kidney (as an essential organ); figuratively, the mind (as the interior self)
Part Of Speech
noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
1) kidneys; 1a) of physical organ (lit.); 1b) of seat of emotion and affection (fig.); 1c) of sacrificial animals; 2) (TWOT) reins
