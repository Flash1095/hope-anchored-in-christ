kilyâh

a kidney (as an essential organ); figuratively, the mind (as the interior self)

Biblical Usage

Kilyah is a kidney, and in the Bible the kidneys are the seat of the inner self. Exodus 29:13 and Leviticus 3:4 take the two kidneys with their fat for the altar, and Deuteronomy 32:14 praises the fat of kidneys of wheat. Job 16:13 cries that God cleaveth my reins asunder, and Psalms 7:9 says the righteous God trieth the hearts and reins, while Proverbs 23:16 promises, my reins shall rejoice, when thy lips speak right things.

Theological Significance

I believe God reads the reins the way men read the face: the kidneys are where motives live, and He tries them. I hold that the same inward parts offered on the altar are the ones that rejoice when truth is spoken, because God made the inside to answer to Him.
