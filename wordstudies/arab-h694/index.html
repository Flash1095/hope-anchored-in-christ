ʼĂrâb

Arab, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Arab is named once, as a town in the hill country of Judah, listed beside Dumah and Eshean in the inheritance of the tribe. It is a place on the map of Judah's allotment, nothing more and nothing less.

Theological Significance

I believe every town in Judah's inheritance matters, because the promise was measured out town by town, and God keeps the whole map.
