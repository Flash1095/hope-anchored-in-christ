Kᵉbâr

Kebar, a river of Mesopotamia

Biblical Usage

Kebar is the river in Babylon where the exiles settled, and where Ezekiel saw the heavens open. Ezekiel 1:1 says, as I was among the captives by the river of Chebar, the heavens were opened, and I saw visions of God. Ezekiel 1:3 says the word of the LORD came expressly unto Ezekiel by the river Chebar, and Ezekiel 3:15 says he came to them of the captivity at Tel-abib, that dwelt by the river of Chebar, and sat where they sat.

Theological Significance

I believe God meets his people in exile, by a foreign river, and the heavens open there as surely as in the temple.
