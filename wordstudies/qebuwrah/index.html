qᵉbûwrâh

sepulture; (concretely) a sepulchre

Biblical Usage

Sepulture is the burial itself: Jacob set a pillar upon Rachel's grave, that is the pillar of Rachel's grave unto this day; God buried Moses in a valley in Moab; Samuel told Saul he would find two men by Rachel's sepulchre. Jehoram's servants carried him to Jerusalem, and buried him in his sepulchre with his fathers.

Theological Significance

Burial in Scripture is never just disposal — I believe it is a statement of hope, because the body is laid down where God can raise it. What holds me is the pillar on Rachel's grave, still standing when the story moves on, and the sepulchre with no marker for Moses, kept by God alone.
