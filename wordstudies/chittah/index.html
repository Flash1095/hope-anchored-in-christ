chiṭṭâh

wheat, whether the grain or the plant

Biblical Usage

Chittah is wheat, the grain of the harvest, and it appears at the turning points of three stories. Ruth gleans through the wheat harvest, the men of Beth-shemesh are reaping their wheat harvest when the ark returns, and Solomon gives Hiram twenty thousand measures of wheat for his household.

Theological Significance

I believe wheat in the Bible is always provision on the way to something more, because Ruth's harvest leads to a home and the ark's return leads to worship. I hold that God feeds His people in the middle of their story, not only at the end of it.
