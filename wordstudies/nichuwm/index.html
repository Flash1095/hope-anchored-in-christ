nichûwm

properly, consoled; abstractly, solace

Biblical Usage

Comfort in the prophets is God's answer to a broken heart. He says He has seen the mourner's ways and will heal him and restore comforts to him and to his mourners; His heart is turned within Him and His repentings are kindled together over Ephraim; and the angel hears good words and comfortable words from the LORD.

Theological Significance

I believe comfort is God's own work: He does not only permit it, He restores it, and His heart turns toward the mourner. God restores comforts to the mourner and to his mourners, and His heart is turned within Him over Ephraim.
