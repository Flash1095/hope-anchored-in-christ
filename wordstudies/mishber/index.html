mishbêr

the orifice of the womb (from which the fetus breaks forth)

Biblical Usage

The word is the breaking forth, the opening of the womb, and Hezekiah uses it in his darkest hour: the children are come to the birth, and there is not strength to bring forth. Hosea turns it into judgment: Ephraim is an unwise son who will not stay in the place of the breaking forth of children.

Theological Significance

I believe the picture is of a crisis that cannot deliver itself: the birth is ready and the strength is gone, and only God can bring it forth.
