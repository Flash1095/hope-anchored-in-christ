mᵉtsullâh

shade

Biblical Usage

Zechariah saw a man riding a red horse standing among the myrtle trees in the bottom, with red horses behind him. The word is the shady hollow, the low place, and the vision begins there, in the quiet dark where the Lord's riders wait.

Theological Significance

I take the bottom as the place where God starts His visions: not on the heights, but down among the myrtles, where a man on a red horse stands ready.
