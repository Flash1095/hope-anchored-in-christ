ʻôsheq

injury, fraud, (subjectively) distress, (concretely) unjust gain

Biblical Usage

Oppression is the wrong done by the strong to the weak. Solomon marvelled at the oppression of the poor and the violent perverting of judgment in the province, Isaiah charged the rulers with trusting in oppression, Jeremiah said the city was full of it, and Ezekiel said the father who used oppression would die in his iniquity.

Theological Significance

I believe oppression is one of the sins God sees most clearly: the poor are crushed quietly, and the prophets name it because God will not let it pass. God hears the oppressed when no one else does, and His justice is the only court that never adjourns.
