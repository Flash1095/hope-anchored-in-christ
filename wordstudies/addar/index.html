ʼAddâr

Addar, a place in Palestine; also an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Addar or Adar = "exceeding glorious"; 1) son of Bela and Benjamin's grandson; 2) city in Judah near Edom

Details

Language: Hebrew
Transliteration: ʼAddâr
Strongs Number: H146
Part Of Speech: proper noun (masculine)
