Shabbᵉthay

Shabbethai, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Shabbethai was a Levite of the return from exile whose name appears where the law was read and explained to the people. He stood with the Levites who caused the people to understand the law at Ezra's reading, and he shared oversight of the outward business of the house of God. The name belongs to three Israelites in all, and each mention places its bearer among the Levites who served the restored temple.

Theological Significance

I believe the Levites who explained the law mattered as much as the one who read it. Shabbethai's name reminds me that God's house runs on quiet, faithful overseers, not only on famous leaders.
