rânan

properly, to creak (or emit a stridulous sound), i.e. to shout (usually for joy)

Biblical Usage

Ranan is to shout — properly, to creak or emit a shrill sound, and by use, to shout for joy. Isaiah says, 'Cry out and shout, thou inhabitant of Zion: for great is the Holy One of Israel in the midst of thee'; the wilderness shall blossom, 'it shall blossom abundantly, and rejoice even with joy and singing'; the psalm says, 'O come, let us sing unto the LORD: let us make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation'; and Zephaniah says, 'Sing, O daughter of Zion; shout, O Israel.'

Theological Significance

My conviction is that shouting is joy that cannot stay quiet: the wilderness shouts, the daughter of Zion shouts, and the redeemed shout because the Holy One is in the midst of them. And I believe the shout is also a weapon — the walls fell when Israel shouted, and the morning stars sang together when the world was made.
