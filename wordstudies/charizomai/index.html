charízomai

middle voice from G5485; to grant as a favor, i.e. gratuitously, in kindness, pardon or rescue:--deliver, (frankly) forgive, (freely) give, grant.

Biblical Usage

Charizomai is granting as a favour, forgiving freely, and it is the verb of grace in action. Paul tells the Corinthians to forgive the repentant man and comfort him, lest he be swallowed up with overmuch sorrow. The crowd desired a murderer to be granted unto them instead of the Holy One. Paul says we have received the things that are freely given to us of God, and he forgave for the Corinthians' sakes in the person of Christ.

Theological Significance

I believe forgiveness is never earned but always granted: the same word that gives a murderer to a crowd gives forgiveness to a brother. Paul forgives because God freely gave, and I am told to do the same. I hold that grace I will not pass on is grace I have not understood.
