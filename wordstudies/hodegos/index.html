hodēgós

from G3598 and G2233; a conductor (literally or figuratively (teacher)):--guide, leader.

Biblical Usage

hodēgós is the word for guide, leader. I meet it 5 times across the Bible, and Matthew is a good place to see it plainly. It says what it says, and I take it at face value.

Theological Significance

I believe hodēgós matters because God guides His people; He does not leave them to find their own way. What I take from it is this — the plain sense is the safe sense.
