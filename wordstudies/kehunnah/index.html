kᵉhunnâh

priesthood

Biblical Usage

Kehunnah means the priesthood, the priest's office: Exodus 29:9 says the priest's office shall be Aaron's and his sons' for a perpetual statute. Numbers 25:13 gives Phinehas the covenant of an everlasting priesthood, because he was zealous for his God. 1 Samuel 2:36 says the fallen house of Eli will beg, put me into one of the priests' offices, that I may eat a piece of bread. Nehemiah 13:29 prays, remember them, because they have defiled the priesthood.

Theological Significance

I believe the priesthood is a gift God gives and a trust men can defile: Phinehas won it by zeal, Eli's house begged for it as bread, and Nehemiah prayed against those who stained it. I hold that the true priesthood is kept by zeal for God, not by birth alone.
