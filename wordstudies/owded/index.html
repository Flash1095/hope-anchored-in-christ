ʻÔwdêd

Oded, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Oded is the name of two prophets. The father of Azariah spoke to Asa and called Judah back to the covenant, and the prophet of Samaria stood against the army of Israel and told them to send their captives home, because the fierce wrath of the Lord was upon them.

Theological Significance

What I hold is that the two Odeds show both sides of the prophet's work: one warned Judah to keep the covenant, and the other warned Israel not to kill their own brothers.
