ophthalmós

from G3700; the eye (literally or figuratively); by implication, vision; figuratively, envy (from the jealous side-glance):--eye, sight.

Biblical Usage

The eye is the window of the soul in Scripture. Paul says eye has not seen what God has prepared for those who love Him, John writes of what his eyes have seen of the Word of life, Peter says the eyes of the Lord are over the righteous, and his fellow apostle warns of eyes full of adultery.

Theological Significance

I am convinced the eye shows where my heart is: what I look at shapes me, and the Lord's eyes are on me whether I remember it or not. So I ask God to guard what I look at, because what I look at is what I become.
