phílēma

from G5368; a kiss:--kiss.

Biblical Usage

Philema is a kiss, and in the New Testament it is the holy kiss of greeting. Paul tells the Romans and the Corinthians to greet one another with a holy kiss, and Jesus tells Simon the Pharisee that the sinful woman has not ceased to kiss His feet since He came in.

Theological Significance

I believe the kiss is the church's family sign: holy because it is honest, and the woman's kisses on the feet of Jesus are worth more than the Pharisee's greeting at the door.
