achreîos

from G1 (as a negative particle) and a derivative of G5534 (compare G5532); useless, i.e. (euphemistically) unmeritorious:--unprofitable.

Biblical Usage

Unprofitable, useless — achreios is the word for a servant who produces nothing. In the parable, the master commands, "cast ye the unprofitable servant into outer darkness"; and Jesus tells His disciples, "when ye shall have done all those things which are commanded you, say, We are unprofitable servants: we have done that which was our duty to do."

Theological Significance

An unprofitable servant is a picture, not a system, and the two parables it serves — the buried talent and the servant who did only his duty — do the teaching.
