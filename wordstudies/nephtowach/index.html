Nephtôwach

Nephtoach, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Nephtoah is a spring on the border between Judah and Benjamin, named twice as the surveyors draw the tribal lines. The border runs from the hilltop down to the fountain of the waters of Nephtoah and on toward Kirjath-jearim, marking where one tribe's land ends and another's begins.

Theological Significance

I believe the boundaries God drew are worth recording: each tribe kept its own ground, and a spring on the line earned its place in the record.
