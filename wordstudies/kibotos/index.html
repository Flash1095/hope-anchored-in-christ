kibōtós

of uncertain derivation; a box, i.e. the sacred ark and that of Noah:--ark.

Biblical Usage

Kibōtos is the ark, and the New Testament uses the one word for both arks. Matthew 24:38 and Luke 17:27 tell of Noah entering the ark before the flood destroyed all, Hebrews 11:7 says he prepared it by faith to the saving of his house, and 1 Peter 3:20 counts the eight souls saved by water. Hebrews 9:4 turns to the other ark, the ark of the covenant overlaid with gold, holding the manna, Aaron's rod and the tables; Revelation 11:19 sees that ark again in the temple of God in heaven.

Theological Significance

I believe the two arks are one story: God saves His people through a wooden box, and He meets them above the mercy seat. I hold that Noah's ark points to Christ, who carries His people through the judgment instead of leaving them in it.
