naʻar

(concretely) a boy (as active), from the age of infancy to adolescence; by implication, a servant; also (by interch. of sex), a girl (of similar latitude in age)

Biblical Usage

Na'ar is a boy, a lad, and by extension a young servant, and it follows some of the best-known young men in Scripture. In Genesis 37:2 Joseph is seventeen years old, a lad feeding the flock with his brothers, and in Exodus 33:11 Joshua is called a young man, the servant who does not depart from the tabernacle.

Theological Significance

I hold that God trusts young people with real work, and the lad in the field is not a footnote; joseph the lad and Joshua the young man both grew into the men God used, and neither started by being important.
