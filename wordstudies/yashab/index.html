yâshab

properly, to sit down (specifically as judge. in ambush, in quiet); by implication, to dwell, to remain; causatively, to settle, to marry

Biblical Usage

Yâshab is the word for sitting and dwelling, for settling down and remaining. David says he will dwell in the house of the LORD for ever, and the psalmist says he that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty. The word covers the king sitting in his house at rest, and Jabal dwelling in tents with his cattle, so that dwelling is both the palace and the pasture.

Theological Significance

I believe the LORD gives His people a place to dwell, and the house of the LORD is the home no enemy can take. My position is that abiding is not idleness but the deepest work of faith, to sit still under His shadow. And I hold that the God who dwells with me is the God I dwell with, and that is enough.
