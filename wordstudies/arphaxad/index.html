Arphaxád

of Hebrew origin (H775); Arphaxad, a post-diluvian patriarch:--Arphaxad.

Biblical Usage

Arphaxad is named once in the New Testament, in the genealogy of Jesus, as the son of Cainan and the father of Sala. Luke carries the line back through him to Shem and to Noah, and on to Adam and to God.

Theological Significance

One name in a genealogy carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the line, that Jesus is the son of Arphaxad and the son of Adam, and the promise given to Shem's house comes home in Him.
