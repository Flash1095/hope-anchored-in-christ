Yᵉhôwʼâsh

Jehoash, the name of two Israelite kings

Biblical Usage

Jehoash = "given by the Lord"; 1) son of king Ahaziah and the 8th king of Judah; 2) son of king Jehoahaz and the 12th king of the northern kingdom of Israel

Details

Language: Hebrew
Transliteration: Yᵉhôwʼâsh
Strongs Number: H3060
Part Of Speech: proper noun (masculine)
