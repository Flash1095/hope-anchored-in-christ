ruach

Spirit, breath and wind — the life-giving presence and power of God, and the breath that animates every living creature.

Biblical Usage

Ruach hovers over creation, breathes life into Adam, fills prophets and craftsmen, and in Ezekiel raises dry bones to life. Its range — wind, breath, Spirit — shows one powerful, personal presence at work.

Theological Significance

Ruach is hope for the lifeless places: the same Spirit who raised dry bones in Ezekiel and raised Jesus from the dead lives in believers, renewing what seemed beyond repair.
