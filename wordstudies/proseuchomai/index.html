proseúchomai

from G4314 and G2172; to pray to God, i.e. supplicate, worship:--pray (X earnestly, for), make prayer.

Biblical Usage

Proseuchomai means to pray, to speak to God in worship and asking. Jesus taught His disciples to pray, Our Father which art in heaven, and told them men ought always to pray, and not to faint. He Himself rose a great while before day and went out to a solitary place and prayed there, and Paul's command is the shortest in the Bible: pray without ceasing.

Theological Significance

I believe Jesus prayed because He needed the Father, and I need Him more. I hold that prayer without ceasing is not talking all day but living all day turned toward God, the way Jesus lived, and the way He taught us to live.
