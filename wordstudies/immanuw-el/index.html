ʻImmânûwʼêl

Immanuel, a type name of Isaiah's son

Biblical Usage

Immanuel means God with us, and Isaiah gives it as the sign of the child to come. In Isaiah 7:14 a virgin shall conceive and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel, and in Isaiah 8:8 the flood of Assyria stretches out its wings and fills the breadth of thy land, O Immanuel.

Theological Significance

I hold that the name is the promise itself: God did not stay far off, he came to be with us, and Matthew says the child of the virgin is the one Isaiah meant.
