ʼAshdôwdîy

an Ashdodite (often collectively) or inhabitant of Asdod

Biblical Usage

The Ashdodites are the people of Ashdod, and they turn up where Israel meets the Philistines. The men of Ashdod rise early and find Dagon fallen on his face before the ark, Joshua names Ashdod among the land that remains to be taken, and Nehemiah finds Jews who have married wives of Ashdod, whose children speak half in the speech of Ashdod.

Theological Significance

I believe the Ashdodites matter because they are the neighbours Israel could never quite conquer and never quite keep away from: their god falls before the ark, their land is left untaken, and their speech creeps into the children's mouths. And I hold that Nehemiah's anger is the right one, that a people who lose their language have lost more than words.
