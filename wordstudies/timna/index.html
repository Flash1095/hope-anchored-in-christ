Timnâʻ

Word
תִּמְנָע
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Timnâʻ
Strongs Number
H8555
Definition
Timna, the name of two Edomites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
Timna or Timnah = "restrained"; n pr m; 1) a chief or duke of Esau and of Edom; n pr f; 2) the concubine of Eliphaz, the son of Esau; 3) daughter of Seir the Horite and sister of Lotan
