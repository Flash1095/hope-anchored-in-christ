menoûnge

from G3303 and G3767 and G1065; so then at least:--nay but, yea doubtless (rather, verily).

Biblical Usage

Menounge means yea doubtless, nay but, the word Paul uses when he will not be argued with. Philippians 3:8 opens with it, Yea doubtless, and I count all things but loss for the excellency of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord, and Romans 9:20 answers the objector with it, Nay but, O man, who art thou that repliest against God?

Theological Significance

I believe the word is Paul drawing a line: some things are not up for debate, and God's right to make as He pleases is one of them.
