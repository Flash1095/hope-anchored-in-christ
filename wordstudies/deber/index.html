deber

a pestilence

Biblical Usage

Pestilence is the judgment that walks with sword and famine, and the word appears wherever God warns His people. David is offered three judgments and chooses the pestilence, the hand of the Lord rather than the hand of man. The psalm promises that the pestilence that walks in darkness will not come near the one who dwells in the secret place of the Most High.

Theological Significance

I believe pestilence teaches us to choose our judge: David chose the hand of the Lord because the Lord's hand is merciful, even in judgment. I hold that the shelter of the Most High is the only refuge when the pestilence walks.
