diamartýromai

from G1223 and G3140; to attest or protest earnestly, or (by implication) hortatively:--charge, testify (unto), witness.

Biblical Usage

To testify solemnly is to charge a thing with the full weight of a witness, and Paul and Peter do it at every turning point. Peter testifies that they should save themselves from this untoward generation, Paul testifies to Jews and Greeks alike of repentance and faith, and Timothy is charged before God to preach the word.

Theological Significance

I believe solemn testimony is the preacher's duty. It is not an opinion offered for discussion but a charge laid down before God, and the gospel deserves to be spoken as though eternity depended on it — because it does.
