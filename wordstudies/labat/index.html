lâbaṭ

to overthrow; intransposed, to fall

Biblical Usage

To fall is the word's work in all three appearances, and it is always the fall of the fool. In Proverbs 10:8 "a prating fool shall fall," and in Hosea 4:14 "the people that doth not understand shall fall."

Theological Significance

In my reading, the proverb is plain enough to live by: the wise in heart receive commandments, and the fool who keeps talking falls, and the fall is the consequence of the talking.
