perikrýptō

from G4012 and G2928; to conceal all around, i.e. entirely:--hide.

Biblical Usage

Perikrypto means to hide entirely, to conceal all around. After those days Elisabeth conceived, and hid herself five months — the mother of John the Baptist, hidden with the child she had waited for all her life.

Theological Significance

I believe God hides His work before He shows it: Elisabeth hid five months, and the child she carried was the forerunner.
