lýchnos

from the base of G3022; a portable lamp or other illuminator (literally or figuratively):--candle, light.

Biblical Usage

The word names a portable lamp, the small oil lamp carried in the hand, and Jesus uses it for the light of the body, the eye, and for the lamp a woman lights to search her house for a lost coin. John the Baptist was a burning and shining lamp, and Peter calls the prophetic word a lamp shining in a dark place until the day dawns.

Theological Significance

I believe a lamp is the right picture for God's word, because it does not light the whole road at once, only the next step. I hold that John was a lamp and not the light itself, and every faithful witness is the same: a small flame pointing to the true Light.
