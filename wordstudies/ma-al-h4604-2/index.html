maʻal

treachery, i.e. sin

Biblical Usage

The word names treachery, unfaithfulness against God, and it is the charge laid against Israel again and again. Achan committed a trespass in the accursed thing and the anger of the LORD was kindled against Israel, the princes of the people trespassed in the holy things, and Ezra confessed that the people had joined themselves to the abominations of the land. Daniel confesses that shame belongs to Israel because of the trespass they trespassed against God.

Theological Significance

I believe treachery is worse than ordinary sin, because it breaks a covenant made in love, and Israel's history is the story of a faithless wife and a faithful Husband. I hold that confession is the only answer to treachery, and God hears the one who names his sin plainly and turns back to Him.
