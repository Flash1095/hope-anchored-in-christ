metakaléō

from G3326 and G2564; to call elsewhere, i.e. summon:--call (for, hither).

Biblical Usage

Joseph sent and called his father Jacob down to Egypt, the angel told Cornelius to send to Joppa and call for Peter, and Felix told Paul he would call for him at a more convenient season. The word is always a summons, someone sent for from somewhere else.

Theological Significance

I see two kinds of calling here: Joseph's call brought his whole family to safety, and Cornelius's call brought Peter to preach, while Felix's call was a delay dressed up as a promise.
