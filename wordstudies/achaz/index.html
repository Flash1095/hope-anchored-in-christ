ʼâchaz

to seize (often with the accessory idea of holding in possession)

Biblical Usage

To seize, to take hold — achaz is a gripping word. The ram was "caught in a thicket" when Abraham looked up; Moses was told to put out his hand and "take it by the tail" when the rod became a serpent; and Potiphar's wife "caught" Joseph by his garment. The same grip appears in deliverance, in obedience, and in temptation.

Theological Significance

I notice the verb cuts both ways: God tells men to take hold, and sinners take hold of the godly. The difference is never the grip — it is what is being held, and who told the hand to close.
