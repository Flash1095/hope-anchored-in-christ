ʼăbâl

nay, i.e. truly or yet

Biblical Usage

This little word means ‘truly’ or ‘indeed’ — a word for emphasis, sometimes for contradiction (‘nay’). Joseph’s brothers say, ‘We are verily guilty concerning our brother’; and the widow of Tekoa tells David, ‘I am indeed a widow woman.’

Theological Significance

It is a small word with no doctrine attached, and I will not force one onto it — though I notice where it lands: on guilt confessed and grief stated plainly.
