kēpos

of uncertain affinity; a garden:--garden.

Biblical Usage

Kepos means a garden: Luke 13:19 says the mustard seed grew into a great tree in a man's garden, and the fowls lodged in its branches. John 18:1 says Jesus went over the brook Cedron, where was a garden, into the which he entered. John 18:26 asks, did not I see thee in the garden with him? John 19:41 says in the place where he was crucified there was a garden, and in the garden a new sepulchre.

Theological Significance

I believe the garden holds the whole story: the seed that grows, the garden where he was betrayed, and the garden where he was buried. I hold that the new tomb in the garden is where death ended, because the gardener himself rose there.
