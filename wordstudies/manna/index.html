mánna

of Hebrew origin (H4478); manna (i.e. man), an edible gum:--manna.

Biblical Usage

mánna is manna, the bread from heaven. In John 6:31 the crowd recalls that their fathers ate manna in the desert, and in John 6:49 Jesus answers that those fathers ate manna and are dead. In Hebrews 9:4 a golden pot of manna lies in the ark, and in Revelation 2:17 the overcomer is promised hidden manna. The word runs from daily bread to a hidden promise.

Theological Significance

I believe mánna teaches me that God feeds His people day by day, and I hold that the bread that kept Israel alive points to the living bread who keeps me alive. I will not live on yesterday's manna.
