Antiócheia

from Ἀντίοχυς (a Syrian king); Antiochia, a place in Syria:--Antioch.

Biblical Usage

Antioch is the great city of Syria where the disciples were first called Christians, where Barnabas brought Saul, and where Paul and Barnabas were sent out by the church. The same name belongs to the city in Pisidia where Paul preached on his first journey, and Antioch is where the gospel first became a church that sent missionaries.

Theological Significance

Antioch shows me what a church is for: it is not only a place to be fed but a place that sends. The disciples were first called Christians there, and I believe that name is still earned the same way, by a people so joined to Christ that the city has to call them something new.
