Lýdda

of Hebrew origin (H3850); Lydda (i.e. Lod), a place in Palestine:--Lydda.

Biblical Usage

Lydda was a town in Palestine where Peter came down to the saints who lived there. When Peter healed Aeneas, all who lived at Lydda and Sharon saw it and turned to the Lord, and the town sent for Peter again when Tabitha died at nearby Joppa.

Theological Significance

I believe Lydda shows how one healing in one town can turn a whole district to the Lord, because God works through ordinary places.
