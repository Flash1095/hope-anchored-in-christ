emporeúomai

Word
ἐμπορεύομαι
Language
Greek
Transliteration
emporeúomai
Strongs Number
G1710
Definition
from G1722 and G4198; to travel in (a country as a pedlar), i.e. (by implication) to trade:--buy and sell, make merchandise.
