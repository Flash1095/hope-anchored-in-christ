Bêyth ham-Merchâq

Beth-ham-Merchak, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Beth-Hammerchaq means the remote house, and it appears once, in 2 Samuel 15:17, on the worst day of David's life: the king went forth, and all the people after him, and tarried in a place that was far off, fleeing from Absalom. The Hebrew is a house far away, and the English hides the name — but the place is where a fleeing king stopped to gather his people.

Theological Significance

I believe even a far-off house can hold a king's last stand, and David's pause there was not defeat but gathering.
