goel

Word
גֹּאֵל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
goel
Strongs Number
H1350
Definition
Redeemer or kinsman-redeemer — the near relative who buys back family land, freedom or honour for one who cannot rescue themselves.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Job 19:25; Isaiah 41:14; Ruth 4:14; Psalm 19:14
Biblical Usage
Boaz as goel redeems Ruth and Naomi's future, and Job cries out, 'I know that my Redeemer lives.' Isaiah applies the title to the Lord Himself, Israel's Redeemer and Holy One.
Theological Significance
Goel makes redemption personal and familial: God is not a distant rescuer but a near kinsman who pays the price Himself. Job's hope — and ours — rests on a living Redeemer.
