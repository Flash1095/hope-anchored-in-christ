môwqêd

a fire or fuel; abstractly, a conflagration

Biblical Usage

Moqed is a burning, a hearth, a fire that does not go out — who among us shall dwell with the devouring fire, Isaiah asks, and the psalmist says my bones are burned as an hearth.

Theological Significance

I believe the fire is both judgment and presence: the same flame that burns the bones is the fire of the God who dwells with His people.
