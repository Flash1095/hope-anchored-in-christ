taʼălâh

an imprecation

Biblical Usage

Jeremiah prays that God would give His enemies sorrow of heart, a curse upon them. The word is an imprecation, a curse called down. It sits in the middle of Lamentations, where the prophet has already said the Lord's mercies are new every morning, and still dares to ask God to judge.

Theological Significance

Lament can hold two things at once, this verse shows me: trust in God's mercy, and an honest cry for His justice on those who destroyed Jerusalem.
