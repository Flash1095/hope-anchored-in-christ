Chêpher

Word
חֵפֶר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Chêpher
Strongs Number
H2660
Definition
Chepher, a place in Palestine; also the name of three Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Hepher = "a well"; n pr m; 1) youngest son of Gilead and head of the family of Hepherites; 2) son of Asher, the father of Tekoa; 3) the Mecherathite, one of David's mighty warriors; n pr loc; 4) a place in ancient Canaan, west of the Jordan, conquered by Joshua site unknown; 4a) a place in Judah, probably the same as 4 above
