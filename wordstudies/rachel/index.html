râchêl

a ewe (the females being the predominant element of a flock) (as a good traveller)

Biblical Usage

Rachel is a ewe — the female of the flock, the predominant element, and a good traveller. Jacob told Laban, 'These twenty years have I been with thee; thy ewes and thy she goats have not cast their young'; Isaiah says He was 'brought as a lamb to the slaughter, and as a sheep before her shearers is dumb'; and the Song compares the beloved's teeth to 'a flock of sheep which go up from the washing.'

Theological Significance

I believe the ewe carries the whole gospel in one image: the silent sheep before the shearers is the Servant who opened not His mouth, and He did it for us.
