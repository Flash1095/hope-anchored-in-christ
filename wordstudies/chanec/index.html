Chânêç

Word
חָנֵס
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Chânêç
Strongs Number
H2609
Definition
Chanes, a place in Egypt
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Hanes = "grace has fled"; 1) a place in Egypt, site uncertain but may be either of the following; 1a) Tahpanhes, a fortified town on the eastern frontier; 1b) a place in lower Egypt, on an island in the Nile south of Memphis
