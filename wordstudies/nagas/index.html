nâgas

to drive (an animal, a workman, a debtor, an army); by implication, to tax, harass, tyrannize

Biblical Usage

Nagas means to drive or oppress, to tax and harass, and it is the word for the taskmasters of Egypt. In Exodus 3:7 the Lord says He has heard the cry of His people by reason of their taskmasters, and in Isaiah 9:4 the rod of the oppressor is broken as in the day of Midian. In Isaiah 14:2 Israel shall rule over their oppressors, and in Zechariah 9:8 the Lord promises no oppressor shall pass through His house any more.

Theological Significance

I believe oppression is the sin God hears first, because the cry of the oppressed reaches Him before the prayer of the comfortable. He saw the taskmasters, He broke the rod, and He promises a day with no oppressor at all. I hold that standing with the oppressed is standing where God stands.
