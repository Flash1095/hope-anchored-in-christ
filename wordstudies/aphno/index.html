áphnō

adverb from G852 (contraction); unawares, i.e. unexpectedly:--suddenly.

Biblical Usage

Suddenly — the sound from heaven filling the house at Pentecost, the great earthquake shaking the prison at Philippi so the doors stood open, and the islanders watching Paul for the swelling that never came. The word marks the moment God breaks in without warning.

Theological Significance

I believe God still comes suddenly, as he did at Pentecost and in the prison: no announcement, no slow build, just the sound and the shaking and the doors standing open. I hold that suddenness is not God's absence from my plans but his freedom from them.
