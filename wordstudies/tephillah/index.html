tᵉphillâh

intercession, supplication; by implication, a hymn

Biblical Usage

The word is prayer, intercession, supplication, and the hymn that carries it. Solomon asks God to have respect unto the prayer of His servant; Daniel set his face unto the Lord God, to seek by prayer and supplications, with fasting, and sackcloth, and ashes; the psalmist says in the night God's song shall be with him, and his prayer unto the God of his life; Isaiah says God's house shall be called a house of prayer for all people; and Nehemiah prays day and night for the children of Israel. The word even heads two psalms, where the title itself calls the psalm a prayer.

Theological Significance

I believe prayer is the breath of the people of God, from Solomon's temple to Daniel's window to Nehemiah's nights. My position is that the God who hears prayer made His house a house of prayer, and the praying man is never praying alone.
