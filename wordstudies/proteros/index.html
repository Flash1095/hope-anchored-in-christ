próteros

comparative of G4253; prior or previous:--former.

Biblical Usage

Proteros means former, prior, previous. Paul says to put off the former conversation, the old man, which is corrupt, and the high priest of the old order offered daily, first for his own sins; Hebrews tells believers to call to remembrance the former days, in which, after they were illuminated, they endured a great fight of afflictions.

Theological Significance

I hold that the former life is a country I have left: the old man is put off, the old priesthood is finished, and the former days of affliction are remembered, not repeated, because the God who carried me then carries me now.
