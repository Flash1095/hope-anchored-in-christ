ogdoḗkonta

from G3590; ten times eight:--fourscore.

Biblical Usage

Ogdoekonta means fourscore, eighty. Anna is a widow of about fourscore and four years in Luke 2:37, serving God with fastings and prayers night and day, and in Luke 16:7 a debtor owes a hundred measures of wheat and is told to write fourscore.

Theological Significance

I believe eighty-four years of prayer is a long obedience, and Anna proves that a life of waiting ends in seeing the Lord.
