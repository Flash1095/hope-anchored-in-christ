nâhar

to sparkle, i.e. (figuratively) be cheerful; hence (from the sheen of a running stream) to flow, i.e. (figuratively) assemble

Biblical Usage

Nahar as a verb means to sparkle or to flow, to be cheerful and bright, and it is used for nations coming to the Lord. In Isaiah 2:2 all nations shall flow unto the mountain of the Lord's house in the last days, and in Jeremiah 31:12 they shall come and sing in the height of Zion, and shall flow together to the goodness of the Lord.

Theological Significance

I believe flowing to God is the right picture of worship, because water does not climb, it runs downhill to where it belongs; the nations flow to the mountain because the goodness of the Lord draws them.
