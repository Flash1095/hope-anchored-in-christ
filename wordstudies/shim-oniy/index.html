Shimʻônîy

a Shimonite (collectively) or descendants of Shimon

Biblical Usage

The Simeonites are the tribe of Simeon. Zimri, a prince of a chief house among them, sins at Peor, the tribe is given its inheritance inside Judah, and its prince Shephatiah rules them in the days of David.

Theological Significance

I believe the tribe that began with a brother's sword is counted, settled, and ruled, and God does not throw away a tribe for its beginning.
