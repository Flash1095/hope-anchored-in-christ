pûwq

to issue, i.e. furnish; causatively, to secure; figuratively, to succeed

Biblical Usage

Puq means to obtain, to bring forth, to draw out. Whoso findeth wisdom findeth life, and shall obtain favour of the LORD, and whoso findeth a wife findeth a good thing, and obtaineth favour of the LORD. And if thou draw out thy soul to the hungry, and satisfy the afflicted soul, then shall thy light rise in obscurity.

Theological Significance

I hold that favour is obtained, not earned: wisdom finds it, marriage finds it, and the man who draws out his soul to the hungry finds his own light rising.
