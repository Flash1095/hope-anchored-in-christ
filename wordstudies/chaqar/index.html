châqar

properly, to penetrate; hence, to examine intimately

Biblical Usage

Chaqar is penetrating examination, searching a matter to the bottom. Jonathan says he will sound his father about David, and show it to him. The law commands that when a rumour of idolatry is heard, the people shall enquire and make search and ask diligently. The Preacher sought out and set in order many proverbs, and the princes of Ammon accused David's servants of coming to search out the land.

Theological Significance

I believe searching is a duty, not a suspicion: God commands His people to enquire diligently before they judge a city, and Jonathan sounds his own father before he trusts him. I hold that a hasty judgment is a sin, and the Bible's remedy is always the same: search it out first.
