Yôwʼâb

Joab, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Joab is named 124 times in the Bible, and the passages here are 1 Samuel 26:6; 2 Samuel 2:13; 1 Kings 1:7. In 1 Samuel 26:6 we read, "Then answered David and said to Ahimelech the Hittite, and to Abishai the son of Zeruiah, brother to Joab, saying..." The same name belongs to more than one man, and 1 Kings 1:7 names another of them.

Theological Significance

Joab is enough for God to remember a life, and I hold that a single mention counts. His name stands in the record, and that is enough for me.
