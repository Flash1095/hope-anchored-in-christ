zânach

reject, forsake, fail

Biblical Usage

Zanach is to reject, to forsake, to cast off. In 1 Chronicles 28:9 David warns Solomon: "if thou forsake him, he will cast thee off for ever." In 2 Chronicles 11:14 the Levites leave their lands because Jeroboam "had cast them off from executing the priest's office." Psalms 43:2 cries, "why dost thou cast me off?"

Theological Significance

I believe this word is the sober side of the covenant, and I take David's warning in 1 Chronicles 28:9 seriously. I hold that forsaking God is never a small drift, it is a casting off, and yet the psalmist in Psalms 43:2 still cries out to the very God he feels cast off by. My position is that zanach teaches me to cling to God, because the one who casts off is also the one who welcomes back.
