ʻÊyphâh

Word
עֵיפָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻÊyphâh
Strongs Number
H5891
Definition
Ephah, the name of a son of Midian, and of the region settled by him; also of an Israelite and of an Israelitess
Part Of Speech
proper noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
Ephah = "gloomy"; n pr m; 1) a son of Midian; 2) a Judaite, son of Jahdai; n pr f; 3) a concubine of Caleb in the line of Judah
