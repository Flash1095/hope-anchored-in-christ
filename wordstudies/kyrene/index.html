Kyrḗnē

of uncertain derivation; Cyrene, a region of Africa:--Cyrene.

Biblical Usage

Cyrene appears once, in the list of the peoples at Pentecost in Acts 2:10: Jews and proselytes from "the parts of Libya about Cyrene" heard the apostles speak in their own tongues.

Theological Significance

What I take from this is that the one mention is enough: the gospel was preached to Libya on the day the church was born, and the ends of the earth were in the room from the start.
