acháristos

from G1 (as a negative particle) and a presumed derivative of G5483; thankless, i.e. ungrateful:--unthankful.

Biblical Usage

Unthankful — the word appears twice. Jesus says God "is kind unto the unthankful and to the evil," and calls us to love our enemies the same way; Paul lists "unthankful" among the marks of men in the last days, lovers of themselves and disobedient to parents. The same word describes the people God is kind to and the people who have forgotten Him.

Theological Significance

Ingratitude is not a small fault in Scripture; it is the mark of a heart turned away from its Giver. I hold that thankfulness is one of the plainest tests of whether we remember who gave us everything.
