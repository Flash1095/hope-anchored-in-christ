klētós

from the same as G2821; invited, i.e. appointed, or (specially), a saint:--called.

Biblical Usage

Kletos means called, invited, appointed. Matthew 22:14 says, for many are called, but few are chosen, Romans 1:1 has Paul called to be an apostle, Romans 1:6 says the Romans are the called of Jesus Christ, 1 Corinthians 1:1 repeats the apostle's calling, Jude 1:1 writes to them that are called, and Revelation 17:14 says they that are with the Lamb are called, and chosen, and faithful.

Theological Significance

I believe called is the Christian's first name: before apostle, before saint, before faithful, we are the called. I hold that many are called and few are chosen, and the difference is not in the call but in the answer.
