kânaʻ

properly, to bend the knee; hence, to humiliate, vanquish

Biblical Usage

Kana is to bend the knee — to humble oneself, to be brought low, and so to be subdued. God promises that if His people shall humble themselves and pray and seek His face, He will hear from heaven and heal their land; the princes of Judah humbled themselves and the wrath was turned away; and Ahab humbled himself before the LORD and the evil was stayed.

Theological Significance

I believe humbling is the one road to healing. God does not heal the proud land or the proud heart — He heals the one that bows, and that is why I bow mine before I ask Him for anything.
