theos

Word
θεός
Language
Greek
Transliteration
theos
Strongs Number
G2316
Definition
God — the supreme Creator, ruler and Father; the central word for deity throughout the Greek Scriptures.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
John 1:1; John 3:16; Romans 8:31; Hebrews 11:6
Biblical Usage
Theos appears over 1,300 times in the New Testament. The Bible never argues theos into existence; it announces Him — the God who is for us, who so loved the world, who rewards those who seek Him.
Theological Significance
Theos is the anchor behind the anchor: if God is for us, who can be against us? Every word study in this collection finally points back to Him.
