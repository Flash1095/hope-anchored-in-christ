leqach

properly, something received, i.e. (mentally) instruction (whether on the part of the teacher or hearer); also (in an active and sinister sense) inveiglement

Biblical Usage

leqach means something received, and the Bible uses it the way ordinary people use ordinary words — for everything. In Deuteronomy 32:2, "My doctrine shall drop as the rain"; and in Proverbs 4:2, "For I give you good doctrine". In Proverbs 1:5, "and will increase learning"; and in Isaiah 29:24, "and they that murmured shall learn doctrine.".

Theological Significance

I believe true doctrine falls like rain — it does not argue, it soaks in and makes things grow. I hold that a wise man hears and increases learning, and that the erring in spirit come to understanding. My position is that I will take God's teaching the way dry ground takes rain: open, and glad for it.
