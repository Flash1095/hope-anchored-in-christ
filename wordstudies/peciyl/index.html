pᵉçîyl

an idol

Biblical Usage

Pesil is another word for a graven image, and God commands His people to destroy them: break down the altars, cut down the groves, burn the images with fire. The Danites stole Micah's graven image and carried it off to set up for themselves, and the psalm says Israel provoked God to anger and moved Him to jealousy with their graven images.

Theological Significance

I believe God hates graven images because they steal the worship that belongs to Him alone, and He says so plainly: my glory will I not give to another. An image cannot hear, cannot save, and cannot love — and I will not bow to what my own hands could make. That is why the command to destroy them is not cruelty but rescue.
