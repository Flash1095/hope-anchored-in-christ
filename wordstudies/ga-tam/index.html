Gaʻtâm

Gatam, an Edomite

Biblical Usage

Gatam is a son of Eliphaz, named among the dukes of Edom in Genesis 36:11 and again in 1 Chronicles 1:36. He is Esau's grandson, one of the chiefs of the Edomite tribes. His name survives in two genealogies that trace the family of Esau.

Theological Significance

I believe the family lines of Esau matter because they show how God kept His promises to Abraham's line even outside the chosen line. Gatam is a duke of Edom, and I hold that God's hand is on the nations as well as on Israel.
