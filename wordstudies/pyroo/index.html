pyróō

Word
πυρόω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
pyróō
Strongs Number
G4448
Definition
from G4442; to kindle, i.e. (passively) to be ignited, glow (literally), be refined (by implication), or (figuratively) to be inflamed (with anger, grief, lust):--burn, fiery, be on fire, try.
