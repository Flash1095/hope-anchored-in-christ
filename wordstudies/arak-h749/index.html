ʼărak

to suit

Biblical Usage

The word is Aramaic for suiting or being fitting, and it appears once, when the adversaries of Judah write to the king that they have maintenance from the king's palace, and it is not fitting for them to see the king dishonoured.

Theological Significance

Even enemies of God's work can sound reasonable when they talk about what is fitting.
