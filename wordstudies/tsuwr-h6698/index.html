Tsûwr

Tsur, the name of a Midianite and of an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Zur is the name of two men, a Midianite prince killed in the days of Moses and an Israelite of Benjamin. In Numbers 25:15, the daughter of Zur. The fuller story is told on the Zur profile in Biblical People.

Theological Significance

My position is that Zur belongs in the story because God put the name there, and I trust the placing.
