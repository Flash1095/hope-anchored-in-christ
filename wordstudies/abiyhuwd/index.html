ʼĂbîyhûwd

Abihud, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Abihud appears in two places and nowhere else. In Chronicles he is a son of Bela and a grandson of Benjamin — ‘And the sons of Bela were, Addar, and Gera, and Abihud’ — and in Matthew he stands in the genealogy of Jesus after the exile, between Zerubbabel and Eliakim.

Theological Significance

One name in a genealogy, with no story attached — and still the line runs through him to Christ. I believe Abihud is there for people like me who feel unremarkable: God keeps His promise through quiet generations too, and a name in the line is enough.
