kâʼab

properly, to feel pain; by implication, to grieve; figuratively, to spoil

Biblical Usage

Ka-ab is the pain that sits in the body and the heart together — the ache of grief, the soreness of a wound. Job says the flesh upon him shall have pain and his soul within him shall mourn; the proverb says even in laughter the heart is sorrowful; and the psalmist cries out that he is poor and sorrowful, asking God to set him on high.

Theological Significance

I believe God does not dismiss this pain or tell us to get over it. Scripture names it plainly and then sets it before the God who heals — and that is why I take my own grief to Him instead of hiding it.
