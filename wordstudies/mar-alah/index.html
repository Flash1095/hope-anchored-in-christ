Marʻălâh

Maralah, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Marʻălâh is Maralah, a town on the border of Zebulun named once, in Joshua 19:11. There the border goes up toward the sea, and Maralah, and reaches to Dabbasheth and to the river before Jokneam. The name is a mark on the edge of an inheritance.

Theological Significance

I believe Marʻălâh shows me that God bounded His people's land with care, and that even a border town is part of the inheritance.
