epaischýnomai

from G1909 and G153; to feel shame for something:--be ashamed.

Biblical Usage

To feel shame for something. Paul tells Timothy not to be ashamed of the testimony of the Lord or of him His prisoner in 2 Timothy 1:8, and Hebrews says Christ is not ashamed to call us brethren in Hebrews 2:11. Jesus warns that whoever is ashamed of Him and His words will be met with the same shame when He comes in glory in Luke 9:26 and Mark 8:38.

Theological Significance

I believe shame runs both ways — I can be ashamed of Him, or He can be unashamed of me, and the difference is everything. I hold that He who is not ashamed to call me His brother deserves a witness who is not ashamed to call Him Lord.
