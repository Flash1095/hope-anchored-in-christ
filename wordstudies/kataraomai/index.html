kataráomai

middle voice from G2671; to execrate; by analogy, to doom:--curse.

Biblical Usage

To curse is to execrate, and the King will say to those on His left, Depart from me, ye cursed, into everlasting fire, while Peter remembered the fig tree Jesus had cursed and saw it withered. Yet the same Lord says to bless them that curse you and pray for them that despitefully use you, and Paul says the same. The word is both a judgment to fear and a command to reverse.

Theological Significance

I believe the curse on the last day is real, and that is why I bless now instead of cursing. I hold that blessing my enemies is the proof that Christ has changed my heart, because the world curses back and I am called to do the opposite.
