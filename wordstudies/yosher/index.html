yôsher

the right

Biblical Usage

Yosher is what is right and straight, and God delights in it. Deuteronomy 9:5 warns Israel that they do not possess the land for their own uprightness, so the word never becomes a boast. In 1 Kings 9:4 God tells Solomon to walk "in integrity of heart, and in uprightness," and in 1 Chronicles 29:17 David says God "hast pleasure in uprightness." Job 6:25 adds that right words carry force: "How forcible are right words!"

Theological Significance

I believe God loves uprightness because uprightness is what he is. I hold that I cannot claim the land, the promise, or the blessing by my own rightness, as Deuteronomy 9:5 makes plain, and yet he still calls me to walk straight before him. My position is that yosher is both a gift I receive and a road I am asked to walk.
