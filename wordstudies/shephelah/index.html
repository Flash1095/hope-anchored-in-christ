Language

Transliteration
shᵉphêlâh
Strongs Number

Definition
Lowland, i.e. (with the article) the maritime slope of Palestine
Part Of Speech
noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
1) lowland, valley; 1a) lowland; 1a1) strip west of Judean mountains (technical term); 1a1a) the Shephelah; 1a2) strip near coast north of Carmel
