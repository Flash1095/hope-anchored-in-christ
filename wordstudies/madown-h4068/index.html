Mâdôwn

Madon, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Madon was a Canaanite city with its own king, named twice. Joshua 11:1 has Jabin king of Hazor sending to Jobab king of Madon, and Joshua 12:19 lists the king of Madon among the kings Joshua struck down.

Theological Significance

I believe Madon's two verses tell the whole conquest in miniature: a king who joined the alliance, and a king who fell.
