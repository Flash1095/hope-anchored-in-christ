sherets

a swarm, i.e. active mass of minute animals

Biblical Usage

The waters bring forth the moving creature at creation, the flood drowns every creeping thing that moved on the earth, and the law sorts the swarming things into clean and unclean. The word covers the whole small teeming world, from the fish of the sea to the insects of the field.

Theological Significance

I believe the swarm matters because God made it and God judged it and God set bounds around it. Nothing that creeps is beneath His notice, and nothing unclean gets past His law, so I take His ordering of the small things as seriously as His ordering of the great ones.
