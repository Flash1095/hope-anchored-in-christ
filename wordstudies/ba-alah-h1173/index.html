Baʻălâh

Word
בַּעֲלָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Baʻălâh
Strongs Number
H1173
Definition
Baalah, the name of three places in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Baalah = "mistress"; 1) another name for Kirjath-jearim or Kirjath-baal; modern Kuriet el Enab; 2) a town in the south of Judah, also called Balah and Bilhah
