Chăzâʼêl

Chazael, a king of Syria

Biblical Usage

Hazael is the king of Syria whom God tells Elijah to anoint, and his life is written in violence from the start. The king of Syria sends him to Elisha to ask whether he will recover, and the next morning Hazael spreads a wet cloth over the king's face and reigns in his stead.

Theological Significance

I believe Hazael shows that God raises up even cruel kings to do His judgements. Elijah anoints him at God's command, and Elisha weeps over what he will do to Israel.
