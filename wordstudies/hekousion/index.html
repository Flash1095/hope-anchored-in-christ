hekoúsion

neuter of a derivative from G1635; voluntariness:--willingly.

Biblical Usage

Hekousion means willingly, of free will, and it appears once, in Paul's letter about Onesimus. In Philemon 1:14 Paul says he would do nothing without Philemon's mind, that the benefit should not be as it were of necessity, but willingly.

Theological Significance

I believe Paul is showing what love does with authority: he could command, and instead he asks, because a good deed done under pressure is not the same as one done willingly.
