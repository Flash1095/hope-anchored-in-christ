kollybistḗs

from a presumed derivative of κόλλυβος (a small coin; probably akin to G2854); a coin-dealer:--(money-)changer.

Biblical Usage

Kollybistes is a money-changer, a dealer in coins. Matthew 21:12 and Mark 11:15 both have Jesus overthrowing the tables of the moneychangers in the temple, and John 2:15 has Him pouring out the changers' money and overthrowing the tables.

Theological Significance

I believe the overthrown tables are the sign of a cleansing: the house of prayer had become a market, and Jesus drove the market out. I hold that the moneychangers are still with us whenever the church sells what God gives freely, and the tables still need turning.
