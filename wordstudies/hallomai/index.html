hállomai

middle voice of apparently a primary verb; to jump; figuratively, to gush:--leap, spring up.

Biblical Usage

hállomai means leap, spring up. In John 4:14, the text says "but the water that I shall give him shall be in him a well of water springing up into everlasting life." In Acts 3:8, the text says "And he leaping up stood."

Theological Significance

hállomai convinces me that hope is not wishing; it is trusting God for what He has promised, even before we see it. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
