ʼêybâh

hostility

Biblical Usage

ʼêybâh means hostility. It appears in Genesis, Numbers, and Ezekiel, and each time it carries that same plain sense. The word keeps that same sense wherever it appears.

Theological Significance

I take ʼêybâh to mean that rebellion is not freedom; it is slavery to ourselves. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
