cheçer

lack; hence, destitution

Biblical Usage

This is want or lack, and it describes the poor who flee into the wilderness for want and famine, and the man who hastens to be rich and does not consider that poverty shall come upon him. Want follows the hasty and the hungry alike.

Theological Significance

I believe want is the end of haste: the man who rushes to be rich does not see the poverty coming, and God sees it plainly.
