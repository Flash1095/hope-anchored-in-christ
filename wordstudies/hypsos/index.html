hýpsos

from a derivative of G5228; elevation, i.e. (abstractly) altitude, (specially), the sky, or (figuratively) dignity:--be exalted, height, (on) high.

Biblical Usage

hýpsos means height, exaltation, what is high. In Luke 1:78, the text says "whereby the dayspring from on high hath visited us." In Ephesians 3:18, the text says "and height."

Theological Significance

I believe hýpsos shows us that glory is the weight of who God is, and it is the end of everything He does. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
