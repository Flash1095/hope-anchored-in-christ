ʼĂmittay

Amittai, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Once in each verse, the father of a prophet: Jonah the son of Amittai, the prophet, which was of Gath-hepher, by whose word Jeroboam restored the coast of Israel. And the word of the LORD came unto Jonah the son of Amittai, saying, Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city, and cry against it; the name is the father's name, and the son is the prophet who ran.

Theological Significance

Jonah is introduced by his father's name before he is sent anywhere, and that is all Amittai's name does: it places the prophet in a family before it places him in Nineveh.
