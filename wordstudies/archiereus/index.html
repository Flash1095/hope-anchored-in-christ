archiereús

Word
ἀρχιερεύς
Language
Greek
Transliteration
archiereús
Strongs Number
G749
Definition
from G746 and G2409; the high-priest (literally, of the Jews, typically, Christ); by extension a chief priest:--chief (high) priest, chief of the priests.
