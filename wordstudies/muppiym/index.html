Muppîym

Word
מֻפִּים
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Muppîym
Strongs Number
H4649
Definition
Muppim, an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Muppim = "serpent"; 1) a Benjamite and one of the 14 descendants of Rachel who belonged to the original colony of the sons of Jacob in Egypt; 1a) also 'Shupham'
