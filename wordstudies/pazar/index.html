pâzar

to scatter, whether in enmity or bounty

Biblical Usage

Pazar means to scatter, whether in enmity or in bounty. The Jews are scattered among the provinces in Esther 3:8, and God says in Joel 3:2 that He will plead with the nations because they have scattered His people. The psalmist says in Psalm 112:9 that the good man has dispersed, he has given to the poor. Proverbs 11:24 says there is that scattereth, and yet increaseth, and there is that withholdeth more than is meet, but it tendeth to poverty.

Theological Significance

I believe scattering is both judgment and generosity in this word: the nations scatter God's people, and the good man scatters his goods to the poor. My position is that what I scatter for God increases, and what I scatter against God is gathered back in judgment.
