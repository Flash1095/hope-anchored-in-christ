sébomai

middle voice of an apparently primary verb; to revere, i.e. adore:--devout, religious, worship.

Biblical Usage

Sebomai is worshipping, revering, and it cuts both ways. Many of the Jews and religious proselytes followed Paul and Barnabas, persuaded to continue in the grace of God; and the devout and honourable women were stirred up against them. But Jesus warns the other side of it: in vain do they worship me, teaching for doctrines the commandments of men.

Theological Significance

I believe worship is proved by what it obeys, not by how devout it looks. The proselytes who followed Paul worshipped truly; those who worshipped by men's commandments worshipped in vain. And I hold that the difference is still the same — God's word, or man's.
