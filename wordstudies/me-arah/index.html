mᵉʻârâh

a cavern (as dark)

Biblical Usage

A cave is a hiding place and a grave in the Bible, and the word runs through the stories of the fugitives: Lot dwelt in a cave with his daughters after Sodom, David hid in the cave of Adullam and the men in distress gathered to him, and Saul went into a cave to cover his feet while David stood behind him. Abraham bought the cave of Machpelah for a burial place, and five kings hid in a cave at Makkedah.

Theological Significance

I believe the cave is where God's people learn to hide in Him rather than in the rock: Lot's cave ends in shame, David's cave gathers the distressed into an army, and Abraham's cave becomes the family grave where faith waits for the resurrection. The rock can hide you, but only God can keep you.
