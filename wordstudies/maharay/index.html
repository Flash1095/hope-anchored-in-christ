Mahăray

Maharai, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Maharai the Netophathite was one of David's mighty men, named in 1 Chronicles 11:30 and 2 Samuel 23:28, and 1 Chronicles 27:13 says he was the tenth captain for the tenth month, of the Zarhites, with twenty and four thousand in his course. He served as the tenth captain for the tenth month, and his course numbered twenty and four thousand.

Theological Significance

I believe Maharai's place on two lists tells his story: a mighty man in battle and a captain in his month, faithful in both.
