apangéllō

from G575 and the base of G32; to announce:--bring word (again), declare, report, shew (again), tell.

Biblical Usage

This is the ordinary word for bringing news back to someone — the apostles reporting to their own people after their release, Peter telling the household what the angel had done, the women returning from the empty tomb to tell the eleven, and John passing on what he had seen and heard. The word covers good news and hard news alike, and it always carries a report from someone who was there.

Theological Significance

I believe the gospel travels the same way it always has: one person telling another what they saw and heard. John says he writes so that others may share the fellowship he found, and I hold that no report in Scripture matters more than the one that says Christ is risen and seen.
