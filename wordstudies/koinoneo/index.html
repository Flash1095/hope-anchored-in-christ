koinōnéō

from G2844; to share with others (objectively or subjectively):--communicate, distribute, be partaker.

Biblical Usage

Koinoneo means to share, to communicate, to take part with others. Romans 12:13 commands distributing to the necessity of saints and being given to hospitality, Galatians 6:6 says let him that is taught communicate unto him that teacheth in all good things, Philippians 4:15 remembers the church that communicated with Paul in giving and receiving, 1 Timothy 5:22 warns not to be partaker of other men's sins, Hebrews 2:14 says Christ Himself took part of flesh and blood, and 1 Peter 4:13 calls us partakers of Christ's sufferings.

Theological Significance

I believe sharing is the shape of the whole gospel: Christ took part in our flesh and blood so that we might take part in His sufferings and His glory. I hold that the church that communicates in giving and receiving is the church that understands the sharing, and the warning of 1 Timothy 5:22 is its guard.
