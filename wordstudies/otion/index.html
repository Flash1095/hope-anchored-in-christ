ōtíon

diminutive of G3775; an earlet, i.e. one of the ears, or perhaps the lobe of the ear:--ear.

Biblical Usage

The ear appears at the arrest in the garden. Peter cut off the ear of the high priest's servant, and a kinsman of the wounded man asked, Did not I see thee in the garden with Him? and Jesus touched the ear and healed him.

Theological Significance

What I hold is that the ear that Peter cut off and Jesus healed is the picture of my own zeal: I defend Him with the sword, and He heals the wound I made.
