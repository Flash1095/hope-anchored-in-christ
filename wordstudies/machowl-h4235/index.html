Mâchôwl

Machol, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Machol was the father of Ethan, Heman, Chalcol, and Darda, the wise men Solomon is measured against in 1 Kings 4:31, where Solomon was wiser than all men, than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, and Chalcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol. His sons were the wise men of their day, and Solomon was counted wiser than all of them.

Theological Significance

I hold that Machol is remembered only as a father, and that is enough: his sons' wisdom is the record of his house.
