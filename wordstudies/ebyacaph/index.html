ʼEbyâçâph

Ebjasaph, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Ebiasaph is a son of Korah, named in the genealogies of the Levites who served at the tabernacle, and his descendants kept the gates of God's house. His name means my father has gathered, and the lists place him in the line of the Korahites who sang and guarded. He is one of the quiet Levites whose work was to keep the house of God in order.

Theological Significance

I believe the gatekeepers and singers matter as much as the priests, because God's house needs people who show up and serve faithfully. Ebiasaph's line did that for generations.
