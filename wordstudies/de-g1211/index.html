dḗ

probably akin to G1161; a particle of emphasis or explicitness; now, then, etc.:--also, and, doubtless, now, therefore.

Biblical Usage

The little word carries emphasis, and the New Testament uses it to press a point home. Paul writes that we are bought with a price, and the therefore is stronger for it; the Lord says the harvest is plentiful and the labourers are few, and the indeed sharpens the call. A small word, doing the work of a finger on the chest.

Theological Significance

I believe small words carry weight in Scripture: the therefore and the indeed are where the argument lands, and we should not skip them. The small word is the hinge, and the sentence turns on it more than we think.
