bôrîyth

vegetable alkali

Biblical Usage

Soap is the word for lye, the alkali used in washing — and it appears twice, both times about a washing that does not work. In Jeremiah 2:22 God says, though thou wash thee with nitre, and take thee much soap, yet thine iniquity is marked before me. In Malachi 3:2 the Lord is like a refiner's fire and like fullers' soap, and who may abide the day of his coming?

Theological Significance

I believe the soap in Jeremiah is the point: no washing of mine can take away the mark of sin — only the Refiner can, and he comes with fire.
