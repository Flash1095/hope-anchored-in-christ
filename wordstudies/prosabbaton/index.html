prosábbaton

from G4253 and G4521; a fore-sabbath, i.e. the Sabbath-eve:--day before the sabbath. Compare G3904.

Biblical Usage

Prosabbaton is the day before the Sabbath, the preparation day. Mark uses it once: it was the preparation, that is, the day before the Sabbath, when Joseph of Arimathaea came and craved the body of Jesus.

Theological Significance

I believe the day before the Sabbath became the day of the burial, and the preparation was for a rest no one expected.
