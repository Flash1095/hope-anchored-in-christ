cháragma

from the same as G5482; a scratch or etching, i.e. stamp (as a badge of servitude), or sculptured figure (statue):--graven, mark.

Biblical Usage

Charagma is a stamp, an etching, a mark, and it carries the weight of belonging. Paul tells the Athenians that the Godhead is not like gold or silver or stone, graven by art and man's device. In Revelation the mark is the beast's: he causes all to receive a mark in their right hand or in their foreheads, and those who worship the beast and receive his mark drink the wine of God's wrath. The beast and the false prophet who deceived them that had the mark are cast into the lake of fire.

Theological Significance

I believe the mark is the Bible's picture of allegiance made visible: every person bears someone's stamp. Paul says God cannot be stamped by human art, and John says the beast stamps his followers. I hold that the only safe mark is the one God puts on His own, and I want to be sure whose stamp I carry.
