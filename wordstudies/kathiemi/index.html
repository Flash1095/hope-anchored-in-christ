kathíēmi

from G2596; and ἵημι (to send); to lower:--let down.

Biblical Usage

To let down is what the disciples did when they lowered Paul in a basket by the wall, and what Peter saw when the sheet was let down from heaven, and what the friends did when they let the paralytic down through the tiling into the midst before Jesus. The word holds the basket, the sheet, and the bed together: all three are lowered by hands that believe the Lord is on the other end.

Theological Significance

I believe the lowered basket is the picture of the church at its best, because friends who let a man down by a wall are the same friends who let a man down through a roof. I hold that the sheet from heaven and the bed through the roof are the same message: God lets His gifts down to us, and we let one another down to Him.
