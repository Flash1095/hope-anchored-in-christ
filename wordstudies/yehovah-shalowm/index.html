Yᵉhôvâh shâlôwm

Jehovah-Shalom, a symbolical name of an altar in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Jehovah-shalom is the name Gideon gave to the altar he built in Ophrah after the angel appeared to him under the oak. The name means the LORD is peace, and Gideon called the altar by that name because the LORD had said peace be unto thee. The name stands in the verse as two words together, the LORD and peace, because the Hebrew text carries the name under its parts and not as a single word.

Theological Significance

I believe the LORD is peace, and Gideon learned it before he ever lifted a sword. My position is that peace comes before the battle, and the altar at Ophrah is the proof.
