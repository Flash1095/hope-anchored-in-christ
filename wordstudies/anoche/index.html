anochḗ

from G430; self-restraint, i.e. tolerance:--forbearance.

Biblical Usage

Forbearance, the patience that holds back judgment. Paul asks if we despise the riches of God's goodness and forbearance and longsuffering, which leads us to repentance, and says God set forth Christ to declare His righteousness for the remission of sins that are past, through the forbearance of God. The word is God waiting before He judges.

Theological Significance

I believe forbearance is the space God gives me to repent, the judgment He holds back so that I can turn. His goodness is not His blindness, and I hold that every day of His patience is a day meant for turning back to Him.
