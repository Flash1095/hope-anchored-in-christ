katapétasma

Word
καταπέτασμα
Language
Greek
Transliteration
katapétasma
Strongs Number
G2665
Definition
from a compound of G2596 and a congener of G4072; something spread thoroughly, i.e. (specially) the door screen (to the Most Holy Place) in the Jewish Temple:--vail.
