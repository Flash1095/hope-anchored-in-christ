katapétasma

from a compound of G2596 and a congener of G4072; something spread thoroughly, i.e. (specially) the door screen (to the Most Holy Place) in the Jewish Temple:--vail.

Biblical Usage

The veil was the curtain spread across the holy place, and when Jesus died it was torn in two from top to bottom, the sun was darkened, and the way into the holiest was opened. Hebrews says we now have boldness to enter by the blood of Jesus, through the veil, that is to say, His flesh. The tear was from the top, because God did it, not man.

Theological Significance

I believe the torn veil is the loudest sermon the temple ever preached, because it says the way is open and the price is paid. I hold that I do not need a priest to go in for me anymore, because my Priest has gone in and left the door open.
