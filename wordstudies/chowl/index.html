chôwl

sand (as round or whirling particles)

Biblical Usage

Sand is the picture of what cannot be counted, and God promises Abraham offspring as many as the sand on the seashore. Moses hides a body in the sand, and Deuteronomy speaks of the treasures hidden in it. Hosea picks the promise up again, telling Israel they will be as the sand of the sea.

Theological Significance

I believe God chose sand on purpose: a promise too big to count, given to people who could count everything else. My position is that the promise still stands behind every believer, because the God who counts the sand keeps His word. That is why the same picture returns in Hosea, long after Abraham is gone.
