qᵉṭôreth

a fumigation

Biblical Usage

Aaron burns sweet incense every morning on the golden altar, and on the Day of Atonement the high priest carries it within the veil. When wrath went out and the plague began, Moses sent Aaron with the censer to make atonement, and the plague was stayed. The psalmist saw it plainly: let my prayer be set forth before thee as incense.

Theological Significance

I believe incense is prayer made visible, and Aaron's censer stood between the living and the dead because God put it there. I hold that the same smoke still rises when God's people pray, and he still hears it.
