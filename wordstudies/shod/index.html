shôd

violence, ravage

Biblical Usage

Shod is violence, the ravage that men do to each other and that God judges. The prophets use it for the wasting and destruction in the paths of the violent, for the spoil stored up in palaces, and for the robbery that destroys the robber himself. It is never an accident; it is a choice with a harvest.

Theological Significance

I believe violence always comes home. The prophets say the same thing in every generation: the hands that take by force will be emptied by force, and only the Lord's justice ends the cycle.
