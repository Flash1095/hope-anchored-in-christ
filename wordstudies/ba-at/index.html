bâʻaṭ

to trample down, i.e. (figuratively) despise

Biblical Usage

Ba'at means to trample down, to kick — and it is the word for despising what God gave. Eli's sons kicked at the sacrifices of the Lord, and Jeshurun waxed fat and kicked when he was full of God's blessing. Trampling is contempt with the foot.

Theological Significance

I believe kicking at God's gifts is the surest way to lose them, and Jeshurun's story is my warning to stay hungry for him even when I am full.
