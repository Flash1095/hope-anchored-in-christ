elattóō

from G1640; to lessen (in rank or influence):--decrease, make lower.

Biblical Usage

elattóō means to make lower. In John 3:30, the text says "but I [must] decrease." In Hebrews 2:7, the text says "Thou madest him a little lower than the angels."

Theological Significance

I believe elattóō shows us that humility is not thinking less of ourselves; it is thinking of ourselves less, and of God more. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
