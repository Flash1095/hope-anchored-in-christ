ʼĔlîyphaz

Eliphaz, the name of one of Job's friends, and of a son of Esau

Biblical Usage

Eliphaz is the name of one of Job's friends, and of a son of Esau. In Genesis 36:4, the text says "And Adah bare to Esau Eliphaz." The life behind the name is told on the Eliphaz People profile.

Theological Significance

I believe Eliphaz matters because God knows His people by name, and Eliphaz is one of them. That is why I want the name, and the person behind it, kept straight.
