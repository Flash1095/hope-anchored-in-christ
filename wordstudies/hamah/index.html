hâmâh

to make a loud sound (like English 'hum'); by implication, to be in great commotion or tumult, to rage, war, moan, clamor

Biblical Usage

Hamah is the sound of roaring, moaning and murmuring, and it runs through the whole Bible like a low noise underneath the words. The psalmist's soul roars within him in Psalms 42:5, the waters roar in Psalms 46:3, the people mourn like doves in Isaiah 59:11, and Jeremiah cries that his heart makes a noise within him in Jeremiah 4:19.

Theological Significance

I believe God hears the roar before he hears the words, and he is not put off by the noise of a troubled heart. The groaning is itself a prayer, and the psalmist shows me I can bring it to God as it is.
