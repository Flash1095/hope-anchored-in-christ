çôbeʼ

potation, concretely (wine), or abstractly (carousal)

Biblical Usage

Strong drink is wine drunk to excess, and the law calls the stubborn son a glutton and a drunkard. Hosea says the princes love shame more than their drink, Isaiah says the wine is mixed with water, and Nahum says the drunkards will be devoured like stubble. The word is not wine itself; it is wine abused.

Theological Significance

I believe the Bible never condemns the gift, but it always condemns the abuse of it. Strong drink makes a man stubborn, shameful, and dull to God, and the prophets name it plainly. My position is that drunkenness is a small sin with a large harvest, and the harvest is always judgment.
