próskairos

from G4314 and G2540; for the occasion only, i.e. temporary:--dur-(eth) for awhile, endure for a time, for a season, temporal.

Biblical Usage

Proskairos means for a season only, temporary, lasting for a while. The seed on stony ground endures for a while, then when tribulation arises it is offended. Moses chose rather to suffer affliction with the people of God than to enjoy the pleasures of sin for a season. And Paul says the things which are seen are temporal, but the things which are not seen are eternal.

Theological Significance

I believe this word draws the line of my whole life: everything I can see is for a season, and everything that lasts is unseen. I hold that the pleasures of sin are never worth their price, because they are temporary and the loss is eternal.
