chîyn

beauty

Biblical Usage

Chiyn is beauty, the comely proportion of a thing, and it stands once in God's speech about leviathan. The Lord says He will not conceal the creature's parts, nor his power, nor his comely proportion, as if even the strength of the sea beast has a beauty worth naming.

Theological Significance

I hold that God delights in the beauty of what He made, even in creatures that frighten us.
