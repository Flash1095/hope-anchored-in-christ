Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
dûwach
Strongs Number

Definition
to thrust away; figuratively, to cleanse
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to rinse, cleanse away by rinsing, cast out, purge, wash, washing; 1a) (Hiphil); 1a1) to rinse; 1a2) to cleanse (by washing) (fig.)
