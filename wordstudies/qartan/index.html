Qartân

Word
קַרְתָּן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Qartân
Strongs Number
H7178
Definition
Kartan, a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Kartan = "two cities"; 1) a town in Naphtali allotted to the Gershonite Levites; 1a) also 'Kirjathaim'
