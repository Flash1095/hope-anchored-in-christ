ʻAttay

Attai, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Three men bear the name Attai, and the record keeps each one in his own place. One was the son of Jarha the Egyptian, born to Sheshan's daughter, one was a Gadite captain who joined David at Ziklag, and one was the son of Rehoboam and Maachah.

Theological Significance

I believe the three Attais show how the record honours men in very different stations, a servant's son, a soldier, and a king's son, and God counts them all.
