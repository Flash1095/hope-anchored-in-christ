eilikríneia

from G1506; clearness, i.e. (by implication) purity (figuratively):--sincerity.

Biblical Usage

Sincerity is clearness, purity judged in the sunlight, and Paul uses it for the way Christians should live and speak. He says to keep the feast with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth, boasts that his conduct was in simplicity and godly sincerity, and says he speaks in Christ as of sincerity, as of God. The word pictures something held up to the sun to see whether it is clean all the way through.

Theological Significance

I believe sincerity is the test of everything I say about God, because words spoken in the dark cannot stand in His light. I want my life to be clean all the way through, not only on the surface.
