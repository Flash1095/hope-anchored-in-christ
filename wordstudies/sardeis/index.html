Sárdeis

plural of uncertain derivation; Sardis, a place in Asia Minor:--Sardis.

Biblical Usage

Sardis is one of the seven churches, named in the letter John was told to write and send through Asia. The Lord's word to her is blunt: I know thy works, that thou hast a name that thou livest, and art dead. Yet a few names even in Sardis had not defiled their garments, and they shall walk with Him in white, for they are worthy.

Theological Significance

I believe Sardis warns every church that a living reputation is not a living faith. What matters is not the name a church carries, but whether its garments are clean.
