lâvâh

Word
לָוָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
lâvâh
Strongs Number
H3867
Definition
properly, to twine, i.e. (by implication) to unite, to remain; also to borrow (as a form of obligation) or (caus.) to lend
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to join, be joined; 1a) (Qal) to join, be joined, attend; 1b) (Niphal) to join oneself to, be joined unto; 2) to borrow, lend; 2a) (Qal) to borrow; 2b) (Hiphil) to cause to borrow, lend to
