ʻAshtôreth

Word
עַשְׁתֹּרֶת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻAshtôreth
Strongs Number
H6253
Definition
Ashtoreth, the Phoenician goddess of love (and increase)
Part Of Speech
proper noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
Ashtoreth = "star"; 1) the principal female deity of the Phoenicians worshipped in war and fertility; 1a) also 'Ishtar' of Assyria and 'Astarte' by the Greeks and Romans
