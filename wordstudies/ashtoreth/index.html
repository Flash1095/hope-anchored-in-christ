ʻAshtôreth

Ashtoreth, the Phoenician goddess of love (and increase)

Biblical Usage

Ashtoreth is the goddess of the Zidonians, and Solomon's fall is told by her name. Solomon went after Ashtoreth the goddess of the Zidonians, and after Milcom the abomination of the Ammonites; the LORD says they have forsaken Me and worshipped Ashtoreth; and Josiah defiles the high places Solomon built for Ashtoreth, the abomination of the Zidonians, on the right hand of the mount of corruption.

Theological Significance

I believe Ashtoreth is the name that marks Solomon's fall: the wisest king in Israel builds high places for a goddess, and the kingdom breaks in his son's days. And I hold that Josiah's defiling of those high places is the answer, that what one king builds in folly another must tear down in faithfulness.
