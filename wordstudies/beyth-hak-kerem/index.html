Bêyth hak-Kerem

Beth-hak-Kerem, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Beth-Haccerem means house of the vineyard, and it is named twice, in two very different moods. Jeremiah 6:1 tells Benjamin to set up a sign of fire in Beth-haccerem as the enemy comes, and Nehemiah 3:14 shows Malchiah, ruler of part of Beth-haccerem, repairing the dung gate in the rebuilding. A vineyard town that saw both alarm and repair.

Theological Significance

I hold that the same town can stand in a warning and in a rebuilding, and God is in both.
