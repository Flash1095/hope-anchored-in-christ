Símōn

of Hebrew origin (H8095); Simon (i.e. Shimon), the name of nine Israelites:--Simon. Compare G4826.

Biblical Usage

Simon is the name of nine men in the New Testament, but one Simon towers above the rest. Simon called Peter was the fisherman Jesus saw casting a net in Galilee, who fell at His knees confessing himself a sinful man, and whom the risen Lord asked three times whether he loved Him, then told him to feed His lambs.

Theological Significance

I believe Simon Peter is the proof that failure is not final, because the man who denied Him was the man He trusted with His sheep.
