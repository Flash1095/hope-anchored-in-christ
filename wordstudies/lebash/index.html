lᵉbash

{properly, wrap around, i.e. (by implication) to put on a garment or clothe (oneself, or another), literally or figuratively}

Biblical Usage

lᵉbash means to clothe, and the Bible uses it the way ordinary people use ordinary words — for everything. In Daniel 5:7, "shall be clothed with scarlet"; and in Daniel 5:16, "thou shalt be clothed with scarlet". In Daniel 5:29, "and they clothed Daniel with scarlet".

Theological Significance

I believe clothing tells the story of Daniel's night — Belshazzar promised scarlet to any man who could read the writing, and Daniel wore it for an hour before the kingdom fell. I hold that robes and chains cannot save a king who has weighed himself against God. My position is that I would rather be clothed in humility than in scarlet.
