qeṭeb

ruin

Biblical Usage

Moses warns of bitter destruction devouring with burning heat, and Isaiah sees a destroying storm cast down to the earth with the hand. The word is ruin itself, falling like hail.

Theological Significance

I believe destruction is never a word God uses lightly, and when he sends it, the storm does what he says.
