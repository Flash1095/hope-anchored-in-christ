cheimṓn

from a derivative of χέω (to pour; akin to the base of 5490 through the idea of a channel), meaning a storm (as pouring rain); by implication, the rainy season, i.e. winter:--tempest, foul weather, winter.

Biblical Usage

Winter is the rainy season, the time of storm and cold, and it is the season Jesus warns about and Paul sails through. Pray that your flight is not in winter, John notes that the feast of dedication came in winter, the storm takes sun and stars away for many days, and Paul tells Timothy to come before winter.

Theological Significance

I believe winter in the Bible is the hard season God still rules: the flight, the feast, the storm, and the journey are all His. He keeps His people through the cold, and the winter does not last for ever.
