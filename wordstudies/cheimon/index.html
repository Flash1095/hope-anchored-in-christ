cheimṓn

Word
χειμών
Language
Greek
Transliteration
cheimṓn
Strongs Number
G5494
Definition
from a derivative of χέω (to pour; akin to the base of 5490 through the idea of a channel), meaning a storm (as pouring rain); by implication, the rainy season, i.e. winter:--tempest, foul weather, winter.
