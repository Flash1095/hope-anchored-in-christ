pēdálion

neuter of a (presumed) derivative of πηδόν (the blade of an oar; from the same as G3976); a "pedal", i.e. helm:--rudder.

Biblical Usage

A pedalion is a rudder, the small helm that steers a great ship. Paul's shipmates loosed the rudder bands and made for shore as the storm broke, and James says the same of the tongue: a very small helm turns the whole ship wherever the governor wills.

Theological Significance

I am convinced that small things steer great ones, and the tongue is my rudder — a word can turn a whole life toward the rocks or toward shore.
