epéchō

from G1909 and G2192; to hold upon, i.e. (by implication) to retain; (by extension) to detain; (with implication, of G3563) to pay attention to:--give (take) heed unto, hold forth, mark, stay.

Biblical Usage

Epecho means to hold upon — to hold forth, to pay attention, to stay with something. Jesus marks how guests choose the chief rooms; the lame man gives heed to Peter and John, expecting to receive; Paul tells the Philippians to hold forth the word of life, and Timothy to take heed to himself and the doctrine.

Theological Significance

Attention is faithfulness, and what we keep our eyes on shapes what we become. I hold that holding forth the word and holding fast to it are the same posture, and Timothy's charge is mine too: take heed to yourself and to the doctrine.
