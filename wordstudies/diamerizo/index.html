diamerízō

from G1223 and G3307; to partition thoroughly (literally in distribution, figuratively in dissension):--cloven, divide, part.

Biblical Usage

To divide is to part out, and the word stands at the cross and in the church. The soldiers divide Christ's garments among them and cast lots for His coat, and the first believers sell their possessions and divide them to all who have need.

Theological Significance

I believe the dividing at the cross and the dividing in the church are two sides of one coin. Men divided His clothes in greed, and His people divided their goods in love, and the difference is the difference the cross makes.
