oneidismós

from G3679; contumely:--reproach.

Biblical Usage

Oneidismos is reproach, the taunt, the scorn thrown at a person. Christ pleased not Himself in Romans 15:3, and the reproaches of those who reproached God fell on Him. An overseer must have a good report lest he fall into reproach in 1 Timothy 3:7, and the Hebrews were made a gazingstock by reproaches in Hebrews 10:33. Moses esteemed the reproach of Christ greater riches than the treasures of Egypt in Hebrews 11:26.

Theological Significance

I believe reproach is the price of standing with Christ, because the taunts thrown at God fall on those who belong to Him. My position is that the reproach of Christ is greater riches than Egypt, and Moses chose it on purpose.
