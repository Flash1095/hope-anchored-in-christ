zâdôwn

arrogance

Biblical Usage

Zadon is arrogance, the pride that presumes. Deuteronomy 17:12 deals with the man who "will do presumptuously" and refuses to listen to priest or judge, and Proverbs 11:2 gives the rule: "When pride cometh, then cometh shame: but with the lowly is wisdom." In 1 Samuel 17:28 Eliab accuses David of pride when David is only asking about Goliath, and Jeremiah 49:16 answers Edom's pride: "Thy terribleness hath deceived thee, and the pride of thine heart."

Theological Significance

I believe arrogance is the sin that deceives its owner first. I hold that Proverbs 11:2 is the whole story in one line, pride comes and shame follows, and that Eliab in 1 Samuel 17:28 shows how pride in one man misreads courage in another. My position is that zadon is the warning I need most, because pride never looks like pride from the inside.
