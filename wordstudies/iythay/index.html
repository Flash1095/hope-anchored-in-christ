ʼîythay

properly, entity; used only as a particle of affirmation, there is

Biblical Usage

ʼIythay is the Aramaic word for 'there is' or 'there are,' the plain way of saying that something exists. Daniel uses it to tell Nebuchadnezzar there is a God in heaven who reveals secrets, and the three friends use it to say there is a God able to deliver them from the furnace.

Theological Significance

I hold that this little word matters because the whole question of the exile was whether there is a God in heaven who rules kings and rescues His people — and Daniel answers yes.
