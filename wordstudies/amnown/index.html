ʼAmnôwn

Amnon (or Aminon), a son of David

Biblical Usage

Amnon was David's firstborn son, and his name is tied to one of the darkest stories in the family: he loved his half-sister Tamar, forced her, and then hated her, and two years later Absalom had his servants kill him at a feast, and the news reached David as though all his sons were dead. The Bible tells it plainly, without softening what Amnon did or what it cost.

Theological Significance

What strikes me about Amnon is how fast lust turns to hate and how one sin drags a whole family down with it: David did nothing, Absalom took revenge, and the house never recovered. I believe the story is there so we see sin for what it is — not a private slip, but a fire that burns everyone near it.
