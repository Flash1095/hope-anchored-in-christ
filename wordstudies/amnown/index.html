ʼAmnôwn

Amnon (or Aminon), a son of David

Biblical Usage

Amnon = "faithful"; 1) oldest son of David, rapist of Tamar, slain by Absalom; 2) a son of Shimon (of Caleb's clan)

Details

Language: Hebrew
Transliteration: ʼAmnôwn
Strongs Number: H550
Part Of Speech: noun (masculine)
