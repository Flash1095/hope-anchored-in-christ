stenagmós

from G4727; a sigh:--groaning.

Biblical Usage

Groaning is the sound of a people in bondage and the sound of the Spirit praying. God says, I have heard their groaning, and am come down to deliver them, and the Spirit itself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered.

Theological Significance

I believe the groaning is heard twice over: God hears His people, and the Spirit groans for them when they cannot pray.
