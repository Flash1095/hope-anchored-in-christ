ʼăhâhh

Oh!

Biblical Usage

Ahahh is the cry 'Ah!' or 'Alas!' — the sound of a prophet overwhelmed. Jeremiah cries it when God calls him, because he cannot speak and he is a child; he cries it again when he prays over the siege of Jerusalem, 'Ah Lord GOD! thou hast made the heaven and the earth by thy great power'; Ezekiel cries it when God tells him to cook over dung; and Joel cries it over the day of the Lord.

Theological Significance

Jeremiah's cry — Ah, Lord God, I cannot speak, for I am a child — is the whole of it, and the word is only the gasp before the excuse.
