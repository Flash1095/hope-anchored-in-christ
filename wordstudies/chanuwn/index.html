Chânûwn

Chanun, the name of an Ammonite and of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Hanun is a name carried by an Ammonite king and two Israelites. Hanun the son of Nahash became king after his father, and David sent servants to comfort him, but his princes shamed the messengers and brought war on Ammon. Another Hanun, the son of Zalaph, repaired a portion of the wall of Jerusalem.

Theological Significance

I believe the two Hanuns are the two ways a man can answer kindness: one shamed the messengers and lost a war, and the other took up a trowel and rebuilt a wall. I hold that how I receive mercy decides what I build next.
