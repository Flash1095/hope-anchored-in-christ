Pᵉlêthîy

Word
פְּלֵתִי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Pᵉlêthîy
Strongs Number
H6432
Definition
a courier (collectively) or official messenger
Part Of Speech
adjective
Biblical Usage
Pelethites = "couriers"; 1) a collective name for the guardsmen of David; 1a) probably descendants of an unknown person, perhaps Philistine mercenaries
