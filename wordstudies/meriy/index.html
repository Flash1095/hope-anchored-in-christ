mᵉrîy

bitterness, i.e. (figuratively) rebellion; concretely, bitter, or rebellious

Biblical Usage

Meriy is rebellion, bitterness made into a way of life. 1 Samuel 15:23 says rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft, and Deuteronomy 31:27 has Moses say he knows Israel's rebellion and stiff neck. Ezekiel 2:5 names them a rebellious house, and Isaiah 30:9 calls them a rebellious people, lying children, that will not hear the law of the LORD.

Theological Significance

I believe rebellion is bitterness with a will behind it: it hears the word and refuses it, and calls the refusal freedom. My position is that God names it witchcraft because it trades His voice for another master's, and the price is always the throne.
