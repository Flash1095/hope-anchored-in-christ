Nêrᵉgal Sharʼetser

Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Nêrᵉgal Sharʼetser
Strongs Number
H5371
Definition
Nergal-Sharetser, the name of two Babylonians
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Nergal-sharezer = "prince of fire"; 1) chief soothsayer and a ruler in the army of Nebuchadnezzar
