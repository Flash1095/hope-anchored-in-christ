archḗ

from G756; (properly abstract) a commencement, or (concretely) chief (in various applications of order, time, place, or rank):--beginning, corner, (at the, the) first (estate), magistrate, power, principality, principle, rule.

Biblical Usage

The word means beginning, first in time or in rank, and it opens the Bible's biggest claims. In the beginning was the Word, that which was from the beginning we declare to you, Christ is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead, and He calls Himself the beginning of the creation of God.

Theological Significance

I hold that every beginning in Scripture points back to one Beginning. Christ was there before anything began, and He is the first in rank over all that was made, so the beginning is not a moment, it is a Person.
