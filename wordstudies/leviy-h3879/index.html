Lêvîy

{something attached, i.e. a wreath}

Biblical Usage

Lêvîy means the Levites, and it is one of those words that quietly holds a whole theme together. In Ezra 6:16, "and the Levites"; and in Ezra 6:18, "and the Levites in their courses". In Ezra 7:13, "and [of] his priests and Levites"; and in Ezra 7:24, "that touching any of the priests and Levites".

Theological Significance

I believe the Levites in Ezra are the proof that God restores His servants — they kept the dedication, they were set in their courses, and they went up to Jerusalem of their own free will. I hold that the men named here are the Levites, set apart for the service of God's house, and their return is the pattern of every restoration. My position is that serving in God's house is the honour they chose, and I choose it too.
