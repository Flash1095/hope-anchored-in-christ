Zᵉrachyâh

Zerachjah, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Zerahiah is the name of two men in the priestly line. One is the son of Uzzi, an ancestor of Ezra the scribe, standing in the chain that carried the priesthood down to the return from exile; the other is the father of Elihoenai, whose sons came back with Ezra. Their full stories live on their People profiles.

Theological Significance

I believe the priestly lists matter because the priesthood mattered: God kept the line unbroken so His people would always have a priest.
