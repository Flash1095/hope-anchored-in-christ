yᵉkêl

{to be able, literally (can, could) or morally (may, might)}

Biblical Usage

yᵉkêl is the word the Bible uses when it means to be able. In Daniel 2:10, There is not a man upon the earth that can shew the king's matter, and it appears again in Daniel 2:27.

Theological Significance

No man on earth can show the king's matter, and I believe yᵉkêl sets up the whole point of Daniel 2 — only God can.
