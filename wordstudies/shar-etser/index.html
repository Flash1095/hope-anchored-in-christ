Sharʼetser

Word
שַׁרְאֶצֶר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Sharʼetser
Strongs Number
H8272
Definition
Sharetser, the name of an Assyrian and an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Sharezer or Sherezer = "prince of fire"; 1) son of king Sennacherib of Assyria and murderer of his father; 2) an Israelite whom the people sent to the house of God to pray in the time of the prophet Zechariah and king Darius
