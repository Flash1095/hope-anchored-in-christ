lámpō

a primary verb; to beam, i.e. radiate brilliancy (literally or figuratively):--give light, shine.

Biblical Usage

To shine is the verb of light breaking out, and it is used of Christ, of the believer, and of the lightning. In Matthew 5:16 Jesus says, "Let your light so shine before men," in 2 Corinthians 4:6 God "hath shined in our hearts, to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ," and in Acts 12:7 a light shined in Peter's prison.

Theological Significance

I hold that the shining is always God's doing first: He shined in our hearts, and only then does our light shine before men, and the order matters. His light in me comes first; my light before men comes second.
