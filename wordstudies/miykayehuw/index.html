Mîykâyᵉhûw

Mikajah, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Micaiah son of Imlah is the prophet Ahab hates because he never tells the king what he wants to hear. Summoned to bless a war, he first repeats the court prophets' lie and then speaks the truth plainly: Israel scattered on the hills, without a shepherd. Ahab has him fed on bread and water until he returns in peace — and Micaiah answers that if Ahab returns in peace, the LORD has not spoken by him.

Theological Significance

I believe Micaiah is the test of a true prophet: he would rather sit in prison on God's word than stand in the palace on the king's.
