Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Baʻălêy Yᵉhûwdâh
Strongs Number
H1184
Definition
Baale-Jehudah, a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Baale-Judah = "the lords of Judah"; 1) a place in Judah named for the Baalim, also known as Kirjath-jearim, Kirjath-baal; modern Kuriet el Enab
