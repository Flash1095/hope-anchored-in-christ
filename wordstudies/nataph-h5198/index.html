Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
nâṭâph
Strongs Number

Definition
a drop; specifically, an aromatic gum (probably stacte)
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) drop; 2) gum, drops of stacte; 2a) an aromatic gum resin of a shrub used in incense
