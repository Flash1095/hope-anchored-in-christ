massáomai

from a primary μάσσω (to handle or squeeze); to chew:--gnaw.

Biblical Usage

massáomai is to chew, to gnaw, and it occurs once, in Revelation 16:10. There the fifth angel pours out his vial upon the seat of the beast, and his kingdom is full of darkness, and they gnaw their tongues for pain. The word is the bite of suffering with no relief.

Theological Significance

I believe massáomai shows me what pain does to the unrepentant, and I hold that gnawing my tongue in darkness is the end of refusing the light.
