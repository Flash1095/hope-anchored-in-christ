epigínomai

from G1909 and G1096; to arrive upon, i.e. spring up (as a wind):--blow.

Biblical Usage

Epiginomai means to come upon, to spring up — and Luke uses it of a wind: the south wind blew, and the ship came the next day to Puteoli, near the end of Paul's long voyage to Rome.

Theological Significance

After storm and shipwreck, a fair wind is God's quiet kindness carrying Paul home, and even a south wind is worth recording.
