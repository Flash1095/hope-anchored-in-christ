nomízō

Word
νομίζω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
nomízō
Strongs Number
G3543
Definition
from G3551; properly, to do by law (usage), i.e. to accustom (passively, be usual); by extension, to deem or regard:-- suppose, thing, be wont.
