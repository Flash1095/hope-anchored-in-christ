nomízō

from G3551; properly, to do by law (usage), i.e. to accustom (passively, be usual); by extension, to deem or regard:-- suppose, thing, be wont.

Biblical Usage

To suppose is to think a thing is so, and the Bible's supposings are mostly wrong. Jesus says think not that He came to destroy the law; Mary and Joseph suppose the boy is in the company and lose Him for a day; Moses supposes his brethren will understand that God will deliver them by his hand, and they do not.

Theological Significance

I believe supposing is the mind's shortcut, and it fails: the boy is not in the company, the brethren do not understand, and only the fulfilment proves the thought. Moses supposed his brethren would understand, and they did not — the deliverance had to wait forty years for the thought to catch up.
