sâṭân

Word
שָׂטָן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
sâṭân
Strongs Number
H7854
Definition
an opponent; especially (with the article prefixed) Satan, the arch-enemy of good
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) adversary, one who withstands; 1a) adversary (in general-personal or national); 2) superhuman adversary; 2a) Satan (as noun pr)
