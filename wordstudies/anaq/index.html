ʼânaq

to shriek

Biblical Usage

The word means to shriek, to groan aloud: the LORD tells the man with the inkhorn to set a mark on the foreheads of the men who sigh and cry for the abominations done in Jerusalem — and Jeremiah says that in the day of Babylon's judgment, through all her land the wounded shall groan. It is the cry of the wounded and the sigh of the faithful, and God hears both.

Theological Significance

A word for shrieking carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the cry — the men who sigh over Jerusalem's sins marked for life, and the wounded of Babylon groaning under judgment.
