áspondos

Word
ἄσπονδος
Language
Greek
Transliteration
áspondos
Strongs Number
G786
Definition
from G1 (as a negative particle) and a derivative of G4689; literally, without libation (which usually accompanied a treaty), i.e. (by implication) truceless:--implacable, truce-breaker.
