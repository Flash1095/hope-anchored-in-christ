mâdôwn

extensiveness, i.e. height

Biblical Usage

Madon is great stature, height, and it appears once, in 2 Samuel 21:20, describing the man of great stature in the battle at Gath, six fingers on every hand and six toes on every foot, born to the giant.

Theological Significance

I hold that the six fingers are the point of the verse: this was no ordinary fighter, and he still fell.
