parádeisos

of Oriental origin (compare H6508); a park, i.e. (specially), an Eden (place of future happiness, "paradise"):--paradise.

Biblical Usage

Paradeisos is paradise, the garden park of God. Jesus tells the thief, To day shalt thou be with Me in paradise; Paul is caught up into paradise and hears unspeakable words; and the overcomer is promised to eat of the tree of life, which is in the midst of the paradise of God.

Theological Significance

I believe paradise is the garden restored. The thief enters it the same day he dies, Paul hears words too wonderful to repeat, and the tree of life waits for the overcomer.
