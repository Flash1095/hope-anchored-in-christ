paúō

a primary verb ("pause"); to stop (transitively or intransitively), i.e. restrain, quit, desist, come to an end:--cease, leave, refrain.

Biblical Usage

Pauo means to stop, to cease, to restrain, to quit. The apostles cease not to teach and preach Jesus Christ in Acts 5:42, and Paul says in Acts 20:31 that he ceased not to warn every one night and day with tears. Paul says in 1 Corinthians 13:8 that tongues shall cease and knowledge shall vanish away, and Peter says in 1 Peter 3:10 that he that will love life and see good days should refrain his tongue from evil.

Theological Significance

I believe ceasing is the test of the work: the apostles would not cease to preach, Paul would not cease to warn, and the tongue must cease from evil. My position is that what I refuse to stop shows what I love, and the gospel is worth a life that will not stop.
