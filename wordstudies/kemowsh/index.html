Kᵉmôwsh

Kemosh, the god of the Moabites

Biblical Usage

Kemosh, Chemosh, is the god of the Moabites: Numbers 21:29 cries, woe to thee, Moab, thou art undone, O people of Chemosh. Judges 11:24 asks, wilt not thou possess that which Chemosh thy god giveth thee to possess? 1 Kings 11:7 says Solomon built an high place for Chemosh, the abomination of Moab, in the hill before Jerusalem.

Theological Significance

I believe Chemosh is the god who could not save his people, and Solomon's high place for him is the saddest verse in his story.
