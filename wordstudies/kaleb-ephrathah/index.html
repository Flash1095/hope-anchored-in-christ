Kâlêb ʼEphrâthâh

Caleb-Ephrathah, a place in Egypt (if the text is correct)

Biblical Usage

Caleb-Ephrathah is named once, in the genealogy of Judah: after Hezron was dead in Caleb-Ephrathah, his wife Abiah bore him Ashur the father of Tekoa. The text itself is uncertain — the name may join Caleb and Ephrathah, and the place may be in Egypt, if the text is correct.

Theological Significance

I believe the honest uncertainty is part of the record: Scripture does not hide where the text is hard, and I would rather say plainly what I do not know than invent a certainty.
