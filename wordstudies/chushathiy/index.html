Chushâthîy

a Chushathite or descendant of Chushah

Biblical Usage

A Hushathite is a descendant of Hushah, and the name belongs to warriors: Sibbecai kills a giant, and Mebunnai stands among David's mighty men. The word appears only in battle lists, attached to men who fought the Philistines. It is a family name worn into war.

Theological Significance

I believe the Hushathites show that God uses families, not just individuals, and a name can carry a fighting tradition. Sibbecai's victory is told in a single line, and that is enough. I hold that faithfulness in battle is often a family matter, passed from father to son.
