kerem

a garden or vineyard

Biblical Usage

Kerem means a vineyard, a garden of vines: 1 Chronicles 27:27 names Shimei over the vineyards, and Zabdi over the increase of the vineyards for the wine cellars. Isaiah 5:1 sings to the wellbeloved a song touching his vineyard, in a very fruitful hill. Isaiah 36:17 promises a land of corn and wine, a land of bread and vineyards. Zephaniah 1:13 says they shall plant vineyards, but not drink the wine thereof.

Theological Significance

I believe the vineyard is Israel in miniature: planted in a fruitful hill, tended by stewards, and judged when it bears wild grapes. I hold that the land of vineyards is the promise, and the vineyard planted but never drunk from is the judgment.
