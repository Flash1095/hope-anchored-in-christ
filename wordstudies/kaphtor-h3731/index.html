Kaphtôr

Word
כַּפְתֹּר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Kaphtôr
Strongs Number
H3731
Definition
Caphtor (i.e. a wreath-shaped island), the original seat of the Philistines
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Caphtor = "a crown"; 1) the original home of the Philistines, perhaps on the southwest coast of Asia Minor, maybe in Egypt or close by, or more probably on the island of Crete; 2) (TWOT) the island of Crete, homeland or staging area of the Philistines
