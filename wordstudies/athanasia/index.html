athanasía

from a compound of G1 (as a negative particle) and G2288; deathlessness:--immortality.

Biblical Usage

Immortality is the word for deathlessness, and Paul uses it for the change that is coming and for God Himself. This corruptible must put on incorruption and this mortal must put on immortality, and when it does, death is swallowed up in victory. God alone has immortality, dwelling in light no man can approach.

Theological Significance

I believe the promise is as plain as it is wonderful: what God has in Himself He will give to His people, and death will not get the last word. Mortality puts on immortality the way a garment is put on, and I hold that the resurrection morning will show what that looks like.
