koítē

from G2749; a couch; by extension, cohabitation; by implication, the male sperm:--bed, chambering, X conceive.

Biblical Usage

Koite is a bed, and by extension, the marriage bed and what happens in it. Luke 11:7 has the man saying his children are with him in bed and he cannot rise, Romans 9:10 speaks of Rebecca conceiving by Isaac, Romans 13:13 forbids chambering and wantonness, and Hebrews 13:4 says marriage is honourable in all, and the bed undefiled, but whoremongers and adulterers God will judge.

Theological Significance

I believe the marriage bed is honoured when it is kept and judged when it is defiled: Hebrews 13:4 draws the line plainly, and the line is God's. I hold that the same word that names the children's bed in Luke 11:7 names the marriage bed in Hebrews 13:4, because God made the home before He made the law about it.
