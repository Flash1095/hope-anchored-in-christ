ʻIvvâh

Ivvah or Avva, a region of Assyria

Biblical Usage

Ivvah was a region of Assyria whose gods could not save it, and the Bible names it three times in the taunts of the Assyrians. In 2 Kings 17:24 the king of Assyria brings men from Ivvah to settle Samaria, in 2 Kings 18:34 Rabshakeh asks, where are the gods of Hamath, and of Arpad, and of Sepharvaim, of Hena, and of Ivah, and in Isaiah 37:13 the same question is asked again.

Theological Significance

I believe the taunt is the warning: the gods of Ivvah could not save Ivvah, and the God of Israel is not one god among many.
