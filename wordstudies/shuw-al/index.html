shûwʻâl

a jackal (as a burrower)

Biblical Usage

Shual is the fox, or the jackal, the burrower that haunts the ruins. Ezekiel says Israel's prophets are like foxes in the deserts, the psalm of the ruined mountain says the foxes walk on Zion, and the Song bids us catch the little foxes that spoil the vines with their tender grapes.

Theological Significance

I believe little foxes spoil more vines than great beasts do, and the small destroyers are the ones I must catch early.
