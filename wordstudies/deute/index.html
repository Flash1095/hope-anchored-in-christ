deûte

from G1204 and an imperative form of εἶμι (to go); come hither!:--come, X follow.

Biblical Usage

Come is the invitation Jesus gives again and again, and each time it carries a promise with it. He calls fishermen to follow Him, He calls the weary to rest, He calls the blessed to inherit the kingdom, and He calls His disciples to eat with Him after the resurrection.

Theological Significance

I believe no word of Jesus is kinder than this one. He does not drive us or bargain with us; He says come, and the promise that follows is rest, life, and a place at His table.
