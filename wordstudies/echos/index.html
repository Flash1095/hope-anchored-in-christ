ēchos

of uncertain affinity; a loud or confused noise ("echo"), i.e. roar; figuratively, a rumor:--fame, sound.

Biblical Usage

A sound can be a rushing wind, a rumour, or a trumpet, and this word covers all three. On Pentecost a sound like a rushing mighty wind fills the house, the fame of Jesus spreads through the country around Galilee, and Hebrews remembers the sound of the trumpet at Sinai that made Israel beg for silence. The word moves from a noise you hear to news that travels.

Theological Significance

I believe God speaks in sounds we cannot ignore, whether a wind at Pentecost or a trumpet at the mountain, and His news still travels. I want my life to carry His sound, not just make noise.
