nâmal

to become clipped or (specifically) circumcised

Biblical Usage

Namal means to cut off or clip, and to circumcise, and the Bible uses it for both the covenant sign and the cutting down of the wicked. In Genesis 17:11 Abraham is told to circumcise the flesh of the foreskin as the token of the covenant, and in Job 14:2 a man comes forth like a flower and is cut down. In Psalm 37:2 the evildoers shall soon be cut down like the grass, and in Job 24:24 the wicked are cut off as the tops of the ears of corn.

Theological Significance

I believe the cutting in this word cuts both ways, because the same knife that marks the covenant also cuts down the grass. Circumcision says this child belongs to God, and the cutting off says the wicked will not stand, and both are His doing. I hold that I want the mark, not the cutting off.
