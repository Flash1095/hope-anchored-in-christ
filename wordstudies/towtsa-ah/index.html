tôwtsâʼâh

(only in plural collective) exit, i.e. (geographical) boundary, or (figuratively) deliverance, (actively) source

Biblical Usage

Goings out are the borders of a land and the gates of a city. The Gadites dwelt in Bashan and in all the suburbs of Sharon upon their borders, and Ezekiel measures the goings out of the city on the north side, four thousand and five hundred measures.

Theological Significance

I believe the word maps the edges: where a people's land ends, and where a city's gates open, are both called its goings out.
