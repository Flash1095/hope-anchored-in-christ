gâlâh

to denude (especially in a disgraceful sense); by implication, to exile (captives being usually stripped); figuratively, to reveal

Biblical Usage

Galah is a wide word: to uncover, to reveal, and to carry away into exile. God told His servant David what He would do in 1 Chronicles 17:25, and Jonathan and Saul discovered themselves to the Philistines in 1 Samuel 14:11. But the same word carries Israel away: Nebuchadnezzar carried them to Babylon in 2 Chronicles 36:20, and Tiglath-pileser carried Galilee captive to Assyria in 2 Kings 15:29.

Theological Significance

I believe uncovering and exile are two sides of the same word because sin uncovers what should be hidden and exile carries away what should stay home. God reveals His plans to His servants and His judgments to the nations, and I hold that both revelations are mercy: one to build faith, the other to call us home.
