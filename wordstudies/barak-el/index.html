Bârakʼêl

Barakel, the father of one of Job's friends

Biblical Usage

Barakel is named only as the father of Elihu the Buzite, the young man who speaks last in the book of Job. He speaks after the three friends fall silent.

Theological Significance

I hold that a father's name is kept here so we know Elihu is a real man with a family, not a voice out of nowhere, and his words carry weight because he waited his turn to speak.
