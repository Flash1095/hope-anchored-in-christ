aiphnídios

Word
αἰφνίδιος
Language
Greek
Transliteration
aiphnídios
Strongs Number
G160
Definition
from a compound of G1 (as a negative particle) and G5316 (compare G1810) (meaning non-apparent); unexpected, i.e. (adverbially) suddenly:--sudden, unawares.
