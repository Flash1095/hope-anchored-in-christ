katáschesis

from G2722; a holding down, i.e. occupancy:--possession.

Biblical Usage

God gave Abraham no inheritance in the land, not so much as to set his foot on, yet promised it to him and his seed, and the fathers brought the tabernacle in with Joshua into the possession of the Gentiles.

Theological Significance

I believe a promise held by faith is as sure as land held by foot, because God keeps what He promises.
