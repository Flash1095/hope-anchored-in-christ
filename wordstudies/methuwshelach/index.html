Mᵉthûwshelach

Methushelach, an antediluvian patriarch

Biblical Usage

Enoch lived sixty-five years and begat Methuselah, and all the days of Methuselah were nine hundred and sixty-nine years, the longest life in Scripture. The chronicler names him again in the genealogy from Adam, the son of Enoch.

Theological Significance

I believe Methuselah's long life is a quiet sermon on God's patience: he lived longer than any man, and still he died, because even the longest life ends and only the promise lives on.
