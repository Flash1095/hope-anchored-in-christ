mad

properly, extent, i.e. height; also a measure; by implication, a vesture (as measured); also a carpet

Biblical Usage

Mad is a measure, and by extension the garment measured out, and it wears both senses in Scripture. 1 Samuel 17:38 has Saul arming David with his armour, a coat of mail, and Leviticus 6:10 has the priest putting on his linen garment, while Psalms 109:18 turns the word dark: the wicked clothed himself with cursing like as with his garment.

Theological Significance

I take it that a garment in these verses is always a covering that tells the truth about the wearer, whether armour, linen, or cursing.
