maʻărâkâh

an arrangement; concretely, a pile; specifically a military array

Biblical Usage

The word names a battle array, the lines of an army drawn up for war, and it is the word of David's fight. The Philistines put themselves in array against Israel and slew four thousand men, Goliath cried to the armies of Israel to choose a man to come down to him, and David came to the trench as the host was going forth to the fight and shouted for the battle.

Theological Significance

I believe the battle array is where Israel learned that numbers do not win wars, because the Philistines' array broke before a boy with a sling. I hold that the armies of the living God are not counted by heads but by faith, and one man who trusts the LORD is an army by himself.
