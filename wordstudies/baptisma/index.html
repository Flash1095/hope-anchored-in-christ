baptisma

Word
βάπτισμα
Language
Greek
Transliteration
baptisma
Strongs Number
G908
Definition
Baptism — immersion or washing in water signifying union with Christ in His death and resurrection, repentance and new life.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Romans 6:3-4; Matthew 28:19; Acts 2:38; Colossians 2:12
Biblical Usage
Baptisma pictures a burial and a rising: baptised into Christ's death, raised to walk in newness of life. It is the public sign of the washing and union God works through the gospel.
Theological Significance
Baptisma gives believers a moment to point back to: I have been buried and raised with Christ. When assurance wavers, baptism preaches the gospel back to us.
