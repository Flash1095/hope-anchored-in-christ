Pirʻâthôwn

Word
פִּרְעָתוֹן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Pirʻâthôwn
Strongs Number
H6552
Definition
Pirathon, a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Pirathon = "princely"; 1) a town in Ephraim in the mount of the Amalekite; 1a) perhaps modern 'Ferata' located 1.5 miles (2.5 km) south from Jaffa on the road to Nablus
