pâʻal

to do or make (systematically and habitually), especially to practise

Biblical Usage

To do, or to work, is the word for making and acting. Moses sang of the sanctuary which God's hands have established, and warned Israel lest their enemies say, Our hand is high, and the Lord hath not done all this.

Theological Significance

I believe doing is the word for God's work and mine: His hands established the sanctuary, and my hands are to do what He commands.
