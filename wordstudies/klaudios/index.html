Klaúdios

of Latin origin; Claudius, the name of two Romans:--Claudius.

Biblical Usage

Claudius is the name of two Romans. Acts 11:28 dates the great dearth to the days of Claudius Caesar, Acts 18:2 says Claudius had commanded all Jews to depart from Rome, and Acts 23:26 opens the letter of Claudius Lysias to the most excellent governor Felix.

Theological Significance

I believe the pair of Claudiuses frames the story: an emperor whose edict scattered the Jews, and a tribune whose letter carried Paul toward Rome.
