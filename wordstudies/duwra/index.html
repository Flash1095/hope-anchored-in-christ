Dûwrâʼ

Dura, a place in Babylonia

Biblical Usage

Dura was the plain in Babylon where Nebuchadnezzar set up the golden image, sixty cubits high and six broad, and commanded all peoples to fall down and worship it. The plain's name is all we know of it, and its story is the furnace.

Theological Significance

I believe the plain of Dura is where true worship was tested, because three men would not bow on a plain built for bowing. The furnace was seven times hotter, and the fourth man walked in it with them.
