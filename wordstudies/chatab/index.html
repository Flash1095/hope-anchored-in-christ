châṭab

to chop or carve wood

Biblical Usage

To hew is to cut wood, and the word follows the axe from the forest to the altar. A man goes into the wood to hew, the Gibeonites are made hewers of wood and drawers of water for the congregation, and Ezekiel looks ahead to a day when no wood will need cutting because the weapons themselves will burn.

Theological Significance

I hold that even the hewing of wood belongs to God, whether it is daily labour or the service of the altar. The Gibeonites' low work was still work for the LORD, and I believe no honest labour is beneath Him.
