Bêyth-ʼÊl

Beth-El, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Bethel means house of God, and it earns the name twice over. Abram pitched his tent beside it and built an altar in Genesis 12:8, and Jacob dreamed of the ladder there and called the place Bethel in Genesis 28:19. Years later God told Jacob to go back to Bethel and build an altar in Genesis 35:1 — and centuries after that, Jeroboam set a golden calf there in 1 Kings 12:29, turning the house of God into a house of idols.

Theological Significance

I believe Bethel holds both halves of the story: God meets people in a place, and people can still ruin the place. The ground was holy because God came there, not because men kept it holy.
