ochlopoiéō

from G3793 and G4160; to make a crowd, i.e. raise a public disturbance:--gather a company.

Biblical Usage

Ochlopoieo means to gather a crowd, to raise a public disturbance. It occurs once, in Acts 17:5, where the unbelieving Jews take lewd fellows of the baser sort, gather a company, set the city in an uproar, and assault the house of Jason.

Theological Significance

I hold that a crowd can be raised for envy as easily as for truth, and the uproar at Thessalonica is what happens when the gospel is refused.
