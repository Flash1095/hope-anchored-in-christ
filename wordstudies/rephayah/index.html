Rᵉphâyâh

Rephajah, the name of five Israelites

Biblical Usage

Three of the men who share this name are a son of Jesaiah in the royal line, a Simeonite captain who took Mount Seir, and the ruler of half Jerusalem who repaired the wall under Nehemiah. Two more of the same name stand in the lines of Issachar and Benjamin.

Theological Significance

I am convinced that the wall was rebuilt by men whose names repeat, and God knows which Rephaiah held which stone.
