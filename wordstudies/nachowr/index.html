Nâchôwr

Nochor, the name of the grandfather and a brother of Abraham

Biblical Usage

Nachor is Nahor, the brother of Abraham and the father of Terah's other line, and his name runs through the family story. In Genesis 11:22 Serug begets Nahor, and in Genesis 11:24 Nahor lives nine and twenty years and begets Terah, and in Joshua 24:2 the Lord reminds Israel that Terah, the father of Abraham and the father of Nachor, dwelt on the other side of the flood in old time.

Theological Significance

I believe Nahor matters because the family of promise did not start with Abraham alone. The same house that produced the father of the faithful also produced the father of the other branch, and God chose one and blessed the line through him.
