sýndesmos

from G4862 and G1199; a joint tie, i.e. ligament, (figuratively) uniting principle, control:--band, bond.

Biblical Usage

Paul tells the Ephesians to endeavour to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace, and to put on charity, which is the bond of perfectness. The body is knit together by joints and bands, with nourishment ministered from the Head, and it grows with the increase of God. But the same word can bind the other way: Simon is in the gall of bitterness and in the bond of iniquity, held fast by his own sin.

Theological Significance

I believe love is the bond that holds the church together and the bond that makes it perfect, and I hold that peace is not the absence of bonds but the right one. I take the warning with the promise: a bond can hold me to Christ or to iniquity, and I want the bond of peace, not the bond of sin.
