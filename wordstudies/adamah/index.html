ʼădâmâh

soil (from its general redness)

Biblical Usage

Ground, soil, earth — adamah is the red earth man was made from and the ground that bears the curse. God "formed man of the dust of the ground"; then He said, "cursed is the ground for thy sake"; and to Cain, "the earth, which hath opened her mouth to receive thy brother's blood." The word holds creation, curse, and blood in one.

Theological Significance

We are dust, and the ground itself bears the curse — I hold that as the plain reading. But the same ground that drank Abel's blood is the ground God promises to renew, and the whole creation waits for it.
