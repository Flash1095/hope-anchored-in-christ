ʻĂzarʼêl

Azarel, the name of five Israelites

Biblical Usage

Azarel is the name of five different men — a Benjamite who came to David at Ziklag, a Levite singer, a captain of Dan, a son of Bani who put away his foreign wife, and a priest who helped seal the covenant. The name means God has helped, and it turns up in every corner of Israel's story, from David's outlaws to the priests of the return.

Theological Significance

I hold that a name meaning God has helped is a good name to carry, and five men carried it through five different kinds of trouble.
