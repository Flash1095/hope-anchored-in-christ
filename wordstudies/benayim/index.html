bênayim

Word
בֵּנַיִם
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
bênayim
Strongs Number
H1143
Definition
a double interval, i.e. the space between two armies
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) between, space between two armies; 1a) man of the space between armies, i.e. champion (of Goliath) (meton)
