ʼemeth

stability; (figuratively) certainty, truth, trustworthiness

Biblical Usage

Emeth is firmness — truth you can stand on, faithfulness you can trust. David charges Solomon to walk before the LORD in truth with all his heart in 1 Kings 2:4, Samuel calls Israel to serve Him in truth in 1 Samuel 12:24, and Hezekiah pleads that he has walked before God in truth and with a perfect heart in 2 Kings 20:3. The queen of Sheba finds the same thing from the other side: the report she heard was true in 2 Chronicles 9:5.

Theological Significance

I believe truth is not an idea first, it is a Person who keeps His word — and I hold that a life built on emeth, on what is firm and faithful, will stand when everything shaky falls. God is true, so I can be true, and that steadiness is what I want my own word to carry.
