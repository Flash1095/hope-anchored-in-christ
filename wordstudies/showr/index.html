shôwr

a bullock (as a traveller)

Biblical Usage

Shor is the ox, the working beast at the centre of Israel's law and life. The law deals with the ox that gores, the ox that strays, and the ox that must not be muzzled while it treads the grain. Samuel challenges Israel to name whose ox he ever took, Job watches the wicked man's bull breed without fail, and Isaiah says the ox knows his owner while Israel does not know her God.

Theological Significance

I believe the ox knows its master, and that is the indictment. An animal recognises the hand that feeds it, and I hold that I have less excuse than the ox when I forget mine.
