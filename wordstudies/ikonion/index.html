Ikónion

perhaps from G1504; image-like; Iconium, a place in Asia Minor:--Iconium.

Biblical Usage

Iconium was a city in Asia Minor where Paul preached and was nearly stoned. In Acts 13:51 Paul and Barnabas shake off the dust of their feet and come to Iconium, in Acts 14:1 they go into the synagogue and speak so that a great multitude believed, and in Acts 14:19 Jews from Antioch and Iconium come and stone Paul and drag him out of the city.

Theological Significance

I believe Iconium shows both sides of the mission at once: a great multitude believed there, and the same city sent men to stone the preacher.
