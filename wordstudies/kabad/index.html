kâbad

to be heavy, i.e. in a bad sense (burdensome, severe, dull) or in a good sense (numerous, rich, honorable; causatively, to make weighty (in the same two senses)

Biblical Usage

Kabad means to be heavy, and from that weight come two opposite pictures: honour and dullness. The command is to honour father and mother; God promises to honour those who honour Him; the proverb tells us to honour the LORD with our substance — and the same word describes a heart grown heavy and dull, unable to hear.

Theological Significance

I believe honour is weight given where it belongs. God is not honoured by light words but by a life that treats Him as weighty — and a heart that will not honour Him grows heavy the other way, deaf to His voice.
