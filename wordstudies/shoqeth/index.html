shôqeth

a trough (for watering)

Biblical Usage

Shoqeth is a watering trough, and it appears where servants and shepherds water the flocks. Rebekah emptied her pitcher into the trough and drew for all of Abraham's servant's camels, and Jacob set his peeled rods before the flocks at the troughs so they would conceive.

Theological Significance

I believe the trough is where service shows, because Rebekah drew for ten camels as well as one man, and God notices that kind of thorough kindness.
