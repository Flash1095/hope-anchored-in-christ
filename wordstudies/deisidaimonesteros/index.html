deisidaimonésteros

the compound of a derivative of the base of G1169 and G1142; more religious than others:--too superstitious.

Biblical Usage

The word appears once, in Paul's address on the Areopagus: I perceive that in all things you are too superstitious, or more religious than others. The comparative form survives in the manuscripts under the plain adjective, and Paul turns their religiosity into his opening.

Theological Significance

I believe Paul's tact is the model for witness: he begins where they are, even with their superstition, and leads them to the God they do not know.
