Hădôwrâm

Hadoram, a son of Joktan, and the tribe descended from him

Biblical Usage

Hadoram is a son of Joktan, and the tribe descended from him. In Genesis 10:27, the text says "And Hadoram." The life behind the name is told on the Hadoram People profile.

Theological Significance

I believe Hadoram matters because God knows His people by name, and Hadoram is one of them. That is why I want the name, and the person behind it, kept straight.
