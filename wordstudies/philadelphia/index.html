philadelphía

from G5361; fraternal affection:--brotherly love (kindness), love of the brethren.

Biblical Usage

Philadelphia is brotherly love, the affection of family turned toward the household of faith. Peter says to love one another with a pure heart fervently, having purified your souls in obeying the truth, and Paul says to be kindly affectioned one to another with brotherly love, in honour preferring one another. The writer to the Hebrews says let brotherly love continue, and Peter adds brotherly kindness to godliness.

Theological Significance

I believe brotherly love is the proof of the new birth: if I have been born again, I love the family I was born into. It is not a feeling that comes and goes but a duty that continues, a kindness that prefers the other, and a love that is fervent because it is real. My position is that the world will know Christ's people by this love before it knows them by anything else.
