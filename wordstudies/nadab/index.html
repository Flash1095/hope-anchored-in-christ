nâdab

to impel; hence, to volunteer (as a soldier), to present spontaneously

Biblical Usage

Nadab as a verb means to volunteer, to give willingly because the heart is stirred, and it built the tabernacle. In Exodus 25:2 the Lord says to take an offering from every man that giveth it willingly with his heart, and in Exodus 35:21 every one whose heart stirred him up and whose spirit made him willing brought the offering. In 1 Chronicles 29:5 David asks who is willing to consecrate his service to the Lord, and in 2 Chronicles 17:16 Amasiah willingly offered himself to the Lord with two hundred thousand mighty men.

Theological Significance

I believe willing giving is the only kind God asks for, because a forced gift is not a gift at all. The tabernacle was built by stirred hearts, and the temple was supplied the same way, and I hold that God still works that way. A willing heart is the offering He wants first.
