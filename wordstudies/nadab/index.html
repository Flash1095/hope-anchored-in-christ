nâdab

Word
נָדַב
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
nâdab
Strongs Number
H5068
Definition
to impel; hence, to volunteer (as a soldier), to present spontaneously
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to incite, impel, make willing; 1a) (Qal) to incite, impel; 1b) (Hithpael); 1b1) to volunteer; 1b2) to offer free-will offerings
