ʼêlâh

an oak or other strong tree

Biblical Usage

ʼêlâh means an oak or other strong tree. In Genesis 35:4, the text says "and Jacob hid them under the oak which [was] by Shechem." In Judges 6:11, the text says "and sat under an oak which [was] in Ophrah."

Theological Significance

My position is simple: ʼêlâh tells me that creation is God's handiwork, and it still speaks of Him to anyone willing to look. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
