pârats

to break out (in many applications, direct and indirect, literal and figurative)

Biblical Usage

Parats means to break out, to break through, to burst forth. The name of Perez comes from it, when Tamar's son breaks forth first in Genesis 38:29, and David names the place Baal-perazim in 1 Chronicles 14:11, saying God has broken in upon his enemies like the breaking forth of waters. In 1 Samuel 3:1 the word runs the other way: the word of the Lord was precious, and there was no open vision, no breaking forth of revelation. Psalm 80:12 laments that God has broken down the vineyard's hedges.

Theological Significance

I believe God breaks through where He pleases, in blessing and in judgment alike: the waters break over David's enemies, the silence breaks over Samuel's childhood, and the hedge breaks around a vineyard that has forgotten its keeper. I hold that the breach He makes is never aimless: it opens a way for His people, or it opens a way for His judgment, and either way it is His hand that breaks through.
