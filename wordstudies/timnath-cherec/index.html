Timnath Chereç

Timnath-Cheres, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

This is Joshua's town, given him by the word of the Lord. He asked for Timnath-serah in Mount Ephraim, built the city, and dwelt there, and when he died they buried him in the border of his inheritance in Timnath-heres, on the north side of the hill Gaash.

Theological Significance

I believe the place matters because it is where the conqueror came home: the man who led Israel into the land was buried in the town he himself had built, and the two spellings of its name — Timnath-serah, Timnath-heres — are the same ground.
