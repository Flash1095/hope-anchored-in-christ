qinyân

creation, i.e. (concretely) creatures; also acquisition, purchase, wealth

Biblical Usage

Jacob carries away all his cattle and all his goods which he had gotten in Padan-aram, and Gog comes to take a spoil from a people gathered out of the nations, which have gotten cattle and goods. The word is the getting itself — creatures, acquisitions, wealth.

Theological Significance

I believe what a man has gotten is never only his, because God counts the cattle and the goods, and he gives and takes.
