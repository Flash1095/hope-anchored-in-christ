nâçach

to tear away

Biblical Usage

Nasach means to tear away or pluck up, and it is used for the wicked being rooted out of the land. In Deuteronomy 28:63 the Lord says He will rejoice over the disobedient to destroy them and bring them to nought, and to pluck them from the land, and in Proverbs 2:22 the wicked shall be cut off from the earth and the transgressors rooted out of it.

Theological Significance

I hold that being rooted out is the end of a long road, not a sudden accident; the tearing away comes after the warnings, and the land itself is better off without the wicked in it.
