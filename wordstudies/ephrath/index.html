ʼEphrâth

Ephrath, another name for Bethlehem;

Biblical Usage

Ephrath is another name for Bethlehem, the place where Rachel died and was buried on the way, where Ruth was blessed by the elders, and where the prophet says the ruler of Israel shall come forth. Ash-heap and fruitfulness, grief and promise, in one name.

Theological Significance

Rachel's grave and the Messiah's birthplace share one field, and the name holds sorrow and hope together. I believe Ephrath is the whole story in miniature: grief where Rachel died, and promise where the ruler of Israel would come forth.
