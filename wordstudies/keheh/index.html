kêheh

feeble, obscure

Biblical Usage

Keheh means dim, dull, feeble, colourless: Leviticus 13:6 says if the plague be somewhat dark, and spread not, the priest shall pronounce him clean. 1 Samuel 3:2 says Eli's eyes began to wax dim, that he could not see. Isaiah 42:3 says a bruised reed shall he not break, and the smoking flax shall he not quench. Isaiah 61:3 promises the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness.

Theological Significance

I believe God is gentle with the dim: the dark spot is pronounced clean, the bruised reed is not broken, the smoking flax is not quenched. I hold that the spirit of heaviness gets a garment of praise, because God restores what has gone dull.
