nîkos

from G3529; a conquest (concretely), i.e. (by implication) triumph:--victory.

Biblical Usage

Victory in the prophets and the apostles is God's, and it comes gently and finally. The Servant will not break a bruised reed till He sends forth judgment unto victory, and Paul shouts that death is swallowed up in victory and thanks God who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.

Theological Significance

I believe victory is God's to give and mine to receive: the bruised reed is not broken, and death has lost its sting. The Servant does not break the bruised reed, and death has lost its sting — the victory is gentle and it is final.
