hyperḗphanos

from G5228 and G5316; appearing above others (conspicuous), i.e. (figuratively) haughty:--proud.

Biblical Usage

hyperḗphanos means proud. In Luke 1:51, the text says "he hath scattered the proud in the imagination of their hearts." In Romans 1:30, the text says "proud."

Theological Significance

For me, hyperḗphanos is a reminder that pride is the oldest sin, and it always ends the same way — brought low. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
