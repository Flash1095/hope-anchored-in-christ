sâsôwn

cheerfulness; specifically, welcome

Biblical Usage

Sason is joy, gladness, the sound of a people at ease. The Jews had light, and gladness, and joy, and honour when deliverance came; Isaiah says with joy shall ye draw water out of the wells of salvation; and Jeremiah found God's words, and ate them, and the word was unto him the joy and rejoicing of his heart. But Jeremiah also warns the other side: the voice of gladness shall cease from the cities of Judah when the land is desolate.

Theological Significance

I believe joy in Scripture is not a mood but a mark of God's presence — it comes with deliverance, it comes with His word, and it leaves when He withdraws. That is why Jeremiah could rejoice in the word even while he wept over the city. And I hold that the joy that draws water from the wells of salvation never runs dry, because the well is God Himself.
