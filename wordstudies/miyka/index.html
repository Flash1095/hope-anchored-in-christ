Mîykâʼ

Word
מִיכָא
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Mîykâʼ
Strongs Number
H4316
Definition
Mica, the name of two Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Micha = "who is like God"; 1) son of Mephibosheth; 2) a Levite who signed the covenant with Nehemiah; 3) father of Mattaniah, a Gershonite Levite and descendant of Asaph
