Mîykâʼ

Mica, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Micha is the name of two Israelites. The first is the young son of Mephibosheth, kept at David's table with his father, and the second is a Levite whose son Mattaniah oversaw the singers at Jerusalem.

Theological Significance

I believe the boy at the table is the point: Mephibosheth's son ate at the king's table because of Jonathan, and grace runs to the children too.
