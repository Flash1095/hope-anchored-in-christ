mâshal

to liken, i.e. (transitively) to use figurative language (an allegory, adage, song or the like); in transitively, to resemble

Biblical Usage

1) to represent, liken, be like; 1a) (Niphal) to liken, be like, be similar; 1b) (Hiphil) to compare; 1c) (Hithpael) to become like; 2) to speak in a proverb, use a proverb, speak in parables, speak in sentences of poetry; 2a) (Qal) to use a proverb, speak a parable or proverb; 2b) (Piel) to make a parable; 2b1) maker of parables (participle)
