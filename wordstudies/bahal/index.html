bâhal

to tremble inwardly (or palpitate), i.e. (figuratively) be (causative, make) (suddenly) alarmed or agitated; by implication to hasten anxiously

Biblical Usage

Bahal means to be suddenly alarmed — to tremble inwardly, to be troubled in a moment. Joseph's brothers were troubled at his presence when he said, I am Joseph; the chiefs of Edom were amazed and trembling took hold of the mighty men of Moab; Job says his heart trembles at the thunder; and the Preacher says not to be hasty in spirit to be angry. Alarm that comes all at once and shakes the whole frame.

Theological Significance

I believe Joseph's brothers had the right reaction: when the one you wronged turns out to be the one who can save you, trembling is the honest response. His grace after it is the whole gospel.
