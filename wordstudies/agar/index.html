ʼâgar

to harvest

Biblical Usage

Agar means to gather or harvest — and it appears twice, both in Proverbs, both about the ant and the harvest. The ant 'provideth her meat in the summer, and gathereth her food in the harvest,' and the son who 'gathereth in summer' is wise, while the son who sleeps in harvest brings shame.

Theological Significance

This word carries no doctrine of its own; it simply helps the verse say what it says, and that is its whole work; in this case, the verse is Proverbs 6:8.
