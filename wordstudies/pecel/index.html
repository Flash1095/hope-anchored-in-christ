peçel

an idol

Biblical Usage

A pesel is a graven image, a god carved by human hands. The second commandment forbids making one in any likeness, male or female, from heaven or earth or water. Isaiah laughs at the makers — their idols are vanity, they see nothing and know nothing — and the psalm says all who serve graven images will be confounded.

Theological Significance

My position is that idolatry is not just a carved statue; it is trusting something made over the Maker who made us. God will not share His glory with an image, and that is mercy, not jealousy — because an idol can never save, and He can. I hold that every age has its own pesel, and the command still stands: make none.
