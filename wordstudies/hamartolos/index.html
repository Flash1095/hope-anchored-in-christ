hamartōlós

from G264; sinful, i.e. a sinner:--sinful, sinner.

Biblical Usage

A sinner is not a label for other people; it is the word the Bible uses for the ones Jesus came to find. In Luke 15:1 the publicans and sinners draw near to hear him, and in Luke 15:7 he says there is joy in heaven over one sinner that repenteth. Paul says in Romans 5:8 that Christ died for us while we were yet sinners, and in 1 Timothy 1:15 he calls himself the chief of them.

Theological Significance

I believe the word loses its sting only when I take it for myself first. If Paul could call himself chief of sinners and still preach grace, then grace is wide enough for me too.
