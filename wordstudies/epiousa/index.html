epioûsa

feminine singular participle of a compound of G1909 and εἶμι (to go); supervening, i.e. (G2250 or G3571 being expressed or implied) the ensuing day or night:--following, next.

Biblical Usage

Epiousa is the coming day, the next day — the day that follows. Stephen says the next day Moses showed himself to two Israelites striving; Paul's company sails, and the next day comes to Neapolis; and the next day they arrive over against Chios.

Theological Significance

Moses returns, Paul sails on, and God's work does not wait for a convenient morning: the next day is where the story keeps moving.
