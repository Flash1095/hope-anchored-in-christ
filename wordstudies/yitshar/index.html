yitshâr

oil (as producing light); figuratively, anointing

Biblical Usage

Yitshar is oil, and in Scripture oil is never just a commodity. Numbers 18:12 gives God "all the best of the oil" as firstfruits, and Deuteronomy 7:13 lists oil among the blessings of a loved and multiplied people. Even an enemy knew its worth: in 2 Kings 18:32 the Assyrian promises "a land of oil olive and of honey" to lure Judah away, while in 2 Chronicles 31:5 a revived people bring oil in abundance as firstfruits. From the lampstand to the anointing horn, oil carries light and consecration with it.

Theological Significance

I believe oil in the Bible points to the Spirit's work the way a lamp points to light. I hold that God gives the best of it to his own people first, and that no counterfeit promise of plenty, like the Assyrian's in 2 Kings 18:32, can match what he provides. My position is that yitshar reminds me provision and anointing come from the same hand.
