ʼahab

affection (in a good or a bad sense)

Biblical Usage

Ahab is love as a noun — affection, in the plural, as a quality a person carries. Its one place in this study is Proverbs 5:19: "Let her be as the loving hind and pleasant roe." The wife is the loving hind — the word is her lovings, the delight she gives. It is love as delight, not duty: the kind that satisfies a husband at all times.

Theological Significance

I believe this word is honest about love in a way I need. Here love is not a hunger that takes but a delight that satisfies — a wife's lovings, the kind a husband is told to enjoy at all times. The test of love is what it does, not what it feels.
