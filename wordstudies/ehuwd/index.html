ʼÊhûwd

Ehud, the name of two or three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Ehud = "I will give thanks: I will be praised" or "undivided, union" in (1Ch. 8:6) only; 1) Benjamite judge of Israel, deliverer of Israel from Moab; 2) another Benjamite, son of Bilhan (1Ch 8:6)

Details
