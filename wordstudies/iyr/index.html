ʻîyr

Definition
a city (a place guarded by waking or a watch) in the widest sense (even of a mere encampment or post)
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) excitement, anguish; 1a) of terror; 2) city, town (a place of waking, guarded); 2a) city, town
