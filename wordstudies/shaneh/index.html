shâneh

a year (as a revolution of time)

Biblical Usage

A year in the Bible is not only a measure of time, it is a measure of God's patience. The lights were made for signs, and for seasons, and for days, and years, and the first month of the year began at the Passover. The fiftieth year was hallowed and liberty proclaimed, the wilderness wandering ran forty years, a day for a year, and Moses counted our years as threescore years and ten. Joel promises that God will restore the years the locust hath eaten.

Theological Significance

I believe a year is God's unit of patience, because He gives forty years to a wilderness and seventy to a life. The year of jubilee is the proof that no debt is for ever with God, and the years the locust ate are the proof that He can give back what was lost. That is why I count my years as His, and trust Him with the ones I cannot get back.
