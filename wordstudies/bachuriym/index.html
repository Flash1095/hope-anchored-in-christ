Bachurîym

Bachurim, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Bahurim was a village on the road from Jerusalem to the Jordan, where Shimei came out cursing David as he fled from Absalom, and where Jonathan and Ahimaaz hid in a well to escape Absalom's men. David passed it in tears, and the priests' messengers hid in it, and the town is named in the story of both flights.

Theological Significance

I believe Bahurim is the place of the curse and the hiding-place at once, and David's mercy to Shimei there is the picture of a king who forgives what he could have avenged.
