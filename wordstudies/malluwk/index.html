Mallûwk

Malluk, the name of five Israelites

Biblical Usage

Malluk is the name of five Israelites, and the verses show three of them: a Levite ancestor in 1 Chronicles 6:44, a man who put away his strange wife in Ezra 10:29, and a priest who sealed the covenant in Nehemiah 12:2. The five men who share the name are counted separately in the lists, and each one is named for his own part in the story.

Theological Significance

I hold that five men sharing one name is no confusion to God: each Malluk is counted separately, and each one mattered.
