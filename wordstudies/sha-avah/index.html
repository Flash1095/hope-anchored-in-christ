shaʼăvâh

a tempest (as rushing)

Biblical Usage

A sha'avah is a tempest, a rushing desolation, and Wisdom warns with it: when your fear cometh as desolation, and your destruction cometh as a whirlwind, when distress and anguish cometh upon you.

Theological Significance

The tempest is the end of refusing wisdom — fear and destruction come rushing, and there is no shelter then.
