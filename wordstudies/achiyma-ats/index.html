ʼĂchîymaʻats

Word
אֲחִימַעַץ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼĂchîymaʻats
Strongs Number
H290
Definition
Achimaats, the name of three Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Ahimaaz = "my brother is anger (wrath)"; 1) father-in-law of Saul; 2) Zadok's son who aided David during Absalom's rebellion; 3) a supply officer of Solomon
