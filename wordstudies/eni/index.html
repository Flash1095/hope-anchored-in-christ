éni

contraction for the third person singular present indicative of G1751; impersonally, there is in or among:--be, (there) is.

Biblical Usage

Eni is 'there is' — a small word that carries big claims. Paul asks whether there is not a wise man among the Corinthians in 1 Corinthians 6:5, and then declares where there is neither Greek nor Jew in Colossians 3:11, and neither Jew nor Greek, bond nor free, male nor female, in Galatians 3:28. James says with the Father of lights there is no variableness in James 1:17.

Theological Significance

I believe 'there is' is the most hopeful phrase in the Bible when it is followed by 'neither' — in Christ the divisions that run the world simply are not there any more. I hold that the oneness Paul describes is not a dream for heaven but a fact for the church now, because Christ is all, and in all.
