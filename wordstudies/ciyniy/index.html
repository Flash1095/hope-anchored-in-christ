Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Çîynîy
Strongs Number

Definition
a Sinite, or descendant of one of the sons of Canaan
Part Of Speech
adjective
Biblical Usage
Sinite = see Sin "thorn" or "clay"; 1) a tribe of the Canaanites descended from Canaan inhabiting the northern part of the Lebanon district
