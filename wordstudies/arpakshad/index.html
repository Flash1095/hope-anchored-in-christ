ʼArpakshad

Arpakshad, a son of Noah; also the region settled by him

Biblical Usage

Arphaxad is a son of Shem, born two years after the flood, and the father of Salah. Genesis names him in the table of nations and again at the head of the line that runs through Eber and Peleg down to Abram, and the chronicler keeps his place in the same line.

Theological Significance

A name between the flood and Abram matters, because the promise runs through ordinary fathers and sons, and God keeps the line unbroken from Shem to the father of faith.
