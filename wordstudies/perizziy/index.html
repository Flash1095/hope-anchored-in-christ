Pᵉrizzîy

a Perizzite, one of the Canaanitish tribes

Biblical Usage

The Perizzites were one of the peoples of Canaan, living in the land when Abram arrived and still there when Israel came out of Egypt. God promises to bring His people into a land flowing with milk and honey, driving out the Canaanites, Hittites, and Perizzites before them, and Joshua names the Perizzites among the nations the LORD would drive out at the Jordan.

Theological Significance

I hold that the Perizzites remind me the promised land was not empty: God gave it, but His people still had to go in and take it, trusting Him to drive out what they could not.
