ʼÎyzebel

Izebel, the wife of king Ahab

Biblical Usage

Jezebel was the Sidonian princess Ahab married, and she turned Israel toward Baal with a persistence no king before her had matched. She fed the prophets of Baal, hunted Elijah after Carmel, and threatened his life — and in the end she was thrown from her window at Jezreel, just as Elijah had said.

Theological Significance

I believe Jezebel stands as Scripture's clearest warning that idolatry is never only a private belief: it builds altars, kills prophets, and bends a whole nation, and God answers it in His own time.
