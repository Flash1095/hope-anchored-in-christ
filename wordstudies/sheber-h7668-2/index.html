sheber

grain (as if broken into kernels)

Biblical Usage

Corn is food, and in a famine it is life. Joseph's brothers are told to carry corn for the famine of their houses, and when they had eaten it up they must go again to buy a little food. Amos hears the merchants asking when the new moon will be gone that they may sell corn, making the ephah small and the shekel great, and Nehemiah's covenant refuses to buy on the sabbath.

Theological Significance

I believe grain is where faith meets the table. God gave the corn in famine, men cheated with it in the market, and the covenant refused to trade it on God's day, and I hold that daily bread is a spiritual matter.
