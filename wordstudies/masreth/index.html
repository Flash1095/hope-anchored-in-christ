masrêth

a pan

Biblical Usage

masrêth is a pan, and it occurs once, in 2 Samuel 13:9. There Tamar takes a pan and pours the cakes out before Amnon, but he refuses to eat, and sends all the men out. The word is the pan that carries a sister's cooking to a brother's trap.

Theological Significance

I believe masrêth shows me that a pan of cakes can carry a brother's treachery, and that Amnon's sin is a warning about what appetite does.
