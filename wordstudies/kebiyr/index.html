kᵉbîyr

a matrass (of intertwined materials)

Biblical Usage

Kebiyr means a pillow, a quilt, something netted and soft. 1 Samuel 19:13 says Michal put a pillow of goats' hair for David's bolster to fool Saul's messengers, and 1 Samuel 19:16 says the messengers found the image in the bed, with the pillow for his bolster.

Theological Significance

I believe Michal's pillow is a small picture of how God hides his anointed until the danger passes.
