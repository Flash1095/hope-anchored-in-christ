ekeîthen

from G1563; thence:--from that place, (from) thence, there.

Biblical Usage

Thence is the word for from that place, and the Gospels use it for the next step of the journey. Jesus departs thence and comes near the Sea of Galilee, goes out from thence to His own country, and after two days departs thence into Galilee, while Paul and Barnabas, sent forth by the Holy Ghost, depart thence to Seleucia and sail to Cyprus. The word is always a leaving that leads to a new place of work.

Theological Significance

I believe God always has a thence after every there, because His people never settle where they first arrive. The journey goes on, and so does the work.
