ʼâmar

to say (used with great latitude)

Biblical Usage

To say, the commonest verb in the Bible, four thousand times and more. And God said, Let there be light: and there was light. And God said unto Moses, I AM THAT I AM: thus shalt thou say unto the children of Israel, I AM hath sent me unto you; the word is the saying itself, and the Bible begins with God saying and goes on saying until the end.

Theological Significance

Saying is the commonest act in the Bible, and the word itself teaches nothing; the weight is who says it, that when God says, light is, and when God says I AM, Moses has a name to carry into Egypt.
