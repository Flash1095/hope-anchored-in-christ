pûwts

to dash in pieces, literally or figuratively (especially to disperse)

Biblical Usage

Puts means to dash in pieces, to scatter, to disperse. The LORD scattered the builders of Babel abroad upon the face of all the earth. Moses warned Israel that the LORD shall scatter them among the nations. And Jeremiah cries, Woe be unto the pastors that destroy and scatter the sheep of My pasture!

Theological Significance

I believe scattering is God's judgment and God's mercy in the same act: He scattered Babel to stop their pride, He scattered Israel to judge their sin, and He scattered the sheep's enemies so the flock would be gathered again. I hold that every scattering in the Bible ends in a gathering, because the God who disperses is the God who brings back.
