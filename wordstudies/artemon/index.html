artémōn

Word
ἀρτέμων
Language
Greek
Transliteration
artémōn
Strongs Number
G736
Definition
from a derivative of G737; properly, something ready (or else more remotely from G142 (compare G740); something hung up), i.e. (specially) the topsail (rather foresail or jib) of a vessel:--mainsail.
