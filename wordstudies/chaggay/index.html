Chaggai, a Hebrew prophet

Biblical Usage

Haggai was a prophet of the return, and his name is tied to the rebuilding. He prophesied to the Jews in Judah and Jerusalem, the elders prospered through his prophesying and Zechariah's, and his own book opens with the word of the LORD coming by Haggai in the second year of Darius.

Theological Significance

I believe Haggai is the prophet for every stalled building: God sends a word, the hands take up the work again, and the house rises.
