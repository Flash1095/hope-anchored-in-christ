ʼimrâh

{something said}

Biblical Usage

A word, a saying, is what God speaks and what men answer with, and this word follows God's speech through the Bible. In Deuteronomy 32:2 Moses says, my doctrine shall drop as the rain, my speech shall distil as the dew, in 2 Samuel 22:31 David sings that the word of the LORD is tried, in Isaiah 5:24 the people cast away the law and despised the word of the Holy One, and in Genesis 4:23 Lamech says to his wives, hear my speech.

Theological Significance

I believe God's word is like rain in Moses' song: it falls where it will, and it is tried and true in David's. It is despised only by those who cast it away.
