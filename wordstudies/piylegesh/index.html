pîylegesh

a concubine; also (masculine) a paramour

Biblical Usage

Pilegesh is a concubine, a woman taken as a secondary wife. Reuben lay with Bilhah, his father's concubine, in Genesis 35:22, and a Levite's concubine was the cause of the war against Benjamin in Judges 19:1. Saul's concubine Rizpah became the cause of the quarrel between Ish-bosheth and Abner in 2 Samuel 3:7, and Solomon's three hundred concubines turned away his heart in 1 Kings 11:3.

Theological Significance

I believe the concubine's story is one long warning: what God designed as one man and one woman, men multiplied, and every multiplication brought grief. My position is that marriage was never meant to be shared.
