Gᵉmaryâh

Word
גְּמַרְיָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Gᵉmaryâh
Strongs Number
H1587
Definition
Gemarjah, the name of two Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Gemariah = "Jehovah has accomplished"; 1) the son of Shaphan the scribe and father of Michaiah; one of the nobles of Judah who had a chamber in the temple from which Baruch read Jeremiah's alarming prophecy to all the people; 2) the son of Hilkiah who bore Jeremiah's letter to the captive Jews
