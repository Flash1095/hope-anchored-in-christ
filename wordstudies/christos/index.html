christos

Word
Χριστός
Language
Greek
Transliteration
christos
Strongs Number
G5547
Definition
Christ, Messiah, Anointed One — the promised King and deliverer anointed by the Spirit, fulfilled in Jesus of Nazareth.
Part Of Speech
adjective
Key Passages
Matthew 16:16; John 1:41; Acts 2:36; Luke 4:18
Biblical Usage
Christos translates Hebrew Mashiach. Kings, priests and prophets were anointed; Jesus is the Anointed One in whom all three offices meet, confessed by Peter: 'You are the Christ, the Son of the living God.'
Theological Significance
Christos means our hope has a name and a throne. Jesus is not merely a teacher who points the way — He is God's promised King, anointed to save His people.
