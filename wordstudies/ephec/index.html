ʼepheç

cessation, i.e. an end (especially of the earth); often used adverb, no further; also the ankle (in the dual), as being the extremity of the leg or foot

Biblical Usage

Ephes means an end, a ceasing — no further, the uttermost part. Look unto me, and be ye saved, all the ends of the earth; the Lord will judge His people when He sees their power is gone; is there not yet any of the house of Saul, that I may show the kindness of God unto him?

Theological Significance

The ends of the earth are not too far for God's salvation, and the end of our strength is not the end of His mercy. I hold that both are where He begins, and that is why the call goes out to every end of the earth without exception.
