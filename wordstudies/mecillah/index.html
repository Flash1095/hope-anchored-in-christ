mᵉçillâh

a thoroughfare (as turnpiked), literally or figuratively; specifically a viaduct, a staircase

Biblical Usage

A highway is the raised road, and the Bible uses it for the way God makes for His people: prepare the way of the LORD in the wilderness, says Isaiah, and the highway of the upright is to depart from evil. The stars fought from their courses in the highway of heaven, and the psalm says blessed is the man in whose heart are the ways to Zion.

Theological Significance

I believe the highway is the way God builds through the wilderness: it is raised above the flood, it is straight where the desert wanders, and the man whose heart is set on it is blessed. The stars themselves fought from their courses for Israel, because the road was God's.
