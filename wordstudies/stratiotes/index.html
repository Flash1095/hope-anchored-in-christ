stratiṓtēs

from a presumed derivative of the same as G4756; a camper-out, i.e. a (common) warrior (literally or figuratively):--soldier.

Biblical Usage

The soldier is the common warrior, and Paul makes him the picture of the believer. Thou therefore endure hardness, as a good soldier of Jesus Christ, and the centurion says he is a man under authority, having soldiers under him, and he says to one, Go, and he goeth.

Theological Significance

I believe the soldier's virtues are the Christian's: hardness endured, orders obeyed, and authority trusted.
