yâdâh

Word
יָדָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
yâdâh
Strongs Number
H3034
Definition
physically, to throw (a stone, an arrow) at or away; especially to revere or worship (with extended hands); intensively, to bemoan (by wringing the hands)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to throw, shoot, cast; 1a) (Qal) to shoot (arrows); 1b) (Piel) to cast, cast down, throw down; 1c) (Hiphil); 1c1) to give thanks, laud, praise; 1c2) to confess, confess (the name of God); 1d) (Hithpael); 1d1) to confess (sin); 1d2) to give thanks
