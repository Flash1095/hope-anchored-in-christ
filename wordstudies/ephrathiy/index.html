ʼEphrâthîy

an Ephrathite or an Ephraimite

Biblical Usage

Ephrathiy means an Ephrathite or an Ephraimite — a person of Ephrath or Ephraim. At the fords of Jordan the Gileadites challenge the men who would pass, and the Ephraimites are known by their speech; Elkanah is an Ephrathite of Ramathaim; David is the son of Jesse the Ephrathite of Bethlehem.

Theological Significance

A man's people were known by where he came from and how he spoke, and David the Ephrathite was chosen from that small place. I hold that God delights to raise His chosen from overlooked towns, and Bethlehem proves it twice over.
