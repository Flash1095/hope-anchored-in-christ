ʼĂbîyshâlôwm

Abshalom, a son of David; also (the fuller form) a later Israelite

Biblical Usage

Abishalom — Absalom — is David’s third son, born to Maacah in Hebron, who murders Amnon for Tamar, flees to Geshur, returns, steals the hearts of Israel, and rebels against his father before he is caught by his hair in an oak and killed by Joab. The fuller form ‘Abishalom’ also names the father of Maachah in the days of Rehoboam.

Theological Significance

Absalom had everything — beauty, a father's love, a kingdom within reach — and he threw it away for revenge and ambition. I believe his story is the saddest in the Bible about a son: David weeps 'O my son Absalom, my son, my son Absalom! would God I had died for thee', and no victory that day could cover the loss.
