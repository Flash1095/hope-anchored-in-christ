kôach

vigor, literally (force, in a good or a bad sense) or figuratively (capacity, means, produce); also (from its hardiness) a large lizard

Biblical Usage

Koach is strength, power, vigour, in body and in means. Genesis 4:12 says the ground shall not yield her strength to Cain, Exodus 9:16 says God raised Pharaoh up to show His power, Numbers 14:13 and Deuteronomy 4:37 remember the might that brought Israel out of Egypt, Joshua 14:11 has Caleb saying, as my strength was then, even so is my strength now, for war. Leviticus 11:30 carries the odd second sense, naming a large lizard among the creeping things.

Theological Significance

I believe strength in the Bible is always borrowed: the ground keeps hers from Cain, Pharaoh's is raised up to show God's, and Caleb's at eighty-five is the same as at forty because God gave it. I hold that the lizard in Leviticus 11:30 is the joke and the warning both, that the same word names the power of empires and a creature in the dust.
