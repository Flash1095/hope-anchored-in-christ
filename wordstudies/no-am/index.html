nôʻam

agreeableness, i.e. delight, suitableness, splendor or grace

Biblical Usage

Pleasantness is the mark of wisdom and of kind words. Wisdom's ways are ways of pleasantness and all her paths are peace; the words of the pure are pleasant words, and pleasant words are as a honeycomb, sweet to the soul and health to the bones. The shepherd in Zechariah names one of his staves Beauty.

Theological Significance

I believe pleasant words are medicine: they are sweet to the soul and health to the bones, and wisdom's own paths are pleasantness itself. The shepherd's staff called Beauty feeds the flock of slaughter, and pleasant words are honey to the soul.
