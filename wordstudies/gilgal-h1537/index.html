Gilgâl

Gilgal, the name of three places in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Gilgal is the name of three places in Palestine, named 39 times in Scripture, in Deuteronomy, Joshua, and Judges. The full story of the place lives in the Bible itself, and this study covers only the name’s use.

Theological Significance

I take Gilgal seriously as a real place in God’s story, not a made-up one. That is why its name belongs in this study, even when the Bible says little about it.
