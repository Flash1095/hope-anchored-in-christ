maʼămar

something (authoritatively) said, i.e. an edict

Biblical Usage

The word names something said with authority, an edict or command, and it appears in the book of Esther for the laws of the king and the words of Mordecai. The king's commandment went out that Vashti should come no more before him, Esther kept the commandment of Mordecai as she had done when she was brought up with him, and the decree of Esther confirmed the matters of Purim and was written in the book.

Theological Significance

I believe an edict shows where true authority lies, because the king's word could banish a queen and Mordecai's word could save a people. I hold that Esther kept Mordecai's commandment because she trusted the man who had raised her, and obedience to godly authority is the mark of wisdom.
