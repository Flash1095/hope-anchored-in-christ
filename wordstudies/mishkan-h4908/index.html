mishkân

a residence (including a shepherd's hut, the lair of animals, figuratively, the grave; also the Temple); specifically, the Tabernacle (properly, its wooden walls)

Biblical Usage

The tabernacle is the dwelling, the tent where God lived among His people, and the word runs through the whole story of the wilderness. God showed Moses the pattern of the tabernacle on the mountain, the curtains were woven for it, and when it was set up the glory of the LORD filled it; and David remembered that God had gone from tent to tent and from one tabernacle to another.

Theological Significance

I believe the tabernacle is the gospel in canvas. God would rather pitch a tent among sinners than stay in heaven without them, and the tent became flesh and dwelt among us.
