ʼAdbᵉʼêl

Adbeel, a son of Ishmael

Biblical Usage

Adbeel was a son of Ishmael, named in the record of the twelve princes of Ishmael's line, a desert prince counted among the sons of Abraham's firstborn.

Theological Significance

Ishmael's line is named, counted, and blessed in its own way — I hold that as worth saying. The promise belonged to Isaac, but God did not forget the other son, and He kept his sons' names too.
