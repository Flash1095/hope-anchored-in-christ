ʻIbrîy

Ibri, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Ibri was a Merarite Levite of the family of Jaaziah in the time of David. In 1 Chronicles 24:27 he is named among the sons of Jaaziah in the divisions of the Levites, one man in the long list of those set apart to serve the house of God.

Theological Significance

I believe a name like Ibri is worth pausing over: the Levites' service ran on men whose names we barely know, and God knew every one of them.
