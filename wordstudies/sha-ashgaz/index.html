Shaʻashgaz

Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Shaʻashgaz
Strongs Number
H8190
Definition
Shaashgaz, a eunuch of Xerxes
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Shaashgaz = "servant of the beautiful"; 1) the eunuch in the palace of Xerxes who was in charge of the women in the 2nd house
