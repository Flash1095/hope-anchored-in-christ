shᵉmîynîy

eight

Biblical Usage

Eighth marks the day of cleansing and the month of the word. On the eighth day the unclean woman brings her offering, in the eighth day they made a solemn assembly at the dedication, and in the eighth month, in the second year of Darius, came the word of the LORD unto Zechariah. The lists name Johanan the eighth among David's mighty men.

Theological Significance

I believe the eighth day is the day after completion, the day of new beginnings. Cleansing, dedication, and the word all come on the eighth, and I hold that God makes things new after the seventh day's rest.
