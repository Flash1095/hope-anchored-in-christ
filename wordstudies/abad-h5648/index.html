ʻăbad

to do, make, prepare, keep, etc.

Biblical Usage

In the Aramaic chapters of Daniel and Ezra this is the everyday word for doing, making, and serving. The accusers charge that Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego 'serve not thy gods'; Darius tells Daniel that the God 'whom thou servest continually' will deliver him; and the vision promises that all peoples 'shall serve' the Son of Man.

Theological Significance

Two kinds of service face off in Daniel: serving the king's gods, and serving the living God. I hold that the three friends and Daniel show what true service costs — it can put you in a furnace or a lions' den — and that the God they served is the one every nation will serve in the end.
