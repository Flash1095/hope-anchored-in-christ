mēnýō

probably from the same base as G3145 and G3415 (i.e. μάω, to strive); to disclose (through the idea of mental effort and thus calling to mind), i.e. report, declare, intimate:--shew, tell.

Biblical Usage

Menyo means to show, to disclose, to tell what was hidden. 1 Corinthians 10:28 says if any man say unto you, This is offered in sacrifice unto idols, eat not for his sake that shewed it, and Acts 23:30 has the chief captain write that it was told him how the Jews laid wait for Paul.

Theological Significance

I believe showing is a small act with a large result: one word about idol meat, and one warning about a plot, each changed what happened next.
