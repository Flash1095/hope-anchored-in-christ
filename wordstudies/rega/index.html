regaʻ

a wink (of the eyes), i.e. a very short space of time

Biblical Usage

In a moment God would consume a stiffnecked people, yet His anger endures but a moment and His favour is life: weeping may endure for a night, but joy cometh in the morning. After a small moment of forsaking, He gathers with great mercies.

Theological Significance

I believe a moment of God's anger is brief, and a moment of His mercy is the beginning of forever.
