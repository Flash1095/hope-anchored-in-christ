doxa

Word
δόξα
Language
Greek
Transliteration
doxa
Strongs Number
G1391
Definition
Glory, honour and radiance — God's manifest splendour, the praise due to Him, and the future glory awaiting His people.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
John 17:22; Romans 8:18; 2 Corinthians 4:17; Luke 2:14
Biblical Usage
Doxa translates Hebrew kabod. Angels announced it at Bethlehem, Jesus displayed it in signs and transfiguration, and Paul insists present sufferings cannot compare with the doxa to be revealed.
Theological Significance
Doxa gives suffering a horizon: an eternal weight of glory is being prepared that will make today's heaviest trials look light by comparison.
