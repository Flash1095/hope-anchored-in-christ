nystázō

from a presumed derivative of G3506; to nod, i.e. (by implication) to fall asleep; figuratively, to delay:--slumber.

Biblical Usage

Nystazo means to nod off, to slumber, and by extension, to delay. In Matthew 25:5 all ten virgins slumber and sleep while the bridegroom tarries, and in 2 Peter 2:3 the damnation of false teachers slumbers not.

Theological Significance

I hold that the same word warns both ways: men sleep when they should watch, and judgment never sleeps at all.
