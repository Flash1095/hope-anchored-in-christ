nâgash

Word
נָגַשׁ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
nâgash
Strongs Number
H5066
Definition
to be or come (causatively, bring) near (for any purpose); euphemistically, to lie with a woman; as an enemy, to attack; religious to worship; causatively, to present; figuratively, to adduce an argument; by reversal, to stand back
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to draw near, approach; 1a) (Qal) to draw or come near; 1a1) of humans; 1a1a) of sexual intercourse; 1a2) of inanimate subject; 1a2a) to approach one another; 1b) (Niphal) to draw near; 1c) (Hiphil) to cause to approach, bring near, bring; 1d) (Hophal) to be brought near; 1e) (Hithpael) to draw near
