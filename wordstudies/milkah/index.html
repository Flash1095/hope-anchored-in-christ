Milkâh

Milcah, the name of a Hebrewess and of an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Milcah is the name of two women. The first is Nahor's wife, the mother of Bethuel and the grandmother of Rebekah, so the family of the promise runs through her. The second is a daughter of Zelophehad, who stood with her sisters before Moses to claim their father's inheritance.

Theological Significance

I believe Milcah's two lives show the same faithfulness: one kept the family line alive, and the other stood up for what God had promised her family.
