tsêl

shade, whether literal or figurative

Biblical Usage

Tsel is a shadow — the shade a tree gives, the shelter a wing gives, the cover God gives. The psalmist hides under the shadow of God's wings and abides under the shadow of the Almighty; Lot offered his guests the shelter of his roof's shadow; and the LORD says He hid His servant in the shadow of His hand. A shadow is the nearest, simplest picture of refuge in the whole Bible.

Theological Significance

I believe God is the shade we run to when the heat is on — not far away, but close enough to cover us. My position is that the shadow of the Almighty is a real refuge, not a figure of speech for the fortunate. I hold that abiding under His shadow is not hiding from life; it is the place where life is kept.
