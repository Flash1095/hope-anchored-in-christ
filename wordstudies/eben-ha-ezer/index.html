ʼEben hâ-ʻÊzer

Eben-ha-Ezer, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Eben-ha-Ezer means stone of help, and it is the place where Israel twice met the Philistines and where Samuel set up a memorial stone. After God gave the victory, Samuel raised the stone and said that hitherto the Lord had helped them, so the name itself became a reminder. The place keeps the memory of a defeat turned into deliverance.

Theological Significance

I believe we all need an Ebenezer, a plain marker that says God helped us here. Remembering His help in the past is what steadies us for the next battle.
