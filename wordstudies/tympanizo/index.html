tympanízō

Word
τυμπανίζω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
tympanízō
Strongs Number
G5178
Definition
from a derivative of G5180 (meaning a drum, "tympanum"); to stretch on an instrument of torture resembling a drum, and thus beat to death:--torture.
