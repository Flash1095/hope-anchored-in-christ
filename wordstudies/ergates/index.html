ergátēs

from G2041; a toiler; figuratively, a teacher:--labourer, worker(-men).

Biblical Usage

Ergátēs is a worker — a labourer, one who toils at the job. The harvest is plenteous and the labourers are few, Jesus says, so pray the Lord of the harvest to send out labourers; and the workman is worthy of his hire. It is the word for the people God sends into His field.

Theological Significance

I believe ergátēs is a word for me: the harvest is still plenteous, and the labourers are still few. I hold that praying for labourers starts with being willing to be one.
