ekporeúomai

from G1537 and G4198; to depart, be discharged, proceed, project:--come (forth, out of), depart, go (forth, out), issue, proceed (out of).

Biblical Usage

ekporeúomai turns up where the Bible says to come or go forth. In Matthew 3:5, the text says "Then went out to him Jerusalem." In Mark 1:5, the text says "And there went out unto him all the land of Judaea."

Theological Significance

I take ekporeúomai to mean that the gospel was never meant to stay where it started — it goes out, because God sends it out. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
