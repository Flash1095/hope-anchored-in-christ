nâtsach

Word
נָצַח
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
nâtsach
Strongs Number
H5329
Definition
properly, to glitter from afar, i.e. to be eminent (as a superintendent, especially of the Temple services and its music); to be permanent
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to excel, be bright, be preeminent, be perpetual, be overseer, be enduring; 1a) (Niphal) enduring (participle); 1b) (Piel) to act as overseer or superintendent or director or chief
