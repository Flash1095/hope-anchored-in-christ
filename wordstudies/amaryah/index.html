ʼĂmaryâh

Amarjah, the name of nine Israelites

Biblical Usage

Nine men share the name, and the Bible keeps them apart by their place. Amariah the Levite, son of Hebron, is counted among the heads of the Levites in David's census and again in the courses of the priests; Amariah the chief priest stands over Jehoshaphat's court in all matters of the LORD; and the high-priestly line runs through Amariahs from Meraioth to Ahitub, father to son down the generations.

Theological Significance

A name shared by nine men carries no lesson of its own. What it shows is the order of the priesthood, that the same name recurs in the Levites, the chief priests and the high-priestly line, and each man is counted in his own place.
