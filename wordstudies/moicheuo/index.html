moicheúō

from G3432; to commit adultery:--commit adultery.

Biblical Usage

Moicheuo is to commit adultery, and Jesus takes the commandment deeper than the act: whoever looks on a woman to lust after her has committed adultery with her already in his heart. The command stands in the law, in the Sermon on the Mount, and in Paul's summary of the whole law in love.

Theological Significance

I hold that Jesus does not relax the commandment, He presses it home. The law reaches the eyes and the heart, and that is exactly where I need grace.
