shâʼôwn

uproar (as of rushing); by implication, destruction

Biblical Usage

A sha'own is an uproar, a tumult, and the prophets hear it coming. Moab shall die with tumult, with shouting, and with the sound of the trumpet, when fire devours the palaces of Kerioth. And a tumult shall arise among thy people, and all thy fortresses shall be spoiled, as Shalman spoiled Beth-arbel in the day of battle.

Theological Significance

What I take from this is that tumult is the sound of a people falling: shouting, trumpets, and fortresses spoiled, all at once.
