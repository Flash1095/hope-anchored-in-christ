Shîylôh

Shiloh, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

The tabernacle is set up at Shiloh, the house of God stands there in the days of the judges, and Hannah goes up year by year to worship there. Samuel hears God's call at Shiloh, the ark is taken from Shiloh and the city is left desolate, and Jeremiah points to the ruins as a warning.

Theological Significance

I believe Shiloh is the place where God first dwelt among His people, and its ruins are the sermon Jeremiah preached. I hold that a place can lose God's presence and keep its name, and that is the warning I carry from Shiloh.
