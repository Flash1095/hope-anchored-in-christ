Shîylôh

Word
שִׁילֹה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Shîylôh
Strongs Number
H7887
Definition
Shiloh, a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Shiloh = "place of rest"; 1) a city in Ephraim and temporary home of the Ark of the Covenant and the Tabernacle, the place where Samuel grew up
