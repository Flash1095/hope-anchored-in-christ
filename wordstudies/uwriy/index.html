ʼÛwrîy

Uri, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

See, I have called by name Bezaleel the son of Uri, of the tribe of Judah. Geber the son of Uri was Solomon's officer in Gilead.

Theological Significance

I believe God calls men by name for His work: Uri's son built the tabernacle with Spirit-filled hands, and another Uri's son governed Gilead, and both were named because both mattered.
