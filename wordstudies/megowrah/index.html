mᵉgôwrah

affright

Biblical Usage

The word is fear or affright, and it is used where fear comes true and where fear is taken away: the fear of the wicked shall come upon him, says Proverbs, and the psalm says the LORD delivered David from all his fears.

Theological Significance

I hold that the two verses are the two ends of fear: the wicked man's fear finds him, and the righteous man's fear is taken from him, and the difference is who they trusted.
