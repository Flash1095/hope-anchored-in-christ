zîyqâh

Word
זִיקָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
zîyqâh
Strongs Number
H2131
Definition
properly, what leaps forth, i.e. flash of fire, or a burning arrow; also (from the original sense of the root) a bond
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) spark, missile, firebrand, flaming arrow; 2) fetters
