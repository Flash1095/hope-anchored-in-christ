Cheçed

Chesed, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Chesed is the name of two men, and neither must be confused with the word for steadfast love, which is spelled the same. Chesed the son of Nahor is named among the children Milcah bore, and the father of Solomon's officer Ben-hesed is named in the list of the king's commissaries.

Theological Significance

I believe the name is kept straight by the record itself: Nahor's son and the officer's father are two men, and the steadfast love of God is a different word with the same letters. I will not fold them into one.
