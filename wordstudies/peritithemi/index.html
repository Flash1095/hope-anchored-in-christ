peritíthēmi

from G4012 and G5087; to place around; by implication, to present:--bestow upon, hedge round about, put about (on, upon), set about.

Biblical Usage

Peritithemi means to place something around, to put on. The soldiers put a scarlet robe on Jesus and a crown of thorns about His head, and Paul says God has bestowed more abundant honour on the parts of the body that lack it.

Theological Significance

I hold that what men put on Jesus in mockery, God turns to honour: the robe and the thorns became the King's clothing, and the parts we hide are the ones He honours most.
