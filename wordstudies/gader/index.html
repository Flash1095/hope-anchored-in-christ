gâdêr

a circumvallation; by implication, an inclosure

Biblical Usage

Gader is a hedge or a wall, the barrier that guards a vineyard or a city. He that breaks an hedge shall be bitten by a serpent in Ecclesiastes 10:8, and God says He will take away the hedge of His vineyard in Isaiah 5:5. The prophets failed to make up the hedge for Israel in Ezekiel 13:5, but Ezra thanked God for a wall in Judah and Jerusalem in Ezra 9:9.

Theological Significance

I believe the hedge is God's protection and the wall is His mercy. When the hedge is taken away, the vineyard is eaten, and when the wall is given, the city stands. I hold that God still builds walls around His people, and breaks them down only to bring them back.
