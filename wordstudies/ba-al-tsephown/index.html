Baʻal Tsᵉphôwn

Baal-Tsephon, a place in Eqypt

Biblical Usage

Baal-zephon was the place by the sea where God told Israel to turn and camp before they crossed — over against Baal-zephon, between Migdol and the sea — and Pharaoh thought they were entangled in the land, so the Egyptians pursued them there, and the sea opened before them. The name is the last landmark before the deliverance.

Theological Significance

I believe Baal-zephon is the place where God let Pharaoh think he had won, and then drowned his army. He still turns his people back toward the sea on purpose, so the deliverance is unmistakably his.
