ʻâsar

to tithe, i.e. to take or give a tenth

Biblical Usage

To tithe is to give the tenth, and the Bible uses it for the vow, the law and the practice together. Jacob vows that of all God gives him he will surely give the tenth, Moses commands Israel to truly tithe all the increase of the seed year by year, and Nehemiah's people promise the priest shall be with the Levites when they take tithes.

Theological Significance

I believe the tithe teaches what the whole Bible teaches about money: the tenth is the Lord's, and giving it first is how we say so. Jacob vows it before he has anything, the law commands it when the harvest comes in, and I hold that the God who gave the seed is the one who owns the tenth of it.
