gâdaph

to hack (with words), i.e. revile

Biblical Usage

Gadaph means to reproach, to hack at a man with words — and its worst use is against God Himself. Sennacherib's messenger asks whom Hezekiah has reproached and blasphemed in 2 Kings 19:22, and the same words stand in Isaiah 37:23, where the prophet answers him. The fathers blasphemed God by trespass in Ezekiel 20:27, and the soul that sins presumptuously reproaches the LORD in Numbers 15:30.

Theological Significance

I believe words can wound God, and that is a fearful thing. To reproach the Holy One of Israel is not a small sin, and I hold that the tongue that blasphemes will answer for it, while the tongue that repents will be forgiven.
