ʼâphaph

to surround

Biblical Usage

To surround, to wrap around — the waves of death compassing David, the weeds wrapped about Jonah's head in the deep, and evils compassing the psalmist on every side. The word is a closing circle, and it is always trouble closing in.

Theological Significance

I believe this word is honest about trouble: it does surround us, like water closing over a drowning man. But I hold that the same psalms that name the surrounding end with deliverance, and Jonah's weeds gave way to dry land.
