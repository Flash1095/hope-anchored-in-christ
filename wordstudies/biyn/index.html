bîyn

to separate mentally (or distinguish), i.e.(generally) understand

Biblical Usage

To understand is the word for discerning, separating one thing from another in the mind — and it appears over a hundred and sixty times. Proverbs 1:2 says the proverbs are to know wisdom and instruction, to perceive the words of understanding. Proverbs 2:5 promises, then shalt thou understand the fear of the Lord. Daniel 1:17 says God gave the four children skill in all learning and wisdom, and Daniel had understanding in all visions and dreams. And Daniel 9:22 shows Gabriel coming to give Daniel skill and understanding.

Theological Significance

I believe understanding is not cleverness but sight: the fear of the Lord opens the eyes, and Daniel's understanding came because God gave it, not because he took it.
