ʻêrek

a pile, equipment, estimate

Biblical Usage

ʻÊrek is an arrangement — a pile, an order, a valuation, the setting of things in their place. The showbread is set in order on the table; the wood is arranged on the altar; and the priest values a vow at its price. It is the word for things put in order before God.

Theological Significance

I believe ʻêrek is a word for worship: God is a God of order, and His house is set in order. I hold that arranging things rightly — bread, wood, vow — is part of honouring Him.
