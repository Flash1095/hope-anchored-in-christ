Israḗl

of Hebrew origin (H3478); Israel (i.e. Jisrael), the adopted name of Jacob, including his descendants (literally or figuratively):--Israel.

Biblical Usage

Israel is the name God gave to Jacob, and the name of the people who came from him. In Matthew 2:20 the angel tells Joseph to take the child into the land of Israel, in John 1:49 Nathanael confesses, thou art the King of Israel, and in Romans 11:26 Paul writes that all Israel shall be saved.

Theological Significance

I hold that the name carries the whole story: a man who wrestled with God, a people God chose, and a promise God has not forgotten.
