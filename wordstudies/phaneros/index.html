phanerós

from G5316; shining, i.e. apparent (literally or figuratively); neuter (as adverb) publicly, externally:--abroad, + appear, known, manifest, open (+ -ly), outward (+ -ly).

Biblical Usage

Phaneros means apparent, manifest, open to sight, and it is the word for what will not stay hidden. Every man's work shall be made manifest by the day of fire, and nothing is secret that shall not be known and come abroad. Paul says the works of the flesh are manifest, and what may be known of God is manifest in men, because God has showed it to them.

Theological Significance

I believe manifest is the Bible's warning and comfort in one word: nothing stays covered, neither my works nor my sins nor God's own witness in me. The day will declare what I built, the light will show what I hid, and the flesh I fed will stand in the open. My position is to live now as if it were already manifest, because before God it is.
