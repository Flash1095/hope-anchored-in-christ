ʻIddôw

Iddo (or Iddi), the name of five Israelites

Biblical Usage

Iddo is a name carried by several men, and the Bible keeps them distinct by their sons and their times. In Ezra 5:1 Zechariah the son of Iddo prophesies to the Jews, in Ezra 6:14 the elders build by the prophesying of Haggai and Zechariah the son of Iddo, and in Nehemiah 12:16 Zechariah stands among the priests as the son of Iddo.

Theological Significance

I believe the name matters because the prophet came through it: Zechariah the son of Iddo spoke God's word to the builders, and the temple rose on it.
