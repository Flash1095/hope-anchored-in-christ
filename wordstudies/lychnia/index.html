lychnía

from G3088; a lamp-stand (literally or figuratively):--candlestick.

Biblical Usage

The word names the lampstand, the stand that lifts a lamp high enough to light the room, and Jesus says no one lights a lamp and puts it under a basket, but on a lampstand, so it gives light to all in the house. The tabernacle held a golden lampstand before the LORD, and John saw seven golden lampstands in his vision, which are the seven churches.

Theological Significance

I believe the lampstand teaches us that light is meant to be lifted up, not hidden, and the church exists to hold Christ's light where all can see it. I hold that a lampstand does not make its own light; it only holds the flame, and so the church's job is to hold up Jesus and get out of the way.
