metanoia

Word
μετάνοια
Language
Greek
Transliteration
metanoia
Strongs Number
G3341
Definition
Repentance — a change of mind and heart that turns the whole person from sin to God.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Luke 15:7; Acts 2:38; 2 Corinthians 7:10; Matthew 3:2
Biblical Usage
Metanoia literally means a mind transformed. John the Baptist and Jesus both opened their preaching with it, and heaven rejoices over one sinner who repents — godly grief produces a repentance leading to life.
Theological Significance
Metanoia is good news, not bad: turning is possible because God is near and kind. Repentance is not paying for pardon — it is the doorway into joy.
