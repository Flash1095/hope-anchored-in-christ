érēmos

of uncertain affinity; lonesome, i.e. (by implication) waste (usually as a noun, G5561 being implied):--desert, desolate, solitary, wilderness.

Biblical Usage

Érēmos is the wilderness — the lonely, waste place, and also the desert itself. Jesus is driven into the wilderness to be tempted; Israel wanders in the wilderness for forty years; and John cries in the wilderness, 'Prepare ye the way of the Lord'. It is the place where God meets His people alone.

Theological Significance

I believe érēmos is the place of testing and of meeting: God leads His people into the wilderness to speak to them there. I hold that my own wilderness seasons are not abandonment — they are where He does His clearest work.
