châṭam

to stop

Biblical Usage

The word means to hold in or restrain, and God uses it of Himself when He says He will refrain for His name's sake, that He cut not off His people. He holds back the anger they deserved.

Theological Significance

I believe God's restraint is mercy at work, and the only reason I am not cut off is that He holds Himself back for His name's sake.
