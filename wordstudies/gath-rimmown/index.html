Gath-Rimmôwn

Gath-Rimmon, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Gath-rimmon is a Levitical city, named in the lists of the tribe of Dan in Joshua 19:45 and among the cities given to the Kohathites in 1 Chronicles 6:69. It was a city set apart for the priests' families to live in.

Theological Significance

I believe the Levitical cities show that God provides for those who serve Him. Gath-rimmon was a home for the priests' families, and I hold that the Lord still provides a dwelling for His servants.
