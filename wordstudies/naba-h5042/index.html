nâbaʻ

to gush forth; figuratively, to utter (good or bad words); specifically, to emit (a foul odor)

Biblical Usage

Naba means to gush forth or pour out, and it is used for words, for wisdom, and for stinking savour alike. In Proverbs 1:23 Wisdom says, I will pour out my spirit unto you, and in Psalm 19:2 day unto day uttereth speech. In Proverbs 15:2 the mouth of fools poureth out foolishness, and in Ecclesiastes 10:1 dead flies cause the ointment to send forth a stinking savour.

Theological Significance

I believe what pours out of a person shows what is inside them, whether it is wisdom or folly. The same mouth can pour out the Spirit's words or a fool's, and the difference is what fills the heart first. I hold that I want my words to be the kind that pour out life.
