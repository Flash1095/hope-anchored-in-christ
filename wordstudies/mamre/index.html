Mamrêʼ

Mamre, an Amorite

Biblical Usage

Mamrêʼ is Mamre, the Amorite whose oaks gave their name to the plain near Hebron where Abram lived. In Genesis 13:18 Abram dwells in the plain of Mamre and builds an altar to the LORD there, and in Genesis 18:1 the LORD appears to him in the plains of Mamre. In Genesis 23:19 Abraham buries Sarah in the cave of Machpelah before Mamre., and the name marks the ground where God met Abraham again and again.

Theological Significance

I believe Mamre matters because it is ordinary ground made holy by God's appearing, and I hold that He still meets His people where they pitch their tents.
