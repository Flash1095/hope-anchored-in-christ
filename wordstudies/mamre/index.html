Mamrêʼ

Word
מַמְרֵא
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Mamrêʼ
Strongs Number
H4471
Definition
Mamre, an Amorite
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Mamre = "strength" or "fatness"; n pr m; 1) an Amorite who allied himself with Abram; n pr loc; 2) an oak grove on Mamre's land in Palestine where Abraham dwelt; 3) a place near Abraham's burial place, apparently identified with Hebron
