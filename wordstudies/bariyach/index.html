bârîyach

a fugitive, i.e. the serpent (as fleeing), and the constellation by that name

Biblical Usage

Bariyach means a fugitive, one who flees. It is the name for the piercing serpent, Leviathan, whom the Lord will punish with His sore and great and strong sword, while Job says God's hand has formed the crooked serpent and His Spirit has garnished the heavens.

Theological Significance

I believe the fleeing serpent is no match for the God who made him, because the same hand that formed him will pierce him. Job marvels at the creature and Isaiah promises its end, and I hold that the serpent's flight is already written: he runs, and God catches him.
