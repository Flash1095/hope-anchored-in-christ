ktísis

from G2936; original formation (properly, the act; by implication, the thing, literally or figuratively):--building, creation, creature, ordinance.

Biblical Usage

Ktisis is creation itself — the act of creating and everything created. Paul says in Romans 8:19 that "the earnest expectation of the creature waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God," in 2 Corinthians 5:17 anyone in Christ is "a new creature," in Colossians 1:15 Christ is "the firstborn of every creature," and in Mark 16:15 the gospel is to be preached "to every creature."

Theological Significance

I am convinced creation is both the world God made and the new world He is making in Christ: the old creation groans and waits, and the new creature in Christ is the first sign of what is coming. The old creation groans, the new creature hopes, and both wait for the sons of God.
