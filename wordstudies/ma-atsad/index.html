maʻătsâd

an axe

Biblical Usage

The word names an axe, and it appears where men make idols with their own hands. Isaiah sets it in the smith's hands as he works the iron with tongs and hammers, and Jeremiah says the customs of the people are vain, for one cuts a tree out of the forest, the work of the hands of the workman, with the axe.

Theological Significance

I hold that the axe is the tool of idolatry's folly: men cut down a tree, shape it with their hands, and then bow to what their own axe made.
