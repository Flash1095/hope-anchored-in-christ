sparássō

prolongation from σπαίρω (to grasp; apparently strengthened from G4685 through the idea of spasmodic contraction); to mangle, i.e. convluse with epilepsy:--rend, tear.

Biblical Usage

The word is tearing, and every use is a demon tearing a boy. The spirit taketh him and teareth him that he foameth again, the unclean spirit tears the man in the synagogue and cries out, and the spirit rents the boy sore and comes out of him, leaving him as one dead.

Theological Significance

I believe the tearing shows what evil does to a person, and the healing shows what Jesus does: He lets the spirit tear, then He raises the boy.
