ʼEshkôl

Eshcol, the name of an Amorite, also of a valley in Palestine

Biblical Usage

ʼEshkôl was an Amorite, an ally of Abraham, and also the name of the valley where the spies cut the cluster. The name occurs 6 times, most often in Numbers. The fuller story lives on the People profile.

Theological Significance

I take ʼEshkôl as it stands: a real name, in a real record. That is enough reason to slow down and notice the name rather than skip it.
