çâlal

to mound up (especially a turnpike); figurative, to exalt; reflexively, to oppose (as by a dam)

Biblical Usage

çâlal means to mound up, to exalt, and the Bible means it plainly. It occurs 10 times in the Old Testament, in Exodus and Isaiah and elsewhere, and none of those places bends the word out of shape. What it says in one place is what it says in all of them.

Theological Significance

I believe çâlal earns its place. To mound up, to exalt is the kind of thing God repeats because His people need to hear it more than once.
