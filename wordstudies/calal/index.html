çâlal

Word
סָלַל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
çâlal
Strongs Number
H5549
Definition
to mound up (especially a turnpike); figurative, to exalt; reflexively, to oppose (as by a dam)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to lift up, cast up, exalt; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to cast up a highway; 1a2) to cast up a way; 1a3) to lift up (of song); 1b) (Pilpel) to exalt, esteem highly, prize; 1c) (Hithpoel) to exalt oneself
