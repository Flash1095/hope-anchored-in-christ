Mᵉnashsheh

Menashsheh, a grandson of Jacob, also the tribe descended from him, and its territory

Biblical Usage

Manasseh is the name of Joseph's firstborn, of the tribe that came from him, and of the king of Judah who bore his name. Genesis 41:51 says Joseph called him Manasseh, for God hath made me forget all my toil and all my father's house, and 2 Kings 21:1 says the later Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign, and reigned fifty and five years in Jerusalem. 2 Chronicles 33:13 tells the end of that reign, when Manasseh prayed and God was intreated of him, and brought him again to Jerusalem.

Theological Significance

I believe Manasseh holds both sides of the name: forgetting, and being remembered. My position is that the king who caused the exile is the same man who prayed his way home, and that is why his story is in the Bible at all.
