prōtoklisía

from G4413 and G2828; a reclining first (in the place of honor) at the dinner-bed, i.e. preeminence at meals:--chief (highest, uppermost) room.

Biblical Usage

Protoklisia is the chief room, the place of honour at the dinner table. Jesus marked how the guests chose out the chief rooms, and said, When thou art bidden, go and sit down in the lowest room, that when he that bade thee cometh, he may say unto thee, Friend, go up higher. And He said the scribes love the uppermost rooms at feasts.

Theological Significance

I believe the dinner table is where pride shows itself plainest: everyone can see who takes the best seat. I hold that Jesus' rule is the opposite of the world's, because in His house the lowest room is the one that gets called up higher.
