phōstḗr

from G5457; an illuminator, i.e. (concretely) a luminary, or (abstractly) brilliancy:--light.

Biblical Usage

Phoster is a luminary, a light, a shining thing, and it appears twice. Paul says believers are to shine as lights in the world, holding forth the word of life, and John sees the holy Jerusalem having the glory of God, her light like a stone most precious, clear as crystal.

Theological Significance

I hold that the luminary is the church's calling in one word: shine as lights in a crooked generation, until the city itself shines like crystal.
