kathístēmi

from G2596 and G2476; to place down (permanently), i.e. (figuratively) to designate, constitute, convoy:--appoint, be, conduct, make, ordain, set.

Biblical Usage

Kathistemi means to place down permanently, to appoint, to set someone in a place that holds. Romans 5:19 says by one man's disobedience many were made sinners, and by the obedience of one shall many be made righteous. Titus 1:5 has Paul telling Titus to ordain elders in every city, as I had appointed thee. James 3:6 says the tongue is set among our members, and is set on fire of hell, and 2 Peter 1:8 says if these things be in you, they make you neither barren nor unfruitful.

Theological Significance

I believe God is the one who sets things in place: Adam's fall made us sinners, and Christ's obedience makes us righteous. I hold that the same word that appoints elders is the word that decides what we become. My position is simple: I want to be set by God, not by hell.
