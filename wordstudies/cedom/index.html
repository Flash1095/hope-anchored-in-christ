Language

Transliteration

Definition
Sedom, a place near the Dead Sea
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Sodom = "burning"; 1) a Canaanite city, usually paired with Gomorrah, located in the area of the Dead Sea and the Jordan river; both cities destroyed by God in judgment
