ʼarmôwn

a citadel (from its height)

Biblical Usage

The palace is the strong place, the citadel of a king, and the prophets speak of palaces burning more often than of palaces standing. Amos says fire will devour the palaces of Benhadad, Isaiah says the palaces will be forsaken and the forts become dens for ever, and Jeremiah says fire will devour the palaces of Jerusalem if the people break the Sabbath.

Theological Significance

I believe palaces burn in the prophets because a palace is where power forgets God. The strongest walls in the land are no shelter when the people inside them will not keep His day or His law, and I hold that no citadel, however high, stands against the fire of His judgment.
