phthónos

probably akin to the base of G5351; ill-will (as detraction), i.e. jealousy (spite):--envy.

Biblical Usage

Phthonos is envy, ill-will that begrudges another's good. Pilate knew the chief priests delivered Jesus for envy in Matthew 27:18, and Paul lists envy among the works of the flesh in Galatians 5:21. It can even hide inside preaching, as Philippians 1:15 admits, and Titus 3:3 says we all once lived in malice and envy before God's kindness appeared.

Theological Significance

I believe envy is murder in the heart before it ever lifts a hand — it delivered Jesus to the cross. My position is that the only cure is the kindness of God that Titus 3 describes, which turns haters into heirs.
