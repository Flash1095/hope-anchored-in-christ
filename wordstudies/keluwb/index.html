kᵉlûwb

a bird-trap (as furnished with a clap-stick or treadle to spring it); hence, a basket (as resembling a wicker cage)

Biblical Usage

Keluwb means a bird-trap, a cage, and also a basket. Jeremiah 5:27 says as a cage is full of birds, so are their houses full of deceit. Amos 8:1 shows the Lord a basket of summer fruit, and Amos 8:2 says, the end is come upon my people of Israel, I will not again pass by them any more.

Theological Significance

I believe the cage and the basket are the same warning: houses full of deceit are traps, and summer fruit ripens for the end. I hold that God shows the prophet what is coming, and then he does not pass by any more.
