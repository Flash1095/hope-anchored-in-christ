pōlos

apparently a primary word; a "foal" or "filly", i.e. (specially), a young ass:--colt.

Biblical Usage

A polos is a young ass, a colt, and the word matters most on the road into Jerusalem. Jesus sends for a colt no one has ever ridden, and the crowds spread their garments before him as he rides it into the city.

Theological Significance

I believe the colt says something plain about our King: he comes gentle and lowly, not on a war horse but on a young donkey.
