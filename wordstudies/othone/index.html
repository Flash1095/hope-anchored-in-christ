othónē

of uncertain affinity; a linen cloth, i.e. (especially) a sail:--sheet.

Biblical Usage

A sheet is a large cloth, and it appears twice, both times in Peter's vision at Joppa: a great sheet knit at the four corners descended from heaven, full of all manner of fourfooted beasts, and a voice said, Rise, Peter, kill and eat.

Theological Significance

I believe the sheet is the picture of how God changes a man's mind: Peter saw the unclean animals lowered from heaven, and learned that God had cleansed what he called common.
