qâhal

to convoke

Biblical Usage

To convoke is to call the people together on purpose: God tells Moses to gather the people so they may hear His words, Solomon assembles the elders to bring up the ark, David gathers all Israel to Jerusalem for the same ark. The people assemble in the valley of Berachah to bless the LORD after the battle.

Theological Significance

I hold that gathering is an act of obedience, not of convenience, because God calls the assembly and the assembly answers by coming. The assembly is not the crowd, it is the people God called by name, and the calling is what makes the coming holy.
