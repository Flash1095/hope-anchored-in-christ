oudeís

Word
οὐδείς
Language
Greek
Transliteration
oudeís
Strongs Number
G3762
Definition
, including feminine οὐδεμία, and neuter οὐδέν from G3761 and G1520; not even one (man, woman or thing), i.e. none, nobody, nothing:--any (man), aught, man, neither any (thing), never (man), no (man), none (+ of these things), not (any, at all, -thing), nought.
