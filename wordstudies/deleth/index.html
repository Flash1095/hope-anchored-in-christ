deleth

something swinging, i.e. the valve of a door

Biblical Usage

1) door, gate; 1a) a door; 1b) a gate; 1c) (fig.); 1c1) of chest lid; 1c2) of crocodile jaws; 1c3) of doors of the heavens; 1c4) of an easily-accessible woman

Details
