deleth

something swinging, i.e. the valve of a door

Biblical Usage

The door is the swinging valve that guards the house and opens to the guest, and the word gathers the doors of Scripture: the doors of the temple overlaid with gold, the doors of the city shut against the enemy, and the door of the house where the Levite's concubine lay. The door is the threshold between inside and out, and the Lord stands at it.

Theological Significance

I believe the door is the picture of the heart: it swings on its hinges, it opens to the guest, and the Lord knocks at it still. The door swings both ways, and the heart that opens to the Lord is the heart He enters.
