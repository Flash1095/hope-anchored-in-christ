nôpheth

a dripping i.e. of honey (from the comb)

Biblical Usage

Honey from the comb drips from the lips, and the Bible uses it for warning and for love. The strange woman's lips drop as a honeycomb and her mouth is smoother than oil; the father tells his son to eat honey because it is good, and the full soul loathes a honeycomb while the hungry soul finds every bitter thing sweet; the bride's lips drop as the honeycomb, with honey and milk under her tongue.

Theological Significance

I believe honey proves the heart that tastes it: the same sweetness is a snare on strange lips and a blessing on the bride's. The full soul loathes the honeycomb, and the hungry soul finds every bitter thing sweet — the appetite decides what the honey means.
