tachash

a (clean) animal with fur, probably a species of antelope

Biblical Usage

The Lord asks for rams' skins dyed red, and badgers' skins, for the covering of the tabernacle. The priests spread a covering of badgers' skins over the ark when the camp moves, and God says to Jerusalem, I shod thee with badgers' skin, and girded thee with fine linen. The word is the animal with fur, probably a species of antelope, whose skin covered the holy things and clothed the city.

Theological Significance

I believe the skins preach a quiet sermon: the holiest things in Israel travelled under a humble covering, and the city God loved was shod with it, for God covers His own with what the desert provides.
