daʻath

knowledge

Biblical Usage

Knowledge is the fruit of the tree in the garden, the wisdom God fills Bezalel with for the work of the tabernacle, and the knowledge of the Most High that Balaam claims. Hosea says God's people are destroyed for lack of knowledge, and the same word that tempts in the garden also builds in the tabernacle. Knowledge is a gift, and it is a danger, and the difference is the fear of the LORD.

Theological Significance

I believe knowledge without God destroys, and knowledge with God builds. The tree promised to make men like God, and the tabernacle was built by men God filled with His Spirit. My position is that the knowledge that saves is the knowledge of God Himself, and Hosea's warning still stands over every generation that forgets Him.
