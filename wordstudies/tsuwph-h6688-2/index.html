tsûwph

comb of honey (from dripping)

Biblical Usage

tsûwph is the word for a honeycomb. In Psalms 19:10, sweeter also than honey and the honeycomb, and it appears again in Proverbs 16:24.

Theological Significance

What grips me is that God put tsûwph exactly where it is, and I trust His placing of it.
