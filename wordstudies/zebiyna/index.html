Zᵉbîynâʼ

Zebina, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Zebina is one of the sons of Nebo who had married a foreign wife in the days of Ezra, and his name appears in the list of those who put their wives away. His name means bought, and he is remembered only for that one hard decision in the reform.

Theological Significance

I believe Ezra's reform was painful because it was real, and Zebina's name stays in the list as proof that ordinary men faced it and obeyed.
