ʼAççîyr

Assir, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Assir is the name of two Israelites: a son of Korah, named in the genealogy of Levi, and a son of Jeconiah, named in the line of David after the exile — 'the sons of Jeconiah; Assir, Salathiel his son.' One sits in the priestly line, the other in the royal line after the captivity.

Theological Significance

Two men, one name, and both sit in the lines that matter most — Levi and David, priest and king, before and after the exile. I believe the name Assir is a quiet sign that God keeps both lines alive through the captivity: the priesthood and the promise survive together.
