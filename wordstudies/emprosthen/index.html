émprosthen

from G1722 and G4314; in front of (in place (literally or figuratively) or time):--against, at, before, (in presence, sight) of.

Biblical Usage

Emprosthen means 'in front of' — before someone's face. We assure our hearts before Him in 1 John 3:19, our work of faith stands in the sight of God in 1 Thessalonians 1:3, and we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ in 2 Corinthians 5:10. Even prayer stands before God as a memorial, as Cornelius learned in Acts 10:4.

Theological Significance

I believe everything I do is done before Someone — there is no private corner outside God's sight, and that is both sobering and comforting. I hold that living 'before Him' is the whole of the Christian life: my prayers rise as a memorial, my works will be shown, and my heart can rest because the Judge is also my Saviour.
