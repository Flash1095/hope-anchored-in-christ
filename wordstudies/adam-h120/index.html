ʼâdâm

ruddy i.e. a human being (an individual or the species, mankind, etc.)

Biblical Usage

Man, mankind — adam is the word for the human creature. "Let us make man in our image, after our likeness"; "the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life"; and David asks, "What is man, that thou art mindful of him?" The word holds both the dust and the image in one breath.

Theological Significance

We are dust, and we are image-bearers — I hold both together, because the Bible does. The dust keeps us humble; the image keeps us from despair. Man is small, and yet God is mindful of him.
