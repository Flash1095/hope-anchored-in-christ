ʼachărîyth

later

Biblical Usage

Achariyth is the Aramaic word for the latter days, and it appears only once — in Daniel's answer to Nebuchadnezzar: "there is a God in heaven that revealeth secrets, and maketh known to the king Nebuchadnezzar what shall be in the latter days." One word, one verse, and it opens the whole prophetic future.

Theological Significance

One Aramaic word for the latter days is a small peg to hang much on, and the verse itself does the work, showing a God who reveals what is to come.
