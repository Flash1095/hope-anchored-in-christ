onomázō

from G3686; to name, i.e. assign an appellation; by extension, to utter, mention, profess:--call, name.

Biblical Usage

To name something is to claim it and call it what it is. Paul warns that a man who is called a brother yet lives in sin must be put out of the fellowship, and that everyone who names the name of Christ must depart from iniquity, while the sons of Sceva learned the hard way that naming the name of Jesus is not a charm to be borrowed.

Theological Significance

I hold that naming the name of Christ is a serious thing: it puts me under His authority, and a life that names Him must match the name. That is why I tremble a little when I call myself a Christian: the name must be lived, not just worn.
