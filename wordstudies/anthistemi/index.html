anthístēmi

from G473 and G2476; to stand against, i.e. oppose:--resist, withstand.

Biblical Usage

To stand against, to resist, to withstand. Jesus says resist not evil but turn the other cheek, James says resist the devil and he will flee, and Paul says to put on the armour of God so you may withstand in the evil day. The same word names the refusal to fight back and the refusal to give ground.

Theological Significance

I believe standing against the devil and refusing to strike back at a man are the same courage worn two ways. James promises the devil will flee from the one who resists, and I hold that promise when I stand my ground: the enemy runs from a heart that will not yield, and the cheek turned is not defeat but victory.
