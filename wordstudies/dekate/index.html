dekátē

feminine of G1182; a tenth, i.e. as a percentage or (technically) tithe:--tenth (part), tithe.

Biblical Usage

The tenth is the tithe, the portion set apart for the Lord, and the word gathers the great tithing passages: Abraham gives the tenth of all to Melchizedek, the Levites receive the tenth of Israel, and the author of Hebrews shows that Levi himself paid tithes in Abraham. The tenth is the confession that all belongs to God.

Theological Significance

I believe the tithe is the tenth that teaches the whole: the one who gives the tenth confesses that the nine-tenths are also His. The tenth is the confession, and the confession is that all of it belongs to God.
