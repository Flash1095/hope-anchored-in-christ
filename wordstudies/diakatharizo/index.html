diakatharízō

from G1223 and G2511; to cleanse perfectly, i.e. (specially) winnow:--thoroughly purge.

Biblical Usage

To purge thoroughly is to clean out completely, and John the Baptist uses it for the winnowing fork in Christ's hand. He will thoroughly purge His floor, gathering the wheat into His garner and burning the chaff with unquenchable fire.

Theological Significance

I hold that Christ's winnowing is both warning and comfort; the chaff is burned, but the wheat is gathered, and the same fork that separates is the proof that nothing good in God's harvest is lost.
