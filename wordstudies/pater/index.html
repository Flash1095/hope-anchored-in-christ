patḗr

apparently a primary word; a "father" (literally or figuratively, near or more remote):--father, parent.

Biblical Usage

Pater is father, the name for God Himself and for earthly fathers. Jesus teaches His disciples to pray, Our Father which art in heaven, in Matthew 6:9, and says in Matthew 5:16 to let your light shine, that men may glorify your Father which is in heaven. John says in John 1:14 that the Word was made flesh, full of grace and truth, the glory of the only begotten of the Father. Paul says in Ephesians 4:6 there is one God and Father of all, and Jesus warns in Matthew 23:9 to call no man your father upon the earth, for one is your Father, which is in heaven.

Theological Significance

I believe fatherhood tells us who God is before it tells us who we are: He is the Father who provides, corrects, and keeps. That is why I hold that every earthly father is measured by Him, and knowing God as Father is the healing for every father who failed.
