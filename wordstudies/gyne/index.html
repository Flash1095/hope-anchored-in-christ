gynḗ

probably from the base of G1096; a woman; specially, a wife:--wife, woman.

Biblical Usage

gynḗ means wife, woman. In Matthew 1:20, the text says "fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife." In Mark 5:25, the text says "And a certain woman."

Theological Significance

I take gynḗ to mean that family is God's idea, and the bonds He builds there are meant to hold. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
