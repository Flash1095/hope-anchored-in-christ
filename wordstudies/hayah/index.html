hâyâh

to exist, i.e. be or become, come to pass (always emphatic, and not a mere copula or auxiliary)

Biblical Usage

Hayah is the verb to be, to become, to come to pass, and it is the most used word in the whole Bible. In Genesis 1:3 God says let there be light, and there was light, in Genesis 2:7 man became a living soul, in Exodus 3:14 God names himself I AM THAT I AM, and in Exodus 3:12 he promises Moses, Certainly I will be with thee.

Theological Significance

I believe this is the word the whole Bible stands on: God is, and because he is, everything else can become. He does not merely exist, he is with me, and that is the promise I hold when I am afraid.
