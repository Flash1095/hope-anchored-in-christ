pródromos

from the alternate of G4390; a runner ahead, i.e. scout (figuratively, precursor):--forerunner.

Biblical Usage

Prodromos is the forerunner, the scout who runs ahead. The writer to the Hebrews uses it once: whither the forerunner is for us entered, even Jesus, made an high priest for ever.

Theological Significance

I believe the forerunner went in first so that I could follow, and where he is, I shall be also.
