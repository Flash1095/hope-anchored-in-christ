shᵉrîyrûwth

obstinacy

Biblical Usage

Moses warns of the man who hears the curse and still blesses himself in his heart, saying he will have peace though he walks in the stubbornness of his own heart. The psalmist and Jeremiah say the same thing in different words: God gave them up to the stubbornness of their hearts, and they walked in their own counsels.

Theological Significance

I hold that stubbornness is not strength; it is a refusal to be taught, and God answers it by letting the stubborn have their way. I believe the way out is the opposite of what the stubborn do: listen, turn, and walk in His way instead of mine.
