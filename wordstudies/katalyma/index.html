katályma

from G2647; properly, a dissolution (breaking up of a journey), i.e. (by implication) a lodging-place:-- guestchamber, inn.

Biblical Usage

The guestchamber is the lodging place where a journey breaks up, and there was no room for them in the inn when Jesus was born, so Mary laid Him in a manger. Years later the same word names the upper room where the Passover was kept and the Lord's Supper was given. The word runs from the manger to the table, from the night He had no room to the night He made room for us.

Theological Significance

I believe the inn that had no room and the upper room that did are the two answers men give to Jesus. I hold that the table He prepared is still open, and I will take my place at it while there is room.
