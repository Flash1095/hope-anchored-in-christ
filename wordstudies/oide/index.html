ōidḗ

from G103; a chant or "ode" (the general term for any words sung; while G5215 denotes especially a religious metrical composition, and G5568 still more specially, a Hebrew cantillation):--song.

Biblical Usage

Ode is a song, a chant, any words sung. Paul tells the churches to speak in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs in Ephesians 5:19 and Colossians 3:16, singing with grace in their hearts to the Lord. In heaven the song is new: Revelation 5:9 sings of the Lamb who was slain and redeemed us by His blood, and Revelation 14:3 sings the song only the redeemed can learn.

Theological Significance

I believe singing is how the church carries the Word, because a song stays in the heart when a sermon is forgotten. My position is that the new song in heaven is the old song of the redeemed, sung at last by everyone who was bought.
