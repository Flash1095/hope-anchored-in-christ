mâdad

Word
מָדַד
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
mâdad
Strongs Number
H4058
Definition
properly, to stretch; by implication, to measure (as if by stretching a line); figuratively, to be extended
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to measure, stretch; 1a) (Qal) to measure; 1b) (Niphal) to be measured; 1c) (Piel); 1c1) to extend, continue; 1c2) to measure, measure off; 1d) (Po) measured; 1e) (Hithpolel) to extend oneself, stretch oneself
