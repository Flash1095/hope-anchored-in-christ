Mᵉnachêm

Word
מְנַחֵם
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Mᵉnachêm
Strongs Number
H4505
Definition
Menachem, an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Menahem = "comforter"; 1) son of Gadi and king of the northern kingdom of Israel; slew the usurper Shallum to ascend the throne and reigned for ten years; contemporary with prophets Hosea and Amos
