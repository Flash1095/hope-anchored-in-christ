Word
עַל
Language
Aramaic
Transliteration
ʻal
Strongs Number
H5922
Definition
{above, over, upon, or against (yet always in this last relation with a downward aspect) in a great variety of applications}
Part Of Speech
preposition
Biblical Usage
1) upon, over, on account of, above, to, against; 1a) upon, over, on account of, regarding, concerning, on behalf of; 1b) over (with verbs of ruling); 1c) above, beyond (in comparison); 1d) to, against (of direction)
