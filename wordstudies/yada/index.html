yada

Word
יָדַע
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
yada
Strongs Number
H3045
Definition
To know — not merely to hold facts but to know by experience, intimacy and covenant relationship.
Part Of Speech
verb
Key Passages
Genesis 4:1; Jeremiah 31:34; Hosea 6:6; Psalm 139:1
Biblical Usage
Yada describes Adam knowing Eve, God knowing Abraham, and the new covenant promise that all 'shall know the Lord.' God desires experiential knowledge of Him more than empty sacrifice.
Theological Significance
Yada is a comfort and an invitation: we are fully known by God, and eternal life is growing to know Him truly — knowledge that is relationship, not just information.
