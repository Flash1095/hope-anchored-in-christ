pínax

apparently a form of G4109; a plate:--charger, platter.

Biblical Usage

Pinax is a platter, a charger. Herodias's daughter asked for John the Baptist's head in a charger in Mark 6:25, and it was brought in a charger in Matthew 14:11. Jesus turns the same dish into a sermon in Luke 11:39: the Pharisees clean the outside of the platter while their inward part is full of ravening.

Theological Significance

I believe a clean dish with a dirty heart is the Pharisee's religion in one picture.
