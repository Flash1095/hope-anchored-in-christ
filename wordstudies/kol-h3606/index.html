kôl

{properly, the whole; hence, all, any or every (in the singular only, but often in a plural sense)}

Biblical Usage

Kol is the Aramaic word for all, the whole, every. Daniel 2:35 says the stone cut out without hands became a great mountain and filled the whole earth, Daniel 2:44 says the God of heaven shall set up a kingdom which shall break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms, and Daniel 7:14 says all people, nations, and languages shall serve the Son of man.

Theological Significance

I believe all is the word Daniel's visions turn on: the stone fills the whole earth, the kingdom consumes all kingdoms, and all peoples serve the One like the Son of man.
