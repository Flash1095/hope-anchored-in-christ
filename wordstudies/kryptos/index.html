kryptós

from G2928; concealed, i.e. private:--hid(-den), inward(-ly), secret.

Biblical Usage

Hidden is the adjective for what is concealed, and it is the word of the heart's secrets. Jesus says in Matthew 6:4 that the Father "which seeth in secret" shall reward openly, Paul says in 1 Corinthians 4:5 the Lord "will bring to light the hidden things of darkness," and Peter speaks in 1 Peter 3:4 of "the hidden man of the heart."

Theological Significance

I am sure the hidden things are the real things: God sees in secret, He will bring the hidden to light, and the hidden man of the heart is the person He is forming. The hidden man of the heart is the person God is forming, and He sees him clearly.
