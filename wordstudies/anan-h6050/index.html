ʻănan

{a cloud (as covering the sky), i.e. the nimbus or thunder-cloud}

Biblical Usage

The Aramaic word for cloud appears once, in Daniel's night vision: 'I saw in the night visions, and, behold, one like the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him.' It is the cloud of heaven itself, carrying the Son of man to the throne — the same cloud the Hebrew word carries through the whole Old Testament, here in Aramaic for the one verse.

Theological Significance

One cloud in one verse carries the weight of the whole vision: the Son of man coming with the clouds of heaven to receive His kingdom, and I believe that is the day every other cloud in Scripture was pointing toward.
