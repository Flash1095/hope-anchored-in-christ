ʼêth

properly, nearness (used only as a preposition or an adverb), near; hence, generally, with, by, at, among, etc.

Biblical Usage

ʼÊth is the little word for 'with' — nearness, togetherness, being alongside. The LORD was with Joseph, and the psalmist asks one thing of the LORD, to dwell in His house all the days of his life. It is the smallest word in the Bible, and it carries the biggest promise.

Theological Significance

I believe ʼêth is the word for the whole gospel in miniature: God with us. I hold that every 'with' in Scripture is a promise I can stand on, because He does not leave His people alone.
