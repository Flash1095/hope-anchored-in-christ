Libnâh

Libnah, a place in the Desert and one in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Libnah the city is a priestly town in Judah that revolted and was besieged — not the word for whiteness, which shares its name. In Isaiah 37:8, "and found the king of Assyria warring against Libnah: for he had heard that he was departed from Lachish."; and in Jeremiah 52:1, "And his mother's name [was] Hamutal the daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah.". In 2 Kings 8:22, "Then Libnah revolted at the same time.".

Theological Significance

I believe Libnah is the city that revolted and the city where the Assyrian camped — a priestly city that chose its own way, and a battlefield in Hezekiah's day. I hold that God records both its rebellion and its siege, because places have histories too.
