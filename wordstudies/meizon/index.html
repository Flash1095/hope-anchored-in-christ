meîzon

neuter of G3187; (adverbially) in greater degree:--the more.

Biblical Usage

Meizon is the neuter form of the word for greater, and it turns up wherever one thing is weighed against another. Matthew 12:6 has Jesus say that one greater than the temple is in this place, and Matthew 13:32 calls the grown mustard seed the greatest among herbs. James 3:1 warns teachers that they shall receive the greater condemnation.

Theological Significance

I hold that greatness in Scripture is always measured against something, and Christ is the measure that outranks them all.
