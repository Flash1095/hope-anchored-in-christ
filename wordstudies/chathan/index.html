châthan

to give (a daughter) away in marriage; hence (generally) to contract affinity by marriage

Biblical Usage

To make a marriage alliance is the word for becoming a son-in-law, and it shapes kings and nations alike. Jethro brings Zipporah back to Moses his son-in-law, Saul offers his daughter to David, Solomon makes affinity with Pharaoh, and the law forbids marriages that would turn Israel after other gods.

Theological Significance

I hold that marriage alliances are never only personal: they bind houses and they bind hearts to gods. The law draws the line where it does because whom we marry shapes whom we worship.
