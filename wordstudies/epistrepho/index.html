epistréphō

from G1909 and G4762; to revert (literally, figuratively or morally):--come (go) again, convert, (re-)turn (about, again).

Biblical Usage

Epistrepho means to turn, to return, to be converted — to come back to where you belong. He shall turn many of the children of Israel to the Lord their God; repent ye therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out; to open their eyes, and to turn them from darkness to light; and ye were as sheep going astray, but are now returned unto the Shepherd and Bishop of your souls.

Theological Significance

From darkness to light, from straying to the Shepherd — the turning is both God's work and mine. I believe turning is the whole of conversion, and the blotting out of sins follows the turning, not the other way round.
