nâtsâh

Word
נָצָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
nâtsâh
Strongs Number
H5327
Definition
properly, to go forth, i.e. (by implication) to be expelled, and (consequently) desolate; causatively, to lay waste; also (specifically), to quarrel
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) (Qal) to fly; 2) to struggle; 2a) (Niphal) to struggle; 2b) (Hiphil) to struggle; 3) to strip off, make desolate, fall in ruins; 3a) (Qal) to fall in ruins; 3b) (Niphal) desolated, ruined heaps (participle)
