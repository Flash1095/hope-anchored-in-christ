ʼÂçâʼ

Asa, the name of a king and of a Levite

Biblical Usage

Asa is the third king of Judah, son of Abijam, who reigns forty-one years. He does ‘that which was good and right in the eyes of the LORD’, removes the idols and the high places, defeats Zerah the Ethiopian with a million men, and then, in his later years, trusts Syria instead of God and dies diseased in his feet.

Theological Significance

Asa starts well and ends badly — a king who trusted God against a million men and would not trust Him against a sore foot. I believe his story is the warning I need most: a good beginning does not guarantee a good ending, and the God who gave me the victory at the start is the same God I need at the end.
