pēchys

of uncertain affinity; the fore-arm, i.e. (as a measure) a cubit:--cubit.

Biblical Usage

A pechys is a cubit, the length of a forearm, about eighteen inches. The disciples dragged their net about two hundred cubits from land, and Jesus asks which of us can add a single cubit to his stature by worrying.

Theological Significance

I take it that Jesus uses our own measure against our worry: no amount of fretting lengthens a life by a cubit.
