Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Gᵉdôr
Strongs Number
H1446
Definition
Gedor, a place in Palestine; also the name of three Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Gedor = "wall"; n pr m; 1) a son of Penuel; 2) a son of Jered; 3) a Benjamite of Gibeon; n pr loc; 4) a town in the mountainous part of Judah a few miles north of Hebron
