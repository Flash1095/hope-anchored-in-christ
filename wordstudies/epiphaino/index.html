epiphaínō

from G1909 and G5316; to shine upon, i.e. become (literally) visible or (figuratively) known:--appear, give light.

Biblical Usage

Epiphaino means to shine upon, to appear, to become visible. To give light to them that sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, to guide our feet into the way of peace; the grace of God that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men; and after the kindness and love of God our Saviour toward man appeared.

Theological Significance

Light shining on people who sat in darkness is the gospel itself, and once it has appeared, no one can honestly say they never saw it. I believe the appearing is both past and present: grace appeared to all men, and it still gives light to whoever sits in darkness today.
