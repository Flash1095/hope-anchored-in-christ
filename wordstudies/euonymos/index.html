euṓnymos

from G2095 and G3686; properly, well-named (good-omened), i.e. the left (which was the lucky side among the pagan Greeks); neuter as adverbial, at the left hand:--(on the) left.

Biblical Usage

In the New Testament, euṓnymos is the word for (on the) left. In Matthew 20:21, the text says "the one on thy right hand." In Mark 10:40, the text says "But to sit on my right hand and on my left hand is not mine to give."

Theological Significance

I take euṓnymos to mean that God's judgment is just, and it falls where it should — no one escapes it, and no one suffers it unjustly. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
