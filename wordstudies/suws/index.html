sûws

to be bright, i.e. cheerful

Biblical Usage

To rejoice is to be bright with joy, and the prophets make it God's own joy as well as ours. I will greatly rejoice in the LORD, my soul shall be joyful in my God; be ye glad and rejoice for ever in that which I create; the LORD thy God will rejoice over thee with joy, He will joy over thee with singing; and my soul shall be joyful in the LORD, it shall rejoice in His salvation.

Theological Significance

I believe the joy is doubled: we rejoice in Him, and He rejoices over us. I hold that a God who sings over His people is a God worth singing to, and the rejoicing is the sound of salvation.
