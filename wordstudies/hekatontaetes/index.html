hekatontaétēs

from G1540 and G2094; centenarian:--hundred years old.

Biblical Usage

Hekatontaetes means a hundred years old, and it appears once, for Abraham. In Romans 4:19 Abraham considered his own body now dead, being about a hundred years old, and the deadness of Sarah's womb, and he was not weak in faith.

Theological Significance

I believe the verse is the definition of faith: a body a hundred years old, a womb dead, and a man who still believed God could do what he promised.
