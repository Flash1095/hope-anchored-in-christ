lâqach

to take (in the widest variety of applications)

Biblical Usage

lâqach means to take, and Scripture puts it to work in every part of life. In Genesis 2:22, "which the LORD God had taken from man"; and in Genesis 12:5, "And Abram took Sarai his wife". In Exodus 16:33, "Take a pot"; and in Isaiah 53:8, "He was taken from prison and from judgment: and who shall declare his generation?".

Theological Significance

I believe taking is never neutral in Scripture — God takes us to Himself, and we take what He gives or what He forbids. I hold that the hand that takes is the hand that chooses, and every taking answers the question of who we trust. My position is simple: I want to take only what God puts in my hand, and leave the rest alone.
