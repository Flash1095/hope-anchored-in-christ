Word
בְּאֵרֹת בְּנֵי־יַעֲקַן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Bᵉʼêrôth Bᵉnêy-Yaʻăqan
Strongs Number
H885
Definition
Beeroth-Bene-Jaakan, a place in the Desert
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Beeroth Bene-Jaakan or Beeroth = "wells" or "wells of the sons of Jaakan"; 1) an encampment for Israel in the wilderness probably in the country of the Horites
