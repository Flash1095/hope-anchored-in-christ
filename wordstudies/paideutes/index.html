paideutḗs

from G3811; a trainer, i.e. teacher or (by implication) discipliner:--which corrected, instructor.

Biblical Usage

Paideutēs is the trainer, the one who corrects. We have had fathers of our flesh who corrected us, and we gave them reverence, says Hebrews — shall we not much rather be in subjection unto the Father of spirits, and live?

Theological Significance

I believe this one verse steadies me: if I honoured an earthly father's correction, how much more should I honour the Father whose correction gives life.
