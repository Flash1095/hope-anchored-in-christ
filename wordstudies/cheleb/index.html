cheleb

Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
cheleb
Strongs Number
H2459
Definition
fat, whether literally or figuratively; hence, the richest or choice part
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) fat; 1a) fat (of humans); 1b) fat (of beasts); 1c) choicest, best part, abundance (of products of the land)
