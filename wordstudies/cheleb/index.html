cheleb

fat, whether literally or figuratively; hence, the richest or choice part

Biblical Usage

Fat is the best part, the portion that belongs to God on the altar. The priest's servant demands the fat before it is burned, Samuel says obedience is better than the fat of rams, Moses sings of the fat of lambs and the fat of kidneys of wheat, and Abel brings the fat of the firstlings of his flock.

Theological Significance

I believe the fat is the best because God asks for the best: Abel gives it, Samuel says obedience beats it, and the song remembers it. The best belongs to God first, and obedience is the best of the best.
