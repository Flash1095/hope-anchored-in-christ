gᵉbîyrâh

a mistress

Biblical Usage

The plain sense of gᵉbîyrâh is queen, lady. It comes up 6 times, and the passages from 1 Kings, 2 Kings, 2 Chronicles, and Jeremiah show how steadily Scripture uses it. I do not find a second, secret meaning hiding behind it.

Theological Significance

gᵉbîyrâh matters to me because God speaks plainly, and plain words carry real weight. I read this as a reminder that God’s words mean what they say, and I can trust them.
