midbâr

a pasture (i.e. open field, whither cattle are driven); by implication, a desert; also speech (including its organs)

Biblical Usage

God led the people about through the way of the wilderness of the Red Sea, their children wandered forty years in the wilderness, Moses told them to remember all the way the Lord led them forty years to humble and prove them, and Isaiah heard the voice crying in the wilderness to prepare the way of the Lord. The word is the wilderness itself, and it is where God leads, tests, and speaks.

Theological Significance

I believe the wilderness is God's school: forty years to humble and prove, a voice crying to prepare the way, and a people led about until they learned to follow. A people led about for forty years learned to follow, and the wilderness taught them.
