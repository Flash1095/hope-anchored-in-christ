hypokritḗs

from G5271; an actor under an assumed character (stage-player), i.e. (figuratively) a dissembler ("hypocrite":--hypocrite.

Biblical Usage

The word hypokritḗs carries the sense of hypocrite. In Matthew 6:2, the text says "as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and in the streets." In Matthew 6:5, the text says "thou shalt not be as the hypocrites [are]."

Theological Significance

Reading hypokritḗs this way, I believe truth is not whatever we feel; it is what God has said, and it stands. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
