miçpâr

a number, definite (arithmetical) or indefinite (large, innumerable; small, a few); also (abstractly) narration

Biblical Usage

Take the number of all the congregation, God tells Moses, with the number of their names, every male by his polls. The psalmist says He tells the number of the stars and calls them all by name, and the chronicler counts the sons of Tola, whose number in David's days was two and twenty thousand and six hundred. Numbering runs from the census to the stars.

Theological Significance

I believe the number of the stars is the verse that makes counting holy: He who counts the stars calls them by name, and the God who numbers the host of heaven numbers His people too. The God who numbers the host of heaven numbers His people too.
