dâbêq

adhering

Biblical Usage

Adhering is the state of sticking fast, and the wings of the cherubim touch each other in the temple, spanning the house. Moses says those who cleave to the LORD are alive today, every one of them. Proverbs says there is a friend who sticks closer than a brother, and the word is the same bond in three places.

Theological Significance

I believe adhering is the picture of a faith that holds on, and a God who holds on tighter. The cherubim touch wingtip to wingtip, the faithful cleave to the LORD and live, and the friend sticks closer than a brother. My position is that the bond is the blessing: to adhere to God is to be alive.
