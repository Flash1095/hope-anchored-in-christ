ptaíō

a form of G4098; to trip, i.e. (figuratively) to err, sin, fail (of salvation):--fall, offend, stumble.

Biblical Usage

Ptaio means to trip, to stumble, to offend, to fail. James says, Whosoever shall keep the whole law, and yet offend in one point, he is guilty of all, and, In many things we offend all; Paul asks, Have they stumbled that they should fall, and answers, God forbid, but rather through their fall salvation is come unto the Gentiles; and Peter says, if ye do these things, ye shall never fall.

Theological Significance

I believe stumbling is the universal experience: we all offend in many things, and one point of the law is enough to make me guilty of all. I hold that Israel's stumble became the Gentiles' salvation, and my diligence in the graces is the way I make sure I never fall.
