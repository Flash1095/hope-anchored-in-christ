hypostéllō

from G5259 and G4724; to withhold under (out of sight), i.e. (reflexively) to cower or shrink, (figuratively) to conceal (reserve):--draw (keep) back, shun, withdraw.

Biblical Usage

hypostéllō turns up where the Bible says to draw back, to shrink, to withhold. In Acts 20:20, the text says "[And] how I kept back nothing that was profitable [unto you]." In Hebrews 10:38, the text says "but if [any man] draw back."

Theological Significance

I believe hypostéllō shows us that faithfulness is staying when staying is costly — the way God stays with us. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
