ʼĂdônây

the Lord (used as a proper name of God only)

Biblical Usage

Adonay is 'the Lord,' used as a proper name for God alone — the name Abraham calls on when he asks what God will give him, the name Moses uses when he pleads his slowness of speech, the name the psalmist gives to the One who laughs at the raging nations and stands at the right hand to shatter kings. It is the name of the sovereign Master, spoken with reverence in place of the divine name itself.

Theological Significance

I believe Adonay teaches me how to address God: as Lord, as Master, as the One whose authority is total and whose laughter no rebellion can silence. It is a name of submission and of safety at once — the Lord who rules is the Lord who keeps.
