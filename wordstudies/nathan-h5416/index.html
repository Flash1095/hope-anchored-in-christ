Nâthân

Nathan, the name of five Israelites

Biblical Usage

Nathan is the prophet who stands beside David through his whole reign, hearing him say he dwells in a house of cedar while the ark dwells in curtains. He is the one sent to confront David after his sin, and the one who will not follow Adonijah when the crown is in dispute.

Theological Significance

I believe Nathan shows what a true prophet is: he speaks to the king in his palace and in his sin, and he does not bend when the court bends. I want that kind of courage when God's word is unpopular.
