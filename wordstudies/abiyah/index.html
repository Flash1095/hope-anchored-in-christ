ʼĂbîyâh

Abijah, the name of several Israelite men and two Israelitesses

Biblical Usage

Abijah is the name of several men and two women. In the Old Testament it is the king of Judah who defeats Jeroboam in battle, the son of Jeroboam who falls sick and dies under Ahijah’s prophecy, and Samuel’s son who judges with his brother and takes bribes; in the New Testament it is the name in Matthew’s genealogy — ‘Roboam begat Abia’ — and the priestly course of Zacharias, the father of John the Baptist.

Theological Significance

One name, four stories — a corrupt judge, a dying child, a warring king, and a priest’s division — and each one shows a different way a life can turn. I believe the name Abijah is a warning and a hope together: a good name does not make a good life, but God still keeps a place for those who return to Him.
