panourgía

from G3835; adroitness, i.e. (in a bad sense) trickery or sophistry:--(cunning) craftiness, subtilty.

Biblical Usage

Panourgia is craftiness, cunning, trickery. Jesus perceives the craftiness of those who ask about tribute, the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God, for He takes the wise in their own craftiness, and Paul renounces the hidden things of dishonesty, not walking in craftiness nor handling the word of God deceitfully.

Theological Significance

I hold that craftiness is cleverness without honesty. God catches the crafty in their own net, and His servants are told to put it off entirely.
