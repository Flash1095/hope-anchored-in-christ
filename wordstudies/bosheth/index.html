bôsheth

shame (the feeling and the condition, as well as its cause); by implication (specifically) an idol

Biblical Usage

Shame is the word for the feeling and the condition and its cause — and it appears thirty times. In Isaiah 54:4 God says, fear not, for thou shalt not be ashamed, neither be thou confounded. In Jeremiah 3:25 the people lie down in their shame, and their confusion covereth them. In Psalms 69:19 the psalmist says, thou hast known my reproach, and my shame, and my dishonour. And in Habakkuk 2:10 the violent man has consulted shame to his house.

Theological Significance

I believe the shame in Isaiah is the promise and the shame in Jeremiah is the warning: God takes away the shame of his people, and he leaves the shame on the house that chose it.
