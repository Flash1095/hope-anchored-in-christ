neder

a promise (to God); also (concretely) a thing promised

Biblical Usage

Jacob vows a vow at Bethel, promising that if God will be with him and keep him, the LORD shall be his God. The law commands that a man who vows a vow to the LORD shall not break his word, and the preacher warns not to defer paying what was vowed.

Theological Significance

I believe a vow is a bond, not a wish: Jacob binds himself at Bethel, the law binds the vow-maker to his word, and God holds me to what I promised Him. I should vow slowly and pay quickly.
