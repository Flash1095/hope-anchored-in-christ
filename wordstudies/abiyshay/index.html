ʼĂbîyshay

Abishai, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Abishai the son of Zeruiah is David’s nephew and one of his chief captains — the brother of Joab and Asahel. He goes down with David into Saul’s camp and wants to strike the sleeping king; he leads a third of the army against the Ammonites; and he is chief among the three mighty men who broke through the Philistines to bring David water from Bethlehem.

Theological Significance

Abishai is the loyal man in the shadows — brave, fierce, and always at David's side, even when his counsel is wrong. I believe his story shows me that loyalty is not the same as wisdom: David has to restrain him more than once, and the best service is a loyal heart under a wise king's command.
