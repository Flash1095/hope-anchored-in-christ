nossiá

from G3502; a brood (of chickens):--brood.

Biblical Usage

A brood is the hen's chickens under her wings, and Jesus uses it for Jerusalem: how often would I have gathered your children together, as a hen gathers her brood under her wings, and you would not. The picture is the mother bird, and the refusal is the city's.

Theological Significance

I believe the hen's wings are the saddest picture in the Gospels: the gathering was offered, and Jerusalem would not come under them.
