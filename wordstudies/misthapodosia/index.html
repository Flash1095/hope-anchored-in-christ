misthapodosía

from G3406; requital (good or bad):--recompence of reward.

Biblical Usage

The word is a recompense, a repayment, and Hebrews uses it for both sides of the ledger. Every transgression received a just recompense of reward, confidence has a great recompense of reward, and Moses chose the reproach of Christ over the treasures of Egypt because he had respect unto the recompense of the reward.

Theological Significance

I believe the recompense is the reason to hold on. God pays every debt, the punishment and the reward alike, and Moses was right to count the reward greater than Egypt.
