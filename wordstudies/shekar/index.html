shêkâr

an intoxicant, i.e. intensely alcoholic liquor

Biblical Usage

Strong drink is wine that intoxicates, and Scripture treats it as a danger and a mercy both. The priest shall not drink wine nor strong drink when he goes into the tabernacle, lest he die; the priest and prophet have erred through strong drink and are swallowed up of wine. Hannah says she has drunk neither wine nor strong drink, but has poured out her soul before the LORD, and Proverbs says to give strong drink to him that is ready to perish.

Theological Significance

I believe strong drink is a test of who is in control. It took the priest out of the way and it steadies the dying, and I hold that the sober pour out their souls to the Lord instead.
