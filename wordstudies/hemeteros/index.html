hēméteros

from G2349; our:--our, your (by a different reading).

Biblical Usage

Hemeteros means our, belonging to us, and it appears where the apostles share what is theirs. In 1 John 1:3 John declares what he has seen so that the fellowship may be ours with the Father, in Acts 2:11 the crowd hears the wonderful works of God in their own tongues, and in Romans 15:4 the things written aforetime were written for our learning.

Theological Significance

I believe the our is the gift: the fellowship is ours, the learning is ours, and the Scriptures were written for us.
