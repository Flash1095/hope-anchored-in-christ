nôʼd

a (skin or leather) bag (for fluids)

Biblical Usage

A skin bottle is the traveller's water and the deceiver's prop. The Gibeonites carry old wine bottles, rent and bound up, as proof of a long journey; Jael opens a bottle of milk for Sisera; and Jesse sends a bottle of wine to Saul by David's hand.

Theological Significance

I believe the bottle tells the story of its bearer: the Gibeonites' rent bottles are a lie, and Jael's bottle is a kindness before a killing. Jesse's bottle is a gift carried to a king, and the Gibeonites' bottles are a lie carried to a camp — the skin holds whatever the bearer means by it.
