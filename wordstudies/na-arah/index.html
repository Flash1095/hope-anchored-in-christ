naʻărâh

a girl (from infancy to adolescence)

Biblical Usage

Na'arah is a girl, a young woman, and it is the word for the damsel at the well and the maid in the field. In Genesis 24:14 the damsel who offers water to the servant and his camels is the one God has appointed for Isaac, and in Ruth 2:5 Boaz asks his servant whose damsel Ruth is.

Theological Significance

I hold that God watches the ordinary kindness of a young woman at a well and builds a family on it; rebekah's offer of water and Ruth's work in the field are both small acts, and both carry the story forward.
