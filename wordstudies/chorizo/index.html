chōrízō

from G5561; to place room between, i.e. part; reflexively, to go away:--depart, put asunder, separate.

Biblical Usage

Chorizo is to separate, to put asunder, and the Gospels use it for the marriage God has joined. Jesus says what God has joined together, let not man put asunder, and Paul says the wife should not depart from her husband, because the bond is God's own making.

Theological Significance

I believe the word is a guard around marriage, because what God joins is not man's to break. I hold that the separation Paul allows is the exception that proves the bond, and the bond itself is the rule.
