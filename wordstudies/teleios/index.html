téleios

from G5056; complete (in various applications of labor, growth, mental and moral character, etc.); neuter (as noun, with G3588) completeness:--of full age, man, perfect.

Biblical Usage

The word is perfect, complete, full-grown. Jesus says, Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect; and to the rich young man, If thou wilt be perfect, go and sell that thou hast. Paul says when that which is perfect is come, then that which is in part shall be done away; James says let patience have her perfect work, that ye may be perfect and entire; and Paul says to be transformed, that ye may prove what is the good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

Theological Significance

I believe perfect does not mean flawless in my strength; it means complete in His will, grown up into Christ. My position is that the Father's perfection is the pattern, and the renewing of the mind is the way there.
