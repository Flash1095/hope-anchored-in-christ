shibyâh

exile (abstractly or concretely and collectively)

Biblical Usage

Exile is what happens when a people loses the land: Moses warns of daughters carried captive, Solomon prays for the captives who will remember in the land of their captivity, and Nehemiah watches the remnant carry the shame of it. The word names both the scattering and the people scattered.

Theological Significance

I believe exile is the hardest school God has, because it takes away everything familiar and leaves only Him. I hold that the captives who remembered God in Babylon are my example, and that He is the God of the far country as much as the God of Jerusalem.
