deúteros

as the comparative of G1417; (ordinal) second (in time, place, or rank; also adverb):--afterward, again, second(-arily, time).

Biblical Usage

Second is the word for what comes after the first, and it runs through the whole New Testament. Nicodemus asks about a second birth, the second commandment is to love your neighbour, Christ will appear a second time to those who wait for Him, and the second death has no power over the faithful.

Theological Significance

I believe the second things in Scripture are where the promise lies. A second birth, a second coming, a second chance — God is the God who does not stop at the first.
