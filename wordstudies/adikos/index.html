ádikos

from G1 (as a negative particle) and G1349; unjust; by extension wicked; by implication, treacherous; specially, heathen:--unjust, unrighteous.

Biblical Usage

Adikos is 'unjust' or 'unrighteous' — the person who is not right before God. Jesus says the Father sends rain on the just and on the unjust; the Pharisee thanks God he is not like other men, 'unjust'; Paul warns that the unrighteous shall not inherit the kingdom; and Peter says Christ suffered, the just for the unjust, to bring us to God. The word draws the line between the two kinds of people, and then the gospel crosses it.

Theological Significance

This word humbles me, because before Christ I stand on the unjust side of that line. I believe the wonder of the gospel is right here in Peter's sentence: the Just One died for the unjust, so that the unjust could be brought to God.
