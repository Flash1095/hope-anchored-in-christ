keçeph

silver (from its pale color); by implication, money

Biblical Usage

Keceph means silver, and so money: 1 Chronicles 18:10 says Tou sent vessels of gold and silver and brass to David. Ezra 1:10 counts thirty basons of gold, silver basons of a second sort four hundred and ten. Psalms 12:6 says the words of the LORD are pure words, as silver tried in a furnace of earth, purified seven times. Zephaniah 1:18 says neither their silver nor their gold shall be able to deliver them in the day of the LORD's wrath.

Theological Significance

I believe silver is the test of every age: counted in basons, sent as tribute, tried seven times in the furnace. I hold that God's words are the only silver that comes out of the fire pure, and no man's silver can buy him out of the day of wrath.
