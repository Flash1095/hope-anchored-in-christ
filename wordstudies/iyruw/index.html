ʻÎyrûw

Iru, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Iru was a son of Caleb, named in the families of Judah. In 1 Chronicles 4:15 the sons of Caleb the son of Jephunneh are listed, Iru, Elah, and Naam, and the sons of Elah, even Kenaz.

Theological Significance

I hold that Iru's name lives in his father's line: a son of Caleb, counted among the families of Judah.
