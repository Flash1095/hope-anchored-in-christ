Nâbâl

Nabal, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Nabal is the churlish rich man of Carmel whose name means fool, and his story is told in full in one chapter. In 1 Samuel 25:3 he is introduced as churlish and evil in his doings, while his wife Abigail is a woman of good understanding, and in 1 Samuel 25:25 Abigail tells David that as his name is, so is he, and folly is with him. In 1 Samuel 25:38 the Lord smites Nabal about ten days after, and he dies.

Theological Significance

I believe Nabal is the warning label on the whole book of Proverbs: a man can be rich, well-fed, and a complete fool at the same time. His wife's wisdom saved his household once, but no one else could save him from himself, and my position is that a name lived down to is still a choice, and Nabal chose his.
