chashchûwth

necessity

Biblical Usage

Chashchuwth is necessity, what is needful, and it appears once: whatsoever more shall be needful for the house of thy God, bestow it out of the king's treasure house.

Theological Significance

I believe the verse is a king's open purse for God's house: whatever is needful, take it.
