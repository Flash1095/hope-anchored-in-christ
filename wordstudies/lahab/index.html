lahab

a flash; figuratively, a sharply polished blade or point of a weapon

Biblical Usage

The flame or the blade is the bright, sharp point, and it flashes in war and in worship. In Judges 13:20 the angel of the Lord ascends "in the flame of the altar" before Manoah and his wife, in Joel 2:5 the enemy comes "like the noise of a flame of fire that devoureth the stubble," in Job 39:23 the quiver rattles and the glittering spear flashes, and in Isaiah 13:8 the faces of the terrified "shall be as flames."

Theological Significance

I am convinced the flame is both the glory and the terror of God: it carries His angel up to heaven, and it devours the stubble, and the same fire that receives the sacrifice will judge the proud. The same fire carries the angel up and devours the stubble.
