reçen

a halter (as restraining); by implication, the jaw

Biblical Usage

Job says God has loosed his bridle and afflicted him, and Leviathan wears a double bridle no man can come near. The psalm warns not to be as the horse or the mule, which have no understanding and whose mouth must be held in with bit and bridle.

Theological Significance

I take it that the bridle is the picture of restraint, and the man who will not be led gently will be led hard.
