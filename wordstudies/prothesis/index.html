próthesis

from G4388; a setting forth, i.e. (figuratively) proposal (intention); specially, the show-bread (in the Temple) as exposed before God:--purpose, shew(-bread).

Biblical Usage

Prothesis means a setting forth, a purpose, a proposal, and also the showbread set before God in the temple. Paul says all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to His purpose, and that we are predestinated according to the purpose of Him who worketh all things after the counsel of His own will. The purpose of God according to election stands before children are born or have done good or evil. And David entered the house of God and ate the showbread, which was not lawful for him to eat.

Theological Significance

I believe God's purpose is the anchor under my life: it stood before I was born, it works all things after His own counsel, and it cannot be broken by my failures. I hold that the showbread on the table and the purpose in His heart are the same truth, that God sets His provision before His people and His purpose over them.
