stoá

probably from G2476; a colonnade or interior piazza:--porch.

Biblical Usage

The porch is the colonnade where the sick waited and the healed clung. The pool of Bethesda has five porches, Jesus walks in the temple in Solomon's porch, and the healed lame man holds Peter and John in the porch called Solomon's while all the people run together wondering.

Theological Significance

I believe the porches are where the needy gather, and Solomon's porch is where the healing is preached.
