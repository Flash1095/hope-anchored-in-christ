ʻĂrôwʻêr

Aroer, the name of three places in or near Palestine

Biblical Usage

Aroer is the name of three places, and the chief one stands on the edge of the river Arnon. Moses says from Aroer by the river no city was too strong for Israel, Joshua gives it to Reuben as a border town, and Isaiah says the cities of Aroer will be forsaken, left for flocks to lie down in.

Theological Significance

A border town that is taken, given and then forsaken tells me how quickly the map changes: the same walls that marked Israel's edge become sheep pens when judgment comes, and I hold that no border town is safe apart from the God who drew the border.
