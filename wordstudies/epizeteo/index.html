epizētéō

from G1909 and G2212; to search (inquire) for; intensively, to demand, to crave:--desire, enquire, seek (after, for).

Biblical Usage

Epizētéō means to seek after — to search for, to demand, to inquire earnestly. The Gentiles seek after these things, Jesus says; the scribes seek a sign; and the Hebrews seek a country of their own, a heavenly one. It is the word for what a heart is set on finding.

Theological Significance

I believe epizētéō is the word for the direction of my life: what I seek after is what I worship. I hold that seeking first the kingdom is the only seeking that ends in finding.
