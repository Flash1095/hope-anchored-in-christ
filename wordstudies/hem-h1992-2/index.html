hêm

they (only used when emphatic)

Biblical Usage

Hem is the word for they, used when the people themselves are the point, and it is everywhere in the Old Testament. In 1 Samuel 3:1 the word of the Lord is precious and there is no open vision, in 1 Kings 1:41 Adonijah and his guests hear the sound of the city, and in 1 Chronicles 12:1 the men come to David at Ziklag.

Theological Significance

I believe the pronoun does quiet work: it gathers the people into one word, and the story is about what they do together.
