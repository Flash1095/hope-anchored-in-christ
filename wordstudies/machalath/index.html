machălath

Word
מַחֲלַת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
machălath
Strongs Number
H4257
Definition
'Machalath', probably the title (initial word) of a popular song
Part Of Speech
noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
1) Mahalath; 1a) found in headings of Ps 53:1 and Ps 81:1; 1a1) meaning dubious, probably a catchword in a song giving name to tune
