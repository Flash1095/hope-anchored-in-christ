Têymân

Teman, the name of two Edomites, and of the region and descendant of one of them

Biblical Usage

Teman is a grandson of Esau through Eliphaz, and the name of the Edomite region known for its wise men. In Genesis 36:11, the sons of Eliphaz were Teman. The fuller story is told on the Teman profile in Biblical People.

Theological Significance

I hold that Teman shows me God keeps His promises across generations, even through flawed families.
