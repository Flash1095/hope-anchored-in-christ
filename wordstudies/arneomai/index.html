arnéomai

perhaps from G1 (as a negative particle) and the middle voice of G4483; to contradict, i.e. disavow, reject, abnegate:--deny, refuse.

Biblical Usage

To deny is to say no to someone you know, and the New Testament uses it for the worst no and the best no alike. Jesus says whoever denies Him before men will be denied before the Father, and that following Him means denying yourself and taking up your cross; Peter denies Him three times before the cock crows, and Paul warns that if we deny Him, He also will deny us.

Theological Significance

I believe denying is the fork in the road of every Christian life: deny yourself and follow, or deny Christ and be denied. Peter's denial is the warning, and his restoration is the hope, and I hold that the same Lord who heard Peter say 'I know not the man' is the one who asked him three times, 'Lovest thou me?'
