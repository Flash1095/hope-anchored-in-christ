Dâthân

Dathan, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Dathan is the Reubenite who joined Korah's rebellion against Moses and Aaron, and the earth opened its mouth and swallowed him. Moses called him to come, and he refused, saying they had not brought them into a land of milk and honey. The psalm remembers it plainly: the earth opened and swallowed up Dathan, and the fire burned in their company.

Theological Significance

I believe Dathan is the picture of rebellion that will not be reasoned with. He would not come when Moses called, and the ground he stood on gave way beneath him.
