Dâthân

Word
דָּתָן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Dâthân
Strongs Number
H1885
Definition
Dathan, an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Dathan = "belonging to a fountain"; 1) a Reubenite chief, son of Eliab, who, with his brother, Abiram, joined Korah's conspiracy against Moses' authority
