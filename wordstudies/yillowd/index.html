yillôwd

born

Biblical Usage

In Scripture, yillôwd is the word for born. In Exodus 1:22, Every son that is born ye shall cast into the river, and it appears again in Joshua 5:5.

Theological Significance

Every son born is cast into the river, and I hold that yillôwd is the word Pharaoh's cruelty falls on.
