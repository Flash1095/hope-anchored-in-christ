nᵉṭar

to retain

Biblical Usage

To keep in Daniel is to hold a matter in the heart when it troubles you. Daniel hears the vision of the four beasts, his thoughts trouble him and his face changes, but he keeps the matter in his heart and says nothing of it yet.

Theological Significance

I hold that some revelations are kept before they are spoken — Daniel carried the vision in his heart while the meaning was still working in him.
