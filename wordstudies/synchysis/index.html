sýnchysis

from G4797; commixture, i.e. (figuratively) riotous disturbance:--confusion.

Biblical Usage

At Ephesus the whole city is filled with confusion, and the crowd seizes Paul's companions and rushes into the theatre with one accord. Confusion here is not honest doubt but a riot, a city stirred up until it cannot tell what it is angry about.

Theological Significance

My position is that a crowd in confusion is easily led to evil, and I hold that the gospel's answer to riot is not more noise but the truth spoken plainly.
