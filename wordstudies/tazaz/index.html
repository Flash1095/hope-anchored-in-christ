tâzaz

to lop off

Biblical Usage

The word is to lop off, to cut away. Isaiah says that before the harvest, when the bud is perfect and the sour grape is ripening in the flower, God shall both cut off the sprigs with pruning hooks and take away and cut down the branches.

Theological Significance

I believe the pruning comes before the harvest, and the branches that will not bear are cut away in time.
