Kananítēs

of Chaldee origin (compare H7067); zealous; Cananites, an epithet:--Canaanite (by mistake for a derivative from G5477).

Biblical Usage

The Cananites is the name the King James gives to Simon, one of the twelve — Simon the Canaanite, the Zealot. The Greek text behind it reads Cananaean, the Aramaic word for zealous, and the two names are the same man: the apostle whose zeal once burned for Israel and then burned for Christ. The form itself stands only in the Received Text; the older manuscripts read Cananaean.

Theological Significance

I believe Simon's nickname is a small picture of conversion: the zeal that once guarded the law was turned to follow the Lord, and Christ takes zealots and makes them apostles.
