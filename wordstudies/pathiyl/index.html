pâthîyl

twine

Biblical Usage

Pathiyl is a cord, a lace, a thread, a twine. Judah gives Tamar his signet and his bracelets and his staff in Genesis 38:18, the bracelets being the cord of the pledge. The blue lace binds the breastplate to the ephod in Exodus 28:28, so it is not loosed from the ephod.

Theological Significance

I hold that the cord that binds the breastplate and the cord that seals a pledge are the same lesson: what God joins is not to be loosed.
