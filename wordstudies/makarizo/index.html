makarízō

from G3107; to beatify, i.e. pronounce (or esteem) fortunate:--call blessed, count happy.

Biblical Usage

Makarizo means to call blessed, to count happy, and its two appearances are a promise and a song. James 5:11 says we count them happy which endure, and Luke 1:48 has Mary saying all generations shall call me blessed.

Theological Significance

I believe both verses bless the same people: the lowly who endure and the lowly whom God lifts up.
