Maʻazyâh

Word
מַעַזְיָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Maʻazyâh
Strongs Number
H4590
Definition
Maazjah, the name of two Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Maaziah = "consolation of Jehovah"; 1) a priest in charge of the 24th course in the time of David; 2) a priest who signed the covenant with Nehemiah
