Gaʻash

Gaash, a hill in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Gaash is a hill in the land, named as a landmark in the lists of David's mighty men. Hurai is called a man of the brooks of Gaash in 1 Chronicles 11:32, and Hiddai is named the same way in 2 Samuel 23:30. The hill gives its name to the brooks that ran by it, and the men who lived there carried its name into the king's army.

Theological Significance

I believe God marks the small places that send Him faithful men. Gaash is only a hill with brooks, yet two of David's mighty men are known by it, and I hold that the place you come from matters less than the King you serve.
