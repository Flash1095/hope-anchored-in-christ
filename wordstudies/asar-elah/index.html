ʼĂsarʼêlâh

Asarelah, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Asarelah is named once, as a son of Asaph, one of the singers David set over the service of song in the house of the LORD. He is listed among the sons who prophesied with harps and psalteries and cymbals, and the verse gives his father and his office and nothing more.

Theological Significance

A singer named once in a list of musicians tells me that the worship of the temple was a family trade, and that God counts the men who sang His praise by name.
