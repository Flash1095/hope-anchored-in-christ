Mêydᵉbâʼ

Medeba, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Israel laid Medeba waste in the song of victory, Moab howls over Medeba in Isaiah's burden, and the plain of Medeba marked the border of Reuben's land. It is a city of Moab on the high plain, taken and lost and mourned in turn.

Theological Significance

I hold that Medeba shows how the cities of Moab passed between hands: Israel sang over it, Isaiah wept over it, and the land itself kept the record.
