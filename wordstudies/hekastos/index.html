hékastos

as if a superlative of ἕκας (afar); each or every:--any, both, each (one), every (man, one, woman), particularly.

Biblical Usage

Hekastos means each, every one, and it makes the Bible personal in a way no crowd word can. In Matthew 16:27 the Son of man shall reward every man according to his works, in Romans 2:6 God will render to every man according to his deeds, in Galatians 6:5 every man shall bear his own burden, and in Ephesians 4:7 grace is given to every one of us according to the measure of Christ.

Theological Significance

I believe each is the word that will not let me hide in the crowd: every man bears his own burden, every man is rewarded according to his works, and every one of us is given grace. The judgment is personal, and so is the gift.
