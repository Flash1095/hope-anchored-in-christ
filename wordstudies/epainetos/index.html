Epaínetos

from G1867; praised; Epænetus, a Christian:--Epenetus.

Biblical Usage

Epaenetus is the wellbeloved of Paul in Romans 16:5 — the firstfruits of Achaia unto Christ, the first convert of the province. He is greeted in the church that met in Priscilla and Aquila's house.

Theological Significance

I believe being first matters to God — the firstfruits belong to Him, and Epaenetus's name is remembered because he was first to believe in a whole province. His story lives on his People profile.
