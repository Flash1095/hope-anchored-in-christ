Hâmân

Haman, a Persian vizier

Biblical Usage

Haman rose to be the king's favourite, and the promotion went straight to his head. In Esther 3:1 the king sets his seat above all the princes, in Esther 3:5 his rage catches fire because Mordecai will not bow, and in Esther 7:10 the gallows he built for Mordecai takes Haman himself.

Theological Significance

I believe Haman is the warning against pride that cannot bear to be ignored: the trap he set for another man was the one he fell into himself.
