pâs

including all the forms of declension; apparently a primary word; all, any, every, the whole:--all (manner of, means), alway(-s), any (one), X daily, + ever, every (one, way), as many as, + no(-thing), X thoroughly, whatsoever, whole, whosoever.

Biblical Usage

Pas is all, every, the whole. It is one of the most used words in the New Testament, and it carries the biggest claims: God so loved the world that whosoever believeth in Him should not perish, in John 3:16, and all have sinned and come short of the glory of God, in Romans 3:23. All things work together for good to them that love God, in Romans 8:28, and I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me, in Philippians 4:13. Jesus sends the church to teach all nations in Matthew 28:19.

Theological Significance

I believe the little word all is the widest word in the Bible: all have sinned, all things work together, all nations are to be taught, and all things are possible through Christ. That is why I hold that no one is outside the all of John 3:16, and no part of my life is outside the all of Romans 8:28.
