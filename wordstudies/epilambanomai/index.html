epilambánomai

middle voice from G1909 and G2983; to seize (for help, injury, attainment, or any other purpose; literally or figuratively):--catch, lay hold (up-)on, take (by, hold of, on).

Biblical Usage

Epilambanomai means to take hold of, to seize — for help, for rescue, for attainment. Jesus immediately stretched forth His hand and caught Peter sinking; He took a child and set him by Him; Barnabas took Paul and brought him to the apostles; He took on Him the seed of Abraham; and we are told to lay hold on eternal life.

Theological Significance

Christ takes hold of the sinking, the child, and the seed of Abraham, and then tells us to take hold of eternal life the same way. I hold that the gospel is a taking hold, and the hand that caught Peter is the hand that holds me.
