kakopoiós

from G2556 and G4160; a bad-doer; (specially), a criminal:--evil-doer, malefactor.

Biblical Usage

Kakopoios is the evil-doer — the one whose deeds are bad, and in Peter's letters the name the world gives to Christians it wants to accuse. Let your conversation be honest among the Gentiles, that whereas they speak against you as evildoers, they may by your good works glorify God; and let none of you suffer as a murderer, or as a thief, or as an evildoer.

Theological Significance

I believe the Christian's answer to a false charge is not a loud defence but a good life. If they call us evildoers, let the works prove them wrong — and let us never give them a true charge to bring.
