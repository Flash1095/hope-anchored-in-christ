bîynâh

{understanding}

Biblical Usage

Understanding is the Aramaic word for discernment, and it appears once, in Daniel's praise of God. In Daniel 2:21 Daniel says God changeth the times and the seasons, he setteth up kings and removeth kings, and he giveth wisdom unto the wise, and knowledge to them that know understanding. One verse, and it gives understanding its source.

Theological Significance

I believe this verse is the whole doctrine of wisdom in a sentence: God gives it, to whom he will, when he will.
