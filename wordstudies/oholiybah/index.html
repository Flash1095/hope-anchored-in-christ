ʼOhŏlîybâh

Oholibah, a symbolic name for Judah

Biblical Usage

Oholibah is the symbolic name Ezekiel gives to Jerusalem in Ezekiel 23:4, the younger sister of Oholah, which is Samaria. In Ezekiel 23:11 Oholibah sees her sister's judgment and becomes more corrupt than she was, in her whoredoms more than her sister's.

Theological Significance

I hold that Oholibah is the warning that seeing judgment does not always teach, and Jerusalem sinned worse after watching Samaria fall.
