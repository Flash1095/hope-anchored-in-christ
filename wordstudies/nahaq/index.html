nâhaq

to bray (as an ass), scream (from hunger)

Biblical Usage

Nahak means to bray like an ass or to scream from hunger, and it is used for the complaints of the empty. In Job 6:5 Job asks if the wild ass brays when he has grass, or the ox lows over his fodder, and in Job 30:7 the outcasts bray among the bushes and gather under the nettles.

Theological Significance

I believe Job's point is simple: a creature cries out because it lacks something, and so does a man; the bray is not the problem; the hunger behind it is.
