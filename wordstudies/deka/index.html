déka

a primary number; ten:--(eight-)een, ten.

Biblical Usage

Ten is the number of the commandments, the virgins, the lepers, and the days of testing, and the word gathers them all. Ten servants receive ten pounds, ten lepers are cleansed and one returns, and ten horns rise on the beast. Ten is the number of completeness in the counting of men.

Theological Significance

I believe ten in Scripture is the number of the whole: the whole law on two tables, the whole church in ten virgins, and the whole debt in ten thousand talents. The number is complete because God counted it, and the counting is His, not ours.
