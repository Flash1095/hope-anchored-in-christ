kraníon

diminutive of a derivative of the base of G2768; a skull ("cranium"):--Calvary, skull.

Biblical Usage

Kranion is simply the word for a skull, and it names the place where Jesus died. Matthew 27:33, Mark 15:22, and John 19:17 all call it Golgotha, the place of a skull, and Luke 23:33 uses the Latin form the English Bible keeps as Calvary.

Theological Significance

What convinces me here is that the name fits the deed: at the place of the skull, the head of the serpent was crushed, and death itself was put to death. The place of the skull became the place where death died.
