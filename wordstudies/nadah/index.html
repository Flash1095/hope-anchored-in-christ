nâdâh

properly, to toss; figuratively, to exclude, i.e. banish, postpone, prohibit

Biblical Usage

Nadah means to toss aside or banish, to drive away from following the Lord, and it is used for a king who leads a people astray. In 2 Kings 17:21 Jeroboam drave Israel from following the Lord and made them sin a great sin, and in Amos 6:3 the people put far away the evil day while they bring the seat of violence near.

Theological Significance

I believe putting the evil day far away is how people live with sin today and judgment tomorrow; jeroboam drove a whole nation from following the Lord, and the nation followed him.
