Mattithyâh

Mattithjah, the name of four Israelites

Biblical Usage

Mattithiah is a Levite musician who played before the ark when David brought it to Jerusalem. He also stood beside Ezra on the wooden pulpit when the law was read to the returned exiles.

Theological Significance

I believe Mattithiah's life is the shape of a Levite's calling: he made music for the ark, and he stood by the preacher, and the same man served the worship of God in both ages.
