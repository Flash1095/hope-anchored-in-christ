châvâh

properly, to live; by implication (intensively) to declare or show

Biblical Usage

To declare or show is the word for speaking out what one has seen. Eliphaz promises to show Job what he has seen, Elihu is afraid at first to show his opinion and then insists on showing it, and the psalm says day unto day utters speech and night unto night shows knowledge.

Theological Significance

I believe God gives us things to declare, and silence is not always humility. Elihu waited out of respect, but the heavens never wait: they declare God's glory every day.
