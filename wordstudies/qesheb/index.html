qesheb

a hearkening

Biblical Usage

Baal's prophets cry until the evening sacrifice, and there is neither voice, nor any to answer, nor any that regards. Gehazi lays the staff on the dead child's face, and there is neither voice, nor hearing. Twice the word marks an absence: no one hearkening.

Theological Significance

I believe an unheard prayer to a false god is the emptiest sound there is, and the silence on Carmel is the verdict.
