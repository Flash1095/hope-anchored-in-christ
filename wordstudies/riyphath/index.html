Rîyphath

Riphath, a grandson of Japheth and his descendants

Biblical Usage

The name is Riphath, a grandson of Japheth and his descendants. You find the name in 1 Chronicles 1:6, "…sons of Gomer; Ashchenaz, and Riphath, and Togarmah", and again in Genesis 10:3.

Theological Significance

My position is that Riphath counts, because God wrote the name down and I believe he meant it. I hold that no name in Scripture is there by accident.
