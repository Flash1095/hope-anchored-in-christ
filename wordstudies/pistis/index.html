pistis

Word
πίστις
Language
Greek
Transliteration
pistis
Strongs Number
G4102
Definition
Faith — trust, belief and faithfulness; active reliance on God and His promises, expressed in allegiance and obedience.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Hebrews 11:1; Romans 1:17; Ephesians 2:8; Habakkuk 2:4
Biblical Usage
Pistis can mean both our trust in God and faithfulness itself — Hebrews 11 parades men and women whose faith acted, endured and obeyed. Paul insists the righteous live by pistis from first to last.
Theological Significance
Pistis is not believing in spite of evidence but trusting a trustworthy Person. Faith is the hand that receives grace and the feet that keep walking with God.
