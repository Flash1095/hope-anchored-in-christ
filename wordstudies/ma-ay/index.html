Mâʻay

Word
מָעַי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Mâʻay
Strongs Number

Definition
Maai, an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Maai = "compassionate"; 1) one of the sons of Asaph who took part in the musical service at the dedication of the wall of Jerusalem
