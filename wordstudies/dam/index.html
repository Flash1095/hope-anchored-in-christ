dâm

blood (as that which when shed causes death) of man or an animal; by analogy, the juice of the grape; figuratively (especially in the plural) bloodshed (i.e. drops of blood)

Biblical Usage

Blood is the life, and Scripture says so plainly: the life of the flesh is in the blood, and it is given on the altar to make atonement for the soul. God forbids eating blood because it carries life, and He commands the blood of the Passover lamb on the doorposts, so that when He sees the blood He will pass over. The whole sacrificial system runs on this one truth: without shedding of blood there is no remission, and the blood points to Christ.

Theological Significance

I believe the blood of the lambs was always pointing forward, and I hold that the blood of Christ is the only blood that finally cleanses. That is why the Passover still matters: it taught Israel to trust a substitute, and it teaches us the same.
