sōreúō

from another form of G4673; to pile up (literally or figuratively):--heap, load.

Biblical Usage

To heap is to pile up, and the two uses could not be further apart. If thine enemy hunger, feed him, for in so doing thou shalt heap coals of fire on his head, and the false teachers creep into houses and lead captive silly women laden with sins.

Theological Significance

I believe the heaping shows the heart: kindness heaps coals that shame, and sin heaps a load that crushes.
