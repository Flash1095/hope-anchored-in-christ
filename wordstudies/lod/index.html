Lôd

Word
לֹד
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Lôd
Strongs Number
H3850
Definition
Lod, a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Lod = "travail"; n pr loc; 1) a city approx 11 miles (17 km) southeast of Joppa in the plain of Sharon in the territory of Benjamin; n pr m; 2) head of a family of returning exiles
