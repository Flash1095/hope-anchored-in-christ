Mîykâl

Mikal, Saul's daughter

Biblical Usage

Michal is Saul's daughter, who loved David and became his wife, and her story runs from love to contempt. She warned David of her father's plot and let him down through a window by night, and years later she looked out and saw the king dancing before the ark and despised him in her heart.

Theological Significance

I believe Michal's story is the saddest in David's house: she saved his life and lost her own place, and the woman who loved the shepherd came to despise the king.
