dialaléō

from G1223 and G2980; to talk throughout a company, i.e. converse or (genitive case) publish:--commune, noise abroad.

Biblical Usage

To talk abroad is to carry news through a whole company until everyone has heard it. The news of John's birth spreads through all the hill country of Judea, and the Pharisees reason among themselves against Jesus after He heals on the Sabbath.

Theological Significance

I hold that news travels, whether it is good or bad; the birth of John filled the hills with talk, and the healing on the Sabbath filled the Pharisees with anger, and both crowds were talking about the same God at work.
