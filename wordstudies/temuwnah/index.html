tᵉmûwnâh

something portioned (i.e. fashioned) out, as a shape, i.e. (indefinitely) phantom, or (specifically) embodiment, or (figuratively) manifestation (of favor)

Biblical Usage

The word is a form, a likeness, a shape. The Lord spake out of the midst of the fire, and Israel heard the voice of the words, but saw no similitude; Moses warns them to take good heed, for ye saw no manner of similitude on the day the Lord spake. The commandment says, Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image, or any likeness of any thing that is in heaven above; and David says, As for me, I will behold Thy face in righteousness, and shall be satisfied when I awake with Thy likeness.

Theological Significance

I believe the likeness Israel never saw is the likeness David longed to wake to, and no image made by hands can carry it. My position is that God forbids the graven likeness because He will give the true one: His face, seen in righteousness.
