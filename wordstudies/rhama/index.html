Rhamâ

of Hebrew origin (H7414); Rama (i.e. Ramah), a place in Palestine:--Rama.

Biblical Usage

Rama is the town of Benjamin on the road where a voice was heard, lamentation and weeping, Rachel weeping for her children. Matthew reads Jeremiah's lament over the children slain at Bethlehem. The Greek text behind the King James Version spells the name with a circumflex, while the text I have checked spells it Ῥαμά.

Theological Significance

I believe Rama's voice is still heard wherever children suffer, and God hears it before any comfort comes.
