Shaʻalbônîy

a Shaalbonite or inhabitant of Shaalbin

Biblical Usage

A Shaalbonite is a man of Shaalbim. Eliahba the Shaalbonite is named in both lists of David's mighty men — among the thirty, and of the sons of Jashen.

Theological Significance

What strikes me is that the mighty men are named town by town, and Eliahba's town is in his name — his home followed him into the king's guard.
