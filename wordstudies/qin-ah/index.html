qinʼâh

jealousy or envy

Biblical Usage

Phinehas was zealous for God's sake and turned away his wrath, and the LORD says he was jealous for Zion with great jealousy and great fury. The same word is the envy that rots the bones and the zeal that eats up the psalmist, and the jealousy that is cruel as the grave. Scripture will not let us have the word in one sense only.

Theological Significance

I believe God's jealousy is not petty envy but a husband's love for his own, and Phinehas turned away wrath by sharing it. I hold that zeal for God's house still eats up those who love him, and I would rather be consumed by it than stand cool beside it.
