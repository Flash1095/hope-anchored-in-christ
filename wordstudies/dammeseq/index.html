Word

Dammeseq

Definition
Damascus, a city of Syria
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Damascus = "silent is the sackcloth weaver"; 1) an ancient trading city, capital of Syria, located in the plain east of Hermon, 130 (205 km) miles northeast of Jerusalem
