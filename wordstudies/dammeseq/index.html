Dammeseq

Damascus, a city of Syria

Biblical Usage

Damascus is the ancient Syrian capital that stands at the edge of Israel's story from Abraham to the prophets. David struck down its armies when they came to help Hadadezer, and the prophets pronounced judgment on it for its cruelty in war. It is also the city where the road of Paul's conversion runs, the place where the persecutor was struck down and raised up a preacher.

Theological Significance

I believe Damascus shows that no city is outside God's reach, whether for judgment or for mercy. The same streets that heard the prophets' woe later heard the gospel from a man who came there to destroy it.
