pᵉʼêr

an embellishment, i.e. fancy head-dress

Biblical Usage

Pe-er is an embellishment, a fancy head-dress, a crown of beauty. God promises in Isaiah 61:3 to give them beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, and in Isaiah 61:10 the bridegroom decks himself with ornaments and the bride with her jewels. The priests wear their bonnets in Exodus 39:28, and Ezekiel is told in Ezekiel 24:17 to keep his tire on his head in the day of mourning.

Theological Significance

I believe beauty for ashes is the gospel in a head-dress: God takes the mourning clothes off and puts the crown on. That is why I hold that the ornaments of Isaiah 61 are not decoration, they are the sign of a people restored.
