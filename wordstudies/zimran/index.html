Zimrân

Zimran, a son of Abraham by Keturah

Biblical Usage

Zimran is the eldest son of Abraham by Keturah, named first among the sons of the second marriage. He has no story of his own — only a name in the genealogy, repeated in the Chronicler's record. His full story lives on his People profile.

Theological Significance

I believe the sons of Keturah remind me that God's promise ran through Isaac, yet Abraham's other sons were still named and still counted.
