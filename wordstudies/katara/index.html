katára

from G2596 (intensive) and G685; imprecation, execration:--curse(-d, ing).

Biblical Usage

The curse is the sentence hanging over everyone who does not continue in all things written in the law, and Christ redeemed us from it by being made a curse for us, hanging on a tree. The ground that bears thorns is near to cursing, and James says out of the same mouth come blessing and cursing, which ought not so to be. The curse is real, and so is the redemption from it.

Theological Significance

I believe Christ took the curse I earned so that I could receive the blessing I did not, and that exchange is the whole gospel. I hold that my mouth should bless more than it curses, because the One who bore the curse has given me a new tongue.
