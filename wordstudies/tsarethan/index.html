Tsârᵉthân

Tsarethan, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Zarethan was a town in the Jordan valley, near the place where the river stood still for Israel to cross. The waters rose up in a heap far from the city Adam, beside Zarethan; and in the plain of Jordan, between Succoth and Zarethan, Solomon cast the bronze vessels for the temple. One town marks both the crossing and the casting.

Theological Significance

I believe God marks the same ground twice — a river stopped and bronze poured — and Zarethan stands at both. The crossing and the temple belong to the same story.
