kraugázō

from G2906; to clamor:--cry out.

Biblical Usage

To cry out is the loud, public shout, and John's Gospel uses it for the crowds at the cross. In John 12:13 they cry "Hosanna," in John 19:6 they cry "Crucify him," in John 11:43 Jesus cries with a loud voice, "Lazarus, come forth," and in Luke 4:41 the demons cry out that He is Christ the Son of God.

Theological Significance

For me, the same loud voice can bless or curse, and the crowds prove it: one week they cried Hosanna, the next they cried Crucify, and only Christ's own cry raised the dead. I need to watch my own voice, because it can turn that fast.
