ʼăkîylâh

something eatable, i.e. food

Biblical Usage

Once, in the story of Elijah under the juniper tree: he rises, eats and drinks, and went in the strength of that meat forty days and forty nights unto Horeb the mount of God. The word is the meal itself — the food the angel brought — and it carries a worn-out prophet forty days to the mountain where God will speak.

Theological Significance

One meal in one verse carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the God who feeds His servant before He speaks to him, and the forty days of strength that followed.
