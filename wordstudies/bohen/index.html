bôhen

the thumb of the hand or great toe of the foot

Biblical Usage

The thumb is the word for the thumb of the hand and the great toe of the foot, always used of both together. In Exodus 29:20 the blood is put on the thumb of Aaron's right hand and the great toe of his right foot, and in Leviticus 8:23 Moses does the same at the consecration. In Judges 1:6 Adoni-bezek's thumbs and great toes are cut off, and in Judges 1:7 he says seventy kings gathered their meat under his table with their thumbs cut off.

Theological Significance

I believe the thumb and the toe in the consecration are the point: the priest's hands and feet both belong to God, from the tip of the ear to the great toe.
