miqnâh

properly, a buying, i.e. acquisition; concretely, a piece of property (land or living); also the sum paid

Biblical Usage

The word is a purchase, a thing bought with money, and it belongs to the covenant and to Jeremiah's field. Every man-child born in Abraham's house or bought with his money was circumcised, and Jeremiah bought a field and sealed the deed, because the purchase was proof that the land would be bought again.

Theological Significance

I hold that a purchase is a promise in this story: Abraham's bought servants were brought into the covenant, and Jeremiah's deed was a down payment on the return.
