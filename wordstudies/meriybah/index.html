mᵉrîybâh

quarrel

Biblical Usage

Meriybah is strife itself, the quarrel, the contention. Genesis 13:8 has Abram plead, Let there be no strife, I pray thee, between me and thee, and between my herdmen and thy herdmen, for we be brethren. Deuteronomy 32:51 and Numbers 27:14 both recall the waters of Meribah-Kadesh, where Moses and Aaron trespassed and did not sanctify God, and Ezekiel 47:19 names the waters of strife in Kadesh as the border of the promised land.

Theological Significance

I believe strife is the sin that follows God's people everywhere, from Abram's herdmen to Moses at the water. My position is that Abram's answer is still the right one: we be brethren, so let there be no strife.
