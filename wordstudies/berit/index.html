berit

Word
בְּרִית
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
berit
Strongs Number
H1285
Definition
A covenant — a solemn, binding promise and relationship, often sealed with sacrifice, signs and blessings and curses.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Genesis 15:17-18; Exodus 19:5-6; Jeremiah 31:31-34; 2 Samuel 7:12-16
Biblical Usage
Berit structures the whole Bible story: covenants with Noah, Abraham, Israel, David and finally the new covenant in Christ. Each one is initiated by God's grace and calls for a faithful response.
Theological Significance
Because God binds Himself by covenant, hope rests on His promise, not our performance. Berit is why believers can trust that God will finish what He began.
