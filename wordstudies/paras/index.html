pâras

to break apart, disperse, etc.

Biblical Usage

Paras means to spread out, to stretch, to scatter. The cherubim spread out their wings over the mercy seat in Exodus 25:20, Moses spreads out his hands to the Lord in Exodus 9:29 when the thunder stops, and the psalmist spreads forth his hands to God in Psalm 143:6. Ruth asks Boaz to spread his skirt over her in Ruth 3:9, the same word for a covering spread over the one who seeks refuge.

Theological Significance

I believe the spread hand and the spread wing are the same gesture in Scripture: reaching out for God, and God reaching out to cover. That is why I hold that prayer is not a clenched fist but an open hand, and refuge is found under a covering spread by someone stronger than I am.
