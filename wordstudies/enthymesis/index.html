enthýmēsis

from G1760; deliberation:--device, thought.

Biblical Usage

Thought, deliberation, device. Paul tells the Athenians not to think the Godhead is like gold or stone, graven by art and man's device, in Acts 17:29, and Hebrews says the word of God discerns the thoughts and intents of the heart in Hebrews 4:12. Jesus knew the thoughts of the scribes in Matthew 9:4 and of the Pharisees in Matthew 12:25, and answered what they had only thought.

Theological Significance

I believe my thoughts are not private — the Godhead is not a device of my making, and the word that discerns my intents is the same word that can cleanse them. I hold that Jesus knowing my thoughts is a comfort, not a threat, because He answers them with truth.
