tapeinophrosýnē

from a compound of G5011 and the base of G5424; humiliation of mind, i.e. modesty:--humbleness of mind, humility (of mind, loneliness (of mind).

Biblical Usage

Paul makes humility of mind the clothing of the Christian life: with all lowliness and meekness, with longsuffering, forbearing one another in love. It is to be put on like a garment, with kindness and meekness, and in lowliness of mind to esteem others better than ourselves. Peter adds that God resisteth the proud and giveth grace to the humble.

Theological Significance

I hold that humility of mind is not a mood but a practice: it shows in how patiently I bear with others and how highly I think of them. My position is that God gives grace to the lowly, and the mind that stoops is the mind Christ had.
