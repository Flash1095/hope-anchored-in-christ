stoichéō

from a derivative of στείχω (to range in regular line); to march in (military) rank (keep step), i.e. (figuratively) to conform to virtue and piety:--walk (orderly).

Biblical Usage

To walk in step is to keep rank with the Spirit and with Abraham. If we live in the Spirit, let us also walk in the Spirit, and Abraham's children walk in the steps of that faith of our father Abraham, and Paul says, let us walk by the same rule.

Theological Significance

I believe walking in step is the discipline of the Christian life: same rule, same Spirit, same steps as Abraham.
