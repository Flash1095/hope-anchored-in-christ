ʼĂchazyâh

Achazjah, the name of a Jewish and an Israelite king

Biblical Usage

Two kings shared the name Ahaziah, and neither reigned long: Ahab's son ruled Israel two years before he died of his fall, and Jehoram's son ruled Judah one year before Jehu killed him.

Theological Significance

Two short reigns, one lesson: when a king in trouble consults anyone but God, the answer is death. I hold both Ahaziahs up as the same warning — the throne is no shelter from the word of the Lord.
