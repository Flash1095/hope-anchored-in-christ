châzaq

to fasten upon; hence, to seize, be strong (figuratively, courageous, causatively strengthen, cure, help, repair, fortify), obstinate; to bind, restrain, conquer

Biblical Usage

To be strong is the command given to leaders at the hard moments. Joshua is told to be strong and of a good courage because the LORD is with him, David tells Solomon to be strong and do the work of the temple, and Hezekiah tells Judah to be strong and courageous because there are more with us than with the king of Assyria.

Theological Significance

I believe strength in the Bible is borrowed, never owned: Joshua borrows it from God's presence, Solomon from God's promise, and Hezekiah from God's numbers. I am strong only when I am not alone.
