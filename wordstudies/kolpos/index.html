kólpos

apparently a primary word; the bosom; by analogy, a bay:--bosom, creek.

Biblical Usage

Kolpos is the bosom, the fold of the garment where things are carried close. Luke 6:38 promises good measure, pressed down and running over, shall men give into your bosom, Luke 16:22 and Luke 16:23 carry Lazarus into Abraham's bosom, John 1:18 says the only begotten Son is in the bosom of the Father, John 13:23 has the disciple whom Jesus loved leaning on Jesus' bosom, and Acts 27:39 finds a certain creek with a shore for the ship.

Theological Significance

I believe the bosom is the Bible's picture of nearness: the Son in the Father's bosom, the disciple on Jesus' bosom, and Lazarus in Abraham's bosom. I hold that the measure given into the bosom in Luke 6:38 is the same nearness, because God gives back pressed down and running over to those who give.
