misthós

apparently a primary word; pay for service (literally or figuratively), good or bad:--hire, reward, wages.

Biblical Usage

The word is wages, hire, reward, and it runs through the teaching of Jesus and Paul alike. Great is your reward in heaven, says the Sermon on the Mount, and the same sermon warns that alms done to be seen have no reward of the Father; the labourers are called at evening to receive their hire, and every man shall receive his own reward according to his own labour.

Theological Significance

I believe the wages are the measure of the heart. God pays what is promised, and the reward He gives is never for show — it is for the work done where only the Father sees.
