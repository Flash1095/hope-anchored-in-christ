antikathístēmi

from G473 and G2525; to set down (troops) against, i.e. withstand:--resist.

Biblical Usage

The writer of Hebrews tells his readers they have not yet resisted unto blood, striving against sin. The word is the resistance that holds its ground, setting oneself down against an enemy and not moving.

Theological Significance

One word for withstanding carries no doctrine of its own; its challenge is Hebrews', that the fight against sin has not yet cost us blood.
