kainótēs

from G2537; renewal (figuratively):--newness.

Biblical Usage

Kainotes is newness itself — the fresh state that belongs to those raised with Christ. We are buried with Him by baptism into death, that like as Christ was raised up from the dead, we also should walk in newness of life; and we serve in newness of spirit, not in the oldness of the letter.

Theological Significance

I hold that newness is not a feeling but a walk: the risen life shows itself in how we live, and the Spirit writes the law on the heart where the letter could only condemn.
