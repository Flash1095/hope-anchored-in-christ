Maáth

probably of Hebrew origin; Maath, an Israelite:--Maath.

Biblical Usage

Maath stands in the genealogy Luke gives for Jesus, named among the ancestors between Mattathias and the Joseph of that generation, and his name is the only place the word appears in Scripture. The Greek text spells the name with the accent on its first syllable, while Strong's lexicon spells it with the accent on the second.

Theological Significance

I believe Maath matters because Luke's genealogy names every link in the chain, and the Saviour's line runs through men the world never heard of.
