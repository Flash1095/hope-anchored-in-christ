çâmak

to prop (literally or figuratively); reflexively, to lean upon or take hold of (in a favorable or unfavorable sense)

Biblical Usage

Scripture uses çâmak for to prop, to lean upon, to take hold of. Across its 47 occurrences the word stays true to that sense, whether it stands in 2 Chronicles or in 2 Kings. That steadiness is what makes it worth knowing.

Theological Significance

I hold that çâmak proves nothing in Scripture is filler. To prop, to lean upon, to take hold of turns up again and again because God wants it noticed.
