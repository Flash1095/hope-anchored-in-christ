Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
gephen
Strongs Number

Definition
a vine (as twining), especially the grape
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) vine, vine tree; 1a) of Israel (fig.); 1b) of stars fading at Jehovah's judgment (metaph.); 1c) of prosperity
