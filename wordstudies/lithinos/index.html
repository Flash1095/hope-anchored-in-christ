líthinos

from G3037; stony, i.e. made of stone:--of stone.

Biblical Usage

líthinos means of stone, and no one who reads the Bible can walk past it. In 2 Corinthians 3:3, "not in tables of stone"; and in John 2:6, "And there were set there six waterpots of stone". In Revelation 9:20, "and idols of gold, and silver, and brass, and stone".

Theological Significance

I believe stone is the Bible's picture of a heart that will not bend — tables of stone, hearts of stone, and idols of stone. I hold that the new covenant writes on hearts of flesh, not stone, and the waterpots of stone at Cana were waiting to be filled with better wine. My position is that I want my heart written on by the Spirit, not carved by my own hand.
