presbýteros

Word
πρεσβύτερος
Language
Greek
Transliteration
presbýteros
Strongs Number
G4245
Definition
compound of πρέσβυς (elderly); older; as noun, a senior; specially, an Israelite Sanhedrist (also figuratively, member of the celestial council) or Christian "presbyter":-- elder(-est), old.
