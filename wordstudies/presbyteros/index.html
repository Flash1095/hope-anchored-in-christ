presbýteros

compound of πρέσβυς (elderly); older; as noun, a senior; specially, an Israelite Sanhedrist (also figuratively, member of the celestial council) or Christian "presbyter":-- elder(-est), old.

Biblical Usage

Presbyteros is the elder, the older man, and in the church the office-bearer. Paul ordains elders in every church and tells Titus to ordain elders in every city, and Timothy hears that elders who rule well are worthy of double honour. In Revelation the twenty-four elders fall before the throne and cast their crowns down.

Theological Significance

I believe eldership is God's gift to the church, not man's invention, and it carries both authority and humility at once. The elders who rule well on earth will one day lay their crowns at the feet of the Lamb.
