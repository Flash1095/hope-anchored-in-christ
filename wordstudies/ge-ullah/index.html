gᵉʼullâh

redemption (including the right and the object); by implication, relationship

Biblical Usage

gᵉʼullâh is the word for kindred, redemption, right of redemption, price of redemption. I meet it 13 times across the Bible, and Leviticus is a good place to see it plainly. It says what it says, and I take it at face value.

Theological Significance

What gᵉʼullâh tells me is that redemption is something God does, not something we earn. What I take from it is this — the plain sense is the safe sense.
