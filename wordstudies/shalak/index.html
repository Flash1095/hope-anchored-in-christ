shâlak

to throw out, down or away (literally or figuratively)

Biblical Usage

Shalak is the throw that sends something away, and it covers casting into a pit, into the sea, and onto the LORD. Joseph's brothers cast him into a pit, Moses cast the tree into the bitter waters and they were made sweet, and Jonah cried that God had cast him into the deep. The psalm turns the throw into a promise, cast thy burden upon the LORD, and He shall sustain thee.

Theological Significance

I believe casting is the Bible's picture of giving up, because what is cast is no longer in your hands. Joseph was cast into a pit and God raised him out of it, and the same God asks us to cast our burdens on Him. That is why I throw my cares on the LORD instead of carrying them myself.
