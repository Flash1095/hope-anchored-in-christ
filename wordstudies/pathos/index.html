páthos

from the alternate of G3958; properly, suffering ("pathos"), i.e. (subjectively) a passion (especially concupiscence):-- (inordinate) affection, lust.

Biblical Usage

Pathos is passion, suffering, inordinate affection. Paul lists it among the things to put to death in Colossians 3:5, calling it inordinate affection, and in Romans 1:26 he says God gives men up to vile affections. In 1 Thessalonians 4:5 believers are not to walk in the lust of concupiscence, as the Gentiles who know not God.

Theological Significance

I believe passion that is not ruled becomes a master, and Paul's command is to put it to death, not to manage it. My position is that the affections God gave are good only when He governs them, and the moment they govern me they are idolatry.
