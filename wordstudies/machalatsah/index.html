machălâtsâh

a mantle (as easily drawn off)

Biblical Usage

The word names a mantle, a garment easily drawn off, and it appears in judgment and in cleansing alike. Isaiah lists it among the fine clothing God will strip from the proud daughters of Zion, and Zechariah sees the filthy garments taken from Joshua the high priest, and he is clothed with a change of raiment.

Theological Significance

I hold that the mantle is taken from the proud and given to the cleansed, and the same garment means judgment in one place and grace in the other.
