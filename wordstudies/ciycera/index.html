Çîyçᵉrâʼ

Sisera, the name of a Canaanitish king and of one of the Nethinim

Biblical Usage

Sisera is the captain of Jabin's army, with nine hundred chariots of iron, and he oppresses Israel for twenty years. Deborah and Barak defeat him, and he flees to the tent of Jael, where a tent peg ends his life. Samuel reminds Israel of him generations later, as a warning of what happens when they forget God.

Theological Significance

I believe Sisera shows that no army, however iron its chariots, can stand against the God who fights for His people. The captain who terrified Israel dies in a woman's tent, and the song that follows is a song of deliverance.
