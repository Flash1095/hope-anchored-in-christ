tâmîym

entire (literally, figuratively or morally); also (as noun) integrity, truth

Biblical Usage

God says to Abram, I am the Almighty God; walk before Me, and be thou perfect. Moses says, thou shalt be perfect with the Lord thy God. The Passover lamb shall be without blemish, a male of the first year, and David says the law of the Lord is perfect, converting the soul. The word is entire, blameless, without blemish: the man God calls perfect, the lamb without spot, and the law that converts.

Theological Significance

I hold that blamelessness is not sinlessness but wholeness before God. I believe Abram's call says it best: walk before Me, and be thou perfect — the perfection is a walk, not a state, and the Lamb without blemish is the one who makes the walk possible.
