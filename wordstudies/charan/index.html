Chârân

Charan, the name of a man and also of a place

Biblical Usage

Charan is both a man and a place, and the place is the one Scripture keeps returning to. Terah took Abram and Lot and Sarai and went forth to go to Canaan, and they came unto Haran and dwelt there, until Abram departed out of Haran as the LORD had spoken, at seventy-five years old, and took Sarai his wife and Lot and all their substance. Haran also names Terah's son, the father of Lot, who died before his father in Ur.

Theological Significance

I believe Haran is the halfway house of faith: Terah stopped there on the way to Canaan and died there, and Abram had to leave it to follow God. I hold that the place between the call and the promise is the most dangerous ground of all, because it is easy to settle there.
