mᵉnâth

an allotment (by courtesy, law or providence)

Biblical Usage

Menath is a portion, an allotment given by courtesy, by law, or by providence. 2 Chronicles 31:3 gives the king's portion of his substance for the burnt offerings, and 2 Chronicles 31:4 commands the people to give the portion of the priests and the Levites. Nehemiah 12:44 sets men over the chambers to gather the portions of the law for the priests, and Psalms 11:6 turns the word to judgment, where fire and brimstone are the portion of the wicked's cup.

Theological Significance

I believe a portion is whatever God allots, and the same word covers the priest's food and the wicked's cup. My position is that I would rather have the portion God gives than the portion I take, because His portion feeds and theirs burns.
