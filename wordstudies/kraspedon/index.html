kráspedon

of uncertain derivation; a margin, i.e. (specially), a fringe or tassel:--border, hem.

Biblical Usage

The hem or fringe is the border of a garment, and it carries the whole story of the woman who touched it. In Matthew 9:20 and Luke 8:44 she touches the hem of Jesus' garment and is healed at once, in Mark 6:56 and Matthew 14:36 whole crowds beg only to touch it, and in Matthew 23:5 Jesus rebukes the Pharisees for enlarging the borders of their garments to be seen of men.

Theological Significance

In my reading, the hem teaches me that faith does not need a grand gesture: one touch of the edge of His garment was enough, and it still is. One touch was enough then, and one touch is enough now.
