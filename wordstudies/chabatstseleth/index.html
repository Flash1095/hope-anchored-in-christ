chăbatstseleth

probably meadow-saffron

Biblical Usage

Chabatstseleth is a flower of the meadow — a crocus, or a rose. The beloved says she is the rose of Sharon, and Isaiah promises the desert will blossom as the rose.

Theological Significance

I believe God plants His roses in the desert on purpose, so no one can say beauty belongs only to gardens.
