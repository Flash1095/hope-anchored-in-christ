Iechonías

of Hebrew origin (H3204); Jechonias (i.e. Jekonjah), an Israelite:--Jechonias.

Biblical Usage

Jechonias is the Greek form of Jeconiah, the king carried away to Babylon, and Matthew names him at the hinge of the genealogy. In Matthew 1:11 Josias begat Jechonias and his brethren, about the time they were carried away to Babylon, and in Matthew 1:12 after the carrying away, Jechonias begat Salathiel.

Theological Significance

I believe Jechonias stands at the darkest turn in the line: the king in chains, the nation in exile, and the promise still moving through him to Christ.
