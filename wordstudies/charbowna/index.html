Charbôwnâʼ

Charbona or Charbonah, a eunuch of Xerxes

Biblical Usage

Harbona is a chamberlain of King Ahasuerus, and his two mentions frame the whole book of Esther. He is named among the seven who served in the king's presence when the king was merry with wine, and it is Harbonah who points to the gallows fifty cubits high that Haman made for Mordecai, and the king says hang him thereon.

Theological Significance

I believe Harbona's second mention is the turn of the story: the servant who fetched wine now points at the gallows, and the gallows built for Mordecai takes Haman instead. I hold that God turns the instruments of the wicked back on their makers.
