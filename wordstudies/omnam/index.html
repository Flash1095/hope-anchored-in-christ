ʼomnâm

verily

Biblical Usage

Omnam means verily, of a truth, truly. Boaz says in Ruth 3:12 that it is true he is a near kinsman, though there is a kinsman nearer, and Hezekiah prays in 2 Kings 19:17: of a truth, Lord, the kings of Assyria have destroyed the nations.

Theological Significance

I believe of a truth is the word of a man who will not pretend, and both Boaz and Hezekiah speak it when the truth is hard.
