Hagrîy

Word
הַגְרִי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Hagrîy
Strongs Number
H1905
Definition
a Hagrite or member of a certain Arabian clan
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Hagarenes or Hagarites; n pr gent; 1) a people dwelling to the east of Palestine, with whom the tribes of Reuben made war in the time of Saul; adj patr; 2) of one of David's servants; Haggeri = "wanderer"; n pr m; 3) father of Mibhar and one of David's mighty warriors
