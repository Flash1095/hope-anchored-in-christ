shikkôwr

intoxicated, as a state or a habit

Biblical Usage

Hannah is mistaken for a drunken woman as she prays silently in the temple, the king of Israel is found drinking himself drunk in the house of Arza, and Job pictures the drunkard who reels and loses his way. The Bible watches the drunk man closely, and it never calls him free.

Theological Significance

The Bible's picture of drunkenness is a man who has lost his footing and does not know it. I hold that the only safe drink is the one taken with a clear head, because a dulled mind cannot hear God when He speaks.
