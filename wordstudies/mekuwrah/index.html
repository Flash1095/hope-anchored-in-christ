mᵉkûwrâh

origin (as if a mine)

Biblical Usage

Mekuwrah is origin, the place a thing comes from, as if from a mine. Ezekiel 16:3 traces Jerusalem's birth to the land of Canaan, her father an Amorite and her mother an Hittite. Ezekiel 21:30 sends the sword's judgment back to the place where Ammon was created, the land of its nativity, and Ezekiel 29:14 promises Egypt a return to Pathros, the land of its habitation, as a base kingdom.

Theological Significance

I believe where we come from shapes us but does not excuse us: Jerusalem's low birth makes God's choosing of her all grace, and Egypt's return to its origin is judgment measured out. My position is that God judges every nation in the place it began, and He alone decides where it ends.
