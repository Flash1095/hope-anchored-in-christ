path

a bit

Biblical Usage

Path is a morsel, a bit, a piece of bread. Abraham asks for a morsel of bread to be fetched for his visitors in Genesis 18:5, and the widow of Zarephath is asked for a morsel of bread in 1 Kings 17:11. A priest's son begs for a morsel of bread in 1 Samuel 2:36, and the Levite's father-in-law urges him to comfort his heart with a morsel of bread in Judges 19:5.

Theological Significance

I believe the morsel is Scripture's measure of hospitality: a little bread offered to a stranger, a widow, or a priest is never little in God's eyes. That is why I hold that the smallest gift given in His name is counted as a feast.
