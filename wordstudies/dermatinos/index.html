dermátinos

from G1192; made of hide:--leathern, of a skin.

Biblical Usage

This word means made of hide or leather, and it appears only to describe John the Baptist's girdle. He wore a garment of camel's hair with a leather belt around his waist, the dress of a prophet in the wilderness.

Theological Significance

I hold that John's leather girdle is worth noticing because it matched his message; he did not come dressed for a palace, and the roughness of his clothing said the same thing as his preaching: repent, and get ready.
