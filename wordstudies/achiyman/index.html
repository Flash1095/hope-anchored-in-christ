ʼĂchîyman

Achiman, the name of an Anakite and of an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Ahiman was one of the three sons of Anak in Hebron, the giants the spies feared — and forty years later Caleb drove him out, with Sheshai and Talmai, by one old man's faith.

Theological Significance

The lesson is not in the name but in the contrast: ten spies saw giants and despaired, and Caleb saw the same giants and drove them out. I hold that faith does not deny the size of the enemy — it denies him the last word.
