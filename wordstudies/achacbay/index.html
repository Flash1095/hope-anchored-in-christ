ʼĂchaçbay

Achasbai, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Achasbai — Ahasbai in the King James text — is named exactly once, as the father of Eliphelet, one of David's mighty men. Beyond this verse the Bible tells no more of him, and his son's story is told on Eliphelet's People profile.

Theological Significance

A single name in a list carries no doctrine of its own. What it quietly shows is that God keeps a record — the faithful are named, and so are the fathers who raised them.
