kaúsōn

from G2741; a glare:--(burning) heat.

Biblical Usage

Kauson means burning heat, the glare of the midday sun. Matthew 20:12 has the labourers complaining they have borne the burden and heat of the day. Luke 12:55 says when ye see the south wind blow, ye say, there will be heat, and it cometh to pass. James 1:11 says the sun is no sooner risen with a burning heat, but it withereth the grass, and the flower thereof falleth.

Theological Significance

I believe the heat of the day is the test of the labourer and the rich man alike: it withers the flower and shows what the work was worth. I hold that God counts the burden borne in the heat, not the hour hired.
