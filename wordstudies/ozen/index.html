ʼôzen

Word
אֹזֶן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼôzen
Strongs Number
H241
Definition
broadness. i.e. (concrete) the ear (from its form in man)
Part Of Speech
noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
1) ear, as part of the body; 2) ear, as organ of hearing; 3) (subjective) to uncover the ear to reveal; the receiver of divine revelation
