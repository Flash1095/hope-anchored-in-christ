ʼôzen

broadness. i.e. (concrete) the ear (from its form in man)

Biblical Usage

Hearing is the way faith comes. David confessed that there is none like the Lord, according to all that we have heard with our ears, and God told Samuel He would do a thing in Israel at which both the ears of everyone that heard it would tingle.

Theological Significance

I hold that faith comes in through the ear: Samuel's ears would tingle at what God was about to do, and David had heard with his ears that there is no God beside the Lord.
