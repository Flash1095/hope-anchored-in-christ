Chârîyph

Word
חָרִיף
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Chârîyph
Strongs Number
H2756
Definition
Chariph, the name of two Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Hariph = "a plucking off"; 1) head of a family of exiles returning with Zerubbabel; 2) a head of the people who sealed the covenant with Nehemiah
