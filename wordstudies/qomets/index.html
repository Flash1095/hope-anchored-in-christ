qômets

a grasp, i.e. handful

Biblical Usage

In the plenteous years the earth brings forth by handfuls, and the priest takes his handful of the flour and the oil and the frankincense and burns the memorial of it upon the altar. The word is the grasp itself, a handful.

Theological Significance

I believe the handful the priest burns is the memorial of the whole offering, and God accepts the part for the whole.
