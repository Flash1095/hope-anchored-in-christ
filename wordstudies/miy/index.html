mîy

who? (occasionally, by a peculiar idiom, of things); also (indefinitely) whoever; often used in oblique construction with prefix or suffix

Biblical Usage

The word is 'who?', the question that opens the Bible's great conversations. Who told thee that thou wast naked, God asks in the garden; who am I, Moses asks at the burning bush; and who is this that darkeneth counsel, God asks Job out of the whirlwind.

Theological Significance

I believe the question is the doorway: every honest 'who?' leads back to God, who knows the answer before we ask.
