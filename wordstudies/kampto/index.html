kámptō

apparently a primary verb; to bend:--bow.

Biblical Usage

Kampto is to bend — to bow the knee. God tells Elijah He has reserved seven thousand men who have not bowed the knee to Baal; the prophet says every knee shall bow to Him; Paul says, for this cause I bow my knees unto the Father; and at the name of Jesus every knee should bow.

Theological Significance

I believe every knee bows somewhere — to Baal, to Caesar, to self — and the only knee that will not be forced is the one bowed willingly now. I bow mine to the Father, because one day every knee will bow to the Son.
