ʼArtachshashtâʼ

Word
אַרְתַּחְשַׁשְׁתָּא
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼArtachshashtâʼ
Strongs Number
H783
Definition
Artachshasta (or Artaxerxes), a title (rather than name) of several Persian kings
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Artaxerxes = "I will make the spoiled to boil: I will stir myself (in) winter"; 1) son and successor of Xerxes as emperor of Persia, 465-424 BC
