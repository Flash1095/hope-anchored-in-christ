akatápaustos

from G1 (as a negative particle) and a derivative of G2664; unrefraining:--that cannot cease.

Biblical Usage

Once, in Peter's fierce portrait of false teachers: they have eyes full of adultery, and that cannot cease from sin. The word is unrefraining — an appetite that never rests.

Theological Significance

One verse about eyes that cannot cease carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is Peter's warning, that unrepentant sin is not a stumble but a slavery.
