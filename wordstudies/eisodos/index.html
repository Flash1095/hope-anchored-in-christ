eísodos

from G1519 and G3598; an entrance (literally or figuratively):--coming, enter(-ing) in (to).

Biblical Usage

An entrance is a coming in, and the New Testament uses it for the gospel's arrival and for ours. Paul speaks of John's preaching before Christ's coming, the Thessalonians tell how the gospel's entrance among them turned them from idols, and Hebrews says we have boldness to enter into the holiest by the blood of Jesus. Peter promises an entrance shall be ministered abundantly into the everlasting kingdom for those who make their calling sure.

Theological Significance

I believe Christ opened the entrance, and the gospel opens it in every place it arrives, because the same boldness that enters the holiest also enters a new town. I want my life to be an open door, not a shut one.
