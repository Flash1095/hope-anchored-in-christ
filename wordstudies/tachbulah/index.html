tachbulâh

Word
תַּחְבֻּלָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
tachbulâh
Strongs Number
H8458
Definition
(only in plural) properly, steerage (as a management of ropes), i.e. (figuratively) guidance or (by implication) a plan
Part Of Speech
noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
1) direction, counsel, guidance, good advice, (wise) counsel; 1a) direction, guidance; 1b) counsel; 1b1) good or wise counsel; 1b2) of the wicked
