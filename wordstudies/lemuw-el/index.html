Lᵉmûwʼêl

Lemuel or Lemoel, a symbolic name of Solomon

Biblical Usage

Lᵉmûwʼêl names Lemuel or Lemoel, a symbolic name of Solomon, and its appearances in Scripture are few but pointed. In Proverbs 31:1, "The words of king Lemuel"; and in Proverbs 31:4, "nor for princes strong drink:".

Theological Significance

I believe Lemuel's mother's teaching is the Bible's plain wisdom for kings — wine is not for kings, and strength is not for princes who drink. I hold that the words of a mother, taught to her son, are still shaping kingdoms.
