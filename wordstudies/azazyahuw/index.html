ʻĂzazyâhûw

Azazjah, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Azaziah is the name of three men — a Levite musician who played the harp before the ark, a captain of Ephraim in the days of the kings, and a Levite overseer of the offerings in Hezekiah's temple. One name, three servants, all of them set to work in God's house or God's army.

Theological Significance

I believe God uses the same name in different generations for the same kind of work, and Azaziah's three bearers show me that musicians, captains and overseers all serve the same Lord.
