ʻĂzazyâhûw

Word
עֲזַזְיָהוּ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻĂzazyâhûw
Strongs Number
H5812
Definition
Azazjah, the name of three Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Azaziah = "Jehovah is mighty"; 1) a Levite musician in the reign of David who played the harp in the procession which brought the ark from Obed-edom to Jerusalem; 2) a Levite in the reign of king Hezekiah of Judah in charge of the tithes; 3) father of Hoshea, prince of the tribe of Ephraim when David took the census
