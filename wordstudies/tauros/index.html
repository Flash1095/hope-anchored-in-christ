taûros

apparently a primary word (compare H8450, "steer"); a bullock:--bull, ox.

Biblical Usage

The word is a bullock, an ox. The king's dinner is ready, his oxen and fatlings killed, and the guests are bidden; and the writer to the Hebrews says the blood of bulls and of goats cannot take away sins, though the ashes of an heifer sprinkled the unclean.

Theological Significance

I believe the bullock's blood could only point forward, and the feast it prepared was for those who would come.
