Rᵉʼûwbênîy

a Reubenite or descendant of Reuben

Biblical Usage

The Reubenites are the descendants of Reuben, the tribe that took its inheritance east of the Jordan and promised to fight beside their brothers until Canaan was won. Their men of war crossed over armed before the LORD.

Theological Significance

This tells me that the Reubenites show what a promise is worth: they held land already given, yet crossed the river to fight for land not yet theirs.
