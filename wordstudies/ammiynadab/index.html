ʻAmmîynâdâb

Amminadab, the name of four Israelites

Biblical Usage

Four men share the name, and the first is the one the story follows. Amminadab is the father of Elisheba, Aaron's wife, and the father of Nahshon, the prince of Judah who leads the march; His name runs through the genealogies of Judah, through Ruth and through the kings, father to son down to David; and the other Amminadabs are counted in the lines of Levi and Judah, a Levite chief and a son of Kohath.

Theological Significance

The line of Judah runs through this name: the prince who leads the march is the son of Amminadab, and the promise runs through his house.
