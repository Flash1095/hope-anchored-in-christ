âra

a form of G686, denoting an interrogation to which a negative answer is presumed:--therefore.

Biblical Usage

This particle asks a question that expects no for an answer. Jesus asks whether the Son of man will find faith on the earth when He comes, and Philip asks the Ethiopian whether he understands what he is reading. Both questions hang in the air, waiting for the hearer to answer.

Theological Significance

A question that expects no answer can still change a life, if the hearer refuses to give it.
