ʻUzzîyʼêl

Uzziel, the name of six Israelites

Biblical Usage

The sons of Kohath were Amram, and Izhar, and Hebron. Uzziel, and when Nadab and Abihu died, Moses called Mishael and Elzaphan, the sons of Uzziel the uncle of Aaron, to carry their brethren out of the camp.

Theological Significance

I believe Uzziel's line shows how close holiness sits to grief: his sons carried their own cousins out after the fire, and the family that served the sanctuary also buried its dead from it.
