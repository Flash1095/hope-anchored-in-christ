Pᵉʻôwr

Peor, a mountain East of Jordan; Baal Peor, (for a deity worshipped there

Biblical Usage

Peor is a mountain east of Jordan, and the name of Baal-peor, the god worshipped there. Balak brings Balaam to the top of Peor in Numbers 23:28 to curse Israel, and the iniquity of Peor stands in Joshua 22:17 as the sin Israel has not yet been cleansed from. Numbers 25:18 and 31:16 name the matter of Peor, when the people join themselves to Baal-peor and the plague follows.

Theological Significance

I believe Peor is the mountain where blessing refused to turn to cursing, and the valley below it is where the people fell anyway. That is why I hold that a mountain of blessing does not save a people who walk down into idolatry.
