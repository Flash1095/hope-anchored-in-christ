Kasday

a Chaldaean or inhabitant of Chaldaea; by implication, a Magian or professional astrologer

Biblical Usage

Chaldean = "clod breakers"; 1) the inhabitants of Chaldea, living on the lower Euphrates and Tigris; 2) those persons considered the wisest in the land (by extension)

Details

Language: Hebrew
Transliteration: Kasday
Strongs Number: H3779
