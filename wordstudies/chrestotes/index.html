chrēstótēs

from G5543; usefulness, i.e. morally, excellence (in character or demeanor):--gentleness, good(-ness), kindness.

Biblical Usage

Gentleness is listed among the fruit of the Spirit, and Paul names it among the marks of a true minister. Romans says it is the kindness of God that leads to repentance, and Ephesians says God will show the exceeding riches of His grace in His kindness toward us. The word is both God's character and the Christian's calling.

Theological Significance

I believe gentleness is strength under control, and it is one of the clearest proofs that the Spirit is at work. A harsh church cannot show a gentle God, and a gentle church preaches without a word. My position is that kindness is not a garnish on the gospel — it is part of the gospel itself.
