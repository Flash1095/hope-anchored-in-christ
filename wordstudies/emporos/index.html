émporos

from G1722 and the base of G4198; a (wholesale) tradesman:--merchant.

Biblical Usage

The merchant is the wholesale trader. In the kingdom parable of Matthew 13:45 he is seeking goodly pearls, and when he finds one of great price he sells all he has for it. In Revelation the merchants of the earth grow rich from Babylon's delicacies in Revelation 18:3, then weep and mourn when no one buys their merchandise any more in Revelation 18:11, standing afar off for the fear of her torment in Revelation 18:15.

Theological Significance

I believe every one of us is a merchant of something — the question is what we are buying. The pearl merchant sold everything for one pearl, and the merchants of Babylon lost everything when their market fell. I hold that the only trade that never fails is selling all for the kingdom.
