Mahêr Shâlâl Châsh Baz

Word
מַהֵר שָׁלָל חָשׁ בַּז
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Mahêr Shâlâl Châsh Baz
Strongs Number
H4122
Definition
Maher-Shalal-Chash-Baz; the symbolical name of the son of Isaiah
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Mahershalalhashbaz = Maher-shalal-hash-baz "swift is booty, speedy is prey"; 1) symbolic name given by Isaiah by the Lord's direction to Isaiah's son; prophetic indication that Damascus and Samaria were soon to be plundered by the king of Assyria
