Mahêr Shâlâl Châsh Baz

Maher-Shalal-Chash-Baz; the symbolical name of the son of Isaiah

Biblical Usage

Maher-shalal-hash-baz was the symbolic name of Isaiah's son, written first on a great roll in Isaiah 8:1 and then given to the child in Isaiah 8:3, when the prophetess bare a son and the LORD said, call his name Maher-shalal-hash-baz. The name was written on a great roll before the child was born, and it meant Assyria was already on its way.

Theological Significance

I believe the boy's name was a sermon in itself: haste to the spoil, speed to the prey, and Assyria coming soon.
