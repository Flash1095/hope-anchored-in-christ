shâlâl

booty

Biblical Usage

Spoil is the plunder of war, the goods stripped from a fallen enemy. Gideon asked for the earrings of the prey after Midian fell, the Amalekites spread abroad eating and drinking over the great spoil they had taken from Judah, and Isaiah says Assyria was sent to take the spoil and to tread down the people of God's wrath. God gives the spoil of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar as wages for his army.

Theological Significance

I believe spoil is the wage of war, and God decides who collects it. He gave Israel the spoil of their enemies, and He gave Egypt's spoil to Babylon, because the plunder belongs to whoever He sends. That is why I do not trust in riches taken by the sword, for the same God can take them again.
