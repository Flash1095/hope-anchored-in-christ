Çôphereth

Sophereth, a temple servant

Biblical Usage

Sophereth is a servant of Solomon, named twice in the lists of the returning exiles. Her children return with Zerubbabel to rebuild the temple, and her name is kept among the servants. Nothing more is told of her, and the return is her memorial.

Theological Significance

I believe Sophereth matters because the servants of Solomon's house came home, and God counts the families who rebuild.
