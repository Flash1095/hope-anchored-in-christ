netsach

Word
נֶצַח
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
netsach
Strongs Number
H5331
Definition
properly, a goal, i.e. the bright object at a distance travelled towards; hence (figuratively), splendor, or (subjectively) truthfulness, or (objectively) confidence; but usually (adverbially), continually (i.e. to the most distant point of view)
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) eminence, perpetuity, strength, victory, enduring, everlastingness; 1a) eminence; 1b) enduring of life; 1c) endurance in time, perpetual, continual, unto the end; 1d) everlastingness, ever
