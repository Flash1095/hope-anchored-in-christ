Bithrôwn

Bithron, a place East of the Jordan

Biblical Usage

Bithron means division or ravine, and it is named once, as the ground Abner's men crossed. In 2 Samuel 2:29 Abner and his men walked all that night through the plain, and passed over Jordan, and went through all Bithron, and came to Mahanaim. A district in the Jordan valley, crossed in the dark after a battle.

Theological Significance

I believe Bithron's one verse is a picture of retreat after defeat: a long night's walk through divided ground.
