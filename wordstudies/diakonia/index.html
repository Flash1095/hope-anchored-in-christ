diakonía

from G1249; attendance (as a servant, etc.); figuratively (eleemosynary) aid, (official) service (especially of the Christian teacher, or technically of the diaconate):--(ad-)minister(-ing, -tration, -try), office, relief, service(-ing).

Biblical Usage

Ministry is service with a task attached to it, and the New Testament gives it many names: the ministry of the word, the ministry of reconciliation, the perfecting of the saints, and the gift of ministering itself. One word covers them all because one heart drives them all.

Theological Significance

I believe ministry is never a title before it is a towel. The apostles gave themselves to prayer and to the ministry of the word, and the same word names the collection for the poor, because serving God and serving His people are one work.
