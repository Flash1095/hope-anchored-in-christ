pârûwr

a skillet (as flat or deep)

Biblical Usage

Paruwr is a pot, a skillet, a cooking vessel. The priest's servant thrusts the fleshhook into the pan in 1 Samuel 2:14, and the people bake the manna in pans in Numbers 11:8. Gideon puts the broth in a pot in Judges 6:19 when he brings his offering to the angel under the oak.

Theological Significance

I hold that the pot on the fire is where daily bread and sacrifice meet, and God provides for His people in the same vessel they offer back to Him.
