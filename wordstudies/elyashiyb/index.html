ʼElyâshîyb

Word
אֶלְיָשִׁיב
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼElyâshîyb
Strongs Number
H475
Definition
Eljashib, the name of six Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Eliashib = "God restores"; 1) a priest in David's reign; 2) a descendant of David; 3) a high priest in Nehemiah's time; 4) a temple singer with foreign wife; 5) one of the line of Zattu; 6) one of the line of Bani
