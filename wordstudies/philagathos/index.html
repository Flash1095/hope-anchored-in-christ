philágathos

from G5384 and G18; fond to good, i.e. a promoter of virtue:--love of good men.

Biblical Usage

Philagathos means a lover of good men, a promoter of virtue, and it appears once, in Paul's list for an elder. A bishop must be a lover of hospitality, a lover of good men, sober, just, holy, temperate.

Theological Significance

I believe loving good men is an elder's qualification because a shepherd shapes his flock by what he loves: love good men, and the church learns what good looks like.
