Iskariṓtēs

of Hebrew origin (probably H377 and H7149); inhabitant of Kerioth; Iscariotes (i.e. Keriothite), an epithet of Judas the traitor:--Iscariot.

Biblical Usage

Iscariot is the name that follows Judas wherever he goes, the man from Kerioth. In Matthew 10:4 he is named last among the twelve, Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him, in John 13:26 Jesus dips the sop and gives it to Judas Iscariot, and in Luke 22:3 Satan enters into Judas surnamed Iscariot, and he goes to the chief priests.

Theological Significance

I hold that the name is a warning written into the list of the twelve: he sat at the table, he took the sop, and he still went out to betray him.
