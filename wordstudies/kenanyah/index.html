Kᵉnanyâh

Kenanjah, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Kenanyah, Chenaniah, is a Levite chief over the song and over the outward business of Israel: 1 Chronicles 15:22 says Chenaniah, chief of the Levites, was for song: he instructed about the song, because he was skilful. 1 Chronicles 15:27 says he was the master of the song with the singers when David brought up the ark. 1 Chronicles 26:29 says Chenaniah and his sons were for the outward business over Israel, for officers and judges.

Theological Significance

I believe Chenaniah is the Levite who did both: he led the song when the ark came up, and he judged the outward business when the singing was done.
