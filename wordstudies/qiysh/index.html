Qîysh

Kish, the name of five Israelites

Biblical Usage

Kish the son of Abiel is a Benjamite, a mighty man of power, and the father of Saul. Saul and Jonathan are buried in the sepulchre of Kish his father, and another Kish stands in the line of Jeiel. Five men share the name, and the first of them is the king's father.

Theological Significance

I believe Kish is remembered because his son became king, and the sepulchre of Kish held Saul's bones when the land was healed.
