miktâm

an engraving, i.e. (techn.) a poem

Biblical Usage

Miktam is the heading set over six psalms — Psalms 16, 56, 57, 58, 59 and 60 — and it appears nowhere else in Scripture, only in those titles. The psalms under it run from quiet trust to cries for deliverance, and the word itself is a poem or an engraving, a thing cut deep so it lasts.

Theological Significance

I believe the heading is part of the gift: these are psalms carved like inscriptions, meant to be remembered word for word when trouble comes.
