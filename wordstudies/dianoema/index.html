dianóēma

from a compound of G1223 and G3539; something thought through, i.e. a sentiment:--thought.

Biblical Usage

A thought is the reasoning of the heart, and the word stands once, where Jesus knows the thoughts of the Pharisees and answers them before they speak. Their thought is that He casts out devils by Beelzebub, and His answer exposes the thought for what it is.

Theological Significance

I hold that Jesus knows my thoughts before I speak them; that is a frightening truth and a comforting one, because the same Lord who sees the worst of my thinking is the one who came to cleanse it.
