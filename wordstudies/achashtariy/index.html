ʼăchashtârîy

Word
אֲחַשְׁתָּרִי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼăchashtârîy
Strongs Number
H326
Definition
an achastarite (i.e. courier); the designation (rather than name) of an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Haahashtari = "I will diligently observe the searching"; 1) a descendant of Ashur of Judah
