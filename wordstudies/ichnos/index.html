íchnos

from ἰκνέομαι (to arrive; compare G2240); a track (figuratively):--step.

Biblical Usage

A track is the mark a foot leaves behind, and the New Testament tells believers to walk in the tracks left by the faithful. In Romans 4:12 Abraham's children walk in the steps of his faith, in 2 Corinthians 12:18 Paul asks whether he and Titus walked in the same steps, and in 1 Peter 2:21 Christ left us an example that we should follow his steps.

Theological Significance

I believe the tracks matter more than the talk: faith leaves footprints. The ones I follow are Abraham's, Paul's, and Christ's own.
