ʼêzôwb

hyssop

Biblical Usage

Hyssop is a small, common plant, but it does holy work in Scripture. At the first Passover the Israelites dipped hyssop in blood and struck the doorposts in Exodus 12:22, and the same plant appears in the cleansing of the leper in Leviticus 14:4 and in the water of purification in Numbers 19:18. Solomon could speak of trees from the cedar in Lebanon down to the hyssop that springs out of the wall in 1 Kings 4:33.

Theological Significance

I believe God delights to use the lowliest things for the greatest work. Hyssop is no cedar, yet it carried the blood that saved the firstborn and the water that cleansed the unclean. That tells me no one is too small for God to use.
