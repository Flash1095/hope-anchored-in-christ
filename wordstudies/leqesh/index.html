leqesh

the after crop

Biblical Usage

leqesh means the after crop. In Amos 7:1, "he formed grasshoppers in the beginning of the shooting up of the latter growth".

Theological Significance

I believe the latter growth is God's timing in a vision — the grasshoppers formed just as the king's mowings were done, and Amos saw judgment coming.
