bouleutḗs

from G1011; an adviser, i.e. (specially) a councillor or member of the Jewish Sanhedrin:--counsellor.

Biblical Usage

A counsellor is a member of the council, the Sanhedrin — and the word appears twice, both times for Joseph of Arimathea. In Mark 15:43 Joseph of Arimathea, an honourable counsellor, which also waited for the kingdom of God, came and went in boldly unto Pilate and craved the body of Jesus. In Luke 23:50 the same is said again: Joseph, a counsellor, a good man, and a just.

Theological Significance

I believe Joseph's boldness is the model for a man in power: he waited for the kingdom, and when the hour came he went in boldly and asked for the body.
