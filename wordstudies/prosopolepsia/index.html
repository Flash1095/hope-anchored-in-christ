prosōpolēpsía

from G4381; partiality, i.e. favoritism:--respect of persons.

Biblical Usage

Prosopolepsia means respect of persons, partiality, favouring one face over another. Paul says there is no respect of persons with God, masters are to do the same things to their servants, forbearing threatening, knowing their Master is in heaven and there is no respect of persons with Him, and that he that doeth wrong shall receive for the wrong he hath done. James says, have not the faith of our Lord Jesus Christ with respect of persons.

Theological Significance

I believe God's impartiality is the ground of all justice: He does not see the rich man's face or the poor man's face, He sees the deed. I hold that a church that honours faces has borrowed the world's scales, and God will not use them.
