Shᵉlômôh

Shelomah, David's successor

Biblical Usage

Solomon is David's successor, the king who asked for wisdom and built the temple. The genealogies name him among David's sons in Jerusalem, the queen of Sheba brings him gold and spices no one had seen before, and the Song names him as the owner of the vineyard. Two hundred and sixty-three times his name is written, more than almost any other king.

Theological Significance

I believe Solomon is both promise and warning. God gave him wisdom and wealth beyond measure, and his heart still turned, and I hold that no gift is a substitute for keeping the commandments.
