Phoînix

probably the same as G5404; Phœnix, a place in Crete:--Phenice.

Biblical Usage

Phoenix is a haven of Crete, looking toward the south west and north west, and it appears once, in Paul's voyage to Rome. The ship's company, the more part, advise to depart from the Fair Havens and try to reach Phenice to winter there, though Paul warns them not to sail.

Theological Significance

I hold that Phoenix is the haven that was never reached: the majority chose the comfortable harbour over the apostle's warning, and the storm took them instead.
