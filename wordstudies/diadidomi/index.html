diadídōmi

from G1223 and G1325; to give throughout a crowd, i.e. deal out; also to deliver over (as to a successor):--(make) distribute(-ion), divide, give.

Biblical Usage

To distribute is to give out through a crowd until everyone has a share. The apostles lay the money at their feet and distribution is made to every man according to need, Jesus takes the loaves and distributes them to the five thousand, and the rich young ruler is told to sell all and give to the poor.

Theological Significance

I believe distribution is the gospel made practical. When the church shares what it has, no one goes without, and the loaves in Jesus' hands are the picture of a God who gives until the baskets overflow.
