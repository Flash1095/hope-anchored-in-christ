mᵉzûwzâh

a door-post (as prominent)

Biblical Usage

Write the words on the posts of your house and on your gates, Moses says twice, and at the Passover the blood was struck on the two side posts and the upper door post of the houses where they ate the lamb. The doorpost is where the word of God and the blood of the lamb both belong.

Theological Significance

I believe the doorpost is the most ordinary holy place in Israel: the words on the posts of the house, the blood on the posts of the door, and every going out and coming in under God's word. The doorpost is where the word of God meets daily life, and that is where it belongs.
