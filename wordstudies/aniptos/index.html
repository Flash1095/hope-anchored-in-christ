ániptos

from G1 (as a negative particle) and a presumed derivative of G3538; without ablution:--unwashen.

Biblical Usage

The Pharisees eat with unwashen hands, and Jesus says it is not what goes into the mouth that defiles a man. The word means unwashed in the ceremonial sense, hands not cleansed by the ritual washing.

Theological Significance

A word for unwashed hands carries no doctrine of its own; its point is Jesus', that defilement comes from the heart, not from the hands.
