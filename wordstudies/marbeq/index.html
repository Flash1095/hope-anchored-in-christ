marbêq

a stall (for cattle)

Biblical Usage

marbêq is a stall for cattle, the place where the fatted calf is kept. In 1 Samuel 28:24 the woman of Endor has a fat calf in the house and kills it for Saul, and in Malachi 4:2 those who fear God's name shall go forth and grow up as calves of the stall when the Sun of righteousness arises. The word is the stall that fattens and the stall that frees.

Theological Significance

I believe marbêq reminds me that God feeds His own from the stall, and I hold that the calves of the stall grow up under His care.
