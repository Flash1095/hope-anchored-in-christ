aínesis

from G134; a praising (the act), i.e. (specially) a thank(-offering):--praise.

Biblical Usage

Once, in Hebrews 13:15: let us offer the sacrifice of praise to God continually, that is, the fruit of our lips giving thanks to his name. The word names the act itself — praise as an offering, laid on the altar like a sacrifice, and offered continually, not only when we feel like it.

Theological Significance

One verse names praise as a sacrifice, and the word itself carries little beyond that picture; the weight is in Hebrews' command to keep offering it, with our lips, without stopping.
