Sᵉbâm

Sebam or Sibmah, a place in Moab

Biblical Usage

Sebam, or Sibmah, is a place in Moab, and it is remembered for its vine. Isaiah says the vine of Sibmah is broken down, its branches gone over the sea; Jeremiah weeps for it — O vine of Sibmah, I will weep for thee — and Joshua's record sets Sibmah among the towns of Reuben's inheritance.

Theological Significance

I believe the vine of Sibmah shows how a fruitful place can fall: the spoiler is fallen upon the summer fruits and upon the vintage, and the prophet weeps over what was lost.
