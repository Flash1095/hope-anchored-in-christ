qôwl

a voice or sound

Biblical Usage

Adam hears the voice of the LORD walking in the garden, and Israel hears the voice of words out of the fire at Sinai and sees no form. Elijah hears him after the fire, in a still small voice, and the psalmist hears the voice of the LORD upon the waters. The word is the voice or the sound — and in Scripture it is most often his.

Theological Significance

I believe the voice of the LORD is the sound that matters most, and it speaks in a garden, in fire, in a whisper, and on the waters. I hold that hearing his voice is the whole of the life of faith, because the sheep know the shepherd's voice and follow.
