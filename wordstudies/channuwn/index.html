channûwn

gracious

Biblical Usage

Channuwn is gracious, and it is the word God uses about Himself more than any other. The LORD passed by Moses and proclaimed Himself merciful and gracious, longsuffering and abundant in goodness. Nehemiah's prayer calls Him a God ready to pardon, gracious and merciful. Joel tells the people to rend their hearts and turn, for He is gracious and merciful, slow to anger, and Jonah quotes the same words back to God, angry that grace would reach Nineveh.

Theological Significance

I believe this word is the hinge of the whole Bible: God is gracious before we are anything else, and every prophet quotes His own description of Himself. Jonah resented it, Joel preached it, and Nehemiah prayed it. I hold that God's grace is not His mood but His name, and He will not deny it.
