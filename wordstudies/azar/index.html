ʼâzar

to belt

Biblical Usage

Azar is to belt, to gird — to put strength around the loins for work or war. Hannah sings that the Lord girds the weak with strength, David sings that God girded him with strength for battle, Isaiah says the Lord girds himself with strength, and God tells Job to gird up his loins like a man and answer.

Theological Significance

I hold that God girds his people the way a soldier is girded before battle — not for show, but for the work ahead. When he tells Job to gird up his loins, he is telling me to stand ready too.
