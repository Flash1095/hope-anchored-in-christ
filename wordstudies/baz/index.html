baz

plunder

Biblical Usage

Baz is spoil, plunder, the prey taken in war. God says the little ones Israel feared would be a prey will go in and possess the land, Ezekiel says His flock became a prey because there was no shepherd, and Jeremiah says all who devour Israel will be devoured and all who spoil her will be spoiled.

Theological Significance

I believe spoil is the Bible's measure of who is really in charge, because the people who take the prey are the ones God has given the victory to, and the people who spoil Israel are the ones God will spoil in turn. The children Israel feared for are the ones God carries in, and I hold that no one who trusts Him ends as prey.
