yârâh

Word
יָרָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
yârâh
Strongs Number
H3384
Definition
properly, to flow as water (i.e. to rain); transitively, to lay or throw (especially an arrow, i.e. to shoot); figuratively, to point out (as if by aiming the finger), to teach
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to throw, shoot, cast, pour; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to throw, cast; 1a2) to cast, lay, set; 1a3) to shoot arrows; 1a4) to throw water, rain; 1b) (Niphal) to be shot; 1c) (Hiphil); 1c1) to throw, cast; 1c2) to shoot; 1c3) to point out, show; 1c4) to direct, teach, instruct; 1c5) to throw water, rain
