ʼêmᵉtânîy

well-loined (i.e. burly) or mighty

Biblical Usage

A rare Aramaic word for something dreadful and mighty — Daniel uses it once, for the fourth beast in his night vision in Daniel 7:7, a creature dreadful and terrible and strong exceedingly, with great iron teeth.

Theological Significance

I believe Daniel's beast reminds me that earthly power can look terrifying, but it is still only a beast in a vision God gave, and God alone decides where its story ends.
