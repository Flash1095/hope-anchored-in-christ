lachats

distress

Biblical Usage

Oppression is the noun for the crushing weight of a hard master, and it is the word of the exodus. In Exodus 3:9 God says, "I have also seen the oppression wherewith the Egyptians oppress them," in Deuteronomy 26:7 Israel remembers that the Lord "looked on our affliction, and our labour, and our oppression," and in 2 Kings 13:4 He saw "the oppression of Israel" under Syria and hearkened.

Theological Significance

What convinces me is that oppression is never unseen: God saw it in Egypt, He saw it under Syria, and He hears the cry of the oppressed still. No oppression is unseen, and no cry is unheard.
