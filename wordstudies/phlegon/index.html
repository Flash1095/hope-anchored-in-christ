Phlégōn

active participle of the base of G5395; blazing; Phlegon, a Christian:--Phlegon.

Biblical Usage

Phlegon is a Christian at Rome whom Paul greets, and his name means blazing. Paul sends his greeting to Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermas, Patrobas, Hermes, and the brethren which are with them.

Theological Significance

I hold that Phlegon's blazing name is a small picture of the Roman church: ordinary believers, named one by one, burning bright in a dark city.
