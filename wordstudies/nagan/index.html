nâgan

Word
נָגַן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
nâgan
Strongs Number
H5059
Definition
properly, to thrum, i.e. beat a tune with the fingers; expectation. to play on a stringed instrument; hence (generally), to make music
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to play or strike strings, play a stringed instrument; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) player (participle); 1b) (Piel); 1b1) to play; 1b1a) player, minstrel (participle)
