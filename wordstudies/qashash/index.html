qâshash

to forage for straw, stubble or wood; figuratively, to assemble

Biblical Usage

To forage is the word for gathering straw and sticks: let them go and gather straw for themselves, they found a man that gathered sticks upon the sabbath day, and the widow woman was there gathering of sticks when Elijah called to her.

Theological Significance

I read the gathering as the work of the poor and the burdened, and the sticks as the fuel of a last meal.
