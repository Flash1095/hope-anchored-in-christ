Yᵉdîyʻăʼêl

Jediael, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Jediael was a son of Benjamin, named among the three sons of the tribe, and the father of Bilhan, whose sons were Jeush and Benjamin and Ehud. He stands at the head of a house that spread through the tribe.

Theological Significance

I believe God keeps the houses of Benjamin as carefully as the houses of Judah, and Jediael's sons are named because they belong.
