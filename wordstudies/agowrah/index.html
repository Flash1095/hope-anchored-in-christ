ʼăgôwrâh

properly, something gathered, i.e. perhaps a grain or berry; used only of a small (silver) coin

Biblical Usage

Agowrah is a small silver coin — a piece of silver, the price of a loaf — and it appears only once, in the prophecy against the house of Eli: 'every one that is left in thine house shall come and crouch to him for a piece of silver and a morsel of bread, and shall say, Put me, I pray thee, into one of the priests' offices, that I may eat a piece of bread.' The word holds the whole humiliation: the high priest's house, begging for a coin and a crust.

Theological Significance

A piece of silver and a morsel of bread is all the humbled priest asks for, and this word names the scrap — the fall of Eli's house is the sermon.
