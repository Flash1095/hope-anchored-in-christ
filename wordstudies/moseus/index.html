Mōseús

or Μωσῆς, or Μωϋσῆς of Hebrew origin; (H4872); Moseus, Moses, or Mouses (i.e. Mosheh), the Hebrew lawgiver:--Moses.

Biblical Usage

Moses is the lawgiver, and the Gospels never let him go: he appears with Elijah on the mount of transfiguration, the law came by Moses and grace and truth by Jesus Christ, and by faith Moses refused to be called the son of Pharaoh's daughter. In the Greek text these forms stand under the lemma Μωϋσῆς, spelled differently from this study's headword.

Theological Significance

I believe Moses matters because the law came by him and the story does not end with him: he stands on the mountain to see the One the law was pointing to all along.
