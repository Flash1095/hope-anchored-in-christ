Akeldamá

Word
Ἀκελδαμά
Language
Greek
Transliteration
Akeldamá
Strongs Number
G184
Definition
of Chaldee origin (meaning field of blood; corresponding to H2506 and H1818); Akeldama, a place near Jerusalem:--Aceldama.
