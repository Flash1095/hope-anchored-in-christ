ʼattûwn

probably a fire-place, i.e. furnace

Biblical Usage

The burning fiery furnace is the heart of Daniel's third chapter, and this word names it. Whoever would not worship the golden image would be cast into it, Nebuchadnezzar heated it seven times hotter in his rage, and then he came near to its mouth and called the three men out by name.

Theological Significance

I believe the furnace is the Bible's plainest picture of what faith costs and what God does with it, because the fire that was meant to destroy them only burned their bonds. The fourth man in the fire was the difference, and I hold that God does not always keep His people out of the furnace, but He always goes in with them.
