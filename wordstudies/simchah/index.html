simchâh

blithesomeness or glee, (religious or festival)

Biblical Usage

Simchah is joy, gladness of heart, and it runs through the whole Bible like a feast. The Lord warns that joylessness in abundance is itself a curse, the people ate and drank before the Lord with great gladness when Solomon was made king, the returned exiles kept the feast of booths with very great gladness, the psalm says in God's presence is fulness of joy, and Isaiah promises everlasting joy for shame.

Theological Significance

I believe joy is not a mood but a covenant fruit. It grows where God is present and His people are gathered, and I hold that the joy of the Lord is the strength He gives, not the happiness I manufacture.
