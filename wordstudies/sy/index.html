sý

the personal pronoun of the second person singular; thou:-- thou. See also G4571, G4671, G4675; and for the plural G5209, G5210, G5213, G5216.

Biblical Usage

Thou is the word spoken straight to one person. I am Jesus whom thou persecutest, the Lord says to Saul; but thou, O man of God, flee these things, Paul writes to Timothy; and thou therefore, my son, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus.

Theological Significance

I believe the thou is the gospel's directness: the Lord speaks to one man at a time, and His word names him.
