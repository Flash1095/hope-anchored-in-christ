himátion

neuter of a presumed derivative of ennumi (to put on); a dress (inner or outer):--apparel, cloke, clothes, garment, raiment, robe, vesture.

Biblical Usage

When Scripture says himátion, it means a garment, clothing. The word appears 58 times, and in Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John it carries that same straightforward meaning. Nothing hidden sits behind it; the sense is the one you would expect from the word itself.

Theological Significance

I hold that himátion matters because God clothes His people — from skins in Eden to robes in Revelation. My position is simple: I take God at His word here, and I build on that.
