châmad

to delight in

Biblical Usage

Chamad means to delight in, to desire, to covet. Every tree in the garden was pleasant to the sight, the commandment says you shall not covet your neighbour's house or wife or field, and Achan said, when I saw among the spoils a goodly Babylonish garment, then I coveted them and took them.

Theological Significance

I believe desire is the hinge of the whole story: the trees were pleasant and the command was plain, and Achan's eye went from seeing to coveting to taking in one verse. What you delight in, you will in the end obey.
