kâlâh

a completion; adverb, completely; also destruction

Biblical Usage

Kalah is completion — the end, the finishing, and by extension utter destruction. Daniel speaks of the consummation poured out upon the desolate; Isaiah says the Lord will make a consumption, even determined, in the midst of the land; and Jeremiah promises that God will make a full end of the nations but will not make a full end of Israel.

Theological Significance

I believe this word holds judgment and mercy in one breath. God finishes what He determines — the desolation is complete, and so is the deliverance — and that is why I trust His endings as much as His beginnings.
