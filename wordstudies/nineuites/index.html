Nineuḯtēs

from G3535; a Ninevite, i.e. inhabitant of Nineveh:--of Nineve, Ninevite.

Biblical Usage

The men of Nineveh will rise in judgment and condemn this generation, Jesus says, because they repented at the preaching of Jonah — and a greater than Jonah is here. Jonah was a sign to the Ninevites, and the Son of Man is a sign to this generation.

Theological Significance

I hold that the Ninevites' repentance is the standard Jesus sets: a pagan city repented at one prophet's word, and this generation has a greater than Jonah.
