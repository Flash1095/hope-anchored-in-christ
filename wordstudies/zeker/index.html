zêker

a memento, abstractly recollection (rarely if ever); by implication, commemoration

Biblical Usage

Zeker is remembrance, the memorial that keeps a name alive after the person is gone. God tells Moses that His name is His memorial to all generations, and He tells him to write the defeat of Amalek in a book for a memorial. The Psalmist sings that God's name endures forever and His memorial lasts through every generation, and Proverbs says the memory of the just is blessed while the name of the wicked rots.

Theological Significance

I believe God never forgets, and He gives us memorials so that we do not forget either. A name remembered is a life honoured, and a name forgotten is a warning. My position is that the best memorial a person can leave is a life God is glad to remember, because His memory is the one that lasts.
