Knídos

probably of foreign origin; Cnidus, a place in Asia Minor:--Cnidus.

Biblical Usage

Cnidus is a place in Asia Minor, a port on Paul's voyage to Rome. Acts 27:7 says the ship sailed slowly many days, and scarce was come over against Cnidus, the wind not suffering them to sail on.

Theological Significance

I believe Cnidus is the place where the voyage nearly stopped: the wind would not suffer them, and the long road to Rome began with slow sailing.
