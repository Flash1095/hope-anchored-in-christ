leukós

from λύκη ("light"); white:--white.

Biblical Usage

leukós means white — plain enough to miss, and too important to pass over. In Matthew 17:2, "and his raiment was white as the light."; and in Mark 9:3, "so as no fuller on earth can white them.". In Revelation 7:9, "clothed with white robes"; and in Revelation 3:5, "the same shall be clothed in white raiment".

Theological Significance

I believe white is the colour of heaven — Christ's raiment shone white as the light, and the multitude stands before the throne in white robes. I hold that the whiteness is not mine but His: robes washed in the blood of the Lamb. My position is that I will be clothed in white, not because I kept myself clean, but because He washed me.
