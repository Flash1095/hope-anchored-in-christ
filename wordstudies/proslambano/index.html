proslambánō

from G4314 and G2983; to take to oneself, i.e. use (food), lead (aside), admit (to friendship or hospitality):--receive, take (unto).

Biblical Usage

Proslambano means to take to oneself, to receive, to welcome. Paul says to receive him that is weak in the faith, and to receive one another as Christ also received us. Peter took Jesus aside and began to rebuke Him for speaking of the cross.

Theological Significance

I believe receiving is the test of grace: Christ received me, so I receive my brother, but the moment I take Christ aside to correct Him, I have stopped receiving and started rebuking.
