ʻayir

properly, a young ass (as just broken to a load); hence an ass-colt

Biblical Usage

The ayir is a young ass, a colt just broken to the load. Judah's sons rode on white asses in the days of the judges, Jacob blessed his sons over the foal of an ass, Isaiah pictures the wild asses snuffing up the wind in the desert, and Zechariah promises the King coming to Jerusalem, lowly, and riding upon an ass, and upon a colt the foal of an ass.

Theological Significance

I believe the colt matters because the King chose it: he came lowly, riding on an ass's colt. That is the kind of King I can come to without fear.
