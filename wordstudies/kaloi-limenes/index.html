Kaloì Liménes

plural of G2570 and G3040; Good Harbors, i.e. Fairhaven, a bay of Crete:--fair havens.

Biblical Usage

Fair Havens is the bay on the south coast of Crete where Paul's ship put in on the voyage to Rome. The name promised more than the harbour gave: the crew wanted to winter somewhere better, sailed on, and met the storm.

Theological Significance

I believe Fair Havens warns me that a fair name is not a safe harbour. The sailors left a good anchorage for a better one and lost the ship — and I have learned to be thankful for the shelter God gives rather than always sailing on.
