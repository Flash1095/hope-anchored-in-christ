qôheleth

Word
קֹהֶלֶת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
qôheleth
Strongs Number
H6953
Definition
a (female) assembler (i.e. lecturer); abstractly, preaching (used as a 'nom de plume', Koheleth)
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) collector (of sentences), preacher, public speaker, speaker in an assembly, Qoheleth
