Pâdôwn

Padon, one of the Nethinim.

Biblical Usage

Padon is the head of a family of Nethinim, the temple servants, whose descendants return from exile with Zerubbabel. His name means ransom, and his family appears in the roll of the returning exiles.

Theological Significance

I believe the temple servants are not forgotten servants: a family called ransom comes home to serve in the house of the God who ransomed them.
