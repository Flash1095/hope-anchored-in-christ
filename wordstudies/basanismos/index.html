basanismós

from G928; torture:--torment.

Biblical Usage

Basanismos is torment, and in Revelation it is the torment of the locusts that sting men for five months. It is the smoke of torment that rises forever from those who worship the beast, and the torment Babylon's merchants watch from afar when her judgment comes in one hour.

Theological Significance

I believe torment in Revelation is never meaningless pain, because every instance of it is judgment with a name and a reason. The locusts torment those without God's seal, the beast's worshippers are tormented for their worship, and Babylon is tormented for her sins, and I hold that the God who torments in judgment is the same God who warned us plainly so we could flee it.
