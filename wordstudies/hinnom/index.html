Hinnôm

Word
הִנֹּם
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Hinnôm
Strongs Number
H2011
Definition
Hinnom, apparently a Jebusite
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Hinnom = "lamentation"; 1) a valley (deep and narrow ravine) with steep, rocky sides located southwest of Jerusalem, separating Mount Zion to the north from the 'hill of evil counsel' and the sloping rocky plateau of the 'plain of Rephaim' to the south
