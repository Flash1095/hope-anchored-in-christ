râbaʻ

Word
רָבַע
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
râbaʻ
Strongs Number
H7250
Definition
to squat or lie out flat, i.e. (specifically) in copulation
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to lie stretched out, lie down; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to lie down; 1a1a) for repose; 1a1b) for copulation (of woman with beast); 1b) (Hiphil) to cause to lay down (of cattle breeding)
