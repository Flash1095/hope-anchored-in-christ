enochléō

from G1722 and G3791; to crowd in, i.e. (figuratively) to annoy:--trouble.

Biblical Usage

To trouble, to crowd in and annoy. A root of bitterness springing up can trouble many and defile them in Hebrews 12:15, and those vexed with unclean spirits came to Jesus and were healed in Luke 6:18.

Theological Significance

I believe bitterness is a root that troubles a whole church, and the cure is the same as for the vexed — to come to Jesus and be healed.
