châdar

properly, to inclose (as a room), i.e. (by analogy,) to beset (as in a siege)

Biblical Usage

Chadar means to enclose, to beset, to shut in — and it appears once, in the sword-song of Ezekiel, where the sword of the slain enters into their privy chambers.

Theological Significance

I believe a sword that finds the inner chamber is the picture of judgment that cannot be shut out.
