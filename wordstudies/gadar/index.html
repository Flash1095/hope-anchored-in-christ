gâdar

to wall in or around

Biblical Usage

Gadar means to wall in, to build the wall that guards a place. Masons were hired to repair the house of the LORD in 2 Kings 22:6, and God promises to raise up the tabernacle of David and close up its breaches in Amos 9:11. The false prophets did not go up into the gaps or make up the hedge for Israel in Ezekiel 13:5, but the faithful shall be called the repairer of the breach in Isaiah 58:12.

Theological Significance

I believe God is the great repairer of breaches, and He calls His people to the same work. The wall that guards the vineyard and the hedge that stands in the battle are worth building, and I hold that mending what is broken is holy work.
