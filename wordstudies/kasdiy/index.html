Kasdîy

a Kasdite, or descendant of Kesed; by implication, a Chaldaean (as if so descended); also an astrologer (as if proverbial of that people

Biblical Usage

The Chaldeans were the people of the land where Abram's family lived, and centuries later they were the army that broke into Jerusalem and carried Judah away. Their name runs through the story from Ur to Babylon, and Daniel was carried off to live and serve among them. The fuller story of that people and their kings belongs with the people they touched.

Theological Significance

I believe the Chaldeans show how God works through the rise and fall of whole peoples, and I hold that their place in the story is best read beside the kings and prophets who lived under their power.
