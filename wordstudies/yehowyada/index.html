Yᵉhôwyâdâʻ

Jehojada, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Jehoiada is the name of three Israelites, and the priest who bore it crowned Joash king. In 2 Samuel 8:18, Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over both the Cherethites and the Pelethites. The fuller story is told on the Jehoiada, High Priest profile in Biblical People.

Theological Significance

I hold that Jehoiada shows me God keeps His promises across generations, even through flawed families.
