kabod

Word
כָּבוֹד
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
kabod
Strongs Number
H3519
Definition
Glory — literally weightiness and substance; God's radiant majesty and honour, and the dignity He shares with His image-bearers.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Exodus 40:34-35; Psalm 24:7-10; Isaiah 6:3; Ezekiel 10:18
Biblical Usage
God's kabod filled the tabernacle so Moses could not enter, and Isaiah heard the whole earth is full of it. To give God glory is to recognise the weight of who He truly is.
Theological Significance
Kabod anchors our worth and our worship: the King of glory is not far away — He fills His creation and invites His people to reflect His glory.
