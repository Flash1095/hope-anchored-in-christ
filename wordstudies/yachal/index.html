yâchal

to wait; by implication, to be patient, hope

Biblical Usage

Noah stayed yet other seven days, and sent forth the dove, and Samuel told Saul, tarry seven days at Gilgal until I come. Joab said, I may not tarry thus with thee, and the king of Israel said, behold, this evil is of the Lord, what should I wait for the Lord any longer?

Theological Significance

I hold that waiting is the hardest work faith does: Noah waited for the dove, Saul was told to wait and did not, Joab would not wait, and the king in Samaria despaired of waiting altogether. I believe the waiting itself is the test — not whether God will come, but whether His people will still be watching when He does.
