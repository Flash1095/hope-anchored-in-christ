Chelbâh

Chelbah, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Helbah is named once, among the towns Asher fails to drive out, beside Accho and Zidon and Ahlab and Achzib and Aphik and Rehob. The town stays in Canaanite hands, and the failure is recorded.

Theological Significance

I believe God records the towns Israel did not take as honestly as the towns they did. Helbah stands as a witness to an unfinished conquest.
