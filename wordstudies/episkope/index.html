episkopḗ

from G1980; inspection (for relief); by implication, superintendence; specially, the Christian "episcopate":--the office of a "bishop", bishoprick, visitation.

Biblical Usage

Episkope is visitation, oversight, inspection — the office of a bishop. Jerusalem knew not the time of her visitation; let his habitation be desolate, and his bishoprick let another take; if a man desire the office of a bishop, he desireth a good work; and the Gentiles shall glorify God in the day of visitation.

Theological Significance

God visits to redeem and to reckon, and the bishop's office is to watch in the meantime, as one who will give account. I believe visitation is both mercy and judgment, and Jerusalem's failure to know the time of hers is the warning.
