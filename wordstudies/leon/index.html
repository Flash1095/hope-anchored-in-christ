léōn

a primary word; a "lion":--lion.

Biblical Usage

léōn means lion, and it is one of those words that quietly holds a whole theme together. In 1 Peter 5:8, "as a roaring lion"; and in Revelation 5:5, "the Lion of the tribe of Juda". In Hebrews 11:33, "stopped the mouths of lions"; and in 2 Timothy 4:17, "and [that] all the Gentiles might hear: and I was delivered out of the mouth of the lion.".

Theological Significance

I believe the lion is both the enemy and the King — the devil walks about as a roaring lion, and the Lion of Judah has prevailed to open the book. I hold that faith stops the mouths of lions, and the Lord delivers from the lion's mouth. My position is that I will be sober and watchful, because the real Lion is on my side.
