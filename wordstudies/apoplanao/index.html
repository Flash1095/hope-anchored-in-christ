apoplanáō

from G575 and G4105; to lead astray (figuratively); passively, to stray (from truth):--err, seduce.

Biblical Usage

Two uses, and in both someone is led astray. The love of money is the root of all evil, and some coveting after it have erred from the faith in 1 Timothy 6:10; and false Christs and false prophets shall rise and show signs and wonders to seduce, if it were possible, even the elect in Mark 13:22.

Theological Significance

The word is a wandering off the road — and Paul and Jesus agree on where the road bends: money and miracles.
