Iōnâs

of Hebrew origin (H3124); Jonas (i.e. Jonah), the name of two Israelites:--Jonas.

Biblical Usage

Jonas is the Greek form of Jonah, and Jesus makes him the sign of his own death and rising. In Matthew 12:39 he says an evil generation seeks a sign, and no sign shall be given but the sign of the prophet Jonas, in Matthew 12:40 as Jonas was three days and three nights in the whale's belly, so shall the Son of man be in the heart of the earth, and in Luke 11:30 as Jonas was a sign unto the Ninevites, so shall the Son of man be to this generation.

Theological Significance

I believe the sign of Jonah is the gospel in miniature: three days in the dark, and then the prophet walks out alive to preach repentance.
