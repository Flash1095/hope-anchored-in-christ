apokalýptō

from G575 and G2572; to take off the cover, i.e. disclose:--reveal.

Biblical Usage

To take off the cover, to disclose. Jesus thanks the Father because He has hid these things from the wise and prudent and revealed them unto babes, in Matthew 11:25 and again in Luke 10:21 — the same words in both Gospels. God has revealed them unto us by His Spirit in 1 Corinthians 2:10, for the Spirit searcheth the deep things of God, and in Romans 1:17 the righteousness of God is revealed from faith to faith.

Theological Significance

I believe the uncovering is God's work from first to last. The wise miss it because they look for it with the wrong eyes; babes receive it because they only have to be told. The Spirit does the revealing, faith does the receiving, and my part is to stay small enough to be taught.
