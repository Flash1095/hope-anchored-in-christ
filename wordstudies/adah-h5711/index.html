Adah, the name of two women

Biblical Usage

The name Adah belongs to two women: Adah, wife of Lamech and mother of Jabal and Jubal, and Adah, daughter of Elon the Hittite, one of Esau's wives.

Theological Significance

The first Adah's family invented music and the wandering life; the second Adah's marriage grieved Isaac and Rebekah. I hold both as a sober picture: the line of Cain grew clever and godless at once, and Esau's choices cost his parents dear.
