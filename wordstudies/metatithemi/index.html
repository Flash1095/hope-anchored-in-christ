metatíthēmi

from G3326 and G5087; to transfer, i.e. (literally) transport, (by implication) exchange, (reflexively) change sides, or (figuratively) pervert:--carry over, change, remove, translate, turn.

Biblical Usage

Enoch was translated by faith, certain men turned the grace of God into lasciviousness, and the Galatians were being removed from Him who called them into the grace of Christ. The word is transfer, and it swings both ways: God transfers His saints, and men transfer themselves away from grace.

Theological Significance

I hold that Galatians 1:6 is the verse that should frighten us most here: the Galatians were not dragged away, they were removing themselves, and the grace they left was the grace that had called them. The grace they left was the grace that had called them, and there is no other gospel to turn to.
