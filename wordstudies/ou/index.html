Word
οὐ
Language
Greek
Transliteration
ou
Strongs Number
G3756
Definition
, also (before a vowel) οὐκ, and (before an aspirate) οὐχ a primary word; the absolute negative (compare G3361) adverb; no or not:--+ long, nay, neither, never, no (X man), none, (can-)not, + nothing, + special, un(-worthy), when, + without, + yet but. See also G3364, G3372.
