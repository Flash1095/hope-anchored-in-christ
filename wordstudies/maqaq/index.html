mâqaq

Word
מָקַק
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
mâqaq
Strongs Number
H4743
Definition
to melt; figuratively, to flow, dwindle, vanish
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to decay, pine away, rot, fester; 1a) (Niphal); 1a1) to fester (of wounds); 1a2) to rot, rot away; 1a3) to moulder away; 1a4) to pine away; 1b) (Hiphil) to cause to rot
