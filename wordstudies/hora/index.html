hṓra

apparently a primary word; an "hour" (literally or figuratively):--day, hour, instant, season, X short, (even-)tide, (high) time.

Biblical Usage

I take hṓra to mean an hour, a time, a season, nothing more and nothing less. It occurs 99 times, and Matthew and Mark show it doing ordinary work in ordinary sentences. The Bible uses plain words plainly, and this is one of them.

Theological Significance

I hold that hṓra matters because God’s hour is never early and never late. My position is that Scripture does not hide its meaning from honest readers.
