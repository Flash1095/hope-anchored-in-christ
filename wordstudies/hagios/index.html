hagios

Word
ἅγιος
Language
Greek
Transliteration
hagios
Strongs Number
G40
Definition
Holy, sacred and set apart — describing God, the Holy Spirit, and believers called saints, consecrated to God.
Part Of Speech
adjective
Key Passages
1 Peter 1:15-16; Revelation 4:8; Ephesians 1:4; Romans 1:7
Biblical Usage
Hagios is the New Testament's everyday word for Christians: 'saints' — holy ones — not because of their record but because of their position in Christ. Heaven's cry remains 'Holy, holy, holy.'
Theological Significance
Hagios gives ordinary believers an honoured identity: set apart, beloved and being made holy. Who you are in Christ anchors how you live each day.
