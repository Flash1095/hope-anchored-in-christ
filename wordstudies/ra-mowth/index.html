Râʼmôwth

Ramoth, the name of two places in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Ramoth is the name of two places in Palestine — a city of refuge east of the Jordan and a town in Issachar. Moses set apart Ramoth in Gilead for the manslayer, and Joshua gave it to the Levites; the Chronicler counts Ramoth among the towns of Issachar given to the Gershonites.

Theological Significance

The lesson I take is that a city of refuge is mercy built into the map: a man who killed by accident could run to Ramoth and live.
