korçêʼ

a throne

Biblical Usage

Korse is the Aramaic word for a throne. Daniel 5:20 says Belshazzar's father was deposed from his kingly throne when his heart was lifted up and his mind hardened in pride, and Daniel 7:9 sees the thrones cast down, and the Ancient of days sitting, whose throne was like the fiery flame.

Theological Significance

I believe Daniel's two thrones are the whole book of Daniel: the king deposed from his, and the Ancient of days sitting on His.
