diapheúgō

from G1223 and G5343; to flee through, i.e. escape:--escape.

Biblical Usage

To escape is to get away, and the word stands once, where the soldiers plan to kill the prisoners lest any of them escape when the ship breaks apart. The centurion stops them because he wants to save Paul.

Theological Significance

I hold that the soldiers' plan and the centurion's mercy are the whole voyage in miniature; men meant to kill to keep order, and God used one man's regard for Paul to save every soul aboard.
