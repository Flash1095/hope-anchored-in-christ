Baʻal Mᵉʻôwn

Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Baʻal Mᵉʻôwn
Strongs Number
H1186
Definition
Baal-Meon, a place East of the Jordan
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Baal-meon = "lord of the habitation"; 1) a town in Reuben, mentioned in connection with Nebo, and in the time of Ezekiel, Moabite
