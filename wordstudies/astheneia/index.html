asthéneia

from G772; feebleness (of mind or body); by implication, malady; morally, frailty:--disease, infirmity, sickness, weakness.

Biblical Usage

Weakness in the New Testament covers sickness of the body, frailty of the mind, and the limits of our praying. Lazarus's sickness was for the glory of God, Paul's thorn taught him that grace is enough, our High Priest is touched with the feeling of our infirmities, and the Spirit helps our weaknesses when we do not know what to pray.

Theological Significance

I believe weakness is not the opposite of faith, it is the place where faith learns to lean. Christ was never weak toward us, and I hold that His strength shows itself most clearly in the people who know they have none of their own.
