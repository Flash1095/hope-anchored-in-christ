Rhemphán

by incorrect transliteration for a word of Hebrew origin (H3594); Remphan (i.e. Kijun), an Egyptian idol:--Remphan.

Biblical Usage

Yea, ye took up the tabernacle of Moloch, and the star of your god Remphan, figures which ye made to worship them, and I will carry you away beyond Babylon. The Greek text behind the King James Version reads Remphan here, while the text I have checked reads Raiphan, the same star-god under another spelling of the name.

Theological Significance

What I take from this is that Stephen named Remphan because idolatry always has a name, and the name is the charge.
