qirʼâh

an encountering, accidental, friendly or hostile (also adverbially, opposite)

Biblical Usage

David sends men to meet the shamed ambassadors at Jericho and tell them to tarry till their beards are grown, and he reminds Shimei that he came down to meet him at Jordan. The word is the encountering itself, friendly or hostile, as the meeting turns.

Theological Significance

I believe meeting is never neutral in Scripture: men come to meet a king, and the meeting decides things.
