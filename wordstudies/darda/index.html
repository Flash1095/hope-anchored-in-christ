Word

Hebrew

Definition
Darda, an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Darda = "pearl of knowledge"; 1) a son of Mahol, one of four men of great fame for their wisdom, but surpassed by Solomon
