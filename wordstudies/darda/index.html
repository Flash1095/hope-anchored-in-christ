Dardaʻ

Darda, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Darda is named once, among the wise men of Solomon's day: Ethan, Heman, Chalcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol, all surpassed by the king's wisdom. His name is a benchmark, not a biography.

Theological Significance

I believe the verse teaches the same humility as his brother's name: human wisdom, however great, is only the measure of the wisdom God gives.
