bᵉthûwlîym

(collectively and abstractly) virginity; by implication and concretely, the tokens of it

Biblical Usage

bethulim is used for virginity, and it appears 9 times across the Bible. The priest took a wife in her virginity, and Jephthah's daughter bewailed her virginity on the mountains.

Theological Significance

I hold that God cares about a young woman's name and honour, and the Law's care for her tells me how much He values her. That is why I keep coming back to it: bethulim is not a word to rush past.
