mêyshâr

Word
מֵישָׁר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
mêyshâr
Strongs Number
H4339
Definition
evenness, i.e. (figuratively) prosperity or concord; also straightness, i.e. (figuratively) rectitude (only in plural with singular sense; often adverbially)
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) evenness, uprightness, straightness, equity; 1a) evenness, level, smoothness; 1b) uprightness, equity; 1c) rightly (as adv)
