dýnamai

of uncertain affinity; to be able or possible:--be able, can (do, + -not), could, may, might, be possible, be of power.

Biblical Usage

To be able, to have power, the word for what man can and cannot do. The natural man cannot receive the things of the Spirit, and whoever is born of God cannot sin because His seed remains in him. God is faithful and will not let us be tempted beyond what we are able, and even when we believe not, He abides faithful because He cannot deny Himself.

Theological Significance

I believe my cannots are the measure of my need, and God's cannots are the measure of His faithfulness. I cannot receive the Spirit's things on my own, but He can keep me in the temptation, and He cannot deny Himself, and that is the ground I stand on.
