Mârâh

Marah, a place in the Desert

Biblical Usage

Mârâh is Marah, the place of bitter water on the road from the Red Sea. In Exodus 15:23 Israel comes to Marah and cannot drink the waters, for they are bitter, and the place is called Marah. In Numbers 33:8 and Numbers 33:9 Israel pitches in Marah and then removes to Elim, where there are twelve fountains and seventy palm trees., and the name is one stop between bitterness and plenty.

Theological Significance

I believe Mârâh shows me that God turns bitter water sweet for His people, and I hold that the places of my bitterness are where He proves Himself.
