prépō

apparently a primary verb; to tower up (be conspicuous), i.e. (by implication) to be suitable or proper (third person singular present indicative, often used impersonally, it is fit or right):--become, comely.

Biblical Usage

Prepo means to be fitting, suitable, becoming. Paul tells Titus to speak the things which become sound doctrine, and tells Timothy that good works become women professing godliness.

Theological Significance

I hold that doctrine and life must match, and what becomes sound teaching is a life that looks like it.
