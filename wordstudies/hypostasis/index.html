hypóstasis

from a compound of G5259 and G2476; a setting under (support), i.e. (figuratively) concretely, essence, or abstractly, assurance (objectively or subjectively):--confidence, confident, person, substance.

Biblical Usage

hypóstasis is a plain word for substance, assurance, confidence. In 2 Corinthians 11:17, the text says "in this confidence of boasting." In Hebrews 1:3, the text says "and the express image of his person."

Theological Significance

Reading hypóstasis this way, I believe faith is not a feeling but a holding-on — trusting what God has said when nothing else makes sense. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
