machçôwr

deficiency; hence, impoverishment

Biblical Usage

Machsor is want, lack, the empty place where something is missing. Deuteronomy 15:8 commands an open hand to lend a brother sufficient for his need, and Psalms 34:9 promises there is no want to them that fear the LORD. Proverbs 6:11 warns the sluggard that poverty comes as one that travelleth and want as an armed man, while Proverbs 28:27 says he that giveth unto the poor shall not lack.

Theological Significance

I believe want is both a warning and a promise in this word: laziness and a shut hand bring it on, and generosity and the fear of the LORD keep it away. My position is that the open hand in Deuteronomy 15:8 is the cure Proverbs keeps pointing to.
