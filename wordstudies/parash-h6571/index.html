pârâsh

Word
פָּרָשׁ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
pârâsh
Strongs Number
H6571
Definition
a steed (as stretched out to a vehicle, not single nor for mounting); also (by implication) a driver (in a chariot), i.e. (collectively) cavalry
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) horse, steed, warhorse; 2) horseman
