gal

something rolled, i.e. a heap of stone or dung (plural ruins), by analogy, a spring of water (plural waves)

Biblical Usage

Gal is a heap, a wave, a pile of stones — anything rolled together. Jacob's men gathered stones and made a heap at Galeed in Genesis 31:46, and Absalom's body was covered with a very great heap of stones in 2 Samuel 18:17. The sea causes its waves to come up against Tyre in Ezekiel 26:3, and the fenced cities of Judah were laid waste into ruinous heaps in 2 Kings 19:25.

Theological Significance

I believe heaps tell the story of a life. Jacob's heap was a witness of peace, and Absalom's heap was a monument of rebellion. I hold that what we pile up in life — stones of witness or stones of ruin — is the choice we make every day.
