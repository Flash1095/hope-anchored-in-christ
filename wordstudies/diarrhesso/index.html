diarrhḗssō

from G1223 and G4486; to tear asunder:--break, rend.

Biblical Usage

This word means to tear asunder, to rend, and it stands in the forms of a stronger verb for tearing. The net breaks under the catch of fish, and the demoniac tears apart his chains, and the tearing shows a strength no man could master until Christ came.

Theological Significance

I believe the tearing in these verses shows what sin and glory both do; sin tears nets and chains and garments, but the tearing of the veil is what the rending of the high priest's clothes could never do — open the way to God.
