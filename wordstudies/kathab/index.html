kâthab

to grave, by implication, to write (describe, inscribe, prescribe, subscribe)

Biblical Usage

To write is to grave and inscribe, and God wrote the tables with His own finger, and commanded the words to be written on the doorposts of the house, and promised to write His law in their hearts, and told the prophet to write the vision plainly on tables so that he who reads it may run. The word runs from stone to doorpost to heart, and each writing goes deeper than the last.

Theological Significance

I believe God's writing goes deeper than stone, because the law on the heart outlasts the law on the tablet. I hold that the vision written plainly is still running its course, and I will write down what God shows me so I do not forget it.
