Asýnkritos

from G1 (as a negative particle) and a derivative of G4793; incomparable; Asyncritus, a Christian:--Asyncritos.

Biblical Usage

Asyncritus is named once, in Paul's long list of greetings to the believers in Rome, alongside Phlegon, Hermas, Patrobas, Hermes, and the brethren with them. Nothing else is told about him.

Theological Significance

I believe a name in Paul's greeting is no small thing, because it means a brother known and loved by the apostle, and known by God besides.
