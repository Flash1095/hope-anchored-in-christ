kósmios

from G2889 (in its primary sense); orderly, i.e. decorous:--of good behaviour, modest.

Biblical Usage

Kosmios means orderly, decorous, of good behaviour. 1 Timothy 2:9 says women should adorn themselves in modest apparel, and 1 Timothy 3:2 says a bishop must be blameless, vigilant, sober, of good behaviour, given to hospitality, apt to teach.

Theological Significance

I believe good behaviour is the bishop's first credential after blamelessness: orderly in life, because the church is ordered by God.
