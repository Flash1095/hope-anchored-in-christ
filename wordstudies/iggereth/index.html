ʼiggereth

an epistle

Biblical Usage

A letter carries the king's word where the king cannot go, and the Bible shows it doing both good and harm. In 2 Chronicles 30:6 the posts go out with letters from Hezekiah calling Israel to the passover, in Esther 9:26 letters go out to establish Purim, in Nehemiah 6:5 Sanballat sends an open letter to frighten Nehemiah, and in Nehemiah 2:7 Nehemiah asks the king for letters to the governors beyond the river.

Theological Significance

I believe a letter is a small thing with a long reach: Hezekiah's letters called a nation to worship. Sanballat's letters tried to call a builder off the wall.
