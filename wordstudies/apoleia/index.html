apṓleia

from a presumed derivative of G622; ruin or loss (physical, spiritual or eternal):--damnable(-nation), destruction, die, perdition, X perish, pernicious ways, waste.

Biblical Usage

Ruin, loss, destruction — the word for the end of the broad way. Broad is the way that leadeth to destruction in Matthew 7:13, and many there be which go in thereat. Judas is the son of perdition in John 17:12, lost while the rest were kept. Paul says of the enemies of the cross that their end is destruction in Philippians 3:19, and Peter says the heavens and earth are reserved unto fire against the day of judgment and perdition of ungodly men in 2 Peter 3:7.

Theological Significance

I believe this word is the Bible's plainest warning label. Destruction is not an accident on the broad way; it is the destination the way was built to reach. The narrow gate is strait because it is the only road that does not end in ruin — and the choice of roads is made now, while both gates still stand open.
