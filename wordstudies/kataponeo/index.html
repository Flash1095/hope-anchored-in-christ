kataponéō

from G2596 and a derivative of G4192; to labor down, i.e. wear with toil (figuratively, harass):--oppress, vex.

Biblical Usage

Moses saw one of his brethren suffer wrong and defended him, and Lot was vexed with the filthy conversation of the wicked.

Theological Significance

I believe a righteous soul feels the weight of the wickedness around it, and that vexing is a sign the heart is still alive.
