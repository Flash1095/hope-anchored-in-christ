prophēteúō

from G4396; to foretell events, divine, speak under inspiration, exercise the prophetic office:--prophesy.

Biblical Usage

Propheteuo is to prophesy, to speak under inspiration, and it runs from Joel to Revelation. Peter quotes Joel that sons and daughters shall prophesy, Paul urges the church to desire it above other gifts, and John hears the two witnesses prophesying in sackcloth. Jesus warns that many will say they prophesied in his name and hear him answer that he never knew them.

Theological Significance

I believe prophesying is a gift to be desired, not a title to be claimed, and the test of it is whether it builds the church up. The false prophets of the last days will prophesy in his name, so I weigh every word by Scripture.
