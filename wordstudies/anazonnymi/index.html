anazṓnnymi

from G303 and G2224; to gird afresh:--gird up.

Biblical Usage

Peter tells his readers to gird up the loins of their mind, be sober, and hope to the end. The picture comes from a man tucking up his long robe so he can run or work without tripping, applied to the mind getting ready for what is coming.

Theological Significance

One call to gird up the mind carries no system of its own; its force is Peter's, that hope is not daydreaming but a mind made ready to run.
