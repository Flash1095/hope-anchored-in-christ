akatakályptos

from G1 (as a negative particle) and a derivative of a compound of G2596 and G2572; unveiled:--uncovered.

Biblical Usage

Twice, both in Paul's teaching on head coverings in worship: every woman that prayeth or prophesieth with her head uncovered dishonoureth her head, and then the question, Judge in yourselves: is it comely that a woman pray unto God uncovered? The word is unveiled — the head without its covering — and the whole passage turns on what that sign means in the gathered church.

Theological Significance

A word for uncovered carries little weight on its own; its weight is the passage, where worship is ordered and the church's gatherings show God's design rather than hiding it.
