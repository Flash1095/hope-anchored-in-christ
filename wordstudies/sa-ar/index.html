sâʻar

to storm; by implication, to shiver, i.e. fear

Biblical Usage

The word sâʻar means to storm; by implication, to shiver, that is fear. It stands in Daniel 11:40, "…end shall the king of the south push…", and in Deuteronomy 32:17, "…new came newly up, whom your fathers feared…".

Theological Significance

I believe sâʻar is worth knowing, because nothing in Scripture is there by accident.
