qâtsîyn

a magistrate (as deciding) or other leader

Biblical Usage

A leader is the one put forward: Joshua said to the captains, come near, put your feet upon the necks of these kings; the elders said to Jephthah, come, and be our captain, that we may fight with the children of Ammon; and a man shall take hold of his brother, saying, thou hast clothing, be thou our ruler.

Theological Significance

I read the leadership as the call of the moment, whether by victory, by pleading, or by a coat.
