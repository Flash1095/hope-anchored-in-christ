bᵉkôwr

firstborn; hence, chief

Biblical Usage

bekor covers firstborn, and it appears 96 times across the Bible. Canaan's firstborn was Sidon, Israel is called God's firstborn son, and the firstling of the herd belonged to the LORD.

Theological Significance

I hold that the firstborn belongs to God, and that Christ, the firstborn over all creation, took the place the firstborn sons could only point to. That is why I keep coming back to it: bekor is not a word to rush past.
