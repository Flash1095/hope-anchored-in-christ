ekdikéō

from G1558; to vindicate, retaliate, punish:--a (re-)venge.

Biblical Usage

To avenge is to vindicate, to put right what was done wrong, and the Bible gives the job to God, not to us. The widow in the parable cries, Avenge me of mine adversary, and the unjust judge gives in, but Paul says vengeance is God's and He will repay. Revelation says God's judgements are true and righteous because He has avenged the blood of His servants on the great whore. The word belongs to the Judge, and He does not share it.

Theological Significance

I believe leaving vengeance to God is the hardest and freest thing I can do, because my hands want to settle the score and His hands settle it rightly. My position is that I will not take the Judge's seat, because it is not mine.
