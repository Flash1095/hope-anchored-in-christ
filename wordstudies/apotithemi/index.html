apotíthēmi

from G575 and G5087; to put away (literally or figuratively):--cast off, lay apart (aside, down), put away (off).

Biblical Usage

To put away or lay aside is the Bible's picture of leaving the old life behind. Paul says put off the old man with its deceitful lusts, put off anger and malice and filthy language, lay aside every weight and the sin that clings so closely, and lay apart all filthiness and receive the word with meekness.

Theological Significance

I hold that putting off is not a one-time act, it is the daily work of a Christian. The old clothes come off so the new man can be seen, and the race is run lighter without the weights we chose to carry.
