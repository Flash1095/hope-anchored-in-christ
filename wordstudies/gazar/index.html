gâzar

to cut down or off; (figuratively) to destroy, divide, exclude, or decide

Biblical Usage

Gazar means to cut down or off, to divide, and to decree. Solomon said to divide the living child in two in 1 Kings 3:25, and the axe men cut down wood at Jordan in 2 Kings 6:4. Uzziah the leper was cut off from the house of the LORD in 2 Chronicles 26:21, and Ahasuerus remembered Vashti and what was decreed against her in Esther 2:1.

Theological Significance

I believe cutting is the work of kings and of God alike. Solomon cut to find the truth, and God cuts off the leper from His house. I hold that the cut that divides is also the cut that heals, when the hand that holds the knife is just.
