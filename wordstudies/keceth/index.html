keçeth

a cushion or pillow (as covering a seat or bed)

Biblical Usage

Keceth means a cushion or band, the false prophetesses' magic pillows. Ezekiel 13:18 cries woe to the women that sew pillows to all armholes to hunt souls, and Ezekiel 13:20 says God is against those pillows and will tear them from their arms.

Theological Significance

I believe God hates religion that hunts souls, and he tears the pillows away to set the hunted free.
