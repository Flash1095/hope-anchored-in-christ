agrypnéō

ultimately from G1 (as negative particle) and G5258; to be sleepless, i.e. keep awake:--watch.

Biblical Usage

Agrypneo is to watch — to be sleepless, to keep awake. Jesus says, 'Take ye heed, watch and pray'; He tells His disciples to watch and pray always, that they may escape what is coming; Paul says to watch with all perseverance and prayer for all saints; and Hebrews says the leaders watch for your souls, as they that must give account. The word is the night watchman's word: eyes open, lamps lit, no one asleep on duty.

Theological Significance

I believe this word wakes me up in a sleepy world. The Lord is coming, the enemy is prowling, and the saints need prayer — and the watchman who sleeps on duty is no watchman at all. I want to be the one still awake when He comes.
