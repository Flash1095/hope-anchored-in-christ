châbâh

to secrete

Biblical Usage

Chabah is another word for hiding, and it is usually hiding for a purpose. Rahab told the spies to hide in the mountain for three days, Micaiah warned Ahab's friend he would one day hide in an inner chamber, the king of Samaria suspected the Syrians were hiding in the field, and Isaiah told God's people to hide themselves for a little moment until His anger passed.

Theological Significance

I believe there is a hiding that is fear and a hiding that is faith, and the difference is who told you to hide. When God says hide, it is shelter, not shame.
