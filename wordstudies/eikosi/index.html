eíkosi

of uncertain affinity; a score:--twenty.

Biblical Usage

Twenty is a round number, a score, and the Greek text writes it as a form of the word twenty with the final letter sometimes added. A king with ten thousand men considers whether he can meet an enemy coming with twenty thousand, the disciples row about twenty-five furlongs on the sea before they see Jesus walking on the water, and about a hundred and twenty names gather to choose a replacement for Judas. The number itself is plain; the stories around it are not.

Theological Significance

I believe God counts His people exactly, whether twenty thousand enemies or a hundred and twenty disciples, because every number in His story is a real number of real people.
