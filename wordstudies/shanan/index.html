shânan

Word
שָׁנַן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
shânan
Strongs Number
H8150
Definition
to point (transitive or intransitive); intensively, to pierce; figuratively, to inculcate
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to sharpen, whet; 1a) (Qal) to whet, sharpen; 1b) (Piel) to sharpen, teach (incisively); 1c)(Hithpolel) to be pierced
