shânan

to point (transitive or intransitive); intensively, to pierce; figuratively, to inculcate

Biblical Usage

A sharp word cuts like a sword, and Moses commands parents to teach the words diligently unto their children. The LORD whets His glittering sword and takes hold on judgment, the wicked whet their tongues like a sword, and the psalm counts sharp arrows of the mighty among the weapons of the wicked.

Theological Significance

I hold that sharp teaching is a kindness, because a word taught diligently to a child is a sword kept in its sheath. The same sharpness in the mouth of the wicked is a weapon, and the LORD's whetted sword is the answer to it. That is why I teach my children plainly, so their tongues are sharp for truth and not for wounds.
