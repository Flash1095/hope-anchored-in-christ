dᵉhab

gold

Biblical Usage

Gold is the metal of the image and the metal of the temple, and Daniel's book holds both. The head of gold is the glory of Nebuchadnezzar's kingdom, the golden image is the idolatry of the same king, and the vessels of gold are the plunder of the house of God. Ezra weighs the gold for the temple, and the wise men of the east offer it to the child.

Theological Significance

I hold that gold in Daniel's book is always a test: it can be the glory of a kingdom or the god of a king, and the difference is what it is given to. The gold that is given to God is gold that is safe, and the gold that is kept for itself is gold that will rust.
