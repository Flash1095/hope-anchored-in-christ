kisbâh

a young ewe

Biblical Usage

Kisbah is a young ewe, a female lamb of the flock. Leviticus 5:6 brings her as the trespass offering for a sin: a female from the flock, a lamb or a kid of the goats, for a sin offering, and the priest shall make an atonement for him concerning his sin.

Theological Significance

I believe the young ewe is the quiet side of sacrifice: not the great bullock of the feast, but the small female lamb a single sinner could bring, and God accepted it.
