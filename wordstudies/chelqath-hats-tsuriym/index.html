Chelqath hats-Tsurîym

Chelkath Hats-tsurim, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Helkath-hazzurim, the field of sharp swords, is named for a single fight at Gibeon, where Abner's men and Joab's men caught each other by the head and thrust their swords into each other's sides, and fell down together. The place takes its name from the swords.

Theological Significance

I believe the field of sharp swords is a warning written in the ground: the house of Saul and the house of David fought each other to the death, and the place remembers it.
