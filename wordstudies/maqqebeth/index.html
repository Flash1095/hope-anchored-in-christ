maqqebeth

properly, a perforator, i.e. a hammer (as piercing); also (intransitively) a perforation, i.e. a quarry

Biblical Usage

maqqebeth is a hammer, and also the hole it makes, a quarry. In Judges 4:21 Jael takes a hammer in her hand and smites the nail into Sisera's temples, and in Isaiah 51:1 God's people are told to look to the rock from which they are hewn and the hole of the pit from which they are dug. The word is both the blow and the hollow it leaves.

Theological Significance

I believe maqqebeth reminds me that God hews His people out of hard rock, and that the same blow that kills a tyrant can dig a people out of the pit.
