lâmad

properly, to goad, i.e. (by implication) to teach (the rod being an Oriental incentive)

Biblical Usage

1) to learn. teach, exercise in; 1a) (Qal) to learn; 1b) (Piel) to teach; 1c) (Pual) to be taught, be trained
