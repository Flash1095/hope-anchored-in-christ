Tróphimos

from G5160; nutritive; Trophimus, a Christian:--Trophimus.

Biblical Usage

Trophimus was a Christian from Ephesus who travelled with Paul. He is named among Paul's companions sailing from Macedonia, and in Jerusalem the Jews wrongly supposed Paul had brought him into the temple — the charge that helped put Paul in chains. Years later Paul writes that he left Trophimus sick at Miletus.

Theological Significance

I believe Trophimus matters because he shows the cost of travelling with the gospel — misunderstood, accused, and then left sick on the road, yet still named with honour. My position is that the Lord's servants are not forgotten when they are laid aside; Paul names him, and so does Scripture.
