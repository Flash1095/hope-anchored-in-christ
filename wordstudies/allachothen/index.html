allachóthen

from G243; from elsewhere:--some other way.

Biblical Usage

Once, in the parable of the sheepfold: He that entereth not by the door into the sheepfold, but climbeth up some other way, the same is a thief and a robber. The word is the other way in, the climb over the wall instead of the walk through the door.

Theological Significance

One word for some other way carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the warning, that there is one door and every other way in is a thief's way.
