gâʻash

to agitate violently

Biblical Usage

Ga'ash means to shake violently, to tremble and quake. The earth shook and trembled when God was angry in 2 Samuel 22:8, and the foundations of the hills moved and were shaken in Psalms 18:7. The nations shall drink and be moved and be mad because of the sword in Jeremiah 25:16, and even the mighty are troubled at midnight and pass away in Job 34:20.

Theological Significance

I believe the shaking of the earth is God's signature on judgment. Mountains and men alike tremble before Him, and I hold that the only ground that never shakes is the ground of His mercy.
