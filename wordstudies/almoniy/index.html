ʼalmônîy

some one (i.e. so and so, without giving the name of the person or place)

Biblical Usage

So-and-so, without giving the name, and the Bible uses it when the name does not matter or must not be said. Boaz goes up to the gate and says to the nearer kinsman, Ho, such a one, turn aside, sit down here — the man who will not redeem Ruth is not even named. And the king of Syria tells his servants, In such and such a place shall be my camp, and Elisha knows the place before the king gets there.

Theological Significance

A word for so-and-so carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the two scenes, that the man who refused his duty goes nameless and the enemy's secret camp is already known to God.
