ʼimmar

a lamb

Biblical Usage

A lamb is the offering the returning exiles brought to the house of God. In Ezra 6:9 the king commands that lambs be given to the priests day by day, in Ezra 6:17 they offer a hundred bullocks, two hundred rams, and four hundred lambs at the dedication, and in Ezra 7:17 Ezra is told to buy bullocks, rams and lambs with the king's silver.

Theological Significance

I believe the lambs at the dedication say what the whole book is about: the exiles came home to worship. The altar was the first thing they rebuilt.
