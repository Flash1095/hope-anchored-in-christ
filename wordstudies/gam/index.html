gam

properly, assemblage; used only adverbially also, even, yea, though; often repeated as correl. both... and

Biblical Usage

Gam is the little word also, even, yea, though — the word that adds one thing to another. Saul died for his transgression, even against the word of the LORD, and also for asking counsel of a familiar spirit, in 1 Chronicles 10:13. The navy also of Hiram brought gold from Ophir in 1 Kings 10:11; the word is small, but it carries the weight of one more thing.

Theological Significance

I believe the smallest words in Scripture carry the heaviest loads; also and even can turn a list into a judgment, and I hold that God notices every also in our lives.
