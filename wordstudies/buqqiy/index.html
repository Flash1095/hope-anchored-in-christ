Buqqîy

Word
בֻּקִּי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Buqqîy
Strongs Number
H1231
Definition
Bukki, the name of two Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Bukki = "wasting"; 1) son of Abishua and father of Uzzi, fifth from Aaron in the line of the high priests; 2) son of Jogli, prince of the tribe of Dan, one of the ten men chosen to apportion the land of Canaan between the tribes
