ʼĕnâsh

a man

Biblical Usage

Enash is the Aramaic word for man — a human being, or mankind together. The Chaldeans tell Nebuchadnezzar there is not a man on earth who can show the king's matter in Daniel 2:10, and Daniel tells him that wherever the children of men dwell, God has given him rule in Daniel 2:38. In Ezra the men on this side of the river write their letter to Artaxerxes in Ezra 4:11.

Theological Significance

I believe Daniel's 'man' puts us in our place — no man on earth can do what God does, and yet God gives men their kingdoms and their days. I hold that knowing I am only a man is the start of wisdom, because it leaves room for the God who is more.
