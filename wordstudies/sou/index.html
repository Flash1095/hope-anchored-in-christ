soû

genitive case of G4771; of thee, thy:--X home, thee, thine (own), thou, thy.

Biblical Usage

This is the genitive of thou, the of thee and thy that marks what belongs to the person spoken to. Paul is glad to answer before thee, king Agrippa; he has remembrance of thee in his prayers night and day; and the men lying in wait look for a promise from thee.

Theological Significance

I believe the little word carries the weight of a relationship: before thee, remembrance of thee, a promise from thee.
