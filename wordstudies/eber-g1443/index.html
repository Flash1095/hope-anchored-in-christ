Ebér

of Hebrew origin (H5677); Eber, a patriarch:--Eber.

Biblical Usage

Luke names Eber in the genealogy of Jesus, between Serug and Peleg, and the spelling in the Greek text is Eber with a rough breathing, not the form Strong's lists. He is the patriarch whose name the Hebrews carry, and his place in the line keeps the promise moving from Shem toward Abraham.

Theological Significance

I believe every name in that genealogy matters, because God kept His promise through real people in a real line.
