ʻÔwbêd

Obed, the name of five Israelites

Biblical Usage

Obed was the son of Boaz and Ruth, born in Bethlehem, and the father of Jesse and grandfather of David. The women of Bethlehem named him, saying, There is a son born to Naomi, and called his name Obed.

Theological Significance

I believe Obed is the hinge of the whole story of redemption: the son of Ruth and Boaz, the father of Jesse, the grandfather of David, and the great-grandfather of the line that leads to Jesus.
