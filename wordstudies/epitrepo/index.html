epitrépō

from G1909 and the base of G5157; to turn over (transfer), i.e. allow:--give leave (liberty, license), let, permit, suffer.

Biblical Usage

Epitrépō means to allow — to turn a matter over to someone and let them do it. Jesus allows the demons to enter the swine; Paul will not allow a woman to teach or to usurp authority over a man; Agrippa allows Paul to speak for himself. Permission is never casual in Scripture — it is a judgment about what may go ahead.

Theological Significance

I believe epitrépo reminds me that permission is a form of authority: what I allow, I answer for. I hold that the church should give permission slowly and withhold it plainly, the way Paul does.
