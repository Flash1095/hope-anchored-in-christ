diakatelénchomai

middle voice from G1223 and a compound of G2596 and G1651; to prove downright, i.e. confute:--convince.

Biblical Usage

To confute thoroughly is to prove a thing downright, leaving no room for argument, and Apollos does it in public, showing from the Scriptures that Jesus is the Christ. The Jews have no answer because the proof is in the text they already own.

Theological Significance

I believe Apollos is the pattern for answering honest objections; he did not shout the Jews down; he opened the Scriptures and proved his case, and the truth did the convincing.
