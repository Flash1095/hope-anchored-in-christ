kanṓn

from κάνη (a straight reed, i.e. rod); a rule ("canon"), i.e. (figuratively) a standard (of faith and practice); by implication, a boundary, i.e. (figuratively) a sphere (of activity):--line, rule.

Biblical Usage

Kanon is a rule — a straight reed, a measuring rod, and so the measure of a man's work and the rule of his walk. Paul says we will not boast of things without our measure, but according to the measure of the rule which God hath distributed to us; and he blesses all who walk according to this rule, with peace and mercy upon them.

Theological Significance

I believe the rule is God's measure, not mine. Paul would not boast beyond the line God drew for him, and I should not measure my work by another man's rule — only by the one God has given me.
