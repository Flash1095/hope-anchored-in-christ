dektós

from G1209; approved; (figuratively) propitious:-- accepted(-table).

Biblical Usage

Accepted is the word for the time and the offering that God receives: the acceptable year of the Lord, the sacrifice acceptable to God, and the man who is accepted with Him. Paul writes that now is the accepted time, and now is the day of salvation. The word gathers the welcome of God.

Theological Significance

I believe the acceptable time is now, and the acceptable offering is the life laid down in Christ. The accepted time is the time God has given, and the giving is the grace we must not waste.
