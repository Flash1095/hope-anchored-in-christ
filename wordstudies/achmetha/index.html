ʼAchmᵉthâʼ

Achmetha (i.e. Ecbatana), the summer capital of Persia

Biblical Usage

Achmetha — Ecbatana, the summer capital of Persia — is named once, where a roll was found in the palace: Cyrus's decree, whose finding restarted the building of the temple. As a place, Achmetha is covered in the Biblical Places collection rather than in a biography.

Theological Significance

The place itself teaches nothing. But the finding does: a lost decree, found in an archive hundreds of miles away, was the hinge on which the temple was rebuilt.
