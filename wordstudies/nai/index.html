naí

a primary particle of strong affirmation; yes:--even so, surely, truth, verily, yea, yes.

Biblical Usage

Nai is the strong yes, the word of affirmation, and Jesus and Paul both build on it. In Matthew 5:37 Jesus says let your communication be, Yea, yea, and Nay, nay, for whatever is more than these comes of evil, and in 2 Corinthians 1:20 all the promises of God in Him are yea, and in Him Amen, unto the glory of God.

Theological Significance

I believe a plain yes is a Christian duty, because a yes that means yes needs no oath behind it; God's promises are yea in Christ, and my word should be yea in the same way.
