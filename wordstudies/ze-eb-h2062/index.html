Zᵉʼêb

Zeeb, a Midianitish prince

Biblical Usage

Zeeb was one of the two princes of Midian, and his name means wolf. Gideon's men caught him at the winepress of Zeeb and brought his head to Gideon across the Jordan, and the Psalmist later remembers Oreb and Zeeb together as the fate God gives His enemies.

Theological Significance

I believe Zeeb's end shows what happens to those who set themselves against God's people: the wolf becomes the prey. The winepress where he was caught is still called by his name, a reminder that God does not forget.
