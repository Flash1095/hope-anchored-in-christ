minhâg

the driving (of a chariot)

Biblical Usage

The word is the driving of a chariot, and it appears once, on the watchman's lips at Jezreel: the driving is like the driving of Jehu the son of Nimshi, for he driveth furiously.

Theological Significance

I believe the watchman saw what Jehu's pace meant: judgment was coming fast, and it was not slowing down.
