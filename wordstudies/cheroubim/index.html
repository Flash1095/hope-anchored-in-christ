cheroubím

plural of Hebrew origin (H3742); "cherubim" (i.e. cherubs or kerubim):--cherubims.

Biblical Usage

Cherubim is the Greek plural of the Hebrew word for the cherubs, and in the New Testament it stands only once, in Hebrews. There the cherubim of glory shadow the mercy seat above the ark, the place where God met His people, and the writer passes over them because his point is the better covenant that has come. The Greek text spells the word with a different ending than the dictionary form, so the verse is cited as a spelling variant rather than an exact match.

Theological Significance

I hold that the cherubim still point me to the mercy seat, where God meets sinners in mercy rather than in judgment.
