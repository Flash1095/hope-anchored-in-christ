kânâph

an edge or extremity; specifically (of a bird or army) a wing, (of a garment or bedclothing) a flap, (of the earth) a quarter, (of a building) a pinnacle

Biblical Usage

Kanaph is the wing — the edge or extremity of a garment, a bird, or an army. Ruth asks Boaz to spread his skirt over her; the psalm says He shall cover thee with His feathers, and under His wings shalt thou trust; Malachi promises the Sun of righteousness with healing in His wings; and David cuts off the skirt of Saul's robe in the cave.

Theological Significance

I believe the wing is where God's protection and man's appeal meet. Ruth asks for the covering, the psalmist trusts under it, and the Sun rises with healing in His wings — and that is where I hide too.
