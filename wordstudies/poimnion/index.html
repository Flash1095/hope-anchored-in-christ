poímnion

neuter of a presumed derivative of G4167; a flock, i.e. (figuratively) group (of believers):--flock.

Biblical Usage

Poimnion is a little flock, the sheep as a beloved company. Jesus says, Fear not, little flock, for it is your Father's good pleasure to give you the kingdom, in Luke 12:32. Peter tells the elders to feed the flock of God in 1 Peter 5:2, and not to lord it over the flock in 1 Peter 5:3. Paul warns that grievous wolves will enter in among the flock in Acts 20:29.

Theological Significance

I believe little flock is a term of love, not of size — the Father's pleasure is to give the kingdom to the few who trust Him. That is why I hold that the flock is safe, not because it is many, but because it is His.
