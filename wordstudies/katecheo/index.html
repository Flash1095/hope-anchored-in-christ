katēchéō

from G2596 and G2279; to sound down into the ears, i.e. (by implication) to indoctrinate ("catechize") or (genitive case) to apprise of:--inform, instruct, teach.

Biblical Usage

To sound down into the ears is to instruct by word of mouth, and Luke writes so Theophilus may know the certainty of the things he was instructed in, and Apollos was instructed in the way of the Lord, and Paul says let him that is taught in the word share with him that teaches. The word is the passing of truth from mouth to ear, and the church lives on it.

Theological Significance

I believe instruction is how certainty is passed on, because Luke wrote so that we may know, not guess. I hold that the taught should share with the teacher, because the word that feeds me should also feed the one who brought it.
