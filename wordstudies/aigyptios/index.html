Aigýptios

from G125; an Ægyptian or inhabitant of Ægyptus:--Egyptian.

Biblical Usage

Stephen says Moses was learned in all the wisdom of the Egyptians, then tells how Moses killed an Egyptian who was beating a Hebrew — the act that drove him into exile. This study covers only how the word is used; Egypt itself is covered in the Biblical Places collection.

Theological Significance

The word is a label, not a lesson, and I won't force one out of it. What it shows is how Israel's story keeps colliding with Egypt — first as a place of learning, then of oppression, then as a byword for rebellion.
