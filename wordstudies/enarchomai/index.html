enárchomai

from G1722 and G756; to commence on:--rule (by mistake for G757).

Biblical Usage

To begin, to commence. Paul asks the Galatians, having begun in the Spirit, are you now made perfect by the flesh, in Galatians 3:3 — and he is confident that He who began a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ in Philippians 1:6.

Theological Significance

I believe the God who begins a work finishes it, and the danger is not that He will stop but that I will try to finish in the flesh what He started in the Spirit.
