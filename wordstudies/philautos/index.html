phílautos

from G5384 and G846; fond of self, i.e. selfish:--lover of own self.

Biblical Usage

Philautos means a lover of one's own self, selfish, and it appears once, at the head of Paul's list of the last days. Men shall be lovers of their own selves, covetous, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy.

Theological Significance

I believe self-love heads the list because it is the root of the rest: love yourself first, and covetousness and pride and blasphemy follow like children after their father.
