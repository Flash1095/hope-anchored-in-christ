ábyssos

from G1 (as a negative particle) and a variation of G1037; depthless, i.e. (specially) (infernal) "abyss":--deep, (bottomless) pit.

Biblical Usage

The abyss is the bottomless deep — the pit without a floor. The demons beg Jesus not to send them 'into the deep'; Paul asks who will descend 'into the deep' to bring Christ up; a star falls and opens 'the bottomless pit'; and an angel comes down with the key of the bottomless pit to bind Satan for a thousand years.

Theological Significance

The deep is where demons fear to go and where Satan will be shut — and the key is held by an angel, not by the devil. I believe the abyss shows me that evil has a bottom, even if it looks bottomless, and that God holds the key to every prison, including the ones I fear most.
