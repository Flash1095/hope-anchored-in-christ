hesed

Word
חֶסֶד
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
hesed
Strongs Number
H2617
Definition
Steadfast love, mercy and loyal covenant kindness — God's unfailing commitment to His people that never depends on their worthiness.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Exodus 34:6; Psalm 136:1; Lamentations 3:22-23; Micah 6:8
Biblical Usage
Hesed appears over 250 times, most often of God. The Psalms repeat it in refrain — 'His steadfast love endures forever' — and the prophets call Israel to practice hesed toward others, not just feel it.
Theological Significance
Hesed is the love that will not let go. It assures believers that God's kindness is covenant loyalty, not mood — a firm anchor when feelings and circumstances shift.
