mûwg

to melt, i.e. literally (to soften, flow down, disappear), or figuratively (to fear, faint)

Biblical Usage

Mug means to melt, to flow down, to faint — the heathen raged, the kingdoms were moved, and the earth melted at God's voice; the inhabitants of Canaan melt away at the news of the Red Sea; and Rahab says all the inhabitants of the land faint because of you.

Theological Significance

I believe the melting is the fear of God doing its work: kingdoms melt, and the earth itself cannot stand before His voice.
