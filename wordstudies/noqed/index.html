nôqêd

a spotter (of sheep or cattle), i.e. the owner or tender (who thus marks them)

Biblical Usage

A sheepmaster is the man who tends the flocks, and two of them frame the prophets' world. Mesha king of Moab is a sheepmaster who pays Israel a hundred thousand lambs and a hundred thousand rams, and Amos is among the herdmen of Tekoa when the word of the LORD comes to him.

Theological Significance

I believe God calls shepherds to speak: Amos tends the herds of Tekoa, and his words shake two kingdoms.
