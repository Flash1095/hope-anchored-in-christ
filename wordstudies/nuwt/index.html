nûwṭ

to quake

Biblical Usage

Nut means to quake, to shake, to be moved. Psalm 99:1 says the Lord reigns, let the people tremble, and let the earth be moved.

Theological Significance

I hold that the reign of God shakes the earth, and a people who will not tremble before Him will tremble before everything else.
