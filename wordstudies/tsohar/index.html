Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
tsôhar

Definition
a light (i.e. window); dual double light, i.e. noon
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
n m; 1) noon, midday; 1a) noon (as specific time of day); 1b) noon (in simile as bright of happiness, blessing); n f; 2) roof; 2a) meaning dubious
