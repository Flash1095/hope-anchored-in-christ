ʻăzâʼzêl

goat of departure; the scapegoat

Biblical Usage

Azazel is the goat of departure, the scapegoat. On the Day of Atonement Aaron cast lots over two goats — one for the Lord and one for Azazel — and the goat for Azazel was presented alive before the Lord to make atonement, then sent away into the wilderness bearing the iniquities of Israel. The word appears only in this ceremony, and only three times.

Theological Significance

I believe the scapegoat is the picture of what Christ did: my sins carried away, out of sight, into a land not inhabited. The goat went so that I would not have to.
