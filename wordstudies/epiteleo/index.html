epiteléō

from G1909 and G5055; to fulfill further (or completely), i.e. execute; by implication, to terminate, undergo:--accomplish, do, finish, (make) (perfect), perform(X -ance).

Biblical Usage

Epiteleo means to finish, to complete, to perform — to bring a work to its end. He which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ; having begun in the Spirit, are ye now made perfect by the flesh? and the priests accomplish the service of God continually.

Theological Significance

God does not start a good work in me and leave it half done. I hold that the Finisher is the same as the Beginner, and He will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ — that is my assurance, not my achievement.
