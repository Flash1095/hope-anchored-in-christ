râhâh

to fear

Biblical Usage

Rahah is to fear — to be afraid. God says to Israel, 'Fear ye not, neither be afraid: have not I told thee from that time, and have declared it? ye are even my witnesses.'

Theological Significance

I am convinced that God's cure for fear is His own faithfulness: He told them beforehand, and He declared it — and the witnesses need not be afraid.
