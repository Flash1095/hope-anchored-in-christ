proskynéō

from G4314 and a probable derivative of G2965 (meaning to kiss, like a dog licking his master's hand); to fawn or crouch to, i.e. (literally or figuratively) prostrate oneself in homage (do reverence to, adore):--worship.

Biblical Usage

Proskyneo means to worship, to fall down and kiss toward, as a dog licks its master's hand. The wise men fell down and worshipped the child, the disciples worshipped the risen Jesus, and John heard that the true worshippers shall worship the Father in spirit and in truth. But when John fell down to worship the angel, he was told, See thou do it not, worship God.

Theological Significance

I believe worship belongs to God alone: the wise men knew it, the disciples knew it, and the angel himself refused it. I hold that true worship is not a place or a posture but a heart turned to the Father in spirit and in truth, and anything less is not worship at all.
