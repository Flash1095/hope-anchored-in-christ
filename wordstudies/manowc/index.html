mânôwç

a retreat (literally or figuratively); abstractly, a fleeing

Biblical Usage

mânôwç is a refuge, a place to flee to, and sometimes the flight itself. In 2 Samuel 22:3 David calls God his refuge and his saviour, and in Jeremiah 16:19 the LORD is the refuge in the day of affliction. In Psalms 142:4 the psalmist says refuge failed him and no man cared for his soul, and in Job 11:20 the wicked shall not escape. The word holds both the shelter and the failure to find one.

Theological Significance

I believe mânôwç names the God I run to when there is nowhere else. I hold that every other refuge fails, as the psalmist found, and that the LORD alone stands when all else gives way.
