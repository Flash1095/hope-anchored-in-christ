Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
pâqach
Strongs Number

Definition

Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to open (the eyes); 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to open (the eyes); 1a2) to open (the ears); 1b) (Niphal) to be opened
