bréphos

of uncertain affinity; an infant (properly, unborn) literally or figuratively:--babe, (young) child, infant.

Biblical Usage

bréphos covers an infant, a babe, and the Bible uses it the way ordinary people would. You find it 8 times, in 1 Peter among other places, and each time it is doing the same steady work. It is a word you understand best by watching where it goes, not by guessing at it.

Theological Significance

My position is simple: an infant, a babe is part of how God speaks to us, and bréphos is the word He chose for it. That is reason enough to learn it.
