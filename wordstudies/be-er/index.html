bᵉʼêr

a pit; especially a well

Biblical Usage

Beer is a well, a pit dug for water. It is the word for the well God opens for Hagar in the wilderness, for the well where Abraham's servant stops his camels at evening, for the well of springing water Isaac's servants dig in the valley of Gerar, and for the well Israel sings of when God says, gather the people and I will give them water.

Theological Significance

I believe the well is the Bible's picture of God's provision in dry places, because Hagar's eyes are opened to a well she had not seen and Isaac's servants find springing water in a valley of strife. The well is dug by men and opened by God, and I hold that the water of life is still found the same way: by digging where God has promised it.
