Gôwg

Word
גּוֹג
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Gôwg
Strongs Number
H1463
Definition
Gog, the name of an Israelite, also of some nothern nation
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Gog = "mountain"; 1) a Reubenite, son of Shemaiah; 2) the prophetic prince of Rosh, Meshech and Tubal, and Magog
