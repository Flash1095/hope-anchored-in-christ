Yaʻbêts

Word
יַעְבֵּץ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Yaʻbêts
Strongs Number
H3258
Definition
Jabets, the name of an Israelite, and also of a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Jabez = "sorrow"; n pr m; 1) the head of a Calebite family; n pr loc; 2) a town in Judah apparently near Bethlehem
