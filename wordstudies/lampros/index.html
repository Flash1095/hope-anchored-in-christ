lamprós

from the same as G2985; radiant; by analogy, limpid; figuratively, magnificent or sumptuous (in appearance):--bright, clear, gay, goodly, gorgeous, white.

Biblical Usage

Bright or splendid is the adjective for clothing and for glory. In Luke 23:11 Herod arrays Jesus "in a gorgeous robe," in James 2:2 a man comes into the assembly "in goodly apparel," in Acts 10:30 a man stands before Cornelius "in bright clothing," and in Revelation 15:6 the angels are clothed in pure and white linen.

Theological Significance

I am convinced the word's contrast is the sermon: Herod's gorgeous robe mocked the King, and the angels' bright linen is the clothing of those who serve Him, and I want to know which brightness I am wearing. Herod's robe mocked the King, and the angels' linen serves Him.
