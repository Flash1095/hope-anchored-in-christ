chêleq

properly, smoothness (of the tongue); also an allotment

Biblical Usage

A portion is a share, a lot, and the word is used for the shares men claim and the share God gives. David says the part of those who stay with the stuff is the same as those who fight, Israel cries that it has no portion in David, Sheba cries that it has no part in David, and Levi has no part nor inheritance because the LORD is his inheritance.

Theological Significance

I believe the portion that matters is the one God gives: men fight over shares in David, but Levi's share is the LORD Himself. I want the portion that cannot be taken away.
