gîyl

a revolution (of time, i.e. an age); also joy

Biblical Usage

The word gîyl carries the sense of joy, rejoicing. In Psalm 43:4, the text says "unto God my exceeding joy." In Proverbs 23:24, the text says "and he that begetteth a wise [child] shall have joy of him."

Theological Significance

I believe gîyl shows us that worship is not a performance but a response — we answer what God has done with what we are. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
