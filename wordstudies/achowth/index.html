ʼâchôwth

Word
אָחוֹת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼâchôwth
Strongs Number
H269
Definition
a sister (used very widely (like brother), literally and figuratively)
Part Of Speech
noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
1) sister; 1a) sister (same parents); 1b) half-sister (same father); 1c) relative; 1c1)(metaph) of Israel's and Judah's relationship; 1d) beloved; 1d1) bride; 1e) (fig.) of intimate connection; 1f) another
