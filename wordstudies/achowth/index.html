ʼâchôwth

a sister (used very widely (like brother), literally and figuratively)

Biblical Usage

A sister — achowth is used very widely, literally and figuratively, from blood kin to fellow countrywomen. Abraham says of Sarah, "she is my sister"; Miriam "the prophetess, the sister of Aaron, took a timbrel in her hand"; and the law guards the sister's honour in Leviticus 18. The word runs through family, worship, and law alike.

Theological Significance

Sisterhood in Scripture is never a small thing: it is the bond that holds families, and the bond the law protects. I hold that the word's breadth is the point — in God's people, sister means more than blood.
