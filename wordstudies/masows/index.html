mâsôws

delight, concretely (the cause or object) or abstractly (the feeling)

Biblical Usage

mâsôws is delight, joy, and the thing that causes it. In Isaiah 24:8 the mirth of tabrets ceases and the joy of the harp ends, and in Hosea 2:11 God says He will cause all her mirth to cease. In Ezekiel 24:25 the joy of their glory is taken from them, and in Isaiah 8:6 the people rejoice in Rezin and Remaliah's son. The word is the joy that can be lost.

Theological Significance

I believe mâsôws shows me that joy can be taken away when God judges. I hold that the joy He gives is the joy I should guard.
