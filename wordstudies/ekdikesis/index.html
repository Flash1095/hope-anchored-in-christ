ekdíkēsis

from G1556; vindication, retribution:--(a-, re-)venge(-ance), punishment.

Biblical Usage

Vengeance is the vindication itself, the settling of the account, and the New Testament speaks of it as God's work at the end. Jesus asks whether God will not avenge His own elect who cry day and night to Him, and answers that He will avenge them speedily, while Paul warns us not to avenge ourselves but to give place to wrath. The same word describes the Lord taking vengeance in flaming fire on those who do not obey the gospel.

Theological Significance

I believe God's vengeance is His justice, not His temper, because He settles every account exactly and on time. I hold that trusting His timing is part of trusting Him, and I will wait for it.
