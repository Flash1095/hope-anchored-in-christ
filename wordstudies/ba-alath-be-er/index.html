Baʻălath Bᵉʼêr

Word
בַּעֲלַת בְּאֵר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Baʻălath Bᵉʼêr
Strongs Number
H1192
Definition
Baalath-Beer, a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Baalath-beer = "mistress of the well"; 1) a town in the south part of Judah, given to Simeon, which also bore the name of 'Ramath-negeb'
