Yepheth

Word
יֶפֶת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Yepheth
Strongs Number
H3315
Definition
Jepheth, a son of Noah; also his posterity
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Japheth = "opened"; 1) the 3rd son of Noah whose descendants after the flood settled on the coastal lands of the Mediterranean spreading north into Europe and parts of Asia
