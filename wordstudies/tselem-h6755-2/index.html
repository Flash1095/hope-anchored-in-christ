tselem

an idolatrous figure

Biblical Usage

Tselem here is the image Nebuchadnezzar set up — a statue of gold, meant to be worshipped. Daniel saw it first in the king's dream, a great image whose brightness was excellent; then the king built one sixty cubits high and commanded every people to fall down before it at the sound of the music. Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego refused, and their answer still stands: even if God does not deliver us, we will not serve thy gods, nor worship the golden image.

Theological Significance

I believe every age builds its own golden image and demands a bow, and the call is the same in every age. My position is that worship belongs to God alone, and no king, no crowd, and no music can make an idol into a god. I hold that the three who would not bow show us what faith looks like when the furnace is real.
