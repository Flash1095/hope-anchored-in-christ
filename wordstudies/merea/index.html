mêrêaʻ

a friend

Biblical Usage

Mere' a is a friend, a companion, the one who stands beside you. Genesis 26:26 brings Ahuzzath, one of Abimelech's friends, to Isaac at Beersheba, and Judges 14:11 brings thirty companions to be with Samson at his wedding. 2 Samuel 3:8 has Abner boast of his kindness to Saul's house, to his brethren and to his friends, and Proverbs 19:7 warns that the friends of the poor go far from him.

Theological Significance

I believe friendship is proved where it costs something: Abimelech's friend walked to Beersheba, Samson's companions came to the feast, and the poor man's friends went far from him. My position is that a friend who stays when the money is gone is the only kind worth the name.
