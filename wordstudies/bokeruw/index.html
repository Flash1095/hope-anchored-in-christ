Bôkᵉrûw

Bokeru, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Bocheru means firstborn, and he is named twice, in the same line of Benjamin. In 1 Chronicles 8:38 Azel had six sons, and Bocheru is named among them, and in 1 Chronicles 9:44 the same six sons are listed again. A Benjamite, kept on the record in two chapters.

Theological Significance

I hold that a firstborn named twice in Benjamin's line is still kept by God, and the double record is no accident.
