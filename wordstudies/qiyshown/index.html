Qîyshôwn

Kishon, a river of Palestine

Biblical Usage

God draws Sisera to the river Kishon and delivers him into Barak's hand, and the song says the river swept them away, that ancient river. Elijah brings the prophets of Baal down to the brook Kishon and slays them there.

Theological Significance

I believe the Kishon is the river of God's victories, because Sisera's army was swept away there and Baal's prophets died there.
