Kîymâh

a cluster of stars, i.e. the Pleiades

Biblical Usage

Kimah is the Pleiades, the cluster of stars. Job 9:9 says God maketh Arcturus, Orion, and Pleiades, and the chambers of the south, Job 38:31 asks, canst thou bind the sweet influences of Pleiades, or loose the bands of Orion, and Amos 5:8 says to seek him that maketh the seven stars and Orion, and turneth the shadow of death into the morning.

Theological Significance

I believe the Pleiades are God's signature in the sky: He made them, He binds them, and the only right response is to seek Him.
