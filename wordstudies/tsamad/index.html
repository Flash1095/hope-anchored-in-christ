tsâmad

to link, i.e. gird; figuratively, to serve, (mentally) contrive

Biblical Usage

tsâmad means to link, i.e. gird. It appears 5 times in Scripture, including Numbers 25:3 and Psalms 106:28.

Theological Significance

I believe tsâmad shows how carefully God chose His words, and this one earns its place.
