bóthynos

akin to G900; a hole (in the ground); specially, a cistern:--ditch, pit.

Biblical Usage

A ditch is the word for a pit, a hole in the ground — and it appears three times, twice in the same warning. In Matthew 15:14 Jesus says, if the blind lead the blind, both shall fall into the ditch, and in Luke 6:39 he says it again. In Matthew 12:11 he asks, what man shall there be among you, that shall have one sheep, and if it fall into a pit on the sabbath day, will he not lay hold on it, and lift it out?

Theological Significance

I believe the ditch is the warning and the pit is the mercy: the blind fall in together, and the shepherd lifts the sheep out even on the sabbath.
