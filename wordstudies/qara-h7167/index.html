qâraʻ

to rend, literally or figuratively (revile, paint the eyes, as if enlarging them)

Biblical Usage

To rend is the word for tearing cloth and tearing kingdoms: Samuel turned to go and Saul laid hold on the skirt of his mantle and it rent, God told Solomon He would rend the kingdom from him, Isaiah cries, oh that Thou wouldest rend the heavens and come down. Joel says, rend your heart, and not your garments.

Theological Significance

I believe the rent garment is the sign of a rent kingdom, and the rent heavens are the prayer for God to come down and mend what tearing has done. What moves me is Joel's turn: God does not want the cloth, He wants the heart.
