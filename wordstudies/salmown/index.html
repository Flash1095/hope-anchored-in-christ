Salmôwn

Word
שַׂלְמוֹן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Salmôwn
Strongs Number
H8012
Definition
Salmon, an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Salmon = "garment"; 1) father of Boaz, father-in-law of Ruth, and grandfather of David; 1a) also 'Salmah', 'Salma'
