epileípō

from G1909 and G3007; to leave upon, i.e. (figuratively) to be insufficient for:--fail.

Biblical Usage

Epileipo means to fail, to be insufficient — and the writer of Hebrews says, what shall I more say, for the time would fail me to tell of Gideon, and Barak, and Samson, and Jephthae, of David also, and Samuel, and of the prophets.

Theological Significance

The failing of time is the highest praise, I hold: the stories of faith are too many to tell, and the list runs out before the faith does.
