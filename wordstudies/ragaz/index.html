râgaz

to quiver (with any violent emotion, especially anger or fear)

Biblical Usage

Ragaz is to quiver — with anger, with fear, with any violent emotion. David sang, 'Then the earth shook and trembled; the foundations of heaven moved and shook, because he was wroth'; the psalm says, 'Stand in awe, and sin not: commune with your own heart upon your bed, and be still'; and Habakkuk says, 'When I heard, my belly trembled; my lips quivered at the voice.'

Theological Significance

My conviction is that trembling is the body's confession: the earth shakes when God is angry, and the prophet's lips quiver when he hears — and the psalm says the fear that trembles is the beginning of standing still before God.
