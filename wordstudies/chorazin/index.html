Chorazín

of uncertain derivation; Chorazin, a place in Palestine:--Chorazin.

Biblical Usage

Chorazin is a town in Galilee that heard Jesus preach and would not repent, and both Gospels pronounce woe on it. The Lord says if the mighty works done in Chorazin had been done in Tyre and Sidon, they would have repented long ago in sackcloth and ashes.

Theological Significance

I believe Chorazin is the warning that hearing is not believing, and the towns that saw the most are judged the most. I hold that the woe on Chorazin is meant for every place that has heard the gospel and stayed the same.
