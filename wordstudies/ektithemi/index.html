ektíthēmi

from G1537 and G5087; to expose; figuratively, to declare:--cast out, expound.

Biblical Usage

The word ektíthēmi carries the sense of cast out, expound. In Acts 7:21, the text says "And when he was cast out." In Acts 11:4, the text says "and expounded [it] by order unto them."

Theological Significance

Reading ektíthēmi this way, I believe God's Word means what it says, and this word is part of how He speaks plainly to us. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
