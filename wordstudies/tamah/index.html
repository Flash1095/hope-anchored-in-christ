ṭâmâh

to be impure in a religious sense

Biblical Usage

Bildad's friend asks, wherefore are we counted as beasts, and reputed vile in your sight? The word is to be impure in a religious sense, to be counted vile. It appears once, in the friends' wounded question to Job, who has called their wisdom worthless.

Theological Significance

The question shows how the friends saw themselves, I believe: counted as beasts, reputed vile, and the bitterness in it is the sound of men who have run out of answers.
