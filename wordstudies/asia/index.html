Asía

of uncertain derivation; Asia, i.e. Asia Minor, or (usually) only its western shore:--Asia.

Biblical Usage

Asia was the Roman province on the western edge of Asia Minor, and it shows up all through the New Testament. Men from Asia were in Jerusalem at Pentecost, Paul's teaching in Ephesus reached everyone living in Asia within two years, John wrote to the seven churches there, and Peter addressed his letter to believers scattered through Asia and the regions around it.

Theological Significance

I believe Asia matters because it shows how fast the gospel moved beyond Jerusalem, into cities and provinces that had never heard it. The churches there were real congregations with real struggles, and the Lord's letters to them still speak to any church that has lost its first love or grown cold.
