Gâlîyl

Galil (as a special circuit) in the North of Palestine

Biblical Usage

Galil is the Hebrew name for Galilee, the northern circuit of cities. Solomon gave Hiram twenty cities in the land of Galilee in 1 Kings 9:11, and Tiglath-pileser took Galilee captive to Assyria in 2 Kings 15:29. The region passed from a king's gift to a conqueror's spoil.

Theological Significance

I believe the gift of Galilee and the loss of Galilee are the story of Israel in miniature. Solomon gave it away and Assyria took it away, yet the prophets said light would dawn there; I hold that no land is lost to God.
