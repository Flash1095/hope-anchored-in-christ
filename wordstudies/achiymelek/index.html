ʼĂchîymelek

Achimelek, the name of an Israelite and of a Hittite

Biblical Usage

Ahimelech was the priest of Nob who fed David the shewbread and gave him Goliath's sword, and Saul had him killed with eighty-five priests for it — only his son Abiathar escaped.

Theological Significance

Mercy to God's anointed cost Ahimelech his life, and I will not soften that. I hold his death as a shadow of a greater one: the priest who gave bread to the hungry king was murdered for it, and the King he fed would one day give His own body as bread.
