ʼĂchîymelek

Word
אֲחִימֶלֶךְ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼĂchîymelek
Strongs Number
H288
Definition
Achimelek, the name of an Israelite and of a Hittite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Ahimelech = "my brother is king" or "brother of Melek"; 1) a priest murdered by Doeg at Saul's command, for supposedly assisting David; 2) a Hittite warrior under David
