hypereídō

from G5228 and G1492; to overlook, i.e. not punish:--wink at.

Biblical Usage

In the New Testament, hypereídō is the word for wink at. In Acts 17:30, the text says "And the times of this ignorance God winked at."

Theological Significance

This word, hypereídō, does its job quietly — wink at — and I leave it at that.
