Kîyûwn

Word
כִּיּוּן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Kîyûwn
Strongs Number
H3594
Definition
properly, a statue, i.e. idol; but used (by euphemism) for some heathen deity (perhaps corresponding to Priapus or Baal-peor)
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Chiun = "an image" or "pillar"; 1) probably a statue of the Assyrian-Babylonian god of the planet Saturn and used to symbolise Israelite apostasy
