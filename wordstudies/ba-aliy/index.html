Baʻălîy

Word
בַּעֲלִי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Baʻălîy
Strongs Number

Definition
Baali, a symbolical name for Jehovah
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Baali = "my lord"; 1) a deity in the northern kingdom, variation of the name 'Baal'
