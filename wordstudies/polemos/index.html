pólemos

from πέλομαι (to bustle); warfare (literally or figuratively; a single encounter or a series):--battle, fight, war.

Biblical Usage

Polemos is war, a single battle or the whole business of fighting. Jesus says, Ye shall hear of wars and rumours of wars, in Matthew 24:6, and James asks, From whence come wars and fightings among you, in James 4:1. There was war in heaven in Revelation 12:7, and the beast makes war with the saints in Revelation 13:7. But Hebrews 11:34 says the faithful waxed valiant in fight and turned to flight the armies of the aliens.

Theological Significance

I believe war is the shape of a fallen world — nations, churches, and my own heart. My position is that the war in heaven is already decided, and the wars on earth will end the same way.
