miphletseth

a terror, i.e. an idol

Biblical Usage

The word is a horror, an idol, and it appears twice, in the same story told twice: Maachah the queen mother made an idol in a grove, and her son Asa cut it down and burned it at the brook Kidron.

Theological Significance

I hold that Asa did right even against his own mother: no family tie protects an idol, and the grove must be cut down.
