chăzîyz

a flash of lightning

Biblical Usage

This word is the lightning of the thunder, the flash that comes with the storm. God makes a decree for the rain and a way for the lightning, He asks Job who divided a watercourse for the overflowing waters or a way for the lightning, and Zechariah tells Israel to ask the LORD for rain and He will make bright clouds.

Theological Significance

I believe the lightning belongs to God the way the rain does: He decrees its path and no one else does. The same God who flashes the sky gives the showers to the field.
