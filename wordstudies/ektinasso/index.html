ektinássō

from G1537 and tinasso (to swing); to shake violently:--shake (off).

Biblical Usage

ektinássō is the word for shake (off). In Matthew 10:14, the text says "shake off the dust of your feet." In Mark 6:11, the text says "shake off the dust under your feet for a testimony against them. Verily I say unto you."

Theological Significance

My position is simple: ektinássō tells me that God's judgment is just, and it falls where it should — no one escapes it, and no one suffers it unjustly. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
