Karmel

Karmel, the name of a hill and of a town in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Karmel is the hill and the town — the green mountain by the sea where Elijah faced the prophets of Baal, and the town in Judah where Nabal kept his flocks. Ahab gathered all Israel and the prophets to Mount Carmel; Elijah bowed himself to the earth and put his face between his knees; and Nabal of Carmel refused David bread and paid for it with his life.

Theological Significance

I believe Carmel holds two stories at once: the mountain where fire fell and the town where a churl refused bread — and God answered the first with fire and the second with judgment, because He is Lord of both.
