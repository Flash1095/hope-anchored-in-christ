nâshâh

to lend or (by reciprocity) borrow on security or interest

Biblical Usage

The law of release says the creditor shall not exact what his neighbour owes him when the LORD's release comes, and no lender may enter his neighbour's house to take his pledge. Nehemiah rebukes the nobles who exact usury from their own brothers, and the widow's creditor comes to take her two sons as bondsmen.

Theological Significance

I believe lending to a brother in need is never a chance to profit from his trouble. God puts a limit on the creditor, and He stands with the widow whose sons are on the line.
