ʼÂbêl Bêyth-Măʻakâh

Abel of Beth-maakah, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Abel of Beth-maachah is a fortified city in the far north of Israel, near Dan. Sheba flees there when Joab pursues him; a wise woman of the city negotiates with Joab and saves it; Ben-hadad strikes it in Asa's day; and Tiglath-pileser captures it in the fall of the northern kingdom.

Theological Significance

A city known for one wise woman and three invasions: it survives Sheba's rebellion by wisdom and falls at last to Assyria. I believe Abel of Beth-maachah shows both truths — wisdom can save a city, and no wall can save a nation that has left God.
