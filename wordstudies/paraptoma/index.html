paráptōma

from G3895; a side-slip (lapse or deviation), i.e. (unintentional) error or (wilful) transgression:--fall, fault, offence, sin, trespass.

Biblical Usage

Paraptoma is a trespass, a falling aside from the path. Jesus says in Matthew 6:14 that if we forgive men's trespasses, our Father will forgive ours. Paul sets the word against grace in Romans 5:15, where one man's offence is answered by the free gift, and in Ephesians 1:7 and 2:1, where redemption and forgiveness come through His blood while we are dead in trespasses. Colossians 2:13 says God quickened us together with Christ, having forgiven us all trespasses.

Theological Significance

I believe a trespass is a step off the path, and grace does not pretend the step was never taken, it forgives it. That is why I hold that forgiveness received and forgiveness given belong together, and I cannot keep one without the other.
