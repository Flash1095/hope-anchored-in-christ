mâgôwr

a fright (objective or subjective)

Biblical Usage

Magor is fear, terror, the dread that stands on every side. Jeremiah 6:25 says fear is on every side, and Jeremiah 20:10 says the same of the prophet's own familiars, who watched for his halting. Psalms 31:13 has the psalmist hearing the slander of many, fear on every side, while they devise to take away his life, and Lamentations 2:22 says God called terrors round about as in a solemn day.

Theological Significance

I believe this word names the exact feeling of being surrounded: enemies without, false friends within, and no way out but God. My position is that Psalms 31:13 shows the way through it, because the psalmist who names the fear goes on to trust the LORD who is bigger than it.
