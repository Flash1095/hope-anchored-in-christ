ameléō

from G1 (as a negative particle) and G3199; to be careless of:--make light of, neglect, be negligent, no regard.

Biblical Usage

To be careless of, to make light of, to neglect — and the New Testament uses it for the things no one can afford to neglect. They made light of the king's invitation, and went their ways, one to his farm, another to his merchandise. How shall we escape, if we neglect so great salvation? God says of Israel, they continued not in my covenant, and I regarded them not; and Paul warns Timothy, Neglect not the gift that is in thee.

Theological Significance

I believe neglect is the quietest sin in the Bible, and that is why it frightens me. No one sets out to despise the invitation or the salvation or the gift; they make light of it, they go their ways, one to the farm and one to the merchandise, and the door closes behind them. I hold that the opposite of neglect is not effort but attention — to the invitation, to the salvation, to the gift — and attention is the one thing I can give today.
