Bâmôwth

Word
בָּמוֹת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Bâmôwth
Strongs Number
H1120
Definition
Bamoth or Bamoth-Baal, a place East of the Jordan
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Bamoth = "high places" or "great high place"; 1) a town on the river Arnon in Moab
