ʻÔbadyâh

Obadjah, the name of thirteen Israelites

Biblical Usage

Thirteen Israelites share the name Obadiah, and two stand out. The prophet Obadiah speaks the vision of Obadiah 1:1 against Edom, and in 1 Kings 18:3 another Obadiah, governor of Ahab's house, fears the Lord greatly while serving a wicked king.

Theological Significance

I hold that Obadiah proves a name can carry both a prophet and a quiet faithful man, and God uses the prophet who speaks judgment and the governor who fears the Lord in Ahab's house.
