katastréphō

from G2596 and G4762; to turn upside down, i.e. upset:--overthrow.

Biblical Usage

Jesus overthrew the tables of the moneychangers and the seats of them that sold doves.

Theological Significance

I believe the overturned tables are the sign that the temple is for prayer, not for profit.
