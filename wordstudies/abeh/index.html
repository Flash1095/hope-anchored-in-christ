ʼâbeh

longing

Biblical Usage

This word for longing or desire occurs once, in Job 34:36, in Elihu's speech: 'My desire is that Job may be tried unto the end because of his answers for wicked men.' It is one man's stated wish — that Job's testing run its full course.

Theological Significance

A single word for a single wish, and it is not even a kind one — Elihu wants Job tried to the end — and the verse is honest about how people speak into suffering.
