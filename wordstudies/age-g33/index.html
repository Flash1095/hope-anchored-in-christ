áge

imperative of G71; properly, lead, i.e. come on:--go to.

Biblical Usage

Age is a command: 'Come on!' or 'Go to now!' — the word you use to call someone to attention. James uses it twice, both times to summon the self-confident to listen: 'Go to now, ye that say, To day or to morrow we will go into such a city,' and 'Go to now, ye rich men, weep and howl.'

Theological Significance

It does its work quietly inside a verse, and I leave the doctrine where it belongs: in the passage, not in the word; in this case, the verse is James 4:13.
