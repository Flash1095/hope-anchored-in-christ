mᵉnâʼ

to count, appoint

Biblical Usage

Mena' is the Aramaic word to count and to appoint, and it runs through Daniel and Ezra as the word for putting the right man in the right place. Daniel 2:24 says Arioch was the man whom the king had ordained to destroy the wise men of Babylon, and Daniel 2:49 has Daniel set Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego over the affairs of the province. Daniel 3:12 shows the same appointments turned into an accusation, and Ezra 7:25 has Artaxerxes tell Ezra to set magistrates and judges over the people beyond the river.

Theological Significance

I believe appointing is God's work done through kings and priests: Daniel appoints faithful men, and Ezra appoints judges, and the same word covers both. My position is that who is appointed matters as much as what is decreed, because a faithful appointee can save a province.
