châdal

Word
חָדַל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
châdal
Strongs Number
H2308
Definition
properly, to be flabby, i.e. (by implication) desist; (figuratively) be lacking or idle
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to stop, cease, desist, forego, cease to be, leave undone, forbear; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to cease, come to an end; 1a2) to cease, leave off
