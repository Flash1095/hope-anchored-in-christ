doxázō

from G1391; to render (or esteem) glorious (in a wide application):--(make) glorify(-ious), full of (have) glory, honour, magnify.

Biblical Usage

To glorify, to give glory, whether the shepherds returning to glorify God for all they had seen, or the Son asking the Father to glorify Him so He might glorify the Father. Paul calls believers to glorify God with one mind and one mouth, and to glorify God in the body, because they were bought with a price.

Theological Significance

I believe glorifying God is the purpose I was bought for, and it is done with the body as well as the voice. The shepherds went back to their flocks glorifying God, and I go back to my work doing the same.
