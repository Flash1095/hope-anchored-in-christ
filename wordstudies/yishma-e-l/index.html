Yishmâʻêʼl

Word
יִשְׁמָעֵאל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Yishmâʻêʼl
Strongs Number
H3458
Definition
Jishmael, the name of Abraham's oldest son, and of five Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Ishmael = "God will hear"; 1) son of Abraham and Sarah's handmaid Hagar and the progenitor of the Arabian peoples; 2) son of Nethaniah and the murderer of Gedaliah; 3) a Benjamite, one of the sons of Azel a descendant of Saul through Meribbaal or Mephibosheth; 4) a Judaite, father of Zebadiah; 5) a Judaite, son of Johanan and one of the captains who aided Jehoiada in restoring Joash to the throne; 6) a priest of the family of Pashur with a foreign wife whom he was forced to put away by Ezra
