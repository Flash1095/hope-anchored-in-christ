hébdomos

ordinal from G2033; seventh:--seventh.

Biblical Usage

Hebdomos means seventh, and the Bible counts to seven with purpose. In Hebrews 4:4 God rested on the seventh day, in John 4:52 the fever left the nobleman's son at the seventh hour, in Revelation 11:15 the seventh angel sounds and the kingdoms become the Lord's, and in Jude 1:14 Enoch, the seventh from Adam, prophesies.

Theological Significance

I believe seven is the number of completion, and each of these sevens is a promise that God finishes what he starts: the rest, the healing, the kingdom, and the prophecy all arrive on time. I hold that God counts in sevens because he finishes in sevens, and the seventh day, the seventh hour and the seventh angel all say the same thing.
