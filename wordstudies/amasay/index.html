ʻĂmâsay

Word
עֲמָשַׂי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻĂmâsay
Strongs Number
H6022
Definition
Amasai, the name of three Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Amasai = "burdensome"; 1) a warrior and chief of the captains of Judah and Benjamin who joined David at Ziklag; 2) a Kohathite Levite, father of Mahath and ancestor of Samuel; 3) a priest who blew the trumpet before the ark; 4) another Kohathite Levite in the reign of king Hezekiah of Judah
