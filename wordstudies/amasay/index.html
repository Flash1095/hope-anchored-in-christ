ʻĂmâsay

Amasai, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Three men share the name, and the Bible keeps them apart by their errand. Amasai the chief of the captains comes over to David at Ziklag, and the spirit comes upon him, and he says, Thine are we, David, and on thy side, thou son of Jesse; Amasai the priest blows the trumpet before the ark when David brings it to Jerusalem; and Mahath the son of Amasai is a Levite who cleanses the temple under Hezekiah; the name is the same; the men are not.

Theological Significance

The name is the same and the errands are not: one Amasai joins David, one blows the trumpet before the ark, and the third man's son cleanses the house of God.
