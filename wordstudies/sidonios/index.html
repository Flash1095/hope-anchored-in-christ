Sidṓnios

from G4605; a Sidonian, i.e. inhabitant of Sidon:--of Sidon.

Biblical Usage

Sidonios is a Sidonian, a person of Sidon, and it appears where Herod's anger meets a city's hunger and where Elijah's mission crosses a border. The men of Tyre and Sidon came with one accord to Herod desiring peace, because their country was nourished by the king's country, and Elijah was sent to none of the widows of Israel, save to Sarepta, a city of Sidon.

Theological Significance

I believe God feeds His prophets through outsiders, sending Elijah to a Sidonian widow while Israel's own widows went hungry.
