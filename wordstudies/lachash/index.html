lâchash

to whisper; by implication, to mumble a spell (as a magician)

Biblical Usage

To whisper is what servants and enemies do behind a person's back. In 2 Samuel 12:19 David's servants whispered together, and David knew from the whisper that his child was dead; in Psalms 41:7 David says, "All that hate me whisper together against me: against me do they devise my hurt."

Theological Significance

I am sure the whisper is the sound of conspiracy: David heard it in his own house and in the mouths of his enemies, and the psalm gives me the prayer for it.
