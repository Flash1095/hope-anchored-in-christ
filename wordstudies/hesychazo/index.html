hēsycházō

from the same as G2272; to keep still (intransitively), i.e. refrain from labor, meddlesomeness or speech:--cease, hold peace, be quiet, rest.

Biblical Usage

hēsycházō — cease, hold peace, be quiet, rest. That is the whole of it, and it is enough: 5 occurrences, and in Luke, Acts, and 1 Thessalonians the meaning never wavers. I keep coming back to the simple reading because the simple reading is the true one.

Theological Significance

I believe hēsycházō matters because stillness is not laziness — it is trust that God is at work. I hold that the simple reading is the true one, and I will not trade it for a clever one.
