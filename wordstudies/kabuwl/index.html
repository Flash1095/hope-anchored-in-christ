Kâbûwl

Cabul, the name of two places in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Cabul = "binding"; 1) a city on the border of Asher and located approx 10 miles (16 km) east of Akko; modern 'Kabul'; 2) a district in Galilee given by Solomon to Hiram and contemptuously called 'Cabul' by Hiram
