Kâbûwl

Cabul, the name of two places in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Cabul is two places at once: a town on the border of Asher, and the district of twenty towns Solomon gave to Hiram of Tyre. Hiram looked at them and was not pleased, and he named the land Cabul — and the name stuck, a reminder that even a gift from a king can disappoint when it is not what was hoped for.

Theological Significance

I believe Cabul is a small warning about judging God's gifts by first appearances. Hiram saw twenty towns and called them good-for-nothing — and I wonder how often I have done the same with what God has given me.
