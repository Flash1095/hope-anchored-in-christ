ʻÊbêr

Eber, the name of two patriarchs and four Israelites

Biblical Usage

Eber is the son of Salah and the father of Peleg and Joktan, and his name is tied to the word Hebrew itself. Genesis traces the line from Shem through Eber down to Abraham, so he stands at the head of the family God chose. The other men who share his name are a Gadite chief, two Benjamites, and a priest in the days of Joiakim, each named only in the lists.

Theological Significance

I believe Eber's place in the genealogy shows how God works through ordinary families over long stretches of time. The promise did not arrive all at once; it travelled through fathers and sons like him.
