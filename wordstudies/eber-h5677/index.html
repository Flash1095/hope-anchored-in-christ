ʻÊbêr

Word
עֵבֵר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻÊbêr
Strongs Number
H5677
Definition
Eber, the name of two patriarchs and four Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Eber or Heber = "the region beyond"; 1) son of Salah, great grandson of Shem, father of Peleg and Joktan; 2) a Gadite chief; 3) a Benjamite, son of Elpaal and descendant of Sharahaim; 4) a Benjamite, son of Shashak; 5) a priest in the days of Joiakim the son of Jeshua
