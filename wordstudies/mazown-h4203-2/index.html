mâzôwn

{food}

Biblical Usage

The word is the Aramaic for food, and Daniel uses it twice, of the great tree whose meat was for all: the beasts found shadow under it, and the birds lodged in its branches, and all flesh was fed from it.

Theological Significance

I hold that the tree is Nebuchadnezzar himself: a king who fed the whole world forgot who gave him the food, and God cut the tree down to teach him that provision comes from heaven.
