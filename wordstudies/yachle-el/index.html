Yachlᵉʼêl

Jachleel, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

The sons of Zebulun were Sered, and Elon, and Jahleel, who went down into Egypt. In the wilderness the sons of Zebulun were counted by their families, of Jahleel, the family of the Jahleelites.

Theological Significance

I believe Jahleel's family kept his name for forty years in the wilderness: the son who went down into Egypt became a family in the census, and the tribe carried him with them out of Egypt and into the land.
