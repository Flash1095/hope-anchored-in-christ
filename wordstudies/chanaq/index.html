chânaq

Word
חָנַק
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
chânaq
Strongs Number
H2614
Definition
to be narrow; by implication, to throttle, or (reflex.) to choke oneself to death (by a rope)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to strangle, strangle oneself; 1a) (Niphal) to strangle oneself; 1b) (Piel) to strangle
