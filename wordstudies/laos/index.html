laós

apparently a primary word; a people (in general; thus differing from G1218, which denotes one's own populace):--people.

Biblical Usage

Laos is the people, the nation as a whole, and it is the word of the covenant people from one end of the New Testament to the other. In Luke 2:10 the angel brings "good tidings of great joy, which shall be to all people," in 1 Peter 2:9 believers are "a peculiar people," chosen and holy, in 2 Corinthians 6:16 God says, "I will be their God, and they shall be my people," and in Acts 2:47 the Lord added to the church daily.

Theological Significance

In my reading, the word is the gospel's great transfer: a people who were not a people are now the people of God, and the good tidings are to all people, not to one nation only. The good tidings are to all people, and the people of God are those He has called out of darkness.
