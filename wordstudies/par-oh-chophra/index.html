Parʻôh Chophraʻ

Paroh-Chophra, an Egyptian king

Biblical Usage

Pharaoh Hophra, called Apries by the Greeks, is the Egyptian king to whom the remnant of Judah flees after the fall of Jerusalem. The LORD says He will give Pharaoh Hophra into the hand of his enemies, as He gave Zedekiah into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar.

Theological Significance

I believe Hophra's end is the warning to every refuge: the remnant fled to Egypt for safety, and God says the king they trusted will fall just like the king they fled.
