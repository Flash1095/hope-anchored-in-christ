Yâphîyaʻ

Word
יָפִיעַ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Yâphîyaʻ
Strongs Number
H3309
Definition
Japhia, the name of a Canaanite, an Israelite, and a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Japhia = "shining"; n pr loc; 1) a town on the border of Zebulun and Issachar; 1a) perhaps modern 'Yafa'; n pr m; 2) the king of Lachish at the time of the conquest of Canaan by Joshua; 3) a son of David by a concubine
