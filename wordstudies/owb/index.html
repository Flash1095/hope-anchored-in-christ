ʼôwb

properly, a mumble, i.e. a water skin (from its hollow sound); hence a necromancer (ventriloquist, as from a jar)

Biblical Usage

A familiar spirit is a spirit consulted by mediums, and the law forbade it utterly. Saul had put away those that had familiar spirits from the land, yet in his last night he sought the witch of Endor, and the chronicler says he died for inquiring of a familiar spirit instead of the Lord.

Theological Significance

My position is that the familiar spirit is the Bible's warning against seeking the dead for what only God can give: Saul had driven them out, and then in his fear he went to one himself. The dead cannot help me, and the living God can, so I will not trade the one for the other.
