shâthaq

to subside

Biblical Usage

To be quiet is what the sea does when God is done with it, and what strife does when the talebearer is gone. The sailors ask Jonah what to do that the sea may be calm, and he says cast me forth; Proverbs says where there is no talebearer, the strife ceaseth; and the psalm says they are glad because they be quiet, so He brings them to their desired haven.

Theological Significance

I believe quiet is God's gift after the storm. The sea subsides, the strife ceases, the sailors are glad, and I hold that the haven is reached by His hand, not by ours.
