tsânaph

to wrap, i.e. roll or dress

Biblical Usage

In Scripture, tsânaph is the word for to wrap, i.e. roll or dress. It appears 2 times in Scripture, including Leviticus 16:4 and Isaiah 22:18.

Theological Significance

I hold that tsânaph is no accident in Scripture — God put it there on purpose.
