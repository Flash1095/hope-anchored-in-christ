megistânes

plural from G3176; grandees:--great men, lords.

Biblical Usage

The word is the plural of grandees, the great men, and it is used where the powerful gather: Herod's birthday feast is kept with his lords and high captains, the kings and great men of the earth hide in the caves from the wrath of the Lamb, and the merchants of Babylon were the great men of the earth.

Theological Significance

I hold that the great men are the proof that greatness without God is nothing: they feast with Herod, they hide from the Lamb, and their merchandise cannot buy them out of the judgment.
