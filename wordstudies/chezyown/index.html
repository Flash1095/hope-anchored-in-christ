Chezyôwn

Word
חֶזְיוֹן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Chezyôwn
Strongs Number
H2383
Definition
Chezjon, a Syrian
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Hezion = "vision"; 1) king of Syria, father of Tabrimon and grandfather of Ben-hadad; probably identical with 'Rezon' the contemporary of Solomon
