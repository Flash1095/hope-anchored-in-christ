yâmîyn

the right hand or side (leg, eye) of a person or other object (as the stronger and more dexterous); locally, the south

Biblical Usage

Yâmîyn is the right hand, the stronger hand, the side of honour and power. Moses sang that the right hand of the LORD is glorious in power and has dashed the enemy in pieces, and the psalmist says the right hand of the LORD is exalted and does valiantly. The word becomes the seat of the Messiah, told to sit at God's right hand until His enemies are made His footstool, and the promise that God will uphold His people with the right hand of His righteousness.

Theological Significance

I believe the right hand of God is not a figure of distance but of deliverance, and it has already won the victory it promises. My position is that Christ sits at that right hand now, so my enemies are already under His feet. And I hold that the same hand that dashed Pharaoh's army in pieces is the hand that upholds me, so I need not be dismayed.
