ʻEbed Melek

Ebed-Melek, a eunuch of Zedekeah

Biblical Usage

Ebed-Melek was an Ethiopian eunuch in the court of King Zedekiah, and his name means servant of the king. When Jeremiah was sinking in the cistern, Ebed-Melek spoke up for him and pulled him out with ropes and rags, and God promised him his life as a reward for trusting Him.

Theological Significance

I believe Ebed-Melek shows that courage is not about rank or birth, but about doing right when it costs something. A foreign servant trusted God, and God did not forget him.
