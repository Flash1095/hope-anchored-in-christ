místhios

from G3408; a wage-earner:--hired servant.

Biblical Usage

The word is a hired servant, and it appears twice in the prodigal's speech: how many hired servants of my father's have bread enough and to spare, and I am no more worthy to be called thy son — make me as one of thy hired servants.

Theological Significance

I believe the hired servant is the measure of the father's house: even the lowest place there has bread enough, and the son who comes home finds more than he asked for.
