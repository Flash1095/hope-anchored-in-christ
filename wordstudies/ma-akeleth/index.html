maʼăkeleth

something to eat with,i.e. a knife

Biblical Usage

The word names the knife used for eating and for sacrifice, and it appears at the sharpest moments of the Bible. Abraham took the knife in his hand to slay his son on Mount Moriah, and again he stretched out his hand and took the knife before the angel called. The Levite took a knife and divided his concubine into twelve pieces, and the proverb says the teeth of the wicked are as knives, devouring the poor from off the earth.

Theological Significance

I believe the knife in Abraham's hand is the Bible's sharpest picture of faith, because he trusted God would provide even as he raised it. I hold that the same knife in wicked hands shows what happens when men use holy things for violence, and only God can turn the blade aside.
