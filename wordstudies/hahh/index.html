hâhh

ah! expressing grief

Biblical Usage

Hâhh is a cry of grief, the word Ezekiel uses when the day of the LORD draws near. It stands in Ezekiel 30:2, where the prophet cries, Woe worth the day.

Theological Significance

I read hâhh plainly — it means ah, alas, a cry of grief, and I do not build a doctrine on it.
