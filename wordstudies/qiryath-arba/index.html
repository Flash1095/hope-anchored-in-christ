Qiryath ʼArbaʻ

Word
קִרְיַת אַרְבַּע
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Qiryath ʼArbaʻ
Strongs Number
H7153
Definition
Kirjath-Arba or Kirjath-ha-Arba, a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Kirjath-arba = "city of Arba"; 1) early name of the city which after the conquest was called 'Hebron'
