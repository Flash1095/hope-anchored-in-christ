Shimʻath

Word
שִׁמְעַת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Shimʻath
Strongs Number
H8100
Definition
Shimath, an Ammonitess
Part Of Speech
proper noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
Shimeath = "report"; 1) an Ammonitess, mother of Jozachar or Zabad, one of the assassins of king Joash of Judah
