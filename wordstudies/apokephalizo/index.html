apokephalízō

from G575 and G2776; to decapitate:--behead.

Biblical Usage

To behead — four times, and every time about John the Baptist. Herod says in Luke 9:9, John have I beheaded, and in Mark 6:16 he is sure the risen Jesus is John whom he beheaded. Mark 6:27 tells how the king sent an executioner and commanded his head to be brought, and Matthew 14:10 says he sent and beheaded John in the prison.

Theological Significance

I believe the repetition is the point: the Gospels will not let Herod's crime fade into the background. Four times the word falls, and four times it names the same prophet, the same prison, the same guilty king. John's death is not a footnote to Jesus' ministry; it is the warning that speaking the truth can cost a man his head.
