ʼĂphêq

Aphek (or Aphik), the name of three places in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Aphek is the name of three places, and the Bible keeps returning to it: Israel camped at Aphek before the ark was taken, the Philistines gathered there before Gilboa, Ben-Hadad's army was routed there, and the arrow Elisha told Joash to shoot was aimed toward it. One name, three towns, and a long history of battles.

Theological Significance

I believe Aphek matters because battles were fought there again and again: the ground where God's people lost the ark is also the ground where God gave them victory, and I hold that no place is lost to him for good.
