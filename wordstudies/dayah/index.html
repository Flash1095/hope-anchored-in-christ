dayâh

a falcon (from its rapid flight)

Biblical Usage

The falcon is the swift bird of prey, named among the unclean birds of the law and pictured in the ruined land where the birds of prey gather. It flies fast and strikes without warning, and the law keeps it from the table.

Theological Significance

I believe the falcon reminds us that swiftness is not wisdom: the fastest bird in the sky is still unclean.
