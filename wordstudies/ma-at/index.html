mâʻaṭ

Word
מָעַט
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
mâʻaṭ
Strongs Number
H4591
Definition
properly, to pare off, i.e. lessen; in transitively, to be (or causatively, to make) small or few (or figuratively, ineffective)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to be or become small, be few, be diminished; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to diminish; 1a2) to be too small; 1b) (Piel) to become few; 1c) (Hiphil); 1c1) to make small, make few, diminish; 1c2) to give less
