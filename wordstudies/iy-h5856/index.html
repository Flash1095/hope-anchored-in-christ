ʻîy

a ruin (as if overturned)

Biblical Usage

A ruin is what is left when the city falls, and the prophets say Jerusalem will become one. In Jeremiah 26:18 Micah's word is quoted, Zion shall be plowed like a field, and Jerusalem shall become heaps, in Micah 3:12 the same word is spoken, and in Psalms 79:1 the psalmist cries, O God, the heathen are come into thine inheritance, thy holy temple have they defiled, and laid Jerusalem on heaps.

Theological Significance

I hold that the ruin is the warning the prophets would not stop giving: the city God loved became heaps. It became heaps because the people would not hear.
