Qorchîy

a Korchite (collectively) or descendants of Korach

Biblical Usage

The sons of Korah are listed as the families of the Korhites, and Shallum the son of Kore and his brethren keep the gates of the tabernacle. The Levites of the children of the Korhites stand up to praise the LORD with a loud voice. The rebels' sons became the gatekeepers and the singers.

Theological Significance

I believe the Korahites who kept the gates and sang the praise are the proof that God saves a family from its father's sin.
