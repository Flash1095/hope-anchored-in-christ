mâraq

to polish; by implication, to sharpen; also to rinse

Biblical Usage

mâraq is to polish, to sharpen, and to rinse or scour. In Leviticus 6:28 a brazen pot in which the sin offering is sodden shall be both scoured and rinsed in water, and in Proverbs 20:30 the blueness of a wound cleanses away evil, as stripes cleanse the inward parts. The word is scouring that makes clean.

Theological Significance

I believe mâraq shows me that God provides cleansing for His vessels, and that a wound rightly borne can cleanse the heart.
