Yôwʼêl

Joel, the name of twelve Israelites

Biblical Usage

Joel is named 19 times in the Bible, and the passages here are 1 Samuel 8:2; 1 Chronicles 4:35; 2 Chronicles 29:12. In 1 Samuel 8:2 we read, "Now the name of his firstborn was Joel; and the name of his second, Abiah: they were judges in Beer-sheba." The same name belongs to more than one man, and 2 Chronicles 29:12 names another of them.

Theological Significance

Joel is brief in Scripture and long in God's memory, and that is my conviction. His name stands in the record, and that is enough for me.
