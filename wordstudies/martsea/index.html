martsêaʻ

an awl

Biblical Usage

martsêaʻ is an awl, the tool that bores a servant's ear to the door. In Exodus 21:6 the master brings the servant to the door post and bores his ear through with an awl, and he shall serve him for ever. In Deuteronomy 15:17 the same is done for a maidservant., and the word is the tool of a lifelong choice.

Theological Significance

I believe martsêaʻ shows me that a servant who loves his master can choose to stay for ever, and that the mark of that choice is worn in the ear.
