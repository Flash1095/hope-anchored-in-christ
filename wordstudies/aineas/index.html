Ainéas

of uncertain derivation; Ænĕas, an Israelite:--Æneas.

Biblical Usage

Aeneas is a paralyzed man at Lydda who has kept his bed eight years.

Theological Significance

Aeneas carries no teaching of his own; he is a witness, not a theologian. What I take from him is simple: Christ's healing was immediate, public, and complete, and a whole town turned to the Lord because one bedridden man stood up.
