oikonoméō

from G3623; to manage (a house, i.e. an estate):--be steward.

Biblical Usage

Oikonomeo means to be steward, to manage a house or an estate. It occurs once, in Luke 16:2, where the rich man calls his steward to give an account of his stewardship, for he may no longer be steward.

Theological Significance

I believe every steward gives an account, and the question is never whether we manage something, but whose house we are managing.
