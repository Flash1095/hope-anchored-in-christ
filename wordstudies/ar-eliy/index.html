ʼArʼêlîy

Areli (or an Arelite, collectively), an Israelite and his descendants

Biblical Usage

Areli is named as a son of Gad who went down into Egypt with Jacob's household, and his descendants are counted in the wilderness as the family of the Arelites. His name carries the line from Egypt to the plains of Moab.

Theological Significance

I believe a family counted in the wilderness is a family God has not forgotten, even when all we have of them is a name in a list.
