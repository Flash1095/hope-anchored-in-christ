ʻim

adverb or preposition, with (i.e. in conjunction with), in varied applications; specifically, equally with; often with prepositional prefix (and then usually unrepresented in English)

Biblical Usage

With is the smallest word for the greatest promise in the Bible. In Genesis 28:15 God tells Jacob, I am with thee, and will keep thee in all places whither thou goest, in Exodus 3:12 he tells Moses, certainly I will be with thee, and in Hosea 11:12 he says Judah yet ruleth with God, and is faithful with the saints.

Theological Significance

I believe with is the whole of the covenant in one word: God with us, God with Jacob, God with Moses, and God with his faithful ones still.
