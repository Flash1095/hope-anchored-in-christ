dabar

Word
דָּבָר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
dabar
Strongs Number
H1697
Definition
Word, speech, matter or thing — God's spoken word that creates, commands, promises and always accomplishes His purpose.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Isaiah 55:11; Psalm 33:6; Deuteronomy 8:3; Jeremiah 1:9
Biblical Usage
By the dabar of the Lord the heavens were made, and His word 'shall not return to Me empty.' Israel learned to live not by bread alone but by every word from God's mouth.
Theological Significance
Dabar assures us God's promises are not fragile. The word that created galaxies and sustained Israel still accomplishes everything God sends it to do.
