antílēpsis

from G482; relief:--help.

Biblical Usage

Paul lists helps among the gifts God has set in the church, alongside apostles, prophets, teachers, and miracles. The word is the gift of relief, the helping hand that carries what others cannot.

Theological Significance

One word for helps carries no doctrine of its own; its honour is Paul's, that the gift of helping stands in the church beside apostles and prophets.
