perets

a break (literally or figuratively)

Biblical Usage

Perets is a breach, a breaking forth. Pharez broke forth at his birth and took the name, and David called the place Baal-perazim because the LORD had broken forth upon his enemies like the breach of waters. Moses stood in the breach to turn away God's wrath, and Isaiah promises a people who will be called the repairer of the breach.

Theological Significance

My position is that a breach can be a birth or a judgment, and the difference is which side of it I stand on. Pharez broke forth into life, and David's enemies broke before the LORD. I hold that Moses standing in the breach is the picture of intercession — one man between God's wrath and His people — and the repairer of the breach is the one who builds where the wall fell. My position is that I am called to stand in the breach, not to make one.
