mnēmoneúō

from a derivative of G3420; to exercise memory, i.e. recollect; by implication, to punish; also to rehearse:--make mention; be mindful, remember.

Biblical Usage

Mnemoneuo means to remember on purpose. Paul tells Timothy to remember Jesus Christ raised from the dead, Hebrews tells the church to remember the leaders who spoke God's word to them, the risen Lord tells Ephesus to remember where it fell, and Jesus tells His hearers to remember Lot's wife.

Theological Significance

I believe remembering is not nostalgia; it is how faith stays on the road. Forget what God did and who He is, and the next step goes wrong.
