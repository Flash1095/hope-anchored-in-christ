ʼĂbîy Gibʻôwn

Abi-Gibon, perhaps an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Abi-Gibon — the father of Gibeon — is named once, in 1 Chronicles 8:29, at the head of the genealogy of Saul's family: 'And at Gibeon dwelt the father of Gibeon; whose wife's name was Maachah.' He is the ancestor through whom Saul's line is traced.

Theological Significance

One verse, one name, and it anchors a whole royal genealogy. I believe the Bible's genealogies are not filler: they show that God's story runs through real families, and even a man named only as 'the father of Gibeon' has his place in the line that leads to a king.
