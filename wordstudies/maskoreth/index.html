maskôreth

wages or a reward

Biblical Usage

maskôreth is wages or a reward, and it belongs to Jacob's story. In Genesis 29:15 Laban asks Jacob what his wages shall be, and in Genesis 31:7 and Genesis 31:41 Jacob says Laban changed his wages ten times. In Ruth 2:12 Boaz prays that a full reward be given to Ruth by the LORD God of Israel, under whose wings she has come to trust. The word is the pay that can be cheated and the reward that cannot.

Theological Significance

I believe maskôreth shows me that God pays His workers. I hold that a full reward waits for those who trust under His wings.
