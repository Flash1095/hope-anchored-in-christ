laʻănâh

wormwood (regarded as poisonous, and therefore accursed)

Biblical Usage

Wormwood is the bitter, poisonous herb, and Scripture makes it the taste of sin and judgment. In Deuteronomy 29:18 it is "a root that beareth gall and wormwood" in the heart that turns to other gods, in Jeremiah 9:15 God will feed His people "with wormwood, and give them water of gall to drink," in Amos 5:7 the unjust "turn judgment to wormwood," and in Proverbs 5:4 the strange woman's end "is bitter as wormwood."

Theological Significance

What I take from this is that wormwood is the flavour of every sin that promised sweetness: it turns judgment bitter, it turns the heart bitter, and the end of it is always bitterness. Sin promises sweetness and ends in bitterness, and the wormwood proves it.
