chizzâyôwn

a revelation, expectation by dream

Biblical Usage

Chizzayown is a revelation, a vision seen by night or by the prophets, and it carries the weight of God's word. Nathan speaks to David according to all this vision, Isaiah's burden is the valley of vision, Eliphaz is troubled by thoughts from the visions of the night, and Joel promises that young men shall see visions when the Spirit is poured out.

Theological Significance

I believe a true vision always agrees with God's word, because He does not contradict Himself in the night. I hold that Joel's promise is still open, and the Spirit still opens eyes to see what God is doing.
