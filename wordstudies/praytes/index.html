praÿtēs

from G4239; mildness, i.e. (by implication) humility:--meekness.

Biblical Usage

Prautes is meekness, gentleness, and Paul names it among the fruit of the Spirit. He corrects the Corinthians in the spirit of meekness, tells the Galatians to restore the fallen in the same spirit, and James says to receive the engrafted word with meekness. Peter says to give a reason for the hope within with meekness and fear.

Theological Significance

I hold that meekness is how truth is carried: firmly, but never with a clenched fist. The Spirit grows it in me, and it shows most when I am correcting, restoring, or answering someone who disagrees.
