ʻĂthalyâh

Athaljah, the name of an Israelitess and two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Two very different people share this name, and the record keeps them apart. One is Athaliah the daughter of Ahab, who seized the throne of Judah and ruled six years after killing her own grandchildren, until Jehoiada crowned the boy Joash and she was put to death, and the other is a man of the house of Elam whose son Jeshaiah came back with Ezra.

Theological Significance

I believe the queen's story is the Bible's plainest warning that a throne taken by murder cannot stand, because God had already hidden the true king in His house. The other Athaliah's son simply came home, and I hold that the contrast is the point: one name, two roads, and only one of them ends in life.
