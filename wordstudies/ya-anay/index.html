Yaʻănay

Jaanai, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Joel the chief, and Shapham the next, and Jaanai, and Shaphat in Bashan stand in the chiefs of Gad. Jaanai stands third among them, between Shapham and Shaphat.

Theological Significance

I believe the chiefs are listed because the tribe was counted for war: Jaanai is named third among Gad's heads in Bashan, and the record keeps his place in the line.
