matstsâh

a quarrel

Biblical Usage

Strife is the quarrel that pride starts, and Proverbs names the cause plainly: only by pride cometh contention. Loving strife is loving transgression, and Isaiah says the people fast for strife and debate, striking with the fist of wickedness.

Theological Significance

I believe strife is never an accident: it grows where pride is fed, and it shows up even in religion, where people fast and still fight. The cure is not a better argument but a humbler heart.
