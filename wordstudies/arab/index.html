ʼârab

to lurk

Biblical Usage

To lurk, to lie in ambush, is the word for waiting in secret to strike. God tells Joshua to lay an ambush behind Ai, the men of Shechem set liers in wait on the mountains, the gang in Proverbs invites the young man to lie in wait for blood, and Jeremiah says God was to him as a bear lying in wait.

Theological Significance

I hold that ambush is the devil's favourite posture, hidden and patient, and the only defence is to walk openly with God. But when God Himself is the one lying in wait, it is because judgment has been earned, not because He is cruel.
