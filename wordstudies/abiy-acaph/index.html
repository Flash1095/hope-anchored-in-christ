ʼĂbîyʼâçâph

Abiasaph, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Abiasaph is a son of Korah, named in the genealogy of Levi in Exodus 6:24 and in the singers' lines of 1 Chronicles. His descendants are the Korahites — the temple gatekeepers and singers, keepers of the thresholds of the house of God.

Theological Significance

The son of a rebel becomes the father of gatekeepers and singers — that is the story of Abiasaph's line. I believe it shows God's mercy running in families: Korah's rebellion ended in judgment, but his sons were spared, and their children ended up singing at the doors of the house.
