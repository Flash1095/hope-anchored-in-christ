Yâshobʻâm

Word
יָשׇׁבְעָם
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Yâshobʻâm
Strongs Number
H3434
Definition
Jashobam, the name of two or three Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Jashobeam = "the people will return"; 1) a Hachmonite, the leader of the 30 mighty warriors of David; he joined David at Ziklag and was best known as the man who slew 800 or 300 men at one time; also called 'Adino the Eznite'; 2) a Korahite, another of David's mighty warriors; same as 1?
