paríēmi

from G3844 and hiemi (to send); to let by, i.e. relax:--hang down.

Biblical Usage

Pariemi means to let go, to relax, to hang down. It appears once, in Hebrews 12:12, where believers are told to lift up the hands which hang down, and the feeble knees.

Theological Significance

I hold that tired hands are not condemned in Hebrews, they are lifted, and the command is to straighten what has gone slack.
