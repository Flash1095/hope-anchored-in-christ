hekatontaplasíōn

from G1540 and a presumed derivative of G4111; a hundred times:--hundredfold.

Biblical Usage

Hekatontaplasion means a hundred times as much, and Jesus promises it to those who leave everything. In Luke 8:8 the good ground bears fruit a hundredfold, in Mark 10:30 the one who leaves houses and lands for the gospel receives a hundredfold now in this time, and in Matthew 19:29 he shall inherit everlasting life.

Theological Significance

I believe the hundredfold is not a bargain but a promise: what is left for him is not lost, it is multiplied, and the everlasting life at the end is the part that makes the arithmetic true. I hold that the promise covers both the present and the age to come, and the one who leaves everything finds he has lost nothing.
