Baʻănâʼ

Banana, the name of four Israelite

Biblical Usage

Baana is the name of three men — two of Solomon's twelve officers, one over Taanach and Megiddo and one over Asher, and a wall-builder who repaired the wall in Nehemiah's day with his sons. The king's officers and the wall-builder share one name, and both served the kingdom in their own generation.

Theological Significance

I hold that officers who feed a king's table and builders who repair a broken wall do the same work in God's eyes: keeping the kingdom standing.
