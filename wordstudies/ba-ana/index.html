Baʻănâʼ

Word
בַּעֲנָא
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Baʻănâʼ
Strongs Number
H1195
Definition
Banana, the name of four Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Baana or Baanah = "in the affliction"; 1) the son of Ahilud, Solomon's commissariat officer in Jezreel and the north of the Jordan valley; 2) another of Solomon's officers; 3) father of Zadok who assisted in rebuilding the wall of Jerusalem under Nehemiah
