piqqûwd

properly, appointed, i.e. a mandate (of God; plural only, collectively, for the Law)

Biblical Usage

Piqqud is a precept, a mandate from God — the word Psalm 119 uses over and over for His appointed ways. The psalmist says, Through Thy precepts I get understanding, in Psalms 119:104, and, I will never forget Thy precepts, for with them Thou hast quickened me, in Psalms 119:93. God commanded us to keep His precepts diligently in Psalms 119:4, and Psalms 19:8 says the statutes of the LORD are right, rejoicing the heart.

Theological Significance

I believe God's precepts are not a burden to carry but a light to walk by — they give understanding and they give life. That is why I hold that loving His precepts is the same as loving Him.
