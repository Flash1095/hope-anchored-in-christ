Yâkîyn

Word
יָכִין
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Yâkîyn
Strongs Number
H3199
Definition
Jakin, the name of three Israelites and of a temple pillar
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Jachin = "He will establish"; n pr m; 1) the 4th son of Simeon and founder of the family of Jachinites; 2) a priest and the head of the 21st coursein the time of David; 3) a post-exilic priest in the time of Nehemiah; n pr; 4) name of the right-hand pillar before the temple
