oikodomḗ

feminine (abstract) of a compound of G3624 and the base of G1430; architecture, i.e. (concretely) a structure; figuratively, confirmation:--building, edify(-ication, -ing).

Biblical Usage

Oikodome is a building, and by figure, edification, the building up of the church. The disciples point out the buildings of the temple in Matthew 24:1 and Mark 13:1, and Paul turns the word to its true use in Romans 14:19, following after the things that edify, and in 1 Corinthians 3:9, where believers are God's building.

Theological Significance

I believe the temple's stones were never the point, because the building God is raising is His people. That is why I hold that edification is the real architecture: every word that builds a brother up is a stone set in place.
