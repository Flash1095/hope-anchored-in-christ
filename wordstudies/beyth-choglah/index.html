Word

Transliteration
Bêyth Choglâh
Strongs Number

Definition
Beth-Choglah, a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Beth-hoglah = "house of the partridge" or "place of partridge"; 1) a place in Benjamin on the border with Judah
