lithoboléō

from a compound of G3037 and G906; to throw stones, i.e. lapidate:--stone, cast stones.

Biblical Usage

lithoboléō means to stone, to cast stones, and the Bible uses it the way ordinary people use ordinary words — for everything. In Acts 7:59, "And they stoned Stephen"; and in Matthew 21:35, "and stoned another.". In Luke 13:34, "and stonest them that are sent unto thee"; and in Hebrews 12:20, "it shall be stoned".

Theological Significance

I believe the stones thrown at Stephen are the Church's first blood — he called on the Lord Jesus and received His spirit, and Saul watched the coats. I hold that Jerusalem kills the prophets and stones them that are sent, and still the Lord would have gathered her children. My position is that I will stand with Stephen, not with the throwers.
