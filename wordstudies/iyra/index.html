ʻÎyrâʼ

Ira, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Ira is the name of three men who served David, and the Bible keeps them apart by their fathers and their towns. In 2 Samuel 20:26 Ira the Jairite is chief ruler about David, in 2 Samuel 23:26 Ira the son of Ikkesh the Tekoite stands among the mighty men, and in 1 Chronicles 11:28 the same Ira the son of Ikkesh is listed again.

Theological Significance

I hold that the three Iras show how the kingdom ran: a ruler, a mighty man, and a captain of the course, all serving the same king.
