Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
dûwsh
Strongs Number

Definition
to trample or thresh
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to tread out, thresh; 1a) (Qal) to tread on, trample on, thresh; 1b) (Niphal) to be trampled down; 1c) (Hophal) to be threshed
