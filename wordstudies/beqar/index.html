Word

Language

Transliteration
bᵉqar
Strongs Number
H1240
Definition
{properly, to plough, or (generally) break forth, i.e. (figuratively) to inspect, admire, care for, consider}
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to seek, enquire; 1a) (Pael) to enquire; 1b) (Ithpael) to let search be made
