Rᵉchôbôwth

Word
רְחֹבוֹת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Rᵉchôbôwth
Strongs Number
H7344
Definition
Rechoboth, a place in Assyria and one in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Rehoboth = "wide places or streets"; 1) the 3rd of a series of wells dug by Isaac in the territory of the Philistines; 2) one of the 4 cities built by Asshur or by Nimrod in Asshur located close to Nineveh; 3) a city of Saul or Shaul, one of the early kings of the Edomites
