makrós

from G3372; long (in place (distant) or time (neuter plural)):--far, long.

Biblical Usage

Makros means long, far, distant, and it is the word for how far away we were. Ephesians 2:13 says ye who sometimes were far off are made nigh by the blood of Christ, and Ephesians 2:17 says He preached peace to you which were afar off and to them that were nigh. Acts 2:39 says the promise is unto you and to all that are afar off, as many as the Lord our God shall call, while Luke 20:47 turns the word against the scribes who devour widows' houses and for a shew make long prayers.

Theological Significance

I believe this word is the measure of grace: the distance in Ephesians 2:13 was real, and the blood of Christ closed it. My position is that Luke 20:47 is the warning on the other side, because long prayers do not make up for a long way from God.
