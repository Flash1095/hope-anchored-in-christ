Maʻăkâh

Maakah (or Maakath), the name of a place in Syria, also of a Mesopotamian, of three Israelites, and of four Israelitesses and one Syrian woman

Biblical Usage

Maakah is a name worn by many, and Scripture keeps them apart by their stories. One Maacah was the concubine of Nahor, Abraham's brother, and another was the daughter of Talmai, king of Geshur, who became David's wife and the mother of Absalom. A later Maachah was the mother of King Abijam, and the Chronicles call her the mother of Asa as well.

Theological Significance

I believe the many Maakahs remind us that a name is not a person, and God knows each one by her own story.
