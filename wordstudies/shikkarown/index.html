shikkârôwn

intoxication

Biblical Usage

Jeremiah hears that every inhabitant of the land will be filled with drunkenness, Ezekiel watches the cup of astonishment poured out, and the feasters of Gog are told they will drink blood till they are drunken. The word is not the cheer of the wine cup but the stupor of judgment.

Theological Significance

I believe drunkenness in the Bible is mostly a picture of judgment, not pleasure, because God uses it for a people that has lost its senses. I hold that a sober mind is a gift worth guarding, and I do not want the cup Jeremiah describes.
