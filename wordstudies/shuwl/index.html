shûwl

a skirt; by implication, a bottom edge

Biblical Usage

Shul is the hem, the skirt, the bottom edge of a robe, and it appears where holiness and shame meet. The high priest's robe had pomegranates and golden bells around its hem, and the prophets say Jerusalem's skirts were discovered for the greatness of her iniquity, her nakedness shown to the nations.

Theological Significance

I believe the hem tells the truth about the robe. Aaron's hem rang with bells of gold, and Jerusalem's hem was lifted in judgment, and I hold that what hangs at the edge of my life shows what is at the centre of it.
