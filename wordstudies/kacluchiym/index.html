Kaçluchîym

Word
כַּסְלֻחִים
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Kaçluchîym
Strongs Number
H3695
Definition
Casluchim, a people cognate to the Egyptians
Part Of Speech
noun
Biblical Usage
Casluhim = "fortified"; 1) a people or tribe descended from Mizraim (Egypt) progenitors of the Philistines and Caphtorim
