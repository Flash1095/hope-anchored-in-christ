Yachăzîyʼêl

Jachaziel, the name of five Israelites

Biblical Usage

Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests stood with trumpets continually before the ark of the covenant. The Spirit of the Lord came upon Jahaziel the son of Zechariah in the midst of the congregation.

Theological Significance

I believe the two Jahaziels show the priesthood and the prophecy side by side: one stands with trumpets before the ark day after day, and the other speaks once with the Spirit upon him, and both are needed.
