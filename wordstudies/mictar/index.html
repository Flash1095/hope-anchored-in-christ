miçtâr

properly, a concealer, i.e. a covert

Biblical Usage

God promises the treasures of darkness and hidden riches of secret places, and asks whether any man can hide in secret places where He will not see him, and Habakkuk sees the enemy rejoicing as if to devour the poor secretly. The word is the covert itself, the hiding place, and it hides both God's treasure and man's sin.

Theological Significance

I believe the secret place cuts both ways: God keeps His treasures there for those who seek Him, and no man hides his sin there from the God who fills heaven and earth. No man hides his sin in the secret place from the God who fills heaven and earth.
