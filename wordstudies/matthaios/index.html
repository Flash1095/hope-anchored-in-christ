Matthaîos

a shorter form of G3161; Matthæus (i.e. Matthitjah), an Israelite and a Christian:--Matthew.

Biblical Usage

Matthew is the apostle who sat at the receipt of custom when Jesus called him, and followed at once. He is named among the twelve, and he is there in the upper room after the ascension, waiting with the rest for the promise.

Theological Significance

I believe Matthew is the proof that Jesus calls the unlikely: a tax collector left his table the moment he was told to follow, and the man who took Rome's money became the one who wrote the gospel for the Jews.
