nôkach

properly, the front part; used adverbially (especially with preposition), opposite, in front of, forward, in behalf of

Biblical Usage

Before is the facing side, the front of the thing. The lampstand stands over against the table in the tabernacle, the priest sprinkles blood directly before the tabernacle, the border looks toward Gilgal, and Isaac entreats the LORD for his wife, standing before Him.

Theological Significance

I believe the facing side is where worship happens: the table faces the lampstand, and the blood is sprinkled before the tent of meeting. The border looks toward Gilgal, and the lampstand stands over against the table — everything in the tabernacle faces something.
