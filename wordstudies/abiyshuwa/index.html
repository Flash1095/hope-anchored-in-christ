ʼĂbîyshûwaʻ

Abishua, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Abishua is the name of two Israelites. One is the son of Phinehas in the high-priestly line — ‘Phinehas begat Abishua’ — in the genealogy of Aaron and of Ezra himself; the other is a son of Bela in the tribe of Benjamin.

Theological Significance

A priest in Aaron's line and a Benjamite with the same name — one carries the priesthood forward, the other simply belongs to the tribe. I believe the two Abishuas show how God's record works: the famous line and the quiet line are written on the same page, and both are His.
