shîyr

a song; abstractly, singing

Biblical Usage

Shir is the ordinary word for a song, and it covers everything from Moses singing at the sea to David singing his deliverance song to the Levites singing at the dedication of the wall. The word sits at the head of many psalms as their title, and the prophets call for a new song when God does a new thing. Singing is never decoration here; it is how God's people answer what He has done.

Theological Significance

I believe a song is faith you can hear. When I sing what God has done, I am doing what Moses and David did, telling the truth about Him in words I can carry. I hold that a people who stop singing have started forgetting.
