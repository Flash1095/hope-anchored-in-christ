ʼĂrâm

Aram or Syria, and its inhabitants; also the name of the son of Shem, a grandson of Nahor, and of an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Aram is both a man and a nation. Aram the son of Shem stands in the table of nations, the Syrians of Damascus come to help Hadadezer and fall before David, Naaman captains the host of the king of Syria, and Rezin of Syria marches on Jerusalem in the days of Ahaz.

Theological Significance

I hold that Aram is the neighbour Israel could never ignore, sometimes the enemy at the gate and sometimes the servant who carries God's healing home, and God used them both ways.
