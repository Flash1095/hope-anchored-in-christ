aneleḗmōn

from G1 (as a negative particle) and G1655; merciless:--unmerciful.

Biblical Usage

Paul lists the unmerciful among the marks of a mind given over to itself: without understanding, covenantbreakers, without natural affection, implacable, unmerciful. The word names the heart that refuses pity to anyone.

Theological Significance

A single word for mercilessness holds no doctrine of its own; its darkness is Paul's, that a heart without mercy is one of the signs of a heart God has left to itself.
