bâdad

to divide, i.e. (reflex.) be solitary

Biblical Usage

Badad as a verb means to be solitary, to separate oneself — and Hosea says Ephraim is a wild ass alone by himself, Isaiah says the burden of the wilderness shall be borne alone, and Leviticus commands the leper to dwell alone outside the camp. Solitude can be rebellion, or judgment, or the lonely road of the outcast.

Theological Significance

I hold that the wild ass alone by himself is Ephraim's picture and mine, whenever I hire lovers and run from God. The leper outside the camp is the other side: solitude imposed by law, until the cleansing comes.
