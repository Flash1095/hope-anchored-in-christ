Nangaí

probably of Hebrew origin (compare H5052); Nangæ (i.e. perhaps Nogach), an Israelite:--Nagge.

Biblical Usage

Nangai is a name in the genealogy of Jesus, and it appears once in Luke's line. In Luke 3:25 the line runs from Mattathias through Amos and Naum and Esli down to Nagge, in the generations between David and Christ.

Theological Significance

I believe a name in the genealogy is a link, and the chain is only as strong as its links. Nagge holds his place at the foot of that little run, the father of Esli, and the line goes on through him.
