môwrâʼ

fear; by implication, a fearful thing or deed

Biblical Usage

Mora is fear, dread, a fearful thing. The fear and dread of man falls on every beast, God redeems Israel with a mighty hand and great terrors, Isaiah says sanctify the LORD and let Him be your fear, and Levi's covenant was life and peace, and he feared God and was afraid before His name.

Theological Significance

I believe there are two fears and they are opposites. The fear of man is a snare, and the fear of God is the beginning of freedom.
