hârag

to smite with deadly intent

Biblical Usage

Harag means to smite with deadly intent, to kill, and its first appearance is the worst of all. In Genesis 4:8 Cain rose up against Abel and slew him, in Exodus 2:14 Moses is accused of slaying the Egyptian, in 1 Samuel 24:10 David refuses to slay Saul when he has him in his power, and in 2 Samuel 12:9 Nathan charges David with slaying Uriah by the sword.

Theological Significance

I believe the word is blunt on purpose: the Bible does not dress killing up. Cain did it in anger, Moses did it in fear, David refused it once and did it once, and the difference between those choices is the whole story of a life.
