Tishbîy

Word
תִּשְׁבִּי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Tishbîy
Strongs Number
H8664
Definition
a Tishbite or inhabitant of Tishbeh (in Gilead)
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Tishbite = "captivity"; n pr loc; 1) the home of Elijah; 1a) site unknown but maybe the town of Tishbe; adj gent; 2) an inhabitant of Tishbe or Tishbi or Tesheb; 2a) site unknown and name uncertain
