Tishbîy

a Tishbite or inhabitant of Tishbeh (in Gilead)

Biblical Usage

The Tishbite is Elijah, and the title is his by origin. He comes from the inhabitants of Gilead and stands before Ahab to say there shall be no dew nor rain but by his word, and the angel still calls him Elijah the Tishbite when he is sent to meet the messengers of Samaria's king.

Theological Significance

I believe the title matters because Elijah never loses it: prophet, fugitive, fire-caller, and always the man from Tishbeh, whose hometown stays attached to his name through every story. I hold that God uses nobodies from nowhere places, and the Tishbite is the proof.
