kosméō

from G2889; to put in proper order, i.e. decorate (literally or figuratively); specially, to snuff (a wick):--adorn, garnish, trim.

Biblical Usage

Kosmeo means to adorn, to put in proper order, to garnish. Matthew 12:44 and Luke 11:25 have the unclean spirit finding his house empty, swept, and garnished, 1 Timothy 2:9 says women should adorn themselves in modest apparel, Titus 2:10 says servants should adorn the doctrine of God our Saviour in all things, 1 Peter 3:5 says the holy women of old adorned themselves, trusting in God, and Revelation 21:2 sees the holy city, new Jerusalem, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband.

Theological Significance

I believe adorning is the question of every life: the swept and garnished house is empty and the devil returns, but the bride adorned for her husband is full of glory. I hold that Titus 2:10 is the true adornment, because a servant's fidelity adorns the doctrine of God better than gold or pearls.
