Kornḗlios

of Latin origin; Cornelius, a Roman:--Cornelius.

Biblical Usage

Cornelius is a Roman centurion, the first Gentile convert. Acts 10:1 names him a centurion of the band called the Italian band, Acts 10:3 shows him seeing an angel of God in a vision, and Acts 10:17 has the men sent from Cornelius standing at Simon's gate while Peter doubted what his vision meant.

Theological Significance

I believe Cornelius is the door of the Gentiles: a Roman soldier who prayed and gave alms, and whose vision sent for Peter. I hold that the angel coming to a centurion is the sign that the gospel was never only for Israel, because God heard a Gentile's prayers.
