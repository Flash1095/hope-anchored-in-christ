ʼârêk

long

Biblical Usage

The word means long, and it is the Bible's picture of God's patience. The Lord proclaims Himself longsuffering, slow to anger, Moses pleads the same name when Israel rebels, Jonah complains that God is slow to anger and ready to relent, and Nehemiah remembers that God was gracious and merciful and slow to anger when His people refused to listen.

Theological Significance

I hold that God's longsuffering is the reason any of us are still here. He is slow to anger and ready to pardon, and Jonah's complaint about it is the best news in the Bible for sinners.
