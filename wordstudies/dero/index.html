dérō

a primary verb; properly, to flay, i.e. (by implication) to scourge, or (by analogy) to thrash:--beat, smite.

Biblical Usage

To beat or smite is the violence done to servants, to prophets, and to Christ Himself. The husbandmen beat the servants sent for fruit, the officer struck Jesus for His answer, and Paul reminds the Corinthians that they even bear being struck on the face.

Theological Significance

I believe the beatings in Scripture are the cost of telling the truth. The servants were sent in love and answered with fists, and Christ bore the same blows, so that no suffering servant of His ever suffers alone.
