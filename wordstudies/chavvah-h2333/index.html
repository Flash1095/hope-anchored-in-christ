chavvâh

by implication, an encampment or village

Biblical Usage

A havvah is an encampment or village, a small town taken and named. Jair the son of Manasseh takes the small towns and calls them Havoth-jair, the towns of Jair are counted in Joshua's allotment of Bashan, and Solomon's officer holds the towns of Jair in Gilead.

Theological Significance

I believe God gives His people towns to name, and the naming is part of the taking. Jair called the villages after his own name, and the land remembered it.
