Bilhâh

Word
בִּלְהָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Bilhâh
Strongs Number
H1090
Definition
Bilhah, the name of one of Jacob's concubines; also of a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Bilhah = "troubled"; n pr f; 1) Rachel's handmaid whom she gave to Jacob as a concubine, mother to two of Jacob's children, Dan and Naphtali; n prloc; 2) a city in Simeon
