Bilhâh

Bilhah, the name of one of Jacob's concubines; also of a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Bilhah means troubled, and she is Rachel's handmaid, given to Jacob as a wife, and the mother of Dan and Naphtali. Genesis 29:29 says Laban gave Bilhah to Rachel, Genesis 30:3 shows Rachel giving her to Jacob so that she might have children by her, Genesis 35:22 records Reuben going in to her, and Genesis 35:25 lists Dan and Naphtali as the sons of Bilhah.

Theological Significance

I believe Bilhah's story is one of the hardest in Genesis: a woman used by others, named troubled, and still the mother of two tribes of Israel.
