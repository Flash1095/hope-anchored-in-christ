Charchûwr

Charchur, one of the Nethinim

Biblical Usage

Harhur is named twice, among the children of the Nethinim who returned from exile with Zerubbabel. Both lists place his family among the temple servants who came back to serve.

Theological Significance

I believe the return list is a roll call of the lowly: Harhur's family came home with the priests and the singers.
