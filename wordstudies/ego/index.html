egṓ

a primary pronoun of the first person I (only expressed when emphatic):--I, me. For the other cases and the plural see G1691, G1698, G1700, G2248, G2249, G2254, G2257, etc.

Biblical Usage

I is the word a speaker uses when the emphasis falls on himself, and in the New Testament it carries the weight of who is speaking. Jesus says I am before Abraham was, I am the good shepherd, and I am the way, the truth, and the life, and Paul says I am what I am by the grace of God. The little word matters because the person behind it matters.

Theological Significance

I believe the I am of Jesus is the claim of God Himself, because no mere man can say before Abraham was, I am, and be telling the truth. My position is that everything stands or falls on who He says He is, and I believe Him.
