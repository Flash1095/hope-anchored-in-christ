chămôwth

a mother-in-law

Biblical Usage

Chamowth is a mother-in-law, and Ruth's story is the reason the word feels warm. Ruth clung to Naomi after Orpah kissed her and went back, Boaz praised Ruth for all she had done for her mother-in-law since her husband died, and Naomi herself took up the planning for Ruth's future, saying she would seek rest for her. Micah shows the other side: in a broken house, a daughter-in-law rises against her mother-in-law.

Theological Significance

I believe Naomi and Ruth redeem the word from every joke about mothers-in-law. Loyalty made them family twice over, by marriage and then by choice. I hold that Micah's warning is the shadow of it: when a house forgets God, even the closest bonds turn against each other.
