embaínō

from G1722 and the base of G939; to walk on, i.e. embark (aboard a vessel), reach (a pool):--come (get) into, enter (into), go (up) into, step in, take ship.

Biblical Usage

The word embaínō carries the sense of to enter, to embark. In Matthew 8:23, the text says "And when he was entered into a ship." In Mark 4:1, the text says "so that he entered into a ship."

Theological Significance

embaínō convinces me that the gospel was never meant to stay where it started — it goes out, because God sends it out. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
