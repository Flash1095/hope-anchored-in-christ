pᵉlêyṭâh

deliverance; concretely, an escaped portion

Biblical Usage

Peleytah is deliverance, the escaped portion that survives. Joseph told his brothers God sent him before them to preserve a posterity and save their lives by a great deliverance. Ezra thanked God for leaving a remnant to escape, and Isaiah says the remnant of Israel shall stay upon the LORD. Joel promises that whoever calls on the name of the LORD shall be delivered, for in Mount Zion there shall be deliverance.

Theological Significance

I am convinced that deliverance is always a remnant story: God does not save by the multitude but by the few He keeps. Joseph saw it in Egypt, Ezra saw it after exile, and Joel preached it to all who would call. I hold that calling on the name of the LORD is itself the deliverance — the escape is not a place but a Person, and He leaves a remnant in every age.
