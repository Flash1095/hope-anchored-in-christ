tîymârâh

a column, i.e. cloud

Biblical Usage

A column of cloud is the picture, in judgment and in love. Joel promises wonders in the heavens and the earth, blood and fire and pillars of smoke, and the Song asks who comes out of the wilderness like pillars of smoke, perfumed with myrrh and frankincense.

Theological Significance

I believe the same column means two things at once: the smoke of judgment over the land, and the fragrant procession of the beloved coming home.
