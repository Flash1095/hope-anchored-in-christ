râmam

to rise (literally or figuratively)

Biblical Usage

Ramam is to rise up. Moses and Aaron fell on their faces when God said, 'Get you up from among this congregation, that I may consume them as in a moment'; the psalm says, 'The right hand of the LORD is exalted: the right hand of the LORD doeth valiantly'; and Job says the wicked 'are exalted for a little while, but are gone.'

Theological Significance

This tells me that rising up is God's to command: He tells His people to rise out of judgment, He exalts His own right hand, and He cuts down the wicked who exalted themselves.
