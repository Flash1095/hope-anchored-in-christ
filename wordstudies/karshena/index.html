Karshᵉnâʼ

Karshena, a courtier of Xerxes

Biblical Usage

Karshena is a courtier of Xerxes, one of the seven princes of Persia and Media who saw the king's face and sat first in the kingdom. When the king asked what should be done to Vashti, Karshena was among those who answered.

Theological Significance

I believe Karshena shows how God uses even a king's council: the princes gave their counsel, the queen was set aside, and the way opened for Esther — and none of them knew what they were doing.
