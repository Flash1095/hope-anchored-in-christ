qodqôd

the crown of the head (as the part most bowed)

Biblical Usage

Absalom is praised from the sole of his foot even to the crown of his head, with no blemish in him. The curse runs the same measure: from the sole of thy foot unto the top of thy head.

Theological Significance

I believe Absalom's perfect crown of head could not save him, and the law's curse runs from sole to crown.
