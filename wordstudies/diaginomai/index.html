diagínomai

from G1223 and G1096; to elapse meanwhile:--X after, be past, be spent.

Biblical Usage

This word means time passing by, days going on until a moment arrives. The Sabbath passes and the women come to the tomb, some days pass before Agrippa and Bernice arrive, and Paul's voyage drags on until the fast is past.

Theological Significance

I believe the passing of days is where faith is tested. Nothing happens quickly in these verses — a Sabbath, some days, a long voyage — and God keeps His people through the waiting as surely as through the wonders.
