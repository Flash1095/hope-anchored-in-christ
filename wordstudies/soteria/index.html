soteria

Word
σωτηρία
Language
Greek
Transliteration
soteria
Strongs Number
G4991
Definition
Salvation, deliverance and preservation — God's rescue of His people from sin, death and judgement into life and wholeness.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Luke 19:9; Acts 4:12; Philippians 2:12; Titus 2:11
Biblical Usage
Soteria echoes Exodus rescue language and fills it out in Christ: salvation has come to Zacchaeus's house, and there is salvation in no one else. It spans past rescue, present working-out and future completion.
Theological Significance
Soteria is comprehensive rescue — forgiven, being transformed, and certain of glory. The God who began salvation will complete it, which is why hope does not disappoint.
