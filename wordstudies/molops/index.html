mṓlōps

from μῶλος ("moil"; probably akin to the base of G3433) and probably ὤψ (the face; from G3700); a mole ("black eye") or blow-mark:--stripe.

Biblical Usage

Molops is a bruise, a weal, the mark of a blow — Peter says Christ bore our sins in His own body on the tree, that we should live unto righteousness, by whose stripes ye were healed.

Theological Significance

I believe the bruise is the price written on His body: healing comes through wounds, not around them.
