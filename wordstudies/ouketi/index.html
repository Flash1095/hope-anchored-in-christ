oukéti

, also (separately) οὐκ ἔτι from G3756 and G2089; not yet, no longer:--after that (not), (not) any more, henceforth (hereafter) not, no longer (more), not as yet (now), now no more (not), yet (not).

Biblical Usage

No longer, or no more, marks what has ended. When Philip was caught away, the eunuch saw him no more, and went on his way rejoicing, and Paul said he called God for a record that to spare the Corinthians he came not as yet unto Corinth.

Theological Significance

I believe no longer is the word of a changed life: the eunuch saw Philip no more, and Paul would not come to Corinth again in heaviness — some things end, and that is grace.
