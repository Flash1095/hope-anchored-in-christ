gâbâhh

lofty (literally or figuratively)

Biblical Usage

Gabahh means lofty, high — and it is almost always a warning. The patient in spirit is better than the proud in spirit in Ecclesiastes 7:8, and everyone that is proud in heart is an abomination to the LORD in Proverbs 16:5. The psalmist says God will not suffer a high look and a proud heart in Psalms 101:5, and the Assyrian's high stature in Ezekiel 31:3 is the picture of an empire about to fall.

Theological Significance

I believe height is a good gift until it becomes a high look. The cedar's top among the thick boughs is beautiful, but God cuts down what lifts itself above Him. I hold that the only safe height is the low place at His feet.
