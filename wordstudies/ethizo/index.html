ethízō

from G1485; to accustom, i.e. (neuter passive participle) customary:--custom.

Biblical Usage

The word ethízō covers to accustom. It occurs just once, in Luke.

Theological Significance

I take ethízō as it stands — to accustom — and read the passage with that plain sense in mind.
