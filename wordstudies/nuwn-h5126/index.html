Nûwn

Nun or Non, the father of Joshua

Biblical Usage

Nun is the father of Joshua, and his name is attached to his son's in nearly every mention, Joshua the son of Nun. In Numbers 13:16 Moses renames Oshea the son of Nun as Jehoshua when he sends him to spy out the land, and in Joshua 1:1 the Lord speaks to Joshua the son of Nun after the death of Moses.

Theological Significance

I hold that a father's name can outlive him in his son's story, and Nun is remembered because Joshua was faithful.
