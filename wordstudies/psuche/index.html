psuche

Word
ψυχή
Language
Greek
Transliteration
psuche
Strongs Number
G5590
Definition
Soul, life and inner self — the living person, seat of mind, will and emotion; the Greek counterpart to Hebrew nephesh.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Matthew 16:26; Matthew 11:29; 3 John 1:2; Mark 8:36
Biblical Usage
Psuche is what Jesus says can be gained or lost with the whole world at stake — and what He invites to find rest in Him: 'you will find rest for your souls.' It is the precious, eternal core of a person.
Theological Significance
Psuche guards our priorities: nothing profits a person who trades their soul away. In Christ, the soul finds the rest, worth and safekeeping it was made for.
Related Topics
Soul; Salvation; Rest
Related Books
Matthew; Mark; 3 John
Related People
Jesus
