qârach

to depilate

Biblical Usage

To make bald is the mourning the law forbids and the prophets command: the priests shall not make baldness upon their head, Micah tells the people to make themselves bald for their delicate children, and Jeremiah says neither great nor small shall make themselves bald for the dead.

Theological Significance

I take the baldness as the body's grief, and the law's ban as the line between grief and the worship of the dead.
