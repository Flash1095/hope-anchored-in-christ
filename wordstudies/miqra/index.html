miqrâʼ

something called out, i.e. a public meeting (the act, the persons, or the place); also a rehearsal

Biblical Usage

The convocation is the called assembly, the holy gathering, and the word runs from the feasts to the reading of the law. The first and seventh days of unleavened bread were holy convocations, the appointed feasts were proclaimed as holy convocations, and when Ezra read the law the Levites caused the people to understand the reading.

Theological Significance

I believe the gathering is the point of the feast. God calls His people together to hear His word, and a people that stops assembling stops hearing.
