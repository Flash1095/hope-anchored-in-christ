pûwg

to be sluggish

Biblical Usage

Pug means to be sluggish, to faint, to grow numb. When Jacob was told Joseph was alive, his heart fainted, for he believed them not, and Habakkuk says the law is slacked, and judgment doth never go forth. And David says, I am feeble and sore broken, I have roared by reason of the disquietness of my heart.

Theological Significance

I hold that fainting is the body's answer to too much news at once: Jacob's heart fainted at joy, David's at sorrow, and the law itself went slack when no one would enforce it.
