Tarshîysh

Tarshish, a place on the Mediterranean, hence, the ephithet of a merchant vessel (as if for or from that port); also the name of a Persian and of an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Tarshish is a far-off port, a place of ships and trade, and also the name of men. Jonah rose up to flee unto Tarshish from the presence of the Lord and found a ship going there; Solomon had a navy of Tharshish that came once in three years bringing gold and silver, ivory, apes, and peacocks; and Isaiah says the ships of Tarshish shall bring the sons of Israel from far.

Theological Significance

I believe Tarshish is the far edge of the known world in the Bible, and Jonah's flight there shows how far a man will run from God.
