Shᵉmaryâh

Shemarjah, the name of four Israelites

Biblical Usage

Shemariah is the name of four men: a Haruphite who came to David at Ziklag, and a son of Bani who put away his foreign wife in the days of Ezra. The name means kept by Jehovah.

Theological Significance

I believe kept by Jehovah fits both men: one kept by God in David's band, one who kept the covenant by putting away what God forbade. I hold that being kept and keeping are the same grace.
