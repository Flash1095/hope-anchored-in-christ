ánēthon

probably of foreign origin; dill:--anise.

Biblical Usage

Jesus rebukes the Pharisees for tithing mint and anise and cummin while omitting the weightier matters of the law: judgment, mercy, and faith. The word is dill, a garden herb measured out to the tenth part.

Theological Significance

One herb in one rebuke holds no theology of its own; its point is Jesus', that men will measure dill to the tenth part and skip justice altogether.
