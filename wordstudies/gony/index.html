góny

of uncertain affinity; the "knee":--knee(X -l).

Biblical Usage

In the New Testament, góny is the word for knee(X -l). In Mark 15:19, the text says "and bowing [their] knees worshipped him." In Luke 5:8, the text says "he fell down at Jesus' knees."

Theological Significance

Reading góny this way, I believe worship is not a performance but a response — we answer what God has done with what we are. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
