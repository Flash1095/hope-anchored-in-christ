ʻĂṭârôwth

Word
עֲטָרוֹת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻĂṭârôwth
Strongs Number
H5852
Definition
Ataroth, the name (thus simply) of two places in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Ataroth = "crowns"; 1) a town east of the Jordan in Gilead, taken and built by the tribe of Gad; 2) a place on the boundary of Ephraim and Manasseh; 3) a place between Ephraim and Benjamin
