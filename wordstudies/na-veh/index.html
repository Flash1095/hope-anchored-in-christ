nâʼveh

suitable, or beautiful

Biblical Usage

Na'veh means suitable or beautiful, comely or delicate, and it is used for a woman and for speech. In Jeremiah 6:2 the daughter of Zion is likened to a comely and delicate woman, and in Proverbs 17:7 excellent speech is said to become a fool no more than lying lips become a prince.

Theological Significance

I hold that beauty without truth is only decoration, and the proverb proves it; fine words sound wrong in a fool's mouth because the life does not match the language.
