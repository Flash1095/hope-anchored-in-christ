kritḗrion

neuter of a presumed derivative of G2923; a rule of judging ("criterion"), i.e. (by implication) a tribunal:--to judge, judgment (seat).

Biblical Usage

The judgment seat here is the court, the place where disputes are settled. Paul asks the Corinthians in 1 Corinthians 6:2 whether they are unworthy "to judge the smallest matters," and James says in James 2:6 that the rich "draw you before the judgment seats."

Theological Significance

In my reading, the word warns me twice: the church should be able to settle its own small matters, and the world will drag the poor before its courts, and both are reasons to keep the church honest.
