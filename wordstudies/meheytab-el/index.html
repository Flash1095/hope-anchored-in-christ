Mᵉhêyṭabʼêl

Word
מְהֵיטַבְאֵל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Mᵉhêyṭabʼêl
Strongs Number
H4105
Definition
Mehetabel, the name of an Edomitish man and woman
Part Of Speech
proper noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
Mehetabeel or Mehetabel = "favoured of God"; n pr m; 1) the ancestor of Shemaiah, the false prophet who was hired against Nehemiah by Tobiah and Sanballat; n pr f; 2) the daughter of Matred and wife of king Hadad of Edom
