emunah

Word
אֱמוּנָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
emunah
Strongs Number
H530
Definition
Faithfulness, steadfastness and firm trustworthiness; by extension, the steady faith that trusts and obeys God.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Deuteronomy 32:4; Habakkuk 2:4; Lamentations 3:22-23; Psalm 36:5
Biblical Usage
Emunah most often describes God Himself — 'the Rock, His work is perfect... a God of faithfulness.' Habakkuk's 'the righteous shall live by his faith' became a cornerstone text quoted by Paul.
Theological Significance
Emunah reminds us faith is not a leap in the dark but trust in a God who is utterly steady. Our faith rests on His faithfulness first.

Faith; Faithfulness of God; Trust
