Rhōmaïkós

from G4514; Romaic, i.e. Latin:--Latin.

Biblical Usage

A superscription also was written over Him in letters of Greek, and Latin, and Hebrew: THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS. The Greek text behind the King James Version reads the Latin word here, while the text I have checked carries no list of languages at this verse.

Theological Significance

My position is that the title was written in three tongues because the King it named belongs to the whole world.
