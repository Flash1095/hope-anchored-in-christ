smýrna

apparently strengthened for G3464; myrrh:--myrrh.

Biblical Usage

Myrrh is there at the beginning and at the end. The wise men open their treasures and present gold, frankincense, and myrrh to the child, and Nicodemus brings a mixture of myrrh and aloes, about an hundred pound weight, to bury Jesus.

Theological Significance

I believe the myrrh tells the whole story in one gift: the child worshipped was born to die, and the burial spices were the other side of the worship.
