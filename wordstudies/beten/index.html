beṭen

the belly, especially the womb; also the bosom or body of anything

Biblical Usage

beten covers belly, womb, and it appears 72 times across the Bible. Two nations were in Rebekah's womb, the LORD promised to bless the fruit of the womb, and Ehud's dagger went into Eglon's belly.

Theological Significance

I hold that God knows us before birth, and that the womb is His workshop, not an accident. That is why I keep coming back to it: beten is not a word to rush past.
