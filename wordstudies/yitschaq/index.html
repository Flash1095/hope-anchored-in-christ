Yitschâq

Jitschak (or Isaac), son of Abraham

Biblical Usage

Isaac is the son of promise, and his name is woven through the covenant. In Genesis 17:19 God says, "Sarah thy wife shall bear thee a son indeed; and thou shalt call his name Isaac." Exodus 2:24 remembers the covenant "with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob," and Leviticus 26:42 promises, "Then will I remember my covenant with Jacob, and also my covenant with Isaac."

Theological Significance

I believe Isaac shows me that God's promises do not depend on my strength. I hold that a child of laughter, nearly offered and then given back, is the pattern of a God who provides, and that the covenant remembered in Exodus 2:24 and Leviticus 26:42 still stands.
