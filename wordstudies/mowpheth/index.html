môwphêth

a miracle; by implication, a token or omen

Biblical Usage

Mopheth is a wonder, a sign, a token. God multiplies His signs and wonders in Egypt, Aaron's rod becomes a serpent before Pharaoh, and Joel promises wonders in the heavens and in the earth, blood and fire and pillars of smoke.

Theological Significance

I believe wonders are God's signature. He does not argue His power in the abstract, He shows it, and the showing is the proof.
