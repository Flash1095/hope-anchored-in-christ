ṭâraph

to pluck off or pull to pieces; causatively to supply with food (as in morsels)

Biblical Usage

The word is to tear, to rend like a wild beast. Jacob looked at Joseph's coat and cried, Joseph is without doubt rent in pieces; and Hosea says, Come, and let us return unto the Lord, for He hath torn, and He will heal us. The law says if a beast is torn in pieces, the torn flesh shall be brought for witness, and the owner shall not make it good.

Theological Significance

I believe the God who tears is the God who heals, and the torn coat was not the end of Joseph's story.
