matstsebeth

something stationary, i.e. a monumental stone; also the stock of a tree

Biblical Usage

The word is a standing stone, used for Absalom's pillar in the king's dale, which he raised because he had no son to keep his name in remembrance, and for the pillar Jacob set over Rachel's grave. Isaiah uses the same word for the stock of a tree that remains after it is cut down.

Theological Significance

I hold that the word is about what remains after a life: Absalom built a stone to keep his name alive, and Rachel's pillar still marks a grave, but only the stump Isaiah saw carries the promise of new life.
