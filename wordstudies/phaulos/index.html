phaûlos

apparently a primary word; "foul" or "flawy", i.e. (figuratively) wicked:--evil.

Biblical Usage

Phaulos means foul, worthless, evil, and it is the word for deeds done in the dark. Everyone that does evil hates the light and will not come to it, and Jesus says those who have done evil shall come forth to the resurrection of damnation. James says where envying and strife is, there is confusion and every evil work, and Paul tells Titus to speak so that the one who opposes has no evil thing to say of us.

Theological Significance

I believe phaulos is the right word for sin, because it is not grand rebellion but small foulness, the worthless thing done where no one sees. The light exposes it, the resurrection judges it, and the church's speech is meant to leave it nothing to say. My position is that I cannot call my foul deeds harmless: what is done in the dark will be brought to the light, and only coming to the light now will save me then.
