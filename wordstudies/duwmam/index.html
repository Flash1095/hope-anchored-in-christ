dûwmâm

still; adverbially, silently

Biblical Usage

Silently, in silence, the command to be silent before an idol that cannot speak, and the call to sit in silence as the daughter of the Babylonians falls. Jeremiah says it is good that a man both hope and quietly wait for the salvation of the Lord.

Theological Significance

I believe silence before God is not emptiness but waiting, and the idol that cannot answer deserves silence while the Lord deserves my quiet trust. To wait quietly for His salvation is the hardest and best work I do.
