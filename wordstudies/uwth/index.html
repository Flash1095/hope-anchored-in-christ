ʼûwth

properly, to come, i.e. (implied) to assent

Biblical Usage

Shechem's sons said, in this will we consent unto you, if every male be circumcised, and the priests consented to receive no more money of the people, neither to repair the breaches of the house.

Theological Significance

I believe consent is a heavier word than it sounds: Shechem's sons consented to a bargain that killed them, and the priests consented to stop taking money they were misusing, and in both cases the yes they gave decided what came next.
