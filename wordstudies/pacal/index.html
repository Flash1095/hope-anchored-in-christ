pâçal

to carve, whether wood or stone

Biblical Usage

Pâçal is to hew, to carve, to cut into shape. The LORD tells Moses to hew two tables of stone like the first, Moses hews them and carries them up the mountain, and Habakkuk asks what profit a graven image has, that its maker trusts in it.

Theological Significance

I believe the same cutting shapes two opposite things: stone hewn for God's words, and wood or stone carved into a god that cannot speak. Only one of them is worth trusting.
