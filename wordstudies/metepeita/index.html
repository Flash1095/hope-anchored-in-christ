metépeita

from G3326 and G1899; thereafter:--afterward.

Biblical Usage

Esau found no place of repentance afterward, though he sought it with tears. The word is simply afterward, but Hebrews loads it with the weight of a missed chance: the blessing had passed, and tears could not call it back.

Theological Significance

I believe this one verse is a sober warning: repentance has its season, and Esau's tears were real, but they came after the door had closed.
