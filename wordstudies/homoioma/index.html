homoíōma

from G3666; a form; abstractly, resemblance:--made like to, likeness, shape, similitude.

Biblical Usage

homoíōma means made like to, likeness, shape, similitude. It occurs 6 times in all, and the passages I have picked — Romans, Philippians, and Revelation — show it in its plain sense. I read it as a word that does its work quietly, without dressing anything up.

Theological Significance

I believe homoíōma matters because we are made in God’s likeness, and Christ restores what sin broke. I hold that reading it plainly keeps me honest about what God actually said.
