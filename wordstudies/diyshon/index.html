dîyshôn

Definition
the leaper, i.e. an antelope
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) a clean animal,; 1a) pygarg, a kind of antelope or gazelle; 1b) perhaps mountain goat; 1c) perhaps an extinct animal, exact meaning unknown
