dikaiosyne

Righteousness and justice — God's right character and saving action, and the righteous standing He gives to those in Christ.

Biblical Usage

Dikaiosyne was the word that gripped Martin Luther: in the gospel a righteousness from God is revealed, received by faith. Jesus calls hungering for it blessed and promises it will be filled.

Theological Significance

Dikaiosyne is gospel gold: God is righteous, and in Christ He makes sinners righteous — counted right with God and being made right in daily life.
