ʼĂravnâh

Aravnah (or Arnijah or Ornah), a Jebusite

Biblical Usage

Araunah = "I shall shout for joy" = "make ye to shine" (2 Sa 24:16) or "joyful shouting of Jah" (2Sa 24:18); 1) a Jebusite who sold David the site for an altar

Details

Language: Hebrew
Transliteration: ʼĂravnâh
Strongs Number: H728
Part Of Speech: proper noun (masculine)
