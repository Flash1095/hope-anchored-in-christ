kolobóō

from a derivative of the base of G2849; to dock, i.e. (figuratively) abridge:--shorten.

Biblical Usage

Koloboo means to shorten, to dock, to cut short. Matthew 24:22 says except those days should be shortened, there should no flesh be saved, but for the elect's sake those days shall be shortened, and Mark 13:20 says the same: for the elect's sake, whom he hath chosen, he hath shortened the days.

Theological Significance

I believe the shortened days are mercy measured in time: God cuts the tribulation short because His elect are in it, and no flesh would survive if He did not.
