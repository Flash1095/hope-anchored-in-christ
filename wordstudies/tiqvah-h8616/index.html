Tiqvâh

Tikvah, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Two Israelites bear this name, and both are fathers named in the record. Tikvah was the father of Shallum, whose wife Huldah was the prophetess consulted when the book of the law was found, and another Tikvah was the father of Jahaziah, who was employed about the matter of the strange wives in Ezra's day.

Theological Significance

I believe the two men must not be merged: one Tikvah belongs to Josiah's reform, the other to Ezra's, and the Bible keeps them apart.
