qârachath

Word
קָרַחַת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
qârachath
Strongs Number
H7146
Definition
a bald spot (on the back of the head); figuratively, a threadbare spot (on the back side of the cloth)
Part Of Speech
noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
1) baldness of head, back baldness, bald spot
