ʻAbdᵉʼêl

Abdeel, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Abdeel is named once, in Jeremiah 36:26, as the father of Shelemiah — one of the officers King Jehoiakim sends to arrest Jeremiah and Baruch after the scroll is burned. The verse is a manhunt: the king commands Jerahmeel, Seraiah, and Shelemiah the son of Abdeel to seize the prophet and his scribe.

Theological Significance

Abdeel himself does nothing in the story; he is known only as someone's father, in a verse about arresting God's prophet. I believe that is worth noting — the Bible names even the bystanders of its great scenes, and a name tied to a king's rage against God's word is a sober place to be found.
