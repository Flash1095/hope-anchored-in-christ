Shammûwaʻ

Shammua, the name of four Israelites

Biblical Usage

Four men answer to the name Shammua, and each of them stands in a different generation. One was the spy from the tribe of Reuben, one was a son of David born in Jerusalem, and one was a priest of Bilgah's course.

Theological Significance

I hold that four men can share a name and still be told apart, because God keeps the generations straight even when we cannot.
