ásbestos

from G1 (as a negative particle) and a derivative of G4570; not extinguished, i.e. (by implication) perpetual:--not to be quenched, unquenchable.

Biblical Usage

Unquenchable is the fire that is never put out, and Jesus uses it for the judgment of the chaff and the worm that dieth not. John says He will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire, and Jesus says it is better to enter into life maimed than to go into hell, into the fire that never shall be quenched.

Theological Significance

I believe unquenchable fire is the most solemn word in the Gospels, because it says the judgment is not a fire that burns out and lets the sinner out. It is the fire of a holy God against sin, and I hold that the warning is mercy, that we might flee to the One who was baptized with fire for us.
