hâphak

to turn about or over; by implication, to change, overturn, return, pervert

Biblical Usage

Haphak means to turn over, to overturn, and it can destroy or it can heal. God overthrew Sodom in Genesis 19:25, Jonah warned that Nineveh would be overthrown in Jonah 3:4, and yet the same word turns the other way in Psalms 30:11, where God turned mourning into dancing, and in Deuteronomy 23:5, where he turned the curse into a blessing.

Theological Significance

I believe the God who overturns cities is the same God who overturns my mourning, and I would rather have him turn my sorrow than have him turn against my sin. The word cuts both ways, and I want to be on the side where he turns things for good.
