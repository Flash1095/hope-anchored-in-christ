Meshek

Word
מֶשֶׁךְ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Meshek
Strongs Number
H4902
Definition
Meshek, a son of Japheth, and the people descended from him
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Mesech or Meshech = "drawing out"; 1) son of Japheth, grandson of Noah, and progenitor of peoples to the north of Israel; 1a) descendants of Mesech often mentioned in connection with Tubal, Magog, and other northern nations including the Moschi, a people on the borders of Colchis and Armenia
