apotássomai

middle voice from G575 and G5021; literally, to say adieu (by departing or dismissing); figuratively, to renounce:--bid farewell, forsake, take leave, send away.

Biblical Usage

The word means to say goodbye, to take leave, and by extension to renounce. Jesus says no one can be His disciple without forsaking all he has, a would-be follower asks first to go and bid farewell to his family, Paul takes his leave of the brothers at Corinth, and Jesus sends the crowd away to pray alone.

Theological Significance

I believe farewell is the shape of discipleship. Following Jesus means loosening our grip on everything else, and sometimes the goodbye is the proof that we mean it.
