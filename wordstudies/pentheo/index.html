penthéō

from G3997; to grieve (the feeling or the act):--mourn, (be-)wail.

Biblical Usage

Pentheo means to mourn, to grieve. Jesus blesses those who mourn, for they shall be comforted, and He asks whether the children of the bridechamber can mourn while the bridegroom is with them. Luke warns those who laugh now, for they shall mourn and weep, and James tells sinners to be afflicted and mourn, and turn their laughter to mourning.

Theological Significance

What I hold is that mourning is not a weakness but a doorway: blessed are they that mourn, for they shall be comforted. I hold that grief over sin is the beginning of joy, and laughter that never weeps is the shallow kind. God does not despise a broken heart — He promises to comfort it, and I have found the promise true.
