phónos

from an obsolete primary φένω (to slay); murder:--murder, + be slain with, slaughter.

Biblical Usage

Phonos is murder, the deed itself, and Jesus puts it in the list of what comes out of the heart. Out of the heart proceed evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, and false witness, and Paul says the same of the unrighteous, who are filled with all unrighteousness, murder, and debate. And at the end, men do not repent of their murders, nor of their sorceries, nor of their fornication.

Theological Significance

I believe murder begins in the heart before it reaches the hands: Jesus traces it to evil thoughts, and Paul traces it to a mind given over to unrighteousness. The world will not repent of its murders, but the heart that repents of them finds mercy. My position is that I must guard the heart, because the murder on the street began as a thought in a heart like mine.
