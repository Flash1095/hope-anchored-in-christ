ʼIddôw

Iddo, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Iddo was a chief man at Casiphia, the place where Ezra sent for help on the journey home. In Ezra 8:17 Ezra sends men to Iddo the chief at the place Casiphia, to bring ministers for the house of God, and Iddo sends them the Levites and temple servants they needed.

Theological Significance

I hold that Iddo is one of those quiet helpers the story turns on: Ezra asked, Iddo answered, and the house of God had its servants.
