ʼânaph

to breathe hard, i.e. be enraged

Biblical Usage

The word means to breathe hard, to be enraged: in Horeb Israel provoked the LORD to wrath, so that the LORD was angry with them to have destroyed them — and the psalm asks how long He will be angry, and whether He will draw out His anger to all generations. It is the anger that snorts, the breath that comes hard, and it is used of God's anger against Israel and of Israel asking how long it will last.

Theological Significance

A word for being enraged carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the question — how long will God be angry, and the answer the psalm trusts, that His anger does not last for ever.
