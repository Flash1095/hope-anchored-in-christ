parágō

from G3844 and G71; to lead near, i.e. (reflexively or intransitively) to go along or away:--depart, pass (away, by, forth).

Biblical Usage

Paragō means to pass by, to go along, to pass away. Jesus passing from thence sees a man named Matthew sitting at the receipt of custom and says, Follow me, and John says the world passeth away and the lust thereof, but he that doeth the will of God abideth for ever.

Theological Significance

I hold that passing by is Jesus' way of calling: He passes Matthew's tax booth and stops, and the world passes away while the doer of God's will abides.
