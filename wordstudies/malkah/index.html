malkâh

a queen

Biblical Usage

Malkah is the queen, and the Bible's queens are women who change the story. 1 Kings 10:1 has the queen of Sheba coming to prove Solomon with hard questions, and Esther 5:3 has the king asking queen Esther what her request is, even to the half of the kingdom, while Esther 7:3 has her answering, let my life be given me at my petition, and my people at my request.

Theological Significance

I believe the two queens in these verses show the two ways power meets God: Sheba came to test Solomon's wisdom, and Esther risked her life for her people. My position is that Esther 7:3 is the braver of the two, because she asked for her life and her people's in the same breath, before a king who could have refused both.
