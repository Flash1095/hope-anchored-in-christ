Emmaoús

probably of Hebrew origin (compare H3222); Emmaus, a place in Palestine:--Emmaus.

Biblical Usage

Emmaus is the village two disciples were walking to on the first Easter evening in Luke 24:13, about threescore furlongs from Jerusalem, when the risen Jesus joined them on the road and they did not know Him.

Theological Significance

I believe the road to Emmaus is where many of us live — walking away disappointed while Jesus walks beside us unrecognized — and He still opens our eyes over bread and Scripture.
