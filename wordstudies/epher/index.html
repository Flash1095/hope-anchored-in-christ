ʼêpher

ashes

Biblical Usage

Epher is ashes — dust and ashes, the sign of mourning and of humility. Abraham says, I am but dust and ashes; Job repents in dust and ashes; Daniel seeks God with fasting and sackcloth and ashes; Mordecai puts on sackcloth with ashes when his people are threatened.

Theological Significance

I am dust, and the moment I know it, I am ready to hear God. Ashes teach me the right posture, I believe: Abraham, Job, Daniel, and Mordecai all came to God the same way, low and honest.
