ʼĂkîysh

Akish, a Philistine king

Biblical Usage

Achish is the Philistine king of Gath who shelters David twice.

Theological Significance

Achish is a reminder that God's people sometimes find shelter in strange places. David's madness act is not his finest hour, but God used even a Philistine king's court to keep him alive — and I hold that the same God keeps His people in places they would never have chosen.
