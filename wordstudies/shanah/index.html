shânâh

to fold, i.e. duplicate (literally or figuratively); by implication, to transmute (transitive or intransitive)

Biblical Usage

The dream was doubled unto Pharaoh twice, because the thing is established by God, and God will shortly bring it to pass. David changed his behaviour before Achish and feigned himself mad, and the psalm title remembers that he changed his behaviour before Abimelech. Against all this changing stands the LORD's word, I change not, and My covenant will I not break, nor alter the thing that is gone out of My lips.

Theological Significance

I believe God changes the story but never His word, and that is why a doubled dream is certain. David could change his behaviour to escape a king, yet the LORD who changes not kept him through it. My position is that the unchanging God is the ground of every change He works in us.
