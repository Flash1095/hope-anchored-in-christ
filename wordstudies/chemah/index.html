chêmâh

heat; figuratively, anger, poison (from its fever)

Biblical Usage

Wrath is heat, fury, and poison, and the word burns through the Bible. Josiah hears that great is the wrath of the LORD kindled against Judah, the king's wrath is pacified when Haman is hanged, Phinehas turns God's wrath away from Israel, and Josiah's men hear again that the wrath of the LORD is poured out.

Theological Significance

I believe God's wrath is real and it can be turned: Phinehas turns it away by zeal, and the king's wrath is pacified by justice. Wrath that can be turned is wrath that still leaves room for mercy.
