tsûwts

Word
צוּץ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
tsûwts
Strongs Number
H6692
Definition
to twinkle, i.e. glance; by analogy, to blossom (figuratively, flourish)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to blossom, shine, sparkle; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to flourish; 1a2) to shine, gleam; 1b) (Hiphil) to blossom, put forth blossoms, produce blossoms; 2) (Hiphil) to gaze, peep, glance, make the eyes sparkle
