raʻam

a peal of thunder

Biblical Usage

Thou shalt be visited with thunder, and with earthquake, and great noise, says Isaiah. Job says these are but parts of his ways, and the thunder of his power, who can understand? The word is the peal itself.

Theological Significance

I believe the thunder of God's power is only a whisper of his ways, and Job asks who can understand it.
