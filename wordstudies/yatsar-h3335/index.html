yâtsar

to mould into a form; especially as a potter; figuratively, to determine (i.e. form a resolution)

Biblical Usage

Yâtsar is the word for forming as a potter forms clay, and it is the word for God making man. The LORD formed man of the dust of the ground and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life, and Isaiah says we are the clay and He is our potter, all the work of His hand. Jeremiah watched the potter remake the marred vessel into another vessel, as seemed good to him, and God says He formed Israel and called them by name.

Theological Significance

I believe I am clay in the Potter's hand, and the hand that formed me will not drop me. My position is that God remakes what is marred, and the vessel that fails in my hands succeeds in His. And I hold that the God who formed the mountains and the wind also formed me, and He knows what He is making.
