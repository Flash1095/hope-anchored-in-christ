yâtsar

Word
יָצַר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
yâtsar
Strongs Number
H3335
Definition
to mould into a form; especially as a potter; figuratively, to determine (i.e. form a resolution)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to form, fashion, frame; 1a) (Qal) to form, fashion; 1a1) of human activity; 1a2) of divine activity; 1a2a) of creation; 1a2a1) of original creation; 1a2a2) of individuals at conception; 1a2a3) of Israel as a people; 1a2b) to frame, pre-ordain, plan (fig. of divine) purpose of a situation); 1b) (Niphal) to be formed, be created; 1c) (Pual) to be predetermined, be pre-ordained; 1d) (Hophal) to be formed
