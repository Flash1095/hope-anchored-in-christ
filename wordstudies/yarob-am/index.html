Yârobʻâm

Jarobam, the name of two Israelite kings

Biblical Usage

Jeroboam is the name of two kings of Israel, Jeroboam the son of Nebat who tore the kingdom from Solomon's son, and Jeroboam the son of Joash who reigned in the days of Jotham. The first lifted up his hand against Solomon, and the visions of Iddo the seer were written against him.

Theological Significance

I believe Jeroboam is the warning of every generation, that a man can be raised up by God and still turn the people to idols. My position is that the sin of Jeroboam outlived him, and no king's legacy is safe without obedience.
