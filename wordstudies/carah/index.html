çârâh

apostasy, crime; figuratively, remission

Biblical Usage

When Scripture says çârâh, it means apostasy, crime, and by figure, remission. That is the sense that runs through its 8 occurrences, from Deuteronomy to Isaiah. The word does not change its meaning to suit the passage; the passage leans on the word.

Theological Significance

I hold that çârâh is worth knowing because God gave us the word for apostasy, crime, and by figure, remission. The word is small; the truth it carries is not.
