ʼêbûwç

a manger or stall

Biblical Usage

A manger is a feeding trough for animals, a plain wooden box, and the Bible uses it to make a sharp point. Isaiah says the ox knows his owner and the ass his master's crib, yet Israel does not know God. Job asks whether the wild ox will stay by the crib, and Proverbs says where there are no oxen the crib is clean, but much increase comes by the strength of the ox.

Theological Significance

I believe God notices the small, dirty work of feeding and tending, because He uses a manger to teach His people about knowing Him. The crib is a test of attention, and Israel failed it.
