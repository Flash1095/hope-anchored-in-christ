qeren

a horn (literally or for sound)

Biblical Usage

In Daniel the horn is first a musical instrument: the cornet whose sound commands the nations to bow to the golden image. Then it is the beast's horns — ten kings, and a little horn with eyes and a mouth speaking great things. Daniel is told plainly what the horns are: ten kings that shall arise, and another after them who subdues three.

Theological Significance

I believe Daniel's horns are not riddles to frighten us but names for real powers, and the little horn's great words do not outlast the Ancient of Days. I hold that the beast's horns rise and fall on God's timetable, and the kingdom that stands at the end belongs to the saints, not to the horn that spoke against them.
