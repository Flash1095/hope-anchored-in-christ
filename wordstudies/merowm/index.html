Mêrôwm

Word
מֵרוֹם
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Mêrôwm
Strongs Number
H4792
Definition
Merom, a lake in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Merom = "high place"; 1) the lake in northern Canaan at which Joshua routed the northern confederacy headed by Jabin; 1a) site uncertain; probably the lake formed by the Jordan river about 10 miles (16 km) north of the Sea of Galilee
