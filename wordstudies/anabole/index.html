anabolḗ

from G306; a putting off:--delay.

Biblical Usage

The word appears once: Festus tells Agrippa that when Paul's accusers came to Caesarea, 'without any delay on the morrow I sat on the judgment seat, and commanded the man to be brought forth.' It is a putting off — and here the point is that there was none. Festus moved at once, and Paul's case came before the king.

Theological Significance

One word for delay carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the contrast — Festus did not delay, and the gospel got its hearing before Agrippa the same week.
