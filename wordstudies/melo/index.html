mᵉlôʼ

fulness (literally or figuratively)

Biblical Usage

Melo' is fulness, the whole contents of a thing and everything it holds. Psalms 24:1 says the earth is the LORD's, and the fulness thereof, and Psalms 50:12 says the world is Mine, and the fulness thereof. Deuteronomy 33:16 blesses Joseph with the precious things of the earth and the fulness thereof, and Isaiah 6:3 has the seraphim cry that the whole earth is full of His glory.

Theological Significance

I believe fulness is the word for a world that belongs to God down to the last handful: nothing in it is empty of Him. My position is that the earth is full of His glory whether we see it or not, and the blessing is to live like it is true.
