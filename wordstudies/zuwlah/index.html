zûwlâh

probably scattering, i.e. removal; used adverbially, except

Biblical Usage

Zulah means 'except' or 'besides' — the word that draws the line. Moses tells Israel they saw no form at Sinai, only a voice; the priest at Nob tells David there is no sword besides Goliath's; and Boaz tells the nearer kinsman there is no one besides him to redeem Ruth's field. Except draws the boundary around God and around duty.

Theological Significance

I believe 'except' is the word that keeps faith exclusive: no God besides the LORD, and no redeemer besides the one God appoints.
