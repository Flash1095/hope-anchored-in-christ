Chetsrôwn

Chetsron, the name of a place in Palestine; also of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Hezron is the name of two men in the genealogies, one the son of Reuben and one the son of Pharez, and both lines run toward David. Reuben's son Hezron is counted in the family that went down to Egypt, Pharez's son Hezron stands in the line of Judah, and Ruth's genealogy ends with Hezron among the fathers of David.

Theological Significance

I believe the double Hezron shows how carefully God keeps the lines that lead to the king, and no link in the chain is left out.
