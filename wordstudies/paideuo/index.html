paideúō

from G3816; to train up a child, i.e. educate, or (by implication), discipline (by punishment):--chasten(-ise), instruct, learn, teach.

Biblical Usage

Paideuō is to train up a child, to educate, and to discipline by correction. Pilate offers to chastise Jesus and release Him, the Lord chastens those He loves, the church is chastened so it will not be condemned with the world, and Christ says, as many as I love, I rebuke and chasten.

Theological Significance

I hold that chastening is love with a purpose. The Lord disciplines His own so they will not be lost with the world, and His rebuke is the sign He has not given up on us.
