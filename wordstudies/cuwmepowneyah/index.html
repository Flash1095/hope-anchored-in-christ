çûwmᵉpôwnᵉyâh

a bagpipe (with a double pipe)

Biblical Usage

The bagpipe is one of the instruments that sound when Nebuchadnezzar's image is set up, and everyone must fall down and worship when they hear it. The herald lists it with the cornet, flute, harp, and psaltery, and the sound is the signal for idolatry. The word appears only in Daniel's account of the golden image.

Theological Significance

I believe the bagpipe shows that idolatry always has music, and the music makes the bowing feel like a celebration. The three young men hear the same sound and do not bow, and their refusal is the sermon. I hold that the test of worship is not the music but the object, and no instrument can make a false god true.
