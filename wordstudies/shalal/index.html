shâlal

Word
שָׁלַל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
shâlal
Strongs Number
H7997
Definition
to drop or strip; by implication, to plunder
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) (Qal) to draw out; 2) to spoil, plunder, take spoil; 2a) (Qal) to spoil, plunder; 2b) (Hithpolel) to be spoiled, be plundered
