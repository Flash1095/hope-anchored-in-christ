millôwʼ

Word
מִלּוֹא
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
millôwʼ
Strongs Number
H4407
Definition
a rampart (as filled in), i.e. the citadel
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Millo = "rampart" or "mound"; 1) a place near Shechem; site unknown; 2) a part of the fortifications of Jerusalem
