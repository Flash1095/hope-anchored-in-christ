yârad

to descend (literally, to go downwards; or conventionally to a lower region, as the shore, a boundary, the enemy, etc.; or figuratively, to fall); causatively, to bring down (in all the above applications)

Biblical Usage

Yârad is the word for coming down, for descending from above to below. The LORD came down to see the city and the tower men were building, and to see whether the cry of Sodom was true, and He came down to deliver Israel out of Egypt into a good land. Hannah sang that the LORD brings down to the grave and brings up, so that descent itself is in His hand.

Theological Significance

I believe God comes down before He lifts up, and the God who descended to see Babel is the God who descended to deliver Israel. My position is that no depth is too low for Him to reach, because He has already come down to the grave and risen. And I hold that the descent of God is the beginning of salvation, not its end.
