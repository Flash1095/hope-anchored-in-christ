Kᵉrêthîy

a Kerethite or life-guardsman

Biblical Usage

Kerethiy means a Cherethite, one of David's life-guardsmen: 1 Samuel 30:14 says the Amalekites made an invasion upon the south of the Cherethites, and burned Ziklag with fire. 2 Samuel 20:7 says Joab's men, and the Cherethites, and the Pelethites went out to pursue Sheba. 1 Chronicles 18:17 says Benaiah was over the Cherethites and the Pelethites. Zephaniah 2:5 cries woe to the nation of the Cherethites, for the word of the LORD is against you.

Theological Significance

I believe the Cherethites show how a people can be both guard and judged: they kept David's life, and their own coast fell under the word of the LORD. I hold that nearness to the king is no shelter when the king's God speaks against you.
