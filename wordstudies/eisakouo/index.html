eisakoúō

from G1519 and G191; to listen to:--hear.

Biblical Usage

To hear in this word is to listen and answer, not only to catch a sound, and the Bible uses it for God's hearing of prayer. Jesus warns against thinking we will be heard for our much speaking, the angel tells Zacharias his prayer is heard, Cornelius is told his prayers and alms have come up as a memorial before God, and Jesus offered prayers with strong crying and tears and was heard because He feared. The word always means a hearing that answers.

Theological Significance

I believe God hears prayer the way a father hears a child, because He does not only catch the sound, He answers it. I hold that no honest prayer is ever lost on Him.
