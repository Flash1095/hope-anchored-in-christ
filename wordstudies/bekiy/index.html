Bᵉkîy

a weeping; by analogy, a dripping

Biblical Usage

bekiy is used for weeping, and it appears 27 times across the Bible. Joseph wept aloud so the Egyptians heard him, Israel wept for Moses thirty days, and the king and his sons wept together.

Theological Significance

I believe tears are not weakness in the Bible; Joseph wept, Israel wept, and God keeps our tears in His book. That is why I keep coming back to it: bekiy is not a word to rush past.
