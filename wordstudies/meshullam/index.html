Mᵉshullâm

Meshullam, the name of seventeen Israelites

Biblical Usage

Meshullam is the name of seventeen Israelites, and three of them stand out in the record. 1 Chronicles 3:19 names Meshullam the son of Zerubbabel, and 2 Chronicles 34:12 names Meshullam of the Kohathites, an overseer who set the temple work forward. 2 Kings 22:3 names a third Meshullam as the grandfather of Shaphan the scribe, sent by Josiah to the house of the LORD.

Theological Significance

I believe one name can hold many faithful men: a son of the governor, an overseer of the work, and the grandfather of a scribe, all counted.
