Word

Language

Transliteration
nᵉçîyk
Strongs Number
H5257
Definition
properly, something poured out, i.e. a libation; also a molten image; by implication, a prince (as anointed)
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) poured out, libation, molten image, one anointed; 1a) libation, drink-offering; 1b) molten image; 2) prince, anointed one
