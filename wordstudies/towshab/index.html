tôwshâb

resident alien

Biblical Usage

A sojourner is a stranger living on land that is not his. Abraham calls himself a stranger and a sojourner and asks for a buryingplace, the Lord says the land is His and Israel are strangers and sojourners with Him, the psalmist says he is a stranger with God as all his fathers were, and David confesses we are strangers before Thee, and sojourners, as were all our fathers.

Theological Significance

I hold that the sojourner is the Bible's picture of the believer: no permanent home on this earth, and a God who calls the stranger His own. I believe the word should change how I hold everything I own — as a guest in God's land, not an owner of it.
