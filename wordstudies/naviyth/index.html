Nâvîyth

Navith, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Naioth is the dwelling of the prophets at Ramah, where Samuel lives and David flees for shelter from Saul. Saul sends messengers who fall to prophesying when they come there, and David slips away from Naioth to meet Jonathan.

Theological Significance

I hold that Naioth is the picture of God's shelter: the prophets' dwelling is where the hunted man hides, and even the king's messengers cannot lay hands on him there.
