Word
יָאַל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
yâʼal
Strongs Number
H2973
Definition
properly, to be slack, i.e. (figuratively) to be foolish
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to be foolish, become fools, act foolishly, show wicked folly; 1a) (Niphal); 1a1) to show wicked folly; 1a2) to become fools
