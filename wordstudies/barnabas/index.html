Barnábas

of Chaldee origin (H1247 and H5029); son of Nabas (i.e. prophecy); Barnabas, an Israelite:--Barnabas.

Biblical Usage

Barnabas, whose name means son of consolation, sold a field and laid the money at the apostles' feet. He took Saul by the hand when the church in Jerusalem feared him, was set apart with Saul by the Holy Ghost for the work in Cyprus, and went up with Paul to Jerusalem to settle the question of the Gentiles.

Theological Significance

I believe Barnabas is the friend every believer needs, because he believed in Saul when no one else would and he gave away his field before he ever gave a speech. His life says encouragement is not a soft word but a strong act, and I hold that the church grew because Barnabas kept opening doors for men others had shut.
