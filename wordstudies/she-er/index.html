shᵉʼêr

flesh (as swelling out), as living or for food; generally food of any kind; figuratively, kindred by blood

Biblical Usage

This word is flesh in two senses: the body, and the blood kin who share it. The law says a husband shall not diminish his wife's food, her raiment, and her duty of marriage, and it forbids uncovering the nakedness of a mother's sister, for she is thy mother's near kinswoman. Micah's rulers pluck the flesh from the bones of the people, and Proverbs says the cruel man troubles his own flesh.

Theological Significance

I hold that flesh ties us together whether we like it or not. We are bound to our kin and to our own bodies, and cruelty to either is cruelty to ourselves.
