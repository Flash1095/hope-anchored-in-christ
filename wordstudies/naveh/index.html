nâveh

(adjectively) at home; hence (by implication of satisfaction) lovely; also (noun) a home, of God (temple), men (residence), flocks (pasture), or wild animals (den)

Biblical Usage

Moses sings that the LORD guides His people in His strength to His holy habitation, and the enemy devours Jacob and lays waste his dwelling place. The defenced city is left desolate and its habitation forsaken, yet the LORD is called the habitation of justice for His people.

Theological Significance

I believe home is where God dwells: He leads His people to His own habitation, and when the enemy lays waste the dwelling place, the LORD Himself remains the home His people can return to. The habitation God gives cannot be taken by force, and the home He keeps is the one that lasts.
