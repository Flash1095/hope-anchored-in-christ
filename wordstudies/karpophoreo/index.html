karpophoréō

from G2593; to be fertile (literally or figuratively):--be (bear, bring forth) fruit(-ful).

Biblical Usage

Karpophoreo is to bear fruit — to be fertile, in the field and in the life. The seed sown in good ground brings forth fruit, some an hundredfold, some sixty, some thirty; and the gospel, Paul says, bringeth forth fruit in all the world, as it doth also in you.

Theological Significance

I believe fruit is the proof of the seed: the good ground bears it, the gospel bears it in all the world, and a life that bears none has not yet received the seed. The seed proves itself by the fruit, and the fruit proves the ground.
