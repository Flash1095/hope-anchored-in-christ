miméomai

middle voice from μῖμος (a "mimic"); to imitate:--follow.

Biblical Usage

The word is to imitate, to follow a pattern, and the New Testament uses it for the faith itself. Hebrews tells the church to remember their leaders and follow their faith, Paul tells the Thessalonians to imitate his working with his own hands, and John warns against imitating what is evil.

Theological Significance

I believe imitation is how faith is passed on. Doctrine is taught in words, but the life is taught by watching someone live it, and the church needs examples worth copying.
