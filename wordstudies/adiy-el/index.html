ʻĂdîyʼêl

Word
עֲדִיאֵל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻĂdîyʼêl
Strongs Number
H5717
Definition
Adiel, the name of three Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Adiel = "ornament of God"; 1) a prince of the Simeon of the family of Shimei in the time of king Hezekiah of Judah; 2) a priest, ancestor of Maasiai; 3) ancestor of Azmaveth, the treasurer of David
