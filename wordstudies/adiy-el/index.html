ʻĂdîyʼêl

Adiel, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Adiel is the name of three different Israelites in Chronicles: a prince of Simeon, a priest named as the father of Maasiai, and the father of Azmaveth, who was over King David's treasures. The name means 'ornament of God,' but Scripture gives us no story for any of the three — only their places in the lists, their families, and their duties.

Theological Significance

A name in a list carries no doctrine, and I won't pretend otherwise. What these three Adiels do show is that God's people were counted and remembered — even the treasurers and the priests whose names never made it into a story.
