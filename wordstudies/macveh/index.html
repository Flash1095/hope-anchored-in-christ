maçveh

a veil

Biblical Usage

Masveh is the veil Moses put on his face, and all three of its appearances are in Exodus 34. Exodus 34:33 says Moses put a veil on his face when he had done speaking, Exodus 34:34 says he took the veil off when he went in before the LORD, and Exodus 34:35 says the children of Israel saw his face shine and he put the veil upon his face again.

Theological Significance

I hold that the veil on Moses' face marked a glory too bright for the people to bear: Moses hid it so they could look at him at all. My position is that the veil also points forward, because the glory on Moses' face was fading, while the glory we see in Christ does not fade.
