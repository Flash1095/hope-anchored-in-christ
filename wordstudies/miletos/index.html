Mílētos

of uncertain origin; Miletus, a city of Asia Minor:--Miletus.

Biblical Usage

Miletus is the port city on the coast of Asia where Paul stopped on his way to Jerusalem and sent for the elders of Ephesus to meet him there. Years later he writes that he left Trophimus sick at Miletus, a plain line that shows even the apostle's companions were not always healed.

Theological Significance

I believe Miletus is remembered for the farewell: Paul told the elders he would see their faces no more, and the church at Ephesus wept over the parting.
