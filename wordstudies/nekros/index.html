nekrós

from an apparently primary νέκυς (a corpse); dead (literally or figuratively; also as noun):--dead.

Biblical Usage

Jesus says to let the dead bury their dead, and Paul tells the Romans to reckon themselves dead indeed unto sin but alive unto God. The Corinthians are asked how some can say there is no resurrection of the dead, and the Ephesians are told to awake and arise from the dead.

Theological Significance

I believe dead is the word that holds the gospel: dead to sin, dead to the world, and raised with Christ. The dead who bury their dead stay dead, but the dead who hear His voice rise.
