shalom

Peace, wholeness and complete well-being — not merely the absence of conflict but life flourishing as God intended.

Biblical Usage

Shalom describes health, prosperity, right relationships and rest. Priests blessed Israel with it, prophets promised it under the coming Prince of Peace, and Isaiah tied it to the suffering Servant who was punished to bring us peace.

Theological Significance

Shalom shows salvation is wholeness, not just rescue. In Christ, the God of peace restores what sin fractured — our hearts, our relationships and one day all creation.
