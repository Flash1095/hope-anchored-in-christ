méthē

apparently a primary word; an intoxicant, i.e. (by implication) intoxication:--drunkenness.

Biblical Usage

Paul lists drunkenness among the works of the flesh that shut men out of the kingdom, Jesus warns against hearts overcharged with drunkenness when that day comes unawares, and Romans tells believers to walk honestly, not in rioting and drunkenness. Scripture never treats it as a small vice.

Theological Significance

I believe drunkenness is named so plainly because it steals exactly what the Spirit gives: self-control, watchfulness, and a clear mind for the day of the Lord. A clear mind for the day of the Lord is worth more than any drink.
