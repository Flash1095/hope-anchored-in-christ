Yitshâr

Jitshar, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Izhar is named 9 times in the Bible, and the passages here are Exodus 6:18; Numbers 3:19; 1 Chronicles 6:2. In Exodus 6:18 we read, "And the sons of Kohath; Amram, and Izhar, and Hebron, and Uzziel..." It appears again in 1 Chronicles 6:2: "And the sons of Kohath; Amram, Izhar, and Hebron, and Uzziel."

Theological Significance

Izhar is one of the names that makes the Bible feel like a real history to me. God kept the name, and that tells me he kept the person.
