ánōthen

from G507; from above; by analogy, from the first; by implication, anew:--from above, again, from the beginning (very first), the top.

Biblical Usage

From above, from the first, anew. Jesus tells Nicodemus a man must be born from above, James says every good gift comes down from above, from the Father of lights, and John says Jesus was born from above and speaks of heavenly things. The word joins the new birth to heaven itself.

Theological Significance

I believe being born from above is the only beginning that counts: no man climbs into God's kingdom, he is born into it from above. James says the good gifts come down from the same place, and I hold that my life, my birth, and my hope all come from above, not from below.
