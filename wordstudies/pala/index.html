pâlâʼ

properly, perhaps to separate, i.e. distinguish (literally or figuratively); by implication, to be (causatively, make) great, difficult, wonderful

Biblical Usage

Pâlâ' is to be wonderful, marvellous, surpassing, beyond human power. Is any thing too hard for the LORD, God asks Abraham; the LORD promises to do marvels before all His people; the psalmist praises Him for fearfully and wonderfully made works; and Jeremiah confesses that nothing is too hard for Him.

Theological Significance

I believe wonderful is God's own word for His work. What is too hard for me is the very place His power shows, and my life is one of the marvels He has made.
