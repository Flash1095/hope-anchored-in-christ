katasophízomai

middle voice from G2596 and G4679; to be crafty against, i.e. circumvent:--deal subtilly with.

Biblical Usage

Pharaoh dealt subtly with our kindred, and evil entreated our fathers, so that they cast out their young children to the end they might not live.

Theological Significance

I believe the subtlety of Pharaoh is the devil's craft, and God saw it and answered it with deliverance.
