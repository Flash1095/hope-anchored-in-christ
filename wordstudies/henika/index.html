hēníka

of uncertain affinity; at which time:--when.

Biblical Usage

Henika means when, at the time when, and Paul uses it twice for the veil over the heart. In 2 Corinthians 3:15 even unto this day, when Moses is read, the vail is upon their heart, and in 2 Corinthians 3:16 when it shall turn to the Lord, the vail shall be taken away.

Theological Significance

I believe the when is the promise: the veil stays until the turning, and the turning takes it away in a moment.
