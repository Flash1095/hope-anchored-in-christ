épainos

from G1909 and the base of G134; laudation; concretely, a commendable thing:--praise.

Biblical Usage

Praise — the commendation itself. Every man shall have praise of God when the Lord comes in 1 Corinthians 4:5, the trial of faith will be found unto praise and honour and glory in 1 Peter 1:7, and all God's grace works to the praise of the glory of His grace in Ephesians 1:6. The brother sent with Titus had praise in the gospel throughout all the churches in 2 Corinthians 8:18.

Theological Significance

I believe the praise that matters is the praise of God, not of men — it waits until the Lord comes and brings hidden things to light. I hold that a faith tried by fire and found true will be praised by God Himself, and no human applause can compare.
