chûwm

sunburnt or swarthy (blackish)

Biblical Usage

Brown is the colour of the flock Jacob bargains for, the dark and speckled sheep that will be his wages. Laban agrees, and then removes the brown cattle from the flock before Jacob can claim them. Jacob sets the brown sheep apart, and the word appears only in this one bargain, four times in four verses.

Theological Significance

I believe the brown sheep matter because Jacob's wages depended on them, and God made the bargain work for the man who was cheated. Laban tried to take the brown ones away, and God gave them back. I hold that God sees the cheated bargain and settles it His own way.
