chôwph

a cove (as a sheltered bay)

Biblical Usage

The shore is where the land meets the sea, and Jacob blesses Zebulun with a haven for ships on the coast. Moses sends Israel toward the hill country and the shore, and the tribes settle along it. Asher stays by the shore in Deborah's song instead of joining the battle, and the shore becomes a picture of safety chosen over duty.

Theological Significance

I believe the shore is a place of decision in Scripture, where comfort and calling pull in different directions. Zebulun's blessing is a shore used well, and Asher's shore is a blessing kept for himself. My position is that God gives good places, but He means them to serve His purposes, not our ease.
