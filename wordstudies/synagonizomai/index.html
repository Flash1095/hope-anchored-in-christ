synagōnízomai

from G4862 and G75; to struggle in company with, i.e. (figuratively) to be a partner (assistant):--strive together with.

Biblical Usage

Paul begs the Romans to strive together with him in their prayers to God for him, for the Lord Jesus Christ's sake and for the love of the Spirit. Prayer is not a solo effort but a wrestling match fought side by side, and the apostle asks the church to get in the ring with him.

Theological Significance

My position is that prayer is striving together, and I hold that when I pray for God's servants I am fighting beside them, not merely thinking of them.
