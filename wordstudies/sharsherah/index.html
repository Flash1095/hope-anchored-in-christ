sharshᵉrâh

a chain; (architectural) probably a garland

Biblical Usage

Chains of gold dressed the temple and the priest alike. The pillars wore wreaths of chain work, the greater house was set with palm trees and chains, and the breastplate's chains were of wreathen work of pure gold. The word is used for the chain and for the garland it becomes on the pillars.

Theological Significance

I believe the chains are not bondage but beauty, gold on the pillars and gold on the priest, and I hold that God's house is dressed as a king's house because He is King.
