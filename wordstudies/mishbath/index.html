mishbâth

cessation, i.e. destruction

Biblical Usage

The word is a cessation, a rest, and it appears once, at the end of the verse where Jerusalem remembers her affliction: the adversaries saw her, and did mock at her sabbaths.

Theological Significance

I hold that the mockery is the wound: the enemies laughed at the very rest God had given her, and the city that broke the sabbaths lost them.
