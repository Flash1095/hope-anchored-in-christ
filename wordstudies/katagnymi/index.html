katágnymi

from G2596 and the base of G4486; to rend in pieces, i.e. crack apart:--break.

Biblical Usage

The soldiers broke the legs of the two thieves to hasten their death, but when they came to Jesus they found Him dead already and did not break His. Matthew had already said He would not break the bruised reed, and the same verb holds both pictures: legs broken in cruelty, and a bruised reed left unbroken in mercy. The law said no bone of the Passover lamb should be broken, and He kept it to the letter.

Theological Significance

I believe Jesus was not broken by men because He was already the Lamb, given, not taken. I hold that the bruised reed is safe with Him, and the same hands that refused to break Him will not break me either.
