Armageddṓn

of Hebrew origin (H2022 and H4023); Armageddon (or Har-Meggiddon), a symbolic name:--Armageddon.

Biblical Usage

Armageddon is named once, as the place where the kings of the earth are gathered for the battle of the great day of God Almighty. John says it is called in the Hebrew tongue Armageddon, the mount of Megiddo, the plain where Israel's armies had fought for centuries.

Theological Significance

I believe Armageddon matters because it is a real place with a real history of battles, and God chooses it for the last gathering of the kings. The end of the age is not fought nowhere, it is fought somewhere, and the kings who gather there gather against God.
