diákonos

Word
διάκονος
Language
Greek
Transliteration
diákonos
Strongs Number
G1249
Definition
probably from an obsolete διάκω (to run on errands; compare G1377); an attendant, i.e. (genitive case) a waiter (at table or in other menial duties); specially, a Christian teacher and pastor (technically, a deacon or deaconess):--deacon, minister, servant.
