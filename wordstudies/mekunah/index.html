mᵉkunâh

a spot

Biblical Usage

Mekunah is a base, a resting place, and Zechariah 5:11 sets it in a vision: the woman in the ephah is carried to the land of Shinar, where a house is built for her and she is set upon her own base.

Theological Significance

I believe wickedness gets a house built for it only to be left there, far from God's people.
