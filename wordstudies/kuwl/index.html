kûwl

properly, to keep in; hence, to measure; figuratively, to maintain (in various senses)

Biblical Usage

Kuwl is the word for holding in, and it stretches from containing to sustaining. Solomon's officers "provided victuals" for the king in 1 Kings 4:7, and Barzillai sustained the king in 2 Samuel 19:32. But the same word says in 2 Chronicles 2:6 that the heaven of heavens "cannot contain" God, in Amos 7:10 the land "is not able to bear" Amos's words, and in Ezekiel 23:32 the cup of judgment "containeth much."

Theological Significance

In my reading, this word's two directions tell one truth: God sustains His people and no vessel contains Him, and the cup He gives the unrepentant is a cup no one can hold. He sustains me, and no vessel can contain Him, and I would not have it any other way.
