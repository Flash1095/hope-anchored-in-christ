nᵉqâmâh

avengement, whether the act of the passion

Biblical Usage

Vengeance in the Bible is not a private grudge; it belongs to the LORD, and men ask Him to take it up. Moses is told to avenge Israel on Midian, Jephthah's daughter accepts her father's vow because the LORD has taken vengeance for him, and Jeremiah hands his whole cause to the righteous Judge and asks to see His vengeance.

Theological Significance

I believe vengeance is God's to carry, not mine. I can lay my cause before Him and leave it there, trusting the Judge who tries the reins and the heart to do what is right.
