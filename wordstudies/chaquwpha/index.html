Chăqûwphâʼ

Chakupha, one of the Nethinim

Biblical Usage

Hakupha is named twice, among the children of the Nethinim who returned from exile with Zerubbabel. Ezra and Nehemiah both count his children in the returning families.

Theological Significance

I believe God counts the servants as carefully as the priests: Hakupha's family is on the return list by name.
