árrhēn

or ἄρσην probably from G142; male (as stronger for lifting):--male, man.

Biblical Usage

Male is used from the first page to the last, for the way God made us and for the child who rules the nations. He which made them at the beginning made them male and female, every male that openeth the womb shall be called holy to the Lord, Paul says there is neither male nor female in Christ Jesus, and the woman brings forth a man child who is to rule all nations with a rod of iron.

Theological Significance

I believe male and female is God's design from the beginning, not an accident and not a cage: He made them both, He calls the male child holy, and in Christ the division that the world turns into a war is healed without erasing the difference. And I hold that the man child of Revelation is the proof, that God's King is a man, born of a woman, ruling the nations.
