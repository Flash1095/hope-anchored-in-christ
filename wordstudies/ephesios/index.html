Ephésios

from G2181; an Ephesian or inhabitant of Ephesus:--Ephesian, of Ephesus.

Biblical Usage

Ephesios is an Ephesian, a person of Ephesus. The word rings through the riot: Great is Diana of the Ephesians, the crowd cries for two hours, and Trophimus the Ephesian is the man Paul is accused of bringing into the temple.

Theological Significance

A whole city can be stirred by a threatened idol, and one Ephesian's name could put Paul's life at risk — that is the power of a riot, I believe.
