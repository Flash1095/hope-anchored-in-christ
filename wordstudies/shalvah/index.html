shalvâh

security (genuine or false)

Biblical Usage

Security in Scripture is either true or false, and this word carries both. The psalm prays for peace within the walls and prosperity within the palaces, but the proverb warns that the prosperity of fools shall destroy them. Daniel saw a king who would destroy many by peace, and Ezekiel names the iniquity of Sodom as pride, fulness of bread, and abundance of idleness.

Theological Significance

I believe false security is the most dangerous condition in the Bible, because it feels exactly like the true kind. The peace that destroys is the peace that forgets God, and Sodom's idleness proves that prosperity without humility is a curse. That is why I pray for peace within the walls and watchfulness within the heart.
