phthorá

from G5351; decay, i.e. ruin (spontaneous or inflicted, literally or figuratively):--corruption, destroy, perish.

Biblical Usage

Phthora is decay, the ruin that eats away at what was made good. Paul says the whole creation waits to be delivered from the bondage of corruption in Romans 8:21, and that sowing to the flesh reaps corruption in Galatians 6:8. Our bodies are sown in corruption, says 1 Corinthians 15:42, but Peter promises an escape from the corruption in the world through lust in 2 Peter 1:4.

Theological Significance

I believe decay is the signature of the fall on everything, and resurrection is God's answer to it. That is why I hold that the gospel is not a repair job but a rescue from corruption itself.
