kᵉthôneth

a shirt

Biblical Usage

Kethoneth means a coat, a shirt, a tunic: Genesis 3:21 says unto Adam also and to his wife did the LORD God make coats of skins, and clothed them. Genesis 37:3 says Israel made Joseph a coat of many colours. 2 Samuel 13:18 says Tamar had a garment of divers colours upon her, for with such robes were the king's daughters apparelled. Song of Solomon 5:3 says, I have put off my coat, how shall I put it on?

Theological Significance

I believe the coat tells the story of the Bible: God sewed the first one to cover shame, Jacob sewed one that bred envy, and a king's daughter wore one that was torn away. I hold that the only coat that never tears is the one God sews himself.
