shâphêl

to depress or sink (expectation figuratively, to humiliate, intransitive or transitive)

Biblical Usage

God brings low and lifts up, and the pattern runs through the whole Bible. Hannah sang that the LORD maketh poor, and maketh rich, and bringeth low, and lifteth up, and Isaiah says the lofty looks of man shall be humbled and the LORD alone shall be exalted in that day. The psalm says God is the judge, He putteth down one, and setteth up another, and the proverb says a man's pride shall bring him low, but honour shall uphold the humble in spirit.

Theological Significance

I believe God brings the proud low and lifts the humble up, because that is the shape of every story He tells. Hannah sang it before Samuel was born, Isaiah preached it before the exile, and the psalm says the Judge does it with His own hand. My position is that I would rather be brought low by God than lifted up by pride.
