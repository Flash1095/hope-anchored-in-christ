ʼâshar

to be straight (used in the widest sense, especially to be level, right, happy); figuratively, to go forward, be honest, proper

Biblical Usage

To be straight is to be right, level and happy, and the Bible uses the word for the way that leads to blessing. Leah says happy am I, for the daughters will call me blessed, and calls her son Asher; wisdom is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her, and happy is every one that retaineth her; Isaiah says learn to do well, seek judgment, relieve the oppressed; and Malachi says all nations shall call you blessed.

Theological Significance

I believe straight and happy are the same word for a reason: the life that walks straight is the life that ends blessed. Leah's happiness, wisdom's tree of life and the nations calling Israel blessed are all one promise, and I hold that the straight way is the happy way, because God made it so.
