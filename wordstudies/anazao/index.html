anazáō

from G303 and G2198; to recover life (literally or figuratively):--(be a-)live again, revive.

Biblical Usage

To live again. The father cries that his son was dead and is alive again, and Paul says that when the commandment came, sin revived and he died. The word carries both pictures: a dead son coming home alive, and a dead sin waking up at the sound of God's law.

Theological Significance

I believe this word holds the whole gospel in miniature: what was dead can live again, and what was dormant can wake. The prodigal's return is my own story, and I hold that God's life is stronger than any death I have known.
