asébeia

from G765; impiety, i.e. (by implication) wickedness:--ungodly(-liness).

Biblical Usage

Ungodliness is life lived as if God were not there, and the New Testament names it plainly. The wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men, grace teaches us to deny ungodliness and worldly lusts and live soberly and righteously, and Enoch prophesied that the Lord comes to convince the ungodly of all their ungodly deeds.

Theological Significance

I believe ungodliness is the root sin, deeper than any one deed: it is living without God in the world. Grace does not excuse it, grace teaches us to deny it, and I hold that the wrath revealed against it is the other side of the grace that saves us from it.
