chabbûwrâh

properly, bound (with stripes), i.e. a weal (or black-and-blue mark itself)

Biblical Usage

Chabbuwrah is a bruise, a stripe, a wound. Lamech boasted of a wound he had given, the law answered wound for wound and stripe for stripe, Proverbs says the blueness of a wound cleanses away evil, and Isaiah says the Servant was wounded for our transgressions and with His stripes we are healed.

Theological Significance

I believe every stripe in this word leads to one place: the wounds men give each other, and the wounds Christ took for us. His stripes are the only ones that heal instead of hurt.
