Hebrew
Transliteration

Definition
properly, rubbed; but used abstractly, a rubbing (with perfumery)
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) scraping, rubbing; 1a) of year of preparation of girls for the harem; bodily rubbings
