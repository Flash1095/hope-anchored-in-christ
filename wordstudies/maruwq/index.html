mârûwq

properly, rubbed; but used abstractly, a rubbing (with perfumery)

Biblical Usage

mârûwq is a rubbing with perfumery, the purifying treatment of the women, and it occurs once, in Esther 2:12. There every maid's turn comes after twelve months, six months with oil of myrrh and six months with sweet odours and other things for the purifying of the women. The word is the slow preparation before the king.

Theological Significance

I believe mârûwq shows me the long preparation behind Esther's hour, and that God readies His people long before they see why.
