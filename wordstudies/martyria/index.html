martyría

from G3144; evidence given (judicially or genitive case):--record, report, testimony, witness.

Biblical Usage

martyría is witness, testimony, the record given. In 1 John 5:9 the witness of God is greater than the witness of men, for it is the witness God has testified of His Son, and in 1 John 5:10 he who believes on the Son has the witness in himself. In 1 John 5:11 the record is that God has given us eternal life, and this life is in His Son. In 1 Timothy 3:7 a man must have a good report from those outside, lest he fall into reproach, and the word is the evidence that speaks for Christ.

Theological Significance

I believe martyría teaches me that God's witness is greater than man's. I hold that the witness I carry in myself is the life He has given me.
