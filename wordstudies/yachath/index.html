Yachath

Word
יַחַת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Yachath
Strongs Number
H3189
Definition
Jachath, the name of four Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Jahath = "He will snatch up"; 1) son of Reaiah and grandson of Shobal of the tribe of Judah; 2) a son of Libni and grandson of Gershom and great grandson of Levi; 3) a Gershonite Levite; the eldest son of Shimei and the grandson of Laadan; founder of the house of Jahath; 4) a Levite and son of Shelomoth; 5) a Merarite Levite in the reign of Josiah
