Nᵉbâyôwth

Word
נְבָיוֹת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Nᵉbâyôwth
Strongs Number
H5032
Definition
Nebajoth, a son of Ismael, and the country settled by him
Part Of Speech
proper noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
Nebaioth or Nebajoth = "heights"; 1) a son of Ishmael; 2) people descended from1 called the Nabateans with their capital at Petra
