Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻânâv
Strongs Number

Definition
depressed (figuratively), in mind (gentle) or circumstances (needy, especially saintly)
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) poor, humble, afflicted, meek; 1a) poor, needy; 1b) poor and weak; 1c) poor, weak and afflicted; 1d) humble, lowly, meek
