diákrisis

from G1252; judicial estimation:--discern(-ing), disputation.

Biblical Usage

Discerning is the judgment that tells good from evil, and it belongs to the mature. The gift of discerning of spirits sorts true prophecy from false, the strong meat of the word trains the senses to discern both good and evil, and Paul tells the Romans not to judge one another over doubtful things.

Theological Significance

I believe a trained conscience is a gift of the Spirit. It is not suspicion, and it is not cynicism — it is the steady ability to tell what honours God from what only looks like it.
