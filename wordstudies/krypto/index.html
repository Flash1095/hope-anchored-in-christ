krýptō

a primary verb; to conceal (properly, by covering):--hide (self), keep secret, secret(-ly).

Biblical Usage

To hide is to conceal by covering, and it runs from the hidden treasure to the hidden life. In Matthew 13:44 the kingdom is like treasure hid in a field, in Colossians 3:3 "your life is hid with Christ in God," in Hebrews 11:23 Moses was hid three months by his parents, and in Luke 13:21 the kingdom is like leaven hid in meal until the whole is leavened.

Theological Significance

I take it as given that hiding cuts both ways in Scripture: God hides His people and His treasure for safety, and the kingdom hides like leaven until it works through the whole lump, and both are His doing. What God hides, He keeps; what He plants in secret, He grows.
