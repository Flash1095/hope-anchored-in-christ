pléō

, another form for πλεύω; which is used as an alternate in certain tenses; probably a form of G4150 (through the idea of plunging through the water); to pass in a vessel:--sail. See also G4130.

Biblical Usage

Pleo means to sail, to go by ship. The disciples sailed with Jesus across the lake in Luke 8:23, when the storm came down, and Paul sailed toward Rome in Acts 27:2. Revelation 18:17 says the shipmasters stood afar off when Babylon fell in one hour.

Theological Significance

I believe the sea is no safer than the land when God shakes a city — and no stormier when Jesus is in the boat.
