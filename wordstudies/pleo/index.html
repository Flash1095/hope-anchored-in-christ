pléō

Word
πλέω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
pléō
Strongs Number
G4126
Definition
, another form for πλεύω; which is used as an alternate in certain tenses; probably a form of G4150 (through the idea of plunging through the water); to pass in a vessel:--sail. See also G4130.
