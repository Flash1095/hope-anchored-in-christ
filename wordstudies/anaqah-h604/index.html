ʼănâqâh

some kind of lizard, probably the gecko (from its wail)

Biblical Usage

The word appears once, in the list of unclean creeping things: 'the ferret, and the chameleon, and the lizard, and the snail, and the mole.' It is one of the small creatures of the ground — probably the gecko, named from its wail — and the law counts it unclean, not to be touched or eaten.

Theological Significance

One small creature in one list carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the list — the clean and the unclean set apart down to the smallest creeping thing.
