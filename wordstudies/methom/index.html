mᵉthôm

wholesomeness; also (adverb) completely

Biblical Usage

Isaiah says the whole head is sick and there is no soundness in the nation from foot to head, only wounds and bruises. Judges uses the same word for the completeness of the slaughter at Gibeah, everything that came to hand. Soundness and completeness are one word, and Isaiah shows what happens when both are gone.

Theological Significance

I take Isaiah 1:6 as a diagnosis of a whole people: no soundness anywhere, only wounds, and no one had bound them up.
