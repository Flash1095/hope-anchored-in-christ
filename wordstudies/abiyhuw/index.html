ʼĂbîyhûwʼ

Abihu, a son of Aaron

Biblical Usage

Abihu is the second son of Aaron, named with his brothers Nadab, Eleazar, and Ithamar. He goes up the mountain with Moses and sees God; and then, with Nadab, he offers ‘strange fire before the LORD, which he commanded them not’, and fire from the LORD devours them both.

Theological Significance

Abihu stood on the mountain and saw God — and still died for offering strange fire. I believe his story is the sharpest warning in the priesthood: nearness to God is not a licence, and worship is only accepted on His terms, not mine.
