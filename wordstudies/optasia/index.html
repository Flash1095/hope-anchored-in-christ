optasía

from a presumed derivative of G3700; visuality, i.e. (concretely) an apparition:--vision.

Biblical Usage

A vision is a sight given by God. Zechariah saw one in the temple and came out unable to speak, the women at the tomb reported a vision of angels, and Paul told Agrippa he was not disobedient unto the heavenly vision.

Theological Significance

I believe visions are God's gift, not man's achievement: Zechariah saw one in the temple and Paul on the road, and neither of them sought the sight — God gave it. And the vision I need most is not a sight but a hearing: the word of the Lord, spoken plainly.
