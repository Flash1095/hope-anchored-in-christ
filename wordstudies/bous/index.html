boûs

probably from the base of G1006; an ox (as grazing), i.e. an animal of that species ("beef"):--ox.

Biblical Usage

An ox is the working animal of the farm, and it appears seven times, mostly in Jesus' sabbath arguments. In Luke 13:15 the Lord says, doth not each one of you on the sabbath loose his ox or his ass from the stall, and lead him away to watering? In Luke 14:5 he asks which of you shall have an ass or an ox fallen into a pit, and will not straightway pull him out on the sabbath day? And in 1 Corinthians 9:9 Paul quotes the law, thou shalt not muzzle the mouth of the ox that treadeth out the corn.

Theological Significance

I believe the ox in these verses is God's argument for mercy: if you would water your ox on the sabbath, you can heal a man on it too.
