eleeinós

from G1656; pitiable:--miserable.

Biblical Usage

eleeinós is the word for miserable. In 1 Corinthians 15:19, the text says "we are of all men most miserable."

Theological Significance

This word, eleeinós, does its job quietly — miserable — and I leave it at that.
