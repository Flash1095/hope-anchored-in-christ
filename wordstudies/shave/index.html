shâvᵉʼ

evil (as destructive), literally (ruin) or morally (especially guile); figuratively idolatry (as false, subjective), uselessness (as deceptive, objective; also adverbially, in vain)

Biblical Usage

Vanity is emptiness that pretends to be something. The commandment forbids taking the name of the LORD thy God in vain, Jeremiah says the people burned incense to vanity and stumbled from the ancient paths, and Zechariah says the idols have spoken vanity and the diviners have seen a lie. Ezekiel hears prophets seeing vanity and divining lies to bring men upon the necks of the slain.

Theological Significance

I believe vanity is the root of idolatry: a word with nothing in it, worshipped as if it were God. I hold that the name of the LORD is not to be spent on empty things.
