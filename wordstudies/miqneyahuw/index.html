Miqnêyâhûw

Miknejah, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Mikneiah is a Levite musician, named twice in the procession that brought the ark to Jerusalem. He played the harp on the Sheminith, among the singers and the gatekeepers who served before the ark.

Theological Significance

I believe the harp is the honour: Mikneiah's name is kept with the music that went up with the ark, and the song is part of the story.
