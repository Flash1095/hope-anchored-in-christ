hyperánō

from G5228 and G507; above upward, i.e. greatly higher (in place or rank):--far above, over.

Biblical Usage

The word hyperánō carries the sense of far above, high above. In Ephesians 1:21, the text says "Far above all principality." In Ephesians 4:10, the text says "He that descended is the same also that ascended up far above all heavens."

Theological Significance

Reading hyperánō this way, I believe glory is the weight of who God is, and it is the end of everything He does. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
