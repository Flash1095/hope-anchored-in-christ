miphqâd

an appointment, i.e. mandate; concretely, a designated spot; specifically, a census

Biblical Usage

The word is an appointment, a muster, a numbering. Joab brought David the sum of the muster of the people, and Ezekiel was told to burn the sin offering in the appointed place of the house.

Theological Significance

I hold that numbering is never neutral in Scripture: David's census brought a plague, and the lesson is that counting what God has given can turn into trusting the count instead of Him.
