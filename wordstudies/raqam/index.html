râqam

to variegate color, i.e. embroider; by implication, to fabricate

Biblical Usage

Raqam is to variegate colour, to embroider — and by implication, to fabricate. The hanging for the door of the tent was 'wrought with needlework'; the coat for Aaron was 'embroidered'; and God filled the workmen 'with wisdom of heart, to work all manner of work, of the engraver, and of the cunning workman, and of the embroiderer.'

Theological Significance

I hold that embroidery is theology in thread: God gave wisdom of heart to men who worked in blue and purple and scarlet, because beauty belongs in His house.
