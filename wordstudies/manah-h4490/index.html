mânâh

properly, something weighed out, i.e. (generally) a division; specifically (of food) a ration; also a lot

Biblical Usage

mânâh is a portion, something weighed out and given. In 1 Samuel 1:5 Elkanah gives Hannah a worthy portion because he loves her, and in Esther 9:19 the Jews send portions to one another on the feast day. In Leviticus 7:33 the priest's part of the peace offering is named, and in Psalms 16:5 the LORD Himself is the portion of the psalmist's inheritance and cup. A portion can be food, a share, or God Himself.

Theological Significance

I believe mânâh tells me that God does not deal in scraps. He gives worthy portions to His people, and I hold that the best portion of all is the LORD Himself.
