elpis

Word
ἐλπίς
Language
Greek
Transliteration
elpis
Strongs Number
G1680
Definition
Hope — confident expectation of good to come, grounded in God's faithfulness rather than in circumstances or chance.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Hebrews 6:19; Romans 8:24-25; 1 Peter 1:3; Colossians 1:27
Biblical Usage
Unlike everyday Greek usage, where hope could be uncertain, New Testament elpis rests on Christ's resurrection. Peter calls it a 'living hope,' and Paul names its object: 'Christ in you, the hope of glory.'
Theological Significance
Elpis is this blog's heartbeat: 'We have this hope as an anchor for the soul, firm and secure.' Christian hope is not optimism — it is certainty about God's future, holding us steady today.
