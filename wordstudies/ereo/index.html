eréō

probably a fuller form of G4483; an alternate for G2036 in certain tenses; to utter, i.e. speak or say:--call, say, speak (of), tell.

Biblical Usage

Eréō is the future of the verb for saying — 'I will say', 'he will say'. It is the form Jesus uses when He tells what will happen, and Paul uses when he asks what we shall say then. The future tense is the point: the words are promised before they are spoken.

Theological Significance

I take eréō as it stands — saying, still to come — and read the promises attached to it as promises, not guesses.
