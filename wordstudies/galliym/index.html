Gallîym

Gallim, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Gallim is a place in the hill country, named twice as a landmark of sorrow. Saul gave Michal, David's wife, to Phalti the son of Laish, which was of Gallim, in 1 Samuel 25:44, and Isaiah cries to the daughter of Gallim to lift up her voice in Isaiah 10:30. The town is remembered for a marriage broken and a city threatened.

Theological Significance

I believe Gallim is a place where vows were broken and voices were raised in fear. Michal was given away and a city was warned, and I hold that God remembers the towns where His people suffered.
