chûwsh

Word
חוּשׁ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
chûwsh
Strongs Number
H2363
Definition
to hurry; figuratively, to be eager with excitement or enjoyment
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to haste, make haste, hurry; 1a) (Qal) to make haste; 1b) (Hiphil); 1b1) to show haste, act quickly, hasten, come quickly; 1b2) to enjoy, be excited
