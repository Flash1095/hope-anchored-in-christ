chûwsh

to hurry; figuratively, to be eager with excitement or enjoyment

Biblical Usage

To hurry is to move fast because the matter is urgent: the tribes promise to go armed before Israel until the land is won, and Moses says vengeance is coming in the day their foot shall slide. The ambush hurries against Gibeah, and Jonathan's lad hurries to fetch the arrow. The word is speed with a purpose behind it.

Theological Significance

I believe hurry in Scripture is usually God's people moving to obey, or God's judgment moving to arrive. The tribes hurried to keep a promise, and the lad hurried to serve his master. I hold that speed is good when the direction is right, and dangerous when it is not.
