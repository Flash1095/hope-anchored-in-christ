Shᵉyâʼ

Sheja, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Sheva — spelled here with a yod where the other spelling has a vav — is the scribe in the days of David, named beside Zadok and Abiathar the priests. The same verse carries the name in both spellings, and this row is the scribe.

Theological Significance

A scribe's name deserves its place, because the man who kept the king's records kept the history I read.
