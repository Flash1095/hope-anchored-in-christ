ʻâzab

to loosen, i.e. relinquish, permit, etc.

Biblical Usage

Azab means to loosen, to let go — and in Scripture it is the word for forsaking and for being forsaken, both ways. A man shall leave his father and mother and cleave to his wife, God promises I will never leave thee nor forsake thee, Joshua is told the Lord will not fail him nor forsake him, and Ruth tells Naomi, whither thou goest, I will go, and where thou lodgest, I will lodge.

Theological Significance

I hold that the whole Bible turns on this word's two directions: men forsake God, and God never forsakes his people. Ruth would not leave Naomi, and God will not leave me — that is the promise I stand on.
