mishpat

Word
מִשְׁפָּט
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
mishpat
Strongs Number
H4941
Definition
Justice, judgement and ordinance — God's righteous decisions and the just order He requires in courts, community and daily life.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Micah 6:8; Amos 5:24; Psalm 72:1-2; Deuteronomy 16:18-20
Biblical Usage
Mishpat is what God requires — 'to do justice, love kindness and walk humbly' — and what corrupt courts perverted. Kings were judged by whether the poor received mishpat under their reign.
Theological Significance
Mishpat shows faith is never private only: the God of justice calls His people to defend the vulnerable. His final judgement means wrongs will not have the last word.
