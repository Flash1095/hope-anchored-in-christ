anḗmeros

from G1 (as a negative particle) and ἥμερος (lame); savage:--fierce.

Biblical Usage

Paul warns that in the last days men will be fierce, despisers of those that are good, traitors, heady, highminded. The word means savage, untamed, like a wild animal that cannot be gentled.

Theological Significance

One word for savagery carries no teaching of its own; its warning is Paul's, that the last days will be marked by men who cannot be reasoned with or softened.
