teléō

from G5056; to end, i.e. complete, execute, conclude, discharge (a debt):--accomplish, make an end, expire, fill up, finish, go over, pay, perform.

Biblical Usage

The word is to finish, to end, to complete, to discharge a debt. Jesus cried, It is finished, and bowed His head and gave up the ghost, after He knew all things were now accomplished. Paul says, I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith; and Matthew says when Jesus had finished all these sayings, He told His disciples of the passover, while Luke says Joseph and Mary returned to Nazareth when they had performed all things according to the law.

Theological Significance

I believe it is finished is the greatest sentence ever spoken, and Paul's finished course is the echo of it. My position is that the work of salvation is done, and my course is to keep the faith until it is finished too.
