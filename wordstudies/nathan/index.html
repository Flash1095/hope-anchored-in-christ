nâthan

to give, used with greatest latitude of application (put, make, etc.)

Biblical Usage

God gives every herb bearing seed for food on the first page of the Bible, and He gives His people power to get wealth so that He may establish His covenant. Hannah prays for a child and the LORD gives her the petition she asked, and the psalm promises that He will give the desires of the heart to the one who delights in Him.

Theological Significance

I believe giving is who God is before it is anything I do: He gave the garden, He gave the wealth, He gave the child, and He gives the desires of the heart. Every good gift I hold came from His hand first.
