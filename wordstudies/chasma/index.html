chásma

from a form of an obsolete primary χάω (to "gape" or "yawn"); a "chasm" or vacancy (impassable interval):--gulf.

Biblical Usage

A chasm is a gaping, impassable interval, and Jesus uses it for the great gulf fixed between the rich man in torment and Lazarus in Abraham's bosom. No one can pass from one side to the other.

Theological Significance

I believe Jesus meant the gulf to be taken seriously: the choices of this life fix where we stand in the next, and no crossing is made after death.
