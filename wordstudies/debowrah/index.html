dᵉbôwrâh

the bee (from its systematic instincts)

Biblical Usage

The bee is the small, ordered worker whose sting and whose honey both appear in Scripture. The Amorites chased Israel as bees do, the Lord whistles for the fly and the bee of Assyria, and Samson finds honey in the lion's carcass where the bees have made their hive. The psalmist's enemies compass him about like bees, and are quenched as the fire of thorns.

Theological Significance

I believe the bee is a picture of the ordered life: small, busy, and dangerous to disturb, and the honey it makes is the sweetness God puts in unlikely places. The bee works in order and stings in defence, and the life that is ordered is the life that bears honey.
