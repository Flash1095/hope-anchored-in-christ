ʼayâlâh

a doe or female deer

Biblical Usage

The doe is the female deer, and Scripture uses her for grace and sure footing. Jacob blesses Naphtali as a hind let loose, giving goodly words; David sings that God makes his feet like hinds' feet and sets him on high places; Habakkuk says the Lord is my strength and will make my feet like hinds' feet; and the Song adjures by the hinds of the field not to stir up love until it please.

Theological Significance

I believe God does not just carry me to the high places — he gives me the feet to stand on them myself. That is the promise I hold when the ground feels steep.
