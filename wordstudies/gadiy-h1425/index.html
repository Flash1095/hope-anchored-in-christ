Gâdîy

a Gadite (collectively) or descendants of Gad

Biblical Usage

Gadite means a man of Gad, the tribe that settled east of Jordan. David made rulers over the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half tribe of Manasseh in 1 Chronicles 26:32, and the land of the Gadites is named as the far east of Israel in 2 Kings 10:33. The word marks a people by their father's name.

Theological Significance

I believe the Gadites show that faithfulness is not about where you settle; they lived east of Jordan, across the river from the rest, yet they served the king and the LORD, and I hold that distance from the centre is no distance from God.
