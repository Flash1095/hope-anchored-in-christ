Nachath

a descent, i.e. imposition, unfavorable (punishment) or favorable (food); restfulness

Biblical Usage

Nachath is a descent or an imposition, and in its good sense it means restfulness and quietness. In Ecclesiastes 4:6 a handful with quietness is better than both hands full with travail, and in Isaiah 30:15 the Lord says in returning and rest shall ye be saved, and in quietness and in confidence shall be your strength.

Theological Significance

I hold that quietness is not laziness, it is trust with its hands open; the handful with quietness feeds a person better than two fists full of worry.
