nᵉbîyʼâh

a prophetess or (generally) inspired woman; by implication, a poetess; by association a prophet's wife

Biblical Usage

Miriam the prophetess takes a timbrel and leads the women in song after the sea, and Deborah the prophetess judges Israel under her palm tree. Huldah the prophetess is consulted when the book of the law is found, and Noadiah the prophetess is named among those who would frighten Nehemiah.

Theological Significance

I believe God raises up prophetesses as well as prophets: Miriam sings, Deborah judges, Huldah reads the law, and their voices carry His word whether the age welcomes them or not. Their voices carry His word, and the church that silences them loses what God gave it.
