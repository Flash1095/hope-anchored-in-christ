ʼattâh

thou and thee, or (plural) ye and you

Biblical Usage

Thou is the word God and man speak to each other all through the Bible, and it is the most personal word in the language. The LORD said to Pharaoh, if thou refuse to let my people go, David said, but thou, O LORD, art a shield for me, and again, but thou art holy, O thou that inhabitest the praises of Israel, and the psalm says, thou rulest the raging of the sea.

Theological Significance

I believe thou is the word that makes God near, because it is spoken face to face, not about Him but to Him. Pharaoh heard it as a warning and David spoke it as a prayer, and I hold that the difference between the two is the whole difference between rebellion and faith.
