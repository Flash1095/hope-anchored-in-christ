Shâmîyr

Shamir, the name of two places in Palestine, and of a Levite

Biblical Usage

Shamir is two places and one man, all in the same spelling. One Shamir lies in the mountains of Judah, and another in Mount Ephraim, where Tola dwelt and judged Israel. The same name also belongs to a Levite, Shamir of the sons of Michah, in the courses of the priests.

Theological Significance

I believe God names places and people with the same word, and the Shamir where a judge was buried is as much His as the Shamir who served in the temple.
