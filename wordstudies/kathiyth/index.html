kâthîyth

beaten, i.e. pure (oil)

Biblical Usage

Kathiyth means beaten, pure oil, olive oil pounded fine until it is clear. Exodus 27:20 commands the children of Israel to bring pure oil olive beaten for the light, to cause the lamp to burn always, and Leviticus 24:2 repeats the command, to cause the lamps to burn continually. Numbers 28:5 mixes the daily meat offering with beaten oil, and 1 Kings 5:11 says Solomon gave Hiram twenty measures of pure oil year by year.

Theological Significance

I believe beaten oil is the picture of a life pressed until it is pure: God does not light his lamp with cloudy oil. I hold that the beating is not punishment, it is preparation, and the light that burns always is worth the pressing.
