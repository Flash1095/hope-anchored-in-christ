shârar

to be hostile (only active participle an opponent)

Biblical Usage

Mine enemies are the reason David asks God to lead him, and the psalms name them in every direction. Teach me Thy way, O LORD, and lead me in a plain path, because of mine enemies, and lead me in Thy righteousness because of mine enemies. The psalm promises that God shall reward evil unto mine enemies, and David cries that his enemies would daily swallow him up.

Theological Significance

I believe enemies are the reason to pray for guidance, because a plain path is hardest to find when men are watching it. David never asks God to remove his enemies first, he asks God to lead him straight through them. My position is that the plain path is the safest path, and the enemies are the proof that I need it.
