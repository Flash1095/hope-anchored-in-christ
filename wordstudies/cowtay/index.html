Çôwṭay

Sotai, one of the Nethinim

Biblical Usage

Sotai is one of the Nethinim, named twice in the lists of the servants who return from exile. His children come home with Zerubbabel to serve in the temple, and his name is kept among the temple servants. Nothing more is told of him.

Theological Significance

I believe Sotai matters because the temple servants came home, and the work of God's house depends on people whose names are only in lists.
