niqqud

a crumb (as broken to spots); also a biscuit (as pricked)

Biblical Usage

Crumbs and cracknels are the bread of the journey and the gift to the prophet. The Gibeonites show dry and mouldy bread as the proof of a long road, and Jeroboam's wife carries ten loaves and cracknels to Ahijah to ask about her child.

Theological Significance

I hold that the crumbs tell the story: mouldy bread for a lie, and cracknels for a question no one else could answer.
