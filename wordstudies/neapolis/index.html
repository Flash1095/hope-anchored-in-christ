Neápolis

from G3501 and G4172; new town; Neapolis, a place in Macedonia:--Neapolis.

Biblical Usage

Neapolis is the new town where Paul lands after sailing from Troas, on the way to Philippi in Macedonia. The name is written as two words in the Greek, a new city on the shore of the gospel's road into Europe.

Theological Significance

I hold that the name says what the gospel does: it makes a new town out of an old shore, and Paul's first step into Europe is a step into a place called new.
