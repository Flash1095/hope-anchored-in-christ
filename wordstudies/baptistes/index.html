Baptistḗs

from G907; a baptizer, as an epithet of Christ's forerunner:--Baptist.

Biblical Usage

Baptistes is the title given to John, the Baptist, the man who came preaching repentance in the wilderness and baptizing in Jordan, whom Jesus calls the greatest born of women.

Theological Significance

I hold that John's title is his whole message in one word: he baptized with water, and he pointed to the One who would baptize with the Holy Ghost and with fire.
