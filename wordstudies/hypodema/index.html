hypódēma

from G5265; something bound under the feet, i.e. a shoe or sandal:--shoe.

Biblical Usage

hypódēma means a shoe, a sandal. In Matthew 3:11, the text says "whose shoes I am not worthy to bear." In Mark 1:7, the text says "the latchet of whose shoes I am not worthy to stoop down and unloose."

Theological Significance

I believe hypódēma shows us that God provides, and His provision is enough — even when it does not look like we expected. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
