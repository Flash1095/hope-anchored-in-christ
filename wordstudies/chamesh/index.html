châmêsh

five

Biblical Usage

Chamesh is simply the number five, and it counts everything from years to kings. Seth lived a hundred and five years and begat Enos, Joseph tells his brothers five years of famine remain, Israel reached the wilderness of Sin on the fifteenth day of the second month, and five Amorite kings joined together against Gibeon.

Theological Significance

I believe there is nothing mystical to find in the number itself; Scripture counts with it the way any family counts years and loaves. What matters is not the five but what God does with the five, whether years of famine or kings gathered for battle.
