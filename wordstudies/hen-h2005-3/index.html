hên

lo!; also (as expressing surprise) if

Biblical Usage

Hen means behold, lo, here I am, and it is the word of a servant answering the call. In 1 Samuel 3:4 the child Samuel says, here am I, in 1 Samuel 3:5 he says it again to Eli, here am I, for thou calledst me, and in Isaiah 6:8 the prophet says, here am I, send me.

Theological Significance

I believe the three answers are one answer: here I am; Samuel did not know who was calling, Isaiah did not know where he would be sent, and each one answered before he knew.
