chebel

a rope (as twisted), especially a measuring line; by implication, a district or inheritance (as measured); or a noose (as of cords); figuratively, a company (as if tied together); also a throe (especially of parturition); also ruin

Biblical Usage

A cord is a rope, a measuring line, and a company bound together, and the word stretches across all three. Saul meets a company of prophets coming down from the high place, David measures the Moabites with a line, and Amos warns Amaziah that his land will be divided by line.

Theological Significance

I believe the line measures what God has decided: it gives the prophets their company, the Moabites their judgement, and Amaziah his loss. The cord that binds can also be the cord that divides.
