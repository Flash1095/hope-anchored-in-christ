Dârᵉyâvêsh

{Darejavesh, a title (rather than name) of several Persian kings}

Biblical Usage

Darius is the title of the Persian kings under whom the exiles lived, and the name appears for the Darius who received Daniel's accusers and for the Darius under whom the temple was finished. He is the king who signed the decree that threw Daniel to the lions, and the king whose second year saw the house of God rebuilt.

Theological Significance

I believe Darius shows how God uses even foreign kings: one signed a death warrant and the other a building permit, and the Lord's purpose stood through both.
