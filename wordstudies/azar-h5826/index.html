ʻâzar

to surround, i.e. protect or aid

Biblical Usage

Azar is to surround, to protect — to come around someone the way a shield comes around a soldier. God helped the Levites who carried the ark, he helped Israel when the Syrians surrounded them, David says the Lord helped me and my heart greatly rejoiceth, and Isaiah says the Lord God will help me, therefore shall I not be confounded. Help that comes from outside and stands between.

Theological Significance

I believe God's help is a surrounding help — not a hint from a distance but a shield that comes around me. Fear not, he says, for I am with thee, and I hold that promise when the enemy is on every side.
