mân

who or what (properly, interrogatively, hence, also indefinitely and relatively)

Biblical Usage

mân is the Aramaic word for who or what, a question word that can also mean whomsoever. In Ezra 5:3 the governor asks, Who has commanded you to build this house, and in Daniel 4:17 the Most High gives the kingdom to whomsoever He will. The same small word can challenge a work or confess God's freedom.

Theological Significance

I believe mân puts me in my place, since God gives rule to whomsoever He will and no governor's question can undo it.
