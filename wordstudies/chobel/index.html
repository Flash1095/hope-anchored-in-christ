chôbêl

a sailor

Biblical Usage

Chobel is a sailor, a man who works the ropes of a ship, and the prophets use it for Tyre's proud fleet and Jonah's flight. The sailors of Tyre are the wise men of her ships, her pilots are the princes of the sea, and when the storm rises the shipmaster finds Jonah asleep below and wakes him to call on his God.

Theological Significance

I believe the sailors in these stories know the sea better than the prophets know their duty, and that is the rebuke in Jonah. I hold that God sends storms to wake His sleeping servants, and He uses sailors to do it.
