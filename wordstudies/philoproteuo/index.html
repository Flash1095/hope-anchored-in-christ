philoprōteúō

from a compound of G5384 and G4413; to be fond of being first, i.e. ambitious of distinction:--love to have the preeminence.

Biblical Usage

Philoproteuo means to love being first, to be ambitious for preeminence, and it appears once, in John's letter about Diotrephes. John writes that Diotrephes, who loves to have the preeminence among them, receives us not, and prates against us with malicious words.

Theological Significance

I hold that loving to be first is the oldest sin in the church: Diotrephes wanted the seat, and he cast out the brethren to keep it.
