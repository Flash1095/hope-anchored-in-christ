syzáō

from G4862 and G2198; to continue to live in common with, i.e. co-survive (literally or figuratively):--live with.

Biblical Usage

Paul says if we are dead with Christ, we believe we shall also live with Him. He repeats the promise to Timothy as a faithful saying: if we die with Him, we shall live with Him. And to the Corinthians he writes that he carries them in his heart, to die and live with them. The word is about a shared life, here with one another and hereafter with Christ.

Theological Significance

I hold that living with Christ is not only a future hope but a present bond. Paul ties the two together: the life we share with one another now is a rehearsal of the life we shall share with Him then. I believe that is why Christian fellowship matters so much — it is eternity, begun early.
