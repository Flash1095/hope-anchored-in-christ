gâdad

to crowd; also to gash (as if by pressing into)

Biblical Usage

Gadad means to crowd together, and then to cut or gash as a crowd presses in. The prophets of Baal cut themselves with knives and lancets on Carmel in 1 Kings 18:28, and the law forbids Israel to cut themselves for the dead in Deuteronomy 14:1 and again in Jeremiah 16:6. But the word also gathers: the wicked gather themselves together against the soul of the righteous in Psalms 94:21.

Theological Significance

I believe this word shows how false worship turns on the body and how true trouble gathers against the righteous. The Baal prophets cut themselves and got no answer, and I hold that the God who answers by fire needs no blood from us.
