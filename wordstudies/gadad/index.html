gâdad

Word
גָּדַד
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
gâdad
Strongs Number
H1413
Definition
to crowd; also to gash (as if by pressing into)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to penetrate, cut, attack, invade; 1a) (Qal) to penetrate, cut into; 1b)(Hithpoel); 1b1) to cut oneself; 1b2) to gather in troops or crowds
