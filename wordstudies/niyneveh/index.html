Nîynᵉvêh

Nineveh, the capital of Assyria

Biblical Usage

Nineveh is built by Asshur out of the land of Shinar, and it becomes the capital of Assyria and the home of its kings. Sennacherib returns there after his army is destroyed, and dwells at Nineveh — the city that Jonah preached to, and the city where the proud king hides.

Theological Significance

I believe Nineveh is the city of two endings: it repented once at Jonah's word, and it sheltered Sennacherib after God broke his army.
