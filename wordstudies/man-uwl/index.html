manʻûwl

a bolt

Biblical Usage

manʻûwl is a bolt or lock, the fastening on a door. In Nehemiah 3:3 the builders set up the doors of the fish gate with their locks and bars, and in Song of Solomon 5:5 the beloved's hands drop with myrrh upon the handles of the lock as she rises to open to him. A bolt can shut a city out or keep a lover waiting.

Theological Significance

I believe manʻûwl reminds me that doors matter, and that the heart has its own bolts that only love opens.
