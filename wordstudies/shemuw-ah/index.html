shᵉmûwʻâh

something heard, i.e. an announcement

Biblical Usage

A report is news that travels, good or bad. Tidings came to Joab that turned him to Adonijah, tidings came to David that Absalom had slain all the king's sons, and Isaiah asks, who hath believed our report? But Proverbs says good news from a far country is as cold waters to a thirsty soul.

Theological Significance

I believe the question of Isaiah is the question of the gospel: who hath believed our report? The news of the arm of the LORD is good news, and I hold that believing it is life.
