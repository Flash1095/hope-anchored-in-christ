parzel

iron

Biblical Usage

Parzel is iron. Its weight in the Bible falls in Daniel 2, where Nebuchadnezzar's image has legs of iron and feet part of iron and part of clay: the iron kingdom breaks in pieces and subdues all things in Daniel 2:40, and the stone cut out without hands breaks the iron in Daniel 2:45.

Theological Significance

I hold that iron is the strongest kingdom men can build, and the stone that breaks it is not cut by human hands, because the kingdom that lasts is God's.
