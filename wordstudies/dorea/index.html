dōreá

from G1435; a gratuity:--gift.

Biblical Usage

A free gift, the gift of God that Jesus offered the woman at the well, and the gift of the Holy Ghost promised to all who repent and are baptized. Paul sets the free gift against Adam's offence: where sin abounded, the gift abounded much more, and Hebrews warns those who have tasted the heavenly gift.

Theological Significance

I believe the gift of God is free because it cost Him everything, and it cannot be earned, only received. The living water, the Holy Spirit, and the grace that covers Adam's offence are all one gift with one price already paid.
