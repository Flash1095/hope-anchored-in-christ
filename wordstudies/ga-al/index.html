gâʼal

to soil or (figuratively) desecrate

Biblical Usage

Ga'al means to soil or defile, and it touches food, blood, and the priesthood itself. Daniel purposed in his heart that he would not defile himself with the king's meat in Daniel 1:8, and the priests who could not prove their genealogy were put from the priesthood as polluted in Ezra 2:62. Isaiah says the people's hands are defiled with blood in Isaiah 59:3, and Lamentations sees priests who have polluted themselves with blood in Lamentations 4:14.

Theological Significance

I believe defilement is not a small matter, because it touches what is holy. Daniel would not eat what would soil him, and I hold that the same refusal is asked of us: to keep ourselves unspotted from the world.
