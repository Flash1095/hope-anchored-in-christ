desheʼ

a sprout; by analogy, grass

Biblical Usage

Grass is the first green thing God speaks into being, and it becomes the picture of tender growth everywhere in Scripture. Moses' teaching falls like rain on the tender herb, David says the righteous spring up like grass after rain, and Isaiah promises the bones of God's people will flourish like grass.

Theological Significance

I believe grass is God's picture of life that depends on Him alone. It springs up without our help and withers without His rain, and the same is true of every good thing in my life.
