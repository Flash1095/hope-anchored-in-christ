shâlash

Word
שָׁלַשׁ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
shâlash
Strongs Number
H8027
Definition
to be (causatively, make) triplicate (by restoration, in portions, strands, days or years)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to do a third time, do three times, divide in three parts; 1a) (Piel) to do a third time, divide into three parts; 1b) (Pual) to be three years of age, be threefold
