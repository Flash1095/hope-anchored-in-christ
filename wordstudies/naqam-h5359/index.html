nâqâm

revenge

Biblical Usage

Naqam as a noun is vengeance, the repayment due to the wronged, and the Bible keeps it in God's hands. In Ezekiel 25:12 Edom deals against the house of Judah by taking vengeance, and greatly offends, and in Psalm 58:10 the righteous shall rejoice when he sees the vengeance. In Proverbs 6:34 jealousy is the rage of a man who will not spare in the day of vengeance, and in Ezekiel 25:15 the Philistines take vengeance with a despiteful heart, to destroy for the old hatred.

Theological Significance

I believe vengeance belongs to God because only He can repay without becoming what He repays. Edom took vengeance and the Philistines took vengeance, and the Lord answers both in Ezekiel. I hold that my neighbour is to be loved, and my enemy is to be left to God.
