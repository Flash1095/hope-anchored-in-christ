misthōtós

from G3409; a wage-worker (good or bad):--hired servant, hireling.

Biblical Usage

The word is a hireling, a wage-worker, and its uses draw the line between the hired and the called. James and John left their father in the ship with the hired servants, and Jesus says the hireling is not the shepherd: he sees the wolf coming and flees, because he cares not for the sheep.

Theological Significance

I hold that the hireling is the warning to every leader: the shepherd stays because the sheep are his, and the hireling flees because they are only his wages.
