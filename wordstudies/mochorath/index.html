mochŏrâth

the morrow or (adverbially) tomorrow

Biblical Usage

Mochorath is the morrow, tomorrow — Lot's daughters act the next day, the LORD strikes Egypt's cattle on the morrow, and a worm eats Jonah's gourd the next morning.

Theological Significance

I believe tomorrow belongs to God in a way today does not: it arrives with His doing, not mine.
