patrís

from G3902; a father-land, i.e. native town; (figuratively) heavenly home:--(own) country.

Biblical Usage

Patris is one's own country, the fatherland, the native town. Jesus says in Matthew 13:57 that a prophet is not without honour, save in his own country and in his own house, and the same saying stands in Mark 6:4 and Luke 4:24. John 4:44 says a prophet hath no honour in his own country. Hebrews 11:14 turns the word to heaven: those who seek a country declare plainly that they seek a country of their own.

Theological Significance

I believe the prophet's own country is the hardest place to be heard, because familiarity is the enemy of honour. That is why I hold that the country the saints seek is not the one they were born in, it is the one God has prepared.
