ʻâthaq

Word
עָתַק
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻâthaq
Strongs Number
H6275
Definition
to remove (intransitive or transitive) figuratively, to grow old; specifically, to transcribe
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to move, proceed, advance, move on, become old, be removed; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to move; 1a2) to advance (in years), grow old and weak; 1b) (Hiphil); 1b1) to move forward, proceed, move on; 1b2) to remove; 1b3) to transcribe
