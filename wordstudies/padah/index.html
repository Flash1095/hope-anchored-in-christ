pâdâh

Word
פָּדָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
pâdâh
Strongs Number
H6299
Definition
to sever, i.e. ransom; gener. to release, preserve
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to ransom, redeem, rescue, deliver; 1a) (Qal) to ransom; 1b) (Niphal) to be ransomed; 1c) (Hiphil) to allow one to be ransomed; 1d) (Hophal) redeemed
