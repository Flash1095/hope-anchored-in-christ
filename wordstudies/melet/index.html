meleṭ

cement (from its plastic smoothness)

Biblical Usage

Melet is cement, the clay mortar of the brickkiln, and Jeremiah 43:9 has the prophet hide great stones in the clay in the brickkiln at the entry of Pharaoh's house in Tahpanhes, in the sight of the men of Judah.

Theological Significance

I believe God hid His sign in the very clay of Egypt's building yard: the place Judah trusted would be the place Babylon broke through.
