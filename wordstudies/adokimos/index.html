adókimos

from G1 (as a negative particle) and G1384; unapproved, i.e. rejected; by implication, worthless (literally or morally):--castaway, rejected, reprobate.

Biblical Usage

Adokimos is 'rejected' — metal that fails the test, a coin that does not ring true, a life that does not measure up. Paul says God gave those who refused Him over to a reprobate mind, warns that he himself could become a castaway if he does not discipline his body, tells the Corinthians to test themselves lest they be reprobates, and Hebrews says ground that bears thorns is rejected. The word always carries the picture of something tested and found wanting.

Theological Significance

I believe this is one of the soberest words in the New Testament, and I take it seriously. It is a call to examine myself, not to despair — because the same Paul who warns of being a castaway also tells us to test ourselves, so that we may know we are in the faith.
