Kēphâs

of Chaldee origin (compare H3710); the Rock; Cephas (i.e. Kepha), a surname of Peter:--Cephas.

Biblical Usage

Kephas, Cephas, is the Aramaic name Jesus gave to Simon, the Rock: John 1:42 says, thou art Simon the son of Jona: thou shalt be called Cephas, which is by interpretation, A stone. 1 Corinthians 1:12 shows the church dividing into parties: I am of Paul, and I of Apollos, and I of Cephas, and I of Christ. 1 Corinthians 15:5 says the risen Lord was seen of Cephas, then of the twelve.

Theological Significance

I believe Cephas is the name that holds Peter's whole story: the stone Jesus named, the party men divided over, and the first witness who saw the risen Lord.
