gâlîyl

Word
גָּלִיל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
gâlîyl
Strongs Number
H1550
Definition
a valve of a folding door (as turning); also a ring (as round)
Part Of Speech
adjective
Biblical Usage
adj; 1) turning, folding (of doors); n m; 2) cylinder, rod; 3) circuit, district (on northern border in Naphtali)
