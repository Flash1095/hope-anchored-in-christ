ʼÊl ʼĕlôhêy Yisrâʼêl

El-Elohi-Jisrael, the title given to a consecrated spot by Jacob

Biblical Usage

El-Elohe-Israel is the title Jacob gave to a consecrated spot. In Genesis 33:20, Jacob builds an altar at Shechem and calls it El-elohe-Israel, 'God, the God of Israel', after God brought him safely home. It is a compound name, built from the word for God joined to 'Elohe-Israel'.

Theological Significance

I believe El-Elohe-Israel matters because the names we give to places where God meets us keep the memory of what He did. That is why Jacob's altar at Shechem still has its name.
