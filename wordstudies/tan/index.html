tan

a monster (as preternaturally formed), i.e. a sea-serpent (or other huge marine animal); also a jackal (or other hideous land animal)

Biblical Usage

The Lord tells Ezekiel to take up a lamentation for Pharaoh, king of Egypt: thou art like a young lion of the nations, and thou art as a whale in the seas, troubling the waters with thy feet, and fouling the rivers. The word is a monster, a sea-serpent, a dragon, and also a jackal: the huge creature that troubles the waters. It appears once, in the lament for a king who thought himself a lion.

Theological Significance

I believe the whale in the seas is Pharaoh's own picture turned against him: he troubled the waters, and the waters would close over him, as they once closed over his army.
