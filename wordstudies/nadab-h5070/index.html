Nâdâb

Nadab, the name of four Israelites

Biblical Usage

Nadab is the name of two men who both died for getting worship wrong and for getting power wrong. In Exodus 6:23 Nadab is the firstborn son of Aaron, born to Elisheba, and in Leviticus 10:1 Nadab and Abihu offer strange fire before the Lord, which He commanded them not, and are consumed. In 1 Kings 15:25 another Nadab, the son of Jeroboam, begins to reign over Israel and walks in the way of his father.

Theological Significance

I believe the two Nadabs are one warning told twice: nearness to God is not a licence to invent worship, and a throne is not a licence to invent a religion. The priest's son and the king's son both learned that God is holy, and neither lived to use the lesson.
