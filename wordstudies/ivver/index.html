ʻivvêr

blind (literally or figuratively)

Biblical Usage

Blind is the word for eyes that cannot see, and the Bible says God is the one who opens them. In Exodus 4:11 the LORD asks, who maketh the dumb, or the deaf, or the seeing, or the blind, have not I the LORD, in Isaiah 29:18 the eyes of the blind shall see out of obscurity, and out of darkness, in Isaiah 35:5 the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and in Deuteronomy 27:18 cursed be he that maketh the blind to wander out of the way.

Theological Significance

I hold that the blind in these verses are the promise and the warning together: God opens the eyes of the blind. Cursed is the man who leads them astray.
