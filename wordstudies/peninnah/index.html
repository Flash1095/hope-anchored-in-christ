Pᵉninnâh

Peninnah, an Israelitess

Biblical Usage

Peninnah was Elkanah's second wife, the mother of his children, while Hannah had none. When Elkanah offered sacrifice he gave portions to Peninnah and to all her sons and her daughters, and Peninnah provoked Hannah year by year for her barrenness.

Theological Significance

My position is that God sees the woman who has everything and the woman who has nothing, and the story ends with the barren woman singing — but Peninnah's children are not forgotten either.
