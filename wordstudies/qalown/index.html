qâlôwn

disgrace; (by implication) the pudenda

Biblical Usage

Disgrace is the end of the road for pride: the wise shall inherit glory, but shame shall be the promotion of fools; the priests' glory is changed into shame; the drunkard is filled with shame for glory. God says He will discover Jerusalem's skirts upon her face, that her shame may appear.

Theological Significance

I believe shame in Scripture is not embarrassment, it is exposure, and God uncovers what we hide so that we will turn. My position is that the shame which leads to repentance is mercy, and the shame which is hidden is the danger.
