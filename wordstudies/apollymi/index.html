apóllymi

from G575 and the base of G3639; to destroy fully (reflexively, to perish, or lose), literally or figuratively:--destroy, die, lose, mar, perish.

Biblical Usage

To destroy fully, to perish, to lose — eighty-four times, and the whole gospel turns on it. God gave His Son that whosoever believeth in Him should not perish in John 3:16, and Jesus says His sheep shall never perish in John 10:28. The lost son was lost and is found in Luke 15:24, and Jesus warns in Matthew 10:39 that he that findeth his life shall lose it, and he that loseth his life for My sake shall find it.

Theological Significance

I believe this is the word that makes John 3:16 what it is: perishing is the alternative, and it is real. But the same word that means destruction also means a sheep lost on the hillside and a life laid down — and in both cases the losing is not the end. The son who was lost is found; the life lost for Christ's sake is found again. Perishing is final only for those who refuse the hand that saves.
