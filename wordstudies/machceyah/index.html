Machçêyâh

Machsejah, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Machsejah, also spelled Maaseiah, is named as a father rather than for himself. Jeremiah 32:12 calls Baruch the son of Neriah, the son of Maaseiah, the man who received the evidence of the purchase, and Jeremiah 51:59 names Seraiah the son of Neriah, the son of Maaseiah, who carried Jeremiah's word to Babylon.

Theological Significance

I hold that a father's name in these verses is a thread: it ties Baruch and Seraiah to one family that kept showing up where God's word was being carried.
