ʼăbaddôh

a perishing

Biblical Usage

This noun for perishing or destruction occurs twice. Job says the fire of lust 'is a fire that consumeth to destruction'; and the proverb pairs it with hell itself — 'Hell and destruction are never full; so the eyes of man are never satisfied'.

Theological Significance

Twice is enough to hear the warning: destruction is a real end, and human appetite is never satisfied on its own. I believe the proverb is honest about my own heart — my eyes are never full either — and that is why I need a satisfaction that comes from outside me.
