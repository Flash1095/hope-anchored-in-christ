ʻĂsâyâh

Asajah, the name of three or four Israelites

Biblical Usage

Asaiah is the name of several men, and three of them are worth naming. The king's servant Asaiah goes with Hilkiah to inquire of the prophetess Huldah about the book of the law, a chief of the Levites named Asaiah helps carry the ark to Jerusalem, and another Asaiah of the Shilonites dwells in Jerusalem after the return.

Theological Significance

A name shared by a king's servant, a Levite chief and a returned exile tells me that God's work runs through men of the same name in different generations, and each one is counted in his own place.
