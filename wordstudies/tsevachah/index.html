tsᵉvâchâh

a screech (of anguish)

Biblical Usage

Scripture uses tsᵉvâchâh where it means a screech, a cry of anguish. In Psalms 144:14, that there be no complaining in our streets, and it appears again in Isaiah 24:11.

Theological Significance

I believe God hears the cry this word names, and the quiet streets of Psalm 144 are the peace He promises instead.
