Çelaʻ ham-machlᵉqôwth

Word
סֶלַע הַמַּחְלְקוֹת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Çelaʻ ham-machlᵉqôwth
Strongs Number
H5555
Definition
Sela-ham-Machlekoth, a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Sela-hammahlekoth = "the cliff of escapes" or "the cliff of divisions"; 1) a rock or cliff in the wilderness of Maon, southeast of Hebron, the scene of an escape of David from Saul
