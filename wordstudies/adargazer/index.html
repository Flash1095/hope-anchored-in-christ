ʼădargâzêr

a chief diviner, or astrologer

Biblical Usage

The adargazer were the counsellors, the judges — Nebuchadnezzar's officials summoned to the dedication of the golden image: "the princes, the governors, and the captains, the judges, the treasurers, the counsellors, the sheriffs, and all the rulers of the provinces." They gathered to bow, and three men among them would not.

Theological Significance

A counsellor's title is only a job in Nebuchadnezzar's court, and the scene it sits in — a whole court bowing, and three men standing — does the preaching.
