taqqîyph

{powerful}

Biblical Usage

The word is mighty, strong, powerful. Daniel says the fourth kingdom shall be strong as iron, breaking in pieces and bruising; the kingdom of the Most High is an everlasting kingdom; and the fourth beast was dreadful and terrible, and strong exceedingly, with great iron teeth.

Theological Significance

I believe the strong kingdoms of this world break and bruise, but the Most High's kingdom is the strong one that lasts.
