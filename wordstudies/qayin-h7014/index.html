Qayin

Kajin, the name of the first child, also of a place in Palestine, and of an Oriental tribe

Biblical Usage

Cain is the first child born after the fall: Eve said, I have gotten a man from the LORD; and Cain talked with Abel his brother, and it came to pass, when they were in the field, that Cain rose up against Abel his brother, and slew him. The LORD set a mark upon Cain, lest any finding him should kill him.

Theological Significance

I believe Cain is the first murderer and the first man marked for mercy at the same time, because God judged him and still protected him. The name also stands for a town in the hill country of Judah and for the Kenites who carried it, and I hold those uses as the long shadow of the first family.
