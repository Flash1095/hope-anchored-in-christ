kardiognṓstēs

from G2588 and G1097; a heart-knower:--which knowest the hearts.

Biblical Usage

Kardiognostes is the heart-knower — God, who knows the hearts of all men. The apostles prayed, Thou, Lord, which knowest the hearts of all men, shew whether of these two Thou hast chosen; and Peter says God, which knoweth the hearts, bare them witness, giving them the Holy Ghost, even as He did unto us.

Theological Significance

I hold that this name is my comfort and my warning: God knows my heart better than I do, and no prayer needs to explain me to Him — but no hiding works either.
