nâtsab

to station, in various applications (literally or figuratively)

Biblical Usage

The LORD stands above Jacob's ladder with His promise, and Hannah stands praying before Eli in the house of the LORD. Officers are set over Solomon's household, and David establishes his dominion by the river as he goes out to war.

Theological Significance

I believe standing is the posture of those God has set in place: the LORD above the ladder, Hannah before the priest, the officer at his post, and the king at the river. God sets His people where He wants them, and they stand.
