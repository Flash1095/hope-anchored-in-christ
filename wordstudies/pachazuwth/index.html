pachăzûwth

frivolity

Biblical Usage

Pachazûwth occurs once: the LORD is against the prophets who prophesy false dreams, cause His people to err by their lies and by their lightness, and do not profit the people at all.

Theological Significance

I believe lightness in a prophet is not a small fault: frivolous words from God's mouthpiece lead a whole people astray.
