ʼeden

a basis (of a building, a column, etc.)

Biblical Usage

The sockets are the heavy bases that hold the tabernacle upright, silver feet under wooden boards, and the Bible counts them with care. Exodus lists the sockets under every board and pillar, Numbers charges the sons of Merari with carrying them, and the Song pictures a lover's legs as pillars of marble set on sockets of fine gold. The foundation is not decoration; it is what keeps the whole house standing.

Theological Significance

I believe God builds on a firm foundation, and He counts the parts nobody sees. The sockets were buried under the boards, but the tabernacle stood on them, and my life stands on Christ the same way.
