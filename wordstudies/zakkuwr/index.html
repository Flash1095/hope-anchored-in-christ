Zakkûwr

Zakkur, the name of seven Israelites

Biblical Usage

Zaccur is named 10 times in the Bible, and the passages here are Numbers 13:4; 1 Chronicles 4:26; Nehemiah 12:35. In Numbers 13:4 we read, "And these were their names: of the tribe of Reuben, Shammua the son of Zaccur." The same name belongs to more than one man, and Nehemiah 12:35 names another of them.

Theological Significance

Zaccur belonged to someone God knew, and I hold that every name in these lists did. A name like this one reminds me that God forgets no one.
