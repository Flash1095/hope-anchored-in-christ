yeshaʻ

liberty, deliverance, prosperity

Biblical Usage

The word yeshaʻ carries the sense of deliverance, salvation. In 2 Samuel 22:3, the horn of my salvation. Its 35 occurrences all press in the same direction.

Theological Significance

I hold that deliverance in Scripture is never an escape God arranges from a distance; He Himself is the deliverance, and that is why I trust Him when no way out is visible. That is why yeshaʻ stays with me long after the verse is closed.
