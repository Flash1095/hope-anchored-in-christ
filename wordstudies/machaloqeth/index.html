machălôqeth

a section (of Levites, people or soldiers)

Biblical Usage

The word names a division, a section of men ordered for service, and it is the word of David's organisation of the kingdom. The sons of Aaron were divided into courses, the children of Israel were ordered by their courses month by month throughout the year, and the priests and Levites kept their divisions in the house of the LORD in Hezekiah's day.

Theological Significance

I believe the divisions show that God's worship is ordered, not random, and every course has its month and its men. I hold that the God who numbers the courses also numbers His people, and no one serves in His house by accident.
