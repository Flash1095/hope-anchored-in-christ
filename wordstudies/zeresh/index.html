Zeresh

Zeresh, Haman's wife

Biblical Usage

Zeresh is Haman's wife, and she is the voice behind his worst idea. When Haman comes home boasting, she and his friends tell him to build the gallows for Mordecai — and when the king's favour turns, she is the one who tells him his fall is certain. Her full story lives on her People profile.

Theological Significance

I believe Zeresh is a warning about the counsel we keep. The people closest to us can push us toward the gallows we build for others, and the gallows, in the end, are ours.
