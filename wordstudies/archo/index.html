árchō

a primary verb; to be first (in political rank or power):--reign (rule) over.

Biblical Usage

The word means to be first, in rank or in time, to rule or to begin. The rulers of the Gentiles exercise lordship over them, the root of Jesse shall rise to reign over the Gentiles, Paul takes bread and begins to eat before the shipwreck, and Jesus begins to warn His disciples about the leaven of the Pharisees.

Theological Significance

I believe being first cuts both ways in the Bible. The Gentiles' rulers use first place to lord it over others, but Christ rises to reign by serving, and the beginning of His teaching is a warning against the pride that loves first place.
