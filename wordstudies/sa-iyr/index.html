sâʻîyr

Word
שָׂעִיר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
sâʻîyr
Strongs Number
H8163
Definition
shaggy; as noun, a he-goat; by analogy, a faun
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
adj; 1) hairy; n m; 2) he-goat, buck; 2a) as sacrificial animal; 2b) satyr, may refer to a demon possessed goat like the swine of Gadara (Mt 8:30-32)
