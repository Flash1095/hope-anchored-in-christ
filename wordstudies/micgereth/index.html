miçgereth

something enclosing, i.e. a margin (of a region, of a panel); concretely, a stronghold

Biblical Usage

The bases of the temple had borders between the ledges, and Ahaz cut off the borders of the bases and took down the sea from the brazen oxen. The word is the enclosing margin, the rim that holds a panel in place, and Ahaz cut it off in his remodelling of the house.

Theological Significance

I notice what Ahaz cut: the borders that held the bases together, and the sea that stood on the oxen, and each cut made the house a little less than Solomon built it.
