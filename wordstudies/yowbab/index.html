Yôwbâb

Jobab, the name of two Israelites and of three foreigners

Biblical Usage

Jobab is named 9 times in the Bible, and the passages here are Genesis 10:29; Joshua 11:1; 1 Chronicles 1:23. In Genesis 10:29 we read, "And Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab: all these were the sons of Joktan." The same name belongs to more than one man, and 1 Chronicles 1:23 names another of them.

Theological Significance

Jobab is brief in Scripture and long in God's memory, and that is my conviction. The Bible does not waste names, and neither does God.
