yârêʼ

fearing; morally, reverent

Biblical Usage

Yârê is the fearing one, the person who lives in reverent fear of God. Obadiah feared the LORD greatly even while he served in Ahab's house, and the preacher says he that feareth God shall come forth of them all. The word also covers the fear of men, as when the prince of the eunuchs feared his lord the king, but the fear of God is the one that saves.

Theological Significance

I believe the fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom because it puts God in His place and me in mine. My position is that a man can fear God in Ahab's house and still be faithful, as Obadiah was. And I hold that the fear of man is a snare, but the fear of God is freedom.
