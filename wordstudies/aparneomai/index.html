aparnéomai

from G575 and G720; to deny utterly, i.e. disown, abstain:--deny.

Biblical Usage

To deny utterly — Peter swearing he never knew Jesus, and the Lord turning to look at him while the cock crowed a second time. The same word is the one Jesus uses for the disciple's daily calling: deny yourself, take up your cross, and follow me.

Theological Significance

I hold that Peter's failure and Jesus' command belong together. Denying myself is the way I stop denying him, and the look Jesus gave Peter tells me that even after the worst denial, his face is still turned toward me.
