chrónos

of uncertain derivation; a space of time (in general, and thus properly distinguished from G2540, which designates a fixed or special occasion; and from G165, which denotes a particular period) or interval; by extension, an individual opportunity; by implication, delay:--+ years old, season, space, (X often-)time(-s), (a) while.

Biblical Usage

Time is the space in which life happens, and Herod asks the wise men diligently when the star appeared. Jesus says the friends of the bridegroom cannot fast while He is with them, and Elizabeth's full time comes for her son to be born. The man at the pool has been sick a long time, and Jesus asks him if he will be made whole.

Theological Significance

I believe time is a gift God gives and a trust He will ask about. Herod uses time to hunt a child, and Jesus uses it to heal one, and the difference is everything. My position is that no season is wasted when it is spent with Christ, however long it lasts.
