phrýganon

Word
φρύγανον
Language
Greek
Transliteration
phrýganon
Strongs Number
G5434
Definition
neuter of a presumed derivative of φρύγω (to roast or parch; akin to the base of G5395); something desiccated, i.e. a dry twig:--stick.
