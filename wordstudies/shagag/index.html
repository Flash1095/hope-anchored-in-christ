shâgag

Word
שָׁגַג
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
shâgag
Strongs Number
H7683
Definition
to stray, i.e. (figuratively) sin (with more or less apology)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to go astray, err, commit sin or error; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to err (mentally); 1a2) to sin (ignorantly or inadvertently)
