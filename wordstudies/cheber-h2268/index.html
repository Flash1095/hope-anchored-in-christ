Cheber

Word
חֶבֶר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Cheber
Strongs Number
H2268
Definition
Cheber, the name of a Kenite and of three Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Heber = "comrade"; 1) the Kenite, husband of Jael, who slew Sisera by driving a nail into his temple; 2) grandson of Asher from whom came the Heberites; 3) father of Socho, a Judahite; 4) a Benjamite; 5) another Benjamite; 6) a Gadite
