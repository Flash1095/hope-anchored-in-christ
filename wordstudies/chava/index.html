chăvâʼ

to show

Biblical Usage

This Aramaic word is to show or declare, and the whole second chapter of Daniel turns on it. The Chaldeans ask the king to tell them the dream so they can show the interpretation, they confess no man can show the king's matter, and Daniel asks for time so that he can show the interpretation.

Theological Significance

I believe the chapter proves that only God can show what men cannot. The wise men of Babylon could not declare the dream, and Daniel could, because the God of heaven reveals secrets.
