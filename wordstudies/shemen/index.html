shemen

grease, especially liquid (as from the olive, often perfumed); figuratively, richness

Biblical Usage

Oil is anointing, light, healing, and food. The priests are appointed over the oil and the frankincense, the trespass offering takes a log of oil, and God washes Jerusalem and anoints her with oil. The Song says the smell of the beloved's ointments is better than all spices, and the love that wears them is better than wine.

Theological Significance

I believe oil is the sign of God's presence on a person and a place. It anoints the priest, it heals the leper, it perfumes the bride, and I hold that the Spirit He pictures with oil still anoints His people.
