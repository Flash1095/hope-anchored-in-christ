gâbôahh

elevated (or elated), powerful, arrogant

Biblical Usage

Gaboahh means high, elevated — and it covers hills, horns, and men's hearts. They built high places on every high hill in 1 Kings 14:23, and the LORD warned Samuel not to look on the height of Eliab's stature in 1 Samuel 16:7. The ram in Daniel's vision had two horns that were high in Daniel 8:3, and Israel set up images on every high hill in 2 Kings 17:10.

Theological Significance

I believe height is a test of the heart. God looks past the height of a man's stature to his heart, and I hold that the high places men build for themselves always fall, while the height of God stands for ever.
