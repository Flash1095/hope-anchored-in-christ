mathkôneth

proportion (in size, number or ingredients)

Biblical Usage

Proportion is the measured amount, and it appears where God fixes the measure: the holy anointing oil must not be made in any other proportion, and Pharaoh demands the same tale of bricks with no straw given. The word is about what is owed in full, whether to God or to a tyrant.

Theological Significance

I hold that the contrast is the lesson: God fixes His measure in holiness, and Pharaoh fixes his in cruelty, and the same word shows which master you serve.
