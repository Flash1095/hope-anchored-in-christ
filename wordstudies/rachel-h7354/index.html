Râchêl

Rachel, a wife of Jacob

Biblical Usage

Rachel was a wife of Jacob, the younger daughter of Laban, beloved above Leah. Jacob served seven years for her and they seemed unto him but a few days, for the love he had to her; he watered Laban's flock at the well when he saw her; and she died on the way to Ephrath and was buried there, on the way to Bethlehem.

Theological Significance

The lesson I take is that Rachel's story is love and sorrow together: loved above all, yet barren for years, and dying as her second son was born — and still her name is remembered wherever mothers weep for their children.
