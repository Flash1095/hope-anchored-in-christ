éthnos

probably from G1486; a race (as of the same habit), i.e. a tribe; specially, a foreign (non-Jewish) one (usually, by implication, pagan):--Gentile, heathen, nation, people.

Biblical Usage

Éthnos is a nation — a people, a tribe, and in the New Testament most often the Gentiles, the nations outside Israel. God promised Abraham that in him all nations would be blessed, and Jesus sends His disciples to make disciples of all nations. It is the word for the whole world God intends to reach.

Theological Significance

I believe éthnos is the word for the scope of the gospel: it was never meant for one nation alone. I hold that every nation, including mine, is called into the blessing promised to Abraham.
