maqqêl

a shoot, i.e. stick (with leaves on, or for walking, striking, guiding, divining)

Biblical Usage

maqqêl is a rod or staff, a shoot cut for walking, striking, or guiding. In Exodus 12:11 Israel eats the passover with a staff in hand, ready to travel, and in 1 Samuel 17:40 David takes his staff in his hand to meet Goliath. In Genesis 30:37 Jacob takes rods of green poplar and piles white streaks in them, and in Zechariah 11:7 the prophet takes two staves, Beauty and Bands, to feed the flock. A staff here is a tool, a weapon, and a sign of care.

Theological Significance

I believe maqqêl teaches me that a staff can be a shepherd's tool, a pilgrim's companion, or a prophet's burden. I hold that what I carry in my hand should serve God's purpose.
