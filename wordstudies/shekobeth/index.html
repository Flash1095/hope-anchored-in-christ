shᵉkôbeth

a (sexual) lying with

Biblical Usage

Lying with is also the law's word for forbidden unions. Thou shalt not lie carnally with thy neighbour's wife, neither shalt thou lie with any beast, and if a man lie with a beast he shall surely be put to death. The trial of jealousy uses it again: if some man have lain with thee beside thine husband.

Theological Significance

I believe the law draws the line where desire would erase it. The neighbour's wife, the beast, the husband's bed: these are not suggestions, and I hold that God's lines protect the people inside them.
