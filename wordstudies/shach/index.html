shach

sunk, i.e. downcast

Biblical Usage

Eliphaz uses this word for men cast down, sunk low by trouble. When men are cast down, he says, then thou shalt say, There is lifting up, and God shall save the humble person.

Theological Significance

I hold that being cast down is not the end of the story when God is the one who lifts up.
