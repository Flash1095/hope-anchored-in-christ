shêthâh

the seat (of the person)

Biblical Usage

Hanun shaves half the beards of David's servants and cuts their garments to the seat, and the king of Assyria leads Egypt captive with their seats uncovered.

Theological Significance

I believe the seat is the place of a man's dignity, and to uncover it is to shame him before his enemies.
