châlîyl

a flute (as perforated)

Biblical Usage

Chaliyl is a flute, a pipe. A company of prophets came down with psaltery and tabret and pipe and harp, the people piped with pipes when Solomon was anointed, Isaiah counts the pipe among the feasts of the careless, and Jeremiah's heart sounds for Moab like pipes.

Theological Significance

I believe the pipe plays for both joy and mourning in Scripture: it sounds at an anointing and at a funeral, and God hears both kinds. Music is not holy or unholy by itself — it is holy or unholy by the heart that plays it.
