ʼăriʼêyl

the altar of the temple

Biblical Usage

Ezekiel uses this word twice, both times for the top of the altar in his vision of the temple, the hearth where the burnt offering burned. It is the same word as Ariel, the lion of God, and here it names the place of fire at the heart of worship.

Theological Significance

A single word for the altar's hearth carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the picture, that God's fire burns at the centre of the house where He meets His people.
