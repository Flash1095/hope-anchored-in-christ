zᵉmân

an appointed occasion

Biblical Usage

Zeman is an appointed time, a season fixed for a purpose, and its most famous use is the opening of Ecclesiastes 3: to every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven. Esther's Jews appoint the days of Purim as a fixed time for their children to remember the deliverance, and Nehemiah's story turns on a set time, when the king asks him how long he will be gone and he names the time.

Theological Significance

I believe God fixes the times even when we cannot see the reason, because Ecclesiastes says He makes everything beautiful in its time. Our job is not to set the clock but to meet the moment He has appointed. My position is that no season of life is wasted when God appointed it, and the time to trust Him is the time we are in.
