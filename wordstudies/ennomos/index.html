énnomos

from G1722 and G3551; (subjectively) legal, or (objectively) subject to:--lawful, under law.

Biblical Usage

Lawful, or under law. Paul says he became as one under the law to Christ to gain those without law in 1 Corinthians 9:21, and the town clerk says other matters must be settled in a lawful assembly in Acts 19:39.

Theological Significance

I believe Paul's freedom was not lawlessness — he was under law to Christ, and that law set him free to reach anyone.
