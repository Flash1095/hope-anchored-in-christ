pylṓn

from G4439; a gate-way, door-way of a building or city; by implication, a portal or vestibule:--gate, porch.

Biblical Usage

Pulon means a gateway, a porch, the entrance of a building or a city. Peter went out into the porch, and another maid saw him there, and at the house of Mary, Rhoda came to hearken at the gate, and knew Peter's voice. Lazarus was laid at the rich man's gate, full of sores, and the New Jerusalem has twelve gates, and the gates of it shall not be shut at all by day, for there shall be no night there.

Theological Significance

I believe the gate is where the story turns: Peter denied Him in the porch, Rhoda heard him at the gate, Lazarus lay at the gate, and the gates of the New Jerusalem never shut. I hold that the gate that shut Peter out is the gate that will never shut again, because the city it guards has no night.
