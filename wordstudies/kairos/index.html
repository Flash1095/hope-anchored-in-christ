kairos

Word
καιρός
Language
Greek
Transliteration
kairos
Strongs Number
G2540
Definition
Time as appointed season or decisive moment — God's opportune, purposeful timing, distinct from chronos, mere clock time.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Mark 1:15; Galatians 4:4; Ephesians 5:15-16; Ecclesiastes 3:1
Biblical Usage
Jesus opens His ministry announcing 'the kairos is fulfilled,' and Paul writes that 'when the fullness of time had come, God sent forth His Son.' Kairos moments are history's turning points, timed by heaven.
Theological Significance
Kairos consoles everyone waiting on God: His timing is not delay but design. The God who sent His Son at exactly the right moment can be trusted with your seasons too.
