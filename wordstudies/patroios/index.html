patrōios

from G3962; paternal, i.e. hereditary:--of fathers.

Biblical Usage

Patroios means of the fathers, paternal, hereditary. Paul says in Acts 22:3 that he was taught according to the perfect manner of the law of the fathers, and in Acts 28:17 that he has committed nothing against the people or customs of our fathers.

Theological Significance

I believe Paul could appeal to the customs of his fathers because he had kept them, and the gospel did not make him a stranger to his own people.
