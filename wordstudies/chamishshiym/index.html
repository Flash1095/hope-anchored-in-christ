chămishshîym

fifty

Biblical Usage

Chamishshiym is the number fifty, and it measures arks, cities, years, and men. The ark was fifty cubits broad, Abraham pleaded for Sodom if fifty righteous could be found there, the fiftieth year is hallowed as jubile with liberty proclaimed through the land, and the Levites entered the service of the tabernacle from thirty years old until fifty.

Theological Significance

I believe fifty in Scripture is a working number, not a secret code: the breadth of a boat, the age a man stops carrying the tabernacle, the year debts are cancelled. God counts plainly, and the jubile shows me His counting always bends toward freedom.
