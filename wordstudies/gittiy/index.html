Gittîy

a Gittite or inhabitant of Gath

Biblical Usage

Gittîy means a Gittite or inhabitant of Gath. In Joshua 13:3, the text says "the Gittites." In 2 Samuel 6:10, the text says "but David carried it aside into the house of Obed-edom the Gittite."

Theological Significance

I hold that gittîy matters because faithfulness is staying when staying is costly — the way God stays with us. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
