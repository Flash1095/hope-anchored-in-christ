maṭṭeh

Word
מַטֶּה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
maṭṭeh
Strongs Number
H4294
Definition
a branch (as extending); figuratively, a tribe; also a rod, whether for chastising (figuratively, correction), ruling (a sceptre), throwing (a lance), or walking (a staff; figuratively, a support of life, e.g. bread)
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) staff, branch, tribe; 1a) staff, rod, shaft; 1b) branch (of vine); 1c) tribe; 1c1) company led by chief with staff (originally)
