bârâd

hail

Biblical Usage

Barad is hail, and it falls as judgment through the whole Bible. The plague on Egypt is hail mingled with fire such as never was in the land, great stones from heaven fall on the Amorites at Gibeon, God asks Job whether he has seen the treasures of the hail reserved for the day of battle, and Haggai says God struck His people with hail and blasting and they still did not turn to Him.

Theological Significance

I believe hail is God's stored weapon, kept for the day He chooses to use it. Pharaoh saw it, the Amorites felt it, Job was asked about it, and I hold that the God who keeps hail in His treasuries is not a God to be trifled with.
