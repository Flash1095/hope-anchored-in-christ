proeuangelízomai

middle voice from G4253 and G2097; to announce glad news in advance:--preach before the gospel.

Biblical Usage

Proeuangelizomai means to preach the gospel beforehand, to announce glad news in advance. Paul uses it once: the scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the heathen through faith, preached before the gospel unto Abraham, saying, In thee shall all nations be blessed.

Theological Significance

I believe Abraham heard the gospel before the law was given, and faith was always the way in.
