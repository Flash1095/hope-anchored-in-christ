merismós

from G3307; a separation or distribution:--dividing asunder, gift.

Biblical Usage

Merismos is a dividing asunder, a distribution, a gift handed out. Hebrews 4:12 says the word of God pierces even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and Hebrews 2:4 says God bore witness with gifts of the Holy Ghost, according to His own will.

Theological Significance

I believe the word cuts both ways: God's word divides what man cannot, and God's gifts are distributed where He wills.
