yᵉdâʼ

to praise

Biblical Usage

Yedâ is the Aramaic word for praising, for giving thanks before God. Daniel thanked and praised the God of his fathers for the wisdom and might He had given, and when the writing was signed he went to his house and kneeled and prayed and gave thanks before his God, as he did aforetime.

Theological Significance

I believe praise is the habit of the faithful, and Daniel thanked God before the lions as he thanked Him after.
