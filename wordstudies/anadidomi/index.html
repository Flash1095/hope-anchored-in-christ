anadídōmi

from G303 and G1325; to hand over:--deliver.

Biblical Usage

The word appears once: the soldiers who brought Paul to Caesarea delivered the epistle to the governor and presented Paul also before him. It is a handing over — the letter and the prisoner both delivered into Felix's hands, and Paul's case passing to the next court.

Theological Significance

One word for handing over carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the chain of custody — Paul passed from soldiers to governor, and the gospel with him.
