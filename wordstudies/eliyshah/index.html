Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼĔlîyshâh
Strongs Number
H473
Definition
Elishah, a son of Javan
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Elishah = "God of the coming (one)"; 1) descendant of Noah, son of Javan; perhaps ancestor of the Aeolians
