lechem

food (for man or beast), especially bread, or grain (for making it)

Biblical Usage

lechem means bread, food, and it is one of those words that quietly holds a whole theme together. In Genesis 3:19, "In the sweat of thy face shalt thou eat bread"; and in Deuteronomy 8:3, "that he might make thee know that man doth not live by bread only". In Psalms 104:15, "and bread [which] strengtheneth man's heart."; and in Exodus 16:15, "This [is] the bread which the LORD hath given you to eat.".

Theological Significance

I believe bread is God's daily sermon — by the sweat of your face you eat it, and yet man does not live by bread alone. I hold that the God who rained manna and gave the psalmist bread to strengthen the heart still feeds His people by His word. My position is that I will ask Him for daily bread, and trust Him for the rest.
