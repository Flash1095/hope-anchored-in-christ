koinonia

Word
κοινωνία
Language
Greek
Transliteration
koinonia
Strongs Number
G2842
Definition
Fellowship, partnership and sharing — the deep common life believers share with God and one another, expressed in generosity.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Acts 2:42; 1 John 1:3; Philippians 1:5; 2 Corinthians 13:14
Biblical Usage
The first church devoted itself to koinonia — shared meals, shared possessions, shared mission. Paul uses it for partnership in the gospel and for our fellowship with the Father and His Son.
Theological Significance
Koinonia says faith was never meant to be solitary. We share life with the Trinity and with one another — an anchored community in a drifting world.
