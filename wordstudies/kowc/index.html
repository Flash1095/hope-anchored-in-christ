kôwç

Word
כּוֹס
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
kôwç
Strongs Number
H3563
Definition
a cup (as a container), often figuratively, a lot (as if a potion); also some unclean bird, probably an owl (perhaps from the cup-like cavity of its eye)
Part Of Speech
noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
n f; 1) cup; n m; 2) a kind of owl (an unclean bird)
