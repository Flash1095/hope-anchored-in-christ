mâlal

to speak (mostly poetical) or say

Biblical Usage

Malal means to speak, to say, to utter, and it is the poet's word for speaking. Genesis 21:7 has Sarah saying, who would have said unto Abraham that Sarah should have given children suck, and Job 33:3 has Elihu saying his lips shall utter knowledge clearly. Psalms 106:2 asks, who can utter the mighty acts of the LORD, who can shew forth all His praise?

Theological Significance

I believe this word is about speech that cannot hold itself back: Sarah's laughter in Genesis 21:7 and the psalmist's question in Psalms 106:2 both come from a wonder too big for silence. My position is that the question in Psalms 106:2 is the right one, because no one can utter all His mighty acts, and that is why we keep trying.
