lâhaṭ

properly, to lick, i.e. (by implication) to blaze

Biblical Usage

To burn or blaze is the word for God's fire, and it devours from Deuteronomy to Malachi. In Deuteronomy 32:22 a fire is kindled in God's anger and burns to the lowest hell, in Joel 1:19 the fire has "devoured the pastures of the wilderness," in Job 41:21 a flame goes out of Leviathan's mouth, and in Malachi 4:1 the day comes that "shall burn as an oven."

Theological Significance

To my mind, the fire in this word is always God's fire: it kindles in His anger, it devours the wilderness, and it will burn the proud as stubble on the day that is coming. The day is coming, and the fire is already kindled.
