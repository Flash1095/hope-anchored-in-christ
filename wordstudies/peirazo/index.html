peirázō

from G3984; to test (objectively), i.e. endeavor, scrutinize, entice, discipline:--assay, examine, go about, prove, tempt(-er), try.

Biblical Usage

Peirazo means to test, to tempt, to try. Jesus was led into the wilderness to be tempted of the devil, forty days without food, and the Pharisees tempted Him with questions meant to trap Him. Yet Hebrews says our high priest was tempted in all points like we are, yet without sin.

Theological Significance

I am convinced that Christ was tested the way I am tested, and He did not fail — so my temptation is not a lonely place. He faced the devil hungry and alone and answered with Scripture, and He faced the hypocrites' questions and saw through them. I hold that because He was tempted and did not sin, He can help me when I am tempted, and His sympathy is not theory but experience.
