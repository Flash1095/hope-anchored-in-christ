katéchō

from G2596 and G2192; to hold down (fast), in various applications (literally or figuratively):--have, hold (fast), keep (in memory), let, X make toward, possess, retain, seize on, stay, take, withhold.

Biblical Usage

To hold down fast is to keep a firm grip, and we are told to hold fast the profession of our faith without wavering, to hold fast the confidence and the rejoicing of hope firm to the end, to prove all things and hold fast that which is good, and warned of those who hold the truth in unrighteousness. The same grip can hold the faith or hold the truth down, and the difference is the heart.

Theological Significance

I believe holding fast is the whole work of the Christian life, because the faith is worth the grip and the hope is worth the holding. I hold that I will prove all things and keep what is good, and I will not let go of the confession He gave me.
