ʼĔlîyshâʻ

Elisha, the famous prophet

Biblical Usage

Elisha is the famous prophet. In 1 Kings 19:16, the text says "and Elisha the son of Shaphat of Abelmeholah shalt thou anoint [to be] prophet in thy room." The life behind the name is told on the Elisha People profile.

Theological Significance

I believe Elisha matters because God knows His people by name, and Elisha is one of them. That is why I want the name, and the person behind it, kept straight.
