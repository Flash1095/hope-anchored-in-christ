hypomonḗ

from G5278; cheerful (or hopeful) endurance, constancy:--enduring, patience, patient continuance (waiting).

Biblical Usage

hypomonḗ means endurance, patience, steadfast waiting. In Luke 8:15, the text says "and bring forth fruit with patience." In Romans 2:7, the text says "To them who by patient continuance in well doing seek for glory and honour and immortality."

Theological Significance

Reading hypomonḗ this way, I believe perseverance is holding on when holding on is hard — and God holds on to us first. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
