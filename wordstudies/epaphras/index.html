Epaphrâs

contracted from G1891; Epaphras, a Christian:--Epaphras.

Biblical Usage

Epaphras was Paul's fellow worker from Colossae, the man who taught the Colossians the gospel and then laboured for them in prayer, always wrestling that they might stand complete in the will of God. Paul calls him a faithful minister of Christ and, in Philemon, his fellow prisoner. His name reaches us through prayer more than preaching: he is remembered on his knees.

Theological Significance

Prayer is real work, done for people you love before the God who hears, and Epaphras did it faithfully. I believe his example rebukes my hurry: the man remembered on his knees accomplished more than the man remembered on a platform.
