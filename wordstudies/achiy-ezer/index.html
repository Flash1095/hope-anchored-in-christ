ʼĂchîyʻezer

Achiezer, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Ahiezer, son of Ammishaddai, was the prince of Dan, named at the census in the wilderness and again when he brought Dan's offering on the tenth day of the dedication. Beyond these verses the Bible tells no more of him.

Theological Significance

No doctrine hangs on this prince. He is one of the twelve whose names mark the order God set on His people — every tribe counted, every tribe placed, none forgotten.
