qᵉlâlâh

vilification

Biblical Usage

A curse is the word set before Israel and the word Shimei spoke against David: behold, I set before you this day a blessing and a curse; he that is hanged is accursed of God. David says, it may be that the LORD will look on mine affliction, and that the LORD will requite me good for his cursing this day.

Theological Significance

I believe the curse is the other side of the blessing, and David's answer is the right one: leave it to God to turn the cursing into good. What holds me is that the hanged man is accursed, and the cross is where that curse was carried for us.
