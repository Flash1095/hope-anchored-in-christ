epieikḗs

from G1909 and G1503; appropriate, i.e. (by implication) mild:--gentle, moderation, patient.

Biblical Usage

Epieikes means gentle, moderate, patient — the word for a spirit that is not harsh or grasping. Let your moderation be known unto all men; a bishop must be no striker, but gentle; we are to speak evil of no man, to be gentle, showing all meekness; and the wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated.

Theological Significance

Gentleness is what heaven looks like when it wears a human face, and the church needs it more than it needs sharpness. I hold that the wisdom from above proves itself by being peaceable and easy to be intreated, and I want that wisdom, not the other kind.
