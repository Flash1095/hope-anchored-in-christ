ʻAlmîy

an Elamite or inhabitant of Elam

Biblical Usage

Once, in the letter the enemies of Judah write to Artaxerxes: Rehum the chancellor, and Shimshai the scribe, and the rest of their companions, the Dinaites, the Apharsathchites, the Tarpelites, the Apharsites, the Archevites, the Babylonians, the Susanchites, the Dehavites, and the Elamites. The name is a people from Elam, counted among the settlers planted in Samaria to stop the temple.

Theological Significance

A people named once in a letter carries no lesson of its own. What it shows is the opposition the returning exiles faced, that the land was full of settled peoples who wrote to the king to stop the building.
