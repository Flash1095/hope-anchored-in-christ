chelqâh

properly, smoothness; figuratively, flattery; also an allotment

Biblical Usage

A field is a portion of ground, and the word marks the ground where the stories turn. Joram and Ahaziah meet Jehu in the portion of Naboth the Jezreelite, Joram's body is cast in the portion of Naboth's field, Ruth lights on a part of the field belonging to Boaz, and David stands in a parcel of ground full of barley against the Philistines.

Theological Significance

I believe God writes His judgements and His mercies on the same kind of ground: Naboth's field is the place of murder and of vengeance, and Boaz's field is the place of gleaning and of grace. The field is His either way.
