ʼachălay

would that!

Biblical Usage

Achalay is the Hebrew for 'would that' — a wish spoken aloud. It appears twice: a captive Israelite girl telling her mistress she wished her lord were with the prophet in Samaria, and the psalmist longing for his ways to be directed to keep God's statutes. One wish heals a leper; the other longs for obedience.

Theological Significance

A wish is a small thing in itself, and what matters here is what the two wishes ask for — healing for a stranger and obedience for the psalmist.
