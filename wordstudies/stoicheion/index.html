stoicheîon

neuter of a presumed derivative of the base of G4748; something orderly in arrangement, i.e. (by implication) a serial (basal, fundamental, initial) constituent (literally), proposition (figuratively):--element, principle, rudiment.

Biblical Usage

The elements are the ABCs, and Scripture uses them three ways. When we were children we were in bondage under the elements of the world, Paul warns against being spoiled through philosophy after the rudiments of the world and not after Christ, Hebrews calls them the first principles of the oracles of God, and Peter says the elements shall melt with fervent heat.

Theological Significance

I believe the elements are the first lessons, and I hold that no one should stay in bondage to the ABCs when Christ has come, nor mistake them for the whole book. The rudiments had their day, and the elements themselves will melt when He returns.
