ʻălâh

a holocaust

Biblical Usage

Once, in the Aramaic of Ezra, where Darius orders supplies for the rebuilt temple: young bullocks, rams, lambs, and the burnt offering, with wheat, salt, wine and oil, day by day without fail. The word is the whole burnt offering, given over entirely to God on the altar.

Theological Significance

One Aramaic word in one verse carries no theology of its own; its weight is the temple worship it supplied, the daily offering that kept going once the exiles came home.
