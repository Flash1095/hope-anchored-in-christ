ekmássō

from G1537 and the base of G3145; to knead out, i.e. (by analogy) to wipe dry:--wipe.

Biblical Usage

ekmássō means to wipe dry. In Luke 7:38, the text says "and did wipe [them] with the hairs of her head." In Luke 7:44, the text says "and wiped [them] with the hairs of her head."

Theological Significance

What ekmássō teaches me is that serving is not earning; it is answering love with love. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
