Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻEqrôwn
Strongs Number
H6138
Definition
Ekron, a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Ekron = "emigration" or "torn up by the roots"; 1) the most northerly of the 5 principal cities of the Philistines; located in the lowlands of Judah and later given to Dan
