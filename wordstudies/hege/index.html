Hêgêʼ

Hege or Hegai, a eunuch of Xerxes

Biblical Usage

Hege was the keeper of the women in the king's house, the eunuch in charge of preparing Esther for the king. In Esther 2:3 the king's servants put the maidens in his hand, in Esther 2:8 Esther is brought to his house, and in Esther 2:15 she asks him for nothing more than what he appoints, and obtains favour in the sight of all who look upon her.

Theological Significance

I believe Hege is the quiet helper in the story: he gives Esther what she needs and nothing extra, and her favour with him is the first step toward her favour with the king.
