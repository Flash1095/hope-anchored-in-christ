zᵉʼêb

a wolf

Biblical Usage

Zeeb is the wolf, and the Bible uses it the way a shepherd would: as a picture of danger and greed. Jacob blesses Benjamin as a wolf that tears its prey in the morning and divides the spoil at night, and Isaiah promises a day when the wolf will lie down with the lamb. The prophets also turn the word on corrupt rulers, calling Jerusalem's princes evening wolves that leave nothing for the morning.

Theological Significance

I hold that the wolf shows us both what we are without God and what He will change, because only His peace can make the wolf lie down with the lamb.
