mâçâh

to dissolve

Biblical Usage

The word means to dissolve or melt, and it is used for tears, beauty and snow alike. The psalmist says he waters his couch with tears all the night, and God makes man's beauty consume away like a moth, and the psalm of the thaw says God sends out His word and melts the ice, and the winds blow and the waters flow. Caleb says his brethren made the heart of the people melt, but he wholly followed the LORD.

Theological Significance

I believe melting is what sorrow does to us and what God's word does to the ice, and the difference is who does the melting. I hold that tears dissolve the strong and God's word dissolves the winter, and the heart that melts before Him will not melt before men.
