bîyrâh

a castle or palace

Biblical Usage

A palace is the word for a castle, a citadel, a temple — and it is the setting of the whole book of Esther. In Esther 1:2 Ahasuerus sat on the throne of his kingdom, which was in Shushan the palace. In Esther 2:5 Mordecai dwelt in Shushan the palace. In Nehemiah 1:1 Nehemiah was in Shushan the palace when the news from Jerusalem came, and in Daniel 8:2 Daniel saw his vision while he was at Shushan in the palace.

Theological Significance

I believe Shushan the palace is where God hides his people in plain sight: a Jew in the citadel, a cupbearer at court, and a kingdom turned by their faithfulness.
