kurios

Lord, master and sir — the title used for God in the Greek Old Testament and confessed of Jesus as divine Lord.

Biblical Usage

Kurios renders God's covenant name in the Septuagint, so calling Jesus kurios is explosive: 'Jesus is Lord' is the church's first creed, and every tongue will one day confess it.

Theological Significance

Kurios settles the question of who is in charge — and it is good news. The Lord who anchors our hope is Jesus, exalted, reigning and returning.
