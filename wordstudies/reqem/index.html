Reqem

Rekem, the name of a place in Palestine, also of a Midianite and an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Rekem was a king of Midian slain with the other kings when Israel avenged itself, a town in Benjamin's lot, and an Israelite, a son of Hebron in Judah's line. The town in Benjamin and the Israelite of Judah show how one name can sit on both sides of a border.

Theological Significance

What I take from this is that one name can belong to an enemy, a town and a tribesman, and God tells them apart.
