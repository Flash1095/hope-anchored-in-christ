hâreh

pregnant

Biblical Usage

Hareh means pregnant, with child, and the word carries some of the greatest promises in the Bible. In Genesis 16:11 the angel tells Hagar she is with child and shall bear Ishmael, in Isaiah 7:14 a virgin shall conceive and bear a son called Immanuel, in Judges 13:5 the wife of Manoah is told she shall bear Samson, and in Genesis 38:24 Tamar is found to be with child.

Theological Significance

I believe the word is a reminder that God writes his promises into women's lives before anyone else can see them. Hagar in the wilderness, the virgin in Isaiah, the barren wife of Manoah: each one carried a future she did not choose and could not have imagined.
