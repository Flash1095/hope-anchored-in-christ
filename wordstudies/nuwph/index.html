nûwph

to quiver (i.e. vibrate up and down, or rock to and fro); used in a great variety of applications (including sprinkling, beckoning, rubbing, bastinadoing, sawing, waving, etc.)

Biblical Usage

Nuph is to lift up and move, to wave, to quiver, to shake something to and fro. It is the word of the wave offering in Leviticus 7:30 and Numbers 5:25, where the priest waves the offering before the Lord, and it is also the word of lifting a tool against the altar stone in Exodus 20:25, and of moving a sickle in a neighbour's standing corn in Deuteronomy 23:25.

Theological Significance

I believe the wave offering teaches that what we lift up to God becomes His, and the same motion that consecrates can also profane when it is done against His command. That is why I hold that our hands must move only as He directs, lifted in worship, never in theft or presumption.
