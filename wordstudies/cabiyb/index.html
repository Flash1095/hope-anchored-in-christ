çâbîyb

(as noun) a circle, neighbour, or environs; but chiefly (as adverb, with or without preposition) around

Biblical Usage

Çâbîyb means around, round about — the circle, the neighbours, the environs. It is the most ordinary word for what lies on every side, and the Bible uses it hundreds of times to set the scene: around the city, around the camp, around the throne. Nothing in Scripture happens nowhere; it always happens somewhere, with something around it.

Theological Significance

I believe God is never far from the middle of the circle. Çâbîyb keeps reminding me that He stands around His people, and I hold that to be true whether I feel it or not.
