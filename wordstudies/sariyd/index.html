sârîyd

a survivor

Biblical Usage

A sarid is one who is left, a survivor, and Scripture uses the word both ways. Jehu slew all that remained of the house of Ahab until he left him none remaining, and Israel left none to remain of Sihon's cities. But Isaiah turns it over: except the LORD of hosts had left unto us a very small remnant, we should have been as Sodom. And Jeremiah says the people which were left of the sword found grace in the wilderness.

Theological Significance

I believe the remnant is the whole hope of Israel in one word. Judgment leaves almost nothing — and then God keeps that almost-nothing, and it is enough. The survivor is not the one who was strong enough to live; he is the one the LORD left.
