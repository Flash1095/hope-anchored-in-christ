qêrêach

bald (on the back of the head)

Biblical Usage

Children mock Elisha as thou bald head on the road to Bethel, and the law says a man whose hair has fallen off his head is bald, yet clean. The same condition is a jeer in one place and no defilement in the other.

Theological Significance

I believe the law's word settles it: baldness is no uncleanness, whatever mockers shout on the road.
