sklērotráchēlos

from G4642 and G5137; hardnaped, i.e. (figuratively) obstinate:--stiffnecked.

Biblical Usage

Sklerotrachelos means stiff-necked, hard of neck, and Stephen throws it at the council that would stone him: ye stiffnecked and uncircumcised in heart and ears, ye do always resist the Holy Ghost, as your fathers did, so do ye.

Theological Significance

I believe a stiff neck is a heart that will not bow, because Stephen saw the same resistance in his judges that their fathers showed to Moses.
