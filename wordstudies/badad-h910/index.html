bâdâd

separate; adverb, separately

Biblical Usage

Badad means alone, separate — by oneself. Israel dwelt alone in safety, the Lord alone did lead his people, the city that was full of people sits solitary in Lamentations, and Micah says the Lord will feed his people alone in the wood, in the midst of Carmel. Separation, sometimes as judgment and sometimes as the Lord's own keeping.

Theological Significance

I believe God keeps his people separate on purpose — a people that dwells alone, led by the Lord alone. And I hold that even Jerusalem's solitude is not abandonment, because he feeds his flock in the wood himself.
