Parbâr

Parbar or Parvar, a quarter of Jerusalem

Biblical Usage

Parbar is a structure on the west side of Solomon's temple, an open apartment or colonnade. It is named in 1 Chronicles 26:18, where the Levites' lots place two porters at the Parbar westward, and in 2 Kings 23:11, where Josiah removes the horses the kings of Judah had given to the sun, by the chamber of Nathan-melech, which is by the Parbar.

Theological Significance

I hold that even a side building of the temple has a place in God's book, and Josiah's cleansing reached the outer rooms as well as the inner court.
