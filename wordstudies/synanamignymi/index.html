synanamígnymi

from G4862 and a compound of G303 and G3396; to mix up together, i.e. (figurative) associate with:--(have, keep) company (with).

Biblical Usage

Paul writes not to company with fornicators, and then sharpens it: if any man called a brother is a fornicator or covetous or an idolater or a railer or a drunkard or an extortioner, do not keep company with him, no, not to eat. And to the Thessalonians he says to note the man who does not obey the word and have no company with him, that he may be ashamed. Keeping company is not a small thing; it is fellowship, and fellowship withdrawn in love is meant to win the brother back.

Theological Significance

My position is that refusing company is sometimes the most loving thing a church can do, because it tells the truth about sin instead of blessing it. I hold that the aim is always restoration, that the ashamed brother would come home.
