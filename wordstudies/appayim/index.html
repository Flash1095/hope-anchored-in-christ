ʼAppayim

Appajim, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Appaim is named in the genealogy of Judah as a son of Nadab, and then as the father of Ishi. He appears only in the family record, one link in the line that runs from Judah toward David.

Theological Significance

I believe God keeps the names of people the world forgets, and a single line in a genealogy is still a life He counted.
