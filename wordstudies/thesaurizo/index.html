thēsaurízō

from G2344; to amass or reserve (literally or figuratively):--lay up (treasure), (keep) in store, (heap) treasure (together, up).

Biblical Usage

In the Bible, thēsaurízō means to lay up treasure. In Matthew 6:19, Lay not up for yourselves treasures upon earth. Wherever it appears, the word means this and not something vaguer.

Theological Significance

What grips me is that God means what He says when He uses this word, and I take Him at His word. That is why I read the passages around thēsaurízō slowly, instead of hurrying past.
