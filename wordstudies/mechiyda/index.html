Mᵉchîydâʼ

Mechida, one of the Nethinim

Biblical Usage

Mechida is a family of the Nethinim, the temple servants who returned from exile with Zerubbabel. The name is kept in the lists of Ezra and Nehemiah.

Theological Significance

I believe the Nethinim are the quiet proof that God keeps His servants: a family that carried water for the temple is named in the record of the return, and no service is too low to be written down.
