ʻâb

Word
עָב
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻâb
Strongs Number
H5645
Definition
properly, an envelope, i.e. darkness (or density, 2 Chronicles 4:17); specifically, a (scud) cloud; also a copse
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) darkness, cloud, thicket; 1a) dark cloud; 1b) cloud mass; 1c) thicket (as refuge)
