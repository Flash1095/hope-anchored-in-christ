pynthánomai

middle voice prolonged from a primary πύθω (which occurs only as an alternate in certain tenses); to question, i.e. ascertain by inquiry (as a matter of information merely; and thus differing from G2065, which properly means a request as a favor; and from G154, which is strictly a demand for something due; as well as from G2212, which implies a search for something hidden; and from G1189, which involves the idea of urgent need); by implication, to learn (by casual intelligence):--ask, demand, enquire, understand.

Biblical Usage

Pynthanomai means to ask, to inquire, to question. Herod demanded of the chief priests and scribes where Christ should be born, and the nobleman enquired of the servants the hour when his son began to amend. And the governor asked of what province Paul was.

Theological Significance

I hold that asking is the beginning of finding: Herod asked where Christ should be born, the nobleman asked when his son was healed, and the governor asked where Paul came from, and every question led to the truth.
