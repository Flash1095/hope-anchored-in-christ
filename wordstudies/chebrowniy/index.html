Chebrôwnîy

Chebronite (collectively), an inhabitant of Chebron

Biblical Usage

The Hebronites are the family of Hebron the son of Kohath, counted among the families of the Kohathites, and Jerijah their chief is sought out in the fortieth year of David among mighty men of valour at Jazer of Gilead. The clan serves and the clan fights.

Theological Significance

I believe God gives the Levites both service and strength: the Hebronites keep the holy things and their chief is found among mighty men.
