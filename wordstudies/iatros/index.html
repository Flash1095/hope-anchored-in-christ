iatrós

from G2390; a physician:--physician.

Biblical Usage

A physician is the one you call when you know you are sick, and Jesus takes that name for himself. In Matthew 9:12 he says they that be whole need not a physician, but they that are sick, in Mark 2:17 and Luke 5:31 he says it again to the Pharisees who murmured, and in Colossians 4:14 Paul greets Luke, the beloved physician, his companion on the road.

Theological Significance

I believe Jesus called himself a physician on purpose: the sick are the ones he came for, and I am sicker than I like to admit. That is good news, not bad, because the Physician is here.
