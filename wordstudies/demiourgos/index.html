dēmiourgós

from G1218 and G2041; a worker for the people, i.e. mechanic (spoken of the Creator):--maker.

Biblical Usage

The word appears once, in the faith chapter: Abraham looked for a city which has foundations, whose builder and maker is God. The maker is the workman of the city, the craftsman of the foundations that do not move.

Theological Significance

I believe the city is the hope of every pilgrim: the foundations are God's, and the builder is God Himself.
