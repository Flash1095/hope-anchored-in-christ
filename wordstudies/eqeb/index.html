ʻêqeb

a heel, i.e. (figuratively) the last of anything (used adverbially, for ever); also result, i.e. compensation; and so (adverb with preposition or relatively) on account of

Biblical Usage

ʻÊqeb is the heel — and from the heel, the end, the consequence, the reward. God blesses Abraham's seed because Abraham obeyed His voice; the psalmist keeps God's statutes to the end, for the reward. It is the word for what follows obedience, step by step.

Theological Significance

I believe ʻêqeb is the word for the long view: obedience has a consequence, and the consequence is blessing. I hold that keeping God's word to the end is worth it, because the reward is at the end, not the beginning.
