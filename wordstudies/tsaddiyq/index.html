tsaddîyq

just

Biblical Usage

Tsaddiq is the righteous person — the one whose life lines up with God's ways. Noah was just and perfect in his generation; the psalmist says the LORD knows the way of the righteous; and Habakkuk gives the line the whole Bible leans on, that the just shall live by his faith. From Genesis to the prophets, the righteous one is the one God owns and keeps.

Theological Significance

I believe the righteous are not the flawless — Noah's story proves that — but those who walk with God and trust Him. I hold that the just live by faith, not by their record, and God Himself is the righteous Judge who keeps them. My position is that righteousness is a relationship before it is a record.
