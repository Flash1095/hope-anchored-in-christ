pᵉshar

an interpretation

Biblical Usage

Peshar is the interpretation, the meaning of a dream or a writing, and it belongs to the Aramaic chapters of Daniel. The Chaldeans ask the king to tell the dream so they can show the interpretation, Daniel tells Nebuchadnezzar the interpretation of his dream, and he reads the interpretation of the writing on the wall for Belshazzar. In a vision of the four beasts, Daniel asks one standing by for the interpretation, and is told the truth of the matter.

Theological Significance

I believe peshar shows me that dreams and visions are locked rooms, and only God holds the key. Men promise interpretations they cannot give, and Daniel can give them only because God reveals secrets. My position is that every mystery in my life has an interpretation too, and it is found the same way: by asking the God who reveals.
