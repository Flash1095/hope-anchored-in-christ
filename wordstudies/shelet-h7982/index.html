sheleṭ

probably a shield (as controlling, i.e. protecting the person)

Biblical Usage

A shield hangs on a wall and speaks of the men who carried it. David took the shields of gold from Hadadezer's servants and brought them to Jerusalem, the priest gave David's spears and shields to the captains, and Tyre hung her shields on her walls and made her beauty perfect. The Song says a thousand bucklers hang on the tower of David, all shields of mighty men.

Theological Significance

I believe shields on a wall are history you can see. Gold taken in war, hung in the temple, and I hold that God's people are still shielded by a greater hand.
