phaínō

prolongation for the base of G5457; to lighten (shine), i.e. show (transitive or intransitive, literal or figurative):-- appear, seem, be seen, shine, X think.

Biblical Usage

Phaino means to shine, to appear, to show itself, and it runs through the New Testament like light. The light shines in the darkness and the darkness does not overcome it, believers are to shine as lights in the world, holding forth the word of life, and the sign of the Son of man shall appear in heaven as lightning shines from the east to the west. And Peter says the prophetic word shines as a light in a dark place, until the day dawns and the day star arises in our hearts.

Theological Significance

I believe phaino is the Bible's word for how truth arrives: not argued into the room but shining, like light. Christ shines in the darkness, His people shine in the world, and His word shines in my heart until the day breaks. My position is that I do not need to make the light; I need to stand where it shines and let it be seen.
