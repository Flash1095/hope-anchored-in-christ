skáphē

a "skiff" (as if dug out), or yawl (carried aboard a large vessel for landing):--boat.

Biblical Usage

Skaphe is the skiff, the small boat carried aboard a large ship, and it appears three times in the storm that wrecked Paul's ship. The sailors had much work to come by the boat under the island of Clauda, they let it down under colour of casting anchors so they could flee, and the soldiers cut off its ropes and let it fall away.

Theological Significance

I believe the skiff is the picture of escape without the Lord's purpose, because the sailors would have fled in it and left Paul to the storm.
