shûwbâh

a return

Biblical Usage

Shubah is a return, and it appears once, in the word that has steadied me more than any other: in returning and rest shall ye be saved, in quietness and in confidence shall be your strength. The Lord offered it, and the people would not.

Theological Significance

I believe return is the whole gospel in one word, because God asked not for striving but for coming back and resting in quietness and confidence.
