machăbêʼ

a refuge

Biblical Usage

The word names a refuge, a hiding place, and it appears in Saul's hunt for David and in Isaiah's promise of shelter. Saul asks the Ziphites to find all the lurking places where David hides himself, and Isaiah says a man shall be as a hiding place from the wind and a covert from the tempest, as rivers of water in a dry place.

Theological Significance

I hold that the hiding place Saul sought to find is the same hiding place Isaiah promises, and the difference is who is looking for it.
