yêrâqôwn

paleness, whether of persons (from fright), or of plants (from drought)

Biblical Usage

yêrâqôwn is the word the Bible uses when it means paleness, blasting. In Deuteronomy 28:22, with blasting, and with mildew, and it appears again in 1 Kings 8:37.

Theological Significance

Blasting and mildew are among the curses, and I hold that yêrâqôwn is what disobedience does to the land.
