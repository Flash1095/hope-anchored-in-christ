yêrâqôwn

Word
יֵרָקוֹן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
yêrâqôwn
Strongs Number
H3420
Definition
paleness, whether of persons (from fright), or of plants (from drought)
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) mildew, paleness, lividness; 1a) mildew, rust; 1b) paleness
