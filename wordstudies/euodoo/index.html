euodóō

from a compound of G2095 and G3598; to help on the road, i.e. (passively) succeed in reaching; figuratively, to succeed in business affairs:--(have a) prosper(-ous journey).

Biblical Usage

Scripture uses euodóō where we would say to prosper on the road, to succeed. In Romans 1:10, the text says "if by any means now at length I might have a prosperous journey by the will of God to come unto you." In 1 Corinthians 16:2, the text says "as [God] hath prospered him."

Theological Significance

For me, euodóō is a reminder that God provides, and His provision is enough — even when it does not look like we expected. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
