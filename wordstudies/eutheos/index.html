euthéōs

adverb from G2117; directly, i.e. at once or soon:--anon, as soon as, forthwith, immediately, shortly, straightway.

Biblical Usage

The word euthéōs carries the sense of immediately, at once. In Matthew 4:20, the text says "And they straightway left [their] nets." In Luke 5:13, the text says "be thou clean. And immediately the leprosy departed from him."

Theological Significance

I hold that euthéōs matters because the gospel was never meant to stay where it started — it goes out, because God sends it out. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
