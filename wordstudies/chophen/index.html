chôphen

a fist (only in the dual)

Biblical Usage

Chophen is a handful, the fistful a hand can hold, and Scripture uses it for ashes, coals and quietness. Moses takes handfuls of ashes and scatters them toward heaven, the priest takes a censer full of burning coals and hands full of incense, the angel fills his hand with coals from the altar, and the preacher says a handful with quietness is better than two handfuls with travail.

Theological Significance

I believe the handful in these verses is the measure of a small thing in God's hand, whether it is judgment or worship. I hold that a handful with quietness is the wisdom I need most, because God does not ask me to hold more than my hand can carry.
