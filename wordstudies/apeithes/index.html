apeithḗs

from G1 (as a negative particle) and G3982; unpersuadable, i.e. contumacious:--disobedient.

Biblical Usage

Disobedient — the angel's promise that John would turn the disobedient to the wisdom of the just, Paul's claim that he was not disobedient to the heavenly vision, and the blunt lists that name disobedience to parents among the marks of a godless age. The word covers children who will not listen and adults who will not obey heaven.

Theological Significance

I believe obedience is the shape love takes when God speaks. John prepared the way by turning disobedient hearts, and I hold that the same turning is what repentance still does in me.
