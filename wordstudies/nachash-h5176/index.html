Nâchâsh

Word
נָחָשׁ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Nâchâsh
Strongs Number
H5176
Definition
Nachash, the name of two persons apparently non-Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Nahash = "serpent"; n pr m; 1) a king of the Ammonites during the time of king Saul; 2) the father of Abigail, the mother of Amasa, the commander of Absalom's army
