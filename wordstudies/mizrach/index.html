mizrâch

Word
מִזְרָח
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
mizrâch
Strongs Number
H4217
Definition
sunrise, i.e. the east
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) place of sunrise, east; 1a) sunrise, east (with 'sun'); 1b) the east (without 'sun'); 1b1) to or toward the place of sunrise; 1b2) to the east, eastward
