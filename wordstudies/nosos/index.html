nósos

of uncertain affinity; a malady (rarely figuratively, of moral disability):--disease, infirmity, sickness.

Biblical Usage

Disease is what Jesus heals wherever He goes: all manner of sickness and all manner of disease among the people, many sick of divers diseases, and all they that have any sick with divers diseases brought to Him at sunset. Even Paul's handkerchiefs carry healing, and the diseases depart from the sick.

Theological Significance

I believe the healings are the kingdom breaking in: every disease brought to Jesus meets the Healer, and none is turned away. The handkerchiefs from Paul's body carried healing to the sick, and the diseases departed — the touch of the apostle was the touch of the Lord.
