kephalḗ

from the primary κάπτω (in the sense of seizing); the head (as the part most readily taken hold of), literally or figuratively:--head.

Biblical Usage

Kephale means the head, the part that leads and rules: Romans 12:20 says if thine enemy hunger, feed him, for in so doing thou shalt heap coals of fire on his head. 1 Corinthians 11:3 says the head of every man is Christ, and the head of the woman is the man, and the head of Christ is God. 1 Corinthians 11:4 says every man praying or prophesying, having his head covered, dishonoureth his head. Ephesians 1:22 says God gave Christ to be the head over all things to the church.

Theological Significance

I believe headship is a chain, not a ladder: God is the head of Christ, Christ is the head of every man, and Christ is the head over all things to the church. I hold that the head serves by ruling, and rules by serving, and that is why coals of fire on an enemy's head means kindness, not revenge.
