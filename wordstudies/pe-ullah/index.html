pᵉʻullâh

(abstractly) work

Biblical Usage

Pe-ullah is work, and the reward of work, what a deed earns. God says in Isaiah 40:10 that His reward is with Him and His work before Him, and in Isaiah 49:4 the servant says his judgment is with the Lord and his work with his God. Isaiah 61:8 promises the reward in truth, and Proverbs 11:18 says the wicked works a deceitful work, but to him that sows righteousness shall be a sure reward.

Theological Significance

I believe the work and the reward belong together in this word, and God keeps the account: the servant's work is with Him, the wicked's work is deceitful, and the reward is sure. My position is that no work done for God is wasted, because He keeps the wage book Himself.
