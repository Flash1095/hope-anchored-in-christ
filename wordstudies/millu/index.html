milluʼ

a fulfilling (only in plural), i.e. (literally) a setting (of gems), or (technically) consecration (also concretely, a dedicatory sacrifice)

Biblical Usage

Millu is the filling of the hands, the consecration of the priests. The ram of consecration was offered when Aaron and his sons were set apart, and the filling lasted seven days, with the priests kept at the door of the tabernacle until their consecration was complete.

Theological Significance

I believe consecration still means filled hands. God does not send empty-handed servants, He fills their hands with His work and keeps them near His house until they are ready.
