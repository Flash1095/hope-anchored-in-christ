peiráō

from G3984; to test (subjectively), i.e. (reflexively) to attempt:--assay.

Biblical Usage

Peirao means to attempt, to try one's hand at something. Paul says the Jews caught him in the temple and went about to kill him — they attempted it, and God kept him alive to stand before kings.

Theological Significance

I hold that men's attempts against God's servants go only as far as God allows, and Paul lived because his work was not finished.
