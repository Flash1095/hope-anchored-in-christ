ʼâtsar

to store up

Biblical Usage

To store up is to gather treasure into a house, and the word follows the treasures of kings and the storehouses of God. Hezekiah was told that all his treasures would be carried to Babylon, Amos says the violent store up robbery in their palaces, and Nehemiah set faithful treasurers over the storehouses of the temple.

Theological Significance

I believe a treasure shows where the heart is, because Hezekiah's storehouse was emptied by pride and Nehemiah's was filled by faithfulness. What is stored for God is kept, and I hold that what is stored for self is already lost.
