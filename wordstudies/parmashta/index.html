Parmashtâʼ

Parmashta, a son of Haman

Biblical Usage

Parmashta is one of the ten sons of Haman, named in Esther 9:9 among the sons who were slain in Shushan after Haman's plot against the Jews was turned back on his own house. His name stands in the list of the ten, and the fall of the house of Haman is told in the names of its sons.

Theological Significance

I hold that the sons of Haman fell with their father's plot, and the gallows Haman built for Mordecai is the measure of how God turns a trap back on its maker.
