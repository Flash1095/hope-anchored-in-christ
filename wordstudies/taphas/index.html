tâphas

to manipulate, i.e. seize; chiefly to capture, wield, specifically, to overlay; figuratively, to use unwarrantably

Biblical Usage

The word is to seize, to lay hold of, to catch. Joseph's garment was caught in Potiphar's wife's hand as he fled, Saul was close on David's heels to take him, and Jeremiah is told that Zedekiah shall be taken by the hand of the king of Babylon.

Theological Significance

I hold that hands that seize can trap the innocent and the guilty alike, and only God's hand holds without harm.
