yâbal

Word
יָבַל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
yâbal
Strongs Number
H2986
Definition
properly, to flow; causatively, to bring (especially with pomp)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to bring, lead, carry, conduct, bear along; 1a) (Hiphil); 1a1) to bear along, bring; 1a2) to carry away, lead away; 1a3) to lead, conduct; 1b) (Hophal); 1b1) to be borne along; 1b2) to be borne (to the grave); 1b3) to be brought, be led, be conducted
