biṭchâh

trust

Biblical Usage

Confidence is the word here, and it appears once, in one of Isaiah's plainest sentences. In Isaiah 30:15 the Lord says, in returning and rest shall ye be saved, in quietness and in confidence shall be your strength — and ye would not. Trust offered, and refused.

Theological Significance

I believe this one verse is the whole argument of Isaiah in a sentence: strength is in quiet confidence, and the people would not have it.
