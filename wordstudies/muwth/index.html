mûwth

to die (literally or figuratively); causatively, to kill

Biblical Usage

Muth is to die. In the day that thou eatest thereof thou shalt surely die, God says, and the serpent says ye shall not surely die, and the soul that sinneth, it shall die, and Job asks, if a man die, shall he live again?

Theological Significance

I believe death is the Bible's first warning and its last enemy. It enters through one act of disobedience, it falls on every soul that sins, and it is the question Job asks that only the resurrection answers.
