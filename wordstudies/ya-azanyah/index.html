Yaʼăzanyâh

Jaazanjah, the name of four Israelites

Biblical Usage

When the captains heard that the king of Babylon had made Gedaliah governor, Jaazaniah came with his men. Jeremiah took Jaazaniah the son of Jeremiah the Rechabite, and his brethren, into the house of the Lord.

Theological Significance

I believe the four Jaazaniahs must be kept apart: the captain who came to Gedaliah is not the Rechabite who was tested in the temple, and the Bible names each man's father so no one confuses them.
