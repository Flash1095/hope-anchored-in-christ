Definition

Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) (Qal) murmur, roar, crowd, abundance, tumult, sound; 1a) sound, murmur, rush, roar; 1b) tumult, confusion; 1c) crowd, multitude; 1d) great number, abundance; 1e) abundance, wealth
