hâmôwn

a noise, tumult, crowd; also disquietude, wealth

Biblical Usage

Hamon is a crowd, a noise, a multitude, and it can be the sound of rain or the sound of trouble. Elijah hears the sound of abundance of rain in 1 Kings 18:41, Jehoshaphat cries that a great multitude has come against him in 2 Chronicles 20:12, Amos hears the noise of songs God will not hear in Amos 5:23, and Ezekiel warns that violence has risen up in Ezekiel 7:11.

Theological Significance

I believe the word teaches me to listen to what a crowd really is: a multitude can be God's rain coming, or it can be the noise of a people God has stopped listening to. The difference is whether the crowd is turned toward him.
