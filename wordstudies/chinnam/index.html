chinnâm

gratis, i.e. devoid of cost, reason or advantage

Biblical Usage

Chinnam means freely, without cost or without cause, and it cuts both ways in Scripture. The servant goes out free for nothing, David is slain without a cause, God tells Satan that Job is destroyed without cause, and the psalmist says his enemies hide a net for him without cause.

Theological Significance

I believe the word holds two truths together: God's gifts come freely, and His people suffer innocently. I hold that both are true of Christ, who gave freely and was hated without a cause.
