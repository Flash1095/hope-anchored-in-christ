mâraṭ

to polish; by implication, to make bald (the head), to gall (the shoulder); also, to sharpen

Biblical Usage

mâraṭ is to polish, to make bright, and by implication to sharpen. In 1 Kings 7:45 the vessels Hiram makes for Solomon's house are of bright brass, and in Ezekiel 21:9 the sword is sharpened and also furbished. The word is the shine on a vessel and the edge on a sword.

Theological Significance

I believe mâraṭ shows me that God polishes His vessels bright and sharpens His sword, and that the same hand does both.
