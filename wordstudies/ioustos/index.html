Ioûstos

of Latin origin ("just"); Justus, the name of three Christian:--Justus.

Biblical Usage

Justus is the name of three men in the New Testament, and each one is named for a different reason. In Acts 1:23 Joseph called Barsabas, who was surnamed Justus, stands as a candidate to take Judas' place, in Acts 18:7 Paul enters the house of Justus, one that worshipped God, whose house joined hard to the synagogue, and in Colossians 4:11 Jesus, which is called Justus, sends his greeting as a fellow worker.

Theological Significance

I hold that the three men who share the name show how common faithfulness is: a candidate, a host, and a fellow worker, all called Justus.
