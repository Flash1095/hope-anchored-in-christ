enéchō

from G1722 and G2192; to hold in or upon, i.e. ensnare; by implication, to keep a grudge:--entangle with, have a quarrel against, urge.

Biblical Usage

Enecho is a word with three faces: to be entangled, to hold a grudge, and to press hard. Paul says do not be entangled again with the yoke of bondage in Galatians 5:1, Herodias had a quarrel against John and would have killed him in Mark 6:19, and the scribes began to urge Jesus vehemently in Luke 11:53.

Theological Significance

I believe the thread through all three is grip — a yoke that grips, a grudge that grips, a crowd that grips at Jesus with questions. I hold that Christ came to loose every grip but His own, and His yoke is easy because His hands are kind.
