châthal

to swathe

Biblical Usage

To swathe is to wrap a newborn child, and Jerusalem is pictured as a baby whose navel was not cut and who was not swaddled at all on the day she was born. The picture is of a child nobody cared for.

Theological Significance

I believe this verse is meant to break the heart: Jerusalem was abandoned at birth, and everything God did for her afterwards was pure grace.
