Nazōraîos

from G3478; a Nazoræan, i.e. inhabitant of Nazareth; by extension, a Christian:--Nazarene, of Nazareth.

Biblical Usage

At the high priest's palace a maid says this fellow was also with Jesus of Nazareth, and Pilate writes the title over the cross: Jesus of Nazareth, the King of the Jews. Peter preaches Jesus of Nazareth as a man approved of God, and the blind man is told that Jesus of Nazareth is passing by.

Theological Significance

I believe the name follows Jesus from the courtyard to the cross: men use it to accuse Him, Pilate uses it to mock Him, and Peter uses it to preach Him. The Nazarene is my King, and the title Pilate meant as scorn is my confession.
