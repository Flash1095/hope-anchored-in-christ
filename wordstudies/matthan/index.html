Matthán

of Hebrew origin (H4977); Matthan (i.e. Mattan), an Israelite:--Matthan.

Biblical Usage

Matthan stands in Matthew's genealogy of Jesus, between Eleazar and Jacob. He is the father of Jacob and the grandfather of Joseph.

Theological Significance

I hold that the quiet names in the genealogy are the point: Jesus comes through men nobody preached about, and God builds His greatest work on ordinary fathers.
