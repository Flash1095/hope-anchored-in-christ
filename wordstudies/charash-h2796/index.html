chârâsh

a fabricator or any material

Biblical Usage

Charash is the craftsman, the fabricator, the man who works material with his hands. There was no smith in Israel because the Philistines would not let the Hebrews make swords or spears. David tells Solomon there are workmen in abundance, hewers and workers of stone and timber and all manner of cunning men, and the money for the temple repair went to the artificers and builders to buy hewn stone and timber.

Theological Significance

I believe the craftsman in Scripture is never a background figure: God gave Bezalel skill, and He gave David workmen in abundance to build His house. The Philistines knew it too, and took the smiths away first. I hold that skill is a gift from God, and a people without craftsmen is a people who cannot build.
