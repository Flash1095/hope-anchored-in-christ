nachar

a snorting

Biblical Usage

Nachar is a snorting, the sound of a horse ready for battle, and it is used for warhorses and for the snorting of the sea beast. In Jeremiah 8:16 the snorting of the enemy's horses is heard from Dan, and the whole land trembles at the sound, and in Job 39:20 the glory of the warhorse's nostrils is terrible.

Theological Significance

I hold that the snort of a horse is the sound of war coming, and the prophets use it because everyone in their day knew it; when the horses are heard, the time for talking is over.
