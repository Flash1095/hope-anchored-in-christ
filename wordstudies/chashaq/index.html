châshaq

to cling, i.e. join, (figuratively) to love, delight in; elliptically; to deliver

Biblical Usage

Chashaq is clinging, joining, loving, delighting in, and it holds both God's love and a builder's hooks. The LORD did not set His love upon Israel because they were many, for they were the fewest of all people, and He had a delight in their fathers to love them. The same word names the hooks of the pillars in the tabernacle court, and all that Solomon desired to build in Jerusalem and Lebanon.

Theological Significance

I believe the word joins what it touches: God clings to a people He chose when they were fewest, and silver hooks join the pillars of His court. Solomon's desires and God's delight are the same verb, and the difference is the object. I hold that love in the Bible is a clinging, not a feeling, and God clings to His people first.
