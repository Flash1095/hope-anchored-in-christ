ʻăbôdâh

work of any kind

Biblical Usage

This is the noun for work or service — the labour itself, whether Jacob's seven years for Rachel, Egypt's 'rigorous service' laid on Israel, the 'servile work' forbidden on the holy days, or the Levites' 'service of the ministry' in the tabernacle. The same word covers the slave's burden and the priest's duty.

Theological Significance

One word holds both slavery and worship, and the difference is the master. I believe that is the Bible's whole teaching on work: service is not degrading in itself — it is degrading only when the master is cruel, and holy when the Master is God.
