ʼÛwlâm

Ulam, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Two Israelites bear this name, and the record keeps them apart. Ulam the son of Peresh stands in Manasseh's line, the father of Bedan, and Ulam the son of Eshek stands in Benjamin's line, the father of archers.

Theological Significance

I believe the two men must not be merged: one Ulam belongs to Manasseh and the other to Benjamin, and the Bible names each one's sons so neither is lost in the other.
