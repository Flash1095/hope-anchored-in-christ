euschēmosýnē

from G2158; decorousness:--comeliness.

Biblical Usage

euschēmosýnē means comeliness. In 1 Corinthians 12:23, the text says "and our uncomely [parts] have more abundant comeliness."

Theological Significance

In euschēmosýnē I find no hidden theology; it simply means comeliness.
