gath

a wine-press (or vat for holding the grapes in pressing them)

Biblical Usage

Gath is the winepress, the vat where grapes are trodden. Gideon threshed wheat by the winepress to hide it from the Midianites in Judges 6:11, and Nehemiah saw men treading wine presses on the Sabbath in Nehemiah 13:15. The prophets use it for judgment: the LORD treads the virgin daughter of Judah as in a winepress in Lamentations 1:15, and He asks why His garments are red like one who treads in the winefat in Isaiah 63:2.

Theological Significance

I believe the winepress is both a place of work and a picture of wrath. Grapes are trodden to make wine, and the wicked are trodden in judgment. I hold that the winepress of God's wrath fell on Christ, so that we might drink the wine of His mercy.
