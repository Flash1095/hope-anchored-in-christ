epiporeúomai

from G1909 and G4198; to journey further, i.e. travel on (reach):--come.

Biblical Usage

Epiporeuomai means to journey on, to come together from every side — and when much people were gathered together, and were come to Him out of every city, Jesus spake by a parable.

Theological Significance

People came from every city to hear Him, and He answered with a story about seed — that is the hunger the gathering shows.
