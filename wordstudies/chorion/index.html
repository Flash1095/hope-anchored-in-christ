chōríon

diminutive of G5561; a spot or plot of ground:--field, land, parcel of ground, place, possession.

Biblical Usage

Chorion is a field, a plot of ground, and the Gospels and Acts use it for the ground that changes hands. Jacob gives Joseph a parcel of ground, the field bought with the price of blood is called the field of blood, and Gethsemane is a field where Jesus prays while His disciples sleep.

Theological Significance

The fields in these verses are ground where God's story turns, whether it is a gift, a purchase or a prayer. I hold that Gethsemane is the field that matters most, because there the will of God was settled in the dark.
