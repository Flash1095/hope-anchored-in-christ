shâʻâh

to gaze at or about (properly, for help); by implication, to inspect, consider, compassionate, be nonplussed (as looking around in amazement) or bewildered

Biblical Usage

Sha'ah is gazing, looking about for help, and regarding — and both verses show a look that fails or is refused. David's enemies looked, but there was none to save, even unto the LORD, but He answered them not. And Pharaoh said, let there more work be laid upon the men, and let them not regard vain words.

Theological Significance

I believe the two looks are the two ends of trust: one looks for help where there is none, and the other refuses to look at the word of God at all.
