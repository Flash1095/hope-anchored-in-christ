shᵉdêmâh

a cultivated field

Biblical Usage

A field is where the harvest grows, and where it fails. Habakkuk says the fields shall yield no meat, Isaiah says the fields of Heshbon languish, and Josiah burns the vessels of Baal in the fields of Kidron. Jeremiah looks further: the whole valley of dead bodies and all the fields unto the brook of Kidron shall be holy unto the LORD.

Theological Significance

I believe fields tell the state of a people. When the fields fail, the people are judged; when the fields are made holy, the people are restored, and I hold that God reclaims the ground judgment took.
