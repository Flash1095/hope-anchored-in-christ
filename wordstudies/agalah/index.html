ʻăgâlâh

something revolving, i.e. a wheeled vehicle

Biblical Usage

Agalah is a cart or wagon — the wagons Joseph sent to carry his father Jacob down to Egypt, the wagons the princes of Israel offered for the tabernacle, the new cart the Philistines built to send the ark home, and the new cart David used to carry the ark, against God's command, until Uzzah died. The word rolls through the story carrying people, offerings, and the ark itself — and the ark's journey shows that even a cart must follow God's way, not man's.

Theological Significance

It names something ordinary — a thing, a place, a manner of speaking — and I take its meaning from the verse, not from the word alone; in this case, the verse is Genesis 45:19.
