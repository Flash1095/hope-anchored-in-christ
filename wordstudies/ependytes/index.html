ependýtēs

from G1902; a wrapper, i.e. outer garment:--fisher's coat.

Biblical Usage

Ependytes is an outer garment, a fisher's coat. When John tells Peter it is the Lord, Peter girds his fisher's coat about him — he was naked — and casts himself into the sea to reach Jesus.

Theological Significance

Peter's rush, coat and all, says more than words could, I believe: he would rather swim to Jesus than wait for the boat.
