kippâh

a leaf of a palmtree

Biblical Usage

Kippah is the branch of a palm tree, the top of the tree. Job 15:32 says the wicked man's branch shall not be green, and Isaiah 9:14 and Isaiah 19:15 both use it with head and tail: the LORD will cut off from Israel head and tail, branch and rush, in one day, and no work shall remain for Egypt which the head or tail, branch or rush, may do.

Theological Significance

I believe branch and rush are the whole nation from top to bottom: when God cuts them off, nothing is left to do the work.
