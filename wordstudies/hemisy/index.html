hḗmisy

neuter of a derivative from an inseparable prefix akin to G260 (through the idea of partition involved in connection) and meaning semi-; (as noun) half:--half.

Biblical Usage

Hemisy means half, and it appears where half is the measure of a promise or a repentance. In Luke 19:8 Zacchaeus says, behold, Lord, the half of my goods I give to the poor, in Mark 6:23 Herod offers the dancer whatsoever she asks, even to the half of my kingdom, and in Revelation 11:9 the dead bodies lie in the street for three days and a half.

Theological Significance

I believe the half shows the heart: Zacchaeus gives half his goods gladly, Herod offers half his kingdom rashly, and the dead lie half a week before they rise; half measures reveal whole men.
