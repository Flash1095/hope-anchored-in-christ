brontḗ

akin to βρέμω (to roar); thunder:--thunder(-ing).

Biblical Usage

brontḗ means thunder, and the Bible means it plainly. It occurs 11 times in the New Testament, in John and Mark and elsewhere, and none of those places bends the word out of shape. What it says in one place is what it says in all of them.

Theological Significance

I believe brontḗ earns its place. Thunder is the kind of thing God repeats because His people need to hear it more than once.
