éthos

from G1486; a usage (prescribed by habit or law):--custom, manner, be wont.

Biblical Usage

Éthos is custom — the usage, the way things are done, the practice handed down. The Jews accuse Paul of teaching customs against Moses, and Paul keeps the customs of the law where he can. It is the word for the habits that hold a people together.

Theological Significance

I believe éthos is a word for the weight of habit: customs shape a people more than speeches do. I hold that the customs worth keeping are the ones that point to Christ, and the ones that do not should be let go.
