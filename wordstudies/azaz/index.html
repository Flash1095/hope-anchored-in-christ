ʻâzaz

to be stout (literally or figuratively)

Biblical Usage

Azaz means to be stout, strong — and it shows up as stubborn strength as often as good strength. The Lord was with Othniel and his hand prevailed against the king of Mesopotamia, the wise man is strong and a man of knowledge increaseth strength, and Proverbs says the wicked man hardeneth his face. Strength, for good or for ill, is a choice of direction.

Theological Significance

I hold that strength is a gift with a direction: Othniel's strength delivered Israel, and the wicked man's strength only hardens his face. I want mine aimed the right way.
