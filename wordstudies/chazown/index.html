châzôwn

a sight (mentally), i.e. a dream, revelation, or oracle

Biblical Usage

A vision is what God shows, and the word marks the times when He speaks. In Samuel's day there is no open vision and the word of the LORD is precious, Daniel is given understanding in all visions and dreams, and a vision appears to him in the third year of Belshazzar.

Theological Significance

I believe a vision is God's initiative, not man's invention: it is precious when it is rare, and it is given to the faithful when it comes. Daniel receives visions because he resolved to be faithful first.
