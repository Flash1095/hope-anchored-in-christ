bârâʼ

(absolutely) to create; (qualified) to cut down (a wood), select, feed (as formative processes)

Biblical Usage

Bara is the word for create, and it belongs to God alone. In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth, He created man in His own image, Isaiah says He who created Jacob also formed Israel and will redeem him, and David prays for God to create in him a clean heart.

Theological Significance

I believe create is the word that settles who God is and who we are. He made the world out of nothing, He made us in His image, and only He can make a clean heart where sin has ruined one, and I hold that the God who created once is still creating in His people today.
