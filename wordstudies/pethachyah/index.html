Pᵉthachyâh

Word
פְּתַחְיָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Pᵉthachyâh
Strongs Number
H6611
Definition
Pethachjah, the name of four Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Pethahiah = "freed by Jehovah"; 1) a priest, in charge of the 19th course, in the time of David; 2) a Levite and returning exile who had married a foreign wife; probably the same as 3; 3) a Levite who helped lead in the confession of the people in the time of Ezra; probably the same as 2; 4) son of Meshezabeel, descendant of Zerah the son of Judah; deputy of the king in all matters concerning the people
