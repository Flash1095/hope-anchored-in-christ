Definition
Ashteroth-Karnaim, a place East of the Jordan
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Ashtoreth-karnaim = "Ashtoreth of the two horns or peaks"; 1) a city in Bashan east of the Jordan given to Manasseh; 1a) same as H06252
