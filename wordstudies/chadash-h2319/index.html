châdâsh

new

Biblical Usage

Chadash means new. A new king arose over Egypt who knew not Joseph, the feasts brought a new meat offering, the firstfruits were a new offering, and the law excused the man who had built a new house and not yet dedicated it.

Theological Significance

I believe new in Scripture is a mixed word: a new king can mean forgetfulness, a new offering can mean firstfruits, and a new house can mean a blessing not yet enjoyed. New is not good or bad by itself — it depends on whose hands hold it.
