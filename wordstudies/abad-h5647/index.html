ʻâbad

to work (in any sense); by implication, to serve, till, (causatively) enslave, etc.

Biblical Usage

ʻÂbad covers work, service and worship — the same word for Jacob working seven years for Rachel and for Israel serving God. In the Bible, serving is never just a task; it is who you belong to. You serve the one you have chosen as master.

Theological Significance

I hold that serving God is not earning His love, it is answering it. Joshua's call still stands: choose this day whom you will serve. True freedom is not serving no one — it is serving the right Master, the one who first served us.
