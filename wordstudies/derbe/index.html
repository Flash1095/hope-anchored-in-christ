Dérbē

of foreign origin; Derbe, a place in Asia Minor:--Derbe.

Biblical Usage

Derbe was a small town in Lycaonia where Paul fled when Lystra turned against him, and where he returned to strengthen the disciples after being stoned. It was on the road through Derbe and Lystra that Timothy joined Paul's company, the beginning of a partnership that shaped the churches.

Theological Significance

I believe Derbe shows how God uses the quiet places. It was a refuge after a stoning and a starting point for Timothy's ministry, and the gospel kept moving because small towns opened their doors to it.
