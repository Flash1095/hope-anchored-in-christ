skeûos

of uncertain affinity; a vessel, implement, equipment or apparatus (literally or figuratively [specially, a wife as contributing to the usefulness of the husband]):--goods, sail, stuff, vessel.

Biblical Usage

Skeuos is a vessel, and it holds whatever the Master puts in it. Paul is a chosen vessel to bear Christ's name before Gentiles and kings, the potter makes one vessel unto honour and another unto dishonour from the same lump, the sanctified man is a vessel unto honour prepared unto every good work, and each believer is told to possess his vessel in sanctification and honour.

Theological Significance

I believe I am a vessel, not the potter. The clay does not choose its use, and I hold that honour is being clean and ready in the Master's hand.
