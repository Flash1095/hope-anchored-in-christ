mamlâkûwth

{dominion, i.e. (abstractly) the estate (rule) or (concretely) the country (realm)}

Biblical Usage

mamlâkûwth is the same kingdom word in its Aramaic-tinged form, the realm as a possession that can be given and taken away. In 1 Samuel 15:28 Samuel tells Saul the LORD has rent the kingdom of Israel from him and given it to a neighbour better than he. In Hosea 1:4 God says He will cause the kingdom of the house of Israel to cease, and in 2 Samuel 16:3 Ziba claims the kingdom will be restored to him. In Joshua 13:12 it names the kingdom of Og in Bashan, a kingdom held in the hand that God can open.

Theological Significance

I believe mamlâkûwth shows me that no kingdom is owned outright, only held on loan from God. He gives it and He takes it, and I hold that the only safe trust is in the King who gives.
