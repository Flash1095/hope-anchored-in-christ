ésthēsis

from a derivative of G2066; clothing (concretely):--government.

Biblical Usage

Ésthēsis means clothing — a garment, the concrete thing worn. It stands in the Textus Receptus reading of the angels' shining garments at the empty tomb, where the common Greek text reads a different word for clothing. The word itself is plain; the interest is in the reading.

Theological Significance

I take ésthēsis as it stands — clothing — and I am content to let the two readings stand side by side, since both clothe the angels in light.
