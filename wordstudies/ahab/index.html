ʼâhab

to have affection f or (sexually or otherwise)

Biblical Usage

Ahab is the great verb 'to love' in the Old Testament, used hundreds of times. God tells Abraham to take Isaac, 'whom thou lovest'; Moses commands Israel, 'thou shalt love the LORD thy God with all thine heart'; Jonathan's soul was knit to David's and he loved him as his own soul; and God says to Israel, 'I have loved thee with an everlasting love.' The word covers love for God, love for a son, love for a friend, and God's love for His people — and it always means love that acts.

Theological Significance

I hold that this word is the whole Old Testament in a single verb. Love the Lord with all your heart — that is the command — and I have loved thee with an everlasting love — that is the promise. The command rests on the promise, and the promise is why the command can be obeyed.
