chêsheb

a belt or strap (as being interlaced)

Biblical Usage

Chesheb is the woven band of the ephod, the curious girdle that bound the high priest's garments together. Exodus describes it as the work of the ephod itself, of gold and blue and purple and scarlet, and Leviticus shows Moses girding Aaron with it when he dresses him for the priesthood.

Theological Significance

I believe the band that held the priest's garments together pictures how God holds His servants together for His service. I hold that nothing about the priest's dress was decoration alone, because every thread pointed to holiness.
