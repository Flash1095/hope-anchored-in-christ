epitynchánō

from G1909 and G5177; to chance upon, i.e. (by implication) to attain:--obtain.

Biblical Usage

Epitynchánō means to chance upon — to attain, to obtain what you sought. Abraham obtained the promise; the elders obtained a good report through faith; and Israel did not obtain what it sought, because it sought it by works. It is the word for getting hold of what God promised.

Theological Significance

I believe epitynchánō is the word for faith's reward: those who seek God by faith obtain what they seek. I hold that the difference is the way — by faith, not by works — and the promise is sure to those who come that way.
