kataphiléō

from 2596 and 5368; to kiss earnestly:--kiss.

Biblical Usage

To kiss earnestly is to kiss again and again, and the sinful woman kissed the feet of Jesus and the father fell on the neck of his returning son and kissed him, while Judas came and kissed the Master to betray Him. The same act carries worship, welcome, and treachery, and the difference is the heart behind the lips. One kiss says I love you, another says I have sold you.

Theological Significance

I believe the kiss is the test of the heart, because lips can lie and still be warm. I hold that the father's kiss is the picture of my welcome home, and the traitor's kiss is the warning that closeness without love is the worst betrayal of all.
