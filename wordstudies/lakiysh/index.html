Lâkîysh

Lakish, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Lachish was a fenced city of Judah, and its name runs through the wars of the kings. In 2 Chronicles 11:9 Rehoboam fortified it, in 2 Kings 14:19 Amaziah fled to Lachish and was slain there, and in Isaiah 36:2 Sennacherib sent Rabshakeh "from Lachish to Jerusalem" to demand Hezekiah's surrender.

Theological Significance

I am convinced Lachish is the strong city that could not save: it was fortified, it was besieged, and kings fled to it and died in it, because no wall stands when God has given the city up.
