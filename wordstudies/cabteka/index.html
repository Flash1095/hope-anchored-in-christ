Çabtᵉkâʼ

Sabteca, the name of a son of Cush, and the region settled by him

Biblical Usage

Sabteca was a son of Cush, named in the table of nations with his brothers Seba, Havilah, Sabta and Raamah, and the name also stands for the region his people settled. Like his brothers, he marks one more branch of the family spreading across the earth after the flood.

Theological Significance

I believe every nation has a beginning God remembers, and Sabteca is one of the names that proves it.
