Ribqâh

Ribkah, the wife of Isaac

Biblical Usage

The name is Ribkah, the wife of Isaac. You find the name in Genesis 22:23, "…Bethuel begat Rebekah: these eight Milcah did bear…", and again in Genesis 24:15 and Genesis 24:29.

Theological Significance

I believe Ribkah matters because God names his people one by one, and none of them is forgotten. I hold that no name in Scripture is there by accident.
