ʼĂdrammelek

Adrammelek, the name of an Assyrian idol, also of a son of Sennacherib

Biblical Usage

Adrammelech is two things at once: the god of Sepharvaim, to whom the settlers in Samaria burnt their children in the fire, and the son of Sennacherib who, with his brother Sharezer, murdered his father in the temple of Nisroch and fled to Armenia. The idol and the murderer share a name, and both stand for the same thing — the violence of a world that has turned from the true God.

Theological Significance

I believe this name is a warning written twice: a god that demands children in the fire, and a son who murders his father in a temple. Both show what idolatry does to a people, and both fell before the God of Israel, who needs no such worship and permits no such crime.
