ʼĂdrammelek

Word
אֲדְרַמֶּלֶךְ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼĂdrammelek
Strongs Number
H152
Definition
Adrammelek, the name of an Assyrian idol, also of a son of Sennacherib
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Adrammelech = "honour of the king" or "Adar is prince" or "Adar is Counsellor, Decider"; 1) an idol or god of the Sepharvites, introduced to Israel by Shalmaneser the fifth; 2) the son and murderer of Sennacherib
