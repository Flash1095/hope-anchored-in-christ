Kôzᵉbîy

Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Kôzᵉbîy
Strongs Number
H3579
Definition
Cozbi, a Midianitess
Part Of Speech
proper noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
Cozbi = "my lie"; 1) a daughter of Zur, a chief of the Midianites, who was slain by Phinehas because of her adultery
