augḗ

of uncertain derivation; a ray of light, i.e. (by implication) radiance, dawn:--break of day.

Biblical Usage

After Paul raised Eutychus, he went up and broke bread and talked a long while, even till break of day, and so departed. The word means the dawn, the first ray of morning.

Theological Significance

I hold that the dawn after a long night of preaching is a small picture of the resurrection morning, when the talking will give way to the seeing.
