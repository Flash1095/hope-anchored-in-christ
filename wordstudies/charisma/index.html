chárisma

Word
χάρισμα
Language
Greek
Transliteration
chárisma
Strongs Number
G5486
Definition
from G5483; a (divine) gratuity, i.e. deliverance (from danger or passion); (specially), a (spiritual) endowment, i.e. (subjectively) religious qualification, or (objectively) miraculous faculty:--(free) gift.
