Chebrôwn

Chebron, a place in Palestine, also the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Hebron is a city and also a man, and the name means association. Hebron the son of Kohath is named through Mareshah the father of Hebron, David's sons are born to him in Hebron the city, and all Israel gathers to David at Hebron to make him king.

Theological Significance

I believe Hebron is where the promise becomes a throne: David's sons are born there and Israel crowns him there, and the city of association becomes the city of the king.
