maddûwaʻ

what (is) known?; i.e. (by implication) (adverbially) why?

Biblical Usage

Maddua is the question why, wherefore, and it is the most honest word in the Bible, asked seventy-one times. Exodus 3:3 has Moses turning aside to see why the bush is not burnt, and Job 21:7 asks wherefore do the wicked live and become old, while Jeremiah 12:1 puts the same question to God Himself: wherefore doth the way of the wicked prosper?

Theological Significance

I believe God leaves the why in His book on purpose, because a faith that cannot ask it is not faith at all.
