merchâb

enlargement, either literally (an open space, usually in a good sense), or figuratively (liberty)

Biblical Usage

Merchab is breadth, enlargement, an open space, and the freedom that goes with it. 2 Samuel 22:20 has David sing that God brought him forth into a large place, because He delighted in him, and Habakkuk 1:6 has the Chaldeans march through the breadth of the land to possess dwellingplaces that are not theirs.

Theological Significance

I believe breadth is both gift and threat: God brings His own into a large place, and the proud march through the breadth of the land to take it.
