çiryôn

a coat of mail

Biblical Usage

A coat of mail is the armour of the horseman, and Jeremiah tells Egypt to harness the horses and put on the coats of mail. Babylon is told to bend the bow and lift up the coat of mail, and the armour is part of the judgment on both nations.

Theological Significance

I believe armour in Jeremiah is a picture of trust in the wrong things, because no coat of mail can stand against the LORD.
