râqîyaʻ

properly, an expanse, i.e. the firmament or (apparently) visible arch of the sky

Biblical Usage

Raqia is the expanse, the firmament — the visible arch of the sky, beaten out like a sheet of metal. God said, 'Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters'; He made the firmament and divided the waters above from the waters below, and called the firmament Heaven; and the psalm says, 'The heavens declare the glory of God; and the firmament sheweth his handywork.'

Theological Significance

I am convinced that the firmament is the roof God hammered out: He stretched the sky above us like a beaten sheet of gold, and it still declares His glory every day. And I hold that the waters above and below are held apart by His word alone — the sky stays up because He said so.
