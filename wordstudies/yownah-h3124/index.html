Yôwnâh

Jonah, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Jonah is the prophet who ran from God and was brought back by a fish. In 2 Kings 14:25 he is the prophet of Gath-hepher whose word restored Israel's border, and in Jonah 1:1 "the word of the LORD came unto Jonah." Jonah 1:3 shows him fleeing: he "rose up to flee unto Tarshish from the presence of the LORD," and the book that bears his name is the story of a reluctant prophet and a merciful God.

Theological Significance

I believe Jonah is the mirror I need when I want to run. I hold that the prophet who fled in Jonah 1:3 and the God who pursued him anyway show me mercy is wider than my obedience, and that Nineveh's repentance is proof no city is beyond his reach.
