pentherá

feminine of G3995; a wife's mother:--mother in law, wife's mother.

Biblical Usage

Penthera is a mother-in-law, a wife's mother. Simon's wife's mother lay sick with a great fever, and they besought Jesus for her, and He came to her bedside.

Theological Significance

I am sure that Jesus heals in the home as well as in the synagogue: they told Him about Peter's mother-in-law, and He came.
