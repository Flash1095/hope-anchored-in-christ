torah

Instruction, teaching and law — God's gracious direction for life, centred in the books of Moses but embracing all His revealed guidance.

Biblical Usage

Torah is broader than rules: it is a Father's instruction to be loved, meditated on day and night and taught to children. The Psalms delight in it rather than merely enduring it.

Theological Significance

Torah reframes God's commands as a gift, not a burden. For Christians, Christ fulfils the Torah and the Spirit writes its heart on us, guiding us in grateful obedience.
