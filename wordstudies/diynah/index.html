Dîynâh

Dinah, the daughter of Jacob

Biblical Usage

Dinah was the daughter of Jacob by Leah, named at her birth in the record of Jacob's family. Genesis tells how she went out to see the daughters of the land, and how Shechem the Hivite took her and spoke kindly to her afterward.

Theological Significance

I believe Dinah's story is told without hiding what happened to her, and the Bible does not look away from her pain. Her brothers' revenge and her father's silence are both recorded, and I read them as a warning about what happens when justice is taken into angry hands.
