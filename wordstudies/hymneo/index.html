hymnéō

Word
ὑμνέω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
hymnéō
Strongs Number
G5214
Definition
from G5215; to hymn, i.e. sing a religious ode; by implication, to celebrate (God) in song:--sing a hymn (praise unto).
