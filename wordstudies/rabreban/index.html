rabrᵉbân

a magnate

Biblical Usage

Rabreban is a magnate, a lord of the realm. Belshazzar made a great feast to a thousand of his lords; the queen came in because of the words of the king and his lords; and the king sealed the den with his own signet and with the signet of his lords.

Theological Significance

I take it that the lords of Babylon are a warning: a thousand lords feasting, and not one of them could read the writing on the wall.
