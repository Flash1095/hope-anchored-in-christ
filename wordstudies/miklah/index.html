miklâh

completion (in plural concrete adverbial, wholly)

Biblical Usage

The flowers and the lamps and the tongs were made of gold, and that perfect gold. The word is completion itself, wholly, and the chronicler uses it for gold that is perfect through and through.

Theological Significance

I notice that the temple's gold is described as perfect, not merely pure: the flowers, the lamps and the tongs were all of that perfect gold, because God's house is finished in gold.
