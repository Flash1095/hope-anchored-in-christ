kynárion

neuter of a presumed derivative of G2965; a puppy:--dog.

Biblical Usage

The little dog is the diminutive Jesus uses with the Syrophenician woman, and her answer turns the word into faith. In Mark 7:27 and Matthew 15:26 Jesus says it is not meet to take the children's bread and cast it to the dogs, and in Mark 7:28 and Matthew 15:27 she answers, "yet the dogs under the table eat of the children's crumbs."

Theological Significance

To my mind, her answer is one of the greatest in the Bible: she did not argue the name, she took the crumbs, and Jesus praised her faith and healed her daughter. She took the crumbs, and the crumbs were enough.
