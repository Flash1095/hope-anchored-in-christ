Nᵉbûwkadnetstsar

{Nebukadnetstsar (or -retstsar, or -retstsor), king of Babylon}

Biblical Usage

Nebuchadnezzar is told there is a God in heaven who reveals secrets and makes known what shall be in the latter days, and he makes an image of gold and sets it up for all to worship. He then addresses all peoples and nations with his testimony to the Most High.

Theological Significance

I believe the same king holds both ends of the story: the idol-maker and the witness, the man who raises the image and the man who confesses the God of heaven. God can humble a king and then use his mouth.
