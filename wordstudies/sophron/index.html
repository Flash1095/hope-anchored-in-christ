sṓphrōn

from the base of G4982 and that of G5424; safe (sound) in mind, i.e. self-controlled (moderate as to opinion or passion):--discreet, sober, temperate.

Biblical Usage

The sober-minded man is the one Paul wants in charge and the one he wants in the home. A bishop must be sober, the aged men must be sober and grave and temperate, and the young women are to be taught to be discreet, chaste, keepers at home.

Theological Significance

I believe sober-mindedness is the quiet backbone of the church: clear heads, steady lives, and no show.
