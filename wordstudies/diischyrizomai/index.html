diïschyrízomai

from G1223 and a derivative of G2478; to stout it through, i.e. asservate:--confidently (constantly) affirm.

Biblical Usage

To insist strongly, to affirm something with full confidence even when others doubt it. Rhoda insisted Peter really stood at the door, and another bystander insisted Peter was a follower of Jesus.

Theological Significance

I hold that some truths are worth insisting on even when the room disagrees, because confidence is not stubbornness when the fact is true.
