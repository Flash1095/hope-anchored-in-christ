cheres

a piece of pottery

Biblical Usage

Cheres is a potsherd, a broken piece of pottery, and Scripture uses it for both judgment and lowly suffering. Isaiah says a nation can be broken as a potter's vessel is broken, Jeremiah is told to take a potter's earthen bottle, Ezekiel breaks the sherds of a cup, and Job takes a potsherd to scrape himself as he sits in the ashes.

Theological Significance

I believe God breaks what is proud and keeps what is broken, because a potsherd in His hand can still serve. I hold that Job scraping his sores with a shard is one of the truest pictures of suffering in the Bible.
