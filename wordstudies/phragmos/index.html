phragmós

from G5420; a fence, or inclosing barrier (literally or figuratively):--hedge (+ round about), partition.

Biblical Usage

Phragmos is a fence, a hedge, an enclosing barrier, and it appears at the vineyard and at the wall. The owner of the vineyard sets a hedge about it, the master tells his servant to go out into the highways and hedges and compel them to come in, and Christ has broken down the middle wall of partition between Jew and Gentile.

Theological Significance

I believe the hedge teaches me both sides of God's fence: the vineyard is hedged to keep it safe, and the wall between peoples is broken so that all may come in.
