ʻÎyrîy

Iri, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Iri was a son of Bela, named in the genealogy of Benjamin. In 1 Chronicles 7:7 the sons of Bela are listed, Ezbon, and Uzzi, and Uzziel, and Jerimoth, and Iri, five heads of the house of their fathers, mighty men of valour.

Theological Significance

I believe Iri's name is carried by his brothers: five sons of Bela, five heads of houses, and all mighty men of valour.
