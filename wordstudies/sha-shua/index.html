shaʻshuaʻ

enjoyment

Biblical Usage

A sha'shua' is a delight, an enjoyment, and God and Wisdom both claim it. Judah is the LORD's pleasant plant, the delight of His vineyard; Ephraim is His dear son, His pleasant child, whom He earnestly remembers still and will surely have mercy on. And Wisdom says she was daily His delight, rejoicing always before Him, and her delights were with the sons of men.

Theological Significance

I believe delight is the word for how God looks at His people: Judah His pleasant plant, Ephraim His dear son, and Wisdom delighting in the sons of men. He does not merely keep us — He enjoys us. And I hold that the delight runs both ways: God's delight is in His people, and Wisdom's delight is with them.
