ʻAzgâd

Word
עַזְגָּד
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻAzgâd
Strongs Number
H5803
Definition
Azgad, an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Azgad = "Gad is mighty"; 1) ancestor of a family of laymen who returned from exile with Zerubbabel; 2) a man who sealed the covenant with Nehemiah
