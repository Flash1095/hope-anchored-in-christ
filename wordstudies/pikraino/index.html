pikraínō

from G4089; to embitter (literally or figuratively):--be (make) bitter.

Biblical Usage

Pikraino means to make bitter. Husbands are told not to be bitter against their wives in Colossians 3:19, and John eats the little book in Revelation 10:9 — sweet as honey in his mouth, but bitter in his belly in Revelation 10:10.

Theological Significance

I believe God's word can taste sweet and sit heavy, and a husband's bitterness has no place in a Christian home.
