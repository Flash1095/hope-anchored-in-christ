phyteúō

from a derivative of G5453; to set out in the earth, i.e. implant; figuratively, to instil doctrine:--plant.

Biblical Usage

Phyteuo means to plant, to set something in the earth to grow. Paul planted and Apollos watered, but God gave the increase, in 1 Corinthians 3:6 — and neither the planter nor the waterer is anything, says 1 Corinthians 3:7. Jesus plants vineyards in His parables, as in Mark 12:1, and warns that every plant His Father has not planted shall be rooted up in Matthew 15:13.

Theological Significance

I believe my work is planting and watering, and God's work is the increase. That frees me from both pride in the harvest and despair over slow growth.
