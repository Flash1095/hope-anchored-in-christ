ʼEphrayim

Ephrajim, a son of Joseph; also the tribe descended from him, and its territory

Biblical Usage

Ephraim — I shall be doubly fruitful — is Joseph's second son, blessed by Jacob with his right hand over the younger. His name became the tribe's, and then the name of the whole northern kingdom in the prophets.

Theological Significance

The blessing crossed hands, and the second son became the greater, because God gives as He wills and not as men expect. I believe Ephraim is the picture of God's freedom to choose the younger, and Joseph's joy at his fruitfulness was only the beginning.
