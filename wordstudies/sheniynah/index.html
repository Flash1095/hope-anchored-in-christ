shᵉnîynâh

something pointed, i.e. a gibe

Biblical Usage

A byword is what a people becomes when God hands them over to scorn: Deuteronomy warns that Israel will be a proverb and a byword among all nations, and the kings hear the same warning at the dedication of the temple. Jeremiah watches it come true as Judah is carried away to be a byword in every place.

Theological Significance

I believe a byword is the saddest title a people can earn, because it means their name is no longer spoken with respect but used as a warning. I hold that the fear of God is what keeps a people from becoming one.
