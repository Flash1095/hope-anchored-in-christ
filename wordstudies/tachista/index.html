táchista

neuter plural of the superlative of G5036 (as adverb); most quickly, i.e. (with G5613 prefixed) as soon as possible:--+ with all speed.

Biblical Usage

When Paul left Berea, his companions brought him to Athens, and those who conducted him received a commandment to Silas and Timothy to come to him with all speed. The word is the superlative of swift: most quickly, as soon as possible. It is the one verse where the King James text carries this form, and the hurry in it is Paul's: he would not be left alone in Athens for long.

Theological Significance

I believe the hurry tells us something about Paul: even an apostle wanted his friends beside him, and wanted them soon.
