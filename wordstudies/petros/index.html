Pétros

apparently a primary word; a (piece of) rock (larger than G3037); as a name, Petrus, an apostle:--Peter, rock. Compare G2786.

Biblical Usage

Petros is Peter, the fisherman Jesus named Rock, and his story runs through the whole New Testament. Jesus gives him the name at their first meeting, calls him the rock on which the church will be built at Caesarea Philippi, and Paul says the gospel of the circumcision was committed to Peter as the gospel of the uncircumcision was to him.

Theological Significance

I believe Peter matters because his name is a promise: a shifting fisherman made into rock by the word of Christ. His life is on his profile; here the name alone tells me that Christ renames His people and makes them stand.
