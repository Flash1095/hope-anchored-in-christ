Tophteh

Word
תׇּפְתֶּה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Tophteh
Strongs Number
H8613
Definition
Tophteh, a place of cremation
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Tophet = "place of fire"; 1) a place in the southeast end of the valley of the son of Hinnom south of Jerusalem; 1a) same as H08612
