dógma

from the base of G1380; a law (civil, ceremonial or ecclesiastical):--decree, ordinance.

Biblical Usage

A decree or ordinance, the law as it is handed down, from Caesar's decree that all the world should be taxed to the decrees of the Jerusalem council carried to the churches. Paul says Christ blotted out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us and abolished the law of commandments in ordinances.

Theological Significance

I believe the decrees of men pass, but the decree of the cross stands. Caesar taxed the world and the council wrote letters, yet it is Christ who nailed the ordinances against me to His cross, and I live free of them.
