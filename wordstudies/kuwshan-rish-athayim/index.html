Kûwshan Rishʻâthayim

Word
כּוּשַׁן רִשְׁעָתַיִם
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Kûwshan Rishʻâthayim
Strongs Number
H3573
Definition
Cushan-Rishathajim, a Mesopotamian king
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Chushan-rishathaim = "twice-wicked Cushan"; 1) a king of Mesopotamia defeated by the judge Othniel the son-in-law of Caleb
