parthénos

of unknown origin; a maiden; by implication, an unmarried daughter:--virgin.

Biblical Usage

Parthenos is a virgin, a maiden, an unmarried daughter. Jesus tells the parable of the ten virgins in Matthew 25:1, five wise and five foolish, who took their lamps to meet the bridegroom. Philip has four daughters who are virgins and prophesy in Acts 21:9. Paul says in 1 Corinthians 7:34 that the unmarried woman cares for the things of the Lord, and in 2 Corinthians 11:2 he has espoused the Corinthians to one husband, to present them as a chaste virgin to Christ.

Theological Significance

I believe the virgin is Scripture's picture of a church kept for one Husband, and Paul's jealousy over the Corinthians is the jealousy of a friend of the Bridegroom. That is why I hold that purity is not prudishness, it is faithfulness to the One who is coming.
