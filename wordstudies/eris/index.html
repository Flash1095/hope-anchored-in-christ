éris

of uncertain affinity; a quarrel, i.e. (by implication) wrangling:--contention, debate, strife, variance.

Biblical Usage

Éris is strife — quarrelling, wrangling, the contention that splits a church. Paul lists it among the works of the flesh, and he writes to Corinth because there is strife among them. It is the word for the fighting that proves the flesh is still in charge.

Theological Significance

I believe éris is the test of a church: where there is strife, the Spirit is being quenched. I hold that the answer is not to win the argument, but to put the flesh to death.
