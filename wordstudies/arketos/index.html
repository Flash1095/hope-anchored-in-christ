arketós

from G714; satisfactory:--enough, suffice (-ient).

Biblical Usage

Enough is enough, and Jesus and Peter both draw the line there. Sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof, says Jesus, and it is enough for the disciple to be as his master; Peter says the time past of our life may suffice us to have wrought the will of the Gentiles.

Theological Significance

I hold that 'enough' is one of the kindest words in the Bible. God does not ask us to carry tomorrow's trouble today, or to be more than a disciple, or to keep on in a past we have left, enough is enough, and His grace covers the day in front of us.
