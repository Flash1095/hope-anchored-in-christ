ʻêtsâh

advice; by implication, plan; also prudence

Biblical Usage

ʻÊtsâh is counsel — advice, a plan, the guidance of the wise. The counsel of the LORD stands forever, the psalmist says, while the counsel of the nations comes to nothing; and a man's friends and counsellors shape his way. It is the word for the advice you live by.

Theological Significance

I believe ʻêtsâh is a word for choosing whose counsel I take: the LORD's counsel stands, and man's counsel fails. I hold that the blessed man of the first psalm is blessed because he walks in no counsel but God's.
