mâlak

to reign; inceptively, to ascend the throne; causatively, to induct into royalty; hence (by implication) to take counsel

Biblical Usage

Malak means to reign, to be king, and it is the verb of every throne in the Bible. 1 Samuel 8:7 has God telling Samuel the people have not rejected him but have rejected Me, that I should not reign over them, and 1 Chronicles 16:31 says let men say among the nations, the LORD reigneth. Psalms 97:1 says the LORD reigneth, let the earth rejoice, and Jeremiah 23:5 promises a King who shall reign and prosper and execute judgment and justice in the earth.

Theological Significance

I believe this word is the argument of the whole Bible: men keep asking for a king, and God keeps telling them He is the King. My position is that Jeremiah 23:5 is the end of the argument, because the King who reigns in righteousness is the one Israel asked for all along.
