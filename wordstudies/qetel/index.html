qeṭel

a violent death

Biblical Usage

Obadiah says Teman's mighty men shall be dismayed, to the end that every one of the mount of Esau may be cut off by slaughter. One verse, and the word is the violent death itself.

Theological Significance

I believe Edom's mighty men learned that no mountain keeps God's judgment out, and Teman's strength failed on the day he said.
