ʻăyâm

probably meaning strength

Biblical Usage

This word seems to mean strength or might, and it appears only twice — Hosea says God will chastise his people when it is his desire, and Isaiah says the Lord will shake his hand over the river with his mighty wind. Strength, used by God against his enemies and for his people.

Theological Significance

I hold that God's strength is never wasted: it shakes rivers and chastises nations, and it is the same strength that carries me.
