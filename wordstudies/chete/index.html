chêṭᵉʼ

a crime or its penalty

Biblical Usage

Chete is a crime and its penalty, the sin counted against a person and the punishment it brings, and the prophets and kings both use it this way. Daniel confesses that for their sins Jerusalem has become a reproach, Deuteronomy warns that a grudging heart toward the poor will be sin, the chronicler says every man shall die for his own sin, and Jehu is charged with the sins of Jeroboam.

Theological Significance

I believe chete is honest about sin: it is not only a mistake but a debt that must be paid. I hold that this is why the cross matters, because there the penalty fell on Christ instead of on us.
