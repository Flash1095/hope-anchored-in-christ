lâʼ

{not (the simple or abs. negation); by implication, no;}

Biblical Usage

La is the Aramaic word for "not" and "no," the simple negation of Daniel and Ezra. In Daniel 2:5 the king says, "if ye will not make known unto me the dream, ye shall be cut in pieces," and in Ezra 4:13 the adversaries warn that if Jerusalem is built, the king will have "no" tribute, custom, or toll.

Theological Significance

I am convinced a small word like "not" carries the whole weight of the story: the king's threat, the enemies' accusation, and Daniel's answer all turn on what is not and what cannot be.
