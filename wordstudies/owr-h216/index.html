ʼôwr

illumination or (concrete) luminary (in every sense, including lightning, happiness, etc.)

Biblical Usage

Light begins the Bible. God said, Let there be light, and there was light, and David called the Lord his light and his salvation, and Isaiah promised that the people who walked in darkness would see a great light.

Theological Significance

I believe light is the first word God spoke and the last word He will speak: Let there be light, and the darkness did not overcome it. The light that began creation is the light that saves me, and it is the light I am called to walk in.
