Bêyth Shemesh

Word
בֵּית שֶׁמֶשׁ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Bêyth Shemesh
Strongs Number
H1053
Definition
Beth-Shemesh, a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Beth-shemesh = "house of the sun" or "sun-temple"; 1) a town in southwest Judah; 2) a town in Naphtali; 3) a town in Issachar; 4) a town in Egypt
