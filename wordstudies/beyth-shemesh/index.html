Bêyth Shemesh

Beth-Shemesh, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Beth-Shemesh means house of the sun, and it is the town where the ark came home. In 1 Samuel 6:12 the cows took the straight way to Beth-shemesh, lowing as they went, and the men of the town rejoiced to see the ark — but 1 Samuel 6:19 says the Lord smote the men of Beth-shemesh because they looked into the ark. Centuries later, 2 Kings 14:11 says Amaziah and Joash faced each other at Beth-shemesh, king against king.

Theological Significance

I believe Beth-shemesh teaches me the same lesson twice: God's presence is a joy, and it is not a toy. The town rejoiced, then it looked too closely, and then kings fought there.
