dyskólōs

adverb from G1422; impracticably:--hardly.

Biblical Usage

Hardly, with difficulty, as Jesus said it three times, in three Gospels, about the rich entering the kingdom of God. How hardly shall they that have riches enter, He said, looking at the young ruler who went away sorrowful.

Theological Significance

I believe Jesus repeated the warning because riches make the narrow gate look unnecessary, and the camel and the needle are His way of saying it is impossible with men. What is impossible with men is possible with God, and that is the only hope the rich man has.
