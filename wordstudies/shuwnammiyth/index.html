Shûwnammîyth

a Shunammitess, or female inhabitant of Shunem

Biblical Usage

Shunammith is a Shunammite woman, a woman of Shunem, and the name covers two women. One was Abishag, the fair damsel brought to old King David, and the other was the great woman of Shunem who fed Elisha and whose son Elisha raised from the dead. The same word names them both.

Theological Significance

I believe God honours the women who serve His servants. Abishag tended a dying king and the Shunammite fed a prophet, and I hold that both kinds of service are written down.
