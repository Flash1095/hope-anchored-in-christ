áchri

or ἄχρις; akin to G206 (through the idea of a terminus); (of time) until or (of place) up to:--as far as, for, in(-to), till, (even, un-)to, until, while. Compare G3360.

Biblical Usage

Until, as far as — achri marks the boundary. The days before the flood, when men ate and drank "until the day that Noe entered into the ark"; and the years in Egypt, "till another king arose, which knew not Joseph." The word draws a line and says: this far, and no further.

Theological Significance

A boundary word like 'until' teaches nothing on its own, and the weight lies in what stands on either side of each line it draws.
