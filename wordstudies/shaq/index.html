shâq

the leg

Biblical Usage

The image's legs were of iron, and its feet part of iron and part of clay. Daniel's word for the legs is a single Aramaic noun, and it stands at the hinge of the vision, where the strength of iron gives way to the weakness of clay.

Theological Significance

I hold that legs of iron and feet of clay are the picture of every empire, strong at the top and crumbling at the bottom.
