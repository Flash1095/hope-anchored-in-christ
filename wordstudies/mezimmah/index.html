mᵉzimmâh

a plan, usually evil (machination), sometimes good (sagacity)

Biblical Usage

Proverbs gives discretion to the simple, says discretion shall preserve and understanding shall keep, calls the young man to regard discretion, and says wisdom dwells with prudence and finds out knowledge of witty inventions. The word is a plan, usually evil, sometimes good, and Proverbs turns it to the good: discretion, the plan that keeps a man safe.

Theological Significance

I believe discretion is the plan that saves: the same word that names the schemes of the wicked names the prudence of the wise, and the difference is whose counsel the plan follows. The difference is whose counsel the plan follows, and wisdom follows the Lord's.
