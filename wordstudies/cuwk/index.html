çûwk

properly, to smear over (with oil), i.e. anoint

Biblical Usage

Anointing is the smearing of oil on the body, and the law says the olive will cast its fruit, so the anointing will do no good. Ruth washes and anoints herself before she goes to the threshing floor, and David anoints himself after his child dies. The captives are anointed by the men of Judah on their way home, and the word moves from the body to the burial and back to mercy.

Theological Significance

I believe anointing with oil is the Bible's picture of care: for the body, for the dead, and for the stranger on the road. Ruth anoints herself for a new life, David anoints himself to go on living, and the captives are anointed by their neighbours on their way home. I hold that oil on the skin is a small mercy, and small mercies are how God holds people together.
