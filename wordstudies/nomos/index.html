nómos

from a primary νέμω (to parcel out, especially food or grazing to animals); law (through the idea of prescriptive usage), genitive case (regulation), specially, (of Moses (including the volume); also of the Gospel), or figuratively (a principle):--law.

Biblical Usage

The law is the word that carries the whole Old Testament, and the New Testament measures it against Christ. Jesus says He came not to destroy the law but to fulfil it, the child is presented according to the law of Moses, John says the law was given by Moses but grace and truth came by Jesus Christ, and the false witnesses charge Stephen with speaking against the law.

Theological Significance

I believe the law is fulfilled, not discarded: Moses gave it, Christ fulfilled it, and grace and truth came by Him who kept it whole. The false witnesses charged Stephen with speaking against the law, and Stephen answered with the whole history of it.
