hypotíthēmi

Word
ὑποτίθημι
Language
Greek
Transliteration
hypotíthēmi
Strongs Number
G5294
Definition
from G5259 and G5087; to place underneath, i.e. (figuratively) to hazard, (reflexively) to suggest:--lay down, put in remembrance.
