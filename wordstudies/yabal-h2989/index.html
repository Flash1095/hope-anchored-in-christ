Yâbâl

Word
יָבָל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Yâbâl
Strongs Number
H2989
Definition
Jabal, an antediluvian
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Jabal = "stream of water"; 1) the son of Lamech by Adah and brother of Jubal; described as the father of such as dwell in tents and have cattle
