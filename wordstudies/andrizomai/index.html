andrízomai

middle voice from G435; to act manly:--quit like men.

Biblical Usage

Paul closes his first letter to Corinth with a soldier's call: watch, stand fast in the faith, quit you like men, be strong. The word means to act like a man in the old sense, to show courage and hold your ground.

Theological Significance

A single soldier's call holds no doctrine of its own; its force is Paul's, that faith is not passive but a stand to be held.
