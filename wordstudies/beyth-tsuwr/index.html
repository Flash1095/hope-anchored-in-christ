Bêyth Tsûwr

Beth-Tsur, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Beth-Zur means house of the rock, and the name covers both a town and a man. Joshua 15:58 lists the town among Judah's hill cities, and 2 Chronicles 11:7 shows Rehoboam fortifying it. But 1 Chronicles 2:45 says Maon was the father of Beth-zur — the same name carried by a son of Maon.

Theological Significance

I believe a name meaning house of the rock fits both: a fortified town and a man in Judah's line, each set on something solid.
