ktísma

from G2936; an original formation (concretely), i.e. product (created thing):--creature.

Biblical Usage

The creature is the thing created, and the word gathers up everything God made. In James 1:18 God begat us "that we should be a kind of firstfruits of his creatures," in 1 Timothy 4:4 "every creature of God is good, and nothing to be refused, if it be received with thanksgiving," and in Revelation 5:13 "every creature which is in heaven, and on the earth" gives blessing and honour to the Lamb.

Theological Significance

For me, the word keeps me from despising what God made: every creature is good, the new birth makes us firstfruits of His creatures, and one day every creature will join the song. Every creature is good, and every creature will one day sing.
