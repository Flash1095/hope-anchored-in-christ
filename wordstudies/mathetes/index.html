mathētḗs

from G3129; a learner, i.e. pupil:--disciple.

Biblical Usage

A disciple is a learner, and Jesus defines the word by the life that goes with it: the disciple is not above his master, and anyone who will come after Him must deny himself, take up his cross, and follow. John says the mark of a real disciple is continuing in His word, and that the world will know His disciples by their love for one another. At Antioch the disciples were called Christians first, because the learning had become a life.

Theological Significance

I believe a disciple is not someone who merely agrees with Jesus, but someone who follows Him where He goes, even to a cross. The test is not what we know but whether we keep His word and love each other when it costs us something. That is why the name Christian stuck at Antioch: the disciples looked like their Master.
