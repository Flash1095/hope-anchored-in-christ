Naphtâlîy

Naphtali, a son of Jacob, with the tribe descended from him, and its territory

Biblical Usage

Naphtali is the sixth son of Jacob, born to Rachel through Bilhah, and the tribe that bears his name. In Genesis 30:8 Rachel says she has wrestled with her sister and prevailed, and calls his name Naphtali, and in Genesis 49:21 Jacob blesses him as a hind let loose, giving goodly words. In Deuteronomy 33:23 Moses blesses Naphtali as satisfied with favour and full with the blessing of the Lord.

Theological Significance

I believe Naphtali's name is a wrestling match turned into a blessing, because Rachel's struggle became her son's name and Jacob's blessing turned it into praise. The hind let loose and the tribe full of favour are the same story: God takes a wrestling and makes it a blessing.
