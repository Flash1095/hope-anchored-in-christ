Gittayim

Gittajim, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Gittajim is a place in Palestine, named 2 times in Scripture, in 2 Samuel and Nehemiah. The full story of the place lives in the Bible itself, and this study covers only the name’s use.

Theological Significance

God met His people at Gittajim, a real place in His story, and that is why I take its name seriously. I hold that even a place the Bible names only briefly is worth knowing.
