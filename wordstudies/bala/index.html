bâlaʻ

to make away with (specifically by swallowing); generally, to destroy

Biblical Usage

Bala means to swallow up, to destroy by swallowing. The earth opens and swallows Korah's company, the sea swallows Egypt's army, and a great fish swallows Jonah, yet the same word turns around in Isaiah, where God promises to swallow up death itself in victory.

Theological Significance

I hold that swallowing is the Bible's picture of a power too big to resist, and that is why Isaiah's promise lands so hard. Death has swallowed men whole since Eden, but God will swallow death, and the eater will finally be eaten.
