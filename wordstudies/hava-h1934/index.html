Language

Transliteration
hâvâʼ
Strongs Number

Definition

Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to come to pass, become, be; 1a) (P'al); 1a1) to come to pass; 1a2) to come into being, arise, become, come to be; 1a2a) to let become known (with participle of knowing); 1a3) to be
