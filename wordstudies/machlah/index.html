Machlâh

Word
מַחְלָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Machlâh
Strongs Number
H4244
Definition
Machlah, the name apparently of two Israelitesses
Part Of Speech
proper noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
Mahlah = "disease"; 1) the eldest of 5 daughters of Zelophehad the grandson of Manasseh; 2) a Gileadite
