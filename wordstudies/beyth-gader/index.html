Bêyth-Gâdêr

Beth-Gader, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Beth-Gader means house of the wall, and it is named just once, in a genealogy: Hareph the father of Beth-gader, in 1 Chronicles 2:51. No story is told about it, no battle fought there — it survives only as a father's name attached to a town in Judah.

Theological Significance

I hold that even a town named once matters, because God kept the record of it.
