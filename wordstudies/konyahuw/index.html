Konyâhûw

Conjah, an Israelite king

Biblical Usage

Coniah is a king of Judah, the son of Jehoiakim, also called Jeconiah. Jeremiah 22:24 says though Coniah were the signet upon God's right hand, He would pluck him thence, Jeremiah 22:28 asks, is this man Coniah a despised broken idol, a vessel wherein is no pleasure, and Jeremiah 37:1 says king Zedekiah reigned instead of Coniah, whom Nebuchadrezzar made king.

Theological Significance

I believe Coniah is the king God plucked from His own hand: the signet ring cast off, the vessel with no pleasure in it, and the throne given to another.
