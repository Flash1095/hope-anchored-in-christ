thymomachéō

Word
θυμομαχέω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
thymomachéō
Strongs Number

Definition
from a presumed compound of G2372 and G3164; to be in a furious fight, i.e. (figuratively) to be exasperated:--be highly displeased.
