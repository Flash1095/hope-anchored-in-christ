nâgîyd

a commander (as occupying the front), civil, military or religious; generally (abstractly, plural), honorable themes

Biblical Usage

Nagid is a commander or ruler, one who stands at the front, and it is the word for Saul, for David, and for the Messiah. In 1 Samuel 9:16 Saul is to be anointed captain over Israel to save the people from the Philistines, and in 1 Samuel 13:14 the Lord has sought a man after His own heart and commanded him to be captain over His people. In 1 Chronicles 11:2 David is told he shall feed God's people and be ruler over Israel, and in Daniel 9:25 the Messiah the Prince is promised from the going forth of the commandment to restore Jerusalem.

Theological Significance

I believe the ruler in the Bible is measured by whether he saves the people or uses them, and the line runs from Saul to David to the Messiah. Saul was anointed and failed, David was chosen after God's own heart, and the Prince in Daniel 9:25 is the one who does not fail. I hold that every captain points to the one Captain who saves for good.
