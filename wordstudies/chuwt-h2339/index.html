chûwṭ

a string; by implication, a measuring tape

Biblical Usage

A thread is the smallest thing worth counting: Abram swears he will not take so much as a thread from the king of Sodom, and Rahab ties a scarlet thread in her window. Samson snaps new ropes like a thread, and the temple pillars are measured in lines of brass. The word runs from a refusal to a rescue to a measurement.

Theological Significance

I believe the thread shows how God uses small things to hold great ones. Abram's thread is integrity, Rahab's thread is salvation, and Samson's thread is strength broken. My position is that the scarlet thread is the picture I keep: a thin red line, and a whole household saved by it.
