Dióskouroi

Word
Διόσκουροι
Language
Greek
Transliteration
Dióskouroi
Strongs Number
G1359
Definition
from the alternate of G2203 and a form of the base of G2877; sons of Jupiter, i.e. the twins Dioscuri:--Castor and Pollux.
