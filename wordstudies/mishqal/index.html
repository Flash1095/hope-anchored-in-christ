mishqâl

weight (numerically estimated); hence, weighing (the act)

Biblical Usage

The word is a weight, the measure in shekels and talents, and it weighs the famous things: Goliath's coat of mail weighed five thousand shekels of brass, the crown of the Ammonite king weighed a talent of gold, and Solomon's yearly revenue was six hundred and sixty-six talents.

Theological Significance

I hold that the scales tell the story: the giant's armour was weighed and found wanting, the crown was taken from a conquered king, and the gold that weighed Solomon down was the beginning of his fall.
