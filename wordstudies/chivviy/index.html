Chivvîy

Word
חִוִּי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Chivvîy
Strongs Number
H2340
Definition
a Chivvite, one of the aboriginal tribes of Palestine
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Hivite = "villagers"; 1) 6th generation of descendants of Canaan, the son of Ham, who were living in northern Canaan near Mount Hermon at the time of the conquest
