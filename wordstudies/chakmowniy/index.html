Chakmôwnîy

Word
חַכְמוֹנִי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Chakmôwnîy
Strongs Number
H2453
Definition
Chakmoni, an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Hachmoni or Hachmonite = "wise"; n pr m; 1) father of one of David's mighty warriors; 2) father of the tutor of David's sons; n patr m; 3) the family of one of David's servants
