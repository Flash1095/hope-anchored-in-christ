Yardên

Jarden, the principal river of Palestine

Biblical Usage

Jordan is the great river of the land, the boundary Israel crossed to enter the promise. The towns on its far side were given to the Levites, and David remembered Shimei coming down to meet him at Jordan after the curse. The river marks the line between the wilderness and the promise, and every crossing of it is a new beginning.

Theological Significance

I believe the Jordan is the river of new beginnings, and the God who parted it once will part it again for His people.
