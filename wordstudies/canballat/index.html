Çanballaṭ

Word
סַנְבַלַּט
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Çanballaṭ
Strongs Number
H5571
Definition
Sanballat, a Persian satrap of Samaria
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Sanballat = "strength"; 1) a Moabite of Horonaim and the opponent of Nehemiah and the returned exiles in their goal of rebuilding the walls of Jerusalem
