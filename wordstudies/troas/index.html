Trōás

from Τρός (a Trojan); the Troad (or plain of Troy), i.e. Troas, a place in Asia Minor:--Troas.

Biblical Usage

Troas is the port where Paul's journeys turn. He came down to Troas and passed by Mysia, his companions tarried for him at Troas, and when he came to Troas to preach Christ's gospel a door was opened to him of the Lord.

Theological Significance

I believe Troas is where the mission bends: it is the harbour of the Macedonian call, the meeting place of the company, and the open door Paul walked through. I hold that God opens doors in ports as well as in pulpits, and Troas is the proof.
