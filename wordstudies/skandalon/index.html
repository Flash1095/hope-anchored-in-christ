skándalon

Word
σκάνδαλον
Language
Greek
Transliteration
skándalon
Strongs Number
G4625
Definition
("scandal"); probably from a derivative of G2578; a trap-stick (bent sapling), i.e. snare (figuratively, cause of displeasure or sin):--occasion to fall (of stumbling), offence, thing that offends, stumblingblock.
