mᵉshôwʼâh

(a) ruin, abstractly (the act) or concretely (the wreck)

Biblical Usage

Meshow'ah is ruin, desolation and waste, the wreck of a land. Job 30:3 has the solitary fleeing into the wilderness in former time desolate and waste, and Job 38:27 has God satisfy the desolate and waste ground and cause the bud of the tender herb to spring forth. Zephaniah 1:15 makes it the day itself, a day of wasteness and desolation, a day of darkness and gloominess.

Theological Significance

I believe desolation is not the last word: the same God who names the waste ground satisfies it with rain and makes the tender herb spring. My position is that ruin is real and judgment is real, and so is the watering that follows them both.
