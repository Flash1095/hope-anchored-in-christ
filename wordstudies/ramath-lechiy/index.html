Râmath Lechîy

Word
רָמַת לֶחִי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Râmath Lechîy
Strongs Number
H7437
Definition
Ramath-Lechi, a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Ramath-lehi = "height of a jawbone"; 1) a place by the rock Elam in northern Judah near the border of the Philistines; named by Samson after his slaughter of the 1000 Philistines with the jawbone of an ass
