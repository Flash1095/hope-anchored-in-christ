kâlâʼ

to restrict, by act (hold back or in) or word (prohibit)

Biblical Usage

Kala is to hold back — to restrain by act or by word. The rain from heaven was restrained after the flood; Moses had to restrain the people from bringing more for the tabernacle; and David says he has not refrained his lips from declaring God's righteousness, and has refrained his feet from every evil way.

Theological Significance

I believe restraint cuts both ways: God restrains the rain and the offering, and I must restrain my feet from evil while refusing to restrain my lips from His praise. The same hand that holds back the rain opens the mouth of praise, and I want both restraints in my life.
