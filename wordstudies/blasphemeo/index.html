blasphēméō

from G989; to vilify; specially, to speak impiously:--(speak) blaspheme(-er, -mously, -my), defame, rail on, revile, speak evil.

Biblical Usage

To blaspheme is to speak evil against God, to vilify him with words — and the word appears thirty-four times. In Matthew 9:3 the scribes say within themselves, this man blasphemeth, when Jesus forgives sins. In Matthew 26:65 the high priest rends his clothes and says, he hath spoken blasphemy. In Luke 12:10 Jesus warns that blasphemy against the Holy Ghost shall not be forgiven. And in Acts 13:45 the Jews contradict Paul and blaspheme.

Theological Significance

I believe blasphemy is the sin of calling God's work evil, and the scribes and the high priest show me how easily religious men do it.
