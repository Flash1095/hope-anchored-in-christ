Dᵉlâyâh

Delajah, the name of five Israelites

Biblical Usage

Delaiah is the name of five Israelites, and the word gathers them all: the priest of the fifth course, the son of Shemaiah who tried to stop Jeremiah, the companion of the return, and the men of the registers. One name, five men, all in the service of the house of God.

Theological Significance

I believe the name honours the many who serve without a story: five Delaiahs, and the Lord knows them all.
