gâram

Word
גָּרַם
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
gâram
Strongs Number
H1633
Definition
(causative) to bone, i.e. denude (by extensive, craunch) the bones
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to cut off, reserve, lay aside, leave, save; 1a) (Qal) to reserve; 2) (Piel) to break bones, gnaw bones, break
