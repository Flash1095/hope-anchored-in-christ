Yᵉhûwdâh

Jehudah (or Judah), the name of five Israelites; also of the tribe descended from the first, and of its territory

Biblical Usage

Judah is the name of five Israelites, and of the tribe and the territory that came from the first of them, the son of Jacob. In Genesis 29:35, therefore she called his name Judah. The fuller story is told on the Judah profile in Biblical People.

Theological Significance

I hold that Judah shows me God keeps His promises across generations, even through flawed families.
