apospáō

Word
ἀποσπάω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
apospáō
Strongs Number
G645
Definition
from G575 and G4685; to drag forth, i.e. (literally) unsheathe (a sword), or relatively (with a degree of force implied) retire (personally or factiously):--(with-)draw (away), after we were gotten from.
