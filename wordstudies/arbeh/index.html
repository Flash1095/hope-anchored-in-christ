ʼarbeh

a locust (from its rapid increase)

Biblical Usage

The locust is the plague that eats a land bare, and it multiplies faster than any army. God sends the locusts over Egypt, Joel says what the palmerworm left the locust has eaten, the psalm remembers that God spoke and the locusts came, and the Proverbs note that locusts have no king yet go forth all of them by bands.

Theological Significance

I hold that the locust is the smallest picture of the largest judgment. No king, no army, no plan, only a swarm that does what God says, and a land that cannot stop it.
