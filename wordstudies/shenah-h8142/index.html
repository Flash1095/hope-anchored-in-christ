shênâh

sleep

Biblical Usage

Sleep comes to Jacob on the hard ground at Bethel, and he wakes saying the LORD was in that place and he did not know it. Proverbs warns the sluggard who loves it too much, and the king of Persia finds his own sleep taken from him on the night Esther's story turns. Psalm 127 calls it a gift: God gives His beloved sleep.

Theological Significance

I believe sleep is one of God's plainest mercies, and also one of His plainest warnings. He gives rest to those who trust Him, and He takes it from those who will not rest in Him, so I take my sleep as something He governs, not something I control.
