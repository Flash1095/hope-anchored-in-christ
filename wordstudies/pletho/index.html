plḗthō

Word
πλήθω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
plḗthō
Strongs Number
G4130
Definition
; a prolonged form of a primary πλέω (which appears only as an alternate in certain tenses and in the reduplicated form πίμπλημι); to "fill" (literally or figuratively (imbue, influence, supply)); specially, to fulfil (time):--accomplish, full (…come), furnish.
