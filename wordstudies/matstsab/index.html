matstsâb

a fixed spot; figuratively, an office, a military post

Biblical Usage

A garrison is a military post, and the word is used where the post changes hands: Jonathan goes over to strike the Philistines' garrison, and the LORD tells Shebna that He will drive him from his station and pull him down from his state.

Theological Significance

I hold that a post is only as secure as the God who gives it: Jonathan takes a Philistine garrison by faith, and Shebna loses his own post by pride.
