pseudomártyr

from G5571 and a kindred form of G3144; a spurious witness, i.e. bearer of untrue testimony:--false witness.

Biblical Usage

Pseudomartyr means a false witness, one who bears untrue testimony. At the trial of Jesus, many false witnesses came, yet their testimony agreed not together. And Paul says that if the dead rise not, then we are found false witnesses of God, because we have testified that He raised up Christ.

Theological Significance

I believe the whole gospel stands or falls on this word: if Christ is not risen, the apostles are false witnesses, and if He is risen, their testimony is true.
