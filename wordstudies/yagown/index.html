yâgôwn

affliction

Biblical Usage

Jacob said, my son shall not go down with you, for his brother is dead, and if mischief befall him, ye shall bring down my gray hairs with sorrow to the grave. The month was turned from sorrow to joy for the Jews, the psalmist has sorrow in his heart daily, and the ransomed shall return with everlasting joy, and sorrow and sighing shall flee away.

Theological Significance

I hold that sorrow is the word that follows God's people through the whole story: Jacob's gray hairs, Esther's month turned to joy, the psalmist's daily sorrow, and the ransomed returning with joy. I believe sorrow is not the end of the story — it is the road the ransomed walk, and at the end of it sorrow itself flees away.
