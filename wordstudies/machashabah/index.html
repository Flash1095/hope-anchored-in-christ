machăshâbâh

a contrivance, i.e. (concretely) a texture, machine, or (abstractly) intention, plan (whether bad, a plot; or good, advice)

Biblical Usage

Machashabah is the thought, the plan, the device of the heart, and it runs both ways in Scripture. Genesis 6:5 says every imagination of the thoughts of man's heart was only evil continually, while Jeremiah 29:11 says God's thoughts toward His people are thoughts of peace and not of evil. Isaiah 55:8 draws the line between the two: God's thoughts are not our thoughts, and Proverbs 16:3 tells us to commit our works to the LORD so that our thoughts shall be established.

Theological Significance

I believe this word is a mirror: it shows me that thoughts are never neutral, they are either devices I hatch against God or plans He plants in me. My position is that the only safe thoughts are the ones handed over to Him first, because Jeremiah 29:11 promises His thoughts toward us are peace, and Proverbs 16:3 shows the way to share in them.
