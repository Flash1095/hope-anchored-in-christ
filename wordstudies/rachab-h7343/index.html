Râchâb

Word
רָחָב
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Râchâb
Strongs Number
H7343
Definition
Rachab, a Canaanitess
Part Of Speech
proper noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
Rahab = "wide"; 1) a harlot of Jericho who aided the spies to escape; saved from the destruction of Jericho; married Salmon, an ancestor of David and of Christ; commended for her faith in the book of James
