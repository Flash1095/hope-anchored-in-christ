dialogismós

from G1260; discussion, i.e. (internal) consideration (by implication, purpose), or (external) debate:--dispute, doubtful(-ing), imagination, reasoning, thought.

Biblical Usage

Thoughts are the reasonings of the heart, and the Gospels show them at work in friend and enemy alike. Jesus perceives the thoughts of the Pharisees, evil thoughts proceed from the heart to defile a man, and Paul tells the Philippians to do all things without disputing.

Theological Significance

I believe my thoughts are never private from God. He reads them as plainly as words, and the question is whether they are turned toward Him or curled inward on myself.
