Shimrôwn

Word
שִׁמְרוֹן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Shimrôwn
Strongs Number
H8110
Definition
Shimron, the name of an Israelite and of a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Shimron = "watch-height"; n pr m; 1) 4th son of Issachar and the progenitor of the family of Shimronites; n pr loc; 2) a Canaanite royal city allotted to Zebulun
