Shimrôwn

Shimron, the name of an Israelite and of a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Shimron is the son of Issachar named in the family that goes down into Egypt, and the head of the Shimronite family in the wilderness. He is also the Canaanite city whose king joins Jabin against Joshua.

Theological Significance

I believe one name can hold a son and a city, and both are counted: the son in the census and the city in the conquest. I hold that God writes the names of His people and the names of their enemies in the same book, and He knows which is which.
