Adramyttēnós

from Ἀδραμύττειον (a place in Asia Minor); Adramyttene or belonging to Adramyttium:--of Adramyttium.

Biblical Usage

Adramyttenos means 'of Adramyttium' — the ship Paul boarded at Caesarea was a ship of Adramyttium, a port on the coast of Asia Minor, bound for the ports along that coast. Luke names the ship's home the way a sailor would, and it is the only time the word appears; the place itself is covered in the Biblical Places collection.

Theological Significance

This word carries no doctrine — it is a ship's registry, not a sermon, and I won't make it one. Its value is the plain fact it anchors: Paul really sailed, on a real ship, from a real port, on his way to stand before Caesar.
