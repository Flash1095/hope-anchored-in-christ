châbal

Word
חָבַל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
châbal
Strongs Number
H2254
Definition
to wind tightly (as a rope), i.e. to bind; specifically, by a pledge; figuratively, to pervert, destroy; also to writhe in pain (especially of parturition)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to bind; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to bind; 2) to take a pledge, lay to pledge; 2a) (Qal) to hold by a pledge, take in pledge, hold in pledge; 2b) (Niphal) to give a pledge, become pledged; 3) to destroy, spoil, deal corruptly, offend; 3a) (Qal) to spoil, corrupt, offend; 3b) (Niphal) to be ruined; 3c) (Piel) to destroy, ruin; 3d) (Pual) to be ruined, be broken; 4) to bring forth, travail; 4a) (Piel) to writhe, twist, travail
