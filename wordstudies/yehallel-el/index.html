Yᵉhallelʼêl

Word
יְהַלֶּלְאֵל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Yᵉhallelʼêl
Strongs Number
H3094
Definition
Jehallelel, the name of two Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Jehaleleel or Jehalelel = "God is praised"; 1) a Calebite descendant of Judah; 2) father of Azariah and a Merarite Levite in the time of Hezekiah
