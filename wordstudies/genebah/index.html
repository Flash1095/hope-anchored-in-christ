gᵉnêbâh

stealing, i.e. (concretely) something stolen

Biblical Usage

gᵉnêbâh means thing stolen, theft. It turns up twice, in Exodus.

Theological Significance

I take gᵉnêbâh as it stands — thing stolen, theft — and read the passage with that plain sense in mind.
