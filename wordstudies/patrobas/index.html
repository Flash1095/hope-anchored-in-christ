Patrobâs

perhaps a contraction for Πατρόβιος (a compound of G3962 and G979); father's life; Patrobas, a Christian:--Patrobas.

Biblical Usage

Patrobas is a Christian at Rome, greeted once in Romans 16:14, where Paul sends greetings to Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermas, Patrobas, Hermes, and the brethren which are with them. His name stands among the brethren greeted at Rome, and the greeting is the whole of the story Scripture tells of him.

Theological Significance

I hold that a greeting in Romans is a kind of honour, and God keeps the names of believers whose whole story is that they were in the church at Rome.
