kavah

Word
קָוָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
kavah
Strongs Number
H6960
Definition
To wait for, hope in and expect — the tense, trusting waiting of a watchman or a taut cord stretched in anticipation.
Part Of Speech
verb
Key Passages
Psalm 27:14; Isaiah 40:31; Lamentations 3:25; Psalm 130:5
Biblical Usage
Those who kavah — who wait for the Lord — 'shall renew their strength' and mount up like eagles. The word's link to a stretched cord captures hope under tension that does not snap.
Theological Significance
Kavah dignifies waiting: biblical hope is not wishful thinking but strong, expectant trust. The cord holds because the One we wait for is faithful.
Related Topics
Hope; Perseverance; Trust
Related Books
Psalms; Isaiah; Lamentations
Related People
Isaiah; King David
