lehâbâh

{a flash; figuratively, a sharply polished blade or point of a weapon}

Biblical Usage

lehâbâh means a flash, and Scripture puts it to work in every part of life. In Isaiah 5:24, "and the flame consumeth the chaff"; and in Ezekiel 20:47, "and every dry tree: the flaming flame shall not be quenched". In Hosea 7:6, "in the morning it burneth as a flaming fire."; and in Daniel 11:33, "yet they shall fall by the sword, and by flame".

Theological Significance

I believe the flame is God's honest picture of judgment — it devours stubble, it burns in the forest, and it falls on the wise who instruct many. I hold that the same fire that destroys the chaff also lights the assemblies of Zion. My position is that I want to be the one the flame refines, not the one it consumes.
