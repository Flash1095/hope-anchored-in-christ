châshak

Word
חָשַׁךְ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
châshak
Strongs Number
H2821
Definition
to be dark (as withholding light); transitively, to darken
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to be or become dark, grow dim, be darkened, be black, be hidden; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to be or grow dark; 1a2) to have a dark colour; 1a3) to grow dim; 1b) (Hiphil); 1b1) to make dark, cause to be dark; 1b2) to hide, conceal; 1b3) to obscure, confuse (fig.)
