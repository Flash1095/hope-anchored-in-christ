Ioudaîos

from G2448 (in the sense of G2455 as a country); Judæan, i.e. belonging to Jehudah:--Jew(-ess), of Judæa.

Biblical Usage

A Jew is one of the people of Judah, and Paul uses the name for the people he came from and the people he preached to. In 1 Corinthians 1:22 the Jews require a sign, and the Greeks seek after wisdom, in 1 Corinthians 9:20 Paul says, unto the Jews I became as a Jew, that I might gain the Jews, and in 1 Corinthians 10:32 he says, give none offence, neither to the Jews, nor to the Gentiles, nor to the church of God.

Theological Significance

I believe Paul's heart for his own people runs through these verses: he became as a Jew to win Jews, and he would not offend them, or anyone, on the way.
