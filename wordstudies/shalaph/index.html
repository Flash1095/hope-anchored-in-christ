shâlaph

to pull out, up or off

Biblical Usage

To draw a sword is the commonest use of this word, and it runs through the battles of Israel. David drew Goliath's sword out of its sheath and slew him with it, Joshua met a man with a drawn sword who called himself captain of the host of the LORD, and the kinsman at the gate drew off his shoe to confirm the redemption of Ruth's field.

Theological Significance

I hold that a drawn sword tells the truth about a man, whether he draws it for God or for himself.
