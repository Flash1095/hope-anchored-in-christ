móschos

probably strengthened for όσχος (a shoot); a young bullock:--calf.

Biblical Usage

Moschos is a young calf — the father kills the fatted calf for the son who was dead and is alive again, and the blood of calves and goats could never take away sins, but Christ entered once into the holy place with His own blood.

Theological Significance

I believe the calf carries both stories: the feast of a father who forgives, and the blood that could never finish the work.
