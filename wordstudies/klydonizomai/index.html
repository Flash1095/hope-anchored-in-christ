klydōnízomai

middle voice from G2830; to surge, i.e. (figuratively) to fluctuate:--toss to and fro.

Biblical Usage

Klydonizomai means to be tossed to and fro like a wave. Ephesians 4:14 warns that we be no more children, tossed to and fro, and carried about with every wind of doctrine, by the sleight of men, and cunning craftiness, whereby they lie in wait to deceive.

Theological Significance

I believe the tossing is the danger of childhood in the faith: the wind of doctrine carries the child, and only the truth in love holds him steady.
