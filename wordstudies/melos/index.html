mélos

of uncertain affinity; a limb or part of the body:--member.

Biblical Usage

Melos is a member, a limb or part of the body, and Paul makes it the picture of the church. 1 Corinthians 6:15 asks whether our bodies are the members of Christ, and Colossians 3:5 commands us to mortify our members which are upon the earth. Ephesians 4:25 says we are members one of another, and James 3:5 warns that the tongue is a little member that boasts great things.

Theological Significance

I believe the body is the honest picture of the church: no member stands alone, and a small one can set the whole on fire. My position is that what I do with my members is what I do with Christ's, and that thought should steady my tongue.
