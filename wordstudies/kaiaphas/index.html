Kaïáphas

of Chaldee origin; the dell; Caiaphas (i.e. Cajepha), an Israelite:--Caiaphas.

Biblical Usage

Caiaphas was the high priest who judged Jesus, and his name is tied to the night of the trial. It was he who said it was expedient that one man should die for the people, and it was to his house that Jesus was led bound after Gethsemane.

Theological Significance

I believe Caiaphas shows how a true word can be spoken by a false heart. He prophesied that one man would die for the people, and he meant it as politics — but God meant it as the gospel, and the cross turned his sentence into salvation.
