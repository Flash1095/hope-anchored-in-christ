çâbal

Word
סָבַל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
çâbal
Strongs Number
H5445
Definition
to carry (literally or figuratively), or (reflexively) be burdensome; specifically, to be gravid
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to bear, bear a load, drag oneself along; 1a) (Qal) to bear (a load); 1b) (Pual) laden (participle); 1c)(Hithpael) to make oneself a burden, drag oneself along
