poimḗn

of uncertain affinity; a shepherd (literally or figuratively):--shepherd, pastor.

Biblical Usage

Poimen is a shepherd, the one who keeps the flock. Jesus says, I am the good shepherd, in John 10:11, and the good shepherd gives His life for the sheep. The sheep without a shepherd moved Him with compassion in Matthew 9:36 and Mark 6:34, and God raised up Jesus as the great shepherd of the sheep in Hebrews 13:20. Peter calls Him the Shepherd and Bishop of our souls in 1 Peter 2:25.

Theological Significance

I believe the shepherd is the whole gospel in one word — He knows His sheep by name, and He lays down His life for them. My position is that I am safe because my Shepherd is good, not because I am.
