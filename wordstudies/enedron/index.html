énedron

neuter of the same as G1747; an ambush, i.e. (figuratively) murderous design:--lying in wait.

Biblical Usage

The same ambush as its sister word: in Acts 25:3 the Jews ask Festus to bring Paul to Jerusalem, laying wait in the way to kill him — the ambush laid in the road, and the plot never reaches Paul.

Theological Significance

I believe God lets us see the ambush in Scripture so we will not be surprised by our own — the road to Jerusalem is still watched, and the Lord still watches the road.
