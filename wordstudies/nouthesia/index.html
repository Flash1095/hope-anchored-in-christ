nouthesía

from G3563 and a derivative of G5087; calling attention to, i.e. (by implication) mild rebuke or warning:--admonition.

Biblical Usage

Nouthesia is admonition, the kind of warning that puts a truth back into someone's mind before they forget it. Paul says the history of Israel was written for our admonition in 1 Corinthians 10:11, and he tells fathers to bring their children up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord in Ephesians 6:4. Titus 3:10 shows the harder edge: after a first and second admonition, a divisive man is to be rejected.

Theological Significance

I hold that admonition is love with its eyes open, because it would rather warn a brother now than watch him wander later. My position is that a church which never warns has stopped caring, and a home which never corrects has stopped raising its children.
