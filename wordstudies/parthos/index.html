Párthos

probably of foreign origin; a Parthian, i.e. inhabitant of Parthia:--Parthian.

Biblical Usage

Parthos is a Parthian, an inhabitant of Parthia. The Parthians are named in Acts 2:9 among the peoples who hear the apostles speak in their own tongues on the day of Pentecost: Parthians, and Medes, and Elamites, and the dwellers in Mesopotamia.

Theological Significance

I hold that the Parthians heard the gospel in their own tongue on the first day it was preached to the nations, and no people is too far from Jerusalem for the Spirit to reach.
