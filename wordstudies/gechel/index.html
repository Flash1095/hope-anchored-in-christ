gechel

an ember

Biblical Usage

gechel is the word for coal, burning coal, coals of fire, hot coals. I meet it 18 times across the Bible, and Leviticus is a good place to see it plainly. It says what it says, and I take it at face value.

Theological Significance

What gechel tells me is that God speaks plainly, and plain words carry real weight. What I take from it is this — the plain sense is the safe sense.
