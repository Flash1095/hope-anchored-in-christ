ʼAchyôw

Achio, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Ahio drove the new cart carrying the ark of God from the house of Abinadab, walking before it with Uzzah his brother, on the day David learned that good intentions do not make an unholy way holy.

Theological Significance

Ahio himself is not judged in the text, and I will not judge him either. But the scene is a sober one: the ark was to be carried on priests' shoulders, not hauled on a cart, and the cost of forgetting that fell on the man beside him.
