mᵉrôrâh

properly, bitterness; concretely, a bitter thing; specifically bile; also venom (of a serpent)

Biblical Usage

Merorah is bitterness itself, gall and venom, the bitter thing in the body and in the soul. Deuteronomy 32:32 says the vine of Sodom bears grapes of gall, and Job 13:26 says God writest bitter things against me. Job 20:14 turns a rich man's meat to the gall of asps within him, and Job 20:25 brings the glittering sword out of his gall, with terrors upon him.

Theological Significance

I believe gall is what sin turns into inside a man: sweet going down, poison in the bowels, and terror at the end. My position is that Job's bitter things are not God's cruelty but the truth about what his accusers' plenty really was.
