mâqôwr

Word
מָקוֹר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
mâqôwr
Strongs Number
H4726
Definition
properly, something dug, i.e. a (general) source (of water, even when naturally flowing; also of tears, blood (by euphemism, of the female pudenda); figuratively, of happiness, wisdom, progeny)
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) spring, fountain; 1a) spring; 1a1) of source of life, joy, purification (fig.); 1b) of the eye (fig.); 1c) source (of menstruous blood); 1d) flow (of blood after child birth)
