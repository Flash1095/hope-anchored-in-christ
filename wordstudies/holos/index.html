hólos

a primary word; "whole" or "all", i.e. complete (in extent, amount, time or degree), especially (neuter) as noun or adverb:--all, altogether, every whit, + throughout, whole.

Biblical Usage

hólos means all, altogether, every whit, + throughout, whole. It occurs 96 times in all, and the passages I have picked — Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John — show it in its plain sense. I read it as a word that does its work quietly, without dressing anything up.

Theological Significance

What hólos tells me is that God wants the whole of us, not a part. I hold that reading it plainly keeps me honest about what God actually said.
