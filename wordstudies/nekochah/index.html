nᵉkôchâh

properly, straightforwardness, i.e. (figuratively) integrity, or (concretely) a truth

Biblical Usage

Amos says the violent know not how to do right, and Isaiah says the wicked will not learn righteousness even when favour is shown them. The people tell the prophets to speak smooth things, and truth falls in the street while justice stands far off.

Theological Significance

I believe uprightness is the test of the heart: the violent cannot do right, the wicked will not learn it, and the people who ask for smooth words have already turned truth out of the street. God calls the straight thing by its name, and I must walk in it.
