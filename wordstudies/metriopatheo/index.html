metriopathéō

from a compound of the base of G3357 and G3806; to be moderate in passion, i.e. gentle (to treat indulgently):--have compassion.

Biblical Usage

The high priest can have compassion on the ignorant and on those out of the way, because he himself is compassed with infirmity. The word is a measured gentleness, a middle path between harshness and softness, and Hebrews makes it the fitness of a priest.

Theological Significance

I believe a priest who has never been weak cannot be gentle with the weak: Christ's priesthood is compassionate because He knows infirmity from the inside.
