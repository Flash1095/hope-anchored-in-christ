ʻAmrâm

Amram, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Amram was the son of Kohath and the father of Aaron and Moses: he took Jochebed his father's sister to wife, and she bore him Aaron and Moses, and Miriam their sister, and the genealogies name him twice over — in Exodus and in Numbers — with his years counted at a hundred and thirty-seven. There is a second Amram, a son of Bani who had taken a foreign wife in Ezra's day, but the father of Moses is the Amram the Bible remembers.

Theological Significance

Amram's whole story is his children: Aaron the priest, Moses the deliverer, Miriam the prophetess. I believe that is how God often works — a faithful father whose name is carried by what his children do, and whose house shaped the whole history of Israel.
