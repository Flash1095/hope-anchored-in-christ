Miryâm

Mirjam, the name of two Israelitesses

Biblical Usage

Miriam is the prophetess, the sister of Aaron and Moses, and her name runs through the whole wilderness story. She took a timbrel and led the women in song at the sea, and she spoke against Moses and was struck leprous for seven days, shut out of the camp until she was received again.

Theological Significance

I believe Miriam shows both sides of leadership: the song at the sea is the high point, and the leprosy is the warning that even a prophetess must not speak against God's chosen servant.
