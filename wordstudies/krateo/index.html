kratéō

from G2904; to use strength, i.e. seize or retain (literally or figuratively):--hold (by, fast), keep, lay hand (hold) on, obtain, retain, take (by).

Biblical Usage

Krateo is the word for holding on with strength, and it covers everything from taking someone by the hand to holding fast to the truth. In Hebrews 4:14 we are told to "hold fast our profession," in 2 Thessalonians 2:15 to "hold the traditions" taught by the apostles, in John 20:23 the apostles are given power to retain sins, and in Revelation 2:13 the church at Pergamos is praised because it held fast Christ's name.

Theological Significance

I hold that the Christian life is largely a holding: holding fast the confession, holding the traditions once delivered, and holding on to Christ Himself when the world pulls the other way. What I hold fast to holds me, and that is why I will not let go.
