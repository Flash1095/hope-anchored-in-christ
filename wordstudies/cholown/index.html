Chôlôwn

Word
חֹלוֹן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Chôlôwn
Strongs Number
H2473
Definition
Cholon, the name of two places in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Holon = "sandy"; 1) a town in the mountains of Judah given to the priests; 2) a city of Moab; site unknown
