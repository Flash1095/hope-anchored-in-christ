Ṭâphath

Taphath, an Israelitess

Biblical Usage

Taphath was Solomon's daughter, married to Ben-Abinadab, one of the twelve officers who provided food for the king's household. Her name means ornament, and she stands in the list of Solomon's court as a daughter given in marriage to a trusted officer.

Theological Significance

I believe the daughters of kings matter to God as much as the officers, and Taphath's place in the record is no accident.
