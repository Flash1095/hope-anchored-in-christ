Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻĂnâthôwth
Strongs Number
H6068
Definition
Anathoth, the name of two Israelites, also of a place in Pal
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Anathoth = "answers to prayer"; n pr m; 1) son of Becher and grandson of Benjamin; 2) one of the heads of the people who signed the covenant with Nehemiah; n pr loc; 3) a city of Benjamin allotted to the priest; located approximately 3 miles (5 km) from Jerusalem; birthplace of the prophet Jeremiah
