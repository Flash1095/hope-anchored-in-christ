ʼêmer

something said

Biblical Usage

Emer is simply something said — a word, a saying, a promise, a command. Moses opens his song with it in Deuteronomy 32:1, calling heaven and earth to hear the words of his mouth, and Jacob's blessing gives Naphtali 'goodly words' in Genesis 49:21. But words cut both ways: Hosea says God hewed His people by the words of His mouth in Hosea 6:5, and Isaiah warns of lying words that destroy the poor in Isaiah 32:7.

Theological Significance

I believe words are never just sounds — God's words create and judge, and our words bless or destroy. That is why I want my own words weighed before they are spoken, because Scripture treats a word as a deed with consequences.
