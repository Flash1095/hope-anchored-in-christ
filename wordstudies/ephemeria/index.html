ephēmería

from G2184; diurnality, i.e. (specially) the quotidian rotation or class of the Jewish priests' service at the Temple, as distributed by families:--course.

Biblical Usage

Ephemeria is a course, a turn of service — the daily rotation of the priests at the temple. Zacharias was of the course of Abia, and it was his turn to burn incense when the angel came with the news of John.

Theological Significance

The ordinary turn of duty is where God often meets us, I believe: Zacharias was simply doing his course when heaven broke in.
