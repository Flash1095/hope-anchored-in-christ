hypobállō

from G5259 and G906; to throw in stealthily, i.e. introduce by collusion:--suborn.

Biblical Usage

hypobállō means to suborn, to bribe into false witness. In Acts 6:11, the text says "Then they suborned men."

Theological Significance

For me, hypobállō means to suborn, to bribe into false witness, and I leave it at that.
