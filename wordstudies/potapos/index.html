potapós

apparently from G4219 and the base of G4226; interrogatively, whatever, i.e. of what possible sort:--what (manner of).

Biblical Usage

Potapos asks what manner of, of what possible sort. The disciples ask it in the storm, what manner of man is this that even the winds and the sea obey him, and John asks it in wonder, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us.

Theological Significance

I believe wonder is the right answer to both questions, because no category we own is big enough for him.
