Yᵉhôwshâphâṭ

Word
יְהוֹשָׁפָט
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Yᵉhôwshâphâṭ
Strongs Number
H3092
Definition
Jehoshaphat, the name of six Israelites; also of a valley near Jerusalem
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Jehoshaphat = "Jehovah has judged"; n pr m; 1) son of king Asa and himself king of Judah for 25 years; one of the best, most pious, and prosperous kings of Judah; 2) son of Nimshi and father of king Jehu of the northern kingdom of Israel; 3) son of Ahilud and chronicler under David and Solomon; 4) son of Paruah and one of the 12 commissary officers under Solomon; 5) a priest and trumpeter in the time of David; n pr loc; 6) symbolical name of a valley near Jerusalem which is the place of ultimate judgment; maybe the deep ravine which separates Jerusalem from the Mount of Olives through which the Kidron flowed
