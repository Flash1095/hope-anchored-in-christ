zâkûwr

a male (of man or animals)

Biblical Usage

zâkûwr means a male (of man or animals). In Exodus 23:17 we read, "Three times in the year all thy males shall appear before the Lord GOD." It appears again in Exodus 34:23: "Thrice in the year shall all your men children appear before the Lord GOD, the God of Israel."

Theological Significance

zakuwr is a male (of man or animals), and I believe God put it in his book for a reason, so I want to hear what it says.
