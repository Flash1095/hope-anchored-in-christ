splánchnon

Word
σπλάγχνον
Language
Greek
Transliteration
splánchnon
Strongs Number
G4698
Definition
probably strengthened from σπλήν (the "spleen"); an intestine (plural); figuratively, pity or sympathy:--bowels, inward affection, + tender mercy.
