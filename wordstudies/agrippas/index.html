Agríppas

apparently from G66 and G2462; wild-horse tamer; Agrippas, one of the Herods:--Agrippa.

Biblical Usage

Agrippa — Herod Agrippa II, the last of the Herods — came to Caesarea with Bernice to greet Festus, and stayed to hear Paul. Paul told him the whole gospel, from the light on the Damascus road to the resurrection, and Agrippa answered, 'Almost thou persuadest me to be a Christian.'.

Theological Significance

I believe Agrippa's 'almost' is the saddest word in Acts. He heard the gospel from Paul himself, he knew it was true, and he walked away a king who was almost persuaded — and almost is not enough, because the gospel demands a yes, not an almost.
