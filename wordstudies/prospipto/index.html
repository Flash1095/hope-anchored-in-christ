prospíptō

from G4314 and G4098; to fall towards, i.e. (gently) prostrate oneself (in supplication or homage), or (violently) to rush upon (in storm):--beat upon, fall (down) at (before).

Biblical Usage

Prospipto means to fall toward, to fall down before someone. The woman with the issue of blood came fearing and trembling, and fell down before Jesus and told Him all the truth. Simon Peter fell down at Jesus' knees, saying, Depart from me, for I am a sinful man, O Lord, and the jailer at Philippi came trembling, and fell down before Paul and Silas.

Theological Significance

I hold that falling down is the body's confession: the healed woman, the sinful fisherman, and the frightened jailer all fell in the same place, at the feet of the One who could save.
