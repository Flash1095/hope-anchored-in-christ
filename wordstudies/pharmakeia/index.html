pharmakeía

from G5332; medication ("pharmacy"), i.e. (by extension) magic (literally or figuratively):--sorcery, witchcraft.

Biblical Usage

Pharmakeia is sorcery, the use of drugs and spells, the word behind our pharmacy, and Scripture names it among the works of the flesh. Paul lists it beside idolatry and hatred and strife, as a deed of the flesh that keeps men out of the kingdom of God. And in the fall of Babylon, the light of the candle shall shine no more in her, because by her sorceries were all nations deceived.

Theological Significance

I believe pharmakeia is not a harmless curiosity: it is the attempt to get power without God, and it ends in deception that swallows whole nations. My position is that the church has no business with spells and potions, because the Spirit gives power freely, and sorcery only ever sells a counterfeit.
