rᵉʼêm

a wild bull (from its conspicuousness)

Biblical Usage

God brought Israel out of Egypt, and he hath as it were the strength of the wild ox. Job is asked whether the wild ox will be willing to serve him or abide by his crib, and the psalmist sings that God will exalt his horn like the horn of the wild ox.

Theological Significance

I believe the wild ox stands for strength no man can tame, and God alone holds it in His hand.
