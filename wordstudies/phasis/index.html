phásis

from G5346 (not the same as "phase", which is from G5316); a saying, i.e. report:--tidings.

Biblical Usage

Phasis is a saying, a report, tidings, and it appears once, in the riot at Jerusalem. As the crowd goes about to kill Paul, tidings come to the chief captain of the band that all Jerusalem is in an uproar.

Theological Significance

I believe the tidings that saved Paul were God's timing in a sentence: a report reaches the right ears at the right moment, and a life is spared.
