zṓnē

probably akin to the base of G2218; a belt; by implication, a pocket:--girdle, purse.

Biblical Usage

Zone is a belt or girdle — John the Baptist wore a leather one in the wilderness, and the prophet Agabus took Paul's belt and bound his own hands and feet with it to show what awaited Paul in Jerusalem. A belt is the working man's gear, and in Acts it becomes a prophecy.

Theological Significance

I believe a simple belt can carry a heavy message: John's said repent, and Agabus's said that following Christ would cost Paul his freedom.
