kerameús

from G2766; a potter:--potter.

Biblical Usage

Kerameus means a potter, one who shapes clay. Matthew 27:7 says they bought with the thirty pieces the potter's field, to bury strangers in. Matthew 27:10 says, and gave them for the potter's field, as the Lord appointed me. Romans 9:21 asks, hath not the potter power over the clay, of the same lump to make one vessel unto honour, and another unto dishonour?

Theological Significance

I believe the potter's field is the price of betrayal turned into a burial ground for strangers, and the potter's power is the answer to every question about God's justice. I hold that I am the clay, not the potter, and the lump belongs to him.
