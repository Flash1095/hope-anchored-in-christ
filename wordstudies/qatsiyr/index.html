qâtsîyr

severed, i.e. harvest (as reaped), the crop, the time, the reaper, or figuratively; also a limb (of a tree, or simply foliage)

Biblical Usage

Harvest is the feast and the law and the promise: thou shalt keep the feast of harvest, the firstfruits of thy labours; when thou cuttest down thine harvest in thy field, and hast forgot a sheaf, thou shalt not go again to fetch it, it shall be for the stranger; while the earth remaineth, seedtime and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night shall not cease. They of Beth-shemesh were reaping their wheat harvest in the valley when the ark came back.

Theological Significance

I believe the harvest is where God's promise meets man's work, because He guarantees the seasons and commands the gleaning. What holds me is that the forgotten sheaf is not waste, it is provision for the stranger, and the harvest that never ceases is the sign of a covenant that never fails.
