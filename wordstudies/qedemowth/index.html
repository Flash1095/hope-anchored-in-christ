Qᵉdêmôwth

Definition
Kedemoth, a place in eastern Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Kedemoth = "eastern"; 1) one of the towns in the district east of the Dead Sea allotted to the tribe of Reuben and given to the Merarite Levites
