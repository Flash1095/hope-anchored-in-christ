Mîykâh

Micah, the name of seven Israelites

Biblical Usage

Micah is the name of seven Israelites, and the three that matter most could hardly differ more. One was the man of Mount Ephraim who made an ephod and teraphim, set up a house of gods, and hired a Levite for his shrine, one was the son of Mephibosheth, and one was the prophet Micah the Morasthite, who prophesied in the days of Hezekiah that Zion would be plowed as a field.

Theological Significance

I hold that the name is a warning in itself: the same name belongs to a prophet and to an idol-maker, and what matters is not the name but the God served.
