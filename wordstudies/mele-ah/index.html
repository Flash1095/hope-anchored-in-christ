mᵉlêʼâh

something fulfilled, i.e. abundance (of produce)

Biblical Usage

Mele'ah is fulness, the full produce of a field or a harvest. 2 Samuel 23:11 sets it in a field full of lentiles where Shammah stood his ground, and Amos 2:13 pictures God pressed under His people as a cart pressed full of sheaves.

Theological Significance

I believe a full field is a gift and a weight at once: plenty that God gives, and plenty His people can crush Him under by their sin.
