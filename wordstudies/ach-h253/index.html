ʼâch

Oh! (expressive of grief or surprise)

Biblical Usage

Ach is a cry, not a word with a definition you can pin down — it appears only twice, both times in Ezekiel, both times as "Alas" or "Ah!" In Ezekiel 6:11 the prophet is told to stamp his foot and cry it over Israel's abominations; in Ezekiel 21:15 it breaks out over the glittering sword drawn for judgment. It is the sound a man makes when words run out.

Theological Significance

A gasp carries no doctrine, and the weight here sits in Ezekiel's grief over the judgment he had to announce, not in the cry itself.
