qôbâh

the abdomen (as a cavity)

Biblical Usage

Phinehas goes after the man of Israel into the tent and thrusts both of them through, the man and the woman through her belly, and the plague is stayed. The word is the abdomen, the cavity itself.

Theological Significance

I believe the spear that stayed the plague went through the belly, and the word names the place of the wound.
