diátagma

from G1299; an arrangement, i.e. (authoritative) edict:--commandment.

Biblical Usage

A command is the word of a king, and the word stands once, for the commandment of the king of Egypt, whose word was law. Moses' parents hid him by faith, not fearing the king's commandment.

Theological Significance

I hold that the king's commandment in Hebrews is the test of faith; the parents of Moses feared God more than Pharaoh, and the child they hid became the deliverer of a nation.
