ámpelos

probably from the base of G297 and that of G257; a vine (as coiling about a support):--vine.

Biblical Usage

Jesus says, 'I am the true vine, and my Father is the husbandman,' and then, 'I am the vine, ye are the branches: he that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing.' At the last supper He says He will not drink again of the fruit of the vine until He drinks it new with His disciples in His Father's kingdom. The vine is not a picture of trying harder — it is a picture of staying joined to Him.

Theological Significance

I hold that this is the whole Christian life in one image: I do not produce fruit by effort, I bear it by abiding. Cut off from the vine, a branch does nothing; joined to it, it cannot help but bear. And the promise at the table still stands — there is a day coming when He drinks the fruit of the vine new with us, and that is the hope I live toward.
