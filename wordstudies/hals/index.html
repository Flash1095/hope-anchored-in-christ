háls

a primary word; "salt":--salt.

Biblical Usage

Salt keeps food from rotting and gives it flavour, and Jesus tells his disciples that is what they are meant to be. In Matthew 5:13 he calls them the salt of the earth, in Mark 9:50 he tells them to have salt in themselves and be at peace with one another, and in Luke 14:34 he warns that salt without its savour is fit for nothing.

Theological Significance

I hold that a disciple who has lost the saltiness has lost the very thing that made them useful, and Jesus does not soften that warning.
