Word

Language

Transliteration
ʻĂmôrâh

Definition
Amorah, a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Gomorrah = "submersion"; 1) the twin-city in evil with Sodom, both destroyed in judgment by God with fire from heaven; 1a) of iniquity (fig.)
