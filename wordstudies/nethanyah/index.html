Nᵉthanyâh

Nethanjah, the name of four Israelites

Biblical Usage

Nethaniah is another name shared by four men, and the picks show three of them at work. One is the father of Ishmael who murders Gedaliah, one is a son of Asaph who prophesies with music under the king's order, and one is a Levite Jehoshaphat sends out to teach.

Theological Significance

I hold that the same name can carry a murderer, a musician, and a teacher — and God judges each man by his own deeds, not his name.
