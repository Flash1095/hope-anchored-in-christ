perissóteros

comparative of G4053; more superabundant (in number, degree or character):--more abundant, greater (much) more, overmuch.

Biblical Usage

The comparative perissoteros means more abundant, greater. Paul says the members of the body we think less honourable receive more abundant honour, and the forgiven brother must be comforted, lest he be swallowed up with overmuch sorrow.

Theological Significance

I am convinced that God gives more honour where men give less: the uncomely parts are clothed with abundance, and the penitent is comforted, not crushed.
