ʼabdân

a perishing

Biblical Usage

This noun for perishing or destruction occurs once, in Esther 9:5: the Jews 'smote all their enemies with the stroke of the sword, and slaughter and destruction, and did what they would unto those that hated them.' It is the word for the ruin that fell on those who had planned ruin for the Jews.

Theological Significance

One verse turns the tables — the destruction Haman planned for the Jews falls on his own party — and I believe Esther’s reversal is the pattern of God’s justice.
