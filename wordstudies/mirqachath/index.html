mirqachath

an aromatic unguent; also an unguent-pot

Biblical Usage

The word is the compounding of ointment, the apothecary's art, and it belongs to the holy anointing oil and to the sweet spices. The oil was compounded after the art of the apothecary, the priests' sons made the ointments, and Asa was laid in a bed filled with spices prepared by the same art.

Theological Significance

I believe the compounding shows care: holy things are not thrown together, they are measured and mixed by skilled hands, and the same care goes into honouring the dead.
