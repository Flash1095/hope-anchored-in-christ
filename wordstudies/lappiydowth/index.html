Lappîydôwth

Lappidoth, the husband of Deborah

Biblical Usage

Lapidoth appears once, as the husband of Deborah in Judges 4:4. Deborah, a prophetess, judged Israel at that time, and nothing more is said of him, only that he was her husband.

Theological Significance

I hold that the one verse is enough for a husband: he is named beside his wife, and the wife God raised up to judge Israel was his, and the record honours him by naming him.
