Bôʻaz

Boaz, the ancestor of David; also the name of a pillar in front of the temple

Biblical Usage

Boaz means fleetness, and he is the kinsman-redeemer of Ruth, the ancestor of David. In Ruth 2:1 he is introduced as a mighty man of wealth, of the kindred of Elimelech. In Ruth 2:8 he tells Ruth to abide by his maidens and glean in his field. In Ruth 4:9 he buys all that was Elimelech's before the elders, and takes Ruth to wife. And 1 Kings 7:21 names the other Boaz: the left pillar of Solomon's temple, set up and called Boaz.

Theological Significance

I believe Boaz is the picture of Christ in the Old Testament: a redeemer with the wealth to pay, the will to do it, and the welcome for a foreigner who had nothing.
