yaʻălâh

{an ibex (as climbing)}

Biblical Usage

Let her be as the loving hind and pleasant roe, says the proverb of a wife, let her breasts satisfy thee at all times, and be thou ravished always with her love.

Theological Significance

I believe the roe is chosen because it is sure-footed and free: the proverb does not picture a caged love but one that climbs and delights, and a husband is told to be ravished by his own wife, not by another's.
