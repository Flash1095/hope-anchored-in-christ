Baʻălêy Bâmôwth

Baale-Bamoth, a place East of the Jordan

Biblical Usage

Baale-bamoth is a compound name — the high places of Baal — and it comes to us as two words, Bamoth and Baal, joined together: Joshua lists Bamoth-baal among the cities of Reuben on the plain, and Numbers tells how Balak brought Balaam up to the high places of Baal to see the camp of Israel. The name is a place and a warning at once.

Theological Significance

I believe the high places of Baal are the picture of worship gone up the hill and away from God, and Balaam standing on them to curse Israel is the picture of how close cursing can come to blessing.
