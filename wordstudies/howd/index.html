hôwd

grandeur (i.e. an imposing form and appearance)

Biblical Usage

hôwd is a plain word for majesty, splendour, grandeur. In Numbers 27:20, the text says "And thou shalt put [some] of thine honour upon him." In 1 Chronicles 16:27, the text says "Glory and honour [are] in his presence."

Theological Significance

Reading hôwd this way, I believe glory is the weight of who God is, and it is the end of everything He does. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
