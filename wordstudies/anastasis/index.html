anástasis

from G450; a standing up again, i.e. (literally) a resurrection from death (individual, genitive case or by implication, (its author)), or (figuratively) a (moral) recovery (of spiritual truth):--raised to life again, resurrection, rise from the dead, that should rise, rising again.

Biblical Usage

Jesus told Martha, 'I am the resurrection, and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live.' Paul says that as by man came death, by man came also the resurrection of the dead, and the apostles gave witness of the resurrection of the Lord Jesus with great power. He adds that if we have been planted together in the likeness of His death, we shall be also in the likeness of His resurrection. The resurrection is not only something that happened to Jesus — it is something that will happen to us, in His likeness.

Theological Significance

I believe the resurrection is the whole Christian hope in one word: Christ rose, so death is not the end, and I will rise in His likeness because I have been planted together with Him. It is not a doctrine I hold at arm's length — it is the reason I can face death without fear, and the reason the apostles preached with great power when everything was against them.
