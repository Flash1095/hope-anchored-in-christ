Kilyôwn

Kiljon, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Chilion is one of the two sons of Elimelech and Naomi. Ruth 1:2 names Mahlon and Chilion, Ephrathites of Bethlehem-judah, who came into the country of Moab, and Ruth 1:5 says both sons died there, leaving Naomi a widow of her two sons and her husband.

Theological Significance

I believe Chilion's short life is the hinge of Ruth: his death in Moab leaves Naomi empty, and her emptiness is what brings Ruth to Bethlehem and to Boaz.
