ʼăchuzzâh

something seized, i.e. a possession (especially of land)

Biblical Usage

A possession — achuzzah is what is seized and held, especially land. God promised Abraham the land of Canaan "for an everlasting possession"; Abraham bought a burial plot, saying, "give me a possession of a buryingplace"; and at the jubilee "ye shall return every man unto his possession." The word holds both gift and purchase, promise and homecoming.

Theological Significance

The land was God's gift held in trust, and I hold that as a picture of everything we truly own: it is given, not taken, and it returns to its rightful owner in the end. Our true possession is the Lord Himself.
