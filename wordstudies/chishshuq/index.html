chishshuq

conjoined, i.e. a wheel-spoke or rod connecting the hub with the rim

Biblical Usage

Chishshuq is a spoke, the rod that joins the hub of a wheel to its rim, and it stands once in the description of Solomon's temple. The wheels of the ten bases are made like a chariot wheel, with their axletrees, felloes, spokes and naves all cast in one piece.

Theological Significance

I hold that even the spokes of the temple were made with care, because nothing in God's house is careless work.
