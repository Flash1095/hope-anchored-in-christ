ʼôwtsâr

a depository

Biblical Usage

The treasury is where the dedicated things are kept. The Levites were over the treasuries of the house of God, and Solomon brought in the things his father had dedicated, the silver and the gold and the vessels, into the treasuries of the house of the Lord.

Theological Significance

I hold that the treasuries of the house of God are the proof that God's people give: Solomon brought in the silver and the gold and the vessels, and the Levites kept them.
