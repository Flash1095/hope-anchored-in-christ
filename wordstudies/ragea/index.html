râgêaʻ

restful, i.e. peaceable

Biblical Usage

Rage'a means restful, peaceable — the quiet ones. David says of his enemies, 'For they speak not peace: but they devise deceitful matters against them that are quiet in the land.'

Theological Significance

What I see here is that the quiet in the land are God's own: they speak peace, and the deceitful speak against them — and God hears both.
