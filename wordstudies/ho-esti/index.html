hó esti

from the neuter of G3739 and the third person singular present indicative of G1510; which is:--called, which is (make), that is (to say).

Biblical Usage

Hó esti is two words together — ὅ, “which”, and ἐστί, “is” — “which is”, the little phrase that explains a name. It stands 29 times in the Greek New Testament; Matthew and Mark show it in use.

Theological Significance

My position is simple here: hó esti means “which is”, and I do not stretch it further.
