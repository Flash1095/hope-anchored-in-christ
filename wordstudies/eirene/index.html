eirene

Word
εἰρήνη
Language
Greek
Transliteration
eirene
Strongs Number
G1515
Definition
Peace — harmony, well-being and reconciliation with God, translating the wholeness of Hebrew shalom into the New Testament.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
John 14:27; Romans 5:1; Philippians 4:7; Ephesians 2:14
Biblical Usage
Eirene flows from justification: 'having been justified by faith, we have peace with God.' Christ Himself 'is our peace,' leaving His own peace with His disciples on the night before the cross.
Theological Significance
Eirene is not the world's fragile calm. It is Christ's own peace guarding hearts and minds — peace with God that becomes the peace of God.
