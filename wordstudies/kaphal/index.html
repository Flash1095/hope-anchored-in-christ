kâphal

to fold together; figuratively, to repeat

Biblical Usage

Kaphal is to fold together — to double, to repeat. The curtains of the tabernacle were coupled together, five and five, and the sixth curtain doubled over at the forefront; the breastplate was foursquare, doubled, a span long and a span broad; and the sword of the king of Babylon is doubled the third time.

Theological Significance

I believe the doubling is God's way of making things strong and sure: the curtain doubled, the breastplate doubled, and the judgment doubled — what God repeats, He means. What He doubles, He confirms.
