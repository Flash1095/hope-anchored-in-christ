ʻashtᵉrâh

increase

Biblical Usage

The increase is the young of the flock and the herd, and the blessing of the land, and Moses promises it to obedience and takes it away for disobedience. The LORD will love thee and bless thee, and bless the fruit of thy womb and the fruit of thy land, and the increase of thy kine and the flocks of thy sheep; blessed shall be the fruit of thy body and the fruit of thy ground and the fruit of thy cattle; and the enemy shall eat the increase of thy cattle and the fruit of thy land until thou be destroyed.

Theological Significance

I believe the increase of the flock is the test of the covenant: obedience fills the folds, and disobedience empties them. The same flocks are the blessing and the loss, and I hold that God means it when He says the land's fruitfulness follows His people's faithfulness.
