Pîy ha-Chîyrôth

Word
פִּי הַחִירֹת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Pîy ha-Chîyrôth
Strongs Number
H6367
Definition
Pi-ha-Chiroth, a place in Egypt
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Pi-hahiroth = "place where sedge grows"; 1) the 3rd encampment of the Israelites after leaving Goshen in Egypt and the last one before crossing the Red Sea
