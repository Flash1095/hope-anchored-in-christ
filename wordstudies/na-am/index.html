nâʼam

properly, to whisper, i.e. (by implication) to utter as a oracle

Biblical Usage

Na'am means to whisper or to utter as an oracle, and its one occurrence is a rebuke of false prophets. In Jeremiah 23:31 the Lord says He is against the prophets who use their tongues and say, He saith, putting His name on words He never spoke.

Theological Significance

I hold that claiming God said what He did not say is one of the oldest sins in the book; a whisper dressed up as an oracle is still a lie, and the Lord answers it Himself.
