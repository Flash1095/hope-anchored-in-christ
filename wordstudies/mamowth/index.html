mâmôwth

a mortal disease; concretely, a corpse

Biblical Usage

mâmôwth names a deadly disease and, by extension, the dead themselves. In Jeremiah 16:4 the people shall die of grievous deaths, unlamented and unburied, and in Ezekiel 28:8 the prince of Tyre shall die the deaths of those slain in the midst of the seas. In 2 Kings 11:2 Jehosheba steals the child Joash from among the king's sons who were slain. The word hangs over scenes of death without honour.

Theological Significance

I believe mâmôwth shows me that death without God is a grievous thing, and I hold that the worst death is not the sword but dying unlamented and unremembered. My hope is in the God who raises the slain.
