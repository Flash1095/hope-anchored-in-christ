Chânân

Chanan, the name of seven Israelites

Biblical Usage

Hanan is a name carried by seven Israelites, and Scripture keeps them apart by their fathers and their work. One Hanan is counted among the sons of Shashak in Benjamin, another is among the Nethinim who returned with Zerubbabel, and another is Hanan the son of Igdaliah, whose chamber in the temple held the sons of the Rechabites.

Theological Significance

I believe seven men sharing one name is a reminder that God counts servants, not only leaders: a Nethinim, a Benjamite, and a temple chamber-keeper are all on the record by name.
