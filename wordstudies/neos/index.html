néos

including the comparative νεότερος; a primary word; "new", i.e. (of persons) youthful, or (of things) fresh; figuratively, regenerate:--new, young.

Biblical Usage

No one puts new wine into old bottles, and the younger women are to be treated as sisters and the younger men to submit to the elders. The new man is put on, renewed in knowledge after the image of Him who created him.

Theological Significance

I believe new is the gospel's word for the Christian: new wine, new man, new life, and the old bottle cannot hold what God is pouring. My newness is not my youth but my renewal in His image.
