logos

Word, message, reason and account — God's self-expression, culminating in Jesus Christ, the Word made flesh.

Biblical Usage

Logos carried rich meaning for Jews (God's creative word) and Greeks (the reason ordering the universe). John seizes both: the Logos was God, was with God, and became flesh in Jesus.

Theological Significance

Logos declares that God has spoken — finally and personally — in His Son. We do not guess at God's heart; we look at Jesus, the Word made flesh.
