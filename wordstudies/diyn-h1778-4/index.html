dîyn

to judge

Biblical Usage

To judge, as Ezra was told to appoint magistrates and judges who would judge the people beyond the river according to the law of God.

Theological Significance

I hold that true judgment starts with knowing God's law, and Ezra's judges were to teach before they sentenced.
