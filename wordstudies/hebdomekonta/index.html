hebdomḗkonta

from G1442 and a modified form of G1176; seventy:--seventy, three score and ten.

Biblical Usage

Seventy is a number with a history: seventy elders, seventy souls, and in the New Testament, seventy messengers. In Luke 10:1 the Lord appoints seventy others and sends them two by two, in Luke 10:17 the seventy return with joy because the devils are subject to them, and in Acts 27:37 there are two hundred and seventy-six souls on the ship with Paul.

Theological Significance

I believe the seventy show me that the mission was never meant for twelve men only: Jesus sent seventy, and he still sends ordinary people two by two. The number seventy is the number of the nations in Genesis 10, and I take that as no accident: the mission was always meant for the whole world.
