çâbab

to revolve, surround, or border; used in various applications, literally and figuratively

Biblical Usage

Çâbab means to revolve, to surround, to border, and it is everywhere in the Old Testament — one of the most used verbs in the Bible. It is the word for drawing a circle, whether around a city, a camp, or a whole land, and it does the same work wherever it lands: it marks what is inside and what is outside. You cannot read the histories without it.

Theological Significance

I believe God is the one who draws the circle around His people. The word for surrounding is used of enemies too, but I hold that the LORD's surrounding is the one that lasts, and the one that keeps.
