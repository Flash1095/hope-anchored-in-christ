Mattân

Word
מַתָּן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Mattân
Strongs Number
H4977
Definition
Mattan, the name of a priest of Baal, and of an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Mattan = "a gift"; 1) a priest of Baal in Jerusalem in the time of Athaliah; 2) father of Shephatiah in the time of Jeremiah
