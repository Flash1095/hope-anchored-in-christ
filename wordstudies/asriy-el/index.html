ʼAsrîyʼêl

Asriel, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Two men carry this name in Scripture, and the record keeps them apart. One is Asriel the son of Manasseh, named in the genealogy of the tribe, and the other is Asriel the son of Gilead, whose family was counted in the wilderness and given a share in the land.

Theological Significance

I believe the two Asriels show how carefully the record is kept, because the same name can belong to an uncle and a great-grandson, and God knows which is which.
