Shîyzâʼ

Shiza, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Shiza was a Reubenite whose son Adina served among David's mighty men, a captain of the Reubenites with thirty men under him. His name survives only in that one roll call of David's warriors.

Theological Significance

I believe the men who stood behind David matter to God even when history gives them a single line. Adina's captaincy is recorded, and so is the father who raised him.
