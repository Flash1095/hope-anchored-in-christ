mâlâch

a rag or old garment

Biblical Usage

Malach is a rag, an old cast clout, a rotten garment, and both of its appearances are in the dungeon where Jeremiah lay in the mire. Jeremiah 38:11 has Ebed-melech taking old cast clouts and rotten rags down by cords into the dungeon, and Jeremiah 38:12 has him telling the prophet to put them under his armholes under the cords, so he could be lifted out without the ropes cutting him.

Theological Significance

I believe the rags here are the tenderness inside a cruel story: a stranger thought about the prophet's armpits before he pulled him out of the mud.
