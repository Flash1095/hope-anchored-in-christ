hēmōn

genitive case plural of G1473; of (or from) us:--our (company), us, we.

Biblical Usage

Hemon is our in the genitive, of us, from us, and it carries the two greatest phrases in the Bible. In Matthew 6:9 Jesus teaches us to pray, Our Father which art in heaven, in Matthew 1:23 the child is called Emmanuel, God with us, and in Romans 5:8 Christ died for us while we were yet sinners.

Theological Significance

I believe the three verses are one gospel: our Father, God with us, Christ for us; the our is the whole of it, and I am in it.
