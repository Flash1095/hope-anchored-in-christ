yêtser

a form; figuratively, conception (i.e. purpose)

Biblical Usage

yêtser is the word for imagination, purpose. In Genesis 6:5, that every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually. That is the sense it carries through all 9 of its occurrences.

Theological Significance

My position is that the imagination of the heart is where sin begins before the hand ever moves, and that is why I ask God to shape what I picture, not only what I do. That is why I hold yêtser fast when easier words tempt me away.
