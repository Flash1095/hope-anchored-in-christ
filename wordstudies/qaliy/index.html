qâlîy

roasted ears of grain

Biblical Usage

Parched corn is the food of the journey: Israel may eat neither bread nor parched corn until the offering is brought, Jesse sends an ephah of it to his sons in the camp, and Boaz reaches it to Ruth at mealtime until she is sufficed.

Theological Significance

I take the parched corn as the taste of provision on the way, plain food shared in the field.
