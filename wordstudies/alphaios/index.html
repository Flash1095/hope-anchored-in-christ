Alphaîos

of Hebrew origin (compare H2501); Alphæus, an Israelite:--Alpheus.

Biblical Usage

Once in each list, the father of an apostle: James the son of Alphaeus, named among the twelve in Matthew and in Mark. The name is all the Bible says of the man, and his son is the apostle called James the Less, to tell him from James the son of Zebedee.

Theological Significance

A name that is only a father's name carries no lesson of its own. What it shows is how the apostles are told apart, that two men named James are kept straight by their fathers' names.
