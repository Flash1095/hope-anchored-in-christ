Shiṭṭîym

Shittim, a place East of the Jordan

Biblical Usage

Israel camps at Shittim when the sin at Peor begins, Joshua sends the spies from Shittim, and the prophet Micah tells Israel to remember what happened from Shittim to Gilgal. Joel promises a fountain will come forth from the house of the LORD and water the valley of Shittim.

Theological Significance

I believe Shittim is the last camp before the Jordan, and that is why it matters. I hold that the place where Israel sinned and the place where the spies set out are the same ground, and God still brings His people across from it.
