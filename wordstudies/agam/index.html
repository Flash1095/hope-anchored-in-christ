ʼăgam

Word
אֲגַם
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼăgam
Strongs Number
H98
Definition
a marsh; hence a rush (as growing in swamps); hence a stockade of reeds
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) pool, troubled pool; 1a) troubled or muddy (gloomy) pools, marshes; 1b) any pool, pond; 1c) swamp reeds, reeds, rush(es)
