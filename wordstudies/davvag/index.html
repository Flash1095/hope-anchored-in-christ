davvâg

a fisherman

Biblical Usage

The fisherman appears twice, in the prophets' pictures of the end: the fishers who will be set over the waters of the Dead Sea, and the fishers who will fish for men among the nations. The net is the picture of judgment and of the gospel both.

Theological Significance

I believe the fisherman's trade became the apostles' calling: Christ took men who fished for fish and made them fishers of men.
