mᵉchâʼ

to strike in pieces; also to arrest; specifically to impale

Biblical Usage

To smite is the Aramaic word for striking down, and Daniel uses it for the stone cut out without hands that smote the image and became a great mountain. The same word is used for God doing according to His will among the inhabitants of the earth, and for the man hanged on timber for altering the king's decree.

Theological Significance

I believe the stone is the whole gospel in a dream: a kingdom not made by hands smites every kingdom men have built, and the smiting is not the end but the beginning — the stone becomes a mountain that fills the earth. The kingdoms of men are all images with feet of clay, and the stone is still falling.
