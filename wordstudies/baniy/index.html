Bânîy

Bani, the name of five Israelites

Biblical Usage

Bani is the name of five Israelites, and each one is named in a different setting. There is Bani the Gadite among David's mighty men, a Bani in the genealogy of those who returned to Jerusalem, the children of Bani who came up from exile, Rehum the son of Bani repairing the wall, and Uzzi the son of Bani overseeing the Levites.

Theological Significance

I believe a name repeated across five different men shows how God keeps His people distinct even when the names repeat, and each Bani is remembered for his own place in the story.
