latreúō

from λάτρις (a hired menial); to minister (to God), i.e. render religious homage:--serve, do the service, worship(-per).

Biblical Usage

latreúō means to serve God, to worship, and the contexts could hardly be more different. In Acts 24:14, "so worship I the God of my fathers"; and in Philippians 3:3, "which worship God in the spirit". In Hebrews 12:28, "whereby we may serve God acceptably with reverence and godly fear:"; and in 2 Timothy 1:3, "whom I serve from [my] forefathers with pure conscience".

Theological Significance

I believe serving God is the work of a free person, not a hireling — Paul served with a pure conscience from his forefathers, and I want that same clean conscience. I hold that true worship is not what I do on Sunday only; it is whose I am every day. My position is that I belong to God, and serving Him is not my burden but my freedom.
