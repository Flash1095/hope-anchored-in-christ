nâqam

to grudge, i.e. avenge or punish

Biblical Usage

Naqam as a verb means to avenge or take vengeance, to grudge and repay, and it is claimed by God for Himself. In Numbers 31:2 the Lord tells Moses to avenge the children of Israel of the Midianites, and in Judges 16:28 Samson asks to be avenged of the Philistines for his two eyes. In 1 Samuel 24:12 David says the Lord shall judge between him and Saul, and avenge him of Saul, and in Nahum 1:2 God is jealous and the Lord revengeth, and He reserves wrath for His enemies.

Theological Significance

I believe the Bible gives vengeance to God and takes it from me, and that is a mercy, not a loss. Samson asked for it once and died with the asking, and David leaves his cause with the Lord in 1 Samuel 24:12. I hold that the God who reserves wrath is the God I can trust with my wrongs.
