Gaʻal

Gaal, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Gaal son of Ebed came to Shechem with his brethren in Judges 9:26, and the men of Shechem put their confidence in him. He boasted against Abimelech in Judges 9:28, asking who Abimelech was that they should serve him, and urging the men to serve the men of Hamor instead. His rising ended in battle at the gate, and Abimelech drove him out.

Theological Significance

I believe Gaal is a warning about confidence placed in the wrong man. Shechem trusted a loud stranger over their own king, and the city paid for it; I hold that loud promises are no substitute for a leader God has set in place.
