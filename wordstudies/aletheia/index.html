aletheia

Word
ἀλήθεια
Language
Greek
Transliteration
aletheia
Strongs Number
G225
Definition
Truth — reality as it truly is, God's faithfulness and reliability, embodied and spoken by Jesus Christ.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
John 14:6; John 8:32; John 17:17; Ephesians 4:15
Biblical Usage
Aletheia in Greek thought meant unconcealed reality. Jesus claims not merely to teach it but to be it — 'I am the way, and the truth, and the life' — and promises the truth will set people free.
Theological Significance
Aletheia assures a confused age that truth is not a weapon or a trend but a Person who can be known and trusted. His word is truth, and it sanctifies.
