plēsíon

neuter of a derivative of πέλας (near); (adverbially) close by; as noun, a neighbor, i.e. fellow (as man, countryman, Christian or friend):--near, neighbour.

Biblical Usage

Plesion is the neighbour, the one close by. Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself, says Matthew 22:39, and the same command stands in Mark 12:31 and Luke 10:27. The lawyer asked, Who is my neighbour, and Jesus answered with the Samaritan in Luke 10:36. Paul says love works no ill to his neighbour in Romans 13:9 — therefore love is the fulfilling of the law.

Theological Significance

I believe my neighbour is whoever God puts next to me, even the man I would walk past. That is why I hold that love is not a feeling about people in general, but a deed for the person in front of me.
