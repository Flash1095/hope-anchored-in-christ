kályma

from G2572; a cover, i.e. veil:--vail.

Biblical Usage

Kalyma is a veil — the cover Moses put over his face when he came down from the mountain, because the children of Israel could not look at the glory. Paul says the same veil lies on the heart whenever Moses is read, but when it shall turn to the Lord, the veil shall be taken away.

Theological Significance

I believe the veil is still the difference between reading and seeing, for Moses is read with a veil until the heart turns to the Lord, and when it turns, the veil comes off and we see the glory with open face.
