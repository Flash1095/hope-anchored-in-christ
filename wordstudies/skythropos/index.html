skythrōpós

Word
σκυθρωπός
Language
Greek
Transliteration
skythrōpós
Strongs Number
G4659
Definition
from σκυθρός (sullen) and a derivative of G3700; angry-visaged, i.e. gloomy or affecting a mournful appearance:--of a sad countenance.
