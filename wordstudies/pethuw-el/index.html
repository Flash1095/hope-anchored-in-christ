Pᵉthûwʼêl

Pethuel, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Pethuel is an Israelite named only once, as the father of the prophet Joel. The word of the LORD came to Joel the son of Pethuel, and that single line is all Scripture records of the father.

Theological Significance

I hold that a father's name in the first verse of a prophecy is no small thing: God remembers the man who raised the prophet, even if the prophet's book says nothing more of him.
