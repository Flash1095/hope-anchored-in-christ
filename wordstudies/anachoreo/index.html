anachōréō

from G303 and G5562; to retire:--depart, give place, go (turn) aside, withdraw self.

Biblical Usage

The word means to withdraw, to step aside — and it follows both Joseph and Jesus. Joseph took the young child and his mother by night and departed into Egypt, and Jesus, when He heard of John's death, departed by ship into a desert place apart. Fourteen times the word marks a quiet leaving: to Egypt, to the wilderness, to a place apart, and once when Judas himself departed and went and hanged himself.

Theological Significance

A word for stepping aside carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is in the leaving — Joseph slipping away by night to save the child, and Jesus withdrawing to pray before the crowds found Him again.
