desmón

or δεσμός neuter and masculine respectively from G1210; a band, i.e. ligament (of the body) or shackle (of a prisoner); figuratively, an impediment or disability:--band, bond, chain, string.

Biblical Usage

A bond is the chain itself, or the thing that holds a person fast. The earthquake in Philippi loosed the bands of every prisoner, Paul warns the elders that bonds await him in Jerusalem, and the demon-bound man broke his bonds asunder.

Theological Significance

I hold that bonds in Acts are never the last word. Chains fall off, prisoners go free, and even the man no chain could hold is loosed by Christ, who breaks every bond sin fastens on us.
