hálōn

probably from the base of G1507; a threshing-floor (as rolled hard), i.e. (figuratively) the grain (and chaff, as just threshed):--floor.

Biblical Usage

A threshing floor was where the wheat was separated from the chaff, and John the Baptist uses that picture for the coming judgment. In Matthew 3:12 and Luke 3:17 he says the Lord will purge his floor, gathering the wheat into the garner and burning the chaff with unquenchable fire.

Theological Significance

I hold that the threshing floor is a warning and a comfort together: God knows how to tell the wheat from the chaff, and I want to be found among the wheat.
