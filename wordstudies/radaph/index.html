râdaph

to run after (usually with hostile intent; figuratively (of time) gone by)

Biblical Usage

Radaph is to run after, to pursue — usually with hostile intent, as an army pursues a fleeing enemy. Pharaoh pursued Israel to the sea; Saul pursued David through the wilderness; and the psalm turns the word over: 'Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life.'

Theological Significance

The lesson I take is that the psalm redeems the word: the same verb that chased David through the desert is the verb for God's goodness chasing him home.
