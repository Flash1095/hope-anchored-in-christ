dokós

from G1209 (through the idea of holding up); a stick of timber:--beam.

Biblical Usage

A beam, the great log in my own eye, set against the speck in my brother's. Jesus asks why I look at the mote in his eye and never notice the beam in my own, and tells me to cast the beam out first so I can see clearly.

Theological Significance

I believe judgment starts at home, with the beam, not the mote. When I deal with my own log first, I can finally help my brother instead of condemning him.
