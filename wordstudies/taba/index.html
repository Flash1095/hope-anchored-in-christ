ṭâbaʻ

to sink

Biblical Usage

Pharaoh's chariots sank like lead in the mighty waters, and Moses sings of it. Jeremiah sank in the mire of the dungeon pit, and the psalmist cries, I sink in deep mire, where there is no standing. David's stone sank into Goliath's forehead. The word is sinking, going down: into water, into mud, into the earth, into death.

Theological Significance

I hold that sinking is the Bible's picture of being beyond one's own rescue. I believe the psalmist's cry is the right prayer for it: I sink, and only God's hand reaches that deep. The same God who sank Pharaoh's army lifted Jeremiah out of the pit.
