kréas

perhaps a primary word; (butcher's) meat:--flesh.

Biblical Usage

Meat here is butcher's meat, flesh as food, and both appearances are about the conscience of a brother. Paul says in 1 Corinthians 8:13 that if meat makes his brother offend, he will eat no flesh while the world stands, and in Romans 14:21 it is good neither to eat flesh nor drink wine where a brother stumbles.

Theological Significance

I am sure Paul's rule still stands: my freedom at the table ends where my brother's faith begins, and love gives up the meat.
