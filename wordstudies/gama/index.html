gâmâʼ

to absorb

Biblical Usage

Gama means to swallow or drink down, to take in eagerly. Abraham's servant ran to meet Rebekah and said, Let me, I pray thee, drink a little water of thy pitcher, in Genesis 24:17. Job uses the same word for the war horse, which swallows the ground with fierceness and rage in Job 39:24.

Theological Significance

I believe the servant's request and the horse's charge show the two sides of thirst; one drinks humbly from a pitcher, the other swallows the ground in war, and I hold that God gives water to the humble and restrains the violent.
