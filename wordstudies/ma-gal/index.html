maʻgâl

a track (literally or figuratively); also a rampart (as circular)

Biblical Usage

The word names a track or path, a rut worn by feet, and Proverbs and Isaiah both weigh it. Wisdom says the path of the just is uprightness, and the LORD weighs the path of the just, while the ways of the wicked are crooked and they are froward in their paths. David came to the trench as the host went forth to the fight, and the psalm says God leads His people in the paths of righteousness for His name's sake.

Theological Significance

I believe the track is the picture of a life: every step wears the path deeper, and the only safe ruts are the ones God has weighed. I hold that He does not only point to the right path, He walks it with us, and the paths of righteousness are His own leading.
