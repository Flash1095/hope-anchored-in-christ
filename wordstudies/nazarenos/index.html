Nazarēnós

from G3478; a Nazarene, i.e. inhabitant of Nazareth:--of Nazareth.

Biblical Usage

Demons cry out at Jesus of Nazareth, the Holy One of God, and the people ask whether He is the prophet from Nazareth. The blind man hears that Jesus of Nazareth is passing by, and calls out for mercy.

Theological Significance

I believe the name Nazareth carries the whole offence and the whole glory of Jesus: the demons know Him, the blind man calls Him, and the town no one expected becomes the name the world cannot forget. God chooses the lowly place for His glory, and I must not despise the small beginning.
