apothnḗskō

from G575 and G2348; to die off (literally or figuratively):--be dead, death, die, lie a-dying, be slain (X with).

Biblical Usage

To die is the plainest word in the Bible. It runs from Jairus's daughter, whom Jesus says is not dead but sleeping, to Lazarus, whose death Jesus states outright. Paul adds the claim that if we are dead with Christ we shall also live with Him, and the sweeping truth that in Adam all die.

Theological Significance

I believe death is real and it is not the end. In Adam all die, but in Christ the dying itself is remade, so that being dead with Him is the doorway to living with Him.
