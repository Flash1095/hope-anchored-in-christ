anakainóō

from G303 and a derivative of G2537; to renovate:--renew.

Biblical Usage

Paul says our outward man perishes, but the inward man is renewed day by day — and the new man we have put on is renewed in knowledge after the image of Him that created him. It is not a single event but a daily work: the body wears down while the inner life is made new again every morning, growing back toward the likeness of God.

Theological Significance

I believe renewal is daily, not once: the outward man perishes a little every day, and the inward man is renewed a little every day, and the two run side by side until the end. The new man is renewed in knowledge — knowing God better is what makes us more like Him — and that is the work I can ask for every morning.
