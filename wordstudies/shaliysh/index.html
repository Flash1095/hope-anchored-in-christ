shâlîysh

a triple, i.e. (as a musical instrument) a triangle (or perhaps rather threestringed lute); also (as an indefinite, great quantity) a three-fold measure (perhaps a treble ephah); also (as an officer) a general of the third rank (upward, i.e. the highest)

Biblical Usage

The captain on whose hand the king leaned carries this word, the officer closest to the throne. Elisha's doubting lord leaned on the king's hand and was trodden down in the gate, Pharaoh's chosen captains drowned in the Red Sea, and the women came out with tabrets and with instruments of music when David returned from the Philistine.

Theological Significance

I hold that a captain's rank is measured by whose hand he leans on, and the lord who doubted God's word fell at the gate he was set to guard.
