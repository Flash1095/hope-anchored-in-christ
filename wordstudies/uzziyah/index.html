ʻUzzîyâh

Uzzijah, the name of five Israelites

Biblical Usage

Shallum the son of Jabesh began to reign in the nine and thirtieth year of Uzziah king of Judah. In the Levite line, Tahath's son was Uriel, Uriel's son was Uzziah, and Uzziah's son was Shaul.

Theological Significance

I believe the name Uzziah must be held apart man by man: the king whose pride ended in leprosy is not the Levite whose son was Shaul, and the Bible never lets one man's story swallow the other's.
