ʻUzzîyâh

Word
עֻזִּיָּה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻUzzîyâh
Strongs Number
H5818
Definition
Uzzijah, the name of five Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Uzziah = "my strength is Jehovah"; 1) son of king Amaziah of Judah and king of Judah himself for 52 years; also 'Azariah'; 2) a Kohathite Levite and ancestor of Samuel; 3) a priest of the sons of Harim who took a foreign wife in the time of Ezra; 4) a Judaite, father of Athaiah or Uthai; 5) father of Jehonathan, one of David's overseers
