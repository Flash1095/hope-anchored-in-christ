Shᵉʼâr Yâshûwb

Shear-Jashub, the symbolic name of one of Isaiah's sons

Biblical Usage

Shear-jashub means a remnant shall return, and Isaiah was told to take his son by that name to meet King Ahaz at the conduit of the upper pool. The boy's name was the sermon: whatever Ahaz feared, God would keep a remnant and bring it back.

Theological Significance

I believe God sometimes preaches through a child's name. Shear-jashub stood beside his father as a living promise, and I hold that the remnant promise still stands.
