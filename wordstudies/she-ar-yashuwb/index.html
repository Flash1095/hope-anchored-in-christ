Language

Shᵉʼâr Yâshûwb
Strongs Number

Definition
Shear-Jashub, the symbolic name of one of Isaiah's sons
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Shear-jashub = "a remnant shall return"; 1) a symbolical name for the son of Isaiah the prophet
