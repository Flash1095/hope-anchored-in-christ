epikrínō

from G1909 and G2919; to adjudge:--give sentence.

Biblical Usage

Epikrino means to give sentence, to adjudge — and Pilate gave sentence that it should be as they required, and delivered Jesus to their will.

Theological Significance

A judge who knew Jesus was innocent, giving sentence to please a crowd, is the darkest verdict in history.
