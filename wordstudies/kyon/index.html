kýōn

a primary word; a dog ("hound") (literally or figuratively):--dog.

Biblical Usage

The dog is the unclean scavenger of the ancient world, and Scripture uses it for those outside the covenant and for those who return to sin. Jesus says in Matthew 7:6 not to give what is holy unto the dogs, Paul warns in Philippians 3:2, "Beware of dogs, beware of evil workers," and Peter quotes the proverb in 2 Peter 2:22: "The dog is turned to his own vomit again."

Theological Significance

I believe the word is a warning I need to hear straight: the dog returns to what it left, and so does the heart that has been washed but not changed. The washed dog returns to the mire only if the washing never reached the heart.
