eiskaléō

from G1519 and G2564; to invite in:--call in.

Biblical Usage

This word means to call in or invite in, and the Greek text carries it in the middle voice, as having called in, where Strong's lists the plain active form. Peter calls Cornelius's men in and lodges them, the first step toward a Gentile's house and a Gentile's faith. One invitation opens the door to the nations.

Theological Significance

I believe God calls outsiders in, because Peter's invitation to strangers was the beginning of the gospel reaching the Gentiles, and me.
