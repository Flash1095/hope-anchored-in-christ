kad

properly, a pail; but generally of earthenware; a jar for domestic purposes

Biblical Usage

Kad is the household jar — the earthen pitcher for water, meal, and oil. Rebekah carries her pitcher to the well; Gideon gives each man a pitcher with a torch hidden inside; the widow of Zarephath has only a handful of meal in a barrel and a little oil in a cruse; and Elijah has the altar drenched with four barrels of water.

Theological Significance

I believe God loves to work through jars. The pitcher at the well, the broken pitcher at Midian, the widow's barrel that never ran dry — He fills the ordinary vessels, and that is why I do not wait to be impressive before I offer Him what I have.
