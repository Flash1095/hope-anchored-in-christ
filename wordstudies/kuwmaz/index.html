kûwmâz

a jewel (probably gold beads)

Biblical Usage

The kumaz is a gold ornament, and both appearances are offerings. In Exodus 35:22 willing-hearted men and women brought bracelets, earrings, rings, and tablets of gold for the tabernacle, and in Numbers 31:50 the captains brought jewels of gold, chains and bracelets and tablets, as an oblation to make atonement for their souls.

Theological Significance

To my mind, the gold in both verses says the same thing: what was once worn for beauty was laid down for God, and the offering became the atonement.
