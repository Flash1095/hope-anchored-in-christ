ʼĂdâr

Adar, the 12th Hebrew month

Biblical Usage

Adar is the twelfth month of the Hebrew year: Haman cast the lot and it fell on Adar, in Adar the Jews instead destroyed their enemies, and in Adar the temple was finished in the sixth year of Darius. As a month, Adar has no People profile — its story is told in Esther and Ezra.

Theological Significance

I hold the turn of Adar as one of the clearest pictures of providence in the Bible: the month chosen by lot for murder became the month of rescue, and the same month saw the house of God finished. The lot is cast, but the disposing is of the Lord.
