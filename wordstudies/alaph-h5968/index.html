ʻâlaph

to veil or cover; figuratively, to be languid

Biblical Usage

The word covers and it faints. Tamar veils herself and sits by the wayside, covered so Judah will not know her. And Jonah sits east of the city under his gourd, the sun beats on his head, and he faints and wishes himself dead; the same root covers a face and drains the strength from a body.

Theological Significance

A word that means both to cover and to faint carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the two scenes, a woman hiding her face to get justice and a prophet wilting in the sun because God would not do what he wanted.
