sbénnymi

a prolonged form of an apparently primary verb; to extinguish (literally or figuratively):--go out, quench.

Biblical Usage

Sbennymi is quenching, putting out a fire, and Scripture uses it both ways. Paul commands it plainly: quench not the Spirit. The shield of faith quenches all the fiery darts of the wicked; the heroes of faith quenched the violence of fire and escaped the sword. But in the place of judgment, the fire is not quenched, and the worm dieth not.

Theological Significance

I believe the Spirit is a fire we can grieve and quench, and that is a sober warning, not a small one. Faith is the shield that puts out the enemy's fire, and the same fire that the faithful quench is the fire the lost cannot escape. My position is that we should tend the fire God lit, not tread it out.
