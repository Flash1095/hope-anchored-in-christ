châm

hot

Biblical Usage

Cham means hot, warm. The Gibeonites showed bread they had taken hot from their houses, now dry and mouldy, and Elihu asks how garments are warm when God quiets the earth by the south wind.

Theological Significance

I believe hot bread and warm garments are the small mercies of the road, and the Gibeonites' lie is the warning that even bread can be a deceit.
