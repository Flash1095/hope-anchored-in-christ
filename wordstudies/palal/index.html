pâlal

Word
פָּלַל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
pâlal
Strongs Number
H6419
Definition
to judge (officially or mentally); by extension, to intercede, pray
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to intervene, interpose, pray; 1a) (Piel) to mediate, judge; 1b)(Hithpael); 1b1) to intercede; 1b2) to pray
