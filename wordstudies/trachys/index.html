trachýs

perhaps strengthened from the base of G4486 (as if jagged by rents); uneven, rocky (reefy):--rock, rough.

Biblical Usage

The sailors on Paul's ship feared falling upon rocks and cast four anchors out of the stern, and Isaiah promises the rough ways shall be made smooth when the Lord comes. The sailors on Paul's ship feared falling upon rocks and cast four anchors out of the stern, and Isaiah promises the rough ways shall be made smooth when the Lord comes.

Theological Significance

I believe the two uses are one promise: the rocks men fear at sea are the same rough ways God promises to smooth.
