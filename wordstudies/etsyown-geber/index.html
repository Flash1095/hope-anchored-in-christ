ʻEtsyôwn Geber

Word
עֶצְיוֹן גֶּבֶר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻEtsyôwn Geber
Strongs Number
H6100
Definition
Etsjon-Geber, a place on the Red Sea
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Ezion-geber = "backbone of a man"; 1) the last station during the exodus of the Israelites before they came to the wilderness of Zin; located near Elath at the head of the Gulf of Akaba
