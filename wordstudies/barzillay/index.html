Barzillay

Barzillai, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Barzillai is the name of three Israelites, and the chief of them is Barzillai the Gileadite, the old man of eighty who brought beds and basins and food to David when he fled from Absalom, who refused the king's offer of a place in Jerusalem, and whom David remembered on his deathbed with kindness to his sons. A later Barzillai is named among those who returned from exile.

Theological Significance

I believe Barzillai is the picture of loyalty that asks for nothing, because he fed a fugitive king and went home to die in his own city. David never forgot it, and I hold that the kindness shown to a king in his lowest hour is the kindness God remembers longest.
