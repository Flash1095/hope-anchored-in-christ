Barzillay

Word
בַּרְזִלַּי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Barzillay
Strongs Number
H1271
Definition
Barzillai, the name of three Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Barzillai = "my iron"; 1) a Gileadite leader who helped David defeat Absalom's rebellion; 2) a priest, son-in-law to Barzillai the Gileadite; 3) an Israelite from Mahalath whose son Adriel married Michal, Saul's daughter
