dâʼab

to pine

Biblical Usage

To pine is to waste away with longing or grief, and Jeremiah says the soul of the weary will be satisfied and will not sorrow any more. The LORD says He will satiate the soul of the priests with fatness, and the psalmist's eye mourns by reason of affliction. The word is the slow wasting of a heart that waits.

Theological Significance

I believe pining is the honest condition of the afflicted, and God does not rebuke it; He satisfies it. Jeremiah promises a day when the soul will sorrow no more, and that promise is the anchor for every pining heart. I hold that God counts the tears of the wasting, and He will repay them with fullness.
