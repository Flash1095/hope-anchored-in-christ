higgâyôwn

Word
הִגָּיוֹן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
higgâyôwn
Strongs Number
H1902
Definition
a murmuring sound, i.e. a musical notation (probably similar to the modern affettuoso to indicate solemnity of movement); by implication, a machination
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) meditation, resounding music, musing; 1a) resounding music; 1b) meditation, plotting
