pôʻal

an act or work (concretely)

Biblical Usage

Poal is a work, an act, a deed done. His work is perfect, says Deuteronomy 32:4, and the psalmist prays, Let Thy work appear unto Thy servants, in Psalms 90:16. I will triumph in the works of Thy hands, says Psalms 92:4, and Habakkuk prays, O LORD, revive Thy work in the midst of the years, in Habakkuk 3:2.

Theological Significance

I believe God's work is perfect and mine is not. The gap between them is why I pray for Him to revive His work — not mine.
