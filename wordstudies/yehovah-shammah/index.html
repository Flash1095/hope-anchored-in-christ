Yᵉhôvâh shâmmâh

Jehovah-Shammah, a symbolic title of Jerusalem

Biblical Usage

Jehovah-shammah is the name given to the city in Ezekiel's vision, the name that shall be from that day onward. The name means the LORD is there, and the city is Jerusalem, measured and named by God Himself. The name stands in the verse as two words together, the LORD and there, because the Hebrew text carries the name under its parts and not as a single word.

Theological Significance

I believe the LORD is there, and the city of God is named by His presence, not by its walls. My position is that the promise of Ezekiel is the promise of the whole Bible, that God will dwell with His people.
