epipothéō

Word
ἐπιποθέω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
epipothéō
Strongs Number
G1971
Definition
from G1909 and potheo (to yearn); to dote upon, i.e. intensely crave possession (lawfully or wrongfully):--(earnestly) desire (greatly), (greatly) long (after), lust.
