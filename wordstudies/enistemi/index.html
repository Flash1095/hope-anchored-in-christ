enístēmi

from G1722 and G2476; to place on hand, i.e. (reflexively) impend, (participle) be instant:--come, be at hand, present.

Biblical Usage

Enistemi is what is present, at hand, pressing in. Things present and things to come are all yours in 1 Corinthians 3:22, Paul warns the Thessalonians not to be troubled as though the day of Christ is at hand in 2 Thessalonians 2:2, and he says perilous times shall come in the last days in 2 Timothy 3:1. Christ gave Himself to deliver us from this present evil world in Galatians 1:4.

Theological Significance

I believe the present age is evil but it is not final — Christ has already delivered us from it, even while we still live in it. I hold that the things present are mine to use, not mine to fear, because the One who gave Himself for me is greater than the age I live in.
