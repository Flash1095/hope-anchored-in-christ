Sᵉgûwb

Word
שְׂגוּב
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Sᵉgûwb
Strongs Number
H7687
Definition
Segub, the name of two Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Segub = "exalted"; 1) the youngest son of Hiel the Bethelite who rebuilt Jericho; 2) a Judaite, son of Hezron
