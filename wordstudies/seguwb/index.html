Sᵉgûwb

Segub, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Segub is the name of two Israelites, and both carry a father's story. One was the son of Hezron by the daughter of Machir, and he begat Jair, who had three and twenty cities in the land of Gilead. The other was the youngest son of Hiel the Beth-elite, in whom the gates of Jericho were set up, according to the word of the LORD spoken by Joshua.

Theological Significance

I believe the two Segubs show both sides of a name: one builds a family of cities, and the other pays the price of rebuilding what God cursed.
