enkombóomai

Word
ἐγκομβόομαι
Language
Greek
Transliteration
enkombóomai
Strongs Number
G1463
Definition
middle voice from G1722 and κομβόω (to gird); to engirdle oneself (for labor), i.e. figuratively (the apron as being a badge of servitude) to wear (in token of mutual deference):--be clothed with.
