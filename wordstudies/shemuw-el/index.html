Shᵉmûwʼêl

Shemuel (Samuel), the name of the prophet Samuel and of two other Israelites

Biblical Usage

This is the name of the prophet Samuel, the son of Elkanah and Hannah, whose birth, calling, and anointing of kings fill the books that bear his story. The same name belongs to two other Israelites, Shemuel the son of Ammihud who divides the land, and Shemuel the son of Tola in the genealogy of Issachar. The Chronicler names Samuel's sons in the genealogy of Levi.

Theological Significance

I believe Samuel is the hinge of Israel's story, the last judge and the maker of kings, and his name means heard of God. I hold that the God who heard Hannah still hears, and the prophet He raised still speaks.
