ʼĂchîynôʻam

Word
אֲחִינֹעַם
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼĂchîynôʻam
Strongs Number
H293
Definition
Achinoam, the name of two Israelitesses
Part Of Speech
proper noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
Ahinoam = "my brother is delight"; 1) wife of Saul, daughter of Ahimaaz; 2) wife of David, mother of Amnon, a Jezreelitess
