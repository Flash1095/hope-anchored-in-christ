apekdéchomai

from G575 and G1551; to expect fully:--look (wait) for.

Biblical Usage

To wait eagerly, looking for what is coming — creation waiting for the revealing of the sons of God, believers waiting with patience for what they hope for, our citizenship in heaven from which we look for the Saviour, and Christ appearing a second time to those who look for him. This is not idle waiting; it leans forward.

Theological Significance

I believe waiting like this changes how I live today. If creation itself is on tiptoe for the revealing of God's children, then I hold that my hope is not an escape from the world but the world's own future arriving.
