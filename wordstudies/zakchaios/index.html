Zakchaîos

of Hebrew origin (compare H2140); Zacchæus, an Israelite:--Zacchæus.

Biblical Usage

Zacchaeus is the chief publican of Jericho, rich and short of stature, who climbs a tree to see Jesus. In Luke 19:2 he is "the chief among the publicans, and he was rich," and in Luke 19:5 Jesus looks up and says, "Zacchaeus, make haste, and come down; for to day I must abide at thy house." In Luke 19:8 Zacchaeus stands and promises to give half his goods to the poor and restore fourfold what he took wrongly.

Theological Significance

I believe Zacchaeus is the proof that no one is too far gone. I hold that Jesus calling him down by name in Luke 19:5, and Zacchaeus answering with restitution in Luke 19:8, show me what real repentance looks like, and that salvation came to his house that very day.
