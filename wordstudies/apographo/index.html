apográphō

from G575 and G1125; to write off (a copy or list), i.e. enrol:--tax, write.

Biblical Usage

To enrol, to write a name on a list. Caesar Augustus decreed that all the world should be taxed in Luke 2:1, and all went to be taxed, every one into his own city, in Luke 2:3 — Joseph going up with Mary his espoused wife in Luke 2:5. Hebrews 12:23 sets another list beside it: the general assembly and church of the firstborn, which are written in heaven.

Theological Significance

I believe the two enrolments are the whole contrast. Rome wrote names on a tax roll to take from its subjects; God writes names in heaven to keep His children. Joseph's journey to Bethlehem put Jesus on Caesar's list, but Hebrews says my name is on a better one — and no emperor can tax it away.
