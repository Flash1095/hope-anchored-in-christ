ʼĂrân

Aran, an Edomite

Biblical Usage

Aran is named as a son of Dishan among the Horite chiefs of Seir, in the genealogy of Esau and again in the chronicle of Adam's line. His name survives only in the two lists.

Theological Significance

I believe the Horite lists are kept because Esau's house, like Jacob's, was counted by God, even when the story moves on.
