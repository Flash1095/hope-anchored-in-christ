ʼachêr

properly, hinder; generally, next, other, etc.

Biblical Usage

Acher means other, another, next — the word for what is not this one. "Thou shalt have no other gods before me"; Noah waited "yet other seven days" before sending the dove again; Isaac's servants "digged another well." It is one of the commonest words in the Bible, doing humble work in commandments, calendars, and wells.

Theological Significance

The word for 'other' is too small to build on, and its weight comes entirely from the commandment it serves — no other gods before the Lord.
