Klaúdē

of uncertain derivation; Claude, an island near Crete:--Clauda.

Biblical Usage

Clauda is an island near Crete, called Cauda in the Greek text. Acts 27:16 says that running under a certain island which is called Clauda, Paul's ship had much work to come by the boat in the storm.

Theological Significance

I believe Clauda is the small shelter in a great storm: the ship runs under it for a moment, and the moment is all the crew gets before the wind takes them again.
