Gîychôwn

Word
גִּיחוֹן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Gîychôwn
Strongs Number
H1521
Definition
Gichon, a river of Paradise; also a valley (or pool) near Jerusalem
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Gihon = "bursting forth"; 1) one of the four rivers of the Garden of Eden; 2) a spring near Jerusalem where the anointing and proclaiming of Solomon as king took place
