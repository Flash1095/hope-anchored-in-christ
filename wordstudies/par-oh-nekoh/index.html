Parʻôh Nᵉkôh

Word
פַּרְעֹה נְכֹה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Parʻôh Nᵉkôh
Strongs Number
H6549
Definition
Paroh-Nekoh (or -Neko), an Egyptian king
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Pharaoh-necho = "great house-he is smitten"; 1) the Pharaoh of Egypt who fought king Josiah of Judah at Megiddo and killed him
