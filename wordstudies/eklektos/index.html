eklektós

from G1586; select; by implication, favorite:--chosen, elect.

Biblical Usage

Elect means chosen, select, a favourite, and the New Testament uses it for angels, for Christ, and for God's people. Jesus says the angels will gather His elect from the four winds, and asks whether God will not avenge His own elect who cry day and night, while Paul asks who shall lay anything to the charge of God's elect, and tells the Colossians to put on the character of the elect, holy and beloved. The word is always God's choosing, never man's earning.

Theological Significance

I believe I am elect because God chose me, and that choice is my confidence when accusations come. My position is that election is not a puzzle to solve but a promise to rest in, because the One who chose me will keep me.
