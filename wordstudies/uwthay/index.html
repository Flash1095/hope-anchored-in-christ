ʻÛwthay

Word
עוּתַי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻÛwthay
Strongs Number
H5793
Definition
Uthai, the name of two Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Uthai = "helpful"; 1) son of Ammihud and a descendant of Pharez of Judah; 2) one of the sons of Bigvai who returned from exile with the 2nd caravan with Ezra
