châshuq

Word
חָשֻׁק
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
châshuq
Strongs Number
H2838
Definition
attached, i.e. a fence-rail or rod connecting the posts or pillars
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) fillets, binders; 1a) rings clasping a pillar of the tabernacle or silver rods between the pillars
