Mithrᵉdâth

Mithredath, the name of two Persians

Biblical Usage

Mithredath is the name of two Persians. The first was the treasurer who handed the temple vessels back to Sheshbazzar by the order of Cyrus, and the second wrote to Artaxerxes with the enemies of Judah to stop the building.

Theological Significance

I hold that the two men show the two hands of empire: one hand returns what was stolen, and the other writes letters to stop the work, and God uses both.
