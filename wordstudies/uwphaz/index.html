ʼÛwphâz

Uphaz, a famous gold region

Biblical Usage

Silver spread into plates is brought from Tarshish, and gold from Uphaz, the work of the workman. Daniel's vision of the man clothed in linen shows his loins girded with fine gold of Uphaz.

Theological Significance

I believe the finest gold in Scripture is always named by its place: Uphaz gold dresses an idol in Jeremiah and a heavenly messenger in Daniel, and the metal is the same — only the hands that shape it differ.
