deiknýō

a prolonged form of an obsolete primary of the same meaning; to show (literally or figuratively):--shew.

Biblical Usage

To show is what Jesus does when He opens the future to His disciples and what the angel does when He shows John the holy city. The word survives in the manuscripts under the shorter verb form, and its three showings span the gospel: the suffering that must come, the sign the Jews demand, and the city that will come down from heaven.

Theological Significance

I believe showing is how God teaches: He does not only tell, He shows, and the showing makes the promise sure.
