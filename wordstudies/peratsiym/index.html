Transliteration
pᵉrâtsîym
Strongs Number
H6559
Definition
Peratsim, a mountain in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Perazim = "breaches"; 1) a mountain in Palestine; 1a) possibly the same as 'Baal-perazim' which was the scene of a victory of David over the Philistines located in the valley of Rephaim, south of Jerusalem, on the road to Bethlehem
