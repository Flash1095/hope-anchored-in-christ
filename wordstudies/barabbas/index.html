Barabbâs

of Chaldee origin (H1347 and H5); son of Abba; Bar-abbas, an Israelite:--Barabbas.

Biblical Usage

Barabbas was a notable prisoner, a robber and murderer held in Jerusalem at the Passover, and the crowd chose him over Jesus when Pilate offered to release one of them. The people cried for Barabbas to be freed and for Jesus to be crucified, and Pilate released the murderer and delivered Jesus to be scourged and crucified.

Theological Significance

I believe Barabbas is every one of us standing in the prisoner's place. A guilty man walked free while the innocent One took his cross, and I hold that no picture in the Gospels shows substitution more plainly than that exchange.
