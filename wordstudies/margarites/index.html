margarítēs

from μάργαρος (a pearl-oyster); a pearl:--pearl.

Biblical Usage

margarítēs is a pearl, and Jesus makes it the picture of the kingdom. In Matthew 13:45 a merchant seeks goodly pearls, and in Matthew 13:46, when he finds one pearl of great price, he sells all he has and buys it. In Matthew 7:6 Jesus warns not to cast pearls before swine, and in 1 Timothy 2:9 pearls stand for costly adornment. The word is beauty that is worth everything.

Theological Significance

I believe margarítēs shows me what the kingdom is worth. I hold that selling all to gain it is no loss at all.
