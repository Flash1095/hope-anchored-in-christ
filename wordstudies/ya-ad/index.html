yâʻad

to fix upon (by agreement or appointment); by implication, to meet (at a stated time), to summon (to trial), to direct (in a certain quarter or position), to engage (for marriage)

Biblical Usage

A master who betrothed a maidservant to himself fixed her standing by appointment, the assembly was to gather at the door of the tabernacle at the sound of the trumpets, all the northern kings met together at the waters of Merom. Amasa tarried longer than the set time which the king had appointed him.

Theological Significance

I hold that appointment is how God orders His people's life: the meeting at the tabernacle was set, the kings' meeting was set, and Amasa's set time was set — and the one who missed his appointment lost his place. I believe God's appointments are not suggestions, and the Bible records the set times because keeping them is part of keeping faith.
