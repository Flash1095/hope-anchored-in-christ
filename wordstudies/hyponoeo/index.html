hyponoéō

from G5259 and G3539; to think under (privately), i.e. to surmise or conjecture:--think, suppose, deem.

Biblical Usage

The word hyponoéō carries the sense of to think, to suppose, to surmise. In Acts 25:18, the text says "they brought none accusation of such things as I supposed." In Acts 27:27, the text says "about midnight the shipmen deemed that they drew near to some country."

Theological Significance

I believe hyponoéō shows us that discernment is learning to tell the true from the almost-true, by God's Word. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
