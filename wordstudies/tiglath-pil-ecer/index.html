Tiglath Pilʼeçer

Tiglath-Pileser or Tilgath-pilneser, an Assyrian king

Biblical Usage

Tiglath-pileser, king of Assyria, is the iron hand of God's judgment in the histories. He carried Beerah, prince of the Reubenites, away captive, and when Ahaz of Judah called on him for help he came and distressed him instead of strengthening him.

Theological Significance

I hold that his story warns me about trusting the wrong deliverer: the king Ahaz bought with silver turned and squeezed Judah, and the captivity he began is a sober picture of what happens when a nation looks to Assyria instead of to God.
