parapikraínō

from G3844 and G4087; to embitter alongside, i.e. (figuratively) to exasperate:--provoke.

Biblical Usage

Parapikraino means to provoke, to embitter alongside. It appears once, in Hebrews 3:16, of the generation that heard God's voice in the wilderness and provoked Him, the very people Moses led out of Egypt.

Theological Significance

I believe hearing God's voice and hardening the heart against it is the oldest sin in the wilderness, and it is still the one I must watch in myself.
