Nᵉkôw

Neko, an Egyptian king

Biblical Usage

Neco is the king of Egypt who comes up to fight at Carchemish, and Josiah goes out against him though the king warns him not to meddle. After Josiah's death Neco sets Eliakim on the throne and turns his name to Jehoiakim.

Theological Significance

I believe Neco is the reminder that Egypt still moves kings: he kills Josiah at Megiddo and names the next king of Judah, and the throne of David bends to Pharaoh's word until God takes it back.
