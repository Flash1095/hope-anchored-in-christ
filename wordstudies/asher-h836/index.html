ʼÂshêr

happy; Asher, a son of Jacob, and the tribe descended from him, with its territory; also a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Asher is the son of Jacob by Zilpah, and the tribe that comes from him, and his name means happy. Leah says happy am I and calls him Asher, Jacob blesses him that out of Asher his bread shall be fat and he shall yield royal dainties, Moses says let Asher be blessed with children and dip his foot in oil, and Joshua gives his tribe the coast from Helkath to the sea.

Theological Significance

A son named Happy, blessed with fat bread and oil and a coast by the sea, tells me that God's blessing is not thin: Asher gets the best of the land, and the name his mother gave him in joy is the name his tribe carries into the inheritance.
