Yêhûwʼ

Word
יֵהוּא
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Yêhûwʼ
Strongs Number
H3058
Definition
Jehu, the name of five Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Jehu = "Jehovah is He"; 1) the king of the northern kingdom Israel who overthrew the dynasty of Omri; 2) son of Hanani and an Israelite prophet in the time of Baasha and Jehoshaphat; 3) the Antothite, a Benjamite, one of David's mighty warriors; 4) a descendant of Judah of the house of Hezron; 5) son of Josibiah and a chief of the tribe of Simeon
