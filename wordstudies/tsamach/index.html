tsâmach

to sprout (transitive or intransitive, literal or figurative)

Biblical Usage

Tsamach is the growing God does — He makes things sprout out of the ground. Out of the ground He made every tree to grow in Eden; the vine in Ezekiel's parable grew and became a spreading vine; and the psalmist says He causes the grass to grow for the cattle. Isaiah promises that as the earth brings forth her bud, so the Lord GOD will cause righteousness and praise to spring forth.

Theological Significance

I believe growth is God's work before it is ours — He makes things sprout, and we tend what He grew. My position is that the same God who makes grass grow causes righteousness to spring forth in His people. I hold that nothing truly grows apart from Him, and nothing He plants fails to grow in its season.
