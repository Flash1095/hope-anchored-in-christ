Rhódē

probably for rhode (a rose); Rode, a servant girl:--Rhoda.

Biblical Usage

When Peter knocked at the door of the gate, a damsel came to hearken, named Rhoda, and when she knew Peter's voice, she opened not the gate for gladness, but ran in and told how Peter stood before the gate. They did not believe her at first, but she kept saying it was Peter, and at last they opened the door.

Theological Significance

I believe Rhoda's joy is the most human moment in the book of Acts, and God kept her name because He loves gladness that forgets the door.
