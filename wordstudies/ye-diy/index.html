Yeʻdîy

Jedi, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Jedi is the name as it is written in the record of Solomon's acts, where the King James reads Iddo the seer. The visions of Iddo were written against Jeroboam the son of Nebat, and the name stands at the end of Solomon's story as the prophet who saw what came after.

Theological Significance

I believe the seer who saw Jeroboam's rise also saw Solomon's end, and the record keeps both.
