ʻĂbêd Nᵉgôwʼ

Abed-Nego, the name of Azariah

Biblical Usage

Abed-Nego is the Babylonian name given to Azariah, one of Daniel's three friends — 'unto Azariah, of Abed-nego.' He is set over the affairs of Babylon, accused of refusing to serve the king's gods or worship the golden image, and thrown into the burning fiery furnace with Shadrach and Meshach.

Theological Significance

His Hebrew name meant 'the LORD has helped'; his Babylonian name praised a foreign god — and he would not bow to it. I believe Abed-Nego's story is about a name you refuse to live by: the world can rename you, but it cannot make you worship what it worships, and the God who helped Azariah walks in the furnace with His own.
