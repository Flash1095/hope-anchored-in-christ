eniautós

prolongation from a primary ἔνος (a year); a year:--year.

Biblical Usage

A year — the full round of seasons. Paul and Barnabas spent a whole year assembling with the church at Antioch in Acts 11:26, the high priest entered the second tabernacle once every year in Hebrews 9:7, and James warns the planners who say they will spend a year in a city buying and selling in James 4:13. Paul also rebukes the Galatians for observing days and months and years in Galatians 4:10.

Theological Significance

I believe a year is long enough to change a church — Antioch did more in one year than most do in ten — and short enough to humble a planner. I hold that my years are not mine to schedule but God's to spend, and the high priest's once-a-year entry reminds me that access to God was once rare and is now open.
