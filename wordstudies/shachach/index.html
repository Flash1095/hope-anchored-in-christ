shâchach

Word
שָׁחַח
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
shâchach
Strongs Number
H7817
Definition
to sink or depress (reflexive or causative)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to bow, crouch, bow down, be bowed down; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to be bowed down, be prostrated, be humbled; 1a2) to bow (in homage); 1a3) to bow (of mourner); 1a4) to crouch (of wild beast in lair); 1b) (Niphal) to be prostrated, be humbled, be reduced, be weakened, proceed humbly, be bowed down; 1c) (Hiphil) to prostrate, lay low, bow down; 1d) (Hithpolel) to be cast down, be despairing
