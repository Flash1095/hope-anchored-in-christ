zemer

a gazelle (from its lightly touching the ground)

Biblical Usage

The gazelle — or mountain sheep; the exact animal is uncertain — shows up just once, in the list of clean animals Israel was allowed to eat. Deuteronomy 14:5 names it among the wild game that could lawfully come to the table, a small detail in a law about what sets God's people apart.

Theological Significance

I take comfort in how carefully God spelled out even the food laws: holiness reached into ordinary life, right down to what was on the table.
