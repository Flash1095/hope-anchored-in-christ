zemer

Word
זֶמֶר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
zemer
Strongs Number
H2169
Definition
a gazelle (from its lightly touching the ground)
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) mountain sheep, mountain goat, moufflon, gazelle, chamois (meaning uncertain); 1a) a certain animal allowed as food (specific species uncertain); 1b) perhaps an extinct animal, exact meaning unknown
