ṭâphach

to flatten out or extend (as a tent); figuratively, to nurse a child (as promotive of growth);

Biblical Usage

The word is to spread out or dandle, to carry a child on the palms. God's right hand spanned the heavens, and Lamentations speaks of the children the prophet had swaddled and brought up, now consumed by the enemy in the day of anger.

Theological Significance

I hold that the same hands that spanned the heavens once dandled children, and the judgment that fell on them was real grief, not indifference.
