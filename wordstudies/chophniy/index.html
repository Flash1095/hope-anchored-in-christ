Chophnîy

Chophni, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Hophni is the elder son of Eli, a priest who profaned the priesthood and died for it. He serves with his brother Phinehas at Shiloh, the Lord promises that both sons shall die in one day as a sign against Eli, and in the battle with the Philistines both are slain and the ark is taken.

Theological Significance

I believe Hophni's story is the warning that a priesthood without holiness is worse than no priesthood at all. I hold that God will not let His name be carried by men who despise it, and the ark's capture is the proof.
