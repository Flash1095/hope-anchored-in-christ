Yᵉhôwtsâdâq

Word
יְהוֹצָדָק
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Yᵉhôwtsâdâq
Strongs Number
H3087
Definition
Jehotsadak, an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Jehozadak or Josedech = "Jehovah is righteous"; 1) grandson of the high priest Hilkiah; son of the high priest Seraiah; and father of the high priest Joshua; he never attained the office of high priest himself because he was carried captive to Babylon by Nebuchadnezzar
