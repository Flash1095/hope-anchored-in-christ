tíktō

Word
τίκτω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
tíktō
Strongs Number
G5088
Definition
a strengthened form of a primary τέκω (which is used only as alternate in certain tenses); to produce (from seed, as a mother, a plant, the earth, etc.), literally or figuratively:--bear, be born, bring forth, be delivered, be in travail.
