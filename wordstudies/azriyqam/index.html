ʻAzrîyqâm

Azrikam, the name of four Israelites

Biblical Usage

Azrikam is the name of four men — a son in the line of Zerubbabel, a Levite who dwelt at Jerusalem after the return, a chief of the house of Elpaal, and the ruler of the king's house who was slain by Zichri the Ephraimite when Pekah invaded Judah. The name runs from the exile to the kingdom, and one of its bearers died defending the king's house.

Theological Significance

I believe God keeps the names of men who served in quiet posts — a Levite in Jerusalem, a ruler in the king's house — because faithful service in small places is still service to him.
