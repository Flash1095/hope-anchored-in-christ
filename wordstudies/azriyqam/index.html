ʻAzrîyqâm

Word
עַזְרִיקָם
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻAzrîyqâm
Strongs Number
H5840
Definition
Azrikam, the name of four Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Azrikam = "help against the enemy"; 1) son of Neariah and descendant of Zerubbabel of the royal line of Judah; 2) a Benjamite, eldest son of Azel and descendant of Saul; 3) a Levite, ancestor of Shemaiah in the time of Nehemiah; 4) governor of the palace of king Ahaz of Judah and victim of murder by Zichri
