ṭâbᵉʼêl

Word
טָבְאֵל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ṭâbᵉʼêl
Strongs Number
H2870
Definition
Tabeel, the name of a Syrian and of a Persian
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Tabeal = "God is good"; 1) a man whose son was either in the army of Pekah or the army of Rezin and whom Pekah and Rezin proposed to make king of Israel; Tabeel = "God is good"; 2) an officer of the Persian government in Samaria in the reign of Artaxerxes
