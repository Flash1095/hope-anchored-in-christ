ṭâbᵉʼêl

Tabeel, the name of a Syrian and of a Persian

Biblical Usage

Tabeel is the name of two men. One was the father of the man Rezin and Pekah proposed to set on the throne of Judah in place of Ahaz, a plot Isaiah names and God overturns. The other was an officer of the Persian government in Samaria who wrote against the rebuilding of Jerusalem, and the name means God is good — yet both men stand against God's purposes.

Theological Significance

I believe the name is the irony of the entry: God is good, says the name, and the men who bore it schemed against His king and His city, and both schemes failed.
