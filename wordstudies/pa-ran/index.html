Pâʼrân

Word
פָּארָן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Pâʼrân
Strongs Number
H6290
Definition
Paran, a desert of Arabia
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Paran = "place of caverns"; 1) wilderness area bounded on the north by Palestine, on the west by the wilderness of Etham, on the south by the desert of Sinai, and on the east by the valley of Arabah; the exodus was through this area and probably all 18 stops were in this area
