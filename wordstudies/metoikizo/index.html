metoikízō

from the same as G3350; to transfer as a settler or captive, i.e colonize or exile:--carry away, remove into.

Biblical Usage

Abraham leaves the land of the Chaldeans and dwells in Haran, and later Stephen says God will carry the people away beyond Babylon. The word is the settling of a people in a new land, whether by God's leading or by His judgment.

Theological Significance

I notice that God moves His people both ways with this word: Abraham is carried into the land of promise, and Israel is carried beyond Babylon, and in both cases the move is God's doing.
