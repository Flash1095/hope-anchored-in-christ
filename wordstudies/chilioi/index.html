chílioi

plural of uncertain affinity; a thousand:--thousand.

Biblical Usage

Chilioi is a thousand, and its most famous use is Peter's word that one day is with the Lord as a thousand years. John hears the witnesses prophesy a thousand two hundred and threescore days, the woman is fed in the wilderness for the same thousand days, and the blood runs to the horse bridles for a thousand and six hundred furlongs.

Theological Significance

I believe Peter's thousand years teaches me patience, because God's slowness is not His absence. I hold that the thousand in Revelation is God's measure, not man's, and that is why I trust His timing.
