ḗdē

apparently from G2228 (or possibly G2229) and G1211; even now:--already, (even) now (already), by this time.

Biblical Usage

Already means the time has come, and the New Testament uses it for things that are true now, not only later. Paul says it is high time to awake out of sleep, John says the darkness is past and the true light already shines, and Paul, near the end, says he is already being offered up. The word presses the present moment, because the hour is now.

Theological Significance

I believe the gospel makes already a word of urgency, because salvation is here now and the night is far spent. I do not want to live as if God's hour were still far off when it is already here.
