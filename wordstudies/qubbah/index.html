qubbâh

a pavilion (as a domed cavity)

Biblical Usage

The tent itself is the word. Phinehas goes after the man of Israel into the pavilion, the domed chamber, and thrusts both of them through, and the plague is stayed. The word is the pavilion itself.

Theological Significance

I believe the tent where the sin was done is named, because God sees the place as well as the act.
