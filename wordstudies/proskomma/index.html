próskomma

from G4350; a stub, i.e. (figuratively) occasion of apostasy:--offence, stumbling(-block, (-stone)).

Biblical Usage

Proskomma is a stumbling block, something set in the path that trips a man up. Israel stumbled at the stumblingstone because they sought righteousness by works, not by faith. Paul warns not to put a stumblingblock in a brother's way, and not to let liberty become a stumblingblock to the weak. Peter calls Christ a stone of stumbling to them which stumble at the word, being disobedient.

Theological Significance

I believe the same stone trips two kinds of men: the one who will not trust Christ, and the brother my carelessness trips up behind me. I hold that I am responsible for both stumblings, the one I cause in myself and the one I cause in others.
