gâbîysh

crystal (from its resemblance to ice)

Biblical Usage

Gabish is crystal, a clear, shining stone, though the King James renders it pearls in Job 28:18. No mention shall be made of coral or of pearls, says Job, for the price of wisdom is above rubies. The word names something precious and clear, and Job sets it beside the treasures men chase.

Theological Significance

I believe Job is right: wisdom outshines crystal and pearls alike; men dig for stones and dive for pearls, but the price of wisdom is above them all, and I hold that knowing God is the only treasure that never loses its value.
