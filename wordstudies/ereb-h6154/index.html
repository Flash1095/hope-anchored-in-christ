ʻêreb

the web (or transverse threads of cloth); also a mixture, (or mongrel race)

Biblical Usage

ʻÊreb is the web — the transverse threads of cloth — and by extension a mixture, a mingled crowd. The law speaks of the web in a garment; a mixed multitude went up with Israel out of Egypt, and Nehemiah separated the mixed multitude from Israel; and the prophets name the mingled peoples among the nations. It is the word for what is woven together, and for who is mixed in.

Theological Significance

I believe ʻêreb is a word for the mixture in the midst of God's people: the web in the cloth, and the crowd in the camp. I hold that the mixed multitude is a warning — not everyone who comes out of Egypt belongs to Israel.
