Pashchûwr

Pashchur, the name of four Israelites

Biblical Usage

Pashchur is the name of four Israelites, and the one who stands out is the priest of Jeremiah's day. Pashur the son of Immer smites Jeremiah the prophet and puts him in the stocks in Jeremiah 20:1, and in Jeremiah 20:3 the prophet renames him Magor-missabib, terror on every side. In Jeremiah 21:1 the same Pashur is sent by King Zedekiah to ask Jeremiah whether the Lord will deal with them against Nebuchadnezzar.

Theological Significance

I hold that Pashur is the priest who struck the prophet and was renamed terror, because the man who silences God's word ends up living inside the judgment he tried to stop.
