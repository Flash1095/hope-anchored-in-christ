yâʼash

Word
יָאַשׁ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
yâʼash
Strongs Number
H2976
Definition
to desist, i.e. (figuratively) to despond
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to despair; 1a) (Niphal) to be despaired, be desperate; 1b) (Piel) to cause to despair; interj; 2) it is hopeless!
