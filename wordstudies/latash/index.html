Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
lâṭash
Strongs Number

Definition
properly, to hammer out (an edge), i.e. to sharpen
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to sharpen, hammer, whet; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to hammer; 1a2) to sharpen (sword); 1a3) hammerer (participle); 1b) (Pual) to be sharpened
