nâhal

properly, to run with a sparkle, i.e. flow; hence (transitively), to conduct, and (by inference) to protect, sustain

Biblical Usage

Nahal means to lead gently, to conduct with care, and it is the shepherd's word. In Psalm 23:2 He leadeth me beside the still waters, and in Isaiah 40:11 He shall gently lead those that are with young, carrying the lambs in His bosom. In Isaiah 49:10 He that hath mercy on them shall lead them, even by the springs of water shall He guide them, and in Genesis 33:14 Jacob says he will lead on softly, according as the cattle and the children are able to endure.

Theological Significance

I believe gentle leading is how God treats His people, because He leads at the pace of the lambs and the children. Jacob led softly so the little ones could keep up, and the Lord does the same for me. I hold that the still waters are not an accident; they are where the Shepherd leads.
