Lûwz

Luz, the name of two places in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Luz was the old name of Bethel, the city where Jacob dreamed of the ladder reaching to heaven and called the place the house of God. Jacob renamed it Bethel, but Scripture keeps the old name so we know it is the same city. Long after, the house of Joseph sent men to spy out Bethel, and the text notes again that the name of the city before was Luz.

Theological Significance

I believe Luz matters because God met Jacob there before the place had a holy name, and He still meets people in ordinary places and renames their lives.
