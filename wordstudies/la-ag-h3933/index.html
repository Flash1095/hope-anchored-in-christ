laʻag

derision, scoffing

Biblical Usage

Derision is the noun for being laughed to scorn, and it is mostly what God's people suffer at the hands of their enemies. In Psalms 44:13 God has made Israel "a scorn and a derision to them that are round about us," in Job 34:7 Job is accused of drinking up scorning like water, and in Ezekiel 23:32 Jerusalem will drink her sister's cup and be "laughed to scorn and had in derision."

Theological Significance

My position is that derision is the lot of God's people in a mocking world, and the psalms give me the words for it: they laughed at Israel, they laughed at Job, and God heard every laugh. The laughter of the proud is the lot of the righteous, and God will answer it.
