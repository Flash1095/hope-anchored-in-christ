ʼĂbîyâm

Word
אֲבִיָּם
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼĂbîyâm
Strongs Number
H38
Definition
Abijam (or Abijah), a king of Judah
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Abijam = "my father is the sea" or "Yah(u) is (my) father"; 1) king of Judah, son and successor of Rehoboam
