chărâdâh

fear, anxiety

Biblical Usage

Charadah is fear and anxiety, the trembling that takes hold of the whole body. Isaac trembled very exceedingly when Esau came in and he knew the blessing had gone to Jacob. Elisha asks the Shunammite what can be done for her, since she has been careful for them with all this care. Daniel's companions fled to hide themselves when a great quaking fell upon them, and Isaiah says fearfulness affrighted him and the night of his pleasure turned to fear.

Theological Significance

I believe anxiety in Scripture is not a modern invention: Isaac trembled, Isaiah's heart panted, and a whole company fled from a vision they could not bear. The Shunammite's carefulness is the same word turned toward kindness. I hold that fear is honest, and the Bible's answer is not to deny it but to bring it to the God who steadies trembling hands.
