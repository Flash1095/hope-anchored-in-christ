bâʻath

to fear

Biblical Usage

Ba'ath means to be terrified, to be struck with sudden fear. The evil spirit terrified Saul and his servants said, behold, an evil spirit from God troubleth thee; Daniel fell on his face in fear when Gabriel came near; the waters of the sea terrified the prophet; and Job says the terrors of God set themselves in array against him. Fear that comes like a wave and overwhelms.

Theological Significance

I hold that there is a fear that comes from God and a fear that comes from the enemy, and Daniel shows me the difference: he fell on his face before Gabriel, and the angel lifted him up. The fear of the Lord does not crush; it raises.
