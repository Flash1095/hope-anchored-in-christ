dexiós

from G1209; the right side or (feminine) hand (as that which usually takes):--right (hand, side).

Biblical Usage

The right hand is the place of honour and power, and Scripture puts it everywhere that matters. The sheep stand at the King's right hand, Christ sits at the right hand of God, and the Father's right hand raised Him and set Him above all.

Theological Significance

I believe the right hand is where Christ belongs and where my hope sits. He is not a distant figure but the risen Lord at the Father's side, and His right hand holds the church the way a shepherd holds his flock.
