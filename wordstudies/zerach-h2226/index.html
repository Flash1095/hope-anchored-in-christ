Zerach

Zerach, the name of three Israelites, also of an Idumaean and an Ethiopian prince

Biblical Usage

Zerah is a name worn by several men, and Scripture keeps them straight by their fathers and their tribes. There is Zerah the son of Reuel, a duke of Edom; Zerah the twin of Pharez, son of Judah and Tamar, whose line runs through the Zarhites; and Zerah the Ethiopian, who marched against Asa with a vast army and was broken before the LORD. Their full stories live on their People profiles.

Theological Significance

I believe the shared name is a reminder that God deals with men one by one, not by label. The same name covers a duke, a twin, and a defeated invader — and the LORD knew each of them by his own story.
