Zerach

Word
זֶרַח
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Zerach
Strongs Number
H2226
Definition
Zerach, the name of three Israelites, also of an Idumaean and an Ethiopian prince
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Zerah or Zarah = "rising"; 1) a son of Reuel and grandson of Esau, one of the dukes of the Edomites; 2) twin brother of Pharez, sons of Judah and Tamar; descendants are called Zarhites, Ezrahites, and Izrahites; 3) son of Simeon; also called 'Zohar'; 4) a Gershonite Levite, son of Iddo of Adaiah; 5) the Ethiopian or Cushite, invader of Judah in the reign of Asa who defeated the invaders; probably the same as the Egyptian king Usarken I, second king of the Egyptian 22nd dynasty or perhaps more probably Usarken II, his 2nd successor; 6) another Edomite leader; possibly same as 1
