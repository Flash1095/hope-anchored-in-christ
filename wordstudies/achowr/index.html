ʼâchôwr

Word
אָחוֹר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼâchôwr
Strongs Number
H268
Definition
the hinder part; hence (adverb) behind, backward; also (as facing north) the West
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) the back side, the rear; 1a) backwards; 1b) hereafter (of time); 1c) behind
