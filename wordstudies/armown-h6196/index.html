ʻarmôwn

the plane tree (from its smooth and shed bark)

Biblical Usage

The plane tree is a smooth-barked tree, and it is used twice. Jacob takes rods of green poplar, hazel and chestnut and peels white strakes in them for his flocks, and Ezekiel says no tree in the garden of God, plane trees among them, was like the great cedar in beauty.

Theological Significance

One tree named twice carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the picture, that even the finest trees of Eden stood below the cedar God compares to Assyria.
