ʻâqâr

sterile (as if extirpated in the generative organs)

Biblical Usage

Barren is the word for a womb that has not borne, and it follows the mothers of the promise. Sarai was barren, Rebekah was barren until Isaac prayed for her, Hannah sings that the barren has borne seven, and Isaiah tells the barren woman to sing because her children will outnumber the married wife's.

Theological Significance

I hold that barrenness in Scripture is never the end of the story. God opens closed wombs, and He turns the song of the barren into the song of the church, which bears children it never carried.
