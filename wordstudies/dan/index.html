Dân

Dan, one of the sons of Jacob; also the tribe descended from him, and its territory; likewise a place in Palestine colonized by them

Biblical Usage

Dan is the son of Jacob and the tribe that bears his name, and his story runs from the blessing to the border. Jacob prophesied that Dan would judge his people as one of the tribes of Israel, and the tribe settled in the north, where its city gave the land its northern boundary, from Dan to Beersheba. The tribe's allotment, its judge Samson, and its later idolatry all belong to this name.

Theological Significance

I believe Dan's story is a warning about the edges: a tribe at the border can guard the land or open the gate, and Dan did both. My position is that blessing carries responsibility, and the tribe that was promised judgment ended up needing it.
