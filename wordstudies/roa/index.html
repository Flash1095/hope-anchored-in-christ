rôaʻ

badness (as marring), physically or morally

Biblical Usage

Read rôaʻ as badness (as marring), physically or morally. In 1 Samuel 17:28, we read, "…men; and Eliab's anger was kindled against David…"; in Deuteronomy 28:20, "…be destroyed, and until thou perish quickly; because…". You hear it again in Ecclesiastes 7:3 — "Sorrow better than laughter: for by the sadness…" — and in Genesis 41:19, "…poor and very ill favoured and leanfleshed, such…".

Theological Significance

I believe this word rôaʻ is in the Bible for a reason, and it carries weight far beyond its size in the text. And I hold that rôaʻ is as true now as when it was first written, because God does not change.
