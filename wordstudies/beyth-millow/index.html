Bêyth Millôwʼ

Word
בֵּית מִלּוֹא
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Bêyth Millôwʼ
Strongs Number
H1037
Definition
Beth-Millo, the name of two citadels
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Beth-millo = "house of Millo" or "house of the rampart"; 1) a place near Shechem, site unknown; 2) a citadel in Jerusalem
