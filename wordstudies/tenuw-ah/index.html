tᵉnûwʼâh

alienation; by implication, enmity

Biblical Usage

The word is alienation, enmity, a turning against. God says Israel shall bear their iniquities and know His breach of promise, after the forty days of spying, a day for a year; and Elihu says Job counts God as his enemy, finding occasions against him.

Theological Significance

I believe enmity with God is the fruit of unbelief, and the forty years proved it.
