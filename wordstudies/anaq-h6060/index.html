ʻânâq

a necklace (as if strangling)

Biblical Usage

The word is a necklace, a chain about the neck: Proverbs says a father's instruction and a mother's law shall be an ornament of grace unto your head and chains about your neck — and the spoil of Midian included collars and purple raiment that were on the kings, and chains about their camels' necks. The same word is wisdom worn as an ornament and gold stripped from a defeated king.

Theological Significance

A word for a necklace carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the wearing — wisdom as chains about the neck, and a king's collars carried off as spoil.
