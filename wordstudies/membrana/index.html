membrána

of Latin origin ("membrane"); a (written) sheep-skin:--parchment.

Biblical Usage

Membrana is a parchment, a sheet of prepared skin for writing, and Paul's last letter asks for one. 2 Timothy 4:13 has him ask Timothy to bring the cloke left at Troas with Carpus, and the books, but especially the parchments.

Theological Significance

I believe the parchments matter because a dying apostle still wanted the written word beside him, and he asked for it plainly.
