cheírōn

irregular comparative of G2556; from an obsolete equivalent χέρης (of uncertain derivation); more evil or aggravated (physically, mentally or morally):--sorer, worse.

Biblical Usage

Worse is the word for the second state being worse than the first. Jesus warns the healed man lest a worse thing come upon him, the unclean spirit returns with seven worse than himself, Peter warns that the latter end is worse than the beginning for those who turn back, and Hebrews asks how much sorer punishment belongs to those who tread the Son of God under foot.

Theological Significance

I believe worse is a real direction, not only a word: turning back after knowing the truth leaves a man worse than before. The warning is meant to keep me from going back.
