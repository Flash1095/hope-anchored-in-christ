ekpeirázō

from G1537 and G3985; to test thoroughly:--tempt.

Biblical Usage

ekpeirázō means tempt. In Matthew 4:7, the text says "Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God." In Luke 4:12, the text says "Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God."

Theological Significance

Reading ekpeirázō this way, I believe temptation is real and so is God's way through it — He never leaves us to face it alone. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
