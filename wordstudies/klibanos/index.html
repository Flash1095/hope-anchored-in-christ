klíbanos

of uncertain derivation; an earthen pot used for baking in:--oven.

Biblical Usage

Klibanos is an oven, an earthen pot for baking. Matthew 6:30 and Luke 12:28 both say the grass of the field is today, and tomorrow is cast into the oven, and ask, shall God not much more clothe you, O ye of little faith?

Theological Significance

I believe the oven is the measure of God's care: if He clothes grass that is burned tomorrow, He will clothe His children who live forever.
