Dᵉbîyr

Debir, the name of an Amoritish king and of two places in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Debir is a name that belongs to three places at once: an Amorite king who fought against Joshua, and two towns in the land. The king of Debir is named among the kings Joshua defeated, the town in the south is taken by the tribe of Judah, and the town in the east is a city of refuge on the border of Gad. One name, a king and two cities, all on the map of conquest.

Theological Significance

I believe the name reminds us that the conquest was a real campaign against real kings in real towns, and the Lord gave them all into Israel's hand.
