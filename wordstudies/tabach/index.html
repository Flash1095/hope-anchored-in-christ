ṭâbach

to slaughter (animals or men)

Biblical Usage

Joseph tells his steward to slay and make ready, for the men shall dine with him at noon. Wisdom has killed her beasts and furnished her table. Jeremiah says he was like a lamb brought to the slaughter, and did not know they had devised devices against him, while the law says the thief who kills the ox he stole shall restore five oxen for it. The word is slaughter, butchery: killing for the table, for the sacrifice, and for murder.

Theological Significance

The lamb led to the slaughter is the verse that gives this word its weight, I hold. I believe Jeremiah did not know the whole meaning of his own words: the Lamb he pictured would be led to slaughter for us, and His silence would be our salvation.
