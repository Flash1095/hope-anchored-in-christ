tsânaʻ

to be humble, lowly; to walk humbly

Biblical Usage

Tsana is humility — walking low before God, not high before men. Micah's answer to what God requires is to do justly, love mercy, and walk humbly with thy God; and Proverbs says that with the lowly is wisdom. The word appears only twice, and both times it marks the posture God loves: low, quiet, and near to Him.

Theological Significance

I believe humility is not thinking little of yourself but walking close to God, and that is what Micah asks of me. My position is that wisdom lives with the lowly, not the proud — and I would rather be low with God than high without Him.
