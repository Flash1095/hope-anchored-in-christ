lēnós

apparently a primary word; a trough, i.e. wine-vat:--winepress.

Biblical Usage

lēnós means winepress — plain enough to miss, and too important to pass over. In Matthew 21:33, "and digged a winepress in it"; and in Revelation 14:19, "and cast [it] into the great winepress of the wrath of God.". In Revelation 19:15, "that with it he should smite the nations: and he shall rule them with a rod of iron: and he treadeth the winepress of the fierceness and wrath of Almighty God."; and in Revelation 14:20, "And the winepress was trodden without the city".

Theological Significance

I believe the winepress is God's last picture of harvest — the householder digs one for his vineyard, and the angel casts the vine of the earth into the great winepress of His wrath. I hold that Christ treads it alone, with a sword from His mouth and a rod of iron. My position is that I want to be gathered into His barn, not trodden in His press.
