nâchâh

to guide; by implication, to transport (into exile, or as colonists)

Biblical Usage

Nachah means to guide or lead, and it is most often God leading His people by hand. In Exodus 13:17 God led the people not through the way of the Philistines, though it was near, and in Psalm 23:3 He leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for His name's sake. In Psalm 31:3 David asks God to lead and guide him for His name's sake, and in Exodus 15:13 He in His mercy has led forth the people He has redeemed.

Theological Significance

I believe the difference between wandering and travelling is the guide, and Israel had the best one. God led them the long way on purpose, and He leads me the same way, in mercy and by His own hand. I hold that being led is better than being left to choose my own road.
