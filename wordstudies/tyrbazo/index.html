tyrbázō

from τύρβη (Latin turba, a crowd; akin to G2351); to make "turbid", i.e. disturb:--trouble.

Biblical Usage

tyrbázō is the Bible's word for to trouble, to disturb. In Luke 10:41, thou art careful and troubled about many things. The King James Version reads the word here, while the Greek text behind the modern editions reads differently: SBL reads θορυβάζῃ, lemma θορυβάζω (not a site Word, and the related θορυβέω is G2350, a different lemma).

Theological Significance

What grips me is that God put tyrbázō exactly where it is, and I trust His placing of it.
