ʻâthîyd

prepared; by implication, skilful; feminine plural the future; also treasure

Biblical Usage

This word means prepared or ready, and it looks forward to a day that is coming. Vengeance and recompense are ready for the enemies of God, the decree against the Jews was prepared and published in every province, and Job calls on those who are ready to rouse up Leviathan.

Theological Significance

I believe the future is not an accident waiting to happen, it is prepared, and the God who prepares judgment also prepares deliverance. Haman's decree and Mordecai's counter-decree were both ready on their day, and I hold that God's timing is never late.
