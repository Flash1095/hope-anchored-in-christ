deshen

Word
דֶּשֶׁן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
deshen
Strongs Number
H1880
Definition
the fat; abstractly fatness, i.e. (figuratively) abundance; specifically the (fatty) ashes of sacrifices
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) fat ashes, fatness; 1a) fatness; 1a1) of fertility; 1a2) of blessing (fig.); 1b) fat ashes (ashes of victims, mixed with the fat)
