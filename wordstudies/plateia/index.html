plateîa

feminine of G4116; a wide "plat" or "place", i.e. open square:--street.

Biblical Usage

Plateia is a broad place, a street, an open square. Jesus says broad is the gate and wide is the way that leads to destruction in Matthew 7:13, and the street of the new Jerusalem is pure gold in Revelation 22:2. The servant is sent into the streets to bring in the poor and maimed in Luke 14:21, and the sick were laid in the streets in Acts 5:15, hoping Peter's shadow would fall on them.

Theological Significance

I believe every street tells a story — the broad one that destroys, the golden one that heals, and the dusty one where the sick wait for a shadow. My position is that the gospel belongs in the streets, not only in the synagogue.
