Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼArgôb
Strongs Number
H709
Definition
Argob, a district of Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Argob = "heap of clods"; n pr loc; 1) a district or area in Bashan; n pr m; 2) one of king Pekah's officers
