Pîy-Beçeth

Word
פִּי־בֶסֶת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Pîy-Beçeth
Strongs Number
H6364
Definition
Pi-Beseth, a place in Egypt
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Pi-beseth = "mouth of loathing"; 1) a town of lower Egypt located on the west bank of the Pelusiac branch of the Nile about 40 miles from Memphis; 1a) same as 'Bubastis' named after the goddess of the same name
