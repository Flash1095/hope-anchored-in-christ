epikályma

from G1943; a covering, i.e. (figuratively) pretext:--cloke.

Biblical Usage

Epikalyma is a covering, a cloak — and Peter warns against using liberty as a cloke of maliciousness. Freedom is not a cover for wrongdoing; it is the dress of servants of God.

Theological Significance

The same freedom that serves God can be pulled over evil, and Peter tells us not to wear it that way.
