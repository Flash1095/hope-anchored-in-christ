qâbab

to scoop out, i.e. (figuratively) to malign or execrate (i.e. stab with words)

Biblical Usage

Balak hired Balaam to curse Israel, and the word follows the bargain through the whole story: come now, curse me this people. Balaam's answer is, how shall I curse, whom God hath not cursed, and Balak's frustration, I took thee to curse mine enemies, and thou hast blessed them altogether.

Theological Significance

I hold that no curse can land where God has not spoken it, and Balak's money could buy the prophet's journey but never God's word. What Balaam could not do for money, God would not do at all, and that is the comfort in the story.
