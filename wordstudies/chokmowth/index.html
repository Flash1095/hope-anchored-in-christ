chokmôwth

wisdom

Biblical Usage

Chokmowth is Wisdom herself, speaking aloud in the streets as a woman calling to the simple. Wisdom cries without and lifts her voice in the streets, the psalmist says his mouth shall speak of wisdom, Wisdom has built her house with seven pillars, and every wise woman builds her house.

Theological Significance

I believe Wisdom in Proverbs is the voice of God calling in the open, not hiding in a corner. I hold that she is to be heard in the street as well as in the temple, because her call is for everyone.
