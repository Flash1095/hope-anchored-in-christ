Nikódēmos

from G3534 and G1218; victorious among his people; Nicodemus, an Israelite:--Nicodemus.

Biblical Usage

Nicodemus comes to Jesus by night, a ruler of the Jews, and asks the question that opens the whole teaching on being born again. How can a man be born when he is old, he asks, and how can these things be — and Jesus answers him with the wind and the serpent and the Son lifted up.

Theological Significance

I believe Nicodemus is the night visitor every seeker knows: he comes in the dark with an honest question, and the answer is bigger than he asked for.
