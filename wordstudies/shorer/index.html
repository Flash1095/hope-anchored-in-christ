shôrer

the umbilical cord, i.e. (by extension) a bodice

Biblical Usage

Shorer is the navel, and it appears once, in the Song, where the beloved's navel is compared to a round goblet that never lacks liquor, and her body to a heap of wheat set about with lilies. The verse is a lover's praise of wholeness and plenty.

Theological Significance

I believe the Song is not embarrassed by the body, because God gave love a poetry of its own, and delight in the beloved is part of His design.
