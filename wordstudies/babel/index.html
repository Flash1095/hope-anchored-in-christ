Bâbel

Babel (i.e. Babylon), including Babylonia and the Babylonian empire

Biblical Usage

Babel is Babylon — the city of the tower, the city of the exile, the city of the great king. Genesis says its name is Babel because there the Lord confounded the language of all the earth; Isaiah says Babylon the glory of kingdoms shall be as Sodom and Gomorrah; Jeremiah says Babylon is a golden cup in the Lord's hand that made all the earth drunken; and Daniel was carried there as a boy and served its kings with a clear conscience.

Theological Significance

I believe Babylon is the city of man's pride from the tower to the exile, and God judges it every time — yet he also keeps his Daniels in it. I hold that I can live in Babylon without bowing to it.
