stolḗ

from G4724; equipment, i.e. (specially), a "stole" or long-fitting gown (as a mark of dignity):--long clothing (garment), (long) robe.

Biblical Usage

The long robe is honour in one place and pride in another. The father says, bring forth the best robe and put it on the returning son, the scribes love to go in long clothing and love salutations in the marketplaces, and the multitude before the throne stands clothed with white robes.

Theological Significance

I believe the robe says whose honour it is: the father's gift, the scribe's show, and the Lamb's washing.
