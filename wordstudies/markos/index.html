Márkos

of Latin origin; Marcus, a Christian:--Marcus, Mark.

Biblical Usage

Márkos is Mark, John Mark, whose house in Jerusalem sheltered the praying church. In Acts 12:12 Peter comes to the house of Mary the mother of John, whose surname was Mark, where many are gathered praying. In Colossians 4:10 Paul calls Marcus the sister's son of Barnabas, and in 2 Timothy 4:11 he asks for Mark, saying he is profitable to him for the ministry., and his story lives on his People profile.

Theological Significance

I believe Márkos shows me that a man who once left the work can come back to it, and I hold that Mark's restoration is one of the kindest stories in the New Testament.
