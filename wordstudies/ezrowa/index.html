ʼezrôwaʻ

the arm

Biblical Usage

Ezroa is the arm, and Scripture uses it for the strength that acts. God brought Israel out of Egypt with a strong hand and a stretched out arm in Jeremiah 32:21, and Job, swearing his innocence, says let his arm fall from his shoulder blade if he has wronged the fatherless in Job 31:22. The arm is the picture of power put to work.

Theological Significance

I believe the arm of God is the strongest thing in the Bible; it brought a nation out of slavery, and I hold that the same arm still reaches for His people today.
