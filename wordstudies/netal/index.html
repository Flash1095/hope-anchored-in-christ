nᵉṭal

to raise

Biblical Usage

To lift up in Daniel is the movement of a humbled king and a rising beast. Nebuchadnezzar lifts up his eyes to heaven at the end of his days, and his understanding returns to him; the first beast is lifted up from the earth and made to stand on its feet as a man.

Theological Significance

I believe lifting the eyes to heaven is the first act of a restored mind — Nebuchadnezzar's reason came back when his eyes went up.
