Word
סוּת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
çûwth
Strongs Number
H5496
Definition
properly, to prick, i.e. (figuratively) stimulate; by implication, to seduce
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to incite, allure, instigate, entice; 1a) (Hiphil); 1a1) to incite (to a request); 1a2) to allure, lure; 1a3) to instigate (bad sense)
