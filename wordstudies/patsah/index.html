pâtsâh

Word
פָּצָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
pâtsâh
Strongs Number
H6475
Definition
to rend, i.e. open (especially the mouth)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to part, open, separate, set free; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to open (mouth), utter; 1a2) to snatch away, set free
