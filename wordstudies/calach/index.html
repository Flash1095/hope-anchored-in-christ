çâlach

to forgive

Biblical Usage

Çâlach is the word for forgive, and in Scripture it belongs to God in a way no other word does. Moses pleads with it, Solomon prays with it at the dedication of the temple, and Daniel confesses with it: forgive, and hear, and do not defer. It is not a light word; it is the word for a debt cancelled and a people restored.

Theological Significance

I believe forgiveness is God's work before it is ours to give. He forgives because He is merciful, and when He forgives, the sin is gone — that is why I can forgive others, and why I can ask Him to forgive me.
