ekeînos

from G1563; that one (or (neuter) thing); often intensified by the article prefixed:--he, it, the other (same), selfsame, that (same, very), X their, X them, they, this, those. See also G3778.

Biblical Usage

That one is the word that points, and John uses it more than anyone to point at Jesus. John the Baptist says, He that sent me to baptise with water, the same said unto me, and, He must increase, but I must decrease, while the letter of John says whoever claims to abide in Him ought to walk as He walked, and everyone who has this hope in Him purifies himself. The word always points away from the speaker and toward Christ.

Theological Significance

I believe pointing at Christ is the whole job of a witness, because John the Baptist did it with one word and I should do it with my whole life. He must increase, and I must decrease.
