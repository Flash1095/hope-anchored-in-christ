dîy

that, used as relative conjunction, and especially (with a preposition) in adverbial phrases; also as preposition of

Biblical Usage

That, who, which, the little Aramaic link word that ties a sentence together, used hundreds of times in Daniel and Ezra. Nebuchadnezzar demands the dream and its meaning, the king's officers write that they are the men of the king, and Jeremiah's single Aramaic verse warns that the gods that did not make heaven and earth shall perish.

Theological Significance

I hold that small words carry the weight of the sentence, and the God who speaks Aramaic in exile is the same God who speaks Hebrew at home. Nothing about His people is foreign to Him, not even their language.
