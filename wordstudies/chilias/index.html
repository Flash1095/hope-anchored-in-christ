chiliás

from G5507; one thousand ("chiliad"):--thousand.

Biblical Usage

Chilias is a thousand, a counted multitude, and the New Testament uses it for crowds, armies and angels alike. Three and twenty thousand fall in one day in the wilderness, the number of believers grows to about five thousand, a king counts ten thousand against twenty thousand, and the angels round the throne are ten thousand times ten thousand.

Theological Significance

I believe the thousands in Scripture are never just numbers, because each one is a soul God counts. I hold that the angels round the throne show how small our crowds are beside His.
