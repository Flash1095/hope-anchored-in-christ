mâshach

to rub with oil, i.e. to anoint; by implication, to consecrate; also to paint

Biblical Usage

mâshach is to anoint, to rub with oil and consecrate. In 1 Samuel 10:1 Samuel pours oil on Saul's head and says the LORD has anointed him captain over His inheritance, and in 1 Chronicles 11:3 the elders anoint David king over Israel. In 1 Kings 1:34 Zadok and Nathan anoint Solomon, and in 1 Chronicles 14:8 the Philistines hear that David is anointed king over all Israel. The word is God's choosing made visible in oil.

Theological Significance

I believe mâshach is the mark of God's choosing. I hold that the anointing oil on a king, a priest, or a prophet points to the Anointed One, Jesus, in whom I am chosen too.
