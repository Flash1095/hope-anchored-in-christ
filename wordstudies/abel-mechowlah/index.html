Abel-Mecholah, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Abel-meholah — the meadow of dancing — is a town in the Jordan valley. Gideon's enemies flee 'unto the border of Abel-meholah'; it is listed among Solomon's districts; and it is the home of Elisha, 'the son of Shaphat of Abel-meholah', whom Elijah is told to anoint.

Theological Significance

A town where armies flee, a district on a tax list, and the home of a prophet — the same place holds all three. I believe Abel-meholah shows how God plants His servants in unremarkable towns: Elisha was ploughing a field there when the call came, and the call found him working.
