ʻâṭaph

to shroud, i.e. clothe (whether transitive or reflex.); hence (from the idea of darkness) to languish

Biblical Usage

To be overwhelmed is to faint under a weight too heavy to carry, and the psalms use this word for the spirit at its lowest. David cries from the end of the earth when his heart is overwhelmed, Asaph says his spirit was overwhelmed when he remembered God, the hungry and thirsty fainted in the wilderness, and Jonah's soul fainted within him in the belly of the fish.

Theological Significance

I believe the overwhelmed heart is not the end of the story, it is the place where prayer begins. Jonah remembered the LORD when his soul fainted, and I hold that God hears the cry that comes from the very bottom.
