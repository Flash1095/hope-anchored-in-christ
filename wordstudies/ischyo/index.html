ischýō

from G2479; to have (or exercise) force (literally or figuratively):--be able, avail, can do(-not), could, be good, might, prevail, be of strength, be whole, + much work.

Biblical Usage

To be able is to have the strength to do it, and the New Testament measures that strength by God, not by us. In Acts 19:20 the word of God grew mightily and prevailed, in Galatians 5:6 neither circumcision availeth any thing, nor uncircumcision, but faith which worketh by love, and in Philippians 4:13 Paul says, I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me.

Theological Significance

I hold that the strength Paul claims is not his own: Christ strengthens him. The word prevails because God is the one doing the prevailing.
