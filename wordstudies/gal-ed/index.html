Galʻêd

Galed, a memorial cairn East of the Jordan

Biblical Usage

Galeed is the heap of witness, the pile of stones Jacob and Laban raised between them. Jacob called it Galeed in Genesis 31:47, and Laban said in Genesis 31:48 that this heap is a witness between me and thee this day. The name means heap of witness, and the stones stood as a boundary neither man would cross to harm the other.

Theological Significance

I believe God sets stones as witnesses between men who cannot trust each other. Jacob and Laban parted with a heap between them, and I hold that a boundary kept in peace is better than a quarrel won.
