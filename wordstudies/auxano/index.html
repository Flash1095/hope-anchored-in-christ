auxánō

a prolonged form of a primary verb; to grow ("wax"), i.e. enlarge (literal or figurative, active or passive):--grow (up), (give the) increase.

Biblical Usage

Paul planted and Apollos watered, but God gave the increase — that is the heart of this word. It is the growth of a plant, a child, a church, and a believer: Luke says the child Jesus grew, John says Christ must increase while he decreases, and Peter ends his letter by telling us to grow in grace. The growth is real, but it is never self-made.

Theological Significance

I believe growth in the Christian life is God's work in me, not my achievement. My part is to plant and water faithfully; his part is the increase, and that takes the pressure off me and puts the glory where it belongs.
