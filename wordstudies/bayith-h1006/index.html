Bajith, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Bajith is a place in Moab, named once. The people go up to the high places to weep over the judgment coming on their land.

Theological Significance

I hold that the weeping at Bajith is the sound of a nation that trusted in its high places and found them empty.
