ʻAdnâh

Adnah, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Adnah is the name of two men: a captain of Manasseh who defected to David at Ziklag, and a captain of Judah under Jehoshaphat, in command of three hundred thousand mighty men of valour. One joined David when he was still an outlaw; the other stood in the ranks of a settled kingdom.

Theological Significance

Two captains, one name, no doctrine hidden in it — I won't pretend otherwise. What I take from them is simpler: God used commanders as well as kings, and the men who brought their thousands to David and to Jehoshaphat are counted among the faithful.
