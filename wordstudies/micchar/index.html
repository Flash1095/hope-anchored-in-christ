miçchâr

trade

Biblical Usage

Solomon's revenue came beside what he had of the merchantmen and the traffick of the spice merchants, and of all the kings of Arabia. The word is trade itself, and the verse shows a kingdom grown rich on the roads of the merchants.

Theological Significance

I believe Solomon's traffick is a quiet warning: the richest king in the land grew rich on trade, and the trade brought the gold and the wives and the gods with it.
