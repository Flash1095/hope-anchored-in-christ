Shammâh

Shammah, the name of an Edomite and four Israelites

Biblical Usage

Shammah belongs to an Edomite duke, a son of Jesse, and a mighty man, all in the same spelling. The duke Shammah was a son of Reuel, Jesse's son Shammah passed before Samuel and was not chosen, and Shammah the Hararite stands in David's list of mighty men.

Theological Significance

I believe God passes over the tall son and chooses the youngest, and Shammah's passing before Samuel is the proof of it.
