chôq

an enactment; hence, an appointment (of time, space, quantity, labor or usage)

Biblical Usage

Choq is a statute, an enactment appointed by God, and it runs through the covenant from Abraham to the temple. The Lord confirms His word to Jacob for a law and to Israel for an everlasting covenant, He promises Solomon long life if he keeps His statutes, David makes the division of spoil a statute for Israel, and the Lord tells Solomon that keeping His statutes is the condition of the kingdom.

Theological Significance

I believe a statute is not a suggestion but an appointment, because God sets the terms of His covenant. I hold that the keeping of His statutes is the proof of love, and David and Solomon both knew the price of forgetting them.
