ʻâçîyç

must or fresh grape-juice (as just trodden out)

Biblical Usage

Sweet wine, new wine, juice fresh from the press — asiyc is the drink of joy and the drink of judgment both. The bride offers "spiced wine of the juice of my pomegranate"; the oppressors are made "drunken with their own blood, as with sweet wine"; and in the restored age "the mountains shall drop down new wine." The same cup, opposite ends.

Theological Significance

Sweet wine is a drink, not a doctrine, and the verses hold both its faces — joy in the bride's offer and judgment in the oppressor's cup.
