chărab

to demolish

Biblical Usage

Charab appears once, in the letter against Jerusalem: search the book of the records, and you will find that this city is a rebellious city, hurtful unto kings, and that they have moved sedition within the same of old time.

Theological Significance

I believe the verse shows how enemies quote history: the charge is true in part, and that is what makes it dangerous.
