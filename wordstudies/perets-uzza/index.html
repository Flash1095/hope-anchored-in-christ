Perets ʻUzzâʼ

Word
פֶּרֶץ עֻזָּא
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Perets ʻUzzâʼ
Strongs Number
H6560
Definition
Perets-Uzza, a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Perez-uzza or Perezuzzah = "breach of Uzza"; 1) the place where Uzzah was slain by God for touching the Ark; located close to Jerusalem
