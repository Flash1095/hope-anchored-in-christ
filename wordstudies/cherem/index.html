chêrem

physical (as shutting in) a net (either literally or figuratively); usually a doomed object; abstractly extermination

Biblical Usage

Cherem is a thing devoted to God for destruction, set apart so completely that no one may take it for himself. Jericho is made a curse and its spoil is forbidden, Achan takes of the accursed thing and brings trouble on Israel, and Saul keeps the spoil of the devoted Amalekites against the Lord's command.

Theological Significance

I believe cherem teaches me that what God has devoted is not mine to keep, and taking it is never a small sin. My position is that obedience is proved exactly where the prize looks worth keeping.
