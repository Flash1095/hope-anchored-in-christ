ʻârar

to bare; figuratively, to demolish

Biblical Usage

The word means to bare, to strip and demolish. The Chaldeans raise up Tyre's palaces and bring it to ruin, the careless women are told to strip and make themselves bare, and the broad walls of Babylon shall be utterly broken and her gates burned.

Theological Significance

I hold that stripping a city bare is God's judgment on pride that dressed itself in palaces. What is built to impress men is laid bare before God.
