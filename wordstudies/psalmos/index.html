psalmós

from G5567; a set piece of music, i.e. a sacred ode (accompanied with the voice, harp or other instrument; a "psalm"); collectively, the book of the Psalms:--psalm. Compare G5603.

Biblical Usage

Psalmos is a psalm, a set piece of sacred music, a song sung with the voice or the harp. Paul tells the churches to speak in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, and to teach one another in psalms. God fulfilled the promise to the fathers in raising Jesus, as it is written in the second psalm, Thou art My Son, this day have I begotten Thee. And Jesus said all things must be fulfilled which were written in the law of Moses, and in the prophets, and in the psalms, concerning Me.

Theological Significance

I believe the psalms are not just Israel's songbook but Christ's own story, written centuries before He came. I hold that when I sing a psalm, I am singing what Jesus sang, and what was written about Him before He was born.
