zâhâb

gold, figuratively, something gold-colored (i.e. yellow), as oil, a clear sky

Biblical Usage

zâhâb means gold, figuratively, something gold-colored (that is yellow), as oil, a clear sky. In Genesis 2:11 we read, "The name of the first is Pison: that is it which compasseth the whole land of Havilah, where there is gold;". It appears again in Exodus 3:22: "But every woman shall borrow of her neighbour, and of her that sojourneth in her house, jewels of silver..."

Theological Significance

zahab is gold, figuratively, something gold-colored (that is yellow), as oil, a clear sky, and I believe God does not use words carelessly, so this one does the work he gave it.
