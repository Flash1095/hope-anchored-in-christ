epiphōnéō

from G1909 and G5455; to call at something, i.e. exclaim:--cry (against), give a shout.

Biblical Usage

Epiphoneo means to cry out against, to shout at — and in every case the subject is a crowd. They cried, saying, Crucify him, crucify him; the people gave a shout, It is the voice of a god, and not of a man; some cried one thing, some another; and they cried against Paul until the chief captain could not learn the certainty.

Theological Significance

A crowd's shout crucified Jesus, crowned Herod, and nearly killed Paul, and I must beware of joining a shout I have not examined. I believe the crowd's voice is the most dangerous sound in the Gospels, because it feels like agreement and acts like judgment.
