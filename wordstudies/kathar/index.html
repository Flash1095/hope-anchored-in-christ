kâthar

Word
כָּתַר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
kâthar
Strongs Number
H3803
Definition
to enclose; hence (in a friendly sense) to crown, (in a hostile one) to besiege; also to wait (as restraining oneself)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to surround; 1a) (Piel) to surround; 1b) (Hiphil) to surround, encircle
