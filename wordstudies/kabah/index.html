kâbâh

to expire or (causatively) to extinguish (fire, light, anger)

Biblical Usage

Kabah is a fire or a lamp going out — to expire, to be quenched, to be put out. The law says the fire on the altar shall never go out; Isaiah promises that the Servant will not quench the smoking flax; the proverb says where there is no wood the fire goes out; and in the tabernacle the lamp of God went out before Samuel was called.

Theological Significance

I believe this word holds both a warning and a comfort. The fire God kindles is not ours to let die — yet the bruised and smouldering, the smoking flax of a weak faith, He will not quench. He tends the flame; our part is to keep coming to Him for oil.
