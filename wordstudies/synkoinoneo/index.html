synkoinōnéō

from G4862 and G2841; to share in company with, i.e. co-participate in:--communicate (have fellowship) with, be partaker of.

Biblical Usage

Paul tells the Ephesians to have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather to reprove them, and he thanks the Philippians because they communicated with his affliction, sharing in it with him. The voice from heaven says, Come out of her, my people, that you be not partakers of her sins, and that you receive not of her plagues. Sharing is the word in all three: we share in darkness or in affliction or in sin, and the gospel tells us which sharings to refuse and which to embrace.

Theological Significance

I hold that I must refuse fellowship with the works of darkness and embrace fellowship with the afflicted, and I believe the difference is everything. My position is simple: I will not be a partaker of Babylon's sins, but I will gladly be a partaker of Paul's affliction.
