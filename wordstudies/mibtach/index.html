mibṭâch

properly, a refuge, i.e. (objective) security, or (subjective) assurance

Biblical Usage

Blessed is the man who makes the Lord his trust, the psalmist says the Lord has been his trust from his youth, Proverbs says trust in the Lord is the reason the word is taught, and Jeremiah blesses the man whose hope the Lord is. The word is trust itself, the refuge a man puts his weight on.

Theological Significance

I believe trust is the one refuge that never gives way: the proud turn aside to lies, but the man who makes the Lord his trust is blessed, and his hope is the Lord Himself. The proud turn aside to lies, but the man who makes the Lord his trust is blessed.
