mibṭâch

Word
מִבְטָח
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
mibṭâch
Strongs Number
H4009
Definition
properly, a refuge, i.e. (objective) security, or (subjective) assurance
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) trust, confidence, refuge; 1a) act of confiding; 1b) object of confidence; 1c) state of confidence, security
