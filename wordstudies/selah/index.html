selah

A musical and liturgical marker in the Psalms, likely signalling a pause to reflect, lift up the voice, or let the truth settle.

Biblical Usage

Selah appears 71 times in the Psalms and three times in Habakkuk 3, always after a weighty statement about God. Whatever its exact musical meaning, it creates space to stop and consider.

Theological Significance

Selah teaches the rhythm of hope: pause, breathe and ponder who God is before rushing on. Reflection turns true words into anchored convictions.
