châkâh

properly, to adhere to; hence, to await

Biblical Usage

Chakah means to wait, to tarry, to hold on. The four lepers said if they tarried till morning light, mischief would come on them; Job says men long for death and dig for it like hidden treasure; the psalm says our soul waits for the LORD, our help and shield; and Isaiah says he will wait on the LORD who hides His face from the house of Jacob.

Theological Significance

I believe waiting is the Bible's hardest work: the lepers waited wrongly, Job waited in darkness, and Isaiah waited on a hidden face. The waiting that saves is the waiting that looks for Him.
