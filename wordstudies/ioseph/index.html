Iōsḗph

of Hebrew origin (H3130); Joseph, the name of seven Israelites:--Joseph.

Biblical Usage

Joseph is the name of the patriarch, the husband of Mary, and the rich man of Arimathea, and the New Testament holds them apart by their deeds. In Matthew 1:20 the angel tells Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife, in Matthew 27:59 Joseph of Arimathea takes the body of Jesus and wraps it in clean linen, and in Acts 7:9 the patriarchs, moved with envy, sold Joseph into Egypt.

Theological Significance

I hold that the three Josephs show three kinds of faithfulness: one believed a dream, one gave his tomb, and one forgave the brothers who sold him.
