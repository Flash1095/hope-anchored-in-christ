empíplēmi

or ἐμπλήθω from G1722 and the base of G4118; to fill in (up), i.e. (by implication) to satisfy (literally or figuratively):--fill.

Biblical Usage

To fill up, to satisfy. Mary sings that God has filled the hungry with good things in Luke 1:53, and after the feeding of the five thousand the disciples were filled and gathered up the fragments in John 6:12.

Theological Significance

I believe God fills the hungry and sends the full away empty — His filling is for the needy, and I would rather come hungry than come full.
