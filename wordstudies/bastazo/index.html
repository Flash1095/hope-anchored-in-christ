bastázō

perhaps remotely derived from the base of G939 (through the idea of removal); to lift, literally or figuratively (endure, declare, sustain, receive, etc.):--bear, carry, take up.

Biblical Usage

Bastazo means to lift, to bear, to carry a load. Jesus takes our infirmities and bears our sicknesses, Paul tells us to bear one another's burdens and so fulfil the law of Christ, Jesus bears His own cross to Golgotha, and He says whoever does not bear his cross and come after Him cannot be His disciple.

Theological Significance

I believe bearing is the shape of the Christian life, because Jesus bore our sicknesses first and then asks us to bear one another's burdens and our own cross. The load is real and the command is plain, and I hold that no one bears alone what Christ has already carried.
