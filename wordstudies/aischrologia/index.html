aischrología

from G150 and G3056; vile conversation:--filthy communication.

Biblical Usage

Once, in Paul's list of what believers must put off: anger, wrath, malice, blasphemy, filthy communication out of your mouth. The word is foul talk — speech that is shameful to hear and shameful to say.

Theological Significance

One word for foul talk carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is Paul's list, where dirty speech sits beside anger and malice as a garment to be put off.
