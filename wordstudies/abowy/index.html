ʼăbôwy

want

Biblical Usage

This word for woe or want occurs once, in Proverbs 23:29: 'Who hath woe? who hath sorrow? who hath contentions? who hath babbling? who hath wounds without cause? who hath redness of eyes?' It opens the proverb's portrait of the drunkard — six questions, and the answer is the man who tarries long at the wine.

Theological Significance

One word opens a whole portrait of ruin — woe, sorrow, contentions, babbling, wounds, red eyes — and I believe the proverb asks ‘who hath woe?’ so that I will answer honestly and turn away before the woe is mine.
