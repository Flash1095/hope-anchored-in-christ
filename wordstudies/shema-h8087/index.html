Shemaʻ

Shema, the name of a place in Palestine and of four Israelites

Biblical Usage

Shema = "hear"; n pr m; 1) a Reubenite, son of Joel, father of Azaz, and grandfather of Bela; 2) a Judaite, son of Hebron and father of Raham; 3) a Benjamite, son of Elpaal who along with his brother Beriah were heads of their fathers houses in Aijalon and who drove out the inhabitants of Gath. Probably the same as 'Shimhi'; 4) an Israelite who stood on the right hand of Ezra as he read the Law to the people
