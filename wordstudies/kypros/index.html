Kýpros

of uncertain origin; Cyprus, an island in the Mediterranean:--Cyprus.

Biblical Usage

Cyprus is the island where the gospel first sailed west. In Acts 11:19 believers scattered by the persecution about Stephen travelled as far as Cyprus, in Acts 13:4 Paul and Barnabas sailed to Cyprus on the first missionary journey, and in Acts 15:39 Barnabas took Mark and sailed to Cyprus after parting from Paul.

Theological Significance

My position is that Cyprus is where the church learned to go: persecution scattered the believers, the Spirit sent the apostles, and the island became a sending ground, not just a refuge. The church learned to go because it was first driven out.
