nâtsal

to snatch away, whether in a good or a bad sense

Biblical Usage

At the burning bush the LORD comes down to deliver His people out of the hand of the Egyptians, and David says the same LORD delivered him out of the paw of the lion and will deliver him out of the hand of the Philistine. The king is told that God will deliver him out of the hand of all his enemies, and the righteous cry and the LORD delivers them out of all their troubles.

Theological Significance

I believe deliverance is God's trade: He snatches His people out of hands too strong for them, whether the hand is Egypt's, the lion's, or the Philistine's. What I cannot tear myself away from, He tears me away from.
