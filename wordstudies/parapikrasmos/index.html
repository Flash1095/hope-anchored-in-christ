parapikrasmós

from G3893; irritation:--provocation.

Biblical Usage

Parapikrasmos is provocation, the bitter rebellion in the wilderness. Hebrews 3:8 and 3:15 both quote the same warning: harden not your hearts, as in the provocation, in the day of temptation in the wilderness.

Theological Significance

I hold that the wilderness provocation is not ancient history, it is the warning written over every generation that hears God and refuses Him.
