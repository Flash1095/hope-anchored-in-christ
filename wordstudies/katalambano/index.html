katalambánō

Word
καταλαμβάνω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
katalambánō
Strongs Number
G2638
Definition
from G2596 and G2983; to take eagerly, i.e. seize, possess, etc. (literally or figuratively):--apprehend, attain, come upon, comprehend, find, obtain, perceive, (over-)take.
