nomikós

from G3551; according (or pertaining) to law, i.e. legal (ceremonially); as noun, an expert in the (Mosaic) law:--about the law, lawyer.

Biblical Usage

A lawyer is an expert in the law, and the lawyers of the Gospels keep testing Jesus. One asks Him a question, tempting Him; the Pharisees and lawyers reject the counsel of God by refusing John's baptism; another stands up and tempts Him about eternal life — and Paul tells Titus to avoid foolish questions and strivings about the law.

Theological Significance

I believe the lawyers show the law's danger: it can be used to test Jesus instead of to obey Him, and the questions that tempt are the ones to avoid. The lawyer who tempted Jesus about the greatest commandment heard the answer from the law itself.
