ousía

from the feminine of G5607; substance, i.e. property (possessions):--goods, substance.

Biblical Usage

Goods, or substance, is what the prodigal asked for and wasted. He said, Father, give me the portion of goods that falleth to me, and not many days after he gathered all together and took his journey into a far country, and there wasted his substance with riotous living.

Theological Significance

My position is that the portion of goods is the picture of what I do with the Father's gifts: the younger son wasted his substance in riotous living, and the Father still ran to meet him.
