Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼÂven
Strongs Number
H206
Definition
Aven, the contemptuous synonym of three places, one in Coele-Syria, one in Egypt (On), and one in Palestine (Bethel)
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Aven = "vanity"; 1) a name used contemptuously for the following places of idolatrous worship; 1a) a city in Egypt, possibly On (Eze 30:17); 1b) Bethel with its calf worship (Ho 10:8); 1c) A town or region in Syria (Am 1:5)
