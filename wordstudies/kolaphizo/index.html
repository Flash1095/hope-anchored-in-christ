kolaphízō

from a derivative of the base of G2849; to rap with the fist:--buffet.

Biblical Usage

Kolaphizo means to buffet, to strike with the fist. Matthew 26:67 has the council buffeting Jesus and smiting Him with their palms, Mark 14:65 has the servants buffeting Him and saying, prophesy, 1 Corinthians 4:11 has Paul saying, we are buffeted, and have no certain dwellingplace, 2 Corinthians 12:7 calls the thorn in the flesh a messenger of Satan to buffet Paul, and 1 Peter 2:20 asks what glory it is to take patiently a buffeting for your faults.

Theological Significance

I believe the buffet is the suffering that has no dignity in it: spat on, struck with palms, buffeted by servants. I hold that Paul's thorn and Peter's question belong together, because the buffet for Christ's sake is taken patiently, and the buffet for our faults is taken quietly.
