eáō

of uncertain affinity; to let be, i.e. permit or leave alone:--commit, leave, let (alone), suffer. See also G1436.

Biblical Usage

To allow, to permit, to let alone, as God will not suffer you to be tempted above what you are able, and in times past He suffered all nations to walk in their own ways. Jesus did not suffer the devils to speak because they knew Him, and the goodman of the house would not have suffered his house to be broken up if he had known the hour.

Theological Significance

I believe God permits what He does not approve, and His permission is never His approval. He suffered the nations to walk their own ways for a season, yet He never left Himself without witness, and He will not suffer my temptation to outrun my strength.
