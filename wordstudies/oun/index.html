oûn

apparently a primary word; (adverbially) certainly, or (conjunctionally) accordingly:--and (so, truly), but, now (then), so (likewise then), then, therefore, verily, wherefore.

Biblical Usage

Therefore is the hinge of every argument in the New Testament. Paul asks, Who then is Paul, and who is Apollos, but ministers by whom ye believed, and Peter says, Wherefore laying aside all malice and all guile, and hypocrisies, and envies, and all evil speakings, desire the sincere milk of the word.

Theological Significance

I believe therefore is the word that ties my duty to God's grace: because of what He has done, I lay aside malice and envy and evil speakings.
