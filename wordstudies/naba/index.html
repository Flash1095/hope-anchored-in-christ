nâbâʼ

to prophesy, i.e. speak (or sing) by inspiration (in prediction or simple discourse)

Biblical Usage

Naba means to prophesy, to speak by inspiration, and it runs from the elders in the wilderness to the promise of the Spirit. In Numbers 11:25 the Spirit rests on the seventy elders and they prophesy, and in 1 Samuel 10:5 Saul meets a company of prophets coming down from the high place. In Ezekiel 37:9 the prophet is told to prophesy to the wind, that the breath may come upon the slain, that they may live, and in Joel 2:28 sons and daughters shall prophesy when the Spirit is poured out.

Theological Significance

I believe prophesying is not guessing the future, it is speaking what God has given to speak. The Spirit that rested on the elders and told Ezekiel to prophesy to the wind is the same Spirit Joel promised to pour out on all flesh, and I hold that God still speaks through His word today.
