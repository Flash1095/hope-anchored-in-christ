bēma

from the base of G939; a step, i.e. foot-breath; by implication, a rostrum, i.e. a tribunal:--judgment-seat, set (foot) on, throne.

Biblical Usage

bema covers a judgment seat, and it appears 12 times across the Bible. Paul says we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, and the same word names the raised platform where Pilate sat.

Theological Significance

I hold that the judgment seat of Christ is real and coming, and that knowing I will stand there makes me want to live honestly now. That is why I keep coming back to it: bema is not a word to rush past.
