môwdaʻath

acquaintance

Biblical Usage

Modaath is a kinswoman, an acquaintance of the family — is not Boaz of our kindred, Naomi asks Ruth, with whose maidens thou wast?

Theological Significance

I believe the kinswoman's question opens the whole redemption: a relative is near, and nearness is what saves.
