maṭṭâh

downward, below or beneath; often adverbially with or without prefixes

Biblical Usage

Below or beneath is the word for the low place, and Moses uses it for the curse and the blessing together: if Israel obeys, it will be above only and not beneath, and if it disobeys, the stranger will get above it. Proverbs says the way of life is above to the wise, that he may depart from hell beneath.

Theological Significance

I hold that above and beneath are not about geography but about who rules: obedience lifts a people up, and disobedience puts them under, and the wise man walks the way that leads up.
