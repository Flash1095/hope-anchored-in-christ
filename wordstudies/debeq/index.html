debeq

a joint; by implication, solder

Biblical Usage

The word names the joint where the spear finds its way between the plates of armour, and the solder that holds metal together. Ahab is wounded at the joint of his harness, and the workman strengthens the solder of the idol. The joint is the weak place and the joining place both.

Theological Significance

I believe the joint is a picture of the body: where we are joined we are strong, and where the joint fails the whole armour fails. The joint is where the body holds together, and the body that holds together is the body that stands.
