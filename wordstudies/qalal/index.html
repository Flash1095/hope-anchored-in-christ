qâlal

to be (causatively, make) light, literally (swift, small, sharp, etc.) or figuratively (easy, trifling, vile, etc.)

Biblical Usage

To make light covers both curse and relief: God tells Eli that they that despise Him shall be lightly esteemed, Goliath cursed David by his gods, the people begged Rehoboam to make the heavy yoke lighter. Ahab treated walking in Jeroboam's sins as a light thing.

Theological Significance

I believe the word warns both ways: making light of God is a curse, and making a burden lighter is mercy, and wisdom is knowing which is which. The same tongue that curses can also lighten a load, and the difference is whether it serves God or itself.
