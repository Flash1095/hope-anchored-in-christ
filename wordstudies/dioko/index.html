diṓkō

a prolonged (and causative) form of a primary verb δίω (to flee; compare the base of G1169 and G1249); to pursue (literally or figuratively); by implication, to persecute:--ensue, follow (after), given to, (suffer) persecute(-ion), press forward.

Biblical Usage

To pursue, the same word that covers chasing after righteousness and being chased for it. Paul presses toward the mark for the prize, tells the Romans to pursue hospitality, and Jesus blesses those who are persecuted for righteousness' sake.

Theological Significance

I believe the Christian life is a pursuit, not a drift. I press on because Christ first pursued me, and the prize is not my effort but the upward call of God in Him.
