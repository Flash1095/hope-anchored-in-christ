basanistḗs

from G928; a torturer:--tormentor.

Biblical Usage

A basanistes is a torturer, a jailer who torments, and the unforgiving servant is delivered to the tormentors until he pays all that he owes.

Theological Significance

I hold that the tormentors in the parable are the end of unforgiveness, because the servant who would not forgive is given over to those who will not forgive him.
