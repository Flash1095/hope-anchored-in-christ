hălâk

properly, a journey, i.e. (by implication) toll on goods at a road

Biblical Usage

hălâk is the word for properly, a journey, i.e. (by implication) toll on goods at a road. In Ezra 4:13, the text says "then] will they not pay toll." In Ezra 4:20, the text says "and toll."

Theological Significance

I take hălâk to mean that obedience is not drudgery; it is trusting that God's way is the good way. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
