mahpeketh

a wrench, i.e. the stocks

Biblical Usage

Mahpeketh is the stocks, the wrench that held a man fast, and its sharpest appearances belong to Jeremiah: Jeremiah 20:2 has Pashur putting the prophet in the stocks in the high gate of Benjamin, and Jeremiah 20:3 has him brought out of the stocks the next morning.

Theological Significance

I take it that the stocks could hold Jeremiah's body for a night but not his word, which came out the next morning hotter than before.
