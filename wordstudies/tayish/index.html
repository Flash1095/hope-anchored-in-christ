tayish

a buck or he-goat (as given to butting)

Biblical Usage

The word is the he-goat, the buck. Laban removed the he-goats from the flock on the day Jacob's wages were set, and Jacob sent Esau two hundred she-goats and twenty he-goats as a present. Proverbs names the he-goat among the four things that go well, with the lion and the greyhound and the king.

Theological Significance

I hold that the he-goat's place in the proverb is about a creature that walks without fear, and the king is counted with it.
