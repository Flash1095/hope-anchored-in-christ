asteîos

from ἄστυ (a city); urbane, i.e. (by implication) handsome:--fair.

Biblical Usage

Moses was fair to God as a child, and his parents hid him by faith because they saw he was a beautiful child. The word means handsome, but both places tie his beauty to God's purpose for him.

Theological Significance

I believe God can mark a child for His work before anyone knows what that work will be, and a mother's eyes saw it first.
