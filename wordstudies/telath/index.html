tᵉlath

a tertiary rank

Biblical Usage

The word is a third, a third part, a rank. Belshazzar promises that whoever reads the writing shall be the third ruler in the kingdom, and Daniel is clothed with scarlet and given the third place.

Theological Significance

I hold that the third place in a falling kingdom is a poor prize, and Daniel took it without asking for it.
