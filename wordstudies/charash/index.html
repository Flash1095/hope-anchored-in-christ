chârash

Word
חָרַשׁ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
chârash
Strongs Number
H2790
Definition
to scratch, i.e. (by implication) to engrave, plough; hence (from the use of tools) to fabricate (of any material); figuratively, to devise (in a bad sense); hence (from the idea of secrecy) to be silent, to let alone; hence (by implication) to be deaf (as an accompaniment of dumbness)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to cut in, plough, engrave, devise; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to cut in, engrave; 1a2) to plough; 1a3) to devise; 1b) (Niphal) to be ploughed; 1c) (Hiphil) to plot evil; 2) to be silent, be dumb, be speechless, be deaf; 2a) (Qal); 2a1) to be silent; 2a2) to be deaf; 2b) (Hiphil); 2b1) to be silent, keep quiet; 2b2) to make silent; 2b3) to be deaf, show deafness; 2c) (Hithpael) to remain silent
