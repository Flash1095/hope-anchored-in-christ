prosaitéō

from G4314 and G154; to ask repeatedly (importune), i.e. solicit:--beg.

Biblical Usage

Prosaito means to beg, to ask again and again. It appears once, when the neighbours of the blind man ask, Is not this he that sat and begged?

Theological Significance

I believe the beggar's old life is the point: everyone knew him by his begging, and no one could deny the change once Jesus opened his eyes.
