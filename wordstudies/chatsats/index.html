châtsats

properly, to chop into, pierce or sever; hence, to curtail, to distribute (into ranks); to shoot an arrow

Biblical Usage

This word cuts and divides: it shoots arrows, it shares out into ranks, and it cuts a life short. The song of Deborah hears the noise of archers at the watering places, the locusts march out in bands with no king, and Job asks what pleasure a man has in his house when his months are cut off.

Theological Significance

I hold that God sets the bands and the boundaries: the locusts march in order without a king, and a man's months are numbered by a hand greater than his own.
