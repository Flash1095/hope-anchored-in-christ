ʻal

properly, the top; specifically, the highest (i.e. God); also (adverb) aloft, to Jehovah

Biblical Usage

The word means the height — the top, the highest place. Isaac blesses Esau with the dew of heaven from above; Jacob's blessing speaks of blessings above; David is called the man raised up on high; and twice Hosea names God Himself the most High — the One Israel will not return to, though the prophets call them upward.

Theological Significance

A word for the height carries little weight on its own; its weight is Hosea's accusation, that Israel calls God the Most High with its lips and walks away with its feet.
