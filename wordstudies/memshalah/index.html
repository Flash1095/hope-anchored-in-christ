memshâlâh

rule; also (concretely in plural) a realm or a ruler

Biblical Usage

Memshalah is dominion, rule, the reach of a king or of God. Genesis 1:16 gives the greater light dominion over the day and the lesser light dominion over the night, and 1 Kings 9:19 counts the cities Solomon built in all the land of his dominion. 2 Chronicles 32:9 sends Sennacherib against Judah with all his power, and Daniel 11:5 promises a king of the south whose dominion shall be a great dominion.

Theological Significance

I believe dominion belongs first to the lights God set in the sky, and every king after them rules on loan. My position is that the greatest dominion in Daniel is still a borrowed one, and it ends where God's begins.
