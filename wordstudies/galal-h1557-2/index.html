gâlâl

dung (as in balls)

Biblical Usage

Galal is dung, and it appears once, in the judgment on Jeroboam's house in 1 Kings 14:10. God says He will take away the remnant of the house of Jeroboam, as a man takes away dung, till it is all gone. The word is as blunt as the judgment it carries.

Theological Significance

I believe God speaks bluntly about the house that leads His people into sin; Jeroboam's line is taken away like dung, and I hold that a house built on idolatry ends in the same place as refuse.
