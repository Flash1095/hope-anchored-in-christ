ídios

of uncertain affinity; pertaining to self, i.e. one's own; by implication, private or separate:--X his acquaintance, when they were alone, apart, aside, due, his (own, proper, several), home, (her, our, thine, your) own (business), private(-ly), proper, severally, their (own).

Biblical Usage

His own is a small phrase that draws a line around what belongs to a person. In 1 Corinthians 3:8 every man shall receive his own reward according to his own labour, in 1 Corinthians 7:2 every man should have his own wife, and in 1 Corinthians 7:7 Paul wishes all men were even as himself, but every man hath his proper gift of God.

Theological Significance

I believe the word keeps me honest: my reward follows my labour, my gift is my own, and I answer for what God gave me, not for what he gave someone else.
