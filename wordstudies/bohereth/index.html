bôhereth

a whitish spot on the skin

Biblical Usage

A bright spot is the word for a white patch on the skin, and it appears eleven times, all in the law of leprosy. In Leviticus 13:2 a man with a rising, a scab, or a bright spot is brought to Aaron the priest. In Leviticus 13:4 the priest shuts up the plague seven days if the bright spot is white in the skin. In Leviticus 13:19 a bright spot after a boil is examined the same way, and in Leviticus 14:56 the law is summed up for the rising, the scab, and the bright spot.

Theological Significance

I believe the bright spot shows how careful God is with his people: the priest looks, waits seven days, and looks again before he calls anything unclean.
