bôçer

{an immature grape}

Biblical Usage

Sour grapes are unripe grapes, and the word is famous for a proverb God rejects. In Isaiah 18:5 the sour grape is ripening in the flower, before the harvest. In Jeremiah 31:29 the proverb says the fathers have eaten a sour grape, and the children's teeth are set on edge — and in Jeremiah 31:30 God answers, every one shall die for his own iniquity. Ezekiel 18:2 quotes the same proverb and gives the same answer.

Theological Significance

I believe God hates the sour-grape proverb because it blames the fathers for the children's sin, and he will have each man answer for his own.
