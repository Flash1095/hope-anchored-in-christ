ʼArnôwn

the Arnon, a river east of the Jordan, also its territory

Biblical Usage

The Arnon is the river that marked the border of Moab, and Israel's story crosses it again and again. They camp on the far side of the Arnon, Moses commands them to rise up and pass over it against Sihon, and Joshua lists the kings they defeated from the Arnon to Mount Hermon.

Theological Significance

A river that keeps turning up as a border tells me how God measures out the land: here is yours, there is theirs, and the crossing is the moment the promise starts to be kept.
