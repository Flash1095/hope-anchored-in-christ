ploutéō

from G4148; to be (or become) wealthy (literally or figuratively):--be increased with goods, (be made, wax) rich.

Biblical Usage

Plouto means to be rich, to become wealthy. God fills the hungry with good things and sends the rich away empty in Luke 1:53, and Christ, though He was rich, for our sakes became poor, that we might be rich, in 2 Corinthians 8:9. Jesus tells Laodicea to buy gold tried in the fire, that she may be rich, in Revelation 3:18, and Romans 10:12 says the Lord is rich unto all that call upon Him.

Theological Significance

I believe true riches run the other way — Christ became poor so I could be rich, and the rich sent away empty were full of themselves. My position is that I am rich only in Him.
