ʻâkar

Word
עָכַר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻâkar
Strongs Number
H5916
Definition
properly, to roil water; figuratively, to disturb or affict
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to trouble, stir up, disturb, make (someone) taboo; 1a) (Qal) to disturb, trouble; 1b) (Niphal); 1b1) to be disturbed, be stirred up; 1b2) disturbance, calamity (participle)
