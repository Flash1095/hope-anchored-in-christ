Regem Melek

Regem-Melek, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Regem-melech was sent with Sharezer to the house of God to pray before the LORD and to ask the priests whether the fasts of the exile should still be kept. The question they brought was whether the fasts of the fifth and seventh months should still be kept now that the exiles had come home.

Theological Significance

My position is that the question he carried is the question of every returning exile: does God still want the sorrow, or is it time to feast?
