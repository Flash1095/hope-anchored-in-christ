pᵉlach

to serve or worship

Biblical Usage

Pelach is the Aramaic word for serving and worshipping, and Daniel is its stage. The Chaldeans accused Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego of serving not Nebuchadnezzar's gods, and the three answered that the God they serve could deliver them from the furnace. Darius told Daniel that the God he served continually would deliver him from the lions, and Daniel's vision gives the Son of Man a kingdom where all peoples serve Him forever.

Theological Significance

I hold that serving God is not a private opinion but a public stand: the three young men and Daniel served Him where everyone could see, and God honoured it. I hold that the question is never whether I will serve something — everyone serves something — but whom. The gods of Babylon demand a bow and give nothing; the God of Daniel delivers, and His kingdom has no end. That is why I serve Him, and Him alone.
