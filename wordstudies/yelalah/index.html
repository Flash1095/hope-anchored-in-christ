yᵉlâlâh

{a howl}

Biblical Usage

In the Bible, yᵉlâlâh means a howl. In Isaiah 15:8, the howling thereof unto Eglaim, and it appears again in Jeremiah 25:36.

Theological Significance

I hear in yᵉlâlâh the sound of a people undone, and I believe God records the howl because He hears it too.
