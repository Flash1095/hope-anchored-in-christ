ʼêtsel

a side; (as a preposition) near

Biblical Usage

ʼÊtsel means beside — at the side of, near, close by. Joseph's garment is left beside her; the child is taken from beside its mother; and the man of God is slain by the lion beside the way. It is the word for nearness, for being at someone's side.

Theological Significance

I believe ʼêtsel is a word for the nearness God gives: He is beside me, not far off. I hold that the side of the road, the side of the bed, the side of the throne — Scripture keeps putting people beside each other, and God beside them.
