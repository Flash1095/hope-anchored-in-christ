ʼĂchîyâh

Achijah, the name of nine Israelites

Biblical Usage

Two men share this name, and both matter: Ahijah the Shilonite, the prophet who tore his garment and gave ten pieces to Jeroboam, and Ahiah son of Ahitub, the priest who wore the ephod with Saul's army.

Theological Significance

I hold the prophet's torn garment as the greater of the two stories: God's word does not merely describe the future, it divides it. When Ahijah tore the cloth, the kingdom was already torn.
