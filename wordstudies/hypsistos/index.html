hýpsistos

superlative from the base of G5311; highest, i.e. (masculine singular) the Supreme (God), or (neuter plural) the heavens:--most high, highest.

Biblical Usage

The word hýpsistos carries the sense of highest, most high. In Matthew 21:9, the text says "Hosanna in the highest." In Luke 1:32, the text says "and shall be called the Son of the Highest."

Theological Significance

I take hýpsistos to mean that God is majestic, and the right response to majesty is worship, not familiarity. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
