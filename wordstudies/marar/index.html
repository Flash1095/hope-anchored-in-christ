mârar

to be (causatively, make) bitter (literally or figuratively)

Biblical Usage

mârar is to be bitter, or to make bitter. In Exodus 1:14 the Egyptians make Israel's lives bitter with hard bondage, in mortar and in brick and in all service in the field. In 2 Kings 4:27 the Shunammite's soul is vexed within her, and in Daniel 8:7 and Daniel 11:11 kings are moved with choler, bitter anger, against their enemies. The word is the taste of oppression and of rage.

Theological Significance

I believe mârar shows me that bitterness is the fruit of bondage. I hold that God who heard Israel in Egypt hears me in mine.
