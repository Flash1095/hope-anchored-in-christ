ʼîylân

a tree

Biblical Usage

A tree is what Nebuchadnezzar sees in his dream, and Daniel tells him the tree is the king himself. In Daniel 4:10 a tree grows in the midst of the earth, in Daniel 4:11 it grows and is strong and its height reaches unto heaven, in Daniel 4:20 the tree that thou sawest is thou, O king, and in Daniel 4:23 the watcher commands to cut it down but leave the stump.

Theological Significance

I hold that the tree is the proudest image in the Bible: a king grown tall as heaven, cut down in a night. He is left with his stump until he learns that heaven rules.
