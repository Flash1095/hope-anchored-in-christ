nótos

of uncertain affinity; the south(-west) wind; by extension, the southern quarter itself:--south (wind).

Biblical Usage

The south is a wind, a queen, and a wall of the city. The queen of the south rises in judgment because she came from the ends of the earth to hear Solomon's wisdom; the south wind blows softly on Paul's voyage and lures the ship out; and the New Jerusalem has three gates on the south.

Theological Significance

I believe the south wind is the test of the sailor: it blew softly and promised an easy voyage, and the storm came after it. The queen of the south will rise in judgment, and the gates of the city stand open on the south — the wind, the queen, and the wall all point the same way.
