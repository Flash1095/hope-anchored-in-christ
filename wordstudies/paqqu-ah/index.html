paqquʻâh

the wild cucumber (from splitting open to shed its seeds)

Biblical Usage

Paqqu'âh is the wild gourd, the wild cucumber. A servant goes out into the field to gather herbs, finds a wild vine, and gathers wild gourds his lap full, and shreds them into the pot of pottage, not knowing what they were, until the sons of the prophets cry, There is death in the pot.

Theological Significance

I hold that the wild gourd is a warning against gathering what I do not know: good intentions in the kitchen can still poison the pot.
