Mêyshâʼ

Word
מֵישָׁא
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Mêyshâʼ
Strongs Number
H4331
Definition
Mesha, a place in Arabia; also an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Mesha = "freedom"; 1) a Benjamite, son of Shaharaim by his wife Hodesh who bore him in the land of Moab
