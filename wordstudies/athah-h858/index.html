ʼâthâh

Word
אָתָה
Language
Aramaic
Transliteration
ʼâthâh
Strongs Number
H858
Definition
{to arrive}
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to come, arrive; 1a) (P'al) to come; 1b) (Aphel) to bring; 1c) (Hophal) to be brought; 2) used in the NT in the phrase "maranatha" -"Lord come"
