ʼĂbîyʻezer

Abiezer, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Abiezer is a Manassite clan name and a man’s name. The clan gets its lot in Joshua 17; when Gideon blows the trumpet, ‘Abiezer was gathered after him’; and Abiezer the Anethothite stands among David’s mighty men.

Theological Significance

The smallest clan in Manasseh gives Israel its judge, and a man of that name stands among David's thirty. I believe Abiezer's story is Gideon's story: God picks the least family and the least man, and the least turns out to be exactly enough.
