mōrós

probably from the base of G3466; dull or stupid (as if shut up), i.e. heedless, (morally) blockhead, (apparently) absurd:--fool(-ish, X -ishness).

Biblical Usage

Moros means foolish, dull, heedless — Jesus calls the man who hears His sayings and does them not a foolish man who builds on sand, and five of the virgins are foolish because they take no oil. Whoever calls his brother a fool is in danger of hell fire, and the foolishness of God is wiser than men.

Theological Significance

I believe the fool in Scripture is not the man who lacks brains but the man who hears and does not do. Hearing without doing is the whole definition.
