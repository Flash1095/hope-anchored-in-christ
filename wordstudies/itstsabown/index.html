ʻitstsâbôwn

worrisomeness, i.e. labor or pain

Biblical Usage

Sorrow in labour is the curse God speaks over the ground and over childbirth. In Genesis 3:16 he tells the woman, I will greatly multiply thy sorrow and thy conception, in Genesis 3:17 he tells the man, in sorrow shalt thou eat of the ground all the days of thy life, and in Genesis 5:29 Lamech names his son Noah, saying, this same shall comfort us concerning our work and toil of our hands.

Theological Significance

I believe the sorrow is real and the comfort is real too: the ground is cursed. A son named Rest is promised in the middle of it.
