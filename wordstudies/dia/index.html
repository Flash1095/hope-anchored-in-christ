diá

a primary preposition denoting the channel of an act; through (in very wide applications, local, causal, or occasional):--after, always, among, at, to avoid, because of (that), briefly, by, for (cause) … fore, from, in, by occasion of, of, by reason of, for sake, that, thereby, therefore, X though, through(-out), to, wherefore, with (-in). In composition it retains the same general importance.

Biblical Usage

Through is the little word that carries the whole gospel, because it names the channel everything comes by. All things were made through Christ, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus, and we are saved through faith, not by our own doing.

Theological Significance

I believe through is the word that keeps grace from becoming my achievement. Everything I have came through Christ, and everything I do for God goes through Him too, so He gets the glory and I get the gift.
