ʻAzmâveth

Azmaveth, the name of three Israelites and of a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Azmaveth is the name of three men and a place — a son of Jehoadah in Benjamin's line, one of David's mighty men, the king's treasurer over the storehouses, and a town in Benjamin where the singers lived. The name turns up in the wars, in the court, and in the choir, and each bearer is a different man.

Theological Significance

I believe a name that belongs to a warrior, a treasurer and a singer's town is a name that shows how wide God's service is — the same Lord, served in battle, in the storehouse, and in song.
