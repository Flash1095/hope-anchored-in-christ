regel

a foot (as used in walking); by implication, a step; by euphemistically the pudenda

Biblical Usage

Put off thy shoes from off thy feet, for the place whereon thou standest is holy ground. Thy word is a lamp unto my feet and a light unto my path, and how beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that bringeth good tidings. God makes my feet like hinds' feet and sets me upon my high places, and the prophet sings the same in Habakkuk.

Theological Significance

I believe the feet are where faith is proved, because they carry us where we say we will go. Holy ground makes a man take off his shoes, the word lights the next step, and the God who gives hinds' feet gives them for the high places, not the flat road. Where your feet go is where your heart already is.
