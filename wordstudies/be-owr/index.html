Bᵉʻôwr

Word
בְּעוֹר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Bᵉʻôwr
Strongs Number
H1160
Definition
Beor, the name of the father of an Edomitish king; also of that of Balaam
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Beor = "burning"; 1) father of Balaam; 2) father of Bela, king of Edom
