eisdéchomai

from G1519 and G1209; to take into one's favor:--receive.

Biblical Usage

This word means to take into one's favour, to receive with welcome, and Paul uses it once, quoting the prophet. Come out from among them, and be separate, and I will receive you, says the Lord, and the promise that follows is that He will be a Father to us and we shall be His sons and daughters. One word of welcome holds the whole adoption.

Theological Significance

I believe God's welcome is the reason to come out from the world, because separation is not His punishment but the way into His arms.
