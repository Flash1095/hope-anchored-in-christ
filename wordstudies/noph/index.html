Nôph

Noph, the capital of Upper Egypt

Biblical Usage

Noph is Memphis, the capital of Upper Egypt, and the prophets name it in the day of Egypt's judgment. Its princes are deceived and seduce Egypt, the children of Noph break the crown of Judah's head, and God says He will destroy the idols and cause the images to cease out of Noph.

Theological Significance

I believe the idols of Noph fall like the idols of every capital: the princes are deceived first, and the images follow them.
