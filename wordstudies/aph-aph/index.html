ʻaphʻaph

an eyelash (as fluttering); figuratively, morning ray

Biblical Usage

The eyelids — Job cursing the day of his birth and longing for the eyelids of the morning never to open on it, the psalmist's eyes watching the sons of men, and the wise man's call to let your eyelids look straight before you. The same word names the first light of dawn, the eyelids of the morning breaking over Leviathan.

Theological Significance

I believe this small word shows how closely God watches and how plainly I am told to watch too. His eyes are on the sons of men, and my eyes are meant to look straight ahead, not wander after trouble.
