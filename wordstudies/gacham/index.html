Gacham

Gacham, a son of Nahor

Biblical Usage

Gacham is a son of Nahor, named once in Genesis 22:24, where Reumah the concubine bore Tebah, and Gaham, and Thahash, and Maachah. The King James spells his name Gaham. He belongs to the family of Abraham's brother, the line that stayed in Haran.

Theological Significance

I believe the families around Abraham matter because they show how wide God's story runs. Gacham never leaves Haran, yet his name is kept, and I hold that no family is too far from Abraham for God to remember it.
