dōron

a present; specially, a sacrifice:--gift, offering.

Biblical Usage

A gift, an offering, the gift laid on the altar, and the gifts the wise men opened before the child. Jesus says if I remember my brother has something against me, I must leave my gift at the altar and be reconciled first. The leper He cleansed was told to offer the gift Moses commanded, and every high priest is appointed to offer gifts and sacrifices.

Theological Significance

I believe God values the heart behind the gift more than the gift itself. The wise men gave gold, frankincense, and myrrh, but the offering He wants most from me is a reconciled heart laid on the altar before the gift.
