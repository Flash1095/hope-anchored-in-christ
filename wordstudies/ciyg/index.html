çîyg

scoria

Biblical Usage

Dross is the scum that rises when silver is refined, and the psalm says God puts away the wicked like dross. Proverbs says take away the dross from the silver, and a vessel comes forth for the finer. Isaiah says Judah's silver has become dross, and Ezekiel says the whole house of Israel has become dross in the furnace.

Theological Significance

I believe dross is the Bible's picture of what refining removes, and every believer knows the furnace. God does not throw away the silver; He takes away the dross so the silver can shine. My position is that the fire is not God's anger at His people — it is His care for them.
