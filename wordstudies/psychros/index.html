psychrós

from G5592; chilly (literally or figuratively):--cold.

Biblical Usage

Psychros means cold, chilly. Jesus says, Whosoever shall give to drink unto one of these little ones a cup of cold water only in the name of a disciple, verily I say unto you, he shall in no wise lose his reward. And to Laodicea He says, I know thy works, that thou art neither cold nor hot: I would thou wert cold or hot. So then because thou art lukewarm, and neither cold nor hot, I will spue thee out of My mouth.

Theological Significance

I believe a cup of cold water is the smallest kindness in the Bible, and it is still rewarded, because God notices what men forget. I hold that cold is better than lukewarm in His eyes, because cold can be warmed and lukewarm thinks it needs nothing.
