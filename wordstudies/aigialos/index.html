aigialós

from ἀΐσσω (to rush) and G251 (in the sense of the sea); a beach (on which the waves dash):--shore.

Biblical Usage

The shore is where Jesus teaches the crowds from a boat, where the fishermen sort the catch in the parable of the net, where the risen Lord stands at dawn while the disciples fish, and where Paul's ship runs aground off Malta. It is the edge between the deep and dry land — a place of teaching, sorting, meeting, and shipwreck.

Theological Significance

A beach is only a place, and the word carries no theology; the weight lies in the scenes on that shore, where Jesus taught, sorted, met His friends, and a ship went down.
