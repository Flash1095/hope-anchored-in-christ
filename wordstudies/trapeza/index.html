trápeza

probably contracted from G5064 and G3979; a table or stool (as being four-legged), usually for food (figuratively, a meal); also a counter for money (figuratively, a broker's office for loans at interest):--bank, meat, table.

Biblical Usage

Paul says you cannot partake of the Lord's table and the table of devils, and the twelve say it is not reason to leave the word of God and serve tables. Theological Significance

I believe the word draws the line exactly: one table or the other, and the church must choose which it sits at.
