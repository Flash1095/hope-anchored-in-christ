Kûwshîy

Word
כּוּשִׁי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Kûwshîy
Strongs Number
H3570
Definition
Cushi, the name of two Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Cushi = "their blackness"; 1) an ancestor of Jehudi (Jer. 36.14); 2) the father of the prophet Zephaniah (Zep. 1.1)
