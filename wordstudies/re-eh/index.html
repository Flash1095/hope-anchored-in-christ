rêʻeh

a (male) companion

Biblical Usage

Zabud the priest was the king's friend in Solomon's court, and Hushai the Archite was David's friend who stayed loyal when Absalom rose against him. Proverbs adds the proverb: better is a neighbour that is near than a brother far off.

Theological Significance

My position is that a true friend is proved in the hour when loyalty costs something.
