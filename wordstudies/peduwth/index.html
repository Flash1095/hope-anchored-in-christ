pᵉdûwth

distinction; also deliverance

Biblical Usage

Peduth means a division, a distinction — and by extension, deliverance. God told Pharaoh He would put a division between His people and Pharaoh's people, and Isaiah asks whether God's hand is shortened that it cannot redeem or whether He has no power to deliver.

Theological Significance

I believe deliverance begins with a line drawn: God separates His people from their enemies, and no power on earth can undo the division He makes.
