bōmós

from the base of G939; properly, a stand, i.e. (specially) an altar:--altar.

Biblical Usage

An altar is the word for a stand, a raised place for sacrifice — and it appears once, in Athens. In Acts 17:23 Paul says, as I passed by and beheld your devotions, I found an altar with this inscription, TO THE UNKNOWN GOD. One altar, built for a god its makers did not know.

Theological Significance

I believe the unknown god's altar is Paul's open door: they knew they were missing someone, and he told them who.
