Definition
Bethul (i.e. Bethuel), a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Bethul = "God destroys" or "man of God" or "dweller in God"; 1) a town of Simeon in the south (also spelled 'Bethuel')
