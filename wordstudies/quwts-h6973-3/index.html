qûwts

to be (causatively, make) disgusted or anxious

Biblical Usage

Rezon abhors Israel all the days of Solomon, and the Egyptians are grieved because of the children of Israel — the more they afflict them, the more they multiply and grow. The word is the disgust, the dread that cannot bear the sight.

Theological Significance

I believe the Egyptians' grief at Israel's growth is the proof that God multiplies his people under affliction.
