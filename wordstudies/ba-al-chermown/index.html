Baʻal Chermôwn

Baal-Chermon, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Baal-hermon is the mountain and the district at the foot of Hermon — the border of the half-tribe of Manasseh east of Jordan, and the limit of the land the Canaanites still held. Chronicles says the Manassites increased from Bashan to Baal-hermon, and Judges names it among the nations left to prove Israel.

Theological Significance

I hold that the borders God drew were honest borders: he told Israel what was theirs and what was still to be taken, and he left nations there to prove them.
