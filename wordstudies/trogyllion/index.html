Trōgýllion

of uncertain derivation; Trogyllium, a place in Asia Minor:--Trogyllium.

Biblical Usage

Trogyllium is a place on the coast of Asia Minor, named once in the King James Bible. When Paul sailed from Samos to Miletus, the King James text says the ship tarried at Trogyllium — but the Greek text behind the modern editions does not carry the name here, so the word has no occurrence in the Greek text I checked. I cite the verse as the King James reads it, and I say plainly that the name rests on the older text, not on the Greek of the modern editions.

Theological Significance

I believe the honest thing is to say where a name comes from, and Trogyllium comes to us through the King James text at Acts 20:15.
