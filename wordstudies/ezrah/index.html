ʻezrâh

aid

Biblical Usage

Ezrah is the word for help, and it most often describes help that fails or help that is sought too late. Ahaz took treasure from the house of the LORD and gave it to Assyria, but he helped him not, in 2 Chronicles 28:21. Pharaoh's army came forth to help Judah in Jeremiah 37:7, only to return to Egypt, and Job asks where his help stands in Job 31:21. The word also carries the cry of a man looking for help and finding none in Isaiah 10:3.

Theological Significance

I believe this word warns me where not to look for help. Assyria did not help Ahaz and Egypt did not help Judah, and I hold that help from men always fails in the end. The help that holds is the help of God alone.
