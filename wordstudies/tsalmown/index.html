Tsalmôwn

Tsalmon, the name of a place in Palestine and of an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Tsalmon is both a man and a mountain. Zalmon the Ahohite was one of David's mighty men; Mount Zalmon is where Abimelech cut boughs before burning the tower of Shechem; and the psalmist pictures snow falling white on Zalmon when kings are scattered. One name covers a warrior, a mountain, and a psalm.

Theological Significance

I believe a name that holds a warrior and a mountain is a reminder that God's story is written on real ground by real men. Zalmon's snow and Zalmon's sword belong to the same God.
