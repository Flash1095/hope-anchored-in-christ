mishpâth

a stall for cattle (only dual)

Biblical Usage

The word is a sheepfold, a stall for cattle, and it appears twice at the edges of a battle: Issachar couches between the burdens like a strong ass, and Deborah asks Reuben why he stayed among the sheepfolds listening to the bleating of the flocks while the battle was fought.

Theological Significance

I hold that the sheepfold is the temptation of comfort: the fold is warm and the flocks are bleating, and the battle goes on without you.
