dâshên

fat; figuratively, rich, fertile

Biblical Usage

Fat is the picture of richness and plenty in Scripture: the fat pastures, the fat of the land, and the people who grow fat and flourish like the palm tree. Isaiah promises fat things full of marrow at the feast of the Lord, and the psalmist pictures the righteous flourishing in the courts of God, bearing fruit in old age.

Theological Significance

I believe fatness in Scripture is not excess but abundance: the Lord feeds His people richly, and the flourishing life is the one planted in His house. The fat of the land is the Lord's table, and the people who eat at it are the people He has fed.
