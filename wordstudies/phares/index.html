Pháres

of Hebrew origin (H6557); Phares (i.e. Perets), an Israelite:--Phares.

Biblical Usage

Phares is Perez, the son of Judah and Tamar, named twice in the genealogy of Jesus. Judah begat Phares and Zara of Thamar, and Phares begat Esrom, and Luke names him again in the line from David back to Adam.

Theological Significance

I hold that Perez's place in the genealogy is grace in a name: a child of a tangled story, named in the line of the Messiah.
