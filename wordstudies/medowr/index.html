mᵉdôwr

a dwelling

Biblical Usage

A dwelling is a home, and the Aramaic word is used where the gods cannot live: the Chaldeans tell Nebuchadnezzar that none can show the dream except the gods, whose dwelling is not with flesh. The king is told his dwelling shall be with the beasts of the field, and his heart shall be made like the beasts' heart.

Theological Significance

I believe the word turns on the king: the gods whose dwelling is not with flesh cannot help him, and the king who thought his dwelling was a palace learns it is with the beasts. God dwells with flesh after all — He came down and lived among us.
