akribóō

from the same as G196; to be exact, i.e. ascertain:--enquire diligently.

Biblical Usage

Twice, both in the Christmas story's dark side. Herod enquired of them diligently what time the star appeared, and then, when he saw that he was mocked of the wise men, he kills the children of Bethlehem according to the time which he had diligently enquired of the wise men.

Theological Significance

A word for diligent enquiry carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the contrast, Herod inquiring exactly to plan a murder and the wise men inquiring to worship.
