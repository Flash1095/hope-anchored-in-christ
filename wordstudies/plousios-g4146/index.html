plousíōs

adverb from G4145; copiously:--abundantly, richly.

Biblical Usage

Plousios as an adverb means richly, abundantly. Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly, says Colossians 3:16, and God shed His Spirit on us abundantly through Jesus Christ in Titus 3:6. An entrance shall be ministered unto you abundantly into the everlasting kingdom, says 2 Peter 1:11.

Theological Significance

I believe God does nothing sparingly — His word, His Spirit, and His kingdom all come richly.
