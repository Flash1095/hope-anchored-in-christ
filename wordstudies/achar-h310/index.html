ʼachar

properly, the hind part; generally used as an adverb or conjunction, after (in various senses)

Biblical Usage

Achar is the plain Hebrew word for "after" — after in time, after in place, after in loyalty. Adam lived "after he had begotten Seth"; Sarah laughed, "after I am waxed old shall I have pleasure"; and a generation arose that "went a whoring after other gods." It does the quiet work of sequence in hundreds of verses.

Theological Significance

A word as common as 'after' carries no theology of its own, and the teaching sits in the story that follows it, not in the preposition.
