eulogētós

from G2127; adorable:--blessed.

Biblical Usage

eulogētós is a plain word for blessed. In Mark 14:61, the text says "the Son of the Blessed?" In Luke 1:68, the text says "Blessed [be] the Lord God of Israel."

Theological Significance

What eulogētós teaches me is that God is who He says He is, and His name carries everything He is. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
