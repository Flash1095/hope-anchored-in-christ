Bûwz

Buz, the name of a son of Nahor, and of an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Buz was the second son of Nahor, Abraham's brother, named beside his firstborn Huz, and the name turns up again for a man of Gad and for a people in the east named in Jeremiah's judgment. One name, three settings: family, tribe, and nation.

Theological Significance

I believe a name can travel further than its first bearer, and Buz shows how one family name spreads across Scripture.
