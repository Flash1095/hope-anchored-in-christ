Bûwz

Word
בּוּז
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Bûwz
Strongs Number
H938
Definition
Buz, the name of a son of Nahor, and of an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Buz = "contempt"; 1) second son of Nahor, Abraham's nephew; 2) a Gadite
