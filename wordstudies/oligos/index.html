olígos

of uncertain affinity; puny (in extent, degree, number, duration or value); especially neuter (adverbially) somewhat:--+ almost, brief(-ly), few, (a) little, + long, a season, short, small, a while.

Biblical Usage

Oligos means few, or little, small in number, in size, in time. Jesus says few find the narrow way in Matthew 7:14, and the labourers are few in Matthew 9:37, and many are called but few are chosen in Matthew 22:14. The question is asked plainly in Luke 13:23: Lord, are there few that be saved?

Theological Significance

I believe few is the Bible's honest arithmetic, and the narrow gate is not crowded because the way is easy. My position is that the few who are chosen are the ones who answer the call, and the harvest still waits for labourers.
