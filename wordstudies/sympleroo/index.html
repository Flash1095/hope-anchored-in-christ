symplēróō

Word
συμπληρόω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
symplēróō
Strongs Number
G4845
Definition
from G4862 and G4137; to implenish completely, i.e. (of space) to swamp (a boat), or (of time) to accomplish (passive, be complete):--(fully) come, fill up.
