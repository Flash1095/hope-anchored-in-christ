ʻÊyn Tannîym

En-Tannim, a pool near Jerusalem

Biblical Usage

En-Tannim is a compound place name, the dragon well, and it stands in Nehemiah 2:13, where Nehemiah goes out by night past the dragon well to look at the broken walls of Jerusalem. The name itself is built from the word for a spring joined to the word for a dragon or sea creature, and the two words are tagged under their own numbers in the text.

Theological Significance

I believe En-Tannim matters because God's story happened in real places, and En-Tannim is one of them.
