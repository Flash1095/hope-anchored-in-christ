shad

the breast of a woman or animal (as bulging)

Biblical Usage

The breast is the Bible's picture of nourishment and comfort, given before a child can ask for it. Jacob blesses Joseph with blessings of the breasts and of the womb, Isaiah promises that Zion's children shall suck and be satisfied with the breasts of her consolations, and the psalmist remembers that God made him hope when he was upon his mother's breasts.

Theological Significance

I believe God gave the breast for nourishment, and then made it His own picture of how He comforts His people.
