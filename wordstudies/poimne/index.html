poímnē

contraction from G4165; a flock (literally or figuratively):--flock, fold.

Biblical Usage

Poimne is a flock, the sheep together. Jesus says there shall be one fold and one shepherd in John 10:16, and Paul asks, who feeds a flock and eats not of the milk of the flock, in 1 Corinthians 9:7. The shepherds were keeping watch over their flock by night in Luke 2:8, and the flock was scattered when the shepherd was smitten in Matthew 26:31.

Theological Significance

I believe the flock is one because the Shepherd is one. The night the shepherd was struck, the flock scattered, but He gathered them again.
