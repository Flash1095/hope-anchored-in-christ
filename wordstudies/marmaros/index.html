mármaros

from μαρμαίρω (to glisten); marble (as sparkling white):--marble.

Biblical Usage

mármaros is marble, white stone that glistens, and it occurs once, in Revelation 18:12. There marble stands in the long list of Babylon's merchandise, gold and silver and precious stones and pearls and fine linen and purple and silk and scarlet and ivory and brass and iron and marble. The word is luxury in a city about to fall.

Theological Significance

I believe mármaros shows me that even the finest stone is cargo in a doomed city, and I will not build my hope on marble.
