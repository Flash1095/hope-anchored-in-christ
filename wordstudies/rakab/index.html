râkab

to ride (on an animal or in a vehicle); causatively, to place upon (for riding or generally), to despatch

Biblical Usage

Rakab is to ride — on an animal or in a vehicle, or to set another upon one. Rebekah arose with her damsels, and they rode upon the camels and followed the man; Moses sang, 'the horse and his rider hath he thrown into the sea'; and Zechariah says, 'behold, thy King cometh unto thee... lowly, and riding upon an ass.'

Theological Significance

This tells me that riding marks who is in charge: Pharaoh's riders sank in the sea, and the King who rides the ass comes lowly — and the difference is everything.
