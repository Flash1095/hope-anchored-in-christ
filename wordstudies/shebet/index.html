shêbeṭ

a scion, i.e. (literally) a stick (for punishing, writing, fighting, ruling, walking, etc.) or (figuratively) a clan

Biblical Usage

A rod is a stick, a sceptre, and a tribe, and this word carries all three. The tribes of Israel are brought before the Lord tribe by tribe when Achan's sin is found out, the tribe of Levi is given no inheritance because the Lord is its portion, and Zechariah hears that the sceptre of Egypt shall depart. The rod that rules and the tribe that gathers are the same word.

Theological Significance

I believe the rod shows what a tribe is: a people under authority. Israel was twelve rods in one hand, and I hold that the sceptre belongs to the Lord who gave the tribes their land.
