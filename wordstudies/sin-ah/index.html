sinʼâh

hate

Biblical Usage

Sinah is hate, and it appears where hate is charged against men and against God Himself. Israel murmured that the Lord hated them and brought them out of Egypt to destroy them, the psalmist asks God to consider his enemies who hate him with cruel hatred, the proverb says hatred stirs up strife while love covers all sins, and Amnon's hatred of Tamar after he forced her was greater than the love he had claimed before.

Theological Significance

I believe hate is the opposite of love in exactly the way the proverb says. Hatred stirs up strife, love covers sins, and I hold that the hatred Amnon felt is the proof that his love was never love at all.
