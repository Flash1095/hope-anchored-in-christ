Rab-Mâg

Rab-Mag, a Babylonian official

Biblical Usage

Rab-Mag was a Babylonian official — a title, 'chief soothsayer' or 'chief of the Magians,' carried as a name. When Jerusalem fell, Nergal-sharezer the Rab-mag sat with the princes of Babylon in the middle gate, and later he was among those sent to take Jeremiah out of the court of the prison.

Theological Significance

I take it that Rab-mag is remembered at the gate of a fallen city: Babylon's wise men sat where Jerusalem's king had fled, and the prophet they freed was the one who had foretold it all.
