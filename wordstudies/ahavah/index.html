ahavah

Word
אַהֲבָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ahavah
Strongs Number
H160
Definition
Love — deep affection, devotion and delight, used of God's love for His people and of human love in marriage, family and friendship.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Deuteronomy 6:5; Song of Solomon 8:7; Jeremiah 31:3; Zephaniah 3:17
Biblical Usage
Ahavah commands our whole-hearted love for God in the Shema and celebrates love 'strong as death' in the Song of Solomon. Jeremiah hears God say, 'I have loved you with an everlasting love.'
Theological Significance
Ahavah shows love in Scripture is covenant devotion, not passing feeling. God's everlasting ahavah is the root from which all our loving grows.
Related Topics
Love of God; Covenant; Marriage
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Song of Solomon; Jeremiah
Related People
Moses; King Solomon
Related Places
