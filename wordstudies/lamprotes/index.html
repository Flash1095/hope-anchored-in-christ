lamprótēs

from G2986; brilliancy:--brightness.

Biblical Usage

Brightness appears once, in Paul's telling of the Damascus light in Acts 26:13: "At midday, O king, I saw in the way a light from heaven, above the brightness of the sun, shining round about me and them which journeyed with me."

Theological Significance

For me, the brightness is the proof of the vision: a light brighter than the noonday sun, and a voice with it, and a persecutor made an apostle in one moment.
