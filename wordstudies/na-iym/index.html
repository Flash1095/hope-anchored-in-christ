nâʻîym

delightful (objective or subjective, literal or figurative)

Biblical Usage

Na'im means delightful or pleasant, and it describes friendship, prosperity, and the love of brothers. In 2 Samuel 1:23 Saul and Jonathan are lovely and pleasant in their lives, and in their death they are not divided, and in Job 36:11 those who obey and serve God spend their years in pleasures.

Theological Significance

I hold that delight is not a small thing to God, because He made friendship and obedience to be pleasant; the word holds both the love of Jonathan and the reward of the obedient, and both are good.
