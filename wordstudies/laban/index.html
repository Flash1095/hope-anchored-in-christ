lâban

to be (or become) white; to make bricks

Biblical Usage

To be white is the verb behind the adjective, and it does two kinds of work. In Genesis 11:3 the builders of Babel say "let us make brick," in Exodus 5:7 Pharaoh stops giving straw "to make brick," and in Isaiah 1:18 God promises, "though your sins be as scarlet, they shall be as white as snow"; in Daniel 11:35 the wise are purged "and to make them white," and in Joel 1:7 the enemy has barked the fig tree and "made it clean bare."

Theological Significance

I am sure the verb's two senses meet in Isaiah 1:18: men make brick to build their own tower, and God makes sinners white, and only one of those makings lasts. Men build towers of brick, and God makes sinners white as snow.
