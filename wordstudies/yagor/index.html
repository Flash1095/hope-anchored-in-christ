yâgôr

to fear

Biblical Usage

Moses said, I was afraid of the anger and hot displeasure, wherewith the Lord was wroth against you to destroy you, and Job says, the thing which I greatly feared is come upon me, and that which I was afraid of is come unto me.

Theological Significance

I believe fear is the honest word in both mouths: Moses feared God's anger for Israel, and Job feared the loss that found him, and the Bible lets fear speak before it answers it.
