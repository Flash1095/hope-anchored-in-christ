pâqad

to visit (with friendly or hostile intent); by analogy, to oversee, muster, charge, care for, miss, deposit, etc.

Biblical Usage

Pâqad is to visit, to attend to, to appoint, to number, to muster — God's attention turned toward His people. The LORD says I have surely visited you and seen what is done to you in Egypt, Moses takes the bones of Joseph because God will surely visit you, the LORD visits Hannah and she bears sons and daughters, and the psalmist prays, O visit me with Thy salvation.

Theological Significance

I believe visitation is God's personal attention. He sees, He remembers, He comes, and His coming changes barren wombs, bones in Egypt, and whole nations.
