ʼayeleth

Word
אַיֶּלֶת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼayeleth
Strongs Number
H365
Definition
a doe
Part Of Speech
noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
1) doe, deer, hind; 2) "Aijeleth Shahar" is part of the title of Ps 22:1 and probably describes to the musician the melody to which the psalm was to be played
