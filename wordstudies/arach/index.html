ʼârach

to travel

Biblical Usage

To travel, to go on the road, is the word for a wayfaring man. An old man in Gibeah sees a traveller in the street and takes him in, Jeremiah asks why God is like a wayfaring man who turns aside to tarry for a night, Elihu accuses Job of walking in company with the workers of iniquity, and Nathan tells of a traveller who came to the rich man.

Theological Significance

I hold that the road is where character shows, because a traveller has no home to hide in. The question is always whose company we keep on the way, and where we turn aside to spend the night.
