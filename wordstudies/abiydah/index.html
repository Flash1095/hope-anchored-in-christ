ʻăbîydâh

labor or business

Biblical Usage

In Aramaic this word means work or business — the affairs of a province. Daniel asks that his friends be 'set over the affairs of the province of Babylon'; the accusers charge that the three 'are set over the affairs' yet serve not the king's gods; and in Ezra the same word is the work of the house of God, which ceased when the builders were stopped.

Theological Significance

The three friends are faithful in the king's business and faithless to the king's gods — both at once. I believe that is the pattern for God's people in a foreign court: do the work well, serve the king honestly, and refuse the one thing that belongs to God alone.
