gymnótēs

from G1131; nudity (absolute or comparative):--nakedness.

Biblical Usage

The word gymnótēs carries the sense of nakedness. In Romans 8:35, the text says "or nakedness." In 2 Corinthians 11:27, the text says "in cold and nakedness."

Theological Significance

What gymnótēs teaches me is that humility is not thinking less of ourselves; it is thinking of ourselves less, and of God more. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
