tsâvâh

(intensively) to constitute, enjoin

Biblical Usage

Tsavah is the word for command — God speaking, and His people being bound to obey. The LORD commanded the man in the garden; He commanded Joshua to be strong and courageous; and Moses told Israel that the words God commands are to be in their hearts. Nearly five hundred times Scripture records God, kings, and parents commanding, because a commanded people is a kept people.

Theological Significance

I believe God's commands are not a burden but a gift — they mark out the way of life. My position is that obedience is not earning God's love; it is answering it, the way Israel was to answer the God who brought them out of Egypt. I hold that the same God who commanded Joshua still commands His people today, and His commands are still for our good.
