déchomai

middle voice of a primary verb; to receive (in various applications, literally or figuratively):--accept, receive, take. Compare G2983.

Biblical Usage

To receive is the heart of the gospel, and the word gathers the receiving of the message, the receiving of the offering, and the receiving of one another. The natural man does not receive the things of the Spirit of God, Paul says, and the grace of God must not be received in vain. The Philippian gift is received as a sacrifice well pleasing to God, and the brother who was once unprofitable is to be received as a brother.

Theological Significance

I believe receiving is the first act of faith: we do not take, we receive, and everything we have is a gift from the hand of God. The receiving is the whole of faith: the hand that is open is the hand that is filled.
