katalýō

from G2596 and G3089; to loosen down (disintegrate), i.e. (by implication) to demolish (literally or figuratively); specially (compare G2646) to halt for the night:--destroy, dissolve, be guest, lodge, come to nought, overthrow, throw down.

Biblical Usage

To loosen down is to demolish, and Jesus says He came not to destroy the law but to fulfil it, and then says of the temple that not one stone shall be left upon another. Paul says if our earthly house is dissolved we have a building of God, and the false witnesses said Jesus would destroy the temple made with hands. The word breaks what men build and frees what God has built.

Theological Significance

I believe Jesus fulfils what He does not destroy, and that is why the law stands and the temple fell. I hold that my earthly house will be dissolved one day, and I am glad the building God has for me is eternal.
