syntéleia

from G4931; entire completion, i.e. consummation (of a dispensation):--end.

Biblical Usage

The harvest is the end of the world, and the reapers are the angels, and the disciples ask what will be the sign of His coming and of the end of the world. Jesus promises to be with His disciples always, even unto the end of the world, and the writer of Hebrews says that now once, in the end of the world, He has appeared to put away sin by the sacrifice of Himself. The end is both a harvest and a presence: the world ends, and He is with us until it does, and His appearing once at the end is what put away sin.

Theological Significance

I hold that the end of the world is a harvest with angels for reapers, and I believe His presence until the end is the promise that carries me through it. I take the Hebrews word as my anchor: He appeared once to put away sin, so the end holds no terror for those He has cleansed.
