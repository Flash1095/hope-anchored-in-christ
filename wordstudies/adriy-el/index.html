ʻAdrîyʼêl

Adriel, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Adriel the Meholathite was the man Saul gave Merab to, though she had been promised to David. Years later, his five sons by Merab were handed over to the Gibeonites and hanged, to atone for Saul's slaughter of Gibeon — and Rizpah, Saul's concubine, guarded their bodies from the birds and the beasts until rain fell.

Theological Significance

This name carries no teaching of its own, and I won't force one onto it. But the story around it is heavy: the sins of one king fell on another man's sons, and a mother's vigil over their bodies is one of the most faithful pictures in all of Scripture.
