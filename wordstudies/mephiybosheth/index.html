Mᵉphîybôsheth

Mephibosheth, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Mephibosheth is the son of Jonathan, lame of his feet from the day his nurse fled with him at five years old, and the man David sought out to show kindness to. 2 Samuel 4:4 tells how he became lame, and 2 Samuel 9:6 has him fall on his face before David and answer, Behold thy servant. 2 Samuel 9:10 gives him a place at the king's table always, with Ziba's sons and servants tilling his land.

Theological Significance

I believe Mephibosheth is the gospel in a life: a lame son of a dead king, sought out, seated at the table, and fed for his father's sake. My position is that David's kindness to him is the clearest picture of grace in the Old Testament.
