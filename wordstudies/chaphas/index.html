châphas

to seek; causatively, to conceal oneself (i.e. let be sought), or mask

Biblical Usage

Chaphas is seeking out and disguising, and it does both in the same breath. Saul tells his men to take knowledge of all the lurking places where David hides, and to search him out through all the thousands of Judah. The same Saul disguises himself to visit the medium at Endor, a prophet disguises himself with ashes to wait for the king, and Josiah disguises himself to fight at Megiddo.

Theological Significance

I believe the word shows two kinds of seeking: Saul seeking David to kill him, and men disguising themselves to hide what they are doing. Both end badly. I hold that a man who must disguise himself to do a thing already knows it should not be done.
