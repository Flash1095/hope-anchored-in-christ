bâtsar

to gather grapes; also to be isolated (i.e. inaccessible by height or fortification)

Biblical Usage

Batsar means to be fenced, fortified, inaccessible, and also to gather grapes. The spies report cities great and fenced up to heaven, Caleb asks for the mountain with its fenced cities and giants, Nehemiah says Israel took strong cities and a fat land, and Jeremiah says the remnant of Israel will be gleaned as a vine.

Theological Significance

I believe the fenced city is the Bible's picture of a strength that is not strength, because the walls that frightened the spies fell before a God who keeps His promises. Caleb asked for the hardest mountain, and I hold that his courage is the answer to every report that says the walls are too high.
