Hierichṓ

of Hebrew origin (H3405); Jericho, a place in Palestine:--Jericho.

Biblical Usage

Jericho is a place in Palestine, named 7 times in Scripture, in Luke, Mark, and Hebrews. The full story of the place lives in the Bible itself, and this study covers only the name’s use.

Theological Significance

I take Jericho seriously as a real place in God’s story, not a made-up one. That is why its name belongs in this study, even when the Bible says little about it.
