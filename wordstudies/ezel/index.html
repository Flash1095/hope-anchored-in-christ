ʼezel

Ezel, a memorial stone in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Ezel is a stone, not a man — a landmark in the field where David hid from Saul, named in 1 Samuel 20:19. Jonathan told David to stay by the stone Ezel for three days while he tested his father's mood. The place is remembered because it marks the spot where a friendship was tested and proved true.

Theological Significance

I believe God marks the places where loyalty is proved, even a bare stone in a field. Ezel has no beauty and no building, yet it stands in Scripture as the witness of Jonathan's faithfulness to David.
