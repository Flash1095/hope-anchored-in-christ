zḗtēma

from G2212; a search (properly concretely), i.e. (in words) a debate:--question.

Biblical Usage

A question, a debate, a matter argued over — the word belongs to the courtroom and the council. In Acts it is the dissension at Antioch, the charge against Paul that Festus cannot make sense of, the questions of Jewish law laid before a Roman governor. The questions are real, but the answers are not found in the arguing.

Theological Significance

I believe the debates in Acts show me something honest: the gospel keeps raising questions the world cannot settle, and only Christ answers them.
