ekchéō

Word
ἐκχέω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
ekchéō
Strongs Number
G1632
Definition
, or (by variation) ἐκχύνω from G1537 and χέω (to pour); to pour forth; figuratively, to bestow:--gush (pour) out, run greedily (out), shed (abroad, forth), spill.
