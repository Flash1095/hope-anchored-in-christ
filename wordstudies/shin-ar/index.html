Shinʻâr

Shinar, a plain in Babylonia

Biblical Usage

Shinar is the plain where the first kingdom rises, where the tower is built, where Achan hides a Babylonish garment, and from which the remnant will be gathered in the last days. Babel stands on it, and the whole story of man's empire starts there.

Theological Significance

I believe Shinar is the ground where man first tried to make a name for himself, and God scattered him for it. I hold that the tower of Babel is the beginning of every empire since, and the gathering from Shinar is the end of them.
