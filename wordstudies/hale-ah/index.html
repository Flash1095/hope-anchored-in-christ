hâlᵉʼâh

to the distance, i.e. far away; also (of time) thus far

Biblical Usage

hâlᵉʼâh is the word for far away, far off. In Leviticus 22:27, the text says "and from the eighth day and thenceforth it shall be accepted for an offering made by fire unto the LORD." The word keeps that same sense wherever it appears.

Theological Significance

What hâlᵉʼâh teaches me is that fear is honest, but it is not the last word — God is bigger than what frightens us. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
