malqôwsh

the spring rain; figuratively, eloquence

Biblical Usage

malqôwsh is the latter rain, the spring rain that ripens the crop before harvest. In Deuteronomy 11:14 God promises the first rain and the latter rain in their season, and in Hosea 6:3 His coming is compared to the latter and former rain on the earth. Joel 2:23 and Zechariah 10:1 both bid God's people ask for it and rejoice in it. It is the rain that finishes what the early rain began.

Theological Significance

I believe malqôwsh teaches me that God finishes what He starts. The latter rain comes late, after long waiting, and I hold that His blessing often arrives the same way, near the end of the season. I will keep asking for rain in its time.
