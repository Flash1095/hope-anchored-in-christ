akōlýtōs

adverb from a compound of G1 (as a negative particle) and a derivative of G2967; in an unhindered manner, i.e. freely:--no man forbidding him.

Biblical Usage

Once, and it is the last word of Acts: Paul preaches the kingdom of God and teaches about the Lord Jesus Christ with all confidence, no man forbidding him. The word is unhindered — no one stopping him, no one in the way.

Theological Significance

One word, the last word of Acts, carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is Luke's ending, a prisoner under arrest preaching freely, because the Word cannot be bound.
