Migdal-ʻÊder

Migdal-Eder, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Israel journeyed and spread his tent beyond the tower of Edar, after burying Rachel. The name is the tower of the flock, and it stands at the edge of the story where Jacob becomes Israel.

Theological Significance

I believe the tower of Edar marks a turning: Rachel buried, the tent spread beyond the tower, and Jacob's house moving on toward Bethlehem.
