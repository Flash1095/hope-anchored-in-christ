Chîyrâh

Chirah, an Adullamite

Biblical Usage

Hirah is the Adullamite, the friend of Judah, named twice in the story of Judah and Tamar. He goes down with Judah to the sheep shearing, and he is the one Judah sends with the kid to redeem the pledge he left with Tamar.

Theological Significance

I believe Hirah is the friend who stands beside Judah in his worst chapter, and God keeps the names of friends as well as kings.
