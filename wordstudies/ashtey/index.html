ʻashtêy

eleven or (ordinal) eleventh

Biblical Usage

Eleven is used for journeys and curtains and captains, and it is the number just short of twelve. In the fortieth year, in the eleventh month, Moses speaks to Israel all the LORD commanded him; the tabernacle has eleven curtains of goats' hair for a covering; and Benaiah the Pirathonite is captain of the eleventh course, with twenty-four thousand in his course.

Theological Significance

I believe eleven is the number that says almost: eleven months into the fortieth year Moses speaks, eleven curtains cover the tabernacle, and the eleventh captain stands in a roster of twelve. It is the count that reminds me the promise is nearly kept, and the last month and the last captain are still to come.
