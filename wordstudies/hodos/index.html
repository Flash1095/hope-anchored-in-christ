hodos

Way, road and journey — a path of life; the earliest name for the Christian faith, and Jesus's own claim about Himself.

Biblical Usage

Before they were called Christians, believers were followers of 'the Way' (hodos). Jesus declares He is the Way — not one route among many but the road home to the Father Himself.

Theological Significance

Hodos pictures faith as a journey with a sure direction. However winding your road feels, Jesus is both the path and the destination — you cannot lose your way while you stay with Him.
