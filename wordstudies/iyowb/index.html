ʼÎyôwb

Ijob, the patriarch famous for his patience

Biblical Usage

Job is the man whose patience James holds up, and the book that bears his name tells his story. In Job 1:1 there is a man in the land of Uz, whose name is Job, and that man is perfect and upright, and one that feareth God, and escheweth evil, in Job 1:8 the LORD asks Satan, hast thou considered my servant Job, that there is none like him in the earth, and in Ezekiel 14:14 God names Noah, Daniel, and Job as the three righteous men who could only deliver their own souls.

Theological Significance

I hold that Job's story is the hardest in the Bible and the most honest: a righteous man who loses everything, and a God who answers him out of the whirlwind.
