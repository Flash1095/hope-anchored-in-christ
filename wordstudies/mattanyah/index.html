Mattanyâh

Mattanjah, the name of ten Israelites

Biblical Usage

Mattaniah is the name of several men, and the most famous is the king: Nebuchadnezzar made Mattaniah king in Zedekiah's place and changed his name to Zedekiah. Another Mattaniah sang in the temple choirs, and another stood among the Levites who taught the people.

Theological Significance

I hold that the name change is the story: a king who owed his throne to Babylon took a new name with it, and the kingdom that traded its identity for survival lost both. The singers named Mattaniah kept the older calling, and that is the better half of the name.
