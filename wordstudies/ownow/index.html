ʼÔwnôw

Ono, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Ono was a town in the plain, settled by the sons of Elpaal, and its people returned from exile with Zerubbabel. Sanballat invited Nehemiah to meet him in the plain of Ono, and Nehemiah refused, because he knew it was a trap.

Theological Significance

I believe Ono is the proof that God watches over the small towns: the men of Lod, Hadid and Ono came home from exile, and the plain of Ono was where Sanballat tried to trap Nehemiah.
