epouránios

from G1909 and G3772; above the sky:--celestial, (in) heaven(-ly), high.

Biblical Usage

Epouránios means heavenly — of the heaven above, not of the earth below. Paul blesses God for every spiritual blessing in heavenly places in Christ, and Hebrews calls believers partakers of the heavenly calling. It is the word for the life that already belongs to heaven while it is still lived on earth.

Theological Significance

I believe epouránios is the word for where my life is seated: with Christ in heavenly places, even while my feet are on the ground. I hold that this is not escape from the world — it is the power to live in it as heaven's citizen.
