châçîyl

the ravager, i.e. a locust

Biblical Usage

Chaciyl is the ravager, the locust, the caterpillar that eats the harvest. Solomon names it in his prayer for famine and pestilence, the psalm remembers how God gave Egypt's increase to the caterpillar, and Isaiah says the spoil will be gathered like caterpillars swarming over the land.

Theological Significance

I believe the caterpillar is God's smallest army: no sword, no siege, only hunger with wings. When the harvest is eaten, the land knows who sent it.
