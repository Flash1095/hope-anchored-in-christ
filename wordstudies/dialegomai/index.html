dialégomai

middle voice from G1223 and G3004; to say thoroughly, i.e. discuss (in argument or exhortation):--dispute, preach (unto), reason (with), speak.

Biblical Usage

To reason together is to argue a thing out, and Paul does it everywhere he goes. He reasons in the synagogue at Thessalonica for three Sabbaths, he speaks boldly for three months in the synagogue at Ephesus, he preaches until midnight at Troas, and in the letter to the Hebrews the word is used of God reasoning with us as sons.

Theological Significance

I believe reasoning is part of preaching, not the opposite of it. Paul opened the Scriptures and reasoned from them, and a faith that cannot be argued from the text is not the faith the apostles preached.
