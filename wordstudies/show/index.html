shôwʼ

a tempest; by implication, devastation

Biblical Usage

Sho is a tempest, a devastation that comes like a storm, and the prophets use it for the day of visitation, for the desolation that rolls over a man like breaking waters, for fear coming as desolation and destruction as a whirlwind. Zephaniah gathers it all into the day of wrath, trouble, distress, wasteness, and darkness.

Theological Significance

I believe devastation has a voice, and the prophets let us hear it before it arrives. Sho is the warning word, and I hold that God warns because He would rather we turn than be swept away.
