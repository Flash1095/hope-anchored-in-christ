dōma

from δέμω (to build); properly, an edifice, i.e. (specially) a roof:--housetop.

Biblical Usage

The housetop, the flat roof where Peter went up to pray at noon, and where the friends of the paralytic let their man down through the tiles. Jesus tells His disciples to shout from the housetops what He whispers in the ear, and warns the one on the housetop not to go back down for his goods.

Theological Significance

I believe the housetop is where private prayer becomes public witness. What I hear from Jesus in secret, I am to proclaim in the open, and I must not climb down to gather what the coming day will burn.
