Mártha

probably of Chaldee origin (meaning mistress); Martha, a Christian woman:--Martha.

Biblical Usage

Mártha is Martha of Bethany, sister of Mary and Lazarus. In John 11:1 Lazarus is sick in the town of Mary and her sister Martha, and in John 11:5 Jesus loved Martha and her sister and Lazarus. In John 11:19 many Jews come to Martha and Mary to comfort them concerning their brother., and her story lives on her People profile, and this study covers only the name.

Theological Significance

I believe Mártha teaches me that serving matters and that worry does not, and I hold that Jesus loved her exactly as she was, busy and anxious, and called her to the better part.
