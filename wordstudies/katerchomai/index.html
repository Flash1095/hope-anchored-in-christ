katérchomai

from G2596 and G2064 (including its alternate); to come (or go) down (literally or figuratively):--come (down), depart, descend, go down, land.

Biblical Usage

To come down is what Philip did to Samaria to preach Christ, and what the prophets did from Jerusalem to Antioch, and what Jesus did to Capernaum to teach on the Sabbath, while James says the wisdom that descends not from above is earthly, sensual, and devilish. The word moves people down from one place to another, and it also names where wisdom comes from, above or below.

Theological Significance

I believe the gospel always comes down to where people are, because Philip went down to Samaria and Jesus went down to Capernaum. I hold that wisdom from above is pure and peaceable, and I will test every wisdom by where it came down from.
