eneulogéō

from G1722 and G2127; to confer a benefit on:--bless.

Biblical Usage

To bless — to confer God's benefit. Peter tells the Jews that in Abraham's seed all the kindreds of the earth shall be blessed in Acts 3:25, and Paul says Scripture preached the gospel to Abraham beforehand, saying, In thee shall all nations be blessed, in Galatians 3:8.

Theological Significance

I believe the blessing promised to Abraham has reached me, a Gentile, and that is the gospel — God blesses the nations through one Seed.
