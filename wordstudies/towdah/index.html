tôwdâh

Word
תּוֹדָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
tôwdâh
Strongs Number
H8426
Definition
properly, an extension of the hand, i.e. (by implication) avowal, or (usually) adoration; specifically, a choir of worshippers
Part Of Speech
noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
1) confession, praise, thanksgiving; 1a) give praise to God; 1b) thanksgiving in songs of liturgical worship, hymn of praise; 1c) thanksgiving choir or procession or line or company; 1d) thank-offering, sacrifice of thanksgiving; 1e) confession
