tôwdâh

properly, an extension of the hand, i.e. (by implication) avowal, or (usually) adoration; specifically, a choir of worshippers

Biblical Usage

Thanksgiving is the offering that opens the hand. Hezekiah's congregation brought sacrifices and thank offerings, Amos ironically tells Israel to offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving with leaven, Ezra says make confession to the Lord God of your fathers, and Isaiah says joy and gladness, thanksgiving and the voice of melody shall be found in Zion.

Theological Significance

I hold that thanksgiving is more than a feeling: it is a sacrifice brought, a confession made, and a song sung, all at once. I believe the word teaches me that gratitude is an act before it is a mood — something to bring, to speak and to sing.
