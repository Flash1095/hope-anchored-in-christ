ʻârîyts

fearful, i.e. powerful or tyrannical

Biblical Usage

The terrible ones are the violent, the ruthless who crush the weak, and the prophets name them plainly. Isaiah says God is a refuge from the storm and a shadow from the heat when the blast of the terrible ones beats like a storm against a wall, and that the terrible one will be brought to nought; Jeremiah says the LORD is with him as a mighty terrible one, and his persecutors will stumble.

Theological Significance

I believe this word is a warning and a comfort together: the terrible are real, and God sees them. He shelters the poor from them, and He will bring them to nothing, which is why I hold that no tyrant, however terrible, gets the last word.
