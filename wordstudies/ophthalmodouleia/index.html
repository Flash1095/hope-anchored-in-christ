ophthalmodouleía

from G3788 and G1397; sight-labor, i.e. that needs watching (remissness):--eye-service.

Biblical Usage

Eye-service is work done only while the master watches. Paul tells servants to obey not with eye-service, as men-pleasers, but as the servants of Christ, doing the will of God from the heart.

Theological Significance

I believe eye-service is the test of whether I work for men or for God: the same task is worship when no one is watching.
