Bᵉʼêr Shebaʻ

Beer-Sheba, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Beer-sheba is the well of the oath, where Abraham and Abimelech swear and Abraham plants a grove and calls on the name of the Lord. It is where Abraham returns after offering Isaac, where Jacob sets out for Haran and dreams of the ladder, and where all Israel from Dan to Beer-sheba knows Samuel is established as a prophet.

Theological Significance

I believe Beer-sheba is the boundary stone of the whole story, because from Dan to Beer-sheba is the measure of Israel, and the well where Abraham swore is the well where Jacob dreamed and Samuel was known. The oath, the offering, the dream, and the prophet all meet at one well, and I hold that God's promises are not scattered but gathered in one place.
