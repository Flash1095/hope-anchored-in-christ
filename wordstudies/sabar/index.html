sâbar

Word
שָׂבַר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
sâbar
Strongs Number
H7663
Definition
to scrutinize; by implication (of watching) to expect (with hope and patience)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to inspect, examine, wait, hope, wait upon; 1a) (Qal) examined (participle); 1b) (Piel); 1b1) to wait (for), wait upon; 1b2) to hope (for)
