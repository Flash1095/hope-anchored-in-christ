sâbar

to scrutinize; by implication (of watching) to expect (with hope and patience)

Biblical Usage

Sâbar carries the sense of to scrutinize; by implication (of watching) to expect (with hope and patience). In Esther 9:1, we read, "…the king's commandment and his decree drew near…"; in Isaiah 38:18, "…the grave cannot praise thee, death can celebrate…". You hear it again in Nehemiah 2:13 — "…dung port, and viewed the walls of Jerusalem…" — and in Psalms 104:27, "…thou mayest give their meat in due season".

Theological Significance

I hold sâbar close because it is part of how Scripture speaks plainly about real life. I believe sâbar is still true for us, because the God who gave it has not changed his mind.
