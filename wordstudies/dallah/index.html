dallâh

properly, something dangling, i.e. a loose thread or hair; figuratively, indigent

Biblical Usage

The word is a thread, a loose hair, a thing that dangles, and it gathers two pictures: the thin and poor, and the lock of hair. Pharaoh's lean cattle are thin-favoured, the poor of the land are left behind when the rest are carried away, and the beloved's hair hangs like a flock of goats on the mountain. The psalmist's life is cut off like a weaver's thread.

Theological Significance

I believe the word shows how Scripture sees the poor and the small: God counts the loose threads, and He does not lose one. The thread is small, but the Weaver does not lose it, and the poor He counts are never forgotten.
