Magdalēnḗ

feminine of a derivative of G3093; a female Magdalene, i.e. inhabitant of Magdala:--Magdalene.

Biblical Usage

Mary Magdalene is the woman from Magdala out of whom Jesus cast seven devils, and she stayed when others left. John 20:1 has her coming early to the sepulchre while it was yet dark, and John 20:18 has her telling the disciples she had seen the Lord, while Mark 16:9 says the risen Jesus appeared first to Mary Magdalene.

Theological Significance

I believe Mary Magdalene is the first witness because she was the last one at the cross and the first one at the tomb: love that stays gets to see the risen Lord first.
