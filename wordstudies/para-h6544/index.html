pâraʻ

to loosen; by implication, to expose, dismiss; figuratively, absolve, begin

Biblical Usage

Pâra' is to loosen, to let go, to expose, to cast off restraint. Aaron makes the people naked to their shame at the golden calf, Wisdom says ye have set at nought all my counsel, and the proverb says where there is no vision the people perish — literally, are let loose.

Theological Significance

I believe restraint is a gift. When a people cast it off, they are exposed, and only a vision from God gathers them back again.
