pᵉthîy

silly (i.e. seducible)

Biblical Usage

Pethiy is the simple one, the silly and seducible person who believes everything and learns nothing. Wisdom calls out asking how long the simple will love simplicity, and the proverb warns that the simple believe every word while the prudent look well to their going. But the same word carries hope: the testimony of the LORD is sure, making wise the simple.

Theological Significance

I believe the simple are not the unintelligent; they are the untaught, and God does not despise them. His word was given to make the simple wise, and the proverbs were written to give subtilty to the simple. My position is that no one has to stay simple: the one who listens to God's word stops believing every word of men.
