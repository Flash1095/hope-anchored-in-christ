állōs

adverb from G243; differently:--otherwise.

Biblical Usage

Once, in Paul's instruction about elders: Likewise also the good works of some are manifest beforehand; and they that are otherwise cannot be hid. The word is otherwise, differently, the works that are not manifest yet and cannot stay hidden forever.

Theological Significance

One word for otherwise carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the patience in it, that what is done in secret comes out in time, good and bad alike.
