Bârûwk

Baruk, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Baruch is the name of three Israelites, and the chief of them is Jeremiah's scribe, who wrote the prophet's words in a book at his mouth, read them in the house of the Lord, and heard God's own word to him when he grew weary. A second Baruch, the son of Zabbai, repaired a measure of the wall in Nehemiah's day.

Theological Significance

I believe Baruch matters because he wrote down words that a king tried to burn, and the words outlived the fire. He was weary and God spoke to him by name, and I hold that the scribe who stays faithful to the prophet's words is remembered with the prophet.
