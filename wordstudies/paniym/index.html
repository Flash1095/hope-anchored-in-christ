pânîym

the face (as the part that turns); used in a great variety of applications (literally and figuratively); also (with prepositional prefix) as a preposition (before, etc.)

Biblical Usage

Pânîym is the face, the front, the presence — the part that turns toward another. Jacob names the place Peniel, for I have seen God face to face, the LORD speaks to Moses face to face as a man speaks to his friend, Aaron is told the LORD make His face shine upon thee, and the psalmist answers, Thy face, LORD, will I seek.

Theological Significance

I believe the face of God is the Bible's deepest longing. Jacob survives seeing it, Moses speaks to it as a friend, and the psalmist's answer is mine too — Thy face, LORD, will I seek.
