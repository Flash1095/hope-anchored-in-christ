ʻÎyîym

Ijim, a place in the Desert.

Biblical Usage

Ijim is a place in the desert, and a town in the south of Judah. In Numbers 33:45 Israel departs from Iim and pitches in Dibon-gad, and in Joshua 15:29 Iim is named among the cities of the tribe of Judah toward the coast.

Theological Significance

I hold that the two mentions are two places with one name: a camp in the wilderness and a town in Judah, both remembered because Israel passed through them.
