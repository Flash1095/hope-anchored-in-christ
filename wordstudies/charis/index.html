charis

Word
χάρις
Language
Greek
Transliteration
charis
Strongs Number
G5485
Definition
Grace — God's free, unmerited favour and gift, and the power His favour gives for living and serving.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Ephesians 2:8-9; John 1:16; Romans 3:24; 2 Corinthians 12:9
Biblical Usage
Charis was the language of gift and thankfulness that Paul transformed into the gospel's signature word: saved 'by grace... through faith... not by works.' Grace upon grace flows from Christ's fullness.
Theological Significance
Charis means your standing with God is gift from start to finish — and when you are weak, that grace is not only pardon but strength: 'My grace is sufficient for you.'
