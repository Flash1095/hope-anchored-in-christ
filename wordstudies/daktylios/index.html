daktýlios

from G1147; a finger-ring:--ring.

Biblical Usage

The ring appears once, on the hand of the returning prodigal. The father orders the best robe, a ring, and shoes: the runaway is not taken back as a servant but restored as a son, with the sign of the family on his finger.

Theological Significance

I believe the ring is grace made visible: the Father does not make us earn our way back, He dresses us as His own.
