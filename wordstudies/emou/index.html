emoû

a prolonged form of G3449; of me:--me, mine, my.

Biblical Usage

Emou is 'of me' — the genitive of 'I.' Jesus blesses those reviled falsely for my sake in Matthew 5:11, and promises that whoever loses his life for my sake shall find it in Matthew 16:25.

Theological Significance

I believe 'for my sake' is the hinge of the whole Christian life — suffering and loss only make sense when they are for Him.
