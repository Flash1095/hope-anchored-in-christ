tâbûwn

intelligence; by implication, an argument; by extension, caprice

Biblical Usage

The Lord gives wisdom, and out of His mouth comes knowledge and understanding. The Lord by wisdom founded the earth, and by understanding He established the heavens. He filled Bezaleel with the Spirit of God, in wisdom and in understanding, for the work of the tabernacle, and Job says, with Him is wisdom and strength, He has counsel and understanding. The word is intelligence, the mind's grasp: given by God, shown in creation, and worked by the craftsman's hands.

Theological Significance

I hold that understanding is a gift before it is an achievement. I believe Proverbs says it plainly: the Lord gives it, and the craftsman, the king, and the sufferer all receive it from the same hand. That is why I ask for it instead of trusting my own.
