katangéllō

from G2596 and the base of G32; to proclaim, promulgate:--declare, preach, shew, speak of, teach.

Biblical Usage

To proclaim is to declare openly, and Paul proclaimed the forgiveness of sins through Christ at Antioch, opened the Scriptures at Thessalonica to show Christ must suffer and rise, and warned every man in all wisdom to present every man perfect in Christ. The word is the apostles' work in one verb: they declared what God had done and would not keep it quiet. Your faith is spoken of throughout the whole world because it was first proclaimed.

Theological Significance

I believe proclaiming is not an option for the church, because the forgiveness of sins is news too good to whisper. I hold that the message is the same everywhere Paul went, Christ crucified and risen, and I will not change it to suit the room.
