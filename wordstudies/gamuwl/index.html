gâmûwl

Gamul, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Gamul is a priest, named once in 1 Chronicles 24:17, where the lots of the priestly courses are drawn. The one and twentieth lot fell to Jachin, and the two and twentieth to Gamul. His course served in the house of the LORD in its turn.

Theological Significance

I believe the lot that fell to Gamul shows that God's house runs on order, not chance. Twenty-two courses, each in its turn, and I hold that the smallest course is as much God's appointment as the first.
