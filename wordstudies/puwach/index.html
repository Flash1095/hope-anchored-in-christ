pûwach

to puff, i.e. blow with the breath or air; hence, to fan (as a breeze), to utter, to kindle (a fire), to scoff

Biblical Usage

Puach means to puff, to blow, to utter. The false witness speaketh lies, and the true witness delivereth souls, but a deceitful witness speaketh lies. And in the Song, the beloved says, Until the day break, and the shadows flee away, turn, my beloved, and be thou like a roe or a young hart upon the mountains of Bether.

Theological Significance

I believe the same breath does two things: it utters lies that destroy, and it waits for the day to break, and the difference is what the breath is filled with.
