ʼăruchâh

a ration of food

Biblical Usage

The allowance is the daily ration given to a man in another's power, and it is used for a captive king and for a dinner table. Jehoiachin eats a continual allowance from the king of Babylon all the days of his life, and the proverb says better is a dinner of herbs where love is, than a stalled ox and hatred therewith.

Theological Significance

I believe the ration of a captive king is one of the saddest pictures in the Bible: a king of Judah fed day by day from a foreign king's table. And I hold that the proverb answers it, that a small meal with love is better than a fat ox with hatred, because the company at the table matters more than what is on it.
