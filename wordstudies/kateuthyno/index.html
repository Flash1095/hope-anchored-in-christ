kateuthýnō

from G2596 and G2116; to straighten fully, i.e. (figuratively) direct:--guide, direct.

Biblical Usage

To straighten fully is to direct, and Zechariah says the dayspring shall guide our feet into the way of peace, and Paul prays that God would direct our way unto the Thessalonians, and that the Lord would direct our hearts into the love of God and the patient waiting for Christ. The word is the prayer of a traveller who knows the road is crooked and asks God to make it straight.

Theological Significance

I believe God directs the feet that ask to be directed, because Zechariah's prayer is still answered today. I hold that the way of peace and the love of God are the two directions my heart needs, and I will ask Him to point me straight every morning.
