mûwr

to alter; by implication, to barter, to dispose of

Biblical Usage

Mur means to change, to barter, to exchange — hath a nation changed their gods, which are yet no gods, but My people have changed their glory for that which doth not profit, and they changed their glory into the similitude of an ox that eateth grass.

Theological Significance

I believe the exchange is the tragedy of Israel in one word: glory traded for nothing, and the trade made willingly.
