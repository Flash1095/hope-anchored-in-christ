ʼôhel

a tent (as clearly conspicuous from a distance)

Biblical Usage

Ohel is a tent, the conspicuous dwelling of nomads and patriarchs. Jabal is the father of those who dwell in tents in Genesis 4:20, and Israel gathers manna each man for those in his tent in Exodus 16:16. The word rises higher in Leviticus 1:1 and Numbers 1:1, where the Lord speaks to Moses out of the tabernacle of the congregation, the tent where God meets His people.

Theological Significance

I believe the tent is the Bible's picture of a God who travels with His people, and the tabernacle is the tent where heaven touches earth. That is why I hold that God never asked His people to come to a palace, He pitched His tent among them, and in Christ He did it again.
