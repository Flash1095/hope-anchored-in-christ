shayith

scrub or trash, i.e. wild growth of weeds or briers (as if put on the field)

Biblical Usage

Briers and thorns are what a vineyard becomes when it is left to itself. God says He will lay His vineyard waste and there shall come up briers and thorns, men shall come with bows because all the land has become briers and thorns, and wickedness burns as the fire and devours them. But God also says, who would set the briers and thorns against me in battle? I would go through them, I would burn them together.

Theological Significance

I believe thorns are judgment with a way back. The vineyard went to briers, and God says He would burn through them Himself, and I hold that He does the same with the thorns in me.
