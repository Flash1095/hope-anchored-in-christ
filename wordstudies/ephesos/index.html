Éphesos

probably of foreign origin; Ephesus, a city of Asia Minor:--Ephesus.

Biblical Usage

Ephesus was the great city of Asia Minor, home of the temple of Artemis, where Paul arrived and preached. It was the city where he called the elders to his farewell, and where the risen Christ later sent a letter to the church.

Theological Significance

A city where Paul arrived and preached, and where the risen Christ later sent a letter: Ephesus carries both the gospel's advance and the Lord's own address to His church. I believe the letter's place in the canon is the warning and the honour at once, because Christ writes to His churches, and He writes to this one first.
