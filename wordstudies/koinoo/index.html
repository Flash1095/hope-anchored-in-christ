koinóō

from G2839; to make (or consider) profane (ceremonially):--call common, defile, pollute, unclean.

Biblical Usage

Koinoo means to make common, to defile, to call unclean. Matthew 15:11 says not that which goeth into the mouth defileth a man, but that which cometh out of the mouth, Matthew 15:18 and Matthew 15:20 press the point, that the things which proceed out of the mouth come forth from the heart, and they defile the man, Mark 7:15 says nothing from without can defile him, but the things which come out of him, Acts 10:15 has the voice saying, what God hath cleansed, that call not thou common, and Hebrews 9:13 remembers the blood of bulls and of goats sanctifying to the purifying of the flesh.

Theological Significance

I believe Jesus moved defilement from the hands to the heart: the mouth does not defile, the heart behind the mouth does. I hold that Acts 10:15 is the turning point, because what God has cleansed no man may call common, and the church has lived by that voice ever since.
