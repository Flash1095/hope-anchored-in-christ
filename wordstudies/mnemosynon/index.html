mnēmósynon

from G3421; a reminder (memorandum), i.e. record:--memorial.

Biblical Usage

Mnemosynon is a memorial, a reminder kept before God. Cornelius's prayers and alms go up as a memorial before Him, and the woman who pours ointment on Jesus is promised that wherever the gospel is preached, what she did will be told for a memorial of her.

Theological Significance

I hold that God keeps memorials. Prayers and quiet acts of love are not forgotten, they are kept before Him like a record.
