ʼashmâh

guiltiness, a fault, the presentation of a sin-offering

Biblical Usage

Guiltiness is the guilt that stays on a people, and the Bible uses it for Israel's sin after the return and for the sin that brings the sword. Ezra says our iniquities are increased over our head, and our trespass is grown up unto the heavens; the people leave the house of the LORD and serve groves and idols, and wrath comes upon Judah for this their trespass; and Amos says they that swear by the sin of Samaria shall fall, and never rise up again.

Theological Significance

I believe guiltiness is guilt that has grown up to heaven and will not be put down: Ezra is ashamed to lift his face, the chronicler says wrath came for it, and Amos says those who swear by it shall fall and never rise. And I hold that the only answer to guilt grown up to heaven is the confession Ezra makes, on his knees, with his face to the ground.
