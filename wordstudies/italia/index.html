Italía

probably of foreign origin; Italia, a region of Europe:--Italy.

Biblical Usage

Italy is the land Paul sails to as a prisoner and writes back to as a friend. In Acts 18:2 Aquila and Priscilla come from Italy because Claudius commanded all Jews to depart from Rome, in Acts 27:1 Paul is delivered to sail into Italy, and in Hebrews 13:24 the writer sends greeting from they of Italy.

Theological Significance

I hold that Italy is where the gospel arrived by ship and by prison: Paul came in chains, and the saints there sent their love back.
