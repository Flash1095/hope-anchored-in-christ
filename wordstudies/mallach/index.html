mallâch

a sailor (as following 'the salt')

Biblical Usage

Mallach is the sailor, the mariner, and all of its appearances belong to Tyre. Ezekiel 27:9 has the ships of the sea with their mariners in Tyre to occupy her merchandise, and Ezekiel 27:27 has the mariners falling into the midst of the seas in the day of Tyre's ruin.

Theological Significance

I take it that Tyre's mariners are the measure of her pride: the men who carried her wealth sank with it.
