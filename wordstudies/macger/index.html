maçgêr

Word
מַסְגֵּר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
maçgêr
Strongs Number
H4525
Definition
a fastener, i.e. (of a person) a smith, (of a thing) a prison
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) a shutting up, locksmith, smith, dungeon, enclosure, builder of bulwarks; 1a) locksmith, smith; 1b) dungeon
