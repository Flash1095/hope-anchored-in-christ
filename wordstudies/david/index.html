Dâvid

David, the youngest son of Jesse

Biblical Usage

David is the youngest son of Jesse, anointed by Samuel while still a shepherd boy, and the man after God's own heart. He slew Goliath with a sling and a stone, fled from Saul, and was made king over all Israel, and the Lord promised him a house and a throne forever. His psalms are the prayer book of the church, and his line is the line of Christ.

Theological Significance

I believe David matters because God chose the least and made him the greatest, and I hold that his life is the pattern of repentance: a great sinner who fell hard and came home harder, and whose throne outlasted his failures.
