châzûwth

Word
חָזוּת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
châzûwth
Strongs Number
H2380
Definition
a look; hence (figuratively) striking appearance, revelation, or (by implication) compact
Part Of Speech
noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
1) vision, conspicuousness; 1a) vision, oracle of a prophet; 1a1) agreement; 1b) conspicuousness in appearance
