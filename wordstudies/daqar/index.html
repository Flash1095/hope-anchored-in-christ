dâqar

Word
דָּקַר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
dâqar
Strongs Number
H1856
Definition
to stab; by analogy, to starve; figuratively, to revile
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to pierce, thrust through, pierce through; 1a) (Qal) to pierce, run through; 1b) (Niphal) to be pierced through; 1c) (Pual) pierced, riddled (participle)
