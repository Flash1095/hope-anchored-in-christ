ʼĂmatsyâh

Amatsjah, the name of four Israelites

Biblical Usage

Four men share the name, and the king is the one the story follows. Amaziah son of Joash is twenty and five years old when he begins to reign, and he reigns twenty and nine years in Jerusalem; he gathers Judah, hires a hundred thousand men of Israel for a hundred talents of silver, and is told to send them home; and the other Amaziahs are counted in the lines of Judah and Simeon and Levi, a king's son, a Simeonite prince, a Levite.

Theological Significance

Four men, one name, but only the king's story is told: Amaziah did what was right but not with a perfect heart, and the pride of his victory over Edom was his undoing.
