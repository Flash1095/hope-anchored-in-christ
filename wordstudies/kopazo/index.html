kopázō

from G2873; to tire, i.e. (figuratively) to relax:--cease.

Biblical Usage

Kopazo means to tire, to relax, and so to cease. Matthew 14:32 says when Jesus and Peter came into the ship, the wind ceased, Mark 4:39 has Him rebuking the wind, and the wind ceased, and there was a great calm, and Mark 6:51 has Him going up into the ship, and the wind ceased, and they were sore amazed.

Theological Significance

I believe the wind ceases when He comes aboard: the storm that tired the rowers relaxes at His word, and the great calm follows the great fear. I hold that the amazement in Mark 6:51 is the right response, because the wind obeys the voice that made it.
