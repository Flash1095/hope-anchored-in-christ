exouthenéō

a variation of G1847 and meaning the same:--contemptible, despise, least esteemed, set at nought.

Biblical Usage

exouthenéō means contemptible, despise, least esteemed, set at nought. In Luke 18:9, the text says "and despised others." In Acts 4:11, the text says "This is the stone which was set at nought of you builders."

Theological Significance

I believe exouthenéō shows us that humility is not thinking less of ourselves; it is thinking of ourselves less, and of God more. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
