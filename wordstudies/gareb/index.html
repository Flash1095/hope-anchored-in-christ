Gârêb

Gareb, the name of an Israelite, also of a hill near Jerusalem

Biblical Usage

Gareb is the name of an Ithrite, one of David's mighty men, named in 1 Chronicles 11:40 and again in 2 Samuel 23:38. Ira the Ithrite, Gareb the Ithrite — the two stand together in the lists. The same name belongs to a hill near Jerusalem, but the man is the one the lists remember.

Theological Significance

I believe the lists of David's mighty men are a roll of honour, and Gareb's name is on it. He fought beside the king, and I hold that standing beside God's anointed is the highest place a man can stand.
