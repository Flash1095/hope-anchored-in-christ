Yôwʼâsh

Joash, the name of six Israelites

Biblical Usage

Joash is named 41 times in the Bible, and the passages here are Judges 6:11; 1 Kings 22:26; 2 Kings 11:2. In Judges 6:11 we read, "And there came an angel of the LORD, and sat under an oak which was in Ophrah, that pertained unto Joash the Abiezrite..." The same name belongs to more than one man, and 2 Kings 11:2 names another of them.

Theological Significance

Joash reminds me that God sees individuals, not just crowds. His name stands in the record, and that is enough for me.
