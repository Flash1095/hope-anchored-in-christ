Mânachath

Manachath, the name of an Edomite and of a place in Moab

Biblical Usage

Mânachath is the name of a son of Shobal in the Edomite genealogy and of a place in Moab. In Genesis 36:23 Manahath is listed among the children of Shobal, and 1 Chronicles 1:40 repeats the line. In 1 Chronicles 8:6 the sons of Ehud are removed to Manahath., and the name survives in lists, not in a story.

Theological Significance

I believe Mânachath shows me that God keeps names in His book even when no story survives, and that no family is forgotten by Him.
