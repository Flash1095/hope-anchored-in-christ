lâʻêg

a buffoon; also a foreigner

Biblical Usage

The mocker or stammerer is the foreigner whose speech sounds like mockery, and the word appears twice. In Isaiah 28:11 God says, "with stammering lips and another tongue will he speak to this people," and in Psalms 35:16 David complains of "hypocritical mockers in feasts" who gnashed upon him with their teeth.

Theological Significance

I believe the two verses show both sides of the same word: God's judgment speaks in a tongue Israel cannot understand, and David's enemies mock him at the table, and in both cases the mocking is the judgment.
