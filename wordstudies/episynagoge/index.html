episynagōgḗ

from G1996; a complete collection; especially a Christian meeting (for worship):--assembling (gathering) together.

Biblical Usage

Episynagoge is a gathering together, an assembling — our gathering together unto Him at His coming, and the assembling of ourselves together, which we are not to forsake, as the manner of some is, but exhorting one another, and so much the more, as ye see the day approaching.

Theological Significance

We gather now because we shall be gathered then, and neither gathering should be forsaken.
