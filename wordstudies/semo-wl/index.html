sᵉmôʼwl

Definition
properly, dark (as enveloped), i.e. the north; hence (by orientation), the left hand
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) the left, the left hand, the left side; 1a) left; 1b) left hand; 1c) north (as one faces east)
