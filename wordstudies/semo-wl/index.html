sᵉmôʼwl

properly, dark (as enveloped), i.e. the north; hence (by orientation), the left hand

Biblical Usage

Semo'wl is the left hand, the north by orientation, and it places men and things. The sons of Merari stood on the left hand in the service of the house, and Solomon put five bases on the right side of the house and five on the left, with the sea on the right side eastward over against the south.

Theological Significance

What I see here is that the left has its appointed place as surely as the right — Merari's sons stood there, and the bases stood there, by design.
