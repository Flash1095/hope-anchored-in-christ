ʼâçuph

collected (only in the plural), i.e. a collection of offerings)

Biblical Usage

Asuppim — the storehouses. The word appears three times, always at the temple gates: the house of Asuppim fell to Obed-edom's lot among the porters, two Levites a day kept the storehouse, and the gatekeepers kept the ward "at the thresholds of the gates." The storehouse held the supplies of the house of God, guarded by men whose names were kept with the rest.

Theological Significance

Storehouses are temple furniture, and the picture worth keeping is a house of God supplied, guarded, and counted by named men.
