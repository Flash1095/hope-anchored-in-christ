ʼĂbîyhayil

Abihail or Abichail, the name of three Israelites and two Israelitesses

Biblical Usage

Abihail is the name of three men and two women. A Levite prince named Abihail fathers Zuriel; Abihail is the father of Esther and the uncle of Mordecai; and a daughter of Eliab named Abihail marries Rehoboam.

Theological Significance

One name across five lives — a Levite, a Gadite, a king's wife, and the father of a queen. I believe the name Abihail is a reminder that God works through families I barely notice: Esther's father dies before the story starts, and still his name is the root of the rescue.
