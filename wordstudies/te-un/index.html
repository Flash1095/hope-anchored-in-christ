tᵉʼun

naughtiness, i.e. toil

Biblical Usage

The word is weariness, the toil that gets nothing. Ezekiel says the pot of Jerusalem hath wearied herself with lies, and her great scum went not forth out of her, so her scum shall be in the fire.

Theological Significance

I believe lies weary a city out, and the scum that will not come off goes into the fire.
