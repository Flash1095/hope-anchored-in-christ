Rᵉmalyâhûw

Remaljah, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Remaliah was the father of Pekah, who reigned over Israel in Samaria, and Pekah joined with Rezin king of Syria to war against Ahaz in Jerusalem. Isaiah was sent to tell Ahaz to take heed and be quiet and fear not the two tails of smoking firebrands.

Theological Significance

My position is that God names the fathers of conspirators so that no plot hides behind its king.
