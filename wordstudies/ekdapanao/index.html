ekdapanáō

from G1537 and G1159; to expend (wholly), i.e. (figuratively) exhaust:--spend.

Biblical Usage

This word means to spend wholly, to exhaust oneself, and the Greek text carries it in the middle voice, as I will be spent, where Strong's lists the plain active form. Paul says he will very gladly spend and be spent for the Corinthians, though the more abundantly he loves them, the less he is loved. The word is the sound of a man pouring himself out for people who do not return it.

Theological Significance

I believe love spends itself without keeping accounts, because Paul spent himself for a church that repaid him with suspicion, and Christ did the same for me.
