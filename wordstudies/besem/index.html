besem

fragrance; by implication, spicery; also the balsam plant

Biblical Usage

besem is used for spice, sweet fragrance, and it appears 25 times across the Bible. Spices went into the anointing oil and the incense, the Queen of Sheba brought them to Solomon, and Hezekiah showed his spices to the Babylonians.

Theological Significance

I hold that the sweet spices of worship were never wasted — what is poured out for God is never lost. That is why I keep coming back to it: besem is not a word to rush past.
