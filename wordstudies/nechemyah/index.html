Nᵉchemyâh

Nechemjah, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Nehemiah is the son of Hachaliah whose words open the book that bears his name, and he stands with Ezra the priest when the law is read to all the people. In the days of Nehemiah the singers and porters receive their portions as the worship is ordered again.

Theological Significance

I believe Nehemiah shows what a governor is for: he weeps, he builds, and he orders the worship, and the wall and the word rise together under his hand.
