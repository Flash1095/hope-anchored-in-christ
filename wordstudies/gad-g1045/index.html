Gád

of Hebrew origin (H1410); Gad, a tribe of Israelites:--Gad.

Biblical Usage

Gad is the tribe, named among the twelve sealed in Revelation 7:5. Of the tribe of Gad were sealed twelve thousand, standing between Reuben and Asher in John's vision. The name reaches the end of the Bible because the tribe that bore it is not forgotten.

Theological Significance

I believe the sealing of Gad is a promise that God keeps His own. Twelve thousand from a tribe that once settled east of Jordan are counted among the sealed, and I hold that no tribe and no believer is left out of God's number.
