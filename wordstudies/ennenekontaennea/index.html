ennenēkontaennéa

from a (tenth) multiple of G1767 and G1767 itself; ninety-nine:--ninety and nine.

Biblical Usage

Ninety and nine — two words in the Greek, one number in the story. The shepherd leaves the ninety and nine in the wilderness and goes after the one that is lost in Luke 15:4, and the same in Matthew 18:12.

Theological Significance

The ninety and nine are not forgotten, I believe — they are left in the wilderness, safe in the shepherd's care, while he goes after the one who is not.
