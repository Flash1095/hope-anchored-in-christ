Rᵉʼûwbên

Reuben, a son of Jacob

Biblical Usage

Reuben was Leah's firstborn, named because the LORD had looked upon her affliction, and he was the eldest of Jacob's twelve sons. He tried to save Joseph from his brothers' hands, yet he lost the birthright for defiling his father's bed. Moses blessed him, saying, let Reuben live and not die, and his tribe settled east of the Jordan.

Theological Significance

I am convinced that Reuben's story is a warning and a comfort at once: the firstborn can lose the first place by his own sin, and still be blessed to live. God does not erase His people for their worst hour.
