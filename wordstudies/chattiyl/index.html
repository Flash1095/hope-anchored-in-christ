Chaṭṭîyl

Word
חַטִּיל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Chaṭṭîyl
Strongs Number
H2411
Definition
Chattil, one of 'Solomon's servants'
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Hattil = "doubtful"; 1) head of a family of the children of Solomon's slaves who returned from exile with Zerubbabel
