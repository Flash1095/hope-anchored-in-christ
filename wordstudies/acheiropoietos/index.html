acheiropoíētos

from G1 (as a negative particle) and G5499; unmanufactured, i.e. inartificial:--made without (not made with) hands.

Biblical Usage

Made without hands — the word is used three times, and every use matters. False witnesses threw it at Jesus: "I will destroy this temple that is made with hands, and within three days I will build another made without hands." Paul says we have "an house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens"; and that in Christ we are circumcised "with the circumcision made without hands."

Theological Significance

I believe this word is the heart of the gospel in miniature: everything God truly builds, no human hand made. The temple they accused Him of threatening, the body He promises us, the cleansing He gives — all of it is God's work, not ours.
