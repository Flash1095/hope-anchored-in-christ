keleʼ

a prison

Biblical Usage

Kele means a prison, confinement: 1 Kings 22:27 has Ahab saying, put this fellow in the prison, and feed him with bread of affliction. 2 Kings 25:27 says Evil-merodach lifted up the head of Jehoiachin out of prison. Isaiah 42:22 says God's people are hid in prison houses, and none delivereth. Jeremiah 52:33 says Jehoiachin changed his prison garments, and did eat bread before the king all the days of his life.

Theological Significance

I believe prison is where God does some of his deepest work: the prophet fed on affliction, the people hid with none to deliver, and the king changed his garments and ate at the table. I hold that no prison holds forever when God lifts up the head.
