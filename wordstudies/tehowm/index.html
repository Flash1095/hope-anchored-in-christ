tᵉhôwm

an abyss (as a surging mass of water), especially the deep (the main sea or the subterranean watersupply)

Biblical Usage

The word is the deep, the abyss of waters. Darkness was upon the face of the deep, and the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters; in the days of Noah all the fountains of the great deep were broken up; the depths covered Pharaoh's army, and they sank into the bottom as a stone. The psalmist says deep calleth unto deep, and Jonah says the waters compassed him about, even to the soul, and the deep closed him round about.

Theological Significance

I believe the deep is where God works and where man drowns without Him: the Spirit moved over it, the flood came from it, and Jonah prayed from it. My position is that the deep belongs to God, and the waters that cover the proud are the waters He commands.
