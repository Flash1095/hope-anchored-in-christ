Definition

Part Of Speech
noun
Biblical Usage
1) deep, depths, deep places, abyss, the deep, sea; 1a) deep (of subterranean waters); 1b) deep, sea, abysses (of sea); 1c) primeval ocean, deep; 1d) deep, depth (of river); 1e) abyss, the grave
