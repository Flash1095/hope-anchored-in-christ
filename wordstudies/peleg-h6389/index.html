Peleg

Peleg, a son of Shem

Biblical Usage

Peleg was the son of Eber, and his name marks the dividing of the earth in his days. Genesis says Eber lived thirty-four years and begat Peleg, and the chronicler repeats it: in his days the earth was divided.

Theological Significance

I am convinced that God marks history by His own divisions, and Peleg's name is the signpost: the earth was one, and in his days it was divided.
