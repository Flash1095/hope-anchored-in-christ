ʼôwth

a signal (literally or figuratively), as a flag, beacon, monument, omen, prodigy, evidence, etc.

Biblical Usage

A sign is a mark God gives to prove His word. God told Moses, this shall be a token unto thee, that I have sent thee, and He showed His power in signs and wonders in Egypt, while Isaiah gave Hezekiah a sign that the Lord would heal him and save the city.

Theological Significance

I believe signs are God's way of saying, I am with you: the burning bush, the signs and wonders in Egypt, the sign of the son — He gives a sign so that I will believe. I ask for signs the way Gideon did, but I hold onto the signs God has already given, because they are enough.
