sŷkon

apparently a primary word; a fig:--fig.

Biblical Usage

The fig is the fruit that proves the tree. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles? Every tree is known by his own fruit, for of thorns men do not gather figs, and James asks, can the fig tree bear olive berries?

Theological Significance

I believe the fig is the test of the tree and of the tongue: what grows shows what the root is.
