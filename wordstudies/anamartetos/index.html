anamártētos

from G1 (as a negative particle) and a presumed derivative of G264; sinless:--that is without sin.

Biblical Usage

The word is used once, and the whole argument is in that one word: when the scribes and Pharisees kept asking Jesus about the woman taken in adultery, He lifted Himself up and said, 'He that is without sin among you, let him first cast a stone at her.' Sinless — the one qualification for throwing the first stone, and no one there had it.

Theological Significance

One word for sinless carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the silence that followed — no one without sin stepped forward, and the stones stayed on the ground.
