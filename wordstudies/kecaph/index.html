kᵉçaph

{silver (from its pale color); by implication, money}

Biblical Usage

Kecaph is the Aramaic word for silver, and so for money. Ezra 7:15 sends the silver and gold freely offered unto the God of Israel, whose habitation is in Jerusalem. Daniel 2:32 says the image's breast and arms were of silver, and Daniel 2:35 says the silver and the gold were broken to pieces together. Daniel 2:45 says the stone cut out without hands brake the silver and the gold in pieces.

Theological Significance

I believe silver is the metal of empires: offered freely to God in Ezra, and smashed by the stone in Daniel. I hold that every kingdom built on silver ends the same way, broken to pieces by the kingdom God cuts out himself.
