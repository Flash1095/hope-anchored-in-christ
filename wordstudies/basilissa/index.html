basílissa

feminine from G936; a queen:--queen.

Biblical Usage

Basilissa is a queen, and it is the word for the queen of the south who came to hear Solomon's wisdom and will rise in judgment against this generation. It is also the word for the Ethiopian eunuch who serves Candace the queen of the Ethiopians, and for Babylon who sits a queen and says she will see no sorrow.

Theological Significance

I believe the queen of the south is the rebuke to every generation that has greater wisdom in front of it and will not hear, because she travelled to the ends of the earth for Solomon while this generation has Christ before them. Babylon calls herself a queen and falls in one hour, and I hold that the difference between the two queens is the difference between seeking wisdom and boasting in pride.
