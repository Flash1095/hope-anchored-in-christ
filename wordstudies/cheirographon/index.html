cheirógraphon

neuter of a compound of G5495 and G1125; something hand-written ("chirograph"), i.e. a manuscript (specially, a legal document or bond (figuratively)):--handwriting.

Biblical Usage

A handwriting is a written bond, the record of ordinances against us, and Paul says Christ blotted it out, took it out of the way, and nailed it to His cross. The debt is cancelled by the nailing.

Theological Significance

I believe the handwriting against me is nailed to the cross, and there is nothing left for the accuser to read.
