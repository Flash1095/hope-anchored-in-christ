zârâh

to toss about; by implication, to diffuse, winnow

Biblical Usage

Zarah is winnowing and scattering, the farmer tossing grain into the wind so the chaff blows away and the good grain falls back down. Ruth comes to the threshing floor where Boaz winnows barley, and the same word turns to judgment in the prophets, where God scatters His people among the nations for their unfaithfulness. The Psalmist cries out from inside that scattering, remembering when God gave His people like sheep and scattered them among the heathen.

Theological Significance

I hold that God winnows His people the way a farmer winnows grain, not to destroy the wheat but to separate it from the chaff. Scattering feels like abandonment, yet the prophets always pair it with a promise of gathering. I believe the God who scatters in judgment is the same God who gathers in mercy, and no scattering of His is ever the end of the story.
