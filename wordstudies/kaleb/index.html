Kâlêb

Caleb, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Caleb is the spy who believed. He and Joshua alone brought back a good report, and when the people refused, Caleb waited forty years and then asked for the mountain. Moses swore that Caleb would enter the land because he wholly followed the LORD, and Joshua gave him Hebron for an inheritance.

Theological Significance

I believe Caleb is the pattern of a faith that outlasts delay. He wholly followed the LORD at forty and at eighty-five, and the mountain he asked for was the one the giants held — and that is the faith I want, that still asks for the hard ground.
