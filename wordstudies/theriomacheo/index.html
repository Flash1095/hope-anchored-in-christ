thēriomachéō

from a compound of G2342 and G3164; to be a beast-fighter (in the gladiatorial show), i.e. (figuratively) to encounter (furious men):--fight with wild beasts.

Biblical Usage

Scripture uses thēriomachéō where it means to fight with wild beasts. In 1 Corinthians 15:32, If after the manner of men I have fought with beasts at Ephesus.

Theological Significance

Paul saying he fought with beasts at Ephesus makes me ask what I would risk if the resurrection were not true, and then to live like it is.
