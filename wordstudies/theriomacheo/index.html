thēriomachéō

Word
θηριομαχέω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
thēriomachéō
Strongs Number
G2341
Definition
from a compound of G2342 and G3164; to be a beast-fighter (in the gladiatorial show), i.e. (figuratively) to encounter (furious men):--fight with wild beasts.
