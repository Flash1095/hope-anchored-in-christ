xýlinos

from G3586; wooden:--of wood.

Biblical Usage

In a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of earth, and the men who would not repent worshipped idols of gold, and silver, and brass, and stone, and of wood.

Theological Significance

I believe wood is the honest material of the house: gold vessels and wooden vessels stand side by side, and the question is never what a vessel is made of but whether its owner is fit for the Master's use.
