gargᵉrôwth

the throat (as used in rumination)

Biblical Usage

Gargeroth is the throat or the neck, and Proverbs uses it for the place where wisdom hangs. Instruction shall be an ornament of grace unto thy head and chains about thy neck in Proverbs 1:9, and mercy and truth shall be life unto thy soul and grace to thy neck in Proverbs 3:22. Let not mercy and truth forsake thee, says Proverbs 3:3, bind them about thy neck, and bind them continually upon thine heart in Proverbs 6:21.

Theological Significance

I believe the neck is where wisdom is worn, close to the voice and the heart. Mercy and truth bound about the neck go everywhere we go, and I hold that the words we carry there shape the life we live.
