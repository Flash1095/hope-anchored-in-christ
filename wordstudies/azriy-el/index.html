ʻAzrîyʼêl

Azriel, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Azriel is the name of three men — a chief of the half-tribe of Manasseh east of Jordan, a captain of Naphtali in David's army, and the father of Seraiah, whom Jehoiakim sent to arrest Jeremiah and Baruch. One name carried by a tribal chief, a royal captain, and the father of a man sent to seize a prophet.

Theological Significance

I hold that the same name can stand on both sides of the story — Azriel the captain served David, and Azriel's son was sent against Jeremiah — and God kept his prophet safe either way.
