ʻăbîy

density, i.e. depth or width

Biblical Usage

This word for thickness or depth is used of things built to last: the brim of the bronze sea ‘was an hand breadth thick’; and the walls of Ezekiel’s temple are five cubits thick. It measures solidity — how much substance a thing has.

Theological Significance

It is a builder’s word, not a preacher’s word, and it carries no doctrine of its own — though it does show me how seriously God takes the house He orders built: measured, solid, and made to last.
