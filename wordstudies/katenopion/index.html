katenṓpion

from G2596 and G1799; directly in front of:--before (the presence of), in the sight of.

Biblical Usage

To be directly in front of is to stand before the presence of, and God chose us in Him before the foundation of the world that we should be holy and without blame before Him in love, and Christ will present us holy and unblameable and unreproveable in His sight, and Jude says He is able to present us faultless before the presence of His glory with exceeding joy. The word is the place we will stand, and the state we will stand in.

Theological Significance

I believe I will stand before Him holy and without blame, not because I am blameless but because Christ presents me. I hold that this is the end of the gospel: faultless before glory, with exceeding joy, and I will live toward that day.
