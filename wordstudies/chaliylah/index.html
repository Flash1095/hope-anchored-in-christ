châlîylâh

literal for a profaned thing; used (interj.) far be it!

Biblical Usage

Chaliylah is the Bible's strongest refusal — far be it, God forbid. Abraham says, that be far from Thee to slay the righteous with the wicked; the tribes say, God forbid that we should rebel against the LORD; God says to Eli, be it far from Me; and Joab says, far be it from me that I should swallow up or destroy.

Theological Significance

I believe far be it is the word for a line that must not be crossed: Abraham draws it for God, the tribes draw it for themselves, and God draws it for Eli. Some things are simply far from us, and far from Him.
