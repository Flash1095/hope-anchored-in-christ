Bêyth hâ-ʻĂrâbâh

Beth-ha-Arabah, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Beth-Arabah means house of the desert valley, and it turns up three times, always in the boundary lists. Joshua 15:6 sets it on Judah's border, Joshua 15:61 counts it among the wilderness towns, and Joshua 18:22 lists it again for Benjamin. A place in the low, dry country, passed from one tribe's list to another's.

Theological Significance

I hold that God drew the borders himself, and even a desert-valley town had its line drawn by his hand.
