gâbahh

to soar, i.e. be lofty; figuratively, to be haughty

Biblical Usage

Gabahh means to soar, to be high — and the Bible watches carefully whether the height is God's or man's. Saul stood higher than any of the people from his shoulders upward in 1 Samuel 10:23, and Jehoshaphat's heart was lifted up in the ways of the LORD in 2 Chronicles 17:6. But the daughters of Zion are haughty in Isaiah 3:16, and Sodom's daughters were haughty and committed abomination in Ezekiel 16:50.

Theological Significance

I believe there is a height that pleases God and a height that provokes Him, and the difference is the direction of the heart. Jehoshaphat's heart was lifted up in the ways of the LORD, and I hold that this is the only lifting up that ends well.
