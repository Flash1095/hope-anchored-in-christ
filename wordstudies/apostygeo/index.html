apostygéō

from G575 and the base of G4767; to detest utterly:--abhor.

Biblical Usage

Paul's command is blunt: abhor what is evil, cling to what is good. The word is utter detestation, the opposite of tolerating evil or making peace with it.

Theological Significance

Love that never hates evil is not love, it is indifference.
