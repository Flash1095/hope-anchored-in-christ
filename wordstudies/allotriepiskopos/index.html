allotriepískopos

Word
ἀλλοτριεπίσκοπος
Language
Greek
Transliteration
allotriepískopos
Strongs Number

Definition
from G245 and G1985; overseeing others' affairs, i.e. a meddler (specially, in Gentile customs):--busybody in other men's matters.
