áriston

Word
ἄριστον
Language
Greek
Transliteration
áriston
Strongs Number
G712
Definition
apparently neuter of a superlative from the same as G730; the best meal (or breakfast; perhaps from ἦρι ("early")), i.e. luncheon:--dinner.
