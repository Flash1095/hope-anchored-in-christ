aréskō

probably from G142 (through the idea of exciting emotion); to be agreeable (or by implication, to seek to be so):--please.

Biblical Usage

To please, to seek to be agreeable, is the word for the aim of a Christian's life, and Paul draws the line sharply. He pleases all men in all things, seeking not his own profit but the profit of many; yet if he still pleased men he would not be the servant of Christ; the strong ought to bear the weak and not please themselves; and the gospel is spoken not as pleasing men, but God.

Theological Significance

I hold that pleasing people and pleasing God are not the same work, and they cannot both be the master. Paul pleased men only where it served their salvation, and the moment pleasing men would cost the truth, he stopped.
