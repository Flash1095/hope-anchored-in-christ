markôleth

a mart

Biblical Usage

markôleth is a mart, a market, and it occurs once, in Ezekiel 27:24. There Tyre's merchants trade in all sorts of things, in blue clothes and embroidered work and in chests of rich apparel, bound with cords and made of cedar, among her merchandise. The word is the market stall of a proud city.

Theological Significance

I believe markôleth shows me that Tyre's markets sold the world its finery, and that every mart of the nations will answer to God.
