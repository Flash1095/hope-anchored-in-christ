chălôwm

a dream

Biblical Usage

Chalowm is a dream. God came to Abimelech in a dream by night, He says He will speak to a prophet in a dream, the law warns that a dreamer of dreams may still be a false prophet, and Gideon's enemy dreamed of a cake of barley bread tumbling into the camp.

Theological Significance

I believe dreams are God's speech and the devil's counterfeit in turn: He speaks to Abimelech to stop a sin, He speaks to prophets to guide a people, and He lets a Midianite dream the defeat before it happens. The test is never the dream — it is the God who sent it.
