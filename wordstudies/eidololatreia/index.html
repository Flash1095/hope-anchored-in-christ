eidōlolatreía

from G1497 and G2999; image-worship (literally or figuratively):--idolatry.

Biblical Usage

This is the worship of images, and the Greek text spells it with a short vowel in the middle, idololatry, where Strong's lists it with a long one. Paul names idolatry among the works of the flesh, tells the Colossians to put it to death as covetousness, and warns the Corinthians to flee from it. The spelling differs, but the sin is the same.

Theological Significance

I believe idolatry is still the worship of anything in place of God, and Paul's command is still the same: flee from it.
