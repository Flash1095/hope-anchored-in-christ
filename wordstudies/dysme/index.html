dysmḗ

from G1416; the sun-set, i.e. (by implication) the western region:--west.

Biblical Usage

The west, where the sun goes down, the quarter from which the cloud rises that brings the shower, and the direction from which many shall come to sit down with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom. The New Jerusalem has three gates on the west, as on every side.

Theological Significance

I believe the west is the picture of the wide welcome of the kingdom, because they shall come from the east and the west to sit at the table. The gates stand open on every side, and the sunset quarter is no farther from the feast than the sunrise.
