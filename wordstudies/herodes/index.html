Hērṓdēs

compound of ἥρως (a "hero") and G1491; heroic; Herod, the name of four Jewish kings:--Herod.

Biblical Usage

Herod is the name of four Jewish kings in the New Testament — Herod the Great, who tried to kill the child Jesus; Herod Antipas, who beheaded John the Baptist; and Herod Agrippa I and II, who persecuted the church. The name stands 43 times in Scripture, in Matthew, Mark, Luke, John and Acts.

Theological Significance

I believe the Herods matter because they show what earthly power does when it meets God’s King — it rages, and it loses. I hold that every Herod in the story is a warning, and every deliverance from them is a promise.
