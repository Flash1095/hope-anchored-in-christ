ʼĂchôwchîy

an Achochite or descendant of Achoach

Biblical Usage

The Ahohite was the family of Eleazar son of Dodo, one of the three mighty men who stood with David against the Philistines, and of Dodai, who commanded the second month's course. The clan is traced through Ahoah, whose name stands at the head of the family.

Theological Significance

No doctrine hangs on a family name. But the honour roll it sits in says something plain: God remembers the men who stood their ground when the army fled.
