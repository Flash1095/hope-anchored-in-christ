shêsh

six (as an overplus beyond five or the fingers of the hand); as ord. sixth

Biblical Usage

Six counts the years of Noah's life when the flood came, the six days a man labours before the Sabbath, the six days Israel gathers manna, and the six years a man sows his field before the Sabbath of the land. Six is the number of work and of the world that waits for its rest.

Theological Significance

I believe six is the number of man at his work, and the Sabbath is God's answer to it. I hold that six days of labour are honest and good, but the seventh belongs to Him, and that is what keeps the six from owning me.
