chôresh

a forest (perhaps as furnishing the material for fabric)

Biblical Usage

Choresh is a forest, a wood where men hide and where cities are built, and David knows it well. David abides in the wood in the wilderness of Ziph while Saul seeks him, Jotham builds castles and towers in the forests of Judah, and the prophets use the forest as the image of a proud nation laid low.

Theological Significance

I believe the forest in David's story is God's hiding place for a hunted man, and He still hides His people in the woods. I hold that a forest can be a refuge or a ruin, and the difference is whether God is in it.
