ʼĔlîytsâphân

Word
אֱלִיצָפָן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼĔlîytsâphân
Strongs Number
H469
Definition
Elitsaphan or Eltsaphan, an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Elizaphan or Elzaphan = "my God has protected"; 1) a Kohathite chief in the wilderness; 2) a Zebulunite chief; 3) Uzziel's son, Mishael's brother in time of Moses
