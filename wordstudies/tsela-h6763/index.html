tsêlâʻ

Word
צֵלָע
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
tsêlâʻ
Strongs Number
H6763
Definition
a rib (as curved), literally (of the body) or figuratively (of a door, i.e. leaf); hence, a side, literally (of a person) or figuratively (of an object or the sky, i.e. quarter); architecturally, a (especially floor or ceiling) timber or plank (single or collective, i.e. a flooring)
Part Of Speech
noun (feminine)
Biblical Usage
1) side, rib, beam; 1a) rib (of man); 1b) rib (of hill, ridge, etc); 1c) side-chambers or cells (of temple structure); 1d) rib, plank, board (of cedar or fir); 1e) leaves (of door); 1f) side (of ark)
