tselaʻ

a limping or full (figuratively)

Biblical Usage

tselaʻ is used for halting, limping. It appears 3 times in Scripture, including Psalms 38:17 and Jeremiah 20:10.

Theological Significance

I believe tselaʻ reminds me that God's Word is exact down to the word, not only in the great themes.
