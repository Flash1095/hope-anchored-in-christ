kŷma

from κύω (to swell (with young), i.e. bend, curve); a billow (as bursting or toppling):--wave.

Biblical Usage

The wave is the billow that rises and breaks, and it is the picture of trouble in all four appearances. In Matthew 8:24 the ship is covered with the waves while Jesus sleeps, in Mark 4:37 the waves beat into the ship, in Matthew 14:24 the ship is tossed with waves, and in Jude 1:13 false teachers are "raging waves of the sea, foaming out their own shame."

Theological Significance

I am sure the waves teach me what the sea always teaches in Scripture: the storm is real, the ship is small, and the Lord who sleeps in the boat is the Lord who stills it. The waves rose against the ship, and the ship held because He was in it.
