anáchysis

from a compound of G303 and χέω (to pour); properly, effusion, i.e. (figuratively) license:--excess.

Biblical Usage

The word appears once, and it is excess: Peter says the Gentiles think it strange that you run not with them to the same excess of riot, speaking evil of you. It is a pouring out without restraint — the flood of loose living the old friends still run in, and cannot understand why you have left.

Theological Significance

One word for excess carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is Peter's picture — the old life as a flood, and the strange look you get for stepping out of it.
