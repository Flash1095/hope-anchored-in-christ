yâphâh

Word
יָפָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
yâphâh
Strongs Number
H3302
Definition
properly, to be bright, i.e. (by implication) beautiful
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to be bright, be beautiful, be handsome, be fair; 1a) (Qal) to be beautiful; 1b) (Piel) to beautify; 1c) (Pual) to be beautiful; 1d) (Hithpael) to beautify oneself
