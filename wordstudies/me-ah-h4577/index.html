mᵉʻâh

only in plural the bowels

Biblical Usage

The word is the bowels, used once in the Aramaic of Daniel, for the belly of the great image: its head was gold, its breast and arms silver, and its belly and thighs brass.

Theological Significance

I hold that the belly of brass is the third kingdom in the dream, and the image stands as a warning: empires look splendid from the head down, and they all fall to a stone cut without hands.
