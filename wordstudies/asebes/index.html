asebḗs

from G1 (as a negative particle) and a presumed derivative of G4576; irreverent, i.e. (by extension) impious or wicked:-- ungodly (man).

Biblical Usage

The ungodly are those without God, and the New Testament will not soften the word. God justifieth the ungodly, and faith is counted for righteousness; the law is made for the lawless and disobedient, for the ungodly and for sinners; Peter asks where the ungodly and the sinner shall appear if the righteous scarcely be saved; and the heavens and earth are reserved unto fire against the day of judgment of ungodly men.

Theological Significance

I believe 'ungodly' is the word that makes the gospel good news: God justifies the ungodly, not the deserving. The same word names the men the fire is reserved for, and I hold that the difference between the two is faith, the ungodly who believe are counted righteous, and the ungodly who will not are left to the judgment.
