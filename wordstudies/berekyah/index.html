Berekyâh

Word
בֶּרֶכְיָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Berekyâh
Strongs Number
H1296
Definition
Berekjah, the name of six Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Berachiah or Berechiah = "Jehovah blesses"; 1) a son of Zerubbabel; 2) a Levite who returned for the exile; 3) father of Meshullam, one of Nehemiah's chiefs who assisted in rebuilding the walls of Jerusalem; 4) father of Zechariah; 5) a Gershonite Levite, the father of Asaph, the singer, doorkeeper of the ark; 6) an Ephraimite chief in the time of Ahaz
