nephesh

Word
נֶפֶשׁ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
nephesh
Strongs Number
H5315
Definition
Soul, life, self or living being — the whole person as a living, desiring, God-breathed creature.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Genesis 2:7; Psalm 42:1; Deuteronomy 6:5; Psalm 103:1
Biblical Usage
Nephesh does not mean a detachable ghost inside the body; Adam 'became a living nephesh.' The Psalms speak of the nephesh thirsting for God, blessing the Lord and finding rest in Him alone.
Theological Significance
Nephesh teaches that God cares for the whole person — body, mind, longings and life itself. The soul that thirsts for God finds its anchor and satisfaction in Him.
