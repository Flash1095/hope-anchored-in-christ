dḗpote

from G1211 and G4218; a particle of generalization; indeed, at any time:--(what-)soever.

Biblical Usage

This little particle means indeed or at any time, and it stands once, in the verse about the pool of Bethesda, where the first man into the troubled water was made whole of whatsoever disease he had. It belongs to the Received Text behind the King James Version, and the verse itself is absent from the Greek text most modern Bibles follow.

Theological Significance

I hold that the verse's disputed standing should be stated plainly; the word is real, the healing it describes is real, and the honest thing is to say where the word is found and where it is not.
