nidneh

a sheath; figuratively, the body (as the receptacle of the soul)

Biblical Usage

A sheath is the body itself, the case the soul lives in. Daniel says he was grieved in his spirit in the midst of his body, and the visions of his head troubled him — the spirit grieves inside its sheath.

Theological Significance

I hold that the body is only the sheath: Daniel's grief is in the spirit, and the visions trouble the head that carries them.
