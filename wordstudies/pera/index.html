peraʻ

the hair (as dishevelled)

Biblical Usage

Pera is the hair, the locks left to grow. The priests must neither shave their heads nor let their locks grow long, but only poll them, and the Nazirite must let the locks of his head grow until his vow is fulfilled.

Theological Significance

I am convinced that God orders even the hair: the priest's locks are trimmed, the Nazirite's are left long, and both are holy in their place.
