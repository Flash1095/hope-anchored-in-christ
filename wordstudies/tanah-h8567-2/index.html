tânâh

to ascribe (praise), i.e. celebrate, commemorate

Biblical Usage

They that are delivered from the noise of archers in the places of drawing water, there shall they rehearse the righteous acts of the Lord. And the daughters of Israel go yearly to lament the daughter of Jephthah, four days in a year. The word is to ascribe praise, to celebrate, to commemorate: rehearsing God's righteous acts, and remembering a daughter's death.

Theological Significance

I believe the rehearsing and the lamenting belong together: Israel remembers God's righteous acts at the wells, and remembers Jephthah's daughter yearly, and the remembering is itself a kind of faithfulness.
