mégas

(including the prolonged forms, feminine μεγάλη, plural μεγάλοι, etc.; compare also G3176, G3187); big (literally or figuratively, in a very wide application):--(+ fear) exceedingly, great(-est), high, large, loud, mighty, + (be) sore (afraid), strong, X to years.

Biblical Usage

Great is the word for what is large in size, in power, and in worth, and the New Testament uses it for the kingdom, the seed, the mystery, and the voice: the least in the kingdom is great, the mustard seed grows greatest among herbs, great is the mystery of godliness, and John hears a great voice on the Lord's day. The angel promises that Jesus shall be great.

Theological Significance

I believe great is the word the gospel keeps for what God does: the kingdom makes the least great, the smallest seed becomes the greatest herb, and the mystery of godliness is great because God was manifest in the flesh. I hold that the greatness of Christ is the measure of all other greatness — everything else is small beside Him.
