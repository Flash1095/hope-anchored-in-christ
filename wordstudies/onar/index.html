ónar

of uncertain derivation; a dream:--dream.

Biblical Usage

Onar is a dream, and in Matthew every dream is a message from God. The angel appears to Joseph in a dream in Matthew 1:20, telling him not to fear to take Mary as his wife, and the wise men are warned in a dream in Matthew 2:12 not to return to Herod. Joseph is warned again in Matthew 2:13 to flee to Egypt, and again in Matthew 2:19, when Herod is dead, to go back.

Theological Significance

I believe God speaks in dreams when His people are asleep, and Joseph's obedience in the night saved the child. My position is that a dream from God is never a suggestion: Joseph rose and went, and the story of Jesus depends on it.
