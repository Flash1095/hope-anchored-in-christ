çibbôleth

an ear of grain

Biblical Usage

Shibboleth is an ear of grain, and it becomes a password at the fords of Jordan: the men of Ephraim cannot say it, and forty-two thousand fall. The word is a test of belonging, and the test is in the tongue.

Theological Significance

I believe the shibboleth shows that words reveal where we come from, and God hears what our speech says about our hearts.
