epirrháptō

from G1909 and the base of G4476; to stitch upon, i.e. fasten with the needle:--sew on.

Biblical Usage

Epirrhapto means to sew on, to stitch upon — and no man seweth a piece of new cloth on an old garment, else the new piece that filled it up taketh away from the old, and the rent is made worse.

Theological Significance

The new covenant is not sewn onto the old life, I hold: it is a new garment altogether, and the stitching proves it.
