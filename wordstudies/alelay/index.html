ʼalᵉlay

alas!

Biblical Usage

Twice, and both times it is a cry of grief. Job says, If I be wicked, woe unto me; and if I be righteous, yet will I not lift up my head. And Micah cries, Woe is me, for I am as when they have gathered the summer fruits, as the grapegleanings of the vintage: there is no cluster to eat; the word is the sound a man makes when there is nothing left to gather.

Theological Significance

A word for alas carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the honesty in it, that the Bible lets a righteous man cry woe over a land with nothing left to eat.
