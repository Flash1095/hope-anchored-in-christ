methístēmi

, or (1 Cor. 13:2) μεθιστάνω from G3326 and G2476; to transfer, i.e. carry away, depose or (figuratively) exchange, seduce:--put out, remove, translate, turn away.

Biblical Usage

God translated us into the kingdom of His dear Son, Paul says faith could remove mountains and still be nothing without love, and God removed Saul and raised up David. The word is a transfer, and Colossians uses it for the greatest one: out of darkness and into the kingdom.

Theological Significance

I believe Colossians 1:13 is the whole gospel in one transfer: we did not move ourselves out of darkness, God delivered us and translated us, and the kingdom we now live in is His Son's. We did not move ourselves; God delivered us and translated us into His Son's kingdom.
