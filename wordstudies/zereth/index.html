zereth

the spread of the fingers, i.e. a span

Biblical Usage

A span — the stretch from thumb to little finger on an open hand — is the Bible's smallest measure, about half a cubit. Goliath stands six cubits and a span, the breastplate is a span square, and Isaiah asks who has measured the heavens with a span. The same hand-measure sizes a giant, a priest's garment, and the sky.

Theological Significance

I hold that the span humbles me: what measures a giant's height is nothing in the hand that measured the heavens.
