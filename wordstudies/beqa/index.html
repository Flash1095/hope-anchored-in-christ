beqaʻ

Word
בֶּקַע
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
beqaʻ
Strongs Number
H1235
Definition
a section (half) of a shekel, i.e. a beka (a weight and a coin)
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) half, part also called "shekel of the sanctuary" or "holy shekel"; 2) half a shekel (meton) There appears to be at least three different shekels, one of silver, gold and copper. See a Bible Dictionary for a complete treatment of the subject.
