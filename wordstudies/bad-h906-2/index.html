bad

flaxen thread or yarn; hence, a linen garment

Biblical Usage

Bad is linen — fine flaxen thread, the cloth of the priest's garments. Aaron's sons wore linen breeches, Samuel ministered before the Lord as a child girded with a linen ephod, David danced before the ark in a linen ephod, and Daniel saw a man clothed in linen above the waters of the river. Linen is the cloth of service before God.

Theological Significance

I believe linen is the right picture for service: plain, clean, and worn before God. Samuel wore it as a child and Daniel saw it on the man above the river, and I hold that the same clean cloth is what God gives his servants to wear.
