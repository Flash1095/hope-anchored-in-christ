heîs

(including the neuter (etc.) ἕν); a primary numeral; one:--a(-n, -ny, certain), + abundantly, man, one (another), only, other, some. See also G1527, G3367, G3391, G3762.

Biblical Usage

Heis means one, and it is the number the whole faith stands on. In Mark 12:29 the Lord our God is one Lord, in John 10:30 Jesus says I and my Father are one, in Ephesians 4:5 there is one Lord, one faith, one baptism, and in Galatians 3:28 all are one in Christ Jesus.

Theological Significance

I believe one is the word that holds the gospel together: one God, one Lord, one faith, one people. The divisions I make are mine, not his, and the oneness he made is the one I am called to keep.
