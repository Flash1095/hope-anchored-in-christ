agenḗs

from G1 (as negative particle) and G1085; properly, without kin, i.e. (of unknown descent, and by implication) ignoble:--base things.

Biblical Usage

Agenes means 'base things' — things without kin, of unknown descent, ignoble in the world's eyes — and it appears once, in Paul's great reversal: 'And base things of the world, and things which are despised, hath God chosen, yea, and things which are not, to bring to nought things that are.' God chooses the nobodies, the things the world calls nothing, and uses them to shame the somethings.

Theological Significance

God chooses the base things of the world, and this word names them — but the choosing is God's doing, and the verse says so.
