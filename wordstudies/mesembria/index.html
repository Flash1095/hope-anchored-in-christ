mesēmbría

from G3319 and G2250; midday; by implication, the south:--noon, south.

Biblical Usage

Mesembria is midday, noon, and by extension the south, where the sun stands at noon. Acts 8:26 sends Philip toward the south, to the desert road from Jerusalem to Gaza, and Acts 22:6 has Paul struck by the light from heaven about noon on the road to Damascus.

Theological Significance

I believe noon is the hour God chooses for turning points: a desert road and a Damascus road, both in the full light of day.
