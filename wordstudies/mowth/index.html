môwth

death

Biblical Usage

Moth is death, in Aramaic — the king's decree says whoever will not do the law of God and the law of the king, let judgment be executed speedily upon him, whether it be unto death, or to banishment, or to confiscation of goods, or to imprisonment.

Theological Significance

I believe the decree shows how seriously the return was guarded: the law was not advice, and death stood at the edge of disobedience.
