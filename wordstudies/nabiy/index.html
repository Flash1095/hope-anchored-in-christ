nâbîyʼ

a prophet or (generally) inspired man

Biblical Usage

Nabi is a prophet, a person who speaks for God, and the word covers nearly three hundred occurrences from Abraham to Malachi. In Genesis 20:7 Abraham is called a prophet who shall pray for Abimelech, and in Deuteronomy 18:18 the Lord promises to raise up a Prophet like Moses and put His words in his mouth. In 1 Kings 18:22 Elijah stands alone as a prophet of the Lord against the prophets of Baal, and in Jeremiah 1:5 Jeremiah is ordained a prophet to the nations before his birth.

Theological Significance

I believe a prophet is not a fortune teller but a mouthpiece, and the test is whether the words are God's or his own. Abraham prayed, Moses spoke, Elijah stood alone, and Jeremiah was chosen before he could choose. I hold that the Prophet like Moses has come, and His words are the ones I live by.
