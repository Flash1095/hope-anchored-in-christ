ʼerez

a cedar tree (from the tenacity of its roots)

Biblical Usage

ʼErez is the cedar — the great tree of Lebanon, the timber of kings. Solomon builds the temple with cedars; the righteous flourish like the cedar; and the cedars of Lebanon are the LORD's planting. It is the word for strength that grows slowly and stands for centuries.

Theological Significance

I believe ʼerez is the picture of the righteous life: planted by God, growing slowly, and standing when storms come. I hold that the cedar's strength is borrowed strength — it stands because He planted it.
