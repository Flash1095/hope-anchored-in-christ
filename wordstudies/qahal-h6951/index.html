qâhâl

assemblage (usually concretely)

Biblical Usage

The congregation is Israel gathered as one body: the whole assembly kills the passover lamb at even, Moses speaks his song in the ears of all the congregation, Solomon blesses all the congregation of Israel at the dedication of the temple, the psalmist vows to declare God's name in the midst of the congregation, Ezra reads the law before the assembly. Joel calls the congregation to fast and weep.

Theological Significance

I believe the congregation is where God's people become visible to each other, and where the individual's faith becomes the people's worship. What holds me is that the same word covers the passover, the law, and the temple, so the congregation is not a meeting, it is the people God called out.
