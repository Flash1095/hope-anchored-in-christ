Shimʻîy

a Shimite (collectively) or descendants of Shimi

Biblical Usage

The Shimites are the family of Shimei the son of Gershon, counted among the Levites in the wilderness. The house of Shimei is named among the families that mourn in the day of the LORD.

Theological Significance

I believe a Levite family in the census is a family set apart for God's service, and the Shimites kept their place in it.
