Dibrîy

Dibri, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Dibri is named only once, as the father of Shelomith, the Israelitish woman whose son blasphemed the Name and was stoned. His name survives in the record of a judgment the law made plain.

Theological Significance

I hold that even a hard judgment is worth recording truly. Dibri's son is named, his mother is named, and the law is carried out, because God's name is not a thing to be spoken lightly.
