Têymânîy

Word
תֵּימָנִי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Têymânîy
Strongs Number
H8489
Definition
a Temanite or descendant of Teman
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Temanite or Temani = see Teman "southward"; 1) an inhabitant of Teman located east of Idumea; 2) a descendant of Teman, the grandson of Esau and a duke of Edom
