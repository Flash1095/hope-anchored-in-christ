lᵉbânâh

properly, (the) white, i.e. the moon

Biblical Usage

lᵉbânâh means the moon — and its range, from the ordinary to the holy, is the point. In Isaiah 24:23, "Then the moon shall be confounded"; and in Isaiah 30:26, "Moreover the light of the moon shall be as the light of the sun". In Song of Solomon 6:10, "fair as the moon".

Theological Significance

I believe the moon will be confounded on the day the Lord reigns — the lesser light steps back when the true Light comes. I hold that even the moon's brightness is borrowed, and one day it will shine sevenfold because God heals His people. My position is that I worship the Maker of the moon, never the moon itself.
