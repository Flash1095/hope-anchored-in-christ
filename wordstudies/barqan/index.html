barqân

a thorn (perhaps as burning brightly)

Biblical Usage

Barqan is a thorn, a brier, and Gideon threatens to tear the flesh of the men of Succoth with thorns and briers of the wilderness, and then does it.

Theological Significance

I hold that Gideon's thorns are a warning to every town that refuses bread to God's men on God's errand, because the judge who was patient with Midian was not patient with Succoth.
