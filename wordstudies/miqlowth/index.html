Miqlôwth

Mikloth, a place in the Desert

Biblical Usage

Mikloth is named in the registers of Benjamin, where he is counted among the sons of Jeiel who lived in Jerusalem. He is named again among the rulers of David's monthly courses, where he served under Dodai in the second month.

Theological Significance

I believe the two mentions show a man who was both a father in the city and a ruler in the army, and the record keeps both.
