ʻelem

properly, something kept out of sight, i.e. a lad

Biblical Usage

ʻelem is the word for a young lad. Saul asks whose son 'this youth' is in 1 Samuel 17:56, and Jonathan sends the same lad after his arrows in 1 Samuel 20:22.

Theological Significance

In ʻelem I find no hidden theology; it simply means a lad.
