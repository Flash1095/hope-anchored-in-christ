mâlôwn

a lodgment, i.e. caravanserai or encampment

Biblical Usage

Malon is the lodging, the inn, the place where travellers stop for the night, and it is where some of the Bible's strangest meetings happen. Exodus 4:24 says it came to pass by the way in the inn that the LORD met Moses and sought to kill him, and Genesis 42:27 has Joseph's brothers opening their sacks in the inn and finding their money returned. Joshua 4:3 has the twelve stones carried to the lodging place where Israel lodged that night, and Jeremiah 9:2 wishes for a lodging place of wayfaring men in the wilderness, to leave a treacherous people behind.

Theological Significance

I believe the inn in Exodus 4:24 is the warning in this word: a traveller can be met by God at any stop, and the meeting is not always gentle. My position is that Jeremiah 9:2 is the other side of it, because sometimes the lodging place is not a danger but a refuge, the only place to get away from a faithless people.
