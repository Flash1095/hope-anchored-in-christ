básanos

perhaps remotely from the same as G939 (through the notion of going to the bottom); a touch-stone, i.e. (by analogy) torture:--torment.

Biblical Usage

Basanos is torment, the touchstone that tests and the pain that proves. They bring to Jesus all who are sick and tormented with diseases, and the rich man lifts up his eyes in hell, being in torments, and begs that his brothers be warned so they do not come into this place of torment.

Theological Significance

I believe torment in the Gospels is always a place you can still be saved from, because the sick are brought to Jesus to be healed and the rich man begs for his brothers while there is still time. His torment is the warning, and I hold that the warning is mercy while we are alive to hear it.
