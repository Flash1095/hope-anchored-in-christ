ʼElnâthân

Word
אֶלְנָתָן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʼElnâthân
Strongs Number
H494
Definition
Elnathan, the name of four Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Elnathan = "God has given"; 1) king Jehoiachin's maternal grandfather; 2) three chief men in Ezra's time; 3) son of Achbor, a military commander under Jehoiakim
