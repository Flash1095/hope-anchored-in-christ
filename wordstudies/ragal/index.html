râgal

Word
רָגַל
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
râgal
Strongs Number
H7270
Definition
to walk along; but only in specifically, applications, to reconnoiter, to be a tale-bearer (i.e. slander); to lead about
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to go on foot, spy out, foot it, go about, walk along, move the feet; 1a) (Qal) to be a tale-bearer, slander, go about; 1b) (Piel); 1b1) to slander; 1b2) to go about as explorer, spy; 1c) (Tiphel) to teach to walk
