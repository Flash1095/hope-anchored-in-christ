kēnsos

of Latin origin; properly, an enrollment ("census"), i.e. (by implication) a tax:--tribute.

Biblical Usage

Kensos means the census tax, the tribute paid to Caesar: Matthew 17:25 has Jesus asking Peter, of whom do the kings of the earth take custom or tribute? Matthew 22:17 asks, is it lawful to give tribute unto Caesar, or not? Matthew 22:19 says, shew me the tribute money, and they brought him a penny. Mark 12:14 asks the same, is it lawful to give tribute to Caesar, or not?

Theological Significance

I believe the tribute question is the trap that caught the trappers: they brought the penny, and the penny bore Caesar's image. I hold that what bears Caesar's image goes to Caesar, and what bears God's image goes to God.
