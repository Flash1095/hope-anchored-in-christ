sᵉʼôr

barm or yeast-cake (as swelling by fermentation)

Biblical Usage

Se'or is leaven, and the law will not have it in the house or in the offering. Seven days shall ye eat unleavened bread; even the first day ye shall put away leaven out of your houses, for whosoever eateth leavened bread shall be cut off from Israel. No meat offering shall be made with leaven, for ye shall burn no leaven in any offering made by fire. And at the feast there shall be no leavened bread seen with thee in all thy coast seven days.

Theological Significance

I believe leaven is the picture of sin's spread: a little hidden in the lump works through the whole. That is why God commanded it out of the house and out of the offering — His people and His worship are to be unleavened. And I hold that the Passover bread without leaven is the bread of a people in a hurry to leave Egypt, with no time to rise.
