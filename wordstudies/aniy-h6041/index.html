ʻânîy

depressed, in mind or circumstances

Biblical Usage

The meek and the afflicted poor are one people in this word's family: the lowly whom God defends and lifts up. Moses is called the meekest man on earth, the good tidings are preached to the meek, and the humble boast in the Lord who hears them.

Theological Significance

I believe meekness is not weakness but strength held low before God, and the afflicted poor are not forgotten by Him but chosen as His special care. I hold that God still lifts the lowly and brings down the proud, and that is where my hope sits.
