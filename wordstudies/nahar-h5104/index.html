nâhâr

a stream (including the sea; expectation the Nile, Euphrates, etc.); figuratively, prosperity

Biblical Usage

Nahar is a river, a stream, and it runs from Eden to the city of God. In Genesis 2:10 a river goes out of Eden to water the garden, and from there it is parted into four heads, and in Psalm 46:4 there is a river, the streams whereof shall make glad the city of God.

Theological Significance

I hold that the river is the Bible's picture of life that comes from God and cannot be dammed; it watered Eden, it gladdens the city, and it still runs.
