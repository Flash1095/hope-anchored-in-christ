akribōs

adverb from the same as G196; exactly:--circumspectly, diligently, perfect(-ly).

Biblical Usage

The adverb of exactness. Herod tells the wise men to search diligently for the young child; Luke writes to Theophilus that he has had perfect understanding of all things from the very first; Paul tells believers to walk circumspectly, exactly, carefully; and the Thessalonians know perfectly that the day of the Lord so cometh as a thief in the night.

Theological Significance

An adverb of exactness carries little weight on its own; its weight is the lives it describes, Luke investigating carefully, Paul commanding careful walking, and the diligence that serves the truth.
