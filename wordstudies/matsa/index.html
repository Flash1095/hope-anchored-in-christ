mâtsâʼ

properly, to come forth to, i.e. appear or exist; transitively, to attain, i.e. find or acquire; figuratively, to occur, meet or be present

Biblical Usage

To find is the word for what happens when seeking meets grace: Noah found grace in the eyes of the LORD, and Moses promises that if Israel seeks God with all its heart it will find Him. Isaiah calls the people to seek the LORD while He may be found, Jeremiah promises that those who search with all their heart will find Him, and Wisdom says those who seek her early shall find her.

Theological Significance

I believe finding is never an accident in Scripture: God is found by those who seek Him, and He is found because He wants to be found. Noah did not earn grace by searching, but grace found him searching, and that is the pattern for us. I hold that the promise still stands — seek Him with all your heart, and you will find Him.
