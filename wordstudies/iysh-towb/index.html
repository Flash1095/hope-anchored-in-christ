ʼÎysh-Ṭôwb

Ish-Tob, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Ish-tob is the land of Tob, whose men the Ammonites hire against David. In 2 Samuel 10:6 the children of Ammon hire twelve thousand men of Ish-tob, and in 2 Samuel 10:8 the men of Ish-tob stand by themselves in the field against Israel.

Theological Significance

I believe Ish-tob is a hired sword in the story: twelve thousand men paid to fight David, and David's captains beat them in the field.
