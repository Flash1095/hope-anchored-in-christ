ʼăraʻ

the earth; by implication (figuratively) low

Biblical Usage

In Aramaic this is the word for earth, the ground beneath every kingdom. The stone cut without hands becomes a mountain and fills the whole earth, a second kingdom will bear rule over all the earth, Nebuchadnezzar dreams of a tree in the midst of the earth, and the four beasts are four kings who rise out of the earth.

Theological Significance

I hold that earth is the stage where kingdoms rise and fall. Daniel's message is that every one of them is measured against the kingdom that will fill the whole earth and never be destroyed.
