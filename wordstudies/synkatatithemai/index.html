synkatatíthemai

middle from G4862 and G2698; to deposit (one's vote or opinion) in company with, i.e. (figuratively) to accord with:--consent.

Biblical Usage

Joseph of Arimathea is the one who had not consented to the counsel and deed of the council, a man who himself waited for the kingdom of God. One vote withheld is sometimes the whole witness: he would not agree to the death of Jesus, and his dissent is remembered.

Theological Significance

My position is that refusing consent to evil is a witness of its own, and I hold that Joseph's withheld vote is worth more than a hundred speeches.
