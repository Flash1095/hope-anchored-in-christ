ʼămânâh

something fixed, i.e. a covenant. an allowance

Biblical Usage

Something fixed, a covenant, an allowance, and Nehemiah uses it twice. Now those that sealed were Nehemiah and the priests and the Levites, the sealed covenant after the wall is built. And the singers have a certain portion, due for every day, by the king's commandment; the word is what is fixed and settled, whether a signature or a daily ration.

Theological Significance

A word for something fixed carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the two fixings, that the covenant was sealed in writing and the singers were fed by the day.
