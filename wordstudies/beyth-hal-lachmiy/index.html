Bêyth hal-Lachmîy

a Beth-lechemite, or native of Bethlechem

Biblical Usage

A Beth-lehemite is simply a man from Bethlehem, house of bread — and the word is famous because of who it describes. In 1 Samuel 16:18 a servant calls David a son of Jesse the Beth-lehemite, and in 1 Samuel 17:58 David answers Saul the same way. In 2 Samuel 21:19 the word marks Elhanan, a Beth-lehemite who slew the brother of Goliath.

Theological Significance

I believe it matters that David is introduced by his town before his throne: God chose a shepherd from Bethlehem, and the town's name went with him.
