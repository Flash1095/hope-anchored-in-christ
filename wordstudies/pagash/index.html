pâgash

to come in contact with, whether by accident or violence; figuratively, to concur

Biblical Usage

Pâgash is to meet, to encounter, to join. Jacob's servants meet Esau with a present, the LORD meets Moses on the way and seeks to kill him, mercy and truth are met together in the psalm, and a bear robbed of her whelps is safer to meet than a fool in his folly.

Theological Significance

I believe meetings decide destinies. Esau is met with gifts, Moses is met by God Himself, and in Christ mercy and truth meet and kiss.
