pâgash

to come in contact with, whether by accident or violence; figuratively, to concur

Biblical Usage

1) to meet, join, encounter; 1a) (Qal) to meet, encounter; 1b) (Niphal) to meet together, meet each other; 1c) (Piel) to meet, encounter
