kuççemeth

spelt (from its bristliness as if just shorn)

Biblical Usage

Spelt is a hardy, wheat-like grain, and it shows up in the farming and judgment passages. In Exodus 9:32 the wheat and the spelt were not smitten by the hail because they were not grown up, and in Ezekiel 4:9 it is one of the grains Ezekiel is told to mix into his bread.

Theological Significance

I am sure the spelt's survival in Exodus 9:32 is a small mercy worth noticing: the hail took the barley and the flax, but the late crop was spared, and God knew the season of each grain.
