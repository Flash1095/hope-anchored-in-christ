ʻôreph

the nape or back of the neck (as declining); hence, the back generally (whether literal or figurative)

Biblical Usage

The back of the neck is where stubbornness lives. Hezekiah's people turned their backs on the Lord and shut the doors of His house, and Israel is charged with hardening their necks, refusing to hear the prophets sent to them.

Theological Significance

I am convinced the stiff neck is the Bible's picture of my own heart: turning my back on God is a posture before it is a decision.
