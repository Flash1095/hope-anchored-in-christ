Paddân

Paddan or Paddan-Aram, a region of Syria

Biblical Usage

Paddan, or Paddan-aram, is the plain of northern Mesopotamia where Jacob's family lives. Isaac sends Jacob there to take a wife, Jacob carries all his substance out of Paddan-aram toward Canaan, and God appears to Jacob again when he returns from Paddan and blesses him.

Theological Significance

I hold that Paddan is the far country of Jacob's story: he leaves home empty-handed and comes back blessed, because God went with him there.
