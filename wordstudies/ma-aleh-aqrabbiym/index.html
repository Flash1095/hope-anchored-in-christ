Maʻălêh ʻAqrabbîym

Steep of Scorpions, a place in the Desert

Biblical Usage

Maaleh-acrabbim, the ascent of scorpions, was a pass on the southern border of the promised land, named for the scorpions that haunted its rocks. The border of Judah went up by it, and the coast of the Amorites ran from the same ascent.

Theological Significance

I believe the scorpion pass reminds us that the border of God's promise was not an easy road, and His people entered it anyway.
