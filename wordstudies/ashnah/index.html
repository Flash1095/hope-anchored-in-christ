ʼAshnâh

Ashnah, the name of two places in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Ashnah is the name of two towns in the low country of Judah, both listed in the inheritance. One stands in the valley with Eshtaol and Zoreah, and the other with Jiphtah and Nezib, and the verse gives the name and the neighbours and nothing more.

Theological Significance

Two towns sharing one name in the same inheritance tell me that the land was given out village by village, and that even the doubled names were written down, so no town would be lost from the record.
