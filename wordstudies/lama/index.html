lamá

or λαμμᾶ; of Hebrew origin (H4100 with prepositional prefix); lama (i.e. why):--lama.

Biblical Usage

Lama is the Aramaic word for "why," and it survives only in the cry from the cross. In Matthew 27:46 Jesus cries, "Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani?" and in Mark 15:34, "My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?" The Greek text carries the word as λεμά, where the King James tradition has λαμά.

Theological Significance

I am sure the word is the deepest question ever asked: the Son crying "why" to the Father, and the answer is the cross itself, borne for me.
