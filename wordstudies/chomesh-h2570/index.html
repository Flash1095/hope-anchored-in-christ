chômesh

the abdomen (as obese)

Biblical Usage

Chomesh is the belly, the soft place under the fifth rib, and it appears only where a man is struck down in murder. Abner smites Asahel under the fifth rib, Joab smites Abner in the same place, Rechab and Baanah smite Ish-bosheth, and Joab smites Amasa, each blow finding the same fatal spot.

Theological Significance

I believe the four murders in one word are a warning about the violence of David's house, because the sword never stayed in its sheath. I hold that the God who forbids murder remembers every blow, even when the histories only record the place of the wound.
