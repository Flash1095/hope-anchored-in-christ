yâʼal

properly, to yield, especially assent; hence (pos.) to undertake as an act of volition

Biblical Usage

Abraham took it upon himself to speak to the Lord, though he was but dust and ashes, and Moses was content to dwell with Jethro, who gave him Zipporah his daughter.

Theological Significance

I believe willingness is the quiet hinge of both stories: Abraham undertakes to plead and Moses consents to stay, and in each case the yes that is given shapes everything that follows.
