kîy

(by implication) very widely used as a relative conjunction or adverb (as below); often largely modified by other particles annexed

Biblical Usage

Kiy is the little word for, because, that, and it holds half the Bible together. Genesis 1:4 says God saw the light, that it was good, Exodus 1:10 has Pharaoh scheming lest Israel multiply, and Leviticus 1:2 opens the law of offerings with if any man bring an offering unto the LORD.

Theological Significance

I believe the small words carry the big reasons: God saw the light, that it was good, and everything since has been God giving the reason for what He does.
