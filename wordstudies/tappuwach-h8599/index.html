Tappûwach

Tappuach, the name of two places in Palestine, also of an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Tappuah is the name of a man and of two towns. The man is a son of Hebron in Judah's line, and the towns are one in the lowland of Judah and one on the border of Ephraim, where the boundary of Manasseh ran by the waters of Tappuah.

Theological Significance

I believe the doubled name is a reminder that Bible places are real ground, and one name can mark both a family and a town.
