machăbath

a pan for baking in

Biblical Usage

The word names a pan for baking, and it is the word of the meat offering and of Ezekiel's sign. The offering baken in a pan was fine flour mingled with oil, and the priest took his portion from it, and Ezekiel took an iron pan and set it for a wall of iron between himself and the city, and laid siege against it as a sign to the house of Israel.

Theological Significance

I believe the pan is holy because God said what goes in it is His, and the same pan became a wall of iron when the prophet acted out the siege. I hold that the ordinary tools of the kitchen become holy in God's service, and the sign of judgment is cooked in the same pan as the offering.
