tâvek

a bisection, i.e. (by implication) the centre

Biblical Usage

The word is the midst, the middle, among. Adam and his wife hid themselves from the presence of the Lord amongst the trees of the garden; Israel went into the midst of the sea upon dry ground; and Solomon says Israel is God's people and inheritance, which He brought forth out of Egypt, from the midst of the iron furnace.

Theological Significance

I hold that God meets His people in the midst: in the garden, in the sea, and in the furnace.
