philotiméomai

middle voice from a compound of G5384 and G5092; to be fond of honor, i.e. emulous (eager or earnest to do something):--labour, strive, study.

Biblical Usage

Philotimeomai means to be fond of honour, to strive, to be ambitious, and Paul turns ambition inside out. He tells the Thessalonians to study to be quiet and to do their own business, he says we labour that whether present or absent we may be accepted of Him, and he strives to preach the gospel where Christ was not named.

Theological Significance

I hold that holy ambition is the paradox of the Christian life: to strive to be quiet, to labour to be accepted, and to preach where no one has preached before.
