chrēmatismós

from G5537; a divine response or revelation:--answer of God.

Biblical Usage

The answer of God is the reply God gave Elijah when he thought he was the only faithful one left: seven thousand have not bowed to Baal. The word appears once, and it carries the whole weight of God's correction to a lonely prophet.

Theological Significance

I believe God always keeps a remnant, even when His servants cannot see them.
