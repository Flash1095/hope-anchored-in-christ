Buqqîyâh

Bukkijah, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Bukkiah was a son of Heman, one of the singers set apart for the service of song in the house of the LORD, and the sixth lot of the musicians fell to him with his sons and brethren. He belongs to the choirs David organized for worship.

Theological Significance

I hold that God counts singers among His servants, and Bukkiah's place in the lots shows that music in worship is ordered, not accidental.
