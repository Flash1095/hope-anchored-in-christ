bâqaq

Word
בָּקַק
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
bâqaq
Strongs Number
H1238
Definition
to pour out, i.e. to empty, figuratively, to depopulate; by analogy, to spread out (as a fruitful vine)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to empty; 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to empty, lay waste; 1a2) to make void (fig.); 1b) (Niphal) to be emptied; 1c) (Polel) to empty out, devastate
