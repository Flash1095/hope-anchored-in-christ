bâqaq

to pour out, i.e. to empty, figuratively, to depopulate; by analogy, to spread out (as a fruitful vine)

Biblical Usage

Baqaq means to empty out, to lay waste, to pour a land out until nothing is left. Hosea says Israel is an empty vine bringing forth fruit to herself, Isaiah says the Lord makes the earth empty and turns it upside down, and Jeremiah says God will make void the counsel of Judah and Jerusalem in the valley of slaughter.

Theological Significance

I believe an emptied land is the fruit of an emptied heart. Israel poured herself out on idols and God poured her out of the land, and I hold that the warning still stands: what we empty ourselves for decides what we are emptied of.
