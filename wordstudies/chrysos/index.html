chrysós

perhaps from the base of G5530 (through the idea of the utility of the metal); gold; by extension, a golden article, as an ornament or coin:--gold.

Biblical Usage

Gold is the first gift the wise men lay before the child, and Paul tells the Athenians that God is not like gold or silver shaped by human hands. The builder's work is tried by fire, and gold survives where wood and stubble burn. James says the rich man's gold is cankered, and the rust testifies against him.

Theological Significance

I believe gold in Scripture is always a question, never an answer: will you worship it, or will you offer it? The wise men gave it to Jesus, and that is the only safe place for gold to go. I hold that whatever survives the fire is what was built on Christ.
