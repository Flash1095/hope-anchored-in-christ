ʼîyn

is it not?

Biblical Usage

Is it not is the question David asks when he needs a weapon in a hurry. In 1 Samuel 21:8 David says to Ahimelech, is there not here under thine hand spear or sword, for I have neither brought my sword nor my weapons with me, because the king's business required haste.

Theological Significance

I hold that the hurried question tells the whole story: David is running, unarmed, and the priest gives him the sword of Goliath.
