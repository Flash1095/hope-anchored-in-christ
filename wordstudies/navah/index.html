nâvâh

Word
נָוָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
nâvâh
Strongs Number
H5115
Definition
to rest (as at home); causatively (through the implied idea of beauty), to celebrate (with praises)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to beautify; 1a) (Hiphil) to beautify, adorn; 2) to dwell; 2a) (Qal) to dwell, abide, keep at home; 3) (Hophal) rest
