tanchûwm

compassion, solace

Biblical Usage

The psalmist says that in the multitude of his thoughts within him, God's comforts delight his soul (Psalms 94:19). Eliphaz asks Job whether the consolations of God are too small for him, and Isaiah pictures God's people satisfied with the breasts of her consolations, while Jeremiah is told that no one will come to comfort in the house of mourning.

Theological Significance

I believe comfort is not a soft extra in the Bible; it is God's own answer to a troubled heart, and the psalmist found His comforts delighting his soul in the very middle of anxious thoughts. My position is that the God who comforts does not leave His people to grieve alone, and the comfort He gives is meant to be passed on.
