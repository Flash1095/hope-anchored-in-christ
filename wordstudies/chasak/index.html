châsak

to restrain or (reflex.) refrain; by implication, to refuse, spare, preserve; to observe

Biblical Usage

Chasak is restraining, holding back, sparing, and it is God's hand on the edge of sin and of judgment. God tells Abimelech He withheld him from sinning against Him, and He tells Abraham he has not withheld his son, his only son, from God. David blesses the LORD who kept His servant from evil, and Ezra says God has punished us less than our iniquities deserve.

Theological Significance

I believe withholding is one of God's kindest works: He held Abimelech back from a sin he did not know he was committing, and He held Abraham's knife back from Isaac. I hold that the times God restrains me are the times I most need Him, and Ezra's prayer shows the other side: He punishes less than we deserve, and that is mercy.
