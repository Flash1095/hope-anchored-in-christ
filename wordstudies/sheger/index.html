sheger

the fetus (as finally expelled)

Biblical Usage

The young of the flock are blessing and curse in the same list. God promises to bless the increase of the kine and the flocks of the sheep if Israel obeys, and Moses warns that disobedience will curse the same increase, and the enemy will eat it until the people are destroyed. The law sets apart every firstling that opens the matrix as the LORD's.

Theological Significance

I believe the flocks preach dependence, for increase is a gift and not a right, and I hold that blessing and curse alike run through the ordinary things of the field.
