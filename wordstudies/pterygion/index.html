pterýgion

neuter of a presumed derivative of G4420; a winglet, i.e. (figuratively) extremity (top corner):--pinnacle.

Biblical Usage

Pterygion means a little wing, a pinnacle, the top corner of the temple. The devil took Jesus and set Him on a pinnacle of the temple, and said, If Thou be the Son of God, cast Thyself down.

Theological Significance

I hold that the pinnacle is the devil's favourite place: high, public, and a short fall from proving yourself to destroying yourself.
