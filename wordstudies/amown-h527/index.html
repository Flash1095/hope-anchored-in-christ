ʼâmôwn

a throng of people

Biblical Usage

The word occurs once, in the list of those Nebuzar-adan carried away to Babylon: the poor of the people, the residue left in the city, those who had fallen to the king, and the rest of the multitude. It is simply a crowd — the leftover people of a fallen city, counted among the captives.

Theological Significance

One word for a crowd in one verse carries no theology of its own; its weight is Jeremiah's account of a city emptied by judgment.
