ʼêmûwn

established, i.e. (figuratively) trusty; also (abstractly) trustworthiness

Biblical Usage

Emuwn is faithfulness — being established, trusty, true. Moses says Israel are children in whom is no faith in Deuteronomy 32:20, Isaiah opens the gates for the righteous nation which keeps the truth in Isaiah 26:2, and Proverbs sets the faithful ambassador against the wicked messenger in Proverbs 13:17 and the faithful witness against the false one in Proverbs 14:5.

Theological Significance

I believe faithfulness is proved in small things — a true witness, a trustworthy messenger, a nation that keeps the truth. I hold that God is faithful even when His people are not, and His faithfulness is what makes my faithfulness possible.
