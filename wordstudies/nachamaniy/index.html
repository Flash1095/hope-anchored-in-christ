Nachămânîy

Nachamani, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Nahamani is one of the leaders who came back from exile with Zerubbabel, named once in the list of returnees. In Nehemiah 7:7 he stands among the men who came with Zerubbabel and Jeshua and Nehemiah, counted with Mordecai and Bigvai and the rest.

Theological Significance

I believe the return from exile was made of names like this, ordinary people who came home. Nahamani is not a hero of the story, but he is on the list, and the list is the story.
