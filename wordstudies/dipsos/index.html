dípsos

of uncertain affinity; thirst:--thirst.

Biblical Usage

Thirst itself, which Paul lists among his sufferings, in fastings often, in cold and nakedness, in hunger and thirst.

Theological Significance

I hold that the apostle's thirst was part of his apostleship, and comfort is not the measure of faithfulness.
