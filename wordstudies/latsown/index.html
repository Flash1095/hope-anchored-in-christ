lâtsôwn

derision

Biblical Usage

lâtsôwn means derision, and the word keeps turning up at decisive moments. In Isaiah 28:14, "ye scornful men"; and in Proverbs 1:22, "and the scorners delight in their scorning". In Proverbs 29:8, "Scornful men bring a city into a snare: but wise [men] turn away wrath.".

Theological Significance

I believe scorn is the one attitude God will not argue with — the scorner delights in scorning, and the city pays for it. I hold that wisdom begins where scorn ends: the moment I stop mocking long enough to listen. My position is that I would rather be corrected by the wise than applauded by the scornful.
