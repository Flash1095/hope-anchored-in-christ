qâtsaʻ

Word
קָצַע
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
qâtsaʻ
Strongs Number
H7106
Definition
to strip off, i.e. (partially) scrape; by implication, to segregate (as an angle)
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to scrape, scrape off, cut off; 1a) (Hiphil) to scrape; 2) to be cornered, be set in corners; 2a) (Hophal) to cut off places, make corners
