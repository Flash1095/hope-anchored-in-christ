bâqash

to search out (by any method, specifically in worship or prayer); by implication, to strive after

Biblical Usage

Baqash means to seek, to search out, to strive after, and it is the word for seeking God Himself. Moses says if you seek the Lord with all your heart and soul you will find Him, Jeremiah says you will seek Me and find Me when you search with all your heart, the psalm says seek the Lord and His strength and His face continually, and Proverbs says to seek wisdom as silver and search for her as for hidden treasure.

Theological Significance

I believe seeking is the whole of the life of faith in one word, because God promises to be found by those who seek Him with everything they have. He is not hiding from us; He is waiting to be sought, and I hold that every true search ends the same way, with the seeker found by the God he was looking for.
