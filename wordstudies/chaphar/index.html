châphar

properly, to pry into; by implication, to delve, to explore

Biblical Usage

Chaphar is digging and prying into, and it is the work of wells and spies alike. Abraham gave seven ewe lambs as a witness that he had digged a well, Isaac digged again the wells his father's servants had digged, and when the Nile turned to blood all the Egyptians digged round about the river for water. In Deuteronomy the people ask to send men to search out the land before they go up.

Theological Significance

I believe digging is the Bible's picture of patient work: a well is not found by wishing, and a land is not known by hearsay. Abraham dug, Isaac re-dug, and Egypt dug in desperation. I hold that God gives water to diggers, and knowledge to those who search.
