merkâbâh

a chariot

Biblical Usage

Merkabah is a chariot, the war machine and the royal carriage, and it runs through the histories. 1 Samuel 8:11 warns that a king will take their sons for his chariots, and 2 Chronicles 1:17 prices a chariot out of Egypt at six hundred shekels of silver. 1 Kings 7:33 makes the temple's wheels like the work of a chariot wheel, and 1 Chronicles 28:18 gives the pattern of the chariot of the cherubim that spread their wings over the ark.

Theological Significance

I believe the chariot is the picture of a king's strength and of God's: man's chariots take sons and cost silver, and God's chariot is cherubim over the ark. My position is that the chariot Israel wanted for safety became the warning Samuel gave, and the one God showed David was not for war at all.
