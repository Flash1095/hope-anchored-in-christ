epeidḗ

from G1893 and G1211; since now, i.e. (of time) when, or (of cause) whereas:--after that, because, for (that, -asmuch as), seeing, since.

Biblical Usage

Epeide means since now, whereas, because — a word for giving the reason things stand as they do. Luke uses it to open a new scene, Paul uses it to explain why God chose foolish preaching to save believers, and why the word went to the Gentiles when it was spoken against.

Theological Significance

Reasons matter to God, and when Scripture says because, I should stop and listen to the reason it gives. I hold that He does not act without cause, and the reasons He gives — foolish preaching, a door opened to the Gentiles — are worth more than my own explanations.
