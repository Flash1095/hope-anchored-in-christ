kýlisma

from G2947; a wallow (the effect of rolling), i.e. filth:--wallowing.

Biblical Usage

Wallowing is the filth a creature rolls in, and it appears once, in the proverb Peter quotes in 2 Peter 2:22: the sow that was washed returns "to her wallowing in the mire." The Greek text carries the word as κυλισμός, where the King James tradition has κύλισμα.

Theological Significance

What I take from this is that Peter's picture is blunt because the sin is blunt: a person washed clean who goes back to the mire proves the washing never reached the heart.
