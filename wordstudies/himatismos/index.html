himatismós

from G2439; clothing:--apparel (X -led), array, raiment, vesture.

Biblical Usage

himatismós is the word for clothing, apparel. I meet it 5 times across the Bible, and Luke is a good place to see it plainly. It says what it says, and I take it at face value.

Theological Significance

I believe himatismós matters because God clothes His people — from skins in Eden to robes in Revelation. What I take from it is this — the plain sense is the safe sense.
