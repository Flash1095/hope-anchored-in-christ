ḗ

a primary particle of distinction between two connected terms; disjunctive, or; comparative, than:--and, but (either), (n-)either, except it be, (n-)or (else), rather, save, than, that, what, yea. Often used in connection with other particles. Compare especially G2235, G2260, G2273.

Biblical Usage

Or, the word that forces a choice between two masters, two ways, two loyalties. No man can serve two masters, Jesus said, and Paul asks whether Christ is divided, or whether he was crucified for the Corinthians. John reminds us that greater is He that is in us than he that is in the world.

Theological Significance

I believe every or in Scripture is a crossroads, and I cannot walk both roads at once. God or mammon, Christ or the world, the choice is mine to make, and the choosing is the life of faith.
