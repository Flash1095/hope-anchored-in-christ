Chîyʼêl

Chiel, an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Hiel is the Bethelite who rebuilt Jericho in the days of Ahab, and the cost of it is the whole story. He laid its foundation in his firstborn Abiram and set up its gates in his youngest son Segub, exactly as the Lord had spoken by Joshua the son of Nun.

Theological Significance

I believe Hiel's story is the warning that God's words outlive the centuries, and a curse spoken long ago still falls true. I hold that rebuilding what God has cursed is never a small thing, whatever it costs.
