periairéō

from G4012 and G138 (including its alternate); to remove all around, i.e. unveil, cast off (anchor); figuratively, to expiate:--take away (up).

Biblical Usage

Periaireo means to take away, to remove all around. When the heart turns to the Lord, the veil is taken away, and when the storm had hidden sun and stars for days, all hope that the sailors would be saved was taken away.

Theological Significance

I take it that God takes away the veil and the storm takes away hope — and only the first taking is His work.
