parádosis

from G3860; transmission, i.e. (concretely) a precept; specially, the Jewish traditionary law:--ordinance, tradition.

Biblical Usage

Paradosis is tradition, what is handed down. The Pharisees hold the tradition of the elders and lay aside the commandment of God, holding the tradition of men; but Paul tells the Thessalonians to hold the traditions which ye have been taught, whether by word or epistle, and he was zealous for the traditions of his fathers.

Theological Significance

I hold that tradition is judged by its source. The traditions of men that set aside God's word are condemned, and the traditions handed down by the apostles are to be held fast.
