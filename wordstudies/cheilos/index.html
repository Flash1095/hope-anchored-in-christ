cheîlos

from a form of the same as G5490; a lip (as a pouring place); figuratively, a margin (of water):--lip, shore.

Biblical Usage

A lip is the edge of the mouth, and also the edge of the sea, the shore. Peter says to refrain the lips from guile, Hebrews calls praise the fruit of the lips giving thanks, Jesus charges a people who honour Him with their lips while their heart is far, and Abraham's seed is numbered like the sand on the sea shore.

Theological Significance

I believe the lip is where the heart shows itself: lips can honour God while the heart is far, and lips can offer praise that pleases Him. God counts the sand on the shore, and He hears every lip.
