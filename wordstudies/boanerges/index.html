Boanergés

of Chaldee origin (H1123 and H7266); sons of commotion; Boanerges, an epithet of two of the apostles:--Boanerges.

Biblical Usage

Boanerges means sons of thunder, and it is the name Jesus gave to James and John, the sons of Zebedee. In Mark 3:17 he surnamed them Boanerges, which is, the sons of thunder. The word is spelled Βοανηργές in the Greek text, with a different vowel from the dictionary form, but it is the same name. Two fishermen, given a thunder-name by their Master.

Theological Significance

I believe the thunder-name fits: James and John were zealous, quick, and loud — and Jesus kept them, thunder and all, and made apostles of them.
