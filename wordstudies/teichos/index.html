teîchos

akin to the base of G5088; a wall (as formative of a house):--wall.

Biblical Usage

The word is a wall. Paul was let down by the wall in a basket by night and escaped Damascus; the walls of Jericho fell down by faith after they were compassed about seven days; and the holy city has a wall great and high, with twelve gates.

Theological Significance

I hold that walls fall by faith and save by stealth, and the last wall is the one with twelve gates.
