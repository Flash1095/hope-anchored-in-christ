ʼâkên

firmly; figuratively, surely; also (advers.) but

Biblical Usage

A particle of certainty — surely, truly, but. Jacob wakes from his dream and says, Surely the LORD is in this place; and I knew it not; Agag says, Surely the bitterness of death is past; Asaph begins, Truly God is good to Israel; and Elihu says, But there is a spirit in man.

Theological Significance

A particle of surely carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is Jacob's surely, a man who went to sleep an exile and woke knowing God had been there all along.
