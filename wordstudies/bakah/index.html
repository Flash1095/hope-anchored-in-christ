bâkâh

to weep; generally to bemoan

Biblical Usage

Bakah is weeping — the tears that run through the whole Bible. Hannah wept sore and prayed, the baby Moses wept in the basket and Pharaoh's daughter had compassion on him, David wept when he heard that his son was dead, and Nehemiah wept and mourned and fasted when he heard the walls of Jerusalem were broken down. Weeping, in every generation, before the same God.

Theological Significance

I believe God keeps every tear, and I hold that the weeping of Hannah and Nehemiah was heard the same day it was wept. He that soweth in tears shall reap in joy, and my tears are sown too.
