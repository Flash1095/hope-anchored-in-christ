Word

Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
châtsâh
Strongs Number

Definition
to cut or split in two; to halve
Part Of Speech
verb
Biblical Usage
1) to divide, cut in two, cut short, live half (of one's life); 1a) (Qal); 1a1) to divide; 1a2) to half, cut in two; 1b) (Niphal) to be divided
