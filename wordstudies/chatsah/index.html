châtsâh

to cut or split in two; to halve

Biblical Usage

To divide is to cut in two, and the word marks the turning points of the story. Jacob divides his household into two bands before meeting Esau, Elijah's mantle divides the Jordan, Gideon divides three hundred men into three companies, and Moses commands the prey of Midian to be divided in two.

Theological Significance

I believe God works through divisions that men make in fear and faith alike. Jacob divided out of fear, Elijah out of power, and God honoured both, because He is the one who parts the waters.
