phōtismós

from G5461; illumination (figuratively):--light.

Biblical Usage

Photismos is illumination, the shining of light, and it appears twice, both times about the gospel. Paul says the god of this world has blinded the minds of them which believe not, lest the light of the glorious gospel of Christ should shine unto them, and God who commanded the light to shine out of darkness has shined in our hearts, to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.

Theological Significance

I believe illumination is God's own work in the heart: He commanded light out of darkness at the beginning, and He commands it again in me. The gospel's light is the knowledge of God's glory, and it shines nowhere else but in the face of Jesus Christ.
