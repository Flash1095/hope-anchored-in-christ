Dinhâbâh

Dinhabah, an Edomitish town

Biblical Usage

Dinhabah was the capital city of Bela, the first king of Edom named in Genesis, and it stands at the head of the Edomite king list. The name means give thou judgment.

Theological Significance

I believe the kings of Edom are listed because God keeps the record of nations outside Israel too, and no kingdom is beneath His notice.
