çâphad

properly, to tear the hair and beat the breasts (as Orientals do in grief); generally to lament; by implication, to wail

Biblical Usage

çâphad covers to lament, to wail, and the Bible uses it the way ordinary people would. You find it 30 times, in 1 Kings among other places, and each time it is doing the same steady work. It is a word you understand best by watching where it goes, not by guessing at it.

Theological Significance

My position is simple: to lament, to wail is part of how God speaks to us, and çâphad is the word He chose for it. That is reason enough to learn it.
