ʼIttay

Ittai or Ithai, the name of a Gittite and of an Israelite

Biblical Usage

Ittai the Gittite was the foreigner who would not leave David when Absalom rose against him. In 2 Samuel 15:19 the king tells him to return, but in 2 Samuel 15:21 Ittai answers, as the LORD liveth, where my lord the king shall be, there will thy servant be, and in 2 Samuel 18:2 David sets him over a third part of the army.

Theological Significance

I hold that Ittai is the loyal stranger the story turns on: a Gittite who swore to stay, and a king who trusted him with a third of the army.
