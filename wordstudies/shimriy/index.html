Shimrîy

Shimri, the name of four Israelites

Biblical Usage

Four men carry this name. First is Shimri of Simeon, the ancestor of the prince Ziza, then Shimri the father of Jediael the mighty man, then Shimri the chief of Hosah's sons who keeps the gates, and last Shimri the Levite who helps cleanse the temple in Hezekiah's days.

Theological Significance

A name carried by a Simeonite, a soldier's father, a gatekeeper, and a Levite is a name that served in every part of the house. I hold that the Shimri who cleansed the temple is the one I most want to follow.
