diábolos

from G1225; a traducer; specially, Satan (compare H7854):--false accuser, devil, slanderer.

Biblical Usage

The devil is the accuser, the slanderer who speaks against God and against men. He tempts Jesus in the wilderness, he puts betrayal into the heart of Judas, he prowls like a roaring lion, and at the end he is cast into the lake of fire.

Theological Significance

I believe the devil is real, defeated, and still dangerous. He is real because Scripture names his work plainly, defeated because Christ bound him at the cross, and dangerous because a cornered accuser still roars — so I resist him, and I do it standing in Christ.
