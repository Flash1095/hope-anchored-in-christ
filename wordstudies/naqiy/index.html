nâqîy

innocent

Biblical Usage

Naqi means innocent or clean, clear of guilt, and it is the word for hands and hearts that can stand before God. In Genesis 24:41 the servant shall be clear from the oath when he comes to Abraham's kindred, and in Deuteronomy 19:10 innocent blood must not be shed in the land, lest blood be upon the people. In 1 Samuel 19:5 Jonathan asks why Saul would sin against innocent blood by slaying David without a cause, and in Psalm 24:4 he that hath clean hands and a pure heart shall ascend into the hill of the Lord.

Theological Significance

I believe innocence in the Bible is not naivety but a clean record, and the clean hands of Psalm 24:4 are the hands that have not taken what is not theirs. The servant is clear, the blood is innocent, and David is innocent of the charge, and the one who climbs the hill is the one whose hands match his heart. I hold that my clean hands are washed, not earned.
