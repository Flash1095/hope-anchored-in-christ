Ephesînos

from G2181; Ephesine, or situated at Ephesus:--of Ephesus.

Biblical Usage

Ephesinos means of Ephesus — an Ephesian, situated at Ephesus. It stands in the older text of the letter to the church of Ephesus, addressed to the angel of the church in that city, the first of the seven letters.

Theological Significance

The word marks a people by their city, and the church of Ephesus is the one the risen Christ addresses first among the seven.
