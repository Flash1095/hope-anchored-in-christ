iáomai

middle voice of apparently a primary verb; to cure (literally or figuratively):--heal, make whole.

Biblical Usage

To heal is what Jesus does with a word, and the Gospels show it happening again and again. In Matthew 8:8 the centurion says, speak the word only, and my servant shall be healed, in Matthew 15:28 Jesus tells the Canaanite woman her daughter is made whole, in Luke 5:17 the power of the Lord is present to heal, and in James 5:16 the prayer of faith is told to heal the sick.

Theological Significance

I hold that healing belongs to Jesus first and to my prayers second: he healed with a word then, and James still tells the church to pray for the sick now. I believe the power has not gone anywhere.
