ʻătsârâh

an assembly, especially on a festival or holiday

Biblical Usage

A solemn assembly is a gathering called for worship, and the word appears at both ends of Israel's story. The law commands a solemn assembly on the seventh day of unleavened bread, Jehu called one to trap the worshippers of Baal, and the prophets say God hates the feasts and the solemn meetings when they are offered with iniquity.

Theological Significance

I believe the assembly is only as true as the hearts that gather in it, because God commanded the meeting and then rejected it when it became a show. Jehu's assembly was a trap, and I hold that God still sees the difference between a crowd and a congregation.
