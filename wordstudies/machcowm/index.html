machçôwm

a muzzle

Biblical Usage

Machsom is a muzzle, a bridle for the mouth, and it appears once, in Psalms 39:1, where the psalmist says he will keep his mouth with a bridle while the wicked is before him.

Theological Significance

I believe the bridle is the right picture for my tongue: some words are better kept behind it than let loose.
