átimos

from G1 (as a negative particle) and G5092; (negatively) unhonoured or (positively) dishonoured:--despised, without honour, less honourable (comparative degree).

Biblical Usage

Without honour is the lot of the apostles in Paul's irony, we are fools for Christ's sake and ye are wise, we are weak and ye are strong, we are despised. Paul says the uncomely parts of the body receive more abundant honour, and Jesus said a prophet is not without honour except in his own country.

Theological Significance

I believe the upside-down honour of the kingdom is the test of whether we see as God sees. The despised apostles carried the gospel to the world, and I hold that the honour Christ gives is never the honour the world offers.
