phaneróō

from G5318; to render apparent (literally or figuratively):--appear, manifestly declare, (make) manifest (forth), shew (self).

Biblical Usage

Phaneroo means to make manifest, to bring into the open, and John uses it for the whole gospel. The life was manifested, and we have seen it and bear witness, and the Son of God was manifested to destroy the works of the devil. At Cana Jesus manifested His glory and His disciples believed, and Paul says the mystery hidden for ages is now made manifest to the saints.

Theological Significance

I believe God does not hide: what was hidden is now made plain in Christ. The life appeared, the glory showed itself, and the mystery was opened to ordinary saints. My position is that manifestation is the shape of my faith too: what Christ has made plain in me should be plain to everyone who knows me.
