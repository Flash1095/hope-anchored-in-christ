shâʼṭ

one contemning

Biblical Usage

Sha't is despising, contemning, and Ezekiel turns it on Jerusalem's neighbours: the daughters of Syria and the daughters of the Philistines despise thee round about. And the promise stands against them: there shall be no more a pricking brier unto the house of Israel, nor any grieving thorn of all that are round about them, that despised them.

Theological Significance

What strikes me is that the despisers become the thorns, and the thorns are taken away — God ends the contempt of the neighbours.
