Shaʻărayim

Shaarajim, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Shaarajim is a town of Judah and of Simeon, named in the borders and in the battle. It stands among the fourteen cities of Judah's lot, and among the cities of Simeon unto the reign of David. And after David slew Goliath, the men of Israel pursued the Philistines, and the wounded fell down by the way to Shaaraim, even unto Gath and Ekron.

Theological Significance

I believe Shaarajim is remembered for the road of the wounded — the Philistines fell along the way to its gates, and David's victory ran past them.
