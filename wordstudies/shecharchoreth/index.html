shᵉcharchôreth

swarthy

Biblical Usage

Black is the word the bride uses for herself: look not upon me, because I am black, because the sun hath looked upon me. The adjective is her own description, spoken before anyone else can say it.

Theological Significance

I believe her sentence is brave, for she names what the sun did to her and asks not to be stared at, and I hold that the Lord sees her as His beloved, not as the sun left her.
