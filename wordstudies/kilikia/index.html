Kilikía

probably of foreign origin; Cilicia, a region of Asia Minor:--Cilicia.

Biblical Usage

Cilicia is a region of Asia Minor, and it sits at the edge of the gospel's first journeys. Acts 6:9 has men of Cilicia disputing with Stephen, Acts 15:23 sends the apostles' letter to the Gentile brethren in Antioch and Syria and Cilicia, and Galatians 1:21 has Paul himself coming into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.

Theological Significance

I believe Cilicia matters because it is where the persecutor came from and where the preacher went back to: Paul's home ground became the gospel's ground.
