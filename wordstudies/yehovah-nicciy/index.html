Yᵉhôvâh niççîy

Jehovah-Nissi, a symbolical name of an altar in the Desert

Biblical Usage

Jehovah-nissi = "Jehovah is my banner"; 1) the name given by Moses to the altar which he built in commemoration of the discomfiture of the Amalekites
