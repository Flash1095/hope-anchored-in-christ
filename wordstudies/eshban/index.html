ʼEshbân

Eshban, an Idumaean

Biblical Usage

ʼEshbân was an Idumaean, a son of Dishan. The name occurs in Genesis and 1 Chronicles. The fuller story lives on the People profile.

Theological Significance

I hold that ʼEshbân is there on purpose, like every name in Scripture. That is enough reason to slow down and notice the name rather than skip it.
