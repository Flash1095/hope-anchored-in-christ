Bᵉʼêrîy

Word
בְּאֵרִי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Bᵉʼêrîy
Strongs Number
H882
Definition
Beeri, the name of a Hittite and of an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Beeri = "my well"; 1) Esau's Hittite father-in-law; 2) father of Hosea
