kâphan

to bend

Biblical Usage

Kaphan is to bend — here, the vine bending its roots toward a second eagle. There was also another great eagle with great wings and many feathers, and behold, this vine did bend her roots toward him, seeking water from the furrows where she was planted.

Theological Significance

I hold that the bending vine is a picture of a faithless people: planted by one eagle, it bent toward another, and God saw the bending and judged it.
