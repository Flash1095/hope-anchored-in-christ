agkura

Word
ἄγκυρα
Language
Greek
Transliteration
agkura
Strongs Number
G45
Definition
An anchor — the heavy iron hook that holds a ship fast against wind and waves; used figuratively in Hebrews for hope in Christ.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Hebrews 6:19; Acts 27:29-30; Acts 27:40
Biblical Usage
Agkura appears literally in Acts 27, where sailors drop four anchors in a storm, and figuratively in Hebrews 6:19, where hope itself is the anchor — fixed not in shifting seabeds but within the veil, where Jesus has gone.
Theological Significance
Agkura turns a nautical tool into a promise: your soul need not drift. Hope is anchored to Christ Himself, already secure in the heavenly holy place on your behalf.
