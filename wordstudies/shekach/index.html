shᵉkach

to discover (literally or figuratively)

Biblical Usage

To find, in Daniel's Aramaic, is what happens when God opens a man's eyes. Arioch says, I have found a man of the captives of Judah who will make the interpretation known; the writing says, thou art weighed in the balances and art found wanting; and Daniel says innocency was found in him before God and before the king. Ezra is told to find all the silver and gold he can for the house of God.

Theological Significance

I believe finding is God's work before it is man's. Daniel was found, Belshazzar was found wanting, and I hold that the balances still weigh us all.
