Arimathaía

of Hebrew origin (H7414); Arimathæa (or Ramah), a place in Palestine:--Arimathæa.

Biblical Usage

Arimathea is named only as the home of Joseph, the rich councillor who asked Pilate for Jesus' body. Each Gospel names the town to place the man, and John adds that Joseph had been a disciple in secret for fear of the Jews.

Theological Significance

I believe Joseph matters because he stepped forward when the apostles had scattered, and a town name is all it takes to fix a real man in a real place at the cross.
