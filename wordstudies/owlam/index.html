ʻôwlâm

properly, concealed, i.e. the vanishing point; generally, time out of mind (past or future), i.e. (practically) eternity; frequentatively, adverbial (especially with prepositional prefix) always

Biblical Usage

Everlasting is the word for what has no end. Moses said, from everlasting to everlasting, Thou art God, Isaiah called Him the everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth, and Daniel said many shall awake to everlasting life.

Theological Significance

I believe everlasting is the word that holds my whole hope: God is from everlasting to everlasting, His covenant is everlasting, and the life He gives is everlasting life. My life is short, but the life He gives has no end, and that is the hope I hold onto.
