Mᵉrêmôwth

Meremoth, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Meremoth is the name of two Israelites, a priest who weighed the temple treasure and a man who put away a foreign wife. Ezra 8:33 has Meremoth the son of Uriah weigh the silver and gold in the house of God, and Nehemiah 3:4 has Meremoth the son of Urijah repair the wall. Ezra 10:36 lists a second Meremoth among those who had taken strange wives.

Theological Significance

I believe the two Meremoths show both sides of the return: one weighed the gold faithfully, and one had to put away what did not belong.
