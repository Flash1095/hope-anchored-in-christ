phoberós

from G5401; frightful, i.e. (objectively) formidable:--fearful, terrible.

Biblical Usage

Phoberos means fearful, terrible, formidable, and it appears three times, all in Hebrews, all about falling into God's hands. There remains a certain fearful looking for of judgment and fiery indignation, and it is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God, and the sight at Sinai was so terrible that Moses said he exceedingly feared and quaked.

Theological Significance

I believe phoberos is the Bible's word for the terror of a holy God: not the fear of a tyrant but the dread of a just Judge whose fire devours the adversaries. My position is that the fearful thing is not falling into God's hands as a sinner but falling into them as a rebel, and the blood of Christ is the only refuge from it.
