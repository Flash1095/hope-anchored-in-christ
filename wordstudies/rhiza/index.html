rhíza

apparently a primary word; a "root" (literally or figuratively):--root.

Biblical Usage

The seed sown on stony places has no root in himself, and when tribulation arises he is offended. The love of money is the root of all evil, and the root of bitterness springing up defiles many. Yet the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has prevailed to open the book, and Jesus says, I am the root and the offspring of David.

Theological Significance

I am convinced that the root decides the tree. A rootless faith withers in the first heat, a bitter root defiles a whole church, and the love of money roots a man in the wrong soil altogether. But the Root of David is a root that cannot be pulled up, and I hold that a life rooted in Him will stand when the heat comes.
