aganáktēsis

from G23; indignation:--indignation.

Biblical Usage

Aganaktesis is indignation — and it appears once, in Paul's joy over the Corinthians' repentance: 'what carefulness it wrought in you, yea, what clearing of yourselves, yea, what indignation, yea, what fear, yea, what vehement desire, yea, what zeal, yea, what revenge!' The indignation here is not against others but against their own sin — a holy anger at what they had tolerated, and the proof that their sorrow was real.

Theological Significance

A single occurrence like this tells me what happened or how something was said — the doctrine, if any, is in the passage, not the word; in this case, the verse is 2 Corinthians 7:11.
