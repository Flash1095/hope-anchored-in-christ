nêthach

a fragment

Biblical Usage

Pieces are what the sacrifice is cut into, and what a body is divided into. The ram is cut in pieces and laid on the altar, the burnt offering is flayed and cut into its pieces, and the Levite's concubine is divided into twelve pieces and sent through all the coasts of Israel.

Theological Significance

I believe the cutting shows the seriousness of it all: on the altar the pieces are worship, and in the house of the Levite they are a cry for justice. The altar pieces are laid in order, and the Levite's pieces are sent as a summons — both of them demand an answer.
