keramikós

from G2766; made of clay, i.e. earthen:--of a potter.

Biblical Usage

Keramikos means of a potter, made of clay. Revelation 2:27 says the overcomer shall rule the nations with a rod of iron, as the vessels of a potter shall they be broken to shivers.

Theological Significance

I believe clay vessels break easily, and that is why I would rather be held by the potter than rule against him.
