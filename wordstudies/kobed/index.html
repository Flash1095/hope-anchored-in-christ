kôbed

weight, multitude, vehemence

Biblical Usage

Kobed is weight, multitude, vehemence. Proverbs 27:3 says a stone is heavy, and the sand weighty, but a fool's wrath is heavier than them both, Isaiah 21:15 flees from the grievousness of war, Isaiah 30:27 says the name of the LORD cometh burning with anger, and the burden thereof is heavy, and Nahum 3:3 counts a multitude of slain and a great number of carcases.

Theological Significance

I believe weight is the Bible's measure of what matters: a fool's wrath outweighs stone and sand, and the name of the LORD comes heavy with fire. I hold that the multitude of slain in Nahum is the end of every weight God does not carry, because only His burden is light.
