Hellēnikós

from G1672; Hellenic, i.e. Grecian (in language):--Greek.

Biblical Usage

Hellenikos means Greek, in the Greek tongue, and it appears once, for the name of the destroyer. In Revelation 9:11 the angel of the bottomless pit has a name in Hebrew, Abaddon, and in the Greek tongue his name is Apollyon.

Theological Significance

I believe the two names are the same warning in two languages: the destroyer answers in Hebrew and in Greek, and no nation can say it does not understand him.
