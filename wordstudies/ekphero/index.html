ekphérō

from G1537 and G5342; to bear out (literally or figuratively):--bear, bring forth, carry forth (out).

Biblical Usage

ekphérō is a plain word for bear, bring forth, carry forth (out). In Mark 8:23, the text says "and led him out of the town." In Luke 15:22, the text says "Bring forth the best robe."

Theological Significance

What ekphérō teaches me is that death is real, but it is not the end — God has the last word over it. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
