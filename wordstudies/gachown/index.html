gâchôwn

the external abdomen, belly (as the source of the faetus)

Biblical Usage

Gachon is the belly, the part of the body that crawls on the ground. The serpent is cursed to go upon his belly and eat dust all the days of his life in Genesis 3:14, and the law forbids eating whatever goes upon the belly in Leviticus 11:42. The word marks the lowest way of moving, close to the dust.

Theological Significance

I believe the serpent's belly is a picture of the fall itself; he was made to crawl in the dust because he brought dust and death to man, and I hold that the seed of the woman would one day bruise his head.
