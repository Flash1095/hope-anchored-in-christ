nōtos

of uncertain affinity; the back:--back.

Biblical Usage

The back is the place of the burden, and Paul quotes the psalm over unbelieving Israel: let their eyes be darkened that they may not see, and bow down their back always. The bowed back is the posture of a people under judgment.

Theological Significance

I hold that the bowed back is what unbelief earns: eyes darkened, and a back bent under the weight it would not lay down.
