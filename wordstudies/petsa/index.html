petsaʻ

a wound

Biblical Usage

Petsa is a wound, and it appears in law, in lament, and in friendship. The law says wound for wound, Isaiah says the whole nation is covered in wounds and bruises and putrifying sores from the sole of the foot to the head, and the proverb says faithful are the wounds of a friend.

Theological Significance

I believe wounds tell the truth about who gives them: the enemy's wound destroys, the law's wound repays, and a friend's wound heals.
