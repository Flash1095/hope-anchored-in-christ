bôwʼ

to go or come (in a wide variety of applications)

Biblical Usage

To come is the word for going in, entering, arriving — and it is the commonest verb of movement in the Old Testament, over two thousand times. In Genesis 7:1 the Lord says to Noah, come thou and all thy house into the ark. In Genesis 19:1 the two angels came to Sodom at even, and Lot sat in the gate. In 1 Samuel 17:45 David says to Goliath, thou comest to me with a sword, and with a spear, and with a shield, but I come to thee in the name of the Lord of hosts. And in 2 Samuel 7:18 David went in, and sat before the Lord.

Theological Significance

I believe the comings in this word are the whole story of faith: Noah coming into the ark, David coming to Goliath in the Lord's name, and David coming in to sit before the Lord — and I come the same way.
