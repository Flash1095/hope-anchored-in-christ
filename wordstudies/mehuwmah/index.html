mᵉhûwmâh

confusion or uproar

Biblical Usage

Confusion is the uproar God sends on His enemies, and the word is used where the confusion does His work: the LORD destroys the nations with a mighty destruction, the Philistines turn their swords on each other, and in the last day a great tumult from the LORD will fall on every man. The chronicler says there was no peace in those days, but great vexations upon all the inhabitants.

Theological Significance

I believe confusion is God's weapon when men will not listen: He turns their swords on each other, and the tumult they meant for His people falls on them. The confusion of the enemy is the deliverance of the righteous.
