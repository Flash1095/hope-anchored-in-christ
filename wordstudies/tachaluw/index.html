tachălûwʼ

a malady

Biblical Usage

The psalmist blesses the Lord who forgives all iniquities and heals all diseases. Moses says the generation to come will see the sicknesses of the land, the diseases God has laid upon it, and Jeremiah says, if I go forth into the field, then behold the slain with the sword, and if I enter into the city, then behold them that are sick with famine. The word is a malady, a disease: healed by God, or laid on a land that broke covenant.

Theological Significance

The psalmist's order is the gospel in miniature, I believe: He forgives first, then He heals, and the diseases are named beside the iniquities because both need the same Physician.
