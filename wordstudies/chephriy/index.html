Chephrîy

a Chephrite (collectively) or descendants of Chepher

Biblical Usage

The Chephrites are the descendants of Hepher, named once in the census of Numbers as a family of Manasseh. They stand in the long count of the tribes on the plains of Moab, one household among the thousands being numbered before the land is divided.

Theological Significance

I hold that God counts households as well as heroes, and no family is lost in His numbering.
