qerets

extirpation (as if by constriction)

Biblical Usage

Egypt is a fair heifer, says Jeremiah, but destruction comes out of the north. One verse, one sentence of judgment, and the word is the cutting-off itself.

Theological Significance

I believe beauty is no shield against judgment, and Egypt's fairness could not hold back what God sent from the north.
