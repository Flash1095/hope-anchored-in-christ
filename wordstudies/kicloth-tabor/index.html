Kiçlôth Tâbôr

Kisloth-Tabor, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Chisloth-tabor = "flanks of Tabor"; 1) a town of Issachar on the slopes of Mount Tabor; also 'Chesulloth' H03694
