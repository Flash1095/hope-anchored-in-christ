maṭmôwn

a secret storehouse; hence, a secreted valuable (buried); generally money

Biblical Usage

Hidden treasure is wealth buried out of sight, and the Bible uses it for wisdom, for God's secret provision, and for the things men chase in vain. Proverbs tells the young man to seek wisdom as silver and search for her as for hid treasures, and Isaiah promises Cyrus the treasures of darkness. Joseph's brothers find their money returned in their sacks, and Job says men dig for death as for hid treasures.

Theological Significance

I believe the word asks what we are digging for: wisdom is the one treasure that repays the digging, because it is God who hides it and God who gives it. The treasures of darkness belong to the LORD too — He gives them to whom He chooses, even to a pagan king. And Job's line is the warning: men will dig just as hard for death, and find it.
