láthra

adverb from G2990; privately:--privily, secretly.

Biblical Usage

láthra means privately, in secret. In Matthew 1:19, "was minded to put her away privily."; and in John 11:28, "and called Mary her sister secretly". In Acts 16:37, "and now do they thrust us out privily?".

Theological Significance

I believe what is done privately still counts — Joseph's quiet mercy, Mary's whispered summons, and Paul's private release all happened out of the public eye, and God saw every one of them.
