parakletos

Helper, Advocate, Comforter and Counsellor — one called alongside to help; Jesus's title for the Holy Spirit, and for Himself.

Biblical Usage

Parakletos is courtroom and family language together: an advocate who speaks for you and a helper who stands beside you. Jesus promises 'another Parakletos' — the Spirit — because He Himself is our advocate with the Father.

Theological Significance

Parakletos means you never face trial, grief or accusation alone. Heaven has stationed an Advocate beside you forever — that is anchored hope for anxious hearts.
