Qᵉmûwʼêl

Kemuel, the name of a relative of Abraham, and of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

The name Kemuel belongs to three different men: Nahor's son and the father of Aram in Abraham's family, a prince of Ephraim who helped divide the land, and the father of Hashabiah, a Levite leader under David. Same name, three lives, each named in his own place.

Theological Significance

I hold that God keeps the names straight even when we cannot, and each Kemuel is known to him in his own generation.
