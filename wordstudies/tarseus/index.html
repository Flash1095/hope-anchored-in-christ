Tarseús

from G5019; a Tarsean, i.e. native of Tarsus:--of Tarsus.

Biblical Usage

The word means a man of Tarsus, a Tarsean. The Lord tells Ananias to go and enquire for one called Saul, of Tarsus, and Paul himself says, I am a man which am a Jew of Tarsus, a city in Cilicia, a citizen of no mean city.

Theological Significance

I hold that Paul never hid where he came from, and the city of Tarsus is part of the apostle's story.
