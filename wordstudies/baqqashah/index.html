baqqâshâh

a petition

Biblical Usage

Baqqashah is a petition, a request laid before a king. Esther's petition is asked three times at the banquets before she names it, the king offers her half the kingdom if she will only say what she wants, and Ezra is called a ready scribe whose every petition the king of Persia granted him.

Theological Significance

I believe a petition is the boldest thing a subject can lay before a king, and Esther shows how it is done: she comes, she waits, she asks. I hold that God still answers the petition that is laid before Him in the right time and the right way.
