monóphthalmos

from G3441 and G3788; one-eyed:--with one eye.

Biblical Usage

Monophthalmos means one-eyed — Jesus says it is better to enter into life with one eye than to be cast into hell fire with two.

Theological Significance

I believe the hard saying is a mercy in disguise: whatever costs me the kingdom is not worth keeping, even if it is my own eye.
