Sōsthénēs

from the base of G4982 and that of G4599; of safe strength; Sosthenes, a Christian:--Sosthenes.

Biblical Usage

Sosthenes is beaten before the judgment seat in Corinth while Gallio cares for none of those things, and later Paul opens his first letter to the Corinthians with Sosthenes our brother beside his own name. The same man who was seized and beaten stands as a brother in the greeting.

Theological Significance

I believe the change in Sosthenes is the gospel in one life: beaten for the synagogue's cause, then counted a brother in Christ.
