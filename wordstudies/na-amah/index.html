Naʻămâh

Word
נַעֲמָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Naʻămâh
Strongs Number
H5279
Definition
Naamah, the name of an antediluvian woman, of an Ammonitess, and of a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Naamah = "loveliness"; n pr f; 1) daughter of Lamech by his wife Zillah and sister of Tubal-cain in the days before the flood; 2) the Ammonite wife of Solomon and mother of king Rehoboam of Judah; n pr f loc; 3) a town in the lowlands of Judah in Philistia
