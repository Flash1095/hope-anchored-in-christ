kóptō

a primary verb; to "chop"; specially, to beat the breast in grief:--cut down, lament, mourn, (be-)wail. Compare the base of G5114.

Biblical Usage

Kopto means to cut down, and specially to beat the breast in grief, to lament. Matthew 11:17 says, we have mourned unto you, and ye have not lamented, Matthew 21:8 and Mark 11:8 have the multitude cutting down branches and strawing them in the way, Matthew 24:30 says all the tribes of the earth shall mourn when they see the Son of man coming, Luke 8:52 has all weeping and bewailing the dead girl, and Revelation 1:7 says all kindreds of the earth shall wail because of Him.

Theological Significance

I believe the cutting and the mourning are one word because they are one act: branches cut for the King, and breasts beaten for the dead. I hold that the tribes mourning in Matthew 24:30 and the kindreds wailing in Revelation 1:7 are the last lamentation, because they mourn the One they pierced.
