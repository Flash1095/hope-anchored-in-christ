gêr

properly, a guest; by implication, a foreigner

Biblical Usage

gêr — sojourner. That is the whole of it, and it is enough: 83 occurrences, and in Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, and Numbers the meaning never wavers. I keep coming back to the simple reading because the simple reading is the true one.

Theological Significance

I believe gêr matters because God has always made room for the stranger, and so should I. I hold that the simple reading is the true one, and I will not trade it for a clever one.
