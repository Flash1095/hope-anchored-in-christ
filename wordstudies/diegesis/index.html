diḗgesis

from G1334; a recital:--declaration.

Biblical Usage

A declaration is a setting forth in order, a narrative drawn up with care, and Luke uses the word once, for the many who undertook to set forth in order the things most surely believed among us. His own Gospel is the declaration he writes to Theophilus.

Theological Significance

I hold that Luke's declaration is the pattern for honest history; he did not invent what he wrote; he set it in order from eyewitnesses, and the order itself is part of the proof.
