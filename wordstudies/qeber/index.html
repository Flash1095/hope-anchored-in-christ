qeber

a sepulchre

Biblical Usage

A sepulchre is where the story rests: the field and the cave of Machpelah were made sure to Abraham for a possession of a buryingplace; the man of God was told his carcase should not come to the sepulchre of his fathers; the old prophet laid his carcase in his own grave, and they mourned over him. Asa was buried in his own sepulchres, which he had made for himself in the city of David.

Theological Significance

I believe the sepulchre is the last honour of a life, and the grave a man prepares for himself says what he trusted. What moves me is that Abraham bought his buryingplace in faith, and the man of God was kept from his fathers' sepulchre, and both endings were exactly what God said they would be.
