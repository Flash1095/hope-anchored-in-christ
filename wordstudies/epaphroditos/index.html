Epaphróditos

from G1909 (in the sense of devoted to) and Ἀφροδίτη (Venus); Epaphroditus, a Christian:--Epaphroditus. Compare G1889.

Biblical Usage

Epaphroditus carried the Philippians' gift to Paul in prison, and Paul calls him his brother, companion in labour, and fellow soldier. He was sent home with honour, and Paul tells the church to hold such men in reputation.

Theological Significance

Paul calls him brother, companion in labour, and fellow soldier, and tells the church to hold such men in reputation. I believe that is how the church should treat its faithful servants: with honour, not silence.
