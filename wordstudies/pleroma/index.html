plḗrōma

from G4137; repletion or completion, i.e. (subjectively) what fills (as contents, supplement, copiousness, multitude), or (objectively) what is filled (as container, performance, period):--which is put in to fill up, piece that filled up, fulfilling, full, fulness.

Biblical Usage

Pleroma is fullness, completion, what fills a thing to the top. In Christ dwells all the fullness of the Godhead bodily, says Colossians 2:9, and of His fullness have we all received, in John 1:16. The church is the fullness of Him that fills all in all, in Ephesians 1:23, and when the fullness of time was come God sent forth His Son, in Galatians 4:4. Romans 11:25 says blindness will last until the fullness of the Gentiles comes in.

Theological Significance

I believe fullness belongs to Christ alone, and I receive it only from Him. That is why I hold that no man, no church, no age is complete without Him — He is the fullness everything else is waiting for.
