zôhar

brilliancy

Biblical Usage

Zohar is brightness, a shining — Daniel promises that the wise shall shine as the brightness of the firmament, and Ezekiel sees a figure with the appearance of brightness from the loins upward. It is the shine of God's glory resting on a man, and the shine God promises to the wise.

Theological Significance

I believe the brightness Daniel promises is not our own light but God's, shining through those who turn many to righteousness.
