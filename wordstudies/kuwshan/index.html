Kûwshân

Cushan, a region of Arabia

Biblical Usage

Cushan appears once, in Habakkuk's vision in Habakkuk 3:7, where the prophet saw the tents of Cushan in affliction. The curtains of the land of Midian trembled beside them, for Cushan was a region in Arabia or Mesopotamia, and the prophet sees its tents shaking at God's coming.

Theological Significance

What I take from this is that the trembling tents are the whole prophecy in one line: when God marches, even far-off peoples feel it, and no tent is too distant to shake.
