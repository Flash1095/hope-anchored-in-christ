dᵉvay

sickness; figuratively, loathing

Biblical Usage

This word means sickness or loathing, the faintness that makes even food unwelcome. Job says his soul refuses what his appetite once craved, and the psalmist says God will strengthen the sick man upon his bed of languishing.

Theological Significance

I hold that this word names the weariness sickness brings, and the promise with it is that God Himself strengthens the one who lies down in it.
