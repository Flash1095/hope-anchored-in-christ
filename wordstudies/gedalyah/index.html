Gᵉdalyâh

Word
גְּדַּלְיָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Gᵉdalyâh
Strongs Number
H1436
Definition
Gedaljah, the name of five Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Gedaliah = "Jehovah is great"; 1) a son of Jeduthun in the time of David; 2) son of Ahikam, the governor of Judea appointed by Nebuchadnezzar; 3) son of Pashur, one of the chiefs of Jerusalem in the time of Jeremiah; 4) son of Amariah and grandson of Hezekiah; 5) a priest of the sons of Jeshua in the time of Ezra
