mᵉnaqqîyth

a sacrificial basin (for holding blood)

Biblical Usage

Menaqqiyth is the sacrificial basin, the bowl that held the blood. Exodus 25:29 orders the bowls of pure gold for the table of shewbread, and Jeremiah 52:19 lists the basons and bowls among the gold and silver the captain of the guard carried away from the temple.

Theological Significance

I believe the basin's story is the temple's story: made of pure gold for holy use, and carried off when the people forgot why it was holy.
