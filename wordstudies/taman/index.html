ṭâman

to hide (by covering over)

Biblical Usage

Achan says, I saw among the spoils a goodly Babylonish garment, and two hundred shekels of silver, and a wedge of gold, and I coveted them, and took them, and behold, they are hid in the earth in the midst of my tent. Moses looks this way and that, and when he sees no man, he slays the Egyptian and hides him in the sand, while the lepers of Samaria take silver and gold and raiment, and go and hide it. The word is to hide by covering over: spoil in a tent, a body in the sand, and treasure in a field.

Theological Significance

I believe the hiding is the same in all three: what is covered over is not gone, and God sees what is hid in the tent, in the sand, and in the field.
