shᵉkan

{to reside or permanently stay (literally or figuratively)}

Biblical Usage

To dwell, in this Aramaic word, is to have a home somewhere. The beasts of the field dwelt under the great tree of Nebuchadnezzar's dream, and Darius prays that the God who has caused His name to dwell in Jerusalem will destroy any king who alters His house.

Theological Significance

I believe dwelling is the question of the whole Bible, for Daniel's tree sheltered beasts while God's name dwells in Jerusalem, and I hold that He dwells now with those who keep His house.
