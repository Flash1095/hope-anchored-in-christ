anabathmós

from G305 (compare G898); a stairway:--stairs.

Biblical Usage

The word appears twice, both times on the stairs of the Antonia fortress in Jerusalem: Paul was carried up the stairs by the soldiers for the violence of the people, and then he stood on the stairs and beckoned to the crowd and spoke to them in Hebrew. The stairs are the place where the mob could not reach him — and where he turned and preached to them.

Theological Significance

Two mentions of stairs carry no doctrine of their own; their weight is the scene — Paul lifted above a violent crowd, and using the height to speak the gospel to the very people who wanted him dead.
