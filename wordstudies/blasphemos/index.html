blásphēmos

from a derivative of G984 and G5345; scurrilious, i.e. calumnious (against men), or (specially) impious (against God):--blasphemer(-mous), railing.

Biblical Usage

A blasphemer is the person who speaks evil against God, and the word appears four times. In 1 Timothy 1:13 Paul says he was before a blasphemer, and a persecutor, and injurious — but he obtained mercy. In Acts 6:11 men are set up to say they heard Stephen speak blasphemous words against Moses and against God. And in 2 Timothy 3:2 Paul lists blasphemers among the lovers of their own selves in the last days.

Theological Significance

I believe Paul's own past is the hope in this word: he was a blasphemer, and he obtained mercy, because he did it ignorantly in unbelief.
