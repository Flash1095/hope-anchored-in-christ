élaion

neuter of the same as G1636; olive oil:--oil.

Biblical Usage

In the New Testament, élaion is the word for oil. In Matthew 25:3, the text says "and took no oil with them." In Mark 6:13, the text says "and anointed with oil many that were sick."

Theological Significance

What élaion teaches me is that creation is God's handiwork, and it still speaks of Him to anyone willing to look. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
