peripoíēsis

from G4046; acquisition (the act or the thing); by extension, preservation:--obtain(-ing), peculiar, purchased, possession, saving.

Biblical Usage

Peripoiesis is acquisition, the purchased possession. The Spirit is the earnest of our inheritance until the redemption of the purchased possession, and God has appointed us not to wrath but to obtain salvation. We are called to the obtaining of the glory of the Lord Jesus Christ, and Peter calls us a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, a peculiar people.

Theological Significance

I believe I am a purchased possession: God bought me, the Spirit seals me, and the redemption is still to come. I hold that the obtaining of salvation and the obtaining of glory are the same purchase seen from two sides — what God began He will finish. And Peter's “peculiar people” is not a label but a deed of ownership, and I am glad to be owned.
