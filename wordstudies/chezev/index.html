chêzêv

a sight

Biblical Usage

Chezev is a sight, a vision seen in the night, and Daniel uses it for the revelations God gives in dreams. The secret is revealed to Daniel in a night vision, Nebuchadnezzar is troubled by the visions of his head on his bed, and Daniel himself has a dream and visions in the first year of Belshazzar.

Theological Significance

I believe God still speaks through visions in the way He chooses, and Daniel shows me to receive them with humility and prayer. I hold that the test of any vision is whether it agrees with God's word, because dreams can deceive but He cannot.
