mâtsôwq

a narrow place, i.e. (abstractly and figuratively) confinement or disability

Biblical Usage

Straitness is the crushing pressure of a siege, and the word is used for the worst of it: in the siege the people eat the flesh of their own children, and Jeremiah says the same of Jerusalem's last days. The psalmist says trouble and anguish took hold of him, and men in distress gathered to David in the cave of Adullam.

Theological Significance

I believe the word names what happens when a people will not listen: the walls close in, and the pressure does what warnings could not. But the psalmist shows the other use of it — trouble took hold of him, and he took hold of God's commandments, and that is the way through.
