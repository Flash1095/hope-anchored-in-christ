ʼâmâh

a maidservant or female slave

Biblical Usage

A maidservant, a handmaid, and the Bible's great prayers come from her lips. Hannah prays, O LORD of hosts, if thou wilt indeed look on the affliction of thine handmaid, and remember me, and not forget thine handmaid, but wilt give unto thine handmaid a man child. And Ruth says to Boaz, I am Ruth thine handmaid: spread therefore thy skirt over thine handmaid; for thou art a near kinsman.

Theological Significance

A word for a handmaid carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the two women who use it, that Hannah and Ruth both ask as servants and both are answered as daughters.
