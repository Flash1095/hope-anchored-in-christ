krátos

perhaps a primary word; vigor ("great") (literally or figuratively):--dominion, might(-ily), power, strength.

Biblical Usage

Kratos is might, dominion, and power, and it belongs first to God. Paul prays in Ephesians 1:19 that believers would know "the exceeding greatness of his power," Colossians 1:11 speaks of being strengthened with all might, and the doxologies give God "honour and power everlasting" in 1 Timothy 6:16 and dominion forever in Jude 1:25.

Theological Significance

I am convinced might is not a thing God has; it is what He is, and every doxology that hands it back to Him is simply telling the truth about who holds it. That is the truth the doxologies sing, and I sing it with them.
