epitagḗ

from G2004; an injunction or decree; by implication, authoritativeness:--authority, commandment.

Biblical Usage

Epitage is a command, an injunction, an authoritative charge. Paul speaks by permission and not of commandment; he gives his judgment as one that hath obtained mercy; he proves the sincerity of love not by commandment; and he is an apostle by the commandment of God our Saviour.

Theological Significance

Not every wise word is a command, and not every command may be softened into advice. I hold that Paul's careful line between permission and commandment teaches me to do the same, and the church is healthier when the two are not confused.
