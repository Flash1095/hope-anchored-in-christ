Shepher

Shepher, a place in the Desert

Biblical Usage

Shepher is a camp in the wilderness where Israel rests on the way from the Red Sea to the Jordan. The march lists it among the stations between the sea and the promised land.

Theological Significance

I believe a camp in the wilderness is a night God counted, and Shepher's two verses are the record of it.
