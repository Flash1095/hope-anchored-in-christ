qûwn

to strike a musical note, i.e. chant or wail (at a funeral)

Biblical Usage

David laments over Abner — died Abner as a fool dieth? — and the sailors wail over Tyre, taking up a lamentation for the city destroyed in the midst of the sea. The word is the chanting, the wailing at a funeral.

Theological Significance

I believe David's lament for Abner is the measure of a king's heart, and the wailing for Tyre is the measure of a city's fall.
