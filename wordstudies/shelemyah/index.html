Shelemyâh

Shelemjah, the name of nine Israelites

Biblical Usage

Shelemiah is the name of nine men, and the lists sort them by their work. One was a porter whose lot fell eastward, one was sent to seize Baruch and Jeremiah, and one was a priest made treasurer over the treasuries because he was counted faithful. The name means whom Jehovah repays.

Theological Significance

I believe the treasurer's verse is the one to remember: Shelemiah the priest was made treasurer because they were counted faithful. I hold that faithfulness is the qualification God looks for.
