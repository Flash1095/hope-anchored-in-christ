therapeúō

from the same as G2324; to wait upon menially, i.e. (figuratively) to adore (God), or (specially) to relieve (of disease):--cure, heal, worship.

Biblical Usage

In Scripture, therapeúō is the word for to heal, to serve, to attend. In Matthew 4:23, healing all manner of sickness and all manner of disease among the people. Its 42 occurrences all press in the same direction.

Theological Significance

I believe healing was never a trick Jesus performed to impress a crowd; it was compassion made visible. That is why I come back to therapeúō whenever I need to remember what is true.
