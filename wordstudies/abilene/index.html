Abilēnḗ

of foreign origin (compare H58); Abilene, a region of Syria:--Abilene.

Biblical Usage

Abilene is named once, in Luke 3:1, in the careful dating of John the Baptist's ministry: 'in the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar... Lysanias the tetrarch of Abilene.' It is a small district in Syria, listed beside Galilee, Ituraea, and Trachonitis to pin the story to real rulers and real places.

Theological Significance

A one-verse district, named only to date the gospel — and that is exactly the point. I believe Luke lists Abilene because the gospel happened in history, under named rulers, in named places, and the Word did not come to a myth but to a world with a calendar.
