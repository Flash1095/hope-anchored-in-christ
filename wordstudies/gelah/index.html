gᵉlâh

{to denude (especially in a disgraceful sense); by implication, to exile (captives being usually stripped); figuratively, to reveal}

Biblical Usage

gᵉlâh — to reveal. That is the whole of it, and it is enough: 8 occurrences, and in Ezra and Daniel the meaning never wavers. I keep coming back to the simple reading because the simple reading is the true one.

Theological Significance

I believe gᵉlâh matters because God reveals what is hidden, and He brings His people home. I hold that the simple reading is the true one, and I will not trade it for a clever one.
