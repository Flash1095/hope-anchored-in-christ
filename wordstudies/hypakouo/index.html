hypakoúō

Word
ὑπακούω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
hypakoúō
Strongs Number
G5219
Definition
from G5259 and G191; to hear under (as a subordinate), i.e. to listen attentively; by implication, to heed or conform to a command or authority:--hearken, be obedient to, obey.
