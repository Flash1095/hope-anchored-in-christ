tôwʻêbah

properly, something disgusting (morally), i.e. (as noun) an abhorrence; especially idolatry or (concretely) an idol

Biblical Usage

Abomination is the Bible's strongest word for what God hates. The kings of Judah walked in the abominations of the nations, burning their children in the fire, and Moses commands the graven images burned, for the silver and gold on them is an abomination to the Lord.

Theological Significance

I hold that the word is not a matter of taste but of holiness: what God calls abomination is not merely unwise or unclean, it is disgusting to Him, and the nations were cast out for it. I believe the word should still make me flinch — where the Bible says abomination, I am not free to call it a difference of opinion.
