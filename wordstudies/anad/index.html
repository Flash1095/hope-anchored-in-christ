ʻânad

to lace fast

Biblical Usage

The word means to bind fast, to lace on tight: Proverbs says bind your father's commandment continually upon your heart and tie it about your neck, and Job says he would take his cause upon his shoulder and bind it as a crown to him. In both places the binding is something worn close — a command on the heart, a vindication on the head.

Theological Significance

Two verses about binding carry no doctrine of their own; their weight is the closeness — God's words tied on like a necklace, and Job's innocence worn like a crown.
