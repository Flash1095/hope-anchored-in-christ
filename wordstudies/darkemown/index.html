darkᵉmôwn

a 'drachma', or coin

Biblical Usage

The drachma is the coin of the returned exiles, counted by the thousand when the people gave for the rebuilding. The chief of the fathers gave gold and silver and garments, and the darics were weighed into the treasury. A small coin, faithfully given, built a temple.

Theological Significance

I believe the verse honours the small gift: the kingdom is built on drachmas, not only on gold talents. The giving was counted coin by coin, and the Lord who counts the coins counts the givers too.
