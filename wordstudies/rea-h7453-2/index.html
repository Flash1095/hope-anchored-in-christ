rêaʻ

an associate (more or less close)

Biblical Usage

The neighbour is the man next to you: you shall not bear false witness against your neighbour, and you shall love your neighbour as yourself. A friend loves at all times, and a neighbour who is near is better than a brother far off. Job's three friends heard of his evil and came every one from his own place to mourn with him and comfort him, and the beloved in the Song is called both beloved and friend.

Theological Significance

I read this as the neighbour is the test of everything we claim to believe. It is easy to love God in the abstract, but the neighbour is the one who hears our words and feels our hands. Love your neighbour as yourself is not a suggestion, it is the whole law in one sentence, and the friend who comes to sit in the ashes with you is the picture of it.
