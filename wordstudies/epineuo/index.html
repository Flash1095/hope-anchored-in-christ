epineúō

from G1909 and G3506; to nod at, i.e. (by implication) to assent:--consent.

Biblical Usage

Epineuo means to nod assent, to consent — and the Jews at Ephesus desire Paul to tarry longer with them, but he consented not, and bade them farewell.

Theological Significance

Paul's no is as instructive as his yes, I hold: he would not stay where God had not sent him, even when the invitation was warm.
