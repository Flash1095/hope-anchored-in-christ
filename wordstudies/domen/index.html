dômen

manure

Biblical Usage

Dung, the refuse left in the field, the end of those who refuse God. Jezebel's body was to be as dung on the face of the field at Jezreel, and Jeremiah says the slain shall be as dung upon the ground, unburied and unlamented. The psalm names the dead at Endor who became as dung for the earth.

Theological Significance

I believe the Bible's blunt word is meant to shock me awake: a life that despises God ends as refuse. The warning is severe because the mercy is real, and I would rather repent now than be left in the field.
