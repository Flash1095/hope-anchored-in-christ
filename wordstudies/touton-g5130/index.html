toútōn

genitive case plural masculine or neuter of G3778; of (from or concerning) these (persons or things):--such, their, these (things), they, this sort, those.

Biblical Usage

Of these — the word that counts the things in front of you. John says God is able of these stones to raise up children to Abraham, Jesus says whoever breaks one of these least commandments shall be called least in the kingdom, and He says the Gentiles seek after all these things, and your Father knows you need them.

Theological Significance

I believe the evidence is gathered together here: these stones, these commandments, these things — and the God who knows what we need before we ask.
