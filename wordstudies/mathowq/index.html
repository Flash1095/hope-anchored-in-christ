mâthôwq

sweet

Biblical Usage

Sweet is the word for what delights the taste, and the Bible uses it for God's word, for kind words, and for riddles. The psalm says God's judgments are sweeter than honey, and Proverbs says pleasant words are as a honeycomb, sweet to the soul. Samson poses the riddle of the sweetness that came out of the strong, and the bride in the Song sits under her beloved's shadow and finds his fruit sweet to her taste.

Theological Significance

I believe sweetness is the Bible's picture of delight that is also good for you: God's word tastes sweet because it is true, and kind words heal because they are true too. Samson's riddle warns the other side of it — sweetness can come out of violence, and what tastes sweet is not always safe. I hold that the test is the source: sweetness from God endures, and sweetness from anywhere else fades.
