tᵉlîythay

third

Biblical Usage

The word is third, in Aramaic. Daniel tells Nebuchadnezzar that after him shall arise another kingdom inferior to his, and a third kingdom of brass, which shall bear rule over all the earth; and Belshazzar promises the third ruler's place to whoever reads the writing.

Theological Significance

I believe the third kingdom of brass is part of the statue's fall, and the third ruler's place was offered on the night the kingdom ended.
