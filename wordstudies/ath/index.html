ʼâth

a portent

Biblical Usage

This Aramaic word means a sign or a wonder, and Nebuchadnezzar uses it twice when he tells what the high God has done for him. He thought it good to show the signs and wonders wrought toward him, and he says how great are His signs and how mighty His wonders. Darius uses it the same way after Daniel came out of the lions' den, saying God works signs and wonders in heaven and in earth.

Theological Significance

I believe the signs did their work on the very kings who ordered them, because the men who built the furnaces and dug the dens ended up praising the God they had defied. A sign that turns a king's heart is no small thing, and I hold that God's wonders still speak to the proud.
