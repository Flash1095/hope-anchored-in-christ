ʼômer

{something said}

Biblical Usage

Omer is something said, a word, a speech, a decree. In Job 22:28 a man decrees a thing and it is established, and in Psalm 19:2 and 19:3 day utters speech to day and night shows knowledge, though there is no speech or language where their voice is not heard. Habakkuk 3:9 calls God's word the oaths of the tribes, even His word.

Theological Significance

I believe speech is the way God fills the world, because the heavens declare His glory without a single word spoken. My position is that the word of God is the surest speech there is, and the decree of man stands only when God establishes it.
