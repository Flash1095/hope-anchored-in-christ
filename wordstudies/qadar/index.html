qâdar

to be ashy, i.e. darkcolored; by implication, to mourn (in sackcloth or sordid garments)

Biblical Usage

To grow dark is the word for mourning made visible: Judah mourns and her gates languish, black unto the ground; Jeremiah says he is black with the hurt of his people; Job went mourning without the sun. The sun and moon are darkened in the day of the LORD.

Theological Significance

I believe darkness here is not weather, it is grief with the light taken out of it, and Scripture lets the sky itself mourn. The darkened gates and the darkened sky say together what words cannot: the whole creation grieves with God's people.
