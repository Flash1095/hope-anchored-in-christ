thanatos

Death — physical death and the spiritual separation from God that sin brings, ultimately defeated by Christ's resurrection.

Biblical Usage

Thanatos enters through sin and reigns over humanity — until Easter. Paul taunts it directly: 'O death, where is your victory?' Revelation promises a day when death shall be no more.

Theological Significance

Thanatos has met its match. Because Christ died and rose, death is a defeated enemy and a doorway, not a terminus — the anchor holds on the far side of the grave.
