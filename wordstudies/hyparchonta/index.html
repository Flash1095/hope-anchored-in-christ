hypárchonta

neuter plural of present participle active of G5225 as noun; things extant or in hand, i.e. property or possessions:--goods, that which one has, things which (one) possesseth, substance, that hast.

Biblical Usage

Hypárchonta is the neuter plural participle of hypárchō used as a noun — a man's goods, what he has. In Matthew 19:21 Jesus tells the rich young man to sell what he has, and in Luke 11:21 a strong man's goods are in peace while he keeps his palace, and in 1 Corinthians 13:3 it is the goods a man bestows to feed the poor. In two other places the same form is simply the participle 'being', and those are not cited here.

Theological Significance

I take hypárchonta as it stands: what a man has, his goods.
