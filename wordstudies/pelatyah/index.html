Pᵉlaṭyâh

Pelatjah, the name of four Israelites

Biblical Usage

Pelatiah = "Jehovah delivers"; 1) son of Hananiah and grandson of Zerubbabel; 2) a Simeonite in the days of king Hezekiah of Judah who was one of the captains of 500 men who defeated the Amalekites who had escaped; 3) one of the heads of the people who sealed the covenant with Nehemiah; 4) son of Benaiah and one of the princes of the people against whom Ezekiel was ordered to prophesy doom
