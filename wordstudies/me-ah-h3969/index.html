mᵉʼâh

a hundred; also as a multiplicative and a fraction}

Biblical Usage

The word is the Aramaic for a hundred, and it appears where the numbers of the exile are counted: Darius sets a hundred and twenty princes over the kingdom, the returned exiles offer a hundred bullocks, and Ezra is given a hundred talents of silver.

Theological Significance

I believe the counting is the point: God numbers the princes, the offerings, and the silver, and nothing in the exile is too small for Him to count.
