Mᵉshillêmôwth

Word
מְשִׁלֵּמוֹת
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Mᵉshillêmôwth
Strongs Number
H4919
Definition
Meshillemoth, an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Meshillemoth = "recompense"; 1) an Ephraimite, one of the chiefs of the tribe in the reign of king Pekah of Israel; 1a) also 'Meshillemith'; 2) a priest, son of Immer; 2a) also 'Meshillemith'
