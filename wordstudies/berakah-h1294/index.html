Bᵉrâkâh

Word
בְּרָכָה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Bᵉrâkâh
Strongs Number
H1294
Definition
Berakah, the name of an Israelite, and also of a valley in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Berachah = "blessing"; n pr m; 1) a Benjamite, one of David's warriors; n pr loc; 2) a valley in the wilderness near Tekoa where Jehoshaphat and his people assembled to bless Jehovah after the overthrow of the hosts of the Moabites
