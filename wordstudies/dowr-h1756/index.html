Dôwr

Dor, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Dor was a coastal town in Manasseh's territory, given with its villages to the sons of Joseph, and the district Solomon's officer Ben-Abinadab governed from the heights of Dor. The name shares its spelling with the word for generation, but this Dor is a place, not a time.

Theological Significance

I believe the towns of the allotment are named because God gave His people a coastline as well as a hill country, and Dor's heights watched over both.
