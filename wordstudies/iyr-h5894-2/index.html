ʻîyr

a watcher, i.e. an angel (as guardian)

Biblical Usage

A watcher is an angel who keeps guard, and Daniel's dream is full of them. In Daniel 4:13 a watcher and an holy one comes down from heaven, in Daniel 4:17 the sentence is by the decree of the watchers, and the demand by the word of the holy ones, and in Daniel 4:23 the watcher commands to cut the tree down but leave the stump.

Theological Significance

I believe the watchers are the proof that heaven is awake: the decree against the proud king is not made on earth. It is made by the holy ones who watch.
