ʼim

used very widely as demonstrative, lo!; interrogative, whether?; or conditional, if, although; also Oh that!, when; hence, as a negative, not

Biblical Usage

If is the hinge the covenant turns on, and the Bible swings it both ways. In Genesis 4:7 God tells Cain, if thou doest well, shalt thou not be accepted, in Exodus 19:5 he tells Israel, if ye will obey my voice indeed, ye shall be a peculiar treasure unto me, and in Deuteronomy 28:1 he promises, if thou shalt hearken diligently unto the voice of the LORD thy God, he will set thee on high above all nations.

Theological Significance

I hold that the if is not a threat but an open door: God puts the choice in our hands, and the blessing waits on the other side of it.
