Definition
Perath (i.e. Euphrates), a river of the East
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Euphrates = "fruitfulness"; 1) the largest and longest river of western Asia; rises from two chief sources in the Armenian mountains and flows into the Persian Gulf
