ōphéleia

from a derivative of the base of G5624; usefulness, i.e. benefit:--advantage, profit.

Biblical Usage

Advantage is what you gain by it. Paul asks what advantage the Jew has, and Jude warns of men who flatter others for advantage, using smooth words to get what they want.

Theological Significance

I hold that the gospel turns advantage upside down: the only advantage worth having is the one God gives, and using people to gain it is the opposite of love.
