cholḗ

feminine of an equivalent perhaps akin to the same as G5514 (from the greenish hue); "gall" or bile, i.e. (by analogy) poison or an anodyne (wormwood, poppy, etc.):--gall.

Biblical Usage

Chole is gall, the bitter bile, and the New Testament uses it for bitterness of soul and for the cross. Peter tells Simon that he is in the gall of bitterness and the bond of iniquity, and the soldiers offer Jesus vinegar mingled with gall before they crucify Him.

Theological Significance

I believe gall is the Bible's picture of a bitterness that poisons from within, and Christ tasted it so that I would not have to drink it.
