psychikós

Word
ψυχικός
Language
Greek
Transliteration
psychikós
Strongs Number
G5591
Definition
from G5590; sensitive, i.e. animate (in distinction on the one hand from G4152, which is the higher or renovated nature; and on the other from G5446, which is the lower or bestial nature):--natural, sensual.
