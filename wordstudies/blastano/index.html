blastánō

from βλαστός (a sprout); to germinate; by implication, to yield fruit:--bring forth, bud, spring (up).

Biblical Usage

To spring up is the word for a seed germinating, budding, bringing forth — and it appears four times. In Matthew 13:26 the blade springs up and brings forth fruit, in Mark 4:27 the seed springs and grows up, he knows not how, in James 5:18 the earth brings forth her fruit after Elijah's prayer, and in Hebrews 9:4 Aaron's rod that budded is kept in the ark.

Theological Significance

I believe the budding rod is the picture I keep coming back to: dead wood, in the ark, that blossomed — because God gives life where there is none.
