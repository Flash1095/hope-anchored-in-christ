rêʼshîyth

the first, in place, time, order or rank (specifically, a firstfruit)

Biblical Usage

In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth, and the fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge. Wisdom was with God in the beginning, before His works of old. Israel was told to bring the first of the firstfruits of the land into the house of the LORD, because the first belongs to God.

Theological Significance

I believe the beginning belongs to God, and that is why the first of everything is His. Give Him the first of your time, your strength and your increase, and the rest finds its proper place. The fear of the LORD is not the end of wisdom but its beginning, and every right beginning starts there.
