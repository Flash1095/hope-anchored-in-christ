chârad

to shudder with terror; hence, to fear; also to hasten (with anxiety)

Biblical Usage

Charad is trembling with terror, and it follows Saul through his whole story. The people followed him trembling, the earth quaked and there was a very great trembling when Jonathan attacked, Saul was afraid and his heart greatly trembled when he saw the Philistines, and all the guests of Adonijah were afraid and rose up and went every man his way.

Theological Significance

I believe trembling is the body's confession when the heart will not make one. Saul trembled before Philistines and before a medium, and Adonijah's guests trembled when they heard Solomon was king. I hold that the fear of God is the only trembling that steadies a man, and Saul's story shows what happens when it is missing.
