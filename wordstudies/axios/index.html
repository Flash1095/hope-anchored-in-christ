áxios

probably from G71; deserving, comparable or suitable (as if drawing praise):--due reward, meet, (un-)worthy.

Biblical Usage

Worthy is the word of measuring — is someone or something deserving of what is given? The workman is worthy of his meat, the prodigal says he is no more worthy to be called a son, the elders sing that the Lamb is worthy to take the book and open its seals, and Paul says Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, a faithful saying and worthy of all acceptation.

Theological Significance

I believe the prodigal was right about himself and wrong about his father, because worthiness was never the measure of the welcome. And I hold that the Lamb is worthy of everything heaven sings to him, because he was slain and has redeemed us to God by his blood.
