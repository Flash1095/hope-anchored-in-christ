blētéos

from G906; fit to be cast (i.e. applied):--must be put.

Biblical Usage

Must be put is the word for fit to be cast, applied — and it appears once, in Jesus' saying about new wine. In Luke 5:38 new wine must be put into new bottles, and both are preserved. One word, for the vessel that fits the wine.

Theological Significance

I believe the new bottle is the point: new wine needs a new vessel, and the gospel needs a heart made new to hold it.
