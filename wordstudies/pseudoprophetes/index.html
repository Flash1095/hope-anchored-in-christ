pseudoprophḗtēs

from G5571 and G4396; a spurious prophet, i.e. pretended foreteller or religious impostor:--false prophet.

Biblical Usage

Pseudoprophetes means a false prophet, a pretended foreteller, a religious impostor. Jesus says, Beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep's clothing, but inwardly they are ravening wolves, and many false prophets shall rise and deceive many. John says, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits, because many false prophets are gone out into the world, and Peter says there were false prophets among the people, even as there shall be false teachers among you.

Theological Significance

I believe the false prophet's danger is his clothing: he comes as a sheep, and only his fruit gives him away. I hold that trying the spirits is not suspicion but obedience, because the world is full of prophets and only some of them speak for God.
