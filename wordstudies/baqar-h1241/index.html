bâqâr

beef cattle or an animal of the ox family of either gender (as used for plowing); collectively, a herd

Biblical Usage

Baqar is cattle, the herd, the oxen and cows that carry a household's wealth. Abraham runs to the herd for a tender calf when strangers visit his tent, Pharaoh's cattle are struck in the plagues, the law appoints the herd for burnt offerings, and Job's seven thousand sheep and five hundred yoke of oxen mark him as the greatest man of the east.

Theological Significance

I believe cattle in Scripture are never just livestock, because they measure blessing, feed the altar, and carry the cost of worship. Abraham gave his best calf to guests he did not know, and I hold that generosity with what God gave us is still the mark of a man who knows God.
