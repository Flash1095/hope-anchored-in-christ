Mâʻôwn

Word
מָעוֹן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Mâʻôwn
Strongs Number
H4584
Definition
Maon, the name of an Israelite and of a place in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Maon or Maonites = "habitation"; n pr m; 1) a man of Judah; 2) the inhabitants of the city of Maon; n pr loc; 3) a city of Judah located 8 miles south of Hebron
