Chavvôwth Yâʻîyr

hamlets of Jair, a region of Palestine

Biblical Usage

Havoth-jair, the villages of Jair, are the towns Jair the son of Manasseh took and named after himself. They are counted in the settlement east of Jordan, in the record of the conquest, and again in the days of Jair the judge, whose thirty sons rode on thirty colts and held thirty cities.

Theological Significance

I hold that the villages of Jair outlasted Jair himself, and the name stayed on the land. What God gives His people to name, He lets them keep.
