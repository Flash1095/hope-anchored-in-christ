ʻEglâh

Eglah, a wife of David

Biblical Usage

Eglah was a wife of David and the mother of his son Ithream, and her name means a heifer. The lists name her in both the record of David's sons born in Hebron and the genealogy of his house, and beyond those two mentions we know nothing more about her. She is one of the wives whose names survive only because her son did.

Theological Significance

I hold that God remembers the mothers as well as the sons, because Eglah's name is kept in the record of the king's house. Her place in the family of David is part of the line that leads to Christ.
