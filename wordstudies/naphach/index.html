nâphach

to puff, in various applications (literally, to inflate, blow hard, scatter, kindle, expire; figuratively, to disesteem)

Biblical Usage

Naphach means to blow or breathe, and it is the word for God breathing life into man and into dry bones. In Genesis 2:7 the Lord God breathes into man's nostrils the breath of life, and man becomes a living soul, and in Ezekiel 22:21 the Lord says He will blow upon them in the fire of His wrath. In Ezekiel 37:9 the breath is told to breathe upon the slain that they may live, and in Malachi 1:13 the people snuff at God's table as if it were weariness.

Theological Significance

I believe the breath of God is the difference between dust and a living soul, and between dry bones and an army. The same wind that drowned Egypt gives life to the slain, and the same mouth that received life can snuff at the table. I hold that every breath I take is borrowed from the One who breathed first.
