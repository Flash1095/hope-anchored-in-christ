ástron

neuter from G792; properly, a constellation; put for a single star (natural or artificial):--star.

Biblical Usage

Stephen charges Israel with carrying the tabernacle of Moloch and the star of their god Remphan, and the writer to the Hebrews says Abraham's descendants became as many as the stars of heaven. Jesus says there will be signs in the sun and moon and stars, and on Paul's ship neither sun nor stars appeared for many days.

Theological Significance

I believe the stars keep two records at once: the idols men worshipped and the promises God kept. Abraham's seed outnumbered the stars, and I hold that the same God who counts them also counts His people.
