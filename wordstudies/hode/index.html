hóde

Word
ὅδε
Language
Greek
Transliteration
hóde
Strongs Number
G3592
Definition
, including the feminine ἥδε, and the neuter τόδε from G3588 and G1161; the same, i.e. this or that one (plural these or those); often used as a personal pronoun:--he, she, such, these, thus.
