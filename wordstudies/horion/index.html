hórion

neuter of a derivative of an apparently primary ὅρος (a bound or limit); a boundary-line, i.e. (by implication) a frontier (region):--border, coast.

Biblical Usage

hórion means border, coast. It occurs 11 times in all, and the passages I have picked — Matthew, Mark, and Acts — show it in its plain sense. I read it as a word that does its work quietly, without dressing anything up.

Theological Significance

What hórion tells me is that God sets boundaries, and His boundaries are for our good. I hold that reading it plainly keeps me honest about what God actually said.
