tsedeq

Word
צֶדֶק
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
tsedeq
Strongs Number
H6664
Definition
Righteousness and justice — rightness measured by God's character, expressed in fair dealing, integrity and setting things right.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
Psalm 89:14; Isaiah 45:8; Amos 5:24; Deuteronomy 16:20
Biblical Usage
Tsedeq and its family of words describe God's reign founded on righteousness and justice. The prophets longed for justice to 'roll down like waters' in a land that had abandoned God's standard.
Theological Significance
Tsedeq assures a hurting world that God sees injustice and will set things right. In Christ, God's righteousness is both revealed and gifted to all who believe.
