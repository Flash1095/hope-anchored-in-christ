hâlam

to strike down; by implication, to hammer, stamp, conquer, disband

Biblical Usage

hâlam turns up where the Bible says to strike down, to hammer. In 1 Samuel 14:16, the text says "and they went on beating down [one another]." In Psalm 74:6, the text says "But now they break down the carved work thereof at once with axes and hammers."

Theological Significance

I believe hâlam shows us that God's judgment is just, and it falls where it should — no one escapes it, and no one suffers it unjustly. That shapes how I read the passages where it appears: not as filler, but as God's own wording for what He is doing.
