ʻillay

supreme (i.e. God)

Biblical Usage

The Most High is the Aramaic name for God enthroned above every king, and Daniel's book rings with it. In Daniel 3:26 Nebuchadnezzar calls the three men servants of the most high God, in Daniel 4:2 he tells the signs the high God has wrought toward him, in Daniel 4:17 the decree stands so the living may know that the most High ruleth in the kingdom of men, and in Daniel 5:18 Daniel tells Belshazzar that the most high God gave his father the kingdom.

Theological Significance

I believe the Most High is the name that humbles every throne: kings rise and fall. The One above them gives and takes away as he wills.
