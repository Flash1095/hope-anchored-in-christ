Chăshabyâh

Chashabjah, the name of nine Israelites

Biblical Usage

Hashabiah is a name carried by nine Israelites, and three of them show the Levites' three works. One is a son of Jeduthun who prophesied with a harp to give thanks, one is the son of Kemuel who ruled the Levites under David, and one is a chief of the Levites who gave passover offerings under Josiah.

Theological Significance

I believe the three Hashabiahs are the Levites in miniature: a musician, a ruler, and a giver, all serving the same house. I hold that God gives different Levites different work, and the house needs all three.
