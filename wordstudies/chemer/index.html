chemer

wine (as fermenting)

Biblical Usage

This is wine, the foaming blood of the grape, and Moses sings that Israel drank the pure blood of the grape along with the butter and the fat and the milk. The wine is part of the plenty God gave.

Theological Significance

I believe the blood of the grape in the song is the picture of God's plenty: the land gives wine as well as bread, and the gift is His.
