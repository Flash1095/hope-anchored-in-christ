Chammôwn

Word
חַמּוֹן
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Chammôwn
Strongs Number
H2540
Definition
Chammon, the name of two places in Palestine
Part Of Speech
proper noun
Biblical Usage
Hammon = "warm springs"; 1) a town in Asher, apparently not far from Zidon-rabbah; 2) a town in Naphtali allotted to the Levites; also called 'Hammath' and 'Hammoth-dor'
