loúō

a primary verb; to bathe (the whole person; whereas G3538 means to wet a part only, and G4150 to wash, cleanse garments exclusively):--wash.

Biblical Usage

loúō means wash, and no one who reads the Bible can walk past it. In John 13:10, "He that is washed needeth not save to wash [his] feet"; and in Hebrews 10:22, "and our bodies washed with pure water.". In Acts 16:33, "and washed [their] stripes"; and in Acts 9:37, "and died: whom when they had washed".

Theological Significance

I believe washing is the picture of a clean conscience — he that is washed need not save to wash his feet, and our bodies are washed with pure water. I hold that the jailer washed the apostles' stripes the same hour he believed, and the dead were washed before they were laid out. My position is that I am washed, and I will live like a washed person.
