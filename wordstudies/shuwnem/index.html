Shûwnêm

Shunem, a place in Pal

Biblical Usage

Shunem was a town in Issachar's portion, and it marks three moments in Israel's story. The Philistines pitched there before the battle of Gilboa, Elisha passed there and found a great woman who constrained him to eat, and Joshua's border list sets it toward Jezreel.

Theological Significance

I believe a small town can hold a great woman. Shunem is a name on a border list, and I hold that the Lord made it the home of a hospitality that raised the dead.
