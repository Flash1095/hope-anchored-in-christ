ʻayin

an eye (literally or figuratively); by analogy, a fountain (as the eye of the landscape)

Biblical Usage

The eye is the lamp of the body and the window of the heart. Eve saw the tree was pleasant to the eyes, Samuel learned that man looks on the outward appearance but the Lord looks on the heart, Elisha prayed for his servant's eyes to be opened to the mountain full of horses and chariots of fire, and Proverbs says the eyes of the Lord are in every place, beholding the evil and the good.

Theological Significance

I believe God sees what I cannot see, and that is both my comfort and my correction. Elisha's servant needed opened eyes, and so do I — to see the Lord's armies, and to see my own heart the way God sees it.
