miçpâchâh

a veil (as spread out)

Biblical Usage

Woe to the women who sew pillows to all armholes and make kerchiefs upon the head of every stature to hunt souls, God says through Ezekiel, and then He says their kerchiefs will be torn and His people delivered out of their hand. The word is the veil itself, the kerchief spread to hunt.

Theological Significance

I hold that the kerchief is a picture of false prophecy at its worst: it hunts souls with a veil, and God tears the veil to set His people free.
