nêts

a flower (from its brilliancy); also a hawk (from it flashing speed)

Biblical Usage

The same word is a blossom and a hawk, and the Bible uses both. The vine in Pharaoh's cup-bearer's dream buds and its blossoms shoot forth, while the law lists the hawk among the birds not to be eaten, and Job asks whether the hawk flies by man's wisdom toward the south.

Theological Significance

I hold that one word can hold a flower and a hunter: the blossom opens in a dream of restoration, and the hawk flies by a wisdom that is not mine. The cup-bearer's blossom means restoration, and Job's hawk means a wisdom above mine — the word holds both.
