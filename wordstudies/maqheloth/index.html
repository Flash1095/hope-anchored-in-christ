Maqhêlôth

Makheloth, a place in the Desert

Biblical Usage

Maqhêlôth is Makheloth, a camp in the wilderness journey, named in Numbers 33:25 and Numbers 33:26. Israel removes from Haradah and pitches in Makheloth, then removes from Makheloth and encamps at Tahath. The name is one night's stop on the long road.

Theological Significance

I believe Maqhêlôth reminds me that the wilderness road was walked one camp at a time, and that God led His people through places whose names we barely remember.
