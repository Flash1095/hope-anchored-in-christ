diáphoros

from G1308; varying; also surpassing:--differing, divers, more excellent.

Biblical Usage

Different is the word for what is more excellent, and Hebrews uses it of Christ and His covenant. He has obtained a more excellent ministry, a better covenant, and a more excellent name than the angels, and the gifts of the Spirit differ one from another.

Theological Significance

I believe the better things in Hebrews are all found in Christ. A better covenant, a better name, a better ministry — the old was good, but the new is more excellent because it rests on His blood, not on ours.
