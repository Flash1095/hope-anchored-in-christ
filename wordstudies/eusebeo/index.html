eusebéō

from G2152; to be pious, i.e. (towards God) to worship, or (towards parents) to respect (support):--show piety, worship.

Biblical Usage

The word eusebéō carries the sense of show piety, worship. In Acts 17:23, the text says "TO THE UNKNOWN GOD. Whom therefore ye ignorantly worship."

Theological Significance

For me, eusebéō means show piety, worship, nothing more and nothing less.
