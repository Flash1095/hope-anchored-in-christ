mâʻôwg

a cake of bread; parasite

Biblical Usage

The word names a cake of bread, and it appears at two tables. The widow of Zarephath tells Elijah she has not a cake, only a handful of meal and a little oil, enough for one last meal for her and her son before they die, and the psalm turns the word against hypocritical mockers at feasts, who gnash upon the sufferer with their teeth.

Theological Significance

I hold that the widow's cake is the measure of faith: she had almost nothing, and God made it enough, while the mockers at the feast had plenty and used it to bite.
