raqqâh

properly, thinness, i.e. the side of the head

Biblical Usage

Raqqah is thinness — the temple, the side of the head. Jael 'took a nail of the tent... and smote the nail into his temples'; Deborah sang that she 'smote off his head, when she had pierced and stricken through his temples'; and the Song says, 'thy temples are like a piece of a pomegranate within thy locks.'

Theological Significance

My conviction is that the temple is the thinnest place: Jael's nail found it, and the Song finds it beautiful — the same thin bone, in war and in love.
