ʼĂdônîyâh

Adonijah, the name of three Israelites

Biblical Usage

Adonijah was David's fourth son, born at Hebron, who tried to seize the throne while his father was dying. He gathered chariots and horsemen, sacrificed at En-rogel, and had himself proclaimed king — but Nathan and Bathsheba moved first, Solomon was anointed at Gihon, and Adonijah's feast broke up in fear.

Theological Significance

I believe Adonijah's story is a warning about grasping for what God has not given. The throne belonged to Solomon by God's word and David's oath, and no feast, no chariots, and no proclamation could take it from him.
