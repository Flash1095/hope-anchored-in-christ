maqqâbâh

properly, a perforatrix, i.e. a hammer (as piercing)

Biblical Usage

maqqâbâh is a hammer, the tool that pierces and shapes. In 1 Kings 6:7 the temple is built of stone made ready beforehand, so that neither hammer nor axe nor any tool of iron is heard in the house while it is building. In Isaiah 44:12 the smith fashions an idol with hammers and works with the strength of his arms until he faints., and the same hammer can build God's house in silence or make an idol.

Theological Significance

I believe maqqâbâh shows me that tools take their meaning from the hand that holds them, and I hold that quiet obedience builds better than loud idolatry.
