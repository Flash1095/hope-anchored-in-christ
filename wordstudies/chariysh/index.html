chârîysh

ploughing or its season

Biblical Usage

Chariysh is ploughing, or its season, and it marks the farmer's year. Six days thou shalt work, but on the seventh day thou shalt rest: in earing time and in harvest thou shalt rest. Joseph tells his brothers five years remain in which there shall neither be earing nor harvest, and Samuel warns that a king will set men to ear his ground and reap his harvest.

Theological Significance

I believe the ploughing season is the Bible's picture of ordinary work under God's ordering: even in earing time and harvest, the Sabbath stands. Joseph's famine took away both earing and harvest, and Samuel's king would take the ploughmen for himself. I hold that work is God's gift, and rest is His command, and neither may swallow the other.
