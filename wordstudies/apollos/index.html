Apollōs

probably from the same as G624; Apollos, an Israelite:--Apollos.

Biblical Usage

The name runs through Acts and Paul's letters wherever the church is learning to work together: Apollos came to Ephesus mighty in the Scriptures in Acts 18:24, and was at Corinth while Paul came to Ephesus in Acts 19:1, and the Corinthians divided over him in 1 Corinthians 1:12, saying, I am of Paul, and I of Apollos. Paul answers that in 1 Corinthians 3:6 — I have planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the increase — and years later he is still caring for him, asking Titus to bring Apollos on his journey diligently in Titus 3:13 and telling the Corinthians in 1 Corinthians 16:12 that he greatly desired Apollos to come to them.

Theological Significance

I believe Apollos matters because he shows me that the work is shared and the increase is God's. Paul planted, Apollos watered, and neither of them could make anything grow — and the man the Corinthians fought over is the man Paul loved, sent for, and provided for.
