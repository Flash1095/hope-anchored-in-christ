tîyrôwsh

must or fresh grape-juice (as just squeezed out); by implication (rarely) fermented wine

Biblical Usage

New wine is the sign of a land doing well. When the commandment went abroad under Hezekiah the people brought in abundance the firstfruits of corn, wine and oil, and the Assyrian Rabshakeh tempts Jerusalem with a promise of a land of corn and wine, of bread and vineyards.

Theological Significance

I believe the word shows how ordinary abundance becomes a spiritual sign: corn and wine in the storehouse mean a people blessed, and a land of corn and wine is what the enemy offers as a substitute for the Lord's own land.
