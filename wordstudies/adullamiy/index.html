ʻĂdullâmîy

an Adullamite or native of Adullam

Biblical Usage

Adullamite means a man from Adullam — and in Scripture it belongs to one man, Hirah, the friend of Judah. Hirah was with Judah when he left his brothers, when he married, and when he went up to the sheep-shearing; and it was Hirah whom Judah sent with the kid to pay Tamar.

Theological Significance

A man's hometown carries no doctrine, and I won't pretend it does. Hirah's value is in the company he kept: he stood beside Judah through choices that would shape a tribe, and his presence is a reminder that the friends we keep are part of the story God is writing.
