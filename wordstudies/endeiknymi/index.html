endeíknymi

from G1722 and G1166; to indicate (by word or act):--do, show (forth).

Biblical Usage

To show, to indicate by word or deed. Christ showed forth all longsuffering in Paul first, for a pattern to believers, in 1 Timothy 1:16, and God will show the exceeding riches of His grace in the ages to come in Ephesians 2:7. Paul also tells the Corinthians to show the proof of their love in 2 Corinthians 8:24 — and Alexander the coppersmith did Paul much evil, as he notes in 2 Timothy 4:14.

Theological Significance

To me, the proof is in people — His longsuffering in Paul, His grace in the ages to come, and our love in the open before the churches. I hold that a faith nobody can see is a faith nobody can follow, so I want my life to show what I say I believe.
