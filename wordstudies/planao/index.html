planáō

from G4106; to (properly, cause to) roam (from safety, truth, or virtue):--go astray, deceive, err, seduce, wander, be out of the way.

Biblical Usage

Planao means to wander, to lead astray, to deceive. Jesus warns, Take heed that no man deceive you, in Matthew 24:4, and tells the Sadducees they err, not knowing the Scriptures, in Matthew 22:29. John writes of those who seduce you in 1 John 2:26, and James says the brother who errs from the truth can be turned back in James 5:19. At the last, Revelation 12:9 names the deceiver of the whole world.

Theological Significance

I believe deception is the devil's oldest trade and the believer's nearest danger. That is why I hold that the cure is not cleverness but knowing the Scriptures and staying in the truth.
