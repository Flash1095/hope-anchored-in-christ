ʻAzbûwq

Word
עַזְבּוּק
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
ʻAzbûwq
Strongs Number
H5802
Definition
Azbuk, an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Azbuk = "strong devastation"; 1) father of Nehemiah, leader of half the district of Beth Zur, and a repairer of the walls of Jerusalem under another Nehemiah
