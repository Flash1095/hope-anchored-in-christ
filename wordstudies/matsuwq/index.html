mâtsûwq

something narrow, i.e. a column or hilltop

Biblical Usage

The word is a narrow crag or column, and it appears once, at the pass where Jonathan climbed to the Philistine garrison: one sharp rock on one side, and another on the other, and the way between them was the only way through.

Theological Significance

I believe the narrow place is the picture of faith: Jonathan did not wait for a wide road, he climbed the crag that was there, and God gave him the victory on the far side.
