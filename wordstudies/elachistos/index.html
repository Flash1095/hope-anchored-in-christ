eláchistos

superlative of ἔλαχυς (short); used as equivalent to G3398; least (in size, amount, dignity, etc.):--least, very little (small), smallest.

Biblical Usage

The word eláchistos carries the sense of the least. In Matthew 2:6, the text says "art not the least among the princes of Juda." In Luke 12:26, the text says "If ye then be not able to do that thing which is least."

Theological Significance

eláchistos convinces me that humility is not thinking less of ourselves; it is thinking of ourselves less, and of God more. That is why I keep this word in mind when I read those passages — it carries more than its size suggests.
