mᵉshammâh

a waste or amazement

Biblical Usage

Meshammah is desolation, a waste, an amazement at what judgment leaves behind. Ezekiel 5:15 says Jerusalem shall be a reproach and a taunt, an instruction and an astonishment unto the nations, and Ezekiel 6:14 promises to make the land desolate, more desolate than the wilderness toward Diblath. Isaiah 15:6 and Jeremiah 48:34 both say the waters of Nimrim shall be desolate, with the hay withered away and no green thing.

Theological Significance

I believe desolation is meant to be seen: a reproach and an instruction to the nations round about, so that they shall know that I am the LORD. My position is that God makes a land desolate not to end the story but to prove He is the one writing it.
