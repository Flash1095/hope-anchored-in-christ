yaʻan

properly, heed; by implication, purpose (sake or account); used adverbially to indicate the reason or cause

Biblical Usage

By myself have I sworn, saith the Lord, for because thou hast done this thing, and hast not withheld thy son, and the land shall enjoy her sabbaths, because the people despised God's judgments.

Theological Significance

I believe because is the word that carries God's reasons: He swears because Abraham obeyed, and the land rests because Israel despised His judgments, and in both cases the because is the hinge.
