ʼImmêr

Immer, the name of five Israelites

Biblical Usage

Immer is the name of a priestly house, and of the priest who struck Jeremiah. In 1 Chronicles 24:14 the sixteenth lot falls to Immer, in Ezra 2:37 the children of Immer come up from Babylon, a thousand and fifty-two, and in Jeremiah 20:1 Pashur the son of Immer, chief governor in the house of the LORD, hears Jeremiah prophesy and smites him.

Theological Significance

I hold that the name carries both sides of the priesthood: a house set apart to serve, and a son of that house who used his office to strike the prophet.
