phréar

of uncertain derivation; a hole in the ground (dug for obtaining or holding water or other purposes), i.e. a cistern or well; figuratively, an abyss (as a prison):--well, pit.

Biblical Usage

Phrear is a well, a pit, a hole dug in the ground, and it appears at Jacob's well and at the bottomless pit. The Samaritan woman says the well is deep and Jesus has nothing to draw with, and John sees a star fall from heaven and the key of the bottomless pit given to him.

Theological Significance

I hold that the well and the pit are the two depths of the Bible: the well where living water is offered, and the pit where the locusts rise.
