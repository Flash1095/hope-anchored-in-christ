ʼachărôwn

hinder; generally, late or last; specifically (as facing the east) western

Biblical Usage

Acharown means last, latter, hindmost — and, facing east, western. Jacob put Rachel and Joseph hindermost; Job confessed his Redeemer "shall stand at the latter day upon the earth"; the Lord declares, "I am the first, and I am the last"; and Haggai promises, "The glory of this latter house shall be greater than of the former." One word stretches from the back of a caravan to the end of the age.

Theological Significance

When God calls Himself the last, He is claiming the end of the story as His own. I hold that as a firm anchor: the God who was first will also be last, and nothing after Him can undo what He has done.
