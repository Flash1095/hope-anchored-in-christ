kaph

the hollow hand or palm (so of the paw of an animal, of the sole, and even of the bowl of a dish or sling, the handle of a bolt, the leaves of a palm-tree); figuratively, power

Biblical Usage

Kaph is the hollow hand — the palm, the sole of the foot, the paw. Abigail tells David his soul shall be bound in the bundle of life; Solomon spreads forth his hands toward heaven in prayer; Elijah's servant sees a little cloud like a man's hand rising out of the sea; and Jonathan took his life in his hand to deliver Israel.

Theological Significance

I believe the hand is the Bible's picture of power and of prayer: the hand that takes its life in its palm, the hands spread toward heaven, the little cloud like a hand that brings the rain — and the hand of God that holds us is stronger than any of them. My hands are weak, but His are not, and I am held by them.
