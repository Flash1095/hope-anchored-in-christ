kᵉphôwr

properly, a cover, i.e. (by implication) a tankard (or covered goblet); also white frost (as covering the ground)

Biblical Usage

Kephowr means a bowl or covered cup, and also the hoarfrost: Exodus 16:14 says upon the face of the wilderness there lay a small round thing, as small as the hoar frost on the ground. 1 Chronicles 28:17 gives pure gold for the bowls and the cups of the temple. Ezra 1:10 counts thirty basons of gold, silver basons of a second sort four hundred and ten. Psalms 147:16 says he scattereth the hoarfrost like ashes.

Theological Significance

I believe the same word holds manna and gold: the frost that fed Israel in the wilderness, and the bowls that served his house. I hold that God provides both the daily bread and the holy vessels, and both are his.
