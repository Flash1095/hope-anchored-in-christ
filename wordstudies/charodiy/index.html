Chărôdîy

Word
חֲרֹדִי
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Chărôdîy
Strongs Number
H2733
Definition
a Charodite, or inhabitant of Charod
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Harodite = see Harod "trembling"; 1) a resident or descendant of Harod otherwise unknown; two of David's 37 guards, Shammah and Elika, called Harodites
