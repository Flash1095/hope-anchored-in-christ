anakaínōsis

from G341; renovation:--renewing.

Biblical Usage

Paul tells the Romans not to be conformed to this world but to be transformed by the renewing of their minds, so they can prove what is the good and acceptable and perfect will of God — and he tells Titus that God saved us not by works of righteousness we have done, but by the washing of regeneration and renewing of the Holy Ghost. The renewing is both a mind made new and a life washed new, and in both places God is the one who does it.

Theological Significance

I hold that renewal starts in the mind and shows in the life: a mind renewed by God's word stops being shaped by the world, and a life washed by the Spirit stops trusting its own works. It is not my effort that renews me — it is the Holy Ghost — but the renewed mind is mine to use, proving God's will day by day.
