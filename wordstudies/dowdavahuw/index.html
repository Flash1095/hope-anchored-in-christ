Dôwdâvâhûw

Word
דּוֹדָוָהוּ
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Dôwdâvâhûw
Strongs Number
H1735
Definition
Dodavah, an Israelite
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Dodavah = "beloved of Jehovah"; 1) a man of Mareshah in Judah, the father of Eliezer, who denounced Jehoshaphat's alliance with Ahaziah
