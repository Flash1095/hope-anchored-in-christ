didaskalía

from G1320; instruction (the function or the information):--doctrine, learning, teaching.

Biblical Usage

Doctrine is the teaching itself, and the letters are full of commands about it. The teacher is to wait on his teaching, Timothy is to give himself to reading and exhortation and doctrine, all Scripture is profitable for doctrine, and the aged are to speak the things which become sound doctrine.

Theological Significance

I believe doctrine matters because it is the teaching of Christ, not the opinions of men. A church that will not endure sound doctrine will endure every wind of teaching instead, and the difference is the difference between life and death.
