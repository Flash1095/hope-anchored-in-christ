Stéphanos

the same as G4735; Stephanus, a Christian:--Stephen.

Biblical Usage

Stephen is chosen first among the seven, a man full of faith and of the Holy Ghost, and he is stoned calling upon God, saying, Lord Jesus, receive my spirit. Paul remembers standing by when the blood of thy martyr Stephen was shed, consenting unto his death.

Theological Significance

I believe Stephen's death is the first seed of the church's martyrs, and the man who consented became the apostle who followed.
