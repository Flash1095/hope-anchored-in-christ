ʼÔwphîyr

Ophir, the name of a son of Joktan, and of a gold region in the East

Biblical Usage

Ophir was a son of Joktan, and the gold region that bore his name. Solomon's ships went to Ophir and brought back gold, and the navy of Hiram brought gold from Ophir with the almug trees and precious stones.

Theological Significance

I believe Ophir is the far country that sent gold to Solomon: the ships came back laden, and the gold of Ophir became the measure of wealth in Israel.
