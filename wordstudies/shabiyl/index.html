shâbîyl

a track or passage-way (as if flowing along)

Biblical Usage

Shabiyl is a track or bypath, the worn way feet follow. Jeremiah charges Israel with stumbling from the ancient paths onto bypaths, while the psalmist sees God's own path running through the sea, where His footsteps are not known.

Theological Significance

I hold that the ancient path is the safe one, and the bypaths we carve for ourselves only lead us astray.
