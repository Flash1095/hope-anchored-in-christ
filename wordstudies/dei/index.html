Language
Greek
Transliteration
deî
Strongs Number

Definition
3rd person singular active present of G1210; also δέον; neuter active participle of the same; both used impersonally; it is (was, etc.) necessary (as binding):--behoved, be meet, must (needs), (be) need(-ful), ought, should.
