chôtsen

{the bosom (as comprised between the arms)}

Biblical Usage

The bosom is where a person carries what is closest to them, and Isaiah pictures God carrying His children home in His bosom. Nehemiah shakes out his own bosom as a sign, emptying it of everything he has taken.

Theological Significance

I hold that what a person carries close shows what they truly value, and God carries His people close.
