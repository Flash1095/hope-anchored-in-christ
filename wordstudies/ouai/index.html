ouaí

a primary exclamation of grief; "woe":--alas, woe.

Biblical Usage

Woe is the cry of grief and of judgment. Jesus said woe unto you that are rich, for ye have received your consolation, and woe to them that are with child in the days of trouble, while Paul said woe is unto me if I preach not the gospel, and Jude said woe unto them who have gone in the way of Cain.

Theological Significance

I believe woe is the other side of blessing: Jesus pronounced woes on the rich and the careless, and Paul felt the woe of his own calling — woe is unto me if I preach not the gospel. The woe is not God's last word, but it is a true one, and I do well to hear it before I need it.
