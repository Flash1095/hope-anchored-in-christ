karmel

a planted field (garden, orchard, vineyard or park); by implication, garden produce

Biblical Usage

Karmel is a planted field — a garden, an orchard, a fruitful vineyard, and by extension the fruitful land itself. Isaiah says Lebanon shall be turned into a fruitful field, and the fruitful field shall be esteemed as a forest; Jeremiah says God brought Israel into a plentiful country to eat the fruit thereof; and the law forbids eating the new grain until the offering is brought.

Theological Significance

I believe the fruitful field is the picture of what God does with a wilderness: He plants it, He waters it, and the desert becomes a garden — and that is what He does with a barren heart too. The wilderness I bring Him is the garden He makes.
