medev

properly, extent, i.e. measure; by implication, a dress (as measured)

Biblical Usage

The word is a measure, a dress, and it appears where Hanun shames David's servants: he shaved off half their beards and cut off their garments in the middle, even to their buttocks, and sent them away.

Theological Significance

I hold that the cut garment is the picture of calculated insult: Hanun measured out the shame exactly, and the war that followed was the price of a joke told at the wrong men.
