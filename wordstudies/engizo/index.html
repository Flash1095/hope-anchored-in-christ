engízō

from G1451; to make near, i.e. (reflexively) approach:--approach, be at hand, come (draw) near, be (come, draw) nigh.

Biblical Usage

To come near, to draw nigh. The end of all things is at hand in 1 Peter 4:7, the time of the promise drew nigh in Acts 7:17, and we draw nigh to God through a better hope in Hebrews 7:19. James gives the promise both ways in James 4:8: draw nigh to God, and He will draw nigh to you.

Theological Significance

I believe nearness to God is not a feeling but a direction — I draw near, and He draws near, and the distance closes from both sides. I hold that the end being at hand is not a threat but an invitation to come close while there is still time.
