merís

feminine of G3313; a portion, i.e. province, share or (abstractly) participation:--part (X -akers).

Biblical Usage

Meris is a part, a portion, a share, and it is used where belonging is decided. Luke 10:42 says Mary hath chosen that good part, which shall not be taken away from her, and Colossians 1:12 thanks the Father who made us meet to be partakers of the inheritance of the saints in light. 2 Corinthians 6:15 asks what part hath he that believeth with an infidel, and Acts 8:21 tells Simon he hath neither part nor lot in the matter.

Theological Significance

I believe a part is the whole question of belonging: Mary's part cannot be taken, Simon's part was never his, and the believer's part is an inheritance. My position is that I want the part Mary chose, the one that lasts.
