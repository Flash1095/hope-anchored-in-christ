melach

properly, powder, i.e. (specifically) salt (as easily pulverized and dissolved)

Biblical Usage

Melach is salt, the common mineral that preserves, flavours, heals, and marks a covenant. 2 Chronicles 13:5 calls God's gift of the kingdom to David a covenant of salt, a bond that does not rot. 2 Kings 2:20 has Elisha ask for salt in a new cruse to heal the waters of Jericho, and 1 Chronicles 18:12 and 2 Samuel 8:13 both name the Valley of Salt, where Abishai and David struck down the Edomites.

Theological Significance

I believe salt is the right picture for God's covenant because salt keeps what it touches from going bad. My position is that a covenant of salt is one God will not let spoil, and the same salt that heals a spring can mark a battlefield.
