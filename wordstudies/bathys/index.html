bathýs

from the base of G939; profound (as going down), literally or figuratively:--deep, very early.

Biblical Usage

Bathys means deep, and it is the word for the deep well at Sychar where the Samaritan woman says Jesus has nothing to draw with. It is also the word for the women who come very early to the tomb, for Eutychus who sinks into a deep sleep while Paul preaches, and for the deep things of Satan the church at Thyatira is warned not to know.

Theological Significance

I believe deep cuts both ways in this word, because the well is deep and the sleep is deep and the things of Satan are deep. The woman at the well found living water deeper than her well, and I hold that the deep things of God are the only deep things worth knowing.
