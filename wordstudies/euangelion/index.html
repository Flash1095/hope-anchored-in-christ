euangelion

Gospel — the good news, originally an announcement of victory or a king's arrival, claimed by the church for Jesus Christ.

Biblical Usage

Euangelion was imperial language — news of Caesar's victories — repurposed for a crucified King. Mark opens 'the gospel of Jesus Christ,' and Paul is not ashamed of it, for it is God's power for salvation.

Theological Significance

Euangelion is announcement before it is advice: something has happened — Christ died, is risen and reigns. Hope rests on news, not on our efforts to be newsworthy.
