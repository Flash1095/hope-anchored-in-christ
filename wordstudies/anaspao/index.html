anaspáō

from G303 and G4685; to take up or extricate:--draw up, pull out.

Biblical Usage

The word means to draw up, to pull out: which of you, Jesus asks, shall have an ass or an ox fallen into a pit and will not straightway pull him out on the sabbath day? And Peter's vision of the sheet was drawn up again into heaven, all three times. It is the pulling out of a pit and the drawing up of a vision — the same motion, up out of where it was.

Theological Significance

A word for pulling out carries no doctrine of its own; its weight is the two drawings — the ox pulled from the pit on the sabbath, and the sheet drawn up into heaven.
