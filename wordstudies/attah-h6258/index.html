ʻattâh

at this time, whether adverb, conjunction or expletive

Biblical Usage

Now is the word that turns a sentence toward the present moment, and it carries some of the Bible's greatest turns. Jacob said, I am not worthy of the least of all the mercies, and now I am become two bands, Solomon said, now the LORD my God has given me rest on every side, Isaiah said the government will be ordered from henceforth even for ever, and Micah cried, now gather thyself in troops, O daughter of troops.

Theological Significance

I believe now is the most honest word in the Bible, because it will not let me live in yesterday's mercies or tomorrow's promises. Jacob's now was a confession, Solomon's now was a rest, and I hold that God's now is always the moment to trust Him.
