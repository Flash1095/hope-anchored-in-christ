paracheimázō

from G3844 and G5492; to winter near, i.e. stay with over the rainy season:--winter.

Biblical Usage

Paracheimazō means to winter somewhere, to stay over the cold season. After three months in Malta, Paul sails in a ship that has wintered there, and he tells Titus to be diligent to come unto him to Nicopolis, for I have determined there to winter.

Theological Significance

I believe wintering is part of the mission: Paul waits three months in Malta and plans a winter in Nicopolis, and the gospel travels at the pace of the seasons.
