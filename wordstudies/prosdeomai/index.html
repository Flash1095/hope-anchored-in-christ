prosdéomai

from G4314 and G1189; to require additionally, i.e. want further:--need.

Biblical Usage

Prosdeomai means to need something more, to want further. It appears once, in Paul's sermon at Athens: God is not worshipped with men's hands, as though He needed any thing, seeing He giveth to all life, and breath, and all things.

Theological Significance

I believe this turns worship right side up: I do not supply God's needs, He supplies mine, and my worship is the answer of a creature who has been given everything.
