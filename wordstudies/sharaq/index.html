shâraq

properly, to be shrill, i.e. to whistle or hiss (as a call or in scorn)

Biblical Usage

A hiss can summon an army or scorn a ruin, and the Bible uses both. The LORD will hiss unto the nations from far, and they shall come with speed swiftly, and Zechariah says, I will hiss for them, and gather them, for I have redeemed them. The other side is the hiss of scorn, where every one that passeth by the house shall be astonished, and shall hiss.

Theological Significance

I believe a hiss can call or curse, and the difference is whose lips make it, the LORD's or the passer-by's.
