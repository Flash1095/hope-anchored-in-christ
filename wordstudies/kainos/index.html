kainós

of uncertain affinity; new (especially in freshness; while G3501 is properly so with respect to age:--new.

Biblical Usage

Kainos is new in the sense of fresh — not new in time but new in kind. If any man be in Christ, he is a new creature; Jesus gives a new commandment, that we love one another; God promises a new covenant; and Peter says we look for new heavens and a new earth wherein dwelleth righteousness.

Theological Significance

I believe this word is the gospel in one syllable. God does not patch the old; He makes new — a new heart, a new covenant, a new creation — and that is my position: what Christ does in a life is not a repair, it is a new beginning.
