ʼêb

{a green plant}

Biblical Usage

Fruit, the Aramaic word for the fruit of Nebuchadnezzar's great tree, whose leaves were fair and whose fruit was much, with food for all under it. The watcher commanded the tree to be cut down and its fruit scattered, and Daniel told the king the tree was him, great and strong.

Theological Significance

I believe the fruit of the tree was the king's greatness, and the scattering of it was God's judgment on pride. A tree that feeds all is a gift, but a tree that forgets who planted it is cut down.
