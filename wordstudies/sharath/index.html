shârath

to attend as a menial or worshipper; figuratively, to contribute to

Biblical Usage

To minister is to stand before the LORD and serve, and the word belongs first to Aaron and the Levites. The bells were upon Aaron to minister when he went in unto the holy place, and the LORD separated the tribe of Levi to stand before the LORD to minister unto Him. The child Samuel did minister unto the LORD before Eli the priest, and Moses rose up, and his minister Joshua went up into the mount of God.

Theological Significance

I believe ministry is standing before the LORD, not standing before men, because Aaron's bells were heard by God first. Levi was separated to serve, Samuel ministered as a child, and Joshua ministered by walking up the mountain with Moses. My position is that the best ministry is the one no crowd sees, done before the LORD alone.
