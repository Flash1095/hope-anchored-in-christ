bᵉrôwsh

a cypress (?) tree; hence, a lance or a musical instrument (as made of that wood)

Biblical Usage

berosh is the word for a fir tree, and it appears 20 times across the Bible. Fir wood made instruments for worship, Hiram sent fir timber for Solomon's temple, and the stork makes her house in the fir trees.

Theological Significance

I believe even the fir trees of Lebanon were made for God's glory, and the temple's beauty was meant to point to Him. That is why I keep coming back to it: berosh is not a word to rush past.
