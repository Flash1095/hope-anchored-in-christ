nâthîyb

a (beaten) track

Biblical Usage

The word of God is a lamp to the feet and a light to the path, and the father warns his son not to walk in the way with sinners. The LORD promises to lead the blind in paths they have not known, and Job says there is a path no bird of prey knows.

Theological Significance

I believe God's word is the path itself, not just a map of it: it lights the next step, it keeps my feet from the sinner's road, and it leads where no eye has gone before. The path no bird knows is still a path God knows, and His word is how I find it.
