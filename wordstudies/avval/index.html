ʻavvâl

evil (morally)

Biblical Usage

Avval is the unjust man — morally evil, the opposite of upright. Job says he broke the jaws of the wicked and plucked the spoil from his teeth; he asks whether destruction is not to the wicked; Bildad says such is the dwelling of him that knoweth not God; and Zephaniah says the unjust knows no shame, morning by morning, while the just Lord brings his judgment to light.

Theological Significance

I believe this word draws the line plainly: there are the just and the unjust, and God is not confused about which is which. Job's life shows me the unjust do not win in the end, even when they seem to win for a season.
