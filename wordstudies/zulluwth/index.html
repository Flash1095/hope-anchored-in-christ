zullûwth

vileness, worthlessness

Biblical Usage

Zulluwth means vileness or worthlessness, and it appears once, in Psalm 12: the wicked walk on every side 'when the vilest men are exalted.' David watches worthless men lifted up and cries out for the LORD to keep the godly. The word is the psalm's diagnosis of a world turned upside down.

Theological Significance

I believe David's cry is mine too: when vileness is exalted, the LORD still keeps His words, pure as silver tried in a furnace.
