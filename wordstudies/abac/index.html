ʼâbaç

to fodder

Biblical Usage

This verb for fattening or feeding up livestock shows up only twice, both times as a participle. Solomon's table is supplied with 'fatted fowl', and the proverb contrasts a dinner of herbs where love is with a 'stalled' — fattened — ox served with hatred.

Theological Significance

It is a kitchen word, not a theology word, and I will not dress it up as one — though the proverb does use it to teach me that God measures a meal by the hearts at it, not by what is on it.
