hēgéomai

middle voice of a (presumed) strengthened form of G71; to lead, i.e. command (with official authority); figuratively, to deem, i.e. consider:--account, (be) chief, count, esteem, governor, judge, have the rule over, suppose, think.

Biblical Usage

Hegeomai means to lead, to count, to esteem, and it covers both ruling and reckoning. In Hebrews 13:7 the church is to remember them which have the rule over them, in Hebrews 13:17 they are to obey them that have the rule, in Philippians 2:3 each is to esteem others better than themselves, and in James 1:2 trials are to be counted all joy.

Theological Significance

I believe the word joins two things I would rather keep apart: leading and counting. The leaders are to be remembered and obeyed, the brother is to be esteemed above me, and the trial is to be counted joy. All three are the same verb, and all three ask me to reckon differently than I want to.
