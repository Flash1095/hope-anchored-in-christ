Yôwʼâchâz

Joachaz, the name of two Israelites

Biblical Usage

Jehoahaz is named 4 times in the Bible, and the passages here are 2 Kings 14:1; 2 Chronicles 34:8; 2 Chronicles 36:2. In 2 Kings 14:1 we read, "In the second year of Joash son of Jehoahaz king of Israel reigned Amaziah the son of Joash king of Judah." The same name belongs to more than one man, and 2 Chronicles 36:2 names another of them.

Theological Significance

Jehoahaz is never just a name, and I believe a real life stands behind it. God kept the name, and that tells me he kept the person.
