apodídōmi

Word
ἀποδίδωμι
Language
Greek
Transliteration
apodídōmi
Strongs Number
G591
Definition
from G575 and G1325; to give away, i.e. up, over, back, etc. (in various applications):--deliver (again), give (again), (re-)pay(-ment be made), perform, recompense, render, requite, restore, reward, sell, yield.
