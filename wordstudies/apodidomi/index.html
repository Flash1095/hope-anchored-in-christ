apodídōmi

from G575 and G1325; to give away, i.e. up, over, back, etc. (in various applications):--deliver (again), give (again), (re-)pay(-ment be made), perform, recompense, render, requite, restore, reward, sell, yield.

Biblical Usage

To give back, to pay over, to render what is due. The Father who sees in secret shall reward openly in Matthew 6:4, and the Son of man shall reward every man according to his works in Matthew 16:27; Revelation 22:12 says the same at the very end — behold, I come quickly, and my reward is with me. Paul turns the word the other way in Romans 12:17: recompense to no man evil for evil.

Theological Significance

I hold that this word keeps two accounts straight. God repays — openly, surely, according to works — and that is why I must not. If I take repayment into my own hands, I am collecting a debt that is not mine to collect. My part is to provide things honest in the sight of all men and leave the rendering to Him.
