aitéō

of uncertain derivation; to ask (in genitive case):--ask, beg, call for, crave, desire, require. Compare G4441.

Biblical Usage

The great asking word of the New Testament. Ask, and it shall be given you — Jesus repeats it in the Sermon on the Mount, ties it to believing prayer, promises to do whatever is asked in His name, and James says the one who lacks wisdom should ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally. It is the simplest word in the Bible's vocabulary of prayer, and the most used.

Theological Significance

I believe God meant asking to be simple. He is not a miser who must be coaxed; He gives liberally, and the only condition James names is faith that does not waver. The door is open because the Father likes to be asked.
