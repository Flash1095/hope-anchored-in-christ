çâphar

properly, to score with a mark as a tally or record, i.e. (by implication) to inscribe, and also to enumerate; intensively, to recount, i.e. celebrate

Biblical Usage

Çâphar means to score a mark, to inscribe, to count — and then, by the same word, to recount and celebrate. It is the word for keeping the record and for telling the story out of it. The Bible uses it for numbering the people, for writing things down, and for declaring what God has done.

Theological Significance

I believe God keeps the record and tells the story, and çâphar holds both together. What He counts, He remembers; and what He remembers, He declares — that is why I trust His promises.
