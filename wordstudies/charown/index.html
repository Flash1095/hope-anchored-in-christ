chârôwn

a burning of anger

Biblical Usage

Charown is the burning of anger, and it is almost always God's. Samuel tells Saul the LORD has done this to him because he did not execute God's fierce wrath upon Amalek. Hezekiah makes a covenant that God's fierce wrath may turn away, Moses is told to hang the heads of the people so the fierce anger of the LORD may turn away, and the law promises that when the cursed thing is removed, the LORD will turn from the fierceness of His anger.

Theological Significance

I believe the burning anger of God is not like man's temper: it has a cause, a cure, and an end. It burns against Amalek and against the cursed thing, and it turns away when His people obey. I hold that the fierceness of His anger is the other side of His mercy, and both are real.
