synklēronómos

from G4862 and G2818; a co-heir, i.e. (by analogy) participant in common:--fellow (joint)-heir, heir together, heir with.

Biblical Usage

If we are children, then we are heirs of God and joint heirs with Christ, if we suffer with Him, that we may also be glorified together, and the Gentiles are fellow heirs, and of the same body, and partakers of His promise in Christ by the gospel. Abraham sojourned in the land of promise as in a strange country, dwelling in tabernacles with Isaac and Jacob, the heirs with him of the same promise, and husbands are to dwell with their wives as being heirs together of the grace of life. The inheritance is shared because the family is shared, and no heir holds it alone.

Theological Significance

I believe I am a joint heir with Christ, not a tenant of His house but a child of it, and I hold that the sharing is the point: the inheritance is sweeter because it is ours together. And I take the husband's charge seriously, because heirs together of the grace of life must live like it, or their prayers are hindered.
