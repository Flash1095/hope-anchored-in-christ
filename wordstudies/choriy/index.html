chŏrîy

a burning (i.e. intense) anger

Biblical Usage

Choriy is burning anger, the fierce heat of a wrath that cannot be held back, and it appears in the stories of Jonathan, Moses and the kings. Jonathan rises from the table in fierce anger at his father's shame, the army returns in great anger, Moses goes out from Pharaoh in great anger, and the Lord asks why His anger is kindled against Israel.

Theological Significance

I believe anger in these verses is a fire that must be watched, because it can be righteous or ruinous. I hold that Moses' anger and Jonathan's anger both show a heart that hates what God hates, and that is the only safe anger.
