astḗr

probably from the base of G4766; a star (as strown over the sky), literally or figuratively:--star.

Biblical Usage

The star led the wise men from the east to Bethlehem, and when they saw it again they rejoiced with exceeding great joy. John sees Christ as the bright and morning star, and Paul says one star differs from another star in glory.

Theological Significance

I believe the star over Bethlehem and the star of the resurrection are the same light, Christ Himself guiding His people home. The stars differ in glory, and I hold that the glory of the risen body will outshine them all.
