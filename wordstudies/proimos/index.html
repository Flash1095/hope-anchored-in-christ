prṓïmos

from G4404; dawning, i.e. (by analogy) autumnal (showering, the first of the rainy season):--early.

Biblical Usage

Proimos is the early rain, the autumnal shower, and James uses it once. The husbandman waits for the precious fruit of the earth, and has long patience for it, until he receive the early and latter rain. The spelling in the Greek text differs by a letter from the dictionary headword, but the word is the same early rain.

Theological Significance

I believe patience is the farmer's virtue and the Christian's too, because the harvest comes in God's season, not mine.
