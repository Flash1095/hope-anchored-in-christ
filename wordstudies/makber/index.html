makbêr

a grate

Biblical Usage

Makber is the grate, the network of brass under the altar, and all of its appearances are in Exodus. Exodus 27:4 commands a grate of network of brass with four brasen rings, and Exodus 38:4 says it was made and set under the compass of the altar.

Theological Significance

I hold that the grate did unseen work: it held the fire up so the sacrifice could burn, and nobody praised it for it.
