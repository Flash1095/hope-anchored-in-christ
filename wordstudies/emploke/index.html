emplokḗ

from G1707; elaborate braiding of the hair:--plaiting.

Biblical Usage

Peter lists the plaiting of hair among the outward adornings in 1 Peter 3:3 — braided hair, gold, and fine apparel — and says a wife's beauty should not be that outward adorning.

Theological Significance

I believe Peter is not banning braids but reordering beauty — the hidden person of the heart outlasts every hairstyle.
