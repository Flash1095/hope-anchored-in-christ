homiléō

from G3658; to be in company with, i.e. (by implication) to converse:--commune, talk.

Biblical Usage

homiléō is the word for commune, talk. I meet it 4 times across the Bible, and Luke is a good place to see it plainly. It says what it says, and I take it at face value.

Theological Significance

I believe homiléō matters because talking together is how the Lord draws near — He met the two on the road while they talked. What I take from it is this — the plain sense is the safe sense.
