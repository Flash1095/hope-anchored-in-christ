Yishshîyâh

Word
יִשִּׁיָּה
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
Yishshîyâh
Strongs Number
H3449
Definition
Jishshijah, the name of five Israelites
Part Of Speech
proper noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
Ishiah or Ishijah or Jesiah = "Jehovah will lend"; 1) a Korhite, one of David's mighty warriors who joined him at Ziklag; 2) the 5th of 5 sons of Izrahiah and one of the heads of the tribe of Issachar in the time of David; 3) a Levite, the 2nd son of Uzziel and grandson of Kohath; 4) another Levite, son of Rehabiah; 5) a descendant of Harim who had a foreign wife
