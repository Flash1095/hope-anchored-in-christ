Parpar

Parpar, a river of Syria

Biblical Usage

Parpar is a river of Syria, one of the two rivers of Damascus. Naaman the Syrian says in 2 Kings 5:12, Are not Abana and Pharpar, rivers of Damascus, better than all the waters of Israel? May I not wash in them, and be clean?

Theological Significance

I believe Naaman's rivers were better water and the wrong answer, because God had named a muddy river in Israel, and healing was in the obedience, not the water.
