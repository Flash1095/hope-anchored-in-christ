dióti

from G1223 and G3754; on the very account that, or inasmuch as:--because (that), for, therefore.

Biblical Usage

Because, for, inasmuch as, the word that gives the reason underneath the command. Paul says he is not fit to be called an apostle because he persecuted the church, and Peter quotes the call to be holy because God is holy. David's psalm is quoted because God would not let His Holy One see corruption.

Theological Significance

I believe every command in Scripture stands on a because, and the because is always who God is or what He has done. Holiness is not bare duty; it is the shape of a life that belongs to a holy God.
