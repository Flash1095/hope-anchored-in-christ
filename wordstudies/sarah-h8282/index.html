sârâh

a mistress, i.e. female noble

Biblical Usage

Sarah is a lady of rank, a princess — the word for a woman who stands high among her people. Jerusalem was great among the nations and princess among the provinces before she sat solitary as a widow, and the ladies of Persia and Media carried the same rank at the king's court.

Theological Significance

What strikes me is that rank does not protect a city or a court from falling, and the princess among the provinces can end up tributary.
