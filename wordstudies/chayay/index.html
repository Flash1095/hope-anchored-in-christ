châyay

to live; causatively to revive

Biblical Usage

To live is the word at the edges of life and death. Pharaoh orders the sons killed and the daughters left to live, God promises that the bitten Israelite who looks on the serpent shall live, Adam is kept from the tree of life lest he live for ever, and the manslayer flees to a city that he might live.

Theological Significance

I believe life is God's to give and to guard: He spares the daughters, He heals the bitten, He bars the tree, and He shelters the slayer. Every one of these verses turns on who decides who lives.
