kybernḗtēs

from the same as G2941; helmsman, i.e. (by implication) captain:--(ship) master.

Biblical Usage

The shipmaster is the captain, the man who steers, and he appears in two storms. In Acts 27:11 the centurion believed the master and the owner of the ship more than Paul's warning, and in Revelation 18:17 every shipmaster stands afar off weeping when Babylon's riches come to nothing in one hour.

Theological Significance

I believe the two shipmasters preach one sermon: the man who trusted the captain over the apostle lost the ship, and the captains who trusted their cargo lost everything in an hour.
