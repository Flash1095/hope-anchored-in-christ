zûwaʻ

to shake (with fear)

Biblical Usage

Zuwa is the Aramaic word for trembling — before Nebuchadnezzar, all peoples trembled and feared, because the king's word was life or death. Darius turns it around in his decree: men must tremble and fear before the God of Daniel, because He is the living God who endures for ever. The same trembling belongs to God, not to kings.

Theological Significance

I believe the trembling Nebuchadnezzar demanded was owed to God all along, and Darius finally said so.
