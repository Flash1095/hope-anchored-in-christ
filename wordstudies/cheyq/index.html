chêyq

the bosom (literally or figuratively)

Biblical Usage

Cheyq is the bosom, the fold of the garment close to the heart, and Scripture uses it for tenderness and for danger together. A mother lays the living child in her bosom, the poor man's lamb lies in his bosom and eats of his morsel, Naomi lays the child in her bosom, and the proverb asks whether a man can take fire in his bosom and not be burned.

Theological Significance

I believe the bosom is where love keeps what it cherishes, and the proverb is right that fire kept there will burn. I hold that what I hold closest to my heart will either warm me or wound me, and I must choose what I carry there.
