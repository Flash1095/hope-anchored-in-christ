zoe

Word
ζωή
Language
Greek
Transliteration
zoe
Strongs Number
G2222
Definition
Life — full, eternal, God-given life, as distinct from mere biological existence; the life Jesus came to give abundantly.
Part Of Speech
noun
Key Passages
John 10:10; John 14:6; John 17:3; 1 John 5:11-12
Biblical Usage
John uses zoe nearly fifty times: eternal life is knowing God, having the Son, and drinking living water now — not only living forever but truly living, starting today.
Theological Significance
Zoe means Christianity is not mere survival or fire insurance. Christ gives life with a capital L — abundant now and unending forever.
