epiginṓskō

Word
ἐπιγινώσκω
Language
Greek
Transliteration
epiginṓskō
Strongs Number
G1921
Definition
from G1909 and G1097; to know upon some mark, i.e. recognize; by implication, to become fully acquainted with, to acknowledge:--(ac-, have, take)know(-ledge, well), perceive.
