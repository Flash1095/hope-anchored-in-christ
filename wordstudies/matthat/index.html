Matthát

probably a shortened form of G3161; Matthat (i.e. Mattithjah), the name of two Israelites:--Mathat.

Biblical Usage

Matthat appears twice in Luke's genealogy of Jesus, once as the son of Levi and once as the son of Jorim. Both stand on the line that runs back to David.

Theological Significance

I believe a name repeated across the generations is a thread God never dropped: two men named Matthat, centuries apart, both carrying the line toward the Christ.
