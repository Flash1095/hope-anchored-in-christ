çêther

a cover (in a good or a bad, a literal or a figurative sense)

Biblical Usage

Sether is a covering, a hiding place, a secret — and it cuts both ways. Moses warns of the brother or friend who entices you secretly, Ehud claims a secret errand for the king, Jonathan warns David in secret that Saul means to kill him, and Nathan tells David his sin was done secretly while God's judgment would be done in the open.

Theological Significance

I believe the secret is the test of the heart: what we hide, God sees, and what He hides us in, no enemy finds. I hold that His covering is the only secret place that is safe.
