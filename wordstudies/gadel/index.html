gâdêl

large (literally or figuratively)

Biblical Usage

Gadel means large, and it describes growth in body and in standing. The child Samuel grew on in 1 Samuel 2:26, in favour with the LORD and with men, and the man Isaac waxed great until he became very great in Genesis 26:13. Jehoshaphat waxed great exceedingly in 2 Chronicles 17:12, and the same word can turn sour: Jerusalem's neighbours are called great of flesh in Ezekiel 16:26.

Theological Significance

I believe growth is good when it grows toward God. Samuel grew in favour, Isaac grew in blessing, and Jehoshaphat grew in strength — but greatness of flesh alone is no blessing. I hold that the size that matters is the size of a life before God.
