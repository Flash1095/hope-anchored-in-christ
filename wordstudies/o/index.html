Ō

the last letter of the Greek alphabet, i.e. (figuratively) the finality:--Omega.

Biblical Usage

Omega is the last letter of the Greek alphabet, and the Lord takes it as His name. In Revelation 1:8, 21:6, and 22:13 He says, I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the ending, and each time the claim is tied to who He is: the Almighty, the one who finishes what He starts, the first and the last.

Theological Significance

I believe the end of the alphabet belongs to Christ because the end of the story does, and the God who began it is the one who will finish it.
