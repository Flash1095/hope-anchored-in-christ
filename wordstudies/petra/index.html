pétra

feminine of the same as G4074; a (mass of) rock (literally or figuratively):--rock.

Biblical Usage

Petra is a mass of rock, a cliff or ledge, and Jesus builds His whole church on it. He tells Peter that upon this rock He will build His church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it, and He pictures the wise man building his house upon a rock that the storm cannot move. Paul says the rock that followed Israel in the wilderness was Christ, and yet the same rock is a stone of stumbling to those who do not believe.

Theological Significance

I believe the rock is Christ Himself: the foundation no storm moves and the stone no unbeliever can step around. A house on the rock stands, a people drinking from the rock lives, and the church built on the rock cannot fall. My position is that every other foundation is sand, and only the rock that is Christ will hold when the rain descends and the winds blow.
