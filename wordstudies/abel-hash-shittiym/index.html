ʼÂbêl hash-Shiṭṭîym

Abel hash-Shittim, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Abel-shittim — the meadow of the acacias — is named once, in Numbers 33:49, as the last camp of Israel before the Jordan: 'they pitched by Jordan, from Beth-jesimoth even unto Abel-shittim in the plains of Moab.' It is the camp where the sin of Peor happens and where Moses speaks his farewell.

Theological Significance

The last camp before the crossing holds both sin and promise: Peor's shame and the plains of Moab where Moses preaches Deuteronomy. I believe that is honest about the Christian life — the ground before the Jordan is where we fail and where we hear God's word most clearly, side by side.
