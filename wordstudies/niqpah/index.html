niqpâh

probably a rope (as encircling)

Biblical Usage

A rope is the girdle that becomes a rent. Isaiah's judgment on the daughters of Zion takes away their finery piece by piece, and instead of a girdle there shall be a rent — the encircling cord torn, the beauty unbound.

Theological Significance

I hold that the torn girdle is the picture of a people stripped: what once adorned them is taken, and sackcloth is girded instead.
