kᵉlal

to complete

Biblical Usage

Kelal is the Aramaic word to complete, to finish: Ezra 4:12 accuses the Jews of building the rebellious city and joining the foundations. Ezra 4:13 warns that if the city be builded, they will not pay toll, tribute, and custom. Ezra 4:16 certifies that if the city be builded again, the king shall have no portion on this side the river. Ezra 5:3 has Tatnai asking, who hath commanded you to build this house, and to make up this wall?

Theological Significance

I believe finishing is the word the enemies feared: a finished wall meant a finished people, and a finished city meant lost revenue. I hold that God finishes what he starts, even when letters from the king say to stop.
