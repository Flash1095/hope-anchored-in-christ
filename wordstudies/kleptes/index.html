kléptēs

from G2813; a stealer (literally or figuratively):--thief. Compare G3027.

Biblical Usage

Kleptes is a thief. Matthew 6:19 warns against treasures where thieves break through and steal, Luke 12:33 promises a treasure in the heavens where no thief approacheth, and John 10:1 calls the man who climbeth up some other way into the sheepfold a thief and a robber. 1 Corinthians 6:10 says thieves shall not inherit the kingdom of God, 1 Thessalonians 5:2 says the day of the Lord so cometh as a thief in the night, and 1 Peter 4:15 warns, let none of you suffer as a thief.

Theological Significance

I believe the thief is the Bible's picture of the wrong way in: he breaks through, he climbs over, he takes what is not his. I hold that the day of the Lord comes as a thief because no one is watching for it, and the only safe treasure is the one no thief can approach.
