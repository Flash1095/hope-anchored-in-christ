dâlaq

to flame (literally or figuratively)

Biblical Usage

To flame is to burn hot, whether with fire or with pursuit or with anger. The word describes Israel pursuing the Philistines after the battle, the drunkard rising early to follow strong drink, and the wicked whose eyes flame with malice. Fire is never neutral in Scripture: it purifies or it consumes.

Theological Significance

I believe the word warns us about what burns in us. A fire for God purifies, but a fire for drink or for revenge only consumes the one who carries it.
