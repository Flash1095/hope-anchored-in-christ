pepoíthēsis

from the perfect of the alternate of G3958; reliance:--confidence, trust.

Biblical Usage

Pepoithesis is confidence, trust, reliance. Paul writes of the confidence that made him plan a second visit to Corinth, and says such trust we have through Christ toward God. In Christ we have boldness and access with confidence by the faith of Him.

Theological Significance

I hold that confidence is not self-trust but Christ-trust: Paul's boldness came through Christ to God, not from himself. I hold that access with confidence is the Christian's right — not to barge in, but to come boldly because Christ has opened the way. My confidence is not in my record but in His, and that is why it holds.
