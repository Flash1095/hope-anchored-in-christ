apántēsis

from G528; a (friendly) encounter:--meet.

Biblical Usage

A meeting, and a joyful one — the cry at midnight that sends the virgins out to meet the bridegroom, the believers from Rome coming as far as Appii Forum to meet Paul, and the promise that we will meet the Lord in the air. In each case someone goes out to welcome the one who is coming.

Theological Significance

I hold that this word turns waiting into welcome. We are not bracing for a stranger; we are going out to meet the Lord we already know, and I believe that meeting is the hope that steadies every hard day now.
