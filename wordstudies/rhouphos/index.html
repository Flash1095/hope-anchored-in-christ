Rhoûphos

of Latin origin; red; Rufus, a Christian:--Rufus.

Biblical Usage

Simon of Cyrene, the father of Alexander and Rufus, was compelled to bear the cross, and Paul greets Rufus chosen in the Lord and his mother and mine in the church at Rome. Paul calls his mother my mother too, as if the family of the cross had become his own.

Theological Significance

My position is that the Rufus of the cross and the Rufus of the church may be one man, and if so, the cross bore fruit in his own house.
