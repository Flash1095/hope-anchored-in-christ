Chôçâh

Chosah, an Israelite; also a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Hosah is both a man and a place, a gatekeeper of the temple and a town on the border of Asher. The Levite Hosah stands with Obed-edom at the doors of the house of God, his sons keep the gates in David's ordering, and the town of Hosah marks the coast where the border of Asher turns to the sea.

Theological Significance

I believe a gatekeeper's name is worth keeping, because the doors of God's house are kept by faithful men. I hold that Hosah's two uses show how one name can serve both the temple and the land.
