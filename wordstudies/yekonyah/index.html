Yᵉkonyâh

Jekonjah, a Jewish king

Biblical Usage

Jekonjah is Jeconiah, a Jewish king, the son of Jehoiakim. In 1 Chronicles 3:16, Jeconiah his son, Zedekiah his son. The fuller story is told on the Jeconiah profile in Biblical People.

Theological Significance

My position is that Jeconiah belongs in the story because God put the name there, and I trust the placing.
