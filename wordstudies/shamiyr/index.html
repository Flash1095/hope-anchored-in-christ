shâmîyr

a thorn; also (from its keenness for scratching) a gem, probably the diamond

Biblical Usage

A thorn and a diamond share this word, because both are sharp enough to cut. God made Ezekiel's forehead as an adamant harder than flint, and Jeremiah says the sin of Judah is written with a pen of iron and with the point of a diamond. Isaiah uses the other side of the word, where the vineyard God lays waste shall come up briers and thorns.

Theological Significance

I hold that a heart can be diamond-hard in two directions, unbreakable for God or unbreakable against Him.
