shâmîyr

Word
שָׁמִיר
Language
Hebrew
Transliteration
shâmîyr
Strongs Number
H8068
Definition
a thorn; also (from its keenness for scratching) a gem, probably the diamond
Part Of Speech
noun (masculine)
Biblical Usage
1) thorn(s), adamant, flint; 1a) thorns, thorn-bushes; 1b) adamant (as sharp); 1c) a sharp stone, flint; 1c1) perhaps a diamond
