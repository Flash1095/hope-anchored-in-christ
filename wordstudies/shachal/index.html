shachal

a lion (from his characteristic roar)

Biblical Usage

Shachal is the lion in his fierceness, the roaring beast that tears and carries off. God takes the picture for Himself, saying He will be to Ephraim as a lion and as a young lion to Judah, tearing and going away with none to rescue. Job felt that hunting, crying that God hunted him as a fierce lion, yet the psalm promises the one who dwells in the secret place that he shall tread upon the lion and the adder.

Theological Significance

I believe the lion shows both faces of God at once, the judge who tears and the shelter who saves from tearing. The same strength that hunts the unrepentant is the strength that tramples the lion under the feet of the trusting.
