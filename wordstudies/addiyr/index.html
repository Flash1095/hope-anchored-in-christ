Language
Hebrew
Transliteration

Definition
wide or (generally) large; figuratively, powerful
Part Of Speech
adjective
Biblical Usage
1) great, majestic; 1a) of waters of sea; 1b) of a tree; 1c) of kings, nations, gods; 2) great one, majestic one; 2a) of nobles, chieftains, servants
