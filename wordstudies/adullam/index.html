ʻĂdullâm

Adullam, a place in Palestine

Biblical Usage

Adullam was a Canaanite city taken by Joshua, a town in the lowland of Judah — and, most famously, the cave where David hid when he fled from Saul. 'David therefore departed thence, and escaped to the cave Adullam,' and everyone in distress, in debt, and discontented gathered to him there; the place itself is covered in the Biblical Places collection.

Theological Significance

I believe the cave of Adullam is one of the kindest pictures in the Bible: a fugitive king in a cave, and the broken and indebted gathering to him — and God made those outcasts into mighty men. If you are in distress, this is where the story says you belong: with David, in the cave, waiting on God.
