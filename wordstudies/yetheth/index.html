Jetheth, an Edomite

Biblical Usage

Jetheth is an Edomite, a chief of Edom. In Genesis 36:40, duke Alvah, duke Jetheth. The fuller story is told on the Jetheth profile in Biblical People.

Theological Significance

My position is that Jetheth belongs in the story because God put the name there, and I trust the placing.
