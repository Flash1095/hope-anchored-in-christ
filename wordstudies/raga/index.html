râgaʻ

properly, to toss violently and suddenly (the sea with waves, the skin with boils); figuratively (in a favorable manner) to settle, i.e. quiet; specifically, to wink (from the motion of the eye-lids)

Biblical Usage

Raga' is to toss violently and suddenly — the sea with waves, the skin with boils — and, by a second sense, to settle and be quiet, and to wink. Jeremiah says the LORD 'divideth the sea when the waves thereof roar'; Isaiah says, 'I am the LORD thy God, that divided the sea, whose waves roared'; and Job says He stirreth up the sea by His power.

Theological Significance

My position is that the sea's tossing is God's doing and God's calming: He stirs it, and He stills it, and the same hand does both.
