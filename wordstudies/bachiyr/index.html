bâchîyr

select

Biblical Usage

Bachiyr means chosen, select — the elect. God says, I have chosen thee in the furnace of affliction; Isaiah calls Israel my chosen, and my servant whom I have chosen; David was told to take the chosen men of Israel to fetch the ark; and the psalm says God remembered his covenant with Abraham his servant, and brought forth his people with joy, and his chosen with gladness.

Theological Significance

I believe being chosen is not a prize for being good — God chose Israel in the furnace of affliction, and he chose me the same way, in the middle of my need. That is the grace I hold on to.
