Scripture
Genesis 31:1-55; Genesis 32:1-32; Genesis 33:1-20; Genesis 35:1-29
Summary
After twenty years with Laban, Jacob left Haran with his wives, children, and flocks. He met angels at Mahanaim, wrestled with God at Penuel and was named Israel, made peace with Esau, built booths at Succoth, camped at Shechem, worshipped at Bethel, and finally reached his father Isaac at Hebron. The stages follow Genesis's own place names in order.
Significance
The return journey is Jacob's transformation in motion: the schemer becomes Israel through wrestling, reconciliation, and worship.
