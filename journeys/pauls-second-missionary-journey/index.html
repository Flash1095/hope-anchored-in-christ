Paul's Second Missionary Journey

After the Jerusalem council, Paul and Silas revisited the churches of the first journey, were joined by Timothy, were forbidden to preach in Asia and Bithynia, and crossed from Troas to Macedonia after the vision of the Macedonian man. They planted churches at Philippi, Thessalonica, and Berea, preached at Athens, spent eighteen months at Corinth, touched at Ephesus and Caesarea, and returned to Antioch. The stages follow Acts' order; the overland stretches between named towns are not traced town by town.

Significance

The second journey carries the gospel into Europe: Philippi's jailer, Berea's examiners, Athens's philosophers, and Corinth's church.
