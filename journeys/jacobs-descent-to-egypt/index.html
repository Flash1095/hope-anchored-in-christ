Scripture
Genesis 46:1-34; Genesis 47:1-12
Summary
At God's word Jacob took his whole household down to Egypt, stopping at Beersheba to offer sacrifices, where God assured him in a vision. Joseph met him in Goshen, and Pharaoh settled the family in the land of Goshen. The text names Beersheba as the one stop on the way.
Significance
Israel enters Egypt as a family of seventy and will leave as a nation: descent and deliverance are one story.
