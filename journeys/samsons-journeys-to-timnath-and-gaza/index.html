Stages
Zorah; Timnath; Gaza
Scripture
Judges 14:1-20; Judges 16:1-31
Summary
Samson went down from Zorah to Timnath to see a Philistine woman, and later went to Gaza, where he tore the city gates from their posts and carried them toward Hebron. The two trips are combined here because both trace Samson's repeated crossings into Philistine country; the text does not date or fully order them against each other.
Significance
Samson's roads run toward entanglement: strength given for deliverance is spent on personal desire until it is lost and restored.
