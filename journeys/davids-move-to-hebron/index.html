From Place

To Place

Stages
Ziklag; Hebron
Scripture
2 Samuel 2:1-11
Summary
After Saul's death David asked God where to go and was told to go up to Hebron. He moved there with his two wives and his men with their households, and the men of Judah anointed him king over Judah at Hebron. The text names no stops between Ziklag and Hebron.
Significance
Hebron begins David's kingship in Judah seven years before all Israel crowns him in Jerusalem.
