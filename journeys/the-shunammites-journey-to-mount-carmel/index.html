The Shunammite's Journey to Mount Carmel

Journey
The Shunammite's Journey to Mount Carmel
From Place
Shunem
To Place
Mount Carmel
Stages
Shunem; Mount Carmel
Scripture
2 Kings 4:8-37
Summary
When her son died suddenly, the Shunammite woman saddled a donkey and rode to Elisha on Mount Carmel, refusing to explain herself to Gehazi and holding fast to Elisha until he came back with her and raised the boy. The text names no stops on the ride. The woman has no people profile, so Travelers is left blank rather than inventing one.
Significance
A mother's urgent ride becomes a picture of stubborn faith: she will not settle for a staff sent ahead; she wants the prophet himself.
