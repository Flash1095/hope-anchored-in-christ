Scripture

Summary
After Stephen's death a great persecution scattered the Jerusalem church through Judea and Samaria, and those who were scattered preached the word wherever they went, reaching Phoenicia (named here; Phoenicia has no place entry, so it is not a stage), Cyprus, and Antioch. Philip's work in Samaria belongs to this scattering. The stages give the named destinations in Acts' order.
Significance
Persecution becomes propagation: the scattering plants the church beyond Jerusalem for the first time.
