Stages
Alexandria; Ephesus; Achaia
Scripture
Acts 18:24-28; Acts 19:1
Summary
Apollos, an eloquent Alexandrian mighty in the Scriptures, came to Ephesus, where Priscilla and Aquila explained the way of God more accurately to him. Encouraged by the brothers, he crossed into Achaia, where he vigorously refuted opponents and helped believers. The text names no stops between these points.
Significance
Apollos's road shows the value of being teachable: eloquence corrected becomes a powerful ministry in Achaia.
