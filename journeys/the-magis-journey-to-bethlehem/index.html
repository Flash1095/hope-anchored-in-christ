The Magi's Journey to Bethlehem

Wise men from the east came to Jerusalem asking where the King of the Jews was born, were directed by Herod's scribes to Bethlehem, and followed the star to the house where the child was. Warned in a dream, they returned to their own country by another way. Matthew does not name their homeland, so no origin stage is given; the Magi have no people profiles, so Travelers is left blank.

Significance

The first Gentiles to seek Jesus come by starlight and Scripture together, and Herod's court shows the opposite response.
