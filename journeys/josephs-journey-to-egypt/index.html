Stages
Hebron; Shechem; Dothan; Egypt
Scripture
Genesis 37:1-36
Summary
Jacob sent Joseph from Hebron to check on his brothers grazing near Shechem; a man directed him on to Dothan, where his brothers stripped him, threw him in a pit, and sold him to Ishmaelite traders. The caravan carried him down into Egypt, where he was sold to Potiphar. The text names no Egyptian town of arrival, so Egypt itself is the final stage.
Significance
Joseph's descent begins the chain by which God will later preserve the whole family in famine.
