The Levite's Journey to Gibeah

Journey
The Levite's Journey to Gibeah
From Place
Bethlehem
To Place
Gibeah
Stages
Bethlehem; Gibeah
Scripture
Judges 19:1-30
Summary
A Levite living in the hill country of Ephraim traveled to Bethlehem to bring back his concubine, then set out for home late in the day and stopped for the night at Gibeah of Benjamin, where the night ended in outrage and murder. The text names no other stops. This entry records the route factually and briefly; it does not retell the crime in detail.
Significance
The journey is a grim marker of the judges' refrain: in those days there was no king, and everyone did what was right in his own eyes.
