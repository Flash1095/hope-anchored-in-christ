Summary
Jonah first fled toward Tarshish, boarding a ship at Joppa; after the storm and the great fish, God spoke a second time, and Jonah went to Nineveh and preached that the city would be overthrown in forty days. Nineveh repented. Tarshish has no place entry and its location is debated, so it is named here as the intended flight, not listed as a stage; the stages give the journey Jonah actually completed.
Significance
Jonah's road runs from refusal to reluctant obedience, and exposes a prophet who wants mercy for himself but not for his enemies.
