Ruth 1:1-22
Summary
During a famine in Judah, Elimelech took Naomi and their two sons from Bethlehem to live in Moab. Elimelech and both sons died there, and Naomi returned to Bethlehem years later with her daughter-in-law Ruth. The text names no stops on either leg of the journey.
Significance
A famine move meant to save a family empties it instead, and sets up Ruth's loyalty and the road back to Bethlehem.
