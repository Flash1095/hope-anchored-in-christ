Stages
Rome; Ephesus
Scripture
Ephesians 6:21-22; Colossians 4:7-9; 2 Timothy 4:12
Summary
Tychicus, Paul's beloved brother and faithful minister, was sent to carry Paul's letters and news to the churches, including Ephesus, and later to Ephesus again from Rome. The stages give the best-attested endpoints; his roads to Colossae and other churches are named in the letters without itineraries, so they are not listed as stages.
Significance
Tychicus is the letter-carrier of the New Testament: without his roads, the epistles do not reach their churches.
