Barnabas and Mark's Journey to Cyprus

Journey
Barnabas and Mark's Journey to Cyprus
Travelers
Barnabas; John Mark
From Place
Antioch
To Place
Cyprus
Stages
Antioch; Cyprus
Scripture
Acts 15:36-41
Summary
After parting from Paul over John Mark, Barnabas took Mark and sailed to Cyprus, his homeland, to continue the work there. Acts names Cyprus as the destination but no Cypriot town on this trip, so only the island is given as a stage.
Significance
A sharp disagreement becomes a second mission: Barnabas's encouragement gives Mark a road back into ministry.
