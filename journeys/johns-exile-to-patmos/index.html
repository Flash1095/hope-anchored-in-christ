John's Exile to Patmos

Stages
Ephesus; Patmos
Scripture
Revelation 1:1-20
Summary
John writes that he was on the island called Patmos for the word of God and the testimony of Jesus when he received the Revelation. Tradition places his exile there from Ephesus under Domitian, but Revelation itself names only Patmos; the Ephesus starting point is traditional, not asserted, and is given here as the customary departure point with that caveat. This was an exile, not a voluntary journey.
Significance
Patmos turns banishment into vision: the exiled apostle receives the book that closes the canon.
