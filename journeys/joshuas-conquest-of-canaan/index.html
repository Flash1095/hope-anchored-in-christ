Summary
After Moses's death Joshua led Israel from Shittim across the Jordan to Gilgal, took Jericho and Ai, secured Gibeon's surrender, defeated the southern coalition, and then struck the northern coalition as far as Hazor. The stages follow Joshua's campaign order in broad outline; the book names many more towns taken in each campaign, and this entry does not list every one.
Significance
The conquest fulfills the land promise while showing that victory depends on obedience, not numbers.
