Scripture
Genesis 11:31-32
Summary
Terah took his son Abram, his grandson Lot, and his daughter-in-law Sarai out of Ur of the Chaldees to go to Canaan, but the family stopped and settled in Haran, where Terah died. This journey sets up Abraham's later call: the family left Ur but did not reach Canaan in Terah's lifetime.
Significance
The journey shows a partial obedience that God then completes through Abraham's call from Haran.
