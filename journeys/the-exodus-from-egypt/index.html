The Exodus from Egypt

Journey

Group
Israelites

Red Sea
Stages
Rameses; Succoth; Etham; Migdol; Pi-hahiroth; Red Sea
Scripture
Exodus 12:31-51; Exodus 13:17-22; Exodus 14:1-31
Summary
After the Passover, Israel left Rameses, camped at Succoth and Etham, then turned back to encamp before Pi-hahiroth between Migdol and the sea. Pharaoh's army pursued them, and God parted the sea so Israel crossed on dry ground while the Egyptians drowned. Debate note: scholars debate the exact locations of several of these stations and the sea crossing itself; the stages given here are the names Exodus uses, in Exodus's order, without fixing modern identifications.
Significance
The exodus is Israel's defining deliverance and the pattern of redemption the rest of Scripture keeps retelling.
