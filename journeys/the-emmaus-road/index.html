Stages
Jerusalem; Emmaus
Scripture
Luke 24:13-35
Summary
On the day of the resurrection, two disciples walked from Jerusalem to Emmaus, about seven miles away, discussing all that had happened. A stranger joined them, opened the Scriptures, and was recognized in the breaking of bread, and they hurried back to Jerusalem the same hour. The other disciple is unnamed in Luke, so only Cleopas is listed as a traveler.
Significance
The Emmaus road is how the risen Jesus is recognized: not at first by sight, but by Scripture and the breaking of bread.
