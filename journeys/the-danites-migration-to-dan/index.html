The Danites' Migration to Dan

Journey
The Danites' Migration to Dan
Group
Danites
From Place
Zorah
To Place
Dan
Stages
Zorah; Eshtaol; Dan
Scripture
Judges 18:1-31
Summary
Finding no inheritance secured for them, the tribe of Dan sent five men to spy out the land; they found Laish, a quiet and unsuspecting town, and the tribe migrated there with their families, renamed it Dan, and set up Micah's idol. Laish is the town later called Dan, so Dan is given as the final stage; the text names no stops between Eshtaol and Laish.
Significance
Dan's migration shows settlement by violence and idolatry rather than by trusting God's allotment.
