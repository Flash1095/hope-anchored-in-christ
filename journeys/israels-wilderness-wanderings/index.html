Stages

Exodus 15:22-27; Exodus 17:1-16; Exodus 19:1-25; Numbers 10:11-36; Numbers 13:1-33; Numbers 20:1-29; Numbers 21:1-35; Numbers 33:1-49
Summary
From the Red Sea, Israel journeyed through the wilderness to Sinai, where the law was given, then on through the wilderness for forty years to the plains of Moab opposite Jericho. The stages here are the major named stations from Exodus and Numbers in order, not the full forty-two-station list of Numbers 33, which the reader can follow station by station. Debate note: the precise modern locations of many stations are debated; this entry gives Scripture's names and order, not modern identifications.
Significance
The wilderness is where a freed people learn to trust daily bread, law, and God's presence before they can inherit the land.
