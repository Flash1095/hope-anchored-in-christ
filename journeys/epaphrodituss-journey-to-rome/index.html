Epaphroditus's Journey to Rome

Journey
Epaphroditus's Journey to Rome
Travelers
Epaphroditus
From Place
Philippi
To Place
Rome
Stages
Philippi; Rome
Scripture
Philippians 2:25-30; Philippians 4:10-20
Summary
The Philippian church sent Epaphroditus to Rome with their gift for Paul and to minister to his needs. He fell seriously ill nearly to death, recovered, and Paul sent him back carrying the letter to the Philippians. The stages give the two endpoints; the return leg is noted in the summary rather than listed twice.
Significance
Epaphroditus's road is partnership in bodily form: a church's gift walks to Rome in a man who nearly dies delivering it.
