Ruth and Naomi's Journey to Bethlehem

After ten years in Moab, Naomi set out to return to Bethlehem when she heard God had visited his people with bread. Orpah turned back, but Ruth clung to her, saying Naomi's people would be her people and Naomi's God her God. The text names no stops on the road.

Significance

Ruth's road home is loyalty made visible: a foreign widow walks into Israel's story and toward David's line.
