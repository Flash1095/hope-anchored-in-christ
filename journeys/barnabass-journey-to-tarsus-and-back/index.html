Barnabas's Journey to Tarsus and Back

Journey
Barnabas's Journey to Tarsus and Back
Travelers

From Place

To Place
Antioch
Stages
Antioch; Tarsus
Scripture
Acts 11:19-30
Summary
When the church at Antioch grew, Barnabas was sent to encourage it; seeing God's grace there, he went on to Tarsus to look for Saul and brought him back to Antioch, where the two taught together for a whole year. The stages give the out-and-back route; the return leg to Antioch is the journey's endpoint.
Significance
Barnabas's errand to Tarsus recruits the apostle to the Gentiles: encouragement becomes partnership.
