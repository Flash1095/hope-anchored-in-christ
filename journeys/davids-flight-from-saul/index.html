Scripture
1 Samuel 19:1-24; 1 Samuel 21:1-15; 1 Samuel 22:1-23; 1 Samuel 23:1-29; 1 Samuel 24:1-22
Summary
Hunted by Saul, David fled from Gibeah to Samuel at Ramah, took consecrated bread at Nob (named here in the summary; Nob has no place entry, so it is not a stage), feigned madness before Achish at Gath, gathered outcasts at the cave of Adullam, rescued Keilah, and kept moving through Ziph and Maon as Saul closed in. The stages follow the narrative's named towns in order; David moved more often than this list shows.
Significance
The fugitive years forge the king: David refuses twice to kill Saul, trusting God to give the kingdom rather than seizing it.
