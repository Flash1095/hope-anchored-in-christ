Scripture
Genesis 26:1-33
Summary
During a famine Isaac went to Gerar in Philistine country rather than down to Egypt. After disputes over wells and a covenant with Abimelech, Isaac moved on and settled at Beersheba, where God renewed the promise made to Abraham. The starting point in Canaan is not named as a town, so the stages begin at Gerar.
Significance
Isaac inherits the promise not by conquest but by digging wells, keeping peace, and trusting God's presence in disputed ground.
