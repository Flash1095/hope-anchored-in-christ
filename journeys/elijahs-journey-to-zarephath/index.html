Elijah's Journey to Zarephath

Journey
Elijah's Journey to Zarephath
Travelers
Elijah
From Place
Brook Cherith
To Place
Zarephath
Stages
Brook Cherith; Zarephath
Scripture
1 Kings 17:1-24
Summary
After announcing drought, Elijah hid by the brook Cherith, where ravens fed him. When the brook dried up, God sent him to Zarephath in Sidonian country, where a widow shared her last meal and her son was later raised from death. The text names no stops between Cherith and Zarephath.
Significance
God provides through the unlikely: a drought, ravens, and a foreign widow's jar become the stage for resurrection.
