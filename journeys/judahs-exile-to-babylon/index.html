Judah's Exile to Babylon

Journey
Judah's Exile to Babylon
Group
Judah's Exiles
From Place
Jerusalem
To Place
Babylon
Stages
Jerusalem; Riblah; Babylon
Scripture
2 Kings 25:1-21; Jeremiah 52:1-30
Summary
After Jerusalem fell, Nebuzaradan carried the people into exile, and the leaders were taken to Nebuchadnezzar at Riblah for judgment before the people were marched to Babylon. The stages follow the narrative's named points; the text does not trace the marching route in detail.
Significance
The exile is judgment with a promise attached: the same prophets who announced it also promised return.
