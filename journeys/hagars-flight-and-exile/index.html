Scripture
Genesis 16:1-16; Genesis 21:8-21
Summary
Hagar first fled from Sarai's harsh treatment and met the angel of the LORD by a spring on the way to Shur, at Beer Lahai Roi, then returned. Years later, after Isaac's birth, Abraham sent Hagar and Ishmael away with bread and water; they wandered in the wilderness of Beersheba and then lived in the wilderness of Paran. The two departures are combined here because both trace Hagar's forced journeys; the text does not name every stop.
Significance
God sees and provides for the outcast: Hagar names God El Roi, the God who sees, and Ishmael's line is preserved by promise.
