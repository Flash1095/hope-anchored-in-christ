Summary
When the days drew near for him to be taken up, Jesus set his face to go to Jerusalem. Luke's travel narrative traces him through Samaritan villages, past Jericho where he met Zacchaeus, up to Bethany, and into Jerusalem for the final week. The stages follow Luke's regional sequence; the narrative includes many teachings and healings along the way that this route entry does not retell.
Significance
The final road is deliberate: Jesus walks toward the cross he has predicted, not away from it.
