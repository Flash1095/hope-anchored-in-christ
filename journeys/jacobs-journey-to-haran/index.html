Stages

Scripture
Genesis 28:1-22; Genesis 29:1-30
Summary
Fleeing Esau's anger, Jacob left Beersheba for Haran to find a wife from Laban's family. At Bethel he dreamed of a ladder reaching to heaven and heard God's promise renewed. He then reached Haran, where he served Laban and married Leah and Rachel. The text names Bethel as the one stop on the way.
Significance
Bethel turns a fugitive's flight into a covenant encounter: God meets Jacob before Jacob has anything to offer.
