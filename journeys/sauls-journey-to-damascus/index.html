Scripture
Acts 9:1-31
Summary
Saul, breathing threats against the disciples, set out from Jerusalem for Damascus with letters to arrest believers there. Near Damascus the risen Jesus appeared to him in light, and Saul, blinded, was led into the city, where Ananias baptized him and his sight returned. The text names no stops on the road. Paul's profile covers Saul before and after conversion, so Paul is listed as the traveler.
Significance
The Damascus road is the sharpest conversion in the New Testament: the persecutor becomes the apostle to the Gentiles.
