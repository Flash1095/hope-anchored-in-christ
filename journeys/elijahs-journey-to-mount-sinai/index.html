Scripture
1 Kings 19:1-21
Summary
Fleeing Jezebel, Elijah left his servant at Beersheba, walked into the wilderness, and after forty days and forty nights reached Horeb, the mountain of God. There God passed by not in wind, earthquake, or fire, but in a still small voice, and sent Elijah back with new commissions. Horeb is another name for the Sinai region; Mount Sinai is given as the stage, and the wilderness stops are not named.
Significance
At the mountain of the law, a burned-out prophet learns God is not only in the spectacular, and is sent back to work.
