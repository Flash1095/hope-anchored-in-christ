The Ethiopian Official's Return to Ethiopia

Journey
The Ethiopian Official's Return to Ethiopia
From Place
Gaza
To Place
Ethiopia
Stages
Gaza; Ethiopia
Scripture
Acts 8:26-40
Summary
The Ethiopian official, treasurer to Candace, had come to Jerusalem to worship and was returning home reading Isaiah when Philip joined his chariot, explained the passage about the suffering servant, and baptized him at water by the road. The official went on his way rejoicing. He has no people profile, so Travelers is left blank rather than inventing one.
Significance
A single chariot ride carries Isaiah's servant song to Africa: the gospel reaches Ethiopia through one reader.
