Scripture

Summary
Nehemiah, cupbearer to Artaxerxes at Susa, wept over Jerusalem's broken walls and asked leave to go and rebuild. The king sent him with letters and an escort, and Nehemiah reached Jerusalem and inspected the walls by night before announcing the work. The text names no stops on the road from Susa.
Significance
Nehemiah's journey turns grief into masonry: prayer in the palace becomes a wall in fifty-two days.
