Joseph and Mary's Journey to Bethlehem

Because of Caesar's decree that all the world be taxed, Joseph went up from Nazareth in Galilee to Bethlehem, the city of David, with Mary, who was great with child. There she gave birth to her firstborn son and laid him in a manger. Luke names no stops on the road.

Significance

The census road fulfills Micah's promise: the Messiah is born in Bethlehem because an empire counted its subjects.
