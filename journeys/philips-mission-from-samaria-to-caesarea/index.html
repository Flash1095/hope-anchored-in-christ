Philip's Mission from Samaria to Caesarea

Stages

Scripture
Acts 8:5-40
Summary
Philip preached Christ in Samaria, was directed to the desert road from Jerusalem to Gaza, where he met and baptized the Ethiopian official, and was then found at Azotus (Ashdod in the place list), preaching through the towns until he reached Caesarea. The stages follow Acts' sequence; Philip's profile covers both his apostolic and evangelistic work, so Philip is listed as the traveler.
Significance
Philip's road runs from a city revival to a desert chariot to a coastal home: the gospel keeps moving outward.
