Abram and Lot's Parting

After returning from Egypt, Abram and Lot's herdsmen quarreled because the land could not support both households. Abram let Lot choose first; Lot chose the well-watered plain of the Jordan toward Sodom, while Abram settled in the land of Canaan near Hebron. The stages give each man's destination, not a shared road.

Significance

The parting shows Abram trusting God's promise rather than grabbing the best land, and it places Lot on the road toward Sodom's judgment.
