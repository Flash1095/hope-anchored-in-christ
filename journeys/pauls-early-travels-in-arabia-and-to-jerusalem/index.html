Paul's Early Travels in Arabia and to Jerusalem

Journey
Paul's Early Travels in Arabia and to Jerusalem
Travelers

From Place

To Place
Jerusalem
Stages
Damascus; Arabia; Jerusalem
Scripture
Galatians 1:13-24; Acts 9:19-31
Summary
After his conversion Paul went away into Arabia and returned to Damascus, then after three years went up to Jerusalem to visit Peter and stayed fifteen days. Galatians gives the sequence but no Arabian town and no road details, so only the three named points are given as stages, in order, with Damascus appearing as both start and return point in the summary rather than as a repeated stage.
Significance
Paul's early years show a gospel received by revelation, tested in obscurity, and only then brought to Jerusalem's apostles.
