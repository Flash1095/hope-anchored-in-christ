Stages
Jerusalem; Antioch
Scripture
Acts 15:1-35
Summary
After the council decided Gentile believers need not be circumcised, the apostles sent Paul, Barnabas, Judas Barsabbas, and Silas back to Antioch with the council's letter. The church rejoiced at the encouragement, and Judas and Silas, themselves prophets, strengthened the brothers. The text names no stops on the return road.
Significance
The delegates' road carries the council's decision home: Gentile freedom is settled by letter, not left to rumor.
