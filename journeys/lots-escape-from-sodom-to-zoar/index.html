Stages
Sodom; Zoar
Scripture
Genesis 19:1-30
Summary
Warned by two angels, Lot fled Sodom at dawn with his wife and two daughters, escaping to the small town of Zoar as fire fell on the cities of the plain. Lot's wife looked back and became a pillar of salt, so only Lot and his daughters reached Zoar. The text gives no intermediate stops.
Significance
The escape is a warning about lingering near judgment and about looking back after being told to flee.
