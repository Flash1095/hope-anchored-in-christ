Timothy's Mission to Thessalonica

Journey
Timothy's Mission to Thessalonica
Travelers
Timothy
From Place
Athens
To Place
Thessalonica
Stages
Athens; Thessalonica
Scripture
1 Thessalonians 3:1-13; Acts 17:1-15
Summary
When Paul could no longer bear separation from the Thessalonian church, he sent Timothy from Athens to establish and exhort them in their faith amid affliction. Timothy returned with good news of their faith and love. The text names the sending city and the destination, not the road between them.
Significance
Timothy's errand is pastoral care at a distance: a young coworker carries encouragement to a persecuted church.
