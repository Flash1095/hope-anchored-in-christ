Jacob's Sons' Grain Journeys to Egypt

Group

From Place

To Place

Stages
Canaan; Egypt
Scripture
Genesis 42:1-38; Genesis 43:1-34; Genesis 44:1-34
Summary
During the famine Jacob sent his sons down to Egypt twice to buy grain, the second time with Benjamin. On the first trip Joseph, unrecognized, held Simeon and demanded Benjamin; on the second, Judah pleaded for Benjamin and Joseph revealed himself. The two trips are combined here because they share one route and one purpose; the text names no stops between Canaan and Egypt.
Significance
The grain road becomes the road to repentance: the brothers who sold Joseph must now stand for Benjamin.
