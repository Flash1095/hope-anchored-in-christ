Moses's Flight to Midian

Journey
Moses's Flight to Midian
Travelers
Moses
From Place

To Place
Midian
Stages
Egypt; Midian
Scripture
Exodus 2:11-25
Summary
After killing an Egyptian and learning Pharaoh sought his life, Moses fled Egypt and sat down by a well in Midian, where he defended Jethro's daughters, married Zipporah, and became a shepherd. The text names no stops on the flight itself.
Significance
Forty years in Midian strip the prince of Egypt down to a shepherd God can send back.
