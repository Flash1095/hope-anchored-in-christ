The Flight to Egypt

Journey
The Flight to Egypt
Travelers
Joseph, Husband of Mary; Mary, Mother of Jesus
From Place
Bethlehem
To Place
Egypt
Stages
Bethlehem; Egypt
Scripture
Matthew 2:13-18
Summary
Warned in a dream that Herod would seek the child's life, Joseph took Mary and the child by night and departed into Egypt, where they stayed until Herod's death. Matthew names no Egyptian town and no stops on the flight.
Significance
The flight replays Israel's story in miniature: the Son goes down into Egypt and will be called back out.
