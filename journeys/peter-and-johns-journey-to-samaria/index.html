Peter and John's Journey to Samaria

Journey
Peter and John's Journey to Samaria
Travelers
Simon Peter; John the Apostle
From Place
Jerusalem
To Place
Samaria
Stages
Jerusalem; Samaria
Scripture
Acts 8:14-25
Summary
When the apostles at Jerusalem heard Samaria had received the word of God, they sent Peter and John, who prayed for the new believers to receive the Holy Spirit and preached in many Samaritan villages on the way back. The text names no villages on the return.
Significance
Peter and John's visit seals the Samaritan mission: the Spirit falls beyond Jerusalem's old boundaries.
