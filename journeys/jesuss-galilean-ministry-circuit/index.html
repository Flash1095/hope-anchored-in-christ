Scripture

Summary
After John's arrest Jesus left Nazareth and made Capernaum his base, teaching in synagogues and healing through the towns and villages of Galilee, including Cana, Bethsaida, Chorazin, and Magdala. The stages list the named Galilean towns of his ministry in narrative order; the Gospels describe a circuit of repeated visits, not a single one-way road, and this entry does not claim a fixed itinerary.
Significance
Galilee becomes the classroom of the kingdom: parables, healings, and callings cluster around these lakeside towns.
