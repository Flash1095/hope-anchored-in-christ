The Antioch Relief Journey

Journey
The Antioch Relief Journey
Travelers
Barnabas; Paul
From Place
Antioch
To Place
Jerusalem
Stages
Antioch; Jerusalem
Scripture
Acts 11:27-30
Summary
When Agabus prophesied a great famine, the disciples at Antioch determined to send relief to the brothers living in Judea, sending their gift by the hands of Barnabas and Saul. The text names no stops on the road to Jerusalem.
Significance
The first Gentile church's first gift runs toward Jerusalem: grace received becomes relief sent.
