The Twelve Spies' Reconnaissance

Scripture
Numbers 13:1-33; Numbers 14:1-45
Summary
From the wilderness of Paran, Moses sent twelve men to explore Canaan. They went up through the Negev as far as Hebron and the Valley of Eshcol, where they cut a cluster of grapes, then returned after forty days. Ten reported fear; Caleb and Joshua urged trust; Israel refused and was sentenced to forty years in the wilderness. Note: this was a reconnaissance mission, not a migration; it is included because it has a route, a destination, and a return, and its report changed Israel's journey. The Valley of Eshcol has no place entry, so it is named in this summary but not listed as a stage.
Significance
The spies' report is the turning point of the wilderness: unbelief at the border costs a generation the land.
