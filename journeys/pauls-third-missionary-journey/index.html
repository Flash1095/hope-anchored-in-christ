Scripture
Acts 18:23-28; Acts 19:1-41; Acts 20:1-38; Acts 21:1-17
Summary
Paul traveled through Galatia and Phrygia strengthening the disciples, spent over two years teaching at Ephesus, revisited Macedonia and Achaia to collect the offering and encourage the churches, then sailed back through Troas, Miletus (where he farewelled the Ephesian elders), and the Levantine ports to Jerusalem. Macedonia appears once in the stage list although Paul crossed it twice; the return leg retraced it. The stages follow Acts' named points in order.
Significance
The third journey is the longest and most settled: Ephesus becomes a base, and the farewell at Miletus looks toward Jerusalem and bonds.
