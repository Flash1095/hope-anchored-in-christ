Scripture
Acts 9:32-43; Acts 10:1-48
Summary
Peter traveled through the coastal towns, healing Aeneas at Lydda (listed here as Lod, the place entry's name) and raising Tabitha at Joppa, then was summoned to Caesarea, where he preached to Cornelius's household and saw Gentiles receive the Holy Spirit. The stages follow Acts' order; the journey's turning point is the Caesarea household, not the road itself.
Significance
Peter's coastal road breaks the Gentile barrier: the gospel reaches a Roman household before any council approves it.
