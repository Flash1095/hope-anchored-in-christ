Paul's First Missionary Journey

From Place
Antioch
To Place
Antioch
Stages
Antioch; Salamis; Paphos; Perga; Antioch in Pisidia; Iconium; Lystra; Derbe; Attalia
Scripture
Acts 13:1-52; Acts 14:1-28
Summary
Sent out by the Antioch church, Paul and Barnabas sailed to Cyprus, preached at Salamis and Paphos, crossed to Perga where John Mark left them, then traveled inland to Antioch in Pisidia, Iconium, Lystra, and Derbe, appointing elders in each church on the way back before sailing home to Antioch. The port of Seleucia has no place entry, so it is not listed as a stage; the return leg retraced the inland towns and is not repeated in the stage list.
Significance
The first journey proves the pattern: synagogue preaching, Gentile response, Jewish opposition, and churches left behind with elders.
