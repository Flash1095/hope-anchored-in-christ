Jesus's Journey to Caesarea Philippi

To Place

Stages
Galilee; Caesarea Philippi
Scripture
Matthew 16:13-28; Mark 8:27-38
Summary
Jesus took his disciples into the region of Caesarea Philippi, at the far north of Israel's territory, and asked them who they said he was. Peter confessed him as the Christ, and Jesus began to speak plainly of his coming suffering. The text names no stops on the way north.
Significance
At the edge of the land, the question of Jesus's identity is asked most plainly, and the answer turns the road toward Jerusalem.
