Scripture
Daniel 1:1-21
Summary
In the third year of Jehoiakim, Nebuchadnezzar besieged Jerusalem and carried off some of the vessels of God's house along with young men of royal and noble birth, including Daniel. Daniel was trained in Babylonian learning yet kept himself undefiled. The text names no stops on the deportation march.
Significance
Exile begins Daniel's lifelong witness: faithfulness in a foreign court without compromise or withdrawal.
