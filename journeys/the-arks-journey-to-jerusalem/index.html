The Ark's Journey to Jerusalem

Captured by the Philistines at Aphek, the ark passed through Ashdod, Gath, and Ekron as plagues followed it, was returned to Beth-shemesh, and rested twenty years at Kiriath-jearim. David then brought it up to Jerusalem with sacrifices and dancing. The stages span decades and several custodians; they trace the ark, not a single party's road.

Significance

The ark's road teaches reverence: God's presence cannot be captured, managed, or carried carelessly.
