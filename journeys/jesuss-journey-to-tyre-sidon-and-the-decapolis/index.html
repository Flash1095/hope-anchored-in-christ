Stages
Galilee; Tyre; Sidon; Decapolis
Scripture
Matthew 15:21-39; Mark 7:24-37; Mark 8:1-10
Summary
Jesus withdrew to the region of Tyre and Sidon, where he healed the Syrophoenician woman's daughter, then returned through the Decapolis, healing the deaf and feeding the four thousand. The stages follow Mark's regional order; the text names towns within the Decapolis only in general terms.
Significance
The journey pushes the kingdom beyond Israel's borders: mercy crosses into Gentile country before the cross.
