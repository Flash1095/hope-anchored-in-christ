Israel's Crossing of the Jordan

Journey
Israel's Crossing of the Jordan
Group
Israelites
From Place
Shittim
To Place
Gilgal
Stages
Shittim; Jordan River; Gilgal
Scripture
Joshua 3:1-17; Joshua 4:1-24
Summary
Israel left Shittim, camped by the Jordan, and crossed on dry ground as the priests carrying the ark stood in the river. Twelve stones from the riverbed were set up at Gilgal as a memorial. This crossing is the opening movement of the conquest and is listed separately because it is a distinct river-crossing journey with its own memorial.
Significance
The Jordan crossing mirrors the Red Sea: the new generation enters the land the way the old generation left Egypt.
