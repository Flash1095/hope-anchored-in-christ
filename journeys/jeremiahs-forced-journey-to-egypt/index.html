Jeremiah's Forced Journey to Egypt

Scripture
Jeremiah 43:1-13; Jeremiah 44:1-30
Summary
After Gedaliah's murder, the remnant in Judah refused Jeremiah's warning and forced him and Baruch to go with them into Egypt, settling at Tahpanhes. There Jeremiah prophesied that Nebuchadnezzar would set his throne over Egypt. The text names Tahpanhes as the destination and no stops on the way.
Significance
The forced journey is the remnant repeating Israel's oldest mistake: fleeing to Egypt against God's word.
