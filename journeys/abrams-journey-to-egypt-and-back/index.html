Abram's Journey to Egypt and Back

Journey
Abram's Journey to Egypt and Back
Travelers
Abraham; Sarah
From Place
Canaan
To Place
Bethel
Stages
Canaan; Egypt; Bethel
Scripture
Genesis 12:10-20; Genesis 13:1-4
Summary
When famine struck Canaan, Abram went down into Egypt to live there for a time. After Pharaoh sent him away, Abram returned to Bethel, to the place where his tent and altar had been at the beginning. The text does not name the Egyptian town where he stayed, so none is given as a stage.
Significance
The journey exposes Abram's fear and failure alongside God's protection of the promise, and it previews Israel's later sojourn in Egypt.
