Moses's Return to Egypt

Journey
Moses's Return to Egypt
Travelers
Moses; Aaron
From Place
Midian
To Place
Egypt
Stages
Midian; Mount Sinai; Egypt
Scripture
Exodus 4:18-31
Summary
At the burning bush God sent Moses back to Egypt, and Aaron met him at the mountain of God on the way. Together they gathered Israel's elders and told them God's words, and the people believed. The mountain meeting is the one named stop; Aaron joins Moses en route rather than starting with him.
Significance
The return reverses the flight: the fugitive goes back carrying God's name and his brother's voice.
