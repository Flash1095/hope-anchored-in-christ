Onesimus's Flight and Return

Journey
Onesimus's Flight and Return
Travelers
Onesimus; Tychicus
From Place
Colossae
To Place
Rome
Stages
Colossae; Rome
Scripture
Philemon 1:1-25; Colossians 4:7-18
Summary
Onesimus, a slave of Philemon at Colossae, reached Paul in Rome, where he became a believer. Paul sent him back to Philemon with Tychicus, carrying the letter to Philemon and no longer asking that he be received as a slave but as a beloved brother. The stages give the flight and the return's endpoints; the text does not say by what road Onesimus reached Rome.
Significance
Onesimus's road turns flight into reconciliation: the gospel sends a runaway back, not as property, but as a brother.
