The Disciples' Journey to Galilee after the Resurrection
Group
The Eleven
From Place
Jerusalem
To Place
Galilee
Stages
Jerusalem; Galilee
Scripture
Matthew 28:1-20
Summary
After the resurrection the risen Jesus told the women to tell his brothers to go into Galilee, and there on a mountain the eleven saw him, worshipped, and received the commission to make disciples of all nations. Matthew does not name the mountain, so no mountain stage is given.
Significance
Galilee closes the circle: the place of calling becomes the place of commissioning to the whole world.
