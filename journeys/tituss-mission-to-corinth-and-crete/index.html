Titus's Mission to Corinth and Crete

Stages

Scripture
2 Corinthians 7:5-16; 2 Corinthians 8:1-24; Titus 1:1-16
Summary
Titus carried Paul's letters and encouragement between Paul and Corinth, bringing back news of the Corinthians' repentance, and later Paul left Titus in Crete to set in order what remained and appoint elders in every town. The two assignments are combined here because both are Titus's recorded missions; the text does not trace his roads.
Significance
Titus is Paul's troubleshooter: the man sent where churches need mending and ordering.
