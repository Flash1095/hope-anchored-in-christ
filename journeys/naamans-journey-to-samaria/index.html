Naaman's Journey to Samaria

Journey
Naaman's Journey to Samaria
Travelers
Naaman
From Place
Damascus
To Place
Samaria
Stages
Damascus; Samaria
Scripture
2 Kings 5:1-27
Summary
Naaman, commander of Syria's army and a leper, traveled to Israel with a letter from his king and gifts, seeking healing. Directed to Elisha, he at first refused to wash in the Jordan, then obeyed and was cleansed. The stages give the journey's endpoints; the Jordan washing is named in the summary, and Samaria stands for the Israelite capital where Elisha was found.
Significance
Healing comes by humble obedience, not by status: a foreign general must wash like anyone else.
