Scripture
Genesis 24:1-67
Summary
Abraham sent his steward to his own kindred to find a wife for Isaac. The servant traveled to the city of Nahor in Mesopotamia, prayed at the well, met Rebekah, and brought her back to Isaac in Canaan. The text names the destination region rather than each stop, so only the endpoints are given as stages.
Significance
The mission shows God's guidance through prayer and providence in an ordinary errand, and it secures the promised line through Isaac.
