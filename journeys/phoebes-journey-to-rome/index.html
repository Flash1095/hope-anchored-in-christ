Phoebe's Journey to Rome

Journey
Phoebe's Journey to Rome
Travelers
Phoebe
From Place
Cenchrea
To Place
Rome
Stages
Cenchrea; Rome
Scripture
Romans 16:1-2
Summary
Phoebe, a servant of the church at Cenchrea, carried Paul's letter to the Romans to Rome, where Paul asks the church to receive her in a manner worthy of the saints and to help her in whatever she may need. The text names no stops on the journey.
Significance
Phoebe's road carries Romans itself: a deacon's journey delivers the most systematic letter in the New Testament.
