The Syro-Ephraimite War

Title
The Syro-Ephraimite War
Era
Divided Monarchy
Date Approx
c. 735-732 BC (approx.)
Key Persons
Pekah, King of Israel; Ahaz, King of Judah; Tiglath-Pileser, King of Assyria
Bible Books
2 Kings; Isaiah
Related Places
Jerusalem; Damascus; Samaria
Events
Pekah of Israel and Rezin of Syria besiege Jerusalem to replace Ahaz; Ahaz appeals to Tiglath-Pileser of Assyria, who destroys Damascus, carries Israel's north into exile, and leaves Judah a vassal.
Scripture Reference
2 Kings 15:29-16:9; Isaiah 7
