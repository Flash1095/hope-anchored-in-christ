Jeremiah Freed After Jerusalem's Fall

Title
Jeremiah Freed After Jerusalem's Fall
Era
Exile
Date Approx
c. 586 BC (after Jerusalem's fall)
Key Persons
Jeremiah; Nebuzaradan
Bible Books
Jeremiah
Related Places
Ramah; Mizpah
Events
After Babylon takes Jerusalem, Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, releases Jeremiah from his chains at Ramah and lets him choose his way, and Jeremiah chooses to remain with Gedaliah and the remnant at Mizpah.
Scripture Reference
Jeremiah 39:11-40:6
