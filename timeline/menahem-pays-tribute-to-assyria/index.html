Title
Menahem Pays Tribute to Assyria
Era
Divided Monarchy
Date Approx
c. 752-742 BC (approx.)
Key Persons
Menahem, King of Israel; Pul, King of Assyria
Bible Books
2 Kings
Related Places
Samaria
Events
After seizing the throne by violence, Menahem exacts silver from Israel's wealthy men to pay Pul of Assyria, buying Assyria's withdrawal and confirming its grip on Israel.
Scripture Reference
2 Kings 15:17-22
