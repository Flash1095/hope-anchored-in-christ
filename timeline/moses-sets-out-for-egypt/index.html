Moses Sets Out for Egypt

Title
Moses Sets Out for Egypt
Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Date Approx
c. 1446 BC or c. 1270 BC (date debated by tradition)
Key Persons
Moses; Zipporah; Gershom
Bible Books
Exodus
Related Places
Midian; Egypt
Events
Moses returns to Jethro, takes his wife and sons, and sets out for Egypt with the rod of God. On the way, the LORD seeks to kill Moses, and Zipporah circumcises their son, after which the LORD lets him go.
Scripture Reference
Exodus 4:18-26
