Paul Before Festus and Agrippa

Title
Paul Before Festus and Agrippa
Era
Early Church
Date Approx
c. AD 57-59 (approx.)
Key Persons
Paul; Festus; Herod Agrippa II; Bernice
Bible Books
Acts
Related Places
Caesarea
Events
Before Festus at Caesarea, Paul appeals to Caesar rather than face trial at Jerusalem, and then defends himself before King Agrippa and Bernice, who conclude he has done nothing worthy of death or bonds.
Scripture Reference
Acts 25:1-27; Acts 26:1-32
