War with the House of Saul

Title
War with the House of Saul
Era
United Monarchy
Date Approx
c. 1010-1000 BC (approx.)
Key Persons
King David; Joab; Abner, Son of Ner; Ish-bosheth, Son of Saul
Bible Books
2 Samuel
Related Places
Hebron; Mahanaim; Gibeon
Events
After Saul's death David rules Judah from Hebron while Ish-bosheth rules the north, and a long civil war follows until Abner and then Ish-bosheth are murdered and all Israel turns to David.
Scripture Reference
2 Samuel 2-4
