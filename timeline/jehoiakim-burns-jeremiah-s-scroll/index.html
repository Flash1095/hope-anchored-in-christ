Jehoiakim Burns Jeremiah's Scroll

Title
Jehoiakim Burns Jeremiah's Scroll
Era
Divided Monarchy
Date Approx
c. 605-604 BC (fourth and fifth years of Jehoiakim)
Key Persons
Jeremiah; Baruch; Jehoiakim, King of Judah; Gemariah, Son of Shaphan
Bible Books
Jeremiah
Related Places
Jerusalem
Events
Baruch writes Jeremiah's words on a scroll and reads them in the temple; after the princes warn the king, Jehoiakim cuts the scroll with a penknife and burns it leaf by leaf in the winter fire, and Jeremiah dictates the scroll again with many more words added.
Scripture Reference
Jeremiah 36
