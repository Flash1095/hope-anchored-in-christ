Jehoiachin's Surrender and the Second Deportation

Title
Jehoiachin's Surrender and the Second Deportation
Era
Exile
Date Approx
597 BC
Key Persons
Jehoiachin, King of Judah; King Nebuchadnezzar
Bible Books
2 Kings
Related Places
Jerusalem; Babylon
Events
After a three-month reign, Jehoiachin surrenders to Nebuchadnezzar, who carries him, his court, and Jerusalem's craftsmen and treasures to Babylon in the second deportation.
Scripture Reference
2 Kings 24:8-17
