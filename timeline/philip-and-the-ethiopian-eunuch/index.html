Philip and the Ethiopian Eunuch

Title
Philip and the Ethiopian Eunuch
Era
Early Church
Date Approx
c. AD 34-37 (approx.)
Key Persons
Philip
Bible Books
Acts
Related Places
Gaza; Ethiopia
Events
Directed by an angel to the desert road to Gaza, Philip joins an Ethiopian official reading Isaiah, explains that the passage speaks of Jesus, and baptises him, after which Philip is caught away.
Scripture Reference
Acts 8:26-40
