Zimri's Coup and Omri's Rise

Title
Zimri's Coup and Omri's Rise
Era
Divided Monarchy
Date Approx
c. 885-874 BC (approx.)
Key Persons
Zimri, King of Israel; Omri, King of Israel
Bible Books
1 Kings
Related Places
Samaria; Tirzah
Events
Zimri murders Elah and reigns only seven days before Omri, proclaimed king by the army, besieges Tirzah; Zimri burns the palace over himself, and Omri founds Samaria as Israel's new capital.
Scripture Reference
1 Kings 16:8-28
