Noah's Blessing and the Curse of Canaan

Title
Noah's Blessing and the Curse of Canaan
Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Date Approx
Primeval period (date varies by tradition)
Key Persons
Noah; Shem; Ham; Japheth; Canaan, Son of Ham
Bible Books
Genesis
Events
Noah becomes drunk and lies uncovered in his tent; Ham dishonours him, while Shem and Japheth cover him. When Noah awakes, he curses Canaan and blesses Shem and Japheth.
Scripture Reference
Genesis 9:20-27
