Jeremiah's Letter to the Exiles

Title
Jeremiah's Letter to the Exiles
Era
Divided Monarchy
Date Approx
c. 597 BC (approx., after Jeconiah's deportation)
Key Persons
Jeremiah; Elasah; Zedekiah, King of Judah
Bible Books
Jeremiah
Related Places
Jerusalem; Babylon
Events
From Jerusalem, Jeremiah sends a letter by Elasah to the exiles already carried to Babylon, telling them to build, plant, and seek the city's peace, for their exile will last seventy years before God brings them back.
Scripture Reference
Jeremiah 29
