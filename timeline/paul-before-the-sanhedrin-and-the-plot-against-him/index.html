Paul Before the Sanhedrin and the Plot Against Him

Title
Paul Before the Sanhedrin and the Plot Against Him
Era
Early Church
Date Approx
c. AD 57-59 (approx.)
Key Persons
Paul; Ananias, the High Priest; Claudius Lysias
Bible Books
Acts
Related Places
Jerusalem; Caesarea
Events
Before the Sanhedrin, Paul's declaration divides Pharisees and Sadducees, a plot of more than forty men to kill him is revealed by his nephew, and the commander sends Paul by night under guard to Caesarea.
Scripture Reference
Acts 22:30-23:35
