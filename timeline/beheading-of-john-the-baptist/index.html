Beheading of John the Baptist

Title
Beheading of John the Baptist
Era
Life of Christ
Date Approx
c. AD 27-30 or AD 30-33 (approx., date debated)
Key Persons
John the Baptist; Herod Antipas; Herodias
Bible Books
Matthew; Mark
Events
Herod Antipas, bound by a rash oath at a birthday feast and pressed by Herodias, orders John the Baptist beheaded in prison, and John's disciples bury his body.
Scripture Reference
Matthew 14:1-12; Mark 6:14-29
