Title

Related Places

Events
After Daniel warns him from a dream of a great tree cut down, Nebuchadnezzar boasts of Babylon and loses his reason, living like a beast until he lifts his eyes to heaven, when his understanding and kingdom are restored and he blesses the Most High.
