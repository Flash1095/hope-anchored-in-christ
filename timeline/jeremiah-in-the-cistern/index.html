Jeremiah in the Cistern

Title
Jeremiah in the Cistern
Era
Exile
Date Approx
c. 588-586 BC (during the Babylonian siege of Jerusalem)
Key Persons
Jeremiah; Ebed-Melech; Zedekiah, King of Judah
Bible Books
Jeremiah
Related Places
Jerusalem
Events
During the siege, Jeremiah is arrested as a deserter, beaten, and lowered into a miry cistern to die, until Ebed-Melech pleads with Zedekiah and lifts him out with old rags and ropes.
Scripture Reference
Jeremiah 37-38
