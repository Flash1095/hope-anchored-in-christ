Jehoiachin Released from Prison

Title
Jehoiachin Released from Prison
Era
Exile
Date Approx
561 BC
Key Persons
Jehoiachin, King of Judah; Evil-Merodach, King of Babylon
Bible Books
2 Kings
Related Places
Babylon
Events
In the first year of Evil-merodach, thirty-seven years after his deportation, Jehoiachin is released from prison, given a seat above the other captive kings, and fed at the king's table for life.
Scripture Reference
2 Kings 25:27-30
