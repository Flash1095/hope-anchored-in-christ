Cursing of the Fig Tree

Title
Cursing of the Fig Tree
Era
Life of Christ
Date Approx
c. AD 30 or AD 33 (approx., date debated)
Key Persons
Jesus
Bible Books
Matthew; Mark
Related Places
Jerusalem; Bethany
Events
On the way from Bethany to Jerusalem, Jesus finds a fig tree with leaves but no fruit, curses it, and by the next morning it has withered from the roots, prompting his teaching on faith and prayer.
Scripture Reference
Matthew 21:18-22; Mark 11:12-26
