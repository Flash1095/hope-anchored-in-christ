Isaiah's Barefoot Sign

Title
Isaiah's Barefoot Sign
Era
Divided Monarchy
Date Approx
c. 711 BC (the year Tartan took Ashdod)
Key Persons
Isaiah; Sargon
Bible Books
Isaiah
Related Places
Ashdod; Egypt; Ethiopia
Events
In the year Assyria's commander captures Ashdod, Isaiah walks naked and barefoot for three years as a sign that Assyria will lead Egypt and Ethiopia away captive, warning Judah not to trust them for deliverance.
Scripture Reference
Isaiah 20
