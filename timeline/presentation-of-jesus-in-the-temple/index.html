Title
Presentation of Jesus in the Temple
Era
Life of Christ
Date Approx
c. 6-4 BC (approx.)
Key Persons
Jesus; Simeon; Anna the Prophetess
Bible Books
Luke
Related Places
Jerusalem
Events
In the temple at Jerusalem, Simeon takes the infant Jesus in his arms and blesses God, and the prophetess Anna gives thanks and speaks of him to all who waited for redemption.
