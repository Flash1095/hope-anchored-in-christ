Shishak Invades Judah

Title
Shishak Invades Judah
Era
Divided Monarchy
Date Approx
c. 925 BC (approx.)
Key Persons
Rehoboam; Shishak, King of Egypt
Bible Books
1 Kings; 2 Chronicles
Related Places
Jerusalem
Events
In Rehoboam's fifth year Shishak of Egypt invades Judah and plunders the treasures of the temple and palace, after which Rehoboam replaces the gold shields with bronze ones.
Scripture Reference
1 Kings 14:25-28; 2 Chronicles 12
