Calling

Topic
Calling
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Calling is God's summons on a person's life — first the general call to follow Christ, then the particular work He gives each believer to do. Every legitimate vocation can be a calling when done for God's glory, whether preaching, farming, parenting, or healing. Discerning a calling takes time, counsel, and attention to gifts, needs, and open doors, and it is tested by faithfulness more than by feelings of certainty.
