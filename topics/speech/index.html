Speech

Topic
Speech
Summary
Our words reveal our hearts: Scripture warns that the tongue is a small fire able to bless or burn, and it calls us to speech that is truthful, gracious, and seasonable. James pictures the tongue as a rudder steering a whole life, while Proverbs treasures the apt word spoken at the right time. Guarding our speech is therefore not politeness but discipleship: learning to speak as people who will answer to God for every careless word.
Key Passages
Proverbs 15:1; Proverbs 18:21; James 3:1-12; Ephesians 4:29
Related Books
Proverbs; James; Ephesians
Related People
