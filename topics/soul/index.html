Soul

Topic
Soul
Category
Theology
Summary
The soul is the immaterial, enduring part of a human being - the seat of mind, will, and emotion that makes us more than bodies. Scripture speaks of the soul as both the whole person (a living soul) and the part that survives death, held by God until resurrection reunites it with a renewed body. Christians therefore care for souls - their own and others' - as the most valuable thing in the world, worth more than everything else combined.
