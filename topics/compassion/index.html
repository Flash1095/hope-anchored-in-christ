Compassion

Topic
Compassion
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Compassion is love that sees suffering and moves toward it, as Jesus did when He wept, touched the untouchable, and fed the hungry. It is more than feeling sorry; it acts, even at cost, and it reflects the heart of a God who is full of compassion. Christians practice compassion in hospitals and homes, in generosity and advocacy, knowing they themselves have received mercy.
