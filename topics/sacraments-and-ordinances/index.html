Sacraments and Ordinances

Topic
Sacraments and Ordinances
Category
Doctrine
Summary
Sacraments and ordinances are the church's sacred practices: most traditions count baptism and the Lord's Supper, while Catholics and Orthodox add others. Churches differ on what to call them and how they work - means of grace, memorials, or both - but agree they were instituted by Christ and mark belonging to His people. Baptism washes into the new covenant community; the Supper feeds it at the Lord's table; both preach the gospel to the senses.
