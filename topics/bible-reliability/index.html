Bible Reliability

Topic
Bible Reliability
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Bible reliability asks whether the Scriptures we have today faithfully preserve what was originally written, and whether their claims about history hold up. The manuscript evidence for the New Testament is vast, the Old Testament was copied with extraordinary care, and archaeology has repeatedly confirmed biblical details. Reliability does not remove the need for faith, but it means faith in the Bible is not blind.
