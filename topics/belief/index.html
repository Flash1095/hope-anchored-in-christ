Belief

Topic
Belief
Summary
Belief is more than agreeing with an idea; it is trusting a Person. Scripture calls us to believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and to stake our lives on that truth. Such belief receives forgiveness, gives new life, and shows itself in obedience.
