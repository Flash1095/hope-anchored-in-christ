Dignity

Topic
Dignity
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Dignity is the worth every person carries as one made in God's image, regardless of age, ability, status, or usefulness. It grounds the Christian defense of the unborn, the elderly, the disabled, and the stranger: no one is disposable because no one is an accident. Honoring dignity means treating every person as someone for whom Christ died.
