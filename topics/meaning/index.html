Meaning
Summary
Life's meaning is the question Ecclesiastes presses hardest: what lasts, what satisfies, what makes the brief years under the sun worth living? Apart from God, even wisdom, wealth, and work turn to vapor; with him, ordinary days gain weight and purpose. Scripture's answer is not a technique but a Person: we are made by God, for God, and find our meaning in fearing him, keeping his commandments, and enjoying his gifts as from his hand.
