Covenant unfaithfulness

Topic
Covenant unfaithfulness
Summary
Israel's story is shadowed by unfaithfulness — idols, injustice, and forgotten vows. The prophets name it plainly: the people broke the covenant their God had kept. Yet even unfaithfulness becomes a stage for grace, as God disciplines, restores, and finally keeps the covenant Himself in Christ.
Key Passages
Judges 2:11-15; Hosea 4:1-6; Jeremiah 11:10; Ezekiel 16:59
