Promises of God

Topic
Promises of God
Category
Theology
Summary
The promises of God are His sworn commitments scattered through Scripture - hundreds of them, covering provision, presence, forgiveness, guidance, and eternal life. Christians read them as a family inheritance: every promise finds its yes in Christ, and each one invites trust in a specific situation. The discipline is to claim promises as God gave them, without twisting them into guarantees He never made or ignoring the conditions He attached.
