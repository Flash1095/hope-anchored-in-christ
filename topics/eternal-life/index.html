Eternal Life

Topic
Eternal Life
Category
Theology
Summary
Eternal life is not merely life that goes on forever, but life in knowing God through Jesus Christ, begun now and completed in the resurrection. It is a gift, not a wage, received by faith and sealed by the Spirit. Eternal life transforms the present — freeing believers from the fear of death — and it will one day mean embodied life in a renewed creation.
