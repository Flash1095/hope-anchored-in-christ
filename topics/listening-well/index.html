Listening Well

Topic
Listening Well
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Listening well is the neglected half of love: giving full attention to another person without planning a reply, correcting, or turning the conversation to oneself. James urges believers to be quick to listen and slow to speak, and Jesus modeled listening that dignified the overlooked. Good listening is a form of hospitality, making room for another's story before offering answers.
Key Passages
Proverbs 18:13; James 1:19; Luke 10:38-42; Philippians 2:4
