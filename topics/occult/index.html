Occult

Topic
Occult
Category
Theology
Summary
The occult covers practices that seek hidden knowledge or power apart from God: divination, spiritism, magic, and astrology. Scripture forbids them consistently, not because they are harmless superstition but because they open doors to spiritual realities God has not given us to traffic in. The Christian answer is not fascination or fear but trust: God's Word and Spirit give all the guidance we need, and Christ's authority is greater than any power the occult claims.
