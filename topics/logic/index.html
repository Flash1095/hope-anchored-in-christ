Logic

Topic
Logic
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Logic is the disciplined study of sound reasoning: identifying valid arguments, exposing fallacies, and following evidence where it leads. Scripture commends reasoned persuasion — Isaiah invites Israel to reason together, and Paul argued daily in synagogues and marketplaces. Logic cannot by itself produce faith, but it serves faith by clarifying claims, testing arguments, and helping believers think clearly about God and His world.
Key Passages
Isaiah 1:18; Acts 17:2-3; Acts 18:4; Romans 12:2; 1 Peter 3:15
