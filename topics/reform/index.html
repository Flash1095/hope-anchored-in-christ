Reform

Topic
Reform
Category
Theology
Summary
Reform is the church's recurring work of returning to Scripture when tradition, power, or complacency have pulled it off course. From Josiah's rediscovered law to the Protestant Reformers' recovery of grace, reform movements share a pattern: a fresh encounter with God's Word, repentance for accumulated error, and costly resistance from those who benefit from the status quo. Reform reminds every generation that the church is always in need of renewal - and that renewal begins with repentance, not with rebranding.
