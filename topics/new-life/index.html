New Life

Topic
New Life
Category
Doctrine
Summary
New life is the fresh start God gives through faith in Christ: the old self buried, a new self raised. The New Testament describes it with several pictures - new birth, new creation, resurrection - all pointing to the same reality: forgiveness of sins and the Spirit's power to live differently. New life begins in a moment but grows over a lifetime, as believers learn to walk in step with the Spirit and put off the old patterns of sin.
