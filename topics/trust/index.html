Trust

Topic
Trust
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Trust is reliance on God's character when circumstances argue otherwise - the practical shape of faith. Proverbs warns against leaning on our own understanding, the Psalms model trust as refuge-taking, and Jesus sleeps in a storm His disciples fear, showing what trust looks like embodied. Trust grows the way all trust grows: through kept promises remembered, through small obediences, and through suffering that proves God faithful when nothing else holds.
