Relativism

Topic
Relativism
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Relativism is the claim that truth, especially moral truth, depends on the individual or culture - true for you, but not for me. It feels tolerant, but it collapses under its own weight: the claim that there is no absolute truth is itself stated absolutely, and it leaves no ground to condemn real evils. Christians answer with both conviction and grace: truth is one and knowable, because it is grounded in God's character, and that conviction frees us to listen patiently rather than forcing agreement.
