Faith and Reason

Topic
Faith and Reason
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Faith and reason are allies, not enemies, in the Christian vision: faith trusts what God has revealed, and reason helps us understand, defend, and apply it. The greatest Christian thinkers have used philosophy, logic, and evidence in service of the gospel, while insisting that reason is a servant, not a master, and that some truths exceed what unaided reason can reach. A faith that fears questions is fragile; a faith that welcomes them grows stronger.
Key Passages
Isaiah 1:18; Acts 17:2-3; Romans 12:2; 1 Peter 3:15; Jude 1:3
