Ministry

Topic
Ministry
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Ministry is service done in Christ's name, and the New Testament insists it belongs to the whole church, not only to professionals. Pastors equip, but every believer is gifted to build up the body: teaching, serving, giving, encouraging, and showing mercy. Healthy ministry flows from character before platform, from a life with God before work for God, and it is measured less by visibility than by faithfulness to the people entrusted to us.
