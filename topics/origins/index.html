Origins

Topic
Origins
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Origins asks where everything came from: the universe, life, and humanity itself. The Bible answers with a Creator who made all things good and purposeful, while Christians differ on how Genesis relates to scientific accounts of deep time and evolution. What unites orthodox Christians is the core confession: the universe is not an accident, human beings bear God's image, and creation's order reflects a rational Creator - convictions that made modern science itself possible.
