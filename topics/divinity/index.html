Divinity

Divinity refers to the nature of being God: eternal, self-existent, all-powerful, all-knowing, and perfectly holy. Christians confess one God whose divinity is shared fully by Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, and they insist that Jesus possesses true divinity, not a lesser or borrowed kind. To speak of divinity is to speak of the One before whom all worship and obedience are due.
