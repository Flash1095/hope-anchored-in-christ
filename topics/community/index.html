Community

Topic
Community
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Community is the shared life of God's people, who are called not merely to believe the same things but to belong to one another. The early church shared meals, possessions, prayers, and burdens, and the New Testament's one-another commands assume Christians live in close, committed fellowship. In a lonely age, the church's community is itself a witness to a gospel that makes strangers into family.
