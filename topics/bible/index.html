Summary
The Bible is the collection of sixty-six books, written over many centuries, that Christians receive as God's word in human words. It tells one unfolding story — creation, fall, redemption, and new creation — with Jesus Christ at its center. Christians read it to know God, to be shaped by His truth, and to find their place in His purposes.
