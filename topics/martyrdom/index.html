Topic
Martyrdom
Category
Theology
Summary
Martyrdom is witness sealed with death: believers who would rather die than deny Christ. The word itself means witness, and the New Testament treats martyrs not as victims of a failed cause but as conquerors whose blood, Tertullian said, is the seed of the church. From Stephen to the persecuted church today, martyrdom raises hard questions about courage, God's protection, and why faithfulness sometimes costs everything.
