Neighbor

Topic
Neighbor
Category
Christian Living
Summary
A neighbor, Jesus taught, is not a category of person but anyone in need whom we have the power to help - including the stranger and the enemy. The parable of the Good Samaritan redefines neighborliness from proximity to mercy: seeing, stopping, and spending yourself for someone else's good. Loving neighbor is how love for God becomes visible, and Scripture insists it includes the poor, the foreigner, the widow, and the outsider.
