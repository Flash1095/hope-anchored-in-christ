God's mercy

Topic
God's mercy
Summary
Mercy is God's compassion toward those who deserve judgment — His steady refusal to give us what our sins have earned. From the golden calf to the prodigal son, Scripture shows mercy triumphing again and again. Those who receive mercy are called to show it.
Key Passages
Exodus 34:6; Psalm 103:8-12; Ephesians 2:4-5; Luke 15:20-24
Related Books
