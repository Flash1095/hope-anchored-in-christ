Church growth

Topic
Church growth
Summary
The church grows as the gospel spreads and believers are added to the fellowship. Acts shows growth through preaching, prayer, shared life, and suffering, never through human technique alone. Healthy growth is both numerical and spiritual: more disciples, and deeper ones.
Key Passages
Acts 2:41-47; Acts 6:7; Acts 9:31; Ephesians 4:11-16
