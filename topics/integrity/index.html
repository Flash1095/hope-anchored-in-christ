Integrity

Topic
Integrity
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Integrity is wholeness of character: the same person in private and public, whose yes means yes and whose life matches their words. Scripture commends the blameless walk, not as sinless perfection but as honest, consistent faithfulness. Integrity is built in small decisions over long years, and it is the quiet credential that makes a believer's witness believable.
Key Passages
Psalm 15:1-5; Proverbs 11:3; Matthew 5:37; Titus 2:7-8; 1 Peter 3:16
