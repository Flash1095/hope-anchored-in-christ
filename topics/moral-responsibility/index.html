Moral Responsibility

Topic
Moral Responsibility
Category
Theology
Summary
Moral responsibility is the truth that people are answerable for their choices: we are not merely products of our circumstances, and our actions genuinely matter. Scripture holds this together with grace - we are responsible, yet unable to save ourselves - and with God's sovereignty, without letting either cancel the other. It shapes how Christians think about guilt, repentance, justice, and parenting: we can and must choose, and our choices form who we become.
