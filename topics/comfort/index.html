Comfort

Topic
Comfort
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Comfort is the consolation God gives His people in trouble, not by removing every sorrow but by His presence within it. Paul calls God the Father of mercies and God of all comfort, who comforts us so that we can comfort others. Christian comfort is honest about pain and confident about hope, pointing sufferers to a Savior who wept and to a future without tears.
Key Passages
Psalm 23:4; Isaiah 40:1-2; 2 Corinthians 1:3-7; Revelation 21:4
Related People
