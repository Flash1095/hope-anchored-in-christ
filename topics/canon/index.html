Canon
Category
Theology
Summary
The canon is the recognized list of books that make up the Bible, settled by the early church's recognition of apostolic and prophetic writings rather than by arbitrary decree. Christians believe the church discerned the canon under God's guidance, receiving books that bore the marks of divine authority and widespread use. Questions about disputed books are best answered by history: which writings did the earliest Christians treat as Scripture?
