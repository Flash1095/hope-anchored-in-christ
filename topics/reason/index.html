Reason

Topic
Reason
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Reason is the God-given capacity to think clearly, weigh evidence, and follow arguments where they lead. Christianity has always claimed that faith is not against reason: Paul reasoned in the synagogues, and the church's greatest thinkers argued that truth cannot contradict truth. Reason has limits - it cannot by itself grasp grace or resurrect the dead - but it is a servant of faith, clearing away bad arguments and helping believers love God with all their minds.
