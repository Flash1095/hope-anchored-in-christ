Revival

Topic
Revival
Category
Theology
Summary
Revival is a season when God stirs His people to renewed repentance, prayer, and mission - sometimes sweeping whole regions, as in the Great Awakenings. Scripture's revivals share a pattern: rediscovered truth, conviction of sin, earnest prayer, and fruit that lasts beyond the excitement. Christians pray for revival while remembering it cannot be manufactured: methods can prepare the ground, but only the Spirit gives the increase, and true revival always shows up in changed lives and renewed churches.
