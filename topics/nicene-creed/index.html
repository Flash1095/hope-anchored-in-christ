Nicene Creed

Topic
Nicene Creed
Category
Doctrine
Summary
The Nicene Creed is the church's ancient summary of the faith, forged at Nicaea in 325 and Constantinople in 381 to defend the full deity of Christ against Arianism. In a few hundred words it confesses one God, Jesus Christ as true God and true man, and the Holy Spirit as Lord and giver of life. Christians across traditions still recite it as a boundary of orthodoxy and a shared confession that predates every modern denomination.
