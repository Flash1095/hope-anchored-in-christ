Creation

Topic
Creation
Category
Theology
Summary
Creation is God's free act of making all things out of nothing by His word, declaring them good, and sustaining them by His power. It reveals His glory, wisdom, and kindness, and gives human beings, made in His image, both dignity and purpose. Caring for creation and marveling at it are fitting responses to a Creator who still upholds every star and sparrow.
