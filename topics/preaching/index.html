Preaching

Topic
Preaching
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Preaching is the public proclamation of God's Word, calling hearers to believe and obey. From the prophets to the apostles to the church today, preaching has carried a double weight: faithfully explaining the text and urgently applying it to real lives. Good preaching is not performance but stewardship - the preacher stands under the Word before standing before the people, and the goal is never admiration but transformation.
