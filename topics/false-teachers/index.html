False teachers

Topic
False teachers
Summary
False teachers are an old danger: voices within the church who twist truth for influence, money, or novelty. Scripture tells believers to test every teaching against the apostles' word and to watch the teacher's life as well as their lips. The best defense against false teaching is knowing the true one deeply.
