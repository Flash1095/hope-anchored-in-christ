Obedience

Topic
Obedience
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Obedience is doing what God asks because we trust the One who asks it. Scripture never treats it as a cold duty: in Deuteronomy it flows from remembering deliverance, in the Psalms from delight in God's law, and in John's Gospel from love for Jesus. Obedience is also where faith becomes visible - Abraham leaving home, Mary saying let it be, Jesus in Gethsemane - and where blessing, discipline, and formation all meet.
