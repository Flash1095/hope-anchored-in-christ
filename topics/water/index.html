Water

Topic
Water
Summary
Water appears at creation, in the Flood, at the Red Sea, and in water from the rock, as a picture of both judgment and life-giving salvation. The prophets promised cleansing water and rivers in the desert, and Jesus offered living water that becomes a spring welling up to eternal life. Baptism unites believers with Christ, and Revelation ends with the river of the water of life.
Key Passages
Genesis 1:2; Exodus 14:21-31; Exodus 17:6; Ezekiel 36:25; John 4:13-14; John 7:37-39; Revelation 22:1-2
