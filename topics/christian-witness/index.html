Christian Witness

Topic
Christian Witness
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Christian witness is the task of making Jesus known through words and life, so that others can see and hear the gospel. It combines clear testimony about what Christ has done with a life that makes that testimony believable, and it is carried out with gentleness and respect. Every believer is a witness, whether by speaking to a friend, serving a neighbor, or standing firm under pressure.
