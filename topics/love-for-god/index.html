Love for God

Topic
Love for God
Summary
Love for God is the first and greatest commandment: to love Him with all heart, soul, mind, and strength. It is not sentiment alone but loyalty shown in obedience, worship, and trust. We love because He first loved us, and that love spills over to every neighbor.
