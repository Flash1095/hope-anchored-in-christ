Definitions

Theology
Summary
Definitions matter in theology because words like grace, faith, and love carry precise biblical meanings that popular use often blurs. Careful definition guards against misunderstanding: grace is not permission to sin, faith is not wishful thinking, and love is not mere sentiment. The church defines its terms by Scripture's own usage, in community across time, so that believers can speak truth clearly to one another and to the world.
