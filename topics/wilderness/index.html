Wilderness

Topic
Wilderness
Summary
The wilderness is where God forms his people: Israel wandered there forty years, Elijah fled there in despair, and Jesus was tested there before his ministry began. It is a place of hunger and danger, but also of manna, of water from the rock, and of God's voice in the silence. No one chooses the wilderness, yet Scripture insists that God meets us there, and leads us out, humbled, fed, and ready.

Deuteronomy 8:2-3; 1 Kings 19:4-8; Matthew 4:1-4; Hosea 2:14-15
