Shelter in Christ

Topic
Shelter in Christ
Summary
To shelter in Christ is to hide in the safest place in the universe: united to him, we are covered by his righteousness, kept by his power, and held through every storm. The psalmist's fortress and the hen's wings both point here: to a refuge that is a person, not a place. Whatever threatens, the believer can say with Paul that nothing can separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord.

Psalm 91:1-2; Colossians 3:3; Romans 8:38-39; John 10:28-29
