Regeneration

Topic
Regeneration
Category
Doctrine
Summary
Regeneration is the Spirit's work of giving new spiritual life to a dead heart - the new birth Jesus described to Nicodemus. It is monergistic in Reformed language: God acts first, and our faith and repentance follow as the fruit, not the cause. Regeneration is instantaneous and invisible, but its effects are visible over time: new desires, new loves, and a new direction that theologians call sanctification.
