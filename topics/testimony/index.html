Testimony

Topic
Testimony
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Testimony is telling what God has done, in your life and in history, as evidence for the gospel. The New Testament treats ordinary believers' stories as powerful witness: the healed demoniac sent home to tell his friends, the Samaritan woman bringing her town to Jesus. Good testimony is specific, honest about the before and after, and centered on Christ rather than on the speaker - less look at me and more look at what the Lord has done.
