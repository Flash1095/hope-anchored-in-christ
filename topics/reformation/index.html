Reformation

Topic
Reformation
Category
Theology
Summary
The Reformation was the sixteenth-century movement that reshaped Western Christianity, sparked by Martin Luther's protest against indulgences and carried forward by reformers across Europe. Its rallying cries - Scripture alone, grace alone, faith alone, Christ alone, God's glory alone - recovered the gospel of free grace for millions and split the Western church. Its legacy is contested: renewal and division in the same event, and modern Christians inherit both the recovered truths and the responsibility to pursue unity without surrendering them.
