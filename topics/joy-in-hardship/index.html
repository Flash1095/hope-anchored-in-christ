Joy in hardship

Topic
Joy in hardship
Summary
Joy in hardship sounds impossible until you see Paul singing in prison or Habakkuk rejoicing with no harvest in sight. Christian joy does not depend on circumstances but on Christ, who is present in them. It is a stubborn gladness that suffering cannot cancel.
Key Passages
James 1:2-4; Philippians 4:4; Habakkuk 3:17-18; Acts 16:25
Related Books
