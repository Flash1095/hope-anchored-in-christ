Family

Topic
Family
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Family is God's first school of love, where children learn trust, obedience, and forgiveness, and where adults learn sacrifice. Scripture honors marriage and parenthood, commands children to honor parents, and warns parents not to provoke their children. The church extends family beyond blood, so that the lonely find a home and every believer has brothers and sisters in Christ.

Genesis 2:24; Deuteronomy 6:6-7; Ephesians 5:25-33; Ephesians 6:1-4; Psalm 127:3-5
