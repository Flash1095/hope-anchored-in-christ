Miracles

Topic
Miracles
Category
Theology
Summary
Miracles are God's acts that break the ordinary pattern of nature to reveal His power and purposes. In Scripture they cluster around decisive moments - the exodus, the ministries of Elijah and Elisha, Jesus, and the apostles - and they function as signs, not spectacles: the feeding of thousands points to the Bread of Life, and healings announce the kingdom breaking in. Christians disagree about whether miraculous gifts continue today in the same way, but agree that God remains free to act and that every miracle points beyond itself to Christ.
