End Times
Category
End Times
Summary
The end times describe the last chapter of history: Christ's return, the resurrection of the dead, the final judgment, and the new heaven and new earth. Christians disagree on details and timelines, but agree on the essentials: Jesus will return bodily, evil will be judged, and God's people will live with Him forever. The proper response to end-times teaching is watchful hope, not fearful speculation.
