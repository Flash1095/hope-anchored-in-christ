Faith

Topic
Faith
Category
Theology
Summary
Faith is trust in God and His promises, receiving Christ and resting on Him alone for salvation. It is a gift of God that unites believers to Jesus and inevitably produces love, obedience, and endurance. The heroes of Hebrews 11 show that faith sees the unseen and acts on God's word even when circumstances argue otherwise.
