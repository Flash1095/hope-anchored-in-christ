Wandering and Return

Topic
Wandering and Return
Summary
Wandering and return is the shape of Israel's story and of every human heart: God's people strayed into the wilderness, into exile, into idolatry, and God kept bringing them back. The wilderness wanderings taught dependence, the exile taught humility, and every return taught grace. Our own wanderings need not end in ruin, because the God who sought Israel still seeks wanderers today.
