Living faith

Topic
Living faith
Summary
Living faith is faith with hands and feet: trusting God and acting on that trust, as Abraham did when he left home and Rahab did when she hid the spies. James insists that faith without works is dead — not because works save, but because real trust always moves. Living faith obeys, gives, forgives, and goes.
