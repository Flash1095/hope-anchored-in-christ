History

Topic
History
Category
Bible Story
Summary
History is the arena of God's work: the Bible presents redemption not as myth but as events in time, with dates, kings, and places that can be investigated. Remembering history is a spiritual discipline in Scripture, commanded again and again so that each generation knows what God has done. Christians study history with humility, learning from both the faithfulness and the failures of those who went before.
