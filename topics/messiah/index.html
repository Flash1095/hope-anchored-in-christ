Messiah

Topic
Messiah
Category
Theology
Summary
Messiah means anointed one, the promised king, priest, and prophet God would send to rescue His people. Israel waited centuries for this figure, and Christians confess that Jesus of Nazareth is He: anointed at His baptism, crucified as the suffering servant, and raised as the reigning Lord. Understanding the Messiah means reading the Old Testament's promises and the Gospels' claims together, since Jesus deliberately fulfilled some expectations and overturned others.
