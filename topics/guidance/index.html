Guidance

Topic
Guidance
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Guidance is God's direction of His people's decisions, given through Scripture, the Spirit's wisdom, counsel, prayer, and providence. The Bible promises that God guides the humble, but it rarely promises a voice from heaven for every choice; more often guidance looks like wisdom applied faithfully. Believers need not fear missing God's will through one wrong turn, because God is able to redirect and redeem.
Key Passages
Psalm 32:8; Proverbs 3:5-6; Isaiah 30:21; James 1:5; Romans 12:2
