Kingdom of God

Topic
Kingdom of God
Category
Theology
Summary
The kingdom of God is God's reign breaking into the world through Jesus, already present wherever Christ is obeyed and not yet complete until He returns. Jesus announced it, taught it in parables, and demonstrated it in healings and deliverance. Believers enter it by new birth and live now as citizens of a kingdom that will never end.
