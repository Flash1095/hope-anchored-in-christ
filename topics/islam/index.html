Islam

Topic
Islam
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Islam is the monotheistic faith founded on the Quran and the teachings of Muhammad, professing one God, Allah, and a lineage through Abraham and Ishmael. Christians and Muslims share belief in one Creator, in prophets, and in final judgment, but differ decisively on the identity of Jesus: Christians confess Him as the divine Son and crucified Savior, while Islam honors Him as a prophet and denies His divinity and crucifixion. Fair Christian engagement describes Islam accurately, loves Muslim neighbors genuinely, and presents Christ clearly without caricature or coercion.
