Abolition

Topic
Abolition
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Abolition is the movement to end slavery, driven in Britain and America largely by Christians convinced that every person bears God's image. Figures like William Wilberforce and Harriet Beecher Stowe argued from Scripture that owning people contradicts the gospel's claim that Christ died for all. The abolition story shows how biblical conviction, sustained over decades, can overturn even deeply entrenched injustice.
