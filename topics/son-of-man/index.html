Son of Man

Topic
Son of Man
Summary
Son of Man was Jesus' favorite name for himself, drawn from Daniel's vision of one like a son of man coming with the clouds to receive an everlasting kingdom. In Jesus the title holds both glory and suffering together: the Son of Man has authority to forgive sins, came to seek and save the lost, and must be lifted up like the serpent in the wilderness. To confess him as Son of Man is to confess a King who rules by laying down his life.
Key Passages
Daniel 7:13-14; Mark 2:10; Luke 19:10; Mark 14:61-62
