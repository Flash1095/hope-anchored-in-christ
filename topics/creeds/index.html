Category

Summary
Creeds are short, shared statements of faith — like the Apostles' Creed and the Nicene Creed — that summarize what Christians believe about God, Christ, and salvation. They arose to guard the gospel against error and to give believers words to confess together. Creeds do not replace Scripture, but they help the church recognize faithful teaching and pass it on intact.
