Resurrection

Topic
Resurrection
Category
Apologetics
Summary
The resurrection is God's raising of Jesus from the dead on the third day, vindicating Him as Lord and guaranteeing believers' future resurrection. It rests on the empty tomb, eyewitness testimony, and the transformed lives of the first disciples. If Christ has been raised, death is not the end and hope is anchored in fact, not wishful thinking.
