Priesthood

Topic
Priesthood
Summary
In the Old Testament, priests stood between God and his people, offering sacrifices and blessing in God's name; Aaron and his sons carried this holy, costly calling. The New Testament announces a better priesthood: Jesus, our great high priest, offered himself once for all, and now all believers are a royal priesthood with direct access to God. We no longer need a human mediator, because the Mediator lives forever to intercede for us.
