Manipulation

Topic
Manipulation
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Manipulation is influence that bypasses honesty and consent, steering people toward a choice they would not freely make. Scripture contrasts it with persuasion that tells the truth and respects the other person's freedom: Paul insists he did not use flattery or hidden motives, and Jesus never coerced belief. Christians are called to spot manipulation - in leaders, in marketing, in relationships, even in their own hearts - and to replace it with plain speech, fair dealing, and respect for conscience.
Key Passages
Matthew 23:25-28; 2 Corinthians 4:2; 1 Thessalonians 2:3-6; James 3:17
Related People
Paul; Jesus
