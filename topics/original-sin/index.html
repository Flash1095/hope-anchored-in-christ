Original Sin

Topic
Original Sin
Category
Doctrine
Summary
Original sin is the doctrine that Adam's first sin in Eden affected all humanity, so every person is born with a fallen nature inclined away from God. It explains why sin is universal and why we need a Savior rather than just a better example. In Christ, the second Adam, God provides the righteousness Adam lost.

Genesis 3; Romans 5:12-21; Psalm 51:5
