Psychology

Topic
Psychology
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Psychology is the scientific study of mind and behavior, and Christians ask how its findings relate to Scripture's wisdom about human nature. Much of psychology describes God's design accurately - how habits form, how trauma wounds, how relationships heal - while its theories about ultimate meaning sometimes conflict with biblical anthropology. The Christian approach is integration without surrender: receive genuine insight gratefully, test every claim against Scripture, and remember that people are souls as well as systems.
