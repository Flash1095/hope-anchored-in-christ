Assurance

Topic
Assurance
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Assurance is the settled confidence that a person who trusts in Christ truly belongs to God and will be kept by Him. It rests not on the strength of our feelings but on God's promises, Christ's finished work, and the Spirit's witness in the heart. Doubt does not necessarily mean unbelief; assurance grows as believers look away from themselves and toward Jesus.
