Son of God

Topic
Son of God
Summary
Son of God is the New Testament's great confession about Jesus: he is not merely a prophet or teacher but God's own Son, sharing the Father's nature and authority. The title rings out at his baptism, in Peter's confession, on the cross from a Roman centurion, and in John's Gospel from first verse to last. To call Jesus the Son of God is to worship him and to trust that in him we meet God himself.
Key Passages
Matthew 3:16-17; Matthew 16:15-17; Mark 15:39; John 1:1-18
