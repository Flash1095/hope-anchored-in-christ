Promise

Topic
Promise
Category
Theology
Summary
A promise is a word you can build a life on, and the Bible is held together by God's promises: to Abraham, to David, to Israel, and finally to all who trust in Christ. Unlike human promises, God's cannot fail, because they rest on His character rather than on circumstances. The Christian life is learning to live between promise and fulfillment - trusting what God has said while waiting for what He has not yet done.
