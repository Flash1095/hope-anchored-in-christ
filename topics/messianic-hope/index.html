Messianic hope

Topic
Messianic hope
Summary
Long before Jesus came, God's people lived on promises: a seed who would crush the serpent, a son of David whose kingdom would never end, a suffering servant who would bear our griefs. That hope sustained exiles, prophets, and ordinary families through centuries of waiting. In Jesus the hope arrived, and because he rose, our hope now looks forward to his return and the renewal of all things.
