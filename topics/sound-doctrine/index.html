Sound doctrine

Topic
Sound doctrine
Summary
Sound doctrine is teaching that is healthy: true to Scripture, centered on Christ, and producing godly lives. Paul urged Timothy to hold the pattern of sound words and to guard the deposit, because what we believe shapes how we live and whom we trust. False teaching spreads like disease; sound doctrine nourishes, steadies, and protects the church.
Key Passages
1 Timothy 4:6; 2 Timothy 1:13-14; Titus 2:1; 2 Timothy 3:16-17
