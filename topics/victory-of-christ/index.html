Victory of Christ

Topic
Victory of Christ
Summary
Christ's victory was won where defeat seemed certain: on the cross he disarmed the powers, in the tomb he broke death's grip, and in his resurrection he announced that the war is over even while battles remain. Believers share that victory now, over sin, over fear, over the accuser, and will see it complete when he returns. To live in his victory is to fight from triumph, not for it.

Colossians 2:15; 1 Corinthians 15:54-57; John 16:33; Revelation 19:11-16
