Atonement

Topic
Atonement
Category
Doctrine
Summary
Atonement is Christ's work on the cross reconciling sinners to a holy God, bearing the penalty our sin deserved. Old Testament sacrifices pictured it for centuries, but only Jesus' once-for-all offering truly takes away sin. Because of the atonement, believers can draw near to God with confidence and peace.
