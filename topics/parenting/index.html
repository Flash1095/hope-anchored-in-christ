Parenting

Topic
Parenting
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Parenting is the God-given calling to raise children in the discipline and instruction of the Lord, teaching them God's words in everyday life. It balances firm guidance with tenderness, aiming not just at good behavior but at hearts that know and love God. Parents depend on the same grace they preach, pointing their children to a perfect Father.
