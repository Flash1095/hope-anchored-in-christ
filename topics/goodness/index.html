Goodness

Topic
Goodness
Category
Theology
Summary
Goodness is both an attribute of God — He is good in Himself and the source of every good gift — and a fruit of the Spirit in believers. God's goodness means His purposes toward His people are kind, even when His ways are hard to understand. Christians reflect that goodness by doing good to all, especially to those of the household of faith.
Key Passages
Psalm 34:8; Psalm 145:9; James 1:17; Galatians 6:9-10; Romans 8:28
Related People
