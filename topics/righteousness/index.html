Righteousness

Topic
Righteousness
Category
Doctrine
Summary
Righteousness is right standing before God and right living before people - two sides of one word in Scripture. No one achieves the first by the second: God credits Christ's righteousness to believers by faith, and that gift then produces a life that increasingly does what is right. The prophets and apostles agree: righteousness is both a status received and a calling pursued, and a church that separates them has misunderstood both.
