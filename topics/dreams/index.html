Dreams

Topic
Dreams
Summary
God sometimes spoke through dreams — to Joseph in Egypt, to Daniel in Babylon, to Joseph the carpenter in Bethlehem. Dreams in Scripture are never treated as private horoscopes; they are tested by God's word and often carry His guidance for others. They remind us that God speaks in ways we do not control, and His messages always serve His purposes.

Genesis 37:5-11; Genesis 41:1-36; Daniel 2:1-23; Matthew 1:20-24; Matthew 2:13-15
