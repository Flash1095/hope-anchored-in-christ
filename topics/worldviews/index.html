Worldviews

Topic
Worldviews
Category
Apologetics
Summary
A worldview is the set of assumptions through which a person interprets everything: what is real, what is true, what is right, and what humans are for. Everyone has one, whether examined or not, and the Christian worldview - creation, fall, redemption, restoration - offers a coherent alternative to naturalism, pantheism, and relativism. Christians study worldviews to understand their neighbors, to test their own thinking, and to show how the gospel answers the deepest questions every worldview must face.
