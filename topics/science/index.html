Science

Topic
Science
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Science is the disciplined study of the natural world: observing, testing, and building models that predict how things work. Christians have historically led in science because their worldview expects an orderly creation made by a rational God - and because studying it is a form of worship, not a threat. Science answers how questions with power and precision; it cannot by itself answer why questions about purpose, value, or God, which is why the conflict is often between philosophies, not between science and faith.
