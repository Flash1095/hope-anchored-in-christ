Mercy

Topic
Mercy
Category
Theology
Summary
Mercy is God's compassion toward the miserable and guilty, withholding the judgment we deserve and moving to relieve our distress. From Exodus to the Gospels, God delights in mercy and calls His people blessed when they show it to others. Every believer lives daily on mercy, receiving fresh compassion each morning.
