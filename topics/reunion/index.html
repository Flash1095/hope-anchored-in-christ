Reunion

Topic
Reunion
Summary
The Bible is full of reunions that picture God's heart: Jacob embracing Esau, Joseph revealing himself to his brothers, the father running to his prodigal son. Each reunion is a small resurrection: what was lost is found, what was dead is alive. The greatest reunion is still to come, when God's scattered people are gathered from every nation to one table with the Lamb.
Key Passages
Genesis 33:4; Genesis 45:14-15; Luke 15:20; Revelation 7:9-10
