Leadership

Topic
Leadership
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Biblical leadership is shepherding, not lording: leading by serving, character, and example as Jesus did when He washed His disciples' feet. God raises leaders to equip His people, guard the truth, and care for the flock at cost to themselves. The measure of a leader is not platform or power but faithfulness and love for people.
