God's Sovereignty

Topic
God's Sovereignty
Category
Theology
Summary
God's sovereignty is His absolute rule over all things: He does whatever He pleases, and no purpose of His can be thwarted. Scripture holds this together with human responsibility and the reality of evil, without fully explaining the mystery. For believers, sovereignty is not a puzzle to solve but a rock to stand on, especially when life feels out of control.
Key Passages
Psalm 115:3; Isaiah 46:9-10; Daniel 4:35; Romans 9:19-21; Ephesians 1:11
