Deliverance (Exodus)

Topic
Deliverance (Exodus)
Summary
God heard Israel's groaning in Egypt and delivered them with a mighty hand through Moses, the Passover, and the parting of the sea. The Exodus became Israel's defining story of salvation, retold at every Passover. The New Testament presents Jesus' work as the greater Exodus, delivering us from slavery to sin and death.
Key Passages
Exodus 3:7-10; Exodus 14:13-31; Deuteronomy 7:8; Luke 9:31; 1 Corinthians 10:1-11
