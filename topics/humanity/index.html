Humanity

Topic
Humanity
Category
Theology
Summary
Humanity is the race God made in His image and crowned with glory, given stewardship over creation and made for communion with Him. The fall fractured that vocation but did not erase the image, and Christ, the true man, restores what Adam lost. Every human being therefore carries a dignity no failure can remove and a destiny no eye has fully seen.
