Strength in weakness

Topic
Strength in weakness
Summary
God's strength shows up most clearly where ours runs out: Paul pleaded three times for his thorn to go, and heard, My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness. Weakness is therefore not the enemy of ministry but its doorway, because it forces us to lean on Christ instead of ourselves. The cross is the pattern: apparent defeat that became the world's salvation.

2 Corinthians 12:9-10; Isaiah 40:29-31; Hebrews 11:34; Philippians 4:13
