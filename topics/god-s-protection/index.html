God's Protection

Topic
God's Protection
Category
Christian Living
Summary
God's protection is His care over His people in danger, promised in images of refuge, fortress, shepherd, and wings. The Psalms are full of prayers for protection, and believers have always found God faithful — though protection does not always mean escape from harm, as the martyrs testify. Ultimate protection is eschatological: no one can snatch God's people from His hand, whatever happens in this life.
Key Passages
Psalm 91:1-4; Psalm 121:1-8; Isaiah 43:1-2; John 10:27-29; Romans 8:38-39
