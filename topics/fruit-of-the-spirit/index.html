Fruit of the Spirit

Topic
Fruit of the Spirit
Summary
The fruit of the Spirit is the character Jesus produces in those who walk with Him: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control. It cannot be manufactured by effort alone; it grows as believers abide in Christ. Where the Spirit rules, the fruit appears.
Key Passages
Galatians 5:22-25; John 15:4-5; Romans 8:5-6; Ephesians 5:8-10
