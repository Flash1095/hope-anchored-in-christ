Old Testament

Topic
Old Testament
Category
Theology
Summary
The Old Testament is the first three-quarters of the Christian Bible: law, history, poetry, and prophecy spanning from creation to the return from exile. Christians read it as God's own story, not as background material - the promises, patterns, and problems that the New Testament claims Jesus fulfills. Reading it well means honoring its genres, its ancient context, and its central arc: a holy God pursuing a wandering people.
