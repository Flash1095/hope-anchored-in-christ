Stereotypes

Topic
Stereotypes
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Stereotypes are shortcuts the mind takes about whole groups of people - sometimes with a grain of truth, often with a load of error, always at the cost of seeing individuals. Scripture confronts them directly: Jonah's prejudice against Nineveh, the disciples' contempt for Samaritans, and James's warning against favoritism all show how group labels blind us to neighbors. The gospel answer is particular love: every person bears God's image and deserves to be known before being judged.
