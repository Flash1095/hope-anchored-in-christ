Ancient Israel

Topic
Ancient Israel
Category
Bible Story
Summary
Ancient Israel was the covenant nation descended from Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, called to be a light to the nations. Its history runs from the exodus and the giving of the Law through the monarchy, exile, and return, and it forms the setting for most of the Old Testament. Understanding ancient Israel — its worship, kings, prophets, and failures — is essential for reading the Bible as the story of God's redeeming work.
