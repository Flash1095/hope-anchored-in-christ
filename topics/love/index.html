Love

Topic
Love
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Love is the defining mark of God Himself and the greatest commandment for His people: to love God fully and love neighbors as ourselves. At the cross we see love's true measure, not that we loved God, but that He loved us and sent His Son. The Spirit pours God's love into believers' hearts so they can love sacrificially, truthfully, and without fear.
