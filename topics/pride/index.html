Pride

Topic
Pride
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Pride is the inflated sense of self that puts us at the center where God belongs - the root sin behind the fall and the quiet engine of most others. Scripture warns that God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble, and it prescribes the cures: remembering we are creatures, receiving everything as gift, and serving others. Humility is not thinking less of ourselves but thinking of ourselves less, because our eyes are fixed on Christ.
