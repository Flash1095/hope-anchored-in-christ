Loyalty

Topic
Loyalty
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Loyalty is steadfast faithfulness to God and to others through changing circumstances, the quality Ruth showed Naomi and Jonathan showed David. It is tested most when loyalty costs something — when a friend fails, a cause falters, or obedience is inconvenient. Christian loyalty is ultimately loyalty to Christ, which orders every lesser loyalty and never asks believers to be loyal to wrongdoing.
