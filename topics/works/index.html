Works

Topic
Works
Summary
Works in Scripture are both warned against and commanded: we are not saved by works, yet we are saved for good works, created in Christ Jesus to walk in them. Paul and James agree: faith without works is dead, and works without faith are empty. Good works are therefore not the price of salvation but its fruit: the visible life of a people already loved, already forgiven, already free.
