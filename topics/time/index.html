Time

Topic
Time
Summary
Time is God's gift and God's stewardship: brief as a breath, yet long enough to know him, love our neighbors, and finish the work he gives. Scripture urges us to number our days, to redeem the time, and to remember that there is a season for everything under heaven. Eternity gives time its weight: because forever is real, today matters more, not less.
Key Passages
Psalm 90:12; Ecclesiastes 3:1-8; Ephesians 5:15-16; James 4:14
