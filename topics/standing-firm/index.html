Standing firm

Topic
Standing firm
Summary
Standing firm is holding your ground when faith is pressured: Paul told the Ephesians to put on God's armor and stand, and told the Corinthians to stand firm in the faith and be strong. Firmness is not stubbornness but rootedness: planted deep in truth, steadied by prayer, and supported by brothers and sisters who stand beside you. The one who stands firm to the end, Jesus promised, will be saved.
Key Passages
Ephesians 6:13-14; 1 Corinthians 16:13; Philippians 4:1; Matthew 24:13
