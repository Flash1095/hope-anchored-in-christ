Grief

Topic
Grief
Summary
Grief is honored in Scripture, not hurried past: psalms of lament, Jesus weeping at Lazarus's tomb, and the promise that God will wipe away every tear. Christian grief is real sorrow held alongside real hope — we grieve, but not as those who have no hope. God is near to the brokenhearted.
Key Passages
Psalm 34:18; John 11:35; 2 Samuel 12:16-23; Revelation 21:4
