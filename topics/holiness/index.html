Holiness

Topic
Holiness
Category
Theology
Summary
Holiness is God's absolute purity and set-apartness, and the calling of His people to reflect His character. God declares believers holy in Christ and then commands them to pursue holiness in thought, word, and deed. Without holiness no one will see the Lord, yet it is God Himself who makes His people holy.
