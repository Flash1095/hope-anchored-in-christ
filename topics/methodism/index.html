Topic
Methodism
Category
Theology
Summary
Methodism is the movement born from John Wesley's preaching in eighteenth-century England, shaped by disciplined methods of prayer, study, and small-group accountability. Its emphases - grace available to all, holiness of heart and life, and faith working through love - spread through revival preaching and the circuit riders who carried the gospel across frontiers. Methodism reminds the church that structure can serve passion, and that personal holiness and social compassion belong together.
