Adam and Eve

Topic
Adam and Eve
Category
Bible Story
Summary
Adam and Eve are the first humans in Genesis, created in God's image and placed in the garden of Eden to work it and keep it. Their disobedience in eating the forbidden fruit brought sin and death into the world, yet God immediately promised a deliverer who would crush the serpent. Their story explains both human dignity and human brokenness, and why every person needs the second Adam, Jesus Christ.
