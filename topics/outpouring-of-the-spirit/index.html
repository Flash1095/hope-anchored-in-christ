Outpouring of the Spirit

Topic
Outpouring of the Spirit
Summary
At Pentecost the promised Spirit fell like wind and fire, filling ordinary disciples with boldness to speak of Jesus in every tongue. The outpouring Joel foresaw had come: sons and daughters prophesying, old men dreaming dreams, God's presence poured out on all flesh. The same Spirit still fills the church today, giving gifts for service and power for witness until Christ returns.
Key Passages
Joel 2:28-29; Acts 2:1-4; Acts 2:17-18; Acts 10:44-46
