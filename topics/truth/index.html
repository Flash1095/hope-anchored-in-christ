Truth

Topic
Truth
Category
Theology
Summary
Truth is what corresponds to reality, and in Scripture it is ultimately personal: Jesus calls Himself the truth, and God's Word is described as truth itself. That makes truth-telling a moral duty and truth-seeking a spiritual one - Christians do not get to invent their own facts, and they do not get to hide behind relativism when the truth is costly. The church's witness depends on it: a gospel proclaimed with lies, exaggerations, or half-truths undermines the very message it carries.
