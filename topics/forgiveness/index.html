Forgiveness

Topic
Forgiveness
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Forgiveness is God's pardon for sin through Christ and the believer's call to release others from the debts they owe us. Scripture links receiving mercy with showing mercy, not because we earn God's favor, but because forgiven people become forgiving people. Forgiveness does not deny the hurt; it entrusts justice to God and opens the door to healing.
