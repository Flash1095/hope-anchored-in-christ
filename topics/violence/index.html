Violence

Topic
Violence
Category
Theology
Summary
Violence in Scripture is a hard problem Christians must face honestly: the Old Testament contains wars commanded by God, and the New Testament reveals a Savior who absorbs violence rather than inflicting it. Interpreters differ on how to hold these together - some stress progressive revelation, others divine judgment, others the ancient context - but all orthodox readings agree on the trajectory: a world broken by violence, a cross that ends its cycle, and a kingdom where swords become plowshares.
