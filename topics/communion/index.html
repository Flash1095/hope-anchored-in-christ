Communion

Topic
Communion
Category
Doctrine
Summary
Communion, or the Lord's Supper, is the meal Jesus instituted on the night before His death, in which believers eat bread and drink wine in remembrance of Him. Churches differ on exactly how Christ is present in the meal, but all agree it proclaims His death, nourishes faith, and binds believers to one another. It is both a backward look to the cross and a forward look to the marriage supper of the Lamb.
