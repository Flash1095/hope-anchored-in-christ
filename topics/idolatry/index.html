Idolatry

Topic
Idolatry
Category
Theology
Summary
Idolatry is worship directed to anything other than God — statues in the ancient world, but also money, success, romance, nation, and self in every age. The prophets exposed idols as empty: they cannot see, hear, or save, and those who make them become like them. The cure is not willpower but renewed worship, loving God above all until lesser loves fall into their proper place.
