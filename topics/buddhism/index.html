Buddhism

Topic
Buddhism
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Buddhism is the tradition founded on the teachings of Siddhartha Gautama, the Buddha, which diagnoses human suffering as the fruit of craving and prescribes the Eightfold Path toward release. Christians can appreciate Buddhism's seriousness about suffering and self-discipline while disagreeing at decisive points: the Bible teaches a personal Creator, the reality of the self, and salvation by grace through Christ rather than by self-effort. Fair engagement means describing Buddhism as its followers understand it, then presenting Jesus with grace.
