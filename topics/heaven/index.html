Heaven

Topic
Heaven
Category
End Times
Summary
Heaven is God's dwelling and the believer's promised home, perfected in the new heaven and new earth where God wipes away every tear. It is not clouds and harps but a renewed creation of worship, work, reunion, and unbroken fellowship with God. The hope of heaven gives Christians courage to endure loss and live generously now.
