Conversion

Topic
Conversion
Category
Theology
Summary
Conversion is the turning of a person from sin and self-rule to God through repentance and faith in Christ. It may be sudden, like Paul's on the Damascus road, or gradual, like a slow dawn, but its marks are consistent: a new allegiance, a new direction, and a new life. Conversion is God's work from first to last, and it is the doorway into the Christian life, not its finish line.
