Superiority of Christ

Topic
Superiority of Christ
Summary
Hebrews argues from first to last that Jesus is superior: superior to angels, to Moses, to Aaron's priesthood, and to every sacrifice that came before. He is the radiance of God's glory and the exact imprint of his nature, the mediator of a better covenant with better promises. To see Christ's superiority is to stop drifting back to shadows and to hold fast to the substance who is Christ himself.
Key Passages
Hebrews 1:3-4; Hebrews 3:3; Hebrews 7:26-27; Hebrews 8:6
