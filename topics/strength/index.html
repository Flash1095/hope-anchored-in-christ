Strength

Topic
Strength
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Strength in Scripture is rarely self-sufficiency: it is God's power made perfect in human weakness, the quiet endurance of people who keep going because He keeps them. Samson had muscle and failed; Paul had a thorn and triumphed, because strength that depends on God does not run out when our own does. Christians find strength in the same places Israel did - God's promises, His people, and the honest admission that we cannot do this alone.
