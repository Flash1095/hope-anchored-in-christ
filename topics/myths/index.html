Myths

Topic
Myths
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Myths are traditional stories that explain the world, and the Bible's relationship to them is more interesting than simple rejection. Some biblical stories share shapes with ancient myths - floods, heroes, creation battles - but retell them with a decisive difference: one Creator, a good creation, and a God who acts in real history. Christians also ask whether myths can carry truth: C. S. Lewis called the gospel a myth become fact, a story with myth's power that actually happened.
