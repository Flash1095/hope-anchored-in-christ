Kingdoms

Topic
Kingdoms
Summary
Scripture contrasts the kingdoms of this world — built on power, wealth, and pride — with the kingdom of God, which lasts forever. Empires rise and fall, from Babel to Rome, but Daniel saw a kingdom that would never be destroyed. Believers live as citizens of the lasting kingdom while sojourning in temporary ones.
Key Passages
Daniel 2:44; Daniel 7:13-14; Revelation 11:15; John 18:36
