Beauty

Topic
Beauty
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Beauty is a gift from the Creator, woven into the world from the first sunrise to the craftsmanship of the tabernacle. Scripture treats beauty as something to enjoy with gratitude, not to worship, and as a pointer to the glory of the God who made it. The Christian vision of beauty reaches its height in Christ, whose character is beautiful even in suffering, and in the new creation where all things will be made lovely.
