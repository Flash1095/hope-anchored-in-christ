Allegory

Topic
Allegory
Category
Theology
Summary
Allegory is a way of reading a story in which characters and events stand for deeper spiritual realities beyond the literal sense. The Bible itself uses allegory at times, as when Paul treats Hagar and Sarah as pictures of two covenants, but allegory becomes dangerous when it replaces the plain meaning of the text. Used with restraint and anchored in the passage's own context, it can illuminate; untethered from context, it can make a text mean anything.
