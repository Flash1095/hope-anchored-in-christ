Angels

Topic
Angels
Category
Theology
Summary
Angels are created spiritual beings who worship God, carry out His commands, and minister to His people. Scripture shows them as messengers, guardians, and warriors, yet always pointing attention to God rather than themselves. Believers respect angels biblically without worshiping them, rejoicing that they rejoice over every sinner who repents.
