Repentance

Topic
Repentance
Category
Doctrine
Summary
Repentance is the turning of the whole person - mind, heart, and direction - away from sin and toward God. It begins with honest recognition, includes sorrow for sin itself rather than merely its consequences, and proves itself in changed behavior: Zacchaeus repaid what he stole, and John the Baptist demanded fruit worthy of repentance. Repentance is not a one-time event but the rhythm of the Christian life, and it is always met by a Father who runs to meet returning children.
