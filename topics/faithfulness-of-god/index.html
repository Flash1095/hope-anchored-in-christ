Faithfulness of God

Topic
Faithfulness of God
Category
Theology
Summary
The faithfulness of God is His steady, covenant-keeping reliability: what He promises, He performs, even when His people fail. Israel's history is a long record of human unfaithfulness met by divine faithfulness, and the cross is its supreme proof, where God kept His ancient promise at infinite cost. Believers stake their lives on this attribute, because a faithful God can be trusted in darkness as well as in light.
