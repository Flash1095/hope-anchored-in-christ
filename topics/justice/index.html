Justice

Topic
Justice
Category
Theology
Summary
Justice is God's righteous character in action: He always does what is right, defends the vulnerable, and will judge evil fairly. Scripture calls God's people to do justice, love mercy, and walk humbly, reflecting their just King in a broken world. At the cross, God's justice and mercy meet, as sin is punished and sinners are pardoned.
