Missions

Topic
Missions
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Missions is the organized work of taking the gospel across cultures and languages, from the apostle Paul's journeys to today's missionaries and translators. It has always involved more than preaching: learning languages, respecting cultures, planting churches, and serving communities. The history of missions includes both remarkable sacrifice and real failures, and modern missions tries to learn from both - partnering with local believers rather than replacing them.
