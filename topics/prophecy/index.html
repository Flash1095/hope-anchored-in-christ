Prophecy

Topic
Prophecy
Category
Bible Story
Summary
Prophecy is God's word spoken through His chosen messengers to warn, comfort, and reveal His plans, including promises fulfilled in Jesus. True prophecy always agrees with God's character and often calls His people back to covenant faithfulness. Studying prophecy helps believers recognize Christ in all Scripture and live in readiness for what God has promised.
