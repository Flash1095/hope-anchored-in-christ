New heart

Topic
New heart
Summary
God promised through Ezekiel to remove hearts of stone and give hearts of flesh, writing his law within and putting his Spirit in his people. A new heart is not self-improvement but resurrection: God replaces rebellion with responsiveness, so that we love what he loves and turn from what we once loved. This is the inward miracle behind every true conversion and every lasting change.

Ezekiel 36:26-27; Jeremiah 31:33; 2 Corinthians 3:3; Psalm 51:10
