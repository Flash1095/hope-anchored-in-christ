Conquest

Topic
Conquest
Summary
The conquest of Canaan was God's gift of the land He had promised, won not by Israel's strength but by His presence. Yet the conquest was incomplete, and the land itself pointed forward to a greater rest. The battles remind us that God's promises are sure, and that victory belongs to the Lord.

Joshua 1:6-9; Joshua 6:16-20; Judges 2:16-19; Hebrews 11:30
