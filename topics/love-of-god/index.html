Love of God

Topic
Love of God
Category
Theology
Summary
The love of God is His steadfast, self-giving commitment to His people, shown most clearly in sending His Son while we were still sinners. It is not sentimental indulgence: God's love disciplines, judges evil, and refuses to abandon. Believers live from this love, not for it — secure enough to love others because they are already loved.
