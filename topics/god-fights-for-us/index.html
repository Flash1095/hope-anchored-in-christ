God Fights for Us

Topic
God Fights for Us
Summary
When Israel stood trapped at the Red Sea, Moses told them to stand still and watch the Lord fight for them. Again and again Scripture shows God as warrior on behalf of His helpless people, winning battles they could never win. The Christian's confidence is the same: the battle belongs to the Lord.
Key Passages
Exodus 14:14; Deuteronomy 20:4; Joshua 10:14; 2 Chronicles 20:15
