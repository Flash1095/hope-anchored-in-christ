Human Nature

Topic
Human Nature
Category
Theology
Summary
Human nature is what people are by God's design and what they have become through the fall: made in God's image, yet corrupted by sin in mind, will, and affections. This dual truth explains both human greatness and human horror, and it forbids both cynicism and utopianism. Redemption does not abolish human nature but restores it, renewing the image of God in Christ.
