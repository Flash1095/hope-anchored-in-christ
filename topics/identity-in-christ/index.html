Identity in Christ

Topic
Identity in Christ
Summary
Identity in Christ answers the question of who we are: new creations, children of God, members of His body, citizens of heaven. It replaces identities built on achievement, ancestry, or approval with one given by grace. To know who you are in Christ is to know what you are for.
Key Passages
2 Corinthians 5:17; Galatians 2:20; Ephesians 1:5; 1 Peter 2:9
