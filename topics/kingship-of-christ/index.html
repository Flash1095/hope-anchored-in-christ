Kingship of Christ

Topic
Kingship of Christ
Summary
Christ's kingship was proclaimed at His birth, mocked at His trial, and vindicated at His resurrection. He reigns now from heaven, and He will return to reign openly, every knee bowing before Him. To confess Jesus as Lord is to pledge allegiance to the true King.
Key Passages
Psalm 2:6-9; John 18:36-37; Philippians 2:9-11; Revelation 19:16
