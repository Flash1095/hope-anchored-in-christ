Word Studies

Topic
Word Studies
Category
Theology
Summary
Word studies are the careful examination of biblical words in their original languages, tracing how Hebrew and Greek terms are used across Scripture. Done well, they unlock precision - the difference between kinds of love, or what covenant meant to its first hearers - and done badly, they mislead, as when a word's root is mistaken for its meaning. Word studies serve the church best when they stay humble: words get their meaning from sentences, sentences from books, and books from the whole canon.
