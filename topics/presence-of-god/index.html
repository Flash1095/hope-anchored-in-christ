Presence of God

Topic
Presence of God
Category
Theology
Summary
The presence of God is His nearness to His people - in the tabernacle's cloud, in the temple, in Jesus' incarnation, and now in the Spirit dwelling within believers. Scripture holds together God's everywhere-presence with His special, covenant presence: He is never absent, yet He draws near in worship, in suffering, and in obedience. The Christian hope is presence made permanent: one day we will see Him face to face, and the ache of distance will end.
