Abundance

Topic
Abundance
Summary
God does not give sparingly. From manna in the wilderness to loaves multiplied for a crowd, Scripture shows a Father who provides more than enough, and His generosity is meant to overflow into generosity toward others. Abundance is never about storing up for ourselves, but about trusting the Giver and sharing what He has given.
Key Passages
Deuteronomy 28:11; Psalm 23:5; John 10:10; 2 Corinthians 9:8; Philippians 4:19
