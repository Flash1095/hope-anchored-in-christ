World Religions

Topic
World Religions
Category
Apologetics
Summary
World religions are the great faith traditions alongside Christianity - Judaism, Islam, Hinduism, Buddhism, and others - each with its own scriptures, practices, and picture of ultimate reality. Christians approach them with both respect and conviction: respect for the people who hold them and the truths they contain, and conviction that Jesus Christ is the unique Son of God and the only Savior. Understanding other faiths fairly matters for honest witness, good neighboring, and answering the question of why Christians believe what they do.
