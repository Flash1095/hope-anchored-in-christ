Life

Topic
Life
Category
Theology
Summary
Life in Scripture is God's gift, breathed into dust, and it means more than mere existence: it is fellowship with the living God. Sin brought death into the world, but Christ came that His people might have life abundantly, now and forever. The Christian hope is not escape from embodied life but its redemption — resurrection life in a renewed creation.
