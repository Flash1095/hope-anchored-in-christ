Diligent work

Topic
Diligent work
Summary
Work was given before the Fall, not as punishment but as purpose: to tend, build, and create. Scripture calls believers to work heartily, as for the Lord, whether in fields, workshops, or homes. Diligent work honors God, serves neighbors, and witnesses to a faith that takes ordinary life seriously.
Key Passages
Genesis 2:15; Ecclesiastes 9:10; Colossians 3:23-24; 2 Thessalonians 3:10-12
