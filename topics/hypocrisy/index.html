Hypocrisy

Topic
Hypocrisy
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Hypocrisy is the gap between profession and practice, the play-acting Jesus condemned most fiercely in religious leaders. It harms the church's witness because the world rightly asks whether faith changes anything. The remedy is not perfection but integrity: honest confession, accountability, and a life in which private character matches public confession.
Key Passages
Matthew 6:1-6; Matthew 23:27-28; Luke 12:1-3; Galatians 2:11-14; 1 John 1:6
Related People
Jesus; Paul; Simon Peter
Related Books
Matthew; Luke; Galatians; 1 John
