Gospel in relationships

Topic
Gospel in relationships
Summary
The gospel remakes relationships: enemies reconciled, masters and servants redefined as brothers, marriages and friendships reshaped by grace. Paul's letter to Philemon shows it in miniature — a runaway slave returned as a beloved brother. Where the gospel takes root, relationships change.
Key Passages
Ephesians 4:32; Colossians 3:12-14; Philemon 15-17; Romans 12:10
