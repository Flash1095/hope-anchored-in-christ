Kingdom of heaven

Topic
Kingdom of heaven
Summary
Matthew's phrase 'kingdom of heaven' describes God's reign breaking into the world through Jesus. It arrives quietly — like seed, yeast, and treasure — yet it overturns every other allegiance. To enter it is to submit to the King and receive a citizenship that outlasts every earthly kingdom.
Key Passages
Matthew 4:17; Matthew 13:31-33; Matthew 25:34; Daniel 2:44
