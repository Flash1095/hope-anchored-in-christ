Fear

Topic
Fear
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Fear is a natural human response to danger and uncertainty, but Scripture repeatedly calls God's people not to be ruled by it because God is with them. The fear of the Lord, reverent awe, actually drives out lesser fears. Again and again, 'do not fear' comes with a promise: I am with you, I will help you, I will never leave you.
