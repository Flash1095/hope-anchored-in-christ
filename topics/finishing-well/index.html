Finishing well

Topic
Finishing well
Summary
A good start does not guarantee a good finish, and Scripture honors those who endure to the end. Paul, near death, could say he had fought the good fight and kept the faith — not because he was flawless, but because he stayed the course. Finishing well means holding fast to Christ through every season, including the last.
