Exile

Topic
Exile
Category
Bible Story
Summary
The Exile is God's judgment on unfaithful Judah, when Babylon destroyed Jerusalem and the temple and carried the people far from home. Far from being the end of the story, exile became a season of refining, fresh revelation, and longing for restoration. God kept a remnant, brought His people home, and pointed them to a greater return in Christ.
