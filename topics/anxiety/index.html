Anxiety

Topic
Anxiety
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Anxiety is the troubled, divided mind Jesus tenderly addresses in the Sermon on the Mount, pointing anxious hearts to a Father who knows their needs and cares for sparrows and lilies. Scripture does not shame the anxious; it invites them to cast their cares on God in prayer and receive His peace. Christian hope gives anxiety an anchor: our future rests in God's hands, not our control.
