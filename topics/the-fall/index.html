The Fall

Topic
The Fall
Category
Bible Story
Summary
The Fall is humanity's first rebellion in Eden, when Adam and Eve disobeyed God, believed the serpent's lie, and brought sin, shame, and death into the world. Its effects reach every person and every part of creation. Yet even in judgment God clothed the couple, promised a serpent-crushing offspring, and began His rescue plan.
