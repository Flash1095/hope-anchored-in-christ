New covenant

Topic
New covenant
Summary
God promised through Jeremiah a covenant written on hearts, not stone, with sins forgiven and God known personally. Jesus inaugurated it at the Last Supper, saying the cup is the new covenant in his blood. We live under it now: forgiven, indwelt by the Spirit, and called to a new life of obedience from the inside out.

Jeremiah 31:31-34; Luke 22:20; Hebrews 8:6-13; 2 Corinthians 3:6
