Early Church

Topic
Early Church
Category
Bible Story
Summary
The early church is the community born at Pentecost, when the Spirit empowered frightened disciples to preach Christ boldly. Its life — teaching, fellowship, breaking of bread, prayer, and mission — became the pattern for churches ever since, and its spread from Jerusalem to Rome shows the gospel crossing every barrier. The early church was far from perfect, but its courage and love remain a challenge to every generation.
