Rest

Topic
Rest
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Rest is the God-given rhythm of stopping - weekly Sabbath, nightly sleep, and the deeper rest of a soul that trusts God enough to cease striving. Scripture treats rest as both command and gift: God rested after creation, Jesus invited the weary to come to Him, and Hebrews promises a Sabbath rest still to come. In a culture that measures worth by output, rest is an act of faith: it confesses that the world runs on God's power, not ours.
