Israel's restoration

Topic
Israel's restoration
Summary
After judgment came restoration: dry bones lived, exiles returned, and the temple rose again. Restoration was never merely political; it was God renewing His covenant and His people from within. The prophets' vision points beyond return from Babylon to the new covenant and the new creation.
Key Passages
Ezekiel 37:1-14; Jeremiah 31:31-34; Isaiah 44:26-28; Ezra 1:1-4
