Gratitude

Topic
Gratitude
Summary
Gratitude is the fitting response to grace: recognizing every good gift as from God's hand and saying so. Scripture commands thanksgiving in all circumstances, not because every circumstance is good, but because God is good in every circumstance. A grateful heart is a worshiping heart.
Key Passages
Psalm 100:4; Colossians 3:17; 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Luke 17:15-19
