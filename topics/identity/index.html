Identity

Topic
Identity
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Identity answers the question of who we are, and Scripture answers it first by whose we are: creatures made by God, sinners in need of grace, and, in Christ, beloved children and new creations. The world offers identities built on achievement, appearance, or affiliation, all of which shift and fail. Christian identity is received, not achieved, and it frees believers to serve without proving themselves.

Genesis 1:27; 2 Corinthians 5:17; Galatians 2:20; Ephesians 1:3-6; 1 Peter 2:9-10
