Deliverance

Topic
Deliverance
Category
Bible Story
Summary
Deliverance is God's rescue of His people from bondage, danger, or oppression, supremely in the exodus from Egypt and ultimately in Christ's victory over sin and death. The pattern repeats across Scripture: God hears cries, acts with a mighty hand, and leads His people into freedom. Deliverance reminds believers that salvation is God's work, not human achievement, and that He still rescues today.
