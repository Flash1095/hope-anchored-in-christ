Covenant love

Topic
Covenant love
Summary
Covenant love is God's steadfast, loyal love — the Hebrew hesed — that keeps His promises even when His people break theirs. It is love bound by oath, shown at Sinai, renewed through the prophets, and poured out fully in Christ. To know covenant love is to rest in a faithfulness that never lets go.
Key Passages
Exodus 34:6-7; Deuteronomy 7:9; Hosea 2:19-20; 1 John 4:9-10
Related Books
