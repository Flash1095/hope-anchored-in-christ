Evidence

Topic
Evidence
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Evidence in the Christian faith includes historical testimony, manuscript data, fulfilled prophecy, transformed lives, and the coherence of the gospel's picture of the world. Christians do not claim proof that compels belief against the will, but they do claim that faith is reasonable and that honest investigation has good grounds to proceed. The strongest evidence, the apostles insisted, is the resurrection of Jesus, attested by eyewitnesses.
