Transformation

Topic
Transformation
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Transformation is the gradual remaking of a person into Christ's likeness, the New Testament's word for what happens after conversion. It is not self-improvement by willpower but renewal by the Spirit: beholding Christ, renewing the mind, and practicing new patterns until they become character. Transformation is slow, uneven, and communal - it happens as we worship, confess, forgive, and serve - and its proof is not perfection but direction: more love, more honesty, more peace than before.
