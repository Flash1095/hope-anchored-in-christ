Conscience

Topic
Conscience
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Conscience is the inner witness that accuses or excuses us, a gift of God that can be trained, dulled, or misled. Paul speaks of a clear conscience and a weak conscience, urging believers to inform conscience by Scripture and to respect the consciences of others. A conscience captive to God's word is a guide to freedom, not a tyrant of guilt.
Key Passages
Romans 2:14-15; 1 Corinthians 8:7-13; 1 Timothy 1:5; Hebrews 9:14
Related People
