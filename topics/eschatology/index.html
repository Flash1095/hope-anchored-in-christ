Eschatology

Topic
Eschatology
Category
End Times
Summary
Eschatology is the study of last things: death, judgment, heaven, hell, Christ's return, and the renewal of all things. It reminds believers that history is going somewhere and that present suffering is not the end of the story. Different traditions order these events differently, but all Christian eschatology ends in hope: God will dwell with His people and wipe away every tear.
