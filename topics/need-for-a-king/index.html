Need for a king

Topic
Need for a king
Summary
After the judges' cycles of failure, Israel cried out for a king, and God gave them first Saul, then David, teaching them that no human king could finally save. The longing itself was right: God's people need a righteous ruler to lead them in God's ways and defend them from their enemies. That need is answered only in Jesus, the King whose reign brings justice without tyranny and victory without violence.
