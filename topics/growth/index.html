Growth

Topic
Growth
Summary
Growth is the normal Christian life: from milk to solid food, from seed to harvest, from new birth to maturity. It comes through God's word, prayer, community, and trials, and it is measured less by speed than by direction. The call is simple: keep growing up into Christ.
