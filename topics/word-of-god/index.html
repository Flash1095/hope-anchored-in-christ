Word of God

Topic
Word of God
Category
Doctrine
Summary
The Word of God is God's self-revelation: Christ Himself, the living Word, and Scripture, the written Word that testifies to Him. Christians distinguish the two without separating them - the written Word's authority flows from the living Word it proclaims. To call the Bible God's Word is to claim it speaks with His voice: it creates, convicts, comforts, and commands, and it stands as the church's final court of appeal.

Deuteronomy 8:3; Psalm 119:105; Isaiah 55:10-11; John 1:1-14; Hebrews 4:12
