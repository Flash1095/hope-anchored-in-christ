Restoration

Topic
Restoration
Category
Theology
Summary
Restoration is God's work of bringing back what was lost or broken: Israel returned from exile, a ruined life rebuilt, and finally a whole creation made new. The prophets picture it as streams in the desert and dry bones living again, and the New Testament extends it to relationships, bodies, and the cosmos itself. Restoration is larger than repair - it is renewal with interest, the thief repaying sevenfold, the prodigal honored as a son rather than hired as a servant.
