Kingship

Topic
Kingship
Summary
Kingship belongs to God: the psalms call Him King over all the earth, and Israel's kings were meant to rule as His deputies. Human kingship failed again and again, which is why the promise of a perfect King mattered so much. All authority is borrowed; only God's kingship is absolute.
Key Passages
Psalm 47:7-8; 1 Timothy 6:15; Revelation 19:16; Isaiah 33:22
