Perseverance

Topic
Perseverance
Category
Doctrine
Summary
Perseverance is the steady continuance of faith through trials, doubts, and long obedience - and, in Reformed theology, the conviction that God preserves His people to the end. Scripture holds both sides: believers must endure, and God is the one who keeps them enduring. The warnings against falling away and the promises that nothing can separate us from God's love are not contradictions but partners, urging vigilance without despair.
