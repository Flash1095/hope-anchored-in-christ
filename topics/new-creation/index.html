New Creation

Topic
New Creation
Summary
The Bible ends where it began, only greater: a new heavens and new earth where God dwells with His people and wipes away every tear. That future has already broken in, for anyone in Christ is a new creation, raised to new life by the Spirit. New creation hope steadies us now and shapes how we care for God's world and one another.
