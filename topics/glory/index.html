Glory

Topic
Glory
Category
Theology
Summary
Glory is the weighty, radiant presence of God made visible — in creation, in the tabernacle, in Christ, and one day in the new creation. To glorify God is to honor Him as He deserves, and the chief end of humanity is to glorify God and enjoy Him forever. Every lesser glory points beyond itself to the One whose glory fills heaven and earth.
