Ethics

Topic
Ethics
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Ethics is the study of right and wrong, and Christian ethics roots moral judgment in God's character revealed in Scripture. It asks not only what rules to follow but what kind of people to become: truthful, just, merciful, and pure. In a world of competing moral visions, Christian ethics offers both clarity about God's commands and grace for those who fail to keep them.
