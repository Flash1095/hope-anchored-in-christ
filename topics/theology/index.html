Summary
Theology is the study of God: what He is like, what He has done, and what He asks of us, drawn from Scripture and organized for understanding. It is not an academic luxury but a practical necessity - everyone is a theologian, because everyone lives by some picture of God, accurate or not. Good theology humbles as much as it informs: it ends, as Paul's does, in worship, because the God it studies is greater than every system built to describe Him.
