Covenant

Topic
Covenant
Category
Theology
Summary
A covenant is God's solemn, binding promise to His people, sealed in love and faithfulness. From Noah and Abraham to the new covenant in Christ's blood, covenants trace God's steady plan to dwell with His people. Every covenant points forward to Jesus, in whom all God's promises find their Yes.
