Nature and Creation

Topic
Nature and Creation
Category
Theology
Summary
Nature is God's first book, displaying His power and care to everyone, everywhere. Scripture treats creation as good, ordered, and purposeful - not divine itself, and not an accident - and calls humans its stewards, not its owners. Nature also witnesses against us: its beauty points to a Creator, and its brokenness groans under the same curse that afflicts us, waiting for the renewal promised in Christ.
