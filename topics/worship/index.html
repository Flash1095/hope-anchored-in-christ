Worship

Topic
Worship
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Worship is the believer's whole-life response to who God is and what He has done, expressed in song, prayer, obedience, and awe. True worship is in spirit and truth, centered on God's worth rather than our preferences. From the tabernacle to the throne room of heaven, worship is both our duty and our delight.
