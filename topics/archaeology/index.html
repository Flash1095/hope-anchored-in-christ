Archaeology

Topic
Archaeology
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Archaeology studies the material remains of the ancient world — cities, inscriptions, coins, and everyday objects — and it regularly illuminates the world of the Bible. Finds like the Cyrus Cylinder, the Dead Sea Scrolls, and the Tel Dan inscription have confirmed names, places, and events once doubted. Archaeology cannot prove every claim of faith, but it anchors the biblical story in real history rather than myth.
