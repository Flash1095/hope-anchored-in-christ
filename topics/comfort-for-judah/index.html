Comfort for Judah

Topic
Comfort for Judah
Summary
When Judah faced exile, Isaiah spoke comfort: her warfare is ended, her iniquity pardoned, and her God is coming. Comfort for Judah was never mere sympathy; it was the promise that God had not abandoned His people and would bring them home. That same comfort reaches all who mourn under judgment, pointing to Christ who bears our sorrows.

Isaiah 40:1-2; Isaiah 51:3; Isaiah 61:1-3; Jeremiah 29:11
