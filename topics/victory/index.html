Victory

Topic
Victory
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Victory in Scripture is won, not earned: Israel's battles were God's before they were theirs, and the decisive victory over sin and death belongs to Christ alone. Christians share in it by faith, which is why Paul can call suffering believers more than conquerors in the middle of hardship rather than after it. Victory therefore looks different from triumph: it is endurance, forgiveness, and faithfulness that hold the field because the war's outcome is already settled.
