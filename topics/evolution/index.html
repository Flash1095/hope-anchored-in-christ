Evolution

Topic
Evolution
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Evolution is the scientific account of how life diversified over time, and Christians differ on how it relates to Genesis. Some read Genesis as teaching a recent six-day creation, others as teaching God's purposeful creation in language that accommodates ancient understanding, and still others hold intermediate views. What all orthodox Christians affirm together is that God is the Creator, that humans bear His image, and that Adam's fall is real history with real consequences.
