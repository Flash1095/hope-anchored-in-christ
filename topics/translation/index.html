Translation

Topic
Translation
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Translation is rendering Scripture from Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek into the languages people actually speak - work the church has done since the Septuagint, three centuries before Christ. Every translation involves interpretation, which is why Christians use several and compare them, and why new translations keep appearing as language changes and manuscripts improve. The doctrine beneath it is confidence: God's Word can be faithfully carried across languages, because its Author intends to be understood.
