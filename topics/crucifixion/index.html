Topic
Crucifixion
Category
Bible Story
Summary
The crucifixion is the execution of Jesus on a Roman cross outside Jerusalem, the event at the center of the Christian faith. The Gospels describe it in stark detail — the mockery, the darkness, the cry of dereliction, the final breath — and the apostles proclaim its meaning: Christ died for our sins, bearing the curse in our place. The cross is both history's darkest hour and the source of the world's hope.
