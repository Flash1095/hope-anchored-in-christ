Sovereignty

Topic
Sovereignty
Category
Theology
Summary
Sovereignty is God's absolute rule over all things: nothing happens outside His knowledge, permission, or purpose. Scripture holds it together with human freedom and moral responsibility without fully resolving the tension, and it anchors comfort: if God is sovereign, suffering is never meaningless and evil never has the last word. The doctrine divides Christians on the details - how God's will relates to human choices - but unites them on the throne: the Lord reigns.
