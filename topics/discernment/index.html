Discernment

Topic
Discernment
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Discernment is the Spirit-shaped ability to distinguish truth from error and good from merely plausible. It grows through Scripture, prayer, community, and experience, and it is needed most where falsehood wears a disguise — in teaching, in guidance, and in cultural pressures. Discernment is humble: it tests everything, holds fast to what is good, and admits it can be wrong.

1 Kings 3:9; Proverbs 2:1-6; Philippians 1:9-11; 1 John 4:1-3; Hebrews 5:14
