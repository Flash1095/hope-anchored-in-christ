Sin

Topic
Sin
Summary
Sin is humanity's rebellion against God, beginning with Adam and Eve and spreading to every heart, bringing death and brokenness to the world. Scripture never minimises sin, but it also never leaves us without hope. From Genesis 3:15 onward, God promises and provides a Saviour who defeats sin at the cross.
Key Passages
Genesis 3:6-19; Psalm 51:5; Isaiah 59:2; Romans 3:23; Romans 6:23; 1 John 1:8-9
