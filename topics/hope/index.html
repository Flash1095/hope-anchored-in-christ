Hope

Topic
Hope
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Christian hope is confident expectation anchored in God's promises and sealed by Christ's resurrection, not mere wishful thinking. It holds fast in suffering because its anchor, Jesus, is already within the veil, interceding for His people. This hope does not disappoint, and it steadies believers to persevere with joy.
