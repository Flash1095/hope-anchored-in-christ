Persecution

Topic
Persecution
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Persecution is hostility faced for following Christ, from mockery and exclusion to imprisonment and death. Jesus promised it plainly and called it blessed, and the New Testament treats it as normal for a church that lives differently from the world around it. Christians respond not with retaliation but with endurance, prayer for enemies, and solidarity with suffering brothers and sisters - and by counting the cost honestly before promising the world that faith is easy.
