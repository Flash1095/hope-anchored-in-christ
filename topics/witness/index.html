Witness

Topic
Witness
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Witness is the church's calling to testify to what God has done, in words and in life. The legal metaphor is deliberate: witnesses do not invent the case, they report what they have seen and heard, and the Spirit makes the testimony effective. Witness takes many forms - a shared story, a defended hope, a life that makes the gospel plausible - but its center is always Jesus: His death, resurrection, and the new life He gives.
