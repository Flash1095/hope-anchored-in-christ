Kindness

Topic
Kindness
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Kindness is love in action toward others, gentle, generous, and undeserved — a fruit of the Spirit and a mark of God's own character. Scripture commends small kindnesses as well as great ones: a cup of water, a word of encouragement, a welcome to the stranger. Kindness is not weakness; it is strength under control, choosing to bless even when retaliation would be easier.
