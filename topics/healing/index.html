Healing

Topic
Healing
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Healing is God's restoration of body, mind, and spirit, seen in Jesus' ministry and promised fully in the new creation. Christians pray for healing with confidence and humility, knowing God sometimes heals now and sometimes sustains through suffering, as with Paul's thorn. Healing ministry must be honest: it never guarantees outcomes, never blames the sick for lack of faith, and always points to the Great Physician.
Key Passages
Exodus 15:26; Psalm 103:2-3; Isaiah 53:5; Mark 5:34; James 5:14-16; 2 Corinthians 12:7-10
