Judgment

Topic
Judgment
Category
Theology
Summary
Judgment is God's righteous verdict on human deeds, promised throughout Scripture and culminating in the final judgment when Christ returns. It is sobering because God sees all and judges justly, yet it is also good news: evil will not have the last word, and wrongs will be set right. For believers, judgment is faced with confidence, not terror, because Christ has borne their condemnation.
