Lament

Topic
Lament
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Lament is the honest prayer of grief, complaint, and longing directed to God, modeled in a third of the Psalms and in the cries of Job and Jeremiah. It refuses both denial and despair: it names pain plainly while still addressing God as Lord and ending, often, in trust. The church needs lament because faith that cannot grieve cannot fully hope.
Key Passages
Psalm 13:1-6; Psalm 42:1-11; Lamentations 3:19-26; Matthew 27:46
Related People
King David; Jeremiah; Jesus
Related Books
