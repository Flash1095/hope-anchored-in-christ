God's Holiness

Topic
God's Holiness
Category
Theology
Summary
God's holiness is His perfect separateness from sin and His absolute moral purity, the attribute that makes angels cover their faces and sinners cry out in awe. It is not cold distance but blazing goodness, and it is why sin cannot stand in His presence apart from atonement. Believers are called to be holy as He is holy — not to earn His love, but because they belong to Him.
