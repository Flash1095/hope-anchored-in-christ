Science and Faith

Topic
Science and Faith
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Science and faith meet at the biggest questions: where the universe came from, why it is intelligible, and what humans are for. History shows several models - conflict, independence, dialogue, integration - and Christians hold them with different emphases, from young-earth creation to evolutionary creationism. What unites the conversation is the conviction that truth is one: if God made the world and wrote the Word, careful science and careful exegesis will not finally contradict, even where they currently seem to.
