Redemption

Topic
Redemption
Category
Doctrine
Summary
Redemption is God's act of buying His people back from slavery to sin at a great price. In the Old Testament God redeemed Israel from Egypt; in the New Testament Christ redeems us by His blood. The redeemed are not just forgiven, they are freed to belong to God forever.
Key Passages
Exodus 6:6; Psalm 130:7-8; Mark 10:45; Ephesians 1:7; Colossians 1:13-14; 1 Peter 1:18-19
