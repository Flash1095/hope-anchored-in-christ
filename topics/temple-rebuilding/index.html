Temple rebuilding

Topic
Temple rebuilding
Summary
After exile, God stirred Zerubbabel, Joshua, and the returned remnant to rebuild his house in Jerusalem, and later Nehemiah to rebuild its wall. The work faced opposition, delay, and discouragement, yet God promised that the glory of the latter house would be greater than the former. The rebuilt temple pointed forward to Christ, in whom God dwells with his people, and to the church as his living temple.
