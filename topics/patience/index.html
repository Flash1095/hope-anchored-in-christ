Patience

Topic
Patience
Summary
Patience reflects God's own character, for He is slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love toward a rebellious world. Abraham waited for a son, Joseph waited in prison, and God's people waited centuries for the Messiah, learning that God's timing is never wasted. The Spirit grows patience in us as we wait hopefully for Christ's return.

Exodus 34:6; Psalm 40:1; Romans 8:25; Galatians 5:22; James 5:7-8; 2 Peter 3:9
