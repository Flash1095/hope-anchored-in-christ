Justification by faith

Topic
Justification by faith
Summary
Justification by faith is the heart of the gospel: sinners are declared righteous before God not by works but by trusting Christ. Abraham believed and it was credited to him as righteousness, and the same faith credits righteousness to all who believe. Justification is a gift received, never a wage earned.
Key Passages
Romans 3:28; Romans 5:1; Galatians 2:16; Philippians 3:9
