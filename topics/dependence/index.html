Dependence

Topic
Dependence
Summary
Dependence is not weakness to be outgrown but the truth of the Christian life: apart from Christ we can do nothing. Like branches on a vine, believers draw every good thing from Him — strength, wisdom, and fruit. To depend on God is to live honestly, admitting need and receiving grace.

John 15:5; Psalm 121:1-2; Proverbs 3:5-6; 2 Corinthians 12:9-10
