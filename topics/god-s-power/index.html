God's Power

Topic
God's Power
Category
Theology
Summary
God's power is His ability to do all that He wills, displayed in creation from nothing, in the exodus, and supremely in raising Jesus from the dead. No force can thwart His purposes, yet His power is exercised with wisdom and love, never as brute domination. Believers draw on that same power through the Spirit, not for self-glory but for endurance, witness, and holiness.
