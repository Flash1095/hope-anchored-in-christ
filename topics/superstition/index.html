Superstition

Topic
Superstition
Category
Theology
Summary
Superstition is belief in supernatural cause and effect without God's revelation to back it: lucky charms, omens, rituals that manipulate outcomes. It differs from faith because it tries to control the spiritual world rather than trust a personal God, and Scripture groups it with the occult practices Israel was told to leave behind. The cure is not cynicism but confidence: a God who speaks clearly in Scripture and acts wisely in providence leaves no need for secret signs.
