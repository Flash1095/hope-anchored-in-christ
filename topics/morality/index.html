Morality

Topic
Morality
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Morality asks what is right and wrong, and the Bible grounds the answer in God's character rather than in majority opinion or personal feeling. The moral law - love God, love neighbor, summarized in the Ten Commandments and the Sermon on the Mount - describes the life God designed for human flourishing. Christians confess we do not live it perfectly, which is why morality in Scripture always walks hand in hand with mercy: the law shows us our need, and grace meets it.
