Karma

Topic
Karma
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Karma is the belief, common in Hinduism and Buddhism, that actions carry moral consequences across lifetimes, shaping future rebirth. Christians recognize the biblical truth that actions have consequences — we reap what we sow — but reject the impersonal cycle of rebirth: Scripture teaches one life, one death, and one judgment, with salvation by grace rather than by accumulated merit. The gospel answers karma's burden with a Savior who bears what we cannot repay.
Key Passages
Galatians 6:7-8; Hebrews 9:27; Romans 3:23-24; Ephesians 2:8-9
