Trinity

The Trinity is the biblical teaching that God is one in essence and three in person: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Each person is fully God, yet there is only one God, a mystery Scripture affirms from creation to Christ's baptism to the Great Commission. Knowing God as Trinity shapes how Christians pray, worship, and understand love itself.
