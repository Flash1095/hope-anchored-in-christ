Reasoning

Topic
Reasoning
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Reasoning is the practice of thinking well: forming beliefs for good reasons, spotting fallacies, and changing your mind when the evidence requires it. Scripture commends it - the Bereans examined the Scriptures daily to test what they heard - and warns against its counterfeits, like rationalization and cleverness in service of pride. For Christians, good reasoning is a spiritual discipline: it protects the church from error, sharpens witness, and honors a God who is Himself the Logos, the divine Reason.
