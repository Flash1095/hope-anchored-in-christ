Easter

Topic
Easter
Category
Bible Story
Summary
Easter is the celebration of Jesus' resurrection from the dead on the third day after His crucifixion, the event that vindicated His claims and defeated death. The empty tomb and the risen Lord's appearances turned despairing disciples into witnesses willing to die for what they had seen. Easter is not only a day but the pattern of Christian hope: suffering is real, but it does not have the last word.
