Seeking God

Topic
Seeking God
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Seeking God is the active pursuit of His presence, will, and face - through prayer, Scripture, worship, and obedience. Scripture promises that those who seek Him will find Him, not because seeking earns His favor but because He has already turned toward us. Seeking is both a season and a posture: the desperate search of the lost, the daily discipline of the faithful, and the lifelong journey of people who have tasted and want more.
Key Passages
Deuteronomy 4:29; Psalm 27:8; Psalm 63:1; Jeremiah 29:13; Matthew 6:33; Hebrews 11:6
