Authority

Topic
Authority
Category
Theology
Summary
Authority in the Christian life asks who gets to say what is true and right: God, speaking in Scripture, holds ultimate authority over conscience and church. Human authorities — parents, rulers, pastors — deserve honor, but their authority is delegated and limited, never absolute. When human commands contradict God's word, believers obey God rather than people, and they do so with respect rather than rebellion.
