Technology

Topic
Technology
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Technology is every tool humans make to extend their reach, from plows to smartphones, and Scripture treats tools as morally loaded: useful for good, dangerous when they master us. The Bible's wisdom applies directly - stewardship over addiction, truth over misinformation, presence over distraction, and Sabbath over the endless scroll. Christians neither fear technology nor worship it; they ask of each new tool whether it helps them love God and neighbor better.
