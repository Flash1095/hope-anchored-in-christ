Doubt

Topic
Doubt
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Doubt is the unsettled questioning many honest believers face about God's goodness, truth, or presence, and Scripture treats doubters with surprising tenderness. From the Psalms to Thomas to John the Baptist in prison, God's people have wrestled and been met with evidence and mercy. Doubt need not be the end of faith; handled honestly, it can deepen it.
