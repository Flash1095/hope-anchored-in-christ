Philosophy

Topic
Philosophy
Category
Apologetics
Summary
Philosophy is the love of wisdom: asking the big questions about truth, goodness, beauty, and existence with reasoned argument. Scripture engages philosophy rather than ignoring it - Paul debated Stoics and Epicureans in Athens, and the church has used philosophical tools to clarify doctrines like the Trinity ever since. The Christian posture is discerning: all truth is God's truth, but human wisdom must bow to revelation where the two conflict, and reason serves faith rather than ruling it.
