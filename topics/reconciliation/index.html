Reconciliation

Topic
Reconciliation
Category
Doctrine
Summary
Reconciliation is the restoration of a broken relationship, and in Scripture it is first God's work toward us: while we were enemies, Christ died to bring us back. That vertical reconciliation creates a horizontal one - Jew and Gentile, offender and offended, enemies made one new people - and the church is entrusted with its ministry. Reconciliation is not pretending nothing happened; it is truth, repentance, forgiveness, and rebuilt trust, in that costly order.
