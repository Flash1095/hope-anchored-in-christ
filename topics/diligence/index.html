Diligence

Topic
Diligence
Summary
Diligence is steady, faithful effort over the long haul — the ant's wisdom, the athlete's discipline, the farmer's patience. Scripture warns against laziness and praises the worker who keeps at the task God has given. Diligence trusts that small, faithful acts compound into a life well lived.
Key Passages
Proverbs 6:6-11; Colossians 3:23; Hebrews 6:11-12; 2 Peter 1:5-8
