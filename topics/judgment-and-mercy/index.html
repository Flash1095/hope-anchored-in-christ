Judgment and mercy

Topic
Judgment and mercy
Summary
Judgment and mercy meet in God's character: He judges sin truly and forgives sinners freely. The psalmist says mercy and truth meet together, and James warns that mercy triumphs over judgment for the merciful. At the cross both are fully satisfied — sin condemned, sinners welcomed.
Key Passages
Psalm 85:10; Micah 6:8; James 2:13; Exodus 34:6-7
