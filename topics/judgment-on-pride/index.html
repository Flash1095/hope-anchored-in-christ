Judgment on pride

Topic
Judgment on pride
Summary
Pride invites judgment because it claims God's place: Babel's tower, Nebuchadnezzar's boast, and every heart that exalts itself. God humbles the proud not out of cruelty but to restore truth — that He alone is great. The humbled, by contrast, are lifted up.
Key Passages
Daniel 4:28-37; Isaiah 2:11-12; James 4:6; Obadiah 1:3-4
Related Books
