Remembrance

Topic
Remembrance
Summary
Remembrance in the Bible is active: God remembers his covenant, and his people are called to remember his works, his words, and his warnings. Memorials: Passover, the stones at Gilgal, the Lord's Supper, were given because human hearts drift. To remember rightly is to live gratefully and obediently, letting yesterday's deliverances fuel today's trust.

Exodus 12:14; Joshua 4:6-7; Psalm 105:5; 1 Corinthians 11:24-26
