Emotions

Topic
Emotions
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Emotions are part of God's good design, not enemies of faith: the Psalms give voice to joy, anger, fear, grief, and longing, and Jesus Himself wept and rejoiced. Emotions inform us but do not rule us; mature faith feels deeply while testing feelings against truth. The goal is not stoic numbness but emotions ordered by love for God and neighbor.
Key Passages
Psalm 42:1-11; Psalm 62:8; John 11:33-36; Philippians 4:6-7; Galatians 5:22-23
