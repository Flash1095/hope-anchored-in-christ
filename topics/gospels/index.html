Summary
The Gospels are the four books — Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John — that tell the story of Jesus' life, teaching, death, and resurrection. Each has its own emphasis: Matthew presents the King, Mark the Servant, Luke the Savior of all, John the divine Son. Together they give the church its founding narrative, and they are meant to be believed and obeyed, not merely admired.
