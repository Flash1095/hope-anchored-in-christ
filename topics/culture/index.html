Culture

Topic
Culture
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Culture is the shared way of life of a people — its stories, customs, arts, and values — and Christians live within cultures without simply absorbing them. The gospel judges every culture, affirming what is good and true in it while challenging what contradicts God's word. Faithful cultural engagement means neither withdrawal nor conformity, but discerning participation for the common good.
Key Passages
Jeremiah 29:4-7; Daniel 1:8-21; John 17:15-18; Romans 12:2
