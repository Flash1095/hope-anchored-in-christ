Evangelism

Topic
Evangelism
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Evangelism is sharing the good news of Jesus Christ with others and calling them to repent and believe. It flows from gratitude for God's grace and compassion for people who are lost, and God uses ordinary believers' words and lives to draw people to Himself. The message is Christ crucified and risen; the power belongs to God, the privilege to us.
