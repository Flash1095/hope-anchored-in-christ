Grace

Topic
Grace
Category
Doctrine
Summary
Grace is God's unmerited favor, His kindness to people who have not earned it and cannot repay it. By grace we are saved, sustained, and shaped, from first faith to final glory. Grace humbles the proud, lifts the broken, and teaches us to extend to others what we have received.
