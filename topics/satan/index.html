Satan

Topic
Satan
Category
Theology
Summary
Satan is the personal, spiritual adversary Scripture calls the accuser, the tempter, and the god of this age - a fallen creature, not God's equal. He appears as the serpent in Eden, the accuser of Job, the tempter of Jesus, and the dragon of Revelation, always working by deception and accusation. Christians take him seriously without fearing him: Christ has disarmed him at the cross, and believers resist him by truth, faith, and the armor of God, knowing his defeat is certain.
