True Worship

Topic
True Worship
Category
Christian Living
Summary
True worship is the whole-life response to God's worth: not only songs on Sunday but obedience on Monday, offered in spirit and truth. Jesus told the Samaritan woman that the Father seeks worshipers who worship this way, and the prophets insisted that ritual without justice is noise to God. True worship therefore has two tests: is it directed to the true God, and does it change how we live toward our neighbor?
