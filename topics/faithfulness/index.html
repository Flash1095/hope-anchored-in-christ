Faithfulness

Topic
Faithfulness
Summary
Faithfulness is loyalty over time — keeping promises when it costs, trusting God when He seems silent, and doing the next right thing. Scripture celebrates it in Daniel, Joseph, and countless unnamed saints, and above all in God Himself, whose faithfulness never fails. To be faithful is to mirror the character of the God we serve.
Key Passages
Lamentations 3:22-23; Matthew 25:21; 1 Corinthians 4:2; Revelation 2:10
