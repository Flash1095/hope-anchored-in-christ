Jesus Christ

Topic
Jesus Christ
Category
Theology
Summary
Jesus Christ is the central figure of the Christian faith: the eternal Son of God who became man, lived sinlessly, died on the cross for sinners, and rose again on the third day. His name joins His humanity (Jesus) with His office (Christ, the anointed King), and the Gospels present Him as the fulfillment of Israel's hopes and the Savior of the world. To know Jesus is, for Christians, to know God Himself.
