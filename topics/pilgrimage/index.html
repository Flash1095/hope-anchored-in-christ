Pilgrimage

Topic
Pilgrimage
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Pilgrimage is a journey to a holy place, undertaken to seek God, remember His acts, or mark a turning point. Israel's feasts drew pilgrims to Jerusalem three times a year, and Christians have walked to sites of biblical memory ever since - while remembering that the destination is a person, not a place. The Christian life itself is often described as pilgrimage: strangers and exiles traveling toward a heavenly country, learning dependence one step at a time.
