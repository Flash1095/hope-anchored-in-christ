Existence of God

Topic
Existence of God
Category
Apologetics
Summary
The existence of God is the question of whether a Creator lies behind the universe, and Christians point to several lines of evidence: the beginning and fine-tuning of the cosmos, the reality of objective moral values, the human hunger for meaning, and the historical person of Jesus. The Bible itself treats God's existence as evident from creation, so that unbelief is a moral as well as intellectual matter. Arguments can open doors, but knowing God comes through Christ.
Key Passages
Psalm 19:1-4; Romans 1:19-20; Hebrews 11:6; Acts 17:24-28
Related People
