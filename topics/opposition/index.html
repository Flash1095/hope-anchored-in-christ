Opposition

Faithfulness in Scripture almost always meets opposition: Nehemiah's wall-builders held a trowel in one hand and a sword in the other, Daniel's enemies plotted against his prayers, and the apostles were ordered to stop speaking in Jesus' name. Opposition is not a sign that God has abandoned his work; often it is the sign that the work matters. God's people answer it with prayer, courage, and steady obedience, trusting the Lord who builds what no enemy can tear down.
