Temple

Topic
Temple
Summary
The temple was the dwelling place of God's presence among Israel, the centre of worship, sacrifice, and prayer in Jerusalem. Jesus honoured the temple, cleansed it, and foretold its destruction, while pointing to His own body as the true temple. Now God's Spirit dwells in His church and in every believer, and the new creation needs no temple because the Lord Himself is there.
