God's Providence

Topic
God's Providence
Category
Doctrine
Summary
God's providence is His wise and loving governance of all things, by which He sustains creation and directs history toward His purposes. It means that nothing is random to God — not the fall of a sparrow, not the rise of empires, not the details of our lives — yet without making God the author of evil. Providence is a doctrine of comfort: the world is not in chaos, but in the hands of a Father.
