Tradition

Topic
Tradition
Category
Theology
Summary
Tradition is the church's handed-down faith: creeds, liturgies, interpretations, and practices passed from generation to generation. Scripture treats tradition with both respect and suspicion - Paul tells churches to hold to what was delivered, while Jesus rebukes traditions that nullify God's commands. The Reformation's rule remains the test: tradition is a faithful servant when it carries Scripture forward, and a tyrant when it replaces it.
