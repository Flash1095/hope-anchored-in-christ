Topic
Full Payment
Summary
The cross was full payment for sin — not a deposit, not a partial settlement, but a complete satisfaction of God's justice. Hebrews stresses that Christ's one offering perfected forever those being made holy. Because the debt is fully paid, believers stand before God without condemnation and without fear.

Romans 3:24-25; Colossians 2:13-14; Hebrews 10:10-14; 1 Peter 1:18-19
