Church Hurt

Topic
Church Hurt
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Church hurt is the pain caused when God's people wound one another — through harsh leadership, betrayal, exclusion, or hypocrisy. Scripture does not minimize such wounds: Jesus warned sternly about those who harm His little ones, and the Psalms give voice to grief over betrayal by close companions. Healing often involves honest lament, wise counsel, boundaries where needed, and, in time, the hard work of forgiveness — without pretending the hurt did not happen.
Key Passages
Psalm 55:12-14; Matthew 18:15-17; Galatians 6:1-2; Hebrews 10:24-25
Related People
King David; Jesus; Paul
