Lordship

Topic
Lordship
Category
Theology
Summary
Lordship is the confession that Jesus is Lord — that He has the right to command every area of life, not only its religious corners. The earliest Christian creed was simply Jesus is Lord, a claim that relativized every other loyalty, including Caesar's. Lordship is good news before it is a demand: the One who rules is the crucified and risen Savior who lays down His life for His people.
