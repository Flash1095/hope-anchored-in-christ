Respectful Dialogue

Topic
Respectful Dialogue
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Respectful dialogue is conversation that honors the other person as made in God's image, even when you deeply disagree. Scripture models it: Jesus asked questions before He answered them, Paul reasoned rather than ridiculed in Athens, and Peter told believers to give reasons with gentleness and respect. It is not compromise - truth still matters - but it refuses contempt, straw men, and the urge to win at the cost of the relationship.
