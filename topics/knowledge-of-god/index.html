Knowledge of God

Topic
Knowledge of God
Category
Theology
Summary
Knowledge of God is more than information about Him; it is personal acquaintance through His word, His works, and His Son. Scripture says eternal life itself is knowing God and Jesus Christ, and it warns that knowledge without love puffs up. True knowledge of God transforms: it humbles the proud, comforts the fearful, and sends believers out in obedience.
Key Passages
Jeremiah 9:23-24; Hosea 6:6; John 17:3; Philippians 3:7-11; 2 Peter 1:2-3
