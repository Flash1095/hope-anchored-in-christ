Generosity

Topic
Generosity
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Generosity is the free, joyful sharing of what God has given, modeled on a God who gave His own Son. The Macedonian churches gave out of poverty, the widow gave out of little, and Paul taught that God loves a cheerful giver. Generosity is not measured by amount but by heart, and it loosens money's grip on the soul while meeting real needs.
