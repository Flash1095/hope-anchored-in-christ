Israel's identity

Topic
Israel's identity
Summary
Israel's identity was given, not earned: chosen by God, set apart as His treasured possession, and called to be a light to the nations. That identity survived slavery, wilderness, and exile because it rested on God's choice, not Israel's performance. In Christ, the church inherits that calling as God's people.

Exodus 19:5-6; Deuteronomy 7:6; Isaiah 43:1; Romans 11:1-2
