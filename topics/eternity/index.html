Eternity

Topic
Eternity
Category
Theology
Summary
Eternity is God's own mode of existence: without beginning or end, unbound by time as creatures know it. Scripture contrasts God's eternity with human brevity — a thousand years are like a day to Him — and promises believers a share in His unending life. Eternity is not endless boredom but endless fullness, life in the presence of the God who is Himself infinite.
Key Passages
Psalm 90:1-4; Isaiah 57:15; 2 Peter 3:8; Revelation 22:13
