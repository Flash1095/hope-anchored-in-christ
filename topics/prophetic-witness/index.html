Prophetic witness

Topic
Prophetic witness
Summary
Prophets were God's mouthpieces, speaking his word to kings and crowds whether they wanted to hear it or not. Their witness combined courage and compassion: denouncing injustice and idolatry while holding out mercy to all who would turn. The church inherits that calling: to speak truth in love, to name sin honestly, and to testify to Jesus, for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy.
