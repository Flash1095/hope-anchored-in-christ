Christ's example

Topic
Christ's example
Summary
Jesus did not only teach the way; He walked it. He washed feet, welcomed children, forgave enemies, and laid down His life, leaving His followers a pattern to follow. To follow His example is not to earn salvation but to live out the salvation He has given.
