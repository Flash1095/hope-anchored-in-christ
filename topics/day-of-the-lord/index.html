Day of the Lord

Topic
Day of the Lord
Summary
The Day of the Lord is the day God steps in decisively — to judge evil, rescue His people, and set the world right. The prophets warned that it would be darkness for the proud and light for the humble, and the New Testament ties it to Christ's return. It is both a warning to the complacent and a hope for the oppressed.
Key Passages
Joel 2:28-32; Amos 5:18-24; Zephaniah 1:14-18; 2 Peter 3:10-13
