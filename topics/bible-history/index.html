Bible History

Topic
Bible History
Category
Bible Story
Summary
Bible history is the true story the Scriptures tell, from creation through the patriarchs, Israel, Jesus, and the early church. Because the Bible's claims are rooted in events that happened in real places at real times, its history can be studied, questioned, and tested like any history. Knowing the sweep of that story helps readers see how each passage fits into God's larger work of redemption.
