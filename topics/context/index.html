Topic
Context
Category
Theology
Summary
Context is the setting that gives a passage its meaning: the surrounding verses, the whole book, the author's world, and the sweep of Scripture. Ignoring context is the surest way to misuse the Bible, turning promises into slogans and commands into weapons. Careful readers ask what a text meant before asking what it means, and let the clear passages interpret the obscure ones.
