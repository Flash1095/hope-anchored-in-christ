Gospel purity

Topic
Gospel purity
Summary
Gospel purity means keeping the good news unmixed: Christ plus nothing, grace without add-ons. Paul was fierce on this point, warning that a different gospel is no gospel at all. Purity protects freedom, for anything added to grace becomes a burden.
Key Passages
Galatians 1:6-9; 2 Corinthians 11:3-4; Philippians 3:8-9; Jude 3
Related Books
Galatians; 2 Corinthians; Philippians; Jude
