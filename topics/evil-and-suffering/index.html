Evil and Suffering

Topic
Evil and Suffering
Category
Theology
Summary
Evil and suffering are the hardest challenge to faith in a good and powerful God, and Scripture meets it with honesty rather than easy answers. The Bible shows a world broken by sin, a God who enters suffering in Christ, and a promise that evil will one day be judged and undone. Christians do not claim to explain every instance of pain, but they trust a God who suffered Himself and who will make all things new.

Genesis 3:1-19; Job 1:1-22; Isaiah 53:4-6; Romans 8:18-25; Revelation 21:4
