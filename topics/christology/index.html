Christology

Topic
Christology
Category
Doctrine
Summary
Christology is the study of who Jesus Christ is: fully God and fully man, two natures in one person. The early church defended this against errors that made Jesus merely human or merely divine, because only a Savior who is both can bridge the gap between God and humanity. Christology matters practically: the Jesus we worship must be great enough to save and near enough to sympathize with our weakness.
