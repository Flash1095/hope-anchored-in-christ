Marriage

Topic
Marriage
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Marriage is God's good design from creation: a lifelong covenant between a man and a woman picturing Christ's faithful love for His church. It calls husbands and wives to sacrificial love, forgiveness, and partnership in God's purposes. Strong marriages are built not on feelings alone but on covenant commitment renewed daily in grace.
