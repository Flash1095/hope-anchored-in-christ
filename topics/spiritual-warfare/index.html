Spiritual Warfare

Topic
Spiritual Warfare
Category
Theology
Summary
Spiritual warfare is the Christian's struggle against the world, the flesh, and the devil - a real conflict that is fought with spiritual weapons, not physical ones. Paul's armor of God names the equipment: truth, righteousness, gospel readiness, faith, salvation, and the Word, all wielded in prayer. The battle is already decided at the cross, yet still fiercely contested in daily life, which is why Scripture pairs vigilance with confidence: resist, stand firm, and the enemy will flee.
