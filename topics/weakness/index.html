Weakness

Topic
Weakness
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Weakness is the human condition Scripture refuses to romanticize or despise: we are frail, limited, and dependent, and God meets us there rather than waiting for us to get strong. Paul's thorn taught him that grace is sufficient precisely where strength fails, and the church's strength has always come from admitting need rather than hiding it. Weakness is therefore not the opposite of faith but often its clearest evidence: trust is easiest to see when there is nothing else to lean on.
