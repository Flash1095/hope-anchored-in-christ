Church History

Topic
Church History
Category
Bible Story
Summary
Church history traces the people of God from Pentecost to the present: through persecution, councils, missions, reformations, and revivals. It is a story of remarkable faithfulness and sobering failure, and knowing it guards Christians against both arrogance and despair. The same gospel that sustained martyrs and missionaries is the gospel the church carries today.
