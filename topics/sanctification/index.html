Sanctification

Topic
Sanctification
Category
Doctrine
Summary
Sanctification is the Holy Spirit's lifelong work of making believers holy in practice, growing them into the likeness of Christ. It begins at conversion, advances through obedience, trials, and the ordinary means of grace, and is completed when we see Jesus face to face. God calls us to pursue holiness while resting in the promise that He will finish what He started.
