Priorities

Topic
Priorities
Summary
Life is short and demands are endless, so Scripture urges us to choose what matters first: God's kingdom, his people, and the work he has actually given us. Martha learned that one thing is necessary; Mary chose the good portion at Jesus' feet. Right priorities are not found by doing more but by seeking first what God seeks, and letting the rest find its place beneath it.

Matthew 6:33; Luke 10:38-42; Philippians 1:9-11; Ephesians 5:15-17
