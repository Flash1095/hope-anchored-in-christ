Church Councils

Topic
Church Councils
Category
Bible Story
Summary
Church councils were gatherings of Christian leaders who settled disputes about doctrine and practice, most famously at Nicaea in 325 and Chalcedon in 451. Their creeds — affirming that Christ is truly God and truly man — still shape what Christians confess today. Councils were not perfect, and their authority rests on their faithfulness to Scripture, but they show how the church has guarded the gospel together across generations.
