Imagination

Topic
Imagination
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Imagination is the God-given capacity to picture what is not present — to envision, to empathize, to create, and to hope. Scripture feeds imagination with stories, visions, and parables, and the Christian imagination is trained by dwelling on what is true, noble, and lovely. Guarded well, imagination serves faith and art; left to fear and fantasy, it can mislead, which is why it needs the anchor of truth.
