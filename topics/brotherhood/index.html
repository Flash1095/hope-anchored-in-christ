Brotherhood

Topic
Brotherhood
Summary
Believers are not merely like-minded individuals; they are brothers and sisters, bound together as one family in Christ. Scripture urges them to love one another warmly, carry one another's burdens, and live at peace. True brotherhood shows the world what the Father's household looks like.

Psalm 133:1; Romans 12:10; 1 John 3:16-18; Hebrews 13:1
