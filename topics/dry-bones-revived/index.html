Dry bones revived

In a valley of dry bones, God asked Ezekiel whether they could live, then breathed life into them by His Spirit. The vision promised Israel's restoration from exile, and it points further to resurrection itself: the God who revives the dead can revive any hopeless situation. No valley is too dry for His breath.
