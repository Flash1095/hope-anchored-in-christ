God's faithfulness

Topic
God's faithfulness
Summary
God's faithfulness is the bedrock of every promise: what He says, He does, across centuries and despite human failure. From the exodus to the return from exile to the coming of Christ, His people learned that His word never returns empty. When everything else shifts, His faithfulness holds.

Lamentations 3:22-23; Deuteronomy 7:9; 1 Corinthians 1:9; 2 Timothy 2:13
