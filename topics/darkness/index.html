Darkness

Topic
Darkness
Category
Theology
Summary
Darkness in Scripture pictures ignorance, evil, and separation from God, set against the light of His presence and truth. The world loved darkness rather than light, John writes, yet into that darkness the true Light has come. Darkness is real and terrible, but it is not ultimate: it cannot overcome the light, and one day night itself will be no more.
Key Passages
Genesis 1:2-4; Isaiah 9:2; John 1:4-9; 1 John 1:5-7; Revelation 22:5
