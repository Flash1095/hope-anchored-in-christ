Compassion for nations

Topic
Compassion for nations
Summary
God's compassion does not stop at Israel's borders. Jonah learned this reluctantly when God spared Nineveh, and Isaiah foresaw a Servant who would be a light to the nations. In Christ, that compassion reaches every tribe and tongue, and the church carries it to the ends of the earth.
Key Passages
Jonah 4:10-11; Isaiah 49:6; Matthew 28:19-20; Revelation 7:9
