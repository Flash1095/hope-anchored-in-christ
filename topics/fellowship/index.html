Fellowship

Topic
Fellowship
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Fellowship is the shared life of believers with God and with one another, deeper than socializing because it is grounded in a common Lord. John writes that true fellowship with one another flows from fellowship with the Father and His Son. It shows itself in worship together, confession, generosity, and bearing one another's burdens — and it is a foretaste of the eternal communion of heaven.
