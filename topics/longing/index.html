Longing

Topic
Longing
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Longing is the ache for what is missing — for home, for wholeness, for God — that runs through human experience and through Scripture. Augustine said our hearts are restless until they rest in God, and the Psalms turn longing into prayer. Christian hope does not deny longing but directs it: every good longing is a signpost toward the God who will one day satisfy it fully.
Key Passages
Psalm 42:1-2; Psalm 63:1; Ecclesiastes 3:11; Philippians 1:23; Revelation 22:17
Related People
