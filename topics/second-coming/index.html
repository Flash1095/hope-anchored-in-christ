Second Coming

Topic
Second Coming
Category
End Times
Summary
The second coming is Christ's promised return in glory to judge the living and the dead, defeat evil, and make all things new. Scripture urges believers not to speculate idly about dates but to watch, work, and encourage one another as the day approaches. His return is the blessed hope that steadies Christians in every trial.
