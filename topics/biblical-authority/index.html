Biblical Authority

Topic
Biblical Authority
Category
Theology
Summary
Biblical authority is the conviction that Scripture, as God's breathed-out word, is the final court of appeal for belief and behavior. It does not mean every question has a proof text, but it does mean that where Scripture speaks clearly, Christians submit even when culture disagrees. This authority is exercised humbly, through careful interpretation in community, not as a weapon for private agendas.
Key Passages
Deuteronomy 6:4-9; Psalm 119:9-16; Matthew 4:4; 2 Timothy 3:16-17
Related People
Moses; Jesus; Paul
Related Doctrines
Inspiration of Scripture; Inerrancy of Scripture
Related Books
Deuteronomy; Psalms; Matthew; 2 Timothy
