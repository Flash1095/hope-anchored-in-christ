Reformed Theology

Topic
Reformed Theology
Category
Doctrine
Summary
Reformed theology is the tradition flowing from John Calvin and the Reformation, organized around God's sovereignty in salvation: grace that initiates, sustains, and completes redemption. Its landmarks include the five points of Calvinism, covenant theology, and confessions like Westminster, all emphasizing that salvation is God's work from first to last. Reformed Christians differ from other believers mainly on the mechanics of grace - election, atonement's extent, and perseverance - while sharing the same gospel of Christ crucified.
