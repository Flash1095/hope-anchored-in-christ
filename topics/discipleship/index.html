Discipleship

Topic
Discipleship
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Discipleship is following Jesus daily, learning from His word, obeying His commands, and helping others do the same. It is more than believing facts about Christ; it is a whole life reordered around Him, whatever the cost. The Great Commission makes disciple-making the normal mission of every believer, not just church leaders.
