Confession

Topic
Confession
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Confession is the honest naming of sin before God and, where fitting, before others, trusting His promise to forgive and cleanse. It is not groveling but agreement with the truth, and it restores fellowship that sin has broken. The church has long practiced confession both privately and together, because sins hidden tend to grow, while sins confessed lose their power.
