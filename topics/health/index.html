Health

Topic
Health
Category
Christian Living
Summary
Health is a gift to steward, not an idol to serve: Scripture treats the body as a temple of the Spirit and commends rest, work, food, and care for the sick. Illness is not necessarily punishment, and healing is not guaranteed in this life, but God cares about bodies as well as souls. Christians pursue health with gratitude and moderation, and they care for the sick as service to Christ Himself.
Key Passages
Exodus 15:26; Proverbs 3:7-8; 1 Corinthians 6:19-20; 3 John 1:2; Matthew 25:36
Related People
