Yahweh (Jehovah)

Name
Yahweh (Jehovah)
Category
Name
Subject
God
Form
Verbatim in places (appears as a name in some passages)
Meaning
The personal covenant name of God, revealed to Moses at the burning bush. The KJV usually renders it 'LORD'; it appears as 'JEHOVAH' in a few places.
Key Scriptures
Exodus 3:14; Exodus 3:15; Exodus 6:3; Psalms 83:18
