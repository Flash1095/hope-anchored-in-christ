Son of Man

Name
Son of Man
Category
Title
Subject
Christ
Form
Verbatim in Scripture (printed as a name in the KJV text)
Meaning
Jesus' most frequent self-title, drawing on Daniel's vision of one like a son of man coming with the clouds of heaven, who came to serve and to seek and save the lost.
Key Scriptures
Daniel 7:13; Matthew 8:20; Mark 10:45; Luke 19:10
