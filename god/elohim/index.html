Name

God
Form
Hebrew word behind the KJV rendering
Meaning
The Hebrew word for God, used throughout Genesis 1 and the Old Testament; the KJV renders it 'God'. Its plural form is used with singular verbs for the one true God.
Key Scriptures
Genesis 1:1; Genesis 1:26; Genesis 1:27
