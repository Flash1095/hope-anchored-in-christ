The Twelve Minor Prophets (4QXIId)

Category
Biblical
Cave
Qumran Cave 4
Completeness
Fragment
Date Approx
late 1st century BC
Description
A single fragment from the beginning of a Twelve Prophets roll, surviving only with Hosea material (Hosea 1:6-2:5 in the standard summary; Sinclair's first publication is titled for Hosea 1:7-2:5). Whether the original roll contained the whole Twelve is unknown: Hartog notes the remaining fragments contain only Hosea and the manuscript may well have had only part of the Twelve, perhaps even just Hosea, while Brooke suggested it is quite possible it contained the whole Twelve with Hosea at the start; that wider claim is not counted here. DJD XV (Fuller) is the edition of record.

Significance
The earliest surviving Hosea material at Qumran outside the larger Twelve scrolls, preserving the opening of Hosea (the naming of Hosea's children and the opening of the marriage accusation in chapter 2). It is evidence that the Twelve could be copied in smaller sets or as a single-book excerpt as well as in full Twelve scrolls, rather than always as one fixed twelve-book collection.
