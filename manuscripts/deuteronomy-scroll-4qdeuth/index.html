Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Deuteronomy from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q35. Publish-prep note: the compact list '43-2:6, 28-30' has been expanded to '43-2:6, 2:28-30' so the site's passage linker resolves the continuation to chapter 2, the end chapter of the range. Same verses claimed, chapter made explicit.
Significance
A witness to Deuteronomy's opening, its laws, and Moses' blessing.
