Description
A Greek manuscript of Leviticus on leather from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q119 (DJD IX; Rahlfs 801), stored in the Rockefeller Museum, Jerusalem. It is a Greek translation, not a Hebrew copy.
Significance
An early Greek witness to Leviticus 26, attesting the Greek text of the Torah at Qumran.
