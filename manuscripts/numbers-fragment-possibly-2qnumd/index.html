Description
A single fragment from Qumran Cave 2, catalogued as 2Q9, listed as 2QNumd with a question mark. DISPUTED: the fragment may belong to 2Q7 (2QNumb), and it has also been read as Leviticus 23:1-3. It is included only with that dispute stated, never as a certain Numbers copy.
Significance
A disputed single fragment; it must not be counted as certain evidence for a fourth Cave 2 Numbers copy.
