Description
A single fragmentary scroll from Qumran Cave 4 that originally contained both Genesis and Exodus; only a small Genesis portion (the end of the Flood narrative) survives alongside substantial Exodus material. It appears ONCE here, with both books in Work, and must not be duplicated as a separate Exodus entry. Publish-prep note: the tail ranges (22:14; 27:38-39, 42-43; 34:17-21) were listed without a book label after the Exodus ranges, which made 27:38-39, 42-43 point past the end of Exodus 27 (21 verses); Ulrich's index of biblical passages lists Genesis 27:38-39, 42-43 (with Genesis 22:14 and 34:17-21) as passages of 4QGen-Exod^a, so the book label has been made explicit as Genesis. The chapter range Exodus 1-4 has been expanded to Exodus 1; 2; 3; 4 so each chapter links.
Significance
One of the earliest combined Pentateuch scrolls from Qumran, showing Genesis and Exodus copied in a single scroll in the Hasmonean period.
