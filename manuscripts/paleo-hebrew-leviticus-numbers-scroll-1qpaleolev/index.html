Description
A paleo-Hebrew scroll from Qumran Cave 1, catalogued as 1Q3, containing Leviticus material and a Numbers passage. A further fragment with Leviticus 27:30-31 has been tentatively assigned to this manuscript (sometimes labelled 1QpaleoLevb) and is NOT counted as certain here; it is noted only as a possible attribution.
Significance
A Cave 1 paleo-Hebrew witness to Leviticus purity and festival laws, with a Numbers census fragment.
