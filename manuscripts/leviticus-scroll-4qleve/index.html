Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Leviticus from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q26a, with remains from Leviticus 2-3 and 19-22. Publish-prep note: the research listed Leviticus 2:11-18, but Leviticus 2 has only 16 verses in the English KJV and in the Hebrew text (SBL Handbook of Style Appendix B lists no versification difference for Leviticus 2), so the range is truncated to 2:11-16 pending verification against DJD. Himbaza's contents table for 4Q26a lists no Leviticus 2 material at all, so the Leviticus 2 fragments should be re-checked by the coordinator. Also, the compact list '27-21:4, 9-12, 21-24' has been expanded to '27-21:4, 21:9-12, 21:21-24' so the linker resolves the continuations to chapter 21, the end chapter of the range, per Himbaza's contents table for 4Q26a (21:1-4, 9-12, 21-24).
Significance
A witness to Leviticus offerings and holiness laws.
