Description
A single lengthy scroll from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q23 (DJD XII), containing Leviticus and Numbers in sequence. It appears ONCE here, with both books in Work. Together with 4Q27 (Batch 1), it is one of the two most extensive Numbers scrolls from the Judaean Desert.
Significance
A major Hasmonean-period witness to Leviticus and the first part of Numbers.
