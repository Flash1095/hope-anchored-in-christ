Description
Fragments of a Hebrew copy of Lamentations from Qumran Cave 5, dated c. AD 50, preserving parts of Lamentations 4 and 5. It is the second-fullest Qumran Lamentations copy by published word count (about 141 words, against about 193 for 4Q111).
Significance
Preserves the closing chapters of Lamentations, including the prayer of chapter 5, in a 1st-century AD Hebrew copy, complementing 4Q111's opening chapters.
