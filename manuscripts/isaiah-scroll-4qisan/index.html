Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich) as 4Q67. It preserves only Isaiah 58:13-14, the Sabbath-delight passage. Cave 4 lists group it with fragments on punishment and on God's blessings for his people, including 4Q69a.

Significance
A two-verse witness to Isaiah 58. It shows the Sabbath passage circulating at Qumran, but nothing more can be claimed from two verses.
