4QRutha (Ruth Scroll, copy a, Cave 4)

Three fragments from Cave 4, including the beginning of the book: the famine, Elimelech's family in Moab, the deaths of Elimelech, Mahlon and Chilion, and Naomi setting out to return to Judah with her daughters-in-law (Ruth 1:1-12). It is the first Cave 4 Ruth copy.

Significance

The earliest-opening witness to Ruth at Qumran, preserving the book's first verses, which no other Qumran Ruth copy preserves; like the other Ruth copies it agrees closely with the Masoretic Text.
