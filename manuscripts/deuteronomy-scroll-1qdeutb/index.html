Deuteronomy Scroll (1QDeutb)

A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Deuteronomy from Qumran Cave 1, catalogued as 1Q5, with remains spanning most of the book.

Significance

A broad Cave 1 witness to Deuteronomy, from chapter 1 to Moses' blessing in chapter 33.
