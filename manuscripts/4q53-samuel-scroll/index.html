4QSamc (Samuel Scroll, copy c)

Category
Biblical
Cave
Qumran Cave 4
Completeness
Fragment
Date Approx
c. 100-75 BCE
Description
Three partially preserved columns and four small fragments from Cave 4, published in DJD XVII, preserving the end of David's dealings with Abigail's household (1 Samuel 25) and the Absalom narrative (2 Samuel 14-15). It is the third Cave 4 Samuel copy, distinct from 4Q51 and 4Q52.

Significance
A Hasmonean-period witness to the Absalom revolt narrative, showing Samuel continued to be copied at Qumran in the century before the Batch 1 copy 4Q51 was written.
