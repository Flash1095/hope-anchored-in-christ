Description
The second, fragmentary copy of Joshua from Qumran Cave 4, distinct from the Batch 1 copy 4Q47 (4QJosha). It preserves scraps from the crossing of the Jordan (Joshua 2-4) and from the allotment of land to Manasseh (Joshua 17). Verse-edge summaries differ slightly between editions: some chapter summaries extend the Jordan-crossing portion to Joshua 3:15-17 and the chapter 17 portion to 17:1-5, 11-15; the range given here is the conservative identified text from the Cave 4 catalogue list.

Significance
With 4Q47 it shows that Joshua circulated at Qumran in at least two copies, both fragmentary. Its Jordan-crossing and land-allotment fragments are early witnesses to the text of Joshua, centuries older than the medieval Masoretic codices.
