5QKgs (Kings Scroll, Cave 5)

Category
Biblical
Cave
Qumran Cave 5
Completeness
Fragment
Date Approx
c. 150-50 BCE
Description
Three fragments from Cave 5, inscribed in a Hasmonean hand, preserving the opening of 1 Kings: David's old age and the succession struggle in which Bathsheba and Nathan secure Solomon's anointing as king (1 Kings 1). It is the only biblical Kings copy from Cave 5.

Significance
The earliest of the three Qumran Kings copies by date range, and the only one preserving the very beginning of Kings; together with 4Q54 and 6Q4 it gives three independent Qumran witnesses to 1 Kings.
