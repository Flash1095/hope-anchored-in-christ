Ezekiel Scroll (4QEzekb)

A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Ezekiel from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV as 4Q74. It preserves only the opening vision of the living creatures and the wheels in Ezekiel 1, from the four faces to the expanse over their heads. It is a different manuscript from Batch 1's 4QEzeka (4Q73).

Significance

One of only two Qumran copies preserving Ezekiel's inaugural vision (with 11QEzek), it lets the vision's earliest Hebrew wording be checked in two independent copies.
