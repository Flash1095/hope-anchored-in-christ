Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich) as 4Q68. It survives as a single composite fragment preserving Isaiah 14:28-15:2. A 2023 study of its material and scribal features argues it is incompatible with a full copy of the book and may instead derive from a small-scale scroll containing only a subsection of Isaiah, though its precise scope cannot be determined; its variants appear exegetically motivated, clarifying cruxes in its proto-Masoretic Vorlage. It is therefore listed here as a biblical Isaiah fragment, not as a full Isaiah scroll.

Significance
The most recently re-examined small Isaiah fragment: it illustrates how a single fragment can still illuminate scribal interpretation, while warning against assuming every Qumran fragment came from a complete book scroll.
