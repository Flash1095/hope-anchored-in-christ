Description
A single fragmentary scroll from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q17 (DJD XII), containing the end of Exodus (tabernacle construction) and the beginning of Leviticus. It appears ONCE here, with both books in Work. Dated by some lists to c. 250 BCE, among the earliest Qumran biblical fragments.
Significance
An early witness to the Exodus-Leviticus seam, showing the two books copied in sequence in one scroll.
