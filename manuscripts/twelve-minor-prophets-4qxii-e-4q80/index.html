The Twelve Minor Prophets (4QXIIe)

Category
Biblical
Cave
Qumran Cave 4
Completeness
Fragment
Date Approx
c. 75-50 BC
Description
Best-preserved Qumran copy for Zechariah only. A Hebrew Twelve Prophets scroll from Qumran Cave 4, dated 75-50 BC, surviving in fragments. Its surviving portions are almost exclusively from Zechariah, with a short Haggai section at the end of Haggai 2.
Language
Hebrew
Location
Qumran, Judaean Desert
Manuscript
The Twelve Minor Prophets (4QXIIe)
Manuscript ID
4Q80 (4QXIIe)
Preserved Passages
Haggai 2:18-21; Zechariah 1:4-6, 9-10, 13-14; 2:6-10; 3:2-10; 4:1-2, 4; 5:8-11; 6:1-3, 5; 8:2-4, 6-7; 12:7-12
Related Books
Haggai; Zechariah
Significance
Best-preserved Qumran biblical copy of Zechariah: it preserves parts of Zechariah 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 12, far more than 4Q76 (Zechariah 14:18 only) or 4Q82 (Zechariah 10-12 fragments). Its text is described as of a textual type related to the Septuagint. For Haggai it is the runner-up, not the best: Haggai 2:18-21 (four verses) against 4Q77's Haggai 1:1-2; 2:2-4 (five verses).
