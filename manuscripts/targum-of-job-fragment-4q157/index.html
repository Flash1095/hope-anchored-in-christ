Description
Fragments of an Aramaic Targum (translation) of Job from Qumran Cave 4, in Herodian script. This is a translation of Job, not a Hebrew copy: it renders Job 3 and Job 4-5 into Aramaic, and it is the smaller of the two Qumran Job targums (the other is 11Q10).
Significance
Evidence that Job was not only copied in Hebrew at Qumran but also translated into Aramaic for hearers or readers who needed it, centuries before the later rabbinic targums; as a translation it witnesses to interpretation of Job, not directly to the Hebrew wording.
