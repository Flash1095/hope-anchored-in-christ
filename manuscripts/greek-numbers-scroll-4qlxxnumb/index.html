Greek Numbers Scroll (4QLXXNumb)

A Greek manuscript of Numbers from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q121 (DJD IX), whose text shows revision toward the Hebrew Pentateuch. Several readings are marked uncertain in the catalogue.

Significance

A Greek witness to Numbers 3-4, notable for its Hebrew-revision character.
