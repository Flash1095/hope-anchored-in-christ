Deuteronomy Scroll (4QDeutk2)

A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Deuteronomy from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q38a, the second fragment grouped as 4QDeutk. Publish-prep note: Deuteronomy 23:22-26 was listed in Hebrew numbering and has been converted to English KJV numbering (Deuteronomy 23:21-25) per SBL Handbook of Style Appendix B (English/Hebrew versification table) (English Deuteronomy 22:30 = Hebrew 23:1; English 23:1-25 = Hebrew 23:2-26).

Significance

An Early Herodian witness to Deuteronomy laws in chapters 19-27.
