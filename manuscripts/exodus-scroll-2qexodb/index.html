Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Exodus from Qumran Cave 2, catalogued as 2Q3. The Exodus 12:26-27 reading is marked uncertain in the catalogue. Publish-prep note: Exodus 21:37-22:2 was listed in Hebrew numbering and has been converted to English KJV numbering (Exodus 22:1-3) per SBL Handbook of Style Appendix B (English/Hebrew versification table) (English Exodus 22:1 = Hebrew 21:37; English 22:2-31 = Hebrew 22:1-30). The uncertain Exodus 12:26-27 reading has been removed from PreservedPassages and is stated in the Description.
Significance
A Cave 2 witness to Exodus laws and covenant material.
