4QKgs (Kings Scroll, Cave 4)

Category
Biblical
Cave
Qumran Cave 4
Completeness
Fragment
Date Approx
c. 50-25 BCE
Description
Nine fragments from Cave 4, seven identified and two unidentified, preserving Solomon's temple furnishings (1 Kings 7: the pillars Jachin and Boaz, the bronze sea and the stands) and the bringing of the ark into the temple (1 Kings 8). One scholarly survey gives the chapter 7 ending as 7:50 rather than 7:51; the range here follows the DSS English Bible edition. It preserves 1 Kings only, unlike the Batch 1 Kings copy 6Q4, which spans 1 and 2 Kings.

Significance
The only Cave 4 copy of Kings and a first-century BCE witness to the temple-building account, complementing the Cave 5 and Cave 6 Kings copies rather than duplicating them.
