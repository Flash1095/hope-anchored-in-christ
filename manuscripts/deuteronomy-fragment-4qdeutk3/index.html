Description
A tiny fragmentary Hebrew copy of Deuteronomy from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q38b, the third fragment grouped as 4QDeutk, preserving only Deuteronomy 30:16-18.
Significance
A single-passage Late Herodian witness to Deuteronomy 30.
