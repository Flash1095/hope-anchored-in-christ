Isaiah Scroll (4QIsaj)

Category

Cave

Completeness

Date Approx

Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich) as 4Q63. It preserves only the opening six verses of Isaiah, the superscription and the opening accusation against Judah.

Significance
One of several Qumran copies preserving Isaiah's opening chapter, showing that the book's first verses were copied independently in multiple Cave 4 scrolls; it proves nothing beyond Isaiah 1:1-6.
