Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich) as 4Q69a. It preserves only Isaiah 54:10-13, the everlasting-covenant passage ('my steadfast love shall not depart from you').

Significance
A four-verse witness to Isaiah 54. Like the other smallest Cave 4 Isaiah fragments, it adds one more early copy for its verses and nothing beyond them.
