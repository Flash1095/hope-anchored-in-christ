Date Approx

Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich) as 4Q65. It preserves only Isaiah 7:14-15, including the Immanuel sign, and Isaiah 8:11-14.

Significance
A tiny but notable witness: it is one of the Qumran copies preserving Isaiah 7:14, a verse widely cited in Christian tradition, in a Hebrew copy predating the New Testament by a century.
