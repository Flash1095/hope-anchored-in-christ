Description
Fragments of a Hebrew Psalms copy from Qumran Cave 4, in Herodian script, ranging across Psalms 91-118. Its sequence differs from the Masoretic Psalter, notably Psalm 103 followed by Psalm 112. Preserved-passage ranges for this manuscript are given in English (KJV) numbering; the manuscript itself carries no modern psalm numbers. Publish-prep note: every PreservedPassages range in this entry was re-versified to English (KJV) numbering against DJD XVI (Skehan/Ulrich/Flint), Qumran Cave 4.XI, via Fitzmyer (2008), with the SBL Handbook of Style Appendix B English/Hebrew versification table. First-pass conversions (over-length ranges only, from the earlier cleanup): Psalms 102:10-29 to 102:9-28. Final-pass conversions: Psalms 92:4-8 to 92:3-7; Psalms 92:13-15 to 92:12-14; Psalms 102:5 to 102:4. All other ranges were verified as already correct English numbering (untitled psalms and first-clause-title psalms do not shift).
Significance
An early Hebrew witness to the Psalms, predating the medieval Masoretic manuscripts by about a thousand years. Its chief value is textual: it shows which psalms and which readings were already in circulation in the Second Temple period, and, where its psalm order differs from the Masoretic Psalter, that the order of the Psalter was not yet fully fixed.
