Category

Cave

Date Approx

Description
A fragmentary Cave 4 copy of Judges, published by Trebolle Barrera in DJD XIV, preserving parts of Judges 6, the opening of the Gideon story. It is the first of the two Cave 4 Judges copies; the Batch 1 Judges copy is the Cave 1 manuscript 1Q6 (1QJudg).

Significance
Emanuel Tov judged this text manifestly non-aligned, possibly reflecting a different literary edition of Judges, so even its few verses matter for the history of the book's text; it is not simply a duplicate of the Masoretic Text.
