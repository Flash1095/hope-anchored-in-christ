Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich) as 4Q64. It preserves only the passage spanning the end of Isaiah 28 and the opening of Isaiah 29, the parable of the farmer and the woe over Ariel.

Significance
A narrow witness to Isaiah 28-29. Its value is confined to those verses; it cannot speak for the rest of the book.
