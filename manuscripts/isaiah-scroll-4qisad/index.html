Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich). Its surviving passages come from Isaiah 46-58, including the Servant passage in Isaiah 53:8-12. It is dated to about AD 30-68, the latest of the Qumran Isaiah copies in the BYU summary table.
