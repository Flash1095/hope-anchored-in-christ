Isaiah Scroll (4QIsab)

Category

Cave
Qumran Cave 4
Completeness
Substantial fragment
Date Approx
c. 75-30 BC
Description
A substantial fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich). Its surviving verses span the whole book, from Isaiah 1 to Isaiah 66, but only in scattered passages; it is not a complete copy.

Significance
One of the two most extensive Cave 4 Isaiah copies (with 4QIsac). Its breadth makes it one of the most useful Cave 4 witnesses for comparing the Qumran Isaiah text with the Masoretic Text across both halves of the book.
