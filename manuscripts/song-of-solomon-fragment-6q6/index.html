Song of Solomon Fragment (6QCant)

A small fragment, in two columns, of a Hebrew copy of the Song of Solomon from Qumran Cave 6, in late Herodian script (c. AD 50). It preserves the opening of the book, Song 1:1-7, including the title 'The Song of Songs, which is Solomon's'.

Significance

The only Song manuscript outside Cave 4, and the latest of the four Qumran Song copies; it shows the Song still being copied at Qumran in the mid-1st century AD.
