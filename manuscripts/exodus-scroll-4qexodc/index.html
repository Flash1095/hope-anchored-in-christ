Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Exodus from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q14 (DJD XII, 36 fragments), with remains from Exodus 7-18. Publish-prep note: Exodus 7:26-8:1 was listed in Hebrew numbering and has been converted to English KJV numbering (Exodus 8:1-5) per SBL Handbook of Style Appendix B (English/Hebrew versification table) (English Exodus 8:1-4 = Hebrew 7:26-29; English 8:5-32 = Hebrew 8:1-28).
Significance
A substantial Herodian-period witness to the plagues, the Exodus, and the wilderness journey.
