Date Approx

Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich). Its surviving passages run from Isaiah 1 to Isaiah 29, including the throne vision in Isaiah 6:3-8, 10-13.

Significance
A Hasmonean-period witness to the opening third of Isaiah. Its readings have been cited in textual studies of Isaiah 6 and 11, but only for the verses it actually preserves.
