Description
Two very small fragments from Qumran Cave 4, identified by E. Puech as 4QGenn (4Q576). The identification is given as possible remains of Genesis 34 and 50, not as a certain large copy; the fragments are tiny and the reading is tentative.
Significance
A tentative, very small witness to Genesis; included with its uncertainty stated, per the Batch 2 dispute rule.
