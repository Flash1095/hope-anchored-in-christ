Date Approx

Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Daniel from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XVI (Ulrich) as 4Q116. It preserves only Daniel 9:12-17, Daniel's prayer of confession over Jerusalem. It is dated elsewhere to the late second century BC; a Flint summary table's 'first half of the second century BC' dating for this manuscript is flagged in that same table as presumably a typo and is not followed here. Because only a single prayer passage survives, the original scope of the scroll cannot be determined from this fragment alone. It is a different manuscript from Batch 1's 4QDana (4Q112).

Significance
One of the two oldest Qumran Daniel copies (with 4QDanc). Its single passage is the confession at the heart of Daniel 9, preserved in Hebrew a century and more before the New Testament period.
