The Twelve Minor Prophets (4QXIIb)

Best-preserved Qumran copy for Zephaniah and Haggai. A Hebrew Twelve Prophets scroll from Qumran Cave 4, dated 150-125 BC, surviving in fragments. Its largest fragment preserves the bottom edge of the manuscript, which helps determine the manuscript's dimensions and reconstruct it. It shows the Twelve Prophets written as one continuous book at Qumran.

Significance

Best-preserved Qumran biblical copy of Zephaniah (Zephaniah 1:1-2; 2:13-15; 3:19-20) and of Haggai (Haggai 1:1-2; 2:2-4, five verses, against 4Q80's four Haggai verses, and the older of the two Haggai copies). Zephaniah's themes of judgment and redemption and Haggai's call to rebuild the temple after 586 BC are both present in this early copy.
