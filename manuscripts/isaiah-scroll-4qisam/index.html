Isaiah Scroll (4QIsam)

A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich) as 4Q66. It preserves only the end of Isaiah 60 and the opening of Isaiah 61, including the passage beginning 'The Spirit of the Lord GOD is upon me'.

Significance

A small witness to Isaiah 60-61, the everlasting-light and good-news passages. Its few verses add one more early Hebrew copy to the evidence for those chapters.
