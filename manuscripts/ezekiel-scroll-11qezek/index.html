Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Ezekiel from Qumran Cave 11, published in DJD XXIII (Herbert) as 11Q4. What could be salvaged from a solid lump was edited by W. H. Brownlee in 1963; only a few words survive from each passage listed above. In orthography and palaeography it is akin to 1QIsab and may be dated at the turn of the era, and it is described as only generally proto-Masoretic, showing distinctive readings alongside agreement with the Masoretic Text. The PreservedPassages above follow the DJD contents list; one secondary compilation instead lists Ezekiel 11:19 in place of 10:11, which is not followed here.

Significance
The only Ezekiel copy from Cave 11 and, with 4QEzeka, one of the two Qumran Ezekiel manuscripts likely to have been a copy of the whole book rather than an excerpt. Its distinctive readings show Ezekiel's text was not yet fully standardized at the turn of the era.
