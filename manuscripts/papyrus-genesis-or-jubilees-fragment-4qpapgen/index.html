Description
A papyrus fragment from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q483, whose text (Genesis 1:28-29) could belong either to a Genesis copy or to the Book of Jubilees, which retells Genesis. DISPUTED IDENTIFICATION: it is included here only with that dispute stated, never as a certain Genesis copy.
Significance
If Genesis, a small papyrus witness to the creation blessing; if Jubilees, it belongs to that retelling instead — hence the entry must not be cited as proof of a Genesis copy.
