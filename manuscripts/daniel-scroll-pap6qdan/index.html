Papyrus Daniel Scroll (Pap6QDan)

Category

Cave

Date Approx
c. AD 50
Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Daniel on papyrus from Qumran Cave 6, published as 6Q7. It is the only Daniel copy from Cave 6 and the only Qumran Daniel copy on papyrus. It preserves passages from Daniel 8 (Gabriel's interpretation), Daniel 10 (the vision by the Tigris) and Daniel 11 (the wise and the king). One secondary compilation lists Daniel 8:20-21 rather than 8:21-22 for the first passage; the Flint detailed list used here gives 8:16-17, 21-22. It is a different manuscript from Batch 1's 4QDana (4Q112).

Significance
