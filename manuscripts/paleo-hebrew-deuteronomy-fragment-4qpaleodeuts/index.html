Description
A single archaic paleo-Hebrew fragment of Deuteronomy from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q46, preserving Deuteronomy 26:14-15 (tithes confession). Among the oldest Qumran biblical fragments.
Significance
One of the earliest surviving Deuteronomy fragments, in archaic paleo-Hebrew script.
