4QRuthb (Ruth Scroll, copy b, Cave 4)

Three minute fragments from Cave 4, preserving parts of Ruth 1: the opening verses and Naomi's farewell to Orpah and Ruth (Ruth 1:1-6, 12-15). It is the second Cave 4 Ruth copy, distinct from 4Q104, and overlaps it only at the book's opening.

Significance

Completes the set of four Qumran Ruth copies (2Q16, 2Q17, 4Q104, 4Q105). Its Herodian date shows Ruth was still being copied at Qumran in the decades before the settlement's destruction.
