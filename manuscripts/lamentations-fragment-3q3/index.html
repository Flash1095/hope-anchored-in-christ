Description
Fragments of a Hebrew copy of Lamentations from Qumran Cave 3, dated c. 30 BC-AD 50. It preserves parts of Lamentations 1:10-12 and 3:53-62, the latter only partially.
Significance
With 4Q111, 5Q6 and 5Q7, one of only four Qumran copies of Lamentations, confirming the book's circulation in Hebrew in the 1st century BC-AD.
