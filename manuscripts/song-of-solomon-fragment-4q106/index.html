Description
One large and three small fragments, from three columns, of a Hebrew copy of the Song of Solomon from Qumran Cave 4, on tan parchment, dated early Herodian by Yardeni. NOTE on an old label: some lists call this a 'Pesher on Song of Songs', following Milik's original list; the DJD edition treats it as a copy of the Song itself, and it is entered here as such, not as a commentary. The Song 6:11 reading is marked uncertain in the published list.
Significance
The earliest Qumran copy of the Song, and the fullest of the four Song manuscripts after 4Q107; together with 4Q107, 4Q108 and 6Q6 it preserves about 47 of the Song's 117 verses.
