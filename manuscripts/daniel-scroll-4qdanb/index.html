Description
A substantial fragmentary copy of Daniel from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XVI (Ulrich) as 4Q113. Its Aramaic section covers Daniel 5:10-7:28 and its Hebrew section Daniel 8:1-16, spanning the handwriting on the wall, the lions' den, the four beasts and the ram-and-goat vision. The reading at Daniel 7:11 is marked uncertain in the Cave 4 list. It is a different manuscript from Batch 1's 4QDana (4Q112). Publish-prep note: Daniel 6:8-22, 27-29 was listed in Hebrew numbering and has been converted to English KJV numbering (Daniel 6:7-21, 26-28) per SBL Handbook of Style Appendix B (English/Hebrew versification table) (English Daniel 5:31 = Hebrew 6:1; English 6:1-28 = Hebrew 6:2-29). The uncertain Daniel 7:11 reading has been removed from PreservedPassages and is stated in the Description.
