Genesis Scroll (4QGenc)

A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Genesis from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q3, preserving parts of the Joseph narrative (Genesis 40-41).

Significance

A Herodian-period witness to the Joseph story in Genesis.
