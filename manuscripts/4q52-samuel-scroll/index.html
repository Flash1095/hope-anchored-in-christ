Date Approx
c. 250 BCE
Description
Twenty-three fragments from Cave 4, published in DJD XVII (Cross, Parry and Saley), preserving parts of 1 Samuel only: Samuel's farewell, Saul's reign, the anointing of David (1 Samuel 16), David's flight from Saul, and David at Keilah. Shorter catalogue summaries compress this to 1 Samuel 16:1-11; 19:10-17; 20:27-42; 21:1-10; 23:9-17; the fuller range here follows the DJD-based survey of Samuel and Kings.

Significance
Dated to around 250 BCE, it is among the oldest surviving manuscripts of biblical Hebrew and the earliest of the four Qumran Samuel copies, predating the Batch 1 copy 4Q51 (4QSama, 50-25 BCE) by about two centuries.
