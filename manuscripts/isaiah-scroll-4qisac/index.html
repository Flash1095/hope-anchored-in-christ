Isaiah Scroll (4QIsac)

Category

Date Approx
c. 50 BC
Description
A substantial fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich). A distinctive feature noted by Emanuel Tov is that it writes the Tetragrammaton and other divine names in palaeo-Hebrew characters, including prefixes and, uniquely, suffixes of 'elohim in palaeo-Hebrew. Its orthography is close to 1QIsaa in overlapping passages, though the two differ in details.

Significance
The most scribally distinctive Cave 4 Isaiah copy: its palaeo-Hebrew divine names show how a Qumran scribe could mark the divine name as special inside an otherwise ordinary biblical copy, and its overlap with 1QIsaa lets the two copies be compared directly.
