Deuteronomy Scroll (4QDeutf)

A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Deuteronomy from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q33, with remains across Deuteronomy 4-27. Publish-prep note: Deuteronomy 23:21-26 was listed in Hebrew numbering and has been converted to English KJV numbering (Deuteronomy 23:20-25) per SBL Handbook of Style Appendix B (English/Hebrew versification table) (English Deuteronomy 22:30 = Hebrew 23:1; English 23:1-25 = Hebrew 23:2-26).

Significance

A broad Late Hasmonean witness to Deuteronomy.
