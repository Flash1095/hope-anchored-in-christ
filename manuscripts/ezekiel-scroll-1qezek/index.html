Ezekiel Scroll (1QEzek)

A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Ezekiel from Qumran Cave 1, published in DJD I (Barthelemy-Milik) as 1Q9. It consists of two fragments, one with letters from three lines that remains unidentified and one preserving legible elements of three lines corresponding to Ezekiel 4:16-5:1, the breaking of the staff of bread in Jerusalem. Its orthography is described as corresponding exactly with the Masoretic Text.

Significance

The only Ezekiel copy from Cave 1 and one of only six Qumran Ezekiel manuscripts in total. Its few legible lines agree exactly with the Masoretic Text, a small early data point for Ezekiel's stability.
