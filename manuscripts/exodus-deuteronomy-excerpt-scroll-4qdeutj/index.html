Description
An excerpted manuscript from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q37, collecting Deuteronomy passages (Decalogue, Shema, covenant blessings) together with Exodus Passover material, in non-canonical order. NOTE: whether the Exodus and Deuteronomy fragments belong to a single manuscript was long unresolved; a 2023 material reconstruction argues they do, but the transition between the two sets is not preserved, so the unity is stated as argued, not as certain. It appears ONCE here, with both books in Work, and is classified as an excerpt collection rather than a continuous Deuteronomy scroll.
Significance
A late Herodian excerpt collection showing which Deuteronomy and Exodus passages were gathered for liturgical or study use.
