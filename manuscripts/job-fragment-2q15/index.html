Description
A single small fragment of a Hebrew copy of Job from Qumran Cave 2, preserving only part of Job 33:28-30. It is the smallest of the six Qumran Job manuscripts (2Q15, 4Q99, 4Q100, 4Q101, 4Q157, 11Q10). No date is stated for it in the published list consulted, so none is given here.
Significance
Its value is narrow but real: even three verses show Job already circulating in Hebrew at Qumran, and its Job 33 text sits alongside the much fuller Job 31-37 witness of 4Q99.
