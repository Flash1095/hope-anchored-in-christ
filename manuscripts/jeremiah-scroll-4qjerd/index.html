Jeremiah Scroll (4QJerd)

A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Jeremiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV as 4Q72a, formerly grouped under the umbrella name 4QJerb (olim 4QJerb). It preserves only Jeremiah 43:2-10, from the aftermath of Jerusalem's fall, and is described as partly similar to the Vorlage of the Septuagint. Its dating is given as like that of 4QJerb (4Q71). It is a separate manuscript from both 4QJerb (4Q71) and Batch 1's 4QJerc (4Q72); the similar numbers 4Q72, 4Q72a and 4Q72b must not be conflated.

Significance

A small but textually important fragment: together with 4QJerb it shows the Septuagint-type Jeremiah was represented by more than one Qumran copy, in a passage about the flight to Egypt after the fall of Jerusalem.
