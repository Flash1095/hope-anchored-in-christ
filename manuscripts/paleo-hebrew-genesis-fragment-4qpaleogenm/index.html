Paleo-Hebrew Genesis Fragment (4QpaleoGenm)

A single paleo-Hebrew fragment from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q12. DJD IX describes it as a fragment with remains of Genesis 26 only. CORRECTION NOTE: some online lists repeat the long Exodus passage list of 4Q22 (4QpaleoExodm, Batch 1) under 4Q12; that repetition is NOT followed here, because DJD IX assigns 4Q12 only Genesis 26 material.

Significance

A small paleo-Hebrew witness to the Isaac narrative in Genesis 26; its value is limited by its size, and the erroneous Exodus attribution found in some lists must not be repeated.
