Paleo-Hebrew Leviticus Fragment (6QpaleoLev)

A paleo-Hebrew fragment of Leviticus from Qumran Cave 6, catalogued as 6Q2, preserving the anointing of Aaron.

Significance

A very small early paleo-Hebrew witness to Leviticus 8.
