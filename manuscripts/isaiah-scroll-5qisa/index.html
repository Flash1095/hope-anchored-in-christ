Date Approx

Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 5, published as 5Q3. It is the only Isaiah copy from Cave 5 and preserves only Isaiah 40:16 and 40:18-19, from the incomparability-of-God passage. It is a different manuscript from the Cave 1 and Cave 4 copies, and from the Wadi Murabba'at Isaiah copy (Mur 3), which is excluded from this batch as a non-Qumran site.

Significance
It completes the Qumran Isaiah count at twenty-one copies (two from Cave 1, eighteen from Cave 4, one from Cave 5), showing Isaiah's exceptional popularity at Qumran despite its tiny surviving content.
