Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Jeremiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV as 4Q72b, formerly grouped under the umbrella name 4QJerb (olim 4QJerb). It preserves only Jeremiah 50:4-6, the opening of the oracle against Babylon, and is described as similar to the Masoretic Text. It is a separate manuscript from 4QJerb (4Q71), 4QJerd (4Q72a) and Batch 1's 4QJerc (4Q72).
