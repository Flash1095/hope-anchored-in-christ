Description
Fragments of Joshua from Qumran Cave 4, published as 4QJosha (DJD 14). The surviving text spans five chapters (5, 6, 7, 8, 10), including the fall of Jericho material (chapter 6), the Achan episode (chapter 7), and the southern campaign (chapter 10). The Leon Levy record shows 44 images of this manuscript.

Significance
The fullest Qumran witness to Joshua. Its most discussed feature is the position of Joshua 8:34–35 before 5:2, showing that the blessings-and-curses passage circulated in a different place in the book; Ulrich cautions that it is not certain the altar-building verses (8:30–31) preceded them, since that text would fall in an unpreserved part of the preceding column. Its text shows variations in wording and omissions against the Masoretic Text, with some readings aligning with the Greek text.
