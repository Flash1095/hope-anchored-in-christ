Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Genesis from Qumran Cave 4, published as 4QGenb in DJD XII (Davila). Its best-preserved portion is a relatively intact column covering the opening of Genesis 1 (Genesis 1:1-25a in the Lyon summary), with further scattered remains from Genesis 2, 4 and 5. The surviving text is essentially identical to the Masoretic Text; one exhibit summary notes a single minor spelling difference in Genesis 1:15.

Significance
An early witness to the stability of the Genesis text: a copy made in the first century AD already agrees with the Masoretic Text that underlies modern Hebrew Bibles, including at the creation account in Genesis 1. Its value is as a textual witness, not as a complete Genesis scroll — only scattered verses survive, so it proves nothing about chapters it does not preserve.
