Amos Fragment (5QAmos)

Category

Date Approx

Description
A single small fragment from Qumran Cave 5, published by Milik in DJD III (pp. 173-174) as an Amos manuscript without further comment in the editio princeps. It preserves only the opening oracle against Damascus (Amos 1:2-5). DATE UNCERTAINTY STATED: DJD III dates the script to the 1st century CE; one widely repeated summary gives 50 BC-AD 50, so the entry uses the broader 1st century AD and does not narrow it further. Hartog notes it may have contained only Amos. Its Amos 1:3 reading, mentioning the pregnant women of Gilead, has been cited as Hebrew support for the longer Septuagint reading against the shorter Masoretic Text; other scholars argue the phrase is a contamination from Amos 1:13 — the reading is noted, not settled, here.

Significance
The only Qumran biblical manuscript of the Minor Prophets outside Cave 4, and the only single-book Amos fragment from Qumran: it shows Amos circulating outside the Cave 4 Twelve scrolls, and it is a key witness in the Amos 1:3 text-critical debate (Masoretic Text versus Septuagint).
