Description
The only identified Qumran copy of the Ezra-Nehemiah work, from Qumran Cave 4, published as 4QEzra (DJD 16, pp. 291-93, pl. 38). Three fragments preserve bits and pieces of Ezra chapters 4, 5 and 6, including the Aramaic section of Ezra (Ezra 4:9-6:5 is in Aramaic in the biblical text itself, and is preserved here in Aramaic — this is the original language of that passage, not a translation). In antiquity Ezra-Nehemiah was one work, and the DJD list labels this manuscript '4QEzra (= Ezra-Nehemiah)'; that label is about the ancient combined work, NOT evidence that Nehemiah text survives in it. Work and RelatedBooks therefore list Ezra only.

Significance
The sole Qumran witness to the Ezra-Nehemiah work, and therefore the best-preserved by default. Its surviving text is consistent with the Masoretic Text tradition. No Nehemiah verse text survives in any identified Qumran manuscript, so Nehemiah has no entry of its own; an earlier reported Nehemiah fragment was challenged as a forgery.
