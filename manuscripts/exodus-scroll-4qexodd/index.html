Description
A single fragment from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q15, in which Exodus 13:15-16 is followed directly by Exodus 15:1 (the Song of the Sea), suggesting an excerpted or hymnic arrangement rather than a continuous Exodus scroll.
Significance
Notable for its jump from the Passover law to the Song of the Sea; its excerpt character means it must not be cited as a continuous Exodus text.
