Description
A Greek papyrus manuscript of Leviticus from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q120 (DJD IX), in 97 fragments. It renders the divine name as IAO (a transliteration) rather than translating it as Lord, a feature of early Greek Scripture manuscripts. Publish-prep note: Leviticus 5:16-24 was listed in Septuagint numbering and has been converted to English KJV numbering (Leviticus 5:16-19; 6:1-5) per SBL Handbook of Style Appendix B (English/Hebrew versification table) (English Leviticus 6:1-7 = Hebrew 5:20-26) and Himbaza's contents table for 4Q120 (5:16-19; 6:1-5 [5:20-24]). The uncertain Leviticus 2:7-8 reading has been removed from PreservedPassages.
Significance
A major Greek witness to Leviticus 1-6, notable for its IAO rendering of the divine name.
