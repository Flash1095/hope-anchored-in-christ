Description
A fragmentary Aramaic copy of Daniel from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XVI (Ulrich) as 4Q115. It preserves passages from Daniel 3 (the furnace), Daniel 4 (Nebuchadnezzar's dream) and Daniel 7 (the beasts and the Ancient of Days), all from the book's Aramaic section. The opening verses at Daniel 3:8-10 are marked uncertain in the Cave 4 list. It is a different manuscript from Batch 1's 4QDana (4Q112).
