Description
An Aramaic translation (targum) of Leviticus from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q156, preserving parts of Leviticus 16 (Day of Atonement). It is a translation, not a Hebrew copy, and is included as such, with that distinction stated.
Significance
The Qumran Aramaic witness to Leviticus 16, showing the Torah also circulated in Aramaic translation.
