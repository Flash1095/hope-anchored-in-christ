Description
A single small fragment (PAM 43.680 frag. 46) identified by Tigchelaar (2020) as a Psalms manuscript, preserving Psalm 41:9-10 in stichometric arrangement with one-colon lines. It is the first Qumran evidence for Psalm 41. No date is stated in the identification note consulted, so none is given here. Preserved-passage ranges for this manuscript are given in English (KJV) numbering; the manuscript itself carries no modern psalm numbers.
Significance
An early Hebrew witness to the Psalms, predating the medieval Masoretic manuscripts by about a thousand years. Its chief value is textual: it shows which psalms and which readings were already in circulation in the Second Temple period, and, where its psalm order differs from the Masoretic Psalter, that the order of the Psalter was not yet fully fixed.
