Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich) as 4Q61. It preserves only Isaiah 42:14-43:24 in part, from the Servant and exodus-renewal passages. It is a different manuscript from 4QIsah (4Q62), which preserves the preceding verses of Isaiah 42; some secondary lists wrongly give both manuscripts the number 4Q61, and that duplication is not followed here.

Significance
A small but focused witness to Isaiah 42-43, the chapters where the Servant and the new-exodus promises stand side by side; its value is confined to those verses.
