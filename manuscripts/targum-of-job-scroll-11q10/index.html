Description
A substantial Aramaic Targum (translation) of Job from Qumran Cave 11, preserved in 39 columns but incompletely, covering Job 17:14 to 42:11 with gaps. It is written in late Herodian script, dated by Zuckerman to c. the first half of the 1st century AD. Some scholars label it more neutrally as an Aramaic version of Job rather than a targum in the later rabbinic sense, since its translation technique (including deliberate omission and re-reading in difficult passages) differs from later targums; that debate is stated, not settled, here.
Significance
The fullest ancient Aramaic witness to Job and the clearest proof that Job was studied in Aramaic at Qumran. Its differences from the Hebrew text show how Job was understood and explained in the 1st century AD, while its gaps mean it cannot be quoted as a complete Aramaic Job.
