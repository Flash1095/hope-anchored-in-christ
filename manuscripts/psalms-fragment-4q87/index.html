Description
Fragments of a Hebrew Psalms copy from Qumran Cave 4, in Herodian script, ranging across Psalms 76-130. Its sequence differs from the Masoretic Psalter, and it is one of the three scrolls (with 11Q5 and 11Q6) attesting the 11QPsalms-type collection. Preserved-passage ranges for this manuscript are given in English (KJV) numbering; the manuscript itself carries no modern psalm numbers. Publish-prep note: every PreservedPassages range in this entry was re-versified to English (KJV) numbering against Eugene Ulrich, The Biblical Qumran Scrolls (2010), Index of Biblical Passages (MT/BHS numbering, DJD XVI-derived), with the SBL Appendix B table. First-pass conversions (over-length ranges only, from the earlier cleanup): Psalms 89:50-53 to 89:49-52. Final-pass conversions: Psalms 76:10-12 to 76:9-11; Psalms 81:2-3 to 81:1-2; Psalms 88:1-4 to 88:1-3; Psalms 89:44-46 to 89:43-45. All other ranges were verified as already correct English numbering (untitled psalms and first-clause-title psalms do not shift). The Psalm 77 superscription is also preserved but carries no English body verse, so it does not appear in the passage ranges. The Psalm 88 superscription is also preserved alongside its body verses.
Significance
An early Hebrew witness to the Psalms, predating the medieval Masoretic manuscripts by about a thousand years. Its chief value is textual: it shows which psalms and which readings were already in circulation in the Second Temple period, and, where its psalm order differs from the Masoretic Psalter, that the order of the Psalter was not yet fully fixed.
