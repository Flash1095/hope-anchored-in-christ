Psalms Fragment (11QPse?)

A single small fragment from Qumran Cave 11, catalogued as 11QPse with a question mark in some editions and described as 'possibly' a Psalms copy in the Cave 11 survey. It preserves only Psalm 50:3-7. It is included here ONLY with that uncertainty stated: its status as a separate Psalms manuscript is not certain. Preserved-passage ranges for this manuscript are given in English (KJV) numbering; the manuscript itself carries no modern psalm numbers.

Significance

An early Hebrew witness to the Psalms, predating the medieval Masoretic manuscripts by about a thousand years. Its chief value is textual: it shows which psalms and which readings were already in circulation in the Second Temple period, and, where its psalm order differs from the Masoretic Psalter, that the order of the Psalter was not yet fully fixed.
