Description
A papyrus fragment from Qumran Cave 6 catalogued with a question mark as pap6QPs (6Q5), preserving Psalm 78:36-37. BOTH its identification as a Psalms manuscript and its date are uncertain in the published literature: Flint lists it as pap6QPs? and states its dating is uncertain, and a later study excludes it from the Psalms scrolls for lack of sufficient evidence. It is included here ONLY with that uncertainty stated, never as a certain Psalms copy.
Significance
If the identification is right, it is the only Qumran Psalms copy on papyrus; if not, it is an unidentified papyrus fragment. Either way it adds nothing certain to the Psalms text.
