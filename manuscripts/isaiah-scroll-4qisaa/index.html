Date Approx

Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich). Its surviving verses come from Isaiah 1-23 only; it is not a complete Isaiah scroll.

Significance
One of eighteen fragmentary Isaiah copies from Cave 4. Its verses are a further independent witness to the early Isaiah text, but a fragment of this size proves nothing about passages it does not preserve.
