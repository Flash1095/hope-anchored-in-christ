The Twelve Minor Prophets (4QXIIf)

Category
Biblical
Cave
Qumran Cave 4
Completeness
Fragment
Date Approx
c. 50 BC
Description
Two small fragments of a Twelve Prophets manuscript: one Jonah fragment (the storm, the casting of lots, and Jonah thrown into the sea, Jonah 1:6-8, 10-16) and one Micah fragment. UNCERTAIN IDENTIFICATION, STATED NOT HIDDEN: the Micah material (Micah 5:1-2, English numbering; Hebrew 4:14-5:1, so the Bethlehem ruler verse is English Micah 5:2) rests on fragment 5 of this manuscript, and Hartog reports that it is not certain that fragment 5 really belongs to 4Q81 (Garcia Martinez criticised Fuller's joining of fragment 5). The Micah passage is therefore carried with a question mark and must not be cited as a certain Micah witness without that caveat. The manuscript was first published as a separate Micah fragment (Fuller, RevQ 1993) before being joined to the Twelve manuscript in DJD XV.

Significance
A second, independent Qumran witness to Jonah 1 beyond the large Twelve scrolls, and — if fragment 5 belongs — the only Qumran witness to the Bethlehem ruler passage (Micah 5:2) outside the larger scrolls. Its value for Micah depends entirely on the disputed fragment join, which is why the Micah attribution is flagged uncertain here.
