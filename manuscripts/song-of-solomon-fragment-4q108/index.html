Description
A tiny fragment of a Hebrew copy of the Song of Solomon from Qumran Cave 4, containing only ten letters from two lines: five letters from Song 3:7 (the end of 'Solomon' and 'sixty') and one word from Song 3:8 ('equipped'). It is demonstrably not part of 4Q106 or 4Q107: its hand differs from 4Q107, and the words it preserves are already accounted for in 4Q106. Its spelling of the Song 3:8 verb includes a vav for the passive vowel, where the Masoretic Text spells the word with four letters.
Significance
Minimal in itself, but it proves a third, distinct Cave 4 copy of the Song, and its spelling variant is a small early witness to the Song's textual fluidity.
