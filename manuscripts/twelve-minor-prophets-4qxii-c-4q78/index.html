The Twelve Minor Prophets (4QXIIc)

Category

Cave

Date Approx
c. 75-50 BC
Description
Best-preserved Qumran copy for Joel only. A Hebrew Twelve Prophets scroll from Qumran Cave 4, surviving in fragments. Its surviving portions cover Hosea, Joel, Amos, Zephaniah and a questionable Malachi fragment. One summary describes it as containing many scribal mistakes but also very many original readings. Note: a possible Malachi 3:6-7 fragment in this scroll is identified only with a question mark and is not counted here as a Malachi copy. A possible Amos 1:1 fragment is likewise identified only with a question mark and is not counted here.

Significance
Best-preserved Qumran biblical copy of Joel: its Joel coverage (Joel 1:10-20; 2:1, 8-23; 3:6, 8-10, 12-19, 21 in English numbering) is substantially larger than 4Q82's Joel coverage. It also preserves a large block of Hosea 4 and Amos 3 and 7, useful for checking the Masoretic Text, but it is not best for Hosea or Amos (both: 4Q82) or Zephaniah (4Q77) or Malachi (4Q76).
