Description
Fragments of a Hebrew copy of Job from Qumran Cave 4, in Herodian script. The surviving verses are scattered across Job 8, 9, 13, 14 and 31, so the copy cannot be reconstructed as a continuous run of text.
Significance
An early Hebrew witness to Job's dialogues and to chapter 31, predating the medieval Masoretic manuscripts by about a thousand years; its fragmentary state limits it to confirming, not revising, the early text of those verses.
