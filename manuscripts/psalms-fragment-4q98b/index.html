Description
Fragments of a Hebrew Psalms copy from Qumran Cave 4, in Herodian script, preserving parts of Psalms 5 and 6. Preserved-passage ranges for this manuscript are given in English (KJV) numbering; the manuscript itself carries no modern psalm numbers. Publish-prep note: every PreservedPassages range in this entry was re-versified to English (KJV) numbering against Eugene Ulrich, The Biblical Qumran Scrolls (2010), Index of Biblical Passages (MT/BHS numbering, DJD XVI-derived), with the SBL Appendix B table. First-pass conversions (over-length ranges only, from the earlier cleanup): Psalms 5:8-13 to 5:7-12. Final pass verified every remaining range as already correct English numbering (untitled psalms and first-clause-title psalms do not shift). The Psalm 6 superscription is also preserved but carries no English body verse, so it does not appear in the passage ranges.
Significance
An early Hebrew witness to the Psalms, predating the medieval Masoretic manuscripts by about a thousand years. Its chief value is textual: it shows which psalms and which readings were already in circulation in the Second Temple period, and, where its psalm order differs from the Masoretic Psalter, that the order of the Psalter was not yet fully fixed.
