Description
Fragments of Ruth from Qumran Cave 2, published as 2QRutha. The surviving text spans three chapters (2, 3 and 4): Ruth's gleaning and her meeting with Boaz (chapter 2), the threshing-floor scene (chapter 3), and the redemption transaction at the gate (chapter 4). The Leon Levy record shows 38 images of this manuscript. It is a Hebrew original, not a translation.

Significance
The fullest Qumran witness to Ruth by chapter span and verse coverage (about 20 verse-ranges across three chapters). Ruth copies at Qumran show only slight variations from the Masoretic Text. See close_calls: 4Q104 is the flagged alternative (earlier, and the only continuous opening of the book).
