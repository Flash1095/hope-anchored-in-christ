Daniel Scroll (1QDana)

Category

Date Approx

Description
A fragmentary copy of Daniel from Qumran Cave 1, published as 1Q71. It preserves Daniel 1:10-17 in Hebrew and Daniel 2:2-6 across the book's language switch, Hebrew up to Daniel 2:4a and Aramaic from 2:4b. It is a different manuscript from Batch 1's 4QDana (4Q112) and from 1QDanb (1Q72).

Significance
Together with 1QDanb it shows Daniel was read at Qumran in Cave 1 copies as well as Cave 4 copies, and it preserves the exact point where Daniel changes language from Hebrew to Aramaic.
