4QChr (Chronicles Fragment)

The only identified Qumran copy of Chronicles, from Qumran Cave 4, published as 4QChr / 4QChron. It is a single fragment with letters from two columns, the first column unidentified and the second offering a few letters from four verses (2 Chronicles 28:27-29:3) — the death of Ahaz and the accession of Hezekiah. In antiquity Chronicles was one work, but only 2 Chronicles text survives in this manuscript, so Work and RelatedBooks list 2 Chronicles only.

Significance

The sole Qumran witness to Chronicles, and therefore the best-preserved by default. No 1 Chronicles passage survives at Qumran in any identified manuscript. Its surviving text is consistent with the Masoretic Text tradition. It is a Hebrew original, not a translation. Only a few letters survive from each of the four verses, so the identification rests on a small amount of text.
