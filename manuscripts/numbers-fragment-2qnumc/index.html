Numbers Fragment (2QNumc)

A tiny fragmentary Hebrew copy of Numbers from Qumran Cave 2, catalogued as 2Q8, preserving only Numbers 7:88 (the sum of dedication offerings).

Significance

A single-verse witness to Numbers 7.
