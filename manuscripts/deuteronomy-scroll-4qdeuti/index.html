Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Deuteronomy from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q36. Publish-prep note: Deuteronomy 23:22-26 was listed in Hebrew numbering and has been converted to English KJV numbering (Deuteronomy 23:21-25) per SBL Handbook of Style Appendix B (English/Hebrew versification table) (English Deuteronomy 22:30 = Hebrew 23:1; English 23:1-25 = Hebrew 23:2-26).
Significance
A Late Hasmonean witness to Deuteronomy laws of warfare and community life.
