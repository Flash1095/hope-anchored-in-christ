Description
Fragments of a Hebrew Psalms copy from Qumran Cave 11, in Herodian script, ranging across Psalms 6-116. Preserved-passage ranges for this manuscript are given in English (KJV) numbering; the manuscript itself carries no modern psalm numbers. Publish-prep note: every PreservedPassages range in this entry was re-versified to English (KJV) numbering against DJD XXIII via Garcia Martinez & Tigchelaar, The Dead Sea Scrolls Study Edition (Hebrew/MT fragment headings), with the SBL Appendix B table. First-pass conversions (over-length ranges only, from the earlier cleanup): Psalms 36:13 to 36:12; 39:13-14 to 39:12-13. Final-pass conversions: Psalms 6:2-4 to 6:1-3; Psalms 9:3-6 to 9:2-5; Psalms 18:26-29 to 18:25-28; Psalms 18:39-42 to 18:38-41; Psalms 45:6-8 to 45:5-7; Psalms 59:5-8 to 59:4-7; Psalms 68:1-5 to 68:1-4; Psalms 68:14-18 to 68:13-17; Psalms 81:4-9 to 81:3-8. All other ranges were verified as already correct English numbering (untitled psalms and first-clause-title psalms do not shift). The superscriptions of Psalms 37, 40 and 68 are also preserved; the passage ranges give body verses only (the Psalm 37 superscription stands as the opening clause of verse 1).
Significance
An early Hebrew witness to the Psalms, predating the medieval Masoretic manuscripts by about a thousand years. Its chief value is textual: it shows which psalms and which readings were already in circulation in the Second Temple period, and, where its psalm order differs from the Masoretic Psalter, that the order of the Psalter was not yet fully fixed.
