Description
A lengthy Hebrew copy of Numbers from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XII (Jastram). Remains of 27 columns survive, covering Numbers 11-36 in fragments, written in early Herodian semiformal Hebrew script. A few lines are written in red ink. It is one of only a handful of Qumran biblical manuscripts with over 1,000 words preserved.

Significance
The fullest Numbers witness from Qumran, and a pre-Samaritan (expansionist) text: many of its variants agree with the Samaritan Pentateuch against the Masoretic Text, demonstrating that differing versions of Numbers circulated in the Second Temple period.
