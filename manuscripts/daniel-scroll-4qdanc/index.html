Daniel Scroll (4QDanc)

Date Approx

Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Daniel from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XVI (Ulrich) as 4Q114. It preserves only Daniel 10-11, the final vision's opening and the kings of the north and south. Palaeographically it is dated to the late second century BC (Flint's table, citing Ulrich, DJD 16), making it the oldest Qumran Daniel copy; one summary table's 'first half of the second century BC' dating for the next Daniel manuscript (4QDane) is noted there as a presumed typo and is not applied here. It is a different manuscript from Batch 1's 4QDana (4Q112).

Significance
The oldest surviving Daniel manuscript. A copy of Daniel 10-11 already existed in the late second century BC, which is central evidence in discussions of when the book of Daniel was composed.
