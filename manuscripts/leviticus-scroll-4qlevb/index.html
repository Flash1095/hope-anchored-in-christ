Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Leviticus from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q24 (DJD XII). NOTE: recent scholarship (Tigchelaar) has argued 4Q24 should be split into two manuscripts (4Q24a, Leviticus 1-3, and 4Q24b, Leviticus 21-25); this entry keeps the traditional single catalogue number, with the split debate stated here, rather than inventing two entries.
Significance
A Hasmonean-period witness to Leviticus offerings, priestly laws, and festivals.
