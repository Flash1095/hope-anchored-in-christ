Jeremiah Scroll (2QJer)

Category
Biblical
Cave
Qumran Cave 2
Completeness
Substantial fragment
Date Approx
1st half of the 1st century AD
Description
The only Jeremiah copy from Qumran Cave 2, published as 2Q13. It preserves parts of Jeremiah 42-49, including the oracles against the nations placed at the end of the book, and is described as semi-Masoretic in text type. Fragments doubtfully identified with this manuscript (Jeremiah 13:22; 32:24-25; 48:2-4, 41-42) are NOT counted in the PreservedPassages above; only the securely identified passages are listed.

Significance
The latest-dated Qumran Jeremiah copy in the group and the only one from Cave 2. Its placement of the oracles against the nations at the end of the book aligns it with the Masoretic order rather than the Septuagint order.
