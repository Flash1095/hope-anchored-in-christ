Deuteronomy Fragment, Possibly (6QDeut)

A fragment from Qumran Cave 6, catalogued as 6Q20, listed as possibly from Deuteronomy. DISPUTED: the catalogue marks it with a question mark and relates it only tentatively to Deuteronomy 11:10. It is included only with that dispute stated, never as a certain Deuteronomy copy.

Significance

A disputed single-verse fragment; it must not be counted as certain evidence for a Cave 6 Deuteronomy copy.
