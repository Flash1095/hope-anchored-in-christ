Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich) as 4Q69b. It preserves only a single verse, Isaiah 30:23, on rain for the seed sown in the ground.

Significance
The smallest Qumran Isaiah manuscript by content: a single-verse fragment. It counts as one of the twenty-one Qumran Isaiah copies, but proves nothing beyond Isaiah 30:23.
