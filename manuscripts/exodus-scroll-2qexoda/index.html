Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Exodus from Qumran Cave 2, catalogued as 2Q2. Two readings are marked uncertain in the catalogue (Exodus 21:18-20 and 30:21).
Significance
A Cave 2 witness spanning the oppression in Egypt, the plagues, the Passover, and the golden calf episode.
