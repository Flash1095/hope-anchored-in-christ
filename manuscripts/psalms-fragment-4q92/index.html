Description
Fragments of a Hebrew Psalms copy from Qumran Cave 4, in Hasmonean script, preserving parts of Psalms 26, 27, 30 and 135. Its sequence (Psalm 135 followed by further material) differs from the Masoretic order. Preserved-passage ranges for this manuscript are given in English (KJV) numbering; the manuscript itself carries no modern psalm numbers. Publish-prep note: every PreservedPassages range in this entry was re-versified to English (KJV) numbering against Eugene Ulrich, The Biblical Qumran Scrolls (2010), Index of Biblical Passages (MT/BHS numbering, DJD XVI-derived), with the SBL Appendix B table. First-pass conversions (over-length ranges only, from the earlier cleanup): Psalms 30:9-13 to 30:8-12. Final pass verified every remaining range as already correct English numbering (untitled psalms and first-clause-title psalms do not shift).
Significance
An early Hebrew witness to the Psalms, predating the medieval Masoretic manuscripts by about a thousand years. Its chief value is textual: it shows which psalms and which readings were already in circulation in the Second Temple period, and, where its psalm order differs from the Masoretic Psalter, that the order of the Psalter was not yet fully fixed.
