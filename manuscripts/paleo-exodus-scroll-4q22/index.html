Description
A lengthy Hebrew copy of Exodus in palaeo-Hebrew script, published in DJD IX (Skehan, Ulrich, Sanderson). The original scroll occupied about 45 columns, spanning Exodus 6:25 to 37:16. It is poorly preserved in detail — no column survives entirely undamaged and many columns are represented only by scattered fragments — but its overall range and word-count (over 1,000 words preserved) make it the most extensive Exodus manuscript from Qumran.

Significance
The key witness to the expansive, pre-Samaritan form of Exodus: it attests harmonistic expansions similar to those in the Samaritan Pentateuch, showing that an expanded Exodus text circulated in the late Second Temple period alongside the shorter tradition that became the Masoretic Text. It therefore proves textual plurality in this period, not a single uniform Exodus text.
