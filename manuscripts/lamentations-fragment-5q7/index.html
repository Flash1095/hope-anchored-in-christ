Description
Fragments of a Hebrew copy of Lamentations from Qumran Cave 5, dated c. 30 BC-AD 50, preserving only Lamentations 4:17-20, the passage about the anointed of the LORD caught in the enemy's pits.
Significance
The smallest Qumran Lamentations copy, but an independent witness to Lamentations 4 alongside the fuller 5Q6 copy from the same cave.
