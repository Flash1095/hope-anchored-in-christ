1QSam (Samuel Scroll, Cave 1)

Seven fragments from Cave 1, published by Barthelemy in DJD I, preserving scraps from 1 Samuel 18 (Saul offering Merab to David) and from three passages in 2 Samuel (the pursuit of Sheba, the fight with the Philistine giants, and David's mighty men). One summary gives the giants passage as 2 Samuel 21:16-19 rather than 21:16-18; the range here follows the DJD-based Samuel survey. It is the only Samuel copy from Cave 1; the other three Qumran Samuel copies are all from Cave 4.

Significance

Shows Samuel was read at Qumran beyond Cave 4, and its variants include a missing long stretch in 2 Samuel 20:8 and distinctive readings of proper names, so it is an independent textual witness, not a copy of the other Samuel scrolls.
