Description
Fragments of a Hebrew copy of Proverbs from Qumran Cave 4, in Herodian script. It is the largest Qumran Proverbs copy: the published statistical count gives it about 126 words against about 42 for 4Q102 (4QProva, Proverbs 1:27-33; 2:1). The surviving verses come from the collections of individual proverbs in chapters 13-15, with two further verses from chapter 7 whose identification is marked uncertain in the published list.

Significance
An early Hebrew witness to Proverbs, about a thousand years older than the medieval Masoretic manuscripts. The surviving text stands close to the Masoretic Text, with only minor variation in the published count, so its chief value is confirming the early stability of these proverb collections rather than revealing a different edition of the book.
