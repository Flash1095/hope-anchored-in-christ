Ezekiel Scroll (3QEzek)

A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Ezekiel from Qumran Cave 3, published in DJD III (Baillet-Milik-de Vaux) as 3Q1. It survives as a small fragment in Herodian script whose identification with Ezekiel 16:31 and 16:33, resting in part on the rare word for a vaulted place, is described as almost certain rather than fully certain; the letters that remain correspond exactly with the orthography of the Masoretic Text. Verse 32 is not counted as preserved.

Significance

The only Ezekiel copy from Cave 3. Its near-certain but not absolute identification is stated here rather than presented as certain, as required for disputed identifications.
