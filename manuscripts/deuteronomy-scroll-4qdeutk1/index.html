Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Deuteronomy from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q38, the first of three fragments grouped as 4QDeutk. Like 4Q41, it belongs to the excerpted-texts group, collecting selected Deuteronomy passages.
Significance
An Early Herodian excerpt witness to Deuteronomy 5, 11 and 32.
