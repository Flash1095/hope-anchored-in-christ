Description
A fragmentary copy of Daniel from Qumran Cave 1, published as 1Q72. It preserves only Daniel 3:22-30, the end of the fiery-furnace story and Nebuchadnezzar's decree, in Aramaic, as in the Masoretic Text's Aramaic section (Daniel 2:4b-7:28). It is a different manuscript from 1QDana (1Q71) and from Batch 1's 4QDana (4Q112).

Significance
A small Aramaic witness to Daniel 3. Its survival in Cave 1, alongside 1QDana, shows the Aramaic stories of Daniel circulated at Qumran independently of the larger Cave 4 copies.
