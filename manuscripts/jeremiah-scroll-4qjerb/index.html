Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Jeremiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Tov) as 4Q71. It is a DIFFERENT manuscript from Batch 1's 4QJerc (4Q72) and must not be merged with it: 4QJerb is similar to the Hebrew Vorlage behind the Septuagint, while 4QJerc is semi-Masoretic. In older literature '4QJerb' was also used as an umbrella term covering what are now separately cataloged as 4QJerd (4Q72a) and 4QJere (4Q72b); those are separate entries in this batch. Within Jeremiah 9-10 only scattered verses are securely legible in detail, though the fragments span the range listed above.

Significance
The key Qumran witness to the shorter, Septuagint-type edition of Jeremiah: it proves that two different editions of Jeremiah circulated side by side in the Second Temple period, rather than a single uniform text.
