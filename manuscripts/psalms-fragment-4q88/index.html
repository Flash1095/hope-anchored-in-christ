Description
Fragments of a Hebrew Psalms collection from Qumran Cave 4, in late Hasmonean script (c. mid-1st century BC). Alongside canonical psalms it preserves three compositions not in the Masoretic Psalter: the Apostrophe to Zion, the Apostrophe to Judah, and an Eschatological Hymn. Those extra pieces are part of this scroll's contents, not separate canonical psalms. Preserved-passage ranges for this manuscript are given in English (KJV) numbering; the manuscript itself carries no modern psalm numbers.
Significance
An early Hebrew witness to the Psalms, predating the medieval Masoretic manuscripts by about a thousand years. Its chief value is textual: it shows which psalms and which readings were already in circulation in the Second Temple period, and, where its psalm order differs from the Masoretic Psalter, that the order of the Psalter was not yet fully fixed.
