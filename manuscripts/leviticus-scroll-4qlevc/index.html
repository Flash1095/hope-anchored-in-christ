Leviticus Scroll (4QLevc)

A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Leviticus from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q25, with remains from Leviticus 1, 3-5 and 8. Publish-prep note: the compact list '3:16-4:6, 12-14, 23-28' has been expanded to '3:16-4:6, 4:12-14, 4:23-28' so the site's passage linker resolves the continuations to chapter 4, the end chapter of the range; resolved to chapter 3, verses 3:23-28 do not exist (Leviticus 3 has 17 verses). Same verses claimed, chapters made explicit.

Significance

A witness to Leviticus offerings and priestly consecration.
