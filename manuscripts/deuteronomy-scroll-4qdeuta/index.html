Deuteronomy Scroll (4QDeuta)

A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Deuteronomy from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q28, among the earliest Deuteronomy copies from Qumran. Publish-prep note: Deuteronomy 23:26-24:8 was listed in Hebrew numbering and has been converted to English KJV numbering (Deuteronomy 23:25-24:8) per SBL Handbook of Style Appendix B (English/Hebrew versification table) (English Deuteronomy 22:30 = Hebrew 23:1; English 23:1-25 = Hebrew 23:2-26).

Significance

One of the oldest Qumran Deuteronomy witnesses, on divorce and related laws.
