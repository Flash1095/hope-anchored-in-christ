Description
A paleo-Hebrew manuscript of Deuteronomy from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q45, in 65 fragments, generally agreeing with the Masoretic Text against the Samaritan expansions. Publish-prep note: Deuteronomy 13:19 was listed in Hebrew numbering and has been converted to English KJV numbering (Deuteronomy 13:18) per SBL Handbook of Style Appendix B (English/Hebrew versification table) (English Deuteronomy 12:32 = Hebrew 13:1; English 13:1-18 = Hebrew 13:2-19). Also, the compact list '30-12:1, 11-12' has been expanded to '30-12:1, 12:11-12' so the linker resolves the continuation to chapter 12, the end chapter of the range.
Significance
A major paleo-Hebrew witness to Deuteronomy, spanning chapters 7-34.
