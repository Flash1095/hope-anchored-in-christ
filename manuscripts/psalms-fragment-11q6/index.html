Description
Fragments of a Hebrew Psalms copy from Qumran Cave 11, in Herodian script. It is one of the three scrolls (with 11Q5 and 4Q87) attesting the 11QPsalms-type collection, and it also preserves part of the previously unknown Plea for Deliverance also found in 11Q5. Preserved-passage ranges for this manuscript are given in English (KJV) numbering; the manuscript itself carries no modern psalm numbers. Publish-prep note: every PreservedPassages range in this entry was re-versified to English (KJV) numbering against DJD XXIII via Garcia Martinez & Tigchelaar, The Dead Sea Scrolls Study Edition (Hebrew/MT fragment headings), with the SBL Appendix B table. First-pass conversions (over-length ranges only, from the earlier cleanup): Psalms 77:18-21 to 77:17-20. Final pass verified every remaining range as already correct English numbering (untitled psalms and first-clause-title psalms do not shift). The superscriptions of Psalms 78, 133 and 144 stand as the opening clauses of their first verses, which are included in the passage ranges.
Significance
An early Hebrew witness to the Psalms, predating the medieval Masoretic manuscripts by about a thousand years. Its chief value is textual: it shows which psalms and which readings were already in circulation in the Second Temple period, and, where its psalm order differs from the Masoretic Psalter, that the order of the Psalter was not yet fully fixed.
