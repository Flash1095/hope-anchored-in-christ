2QRuthb (Ruth Scroll, copy b)

Category
Biblical
Cave
Qumran Cave 2
Completeness
Fragment
Date Approx
c. 50 BCE
Description
Two fragments from Cave 2, published by Baillet in DJD III, of which one fragment is unidentified, preserving the end of Ruth 3: Boaz's promise to act as kinsman-redeemer, the gift of barley, and Naomi's advice to wait (Ruth 3:13-18). It is the second Cave 2 Ruth copy, distinct from the Batch 1 copy 2Q16 (2QRutha).

Significance
One of four Qumran copies of Ruth, all showing only slight variation from the Masoretic Text, so it adds to the evidence that the short book of Ruth was stably transmitted in the late Second Temple period.
