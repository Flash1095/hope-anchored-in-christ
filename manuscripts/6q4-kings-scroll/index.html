Date Approx

Description
A papyrus scroll of Kings from Qumran Cave 6, published as 6QpapKgs (DJD 3). In antiquity Kings was one work, so this single scroll counts once and covers both 1 and 2 Kings. It is poorly preserved on 94 papyrus fragments, of which only 17 have been identified. Its script is described as archaic, written in a less professional manner but with few errors. The Leon Levy record shows 170 images of this manuscript. It is a Hebrew original, not a translation.

Significance
The only one of the three Qumran Kings manuscripts that covers BOTH 1 and 2 Kings, and the fullest by verse coverage under the coverage criterion (about 26 verse-ranges). It carries multiple literary variants, some found in other witnesses; fragment 15 (2 Kings 7:20-8:5) adds 'according to the word of the man of God' twice against the Masoretic Text, agreeing in one case with the Antiochene Greek text. See close_calls: 4Q54 is the flagged alternative (a single, more legible temple passage).
