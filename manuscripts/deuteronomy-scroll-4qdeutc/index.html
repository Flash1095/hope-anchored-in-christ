Description
A lengthy fragmentary Hebrew copy of Deuteronomy from Qumran Cave 4, catalogued as 4Q30, of a textual type related to the Septuagint. Publish-prep note: the compact list notation after cross-chapter ranges has been expanded so the site's passage linker resolves it correctly (same verses claimed, chapters made explicit): '20-17:7, 15-18:1' is now '20-17:7, 17:15-18:1', and '24-28:14, 18-20, 22-25, 29-30, 48-50, 61' is now '27:24-28:14, 28:18-20, 28:22-25, 28:29-30, 28:48-50, 28:61'. Without this, the linker resolved the continuations to chapters 26-27, where verses such as 26:61 do not exist.
Significance
A major Hasmonean witness spanning Deuteronomy 3-32.
