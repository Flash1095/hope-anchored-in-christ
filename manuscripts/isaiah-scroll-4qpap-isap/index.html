Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich) as 4Q69. It is the only Isaiah manuscript from Qumran written on papyrus rather than leather, and it preserves only Isaiah 5:28-30, the closing verses of the Assyrian-invasion passage.

Significance
Its material makes it the exception among Qumran Isaiah copies: a papyrus biblical fragment in a corpus dominated by leather scrolls, showing papyrus was also used for Scripture at Qumran.
