Description
A fragmentary Hebrew copy of Isaiah from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD XV (Skehan-Ulrich) as 4Q62a. It preserves only part of Isaiah 56:7-57:8, in two fragments, one of which still shows stitching to a previous sheet. A study of its scribal practice describes it as the work of a skilled but hurried scribe, and notes it may have been a bookroll or a series of excerpts rather than a professional full copy; its original scope cannot be determined from what survives.

Significance
A small witness to Isaiah 56-57, including the house-of-prayer passage (Isaiah 56:7). Its scribal features, rather than its length, are what scholars have singled out.
