1QJudg (Judges Scroll)

Fragments of Judges from Qumran Cave 1, published as 1QJudg (DJD 1, pp. 62-64, Barthelemy 1955). The surviving text covers parts of chapters 6, 8 and 9, including the Gideon and Abimelech material. The Leon Levy record lists Site Qumran Cave 1, Manuscript Type Biblical Compositions, Composition Type Scripture, Language Hebrew, Script Square, Period Hellenistic-Roman, Material Parchment, and 4 images. It is a Hebrew original, not a translation.

Significance

One of three Qumran copies of Judges (with 4QJudga, covering chapter 6, and 4QJudgb, covering chapters 19 and 21), and the fullest of the three. Its text is close to the Masoretic Text. It is a Hebrew original, not a translation.
