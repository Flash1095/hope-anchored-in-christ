Description
Fragments of Judges from Qumran Cave 1, published as 1QJudg (DJD 1, pp. 62-64, Barthelemy 1955). Preserved in 40 fragments spanning chapters 1, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 17 and 21 — the widest chapter span of any Qumran Judges copy. The Leon Levy record lists Site Qumran Cave 1, Manuscript Type Biblical Compositions, Composition Type Scripture, Language Hebrew, Script Square, Period Hellenistic-Roman, Material Parchment, and 4 images.

Significance
The fullest Qumran witness to Judges. Its extant text rarely disagrees with the Masoretic Text, with variations mainly in prepositions and plural/singular forms, a slightly longer text in some fragments, and a possible shorter text suggested by a lacuna at Judges 6:25. It is a Hebrew original, not a translation.
