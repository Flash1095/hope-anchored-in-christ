Description
A single paleo-Hebrew scroll from Qumran Cave 4, published in DJD IX, containing the end of Genesis (Genesis 50:26) and extensive Exodus material (Exodus 1-36). It appears ONCE here, with both books in Work.
Significance
A major paleo-Hebrew witness to the Pentateuch, showing the old Hebrew script still in use for Torah scrolls in the Hasmonean period.
