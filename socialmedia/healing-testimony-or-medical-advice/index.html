Healing Testimony or Medical Advice?

Post
Healing Testimony or Medical Advice?
Platform
Facebook
Post Summary
Posts share dramatic healing testimonies alongside claims that prayer alone, a supplement, or a natural remedy replaced medical treatment — sometimes urging others to skip doctors.
Biblical Response
God heals, and testimonies of healing are worth celebrating. But Scripture also honors physicians and ordinary means: Luke was a doctor, and Paul advised Timothy about his health. Pray boldly for healing, thank God for every recovery, and do not let a viral story shame you — or anyone else — out of wise medical care. Faith and medicine are partners, not rivals.
Scripture
James 5:14-15; Luke 5:31; Proverbs 12:15; 1 Timothy 5:23
