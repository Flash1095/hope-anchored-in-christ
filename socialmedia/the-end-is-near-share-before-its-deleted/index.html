The End Is Near — Share Before It's Deleted
Platform
Facebook
Post Summary
Posts warn that a specific disaster, war, or political event fulfills prophecy and that the end will come on a specific date — shared with urgency and a warning that the post will be censored.
Biblical Response
Jesus was clear that no one knows the day or the hour, and two thousand years of date-setting have all been wrong. That does not mean prophecy is unimportant: Scripture calls us to watchfulness, not panic, and to readiness expressed as faithful living today, not frantic forwarding. Urgency is good when it drives us to Christ and to our neighbors; fear-bait is not the gospel.
Scripture
Matthew 24:36; Mark 13:32; Luke 21:28; 2 Peter 3:9
