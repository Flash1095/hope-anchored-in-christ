The Lost Books They Hid From You

Post
The Lost Books They Hid From You
Platform
YouTube
Post Summary
Videos claim the church suppressed secret gospels that would change everything, and that the Bible you have is a censored version chosen by powerful men.
Biblical Response
The story is more interesting — and less conspiratorial — than the videos suggest. The books they mean are mostly later writings, often second or third century, that the early church recognized as different from apostolic teaching, not hidden by it. The canon was discerned over time by churches testing writings for apostolic origin and consistency with the gospel already preached. Read the history, not just the thumbnail.
Scripture
2 Timothy 3:16; Luke 24:44; John 20:31; 2 Peter 1:21
