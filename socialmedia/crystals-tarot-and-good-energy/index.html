Crystals, Tarot, and Good Energy

Post
Crystals, Tarot, and Good Energy
Platform
TikTok
Post Summary
Videos show crystals for anxiety, tarot pulls for decisions, and cleansing rituals for bad energy, presented as harmless self-care with spiritual benefits. The practices promise control over an uncertain life.
Biblical Response
Anxiety and uncertainty are real, and the longing for peace is good — but peace does not come from objects or cards. Scripture points us to a God who invites us to cast our cares on Him because He cares for us personally. If a practice promises spiritual power apart from God, it is not neutral self-care; it is a substitute for the relationship you were made for.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 18:10-12; Isaiah 8:19; Psalm 121:1-2; 1 Peter 5:7
