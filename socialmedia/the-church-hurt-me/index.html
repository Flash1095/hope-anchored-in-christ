The Church Hurt Me

Post
The Church Hurt Me
Platform
TikTok
Post Summary
People share painful stories of being judged, controlled, shamed, or ignored by a church or its leaders. The videos resonate because the hurt is real, and many viewers recognize their own story in them.
Biblical Response
If this is your story, it matters, and it deserves to be heard — not minimized, not explained away. God does not defend the people who wounded you in His name; Jesus reserved His sharpest words for religious leaders who burdened others. Healing rarely comes from pretending it did not happen. It comes slowly, through honest lament, safe community, and a Christ who was Himself rejected by religious people and still welcomes you.
Scripture
Psalm 147:3; Matthew 18:15; Hebrews 10:24-25; Romans 12:18
