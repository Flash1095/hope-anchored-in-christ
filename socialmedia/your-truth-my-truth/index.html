Your Truth, My Truth

Post Summary
Posts insist that truth is personal — what is true for you need not be true for anyone else — and that claiming objective truth is arrogant. The slogan sounds tolerant until two truths collide.
Biblical Response
Everyone lives as if some things are true whether we like them or not — gravity, for instance. Christianity claims that truth is ultimately a Person, not just a set of ideas, and that He can be known. That claim is exclusive, but it is not arrogant to share it humbly: if Jesus really rose from the dead, it matters for everyone, and keeping it to ourselves would be the unloving choice.
Scripture
John 14:6; John 8:32; Proverbs 14:12; 2 Timothy 4:3-4
