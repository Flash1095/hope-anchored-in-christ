The Podcast Said the Bible Is a Myth

Clips from popular podcasts declare the Bible a collection of myths borrowed from older religions, delivered with the relaxed authority of a conversation between friends.

Biblical Response

A confident voice in a good conversation is persuasive, but it is not evidence. The Bible's own writers insist they were reporting what they saw and investigated, not inventing stories, and the church has always invited examination. Enjoy podcasts, but test their claims the way you would any other: check the sources, read the text, and ask whether the guest's certainty matches the scholarship.
