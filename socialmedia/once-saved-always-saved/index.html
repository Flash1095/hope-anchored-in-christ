Post Summary
The debate flares up in quote-posts: one side posts assurance verses, the other posts warning verses, and each side accuses the other of distorting grace or denying it.
Biblical Response
Scripture holds both assurance and warning together, and flattening either side distorts the gospel. Believers can rest in Christ's finished work and in the Father's grip — and that very security produces perseverance, not presumption. If you are trusting Christ, you can have confidence; if you are coasting on a past prayer while walking away, that is the moment to return, not to argue.
Scripture
John 10:28-29; Philippians 2:12-13; Romans 8:38-39; Hebrews 3:14
