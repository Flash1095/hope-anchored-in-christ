AI Jesus Will Pray With You

Post
AI Jesus Will Pray With You
Platform
Instagram
Post Summary
Apps and reels now offer AI-generated images of Jesus that speak comforting words, answer questions, and pray with users, blurring the line between a devotional aid and a digital savior.
Biblical Response
Technology can help us read, study, and remember truth, but prayer is a relationship with a living Person, not a conversation with a program. A generated image cannot know you, love you, or intercede for you — and a real High Priest already does all three. Use tools wisely, but do not let a simulation replace the Savior who hears you Himself.
Scripture
John 1:1; Hebrews 4:15-16; John 14:6; Psalm 46:1
