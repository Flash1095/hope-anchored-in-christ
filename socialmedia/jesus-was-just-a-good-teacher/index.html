Jesus Was Just a Good Teacher
Platform

Post Summary
A popular thread concedes Jesus was wise and kind but insists He was only a teacher — not divine, not risen, just a moral example hijacked by religion.
Biblical Response
That middle option is the one Jesus Himself does not leave open. A merely good teacher does not claim to forgive sins, to be one with the Father, or to rise from the dead — and if He made those claims falsely, He was not good. The Gospels present a man whose words and resurrection demand a verdict: liar, lunatic, or Lord. Read them for yourself and decide.
Scripture
John 10:30; John 8:58; Mark 10:18; John 14:9
