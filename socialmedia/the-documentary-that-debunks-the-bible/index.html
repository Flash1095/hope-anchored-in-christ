The Documentary That Debunks the Bible

Post
The Documentary That Debunks the Bible
Platform
YouTube
Post Summary
Slick long-form documentaries present selective archaeology, fringe scholarship, and confident narration as proof that the Bible is invented — and the production values make the case feel settled.
Biblical Response
A well-edited video is not a peer-reviewed argument, and confidence is not evidence. The Bereans were commended for checking even an apostle's claims against Scripture, and the same habit serves us here: look for what the documentary omits, whether its scholars represent the field, and what the primary sources actually say. Christianity has survived two thousand years of scrutiny; it can survive your honest investigation too.
Scripture
1 Peter 3:15; Acts 17:11; Luke 1:1-4; 2 Timothy 3:16
