The Meme That Mocked My Faith

Post
The Meme That Mocked My Faith
Platform
Other
Post Summary
Memes mocking Jesus, prayer, or church life circulate through group chats and feeds, and believers face a choice between laughing along, firing back, or saying nothing.
Biblical Response
Being mocked for faith is not new — Jesus promised it and endured it without returning insult for insult. You do not have to answer every meme, and you do not have to laugh at what dishonors your Lord. A gentle reply, a quiet refusal to pass it on, or simply walking away are all faithful options. The goal is not winning the thread; it is reflecting the Christ who was mocked and still prayed for His mockers.
Scripture
Matthew 5:11-12; 1 Peter 2:23; Romans 12:17-21; Proverbs 15:1
