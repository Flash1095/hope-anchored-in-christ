Prayer Is a Vending Machine

Post
Prayer Is a Vending Machine
Platform
Instagram
Post Summary
Posts teach a formula: pray the right words, with enough faith, and God must deliver the job, the healing, or the relationship. Unanswered prayer then feels like proof you did it wrong.
Biblical Response
God invites bold, persistent prayer — and He sometimes says yes in wonderful ways. But He is a Father, not a machine: Jesus Himself prayed for a cup to pass and submitted to the Father's will. Unanswered prayer is not always failed faith; sometimes it is God protecting you, redirecting you, or answering in a way you will only understand later. Keep asking, and keep trusting the One who hears.
Scripture
Matthew 6:9-10; James 4:3; 1 John 5:14; Luke 22:42

Prayer; Trust; God's Will; Suffering
