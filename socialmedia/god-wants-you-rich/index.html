God Wants You Rich

Post
God Wants You Rich
Platform
TikTok
Post Summary
Influencers in luxury settings teach that wealth is proof of God's favor and that giving — often to them — unlocks financial blessing. Poverty gets framed as a lack of faith.
Biblical Response
God does bless, and wealth itself is not evil, but the Bible never makes riches the measure of faith. Some of God's most faithful people were poor, and Jesus warned more about the danger of wealth than about its absence. Give generously and cheerfully, work honestly, and measure blessing by Christlikeness, not by a lifestyle on camera.
Scripture
1 Timothy 6:6-10; Matthew 6:19-21; Luke 12:15; Philippians 4:11-13
Related Topics
Money; Generosity; Contentment; False Teaching
