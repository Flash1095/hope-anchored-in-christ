Deconstructing My Faith

Post
Deconstructing My Faith
Platform
TikTok
Post Summary
Creators film themselves walking away from the faith they grew up with, listing the questions, hurts, or hypocrisy that pushed them out. The videos often frame leaving as the brave, honest choice and staying as fear or denial.
Biblical Response
Questions are not the enemy of faith, and neither is honest doubt — many of the Psalms wrestle openly with God. But deconstruction is a process, not a destination: take the hard questions to Scripture, to wise counsel, and to God Himself, rather than letting a short video decide them. A faith examined can come out stronger, and a church that hurt you is not the same as the Christ who loves you.
Scripture
Jude 1:3; 2 Timothy 1:12; Psalm 73:16-17; John 6:68

Doubt; Church Hurt; Truth; Perseverance
