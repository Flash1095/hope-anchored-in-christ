Ex-Pastor Tells All

Post
Ex-Pastor Tells All
Platform
YouTube
Post Summary
Former pastors and ministry leaders post tell-all videos about why they left ministry or faith, and their insider status gives their criticisms unusual weight with viewers.
Biblical Response
Listen with both compassion and discernment. A person's story is their own, and ministry wounds are real — but one person's experience, however sincere, is not a verdict on the truth of Christianity. Weigh what is said against Scripture, remember that leaders are fallible people under unique pressure, and resist building your faith — or your unbelief — on someone else's biography.
Scripture
Galatians 6:1; Jude 1:22; Matthew 7:15-16; Hebrews 13:7
Related Topics
Church Hurt; Discernment; Leadership; Perseverance
