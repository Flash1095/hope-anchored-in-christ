Your Horoscope Knows You Better

Post
Your Horoscope Knows You Better
Platform
TikTok
Post Summary
Astrology videos tell viewers their sign explains their personality, their relationships, and what this week holds. The readings feel personal because they are written vaguely enough to fit almost anyone.
Biblical Response
It is comforting to feel known, and God made you to be truly known — by Him, not by the stars. Scripture warns against seeking guidance from the heavens because your life is held by a personal Creator who knows you by name and wrote your days before you lived one. If you want direction, the stars are a poor substitute for prayer, Scripture, and wise counsel.
Scripture
Deuteronomy 18:10-12; Isaiah 47:13-14; Jeremiah 10:2; Psalm 139:16
