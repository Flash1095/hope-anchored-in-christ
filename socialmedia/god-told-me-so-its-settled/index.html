Post
God Told Me — So It's Settled
Platform
Instagram
Post Summary
Influencers announce life decisions — moves, breakups, business ventures — with the claim that God told them directly, presented as beyond question or counsel.
Biblical Response
God does guide His people, often through Scripture, prayer, providence, and wise counsel together. But a private impression is not the same as God's revealed Word, and claiming divine authority for a personal decision shuts down the very testing Scripture commands. If God is truly leading, His leading will not contradict His Word and will survive honest counsel.
Scripture
1 John 4:1; 2 Timothy 3:16; Proverbs 11:14; James 1:5
Related Topics
Guidance; Discernment; God's Will; Wisdom
