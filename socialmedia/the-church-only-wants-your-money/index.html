Post Summary
Posts mock churches as businesses, pointing to large buildings, offering plates, and wealthy pastors as proof that organized religion is a money-making scheme.
Biblical Response
Sadly, some ministries have abused giving, and that abuse should be named. But the New Testament picture of giving is cheerful, voluntary, and aimed at real needs — supporting workers, caring for the poor, and spreading the gospel — not enriching leaders. Judge a church by its fruit and its finances, give where you see integrity, and remember that generosity is worship, not a transaction.
Scripture
2 Corinthians 9:7; Acts 20:35; 1 Timothy 5:17-18; Matthew 6:21
