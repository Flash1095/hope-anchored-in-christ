Celebrity Faith Takes

Post
Celebrity Faith Takes
Platform
Instagram
Post Summary
Clips of famous people talking about God, prayer, or the Bible go viral, and their fame is treated as evidence — either that faith is cool and true, or that it is shallow and fake.
Biblical Response
A famous voice is still just a voice: it proves nothing about whether something is true. Scripture warns us to test every teaching, whoever delivers it, and to judge by fruit over time rather than by follower count. Be glad when anyone speaks well of Christ, and be just as willing to say that a celebrity's opinion — for or against faith — settles nothing.
Scripture
Galatians 1:8; 2 Timothy 3:16; Matthew 7:15-16; 1 John 4:1

Discernment; Culture; Truth; False Teaching
