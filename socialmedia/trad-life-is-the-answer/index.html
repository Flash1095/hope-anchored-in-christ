Trad Life Is the Answer

Post
Trad Life Is the Answer
Platform
Instagram
Post Summary
Curated accounts romanticize an idealized past — homemaking, large families, and old-fashioned gender roles — as the faithful life, implying that modern Christians who live differently are compromised.
Biblical Response
There is nothing wrong with loving home, family, or tradition, and much that is beautiful in them. But godliness is not an aesthetic and not a decade: it is love for God and neighbor lived out in whatever time and place God put you. A filtered feed is not a standard for faithfulness, and no lifestyle brand gets to define what a faithful man or woman looks like — Scripture does.
Scripture
Colossians 2:8; 1 Timothy 4:8; Micah 6:8; Galatians 5:22-23
