A Prayer Chain That Promises a Miracle

Post
A Prayer Chain That Promises a Miracle
Platform
Facebook
Post Summary
Chain posts promise that if you copy, share, or forward a prayer to a set number of people, God will grant a miracle — and imply that breaking the chain invites trouble.
Biblical Response
God is not bound by chain letters, and His blessings are not unlocked by forwarding counts. Prayer is real and powerful precisely because it is a child speaking to a Father, not a spell with instructions. Pray the prayer if it is good, share encouragement freely — but never let a post convince you that God's favor depends on your shares.
Scripture
Matthew 6:7; Ecclesiastes 5:2; James 5:16; 1 Thessalonians 5:17
Related Topics
Prayer; Superstition; Trust; God's Will
