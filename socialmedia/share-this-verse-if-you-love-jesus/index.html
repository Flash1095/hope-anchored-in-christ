Share This Verse If You Love Jesus

Post
Share This Verse If You Love Jesus
Platform
Facebook
Post Summary
Posts use guilt as fuel: share this image to prove you love Jesus, and scrolling past means you are ashamed of Him. The metric of devotion becomes a click.
Biblical Response
Publicly confessing Christ is real and commanded — but a share button is not the measure of it. Jesus warned against performing righteousness for an audience, and He measures love by obedience over time, not by engagement. If a post makes you feel manipulated, that feeling is worth listening to: love for Jesus is proven in a life, not in a feed.
Scripture
Matthew 6:1; Romans 10:9; John 14:15; Matthew 10:32-33
Related Topics
Worship; Hypocrisy; Witness; Manipulation
