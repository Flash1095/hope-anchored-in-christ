Christianity Is Just Hate
Platform
X
Post Summary
Viral threads argue that Christian beliefs about sexuality, sin, and judgment amount to hatred, and that loving people means affirming everything they do. Disagreement itself gets labeled as bigotry.
Biblical Response
Christians have sometimes spoken truth without love, and that failure is real and worth repenting of. But love and agreement are not the same thing: Jesus loved people deeply while calling them to repentance, and He welcomed sinners without affirming sin. True love tells the truth with tears, refuses contempt, and stays at the table — it does not confuse kindness with silence.
Scripture
John 13:35; 1 John 4:7-8; Matthew 5:43-44; Romans 12:9-10
