Post Summary
Threads declare that science has made God unnecessary — the universe, life, and consciousness all have natural explanations, so belief is leftover superstition. The posts treat science and faith as competitors.
Biblical Response
Science is a wonderful gift, and Christians helped build it on the conviction that a rational God made an orderly world worth studying. But science describes how things work, not why anything exists at all or why the universe is intelligible. The heavens still declare glory, and the deeper science looks, the more fine-tuned and astonishing creation appears. Faith and science answer different questions, and they need not be enemies.
Scripture
Psalm 19:1; Romans 1:20; Colossians 1:16-17; Genesis 1:1
