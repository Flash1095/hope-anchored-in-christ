Aesthetic Faith
Platform
Instagram
Post Summary
Feeds turn faith into a look: the right Bible, the right journal, the right coffee, the right verse graphic. Devotion gets measured by how photogenic it is, and quiet, unglamorous obedience feels lesser.
Biblical Response
Beauty is a gift from God, and there is nothing wrong with a lovely quiet time. But God looks at the heart, not the feed: secret prayer, unseen generosity, and ordinary faithfulness matter more to Him than any curated image. If your spiritual life only counts when it photographs well, the audience has quietly replaced the Father who sees in secret.
Scripture
Matthew 23:27-28; 1 Samuel 16:7; James 1:27; Matthew 6:1
