I Left Church but Not God

Post
I Left Church but Not God
Platform
Facebook
Post Summary
Long posts from longtime churchgoers explain that they have stopped attending while insisting their faith is intact — church, they say, was the problem, not God.
Biblical Response
It is possible to love God while being wounded by His people, and no honest answer pretends otherwise. But the New Testament never pictures faith as a solo project: the church is Christ's body, and we need its teaching, correction, encouragement, and communion — even when it is imperfect, because it always will be. If you have stepped away, consider a gentle return: a different congregation, a small group, a first step back toward the people Jesus gave you.
Scripture
Hebrews 10:24-25; Acts 2:42; Ephesians 4:15-16; Psalm 122:1
