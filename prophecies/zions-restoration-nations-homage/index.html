Zion's Restoration and the Nations' Homage

Prophecy
Zion's Restoration and the Nations' Homage
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Isaiah foretold that barren Zion would enlarge her tent, break forth on the right hand and the left, and see her children gather from afar, with the wealth of the nations and kings bringing their glory into her. The sons of strangers would build up her walls, the gates would stand open continually, and her light would draw Gentiles and kings to its brightness. Foreigners and eunuchs who hold fast God's covenant would be given a place in his house and a name better than sons and daughters.
Fulfillment
Jerusalem was rebuilt after the exile, its walls were restored under Nehemiah, and Gentiles did begin to join themselves to the God of Israel, a gathering fulfilled more fully in the Church drawn from all nations. Zion's complete glory, with kings bringing their glory and no more sorrow or oppression, has not yet been seen in full. The prophecy is therefore partially fulfilled, with its historical rebuilding begun and its full glory still to come.
