A Prophet Like Moses

Prophecy
A Prophet Like Moses
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 1406 BC
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Prophecy Summary
Moses promised that God would raise up a prophet like him from among Israel, to whose words the people must listen. For centuries Israel waited for this definitive spokesman of God.
Fulfillment
Peter and Stephen both identify Jesus as that prophet in Acts, greater than Moses because He is the Son over God's house. In Him God has spoken His final word, and listening to Him is life itself.
Fulfillment Status
Fulfilled
