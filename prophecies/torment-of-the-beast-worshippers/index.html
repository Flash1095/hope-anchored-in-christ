The Torment of the Beast-Worshippers

Prophecy
The Torment of the Beast-Worshippers
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Anyone who worships the beast and his image and receives his mark in forehead or hand shall drink the wine of God's wrath poured out undiluted, and shall be tormented with fire and brimstone in the presence of the holy angels and the Lamb, with the smoke of their torment rising for ever and ever and no rest day or night.
Fulfillment
Awaited as a final judgment on beast-worshippers, distinct from the general judgment of the dead; the nature and duration of the torment are debated among interpreters, so the entry states the prediction's terms without settling that debate.
