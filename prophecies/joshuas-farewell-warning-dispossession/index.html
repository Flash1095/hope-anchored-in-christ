Joshua's Farewell Warning of Dispossession

Prophecy
Joshua's Farewell Warning of Dispossession
Prophet
Joshua
Date Approx
c. 1400 BC
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Prophecy Summary
In his farewell addresses Joshua warns that if Israel goes back, intermarries with the remaining nations, and serves other gods, the LORD will no more drive those nations out, they will become snares and thorns, and Israel will perish from the good land.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in stages: the remaining nations become snares in the Judges period, when Israel dwells among them and serves their gods (Judges 3:5-6), and the warning reaches its end in the exile from the land (2 Kings 25:1-21).

Fulfilled
