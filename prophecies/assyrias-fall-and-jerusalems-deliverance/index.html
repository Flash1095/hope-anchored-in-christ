Assyria's Fall and Jerusalem's Deliverance

Prophecy Summary

Isaiah foretold that Assyria, the rod of God's anger, would overreach itself and be broken, and that Jerusalem would not fall to it. The Assyrian king's boasts would end in defeat, his army would be shattered, and the yoke on God's people would be lifted. The same deliverance is promised again in the oracles against trusting Egypt's horses and chariots instead of the LORD.

Fulfillment

In 701 BC Sennacherib's army besieged Jerusalem but withdrew after a devastating loss in its camp, as recorded in 2 Kings 19:35 and Isaiah 37:36, and Jerusalem survived. Assyria's power then waned, and Nineveh itself fell to Babylon and Media in 612 BC. The prediction of Assyria broken and Jerusalem spared was therefore fulfilled in history.
