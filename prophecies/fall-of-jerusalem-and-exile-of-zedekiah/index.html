The Fall of Jerusalem and the Exile of Zedekiah

Prophecy
The Fall of Jerusalem and the Exile of Zedekiah
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 593–588 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
Ezekiel acted out a siege against a clay model of Jerusalem and announced that famine, sword, and pestilence would fall on the city, its people would be scattered, and King Zedekiah would be taken captive to Babylon. The prophecies in chapters 4 to 24 repeat that warning through signs, parables, and direct charges against Jerusalem's idolatry and broken covenant. Zedekiah's attempt to escape by night would fail, and he would be brought blinded before the king of Babylon.
Fulfillment
Nebuchadnezzar besieged Jerusalem, and the city fell in 586 BC after famine and assault, as recorded in 2 Kings 25. Zedekiah fled by night, was captured near Jericho, saw his sons killed, and was then blinded and taken in chains to Babylon. The temple was burned, the walls were broken down, and many survivors were carried into exile.
