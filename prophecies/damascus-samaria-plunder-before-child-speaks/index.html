Damascus and Samaria Plundered Before the Child Speaks

Prophecy
Damascus and Samaria Plundered Before the Child Speaks
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 735 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Before Isaiah's son Maher-shalal-hash-baz learns to say father or mother, the riches of Damascus and the spoil of Samaria will be carried away before the king of Assyria.
Fulfillment
Assyria plundered Damascus in 732 BC and Samaria in 722 BC, within the child's infancy and early childhood as the sign requires.
