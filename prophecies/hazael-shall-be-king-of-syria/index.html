Hazael Shall Be King of Syria

Prophecy Summary

When Hazael came from Ben-hadad to ask whether the Syrian king would recover, Elisha told him the king would recover from his sickness, yet the LORD had shown Elisha that Hazael himself would be king over Syria. Elisha then wept as he described the evil Hazael would do to Israel: setting strongholds on fire, killing young men, dashing children, and ripping open pregnant women.

Fulfillment

Hazael returned, smothered Ben-hadad, and reigned in his place, and in the following decades he oppressed Israel and cut off its eastern territories, as 2 Kings records. The commission to anoint Hazael given to Elijah on Horeb lies behind this word, but this entry records Elisha's own dated prediction to Hazael himself, not the earlier commission. The atrocities Elisha wept over were the price Israel paid for its unfaithfulness, and Elisha did not soften them.
