Jehoiakim's Dishonorable End

Prophecy
Jehoiakim's Dishonorable End
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 598 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Jeremiah foretold that Jehoiakim, king of Judah, would receive no royal lamentation and no honourable burial, but would be buried with the burial of an ass, drawn out and cast beyond the gates of Jerusalem. He added that none of Jehoiakim's seed would sit upon the throne of David, and that his dead body would be cast out to the heat by day and the frost by night. It is a distinct prediction about Jehoiakim himself, not a repeat of the Coniah oracle.
Fulfillment
Jehoiakim died during Nebuchadnezzar's campaign against Judah, his body was not given a king's burial, and the biblical record pointedly gives him no burial notice of the kind other kings receive, while his son's brief reign ended in exile. The dishonour foretold, rather than a state funeral in Jerusalem, is what the record shows. The prophecy was therefore fulfilled in the manner of his death and burial.
