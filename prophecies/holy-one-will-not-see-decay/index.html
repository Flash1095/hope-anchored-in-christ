The Holy One Will Not See Decay

Prophecy
The Holy One Will Not See Decay
Prophet
David
Date Approx
c. 1000 BC
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
David confessed that God would not abandon his soul to the grave or let His Holy One see corruption. David himself died and decayed, so the words reached beyond him to someone greater.
Fulfillment
Peter and Paul both preach this as the resurrection of Jesus, whose body did not decay because God raised Him on the third day. It remains one of the clearest Old Testament anchors for Easter hope.

Fulfilled
