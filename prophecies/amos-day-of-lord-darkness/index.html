The Day of the LORD as Darkness

Prophecy
The Day of the LORD as Darkness
Prophet
Amos
Date Approx
c. 760 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Amos warns those who long for the day of the LORD that it will be darkness and not light, very dark with no brightness in it, inescapable as fleeing a lion only to meet a bear, or leaning on a wall at home only to be bitten by a serpent.
Fulfillment
Partially fulfilled in the darkness of judgment that fell on Israel in the Assyrian conquest; the passage also speaks in the language of a final day of the LORD that is still awaited.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
