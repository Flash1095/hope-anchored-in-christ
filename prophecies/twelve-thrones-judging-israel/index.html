The Twelve Thrones Judging Israel

Prophecy
The Twelve Thrones Judging Israel
Prophet
Jesus Christ
Date Approx
c. AD 30
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
Jesus told the disciples who had followed Him that in the regeneration, when the Son of man sits on the throne of His glory, they also would sit upon twelve thrones. There they would judge the twelve tribes of Israel, sharing in the rule of the coming kingdom. At the Last Supper He repeated the promise, appointing them a kingdom as His Father had appointed Him one.
Fulfillment
No passage in Scripture records the Twelve already sitting on these thrones judging Israel, and the setting Jesus names is the regeneration, a future renewal. The promise is therefore not yet fulfilled in the present age, though the apostles' foundational role in the Church points toward it. It is marked Future, with its full fulfilment awaiting Christ's coming kingdom.
