The Harvest of the Earth and the Winepress of Wrath

Prophecy
The Harvest of the Earth and the Winepress of Wrath
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
One like the Son of man on a white cloud reaps the harvest of the earth with a sharp sickle when it is ripe, and then an angel gathers the clusters of the vine of the earth and casts them into the great winepress of God's wrath, which is trodden outside the city until blood comes out of the winepress, even to the horses' bridles, for sixteen hundred furlongs.
Fulfillment
Debated: the harvest is read by some as the gathering of the righteous and by others as judgment itself, while the winepress is a judgment image; both acts are presented as one end-time reaping sequence whose timing and referents are disputed.
