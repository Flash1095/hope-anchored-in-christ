Saul Rejected, David Anointed

Saul Rejected, David Anointed

Prophecy Summary

After Saul disobeyed at Gilgal, Samuel told him that his kingdom would not continue and that the LORD had sought a man after His own heart to command over Israel. After Saul spared what God had devoted to destruction, Samuel repeated that the LORD had torn the kingdom from Saul and given it to a neighbour better than he, and then Samuel anointed David, the youngest son of Jesse, as king.

Fulfillment

Saul's house did not hold the throne: he died at Gilboa, and after a struggle between his son Ish-bosheth and David, all the tribes came to David at Hebron and anointed him king over Israel. This entry covers the transfer of the kingdom only; the eternal promise of David's throne itself is already covered by the existing entry on the Eternal Throne of David and is not duplicated here.
