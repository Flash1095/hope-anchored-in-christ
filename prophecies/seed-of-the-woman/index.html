The Seed of the Woman

Prophecy
The Seed of the Woman
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 4000 BC
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Prophecy Summary
In the garden God promised that the offspring of the woman would crush the serpent's head, even though his own heel would be struck. It is the first glimmer of the gospel — a descendant of Eve would undo the curse Satan had brought.
Fulfillment
In Jesus, born of a woman, Satan was decisively defeated at the cross and empty tomb, though Christ suffered in the victory. Paul and John both look back to this promise as fulfilled in Him, with its final outworking still to come at His return.
