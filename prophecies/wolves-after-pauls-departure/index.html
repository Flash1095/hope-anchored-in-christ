Wolves After Paul's Departure

Prophecy
Wolves After Paul's Departure
Prophet
Paul
Date Approx
c. AD 57
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
In his farewell to the Ephesian elders at Miletus, Paul warned that after his departing grievous wolves would enter in among them, not sparing the flock. He added that also of their own selves men would arise, speaking perverse things to draw away disciples after them. He therefore urged them to watch and remember his three years of tearful warning night and day.
Fulfillment
Within the first century the churches faced false teachers and divisive figures, as Paul's own later letters and the letter to Ephesus in Revelation show in testing false apostles. The pattern has continued through church history, with new teachers arising from within as well as from outside. The warning is therefore partially fulfilled, already visible in the apostolic age and still unfolding.
