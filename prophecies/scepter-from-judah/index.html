The Scepter Will Not Depart from Judah

Prophecy
The Scepter Will Not Depart from Judah
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 1850 BC
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Prophecy Summary
Jacob blessed Judah as a lion and promised that the scepter would not depart from him until the one to whom it belongs comes. Rulership, in other words, would run through Judah's line to a coming king who would receive the obedience of the nations.
Fulfillment
The promise narrowed through David, of Judah's tribe, and reached its fulfilment in Jesus, the Lion of the tribe of Judah. Revelation presents Him as that promised ruler whose kingdom gathers people from every nation.
