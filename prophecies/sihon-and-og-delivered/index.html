Sihon and Og Delivered into Israel's Hand

Prophecy
Sihon and Og Delivered into Israel's Hand
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 1400 BC
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Prophecy Summary
At the Arnon, God tells Israel to begin to possess Sihon's land and contend with him in battle, and to put dread and fear of Israel on the nations; before Og of Bashan he says, fear him not, for I will deliver him, all his people and his land into Israel's hand, as was done to Sihon.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in Deuteronomy 2:32-3:11: Sihon comes out to battle and is smitten with his sons and all his people, and Og is likewise defeated, his cities taken and his land possessed.

Fulfilled
