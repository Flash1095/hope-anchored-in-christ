Ariel Besieged and Suddenly Delivered

Prophecy
Ariel Besieged and Suddenly Delivered
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Ariel, the city where David dwelt, will be distressed and besieged by encamped enemies, brought low to whisper from the dust, and then its multitude of foes will vanish suddenly, as a dream in the night.
Fulfillment
The Assyrian siege of Jerusalem in 701 BC, lifted suddenly with Sennacherib's army destroyed, fits the sudden deliverance; because Jerusalem was besieged again later, the entry is marked partial rather than tied to one siege alone.

Partially Fulfilled
