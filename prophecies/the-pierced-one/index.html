The Pierced One

Prophecy
The Pierced One
Prophet
Zechariah
Date Approx
c. 480 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
Zechariah promised that God would pour out a spirit of grace on Jerusalem so that they would look on the one they had pierced and mourn for him as for an only son. Piercing, grief, and grace are bound together in a single piercing promise.
Fulfillment
John records that a soldier pierced Jesus' side and quotes Zechariah directly: They will look on him whom they have pierced. Revelation extends the looking to His return, when every eye will see Him and the mourning becomes worldwide.

Fulfilled
