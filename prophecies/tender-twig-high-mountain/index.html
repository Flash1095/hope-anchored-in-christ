A Tender Twig Planted on a High Mountain

Prophecy
A Tender Twig Planted on a High Mountain
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 591 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
God will take a tender twig from the top of the high cedar, plant it on a high mountain of Israel, and it will become a goodly cedar under which birds of every wing will dwell, while all trees know that God brings down the high tree and exalts the low.
Fulfillment
Whether the twig is Zerubbabel, the restored Davidic governor, or the Messiah, or both in a layered sense, is genuinely contested, so the referent doubt is stated and this is marked Debated rather than assigned to one reading.
