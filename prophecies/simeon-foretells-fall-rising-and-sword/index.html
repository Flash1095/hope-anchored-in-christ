Simeon Foretells the Fall and Rising of Many and a Sword for Mary

Prophecy
Simeon Foretells the Fall and Rising of Many and a Sword for Mary
Prophet
Simeon
Date Approx
c. 5 BC
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
Simeon predicts that the child Jesus is set for the fall and rising again of many in Israel and for a sign spoken against, and that a sword shall pierce through Mary's own soul, so that the thoughts of many hearts may be revealed.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled: responses to Jesus divided Israel, he was spoken against throughout his ministry, and Mary stood at the cross where the sword of grief pierced her (John 19:25).
