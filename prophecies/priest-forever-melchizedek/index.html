A Priest Forever After Melchizedek

Prophecy
A Priest Forever After Melchizedek
Prophet
David
Date Approx
c. 1000 BC
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Psalm 110 joins two offices Israel always kept apart: the promised King is also a priest forever, after the order of the mysterious Melchizedek. He rules from Zion and intercedes without end.
Fulfillment
Hebrews shows Jesus as that priest-king, not by Levitical descent but by the power of an indestructible life. Because He lives forever, He always intercedes for those who draw near to God through Him.
