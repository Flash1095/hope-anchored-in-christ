The Ten Plagues Foretold

Prophecy
The Ten Plagues Foretold
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 1440 BC
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Prophecy Summary
Moses is told to warn Pharaoh that if he refuses to let Israel go, God will slay Egypt's firstborn, and each plague is then announced to Pharaoh before it falls: water turned to blood, frogs, swarms of flies, murrain on cattle, boils, hail, locusts, darkness, and the death of the firstborn, with judgment on Egypt's gods.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled across Exodus 7-12, where each announced plague falls in turn and the firstborn die at midnight in Exodus 12:29, after which Pharaoh lets Israel go. Individual plague announcements are grouped here as one sequence rather than ten separate entries.
