Edom's Cup and the End of Zion's Captivity

Prophecy
Edom's Cup and the End of Zion's Captivity
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 586 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
Daughter of Edom, rejoicing in the land of Uz, will herself drink the cup, be drunken and exposed; meanwhile the punishment of daughter of Zion is accomplished and he will no more carry her away into captivity, but will visit Edom's iniquity and discover her sins.
Fulfillment
Zion's Babylonian captivity did end with the return under Cyrus, and Edom was later subdued and lost its distinct standing, but the full visiting of Edom is not tied to a single dated event, so this is partial.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
