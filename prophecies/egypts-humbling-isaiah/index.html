Egypt's Humbling

Prophecy
Egypt's Humbling
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Isaiah foretold that the LORD would ride upon a swift cloud into Egypt, its idols would be moved, its spirit would fail, and Egyptians would fight against each other, city against city and kingdom against kingdom. Its wise counsellors would be confounded, the Nile's waters would fail, and Egypt would be given into the hand of a cruel lord. Yet the oracle also promises that Egypt will one day know the LORD and be healed, with an altar to the LORD in Egypt.
Fulfillment
Egypt was humbled by Assyrian invasion under Esarhaddon and Ashurbanipal, and later by Babylon under Nebuchadnezzar, with internal strife and loss of imperial power matching the oracle's picture. The sign-act of Isaiah walking barefoot for three years pointed to Egypt and Ethiopia led away captive by Assyria, which history confirms. The final healing and worship strand of the oracle has not yet been seen in full, so the prophecy is partially fulfilled.
