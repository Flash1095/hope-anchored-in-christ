The Day of the Lord Comes as a Thief, With Sudden Destruction

Prophecy
The Day of the Lord Comes as a Thief, With Sudden Destruction
Prophet
Paul
Date Approx
c. AD 51
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Paul says the day of the Lord comes as a thief in the night: when people say 'Peace and safety', sudden destruction comes upon them like labour pains on a woman with child, and they will not escape.
Fulfillment
Awaited. The day of the Lord so described, with its sudden destruction on the unsuspecting, has not yet occurred; the 'Peace and safety' sign is the distinctive predictive detail of this entry.
