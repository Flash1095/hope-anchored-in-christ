Jerusalem Measured and Restored

Prophecy
Jerusalem Measured and Restored
Prophet
Zechariah
Date Approx
c. 520 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
Zechariah's early visions promised that Jerusalem, still in ruins after exile, would be measured, rebuilt, and inhabited without walls because God Himself would be a wall of fire around her. The horns that scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem would be broken by craftsmen, the horsemen reported that the earth was at rest, and God declared He had returned to Jerusalem with mercies and His house would be built there. Later in the same book the fasts kept in mourning for the destroyed temple would become joy, gladness, and cheerful feasts, and many peoples would come to seek the LORD in Jerusalem.
Fulfillment
Jerusalem was rebuilt under Zerubbabel, Ezra, and Nehemiah, with temple and walls restored, so the measuring and rebuilding began in the return from exile. The promise of a city without walls, with God as its wall of fire and nations streaming to it, goes beyond the small post-exilic town and is read by Christians as pointing to the Church and to the New Jerusalem. The restoration is therefore partly fulfilled, historically begun and eschatologically awaiting completion.
