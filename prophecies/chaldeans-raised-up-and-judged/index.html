The Chaldeans Raised Up and Judged

Prophecy
The Chaldeans Raised Up and Judged
Prophet
Habakkuk
Date Approx
c. 605 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
When Habakkuk questioned how God could tolerate Judah's injustice, God answered that He was raising up the Chaldeans, a bitter and hasty nation, to march through the breadth of the land and possess dwellings not their own. Habakkuk then questioned how a holy God could use a more wicked nation, and God answered again with five woes on the proud plunderer: woe to him who builds by blood, covets evil gain, and makes neighbours drunk with violence. The same passage promises that the earth shall be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the LORD as the waters cover the sea.
Fulfillment
The Chaldeans, that is the Neo-Babylonians under Nebuchadnezzar, did conquer Judah, destroy Jerusalem in 586 BC, and carry its people into exile, fulfilling the raising up. Babylon itself then fell to the Medes and Persians in 539 BC, fulfilling the woes on the plunderer in the broad sweep of history, even though the five woes are poetic and not each tied to a single dated battle. The knowledge of God's glory filling the earth is still unfolding, so the entry is partly fulfilled.
