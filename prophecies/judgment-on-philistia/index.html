Judgment on Philistia

Prophecy
Judgment on Philistia
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 590–570 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
Because the Philistines took vengeance on Judah with contempt and an old hatred, God said he would stretch out his hand against them. Their coastal strongholds would be cut off, and the remnant on the seacoast would perish.
Fulfillment
The Philistine cities were battered by Babylonian campaigns and later conquests, including Alexander's capture of Gaza, and Philistine identity faded in the centuries that followed. No Philistine kingdom survived into the later ancient period.
