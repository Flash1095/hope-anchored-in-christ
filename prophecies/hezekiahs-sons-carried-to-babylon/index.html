Hezekiah's Sons Carried to Babylon

Prophecy
Hezekiah's Sons Carried to Babylon
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 701 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
After Hezekiah showed the Babylonian envoys all his treasures, Isaiah foretold that the days would come when all that was in his house, and all his fathers had laid up, would be carried to Babylon, and nothing would be left. Of Hezekiah's own sons who would issue from him, some would be taken away and become eunuchs in the palace of the king of Babylon. It is a sober prediction of exile, spoken while Babylon was still a distant power.
Fulfillment
Jerusalem's treasures were carried to Babylon in the conquests of 597 and 586 BC, and young men of royal and noble descent, including Daniel and his companions, were taken to serve in the Babylonian court. The prophecy was therefore fulfilled in the Babylonian exile, in the very terms Isaiah used. It is distinct from Jeremiah's seventy-years entry, which concerns the length of the exile.
