The Widow's Meal and Oil Shall Not Fail

Prophecy
The Widow's Meal and Oil Shall Not Fail
Prophet
Elijah
Date Approx
c. 860 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
At Zarephath Elijah tells the widow that, by the word of the LORD God of Israel, the barrel of meal shall not waste and the cruse of oil shall not fail until the day the LORD sends rain on the earth.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled through the drought: she and her household eat many days, and the barrel of meal wastes not and the cruse of oil fails not, according to the word of the LORD by Elijah (1 Kings 17:15-16). The drought itself is already a live row; this is the separate provision word.
