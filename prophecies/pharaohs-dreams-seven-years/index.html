Pharaoh's Dreams: Seven Years of Plenty and Famine

Prophecy
Pharaoh's Dreams: Seven Years of Plenty and Famine
Prophet
Joseph
Date Approx
c. 1880 BC
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Prophecy Summary
Pharaoh dreamed twice in one night: seven fat cows eaten by seven thin cows, and seven full ears of grain swallowed by seven thin ears. Joseph told Pharaoh that the two dreams were one, that God was showing Pharaoh what He was about to do, and that seven years of great plenty would be followed by seven years of severe famine throughout Egypt and the surrounding lands.
Fulfillment
The seven years of plenty came, Joseph gathered grain in the cities during those years, and then the seven years of famine arrived exactly as interpreted. Egypt had bread because of the stored grain, while peoples from surrounding lands, including Joseph's own family, came to Egypt to buy food. The prediction saved both Egypt and the covenant family, and brought Israel down into Egypt as Genesis 15 had foreseen.
