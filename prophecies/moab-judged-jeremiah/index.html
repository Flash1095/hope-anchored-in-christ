Moab Judged

Moab Judged

Prophecy Summary

Jeremiah's long oracle against Moab foretold that its cities would be laid waste, its pride and its god Chemosh would go into captivity, and Moab would be destroyed from being a people because it had magnified itself against the LORD. Its strongholds would fall, its wine would cease, and its people would be carried away, though the oracle ends with a promise that God would bring again the captivity of Moab in the latter days. It is distinct from Isaiah's earlier Moab lament.

Fulfillment

Moab was conquered by Babylon, its cities were devastated, and Moab ceased to exist as a nation, with its people absorbed into surrounding populations. The destruction from being a people and the captivity of Chemosh's worship match that disappearance. The latter-days restoration strand has no clear historical fulfilment identified, so the entry is marked partially fulfilled, with the judgment fulfilled in full.
