The Final Shaking of Heaven and Earth

Prophecy
The Final Shaking of Heaven and Earth
Date Approx
c. AD 65
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
God promises one final shaking, not of the earth only but also of heaven, so that the things that are made and can be shaken are removed and only the things that cannot be shaken remain, leaving believers a kingdom that cannot be moved.
Fulfillment
Awaited as the final consummation: the created order has not yet been removed in this final sense, and the unshakeable kingdom is received by faith now and in full at the end. The shaking language echoes Haggai's promise of a shaking of heaven and earth.
Fulfillment Status
Future
Key Passages
Hebrews 12:26-28; Haggai 2:6
Related Books
Hebrews; Haggai
