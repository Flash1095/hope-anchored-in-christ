The Lampstand and the Spirit, and the Temple Completed

Prophecy

Prophet

Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
Zechariah saw a golden lampstand with seven lamps, fed by two olive trees, and was told the word of the LORD to Zerubbabel: not by might, nor by power, but by My Spirit. The great mountain of opposition before Zerubbabel would become a plain, he who laid the foundation would finish the temple, and the two olive trees were the two anointed ones standing by the LORD of the whole earth. The vision assured a discouraged remnant that the work would be finished not by imperial favour or human strength, but by God's Spirit.
Fulfillment
Zerubbabel did finish the Second Temple, dedicated in 516 BC, as Ezra records, fulfilling the completion promise in literal history within Zechariah's own generation. Christians have also read the lampstand and the Spirit as pointing to the Spirit's work in the Church, pictured in Revelation with lampstands and the seven Spirits of God. The temple completion is fulfilled, while the Spirit's work continues, so the entry is marked Fulfilled for its dated temple promise, with its wider Spirit meaning ongoing.
