Israel's Exile Foretold

Prophecy
Israel's Exile Foretold
Prophet
Amos
Date Approx
c. 760 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Amos warned that Israel's luxurious injustice, false worship, and oppression of the poor would end in exile beyond Damascus. God showed him a plumb line against a crooked wall, and a basket of summer fruit, meaning the end had come for His people Israel. The sanctuaries would be laid waste, the songs of the temple would turn to wailing, and the people would be sifted among the nations, though not utterly destroyed.
Fulfillment
The warning was fulfilled when Assyria deported Israel in waves in 734-732 BC and then captured Samaria in 722 BC, carrying its people to Halah, Habor, Gozan, and the cities of the Medes, as recorded in 2 Kings 17 and in Sargon II's own annals. Amos's promise that Israel would not be utterly destroyed is reflected in the survival of a remnant and in the later return of Judah, even though the northern kingdom itself never returned as a nation. The exile itself is fulfilled; the sifting and preservation continue in the wider story of God's people.
