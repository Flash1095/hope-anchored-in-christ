The Rejected Stone Becomes the Cornerstone

Prophecy
The Rejected Stone Becomes the Cornerstone
Prophet
The Psalmist
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
The psalm declares that the stone which the builders refused has become the head stone of the corner, and that this is the Lord's doing and marvellous in our eyes. It then cries, 'Blessed be he that cometh in the name of the Lord,' welcoming the coming One into the house of the Lord. The rejected stone and the welcomed King are held together as one work of God.
Fulfillment
Jesus quoted the rejected stone directly against the chief priests and Pharisees, applying it to Himself as the stone they rejected. The apostles repeated it after the resurrection, naming Jesus as the stone set at nought by the builders and become the head of the corner. The welcome cry was also heard at His entry into Jerusalem, when crowds blessed Him who came in the name of the Lord.
