The Cupbearer Restored and the Baker Hanged in Three Days

Prophecy
The Cupbearer Restored and the Baker Hanged in Three Days
Prophet
Joseph
Date Approx
c. 1800 BC
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Prophecy Summary
Joseph interprets the chief butler's dream as restoration to his place within three days, and the chief baker's dream as being hanged within three days, with birds eating his flesh.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled exactly on the third day, Pharaoh's birthday: Genesis 40:20-22 records the butler restored and the baker hanged, as Joseph had interpreted.
Fulfillment Status
Fulfilled
