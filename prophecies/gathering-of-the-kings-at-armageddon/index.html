The Gathering of the Kings at Armageddon

Prophecy
The Gathering of the Kings at Armageddon
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
The river Euphrates is dried up so that the way of the kings of the east may be prepared, and three unclean spirits like frogs from the dragon, the beast, and the false prophet go out working miracles to gather the kings of the earth and of the whole world to the battle of the great day of God Almighty, at a place called in Hebrew Armageddon.
Fulfillment
Debated: whether this is a literal future battle at a literal site, a symbolic gathering of world powers against God, or an event already foreshadowed in history is disputed; the gathering itself is stated as a future confrontation, not dated.
Fulfillment Status
Debated
