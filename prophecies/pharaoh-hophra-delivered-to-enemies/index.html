Pharaoh-Hophra Delivered to His Enemies

Prophecy
Pharaoh-Hophra Delivered to His Enemies
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 586 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
As Zedekiah was given into Nebuchadnezzar's hand, so Pharaoh-Hophra king of Egypt will be given into the hand of his enemies and of those who seek his life.
Fulfillment
Hophra (the Apries of Egyptian history) was deposed by his rival Amasis and lost his throne and life, matching delivery to his enemies; this is distinct from the general Egypt judgment already live, which rests on Jeremiah 46.
