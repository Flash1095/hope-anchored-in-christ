Joshua Cleansed and the Branch

Prophecy
Joshua Cleansed and the Branch
Prophet
Zechariah
Date Approx
c. 520 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
In a night vision, Joshua the high priest stood before the angel of the LORD in filthy garments, with Satan accusing him, and God rebuked the accuser, removed his iniquity, and clothed him in rich apparel with a fair mitre. God then promised His servant the Branch, a stone with seven eyes on which He would engrave an inscription and remove the iniquity of the land in one day. Years later Zechariah was told to crown Joshua with silver and gold and to proclaim the Branch who would build the temple of the LORD and bear glory as priest upon His throne.
Fulfillment
Joshua did serve as high priest in the restored temple, so the cleansing of the priesthood began in the return, but no single day in Joshua's ministry removed the land's iniquity in the full sense Zechariah describes. Christian tradition reads the Branch, priest upon His throne, as Jesus, who is both Davidic king and high priest and who by one offering took away sin, as Hebrews teaches. The vision is therefore partly fulfilled: begun in Joshua, completed in Christ, with its temple-building still pointing to the Church as God's temple.
