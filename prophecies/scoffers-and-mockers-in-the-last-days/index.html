Scoffers and Mockers in the Last Days

Scoffers and Mockers in the Last Days

Prophecy Summary

In the last days scoffers will come, walking after their own lusts and mocking the promise of the Lord's coming; the apostles had already warned that there would be mockers in the last time who walk after their own ungodly lusts.

Fulfillment

Seen through the church age in repeated mockery of the promise of Christ's coming and in teachers who follow their own lusts; the culmination at the end is still awaited, so the prediction is partly fulfilled.

Details

Prophet: Peter
Date Approx: c. AD 67
Timeline Era: Early Church
Fulfillment Status: Partially Fulfilled
Key Passages: 2 Peter 3:3; Jude 1:18
Related Books: 2 Peter; Jude
