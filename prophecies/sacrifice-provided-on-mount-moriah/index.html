Prophecy Summary
On Mount Moriah Abraham declared that God Himself would provide the lamb, and a ram was offered in Isaac's place. The place, the substitute, and the father's willingness all pointed beyond that day.
Fulfillment
Christians see the fulfilment on that same mountain ridge in Jerusalem, where God provided His own Son as the Lamb offered in our place. John's Gospel calls Jesus the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world.
