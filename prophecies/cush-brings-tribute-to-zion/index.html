Prophecy Summary
Isaiah addressed the land beyond the rivers of Ethiopia, whose ambassadors travelled by swift vessels of reeds, and foretold that a people tall and smooth, feared far and near, would be brought as a present to the LORD of hosts at Mount Zion. The oracle pictures distant Cush acknowledging the God of Israel at Jerusalem. Its exact historical referent is not named in the text, and this entry does not invent one.
Fulfillment
No single clear historical event is identified in Scripture as the fulfilment of this tribute, and interpreters differ on whether it points to a past embassy, to the Ethiopian eunuch's worship at Jerusalem, or to a still-future gathering of the nations at Zion. Because the referent and timing are genuinely debated, this entry is marked Debated rather than claimed as fulfilled or future with certainty. The text's promise of tribute to Zion remains, with its fulfilment left open rather than forced.
