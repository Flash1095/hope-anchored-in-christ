The Locust Army and the Day of the LORD

Prophecy
The Locust Army and the Day of the LORD
Prophet
Joel
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Joel described a devastating locust invasion, army upon army stripping the land, as a picture and a warning of the coming Day of the LORD. He called priests and people to lament, fast, and return to God with all their hearts, because the day is great and very terrible. The passage holds judgment and mercy together: repentance is urged before the day arrives, and God promises to restore what the locusts have eaten to a repentant people.
Fulfillment
The locust plague itself struck Judah's crops and temple offerings, and later invasions by Assyria and Babylon brought the kind of national devastation Joel pictured, so the near judgment was fulfilled in history. Christians have also read the Day of the LORD in Joel as pointing beyond any single invasion to a final day of judgment, which has not yet arrived. The entry is therefore partly fulfilled: the historical judgments came, while the ultimate Day remains future.
