Two Sticks: One Nation Under One King

Prophecy
Two Sticks: One Nation Under One King
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 590–570 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
Ezekiel was told to write Judah on one stick and Joseph and Ephraim on another, then join them into one stick in his hand. God said he would make the divided houses one nation in the land, with one king over them, and they would no longer be two nations. His servant David would be their king, and they would dwell in the land under an everlasting covenant of peace.
Fulfillment
Exiles from Judah and Israel returned together after Babylon, but the two houses did not become one restored kingdom under a Davidic king in the Old Testament period. Christians read the one-king promise as pointing to Christ, the Son of David, while the complete political reunification of Israel and Judah remains unfulfilled in history. This entry is therefore marked Partially Fulfilled.
