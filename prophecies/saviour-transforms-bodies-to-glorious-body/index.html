The Saviour Transforms Bodies to His Glorious Body

Prophecy
The Saviour Transforms Bodies to His Glorious Body
Prophet
Paul
Date Approx
c. AD 61
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Paul says believers' citizenship is in heaven, from where they look for the Saviour, the Lord Jesus Christ, who will change their lowly bodies so they are fashioned like his glorious body, according to the power by which he can subdue all things to himself.
Fulfillment
Awaited. The Saviour's coming and the transformation of believers' bodies have not yet occurred; this states the same coming as the trumpet transformation from a different angle (likeness to Christ's glorious body), and the merger may fold it into that entry if it prefers one row per event.
