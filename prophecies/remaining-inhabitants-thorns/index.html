Remaining Inhabitants Shall Be Pricks and Thorns

Prophecy
Remaining Inhabitants Shall Be Pricks and Thorns
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 1400 BC
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Prophecy Summary
God warns that if Israel does not drive out the inhabitants of the land, those left remaining will be pricks in their eyes and thorns in their sides, will vex them in the land, and God will do to Israel as he thought to do to those inhabitants.
Fulfillment
Partly fulfilled: Judges 2:3 records the LORD saying the remaining nations will be as thorns in Israel's sides, and the book of Judges shows those nations vexing Israel repeatedly. It is a conditional warning, marked partial because the vexing recurs across generations rather than ending in one event.
