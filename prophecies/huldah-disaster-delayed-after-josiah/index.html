Huldah: Disaster Delayed Until After Josiah

Prophecy
Huldah: Disaster Delayed Until After Josiah
Prophet
Huldah, Prophetess
Date Approx
c. 622 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
When the Book of the Law was found in the temple, King Josiah sent to inquire of the LORD, and the prophetess Huldah answered that evil would indeed come on Jerusalem and its people for all their idolatry, and the LORD's wrath would not be quenched. But because Josiah's heart was tender and he humbled himself, the disaster would not come in Josiah's days, and he would be gathered to his fathers in peace, his eyes not seeing the evil.
Fulfillment
Josiah died at Megiddo in battle against Pharaoh Neco, before Jerusalem fell, so he did not live to see the Babylonian destruction that came a generation later. His death in battle sits in tension with the words gathered in peace, which are best read as peace from seeing the coming judgment rather than a peaceful manner of death, and this entry is therefore marked Partially Fulfilled. The core of the prophecy held exactly: the disaster was delayed past Josiah's reign and fell on Jerusalem afterwards.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
