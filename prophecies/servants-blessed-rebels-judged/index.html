The Servants Blessed, the Rebels Judged

Prophecy
The Servants Blessed, the Rebels Judged
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
In contrast to rebels who forsake the LORD and prepare a table for fortune, God's servants will eat, drink and rejoice while the rebels go hungry, thirsty and ashamed, and be slain, leaving their name as a curse.
Fulfillment
The distinction between a preserved servant remnant and a judged rebellious generation runs through the exile and beyond, but its final separation is not yet complete, hence partial.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
