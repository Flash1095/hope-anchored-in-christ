Damascus Judged

Damascus Judged

Prophecy Summary

Jeremiah foretold that Hamath and Arpad would be confounded by evil tidings, that Damascus would grow feeble and turn to flee, and that anguish and sorrows would take hold of her as a woman in travail. Fire would be kindled in the wall of Damascus and would consume the palaces of Benhadad. It is a short, distinct oracle against Damascus, separate from Isaiah's earlier Damascus oracle.

Fulfillment

Damascus was conquered by Babylon under Nebuchadnezzar, its power as a regional capital was broken, and its palaces and walls suffered the fire of conquest. The city was later inhabited again under later empires, so the prophecy is fulfilled in the breaking of its kingdom power, not in permanent uninhabitation. It is therefore marked partially fulfilled, with its political judgment fulfilled in full.
