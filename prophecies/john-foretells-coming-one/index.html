John Foretells the Coming One

Prophecy Summary

John the Baptist told the crowds that One mightier than he was coming, whose shoes he was not worthy to bear or to unloose. That Coming One would baptise with the Holy Ghost and with fire, holding His fan in His hand to purge His floor. He would gather the wheat into His garner but burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire, a work of both blessing and judgment.

Fulfillment

Jesus, the Coming One John pointed to, baptised His disciples with the Holy Spirit at Pentecost, fulfilling the Spirit side of the promise in Acts. The gathering and the fiery separation of wheat and chaff, however, still await the final judgment John described. The prophecy is therefore partially fulfilled, with Spirit baptism accomplished and judgment still to come.
