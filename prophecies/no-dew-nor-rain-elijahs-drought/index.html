No Dew nor Rain: Elijah's Drought

Prophecy
No Dew nor Rain: Elijah's Drought
Prophet
Elijah
Date Approx
c. 865 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Elijah the Tishbite stood before King Ahab and declared that, as the LORD God of Israel lived, there would be neither dew nor rain in those years except by his word. The drought was a direct challenge to Baal, who was worshipped as the god of rain, and a judgment on a kingdom that had turned to Baal worship under Ahab and Jezebel.
Fulfillment
The rain stopped, the brook Cherith dried up, and the land suffered famine until Elijah returned in the third year and announced that the LORD would send rain. After the contest on Carmel, a small cloud rose from the sea and a great rain fell, ending the drought exactly as Elijah's word had governed it. The New Testament remembers the drought as lasting three years and six months.
