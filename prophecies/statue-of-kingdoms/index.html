The Statue of Kingdoms

The Statue of Kingdoms

Prophecy Summary

Nebuchadnezzar dreamed of a great statue — gold, silver, bronze, iron, and iron mixed with clay — struck by a stone cut without hands, which became a mountain filling the earth. Daniel explained it as a succession of kingdoms, ending with a kingdom God Himself would set up forever.

Fulfillment

Historians commonly trace the metals through Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, and Rome — the very empire standing when Christ was born. In Jesus, God's everlasting kingdom has broken in, though its mountain-like fullness still lies ahead.
