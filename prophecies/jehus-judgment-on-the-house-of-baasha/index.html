Jehu's Judgment on the House of Baasha

Prophecy
Jehu's Judgment on the House of Baasha
Prophet
Jehu, Son of Hanani
Date Approx
c. 890 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
The word of the LORD comes to Jehu son of Hanani against Baasha: because Baasha walked in Jeroboam's way and made Israel sin, the LORD will take away the posterity of Baasha and his house and make it like the house of Jeroboam, with the dogs eating those who die in the city and the birds those who die in the fields.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled when Zimri destroys all the house of Baasha and leaves him none that pisseth against the wall, according to the word of the LORD by Jehu the prophet (1 Kings 16:11-12).
Fulfillment Status
Fulfilled
