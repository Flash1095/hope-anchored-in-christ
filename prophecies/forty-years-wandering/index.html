Forty Years of Wandering: That Generation Shall Fall

Prophecy
Forty Years of Wandering: That Generation Shall Fall
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 1400 BC
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Prophecy Summary
After the spies' unbelief, God declares that the carcasses of all numbered from twenty years old and upward who murmured will fall in the wilderness, that their children will wander forty years, a year for each day of the forty-day search, and that only Caleb and Joshua will enter the land sworn to Israel.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in the wilderness narrative: Numbers 32:13 records Israel wandering forty years until the generation that did evil was consumed, Deuteronomy 2:14 gives the same span, and Joshua 5:6 records that the nation wandered forty years until the men of war were consumed.
