Zion's Remnant Restored

Prophecy
Zion's Remnant Restored
Prophet
Zephaniah
Date Approx
c. 630 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
After judgment, Zephaniah promised that God will gather the outcasts, save her that halteth, and turn their captivity before their eyes. The LORD will take away judgments, cast out the enemy, and rejoice over Zion with joy, singing, and quiet love, while the meek remnant will trust in His name and no longer do iniquity. The daughter of Zion is told to sing and shout, for the King of Israel is in her midst and she shall not see evil any more.
Fulfillment
A remnant returned from Babylon and rebuilt Jerusalem, so the gathering and the turning of captivity began in history, and Christians read the rejoicing King in the midst as fulfilled in Christ dwelling among His people. Zion has not yet known the complete end of shame and evil that Zephaniah pictures, with all nations gathered and the dispersed fully restored. The promise is therefore partly fulfilled, begun in return and in Christ, and awaiting its final joy.
