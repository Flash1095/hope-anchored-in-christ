Other Sheep Shall Be Brought into One Flock under One Shepherd

Prophecy
Other Sheep Shall Be Brought into One Flock under One Shepherd
Prophet
Jesus
Date Approx
c. AD 30
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
Jesus predicts that he has other sheep not of this fold, that he must bring them also, that they shall hear his voice, and that there shall be one flock and one shepherd.
Fulfillment
Partially fulfilled: Gentile believers have been brought into the one flock under Christ, while the gathering of all his sheep is still ongoing.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
