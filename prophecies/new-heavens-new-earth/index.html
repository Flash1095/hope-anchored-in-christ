Prophecy Summary
Isaiah ends by promising that God will create new heavens and a new earth where former sorrows are remembered no more. Weeping gives way to gladness, and wolf and lamb feed together in peace.
Fulfillment
Peter and John take up Isaiah's promise for the world to come, when righteousness dwells and every tear is wiped away. Believers taste its firstfruits now in new creation through Christ, and await its fullness at His return.
