Jerusalem Wiped as a Dish Under Manasseh

Prophecy
Jerusalem Wiped as a Dish Under Manasseh
Prophet
Prophets of the LORD
Date Approx
c. 650 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Because Manasseh did more evil than the Amorites before him, filling Jerusalem with innocent blood and leading Judah into idolatry, the LORD spoke by His servants the prophets that He would bring such evil on Jerusalem and Judah that whoever heard of it would be stunned. He would wipe Jerusalem as a man wipes a dish, wiping it and turning it upside down, and deliver the remnant into the hand of their enemies.
Fulfillment
Jerusalem fell to Nebuchadnezzar in 586 BC, the temple was burned, the walls broken down, and the people were carried into exile, with only the poorest left in the land. The vivid dish image matched the completeness of the emptying: the city was not merely damaged but turned over and left bare. The prophets are unnamed in the text, so no individual name is invented for them here.
