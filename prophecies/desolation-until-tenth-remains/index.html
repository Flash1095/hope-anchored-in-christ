Desolation Until Only a Tenth Remains

Prophecy
Desolation Until Only a Tenth Remains
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx

Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Asked how long his hardening message must run, Isaiah is told: until cities lie wasted without inhabitants, houses stand empty, the land is utterly desolate and the people are removed far away, yet a holy seed remains like a stump.
Fulfillment
The exile emptied Judah's cities and land, and a remnant returned; the holy-seed remnant is only partly realised in the return, so the entry is not claimed as wholly complete.
