Agabus Foretells Paul's Binding

Prophecy
Agabus Foretells Paul's Binding
Prophet
Agabus
Date Approx
c. AD 57
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
At Caesarea the prophet Agabus took Paul's girdle, bound his own hands and feet, and said that so the Jews at Jerusalem would bind the man who owned the girdle and deliver him to the Gentiles. Paul himself had already said that the Holy Ghost witnessed in every city that bonds and afflictions awaited him. When friends begged him not to go up to Jerusalem, Paul answered that he was ready not only to be bound but also to die for the name of the Lord Jesus.
Fulfillment
At Jerusalem Paul was seized in the temple, bound, and delivered into Roman custody, exactly as Agabus had dramatised. He was then taken through a series of hearings and finally to Rome, where his bonds became the setting for his witness. The binding prophecy is therefore fulfilled in the arrest and imprisonment recorded in Acts.
