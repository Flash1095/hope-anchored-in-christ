Four Beasts, the Little Horn, and Judgment

Prophecy

Prophet

Date Approx
c. 555 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
Four great beasts rise from the sea, diverse from one another, and among the fourth beast's ten horns a little horn rises, speaking great things and wearing out the saints, until the Ancient of Days sits in judgment, the beast is slain, and the kingdom is given to the saints of the Most High for ever.
Fulfillment
The identities of the four beasts and the little horn are genuinely contested between ancient-empire, Antiochene and still-future readings, and the saints' everlasting possession is not yet complete, so this is marked Debated; the Son of Man verses already live (Daniel 7:13-14) are deliberately excluded from this entry's passages.
