A Precious Cornerstone in Zion

Prophecy
A Precious Cornerstone in Zion
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
To rulers in Jerusalem who had made a covenant with death, Isaiah announced that the LORD was laying in Zion a stone, a tried stone, a precious corner stone, a sure foundation, and that whoever believes will not make haste. Judgment would be laid to the line and righteousness to the plummet, sweeping away the refuge of lies. The cornerstone is a foundation for trust, set by God himself in Zion.
Fulfillment
The New Testament identifies this cornerstone with Christ: Peter cites Isaiah 28:16 of Jesus as the living stone chosen by God, and Paul describes believers as built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets with Christ Jesus himself as the chief cornerstone. The refuge of lies in Jerusalem was swept away in its fall, and the sure foundation stands in Christ. The prophecy is therefore fulfilled.
