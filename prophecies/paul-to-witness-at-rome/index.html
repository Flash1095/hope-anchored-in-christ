Paul to Bear Witness at Rome

Prophecy
Paul to Bear Witness at Rome
Prophet
Jesus
Date Approx
c. AD 59
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
After Paul's testimony in Jerusalem, the Lord stands by him at night and says that as he has testified in Jerusalem, so he must bear witness also at Rome.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in Acts: Paul is sent to Rome under guard after his appeal to Caesar and preaches the kingdom of God there (Acts 28:16-31). Paul's earlier purpose to see Rome (Acts 19:21) is the same expectation, stated before the Lord's promise.
