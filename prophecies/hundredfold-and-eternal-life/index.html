Those Who Forsake All Shall Receive a Hundredfold and Eternal Life

Prophecy
Those Who Forsake All Shall Receive a Hundredfold and Eternal Life
Prophet
Jesus
Date Approx
c. AD 30
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
Jesus predicts that everyone who has forsaken houses, family, or lands for his name's sake shall receive a hundredfold (manifold more in this present time, with persecutions) and shall inherit everlasting life.
Fulfillment
Partially fulfilled: believers have received manifold provision and fellowship in this life, with persecutions as Mark states, while the full inheritance of eternal life in the world to come is still awaited.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
Key Passages
Matthew 19:29; Mark 10:29-30; Luke 18:29-30
Related Books
Matthew; Mark; Luke
Related People
Simon Peter
