Micaiah: Ahab Shall Fall at Ramoth-gilead

Prophecy
Micaiah: Ahab Shall Fall at Ramoth-gilead
Prophet
Micaiah, Son of Imlah
Date Approx
c. 853 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
When four hundred prophets promised Ahab victory at Ramoth-gilead, Micaiah son of Imlah alone said he had seen Israel scattered on the hills like sheep without a shepherd, and that the LORD had put a lying spirit in the mouths of Ahab's prophets. He told Ahab plainly that if he returned in peace, the LORD had not spoken by him, and Ahab had him put in prison on bread and water until his return.
Fulfillment
Ahab disguised himself for battle, but a man drew a bow at random and struck him between the joints of his armour, and he died at evening propped up in his chariot. Israel scattered as Micaiah had seen, and Ahab never returned in peace to release him. The account in Chronicles repeats this prophecy and is not counted as a separate entry.

Fulfilled
