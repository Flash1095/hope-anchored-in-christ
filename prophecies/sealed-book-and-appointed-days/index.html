The Sealed Book and the Appointed Days

Prophecy
The Sealed Book and the Appointed Days
Prophet
Daniel
Date Approx

Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
After the vision of resurrection and distress, Daniel was told to shut up the words and seal the book until the time of the end. He heard that the wonders would last for a time, times, and half a time, and he was given two further numbers, one thousand two hundred and ninety days and one thousand three hundred and thirty-five days. Daniel was told to go his way, rest, and stand in his lot at the end of the days.
Fulfillment
These day-counts have been applied to Antiochus IV's desecration, to the destruction of Jerusalem in AD 70, and to a future tribulation period, with no single interpretation commanding scholarly agreement. The numbers are distinct from the resurrection promise in Daniel 12:1 to 3, which is already covered, and they remain the debated detail of this closing vision. Because their referent and calendar are disputed, this entry is marked Debated.
