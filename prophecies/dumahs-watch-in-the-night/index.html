Prophecy Summary
The burden of Dumah is brief and haunting: one calls to the watchman out of Seir, asking what of the night, and the watchman answers that the morning comes, and also the night. It is a word to Edom's region of Dumah, holding out a morning that is still shadowed by night, and an invitation to return and enquire. The text names no dated invasion, and this entry does not supply one.
Fulfillment
No passage of Scripture or securely dated history identifies a single fulfilment for Dumah's night watch, and interpreters differ on whether it points to an Assyrian or Babylonian threat to Edom, to a longer night of judgment, or to a deliberately open-ended watchman's riddle. Because the referent is genuinely debated, this entry is marked Debated rather than tied to an invented campaign. It is included because it is a distinct burden against a named place, not a general saying.
