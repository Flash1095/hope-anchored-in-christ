Provision in the Sabbatical Year

Prophecy
Provision in the Sabbatical Year
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 1440 BC
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Prophecy Summary
Answering the question of what Israel will eat in the seventh year when they neither sow nor gather, God promises to command his blessing in the sixth year so that it brings forth fruit for three years, covering the seventh year, the eighth year's sowing, and eating of old store until the ninth year's fruit comes in.
Fulfillment
Debated as an event, stated as such: no narrative in Scripture records a specific sixth-year triple harvest, and the promise is conditional on keeping the sabbatical year, which Israel often neglected (the land later enjoying its sabbaths in exile, 2 Chronicles 36:21). The promise is drafted as a genuine predictive provision, but its historical fulfilment cannot be verified from a dated narrative account.
