The House of Ahab Condemned

Prophecy
The House of Ahab Condemned
Prophet
Elijah
Date Approx
c. 855 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
After Ahab had Naboth murdered to take his vineyard, Elijah met him there and declared that the LORD would bring evil upon Ahab, cut off his house, and make it like the houses of Jeroboam and Baasha. Dogs would lick Ahab's blood where they had licked Naboth's, and dogs would eat Jezebel by the wall of Jezreel.
Fulfillment
Ahab died of his wound at Ramoth-gilead, and dogs licked his blood at the pool of Samaria as his chariot was washed, while his sons were killed and his house wiped out under Jehu. Jezebel was thrown from a window at Jezreel and eaten by dogs, so that only her skull, feet, and palms remained. Ahab's temporary humbling delayed part of the judgment to his son's days, but the house itself was not spared.
