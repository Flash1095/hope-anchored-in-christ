Restoration of the Booth of David

Prophecy
Restoration of the Booth of David
Prophet
Amos
Date Approx
c. 760 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Amos ends with hope: God would raise up the fallen booth of David, repair its breaches, and restore the days of plenty. The restored people would possess the nations called by God's name and never again be uprooted.
Fulfillment
At the Jerusalem Council, James quoted this very text to show that Gentiles being gathered into Christ's church was David's tent being rebuilt. Its full picture of settled, fruitful restoration still awaits the kingdom's completion.
