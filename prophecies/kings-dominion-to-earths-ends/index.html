The King's Dominion to Earth's Ends

Prophecy
The King's Dominion to Earth's Ends
Prophet
King Solomon
Date Approx
c. 970 BC
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
The psalm prays for a king who will judge the poor with righteousness, deliver the needy, and spare the poor and needy. His dominion is described as reaching from sea to sea and unto the ends of the earth, with all kings falling down before him. His name is to endure for ever, and all nations are to call him blessed.
Fulfillment
Solomon's reign showed only a shadow of this universal dominion, in wisdom and peace but not in rule over all nations. In Christ the dominion is inaugurated, for all authority is given to Him, yet the nations do not yet universally bow to Him. The prophecy is therefore partially fulfilled, awaiting the day when every knee bows and His reign is openly universal.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
