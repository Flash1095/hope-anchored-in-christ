Many from East and West Shall Sit Down in the Kingdom

Prophecy
Many from East and West Shall Sit Down in the Kingdom
Prophet
Jesus
Date Approx
c. AD 30
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
Jesus predicts that many will come from the east and the west and sit down with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven, while the children of the kingdom who reject him will be cast out into outer darkness.
Fulfillment
Partially fulfilled: Gentiles from every direction have entered the kingdom through faith in Christ, while unbelieving Israel was judged; the final banquet and final exclusion still lie ahead.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
Key Passages
Matthew 8:11-12; Luke 13:28-29

Related People
