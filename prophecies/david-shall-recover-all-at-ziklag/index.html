David Shall Surely Overtake and Recover All at Ziklag

Prophecy
David Shall Surely Overtake and Recover All at Ziklag
Date Approx
c. 1010 BC
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Finding Ziklag burned and his people carried off, David enquires of the LORD whether to pursue the troop, and the LORD answers: pursue, for thou shalt surely overtake them, and without fail recover all.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in the pursuit: David recovers all that the Amalekites had carried away, and nothing is lacking, small or great (1 Samuel 30:18-19).
Fulfillment Status
Fulfilled
