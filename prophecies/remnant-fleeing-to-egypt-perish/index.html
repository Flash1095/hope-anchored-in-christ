The Remnant Fleeing to Egypt Will Perish There

Prophecy
The Remnant Fleeing to Egypt Will Perish There
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 586 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
The remnant of Judah that sets its face to enter Egypt, against God's warning, will die there by sword, famine and pestilence, with only a small number escaping, and Egypt, sought as refuge, will become their grave.
Fulfillment
The remnant that fled to Egypt after Gedaliah's murder was caught in Egypt's own disasters and largely perished there, with only a few escaping, as Jeremiah warned.

Fulfilled
