The Blind See and the Lame Leap

Prophecy
The Blind See and the Lame Leap
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Isaiah promised a day when the eyes of the blind would be opened, the ears of the deaf unstopped, and the lame would leap like deer. It would be God's own coming to save His people.
Fulfillment
When John the Baptist asked if Jesus was the one, Jesus simply pointed to this prophecy happening: the blind see, the lame walk, and the poor have good news preached to them.
