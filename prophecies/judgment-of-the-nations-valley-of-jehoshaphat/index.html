Prophecy Summary
Joel foretold that God will gather all nations into the Valley of Jehoshaphat, meaning the LORD judges, and enter into judgment with them there. The charge is specific: they scattered God's people, divided His land, and sold His people for gain. The sun and moon will be darkened, the LORD will roar from Zion, and Judah and Jerusalem will be safe while their enemies face judgment.
Fulfillment
No single historical battle has been identified as the complete fulfillment of this gathering, and the passage's cosmic signs and final safety of Jerusalem point beyond ordinary warfare. Partial judgments on Israel's neighbours did occur in the ancient world, but the universal gathering and final verdict Joel describes have not yet taken place in full. The prophecy is therefore future, with its setting in Jerusalem and Zion still awaiting that final day.
