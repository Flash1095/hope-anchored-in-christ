Egypt's Help Vain

Prophecy
Egypt's Help Vain
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Judah's envoys who go down to Egypt for help will find it vain and to no purpose: Egypt's strength is to sit still, and trusting in Pharaoh's horses and chariots will bring shame, not deliverance.
Fulfillment
Egypt failed to save Judah from Assyria in 701 BC and failed again against Babylon; Judah's Egyptian alliance repeatedly proved empty.
Fulfillment Status
Fulfilled
