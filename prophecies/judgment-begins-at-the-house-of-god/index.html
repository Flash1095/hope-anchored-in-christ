Judgment Begins at the House of God

Prophecy
Judgment Begins at the House of God
Prophet
Peter
Date Approx
c. AD 63
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
The time has come for judgment to begin at the house of God, and if it begins with believers first, the end of those who do not obey the gospel will be far worse; the righteous themselves are scarcely saved.
Fulfillment
Begun in the fiery trials and persecutions borne by the early church, which Peter presents as judgment beginning with God's own people; the final judgment of the disobedient is still awaited, so the prediction stands partly fulfilled.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
Key Passages
1 Peter 4:17-18; 1 Peter 4:12-13
