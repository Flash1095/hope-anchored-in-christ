Edom Judged

Prophecy
Edom Judged
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 600 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Jeremiah foretold that Edom's wisdom would fail, that its people would be made small among the heathen and despised among men, and that its pride had deceived it. Bozrah would become a desolation, a reproach, and a curse, and Edom would become like Sodom and Gomorrah, with no man dwelling there. It is Jeremiah's own Edom oracle, distinct from Isaiah's Edom and day-of-vengeance entry, though it speaks of the same nation's fall.
Fulfillment
Edom was overrun by Babylon and later displaced by the Nabataeans, Bozrah and the Edomite territory were left desolate, and the Edomites as a nation disappeared, surviving only as Idumeans absorbed into later history. The desolation of Bozrah and the end of Edom as a people match the oracle. The prophecy was therefore fulfilled in Edom's disappearance as a nation.
