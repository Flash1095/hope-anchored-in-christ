Nebuchadnezzar's Humbling and Restoration

Prophecy
Nebuchadnezzar's Humbling and Restoration
Prophet
Daniel
Date Approx
6th century BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
Nebuchadnezzar dreamed of a great tree cut down and bound with iron and brass, and Daniel explained that the king himself would be driven from men and live like a beast for seven times. The humbling would last until he acknowledged that the Most High rules in the kingdom of men. After that period his reason and kingdom would be restored to him.
Fulfillment
Daniel 4 reports that the dream came true exactly as interpreted, with Nebuchadnezzar driven out, eating grass like oxen, and restored after he lifted his eyes to heaven and praised the Most High. Extra-biblical records do not clearly document this episode, so the entry rests on Scripture's own report of the event. Within the biblical account the prediction and its restoration are presented as complete.
