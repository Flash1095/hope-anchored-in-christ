Strike the Shepherd, Scatter the Sheep

Prophecy
Strike the Shepherd, Scatter the Sheep
Prophet
Zechariah
Date Approx
c. 480 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
Zechariah heard God's sword awake against His shepherd, the man who stands next to Him: strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered. Yet a refined remnant would survive the scattering and call on God's name.
Fulfillment
Jesus quoted this verse on the night of His arrest to explain why all His disciples would fall away and flee. They did scatter — and, as Zechariah also promised, they were gathered again after the resurrection.
