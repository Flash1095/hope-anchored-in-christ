The Seven-Year Famine Foretold to the Shunammite

Prophecy
The Seven-Year Famine Foretold to the Shunammite
Prophet
Elisha
Date Approx
c. 845 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Elisha warns the woman whose son he had restored to life to leave with her household and sojourn wherever she can, for the LORD has called for a famine, and it will come upon the land seven years.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled over the seven years: she sojourns in the land of the Philistines seven years, returns at the end of the seven years, and appeals to the king for her house and land (2 Kings 8:2-3, 8:6). Distinct from the already-live promise of her son's birth.
Fulfillment Status
Fulfilled
