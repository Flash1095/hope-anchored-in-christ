The Prince of This World Shall Be Cast Out

Prophecy
The Prince of This World Shall Be Cast Out
Prophet
Jesus
Date Approx
c. AD 30
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
Jesus predicts that now is the judgment of this world and now shall the prince of this world be cast out, and that the prince of this world is judged.
Fulfillment
Partially fulfilled: Satan's defeat was secured at the cross and resurrection, yet his final casting out (Revelation 20) still lies ahead, so the casting out is begun but not yet complete.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
