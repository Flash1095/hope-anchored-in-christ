The Curse on Whoever Rebuilds Jericho

Prophecy
The Curse on Whoever Rebuilds Jericho
Prophet
Joshua
Date Approx
c. 1400 BC
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Prophecy Summary
After Jericho fell, Joshua pronounced a solemn curse: whoever rose up to rebuild the city would lay its foundation at the cost of his firstborn son and set up its gates at the cost of his youngest son. The curse was spoken over a city devoted to destruction, and it stood as a warning for generations after the conquest.
Fulfillment
Centuries later, in the days of King Ahab, Hiel of Bethel rebuilt Jericho, and 1 Kings records that he laid its foundation with the loss of his firstborn Abiram and set up its gates with the loss of his youngest Segub, according to the word of the LORD by Joshua. The long gap between curse and fulfilment is itself part of the point: the word waited until someone dared to defy it.
