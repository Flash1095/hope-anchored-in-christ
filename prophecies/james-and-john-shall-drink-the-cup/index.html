James and John Shall Drink of Christ's Cup

Prophecy
James and John Shall Drink of Christ's Cup
Prophet
Jesus
Date Approx
c. AD 30
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
Jesus predicts that James and John shall indeed drink of his cup and be baptized with his baptism, sharing in his suffering, though places at his right and left are not his to give.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in their suffering for Christ: James was killed by Herod (Acts 12:2), and John suffered persecution and exile (Revelation 1:9).
Fulfillment Status
Fulfilled
Key Passages
Matthew 20:22-23; Mark 10:38-39
Related Books
Matthew; Mark
Related People
James, Son of Zebedee; John the Apostle
