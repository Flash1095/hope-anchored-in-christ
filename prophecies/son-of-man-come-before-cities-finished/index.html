The Son of Man Shall Come Before the Cities of Israel Are Gone Over

Prophecy
The Son of Man Shall Come Before the Cities of Israel Are Gone Over
Prophet
Jesus
Date Approx
c. AD 30
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
Jesus tells his disciples, as he sends them out, that they will not have gone over the cities of Israel before the Son of Man comes.
Fulfillment
Disputed: readings differ over whether this refers to the resurrection, the transfiguration, the fall of Jerusalem in AD 70, or the future return of Christ, so no single fulfilment is asserted here.
