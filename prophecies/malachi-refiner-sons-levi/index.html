The Refiner Purifies the Sons of Levi

Prophecy
The Refiner Purifies the Sons of Levi
Prophet
Malachi
Date Approx
c. 430 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
Malachi asks who may abide the day of the messenger's coming, for he is like a refiner's fire and fullers' soap, and will sit as a refiner and purifier of silver, purifying the sons of Levi and purging them as gold and silver so they may offer an offering in righteousness, and will come near to judgment as a swift witness against sorcerers, adulterers, false swearers, and oppressors of the hireling, widow, fatherless, and stranger.
Fulfillment
Partially fulfilled in Christ's first coming, which exposed and judged corrupt worship and began the purification of God's people; the complete purification and judgment it pictures is still awaited.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled

Malachi 3:2-3; Malachi 3:5
