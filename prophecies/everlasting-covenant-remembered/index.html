An Everlasting Covenant Remembered

Prophecy
An Everlasting Covenant Remembered
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 591 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
After Jerusalem's shame is laid bare, God will nevertheless remember his covenant with her in the days of her youth and establish with her an everlasting covenant, so that she remembers, is confounded, and never opens her mouth again because of her shame.
Fulfillment
The return from exile began this remembering, but the everlasting covenant and the silencing of shame are only partly realised in history, so this is partial.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
