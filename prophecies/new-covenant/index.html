The New Covenant

The New Covenant

Prophecy Summary

Jeremiah promised a new covenant unlike the one broken at Sinai: God's law written on hearts, sins forgiven and remembered no more, and every believer knowing the Lord. It was Israel's greatest hope for a healed relationship with God.

Fulfillment

At the Last Supper Jesus lifted the cup and called it the new covenant in His blood, and Hebrews declares the promise kept. Forgiveness and a transformed heart are now the birthright of everyone in Christ.
