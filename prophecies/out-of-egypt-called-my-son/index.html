Prophecy Summary
Hosea looked back to the exodus: when Israel was a child God loved him, and out of Egypt He called His son. It is first a tender history lesson about Israel, not an obvious prediction.
Fulfillment
Matthew sees a deeper pattern fulfilled when the child Jesus, taken to Egypt to escape Herod, is called back out of Egypt just as Israel was. Jesus relives Israel's story — and succeeds where Israel failed.
