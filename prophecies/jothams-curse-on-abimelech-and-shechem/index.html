Jotham's Curse on Abimelech and Shechem

Prophecy Summary

From Mount Gerizim Jotham tells the parable of the trees and the bramble, ending with a curse: if Shechem dealt falsely with Jerubbaal's house, let fire come out from Abimelech and devour Shechem and the house of Millo, and let fire come out from Shechem and Millo and devour Abimelech.

Fulfillment

Fulfilled in mutual destruction: God renders Abimelech's wickedness in slaying his seventy brothers and the evil of the men of Shechem upon their own heads, and the narrative states that upon them came the curse of Jotham (Judges 9:56-57).
