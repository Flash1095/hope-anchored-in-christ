Hananiah's Death Within the Year

Prophecy Summary

When Hananiah the prophet broke Jeremiah's wooden yoke and promised that Babylon's yoke would be broken within two years, Jeremiah answered that Hananiah had made the people trust in a lie, and that the LORD would remove him from the face of the earth within that same year. It was a direct test of a false prophecy, with a dated, verifiable outcome. This entry is small, but it meets the test of a named individual's fate treated by Scripture itself as prophetic.

Fulfillment

Hananiah died in the seventh month of that same year, exactly as Jeremiah had said, as Jeremiah 28:17 records. The false promise of a swift end to Babylon's yoke did not come to pass, and the true prophet's word was vindicated within months. The prophecy was therefore fulfilled in Scripture's own record.
