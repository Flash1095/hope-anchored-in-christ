Samaria Made a Heap of the Field

Prophecy
Samaria Made a Heap of the Field
Prophet
Micah
Date Approx
c. 730 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Micah predicts that God will make Samaria a heap of the field and like plantings of a vineyard, pour its stones into the valley, uncover its foundations, beat its graven images to pieces, and burn its hires with fire.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in the Assyrian siege and capture of Samaria (722 BC), after which the city lost its standing as Israel's capital and its idolatrous images were destroyed.
