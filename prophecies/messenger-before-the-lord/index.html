The Messenger Before the Lord

Prophecy
The Messenger Before the Lord
Prophet
Malachi
Date Approx
c. 430 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
Malachi promised a messenger who would prepare the way before the Lord's sudden coming to His temple, like a refiner's fire purifying the sons of Levi. He closes the Old Testament by pointing to Elijah coming before the great Day of the Lord.
Fulfillment
Jesus identifies John the Baptist as this messenger and as Elijah who was to come, preparing hearts by repentance before Christ's appearing. A future, fuller coming before the final Day is also debated, but the primary fulfilment in John is Jesus' own verdict.
Fulfillment Status
Fulfilled
