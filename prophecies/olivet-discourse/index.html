The Olivet Discourse

Prophecy
The Olivet Discourse
Prophet
Jesus
Date Approx
c. AD 30
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
On the Mount of Olives Jesus foretold the temple's destruction, wars, persecution, false prophets, and the gospel preached to all nations before the end. He also promised His own return in glory and warned His followers to stay awake and ready.
Fulfillment
The temple's fall in AD 70 fulfilled the first horizon of the prophecy with sobering precision, while the final return and judgment still lie ahead. How much belongs to each horizon is honestly debated, so this is marked Partially Fulfilled.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
