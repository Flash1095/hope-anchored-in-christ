The Conquest Promised to Joshua

The Conquest Promised to Joshua

Prophecy Summary

At the Jordan, the LORD tells Joshua that Israel will cross over and take the land sworn to their fathers, that every place their feet tread is given to them, and that no man will be able to stand against Joshua all his life.

Fulfillment

Fulfilled in the conquest narrative: Israel crosses the Jordan, takes Jericho, Ai, and the southern and northern coalitions, and Joshua 21:43-45 records that the LORD gave Israel all the land sworn to their fathers and that none of his good promises failed.
