Disciples Shall Be Expelled from Synagogues and Killed as Service to God

Prophecy
Disciples Shall Be Expelled from Synagogues and Killed as Service to God
Prophet
Jesus
Date Approx
c. AD 30
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
Jesus predicts that the world will hate and persecute his disciples as it persecuted him, that they shall be put out of the synagogues, and that the time will come when whoever kills them will think he does God service, because their persecutors have not known the Father or him.
Fulfillment
Partially fulfilled: synagogue expulsions and killings of believers are recorded in Acts and the early church, and the pattern of persecution for Christ's name has continued since.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled

John 15:18-21; John 16:1-4
