A Son Promised to the Shunammite

Prophecy
A Son Promised to the Shunammite
Prophet
Elisha
Date Approx
c. 850 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
The Shunammite woman had shown Elisha generous hospitality without asking anything in return, though she and her elderly husband had no child. Elisha told her that about that season, according to the time of life, she would embrace a son, a timed promise that she at first begged him not to deceive her about.
Fulfillment
The woman conceived and bore a son at the season Elisha had said, and the child grew until a later illness took his life and Elisha raised him again. This is a borderline personal prediction, included because it is a precisely timed divine promise delivered by a named prophet, not a general blessing, and because the narrative presents it as a prophetic word. The later raising of the boy is a separate miracle, noted here only to complete the story.
