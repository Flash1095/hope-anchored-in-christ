Ruined Cities Rebuilt and the Land Fruitful

Prophecy
Ruined Cities Rebuilt and the Land Fruitful
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 585 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
The mountains of Israel will shoot forth branches and yield fruit for God's people at hand to come, and in the day of cleansing the ruined cities will be inhabited, the wastes built, and the desolate land tilled like the garden of Eden, so the nations will know it was the LORD who built the ruins.
Fulfillment
Post-exilic rebuilding repopulated ruined towns and tilled the land again, but not on the Eden scale described, so this is partial; it uses Ezekiel 36:8-15 and 36:33-38 only, leaving the new-heart verses already live untouched.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
