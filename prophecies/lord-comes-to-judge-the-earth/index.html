Prophecy
The LORD Comes to Judge the Earth
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
The LORD is coming to judge the earth, and he will judge the world with righteousness and the peoples with equity and truth.
Fulfillment
Awaited in its full and final sense: particular judgments in history do not exhaust a universal judgment of the whole earth, which Scripture elsewhere places at the end, so this remains a future fulfilment.
Fulfillment Status
Future
