The Sword Shall Never Depart from David's House

Prophecy
The Sword Shall Never Depart from David's House
Prophet
Nathan
Date Approx
c. 990 BC
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
After David's sin with Bathsheba, Nathan declares that because David despised the LORD and took Uriah's wife, the sword will never depart from his house, and the LORD will raise up evil against him out of his own house, taking his wives before his eyes and giving them to his neighbour in the sight of the sun.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in the strife that follows within David's house: Amnon is slain (2 Samuel 13:28-29), and Absalom rises against his father and takes his concubines publicly (2 Samuel 15:13-14; 16:21-22). This entry covers the house-strife prediction; the child's death is a separate prediction drafted separately.
