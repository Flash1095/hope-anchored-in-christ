Tophet Renamed the Valley of Slaughter

Prophecy
Tophet Renamed the Valley of Slaughter
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 605 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
The place called Tophet and the Valley of the son of Hinnom will no longer bear those names but will be called the Valley of Slaughter, because its people filled it with innocent blood, burned their children, and will themselves fall by the sword and lie unburied.
Fulfillment
Jerusalem's slaughter in 586 BC, with bodies left unburied around the city, matches the renaming; the valley's defilement under Josiah is a separate earlier act, not this prediction's fulfilment.
