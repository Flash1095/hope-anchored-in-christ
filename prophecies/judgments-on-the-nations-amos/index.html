Judgments on the Nations

Prophecy
Judgments on the Nations
Prophet
Amos
Date Approx
c. 760 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Amos delivered eight short oracles, each beginning for three transgressions and for four, against Damascus, Gaza, Tyre, Edom, Ammon, Moab, Judah, and Israel. Each nation is charged with a concrete cruelty or covenant breach: threshing Gilead with iron, selling whole communities into slavery, breaking a covenant of brotherhood, ripping open pregnant women, burning a king's bones, and more. The series ends at home, showing that Judah and Israel will be judged by the same righteous standard as their neighbours.
Fulfillment
These nations did in turn face conquest and displacement, above all under Assyria and then Babylon: Damascus fell to Assyria in 732 BC, the Philistine cities and Ammon, Moab, and Edom lost independence in the same imperial waves, Judah fell to Babylon in 586 BC, and Israel fell to Assyria in 722 BC. The pattern of judgment Amos announced therefore unfolded across the eighth to sixth centuries BC, even though not every detail of each oracle can be matched to a single dated campaign. The series is counted as fulfilled in its national judgments.
