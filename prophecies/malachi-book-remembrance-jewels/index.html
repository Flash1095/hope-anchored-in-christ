The Book of Remembrance and the Jewels

Prophecy
The Book of Remembrance and the Jewels
Prophet
Malachi
Date Approx
c. 430 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
Malachi predicts that those who fear the LORD and think upon his name will have a book of remembrance written before him, and in the day when he makes up his jewels they will be his and he will spare them as a man spares his own serving son, and then people will return and discern between the righteous and the wicked, between him who serves God and him who does not.
Fulfillment
Future: the day of the jewels and the open discernment between righteous and wicked has no completed historical fulfilment and is awaited at the final judgment.
Fulfillment Status
Future
