The Holy City Trodden Forty-Two Months

Prophecy
The Holy City Trodden Forty-Two Months
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
John is told to measure the temple of God, the altar, and those who worship there, but to leave out the outer court, which is given to the Gentiles, and the holy city shall they tread under foot for forty-two months.
Fulfillment
Debated: the forty-two months match the beast's authority elsewhere in Revelation, but whether the trampling is the Roman destruction of Jerusalem, a future occupation, or a symbolic span of Gentile domination is disputed, and the measured temple is read as literal, symbolic, or heavenly by different interpreters.
