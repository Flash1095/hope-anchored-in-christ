Judgment by Fire and the Nations Gathered to Jerusalem

Prophecy
Judgment by Fire and the Nations Gathered to Jerusalem
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
The LORD will come with fire and whirlwind chariots to judge all flesh, gather all nations and tongues to see his glory, send survivors to the distant coastlands to declare it, and bring worshippers from all nations to his holy mountain.
Fulfillment
No historical event gathers all nations to Jerusalem in this way or completes the fire judgment; this remains awaited, distinct from the new-heavens entry already live, which rests on Isaiah 65:17-25.
