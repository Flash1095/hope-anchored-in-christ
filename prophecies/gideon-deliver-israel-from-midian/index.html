Gideon Shall Deliver Israel from Midian

Gideon Shall Deliver Israel from Midian

Prophecy Summary

While Israel was hiding from Midianite raids, the Angel of the LORD appeared to Gideon and told him that he would save Israel from the hand of Midian, and that the LORD would be with him. Gideon doubted, being the least in his father's house, but the word was repeated with the promise that he would strike the Midianites as one man.

Fulfillment

With only three hundred men, trumpets, and torches, Gideon routed the vast Midianite camp by night, and his forces pursued and captured the Midianite princes Oreb and Zeeb. The oppression that had lasted seven years was broken, and the land had rest for forty years. No human prophet mediates this word in the text, so the speaker is recorded as the Angel of the LORD.
