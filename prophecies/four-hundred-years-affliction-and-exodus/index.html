Four Hundred Years of Affliction and the Exodus

Prophecy Summary

In a vision God told Abraham that his offspring would be strangers in a land not theirs, afflicted for four hundred years, and that the nation that afflicted them would be judged. Afterwards, God said, they would come out with great substance, and return to the promised land in the fourth generation, because the iniquity of the Amorites was not yet full.

Fulfillment

Israel sojourned in Egypt, was enslaved, and left at the exodus after the plagues, carrying silver and gold given by the Egyptians, as Exodus 12 records. The four hundred years is a round figure for the sojourn, and Exodus 12:40 gives the longer figure of 430 years, which is why the entry states the promise in its own terms rather than forcing a single arithmetic. The return to Canaan under Joshua completed the movement the prophecy described.
