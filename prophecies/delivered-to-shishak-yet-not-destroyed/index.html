Delivered to Shishak, Yet Not Destroyed

Prophecy
Delivered to Shishak, Yet Not Destroyed
Prophet
Shemaiah
Date Approx
c. 925 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
After Rehoboam abandoned the law of the LORD, the prophet Shemaiah told him and the princes gathered at Jerusalem that because they had forsaken God, God had left them in the hand of Shishak king of Egypt. When they humbled themselves, a second word came: God would not destroy them but would grant them some deliverance, though they would become Shishak's servants so they might learn the difference between serving God and serving the kings of other lands.
Fulfillment
Shishak came up against Jerusalem in Rehoboam's fifth year with a great army and took the treasures of the house of the LORD and the king's house, including the gold shields Solomon had made. Jerusalem itself was not destroyed, Rehoboam humbled himself, and the wrath was turned away, though Judah remained weakened and tributary. This prophecy survives only in Chronicles, not in the parallel Kings account, and is therefore a genuinely new Chronicles item rather than a duplicate.
