Samuel at Endor: Saul and His Sons Fall Tomorrow

Prophecy
Samuel at Endor: Saul and His Sons Fall Tomorrow
Prophet
Samuel
Date Approx
c. 1010 BC
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Through the medium at Endor, Samuel tells Saul that the LORD will deliver Israel with him into the hand of the Philistines, and that tomorrow Saul and his sons will be with him, and the host of Israel delivered to the Philistines.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled the next day on Mount Gilboa: Saul's sons fall, Saul falls on his sword, and Israel flees before the Philistines (1 Samuel 31:1-6).
Fulfillment Status
Fulfilled

1 Samuel 28:19; 1 Samuel 31:1-6
