Nineveh's Destruction in Forty Days

Prophecy
Nineveh's Destruction in Forty Days
Prophet
Jonah
Date Approx
c. 760 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
After Jonah's own deliverance from the great fish, God sent him a second time to Nineveh with a stark message: yet forty days, and Nineveh shall be overthrown. From the king down to the least, the Ninevites believed God, proclaimed a fast, put on sackcloth, and turned from their evil way and violence. The prophecy is therefore both a warning of destruction and an offer of mercy, with the forty days as a window for repentance.
Fulfillment
The immediate destruction did not fall in forty days, because God saw their repentance and relented, as Jonah 3:10 states plainly, so the forty-day timetable was not carried out on that generation. Nineveh did later fall, completely and finally, to the Medes and Babylonians in 612 BC, as Nahum foretold in detail, so the threatened overthrow was delayed by mercy and then executed in history. For that reason this entry is marked Partially Fulfilled: averted in Jonah's day, fulfilled in Nineveh's eventual fall.
