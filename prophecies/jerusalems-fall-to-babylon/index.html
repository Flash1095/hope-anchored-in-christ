Jerusalem's Fall to Babylon

Prophecy Summary

Jeremiah repeatedly foretold that Jerusalem would be given into the hand of the king of Babylon, that the city would be taken, its houses burned, and King Zedekiah would be carried to Babylon after seeing the Babylonian king face to face. Those who stayed in the city would die by sword, famine, and pestilence, while those who surrendered would live. The warnings of Jerusalem becoming like Shiloh, and of the valley of Tophet, belong to this same judgment, and are folded into this entry rather than duplicated.

Fulfillment

In 586 BC Nebuchadnezzar's army broke through Jerusalem's walls, burned the temple and the city, blinded Zedekiah after killing his sons before his eyes, and carried him in chains to Babylon, as 2 Kings 25 records. The famine, sword, and burning Jeremiah described all came to pass in that siege. The prophecy was therefore fulfilled in full and in detail.
