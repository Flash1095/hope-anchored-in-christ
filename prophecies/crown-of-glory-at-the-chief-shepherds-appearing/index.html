The Crown of Glory at the Chief Shepherd's Appearing

Prophecy
The Crown of Glory at the Chief Shepherd's Appearing
Prophet
Peter
Date Approx
c. AD 63
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
When the chief Shepherd appears, faithful elders who shepherded God's flock willingly and by example will receive a crown of glory that does not fade.
Fulfillment
Awaited: the chief Shepherd has not yet appeared in this sense, and the unfading crown is a future reward for faithful shepherding, distinct from the crown of life promised to all who endure.
Fulfillment Status
Future
