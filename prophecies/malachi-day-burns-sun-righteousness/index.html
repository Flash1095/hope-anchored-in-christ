The Day Burns as an Oven, the Sun of Righteousness Rises

Prophecy
The Day Burns as an Oven, the Sun of Righteousness Rises
Prophet
Malachi
Date Approx
c. 430 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
Malachi predicts a coming day that will burn as an oven, when all the proud and all who do wickedly will be stubble burned up leaving neither root nor branch, but to those who fear God's name the Sun of righteousness will arise with healing in his wings, they will go forth and grow up as calves of the stall, and tread down the wicked as ashes under their feet in the day God does this.
Fulfillment
Future: this final day of burning judgment and healing for the God-fearing is not a completed historical event and is awaited.
