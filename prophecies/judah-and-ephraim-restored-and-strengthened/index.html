Judah and Ephraim Restored and Strengthened

Prophecy
Judah and Ephraim Restored and Strengthened
Prophet
Zechariah
Date Approx
c. 5th century BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
Zechariah promised that God will strengthen the house of Judah and save the house of Joseph, bringing them back because He has mercy on them, and they will be as though He had not cast them off. The sons of Zion would be raised against the sons of Greece, Judah would be bent as God's bow and Ephraim as its arrow, and God would whistle for His scattered people, sow them among the peoples, and bring them again from Egypt and Assyria to Gilead and Lebanon. Their children would see it, their hearts would rejoice, and they would walk in the LORD's name.
Fulfillment
The Maccabean victories over Greek rule gave Judah a measure of the strength against Greece that Zechariah pictures, and Jews did return from Egypt, Assyria, and the wider dispersion in the post-exilic centuries. The full reunion of Judah and Ephraim, the northern tribes long lost after 722 BC, has never occurred as a political restoration, and Christians read the gathering as continuing in Christ, who makes one people from Jew and Gentile. The entry is therefore partly fulfilled, with its complete reunion still future.
