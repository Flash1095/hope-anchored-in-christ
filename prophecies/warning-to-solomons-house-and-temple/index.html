Warning to Solomon's House: Israel Cut Off, the Temple Cast Out

Warning to Solomon's House: Israel Cut Off, the Temple Cast Out

Prophecy Summary

After the dedication the LORD warns Solomon and his children: if they turn from following him, serve other gods, and worship them, he will cut off Israel from the land he gave them, cast the hallowed house out of his sight, and Israel will become a proverb and a byword, with passers-by hissing at the ruined house.

Fulfillment

Fulfilled when Nebuzaradan burns the house of the LORD and breaks down Jerusalem's walls (2 Kings 25:8-10), after the long apostasy of the kings.
