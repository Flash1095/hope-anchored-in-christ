The Sealed of Israel

Prophecy
The Sealed of Israel
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Before the winds of judgment were loosed, John heard the number of those sealed: 144,000, twelve thousand from each of the twelve tribes of Israel, sealed with the seal of the living God upon their foreheads. Later he saw the same company standing with the Lamb on Mount Sion, having his Father's name written in their foreheads, singing a new song that no one else could learn, as firstfruits unto God and to the Lamb, undefiled and without fault before the throne. The sealing marks God's people as his own and preserves them through the judgments that follow.
Fulfillment
The identity of the 144,000 is debated: some read them as a literal future remnant of ethnic Israel, twelve thousand from each tribe, while others read the number and tribes symbolically for the whole redeemed people of God. This entry is kept separate from the seven seals entry because the sealing is not itself a judgment — it is the preservation and identification of God's people between the sixth and seventh seals and again in Revelation 14. No tribal census is claimed here, since the text gives a symbolic roll rather than a historical register.
