The House of Jeroboam Cut Off and Israel Exiled

Prophecy
The House of Jeroboam Cut Off and Israel Exiled
Prophet
Ahijah
Date Approx
c. 910 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
When Jeroboam's wife came in disguise to ask about her sick son, the now-blind Ahijah told her that the LORD would raise up a king who would cut off the house of Jeroboam, and that Israel would be uprooted from the good land and scattered beyond the Euphrates. The sign would be immediate and terrible: the child would die as soon as his mother entered the city, and all Israel would mourn for him alone, because some good was found in him.
Fulfillment
The child Abijah died as Ahijah said, and Baasha later struck down the whole house of Jeroboam, leaving none that breathed, as the word had said. The exile foretold in the same oracle came generations later, when Assyria carried Israel away and Samaria fell, and 2 Kings ties that fall to the sins of Jeroboam. One prophecy thus carried both a near judgment on a dynasty and a distant judgment on a nation.
