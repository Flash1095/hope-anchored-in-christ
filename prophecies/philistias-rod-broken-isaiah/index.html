Philistia's Rod Broken

Prophecy
Philistia's Rod Broken
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Philistia rejoiced when the rod that struck it was broken, but Isaiah warned it not to rejoice, for from the serpent's root would come a viper, and its root would be destroyed by famine and the sword. The whole of Philistia would be dissolved, and a smoke would come from the north against it. It is a distinct oracle against Philistia, not merely a part of the Assyrian oracles around it.
Fulfillment
Philistia was repeatedly invaded by Assyria and later by Babylon, its cities were devastated, and the Philistines as a distinct people faded from history. The northern invasion and the destruction foretold match the Assyrian and Babylonian campaigns against the Philistine cities. The oracle was therefore fulfilled in the loss of Philistine independence and identity.
