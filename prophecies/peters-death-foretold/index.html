Peter's Death Foretold

Prophecy
Peter's Death Foretold
Prophet
Jesus Christ
Date Approx
c. AD 30
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
After restoring Peter, the risen Jesus told him that when he was old he would stretch forth his hands and another would gird him and carry him where he would not go. John adds that Jesus spoke this signifying by what death Peter should glorify God. The prediction looked beyond Peter's ministry to the manner of his death, not merely to his age.
Fulfillment
The New Testament does not record Peter's death, so its fulfilment rests on early Christian tradition rather than on a scriptural account. That tradition, preserved by early writers, holds that Peter was martyred at Rome under Nero and crucified, as the stretched-out hands suggest. Because the scriptural record is silent and the tradition, though early, is not contemporary proof, this entry is marked Debated rather than Fulfilled.
