Zeresh: Haman Shall Surely Fall Before Mordecai

Prophecy
Zeresh: Haman Shall Surely Fall Before Mordecai
Prophet
Zeresh
Date Approx
c. 480 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
After Haman told his wife Zeresh and his friends how Mordecai had been honoured, they told him that if Mordecai was of Jewish descent, before whom he had begun to fall, he would not prevail against him but would surely fall before him.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled within the same narrative: that very evening Haman was brought to Esther's banquet, his plot against Mordecai and the Jews was exposed, and he was hanged on the gallows he had prepared for Mordecai.
