Betrayed by a Friend

Prophecy
Betrayed by a Friend
Prophet
David
Date Approx
c. 1000 BC
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
David lamented that his own familiar friend, in whom he trusted and who ate of his bread, had lifted up his heel against him. Jesus applied that verse to His own circle, saying that one who ate bread with Him would betray Him. At the Last Supper He told the disciples plainly that one of them would betray Him, and Judas went out to do it.
Fulfillment
Judas Iscariot, one of the Twelve who shared bread with Jesus, betrayed Him to the chief priests and identified Him with a kiss. The betrayal led directly to Jesus's arrest, trial, and crucifixion, exactly as the passion narrative records. This single entry covers both the psalm and the Gospel predictions, so the betrayal is not counted twice.
