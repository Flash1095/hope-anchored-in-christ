The Spirit Poured Out on All Flesh

The Spirit Poured Out on All Flesh

Prophecy Summary

Joel promised a day when God would pour out His Spirit on all flesh — sons and daughters, old and young, even servants — accompanied by wonders and the great Day of the Lord. Everyone who calls on the Lord's name would be saved.

Fulfillment

Peter stood up at Pentecost and declared, This is what was uttered through Joel, as the Spirit fell on all kinds of people. The cosmic signs and final Day still await completion, so Pentecost is the glorious beginning, not the whole fulfilment.
