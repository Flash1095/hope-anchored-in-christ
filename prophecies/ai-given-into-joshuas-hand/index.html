Ai Given into Joshua's Hand

Ai Given into Joshua's Hand

Prophecy Summary

After the first defeat at Ai, the LORD tells Joshua not to fear, to take all the people of war, and that the king of Ai, his people, his city, and his land are given into Joshua's hand, with an ambush to be laid behind the city.

Fulfillment

Fulfilled when the ambush takes the city and its defenders are defeated (Joshua 8:19-21). The prediction is distinct from the Jericho oracle: a new campaign, a new city, and a different tactic.

Details
