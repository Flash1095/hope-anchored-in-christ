Zion to Babylon, and There Delivered

Prophecy
Zion to Babylon, and There Delivered
Prophet
Micah
Date Approx
c. 730 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Micah tells the daughter of Zion that she will go forth out of the city, dwell in the field, and go even to Babylon, and that there she will be delivered and redeemed from the hand of her enemies.
Fulfillment
Partially fulfilled in the Babylonian exile of Judah and the return under Cyrus's decree; the fuller deliverance and redemption it pictures is treated in the live collection as still unfolding.
