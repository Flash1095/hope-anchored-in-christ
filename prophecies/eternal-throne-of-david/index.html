The Eternal Throne of David

Prophecy
The Eternal Throne of David
Prophet
Nathan
Date Approx
c. 1000 BC
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Through Nathan, God promised David a son whose kingdom and throne would be established forever. No merely human king could carry a promise that large, and Israel learned to hope for a greater Son of David.
Fulfillment
Gabriel announced the fulfilment to Mary: her son Jesus would receive the throne of His father David and reign forever. Peter preached at Pentecost that the risen Christ now sits on that throne.
