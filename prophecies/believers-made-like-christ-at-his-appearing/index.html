Believers Made Like Christ at His Appearing

Prophecy
Believers Made Like Christ at His Appearing
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 90
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Believers are already sons of God, but what they shall be has not yet appeared; when Christ appears they shall be like him, for they shall see him as he is, and may then stand before him with confidence and not shame at his coming.
Fulfillment
Awaited: believers are not yet visibly like Christ, and the transformation is tied to his future appearing, when seeing him as he is makes them like him.
Fulfillment Status
Future
