When the Perfect Comes, the Partial Is Done Away

Prophecy
When the Perfect Comes, the Partial Is Done Away
Prophet
Paul
Date Approx
c. AD 55
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Paul says present knowledge and prophecy are partial, but when that which is perfect comes, that which is in part will be done away, and believers will then know face to face, even as they are known.
Fulfillment
Disputed and unfulfilled on any reading: interpreters disagree whether 'that which is perfect' means the completion of revelation, the maturity of the church, or the consummation at Christ's coming, so no fulfilment date or event is asserted here. The doubt is stated rather than resolved.
Fulfillment Status
Debated
