Judgments on the Surrounding Nations

Prophecy
Judgments on the Surrounding Nations
Prophet
Zephaniah
Date Approx
c. 630 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
In the same Day of the LORD, Zephaniah pronounced judgment on Judah's neighbours in turn: the Philistine cities Gaza, Ashkelon, Ashdod, and Ekron would be forsaken and rooted up; Moab and Ammon would become like Sodom and Gomorrah for reproaching God's people; Ethiopia would be slain by the sword; and Assyria, with proud Nineveh rejoicing and saying I am, and there is none beside me, would become a desolation for beasts to lie down in.
Fulfillment
The Philistine cities, Moab, and Ammon lost independence under Babylon and later powers, Ethiopia faced invasion from the north, and Nineveh fell in 612 BC, within a generation of Zephaniah, fulfilling the Assyrian oracle in dated history. The other nations faded as distinct peoples over the following centuries rather than in a single campaign, so the series is fulfilled in its broad national judgments. The pattern matches Zephaniah's claim that no neighbour escapes the Day.
