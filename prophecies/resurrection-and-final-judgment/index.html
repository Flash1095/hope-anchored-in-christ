Prophecy Summary
Daniel closes with the Old Testament's clearest resurrection promise: many who sleep in the dust will awake, some to everlasting life and some to shame and everlasting contempt. The wise will shine like the stars forever.
Fulfillment
Jesus echoes Daniel's words when He speaks of the resurrection of life and of judgment, and Paul builds his hope on the same promise. It still awaits the day of Christ's return, when it will be fulfilled in full.
