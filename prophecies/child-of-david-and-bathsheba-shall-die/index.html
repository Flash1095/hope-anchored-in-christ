The Child of David and Bathsheba Shall Die

Prophecy
The Child of David and Bathsheba Shall Die
Prophet
Nathan
Date Approx
c. 990 BC
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Nathan adds a distinct sentence to his judgment on David: because by this deed David gave great occasion to the enemies of the LORD to blaspheme, the child born to him shall surely die.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled within days: the child becomes sick and dies on the seventh day (2 Samuel 12:18-19), despite David's fasting and prayer.
Fulfillment Status
Fulfilled
