The Glory of the Second Temple

Prophecy
The Glory of the Second Temple
Prophet
Haggai
Date Approx
c. 520 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
To builders discouraged by the modest second temple, Haggai promised that its latter glory would be greater than the former glory of Solomon's temple, and that in this place God would give peace. The treasures of the nations would come in.
Fulfillment
Many Christians see the greater glory in the fact that Jesus Himself taught and was presented in that temple, while others look for a still-future glory. Because faithful readings differ, this is marked Debated — though no one disputes the promise ends in God's peace in that place.
