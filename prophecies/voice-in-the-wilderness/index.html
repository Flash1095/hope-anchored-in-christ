A Voice in the Wilderness

Prophecy
A Voice in the Wilderness
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Isaiah heard a voice crying in the wilderness to prepare the way of the Lord and make His paths straight. Comfort was coming to Jerusalem, and every valley would be lifted up.
Fulfillment
All four Gospels apply these words to John the Baptist, who prepared the way by preaching repentance in the wilderness. In Jesus, the glory of the Lord Isaiah promised was revealed to all flesh.
