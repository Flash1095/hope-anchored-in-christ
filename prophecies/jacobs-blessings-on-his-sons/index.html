Jacob's Blessings on His Sons

Prophecy
Jacob's Blessings on His Sons
Prophet
Jacob
Date Approx
c. 1850 BC
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Prophecy Summary
On his deathbed Jacob called his sons together and told them what would befall them in the last days, blessing each son in turn with a word fitted to his character and his descendants. This entry gathers the tribal blessings other than Judah's, which is already covered by the existing entry on the scepter: Reuben would lose the pre-eminence, Simeon and Levi would be scattered, Joseph would be fruitful and strong, and the remaining tribes each received their own foretold portion.
Fulfillment
The tribal histories broadly followed the words spoken over them: Reuben lost the birthright, which passed to Joseph's sons, and Simeon and Levi received no single tribal territory, with Levi scattered in cities throughout Israel. Joseph's tribes became the strong northern house, and the other tribes settled in the portions their blessings described. Because not every detail can be matched verse by verse to a single dated event, this entry is marked Partially Fulfilled rather than claiming more precision than the histories allow.
