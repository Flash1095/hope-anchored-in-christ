The New Temple and the Restored Land

Prophecy
The New Temple and the Restored Land
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 573 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
In a vision dated to the twenty-fifth year of exile, Ezekiel was shown a measured temple, its courts and gates, the return of God's glory, a river flowing from the temple that brings life wherever it goes, and the division of the land among the tribes. The vision ends with the city named The LORD Is There, and it includes a prince with a portion in the land. Its scale, river, and land divisions go beyond anything built after the exile.
Fulfillment
No temple matching every measurement and land division in Ezekiel 40 to 48 has been built in recorded history, and the second temple built after the exile did not follow this plan. Some readers expect a future literal temple, others read the vision as a symbolic picture of God's restored presence fulfilled in Christ and the church, and still others combine those readings. Because its nature and timing are debated, this entry is marked Debated.
