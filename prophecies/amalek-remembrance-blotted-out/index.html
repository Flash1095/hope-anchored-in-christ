Amalek's Remembrance Blotted Out

Prophecy
Amalek's Remembrance Blotted Out
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 1440 BC
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Prophecy Summary
After Israel defeats Amalek at Rephidim, the LORD tells Moses to write as a memorial that he will utterly put out the remembrance of Amalek from under heaven, and that the LORD will have war with Amalek from generation to generation.
Fulfillment
Partly fulfilled: Amalek is defeated again under Saul (1 Samuel 15) and David, and the last Amalekites are recorded as destroyed in Hezekiah's days (1 Chronicles 4:43), after which Amalek disappears as a distinct people. It is marked partially rather than fully fulfilled because Scripture itself preserves Amalek's name as a warning, so the blotting out is not a single closed event in the text.
