The Redeemer Comes to Zion

Prophecy
The Redeemer Comes to Zion
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
After picturing a people whose iniquities had separated them from God, Isaiah foretold that the Redeemer would come to Zion, to those who turn from transgression in Jacob, and that God's covenant and Spirit and words would not depart from them or their children. It is a promise of a personal Redeemer who comes to Zion itself, not merely of a general deliverance. The passage is distinct from the Servant entries, which do not cite Isaiah 59.
Fulfillment
The Redeemer came to Zion in Christ, whose coming to Jerusalem and whose work of redemption turned many in Jacob from transgression, and Paul cites this passage in Romans 11:26 of a future turning of Israel. The first coming has fulfilled the Redeemer's arrival, while the full turning of Jacob awaits its completion. The prophecy is therefore partially fulfilled.
