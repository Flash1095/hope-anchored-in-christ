Israel Restored After Judgment

Prophecy
Israel Restored After Judgment
Prophet
Hosea
Date Approx
c. 750 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
After warning of judgment for idolatry and broken covenant, Hosea promised that God would allure Israel back, speak tenderly to her, and restore her as a bride. The people would return to the LORD and to David their king in the latter days, and God would heal their backsliding and love them freely. This restoration runs through Hosea 2-3, is repeated in Hosea 11, and closes the book in Hosea 14 with healing and fruitfulness.
Fulfillment
A remnant did return from exile and Judah was restored after Babylon, so the return from judgment began in history, though the northern kingdom was never restored as a political nation in the same form. Hosea 3:5 is read in Christian tradition as pointing to return to the LORD through the Davidic king, Jesus, and to a gathering that continues in the Church. The promise is therefore partly fulfilled, with its full and final gathering still to come.
