Ishmael: A Great Nation, Twelve Princes, a Wild Man

Prophecy
Ishmael: A Great Nation, Twelve Princes, a Wild Man
Date Approx
c. 1900 BC
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Prophecy Summary
The angel of the LORD tells Hagar her seed will be multiplied beyond numbering, that her son Ishmael will be a wild man whose hand is against every man and every man's hand against him, dwelling in the presence of his brethren; God later tells Abraham that Ishmael will beget twelve princes and become a great nation.
Fulfillment
Partly fulfilled in the narrative itself: Genesis 25:13-16 names Ishmael's twelve sons as twelve princes of their tribes, and Genesis 25:17-18 records his descendants dwelling toward Egypt. The 'wild man' description is characterisation of a continuing pattern of conflict rather than a single closed event, so the entry is not marked simply fulfilled.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
