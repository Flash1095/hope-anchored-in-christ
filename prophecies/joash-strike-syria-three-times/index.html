Joash Shall Strike Syria Only Three Times

Prophecy
Joash Shall Strike Syria Only Three Times
Prophet
Elisha
Date Approx
c. 800 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
On his deathbed Elisha told King Joash to shoot an arrow eastward, calling it the arrow of the LORD's deliverance against Syria, and then to strike the ground with arrows. Joash struck only three times and stopped, and Elisha was angry, telling him that if he had struck five or six times he would have consumed Syria, but now he would strike Syria only three times.
Fulfillment
Joash recovered from Ben-hadad's son the cities that Hazael had taken from his father, defeating Syria three times, exactly as Elisha had said. The limited number was Joash's own doing: his half-hearted obedience set the limit on his victories. Elisha died soon after, and the prophecy stood as his last public word to a king.
