Agabus Foretells the Famine

Prophecy
Agabus Foretells the Famine
Prophet
Agabus
Date Approx
c. AD 44
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
In the church at Antioch, the prophet Agabus stood up and signified by the Spirit that there should be great dearth throughout all the world. The prophecy named a coming famine, not a local shortage, and it was given before the need arose. The disciples therefore determined to send relief to the brethren in Judaea, each according to his ability.
Fulfillment
Luke records that the famine came to pass in the days of Claudius Caesar, matching the reign in which the great famine is known from history. The Antioch church sent its gift by Barnabas and Saul to the elders in Jerusalem, turning the warning into practical help. The prediction and its recorded outcome together mark it as fulfilled.
