Judgment of the Earth and Death Swallowed Up

Prophecy
Judgment of the Earth and Death Swallowed Up
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Isaiah looked beyond any single nation to a day when the LORD would lay the earth waste, scatter its inhabitants, and punish the host of the high ones and the kings of the earth. On Mount Zion he would make a feast for all peoples, swallow up death in victory, and wipe away tears from all faces, and the dead would live and rise. The same section promises Leviathan's defeat and Israel's regathering, a song of trust in a strong city, and a vineyard kept by the LORD.
Fulfillment
The swallowing up of death is claimed by the New Testament in the resurrection of Christ and is applied to the final resurrection, as in 1 Corinthians 15:54, while the universal judgment and the feast for all peoples have not yet been seen in full. The prophecy therefore stands partially fulfilled: death's defeat has begun in Christ, and its completion awaits the last day. It is distinct from the Daniel resurrection entry, which does not cite Isaiah 25.
