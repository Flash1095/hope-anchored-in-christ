Your House Is Left Desolate Until You Say, Blessed Is He That Cometh

Prophecy
Your House Is Left Desolate Until You Say, Blessed Is He That Cometh
Prophet
Jesus
Date Approx
c. AD 30
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
Jesus predicts that Jerusalem's house is left desolate and that its people will not see him again until they say, 'Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord.'
Fulfillment
Partially fulfilled: Jerusalem was left desolate and the temple destroyed within a generation; the future acknowledgment of Christ by Jerusalem, implied by 'until', is still awaited.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
