Joseph's Dreams of Bowing Down

Prophecy
Joseph's Dreams of Bowing Down
Prophet
Joseph
Date Approx
c. 1900 BC
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Prophecy Summary
As a young man Joseph dreamed that his sheaf stood upright while his brothers' sheaves bowed to it, and then that the sun, the moon, and eleven stars bowed down to him. His brothers hated him for the dreams, and even his father rebuked him, asking whether his mother and brothers would indeed come and bow down to him.
Fulfillment
Years later in Egypt, Joseph's brothers came before the governor of the land and bowed with their faces to the earth, first when they came to buy grain and again when they brought Benjamin. Jacob himself came down to Egypt and was reunited with his son, and the family bowed before the brother they had once sold. The dreams were fulfilled in stages, not in a single moment, and Joseph recognised them as God's doing.
