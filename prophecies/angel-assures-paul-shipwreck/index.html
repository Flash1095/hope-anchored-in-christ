The Angel Assures Paul Before the Shipwreck

Prophecy
The Angel Assures Paul Before the Shipwreck
Prophet
Angel of God
Date Approx
c. AD 59
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
On Paul's voyage to Rome, after many days without sun or stars and with all hope of being saved taken away, an angel of God stood by Paul at night. The angel told him not to fear, for he must be brought before Caesar, and that God had given him all those sailing with him. Paul was assured that there would be no loss of any man's life, though the ship itself would be lost.
Fulfillment
The ship ran aground and broke apart, just as the angel had said the vessel would be lost. Yet all two hundred and seventy-six people aboard reached land safely, some on boards and some on broken pieces of the ship. The prediction was fulfilled in both halves, the loss of the ship and the preservation of every life.
