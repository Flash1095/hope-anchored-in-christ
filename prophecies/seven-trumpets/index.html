The Seven Trumpets

Prophecy
The Seven Trumpets
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Seven angels were given seven trumpets, and as each sounded a further woe fell: hail and fire mingled with blood upon the earth, a burning mountain cast into the sea, a star called Wormwood poisoning the waters, darkness over a third of sun, moon and stars, locusts from the abyss tormenting the unsealed, and a vast army loosing death upon a third of mankind. An eagle's warning pronounced woe upon the last three trumpets, and at the seventh trumpet loud voices proclaimed that the kingdom of this world had become the kingdom of our Lord and of his Christ. Even under these judgments, those who remained did not repent of their idolatry and murder, sorcery, immorality and theft.
Fulfillment
As with the seals, the timing and referents of the trumpets are debated — historical, futurist, and symbolic readings all claim support — and this entry records the sequence as given without assigning each trumpet to a dated historical event. The stated purpose is clear in the text: partial, warning judgments calling the world to repentance before the final kingdom is proclaimed. The two witnesses of Revelation 11, who prophesy during the trumpet period, are treated in their own entry.
