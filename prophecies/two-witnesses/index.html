The Two Witnesses

Prophecy
The Two Witnesses
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Two witnesses, clothed in sackcloth, will prophesy for 1,260 days with power to shut heaven so it does not rain, turn waters to blood, and strike the earth with plagues as often as they wish. When they have finished their testimony, the beast from the abyss will kill them, their bodies will lie in the street of the great city for three and a half days while the earth rejoices, and then the breath of life from God will enter them and they will stand up and ascend to heaven in a cloud. Their resurrection and ascension will be followed by a great earthquake in which a tenth of the city falls.
Fulfillment
The identity of the two witnesses is debated — Moses and Elijah are commonly suggested because of the plagues and the shutting of heaven, while others read them as symbolic of the witnessing church — and the timing, whether past, future, or both, is likewise debated. The text is clear that their ministry, death, public vindication, and the earthquake that follows are future to John's vision and are meant as a sign that God's testimony cannot finally be silenced. This entry therefore records the prophecy without settling the witnesses' identities.
