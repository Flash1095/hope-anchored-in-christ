Water in the Valley and Victory Over Moab

Prophecy
Water in the Valley and Victory Over Moab
Prophet
Elisha
Date Approx
c. 850 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
When the kings of Israel, Judah, and Edom marched against rebellious Moab and found no water for their armies, Elisha told them to fill the valley with ditches, promising that they would see neither wind nor rain, yet the valley would be filled with water, and that the LORD would also deliver Moab into their hand.
Fulfillment
Water flowed into the valley at the time of the morning sacrifice, the Moabites mistook the red water for blood and rushed to spoil, and Israel struck them down and ravaged their land. The victory was real but incomplete: when the king of Moab sacrificed his eldest son on the wall, great indignation came upon Israel and they withdrew. The entry is therefore marked Partially Fulfilled, honouring both the water and the victory and the withdrawal that followed.
