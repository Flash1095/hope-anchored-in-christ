The Beast and the False Prophet Cast into the Lake of Fire

Prophecy
The Beast and the False Prophet Cast into the Lake of Fire
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
The beast and the kings of the earth with their armies gather to make war against him who sits on the white horse and his army; the beast is taken, and with him the false prophet who worked miracles before him and deceived those who received the mark and worshipped the image, and both are cast alive into a lake of fire burning with brimstone, while the remnant are slain by the sword from the rider's mouth.
Fulfillment
Awaited as the end of the beast's reign: the identities of the beast and false prophet are debated, but their capture and casting into the lake of fire are presented as a future act of the returning King, after which the devil's own casting follows in the thousand-years sequence.
