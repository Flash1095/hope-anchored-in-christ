Paul Sees the Ephesian Elders No More

Paul Sees the Ephesian Elders No More

Prophecy Summary

Farewelling the Ephesian elders at Miletus, Paul says he knows that all among whom he has preached the kingdom of God will see his face no more.

Fulfillment

Fulfilled within the Acts narrative: Paul goes on to Jerusalem, arrest, and Rome, and the narrative records no return to Ephesus. Later tradition about a possible release and return is outside Acts and is not treated here as overturning the narrative fulfilment.
