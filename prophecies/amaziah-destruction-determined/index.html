God Has Determined to Destroy Amaziah

Prophecy
God Has Determined to Destroy Amaziah
Date Approx
c. 790 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
After Amaziah brought home the gods of Edom and worshipped them, and refused the prophet's counsel, the prophet declared that God had determined to destroy him.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in Amaziah's downfall: defeated and captured by Joash of Israel at Beth-shemesh, Jerusalem's wall broken down and its treasures taken, and after he turned away from following the LORD a conspiracy was made against him in Jerusalem and he was slain at Lachish.
