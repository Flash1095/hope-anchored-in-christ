Judgment on Sidon

Prophecy
Judgment on Sidon
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 586 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
God said he would send pestilence and blood into Sidon's streets and execute judgments in her midst. Sidon would then know that he is the LORD, and Israel would no longer suffer a pricking brier from a neighbour that despised her.
Fulfillment
Sidon suffered under Persian punishment after its revolt and later passed under Alexander and successive empires, losing the independent power it held in Ezekiel's day. Its judgment, unlike Tyre's, is stated as punishment and humbling rather than permanent erasure, and that humbling is what the ancient conquests show.
