Deliverance for Starving Samaria

Prophecy
Deliverance for Starving Samaria
Prophet
Elisha
Date Approx
c. 850 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
While Ben-hadad besieged Samaria and famine had driven food prices to unthinkable heights, Elisha announced that by tomorrow about that time a measure of fine flour and two measures of barley would each sell for a shekel at the gate of Samaria. A royal officer scoffed that even if the LORD made windows in heaven this could not happen, and Elisha told him he would see it with his eyes but not eat of it.
Fulfillment
That night the LORD made the Syrian army hear the sound of chariots and horses, and they fled, leaving their camp behind, so that the starving city plundered it and food prices fell to exactly what Elisha had said. The scoffing officer was trampled to death in the gate as the hungry crowd rushed out, seeing the plenty but not eating of it. The precision of price, place, and time makes this one of the most exactly dated predictions in Kings.
