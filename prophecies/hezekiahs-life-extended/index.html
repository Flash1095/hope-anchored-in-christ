Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
When Hezekiah was sick unto death, Isaiah first told him to set his house in order, for he would die and not live. After Hezekiah prayed with tears, the word of the LORD came again through Isaiah: Hezekiah would live fifteen more years, and both he and Jerusalem would be delivered from the hand of the king of Assyria. The sign given was the shadow going back ten degrees on the dial of Ahaz.
Fulfillment
Hezekiah recovered, lived on, and Jerusalem was delivered from Sennacherib, as the narrative in Isaiah 38 and 2 Kings 20 records. The fifteen years and the deliverance from Assyria both came to pass within Hezekiah's lifetime. This is a dated, personal prophecy with a recorded fulfilment in Scripture itself.
