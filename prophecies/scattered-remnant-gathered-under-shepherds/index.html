The Scattered Remnant Gathered Under Shepherds

Prophecy Summary

Woe to shepherds who scatter God's flock: he will gather the remnant out of all countries where he drove them, bring them back to their folds, and set shepherds over them who will feed them so they fear no more.

Fulfillment

The return from Babylon gathered only part of the scattered remnant, and the fear-free shepherding is not yet complete; this entry uses Jeremiah 23:1-4 and 23:7-8 only, leaving the Righteous Branch verses already live untouched.
