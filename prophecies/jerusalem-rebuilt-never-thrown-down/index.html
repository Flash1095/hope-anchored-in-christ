Jerusalem Rebuilt and Never Thrown Down

Prophecy
Jerusalem Rebuilt and Never Thrown Down
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 587 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
The city will be rebuilt to the LORD from the Tower of Hananeel to the Corner Gate, the measuring line will go out to Gareb and Goath, the whole valley of dead bodies and ashes will be holy, and the city will never again be plucked up or thrown down.
Fulfillment
Jerusalem was rebuilt after the exile along lines recalling this measure, but it was thrown down again in AD 70, so the never-thrown-down clause is not literally complete; this entry is partial and distinct from the general return entry already live.
