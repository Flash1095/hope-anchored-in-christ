Prophecy Summary
The psalm celebrates a king in majesty, girded with glory and honour, whose throne is for ever and ever and whose sceptre is a right sceptre. His bride, in gold of Ophir, is brought to him in joy, and the peoples are called to honour him. The song looks beyond any one royal wedding to a King whose reign and marriage are everlasting.
Fulfillment
Hebrews applies the words 'Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever' directly to the Son, identifying Christ as the anointed King of the psalm. The bridal picture is still unfolding in Scripture, reaching toward the marriage supper of the Lamb in Revelation. The entry is therefore partially fulfilled, with the King's identity settled in Christ and the wedding consummation still to come.
