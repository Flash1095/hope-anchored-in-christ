Jehu's Dynasty to the Fourth Generation

Jehu's Dynasty to the Fourth Generation

Prophecy Summary

After Jehu carried out judgment on the house of Ahab, the LORD told Jehu that because he had done well in executing what was in God's heart against Ahab's house, his sons would sit on the throne of Israel to the fourth generation. The promise was a reward for obedience in a bloody commission, and it set a precise limit on how long Jehu's line would reign.

Fulfillment

Jehu was followed by his son Jehoahaz, then Jehoash, then Jeroboam the second, and then Zechariah, the fourth generation, after which Shallum struck Zechariah down. The narrator of Kings explicitly notes that this was the fulfilment of the word the LORD had spoken to Jehu. No human prophet is named as mediator of this word in the text, so the speaker is recorded as the LORD.
