Balaam's Oracles on the Nations

Balaam's Oracles on the Nations

Prophecy Summary

Hired by Balak to curse Israel, Balaam found he could only bless them, and his oracles went on to foretell the fates of the surrounding nations. Beyond the star out of Jacob, which is already covered by an existing entry, Balaam said that Moab and Edom would be dispossessed, Amalek would perish, the Kenites would be carried away captive, and ships from Kittim would afflict Asshur and Eber.

Fulfillment

Moab was subdued under David and Edom was made subject to Israel, while Amalek was struck down in Saul's and David's campaigns until the people faded from history. The Kenite captivity and the Kittim-Asshur sayings are harder to pin to single dated events, and scholars debate whether they point to Assyrian, Greek, or Roman powers. This entry is therefore marked Partially Fulfilled, covering the nation oracles other than the star, which remains in its own existing entry.
