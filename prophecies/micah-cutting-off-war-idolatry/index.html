War and Idolatry Cut Off in That Day

Prophecy
War and Idolatry Cut Off in That Day
Prophet
Micah
Date Approx
c. 730 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Micah predicts that in that day God will cut off horses and destroy chariots, cut off the cities of the land and throw down its strongholds, cut off witchcrafts, soothsayers, graven images, standing images, and groves, and execute vengeance on the heathen.
Fulfillment
Partially fulfilled in the end of Judah's reliance on fortified cities and idolatry after the exile; the complete removal of war and idolatry it pictures is still awaited.
