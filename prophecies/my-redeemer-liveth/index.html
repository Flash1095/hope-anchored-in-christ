I Know That My Redeemer Liveth

I Know That My Redeemer Liveth

Prophecy Summary

Job, in the midst of suffering and loss, declared that his Redeemer lives and that at the last He will stand upon the earth. He said that though his body should be destroyed, yet in his flesh he would see God, and that his eyes would behold Him for himself. This was a personal hope of vindication and life beyond death, spoken long before Israel's fuller revelation of resurrection.

Fulfillment

Scripture later reveals the Redeemer as the risen Christ, the firstfruits of those who sleep, through whom believers also rise. Job's hope is therefore fulfilled in kind in Christ's resurrection, while its full personal completion for Job himself still awaits the general resurrection. The entry is therefore marked as partially fulfilled rather than claiming Job's own rising has already taken place.
