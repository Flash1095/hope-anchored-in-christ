Balaam: Israel's King Higher Than Agag

Prophecy
Balaam: Israel's King Higher Than Agag
Prophet
Balaam
Date Approx
c. 1400 BC
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Prophecy Summary
In his third oracle Balaam says Israel's seed will be in many waters, his king will be higher than Agag, his kingdom will be exalted, God brought him out of Egypt, he will eat up the nations his enemies, and he lies down as a lion that none will stir up, with blessing on those who bless him and cursing on those who curse him.
Fulfillment
Debated, stated as such: the oracle's king is not named, so whether 'higher than Agag' points to Saul's defeat of Agag, King of Amalek (1 Samuel 15), to David's wider victories, or to a later king is not settled by the passage itself. The blessing-and-cursing line repeats the Abrahamic promise already covered by a live row and is not counted here as a new prediction; this entry covers only the king-above-Agag exaltation, which the live Balaam rows do not include, since they cite Numbers 23:7-10, 23:18-24 and 24:20-24 but not 24:7-9.
