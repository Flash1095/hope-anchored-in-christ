The Seven Seals

Prophecy
The Seven Seals
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
John saw the Lamb open a sealed scroll, and with each of the first six seals a judgment was loosed upon the earth: conquest, war, famine, death, the cry of martyrs under the altar, and cosmic upheaval that made kings and common people hide from the wrath of the Lamb. Between the sixth and seventh seals, God's servants were sealed for protection before the seventh seal brought silence in heaven for about half an hour. The sequence presents God's judgments unfolding in order, with his people marked out and preserved through them.
Fulfillment
Interpreters debate whether the seals describe events already unfolding through history, a future tribulation period, or both in pattern and climax, and this entry does not settle that timing question. The martyrs' cry and the call for endurance show the seals concern real suffering and real divine justice, not mere symbols with no referent. The sealing of God's servants is treated in its own entry, the Sealed of Israel, so this entry covers the judgment sequence itself.
