A Son Promised to Sarah: Isaac

A Son Promised to Sarah: Isaac

Prophecy Summary

When Abraham and Sarah were far past childbearing age, God promised that Sarah herself would bear a son, that his name would be Isaac, and that God's covenant would be established with him. The promise was given first in Genesis 17 and repeated when the visitors came to Mamre, with the added word that Sarah would have a son at the set time in the next year.

Fulfillment

Sarah conceived and bore Isaac at the set time God had spoken, exactly as Genesis 21 records, and the covenant line continued through Isaac rather than through Ishmael. Ishmael was also blessed and promised twelve princes, but the covenant promise itself was kept through Isaac, just as the prophecy had said.
