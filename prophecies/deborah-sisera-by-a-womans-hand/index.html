Deborah: Sisera by a Woman's Hand

Deborah: Sisera by a Woman's Hand

Prophecy Summary

When Barak refused to go into battle without her, Deborah the prophetess told him that the LORD would sell Sisera into the hand of a woman, and that the honour of the victory would not be his. The word was spoken before the battle by the Kishon, where Sisera's nine hundred iron chariots were expected to decide the day.

Fulfillment

The LORD routed Sisera's army, Sisera fled on foot, and Jael, the wife of Heber the Kenite, drove a tent peg through his temple while he slept in her tent. Deborah and Barak's song afterwards blessed Jael above women in the tent, naming her as the one by whose hand the enemy commander fell. This is flagged as a borderline personal prediction, included because it decided a national battle and reversed the expected glory of war, as Scripture itself stresses.
