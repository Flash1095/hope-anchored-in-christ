Ephraim Broken Within Sixty-Five Years

Prophecy
Ephraim Broken Within Sixty-Five Years
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 735 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Within sixty-five years Ephraim will be broken so that it is no longer a people, its head Samaria and Samaria's head the son of Remaliah notwithstanding.
Fulfillment
Assyria conquered Samaria in 722 BC and deported its people, and later Assyrian resettlement broke Ephraim as a distinct people; the exact end-point of the sixty-five-year count is debated, so this is marked partial rather than exact.
