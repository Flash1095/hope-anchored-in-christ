Ahijah: The Kingdom Torn from Solomon's Son

Prophecy
Ahijah: The Kingdom Torn from Solomon's Son
Prophet
Ahijah
Date Approx
c. 930 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Ahijah the Shilonite met Jeroboam outside Jerusalem, tore his new cloak into twelve pieces, and gave Jeroboam ten, saying that the LORD would tear the kingdom out of Solomon's hand and give ten tribes to Jeroboam. One tribe would be left to Solomon's son for David's sake and for Jerusalem's sake, so that David would always have a lamp before the LORD there.
Fulfillment
When Rehoboam refused to lighten the people's burden, ten tribes rebelled and made Jeroboam king, while Judah and Benjamin stayed with Rehoboam at Jerusalem. The division lasted for the rest of the kingdom's history and was never healed, exactly as Ahijah had described it. The promise that David's lamp would remain in Jerusalem held alongside the judgment, and is part of why this entry does not duplicate the existing Eternal Throne entry.
