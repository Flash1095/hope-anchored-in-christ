Judgment on Ammon

Prophecy
Judgment on Ammon
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 590–570 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
Because Ammon rejoiced over the desecration of the sanctuary and the desolation of Israel, God said Ammon would be handed over to the people of the east. Its capital Rabbah would become a desolate place, and Ammon would no longer be remembered as a nation among the peoples.
Fulfillment
Ammon lost its independence in the Babylonian and Persian periods and was absorbed by surrounding powers, including Arab tribes who settled its territory. By the later ancient period Ammon no longer existed as a distinct nation, and Rabbah continued under other rulers and names.
