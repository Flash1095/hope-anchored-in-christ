The House of Eli Cut Off

Prophecy Summary

A man of God came to Eli the priest and charged him with honouring his wicked sons above God, then declared that the LORD would cut off the strength of Eli's house so that no old man would remain in it. Eli's two sons would die on the same day as a sign, a faithful priest would be raised up instead, and Eli's surviving descendants would come begging for a piece of silver and a morsel of bread.

Fulfillment

Hophni and Phinehas died on the same day at Aphek, the ark was captured, and Eli himself died on hearing the news. Generations later Solomon removed Abiathar, the last priest of Eli's line, from the priesthood and put Zadok in his place, fulfilling the word about the faithful priest. The man of God is unnamed in the text, so the speaker is recorded as such rather than given an invented name.
