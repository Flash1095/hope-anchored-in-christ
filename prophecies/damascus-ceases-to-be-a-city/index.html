Damascus Ceases to Be a City

Damascus Ceases to Be a City

Prophecy Summary

Isaiah foretold that Damascus would be taken away from being a city and become a ruinous heap, and that the fortress of Ephraim and the kingdom of Damascus would fail together. The glory of Jacob would be made thin in the same judgment, as Israel's northern kingdom shared Syria's fate. It is a prophecy of the end of Damascus as an Aramean capital and of Ephraim as a kingdom.

Fulfillment

Damascus was conquered by Assyria under Tiglath-Pileser III in 732 BC, its king was killed, and Aram ceased to be an independent power, while Ephraim fell to Assyria in 722 BC. Damascus as a place was later inhabited again, so the prophecy is best read as fulfilled in the end of its kingdom and capital status, not in permanent uninhabitation. It is therefore marked as partially fulfilled, with its political sense fulfilled in full.
