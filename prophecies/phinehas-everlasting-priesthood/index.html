Phinehas: A Covenant of Everlasting Priesthood

Prophecy
Phinehas: A Covenant of Everlasting Priesthood
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 1400 BC
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Prophecy Summary
After Phinehas turns away God's wrath in the matter of Baal-peor, God gives him his covenant of peace, and says he and his seed after him will have the covenant of an everlasting priesthood, because he was zealous for his God and made atonement for Israel.
Fulfillment
Partly fulfilled: the priesthood continues through Phinehas and his son Eleazar's line, notably in the Zadokite line, but the priesthood's later disruption and the question of how far 'everlasting' extends beyond that line mean the promise is not marked as simply closed.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
