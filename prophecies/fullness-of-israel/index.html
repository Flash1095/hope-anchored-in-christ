The Fullness of Israel

Prophecy
The Fullness of Israel
Prophet
Paul
Date Approx
c. AD 57
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Paul revealed a mystery: a partial hardening has come upon Israel until the fullness of the Gentiles has come in, and then all Israel will be saved, as it is written that the Deliverer will come out of Zion and turn away ungodliness from Jacob. This is God's covenant promise that Israel's present stumbling is neither total nor final, and that the ingathering of the Gentiles and the future salvation of Israel belong together in one redemptive plan. The prophecy looks beyond the present age to a future turning of Israel to her Messiah.
Fulfillment
Fulfillment is debated: some interpreters understand all Israel as ethnic Israel turning to Christ in a future national conversion, others as the elect remnant of Israel gathered through the ages together with believing Gentiles, and others as the whole people of God. What is not debated in the passage is that God has not cast away his people and that their future restoration is promised. The entry is kept distinct from the Valley of Dry Bones entry, which is Ezekiel's vision of national restoration cited with Romans 11:15; Romans 11:25-27 adds Paul's distinct mystery of the Gentile fullness preceding Israel's salvation.
