Tyre's Downfall

Prophecy
Tyre's Downfall
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Isaiah foretold that Tyre, the crowning city whose merchants were princes and whose traffickers were the honourable of the earth, would be laid waste and forgotten for seventy years, as the days of one king. Its ships of Tarshish would howl, its harbour would fall silent, and after seventy years Tyre would return to its hire and trade with all the kingdoms of the earth. The oracle covers both Tyre and its daughter Sidon.
Fulfillment
Tyre was besieged for thirteen years by Nebuchadnezzar, lost its island stronghold to Alexander the Great in 332 BC when he built a causeway to it, and never regained its former imperial glory as a merchant empire. A modern city called Tyre exists on the site, so a literal reading of permanent uninhabitation is not claimed here, and the entry is marked partially fulfilled. Its fall as the crowning merchant power, however, is a matter of history.
