The One Hundred and Twenty Years

Prophecy
The One Hundred and Twenty Years
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Prophecy Summary
Before the Flood, the LORD says his Spirit will not always strive with man, and that man's days will be one hundred and twenty years.
Fulfillment
Debated reading, stated as such: some read the 120 years as the interval before the Flood judgment, others as a new limit on human lifespan. Genesis gives no explicit resolution, and later lifespans in Genesis decline gradually rather than stopping at 120, so neither reading is presented as settled.
