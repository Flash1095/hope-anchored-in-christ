Moses' Blessings on the Tribes

Prophecy
Moses' Blessings on the Tribes
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 1400 BC
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Prophecy Summary
Before his death Moses blesses each tribe in turn: Reuben to live and not die, Judah to be brought to his people, Levi to bear Urim and Thummim and teach Israel, Benjamin to dwell safely by the LORD, Joseph blessed with precious things of heaven and earth, Zebulun and Issachar rejoicing in going out and in tents, Gad enlarged dwelling as a lion, Dan a lion's whelp leaping from Bashan, Naphtali satisfied with favour, and Asher blessed with children and dipping his foot in oil. Simeon receives no separate blessing in this chapter, and none is invented for him.
Fulfillment
Partly fulfilled, tribe by tribe rather than as a single event: Levi's teaching and priestly role, Joseph's fruitfulness in Ephraim and Manasseh, and Gad's and Dan's martial descriptions find counterparts in the settlement and later history, while several blessings are open-ended characterisations rather than dated events, so the grouped entry is not marked simply fulfilled. Grouped as one entry, mirroring the live row that groups Jacob's blessings on his sons.
