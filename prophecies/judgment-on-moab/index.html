Judgment on Moab

Prophecy
Judgment on Moab
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 590–570 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
Because Moab said Judah had become like all other nations, God said Moab's frontier cities would be opened to invaders from the east. Moab would be judged, its pride brought low, and its people would know that the LORD had spoken.
Fulfillment
Moab was overrun in the Babylonian period and later absorbed by Arab and Nabataean peoples, losing its independent kingdom. The Moabites ceased to exist as a distinct people in antiquity, while their former territory passed to other rulers.
