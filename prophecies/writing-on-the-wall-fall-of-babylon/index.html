The Writing on the Wall: The Fall of Babylon

Prophecy
The Writing on the Wall: The Fall of Babylon
Prophet
Daniel
Date Approx
539 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
At Belshazzar's feast, a hand wrote MENE, MENE, TEKEL, UPHARSIN on the palace wall, and Daniel explained that God had numbered Belshazzar's kingdom and finished it. The king had been weighed in the balances and found wanting, and his kingdom would be divided and given to the Medes and Persians. That same night the prophecy pointed to the end of Babylonian rule.
Fulfillment
Daniel 5 reports that Belshazzar was slain that very night, and Babylon fell to the Medes and Persians under Cyrus in 539 BC. The sudden fall of the Babylonian kingdom matches the writing's warning that the kingdom was finished and divided. This entry is marked Fulfilled on the strength of the biblical account and the historically attested fall of Babylon in 539 BC.
