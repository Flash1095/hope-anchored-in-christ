Deliverance from the Assyrian Invader

Prophecy Summary

Micah predicts that when the Assyrian comes into the land and treads in the palaces, the promised ruler will be their peace, seven shepherds and eight principal men will be raised against the invader, and they will waste the land of Assyria with the sword and deliver the people from the Assyrian within their borders.

Fulfillment

Partially fulfilled in the deliverance of Jerusalem from Sennacherib's invasion (701 BC), when the Assyrian army was turned back; the passage's fuller wasting of Assyria was completed only later with Assyria's fall, and its peace-ruler language points beyond that event.
