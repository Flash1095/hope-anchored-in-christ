The Highway Home: Return from Exile

Prophecy
The Highway Home: Return from Exile
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Isaiah foretold that God would make a way in the wilderness and rivers in the desert for his people to come home, that he would not forsake them, and that the ransomed of the LORD would return to Zion with singing and everlasting joy. Kings would be nursing fathers and queens nursing mothers to them, and the captives would be released and fed on the way. This entry groups the return and highway oracles of Isaiah 43, 49:8-26, and 51-52 into one distinct prediction of the homecoming itself.
Fulfillment
Under Cyrus's decree the exiles did return to Jerusalem in waves, rebuild the temple, and re-establish worship, as Ezra and Nehemiah record. Not all exiles returned, and the full singing homecoming with everlasting joy has not yet been seen in full, so the prophecy is partially fulfilled: the historical return began it, and its complete joy awaits the final gathering. It is distinct from the Light to the Gentiles entry, which cites only Isaiah 49:6.
