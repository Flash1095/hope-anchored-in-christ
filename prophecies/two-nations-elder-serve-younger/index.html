Two Nations: The Elder Shall Serve the Younger

Prophecy
Two Nations: The Elder Shall Serve the Younger
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 1900 BC
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Prophecy Summary
When Rebekah asked the LORD about the twins struggling within her, He told her that two nations were in her womb, two peoples would be separated, and the elder would serve the younger. The word was given before either child had done good or evil, and it set the course of the two peoples who would come from Jacob and Esau.
Fulfillment
Jacob became Israel and Esau became Edom, and under David Edom was made subject to Israel, with garrisons placed throughout the land. Edom later revolted and broke the yoke in the days of King Joram, just as Isaac's later blessing had also foreseen. Paul cites the oracle in Romans 9 as a word of God's sovereign choice, not of human merit.
