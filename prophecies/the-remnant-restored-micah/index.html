The Remnant Restored

Prophecy
The Remnant Restored
Prophet
Micah
Date Approx
c. 730 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Micah promised that God will assemble the remnant of His flock, the lame and the outcast, and make them a strong nation, with the LORD reigning over them in Mount Zion. That remnant will be among the nations like dew from the LORD and like a lion among beasts, no longer dependent on human help. The book closes with God pardoning iniquity, passing by transgression, casting sins into the depths of the sea, and keeping His mercy promised to Abraham and Jacob.
Fulfillment
A remnant returned from Babylon and rebuilt Jerusalem and the temple, so the gathering began in history, and the Church has read the dew and the pardon as describing God's people gathered from every nation through Christ. The remnant has not yet become the unchallenged strong nation in Zion that Micah pictures in full, and the final reign over the gathered flock awaits consummation. The promise is therefore partly fulfilled, begun in return and in Christ, and awaiting completion.
