The Calf of Beth-aven Carried to Assyria

Prophecy
The Calf of Beth-aven Carried to Assyria
Prophet
Hosea
Date Approx
c. 750 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Hosea predicts that the inhabitants of Samaria will mourn over the calves of Beth-aven, the calf itself will be carried to Assyria as a present to King Jareb, Samaria's king will be cut off like foam on water, and the high places of Aven will be destroyed and overgrown.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in the fall of Samaria and the Assyrian removal of Israel's idolatrous cult objects; the northern monarchy ended and its calf worship came to an end.
