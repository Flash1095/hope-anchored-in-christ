Ransom from the Grave and Death

Prophecy
Ransom from the Grave and Death
Prophet
Hosea
Date Approx
c. 750 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Hosea declares that God will ransom his people from the power of the grave and redeem them from death, saying to death that he will be its plagues and to the grave that he will be its destruction.
Fulfillment
Partially fulfilled in Christ's resurrection, which the New Testament connects with this passage (1 Corinthians 15:54-55); the final defeat of death for all God's people is still awaited.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
