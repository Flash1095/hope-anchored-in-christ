Ammon Judged

Prophecy
Ammon Judged
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 600 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Jeremiah asked why Milcom, the god of Ammon, possessed Gad's inheritance, and foretold that an alarm of war would be heard in Rabbah of the Ammonites, that it would become a desolate heap, and that its daughters would be burned with fire. Israel would then be heir to those who had been his heirs, and Ammon's king and princes would go into captivity. The oracle ends with a promise that God would bring again the captivity of Ammon in the latter days.
Fulfillment
Rabbah was conquered by Babylon, Ammon lost its independence, and the Ammonites as a distinct nation faded from history, with their territory absorbed by later powers. The desolation of Rabbah and the captivity of its leadership match the Babylonian conquest. The latter-days restoration strand has no clear historical fulfilment identified, so the entry is marked partially fulfilled.
