Miseries Coming on the Oppressive Rich

Prophecy
Miseries Coming on the Oppressive Rich
Prophet
James
Date Approx
c. AD 48
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Rich oppressors are told to weep and howl for the miseries that shall come upon them: their riches are corrupted, their rusted gold and silver will witness against them and eat their flesh like fire, and they have heaped up treasure for the last days while defrauding their labourers and living in wanton pleasure.
Fulfillment
Partially seen whenever ill-gotten wealth decays and oppressors face reckoning, and in the repeated falls of the powerful; the full reckoning at the judgment is still awaited, so the prediction is not yet exhausted.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
