The Desolation of Egypt

Prophecy
The Desolation of Egypt
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 587–571 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
Ezekiel announced that Egypt would become desolate from Migdol to Syene, its people scattered among the nations for forty years, and that it would never again rule over other nations. Nebuchadnezzar would be given Egypt as wages for his long siege of Tyre, and Pharaoh's power would be broken. The oracles picture Egypt's pride, its allies, and its great river-god claims all brought low.
Fulfillment
Nebuchadnezzar campaigned against Egypt in 568 BC, and Egypt never again became an empire ruling other nations, passing instead under Persian, Greek, and Roman rule. Scholars debate the forty-year desolation, since no clear forty-year period of total emptiness is documented, so this entry is marked Partially Fulfilled. The lasting loss of imperial dominance is clear, while the precise desolation period remains debated.
