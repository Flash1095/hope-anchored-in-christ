A New Heart and a New Spirit

Prophecy
A New Heart and a New Spirit
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 590–570 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
God promised to gather his people from the nations, sprinkle clean water on them, and give them a new heart and a new spirit. He would remove the heart of stone and put his Spirit within them, so that they would walk in his statutes and keep his judgments. The promise joins cleansing, return to the land, and inward renewal in one covenant gift.
Fulfillment
The return from Babylonian exile began the gathering, and the New Testament presents the gift of the Holy Spirit to believers as the inward renewal this promise describes. The full national and spiritual completion of the promise is still debated among interpreters, with some seeing its fulfillment inaugurated at Pentecost and completed in the future. This entry is therefore marked Partially Fulfilled.
