The Worthless Shepherd

Prophecy
The Worthless Shepherd
Prophet
Zechariah
Date Approx
c. 5th century BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
After the true shepherd was rejected and valued at thirty pieces of silver, Zechariah was told to take the implements of a foolish shepherd, for God will raise up a shepherd in the land who will not visit the cut off, seek the young, heal the broken, or feed the standing, but will eat the flesh of the fat and tear their claws. Woe is then pronounced on the idol shepherd who leaves the flock: the sword will be upon his arm and his right eye, his arm will be clean dried up, and his right eye utterly darkened.
Fulfillment
The identity of this shepherd is debated, and no consensus identification with a single historical ruler has been established: proposals have included bad rulers in the intertestamental period, Roman oppressors, and a future oppressive ruler or antichrist figure. Because the referent is debated, and the judgment on the shepherd has no agreed dated fulfillment, this entry is marked Debated, with the text's own warning about exploitative shepherds stated plainly and no identification asserted as certain.
