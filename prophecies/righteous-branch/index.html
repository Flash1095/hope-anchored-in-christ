The Righteous Branch

Prophecy
The Righteous Branch
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 600 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
As Judah's kings failed, Jeremiah promised a righteous Branch from David who would reign wisely, do justice, and be called The Lord Our Righteousness. In His days Judah would be saved and Israel live in safety.
Fulfillment
Jesus, David's descendant, is that righteous King — and remarkably, He does not only rule righteously, He becomes our righteousness. Paul celebrates that in Christ we have the very righteousness Jeremiah named.
