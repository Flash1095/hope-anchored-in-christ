Moses and Aaron Barred from the Land

Prophecy
Moses and Aaron Barred from the Land
Date Approx
c. 1400 BC
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Prophecy Summary
Because Moses and Aaron did not sanctify God in the eyes of Israel at the waters of Meribah, God tells them they will not bring the congregation into the land he has given them.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in Deuteronomy 34:1-5: Moses views the land from Mount Nebo and dies there in the land of Moab, and does not cross over; Deuteronomy 32:48-52 restates the same sentence and is included as a retelling passage, not a separate entry.
