The Kings of the North and the South

Prophecy
The Kings of the North and the South
Prophet
Daniel
Date Approx
c. 536 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
Daniel was shown a long succession of Persian kings, the rise of a mighty Greek king whose kingdom would be divided to the four winds, and then detailed wars between the king of the north and the king of the south. The account follows intrigue, marriages, betrayals, and battles with unusual precision, ending with a wilful king who exalts himself above every god. The final section turns from named historical succession to a king whose end comes without human help.
Fulfillment
The Persian and Greek succession and the Seleucid and Ptolemaic wars in Daniel 11:2 to 35 match known history in remarkable detail, including Antiochus IV's persecution. The identity of the wilful king in Daniel 11:36 to 45 is debated, with some readers seeing Antiochus and others a future end-times ruler, so the chapter's closing section is not settled in the same way. This entry is therefore marked Partially Fulfilled.
