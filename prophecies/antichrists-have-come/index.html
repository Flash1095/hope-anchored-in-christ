Antichrists Have Come

Prophecy
Antichrists Have Come
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 90
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
John told his readers that it was the last hour, and that just as they had heard an antichrist was coming, many antichrists had already come — and by this they would know it was the last hour. These antichrists went out from the church but were not truly of it, and their mark was denial of Jesus as the Christ and denial of the Father and the Son. The prophecy is therefore both a warning about a coming figure and a present sign: the spirit of antichrist was already at work through those who denied the incarnate Son.
Fulfillment
John states the sign as already present in his own day, with former members denying Christ and leaving the fellowship, and the same denial has recurred ever since. This entry is kept distinct from the man of lawlessness entry because John describes many present antichrists defined by doctrinal denial, rather than Paul's single future figure of lawlessness. Fulfillment is therefore partial and ongoing: begun in the first century and continuing wherever Christ is denied, with the final antichrist still awaited in the broader New Testament picture.
