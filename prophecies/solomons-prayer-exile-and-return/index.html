Solomon's Prayer: Exile and Return Foretold

Prophecy
Solomon's Prayer: Exile and Return Foretold
Prophet
King Solomon
Date Approx
c. 960 BC
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
At the temple dedication Solomon foresees the sequence: if Israel sins, the LORD will be angry and deliver them to the enemy, who will carry them captive far or near; yet if in captivity they bethink themselves, repent, and pray toward their land, the LORD will hear, maintain their cause, forgive them, and give them compassion before their captors.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in the Babylonian captivity, when Judah is carried away (2 Kings 25:11), and in the return under Cyrus, who proclaims that the LORD has charged him to build the house at Jerusalem (Ezra 1:1-3). Drafted with hesitation noted in the report: the passage is cast as prayer and condition, not as a bare oracle.
