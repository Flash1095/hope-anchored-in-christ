Saints Judge the World and Angels

Prophecy
Saints Judge the World and Angels
Prophet
Paul
Date Approx
c. AD 55
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Paul says the saints will judge the world and will judge angels, arguing that believers therefore ought to be able to settle lesser disputes among themselves.
Fulfillment
Awaited. No such judging role for the saints has yet occurred; the passage states it as a future certainty, not as a present practice.
