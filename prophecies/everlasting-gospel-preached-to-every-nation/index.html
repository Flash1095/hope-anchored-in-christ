The Everlasting Gospel Preached to Every Nation

Prophecy
The Everlasting Gospel Preached to Every Nation
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
An angel flies in mid-heaven with the everlasting gospel to preach to those who dwell on the earth, to every nation and kindred and tongue and people, announcing that the hour of God's judgment has come and calling all to fear God, give him glory, and worship the Maker of heaven and earth, sea, and fountains of waters.
Fulfillment
Debated: whether this is a literal angelic proclamation before the end, a symbolic picture of the church's worldwide preaching, or a final warning already in progress is disputed; a universal proclamation preceding judgment is the substance all readings share, and its completion is still awaited.
