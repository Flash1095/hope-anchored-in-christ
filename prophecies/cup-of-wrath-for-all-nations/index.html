The Cup of Wrath for All Nations

Prophecy
The Cup of Wrath for All Nations
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 605 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Jeremiah is made to take the wine cup of God's fury and give every nation to which he is sent to drink it, beginning with Jerusalem and Judah and running through Egypt, Philistia, Edom, Moab, Ammon, Tyre, Arabia and Babylon itself, until all drink, stagger and fall.
Fulfillment
Babylon's campaigns in turn brought these nations under judgment, and Babylon itself drank last; the sequence was fulfilled in history, though the cup's fullest, final sense remains open, hence partial.

Partially Fulfilled
