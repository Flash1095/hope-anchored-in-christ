God's Anointed Son Rules the Nations

God's Anointed Son Rules the Nations

Prophecy Summary

Psalm 2 declares that despite the nations raging, God has installed His King on Zion and given Him the nations as His inheritance. The Son is urged to be kissed — welcomed in glad submission.

Fulfillment

The New Testament applies this psalm to Jesus again and again: at His baptism, in His resurrection, and in His exaltation over every power. His present reign will be openly seen when He returns.
