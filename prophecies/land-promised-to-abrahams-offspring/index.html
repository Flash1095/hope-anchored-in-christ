The Land Promised to Abraham's Offspring

Prophecy
The Land Promised to Abraham's Offspring
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 2000 BC
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Prophecy Summary
God repeatedly promised Abraham that the land he was walking through, from the river of Egypt to the great river Euphrates, would be given to his offspring as an everlasting possession. This land promise was distinct from the promise that all nations would be blessed through Abraham, and it was renewed to Abraham at Shechem, at Bethel, and in the covenant of Genesis 15 and 17.
Fulfillment
After the exodus and the wilderness years, Israel under Joshua took possession of the land, and Joshua 21 records that not one of the good promises of the LORD failed. The land was later lost through disobedience, exactly as Moses warned in Deuteronomy, which is why the land promise and the curses of exile belong together in Scripture's story.
