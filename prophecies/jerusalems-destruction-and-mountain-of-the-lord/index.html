Jerusalem's Destruction and the Mountain of the LORD Exalted

Prophecy
Jerusalem's Destruction and the Mountain of the LORD Exalted
Prophet

Date Approx
c. 730 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Micah charged Jerusalem's rulers, priests, and prophets with judging for reward, teaching for hire, and divining for money, while claiming the LORD was among them. Because of them, he declared, Zion shall be plowed as a field, Jerusalem shall become heaps, and the mountain of the house as the high places of the forest. In the last days, the same mountain will be reversed in glory: the mountain of the LORD's house shall be established on the top of the mountains, and many nations will stream to it to learn His ways.
Fulfillment
Jerusalem was indeed destroyed by Babylon in 586 BC, its temple burned and its walls broken, fulfilling the plowing and heaps in literal history. The exaltation of the mountain began with the return and the rebuilt temple, and Christians read its fuller meaning in the nations coming to Christ and to the heavenly Jerusalem, which is not yet complete. The destruction is fulfilled and the exaltation is partly fulfilled, so the entry as a whole is Partially Fulfilled.
