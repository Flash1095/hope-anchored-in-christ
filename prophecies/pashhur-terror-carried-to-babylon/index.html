Pashhur Made a Terror and Carried to Babylon

Prophecy
Pashhur Made a Terror and Carried to Babylon
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 605 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Pashhur, who struck Jeremiah and put him in the stocks, will become a terror to himself and his friends, see them fall by the sword, and himself go to Babylon and die there, with all Judah's wealth given to Babylon.
Fulfillment
Judah's wealth and people were indeed carried to Babylon, and Pashhur's party shared that captivity; no surviving narrative separately records Pashhur's death in Babylon, so the personal detail is not claimed as independently confirmed.
