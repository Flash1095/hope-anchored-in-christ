The Valley of Vision and Shebna's Fall

Prophecy
The Valley of Vision and Shebna's Fall
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Isaiah pictured Jerusalem, the valley of vision, in a day of trouble and perplexity, with its people fleeing to the housetops and its valley full of chariots at the gates. In the same oracle the LORD announced that Shebna, steward over the house, would be driven from his station and pulled down, and that Eliakim son of Hilkiah would be given the key of the house of David. The chapter joins a siege picture with a named official's displacement.
Fulfillment
Shebna was indeed displaced, and Eliakim son of Hilkiah appears in his place as steward in the Hezekiah narratives, holding office during Sennacherib's siege. Jerusalem suffered the distress of siege, most terribly in the Babylonian conquest of 586 BC, after the earlier Assyrian siege of 701 BC. Because the siege picture finds echoes in more than one siege, the entry is marked partially fulfilled, with Shebna's fall fulfilled in full.
