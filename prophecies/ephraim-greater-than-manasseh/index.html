Ephraim Greater Than Manasseh

Prophecy
Ephraim Greater Than Manasseh
Prophet
Jacob
Date Approx
c. 1850 BC
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Prophecy Summary
When Joseph brought his two sons to Jacob for blessing, Joseph placed Manasseh, the firstborn, at Jacob's right hand and Ephraim at his left, but Jacob deliberately crossed his hands and put his right hand on Ephraim's head. Joseph objected, yet Jacob insisted that Manasseh would also become a people and be great, but that his younger brother Ephraim would be greater, and his offspring would become a multitude of nations.
Fulfillment
Ephraim became the dominant northern tribe: Joshua, who led Israel into the land, was an Ephraimite, and the first capital of the northern kingdom stood at Shechem in Ephraim's hill country. The name Ephraim later came to stand for the northern kingdom as a whole, just as Jacob's word had placed the younger brother first. This entry is a borderline blessing-or-prophecy case, included because Jacob's crossed hands were a deliberate, explicit prediction of relative greatness, not a general good wish.
