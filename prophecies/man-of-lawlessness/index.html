The Man of Lawlessness

Prophecy Summary

Paul warned the Thessalonians that the Day of the Lord would not come until a rebellion came first and the man of lawlessness was revealed, a figure who opposes God, exalts himself above all that is worshipped, and seats himself in God's temple proclaiming himself to be God. He will come with Satan's power, counterfeit signs and wonders, and deceit for those who refuse the truth. The Lord Jesus will destroy him by the breath of his mouth and the splendour of his coming.

Fulfillment

No fulfillment is claimed as settled history, because the identity and timing of this figure are debated among faithful interpreters: some see a future individual, others a recurring pattern or a historical figure such as an emperor or papal figure, and the restrainer's identity is likewise disputed. What Scripture states clearly is the sequence — rebellion, revelation, and destruction at Christ's coming — and that the mystery of lawlessness was already at work in Paul's day. The entry therefore records the prophecy as stated and leaves its referent open rather than forcing an identification.
