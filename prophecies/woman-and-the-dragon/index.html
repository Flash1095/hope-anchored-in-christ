The Woman and the Dragon

Prophecy

Prophet

Date Approx

Timeline Era

Prophecy Summary
John saw a woman clothed with the sun, with the moon under her feet and a crown of twelve stars, crying out in birth pains, and a great red dragon with seven heads and ten horns waiting to devour her child as soon as it was born. The child, who was to rule all nations with a rod of iron, was caught up to God and to his throne, and the woman fled into the wilderness where God had prepared a place for her to be nourished for 1,260 days. War then broke out in heaven, Michael and his angels fought the dragon, the ancient serpent called the devil and Satan was cast down to the earth, and he pursued the woman and made war with the rest of her offspring who keep God's commandments and hold the testimony of Jesus.
Fulfillment
The woman's identity is debated — Israel, the church, Mary, and Eve's offspring have all been proposed — and the wilderness period and heavenly war are read by some as already accomplished at Christ's birth, ascension, and the church's suffering, and by others as still future. This entry is kept distinct from the covered Seed of the Woman entry, which cites Revelation 12:9 only for the serpent's identity; Revelation 12 as a whole adds the birth, ascension, wilderness nourishment, war in heaven, and persecution of the woman's offspring, which the Genesis entry does not contain. Fulfillment is therefore partial and debated rather than simply settled.
