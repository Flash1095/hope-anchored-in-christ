Samuel's Warning: The Manner of the King

Samuel's Warning: The Manner of the King

Prophecy Summary

When Israel demands a king, Samuel warns what the king they choose will do: he will take their sons for chariots and armies, their daughters for his household, their fields, vineyards, and flocks, a tenth of their seed and sheep, and they will become his servants and cry out under him.

Fulfillment

Fulfilled under the monarchy: the people's own later complaint is that Solomon made their yoke grievous (1 Kings 12:4), and the pattern of royal seizure runs through the kings, climaxing in Ahab's seizure of Naboth's vineyard (1 Kings 21).
