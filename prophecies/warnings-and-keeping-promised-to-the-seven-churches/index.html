Warnings and Keeping Promised to the Seven Churches

Prophecy
Warnings and Keeping Promised to the Seven Churches
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Each of the seven churches in Asia receives a predictive word: Ephesus will lose its candlestick unless it repents; Smyrna will suffer tribulation ten days and must be faithful unto death; Pergamum will face Christ's sword unless it repents; Jezebel in Thyatira will be cast into a bed and her followers into great tribulation, and her children killed with death; Sardis will face Christ coming as a thief if it does not watch; the opponents of Philadelphia will come and worship before its feet; and Philadelphia will be kept from the hour of temptation that shall come upon all the world to try earth's dwellers.
Fulfillment
Debated: some read these as warnings fulfilled in the early history of those Asian churches, others as applying through the church age, and the worldwide hour of temptation in particular is variously placed in the past, the present age, or the end. The conditional form means repentance could avert the threatened judgment, so no single dated fulfilment can be asserted.
