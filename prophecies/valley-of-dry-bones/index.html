The Valley of Dry Bones

Prophecy
The Valley of Dry Bones
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 590 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
In a valley of very dry bones, Ezekiel prophesied and watched them come together, receive breath, and stand as a vast army. God explained it plainly: the whole house of Israel, cut off in exile, would be opened from its graves and brought home by His Spirit.
Fulfillment
The return from exile under Cyrus was its first fulfilment, and the Spirit giving new life in Christ is its deeper one. Many also look for a fuller national and spiritual restoration still to come, which is why this prophecy is best marked as begun but not finished.

Partially Fulfilled
