Moab's Devastation

Prophecy
Moab's Devastation
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Isaiah lamented Moab's sudden ruin: in a night Ar and Kir would be laid waste, Moab would wail on its high places, and its fugitives would flee toward Zoar. Its vineyards and fields of Heshbon would languish, its pride would be brought low, and within three years, as a hireling counts them, the glory of Moab would be diminished. The oracle is a sustained lament as well as a prediction, and it is distinct from Jeremiah's later Moab oracle.
Fulfillment
Moab was pressed by Assyria and later by Babylon, its cities were devastated, and Moab ceased to exist as an independent nation, fading from the historical record. The short timescale and the loss of its famed vineyards and pride match the pattern of Moab's decline under the great empires. The oracle was therefore fulfilled in Moab's disappearance as a nation.
