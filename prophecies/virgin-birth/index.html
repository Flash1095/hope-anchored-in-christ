The Virgin Birth

Prophecy
The Virgin Birth
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 735 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
To a faithless king, Isaiah gave a sign: the virgin would conceive and bear a son called Immanuel, God with us. Offered in Ahaz's day, the promise carried a reach far beyond that crisis.
Fulfillment
Matthew declares the fulfilment in the conception of Jesus by the Holy Spirit and His birth to Mary. Immanuel is not just a name but the reality of the whole Gospel — God truly with us in Christ.
