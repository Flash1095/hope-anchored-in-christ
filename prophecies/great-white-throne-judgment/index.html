The Great White Throne Judgment

Prophecy
The Great White Throne Judgment
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
John saw a great white throne and him who sat on it, from whose face earth and heaven fled away, and the dead, small and great, standing before God as books were opened, including the book of life, and each was judged according to their works. Death and hell delivered up their dead, and death and hell themselves were cast into the lake of fire, which is the second death, and whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire. This is the final, universal judgment in which no one is overlooked and the distinction between the saved and the lost is made eternally plain.
Fulfillment
This judgment is presented as future to John's vision and has not yet occurred in the form described, with the books opened and the lake of fire as the second death. The entry is kept distinct from the covered Resurrection and Final Judgment entry, which gathers Daniel 12:1-3 with John 5:28-29 and Matthew 13:43 as the general resurrection and judgment; Revelation 20:11-15 adds the specific throne scene, the books, the surrender of death and hell, and the second death, which the Daniel entry does not contain. Its referent is the same final judgment, but its content is a distinct Revelation scene.
