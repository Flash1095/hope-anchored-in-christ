The Crown of Pride of Ephraim Trodden Down

Prophecy
The Crown of Pride of Ephraim Trodden Down
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 725 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Woe to the fading crown of Ephraim's drunkards at Samaria: their glorious beauty will be cast down and trodden underfoot, like an early fig eaten as soon as it is seen.
Fulfillment
Samaria fell to Assyria in 722 BC and the northern kingdom's pride and capital were destroyed.
