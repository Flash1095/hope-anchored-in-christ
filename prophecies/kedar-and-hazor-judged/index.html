Kedar and Hazor Judged

Prophecy
Kedar and Hazor Judged
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 600 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Jeremiah foretold that Nebuchadnezzar would rise against Kedar and spoil the men of the east, taking their tents, flocks, and camels, and crying terror on every side. Hazor, the desert kingdom of settled Arabs, would become a dwelling for dragons and a desolation for ever, its people scattered to all winds. It is Jeremiah's own Arabia oracle, distinct from Isaiah's Kedar oracle, which concerns an earlier Assyrian campaign.
Fulfillment
Nebuchadnezzar campaigned against the Arabian tribes, Kedar's flocks and tents were plundered, and the desert kingdoms of the region lost their independence under Babylonian pressure. Hazor as a desert kingdom disappeared from history. The oracle was therefore fulfilled in the Babylonian subjugation of Arabia.
