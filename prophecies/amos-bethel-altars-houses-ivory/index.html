The Altars of Bethel and the Houses of Ivory Destroyed

Prophecy
The Altars of Bethel and the Houses of Ivory Destroyed
Prophet
Amos
Date Approx
c. 760 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Amos predicts that when God visits Israel's transgressions he will also visit the altars of Bethel, the horns of the altar will be cut off and fall to the ground, and the winter house, summer house, houses of ivory, and great houses will perish.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in the end of the northern kingdom's Bethel cult and its luxurious houses: Bethel's altar was later desecrated under Josiah (2 Kings 23:15-16) and the wealth of Samaria's elite perished with the kingdom.

Fulfilled
