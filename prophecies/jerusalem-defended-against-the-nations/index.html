Jerusalem Defended Against the Nations

Prophecy
Jerusalem Defended Against the Nations
Prophet
Zechariah
Date Approx
c. 5th century BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
Zechariah foretold that God will make Jerusalem a cup of trembling to all surrounding peoples and a burdensome stone for all nations, so that all who burden themselves with it will be cut in pieces. When the nations gather against Jerusalem, God will smite their horses with astonishment and their riders with madness, open His eyes upon Judah, and make its governors like a hearth of fire among wood and a torch among sheaves. In that day the LORD will defend the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and the feeble among them will be as David, and the house of David as God, as the angel of the LORD before them.
Fulfillment
Jerusalem survived sieges and attacks in the post-exilic and Maccabean periods, and its attackers repeatedly failed to erase it, so the pattern of a burdensome stone has recurred in history. No single siege has matched the full picture of all nations gathered, every horse smitten, and Jerusalem's inhabitants made as David in one day, and Christian tradition reads the complete defense as belonging to the final Day of the LORD. The entry is therefore partly fulfilled in its recurring pattern, with its complete fulfillment future.
