Prophecy Summary
In the days of Jeroboam the second, Jonah son of Amittai, the prophet from Gath-hepher, spoke a word that Israel's borders would be restored from the entering of Hamath to the Sea of the Arabah. The prophecy concerned the territory Israel had lost to Syria, and it was spoken while Israel was still afflicted and without a helper.
Fulfillment
Jeroboam the second did restore the border of Israel from Lebo-hamath to the Sea of the Arabah, and 2 Kings states that he did so according to the word of the LORD spoken by Jonah. The book of Jonah itself, with its mission to Nineveh, belongs to the Minor Prophets researcher; this entry covers only the border prophecy as recorded in the Kings narrative. The recovery did not last, but the prophecy was fulfilled in Jeroboam's own reign.
