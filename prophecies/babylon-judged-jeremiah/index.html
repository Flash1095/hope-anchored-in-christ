Prophecy Summary
Jeremiah's long oracle against Babylon, written and sent to Babylon in Zedekiah's fourth year, foretold that Babylon would be taken, its idols Bel and Merodach confounded and broken, and its mighty men would cease fighting. The land would be made desolate, its treasures spoiled, and the broad walls of Babylon utterly broken down. This entry covers the judgment strand of Jeremiah 50-51; the return strand of those chapters is grouped separately in the Return from Exile entry.
Fulfillment
Babylon fell to Cyrus in 539 BC without a great battle for its walls, its empire ended, its idols proved powerless, and its great city declined over the following centuries until its site was desolate. The judgment foretold while Babylon was still at the height of its power was therefore fulfilled. It is distinct from Isaiah's Babylon oracle, which was spoken more than a century earlier.
