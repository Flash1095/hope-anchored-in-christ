Edom's Doom

Prophecy
Edom's Doom
Prophet
Obadiah
Date Approx
c. 6th century BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
Obadiah, the shortest book in the Old Testament, pronounced total judgment on Edom, descended from Esau, for standing aloof and then joining in the plunder when Jerusalem fell. Edom had gloated over Judah's calamity, blocked fugitives at the crossroads, and handed survivors to the enemy, breaking the bond of brotherhood. God promised that Edom's pride in its rocky strongholds would be brought down, its wise men and warriors destroyed, and no survivor left of the house of Esau, while Mount Zion would be holy and the kingdom would be the LORD's.
Fulfillment
Edom was struck by Babylon in the sixth century BC, then displaced from its homeland by the Nabataeans, surviving only as Idumea to the west before being absorbed under the Hasmoneans and disappearing as a distinct people after the first century AD. The final part of the oracle, the possession of Edom's land and the kingdom belonging to the LORD, is read in Christian tradition as completed in Christ's kingdom and awaiting its final consummation. The national doom of Edom is therefore fulfilled, with its kingdom consummation still future.
