Judah Shall Go Up First

Prophecy
Judah Shall Go Up First
Date Approx
c. 1350 BC
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Prophecy Summary
After Joshua's death Israel asks who will go up first against the Canaanites, and the LORD answers that Judah shall go up, for the land is delivered into his hand.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled at once: Judah goes up, the LORD delivers the Canaanites and Perizzites into their hand, and they take Jerusalem (Judges 1:4, 1:8).

Fulfilled
