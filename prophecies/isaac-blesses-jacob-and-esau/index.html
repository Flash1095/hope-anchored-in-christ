Isaac Blesses Jacob and Esau

Prophecy
Isaac Blesses Jacob and Esau
Prophet
Isaac
Date Approx
c. 1850 BC
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Prophecy Summary
Near the end of his life Isaac blessed Jacob, whom he believed to be Esau, with the dew of heaven, the fatness of the earth, dominion over his brothers, and the promise that those who cursed him would be cursed. When Esau came in, Isaac blessed him too, but with a lesser portion: he would live by the sword, serve his brother, and in time break his brother's yoke from off his neck.
Fulfillment
Jacob's line became Israel and received the covenant blessing, while Esau's line became Edom and lived as a neighbouring warrior people. Edom served Israel under David and broke away under Joram, matching both halves of the blessing in turn. The blessing could not be taken back once spoken, and Isaac himself confirmed that Jacob would indeed be blessed.
