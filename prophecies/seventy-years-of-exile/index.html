The Seventy Years of Exile

Prophecy
The Seventy Years of Exile
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 605 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Jeremiah foretold that Judah and the surrounding nations would serve the king of Babylon for seventy years, and that when those seventy years were accomplished, God would punish the king of Babylon and make his land desolate. Writing to the exiles already in Babylon, he repeated that after seventy years were accomplished at Babylon, God would visit them and bring them back to Jerusalem. The number is given twice, in Jeremiah 25 and Jeremiah 29, and this entry groups both passages as one prophecy.
Fulfillment
From Nebuchadnezzar's dominance after 605 BC to Cyrus's decree in 538 BC is a period of about seventy years, and the exiles did return under Cyrus, as Ezra records. Babylon itself was then punished and fell, exactly as the second half of the prediction said. The seventy years were therefore fulfilled in the exile and the return, in the terms Jeremiah gave.
