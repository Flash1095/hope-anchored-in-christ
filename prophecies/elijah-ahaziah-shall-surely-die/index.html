Elijah: Ahaziah Shall Surely Die

Prophecy
Elijah: Ahaziah Shall Surely Die
Prophet
Elijah
Date Approx
c. 850 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
When Ahaziah, sick from a fall, sends to enquire of Baal-zebub the god of Ekron, Elijah meets the messengers with the word of the LORD: because there is no God in Israel to enquire of, Ahaziah shall not come down from the bed he has gone up to, but shall surely die.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled as spoken: Ahaziah dies according to the word of the LORD which Elijah had spoken (2 Kings 1:17), and Jehoram reigns in his stead.
Fulfillment Status
Fulfilled
