A Star Out of Jacob

A Star Out of Jacob

Prophecy Summary

Hired to curse Israel, Balaam instead blessed them and saw a star and a scepter rise out of Jacob in the distant future. The vision pointed to a king who would triumph over Israel's enemies.

Fulfillment

Christians connect the star with the star of Bethlehem and the scepter with Jesus the King, born centuries later. His victory is not by the sword but by the cross, and it will be completed when He returns.
