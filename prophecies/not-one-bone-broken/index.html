Not One of His Bones Broken

Prophecy
Not One of His Bones Broken
Prophet
David
Date Approx
c. 1000 BC
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
The righteous sufferer is kept by the LORD so that not one of his bones is broken.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled at the crucifixion: John's Gospel records that the soldiers did not break Jesus' legs, that the Scripture might be fulfilled, a bone of him shall not be broken.

Fulfilled
