Ephraim Exiled to Egypt and Assyria

Prophecy
Ephraim Exiled to Egypt and Assyria
Prophet
Hosea
Date Approx
c. 750 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Hosea predicts that Ephraim will no longer dwell in the LORD's land but will return to Egypt and eat unclean food in Assyria, its sacrifices will cease to please God, its glory will fly away, and its people will be cast away as wanderers among the nations.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in the Assyrian conquest of the northern kingdom and the deportation of Israel (722 BC), after which the ten tribes lived scattered among the nations and temple sacrifice in the land ceased.

Fulfilled
