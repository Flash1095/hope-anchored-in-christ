A Light to the Gentiles

Prophecy
A Light to the Gentiles
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
God told His Servant it was too small a thing to restore Israel alone; He would also be a light for the Gentiles, carrying salvation to the ends of the earth. Israel's Messiah was always meant for the world.
Fulfillment
Simeon praised the infant Jesus as that light to the Gentiles, and Paul and Barnabas later quoted the same words as the warrant for their mission. The worldwide church is this prophecy unfolding.
Fulfillment Status
Fulfilled
