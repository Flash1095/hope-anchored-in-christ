Gog and Magog

Prophecy
Gog and Magog
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 590–570 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
Ezekiel pictured a future coalition led by Gog of Magog, with Meshech, Tubal, Persia, Ethiopia, Libya, and other allies, invading a restored and unwalled Israel. God said he would turn Gog back, defeat the invaders on the mountains of Israel, and use the victory to show his holiness to the nations. The aftermath includes the burial of the dead and the burning of weapons over a long period.
Fulfillment
No event in recorded history matches this invasion in all its details, and interpreters place it at different points, including before or after a millennial period or as a symbolic picture of final opposition to God. Revelation 20 uses the names Gog and Magog for a final rebellion, which some readers connect with Ezekiel and others treat as a separate use of the same imagery. Because its timing and referent are debated, this entry is marked Debated.
