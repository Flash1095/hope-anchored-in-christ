Evil Neighbours Plucked Up and Restored

Prophecy
Evil Neighbours Plucked Up and Restored
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 600 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
God's evil neighbours who touch Israel's inheritance will be plucked out of their land, and Judah too will be plucked up; afterwards, if the plucked nations learn the ways of God's people, they will be built in the midst of his people, but a nation that will not obey will be utterly plucked up and destroyed.
Fulfillment
Babylon's campaigns plucked Judah's neighbours as well as Judah; the conditional restoration depended on obedience and was never realised as a wholesale building of those nations among God's people, so this is partial.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
