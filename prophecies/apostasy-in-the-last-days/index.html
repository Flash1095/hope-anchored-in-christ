Apostasy in the Last Days

Prophecy
Apostasy in the Last Days
Prophet
Paul
Date Approx
c. AD 60s
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Paul foretold that in later times some would depart from the faith, giving heed to deceitful spirits and doctrines of demons, including forbidding marriage and commanding abstinence from foods God created to be received with thanksgiving. In the last days, he said, perilous times would come, marked by lovers of themselves, lovers of money, boastfulness, disobedience, unthankfulness, lack of self-control, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God, and a form of godliness that denies its power. These are presented as a widespread moral and doctrinal falling away, not a single event or single figure, which is why the two pastoral passages are grouped here as one prophecy.
Fulfillment
The New Testament itself presents this falling away as already beginning, with false teaching and ungodly conduct appearing in the early churches, and church history since has repeatedly shown the same pattern of doctrinal departure and moral decline. Because the passages describe a recurring condition of the last days rather than one datable event, fulfillment is best understood as partially fulfilled and ongoing, culminating in the final rebellion described in 2 Thessalonians 2. It is kept distinct from the man of lawlessness entry, which concerns a specific revealed figure rather than this general departure.
