The Second Coming and New Creation

Prophecy
The Second Coming and New Creation
Prophet
John the Apostle
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Revelation unveils Jesus returning as King of kings on a white horse to judge and reign, followed by a new heaven and new earth where God dwells with His people. Every tear is wiped away, death and pain are no more, and the curse is lifted forever.
Fulfillment
This is the church's great future hope and has not yet been fulfilled — which is why Revelation ends with longing: Amen. Come, Lord Jesus. Its certainty anchors believers the way Hebrews 6:19 describes hope: firm and secure, like an anchor for the soul.
