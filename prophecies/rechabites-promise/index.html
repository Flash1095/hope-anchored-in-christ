The Rechabites' Promise

Prophecy
The Rechabites' Promise
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 600 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
When the Rechabites refused wine in obedience to their ancestor Jonadab, Jeremiah contrasted their faithfulness with Judah's disobedience and announced God's promise to them: because they had obeyed Jonadab, Jonadab son of Rechab would not want a man to stand before the LORD for ever. It is a small, personal promise to a family, tied to their obedience, and it is included here as a borderline entry because Scripture itself frames it as a divine promise about their future.
Fulfillment
The Rechabites survived the fall of Jerusalem, and later history preserves traces of Rechabite communities, but a continuous, verifiable line standing before the LORD for ever cannot be traced through history to the present day. Because the promise is perpetual in wording while its later history is sparse, this entry is marked Debated rather than claimed as fulfilled in full. The promise stands in the text, with its complete fulfilment left open rather than overstated.
