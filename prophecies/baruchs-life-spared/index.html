Baruch's Life Spared

Prophecy
Baruch's Life Spared
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx

Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
When Baruch, Jeremiah's scribe, despaired over the sorrows added to his pain, the LORD told him not to seek great things for himself, for evil would be brought upon all flesh, but that his life would be given to him for a prey in every place he went. It is a personal prophecy to a named individual, spoken in the fourth year of Jehoiakim, and it is included here as a borderline entry on the same ground as the Rechabites and Ebed-Melech promises.
Fulfillment
Baruch survived the fall of Jerusalem, the turmoil that followed, and the flight into Egypt with the remnant, living through dangers that claimed many around him. His life was indeed preserved wherever he went, as the promise said. The prophecy was therefore fulfilled in his survival through the exile years.
