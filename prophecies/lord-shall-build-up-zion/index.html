The LORD Shall Build Up Zion

Prophecy
The LORD Shall Build Up Zion
Date Approx
c. 550 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
The LORD will arise and have mercy on Zion when the set time to favour her has come; he will build up Zion and appear in his glory, the nations and kings will fear his name, a future people will be created to praise him, and peoples and kingdoms will be gathered to serve the LORD.
Fulfillment
Begun in the return from exile and the rebuilding of Jerusalem and the temple under Cyrus, Zerubbabel, Ezra, and Nehemiah, when Zion was again inhabited and its name declared there; the full gathering of peoples and kingdoms to serve the LORD, and a people created to praise him, extend beyond that restoration and remain partially fulfilled.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
