The Seventy Weeks

Prophecy
The Seventy Weeks
Prophet
Daniel
Date Approx
c. 539 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
Gabriel gave Daniel a timetable of seventy sevens decreed for his people and city, to finish transgression, atone for iniquity, and anoint a most holy place. After sixty-nine sevens, an anointed one would be cut off, and the city and sanctuary would face destruction.
Fulfillment
Many evangelical readers calculate the weeks to the ministry and death of Christ, followed by Jerusalem's fall in AD 70, while others place the final week entirely in the future. Because faithful scholars differ on the details, this prophecy is honestly marked Debated, even though its central focus on the Messiah is widely affirmed.
