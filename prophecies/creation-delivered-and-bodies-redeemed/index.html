Creation Delivered and Bodies Redeemed

Prophecy
Creation Delivered and Bodies Redeemed
Prophet
Paul
Date Approx
c. AD 57
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Paul says the glory to be revealed in believers outweighs present suffering, that creation itself will be delivered from the bondage of corruption into the liberty of God's children, and that believers groan awaiting the adoption, the redemption of the body.
Fulfillment
Partially fulfilled on the passage's own terms: believers already have the firstfruits of the Spirit, but creation's deliverance and the redemption of the body are still awaited.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
