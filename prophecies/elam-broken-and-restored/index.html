Elam Broken and Restored

Prophecy
Elam Broken and Restored
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 600 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Jeremiah foretold that the LORD would break the bow of Elam, the chief of its might, bring the four winds against it, and scatter its people toward all winds until none were left to whom Elam's outcasts would not come. Its king and princes would be dismayed, yet the oracle ends with a promise that in the latter days God would bring again the captivity of Elam. It is the only Elam oracle in the prophets, and it holds judgment and restoration together.
Fulfillment
Elam was broken by Assyria and then absorbed into the Persian empire, its capital Susa becoming a Persian centre, and the Elamites as an independent power disappeared. The scattering of Elam matches that absorption into the empires that followed. The latter-days restoration strand has no clear historical fulfilment identified, so the entry is marked partially fulfilled.
