Blessing from the Day of the Temple Foundation

Prophecy
Blessing from the Day of the Temple Foundation
Prophet
Haggai
Date Approx
c. 520 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
Haggai tells the returned community to consider from the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month, the day the foundation of the LORD's temple was laid, that although seed, vine, fig tree, pomegranate, and olive had not yet brought forth, from that day God will bless them.
Fulfillment
Partially fulfilled in the completion of the Second Temple and the restored worship of the returned community; the passage gives no dated account of the agricultural blessing itself, so its full measure is not pinned to a single recorded event.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
