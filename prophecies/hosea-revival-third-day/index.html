Prophecy Summary
Hosea pictures a returning people saying that after God has torn and smitten them, he will heal and bind them up, revive them after two days, and raise them up on the third day so they may live in his sight and know him as surely as the morning and the rain.
Fulfillment
Debated: some read this as Israel's restoration after judgment, others as pointing to Christ's resurrection on the third day. The passage itself does not name a single dated event, so neither reading is presented here as settled.
