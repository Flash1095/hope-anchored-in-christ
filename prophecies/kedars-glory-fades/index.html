Kedar's Glory Fades Within a Year

Prophecy
Kedar's Glory Fades Within a Year
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Isaiah foretold that within one year, as the years of a hireling are counted, all the glory of Kedar would fail, and the residue of the mighty archers of Kedar would be diminished. The oracle against Arabia pictures its villages and flocks exposed to an invader, with its celebrated bowmen reduced to a remnant. It is a dated, specific prediction against the Arabian tribes, distinct from the Dumah burden that precedes it.
Fulfillment
The Arabian tribes, including Kedar, were attacked and subdued in Assyrian campaigns in the years after Isaiah, with Sennacherib and later Ashurbanipal recording campaigns against the Arabs and their queens. Kedar's independence and glory as a desert power faded under Assyrian and then Babylonian pressure. The short timescale and the diminishing of its archers match that pattern of subjugation.
