God's Name Great Among the Nations

Prophecy
God's Name Great Among the Nations
Prophet
Malachi
Date Approx
c. 450 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
Malachi charged the priests with offering polluted bread and blind, lame, and sick animals, despising God's name while claiming His table was contemptible. Against that small and careless worship, God declared that from the rising of the sun to its going down His name shall be great among the Gentiles, and in every place incense shall be offered to His name, and a pure offering. The LORD of hosts, Malachi repeats, is a great King, and His name is dreadful among the heathen.
Fulfillment
The worship Malachi pictured, Gentiles honouring God's name in every place with a pure offering, began as the Gospel spread beyond Israel and Gentile churches worshipped God across the Roman world and beyond. Christians have read the pure offering as fulfilled in the worship and sacrifice of praise offered through Christ, while the universal honouring of God's name in every place is still unfolding and not yet complete. The entry is therefore partly fulfilled, begun in the Gentile mission and awaiting its fullness.
