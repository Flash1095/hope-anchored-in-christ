A Child Named Josiah Shall Be Born

Prophecy
A Child Named Josiah Shall Be Born
Prophet
Man of God
Date Approx
c. 930 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
While Jeroboam stood burning incense at the altar he had built at Bethel, a man of God from Judah cried out against the altar, saying that a child would be born to the house of David named Josiah. That child, the prophecy said, would sacrifice the priests of the high places upon that very altar and burn men's bones upon it, and the altar itself would be torn down as a sign.
Fulfillment
About three centuries later, King Josiah of Judah came to Bethel, tore down the altar and the high place, burned the high place, and sacrificed the priests of the high places on the altar, fulfilling the word by name. The narrator of Kings pauses to note that Josiah spared only the tomb of the man of God who had spoken the prophecy. Naming a king centuries before his birth makes this one of the most specific predictions in the historical books.
