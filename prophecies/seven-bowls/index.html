The Seven Bowls

Prophecy
The Seven Bowls
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
John saw seven angels pour out seven bowls of the wrath of God upon the earth: painful sores on those with the mark of the beast, the sea and rivers turned to blood, scorching heat from the sun, darkness over the beast's kingdom, the drying of the Euphrates to prepare the way for kings from the east, and a great earthquake with hailstones at the seventh bowl, when Babylon the great was remembered before God. The bowls are described as the completion of God's wrath, fuller and more final than the partial judgments of the seals and trumpets. Even then, those afflicted blasphemed God rather than repenting.
Fulfillment
The bowls are presented as future, final judgments culminating in the fall of Babylon and the gathering at Armageddon, and interpreters debate their precise timing and historical referents, as with the seals and trumpets. No bowl is here matched to a specific dated event in history, since the text itself does not name one. The entry is kept distinct from the seals and trumpets because Revelation presents the bowls as a separate, completing series, not a restatement of the earlier ones.
