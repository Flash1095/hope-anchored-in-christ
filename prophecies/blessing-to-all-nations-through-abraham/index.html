Blessing to All Nations Through Abraham

Prophecy
Blessing to All Nations Through Abraham
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 2000 BC
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Prophecy Summary
God promised Abraham that through his offspring all families of the earth would be blessed. This was more than land and descendants — it pointed to a blessing that would reach the Gentile world.
Fulfillment
Paul identifies the offspring as Christ, through whom the blessing of Abraham and the gift of the Spirit have come to the nations. Every believer from every people group is living proof of this promise being kept.
Fulfillment Status
Fulfilled
