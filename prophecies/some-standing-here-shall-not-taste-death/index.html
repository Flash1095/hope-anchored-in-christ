Some Standing Here Shall Not Taste Death Till They See the Son of Man Coming

Prophecy
Some Standing Here Shall Not Taste Death Till They See the Son of Man Coming
Prophet
Jesus
Date Approx
c. AD 30
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
Jesus predicts that some standing there will not taste death until they see the Son of Man coming in his kingdom, or the kingdom of God come with power.
Fulfillment
Disputed: this is commonly read as the transfiguration (which follows immediately in all three Gospels), the resurrection, or the fall of Jerusalem, and the saying's exact referent is not settled here.
Fulfillment Status
Debated
