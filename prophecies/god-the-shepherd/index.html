God the Shepherd, David the King

Prophecy
God the Shepherd, David the King
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 590 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
Against Israel's false shepherds, Ezekiel promised that God Himself would search for His sheep, and would set over them one shepherd, His servant David. He would feed them, bind up the injured, and make a covenant of peace with them.
Fulfillment
Jesus calls Himself the Good Shepherd who lays down His life for the sheep, gathering one flock under one shepherd. The promised gathering continues today and will be complete when the Chief Shepherd appears.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
