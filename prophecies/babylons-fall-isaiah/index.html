Babylon's Fall Foretold

Prophecy

Prophet

Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Long before Babylon was the dominant power, Isaiah foretold its fall: its golden city would be brought low, its idols Bel and Nebo would stoop and be carried away, and the proud oppressor would be humbled. The burden against Babylon, the night vision of a watchman, and the later taunt against Babylon's sorceries and pride all speak of the same downfall. This entry folds Isaiah 47-48 into the Babylon oracle rather than duplicating it.
Fulfillment
Babylon fell to Cyrus the Persian in 539 BC, its empire ended, and its great city declined until its site was desolate, as the later history of the region shows. The idols that could not save Babylon were indeed carried off as spoil, and the city never regained its imperial glory. The prediction was therefore fulfilled, strikingly, more than a century and a half after Isaiah spoke.
