Works Tested by Fire on the Day

Prophecy
Works Tested by Fire on the Day
Prophet
Paul
Date Approx
c. AD 55
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
Paul says every man's work will be made manifest on the day, revealed by fire that tries its quality: work that abides receives a reward, work that burns brings loss, though the builder himself is saved yet so as by fire.
Fulfillment
Awaited. This testing day for believers' works has not yet occurred; it is distinct from the final judgment of the world in that the person tested is saved even when the work is lost.
