Peter's Denial Foretold

Prophecy
Peter's Denial Foretold
Prophet
Jesus Christ
Date Approx
c. AD 30
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
On the night of His arrest Jesus warned Peter that before the cock crowed, Peter would deny Him three times. Peter protested that he would never deny Him, even if he had to die with Him, and the other disciples said the same. Jesus repeated the warning in each Gospel account, making the denial a specific prediction rather than a general warning about weakness.
Fulfillment
That same night Peter denied knowing Jesus three times, first to a servant girl and then to others by the fire in the high priest's courtyard. When the cock crowed, Peter remembered Jesus's words and went out and wept bitterly. The prediction was fulfilled within hours, exactly in the number and the timing Jesus had named.
