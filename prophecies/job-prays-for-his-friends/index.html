Job Shall Pray for His Friends and Be Accepted

Prophecy
Job Shall Pray for His Friends and Be Accepted
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Prophecy Summary
The LORD told Eliphaz that Job would pray for him and his two friends, and that Job would be accepted, so that God would not deal with them according to their folly.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled immediately in the narrative: Eliphaz, Bildad, and Zophar did as commanded, offered the burnt offering, Job prayed for them, and the LORD accepted Job.
Fulfillment Status
Fulfilled
