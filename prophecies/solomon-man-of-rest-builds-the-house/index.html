Solomon, a Man of Rest, Shall Build the House

Prophecy
Solomon, a Man of Rest, Shall Build the House
Date Approx
c. 970 BC
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
A son will be born to David, named Solomon, who will be a man of rest with peace from his enemies, and he will build a house for the name of the LORD, with his kingdom established.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in part and pointing beyond Solomon: Solomon built the temple in Jerusalem and enjoyed rest and peace in his reign, as recorded in Kings. The promise of a throne established for ever was not exhausted by Solomon, whose kingdom later divided, so the everlasting aspect awaits its full fulfilment in David's greater Son.

Partially Fulfilled
