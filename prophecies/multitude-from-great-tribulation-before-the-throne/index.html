The Multitude from Great Tribulation Before the Throne

Prophecy
The Multitude from Great Tribulation Before the Throne
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
A great multitude that no one can number, from all nations and peoples and tongues, stands before the throne and before the Lamb in white robes; these are they who came out of great tribulation, washed their robes in the blood of the Lamb, and now serve God day and night in his temple, hungering and thirsting no more, with the Lamb feeding them and leading them to living fountains of waters while God wipes away every tear from their eyes.
Fulfillment
Debated: whether the great tribulation is a past persecution, the church's ongoing suffering, or a future end-time distress is disputed, and the same wiping away of tears is promised again at the new creation; the multitude's final state before the throne is therefore stated as the outcome without fixing its date.
