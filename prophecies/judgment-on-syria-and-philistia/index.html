Judgment on Syria and Philistia

Prophecy
Judgment on Syria and Philistia
Prophet
Zechariah
Date Approx
c. 5th century BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
Zechariah announced that the word of the LORD would rest on Hadrach and Damascus, and on Hamath, Tyre, and Sidon, even though Tyre had built herself a stronghold and heaped up silver as dust and gold as mire. God would cast out Tyre's power, smite her wealth in the sea, and burn her with fire, and the Philistine cities Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, and Ashdod would see it and fear, losing their kings and their pride. The passage ends with a turn to mercy: the blood and abominations would be taken from Philistia's mouth, and a remnant would remain for God in Judah.
Fulfillment
Tyre was besieged and broken by Alexander the Great in 332 BC, after earlier pressure from Assyria and Babylon, and the Philistine cities lost independence under successive empires, matching the judgment pattern Zechariah announced. The remnant promise, that survivors would belong to God, is read as begun in the incorporation of Gentiles into God's people and awaiting completion. The national judgments are fulfilled, with the remnant gathering ongoing, so the entry is partly fulfilled.
