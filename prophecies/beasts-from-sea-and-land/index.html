The Beasts from Sea and Land

Prophecy
The Beasts from Sea and Land
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
John saw a beast rise from the sea with ten horns and seven heads, given authority by the dragon, blaspheming God and making war with the saints for forty-two months, and the whole earth wondered after it. A second beast rose from the land with two horns like a lamb but speaking like a dragon, performing great signs, making fire come down from heaven, and causing an image of the first beast to be made and worshipped, with death for all who would not worship it. This second beast caused all, small and great, rich and poor, free and bond, to receive a mark on the right hand or forehead, so that no one could buy or sell without the mark, the name of the beast, or the number of his name, 666. Those who worship the beast and receive its mark will drink of the wine of God's wrath and be tormented, while the saints are called to endurance and faith.
Fulfillment
The beasts' identities are debated — empire and false prophet, a future world ruler and his prophet, or recurring persecuting powers — and the mark's exact form is likewise debated, with buying and selling pointing to economic coercion for loyalty to the beast. The mark is folded into this entry rather than given its own, because Revelation presents it as the second beast's enforcement mechanism, not a separate prophecy. No specific historical ruler is named here, since the text does not name one.
