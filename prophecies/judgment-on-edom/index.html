Judgment on Edom

Prophecy
Judgment on Edom
Prophet
Ezekiel
Date Approx
c. 590–570 BC
Timeline Era
Exile
Prophecy Summary
Because Edom took vengeance on Judah and rejoiced over its fall, God said Edom would be made desolate from Teman to Dedan. In the later oracle against Mount Seir, the same judgment is repeated in stronger terms: Edom's land would become a perpetual desolation for its hatred and bloodshed.
Fulfillment
Edom was weakened by Babylonian pressure and displaced by Nabataean Arabs, and its people continued only as Idumeans under later rulers before disappearing as a distinct nation. The old Edomite heartland in the mountains of Seir no longer bears an Edomite kingdom.
