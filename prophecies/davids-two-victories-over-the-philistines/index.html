David's Two Victories over the Philistines Foretold

Prophecy Summary

Twice David enquires of the LORD against the Philistines in the valley of Rephaim. First the LORD says to go up, for he will doubtless deliver the Philistines into David's hand. The second time the LORD says not to go up directly but to circle behind them, and when the sound of marching is heard in the mulberry trees, then the LORD will go out before him to smite the Philistine host.

Fulfillment

Both victories follow as spoken: David smites the Philistines at Baal-perazim (2 Samuel 5:20) and again from Geba to Gazer after the mulberry-tree signal (2 Samuel 5:25). The pair is kept as one entry following the live set's precedent for paired victories.
