The Thousand Years

Prophecy
The Thousand Years
Prophet
John
Date Approx
c. AD 95
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
John saw an angel bind Satan with a great chain and cast him into the bottomless pit for a thousand years, so that he should deceive the nations no more until the thousand years were finished. Souls of those beheaded for the witness of Jesus and who had not worshipped the beast lived and reigned with Christ a thousand years, in what John called the first resurrection, and blessed and holy is he who has part in it, for the second death has no power over them. When the thousand years are expired, Satan will be loosed for a little season, gather Gog and Magog to battle against the camp of the saints and the beloved city, and then be cast into the lake of fire forever.
Fulfillment
The reading of the thousand years is debated: premillennial interpreters take it as a future literal reign of Christ on earth, amillennial interpreters as the present reign of Christ through the church age, and postmillennial interpreters as a future golden age before Christ's return. The text itself is clear that Satan's binding, the saints' reign, his final rebellion, and his eternal judgment are all foretold, even though their timing is disputed. No interpretation is presented here as settled, and the status is therefore Debated.
