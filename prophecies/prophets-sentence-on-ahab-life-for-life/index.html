The Prophet's Sentence on Ahab: Life for Life

Prophecy
The Prophet's Sentence on Ahab: Life for Life
Date Approx
c. 855 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
After Ahab releases Ben-hadad alive, a prophet tells him by the word of the LORD: because thou hast let go a man appointed to utter destruction, thy life shall go for his life, and thy people for his people.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled at Ramoth-gilead: a bowman draws at a venture and wounds Ahab, who dies that day, and his blood is washed in the pool of Samaria as the LORD had spoken (1 Kings 22:34-38). Distinct from Micaiah's separate Ramoth-gilead prophecy, which is already a live row.
