Thirty Pieces of Silver

Prophecy
Thirty Pieces of Silver
Prophet
Zechariah
Date Approx
c. 520 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
Zechariah, acting out God's shepherd role, was paid thirty pieces of silver — the price of a slave — and threw the insulting sum to the potter in the house of the Lord. It pictured how cheaply God's shepherd would be valued.
Fulfillment
Judas agreed to betray Jesus for thirty pieces of silver, then in remorse threw the money into the temple, and it bought a potter's field. Matthew explicitly connects the painful details with what Zechariah (and Jeremiah's potter imagery) had spoken.
