The Egyptians at the Red Sea Seen No More

Prophecy
The Egyptians at the Red Sea Seen No More
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 1440 BC
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Prophecy Summary
Standing before the Red Sea, Moses tells Israel not to fear: they will see the LORD's salvation that day, and the Egyptians they have seen that day they will see again no more for ever.
Fulfillment
Fulfilled in Exodus 14:27-28, where the sea returns on the Egyptians and covers the chariots and horsemen of Pharaoh's host, so that not so much as one of them remained.
