Apostasy After Moses, and the Song as Witness

Prophecy
Apostasy After Moses, and the Song as Witness
Prophet
Moses
Date Approx
c. 1400 BC
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Prophecy Summary
God tells Moses that after his death the people will rise up and go whoring after the gods of strangers in the land, forsake him and break his covenant; Moses says he knows that after his death they will utterly corrupt themselves, turn aside from the way commanded, and evil will befall them in the latter days. The Song of Moses is appointed as a witness against them, foretelling that Israel will forsake God, provoke him with vanities, and be moved to jealousy by those who are not a people.
Fulfillment
Partly fulfilled: the Judges cycle and the later history of the kings show Israel repeatedly forsaking God for other gods and suffering the evils foretold, while the 'latter days' horizon of Deuteronomy 31:29 keeps the pattern open rather than closed in a single event.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled

Deuteronomy 31:16-21; Deuteronomy 31:27-29; Deuteronomy 32:20-21
