David's Line and the Levitical Priests Endure

David's Line and the Levitical Priests Endure

Prophecy Summary

David will never lack a man to sit on the throne of Israel, the Levitical priests will never lack a man to offer sacrifice before God, and these covenants stand as firmly as day and night; if they could be broken, so could God's covenant with Israel.

Fulfillment

After the exile there was no reigning Davidic king and, after AD 70, no Levitical temple sacrifice, so how this promise endures is genuinely disputed between messianic, spiritualised and future readings; the doubt is stated, not resolved, and this is marked Debated.
