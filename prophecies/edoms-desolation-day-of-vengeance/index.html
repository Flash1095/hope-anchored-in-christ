Edom's Desolation and the Day of Vengeance

Prophecy
Edom's Desolation and the Day of Vengeance
Prophet
Isaiah
Date Approx
c. 700 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Isaiah foretold a sword bathed in heaven that would come down upon Edom, whose land would become burning pitch and a wilderness where no one passes through, inhabited by wild creatures. In the companion vision, one comes from Edom with garments stained red from Bozrah, having trodden the winepress alone in the day of vengeance and the year of his redeemed. The two passages are grouped here as one prediction, since they speak of the same judgment on Edom.
Fulfillment
Edom was overrun and displaced, its territory taken over by the Nabataeans, and the Edomites as a nation disappeared, with their land left desolate as the oracle pictures. The winepress figure of the day of vengeance is also read in the New Testament tradition as pointing to a final day of judgment not yet seen in full. The prophecy is therefore partially fulfilled: Edom's historical desolation has happened, and its final day remains future.
