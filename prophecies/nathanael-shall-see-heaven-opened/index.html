Nathanael Shall See Heaven Opened and Angels Ascending and Descending

Prophecy
Nathanael Shall See Heaven Opened and Angels Ascending and Descending
Prophet
Jesus
Date Approx
c. AD 30
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
Jesus predicts to Nathanael that hereafter he shall see heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of Man.
Fulfillment
Disputed: the referent is unclear — readings include Jacob's ladder imagery fulfilled in Christ's ministry, the signs and resurrection appearances, and a future unveiling — so no single fulfilment is asserted here.
