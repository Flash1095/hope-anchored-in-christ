The Crucifixion Foretold

Prophecy
The Crucifixion Foretold
Prophet
David
Date Approx
c. 1000 BC
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Psalm 22 voices forsakenness, mocking crowds, pierced hands and feet, and soldiers dividing garments and casting lots. Written a thousand years before Christ — and before crucifixion was used in Israel — its detail is startling.
Fulfillment
Jesus cried its opening words from the cross, and the Gospels record its details fulfilled one by one, down to the lot cast for His clothing. The psalm ends in triumph, picturing the nations turning to the Lord.
