Jesus Returns as He Ascended

Prophecy
Jesus Returns as He Ascended
Date Approx
c. AD 33
Timeline Era
Early Church
Prophecy Summary
As Jesus is taken up into heaven before the disciples' eyes, two men in white apparel announce that this same Jesus will come back in the same manner as they have seen him go into heaven.
Fulfillment
Awaited. The return itself has not yet occurred; the prediction fixes its manner (visibly, bodily, from heaven) rather than its date, and no date is stated.
