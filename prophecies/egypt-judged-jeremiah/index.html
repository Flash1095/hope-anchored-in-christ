Egypt Judged

Prophecy
Egypt Judged
Prophet
Jeremiah
Date Approx
c. 605 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Jeremiah foretold Egypt's defeat at Carchemish, where Pharaoh Necho's army would be broken by Nebuchadnezzar, and a later invasion in which Nebuchadnezzar would strike the land of Egypt, set fire to its temples, and carry off its gods. Pharaoh Hophra would be given into the hand of his enemies, as Zedekiah had been given to Nebuchadnezzar. The oracle is distinct from Isaiah's Egypt oracle and from Jeremiah 44's warning to the remnant in Egypt, which is folded into this entry's background rather than duplicated.
Fulfillment
Egypt was defeated at Carchemish in 605 BC, ending its imperial ambitions in the Levant, and Nebuchadnezzar invaded Egypt in 568 BC, in the reign of Pharaoh Amasis after Hophra's fall. Egypt never regained its former imperial power, though it continued as a nation, so the judgment is marked fulfilled in its defeat and humbling. The later promise of Egypt's restoration after forty years, in Ezekiel rather than Jeremiah, is not claimed here.
