Two Victories Over Ben-hadad Foretold

Prophecy
Two Victories Over Ben-hadad Foretold
Prophet
Prophet of the LORD
Date Approx
c. 860 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
When Ben-hadad of Syria besieged Samaria with thirty-two kings, a prophet told Ahab that the LORD would deliver that great multitude into his hand that very day, and that the young men of the princes of the provinces would lead the victory. A year later, when Syria returned saying Israel's God was a god of the hills, another word promised the same deliverance in the plain, so that Syria would know that the LORD is God.
Fulfillment
Both victories happened as spoken: the young men routed the Syrian camp at Samaria, and at Aphek the Israelites defeated a Syrian army, with a wall falling on twenty-seven thousand who remained. Ahab then spared Ben-hadad and made a covenant with him, and a prophet told him his life would answer for Ben-hadad's, a judgment later completed at Ramoth-gilead. The prophet of the first victory is unnamed in the text, so no name is invented for him here.
