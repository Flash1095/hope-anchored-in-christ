Woes on the Unrepentant Cities

Prophecy
Woes on the Unrepentant Cities
Prophet
Jesus Christ
Date Approx
c. AD 30
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Prophecy Summary
Jesus pronounced woes on Chorazin, Bethsaida, and Capernaum, cities where most of His mighty works had been done yet which had not repented. He said it would be more tolerable for Tyre and Sidon, and even for Sodom, in the day of judgment than for those cities. Capernaum, exalted to heaven in privilege, would be brought down to hell for its unbelief.
Fulfillment
Chorazin, Bethsaida, and Capernaum faded from prominence after the first century, and Capernaum in particular lies in ruins today, a stark contrast to its honoured place in Jesus's ministry. Their loss of standing matches the judgment of privilege Jesus pronounced, though the final day of judgment He named still lies ahead. The prophecy is therefore partially fulfilled, with its historical decline visible and its final judgment still future.
