Blessings, Curses, Exile, and Return

Prophecy Summary

Before Israel entered the land, Moses set before them blessing for obedience and curses for disobedience in full detail: a fruitful land and victory if they kept the covenant, and siege, scattering among all peoples, and a trembling heart if they turned away. Yet Moses also promised that when they were in exile and returned to the LORD with all their heart, God would gather them again, circumcise their hearts, and bring them back into the land.

Fulfillment

Israel's history followed the pattern Moses laid out: the northern kingdom fell to Assyria and Judah was carried to Babylon, with the temple destroyed and the people scattered. Under Cyrus the exiles were allowed to return and rebuild, as Ezra records, which fulfilled the return in its first, historical sense. Because the prophets also speak of a fuller gathering that Christians and Jews understand differently, this entry is marked Partially Fulfilled, with the exile and the return under Cyrus as the firmly historical core.
