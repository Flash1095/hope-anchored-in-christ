The Third Part Brought Through the Fire

Prophecy
The Third Part Brought Through the Fire
Prophet
Zechariah
Date Approx
c. 480 BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
Zechariah predicts that in all the land two parts will be cut off and die, but the third will be left, brought through the fire and refined as silver and tried as gold, calling on God's name and being heard, with God saying 'It is my people' and they saying 'The LORD is my God.'
Fulfillment
Partially fulfilled in the refining of a faithful remnant through successive judgments, including the destruction of Jerusalem; the final purified remnant it pictures is still awaited.
Fulfillment Status
Partially Fulfilled
