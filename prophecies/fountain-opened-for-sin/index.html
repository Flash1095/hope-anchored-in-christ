The Fountain Opened for Sin and Uncleanness

Prophecy
The Fountain Opened for Sin and Uncleanness
Prophet
Zechariah
Date Approx
c. 5th century BC
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Prophecy Summary
In that day, Zechariah declared, there shall be a fountain opened to the house of David and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem for sin and for uncleanness. In the same day God will cut off the names of idols from the land so they are remembered no more, and will cause prophets of falsehood and the unclean spirit to pass out of the land. The cleansing is therefore both inward, washing sin, and outward, ending idolatry and false prophecy in the restored community.
Fulfillment
Christians read this fountain as opened at the cross, where blood and water flowed and cleansing from sin was provided, as John and the epistles describe, and as continuing whenever sinners are washed. Jerusalem has not yet known the complete end of idolatry and false prophecy that Zechariah pictures in the same day, and Jewish tradition reads the full cleansing as still future. The fountain is therefore partly fulfilled: opened in Christ, awaiting its complete cleansing of Jerusalem.
