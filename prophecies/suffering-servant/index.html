The Suffering Servant

The Suffering Servant

Prophecy Summary

Isaiah 53 presents a Servant despised and rejected, pierced for our transgressions and crushed for our iniquities, bearing the sin of many like a lamb led to slaughter. By His wounds, others are healed.

Fulfillment

The New Testament returns to this chapter constantly: Jesus bore our sins in silence, and Philip explained this very passage to the Ethiopian official, who believed and was baptized. It is the Old Testament's fullest portrait of the cross.
