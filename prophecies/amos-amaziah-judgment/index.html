Judgment Pronounced on Amaziah the Priest

Prophecy
Judgment Pronounced on Amaziah the Priest
Prophet
Amos
Date Approx
c. 760 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
Amos tells Amaziah, the priest of Bethel who opposed him, that his wife will become a harlot in the city, his sons and daughters will fall by the sword, his land will be divided by line, he will die in a polluted land, and Israel will surely go into captivity out of its land.
Fulfillment
Debated in its details: Scripture records the pronouncement but does not narrate Amaziah's personal fate, so the specific personal outcomes are stated here as predicted but not as historically confirmed; the captivity of Israel itself is confirmed.
