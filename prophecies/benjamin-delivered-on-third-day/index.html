Benjamin Delivered on the Third Day

Benjamin Delivered on the Third Day

Prophecy Summary

After two defeats before Gibeah, Israel asks through Phinehas whether to go out again against Benjamin, and the LORD answers to go up, for tomorrow he will deliver Benjamin into their hand.

Fulfillment

Fulfilled the next day: the LORD smites Benjamin before Israel (Judges 20:35), ending the civil war at Gibeah.
