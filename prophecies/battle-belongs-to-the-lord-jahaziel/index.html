The Battle Belongs to the LORD

Prophecy
The Battle Belongs to the LORD
Prophet
Jahaziel
Date Approx
c. 870 BC
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Prophecy Summary
When a vast army of Moabites, Ammonites, and Meunites came against Jehoshaphat, the Spirit of the LORD came on Jahaziel the Levite, who told the king and all Judah not to be afraid or dismayed, for the battle was not theirs but God's. They would not need to fight in this battle at all, but were to stand still and see the salvation of the LORD, going out against the enemy the next morning with the LORD with them.
Fulfillment
The next morning, as Judah's singers went out praising the LORD, the LORD set ambushes against the invading armies and they destroyed one another, so that when Judah reached the watchtower they saw only dead bodies and no one had escaped. Judah spent three days gathering the spoil, and the surrounding kingdoms feared God after hearing what He had done. This prophecy survives only in Chronicles, with no Kings parallel, and is therefore a genuinely new Chronicles item.
