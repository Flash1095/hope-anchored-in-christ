Gehazi, Servant of Elisha

Gehazi, Elisha's servant, ran after Naaman for a gift Elisha had refused, lied about it, and was struck with Naaman's leprosy forever.

Key Story

Gehazi takes Naaman's gift and is struck with leprosy.

Life Lesson

Greed that trades a prophet's gift for silver inherits the disease it coveted.
