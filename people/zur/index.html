Summary
Zur was a prince of Midian and the father of Cozbi, and one of the five Midianite kings slain by Israel.
Key Story
Zur's daughter was slain with Zimri at Peor, and Zur himself fell to Israel's sword in the war against Midian.
Key Verses
Numbers 25:15; 31:8
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Cozbi; Moses; Phinehas
Life Lesson
The gods of Midian could not protect their own princes.
