Uriah the Priest

Uriah the priest was one of the two faithful witnesses Isaiah took when he wrote the prophecy of Maher-shalal-hash-baz on a great roll. He is a different man from Urijah the prophet of Jeremiah's day.

Key Story

Isaiah took Uriah the priest and Zechariah son of Jeberechiah as faithful witnesses to the written prophecy.

Life Lesson

God's word is given with witnesses, so that no one can later say it was never spoken.
