Aristarchus

Aristarchus was a Macedonian of Thessalonica who travelled with Paul, was seized in the Ephesian riot, accompanied Paul to Jerusalem and later to Rome, and shared his imprisonment there.

Key Story

Aristarchus was dragged into the Ephesian theatre, sailed with Paul to Rome, and shared his prison.

Life Lesson

Aristarchus stayed from riot to prison. Faithful companions do not leave when the journey turns hard.
