Summary
Hamor was a Hivite prince, the father of Shechem, who tried to arrange a marriage alliance with Jacob's family.
Key Story
After his son wronged Dinah, Hamor negotiated for intermarriage — but Simeon and Levi used the negotiations to destroy his city.
