Abinadab, Son of Jesse

Name
Abinadab, Son of Jesse
Role
Son of Jesse
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Abinadab, Jesse's second son, was passed over by Samuel at the anointing and later served in Saul's army against the Philistines at Elah.
Key Story
Abinadab was presented before Samuel and passed over, then fought at Elah beside his brothers.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 16:8; 1 Samuel 17:13; 1 Chronicles 2:13
Related Books
1 Samuel; 1 Chronicles
Related People
Jesse; King David; Eliab, Son of Jesse; Shammah, Son of Jesse
Life Lesson
Being passed over is not the same as being rejected; every brother had his place in the battle.
