Life of Christ
Summary
Matthew, also called Levi, was a tax collector despised as a collaborator until Jesus called him with two words: Follow Me. He hosted a feast for Jesus and wrote the first Gospel, presenting Jesus as King.
Key Story
Matthew left his tax booth immediately at Jesus' call, rising and following Him without finishing the day's accounts.
Key Verses
Matthew 9:9; Matthew 9:10-13; Luke 5:27-28
Related Books
Matthew
Related People
Jesus; Simon Peter
Life Lesson
If Jesus could call a tax collector to write a Gospel, no one is too far gone or too compromised to be called. Follow Me can rewrite a life in a single step of obedience.
