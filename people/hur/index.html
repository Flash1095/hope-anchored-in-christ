Hur

Name
Hur
Role
Helper of Moses
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Hur was a companion of Moses who held up his hands during the battle with Amalek, and who was left in charge of the people with Aaron while Moses was on the mountain.
Key Story
When Moses' hands grew heavy, Aaron and Hur held them up until sunset, and Israel prevailed over Amalek.
Key Verses
Exodus 17:10-13; Exodus 24:14; Exodus 31:2
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses; Aaron; Joshua; Bezaleel
Life Lesson
Victory often comes through the friends who hold your arms up when you cannot.
