Witch of Endor

The medium of Endor — unnamed in the text — was sought by Saul in disguise on the night before Gilboa, after God had stopped answering him. At his urging she brought up Samuel, who foretold Saul's death and Israel's defeat the next day.

Key Story

The medium of Endor called up Samuel for a king God had stopped answering.

Life Lesson

When God is silent because of disobedience, forbidden doors still open — and what comes through them is judgment.
