Seraiah, Son of Azriel

Seraiah, son of Azriel, was one of the officers Jehoiakim sent to seize Jeremiah and Baruch after the scroll was burned, but the Lord hid them. He is a different man from Seraiah son of Neriah, Seraiah the chief priest, and Seraiah son of Tanhumeth.

Key Story

The king commanded Seraiah son of Azriel to take Jeremiah and Baruch, but the Lord hid them.

Life Lesson

A shared name does not mean a shared story — four Seraiahs walk through Jeremiah, and each must be told apart.
