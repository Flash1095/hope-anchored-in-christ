Rezin, King of Syria

Rezin, king of Syria, allied with Pekah against Ahaz, besieged Jerusalem without taking it, took Elath for Syria, and was slain when Tiglath-Pileser took Damascus.

Key Story

Rezin besieges Jerusalem and is slain at Damascus.

Life Lesson

A siege that fails still leaves its mark, and its maker still falls.
