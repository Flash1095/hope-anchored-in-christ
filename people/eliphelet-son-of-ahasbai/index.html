Eliphelet, Son of Ahasbai

Name

Summary
Eliphelet, son of Ahasbai the Maachathite, was among David's thirty mighty men. He is distinct from Eliphelet son of David, born at Jerusalem.
Key Story
Eliphelet son of Ahasbai the Maachathite stood among David's thirty.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 23:34
Related Books
2 Samuel
Related People
King David; Ahasbai, Father of Eliphelet
Life Lesson
Two Eliphelets — a prince and a warrior — both served the same king.
