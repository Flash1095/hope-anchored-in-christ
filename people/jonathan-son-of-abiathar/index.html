Jonathan, Son of Abiathar

Jonathan, son of Abiathar, served as a secret messenger for David during Absalom's revolt, carrying word from Jerusalem with Ahimaaz and hiding from Absalom's men.

Key Story

Jonathan risked his life as a boy-messenger between Jerusalem and David's camp.

Life Lesson

Young messengers in a civil war carry the kingdom on their feet.
