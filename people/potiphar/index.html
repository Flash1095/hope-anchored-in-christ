Potiphar

Name
Potiphar
Role
Officer of Pharaoh
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Potiphar was an officer of Pharaoh and captain of the guard, who bought Joseph as a slave and put him in charge of his whole house.
Key Story
Potiphar saw that the LORD was with Joseph and made him overseer — until his wife's false accusation sent Joseph to prison.
Key Verses
Genesis 37:36; Genesis 39:1-20
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Joseph, Son of Jacob; Potiphar's Wife
Life Lesson
Favor can turn quickly, but God's presence with Joseph did not depend on his position.
