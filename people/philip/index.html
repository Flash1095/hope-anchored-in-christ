Philip

Name
Philip
Role
Apostle
Testament

Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Philip was an apostle from Bethsaida who brought Nathanael to Jesus and later, in Acts, led the Ethiopian official to faith in a desert-road encounter. He asked Jesus to show them the Father at the Last Supper.
Key Story
Philip told a questioning Nathanael simply, Come and see — and Nathanael met Jesus and believed.
Key Verses
John 1:46; John 14:8-9; Acts 8:35-38
Related Books
John; Acts
Related People
Andrew; Bartholomew; Jesus
Life Lesson
'Come and see' is an invitation anyone can offer. You do not need all the answers to bring someone to Jesus — just bring them to Him.
Related Places
Galilee; Samaria
