Summary
Delilah was the woman Samson loved in the Valley of Sorek, whom the Philistine rulers paid to discover the secret of his strength. Her betrayal cost Samson his eyes and his freedom.
Key Story
Four times Samson misled her, and four times she pressed him until he revealed that his strength lay in his Nazirite hair. While he slept, she had it cut, and the Philistines seized him.
Key Verses
Judges 16:4-21; Hebrews 11:32
Related Books
Judges; Hebrews
Related People

Life Lesson
Delilah is a sober warning about love that is bought and trust given away piece by piece. Guard what God has set apart in you — and notice when affection keeps asking you to surrender it.
