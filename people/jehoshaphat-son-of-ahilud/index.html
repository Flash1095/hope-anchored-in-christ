Jehoshaphat, Son of Ahilud

Jehoshaphat, son of Ahilud, was David's recorder — the officer who kept the chronicles of the reign — through David's administration and into the revolt of Sheba.

Key Story

Jehoshaphat kept the chronicle of David's reign from its height through its civil wars.

Life Lesson

Someone must write the chronicle, even the chapters the king would rather forget.
