Nicolas

Name
Nicolas
Role
Deacon; Proselyte of Antioch
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Nicolas was one of the seven men chosen to serve tables in the Jerusalem church, and the only one Acts describes as a proselyte, a Gentile convert to Judaism from Antioch.
Key Story
Nicolas, a proselyte of Antioch, was chosen among the seven, a Gentile convert serving a Jewish church's widows.
Key Verses
Acts 6:5
Related Books
Acts
Related People
Stephen; Philip; Simon Peter
Life Lesson
The gospel's first officers already crossed the Jew-Gentile line. Nicolas served widows not his own by birth.
