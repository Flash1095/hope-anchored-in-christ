Ziba

Name
Ziba
Role
Servant of Saul's House
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Ziba had been a servant of Saul's house. He told David of Mephibosheth, Jonathan's lame son, then slandered him to David during Absalom's revolt, claiming Mephibosheth hoped to regain the kingdom. David divided the land between them, and Ziba kept half.
Key Story
Ziba first restored Mephibosheth to David's table, then slandered him for half his land.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 9:2; 2 Samuel 9:9; 2 Samuel 16:1; 2 Samuel 16:4; 2 Samuel 19:17
Related Books
2 Samuel
Related People
King David; Mephibosheth, Son of Jonathan; King Saul
Life Lesson
A servant's word can restore a prince or rob him — and kings must weigh slander carefully.
