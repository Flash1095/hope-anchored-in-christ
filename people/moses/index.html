Moses

Name
Moses
Role
Prophet; Lawgiver; Deliverer
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Moses led Israel out of slavery in Egypt, received the Ten Commandments on Mount Sinai, and guided God's people through forty years in the wilderness. He is the great prophet and lawgiver of the Old Testament.
Key Story
At the burning bush, a reluctant Moses was commissioned by God, whose name is I AM, to lead Israel out of Egypt.
Key Verses
Exodus 3:10-14; Exodus 14:21; Deuteronomy 34:10-12; Hebrews 11:24-27
Related Books
Exodus; Leviticus; Numbers; Deuteronomy; Hebrews
Related People
Aaron; Miriam; Joshua
Life Lesson
Moses felt inadequate for his calling, and God answered, I will be with you. Whatever God asks of you, His presence is the anchor that makes it possible.
Related Places
Egypt; Goshen; Midian; Mount Sinai; Kadesh Barnea; Plains of Moab
