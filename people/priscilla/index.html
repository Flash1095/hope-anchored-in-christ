Priscilla

Priscilla, with her husband Aquila, was a tentmaker, church host, and gifted teacher in the early church. She helped explain the way of God more accurately to the eloquent Apollos.

Key Story

Priscilla and Aquila invited Apollos home and taught him the way of God more adequately, strengthening a preacher who strengthened many.

Life Lesson

Priscilla's living room became a classroom and a church. God uses open homes and well-taught hearts to shape the next generation of leaders — yours included.
