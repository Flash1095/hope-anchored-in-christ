Eliab, Son of Pallu

Name
Eliab, Son of Pallu
Role
Father of Dathan and Abiram
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Eliab, son of Pallu, was the father of Dathan and Abiram, the Reubenites who joined Korah's rebellion.
Key Story
Eliab's sons Dathan and Abiram rose against Moses and were swallowed by the earth with their households.
Key Verses
Numbers 16:1; 26:8-9
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Dathan; Abiram; Phallu; Moses
Life Lesson
A father's grief can be the warning a whole people needs to hear.
