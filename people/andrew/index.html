Life of Christ
Summary
Andrew was a fisherman from Bethsaida, brother of Peter, and one of the first disciples to follow Jesus. He is known for bringing people to Jesus — first his brother, then a boy with loaves and fishes, then Greek seekers.
Key Story
Andrew first found his brother Simon and told him, We have found the Messiah, and he brought him to Jesus.
Key Verses
John 1:41-42; John 6:8-9; John 12:22

Life Lesson
Andrew's ministry was bringing one person at a time to Jesus. Never underestimate the eternal weight of a single introduction — your brother, neighbour, or friend could be next.
