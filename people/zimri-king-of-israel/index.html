Zimri, King of Israel

Name
Zimri, King of Israel
Role
King of Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Zimri, captain of half Elah's chariots, slew Elah and all Baasha's house, reigned seven days in Tirzah, and when Omri besieged him burned the king's house over himself and died.
Key Story
Zimri's seven-day reign ends in fire.
Key Verses
1 Kings 16:9; 1 Kings 16:18
Related Books
1 Kings
Related People
Elah, King of Israel; Omri, King of Israel
Life Lesson
Seven days of power bought by murder is no reign at all.
