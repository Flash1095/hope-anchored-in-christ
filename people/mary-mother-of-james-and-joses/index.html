Mary, Mother of James and Joses

Mary, the mother of James and Joses, followed Jesus and watched His crucifixion and burial. She came to the tomb on the first day of the week. She is traditionally identified with Mary the wife of Clopas named in John's Gospel, though the texts give the two descriptions separately.

Key Story

This Mary stood at the cross, watched where He was laid, and returned to anoint a body that was already risen.

Life Lesson

She watched the burial and still came to serve. Love does not wait for proof before it shows up.
