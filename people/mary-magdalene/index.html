Mary Magdalene

Mary Magdalene was a woman delivered by Jesus from seven demons who followed and supported His ministry, stood at the cross, and became the first witness of the resurrection. Jesus charged her to announce His rising to the apostles.

Key Story

Weeping at the empty tomb, Mary turned and saw Jesus, mistaking Him for the gardener until He spoke her name: Mary.

Life Lesson

Jesus called Mary by name, and grief turned to mission in a moment. He still calls His weeping ones by name — and sends them out as witnesses.
