Joab

Summary
Joab, son of Zeruiah, commanded David's army through all his wars. He won Jerusalem, killed Abner in revenge for Asahel, murdered Amasa to keep his command, and killed Absalom against David's order. David never removed him, but charged Solomon to deal with him.
Key Story
Joab won David's wars and murdered David's rivals — loyal, ruthless, and finally condemned.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 2:13; 2 Samuel 3:27; 2 Samuel 8:16; 2 Samuel 18:14; 2 Samuel 20:10; Psalm 60:1

Related People

Life Lesson
A commander who kills for revenge and ambition will answer for it, however many battles he won.
