Summary
Secundus was a Thessalonian who accompanied Paul on his journey toward Jerusalem with the collection for the saints, travelling with the company as far as Troas.
Key Story
Secundus of Thessalonica walked with Paul's company toward Jerusalem, bearing the churches' gift.
