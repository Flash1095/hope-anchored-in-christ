Zohar, Son of Simeon

Name
Zohar, Son of Simeon
Role
Son of Simeon

Summary
Zohar was a son of Simeon and a grandson of Jacob, named among the family that went into Egypt.
Key Story
Zohar is named among Simeon's six sons — a different Zohar from the father of Ephron.
Key Verses
Genesis 46:10
Related Books

Life Lesson
