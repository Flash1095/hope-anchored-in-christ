Shallum, Son of Josiah

Shallum, son of Josiah, also called Jehoahaz, reigned three months in Jerusalem before Pharaoh-necho carried him to Egypt, where he died. Jeremiah told his people not to weep for dead Josiah but for Shallum, who would never return.

Key Story

Shallum reigned in his father Josiah's place, was taken to Egypt, and never saw his native land again.

Life Lesson

A three-month reign can still end in a permanent exile — length of rule is no measure of its weight.
