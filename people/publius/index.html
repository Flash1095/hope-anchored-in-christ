Summary
Publius was the chief man of Malta who courteously lodged Paul's shipwrecked company for three days, and whose father Paul healed of fever and dysentery, after which others on the island came and were healed.
Key Story
Publius lodged the shipwrecked company three days, and his father's healing opened the island to Paul.
Key Verses
Acts 28:7

Life Lesson
