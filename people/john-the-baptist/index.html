John the Baptist

Life of Christ
Summary
John the Baptist was the prophesied forerunner who prepared the way for Jesus, baptising crowds in the Jordan and calling Israel to repentance. He baptised Jesus Himself and pointed his own disciples to the Lamb of God.
Key Story
John declared of Jesus, Behold, the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world, and baptised Him as heaven opened.
Key Verses
Matthew 3:11; John 1:29; Luke 3:16
Related Books
Matthew; Mark; Luke; John
Related People
Jesus; Elizabeth; Zechariah, Father of John the Baptist
Life Lesson
John's joy was to point away from himself: He must increase, I must decrease. A life that points others to Jesus is a life well spent, whatever its size.
