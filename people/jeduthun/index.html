Jeduthun

Name
Jeduthun
Role
Psalmist; Musician
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Jeduthun was a musician named in the titles of Psalms 39, 62, and 77, psalms of waiting, silence, and remembered deliverance.
Key Story
His psalms counsel quiet trust: waiting silently for God alone, remembering God's wonders of old when the present feels empty.
Key Verses
Psalm 39:1; Psalm 62:1; 1 Chronicles 16:41; 1 Chronicles 25:1; Nehemiah 11:17; 2 Chronicles 5:12; 2 Chronicles 29:14; 2 Chronicles 35:15
Related Books
Psalms; 1 Chronicles; Nehemiah; 2 Chronicles
Related People
Asaph; Heman, Son of Joel; King Josiah
Life Lesson
Jeduthun's music teaches stillness — sometimes the most faithful thing is to wait silently for God rather than speak.

Jerusalem
