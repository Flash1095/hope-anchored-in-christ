Summary
Chuza was the steward of Herod Antipas' household and the husband of Joanna, who followed and supported Jesus. His household thus touched both Herod's court and Jesus' ministry.
Key Story
Chuza managed Herod's house while his wife Joanna travelled with Jesus and supported Him.
Key Verses
Luke 8:3
Related Books
Luke
Related People
Joanna; Herod Antipas
Life Lesson
God works inside powerful households through ordinary stewards. Chuza's home became a bridge between court and kingdom.
