Joshua, Son of Jehozadak

Name
Joshua, Son of Jehozadak
Role
High Priest
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Summary
Joshua, son of Jehozadak, was the high priest of the returned exiles, working alongside Zerubbabel the governor and the prophets Haggai and Zechariah. Zechariah's visions show him standing before the LORD in filthy garments, cleansed and clothed anew — a picture of Israel's own restoration. He is a different man from Joshua son of Nun.
Key Story
Joshua the high priest stood before the LORD in filthy garments, was cleansed and given a clean turban, and crowned with the hopes of the returning nation.
Key Verses
Haggai 1:1; Zechariah 3:1; Zechariah 6:11
Related Books
Haggai; Zechariah; Ezra
Related People
Haggai; Zechariah the Prophet; Zerubbabel; Jehozadak
Life Lesson
Joshua's filthy garments became clean ones — not by his scrubbing, but by God's word. Restoration is a gift, not an achievement.
