Joab, Son of Zeruiah

Joab, David's commander, slew Abner and Amasa in peacetime, fled to the altar when Solomon took the throne, and was slain there by Benaiah for the blood he had shed.

Key Story

Joab flees to the altar and is slain by Benaiah.

Life Lesson

A career of victories cannot cover peacetime murders: blood returns on its own head.
