Summary
Bera was the king of Sodom who rebelled against Chedorlaomer and lost the battle in the Valley of Siddim.
Key Story
Bera's defeat left Sodom plundered and Lot captured — until Abram rescued them and Bera offered Abram the recovered goods, which Abram refused.
