Judas Iscariot

Name
Judas Iscariot
Role
Apostle; Betrayer
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Judas Iscariot was one of the Twelve who betrayed Jesus to the chief priests for thirty pieces of silver with a kiss in Gethsemane. Overcome with remorse, he ended his own life — a sobering warning in Scripture.
Key Story
Judas betrayed Jesus with a kiss in the garden, identifying Him to the armed crowd that came to arrest Him.
Key Verses
Matthew 26:49; Matthew 27:3-5; John 13:27
Related Books
Matthew; Mark; Luke; John
Related People
Jesus; Simon Peter
Life Lesson
Judas walked with Jesus yet let greed and despair close his heart — while Peter, who also failed, ran back to mercy. When you fail, run to Jesus like Peter, not away like Judas.
Related Places
Jerusalem
Related Events
The Last Supper; Jesus Prays in Gethsemane
