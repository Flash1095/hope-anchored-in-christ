The Samaritan Woman

Name
The Samaritan Woman
Role
Evangelist; Woman at the Well
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
The Samaritan woman met Jesus at Jacob's well, received His offer of living water, and became the first evangelist to Samaria. Her testimony brought her whole town out to meet Jesus.
Key Story
Leaving her water jar behind, she ran to the town saying, Come, see a man who told me everything I ever did — and many Samaritans believed.
Key Verses
John 4:14; John 4:28-29; John 4:39
Related Books
John
Related People
Jesus; Jacob
Life Lesson
Jesus crossed every barrier to offer one thirsty woman living water, and she became a town's witness. He will meet you at your well today — and your story will bring others to Him.
