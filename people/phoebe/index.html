Summary
Phoebe was a deacon of the church at Cenchreae and a benefactor of many, including Paul, who commended her warmly to the Roman church. Paul likely entrusted her to carry his letter to the Romans.
Key Story
Paul asked Rome to receive Phoebe in a way worthy of God's people and give her anything she might need, for she had been the patron of many.
Key Verses
Romans 16:1-2
Related Books
Romans
Related People
Paul; Priscilla
Life Lesson
Phoebe carried the greatest letter ever written in her hands, entrusted because she had served so faithfully. Faithful service in small things qualifies us for sacred trusts.
