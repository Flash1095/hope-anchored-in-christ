Summary
Heman was named among the wise men with whom Solomon's wisdom was compared. Chronicles also names a Heman son of Zerah and a Heman son of Joel; whether any of these is the same man is not settled, so the profiles are kept separate.
Key Story
Heman named among the wise.
Key Verses
1 Kings 4:31; Psalm 88:1
Related Books

Related People

Life Lesson
Wisdom's fame is a gift, not a guarantee.
