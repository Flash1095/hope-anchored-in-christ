Jehoshabeath, Daughter of Jehoram

Name
Jehoshabeath, Daughter of Jehoram
Role
Daughter of the King; Wife of Jehoiada
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Jehoshabeath, daughter of King Jehoram and wife of Jehoiada the priest, stole the baby Joash from among the king's sons being slain and hid him and his nurse in a bedchamber from Athaliah.
Key Story
Jehoshabeath, sister of Ahaziah, hid Joash in the house of God six years while Athaliah reigned over the land.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 22:11; 2 Chronicles 22:12
Related Books
2 Chronicles
Related People
Jehoram, King of Judah; Jehoiada, the Priest; Joash, King of Judah
Life Lesson
One woman's courage preserved David's line and the promise with it.
