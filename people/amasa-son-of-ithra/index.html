Amasa, Son of Ithra

Name
Amasa, Son of Ithra
Role
Commander under Absalom and David
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Amasa, son of Ithra and of Abigail, David's sister, commanded Absalom's army in the revolt. David forgave him and made him commander in Joab's place, and Joab murdered him at Gibeon while greeting him as a brother.
Key Story
Amasa led Absalom's revolt, was forgiven and promoted — then murdered by Joab with a greeting kiss.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 17:25; 2 Samuel 19:13; 2 Samuel 20:4; 2 Samuel 20:10
Related Books

Related People

Life Lesson
Forgiveness from a king is no shield against a rival's dagger.
