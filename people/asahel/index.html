Asahel

Asahel, son of Zeruiah and brother of Joab, was swift of foot as a wild roe. He chased Abner alone at Gibeon and would not turn aside, so Abner killed him with the hinder end of his spear, and Joab never forgave it.

Key Story

Asahel's speed carried him alone after Abner — and Abner killed him for it.

Life Lesson

Speed without wisdom runs alone into a spear.
