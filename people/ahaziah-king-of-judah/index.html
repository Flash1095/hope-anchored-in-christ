Ahaziah, King of Judah

Ahaziah, son of Jehoram and Athaliah, reigned one year, walked in the ways of Ahab's house, and was slain by Jehu while visiting the wounded Jehoram of Israel at Jezreel.

Key Story

Ahaziah visits Jehoram and is slain by Jehu.

Life Lesson

A one-year reign can still reveal a heart: Ahaziah followed his mother's house rather than the LORD.
