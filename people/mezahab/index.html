Mezahab

Mezahab was the father of Matred and the grandfather of Mehetabel, the wife of Hadar, king of Edom.

Key Story

Mezahab is named only at the end of Edom's king list, in his granddaughter's lineage.

Life Lesson

Even a single mention means God did not overlook him.
