Bashemath, Daughter of Ishmael

Name
Bashemath, Daughter of Ishmael
Role
Wife of Esau
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Bashemath was a wife of Esau, the daughter of Ishmael and the sister of Nebajoth, and the mother of Reuel. She is also called Mahalath in Genesis 28.
Key Story
Bashemath tied Esau's house to Ishmael's line, and her son Reuel fathered four dukes of Edom.
Key Verses
Genesis 28:9; Genesis 36:3-4; Genesis 36:10-13
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Esau; Ishmael; Nebajoth; Reuel
Life Lesson
God's story keeps moving through families we know almost nothing about — and He knows them all.
