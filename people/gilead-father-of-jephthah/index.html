Gilead, Father of Jephthah

Gilead is named only as the father of Jephthah the judge, whose other sons drove Jephthah out.

Key Story

Gilead begat Jephthah, and Gilead's wife's sons thrust Jephthah out, saying he should have no inheritance in their father's house.

Life Lesson

A father's house can cast out its own son — and later beg him back.
