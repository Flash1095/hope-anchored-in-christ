Amos
Role
Prophet; Shepherd
Testament

Timeline Era

Summary
Amos was a shepherd and fig farmer from Tekoa sent to confront wealthy, complacent Israel with God's demand for justice. He insisted true worship must flow into righteous living.
Key Story
Amos declared, I am neither a prophet nor the son of a prophet, yet God took him from following the flock to speak His word to kings.
Key Verses
Amos 5:24; Amos 7:14-15; Amos 9:11
Related Books
Amos
Related People
Hosea; Isaiah
Life Lesson
God used a farmer with no credentials to speak truth to a nation — He can use you right where you are. Let justice roll like a river, starting with your own life.

Samaria; Bethel
