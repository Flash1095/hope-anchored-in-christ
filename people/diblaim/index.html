Summary
Diblaim is named only as the father of Gomer, Hosea's wife (Hosea 1:3). Nothing else is told of him.
Key Story
Diblaim's only mention is as the father of Gomer, whom Hosea married at God's command.

Related People

Life Lesson
Diblaim's single verse is enough to place him in the story. God remembers fathers whose only recorded act is a daughter He wove into prophecy.
