Armoni, Son of Saul

Name
Armoni, Son of Saul
Role
Son of Saul by Rizpah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Armoni, son of Saul by his concubine Rizpah, was handed over with his brother Mephibosheth to the Gibeonites in atonement for Saul's slaughter of Gibeon, and was hanged at the beginning of barley harvest.
Key Story
Armoni, Rizpah's son, was hanged for a bloodguilt he did not commit.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 21:8
Related Books
2 Samuel
Related People
King Saul; Rizpah, Daughter of Aiah; Mephibosheth, Son of Saul
Life Lesson
The sins of kings are sometimes paid by sons who never sat on a throne.
