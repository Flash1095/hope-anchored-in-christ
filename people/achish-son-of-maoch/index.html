Achish, Son of Maoch

Name
Achish, Son of Maoch
Role
King of Gath
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Achish, son of Maoch and king of Gath, twice sheltered David. David feigned madness before him to escape, and later lived under his protection at Ziklag, raiding Israel's enemies while Achish believed he fought for Philistia.
Key Story
Achish sheltered the very man destined to defeat his people, fooled twice by David's acting.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 21:10; 1 Samuel 27:2; 1 Samuel 27:6; 1 Samuel 29:6
Related Books
1 Samuel
Related People
King David; Maoch
Life Lesson
God can hide his anointed in the enemy's own city when the time is not yet ripe.
