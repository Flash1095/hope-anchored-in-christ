King Asa

Name
King Asa
Role
King of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Asa was a good king of Judah who removed idols, renewed the covenant, and trusted God against a vast Cushite army. His later years sadly turned from seeking the Lord.
Key Story
Outnumbered, Asa cried to God that there is no one like Him to help the powerless, and God struck down the Cushites before Judah.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 14:11; 2 Chronicles 15:2; 1 Kings 15:14; 1 Kings 15:9; 1 Kings 15:13
Related Books
1 Kings; 2 Chronicles
Related People
King Jehoshaphat; Maachah, Daughter of Absalom; Ben-Hadad I, King of Syria; Baasha, King of Israel
Life Lesson
Asa's cry is a perfect prayer for the overwhelmed: Lord, help the powerless. Bring Him your impossible odds — He specialises in battles you cannot win alone.
