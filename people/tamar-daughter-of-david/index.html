Tamar, Daughter of David

Name
Tamar, Daughter of David
Role
Daughter of David
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Tamar, daughter of David and sister of Absalom, was assaulted by her half-brother Amnon, who then cast her out. She lived desolate in Absalom's house, and her brother's vengeance for her began the road to civil war.
Key Story
Tamar's desolation after Amnon's crime set Absalom on the path to rebellion.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 13:1; 2 Samuel 13:19; 2 Samuel 13:20; 2 Samuel 13:32
Related Books
2 Samuel
Related People
King David; Absalom; Amnon; Maacah, Daughter of Talmai
Life Lesson
A wronged sister's grief, left unjudged by her father, can burn down a kingdom.
