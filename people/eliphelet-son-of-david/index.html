Eliphelet, Son of David

Name
Eliphelet, Son of David
Role
Son of David
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Eliphelet, son of David, was born at Jerusalem and named last among the sons born to him there. He is distinct from Eliphelet son of Ahasbai, one of the mighty men.
Key Story
Eliphelet son of David was born at Jerusalem, sharing a name with one of his father's warriors.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 5:16; 1 Chronicles 3:6; 1 Chronicles 3:8; 1 Chronicles 14:5; 1 Chronicles 14:7
Related Books
2 Samuel; 1 Chronicles
Related People
King David
Life Lesson
A king's son and a king's warrior can share a name and a lifetime.
