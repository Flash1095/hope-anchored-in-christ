Ezra

Name
Ezra
Role
Priest; Scribe
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Summary
Ezra was a priest and expert scribe who led a second return from exile and read the Law aloud to a weeping, repenting Jerusalem. He devoted himself to studying, obeying, and teaching God's word.
Key Story
Ezra stood on a wooden platform and read the Book of the Law from daybreak till noon, while the people listened, wept, and then celebrated with joy.
Key Verses
Ezra 7:10; Nehemiah 8:9-10
Related Books
Ezra; Nehemiah
Related People
Nehemiah; Zerubbabel
Life Lesson
Ezra's pattern still builds strong lives: study the Word, do it, then teach it. And his day of tears ended in feasting, for the joy of the Lord is your strength.
Related Places
Babylon; Jerusalem
