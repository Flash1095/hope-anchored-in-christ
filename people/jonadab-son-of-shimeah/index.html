Jonadab, Son of Shimeah

Jonadab, son of Shimeah, David's brother, was a very subtil man and Amnon's friend. He devised the trick by which Amnon lured Tamar into his chamber, and later assured David that only Amnon, not all his sons, had been slain.

Key Story

Jonadab's clever plan put Tamar in Amnon's chamber — and his counsel was the crime's first step.

Life Lesson

Clever friends who plan another's sin share the guilt of it.
