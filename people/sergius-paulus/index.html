Summary
Sergius Paulus was the Roman proconsul of Cyprus, a prudent man who called for Barnabas and Saul to hear the word of God. When Elymas opposed them and was struck blind, the proconsul believed, astonished at the doctrine of the Lord.
Key Story
Sergius Paulus sent for the word of God, watched his sorcerer go blind, and believed the doctrine of the Lord.
Key Verses
Acts 13:7

Life Lesson
A prudent ruler can still be astonished by the gospel. Sergius Paulus listened, watched, and believed.
