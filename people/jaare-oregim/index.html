Jaare-oregim

Jaare-oregim of Bethlehem is named as the father of Elhanan, who struck down a Philistine giant at Gob.

Key Story

Jaare-oregim's son fought giants in David's later wars.

Life Lesson

A father's name survives in a son's single great blow.
