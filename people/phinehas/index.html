Summary
Phinehas was the son of Eleazar and the grandson of Aaron, and a priest known for his zeal for God's honor.
Key Story
Phinehas turned away God's wrath in the wilderness by acting against open sin, and God gave him a covenant of an everlasting priesthood.
Key Verses
Exodus 6:25; Numbers 25:7-13
