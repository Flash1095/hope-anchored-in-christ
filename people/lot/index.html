Lot

Name
Lot
Role
Nephew of Abraham
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Lot was Abraham's nephew who travelled with him to Canaan and settled near Sodom. God rescued him before judging that wicked city.
Key Story
When angels warned Lot to flee Sodom, he hesitated, and the angels took him by the hand because the Lord was merciful to him.
Key Verses
Genesis 13:11-12; Genesis 19:16; 2 Peter 2:7-8
Related Books
Genesis; 2 Peter
Related People
Abraham; Sarah
Life Lesson
Lot shows how compromise slowly pulls us toward danger, yet God's mercy still reached for him. Mercy can take your hand today and lead you out, too.

Canaan
