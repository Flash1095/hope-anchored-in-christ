Isaiah

Isaiah was the great prophet of Judah whose book contains sweeping promises of the coming Messiah, the Suffering Servant, and a new heavens and earth. He ministered under several kings including Hezekiah.

Key Story

In the Temple Isaiah saw the Lord high and lifted up, cried Woe is me, was cleansed by a coal from the altar, and answered, Here am I; send me.

Life Lesson

Isaiah promises that those who hope in the Lord will renew their strength and soar on wings like eagles. The hope he preached points straight to Jesus, wounded for our healing.
