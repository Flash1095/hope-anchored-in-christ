Bani the Levite

Levite who taught the law at Ezra's reading, prayed at the covenant renewal, and sealed the covenant; father of Uzzi the overseer (Nehemiah 8:7, 9:4, 9:5, 10:13, 11:22).

Key Story

Ezra reads the law

Life Lesson

Teaching, praying, and signing: one Levite did all three.
