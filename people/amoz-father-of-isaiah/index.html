Amoz, Father of Isaiah

Amoz was the father of Isaiah the prophet, who wrote the acts of Uzziah and prayed with Hezekiah against Sennacherib.

Key Story

Amoz is named as the father of Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz.

Life Lesson

A prophet's father is remembered because his son spoke for God.
