Ebed, Father of Gaal

Ebed is named only as the father of Gaal, who led the revolt at Shechem against Abimelech.

Key Story

Ebed appears in Judges only as Gaal's father, arriving with him at Shechem.

Life Lesson

A father's name rides on a son's rebellion.
