Summary
Ethan the Ezrahite is named in the title of Psalm 89, a psalm that celebrates God's covenant with David and then wrestles with its apparent failure. Chronicles also names an Ethan son of Zerah, an Ethan son of Zimmah, and an Ethan son of Kushaiah; whether any of these is the same man is not settled, so the profiles are kept separate.
Key Story
Psalm 89 moves from praise for God's promises to David to a raw question — how long will the covenant seem broken? — and ends still appealing to God's steadfast love.

Life Lesson
Ethan models holding God to His promises honestly: praise and protest can live in the same prayer.
