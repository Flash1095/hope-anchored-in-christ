Summary
Cush the Benjamite is named in the title of Psalm 7, a psalm David sang concerning Cush's words against him — a distinct man from Cush the son of Ham.
Key Story
David answers slander with an appeal to the righteous Judge, asking God to vindicate him if he is innocent and to stop the violence of his accuser.

Life Lesson
When falsely accused, David's example is to take the case to God rather than take revenge into one's own hands.
