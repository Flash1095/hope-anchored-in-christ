Ishmael

Name
Ishmael
Role
Son of Abraham
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Ishmael was Abraham's son by Hagar, born before Isaac. God heard his mother's cries, blessed him, and made him father of twelve princes.
Key Story
Cast out into the wilderness of Beersheba, Ishmael was dying of thirst when God opened Hagar's eyes to a well of water.
Key Verses
Genesis 17:20; Genesis 21:17-20
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Abraham; Hagar; Isaac
Life Lesson
God heard the cry of a boy the world had cast aside. No one is outside the hearing of God, and no child of yours is beyond His care.
Related Places
Canaan; Beersheba
