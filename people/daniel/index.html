Summary
Daniel was a young exile who resolved not to defile himself in Babylon and rose to advise kings of Babylon and Persia. God gave him wisdom, visions of coming kingdoms, and protection in a den of lions.
Key Story
Daniel spent a night in a lions' den for praying openly to God, and at dawn the king found him unharmed because he had trusted his God.
Key Verses
Daniel 1:8; Daniel 6:22; Daniel 2:20-21

Related People

Life Lesson
Daniel thrived in a culture hostile to his faith without surrendering his convictions. You can stand firm in your Babylon too — God honours those who honour Him.
