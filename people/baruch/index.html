Summary
Baruch, son of Neriah, was Jeremiah's scribe. He wrote Jeremiah's words in a roll, read them in the temple, rewrote the scroll after Jehoiakim burned it, bought the field at Anathoth as witness to the promise, and was carried to Egypt with Jeremiah. When he despaired of his own sorrows, God told him to seek not great things for himself.
Key Story
Baruch wrote at Jeremiah's mouth all the words of the Lord in a roll, read it in the Lord's house, and wrote it again after the king burned it, with many like words added.
