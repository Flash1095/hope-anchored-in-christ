Life of Christ
Summary
Simon the Pharisee invited Jesus to eat at his house, where a sinful woman washed Jesus' feet with tears and anointed them. Jesus answered Simon's silent judgment with the parable of the two debtors.
Key Story
Simon gave Jesus a meal but no water for His feet, and watched a weeping woman love much because she was forgiven much.
Key Verses
Luke 7:36
Related Books
Luke
Related People
Jesus
Life Lesson
Simon kept the rules of hospitality and missed its heart. Forgiveness, not formality, teaches love.
