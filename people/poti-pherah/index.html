Poti-pherah

Potiphera was the priest of On, and the father of Asenath, Joseph's wife.

Key Story

Potiphera is named only as Asenath's father, in the marriage that joined Joseph to an Egyptian priestly family.

Life Lesson

Even a single mention means God did not overlook him.
