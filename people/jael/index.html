Jael

Name
Jael
Role
Heroine; Wife of Heber
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Jael was the wife of Heber the Kenite, whose tent became the unlikely end of Canaan's feared general Sisera. Deborah's song calls her most blessed among women.
Key Story
When Sisera fled the battle and sought shelter in her tent, Jael gave him milk and a place to rest — then drove a tent peg through his temple while he slept, ending twenty years of oppression.
Key Verses
Judges 4:17-22; Judges 5:24-27
Related Books
Judges
Related People
Deborah; Barak
Life Lesson
Jael's courage came in an ordinary tent, with ordinary tools, in a moment she did not plan. God can use whoever is willing, right where they are, to break a long oppression.
