Joah

Joah, son of Asaph, was Hezekiah's recorder and one of the three officials who met Assyria's envoys at Jerusalem's wall.

Key Story

Joah stood with Eliakim and Shebna at the wall and asked the Rabshakeh to speak in Syrian rather than in the Jews' language, so the people on the wall would not hear.

Life Lesson

Even a minor official's careful words matter when a city is under threat.
