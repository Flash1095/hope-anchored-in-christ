Bathsheba

Name
Bathsheba
Role

Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Bathsheba was the wife of Uriah whom David took, becoming later David's wife and Solomon's mother. She is named in Jesus' genealogy, a sign of grace over a painful story.
Key Story
Bathsheba interceded with an aging David to secure Solomon's throne, ensuring God's chosen successor was crowned.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 12:24; 1 Kings 1:28-31; Matthew 1:6
Related Books
2 Samuel; 1 Kings; Matthew
Related People
King David; King Solomon; Nathan
Life Lesson
God brought Solomon — whose name is linked to peace — from Bathsheba's sorrow, and placed her in the Messiah's line. God can bring peace and purpose from your most painful chapter.
