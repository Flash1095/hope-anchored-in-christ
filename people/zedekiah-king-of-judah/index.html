Zedekiah, King of Judah

Name
Zedekiah, King of Judah
Role
King of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Summary
Zedekiah, born Mattaniah, was made king by Nebuchadnezzar, rebelled in his ninth year, and watched Jerusalem fall; his sons were slain before his eyes, he was blinded, and he died a prisoner in Babylon.
Key Story
Zedekiah rebels, Jerusalem falls, and he is blinded and bound.
Key Verses
2 Kings 24:17; 2 Kings 25:7; 2 Chronicles 36:10; 2 Chronicles 36:12; 2 Chronicles 36:13; Jeremiah 21:1; Jeremiah 32:4-5; Jeremiah 38:14-23; Jeremiah 39:5-7

Life Lesson
