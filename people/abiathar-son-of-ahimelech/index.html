Abiathar, Son of Ahimelech

Name
Abiathar, Son of Ahimelech
Role
High Priest
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Abiathar, son of Ahimelech, alone escaped the massacre at Nob, fleeing to David with the ephod. He served as David's priest through his fugitive years and his reign, bringing the ephod whenever David inquired of God.
Key Story
Abiathar escaped Nob with the ephod and carried God's guidance to David for forty years.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 22:20; 1 Samuel 23:6; 1 Samuel 30:7; 2 Samuel 8:17; 2 Samuel 15:24
Related Books
1 Samuel; 2 Samuel
Related People
Ahimelech, Son of Ahitub; King David; Zadok, Son of Ahitub; Ahimelech, Son of Abiathar
Life Lesson
Surviving a massacre is not the end of a calling; Abiathar served a lifetime after his worst day.
