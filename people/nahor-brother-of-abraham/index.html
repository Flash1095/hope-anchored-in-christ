Nahor, Brother of Abraham

Name
Nahor, Brother of Abraham
Role
Brother of Abraham
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Nahor was a son of Terah and the brother of Abraham and Haran. He married Milcah, the daughter of his brother Haran.
Key Story
Nahor stayed in the family's homeland while Abraham journeyed to Canaan; his children through Milcah and Reumah became the family Abraham later turned to for Isaac's wife.
Key Verses
Genesis 11:26-29; Genesis 22:20-24
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Terah; Abraham; Milcah; Bethuel; Rebekah
Life Lesson
God works through the family members who stay as well as those who go.
