Hadar, King of Edom

Hadar reigned in Edom after Baal-hanan died, from the city of Pau. His wife was Mehetabel.

Key Story

Hadar is the last king named in Edom's king list in Genesis — a different Hadar from Ishmael's son.

Life Lesson

Kingdoms rise and fall, but God's promise to Israel outlasted every throne of Edom.
