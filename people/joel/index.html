Summary
Joel, son of Pethuel, prophesied to Judah, likely in the divided-monarchy period (his exact date is debated). Using a devastating locust plague as his picture, he called the people to repent and promised that God would pour out His Spirit on all flesh.
Key Story
Joel saw locusts strip the land bare and called priests and people to a solemn fast and repentance, promising restoration and the outpouring of God's Spirit.
Key Verses
Joel 1:1; Joel 2:28

Life Lesson
When disaster strips everything bare, Joel's answer is not despair but return — rend your heart, not your garments, and God restores.
