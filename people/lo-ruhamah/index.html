Summary
Lo-Ruhamah (KJV 'Lo-ruhamah'), daughter of Hosea and Gomer, was named by God as a sign: her name means 'not pitied' or 'no mercy,' declaring that God would no longer have mercy on the house of Israel, though He would still have mercy on Judah.
Key Story
God named Hosea's daughter Lo-Ruhamah — 'not pitied' — as a living sign that His patience with Israel's idolatry had run out.
Key Verses
Hosea 1:6; Hosea 1:8
Related Books
Hosea
Related People
Hosea; Gomer
Life Lesson
Mercy refused long enough becomes judgment pronounced. Lo-Ruhamah's name is a sobering reminder that God's patience, though long, is not endless.
