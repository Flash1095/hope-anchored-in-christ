Summary
Regemmelech (KJV 'Regem-melech') was sent with Sherezer from Bethel to Jerusalem to pray before the LORD and ask about the fasts of the exile (Zechariah 7:2).
Key Story
Regemmelech, sent with Sherezer from Bethel, asked whether the fasts of the exile should continue — and heard God's answer about justice over ritual.

Life Lesson
Regemmelech's question got an answer bigger than fasting: God wanted mercy, not mourning. Ask God about your habits; expect Him to examine your heart.
