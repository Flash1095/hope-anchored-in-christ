Summary
Elijah was a fearless prophet who confronted Baal worship, called down fire on Mount Carmel, and was taken to heaven in a chariot of fire. He appeared with Moses at Jesus' transfiguration.
Key Story
On Mount Carmel, Elijah prayed and the fire of the Lord fell, consuming the sacrifice and turning Israel's heart back to God.
Key Verses
1 Kings 18:36-39; 1 Kings 19:11-12; Malachi 4:5; James 5:17

Life Lesson
Elijah was a human being just like us, yet his prayers moved heaven — and after Carmel he battled despair and met God's gentle whisper. Both your boldness and your burnout are known to God.
