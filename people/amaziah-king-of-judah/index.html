Amaziah, King of Judah

Name
Amaziah, King of Judah
Role
King of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Amaziah defeated Edom but brought Edom's gods home and worshipped them, then challenged Jehoash of Israel and was captured, and was later slain in a conspiracy at Lachish.
Key Story
Amaziah defeats Edom, worships its gods, and falls to Jehoash.
Key Verses
2 Kings 14:1; 2 Kings 14:19; 2 Chronicles 25:1; 2 Chronicles 25:14; 2 Chronicles 25:27
Related Books
2 Kings; 2 Chronicles
Related People
Jehoash, King of Israel; Jehoaddan, Mother of Amaziah; Joash, King of Judah; Jehoaddan, of Jerusalem
Life Lesson
Victory can become a snare when the victor bows to the gods he just defeated.
