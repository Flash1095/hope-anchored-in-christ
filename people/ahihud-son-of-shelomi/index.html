Ahihud, Son of Shelomi

Ahihud, Son of Shelomi was the prince of Asher appointed to divide the land of Canaan by inheritance among the tribes.

Key Story

Ahihud was named by the LORD, with Eleazar and Joshua, to apportion the promised land to the nine and a half tribes.

Life Lesson

Dividing what God gives takes honest hands.
