Asaiah, Servant of the King

Name
Asaiah, Servant of the King
Role
King's Servant
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Asaiah, a servant of the king, was one of the men Josiah commanded to enquire of the LORD concerning the found book, and he went to Huldah the prophetess.
Key Story
The king commanded Hilkiah, Ahikam, Abdon, Shaphan and Asaiah a servant of the king's to enquire of the LORD.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 34:20
Related Books

Related People

Life Lesson
Even a king's servant shared the errand of enquiring of God.
