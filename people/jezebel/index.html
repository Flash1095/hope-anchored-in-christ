Jezebel

Jezebel was Ahab's Sidonian wife who aggressively promoted Baal worship, persecuted God's prophets, and arranged Naboth's murder. Her name became a byword for brazen wickedness and idolatry.

Key Story

Jezebel threatened Elijah's life after Mount Carmel and forged letters in Ahab's name to steal Naboth's vineyard.

Life Lesson

Jezebel was given space to repent and refused it. God's patience is real, but so is the wisdom of repenting today rather than hardening our hearts.
