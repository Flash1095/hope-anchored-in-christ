King Hezekiah

Hezekiah was one of Judah's greatest reforming kings, who reopened the Temple, celebrated Passover, and trusted God when Assyria besieged Jerusalem. God added fifteen years to his life after prayer.

Key Story

When Sennacherib's army surrounded Jerusalem, Hezekiah spread the threatening letter before the Lord in the Temple and prayed, and God delivered the city overnight.

Life Lesson

Hezekiah spread his threatening letter before the Lord — do the same with your diagnosis, bill, or bad news. Lay it out before God; He still delivers those who trust Him.
