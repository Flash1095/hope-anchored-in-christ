Shemuel, Son of Ammihud

Shemuel, Son of Ammihud was the prince of Simeon appointed to divide the land of Canaan by inheritance among the tribes.

Key Story

Shemuel was named by the LORD, with Eleazar and Joshua, to apportion the promised land to the nine and a half tribes.

Life Lesson

Dividing what God gives takes honest hands.
