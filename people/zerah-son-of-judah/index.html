Zerah, Son of Judah

Zerah was the twin son of Judah and Tamar, whose hand came out first at birth, marked with a scarlet thread.

Key Story

Zerah's scarlet thread marked him first, but his brother Pharez was born before him and carried the line forward.

Life Lesson

Being first out of the gate is not the same as carrying the promise.
