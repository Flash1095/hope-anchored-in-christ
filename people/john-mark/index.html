John Mark

John Mark, surnamed Mark, was the son of Mary of Jerusalem, at whose house the church prayed for Peter. He travelled with Paul and Barnabas, left them at Perga, and later became the occasion of their sharp parting, before Paul again called him profitable for the ministry. Tradition identifies him as the author of Mark's Gospel. Also known as Mark, Cousin of Barnabas.

Key Story

Mark left the first journey at Perga, split two apostles over his return, and was restored to usefulness in the end.

Life Lesson

A failed first journey is not a final verdict. Mark's restoration is hope for every quitter who returns.
