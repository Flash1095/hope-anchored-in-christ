Eden, Son of Joah

Eden, son of Joah of the Gershonites, was one of the Levites who arose to cleanse the house of the LORD under Hezekiah.

Key Story

Of the Gershonites, Joah the son of Zimmah and Eden the son of Joah arose to cleanse the house.

Life Lesson

Sons followed fathers into the work of restoring worship.
