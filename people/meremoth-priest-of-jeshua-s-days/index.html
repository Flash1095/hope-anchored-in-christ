Meremoth, Priest of Jeshua's Days

Name
Meremoth, Priest of Jeshua's Days
Role
Priest; Course head
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Summary
Meremoth was a priestly course head in the days of Jeshua son of Jozadak. NOTE ON IDENTITY: a priest named Meremoth son of Uriah appears in Ezra and Nehemiah's wall and covenant lists; the text does not say whether the course head of Nehemiah 12:3 is that man, so this profile covers only the Nehemiah 12:3 course head and does not merge them.
Key Story

Life Lesson
The courses of priests kept worship ordered across generations.
