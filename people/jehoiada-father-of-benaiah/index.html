Jehoiada, Father of Benaiah

Jehoiada is named as the father of Benaiah, the valiant captain of David's guard who slew lions and giants.

Key Story

Jehoiada's son became the most feared warrior of David's guard.

Life Lesson

A father's name is honoured by a son's valour.
