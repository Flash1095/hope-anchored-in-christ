Joash, the King's Son

Name
Joash, the King's Son
Role
King's Son
Testament

Timeline Era

Summary
Joash, the king's son at Ahab's court, was named with Amon the governor as custodian of the imprisoned prophet Micaiah.
Key Story
Ahab ordered Micaiah carried back to Amon the governor of the city and to Joash the king's son.
