Delaiah

Name
Delaiah
Role
Prince
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Delaiah, son of Shemaiah, was a prince of Jehoiakim's court who heard Jeremiah's roll and joined the princes pleading with the king not to burn it.
Key Story
Delaiah sat among the princes who heard the roll and besought the king that he would not burn it, but the king would not hear.
Key Verses
Jeremiah 36:12; Jeremiah 36:25
Related Books
Jeremiah
Related People
Jeremiah; Baruch; Jehoiakim, King of Judah
Life Lesson
A plea refused is still a plea made — God records the dissent, not only the decree.
