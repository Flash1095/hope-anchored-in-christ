Eleazar, the Singer

Eleazar was one of the singers at the dedication of Jerusalem's wall, listed with Maaseiah, Shemaiah, Elam, Uzzi, Jehohanan, Malchijah, and Ezer. NOTE ON IDENTITY: this singer is not Eleazar son of Aaron or any other Eleazar in the text; the text does not identify him further, so this profile covers only the Nehemiah 12:42 singer.

Key Story

Singers at the dedication

Life Lesson
