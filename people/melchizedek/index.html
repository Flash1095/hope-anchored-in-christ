Melchizedek

Melchizedek was the mysterious king of Salem and priest of God Most High who blessed Abraham after battle. Scripture presents him as a picture of Christ's eternal priesthood.

Key Story

Melchizedek brought out bread and wine to Abraham, blessed him, and received a tenth of everything from him.

Life Lesson

Long before the Law, God was already pointing to Jesus, our perfect and eternal High Priest. In Christ we have a priest who never leaves His post.
