Simon the Sorcerer

Name
Simon the Sorcerer
Role
Samaritan Magician
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Simon the Sorcerer, also called Simon Magus, amazed Samaria with sorcery and claimed to be someone great. He believed and was baptised under Philip's preaching, then tried to buy the gift of the Holy Spirit from Peter and was sharply rebuked.
Key Story
Simon offered money for the power of the Spirit and heard Peter say, Your money perish with you.
Key Verses
Acts 8:9
Related Books
Acts
Related People
Philip; Simon Peter
Life Lesson
Faith cannot be bought, and power is not a product. Simon's error still tempts every ministry that trades in gifts.
