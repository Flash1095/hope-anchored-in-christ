Summary
Agabus was a prophet who came from Jerusalem to Antioch and foretold a great dearth throughout the world, which came to pass in the days of Claudius. Years later at Caesarea he bound his own hands and feet with Paul's girdle, foretelling Paul's arrest at Jerusalem.
Key Story
Agabus bound himself with Paul's girdle and said, So shall the Jews bind the man who owns this girdle.

Life Lesson
