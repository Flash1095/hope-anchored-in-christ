Cozbi

Name
Cozbi
Role
Daughter of Zur
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Cozbi, daughter of Zur, a prince of Midian, was brought into the camp by Zimri at the height of Israel's idolatry at Peor.
Key Story
Cozbi was slain with Zimri by Phinehas, and her death marked the end of the plague on Israel.
Key Verses
Numbers 25:15-18
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Zimri, Son of Salu; Zur; Phinehas
Life Lesson
The pleasures of idolatry end where God's holiness begins.
