Summary
Jambres is named in 2 Timothy with Jannes as one who withstood Moses, an example of men of corrupt minds, reprobate concerning the faith. Jewish tradition identifies Jambres and Jannes as magicians in Pharaoh's court, though Exodus itself does not name them.
