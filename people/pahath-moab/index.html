Pahath-moab

Name
Pahath-moab
Role
Family Head
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Summary
Head of a large returning family (2,812 in Ezra 2:6); his sons returned with Ezra, built the wall, and sealed the covenant (Ezra 2:6, 8:4, 10:30; Nehemiah 3:11, 7:11, 10:14).
Key Story
Return from exile

Related Books

Life Lesson
