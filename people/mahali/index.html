Mahali

Mahali was a son of Merari and a grandson of Levi, named in the genealogy of Moses' tribe.

Key Story

Mahali is named with his brother Mushi among Merari's sons in the record of Levi's house.

Life Lesson

God's story keeps moving through families we know almost nothing about — and He knows them all.
