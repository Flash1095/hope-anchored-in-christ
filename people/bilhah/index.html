Summary
Bilhah was Rachel's handmaid, given to Jacob as a wife, and the mother of Dan and Naphtali.
Key Story
When Rachel could not bear children, she gave Bilhah to Jacob, and Bilhah bore Dan and then Naphtali.
Key Verses
Genesis 29:29; Genesis 30:1-8; Genesis 46:25
