Jonathan

Jonathan was Saul's son and David's loyal friend, a brave warrior who surrendered his claim to the throne for God's chosen king. His covenant friendship with David is one of Scripture's finest.

Key Story

Jonathan attacked a Philistine outpost with only his armour-bearer, declaring that nothing can hinder the Lord from saving by many or by few.

Life Lesson

Jonathan loved God's purpose more than his own position. True friendship cheers another's calling even at cost to itself — and God honours that kind of heart.
