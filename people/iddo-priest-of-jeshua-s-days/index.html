Summary
Iddo was a priestly course head in the days of Jeshua son of Jozadak. NOTE ON IDENTITY: this Iddo is not identified in the text with Iddo the grandfather of Zechariah the prophet or any other Iddo, so this profile covers only the Nehemiah 12:4 course head.
