Berechiah

Name
Berechiah
Role
Father of Zechariah
Testament

Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Summary
Berechiah is named as the father of the prophet Zechariah (Zechariah 1:1, 1:7). Nothing else is told of him.
Key Story
Berechiah's mentions are as Zechariah's father, in the opening verses of his son's prophecy.
Key Verses
Zechariah 1:1; Zechariah 1:7
Related Books
Zechariah
Related People
Zechariah the Prophet; Iddo
Life Lesson
Berechiah, like Beeri and Pethuel, is remembered for a son God used. The quiet work of a household echoes in prophecy.
