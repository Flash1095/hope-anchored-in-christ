Ahithophel the Gilonite

Name
Ahithophel the Gilonite
Role
Counsellor of David and Absalom
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Ahithophel the Gilonite was David's counsellor, whose counsel was prized as if it were the oracle of God. He defected to Absalom, advised the rebellion, and when his counsel was rejected for Hushai's, he went home, set his house in order, and hanged himself.
Key Story
Ahithophel's counsel was treated as God's own oracle — until one rejection drove him to hang himself.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 15:12; 2 Samuel 16:23; 2 Samuel 17:23; 2 Samuel 23:34
Related Books

Related People
King David; Absalom; Hushai the Archite; Eliam
Life Lesson
Brilliance without loyalty ends in despair; counsel is only as good as the master it serves.
