Ahuzzath

Name
Ahuzzath
Role
Friend of Abimelech
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Ahuzzath was a friend of Abimelech, king of Gerar, who came with the king and Phichol to make a covenant with Isaac.
Key Story
Ahuzzath witnessed the treaty at Beersheba, where Abimelech admitted that God was plainly with Isaac.
Key Verses
Genesis 26:26-31
Related Books
Genesis

Life Lesson
