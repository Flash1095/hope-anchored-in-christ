Summary
Malachi, whose name means 'my messenger,' was the last of the Old Testament prophets, ministering to post-exile Judah around 460–430 BC (the exact date is debated). He confronted a weary, half-hearted people — corrupt priests, broken covenants, withheld tithes — and promised the coming messenger and the Sun of Righteousness, closing the Old Testament with a call to remember Moses and expect Elijah.
Key Story
Malachi charged Judah's priests and people with robbing God and dishonouring His name, then promised a refining messenger and Elijah before the great day of the LORD.

Life Lesson
