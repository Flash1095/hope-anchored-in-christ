Oreb, Prince of Midian

Name
Oreb, Prince of Midian
Role
Prince of Midian
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Oreb, a prince of Midian, was captured and killed by Ephraim at the rock Oreb during Gideon's victory.
Key Story
After Gideon's night attack, Ephraim took the waters and slew Oreb at the rock Oreb and Zeeb at the winepress of Zeeb.
Key Verses
Judges 7:25; Judges 8:3; Psalm 83:11
