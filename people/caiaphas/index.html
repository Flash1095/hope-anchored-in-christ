Caiaphas

Name
Caiaphas
Role
High Priest
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Caiaphas was high priest during Jesus' ministry and trial. He advised that one man should die for the people, presided over the night hearing that condemned Jesus, and later opposed the apostles.
Key Story
Caiaphas tore his clothes at Jesus' claim and declared Him guilty of blasphemy before the council.
Key Verses
Matthew 26:57; John 11:49; John 18:13
Related Books
Matthew; John
Related People
Annas; Simon Peter; Jesus
Life Lesson
Caiaphas was right that one man would die for the people, and wrong about everything it meant. God can speak truth through unwilling lips.
Related Places
Jerusalem
