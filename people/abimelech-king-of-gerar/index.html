Abimelech, King of Gerar

Name
Abimelech, King of Gerar
Role
King of Gerar
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Abimelech was the king of Gerar who dealt with both Abraham and Isaac over their wives, and learned to fear God after being warned in a dream.
Key Story
God warned Abimelech in a dream not to touch Sarah, and later Abimelech made a covenant of peace with Abraham and with Isaac at Beersheba.
Key Verses
Genesis 20; Genesis 21:22-32; Genesis 26:1-31
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Abraham; Sarah; Isaac; Rebekah; Phichol
Life Lesson
Even a foreign king listened when God spoke — may we be as quick to heed Him.
Related Places
Gerar; Beersheba
