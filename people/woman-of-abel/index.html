Woman of Abel

Timeline Era

Summary
The wise woman of Abel Beth-maachah — unnamed in the text — called to Joab from the wall while he besieged her city for Sheba's life, and bargained Sheba's head for the city's peace. Joab agreed, and the siege ended.
Key Story
The woman of Abel bought her city's peace with a traitor's head, thrown over the wall.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 20:16; 2 Samuel 20:19; 2 Samuel 20:22

Life Lesson
