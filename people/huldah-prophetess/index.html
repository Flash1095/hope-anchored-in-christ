Huldah, Prophetess

Summary
Huldah the prophetess, wife of Shallum keeper of the wardrobe, told Josiah's messengers that Jerusalem would indeed face the curses of the book, but because Josiah's heart was tender he would be gathered to his grave in peace.
Key Story
Huldah confirms judgment and promises Josiah peace.
Key Verses
2 Kings 22:14; 2 Kings 22:20
Related Books
2 Kings
Related People
King Josiah; Hilkiah, High Priest; Shallum, Husband of Huldah
Life Lesson
A tender heart does not cancel judgment, but it does find mercy within it.
