Belshazzar

Belshazzar was king of Babylon who, at a great feast for a thousand lords, drank wine from the gold and silver vessels Nebuchadnezzar had taken from the temple, praising gods of gold and silver; a hand wrote Mene, Mene, Tekel, Upharsin on the wall, Daniel interpreted it — God had numbered his kingdom and finished it — and that very night Belshazzar was slain and Darius the Mede took the kingdom.

Key Story

At Belshazzar's feast the fingers of a man's hand wrote on the plaster of the wall, and Daniel read the writing: thou art weighed in the balances, and art found wanting.

Life Lesson

Drinking from vessels taken from God's house while praising idols is the last feast before the writing on the wall.
