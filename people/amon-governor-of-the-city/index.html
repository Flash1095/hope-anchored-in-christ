Summary
Amon, governor of the city, was ordered by Ahab to hold Micaiah in prison on bread and water of affliction until the king returned in peace.
Key Story
Ahab commanded that Micaiah be carried back to Amon the governor of the city and to Joash the king's son, and put in prison.
