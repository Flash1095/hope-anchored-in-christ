So, King of Egypt

So, king of Egypt, was the ruler to whom Hoshea sent messengers and withheld Assyria's tribute, a conspiracy Shalmaneser discovered before Samaria fell.

Key Story

So, Hoshea's Egyptian ally.

Life Lesson

Egypt's help has always been a broken reed for kings who will not trust God.
