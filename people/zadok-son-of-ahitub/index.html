Zadok, Son of Ahitub

Timeline Era

Summary
Zadok, son of Ahitub, was priest alongside Abiathar through David's reign. He carried the ark back to Jerusalem when David fled Absalom, refused to leave the city, and later anointed Solomon king.
Key Story
Zadok carried the ark back into danger and stayed loyal when the kingdom split.
Key Verses
