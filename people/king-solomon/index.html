King Solomon

Name
King Solomon
Role
King of Israel; Wise King
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Solomon was David's son, famed for God-given wisdom, who built the First Temple in Jerusalem and wrote much of Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, and the Song of Songs. His later compromise with idols divided the kingdom after his death.
Key Story
When God offered Solomon anything, he asked for wisdom to govern God's people, and God gave him wisdom, riches, and honour.
Key Verses
1 Kings 3:9-12; 1 Kings 8:22-53; Ecclesiastes 12:13
Related Books
1 Kings; Proverbs; Ecclesiastes; Song of Solomon
Related People
King David; Bathsheba
Life Lesson
Solomon's wisdom began with asking God — and we are invited to do the same. If any of you lacks wisdom, ask God, who gives generously without finding fault.
Related Places
Jerusalem; Gibeon
