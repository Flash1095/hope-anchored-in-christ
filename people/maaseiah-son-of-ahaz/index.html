Maaseiah, Son of Ahaz

Maaseiah, the king's son, was slain by Zichri, a mighty man of Ephraim, in Pekah's invasion of Judah.

Key Story

Zichri slew Maaseiah the king's son, and Azrikam the governor of the house, and Elkanah next to the king.

Life Lesson

A king's idolatry cost even his own son's life.
