Summary
Gaius of Macedonia was one of Paul's travelling companions seized by the Ephesian mob and dragged into the theatre during the riot of the silversmiths. He is distinguished by his homeland from Gaius of Derbe.
Key Story
Gaius of Macedonia was seized in the Ephesian riot and dragged with Aristarchus into the theatre.
