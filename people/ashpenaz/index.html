Ashpenaz

Ashpenaz was the master of Nebuchadnezzar's eunuchs charged with bringing certain of Israel's seed, skillful and handsome youths, to stand in the king's palace, teaching them the learning and tongue of the Chaldeans; Daniel asked him for vegetables and water instead of the king's meat, and God gave Daniel favour and tender love with him.

Key Story

Ashpenaz was ordered to feed the Judean youths the king's meat and wine, but Daniel proved him with pulse and water for ten days, and the youths appeared fairer than all who ate the king's portion.

Life Lesson

God can give a captive favour with his captor — and a captor can choose to listen.
