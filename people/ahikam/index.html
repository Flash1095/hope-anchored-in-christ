Summary
Ahikam, son of Shaphan, was a court official whose hand protected Jeremiah when the priests and prophets demanded his death, arguing that Hezekiah had spared Micah for like words. His son Gedaliah later became governor, and Ahikam's protection extended to Jeremiah's release to Gedaliah's care.
Key Story
When the priests demanded Jeremiah's death, the hand of Ahikam son of Shaphan was with Jeremiah, so that he was not given into the people's hand to be put to death.
