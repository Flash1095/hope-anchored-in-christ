Nebuzaradan

Summary
Nebuzaradan (KJV: Nebuzar-adan) was Nebuchadnezzar's captain of the guard who burned the temple and the king's house, broke down Jerusalem's walls, carried the people to Babylon, and — on his king's orders — protected Jeremiah and gave him the choice of going to Babylon or staying in the land.
Key Story
Nebuzaradan burned the house of the Lord and all the houses of Jerusalem, then took Jeremiah out of the prison court, loosed his chains, and told him to go wherever seemed good to him.
