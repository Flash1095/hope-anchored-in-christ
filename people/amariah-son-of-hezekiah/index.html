Amariah, Son of Hezekiah

Amariah, son of Hezekiah, is named only in Zephaniah's genealogy (Zephaniah 1:1), as the prophet's great-grandfather. He is one of several Amariahs in Scripture; this one is identified by his place in Zephaniah's line.

Key Story

Amariah appears only in Zephaniah's genealogy, as the prophet's great-grandfather between Gedaliah and Hezekiah.

Life Lesson

Amariah's one verse holds a whole generation together. Most of God's people live their lives in a single line of a genealogy — and that is enough.
