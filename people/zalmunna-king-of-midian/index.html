Summary
Zalmunna, a king of Midian, fled with Zebah from Gideon, was captured at Karkor, and was killed by Gideon.
Key Story
Zalmunna fled with Zebah beyond Jordan, was taken at Karkor, and taunted Gideon before Gideon rose and slew them both.
