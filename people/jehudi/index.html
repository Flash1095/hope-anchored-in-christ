Jehudi

Jehudi, son of Nethaniah, was the court official sent to fetch Jeremiah's roll from Elishama's chamber and read it to Jehoiakim, who cut it up and burned it column by column.

Key Story

Jehudi read the roll in the king's ears in the winter house, and as he read each column, Jehoiakim cut it with a penknife and cast it into the fire.

Life Lesson

Reading God's word aloud to power is faithful even when power answers with a knife and a fire.
