Summary
Micah was a small-town prophet who denounced injustice, foretold Bethlehem as the Messiah's birthplace, and summed up God's requirements in one famous verse. His book ends in wonder at God's pardoning love.
Key Story
Micah prophesied that from tiny Bethlehem would come the ruler over Israel whose origins are from ancient times.
Key Verses
Micah 5:2; Micah 6:8; Micah 7:18-19
Related Books
Micah; Matthew
Related People
Isaiah; King Hezekiah
Life Lesson
Do justice, love mercy, walk humbly — Micah keeps faith beautifully simple. And his God delights to pardon, hurling our sins into the depths of the sea.
