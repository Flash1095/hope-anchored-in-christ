Shephatiah, Son of Mattan

Name
Shephatiah, Son of Mattan
Role
Prince
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Summary
Shephatiah, son of Mattan, was one of the princes who demanded Jeremiah's death, charging that his words weakened the hands of the men of war and all the people.
Key Story
Shephatiah joined Gedaliah son of Pashur and others in telling Zedekiah that Jeremiah ought to die for weakening the city's hands.
Key Verses
Jeremiah 38:1-4
Related Books
Jeremiah
Related People
Zedekiah, King of Judah; Jeremiah; Gedaliah, Son of Pashur
Life Lesson
Calling a prophet a traitor for telling the truth is a charge that outlives the siege.
Related Places
Jerusalem
