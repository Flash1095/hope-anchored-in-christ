Jehoram, Son of Ahab

Name

Role

Testament

Timeline Era

Summary
Jehoram, son of Ahab and king of Israel, also called Joram, was wounded by the Syrians at Ramoth-gilead and was later slain when Jehu, anointed to cut off the house of Ahab, came against him.
Key Story
Ahaziah went down to see Jehoram son of Ahab at Jezreel because he was sick, and his coming to Joram became the occasion of his destruction by Jehu.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 22:5; 2 Chronicles 22:6; 2 Chronicles 22:7
