Jabin, King of Canaan

Jabin, king of Canaan, who reigned in Hazor, oppressed Israel twenty years with nine hundred chariots of iron until Deborah and Barak defeated his captain Sisera at the Kishon. He is distinct from the earlier Jabin of Hazor in Joshua.

Key Story

Jabin's iron chariots terrorised Israel for twenty years until Deborah sent Barak against Sisera, and God discomfited the whole host at the Kishon.

Life Lesson

Iron chariots look unbeatable until the river rises.
