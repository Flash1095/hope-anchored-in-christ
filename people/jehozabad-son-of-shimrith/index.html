Jehozabad, Son of Shimrith

Jehozabad, son of Shimrith a Moabitess, was one of the servants who conspired against Joash and slew him on his bed.

Key Story

Jehozabad the son of Shimrith joined Zabad in conspiring against Joash for the blood of Jehoiada's sons.

Life Lesson

Those who avenge innocent blood can themselves become murderers.
