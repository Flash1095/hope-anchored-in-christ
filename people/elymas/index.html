Elymas

Elymas, also called Bar-Jesus (Barjesus in the King James Version), was a Jewish false prophet and sorcerer at the court of Sergius Paulus in Cyprus. He opposed Paul and Barnabas, and Paul struck him blind for a season, after which the proconsul believed.

Key Story

Elymas tried to turn the proconsul from the faith, and went out blind, groping for a hand to lead him.

Life Lesson

Opposition to the gospel blinds its maker. Elymas groped in the darkness he had tried to spread.
