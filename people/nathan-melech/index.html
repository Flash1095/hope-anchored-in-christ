Summary
Nathan-melech was a chamberlain whose chamber stood at the entering in of the house of the LORD, where horses dedicated to the sun were kept. King Josiah took those horses away and burned the chariots of the sun with fire as part of his reform. He is named only once, and nothing further is recorded about him.
