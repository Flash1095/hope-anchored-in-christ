Sapphira

Name
Sapphira
Role
Church Member at Jerusalem
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Sapphira conspired with her husband Ananias to keep back part of their land's price while claiming the whole. When Peter questioned her three hours after her husband's death, she repeated the lie and fell dead as well.
Key Story
Sapphira agreed with her husband's lie and, questioned alone, told it again and fell at Peter's feet.
Key Verses
Acts 5:1
Related Books
Acts
Related People
Ananias, Husband of Sapphira; Simon Peter
Life Lesson
A lie agreed together is still a lie each one tells. Sapphira had three hours to repent and used them to repeat.
