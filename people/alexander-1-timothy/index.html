Alexander (1 Timothy)

Alexander is named in 1 Timothy alongside Hymenaeus as one delivered unto Satan that they might learn not to blaspheme. He is listed separately from Alexander the coppersmith of 2 Timothy, since the letters do not identify them as the same man.

Life Lesson
