Summary
Mahath, son of Amasai of the Kohathites, was one of the Levites who arose to cleanse the house of the LORD under Hezekiah.
Key Story
The Levites arose, Mahath the son of Amasai and Joel the son of Azariah, of the sons of the Kohathites, to cleanse the house.
