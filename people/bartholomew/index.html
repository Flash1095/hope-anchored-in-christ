Life of Christ
Summary
Bartholomew, often identified with Nathanael, was an apostle praised by Jesus as a true Israelite in whom there was no deceit. He came to faith after Philip invited him to come and see Jesus.
Key Story
Jesus told Nathanael he had seen him under the fig tree before Philip called him, and Nathanael declared, You are the Son of God.
Key Verses
John 1:47-49; Matthew 10:3
Related Books
Matthew; Mark; Luke; John
Related People
Philip; Jesus
Life Lesson
Jesus saw Nathanael in his private place under the fig tree before anyone called him. You are seen and known by Jesus in your unseen places today.
