Shimei, Son of Gera

Name
Shimei, Son of Gera
Role
Benjamite of Bahurim
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Shimei, son of Gera of Bahurim, cursed David as he fled from Absalom, throwing stones and dust and calling him a bloody man. David forbade revenge, and on his return spared Shimei, who came first among Benjamin to meet him.
Key Story
Shimei cursed the fleeing king with stones and dust — and the returning king spared him.
