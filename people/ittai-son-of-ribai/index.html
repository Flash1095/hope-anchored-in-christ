Ittai, Son of Ribai

Ittai, son of Ribai of Gibeah of Benjamin, was among David's thirty mighty men. He is distinct from Ittai the Gittite, who stayed loyal to David in Absalom's revolt.

Key Story

Ittai son of Ribai of Gibeah stood among David's thirty.

Life Lesson

Two Ittais served David — a Benjamite and a Gittite — and both were loyal.
