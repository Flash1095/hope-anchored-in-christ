Summary
Eli was high priest and judge at Shiloh who raised the young Samuel in the tabernacle. His failure to restrain his wicked sons brought judgment on his house.
Key Story
When Hannah prayed silently, Eli first misjudged her, then blessed her — and God answered her prayer with Samuel.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 1:17; 1 Samuel 3:18; 1 Samuel 4:18
Related Books
1 Samuel
Related People
Samuel; Hannah
Life Lesson
Eli's story warns us to honour God in our own homes first. It is never too late to say, like Eli finally did, He is the Lord; let Him do what is good.
