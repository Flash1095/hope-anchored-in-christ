Maaseiah, Son of Shallum

Name
Maaseiah, Son of Shallum
Role
Keeper of the Door
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Maaseiah, son of Shallum, was keeper of the door of the temple; the chamber of the sons of Hanan was by the chamber of the princes above his chamber, where Jeremiah tested the Rechabites.
Key Story
Jeremiah brought the Rechabites into the chamber of the sons of Hanan, above the chamber of Maaseiah the keeper of the door, and set wine before them.
Key Verses
Jeremiah 35:4
Related Books
Jeremiah
Related People
Zephaniah, Son of Maaseiah; Hanan, Son of Igdaliah
Life Lesson
Even a doorkeeper's chamber can become the room where faithfulness is tested.
