Peninnah

Peninnah was Elkanah's other wife. She had children and provoked Hannah bitterly because Hannah had none, year after year at Shiloh.

Key Story

Peninnah's provoking drove Hannah to pray the prayer that gave Israel Samuel.

Life Lesson

Words used to wound can become the pressure that drives someone to God — and a warning about how we use our own.
