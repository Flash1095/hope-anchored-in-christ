Jabin, King of Hazor

Name
Jabin, King of Hazor
Role
King of Hazor
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Jabin was king of Hazor when he gathered the kings of the north against Israel at the waters of Merom.
Key Story
Jabin's great host, with horses and chariots, was defeated by Joshua at Merom, and Hazor itself was burned with fire.
Key Verses
Joshua 11:1-11
Related Books

Related People
Jobab, King of Madon; Joshua
Life Lesson
Chariots and numbers are no answer to the LORD of hosts.
