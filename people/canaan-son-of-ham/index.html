Canaan, Son of Ham

Canaan was the youngest son of Ham and the father of the Canaanite peoples.

Key Story

After the flood, Noah's words about Canaan set the course for the land that would later bear his name.

Life Lesson

Sin has consequences that reach beyond one person — which is why grace matters so much.
