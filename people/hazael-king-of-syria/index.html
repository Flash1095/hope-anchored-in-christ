Hazael, King of Syria

Name
Hazael, King of Syria
Role
King of Syria
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Hazael, Elisha's anointed scourge, murdered Ben-Hadad, oppressed Israel through Jehu's and Jehoahaz's days, and died leaving his son Ben-Hadad on the throne.
Key Story
Hazael murders Ben-Hadad and oppresses Israel.
Key Verses
1 Kings 19:15; 2 Kings 8:15; 2 Chronicles 22:5
Related Books
1 Kings; 2 Kings; 2 Chronicles
Related People
Elisha; Ben-Hadad II, King of Syria; Ben-Hadad III, King of Syria; Jehu, King of Israel; Jehoahaz, King of Israel; Ahaziah, King of Judah; Jehoram, Son of Ahab
Life Lesson
Elisha wept over the cruelty Hazael would do; God uses even cruel kings, and judges them too.
