Jacob

Name
Jacob
Role
Patriarch; Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Jacob was Isaac's son who wrestled for the blessing and became father of the twelve tribes of Israel. God renamed him Israel after a night of wrestling.
Key Story
At Bethel Jacob dreamed of a ladder reaching to heaven, and years later he wrestled with God at Peniel until daybreak.
Key Verses
Genesis 28:12-15; Genesis 32:28; Hosea 12:3-4
Related Books
Genesis; Hosea; Hebrews
Related People
Isaac; Rebekah; Rachel; Joseph, Son of Jacob
Life Lesson
God took a deceiver and made him Israel, the father of a nation. He is still in the business of giving strugglers a new name and a new future.
Related Places
Haran; Canaan; Shechem; Bethel; Hebron; Beersheba; Egypt
Related Events
Jacob Wrestles with God; Joseph and His Brothers
