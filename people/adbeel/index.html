Adbeel

Adbeel was a son of Ishmael, named among the children born after Abraham's first family.

Key Story

Adbeel appears in the genealogy of Abraham's later children and their descendants.

Life Lesson

God's story keeps moving through families we know almost nothing about — and He knows them all.
