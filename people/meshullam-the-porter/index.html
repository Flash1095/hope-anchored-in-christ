Summary
Meshullam was one of the porters at the temple gates in Nehemiah's day, listed with Mattaniah, Bakbukiah, Obadiah, Talmon, and Akkub. NOTE ON IDENTITY: several men named Meshullam appear in Ezra and Nehemiah; the text does not say whether this porter is one of them, so this profile covers only the Nehemiah 12:25 porter and does not merge him with any other Meshullam.
