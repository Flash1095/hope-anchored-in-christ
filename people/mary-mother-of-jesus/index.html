Life of Christ
Summary
Mary was the young virgin of Nazareth chosen to bear Jesus, the Son of God. Her humble surrender and lifelong faith, from manger to cross to upper room, make her a model of trust.
Key Story
When the angel announced she would conceive the Son of the Most High, Mary answered, I am the Lord's servant; may Your word to me be fulfilled.
Key Verses
Luke 1:38; Luke 1:46-47; Luke 2:19; John 19:25

Life Lesson
Mary treasured and pondered God's work even when a sword pierced her own soul. Say yes to God, treasure His word, and trust Him through both wonder and pain.
