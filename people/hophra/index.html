Hophra

Name
Hophra
Role
Pharaoh of Egypt
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Summary
Hophra was Pharaoh of Egypt in the days after Jerusalem's fall; Jeremiah declared that God would give Hophra into the hand of his enemies, just as Zedekiah had been given into Nebuchadnezzar's hand.
Key Story
Jeremiah gave the Jews who fled to Egypt a sign: Pharaoh-hophra would be delivered to those seeking his life, as surely as Zedekiah had been delivered to Babylon.
Key Verses
Jeremiah 44:30
Related Books
Jeremiah
Related People
Jeremiah; Zedekiah, King of Judah
Life Lesson
Fleeing to Egypt for safety only moved the judgment to Egypt.
