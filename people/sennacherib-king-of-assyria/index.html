Sennacherib, King of Assyria

Sennacherib, king of Assyria, took Judah's fenced cities, demanded Hezekiah's surrender, mocked the living God through the Rabshakeh, and lost a hundred and fourscore and five thousand men in one night to the angel of the LORD.

Key Story

Sennacherib mocks God and loses his army in a night.

Life Lesson

Mocking the living God is the last boast an empire makes before morning.
