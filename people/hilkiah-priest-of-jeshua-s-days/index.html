Hilkiah, Priest of Jeshua's Days

Name
Hilkiah, Priest of Jeshua's Days
Role
Priest; Course head
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Summary
Hilkiah was a priestly course head in the days of Jeshua son of Jozadak. NOTE ON IDENTITY: this Hilkiah is not identified in the text with Hilkiah the high priest of Josiah's day or any other Hilkiah, so this profile covers only the Nehemiah 12:7 course head.
Key Story
Priests in Jeshua's days
Key Verses
Nehemiah 12:7
Related Books

Related People

Life Lesson
The courses of priests kept worship ordered across generations.
