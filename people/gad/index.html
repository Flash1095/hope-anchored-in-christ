Gad

Name
Gad
Role
Son of Jacob
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Gad was the seventh son of Jacob, the first by Zilpah, and the father of Ziphion, Haggi, Shuni, Ezbon, Eri, Arodi, and Areli.
Key Story
Gad's tribe later settled east of the Jordan and became known as warriors, just as Jacob had blessed him.
Key Verses
Genesis 30:10-11; Genesis 46:16; Genesis 49:19
