Summary
Rahab was a Canaanite woman in Jericho who hid Israel's spies and confessed faith in Israel's God. She was spared in Jericho's fall and became an ancestor of Jesus.
Key Story
Rahab hid the spies under flax on her roof and hung a scarlet cord in her window, the sign by which her household was saved.
Key Verses
Joshua 2:9-11; Joshua 6:25; Hebrews 11:31; Matthew 1:5
Related Books
Joshua; Hebrews; James; Matthew
Related People
Joshua; Boaz; King David
Life Lesson
Rahab's past did not disqualify her from God's future — she is in the family line of Jesus. No history is too broken for God to weave into redemption.
