Merab

Name

Role

Testament

Summary
Merab, Saul's elder daughter, was promised to David as a reward for fighting the Lord's battles, but Saul gave her instead to Adriel the Meholathite.
Key Story
Merab was used as a bargaining piece in Saul's jealous war against David.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 14:49; 1 Samuel 18:17; 1 Samuel 18:19
Related Books
1 Samuel
Related People
King Saul; Michal; Adriel the Meholathite
Life Lesson
Being treated as a token in someone else's jealousy is a real wound, and God sees it.
