Ananias, the High Priest

Name
Ananias, the High Priest
Role
High Priest
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Ananias the high priest ordered Paul struck during his defence before the Sanhedrin, and later came down to Caesarea with Tertullus to accuse Paul before Felix. He is distinct from Ananias of Damascus and from Ananias the husband of Sapphira.
Key Story
Ananias ordered Paul struck on the mouth for speaking, and Paul answered with a prophecy he did not know he spoke.
Key Verses
Acts 23:2; Acts 24:1
Related Books
Acts
Related People
Paul; Tertullus; Felix
Life Lesson
Office does not make a man just. Ananias used the high priesthood to strike a prisoner mid-sentence.
Related Places
Jerusalem
