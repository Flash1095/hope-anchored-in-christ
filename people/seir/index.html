Testament

Timeline Era

Summary
Seir was a Horite, the father of Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan, and the namesake of Mount Seir.
Key Story
Seir's children were the dukes of the Horites in the land that Esau would later possess as Edom.
Key Verses
Genesis 36:20-21; Genesis 36:29-30
