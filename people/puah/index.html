Puah

Name
Puah
Role
Hebrew Midwife
Testament

Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Puah was one of the two Hebrew midwives who feared God and refused Pharaoh's order to kill newborn Hebrew boys.
Key Story
When Pharaoh commanded the midwives to kill every Hebrew son at birth, Puah and Shiphrah let the boys live — and God gave them families of their own.
Key Verses
Exodus 1:15-21

Related People

Life Lesson
