Judah

Name
Judah
Role
Patriarch; Tribe Leader
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Judah was Jacob's fourth son, ancestor of kings and of Jesus Christ, called the Lion of Judah. His life moved from betrayal of his brother to selfless intercession for him.
Key Story
Judah offered himself as a slave in Benjamin's place before Joseph, showing how God had transformed his heart.
Key Verses
Genesis 44:33; Genesis 49:9-10; Revelation 5:5
Related Books
Genesis; Matthew; Revelation
Related People
Jacob; Joseph, Son of Jacob; Tamar; King David
Life Lesson
Judah proves a person can change — from selling a brother to saving one. God's grace can rewrite the worst chapters of a life into a royal lineage.
Related Places
Canaan; Egypt
