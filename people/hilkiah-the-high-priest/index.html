Hilkiah, the High Priest

Name
Hilkiah, the High Priest
Role
High Priest
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Hilkiah the high priest received the temple repair money, found the book of the law of the LORD given by Moses, gave it to Shaphan, and went with the king's men to Huldah the prophetess; he also gave Passover offerings as a ruler of the house of God.
Key Story
Hilkiah said to Shaphan, 'I have found the book of the law in the house of the LORD,' and the reading of it rent the king's clothes and renewed the covenant.
