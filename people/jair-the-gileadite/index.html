Jair, the Gileadite

Name
Jair, the Gileadite
Role
Judge of Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Jair, a Gileadite, judged Israel twenty-two years; he had thirty sons who rode on thirty ass colts and controlled thirty cities called Havoth-jair. He is distinct from Jair, son of Manasseh, in Numbers.
Key Story
Jair judged Israel twenty-two years from Gilead, and his thirty sons rode thirty ass colts over thirty cities named Havoth-jair; he died and was buried in Camon.
Key Verses
Judges 10:3-5
Related Books

Life Lesson
Thirty sons and thirty cities — a judge's house looked like a small kingdom.
