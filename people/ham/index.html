Ham

Name

Son of Noah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Ham was one of Noah's three sons, carried through the flood in the ark. His descendants settled in Cush, Egypt, Put, and Canaan, nations that feature throughout the Old Testament.
Key Story
Ham saw his father's nakedness after Noah became drunk and told his brothers, while Shem and Japheth covered Noah respectfully. Noah's words over Ham's son Canaan shaped the story of the land Israel would enter.
Key Verses
Genesis 9:20-27; Genesis 10:6
Related Books
Genesis
Related People

Life Lesson
Ham's failure shows how a careless moment can echo through a family. Honour protects dignity — our own and others' — even when someone we love has fallen short.
