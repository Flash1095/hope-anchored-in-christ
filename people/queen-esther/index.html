Queen Esther

Esther was a Jewish exile who became queen of Persia and courageously risked her life to save her people from Haman's plot of annihilation. Her story celebrates God's hidden providence.

Key Story

Esther approached the king unsummoned, saying, If I perish, I perish, and exposed Haman's plot at a banquet she prepared.

Life Lesson

You may be in your position for such a time as this. God works behind the scenes in ordinary placements to deliver His people at the decisive moment.
