Joash, King of Israel

Joash, son of Jehoahaz son of Jehu and king of Israel, answered Amaziah's challenge with the parable of the thistle and the cedar, defeated Judah at Beth-shemesh, broke down Jerusalem's wall, and took the temple treasures to Samaria.

Key Story

Joash warned Amaziah to abide at home, but Amaziah would not hear, and Judah was put to the worse before Israel.

Life Lesson

Pride after a victory can provoke a defeat far worse than the first battle.
