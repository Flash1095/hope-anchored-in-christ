Malchi-shua, Son of Saul

Malchi-shua, spelled Melchi-shua in 1 Samuel 14, was a son of Saul who died with his father and brothers on Mount Gilboa.

Key Story

Malchi-shua died beside his father on the day Israel's first kingdom fell.

Life Lesson

A dynasty's fall sweeps up sons who never chose the throne.
