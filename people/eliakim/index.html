Eliakim

Name
Eliakim
Role
Steward of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Eliakim, son of Hilkiah, was set over Hezekiah's household in Shebna's place and was one of the three officials who met the Assyrian officers at Jerusalem's wall.
Key Story
Eliakim, Shebna, and Joah went out to hear the Rabshakeh at the conduit of the upper pool, then tore their clothes and reported his words to Hezekiah.
Key Verses
Isaiah 22:20-24; Isaiah 36:3; Isaiah 36:22; Nehemiah 12:41

Life Lesson
