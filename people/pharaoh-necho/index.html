Pharaoh Necho

Name
Pharaoh Necho
Role
King of Egypt
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Pharaoh-necho was king of Egypt whose army was defeated by Nebuchadnezzar at Carchemish by the Euphrates; Jeremiah's oracle against Egypt is dated to that battle, and Necho had earlier carried Shallum son of Josiah away to Egypt.
Key Story
At Carchemish, Nebuchadnezzar smote the army of Pharaoh-necho, and Jeremiah declared that Egypt's day of the Lord's vengeance had come.
Key Verses
Jeremiah 46:2
Related Books
Jeremiah; 2 Kings; 2 Chronicles
Related People
Shallum, Son of Josiah; King Nebuchadnezzar
Life Lesson
Egypt's power, like every empire's, had a Carchemish waiting for it.
