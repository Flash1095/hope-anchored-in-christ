Summary
Adrammelech was a son of Sennacherib who, with his brother Sharezer, murdered his father as he worshipped in the house of Nisroch and fled to Ararat.
Key Story
After Sennacherib returned to Nineveh, his sons Adrammelech and Sharezer struck him down with the sword in the temple of his god.
