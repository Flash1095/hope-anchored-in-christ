Summary
Amram was a son of Kohath, of the tribe of Levi, and the husband of Jochebed. His children were Aaron, Moses, and Miriam.
Key Story
Amram married Jochebed, and their son Moses, hidden at birth from Pharaoh's decree, became the deliverer of Israel.
Key Verses
Exodus 6:18-20
