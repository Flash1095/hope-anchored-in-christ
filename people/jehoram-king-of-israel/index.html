Summary
Jehoram, also called Joram, son of Ahab, put away Baal's image but clung to Jeroboam's sins, warred with Moab alongside Jehoshaphat, and was slain by Jehu at Jezreel.
Key Story
Jehoram wars with Moab and is slain by Jehu.

Life Lesson
