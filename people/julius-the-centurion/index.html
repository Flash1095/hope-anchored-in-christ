Julius, the Centurion

Julius was the centurion of Augustus' band who had Paul in charge on the voyage to Rome. He treated Paul kindly, letting him visit friends at Sidon, and when the ship wrecked he stopped the soldiers from killing the prisoners so that Paul would be saved.

Key Story

Paul's voyage to Rome

Life Lesson

Kindness from an unexpected quarter can save lives.
