Deborah

Deborah was a prophetess and the only female judge of Israel, who led the nation to victory over Canaanite oppression. She sang one of Scripture's oldest songs of praise.

Key Story

Deborah summoned Barak and went with him into battle, and God routed Sisera's nine hundred iron chariots before Israel.

Life Lesson

Deborah rose as a mother in Israel when leaders hesitated. God raises up whoever will trust Him — step into the place He is calling you to fill.
