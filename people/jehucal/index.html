Jehucal

Name
Jehucal
Role
Prince
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Summary
Jehucal (also spelled Jucal), son of Shelemiah, was a prince whom Zedekiah twice sent to Jeremiah — once to ask him to pray for the city, and once among those demanding his death.
Key Story
Zedekiah sent Jehucal to ask Jeremiah to pray to the Lord for them, yet Jehucal later joined the princes who said Jeremiah should die.
Key Verses
Jeremiah 37:3; Jeremiah 38:1-4
Related Books
Jeremiah
Related People
Zedekiah, King of Judah; Jeremiah
Life Lesson
The same messenger can carry a prayer request one day and a death sentence the next.
