Noah

Noah was a righteous man in a corrupt generation whom God chose to preserve humanity and the animals through a great flood. He built an ark in obedience long before rain fell.

Key Story

Noah built the ark exactly as God commanded, gathered his family and the animals, and trusted God through the flood and into a new beginning.

Life Lesson

Noah obeyed God when obedience looked foolish to everyone around him. Hope anchors us to obey today what God has promised for tomorrow.
