Life of Christ
Summary
Joanna, wife of Chuza, Herod's steward, was healed by Jesus and then travelled with Him, supporting His ministry from her own means. She was among the women who found the empty tomb and told the apostles.
Key Story
Joanna went from Herod's household to the empty tomb, and carried the resurrection news to the apostles.
Key Verses
Luke 8:3; Luke 24:10

Life Lesson
