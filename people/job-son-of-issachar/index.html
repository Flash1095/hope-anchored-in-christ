Job, Son of Issachar

Job was a son of Issachar and a grandson of Jacob, named among the family that went into Egypt.

Key Story

Job is named among Issachar's four sons — a different Job from the patriarch of Uz.

Life Lesson

Two men can share a name and walk very different paths; God knows each one.
