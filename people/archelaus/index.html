Archelaus

Archelaus was a son of Herod the Great who ruled Judea, Samaria, and Idumea after his father's death. Joseph, returning from Egypt, turned aside to Galilee when he heard Archelaus reigned in Judea.

Key Story

One sentence in Matthew explains why Jesus grew up in Nazareth: Joseph feared Archelaus and settled in Galilee instead.

Life Lesson

God guides His people through ordinary prudence as well as dreams. Joseph's detour to Nazareth fulfilled prophecy.
