Judas, Son of James

Name
Judas, Son of James
Role
Apostle, also called Thaddaeus
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Judas son of James was one of the twelve apostles, listed by Luke and in Acts. Matthew and Mark call him Thaddaeus, and Matthew adds the name Lebbaeus. At the Last Supper he asked Jesus why He would manifest Himself to the disciples and not to the world.
Key Story
Thaddaeus asked the question every disciple wonders: Lord, why do You show Yourself to us and not to the world?
Key Verses
Luke 6:16; Acts 1:13; Matthew 10:3

Life Lesson
A quiet apostle asked one honest question at the table. Jesus answered that love keeps His word, and the Father comes to dwell.
