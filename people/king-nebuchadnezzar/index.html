King Nebuchadnezzar

Name
King Nebuchadnezzar
Role
King of Babylon
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Summary
Nebuchadnezzar was Babylon's mighty king who conquered Jerusalem and carried Judah into exile, yet through Daniel God humbled and taught him. He ended by praising the King of Heaven in a public decree.
Key Story
After seven years living like an animal for his pride, Nebuchadnezzar raised his eyes to heaven, his sanity was restored, and he blessed the Most High.

Life Lesson
