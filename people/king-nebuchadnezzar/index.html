King Nebuchadnezzar

Name
King Nebuchadnezzar
Role
King of Babylon
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Summary
Nebuchadnezzar was Babylon's mighty king who conquered Jerusalem and carried Judah into exile, yet through Daniel God humbled and taught him. He ended by praising the King of Heaven in a public decree.
Key Story
After seven years living like an animal for his pride, Nebuchadnezzar raised his eyes to heaven, his sanity was restored, and he blessed the Most High.
Key Verses
Daniel 4:34-37; Daniel 1:1-2; Jeremiah 25:9; 2 Kings 24:1; 2 Kings 25:1; Jeremiah 27:6-8; Jeremiah 39:1-7; Daniel 2:1; Daniel 4:28-37; Ezra 2:1; Nehemiah 7:6

Life Lesson
If God could reach the proudest king on earth, He can reach anyone you are praying for. No heart is too hard and no throne too high for heaven to humble and heal.
