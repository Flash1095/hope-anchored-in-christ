Summary
Birsha was the king of Gomorrah who joined Sodom's rebellion against Chedorlaomer and was defeated with him.
Key Story
Birsha's city fell with Sodom's in the Valley of Siddim, and its people were carried off until Abram's rescue.

Genesis 14:2
