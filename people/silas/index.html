Silas

Silas, also called Silvanus, was a prophet and leading man among the Jerusalem brethren, sent with Judas Barsabbas to confirm the Council's letter. He became Paul's companion on the second missionary journey, was beaten and imprisoned with him at Philippi, and sang hymns at midnight before the earthquake.

Key Story

Silas sang in a Philippian prison at midnight, and the earthquake opened every door.

Life Lesson

Silas sang where most would curse. Worship at midnight is the missionary's loudest sermon.
