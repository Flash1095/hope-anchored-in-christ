Jahaziel, Son of Zechariah

Key Story
He declared to Jehoshaphat and all Judah, Be not afraid nor dismayed by reason of this great multitude; for the battle is not yours, but God's — and told them they would not need to fight, but only stand and see the salvation of the LORD.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 20:14-17
Life Lesson
God's word through one faithful voice steadied a whole nation. When the battle is bigger than you, stand still and watch what God does.
