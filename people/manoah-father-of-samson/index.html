Manoah, Father of Samson

Manoah of Zorah, a Danite, was Samson's father; he and his wife received the angel's announcement of Samson's birth and offered a sacrifice, and Manoah later went with Samson to Timnath.

Key Story

Manoah prayed to see the man of God again, offered a kid and an offering on the rock, watched the angel ascend in the flame, and afterwards accompanied Samson to Timnath.

Life Lesson

Manoah feared he would die for seeing God — and learned that God's message was life, not death.
