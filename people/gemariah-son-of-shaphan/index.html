Gemariah, Son of Shaphan

Gemariah, son of Shaphan, was a scribe and prince in whose chamber Baruch read Jeremiah's roll to the people; he and other princes pleaded with Jehoiakim not to burn the roll. He is a different man from Gemariah son of Hilkiah.

Key Story

Baruch read Jeremiah's words in the chamber of Gemariah son of Shaphan in the Lord's house, and Gemariah joined the princes who begged the king not to burn the roll.

Life Lesson

Hearing God's word in a side chamber still demands a public stand when the king reaches for the fire.
