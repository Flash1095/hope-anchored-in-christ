Summary
Achan, son of Carmi, took devoted plunder at Jericho and brought defeat on Israel at Ai, and he and his household were stoned in the Valley of Achor.
Key Story
When Israel fled before Ai, Joshua found that Achan had hidden a Babylonian garment, silver, and gold in his tent, and Israel stoned him and burned what was his.
