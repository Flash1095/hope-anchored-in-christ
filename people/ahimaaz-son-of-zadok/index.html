Ahimaaz, Son of Zadok

Ahimaaz, son of Zadok, ran messages for David during Absalom's revolt, hiding with Jonathan son of Abiathar at En-rogel, and later outran the Cushite to bring David news of Absalom's death.

Key Story

Ahimaaz outran every messenger to bring David the news he dreaded.

Life Lesson

The fastest runner still has to deliver hard news.
