Jonadab

Name
Jonadab
Role
Ancestor of the Rechabites
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Jonadab, son of Rechab, commanded his descendants to drink no wine, build no houses, sow no seed, and live in tents; generations later his sons still obeyed him, and Jeremiah held their obedience up against Judah's refusal to obey God.
Key Story
Jonadab's command outlived him by centuries: his sons refused Jeremiah's wine in the temple because their father had forbidden it.
Key Verses
Jeremiah 35:6-19
Related Books
Jeremiah
Related People
Jaazaniah, Son of Jeremiah; Jeremiah
Life Lesson
A father's faithful command can echo for generations — and God notices when children keep it better than his own people keep his.
