Seraiah

Name

Role

Testament

Summary
Seraiah was the scribe in David's administration, recording the kingdom's affairs beside Joab, Jehoshaphat, Zadok, and Abiathar.
Key Story
Seraiah kept the records of David's kingdom at its height.
