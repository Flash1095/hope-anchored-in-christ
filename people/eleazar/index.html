Eleazar

Eleazar was a son of Aaron and Elisheba, and the priest who succeeded Aaron after his death.

Key Story

Eleazar served through the wilderness years, stood with Joshua at the division of the land, and carried the priesthood after Aaron.

Life Lesson

Faithfulness over a long life is its own kind of greatness.
