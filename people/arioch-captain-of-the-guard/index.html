Arioch, Captain of the Guard

Arioch was captain of Nebuchadnezzar's guard who was sent to slay the wise men of Babylon when none could tell the king's dream; Daniel asked him why the decree was so hasty, and Arioch brought Daniel before the king. He is a different man from Arioch, king of Ellasar, who already has a profile.

Key Story

Arioch was commanded to destroy the wise men, but Daniel sought him with counsel and wisdom, and Arioch brought Daniel in haste before the king.

Life Lesson

Even an executioner's errand can be interrupted by a wise question.
