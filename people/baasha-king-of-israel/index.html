Baasha, King of Israel

Baasha of Issachar conspired against Nadab, wiped out Jeroboam's house as prophesied, yet walked in Jeroboam's sins himself and warred with Asa of Judah.

Key Story

Baasha wipes out Jeroboam's house and repeats his sin.

Life Lesson

God may use a man to judge another's sin and still judge that man for his own.
