Summary
Abednego (KJV: Abed-nego) was the Babylonian name given to Azariah, one of Daniel's three companions; he was set over the affairs of the province of Babylon and, with Shadrach and Meshach, refused the golden image and walked out of the fiery furnace unharmed. ONE profile covers both names; he is a different man from Azariah son of Hoshaiah.
Key Story
Abednego was bound and cast into the midst of the burning fiery furnace with his two friends, and the king saw four men walking loose in the fire, unhurt.
