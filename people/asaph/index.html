Asaph

Name
Asaph
Role
Psalmist; Musician
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Asaph was a psalmist whose name heads twelve psalms, from the great Psalm 50 to the laments and histories of Psalms 73 to 83.
Key Story
His psalms wrestle honestly with the prosperity of the wicked, the destruction of the sanctuary, and God's faithfulness to Israel's story.
Key Verses
Psalm 50:1; Psalm 73:1; 1 Chronicles 6:39; 1 Chronicles 15:17; 1 Chronicles 16:5; Ezra 2:41; Nehemiah 7:44; Nehemiah 11:17; Nehemiah 12:35; 2 Chronicles 5:12; 2 Chronicles 29:30; 2 Chronicles 35:15
Related Books
Psalms; 1 Chronicles; Ezra; Nehemiah; 2 Chronicles
Related People
King David; King Solomon; King Hezekiah
Life Lesson
Asaph teaches that doubt and grief belong in worship — he brought his hardest questions to God instead of away from Him.
Related Places
Jerusalem
Related Events
