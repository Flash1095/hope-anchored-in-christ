Hebron, Son of Kohath

Name

Son of Kohath
Testament

Timeline Era

Summary
Hebron was a son of Kohath and a grandson of Levi, named in the genealogy of Moses' tribe.
Key Story
Hebron is named among Kohath's four sons — Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel — in the record of Levi's house.
