John the Apostle

Name
John the Apostle
Role
Apostle; Evangelist
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
John, brother of James, was the disciple whom Jesus loved, who leaned on Jesus at the Last Supper, stood at the cross, and cared for Mary afterwards. He wrote a Gospel, three letters, and Revelation, and lived to old age in exile on Patmos.
Key Story
At the cross, Jesus entrusted His mother to John, and from that hour John took her into his own home.
Key Verses
John 19:26-27; John 21:7; Revelation 1:9
Related Books
John; 1 John; 2 John; 3 John; Revelation
Related People
James, Son of Zebedee; Jesus; Simon Peter
Life Lesson
John's long life shows the fruit of abiding close to Jesus' heart. Stay near Him, beloved — love is both the message and the method of a life that lasts.
Related Places
Galilee; Capernaum; Jerusalem; Patmos; Ephesus
