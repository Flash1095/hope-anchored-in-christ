Summary
Medad was one of the seventy elders on whom the Spirit rested in the wilderness; though he stayed in the camp, he prophesied there.
Key Story
When the Spirit fell on the elders, Medad prophesied in the camp, and Moses refused to stop him, wishing all the LORD's people were prophets.
