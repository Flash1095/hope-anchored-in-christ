Dodo, Father of Puah

Dodo is named in Tola's lineage, as the father of Puah.

Key Story

Dodo appears in Judges only in Tola's genealogy: Tola the son of Puah, the son of Dodo.

Life Lesson

A grandfather's name, kept alive in a grandson's introduction.
