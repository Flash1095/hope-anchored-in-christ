Shemaiah, of the First Company

Shemaiah went in the first company at the dedication procession, with Judah, Benjamin, and Jeremiah. NOTE ON IDENTITY: several men named Shemaiah appear in Nehemiah; the text does not say whether this is the Shemaiah of the covenant sealers or the dedication singers, so this profile covers only the Nehemiah 12:34 company leader and does not merge him with any other Shemaiah.

Key Story

The dedication procession

Life Lesson

Worship goes first, in ordered ranks.
