Machir, Son of Ammiel

Name
Machir, Son of Ammiel
Role
Lord of Lo-debar
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Machir, son of Ammiel of Lo-debar, had sheltered Mephibosheth, Jonathan's lame son, in his house. He later brought provisions to David at Mahanaim during Absalom's revolt.
Key Story
Machir sheltered Jonathan's son in Lo-debar, then fed David at Mahanaim.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 9:4; 2 Samuel 17:27
Related Books
2 Samuel
Related People
Mephibosheth, Son of Jonathan; King David; Ammiel, Father of Machir
Life Lesson
The man who shelters the forgotten is ready to feed the king.
