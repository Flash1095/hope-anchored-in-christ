Jabal

Jabal was the son of Lamech and Adah, and the father of those who dwell in tents and keep cattle.

Key Story

Jabal pioneered the nomadic life of herdsmen, a whole way of living that began with him.

Life Lesson

Ordinary work — raising herds, pitching tents — is part of God's good design for human life.
