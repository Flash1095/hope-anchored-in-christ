Gaius (3 John)

Name
Gaius (3 John)
Role
Wellbeloved host commended by John
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Gaius was the wellbeloved recipient of John's third letter, commended because he walked in the truth and showed hospitality to travelling brethren. He is treated here as distinct from Gaius of Corinth, since the letters place them in different churches.
