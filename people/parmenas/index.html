Parmenas

Parmenas was one of the seven men chosen to serve tables in the Jerusalem church. Acts names him among the seven and records nothing further of him by name.

Key Story

Parmenas was chosen with the seven, men of honest report, full of the Holy Spirit and wisdom.

Life Lesson

A church that feeds its widows fairly needs honest hands. Parmenas was one of them.
