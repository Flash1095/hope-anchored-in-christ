Achbor, Son of Michaiah

Achbor, son of Michaiah, was one of Josiah's messengers sent to inquire of Huldah concerning the found book.

Key Story

Achbor sent to inquire of Huldah.

Life Lesson

Inquiry is the right response to a word that condemns us.
