Gedaliah, Son of Pashur

Name
Gedaliah, Son of Pashur
Role
Prince of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Summary
Gedaliah, son of Pashur, was one of the princes who demanded Jeremiah's death, charging that his prophecies weakened the hands of the soldiers and the people. He is a different man from Gedaliah the governor.
Key Story
Gedaliah son of Pashur told Zedekiah that Jeremiah ought to be put to death, and Jeremiah was let down into the dungeon of Malchiah.
