Methuselah

Name
Methuselah
Role
Longest-Lived Patriarch
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Methuselah was the son of Enoch and the grandfather of Noah, living 969 years — the longest lifespan recorded in Scripture. His life spanned the generations between Enoch's walk with God and the flood.
Key Story
Methuselah's long life meant that for centuries, a living link to Enoch's godly witness remained on earth. He died in the very year the flood came, closing the long age of patience before judgment.
Key Verses
Genesis 5:21-27; Luke 3:37
Related Books
Genesis; Luke
Related People
Enoch; Noah
Life Lesson
Methuselah's 969 years are a picture of God's long patience. Judgment was delayed for generations — a reminder that God's slowness is mercy, giving time to turn.
