Summary
Mishael was a son of Uzziel and a grandson of Kohath, named in the genealogy of Moses' tribe.
Key Story
Mishael and his brother Elzaphan were called by Moses to carry the bodies of Nadab and Abihu out of the camp.

Exodus 6:22; Leviticus 10:4
