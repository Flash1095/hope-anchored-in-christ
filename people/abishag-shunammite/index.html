Abishag, Shunammite

Name
Abishag, Shunammite
Role
Attendant of David
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Abishag, a fair young Shunammite, was brought to cherish the aged David in his last days, and after his death Adonijah's request for her as wife exposed his renewed claim to the throne.
Key Story
Abishag cherishes David and becomes Adonijah's pretext.
Key Verses
1 Kings 1:3; 1 Kings 2:22
Related Books
1 Kings
Related People
King David; King Solomon; Adonijah, Son of Haggith
Life Lesson
Even a quiet servant can become the occasion that exposes a hidden ambition.
