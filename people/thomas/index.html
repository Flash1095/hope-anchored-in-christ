Life of Christ
Summary
Thomas was an apostle known for honest doubt who, after seeing the risen Jesus' wounds, made one of the greatest confessions in Scripture: My Lord and my God. Tradition holds he carried the gospel as far as India.
Key Story
Thomas refused to believe until he touched Jesus' wounds, and a week later the risen Lord invited him to do exactly that — and stop doubting and believe.
Key Verses
John 11:16; John 20:27-28
Related Books
John
Related People
Jesus; Simon Peter
Life Lesson
Jesus did not shame Thomas for his questions; He showed him His wounds. Bring your honest doubts to the risen Jesus — He meets questioners with evidence and grace.
