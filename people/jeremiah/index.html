Jeremiah

Name
Jeremiah
Role
Prophet; The Weeping Prophet
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Jeremiah prophesied through Judah's final decades, warning of exile, weeping over Jerusalem, and announcing God's new covenant of hearts made new. He suffered imprisonment and rejection yet never stopped speaking God's word.
Key Story
From a prison courtyard, Jeremiah bought a field in a besieged land because God promised houses and fields would again be bought in Israel.
Key Verses
Jeremiah 1:5; Jeremiah 29:11; Jeremiah 32:15; Lamentations 3:22-23
Related Books
Jeremiah; Lamentations
Related People
King Josiah; Ezekiel; Daniel
Life Lesson
Jeremiah's God declares, I know the plans I have for you — spoken to exiles, not the comfortable. Even in a season that feels like exile, His plans for you are good and full of hope.
Related Places
Jerusalem; Egypt
