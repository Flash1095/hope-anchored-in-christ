James, Son of Alphaeus

Name
James, Son of Alphaeus
Role
Apostle
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
James, son of Alphaeus, was one of the Twelve apostles, sometimes called James the Less to distinguish him from James son of Zebedee. Though Scripture records little of him, he was chosen by Jesus and present in the upper room after the ascension.
Key Story
James was named by Jesus in the choosing of the Twelve and waited with the other apostles in Jerusalem for the promised Holy Spirit.
Key Verses
Matthew 10:3; Acts 1:13
Related Books
Matthew; Mark; Luke; Acts
Related People
Jesus; Thaddaeus
Life Lesson
James the Less reminds us that God honours faithful people whose names history barely records. You do not need a spotlight to have a significant place in Jesus' mission.
