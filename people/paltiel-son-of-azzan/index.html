Paltiel, Son of Azzan

Role

Testament

Timeline Era

Summary
Paltiel, Son of Azzan was the prince of Issachar appointed to divide the land of Canaan by inheritance among the tribes.
Key Story
Paltiel was named by the LORD, with Eleazar and Joshua, to apportion the promised land to the nine and a half tribes.
