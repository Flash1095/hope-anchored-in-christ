Korah, Son of Esau

Name
Korah, Son of Esau
Role
Son of Esau
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Korah was a son of Esau by Aholibamah, and one of the dukes of Edom.
Key Story
Korah is named among Aholibamah's sons and among Edom's dukes — a different Korah from the rebel in Moses' day.
Key Verses
Genesis 36:5-14; Genesis 36:18
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Esau; Aholibamah; Jeush; Jaalam
Life Lesson
Two men can share a name and walk very different paths; God knows each one.
