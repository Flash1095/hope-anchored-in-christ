Amon, King of Judah

Amon, son of Manasseh, walked in all his father's ways and served the idols his father served, and after two years his servants conspired and slew him.

Key Story

Amon's two-year reign and assassination.

Life Lesson

Following a father's sins without his repentance is a short road to ruin.
