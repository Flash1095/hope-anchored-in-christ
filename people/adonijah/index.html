Summary
Adonijah, David's fourth son by Haggith, was born at Hebron. In 2 Samuel he appears only in the list of David's sons born there; his later attempt to seize the throne belongs to the story of Kings.
Key Story
Adonijah, son of Haggith, was David's fourth son, born at Hebron.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 3:4; Nehemiah 10:16
Related Books
2 Samuel; Nehemiah
Related People
King David; Haggith; Absalom
Life Lesson
Birth order is not destiny; the fourth son's story would wait for another book.
