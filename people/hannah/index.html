Hannah

Name
Hannah
Role

Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Hannah was a barren woman whose tearful prayer at Shiloh was answered with a son, Samuel, whom she dedicated to the Lord. Her song of praise became a pattern for Mary's Magnificat.
Key Story
Weeping in the tabernacle, Hannah vowed her child to God, and the Lord remembered her and gave her Samuel and later more children.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 1:10-20; 1 Samuel 2:1-10
Related Books
1 Samuel
Related People
Samuel; Eli; Elkanah
Life Lesson
Hannah poured out her sorrow before God and left the altar with a lifted face before anything had changed. Prayer anchors hope long before the answer arrives.
