Deborah, Rebekah's Nurse

Deborah was the nurse who came with Rebekah from her homeland and stayed with her household until her death.

Key Story

Deborah died near Bethel on Jacob's journey home and was buried under an oak called Allon-bachuth, the oak of weeping.

Life Lesson

A lifetime of quiet, faithful service is mourned deeply — Deborah's oak was named for weeping.
