Pekah, Son of Remaliah

Name
Pekah, Son of Remaliah
Role
King of Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Pekah, son of Remaliah, slew a hundred and twenty thousand valiant men of Judah in one day because they had forsaken the LORD, and carried away two hundred thousand captives to Samaria.
Key Story
Pekah's slaughter of Judah was answered by Oded's rebuke, and the captives were clothed, fed and returned to Jericho.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 28:6; 2 Chronicles 28:8
Related Books
2 Chronicles
Related People
Ahaz, King of Judah; Oded, Prophet of Samaria
Life Lesson
Victory won in rage against brethren invites God's rebuke, not His blessing.
Related Places
Samaria
