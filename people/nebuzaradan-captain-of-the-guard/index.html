Nebuzaradan, Captain of the Guard

Name
Nebuzaradan, Captain of the Guard
Role
Captain of Babylon's Guard
Testament
Old
Timeline Era

Summary
Nebuzaradan, captain of Nebuchadnezzar's guard, burned the temple and the king's house, broke Jerusalem's walls, carried the people away, and slew the chief priests at Riblah.
Key Story
Nebuzaradan burns Jerusalem and carries Judah away.
Key Verses
2 Kings 25:8; 2 Kings 25:11
Related Books

Life Lesson
