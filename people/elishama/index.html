Elishama

Role

Testament
Old
Timeline Era

Summary
Elishama the scribe was a prince of Jehoiakim's court in whose chamber the princes laid Jeremiah's roll before taking it to the king, who then sent Jehudi to fetch it and burned it.
Key Story
The princes laid the roll in the chamber of Elishama the scribe, then told the king its words, and the king sent Jehudi to bring the roll and burned it.
Key Verses
Jeremiah 36:12; Jeremiah 36:20-21
Related Books
Jeremiah
Related People
Jeremiah; Baruch; Jehudi
Life Lesson
A scribe's chamber can hold God's word for an hour and lose it the next — custody is not the same as obedience.
