Summary
Nabal was a wealthy but foolish landowner of Maon whose name means 'fool.' His refusal to feed David's men nearly brought destruction on his household until Abigail intervened.
Key Story
When David's men, who had protected Nabal's shepherds, asked for provisions, Nabal insulted them. Abigail hurried out with food and wisdom, saving her household; days later Nabal died and David married Abigail.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 25:2-38
Related Books
1 Samuel
Related People
Abigail; King David
Life Lesson
Nabal's wealth could not buy wisdom, and his pride nearly cost everyone around him. A generous, timely word — like Abigail's — can still turn a foolish moment away from disaster.
