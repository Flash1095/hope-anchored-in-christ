Obadiah

Obadiah is the shortest book of the Old Testament: a single-chapter prophecy against Edom, probably delivered around or after Jerusalem's fall in 586 BC (the exact date is debated). He condemned Edom for standing by — and joining in — when Babylon plundered Judah, and promised that the kingdom shall be the LORD's.

Key Story

Obadiah pronounced judgment on Edom for its pride and its violence against brother Jacob in the day of Jerusalem's calamity.

Life Lesson

Pride falls hardest when it feeds on a brother's suffering. Do not rejoice over another's calamity — the day of the LORD comes to all nations alike.
