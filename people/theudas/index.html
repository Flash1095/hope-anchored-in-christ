Summary
Theudas was a rebel leader who rose before the days of the census, claiming to be somebody, and gathered about four hundred men before he was slain and his followers scattered. Gamaliel cited him to counsel restraint toward the apostles.
Key Story
Theudas rose, boasting himself to be somebody, and was slain, and all who obeyed him were scattered.

Life Lesson
Movements built on somebody scatter when somebody falls. Theudas warns every cause that rests on a man.
