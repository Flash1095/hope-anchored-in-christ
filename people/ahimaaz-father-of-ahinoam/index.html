Ahimaaz, Father of Ahinoam

Ahimaaz is named as the father of Ahinoam, Saul's wife, and so the grandfather of Saul's children.

Key Story

Ahimaaz's daughter married into the first royal house of Israel.

Life Lesson

Family ties bind us into stories far larger than our own.
