Summary
Hadadezer, son of Rehob and king of Zobah, was David's great Syrian enemy. David smote him as he went to recover his border at the Euphrates, took his chariots and horsemen, and later defeated his confederate army under Shobach. The name is spelled Hadarezer in 2 Samuel 10.
Key Story
Hadadezer's Syrian empire broke against David at the Euphrates and again at Helam.
