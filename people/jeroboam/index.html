Jeroboam

Name
Jeroboam
Role
King of Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Jeroboam was the first king of the northern kingdom of Israel after the division, who set up golden calves at Bethel and Dan and led Israel into idolatry. His sin became the sad refrain of Israel's history.
Key Story
Fearing his people would return to Jerusalem, Jeroboam made two golden calves and declared, Here are your gods, O Israel.
Key Verses
1 Kings 12:28-30; 1 Kings 14:16
Related Books
1 Kings; 2 Kings
Related People
Rehoboam; King Solomon
Life Lesson
Jeroboam traded God's promise for political security and built idols instead. Trust God's promises rather than securing yourself with substitutes that lead you away from Him.
Related Places
Shechem; Bethel
