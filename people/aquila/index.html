Summary
Aquila was a Jewish tentmaker from Pontus who, with his wife Priscilla, worked alongside Paul in Corinth, hosted a church in their home, and risked his life for Paul. He travelled with Paul and settled in Ephesus and later Rome.
Key Story
Aquila and Priscilla worked at tentmaking beside Paul by day and hosted the church in their house, serving across three major cities.
Key Verses
Acts 18:2-3; Romans 16:3-4; 1 Corinthians 16:19
Related Books
Acts; Romans; 1 Corinthians
Related People
Priscilla; Paul
Life Lesson
Aquila shows that ordinary work and an open home can carry an extraordinary ministry. Your trade and your table are sacred ground when offered to God.

Corinth; Ephesus; Rome
