Elnathan

Name
Elnathan
Role
Prince
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Elnathan, son of Achbor, was a prince of Jehoiakim's court who was sent to Egypt to seize the prophet Urijah and bring him back to be killed, and who later pleaded with the king not to burn Jeremiah's roll.
Key Story
Elnathan fetched Urijah from Egypt for Jehoiakim, yet later joined the princes who begged the king not to burn Jeremiah's roll.
Key Verses
Jeremiah 26:22; Jeremiah 36:12; Jeremiah 36:25; Ezra 8:16
Related Books
Jeremiah; Ezra
Related People
Jehoiakim, King of Judah; Jeremiah; Urijah, Son of Shemaiah; Baruch; Ezra
Life Lesson
The same man can carry out a king's cruelty and then plead against it — partial courage is still recorded.
Related Places
Jerusalem; Egypt
