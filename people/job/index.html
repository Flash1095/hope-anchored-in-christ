Job

Job

Suffering Patriarch
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Job was a blameless and upright man of Uz who lost his wealth, children, and health yet refused to curse God. His story wrestles honestly with suffering and God's sovereignty.
Key Story
After losing everything, Job tore his robe, fell to the ground in worship, and said the Lord gave and the Lord has taken away; blessed be the name of the Lord.
Key Verses
Job 1:21; Job 42:10; Ezekiel 14:14; James 5:11
Related Books
Job; Ezekiel; James
Related People
Eliphaz; Bildad; Zophar
Life Lesson
Job shows you can bring your raw questions to God and still be held by Him. In the end God restored Job doubly — our suffering is real, but it is not final.
