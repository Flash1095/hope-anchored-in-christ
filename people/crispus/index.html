Crispus

Name
Crispus
Role
Ruler of the Corinthian Synagogue
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Crispus was the chief ruler of the synagogue at Corinth who believed on the Lord with all his house after Paul's preaching, and was baptised by Paul himself.
Key Story
Crispus, the synagogue ruler, believed with all his house and was baptised by Paul's own hand.
Key Verses
Acts 18:8; 1 Corinthians 1:14
Related Books
Acts; 1 Corinthians
Related People
Paul; Sosthenes
Life Lesson
The synagogue ruler became a church member. Crispus crossed the line his office had drawn.
Related Places
Corinth
