Summary
Zilpah was Leah's handmaid, given to Jacob as a wife, and the mother of Gad and Asher.
Key Story
When Leah stopped bearing children, she gave Zilpah to Jacob, and Zilpah bore Gad and then Asher.

Genesis 29:24; Genesis 30:9-13; Genesis 46:18
