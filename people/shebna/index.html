Shebna

Name
Shebna
Role
Steward and Scribe of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Shebna was the steward over Hezekiah's household and a scribe; Isaiah rebuked him for carving himself a grand tomb in the rock, and foretold that he would be thrust from office and replaced by Eliakim.
Key Story
Isaiah confronted Shebna the treasurer, who was hewing out a sepulchre on high, and announced that God would drive him from his station.
Key Verses
Isaiah 22:15-19; Isaiah 36:3; Isaiah 36:22
Related Books
Isaiah; 2 Kings
Related People

Life Lesson
Office used for self-glory is taken away; God gives it to another.
