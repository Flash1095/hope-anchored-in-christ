Lapidoth, Husband of Deborah

Lapidoth is named only as the husband of Deborah, the prophetess and judge.

Key Story

Lapidoth appears in Judges only as the husband of Deborah, who judged Israel under the palm tree between Ramah and Bethel.

Life Lesson

Standing beside a great calling is itself a place in the story.
