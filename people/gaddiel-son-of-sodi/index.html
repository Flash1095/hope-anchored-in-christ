Summary
Gaddiel, Son of Sodi was one of the twelve spies Moses sent from the tribe of Zebulun to search out the land of Canaan.
Key Story
Gaddiel spent forty days spying out Canaan, then joined the ten who brought back a fearful report that turned Israel back into the wilderness.
Key Verses
Numbers 13:10
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Sodi; Moses; Caleb; Joshua
Life Lesson
Seeing the same land as Caleb, he saw only giants — faith is a choice about what you trust.
