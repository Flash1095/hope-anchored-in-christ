Summary
Adoni-bezek was a Canaanite king captured at Bezek by Judah and Simeon; after his thumbs and great toes were cut off, he acknowledged that God had repaid him as he had done to seventy kings, and he died at Jerusalem.
Key Story
Judah and Simeon found Adoni-bezek at Bezek, cut off his thumbs and great toes, and brought him to Jerusalem, where he died.

Life Lesson
