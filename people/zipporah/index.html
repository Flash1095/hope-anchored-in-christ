Zipporah

Name
Zipporah
Role
Wife of Moses
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Zipporah was a daughter of Jethro, priest of Midian, and the wife of Moses. She bore him Gershom and Eliezer.
Key Story
Zipporah married Moses during his years in Midian, and on the journey back to Egypt she acted swiftly to spare Moses' life.
Key Verses
Exodus 2:21-22; Exodus 4:24-26; Exodus 18:2-4
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Moses; Jethro; Gershom; Eliezer, Son of Moses
Life Lesson
A spouse's quick, brave action can save the very mission God gave.

Midian; Egypt
