Uri, Father of Bezaleel

Uri, son of Hur, was the father of Bezaleel, the craftsman who made the bronze altar.

Key Story

Uri is named only as the father of Bezaleel, son of Hur, maker of the bronze altar set before the tabernacle.

Life Lesson

A faithful line can be remembered through the work God gives the next generation.
