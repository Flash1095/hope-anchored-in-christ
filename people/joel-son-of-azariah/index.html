Joel, Son of Azariah

Joel, son of Azariah of the Kohathites, was one of the Levites who arose to cleanse the house of the LORD under Hezekiah.

Key Story

The Levites arose, Mahath the son of Amasai and Joel the son of Azariah, of the sons of the Kohathites.

Life Lesson

Two Levites named together began eight days of cleansing.
