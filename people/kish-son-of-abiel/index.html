Kish, Son of Abiel

Kish, son of Abiel, was a mighty man of power from Benjamin. His lost donkeys sent Saul searching, and that search led Saul to Samuel and to the throne.

Key Story

Kish's lost donkeys set in motion the day his son was anointed king.

Life Lesson

God often steers a life through the most ordinary errand.
