Hadad, Prince of Edom

Name
Hadad, Prince of Edom
Role
Edomite Prince
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Hadad, an Edomite prince of the king's seed, fled to Egypt when Joab smote Edom, was given Pharaoh's sister-in-law to wife, and returned to trouble Solomon after David's death.
Key Story
Hadad flees to Egypt and returns as Solomon's adversary.
Key Verses
1 Kings 11:14; 1 Kings 11:17
Related Books
1 Kings
Related People
King Solomon; Tahpenes, Queen of Egypt; Genubath, Son of Hadad
Life Lesson
An old enemy spared becomes a new adversary: mercy without repentance repeats itself.
