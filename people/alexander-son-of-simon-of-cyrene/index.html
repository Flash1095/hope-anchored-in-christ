Alexander, Son of Simon of Cyrene

Alexander was a son of Simon of Cyrene, named by Mark alongside his brother Rufus, which suggests both brothers were known to Mark's readers as members of the early church.

Key Story

Mark names Alexander only as the son of the man who carried Jesus' cross.

Life Lesson

A father's one afternoon at the cross shaped a family's faith. Alexander is remembered because Simon carried it.
