Cain

Name
Cain
Role
Firstborn Son; First Murderer
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Cain was the firstborn son of Adam and Eve, a farmer whose offering God did not regard. In jealous anger he killed his brother Abel, becoming the first murderer in Scripture.
Key Story
God warned Cain that sin was crouching at his door and urged him to master it, but Cain lured Abel into a field and killed him. Even then God marked Cain to protect his life.
Key Verses
Genesis 4:3-8; Genesis 4:6-7; 1 John 3:12
Related Books
Genesis; 1 John
Related People
Adam; Eve; Abel; Seth
Life Lesson
Cain's story is a warning to deal with anger before it deals with us. God still speaks before we fall — and even after failure, He sets limits on vengeance and leaves room to turn.
