Life of Christ
Summary
Rufus was a son of Simon of Cyrene, named by Mark alongside his brother Alexander. Paul later greets a Rufus, chosen in the Lord, in Rome, who may be the same man, though the text does not say so.
Key Story
Mark names Rufus only as the son of the man who carried Jesus' cross.
Key Verses
Mark 15:21
Related Books
Mark
Related People
Simon of Cyrene; Alexander, Son of Simon of Cyrene
Life Lesson
A name in one verse can outlive an empire. Rufus is remembered because his father carried a cross.
