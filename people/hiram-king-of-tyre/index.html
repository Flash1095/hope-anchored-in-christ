Hiram, King of Tyre

Hiram, king of Tyre, sent messengers, cedar trees, carpenters, and masons to David and built him a house at Jerusalem, a sign that David's kingdom was established. Also known as Huram.

Key Story

Hiram of Tyre built David a cedar house, the first foreign recognition of his kingdom.

Life Lesson

Foreign kings honouring God's anointed is itself a testimony.
