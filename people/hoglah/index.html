Hoglah

Name
Hoglah
Role
Daughter of Zelophehad
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Hoglah was one of the five daughters of Zelophehad who asked Moses for their father's inheritance.
Key Story
Hoglah stood with her sisters before Moses and the congregation, and God granted daughters the right to inherit when there is no son.
Key Verses
Numbers 27:1-11; 36:11
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Zelophehad; Mahlah; Noah, Daughter of Zelophehad; Milcah, Daughter of Zelophehad; Tirzah; Moses
Life Lesson
Five sisters standing together changed the law of inheritance.
