Gamaliel, the Pharisee

Gamaliel was a Pharisee and honoured teacher of the law in Jerusalem, and Paul's teacher. Before the Sanhedrin he counselled restraint toward the apostles, arguing that if their work were of men it would fail, but if of God it could not be overthrown. He is distinct from Gamaliel son of Pedahzur of the wilderness generation.

Key Story

Gamaliel told the council to leave the apostles alone, for if this work were of God, no man could overthrow it.

Life Lesson

Gamaliel's counsel still stands: works of men fail on their own, and works of God cannot be stopped by councils.
