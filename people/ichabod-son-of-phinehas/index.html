Ichabod, Son of Phinehas

Name
Ichabod, Son of Phinehas
Role
Son of Phinehas
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Ichabod was born as his mother lay dying on the day the ark was captured and his father and grandfather died. She named him Ichabod, meaning 'the glory is departed from Israel.'
Key Story
Ichabod's very name marked the day Israel lost the ark, his father, and his grandfather at once.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 4:19; 1 Samuel 4:21; 1 Samuel 14:3
Related Books
1 Samuel
Related People
Phinehas, Son of Eli
Life Lesson
A name can carry a nation's grief — and point to how much God's presence is worth.
