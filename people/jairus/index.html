Life of Christ
Summary
Jairus was a ruler of the synagogue whose twelve-year-old daughter was dying. He begged Jesus to come, believed through the news of her death, and saw Jesus take her by the hand and raise her.
Key Story
Jairus heard that his daughter was dead, and Jesus told him, Fear not, believe only, and she shall be made whole.
Key Verses
Mark 5:22; Luke 8:41
Related Books
Mark; Luke
Related People
Jesus
Life Lesson
Bad news is not the last word when Jesus is on the way. Jairus believed past the point of hope.
