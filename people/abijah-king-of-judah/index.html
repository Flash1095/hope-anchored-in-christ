Abijah, King of Judah

Name
Abijah, King of Judah
Role
King of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Abijah, son of Rehoboam and Maachah, reigned three years in Jerusalem; he trusted the LORD against Jeroboam's far larger army and won a great victory, though he married fourteen wives.
Key Story
Abijah stood on Mount Zemaraim, appealed to the covenant with David, and Judah prevailed because they relied on the LORD God of their fathers.
Key Verses
