Summary
Blastus was the king's chamberlain through whom the people of Tyre and Sidon sought peace with Herod Agrippa I, having made him their friend, because their country was nourished by the king's country.
Key Story
Tyre and Sidon made Blastus their friend to sue for peace with the king who fed them.
