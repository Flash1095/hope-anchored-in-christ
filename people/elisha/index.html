Elisha

Name
Elisha
Role
Prophet; Successor of Elijah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Elisha succeeded Elijah and performed twice as many miracles, showing God's compassion to widows, kings, and a foreign commander. His ministry spanned several kings of Israel.
Key Story
Elisha received Elijah's mantle at the Jordan and healed Naaman the Syrian, who washed seven times and was cleansed of leprosy.
Key Verses
2 Kings 2:9-14; 2 Kings 5:14; Luke 4:27
Related Books
2 Kings; Luke
Related People
Elijah; The Shunammite Woman
Life Lesson
Elisha asked for a double portion and then simply got to work serving people. Ask God boldly, then spend that blessing on compassion for those around you.
Related Places
Samaria; Jericho; Gilgal; Mount Carmel
