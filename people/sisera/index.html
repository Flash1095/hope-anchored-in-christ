Sisera

Name

Role

Testament
Old
Timeline Era

Summary
Sisera commanded Jabin's army with nine hundred chariots of iron; defeated at the Kishon, he fled to Jael's tent, where she killed him with a tent peg.
Key Story
Sisera's host was routed at the Kishon, and he fled on foot to the tent of Jael, wife of Heber, who gave him milk, covered him, and drove a nail through his temples as he slept.
