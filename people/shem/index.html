Shem

Shem was one of Noah's three sons and the ancestor of the Semitic peoples, including Abraham. Noah blessed him, saying the Lord would be the God of Shem.

Key Story

After the flood, Shem and Japheth walked backward with a garment to cover their father's shame without looking on him. Through Shem's line came Abraham, Israel, and ultimately Jesus.

Life Lesson

Shem honoured his father when it cost him dignity to do so. Quiet, honouring choices in private moments are often the ones God weaves into His largest promises.
