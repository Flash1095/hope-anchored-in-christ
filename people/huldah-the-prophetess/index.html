Huldah, the Prophetess

Name
Huldah, the Prophetess
Role
Prophetess
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Huldah the prophetess, wife of Shallum keeper of the wardrobe, answered Josiah's messengers that evil would come on Jerusalem for forsaking the LORD, but because Josiah's heart was tender and he humbled himself, he would be gathered to his grave in peace and not see the evil.
Key Story
Huldah declared, 'Because thine heart was tender, and thou didst humble thyself before God... I have even heard thee also, saith the LORD.'
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 34:22; 2 Chronicles 34:27

Related People

Life Lesson
A tender, humbled heart can win peace even when judgment is certain.
