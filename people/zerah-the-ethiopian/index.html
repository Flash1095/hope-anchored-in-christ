Zerah, the Ethiopian

Zerah the Ethiopian came against Asa with an army of a thousand thousand and three hundred chariots, and was defeated at Mareshah when Asa cried to the LORD.

Key Story

Asa prayed that it is nothing with the LORD to help with many or with few, and the LORD smote the Ethiopians before Asa.

Life Lesson

No multitude is too great for the LORD to help against, when His people rest on Him.
