Zephaniah, Son of Maaseiah

Zephaniah, son of Maaseiah, was a priest — second priest at the fall — through whom Zedekiah repeatedly sent to Jeremiah, and to whom Shemaiah the Nehelamite wrote demanding Jeremiah's imprisonment. At Jerusalem's fall he was taken to Riblah and slain. He is a different man from Zephaniah the prophet.

Key Story

Zedekiah sent Zephaniah the priest to ask Jeremiah to pray for the city, and after the fall Nebuzaradan took Zephaniah to the king of Babylon at Riblah, where he was slain.

Life Lesson

Carrying messages between a king and a prophet is a dangerous middle place — and it ends where the city's sins end.
