Ishmael, Son of Nethaniah

Ishmael, son of Nethaniah, of the seed royal, came with ten men to Gedaliah at Mizpah, ate with him, and slew him and the Jews and Chaldeans with him, then fled to Ammon.

Key Story

Ishmael murders Gedaliah at Mizpah.

Life Lesson

Royal blood without loyalty is the most dangerous kind of treason.
