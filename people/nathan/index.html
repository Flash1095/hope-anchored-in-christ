Nathan

Name
Nathan
Role

Testament

Summary
Nathan was David's court prophet who announced God's everlasting covenant and later confronted David over his sin with Bathsheba. He remained loyal to God's purposes into Solomon's reign.
Key Story
Nathan told David the parable of the poor man's lamb, then declared, You are the man, leading David to repentance.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 7:12-16; 2 Samuel 12:7; Psalm 51:1
Related Books
2 Samuel; 1 Kings; Psalms
Related People
King David; Bathsheba; King Solomon
Life Lesson
Nathan loved David enough to tell him the hard truth. Real hope sometimes begins with honest conviction — and God meets the repentant with mercy, not rejection.
