Mahalath, Daughter of Jerimoth

Summary
Mahalath, daughter of Jerimoth son of David, was a wife of Rehoboam and bore him Jeush, Shamariah and Zaham.
Key Story
Mahalath bore Rehoboam three children: Jeush, Shamariah and Zaham.
