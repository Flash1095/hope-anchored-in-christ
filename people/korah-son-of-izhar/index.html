Korah, Son of Izhar

Name
Korah, Son of Izhar
Role
Son of Izhar
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Korah was a son of Izhar, a Levite, and the father of Assir, Elkanah, and Abiasaph — a different Korah from Esau's son.
Key Story
Korah is named in Exodus among Levi's house; his later rebellion against Moses is told in Numbers, where the earth swallowed him and his followers.
Key Verses
Exodus 6:21; Numbers 16
Related Books
Exodus
Related People
Izhar; Assir, Son of Korah; Elkanah, Son of Korah; Abiasaph, Son of Korah; Moses; Aaron
Life Lesson
Gifts and position do not equal God's calling: Korah wanted Aaron's place and lost everything.

Egypt
