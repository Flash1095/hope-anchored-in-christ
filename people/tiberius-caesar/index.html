Tiberius Caesar

Name
Tiberius Caesar
Role
Roman Emperor
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Tiberius Caesar was emperor when John the Baptist began preaching and when Jesus was crucified under Pontius Pilate. Luke dates the Baptist's ministry to the fifteenth year of his reign.
Key Story
Luke dates the gospel to the fifteenth year of Tiberius, tying John's preaching to a real emperor's reign.
Key Verses
Luke 3:1
Related Books
Luke
Related People
John the Baptist; Pontius Pilate
Life Lesson
The gospel happened under a named emperor in a numbered year. Faith here rests on history, not legend.
