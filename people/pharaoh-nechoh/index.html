Pharaoh-nechoh

Pharaoh-nechoh, also called Neco, was king of Egypt when Judah's last kings fell. He killed King Josiah at Megiddo, deposed Jehoahaz after three months, and set up Jehoiakim as a tributary king. He is the pharaoh Jeremiah names in the oracle against Egypt at Carchemish.

Key Story

Josiah's death at Megiddo

Life Lesson

Even great kings fall when they fight battles God did not send them to.
