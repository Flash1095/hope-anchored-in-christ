Ephron

Name
Ephron
Role
Son of Zohar the Hittite
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Ephron was a Hittite landowner who sold Abraham the cave of Machpelah as a burial place for Sarah.
Key Story
Ephron offered the field freely, but Abraham insisted on paying the full price — four hundred shekels of silver — so the burial ground would be indisputably his.
Key Verses
Genesis 23
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Abraham; Sarah; Zohar, Father of Ephron
Life Lesson
Honest dealing matters: Abraham paid full price rather than accept a gift that could be disputed.
