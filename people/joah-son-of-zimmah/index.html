Joah, Son of Zimmah

Name
Joah, Son of Zimmah
Role
Gershonite Levite
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Joah, son of Zimmah of the Gershonites, was one of the Levites who arose to cleanse the house of the LORD under Hezekiah, and the father of Eden.
Key Story
Of the Gershonites, Joah the son of Zimmah and Eden the son of Joah arose to cleanse the house.
Key Verses
1 Chronicles 6:21; 2 Chronicles 29:12
Related Books
1 Chronicles; 2 Chronicles
Related People
Eden, Son of Joah; King Hezekiah; Zimmah, Father of Joah; Zimmah, Son of Jahath
Life Lesson
Named in the covenant line, Joah stands among the individuals God placed within His people's story in 1 Chronicles.
