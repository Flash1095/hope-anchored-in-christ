Seraiah the Chief Priest

Seraiah the chief priest was taken with Zephaniah the second priest to Riblah after Jerusalem fell, where the king of Babylon slew them. He is a different man from Seraiah son of Neriah, Seraiah son of Azriel, and Seraiah son of Tanhumeth.

Key Story

Nebuzaradan took Seraiah the chief priest and Zephaniah the second priest to Riblah, and the king of Babylon smote them and put them to death.

Life Lesson

The highest religious office offers no shelter when the city it served has fallen.
