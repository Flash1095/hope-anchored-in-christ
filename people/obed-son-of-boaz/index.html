Obed, Son of Boaz

Obed, son of Boaz and Ruth, was born in Bethlehem and became the father of Jesse and grandfather of David, carrying the line named in Ruth's genealogy.

Key Story

When Ruth bore a son, the women of Bethlehem named him Obed and told Naomi he would be a restorer of her life; Obed became the father of Jesse, the father of David.

Life Lesson

A child born to a foreign widow became the hinge of a king's line.
