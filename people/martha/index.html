Life of Christ
Summary
Martha was Mary and Lazarus's sister in Bethany who welcomed Jesus into her home and confessed Him as the Messiah before her brother was raised. Jesus loved this family dearly.
Key Story
Distracted by serving, Martha was gently corrected by Jesus, and later at Lazarus's tomb she declared, I believe You are the Messiah, the Son of God.
Key Verses
Luke 10:38-42; John 11:27; John 12:2
Related Books
Luke; John
Related People
Mary of Bethany; Lazarus; Jesus
Life Lesson
Martha's story holds both service and faith: Jesus invites busy hearts to sit, listen, and believe. At a graveside she confessed hope — and then watched her brother walk out alive.
