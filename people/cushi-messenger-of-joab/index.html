Cushi, Messenger of Joab

Name
Cushi, Messenger of Joab
Role
Messenger to David
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Cushi is identified only by this name when Joab sent him to tell David of Absalom's death. He ran after Ahimaaz, delivered the news plainly, and answered David's question about Absalom with the words that broke the king's heart.
Key Story
Cushi, known only by his name, carried the news of Absalom's death to David.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 18:21; 2 Samuel 18:31; 2 Samuel 18:32
Related Books
2 Samuel
Related People
King David; Ahimaaz, Son of Zadok; Joab
Life Lesson
The bearer of the hardest news deserves to be remembered for telling it straight.
