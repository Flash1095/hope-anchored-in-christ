Summary
Tertullus was the orator hired by the high priest Ananias to present the Jews' case against Paul before Felix at Caesarea, accusing Paul of sedition, of being a ringleader of the Nazarenes, and of profaning the temple.
Key Story
Tertullus flattered Felix and accused Paul of sedition, calling him a ringleader of the sect of the Nazarenes.

Life Lesson
Hired eloquence can dress up any charge. Tertullus flattered the judge and slandered the prisoner.
