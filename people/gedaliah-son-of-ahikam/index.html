Gedaliah, Son of Ahikam

Gedaliah, son of Ahikam, was made governor over Judah's remnant by Nebuchadnezzar, gathered the scattered captains at Mizpah, and was murdered by Ishmael son of Nethaniah in the seventh month.

Key Story

Gedaliah governs the remnant and is murdered.

Life Lesson

A governor who trusted too easily died for the remnant's last hope.
