Summary
Darius I, king of Persia, is the Darius of Haggai and Zechariah: the Temple rebuilding resumed and was completed in his reign, in the second year of which both prophets began to preach. He is distinct from Darius the Mede of Daniel's time.
Key Story
In the second year of Darius, Haggai and Zechariah called the returned exiles back to the unfinished Temple, and the work resumed under his rule.

Life Lesson
