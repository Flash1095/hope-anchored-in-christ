Merodach-baladan

Name
Merodach-baladan
Role
King of Babylon
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Merodach-baladan, son of Baladan, was king of Babylon who sent letters and a present to Hezekiah after hearing of his sickness and recovery; Hezekiah showed the envoys all his treasures, and Isaiah foretold that Babylon would one day carry them away.
Key Story
Babylon's envoys came to Hezekiah, who showed them his silver, gold, spices, and armoury — and Isaiah announced that everything in the palace would be carried to Babylon.
Key Verses
Isaiah 39:1; Isaiah 39:2-8
Related Books
Isaiah; 2 Kings
Related People
King Hezekiah; Isaiah
Life Lesson
Showing off what God gave you to impress a foreign power invites the very loss you boast about.
