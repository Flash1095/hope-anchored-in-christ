Summary
Abiel was the father of Kish and of Ner, making him the grandfather of Saul and of Abner, Saul's commander.
Key Story
Abiel's two sons fathered both Israel's first king and his army's captain.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 9:1; 1 Samuel 14:51
Related Books

Related People

Life Lesson
