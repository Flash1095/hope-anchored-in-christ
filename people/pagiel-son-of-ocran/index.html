Pagiel, Son of Ocran

Pagiel, son of Ocran, was the prince of the tribe of Asher at the census in the wilderness.

Key Story

Pagiel led Asher's 41,500 men and brought the tribe's offering for the dedication of the altar.

Life Lesson

Every offering given in obedience is remembered by name.
