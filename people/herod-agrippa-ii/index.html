Summary
Herod Agrippa II was the last Herodian king, ruling parts of the north-east. Festus brought Paul before him at Caesarea, and Agrippa heard Paul's defence, remarking that Paul almost persuaded him to be a Christian.
Key Story
Agrippa listened to Paul's whole story and sent him to Rome, judging he had done nothing worthy of death.

Acts 25:13; Acts 26:28
