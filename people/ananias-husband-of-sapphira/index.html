Ananias, Husband of Sapphira

Ananias, with his wife Sapphira, sold land and secretly kept back part of the price while presenting the rest as the whole gift. Confronted by Peter for lying to the Holy Spirit, he fell dead, and fear came on the whole church.

Key Story

Ananias laid part of the price at the apostles' feet as if it were all, and fell dead at Peter's question.

Life Lesson

God does not demand everything, but He will not be lied to about it. Ananias kept his money and lost his life.
