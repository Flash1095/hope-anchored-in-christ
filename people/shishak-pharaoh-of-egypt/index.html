Shishak, Pharaoh of Egypt

Name
Shishak, Pharaoh of Egypt
Role
Pharaoh of Egypt
Testament
Old
Timeline Era

Summary
Shishak, king of Egypt, sheltered Jeroboam from Solomon, then in Rehoboam's fifth year came up against Jerusalem and took the temple treasures and Solomon's gold shields.
Key Story
Shishak plunders Jerusalem in Rehoboam's fifth year.
Key Verses
1 Kings 11:40; 1 Kings 14:25
Related Books
1 Kings
Related People
King Solomon; Jeroboam; Rehoboam
Life Lesson
