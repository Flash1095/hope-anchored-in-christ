Summary
Pelatiah, son of Benaiah, was one of the twenty-five princes at the east gate of the temple; as Ezekiel prophesied against them in vision, Pelatiah fell down dead, and Ezekiel cried out at the judgment.
Key Story
While Ezekiel prophesied against the princes at the east gate, Pelatiah son of Benaiah died, and Ezekiel fell on his face and cried out to the Lord.
Key Verses
Ezekiel 11:1; Ezekiel 11:13; Nehemiah 10:22
