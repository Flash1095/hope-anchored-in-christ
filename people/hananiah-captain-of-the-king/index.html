Hananiah, Captain of the King

Hananiah, one of the king's captains, oversaw the numbering of Uzziah's army by Jeiel the scribe and Maaseiah the ruler.

Key Story

The army's account was under the hand of Hananiah, one of the king's captains.

Life Lesson

A captain's oversight kept a great host in order.
