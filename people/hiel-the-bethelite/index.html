Hiel the Bethelite

Name
Hiel the Bethelite
Role
Builder of Jericho
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Hiel the Bethelite rebuilt Jericho in Ahab's days, laying its foundation at the cost of his firstborn Abiram and its gates at the cost of his youngest Segub, according to Joshua's curse.
Key Story
Hiel rebuilds Jericho at the cost of his sons.
Key Verses
1 Kings 16:34
Related Books
1 Kings
Related People
King Ahab; Abiram, Son of Hiel; Segub, Son of Hiel
Life Lesson
An old curse is not old news: Hiel paid for Jericho in his own sons.
