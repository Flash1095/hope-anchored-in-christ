Havilah, Son of Joktan

Havilah was a son of Joktan and a descendant of Eber, in the line of Shem.

Key Story

Havilah is named among Joktan's sons, whose dwelling stretched from Mesha toward Sephar.

Life Lesson

God's promise to fill the earth moved forward through families like this one.
