Simon, Brother of Jesus

Name
Simon, Brother of Jesus
Role
Brother of Jesus
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Simon was one of the brothers of Jesus named by the people of Nazareth, alongside James, Joses, and Judas. He is distinct from Simon Peter, Simon the Zealot, and every other Simon in the Gospels.
Key Story
Nazareth named Simon among Jesus' brothers, and took offence at the carpenter's son in their midst.
Key Verses
Matthew 13:55; Mark 6:3
Related Books
Matthew; Mark
Related People
Jesus; James, Brother of Jesus; Mary, Mother of Jesus
Life Lesson
Jesus' own town could name His family and still not know Him. Nearness to Jesus is not the same as faith in Him.
Related Places
Nazareth
