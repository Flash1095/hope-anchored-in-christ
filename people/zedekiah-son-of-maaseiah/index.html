Zedekiah, Son of Maaseiah

Summary
Zedekiah, son of Maaseiah, was a false prophet among the exiles in Babylon whom Jeremiah denounced alongside Ahab son of Kolaiah; Jeremiah foretold that Nebuchadnezzar would roast them in the fire. He is a different man from Zedekiah the king.
Key Story
Jeremiah told the exiles that Zedekiah son of Maaseiah and Ahab son of Kolaiah, who prophesied lies, would become a curse and a byword.
