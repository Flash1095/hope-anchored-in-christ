Abner, Son of Ner

Abner, son of Ner, commanded Saul's army, made Ish-bosheth king after Saul's death, and then defected to David, bringing the northern tribes with him. Joab murdered him at Hebron in revenge for his brother Asahel, and David publicly mourned him.

Key Story

Abner held Saul's kingdom together, then handed it to David — and was murdered for an old battlefield debt.

Life Lesson

Revenge dressed as justice still murders; David's mourning shows what a kingdom should grieve.
