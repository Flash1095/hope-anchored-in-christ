Aholiab

Aholiab, also spelled Oholiab, was the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, and Bezaleel's fellow craftsman on the tabernacle.

Key Story

God gave Aholiab skill alongside Bezaleel, and together they made the tabernacle, the ark, and the priestly garments.

Life Lesson

Art and craftsmanship are Spirit-given gifts, made for God's house.
