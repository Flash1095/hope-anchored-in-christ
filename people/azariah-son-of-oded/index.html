Azariah, Son of Oded

Azariah, son of Oded, met Asa with the word that the LORD is with His people while they are with Him, and Asa took courage and put away idols.

Key Story

The Spirit of God came upon Azariah the son of Oded, and his message moved Asa to renew the altar and gather Judah into covenant.

Life Lesson

A timely word from God can turn a king's heart back to covenant faithfulness.
