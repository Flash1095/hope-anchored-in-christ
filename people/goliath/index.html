Goliath

Goliath of Gath, nearly ten feet tall and clad in bronze, challenged Israel for forty days until David, a shepherd boy with a sling, struck him down and cut off his head with his own sword.

Key Story

Goliath's forty-day taunt ended with a single stone from a boy who trusted the Lord of hosts.

Life Lesson

The battle is the Lord's — giants fall to faith, not to armour.
