Summary
Ittai the Gittite, a Philistine from Gath with six hundred men, refused to leave David when he fled from Absalom, saying that where the king was, there would his servant be. He commanded a third of David's army in the battle against Absalom.
Key Story
Ittai the Gittite, a foreigner with six hundred men, refused to abandon the fleeing king.
