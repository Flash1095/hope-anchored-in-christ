Summary
Hosea was a prophet whose marriage to unfaithful Gomer became a living picture of God's relentless love for wayward Israel. He bought back his wife as God redeems His people.
Key Story
God told Hosea to go and love his wife again, though she was unfaithful, just as the Lord loves Israel though they turned to other gods.

Life Lesson
Hosea shows a God whose heart cries, How can I give you up? If you have wandered, His love is actively pursuing you — come home, and He will heal your waywardness.
