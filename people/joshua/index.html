Joshua

Name
Joshua
Role
Commander; Successor of Moses
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Joshua was Moses' faithful aide who led Israel into the Promised Land after Moses' death. God commissioned him to be strong and courageous, for the Lord would be with him.
Key Story
Joshua led Israel across the Jordan and around Jericho, where the walls fell after seven days of marching and a shout.
Key Verses
Joshua 1:9; Joshua 6:20; Hebrews 11:30
Related Books
Joshua; Deuteronomy; Hebrews
Related People
Moses; Caleb; Rahab
Life Lesson
Joshua stepped into enormous shoes with one repeated promise: I will be with you. Courage is not the absence of fear but confidence that God goes with you.
Related Places
Jordan River; Jericho; Gilgal; Ai; Shechem; Shiloh; Canaan
Related Events
Crossing the Jordan; The Walls of Jericho
