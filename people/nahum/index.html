Nahum

Nahum the Elkoshite prophesied against Nineveh, capital of Assyria, around 660–630 BC, about a century after Jonah's visit. Where Jonah preached mercy to a repenting city, Nahum announced its coming fall: a jealous, avenging God would not acquit the wicked, though He is a stronghold for those who trust Him.

Key Story

Nahum pictured Nineveh's chariots, flooded river gates, and plundered palaces, and declared that the city that once terrorized nations would itself be laid waste.

Life Lesson

God is both a stronghold for the trusting and an avenger of the cruel. Mercy refused hardens into judgment — but those who run to Him are never turned away.
