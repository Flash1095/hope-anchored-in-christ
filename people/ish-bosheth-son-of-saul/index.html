Ish-bosheth, Son of Saul

Name
Ish-bosheth, Son of Saul
Role
King of Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Ish-bosheth, son of Saul — also called Eshbaal — was made king over Israel by Abner after Saul's death and reigned two years from Mahanaim while David reigned at Hebron. After Abner defected, Ish-bosheth was murdered in his sleep by Rechab and Baanah, and David had the murderers executed.
Key Story
Ish-bosheth held Saul's throne for two years, then was murdered in his own bed.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 2:8; 2 Samuel 2:10; 2 Samuel 3:7; 2 Samuel 4:5; 2 Samuel 4:12
Related Books
2 Samuel
Related People
King Saul; Abner, Son of Ner; Michal; Merab
Life Lesson
A throne held by another man's strength falls when that strength walks away.
