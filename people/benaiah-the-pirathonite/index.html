Benaiah the Pirathonite

Benaiah the Pirathonite was among David's thirty mighty men. He is distinct from Benaiah son of Jehoiada, captain of the guard.

Key Story

Benaiah the Pirathonite stood among David's thirty.

Life Lesson

Two Benaiahs served David — a captain and a thirty-man — and both were valiant.
