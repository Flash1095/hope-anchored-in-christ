Elon, Judge of Israel

Elon, a Zebulonite, judged Israel ten years and was buried in Aijalon in Zebulun. He is distinct from Elon, son of Zebulun, in Genesis.

Key Story

After Ibzan, Elon the Zebulonite judged Israel ten years, and died and was buried in Aijalon in the country of Zebulun.

Life Lesson

Ten quiet years, one burial place — the whole record.
