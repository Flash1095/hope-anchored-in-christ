Nergal-sharezer

Name
Nergal-sharezer
Role
Prince of Babylon
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Summary
Nergal-sharezer was a prince of the king of Babylon who sat in the middle gate of Jerusalem when the city fell, and later — called Rab-mag — was among those sent to release Jeremiah from prison.
Key Story
When Jerusalem was taken, the princes of Babylon, including Nergal-sharezer, sat in the middle gate, and Nergal-sharezer later helped send for Jeremiah's release.
Key Verses
Jeremiah 39:3; Jeremiah 39:13
Related Books
Jeremiah
Related People
King Nebuchadnezzar; Jeremiah; Nebuzaradan
Life Lesson
The fall of a city is carried out by named men, not by faceless forces.
