Name
Jehoahaz, King of Judah
Role
King of Judah
Testament

Summary
Jehoahaz, son of Josiah, reigned three months in Jerusalem, did evil, and was deposed by Pharaoh Necho, who carried him to Egypt where he died.
Key Story
Jehoahaz deposed after three months and taken to Egypt.
Key Verses
2 Kings 23:30; 2 Kings 23:34

Related People

Life Lesson
Three months is enough to show a heart: Jehoahaz chose evil and lost the throne his father had honoured.
