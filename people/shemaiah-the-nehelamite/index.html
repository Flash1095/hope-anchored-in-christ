Shemaiah the Nehelamite

Shemaiah the Nehelamite was a false prophet among the exiles who wrote letters from Babylon ordering the priest Zephaniah to imprison Jeremiah as a madman, and who wrote to the exiles telling them to settle in for a long captivity. Jeremiah answered that Shemaiah would be punished and none of his seed would see the good God would do.

Key Story

Shemaiah's letters demanded Jeremiah's imprisonment, and Jeremiah replied with a prophecy that Shemaiah's line would not dwell among the people to see their return.

Life Lesson

Writing letters to silence a prophet is still an attempt to silence God.
