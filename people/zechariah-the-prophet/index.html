Zechariah the Prophet

Name
Zechariah the Prophet
Role
Prophet
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Summary
Zechariah was a post-exile prophet whose vivid visions encouraged Temple rebuilding and foretold the coming King riding on a donkey and being pierced. He called God's people to return to God so He would return to them.
Key Story
Zechariah saw a man with a measuring line and heard God promise Jerusalem would overflow with people and that He Himself would be a wall of fire around her.
Key Verses
Zechariah 1:3; Zechariah 9:9; Zechariah 4:6
Related Books
Zechariah; Ezra
Related People
Haggai; Zerubbabel
Life Lesson
Zechariah's promise still stands: Return to Me, and I will return to you. However far the drift, the distance home is one honest turn toward a waiting God.
