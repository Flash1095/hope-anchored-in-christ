Summary
Sargon was king of Assyria who sent his commander against Ashdod and captured it; Isaiah used the event as a sign of Assyria's coming captivity of Egypt and Ethiopia.
Key Story
In the year Tartan came to Ashdod, sent by Sargon king of Assyria, Isaiah walked naked and barefoot for three years as a sign against Egypt and Ethiopia.
