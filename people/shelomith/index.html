Shelomith

Role

Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Shelomith was the daughter of Dibri, of the tribe of Dan, and the mother of the man who blasphemed the Name and was stoned.
Key Story
Shelomith's son fought with an Israelite in the camp, blasphemed the Name, and was put in custody until Moses inquired of the LORD — and her name is preserved in the law that followed.
Key Verses
Leviticus 24:10-11
Related Books

Related People

Life Lesson
A mother's name can be remembered even in her family's hardest chapter — God does not look away.
