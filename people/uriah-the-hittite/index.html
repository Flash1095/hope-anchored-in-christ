Uriah the Hittite

Name
Uriah the Hittite
Role
Hittite Warrior; Husband of Bathsheba
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Uriah the Hittite was one of David's mighty men and the husband of Bathsheba. While Uriah fought at Rabbah, David took Bathsheba, then had Joab place Uriah where the fighting was fiercest so that he was killed. He is named last among the thirty-seven, as if the list itself remembers him.
Key Story
Uriah refused comfort at home while the ark and Israel camped in the open — and was murdered for his loyalty.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 11:3; 2 Samuel 11:6; 2 Samuel 11:17; 2 Samuel 23:39; 1 Kings 15:5; 1 Chronicles 11:41
Related Books
2 Samuel; 1 Kings; 1 Chronicles
Related People
Bathsheba; King David; Joab
Life Lesson
Loyalty that refuses privilege shames the king who abused it — and God did not forget.
