Summary
Lydia was a dealer in purple cloth from Thyatira and a worshipper of God in Philippi, the first recorded convert in Europe. The Lord opened her heart to Paul's message, and her home became the base of the Philippian church.
Key Story
After Lydia and her household were baptised, she insisted Paul's team stay at her house, and the church later met there after his release from prison.
Key Verses
Acts 16:14-15; Acts 16:40
Related Books
Acts; Philippians
Related People
Paul; Timothy
Life Lesson
God opened Lydia's heart by a riverside, and she opened her home to a continent's first church. An opened heart and an opened home can change a city's spiritual future.
