Barnabas

Name
Barnabas
Role
Apostle; Encourager
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Barnabas, whose name means son of encouragement, was a Levite from Cyprus who sold land to help the church, vouched for the newly converted Paul, and partnered with him on the first missionary journey. He championed second chances, notably with John Mark.
Key Story
When no one in Jerusalem would trust Paul, Barnabas took him to the apostles and told how the Lord had changed him.
Key Verses
Acts 4:36-37; Acts 9:27; Acts 11:23-24
Related Books
Acts
Related People
Paul; Timothy
Life Lesson
Barnabas used his credibility to open doors for people others distrusted. Be an encourager who vouches for the redeemed — someone may flourish because you believed God's grace in them.
Related Places
Antioch
Related Events
Paul's Missionary Journeys; Jerusalem Council
