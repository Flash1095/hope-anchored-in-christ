Summary
Boaz was a wealthy and godly landowner of Bethlehem who acted as kinsman-redeemer for Ruth and Naomi. He is a beautiful picture of Christ, our Redeemer.
Key Story
Boaz protected Ruth in his fields, spoke kindly to her, and redeemed her at the city gate, taking her as his wife.
Key Verses
Ruth 2:12; Ruth 4:9-10; Ruth 4:14
Related Books
Ruth
Related People
Ruth; Naomi; King David
Life Lesson
Boaz shows us a Redeemer who notices, protects, and pays the full price. That is exactly what Jesus has done for you — you are redeemed, wanted, and covered.
