Cushi, Father of Zephaniah

Cushi is named only as the father of the prophet Zephaniah (Zephaniah 1:1), the first link in the four-generation genealogy that traces Zephaniah back to King Hezekiah.

Key Story

Cushi's only mention is as Zephaniah's father, opening the prophet's genealogy.

Life Lesson

Cushi's single verse anchors a prophet in a family. God works through generations, not just individuals.
