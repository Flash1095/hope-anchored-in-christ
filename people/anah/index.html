Anah

Anah was a child of Zibeon the Horite, and the parent of Dishon and Aholibamah.

Key Story

Anah is remembered for finding hot springs in the wilderness while feeding his father Zibeon's donkeys.

Life Lesson

Even a small discovery in the wilderness earned Anah a place in the record.
