Abinoam, Father of Barak

Abinoam is named only as the father of Barak, from Kedesh-naphtali, whom Deborah summoned to lead Israel against Sisera.

Key Story

Abinoam appears in Judges only as Barak's father.

Life Lesson

A father's name carried into a battle he never fought.
