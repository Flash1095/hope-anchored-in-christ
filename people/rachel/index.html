Rachel

Name
Rachel
Role
Matriarch; Wife of Jacob
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Rachel was Jacob's beloved wife and the mother of Joseph and Benjamin. Jacob worked fourteen years to marry her because of his great love.
Key Story
Rachel met Jacob at a well where he rolled away the stone for her father's sheep, beginning one of Scripture's great love stories.
Key Verses
Genesis 29:18; Genesis 30:22; Jeremiah 31:15
Related Books
Genesis; Jeremiah
Related People
Jacob; Leah; Joseph, Son of Jacob; Benjamin
Life Lesson
Rachel waited and wept, and Scripture says God remembered Rachel. Being remembered by God changes everything — He has not forgotten you in your waiting.
Related Places
Haran; Canaan; Bethlehem
