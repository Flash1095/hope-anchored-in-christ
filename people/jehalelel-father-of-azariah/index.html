Jehalelel, Father of Azariah

Jehalelel was the father of Azariah, a Merarite Levite who cleansed the temple under Hezekiah.

Key Story

Jehalelel is named only as the father of Azariah the son of Jehalelel.

Life Lesson

A father's name anchors a Levite in his clan.
