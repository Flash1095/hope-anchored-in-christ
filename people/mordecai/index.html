Mordecai

Name
Mordecai
Role
Jewish Official; Guardian of Esther
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Summary
Mordecai was Esther's cousin and guardian who raised her, uncovered an assassination plot, and refused to bow to Haman. He rose to second only to the king and worked for the good of his people.
Key Story
Mordecai challenged Esther not to remain silent, asking who knows whether she had come to the kingdom for such a time as this.
Key Verses
Esther 4:14; Esther 10:3; Esther 2:21-23

Life Lesson
Mordecai did the right thing quietly for years before honour came. Keep doing good in obscurity — God keeps the records, and His timing for recognition is perfect.
