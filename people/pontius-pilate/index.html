Pontius Pilate

Name
Pontius Pilate
Role
Roman Governor of Judea
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Pontius Pilate was the Roman governor who tried Jesus. He repeatedly declared he found no fault in Him, yet handed Him over to be crucified to satisfy the crowd, washing his hands of the verdict.
Key Story
Pilate asked, What is truth?, then gave the order he did not believe in.
Key Verses
Matthew 27:2; John 18:29; John 19:16
Related Books
Matthew; John
Related People

Life Lesson
Knowing the right verdict is not the same as giving it. Pilate warns us against trading conscience for quiet.
