King Ahab

Name
King Ahab
Role
King of Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Ahab was a king of Israel who did more evil than those before him, promoting Baal worship under his wife Jezebel's influence. Yet even to Ahab, God sent Elijah with calls to repent.
Key Story
Ahab coveted Naboth's vineyard, and after Jezebel had Naboth murdered, Elijah confronted Ahab with God's judgment.
Key Verses
1 Kings 16:30; 1 Kings 21:19; 1 Kings 21:29; 1 Kings 16:29; 1 Kings 22:34; Micah 6:16
Related Books
1 Kings; Micah; 2 Kings
Related People
Jezebel; Elijah; Omri, King of Israel; Naboth the Jezreelite; Micaiah, Son of Imlah; King Jehoshaphat; Ahaziah, King of Israel; Jehoram, King of Israel
Life Lesson
When Ahab humbled himself, God noticed and delayed judgment. If God responded to even Ahab's partial humility, He will surely respond to your sincere turning toward Him.
Related Places
Samaria; Mount Carmel
