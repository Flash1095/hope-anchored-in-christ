Barzillai the Gileadite

Name
Barzillai the Gileadite
Role
Gileadite Supporter of David
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Barzillai the Gileadite, a very aged and wealthy man of Rogelim, brought beds, basins, wheat, barley, and food to David and his exhausted people at Mahanaim during Absalom's revolt. He refused David's invitation to Jerusalem and asked only that Chimham go in his place.
Key Story
Barzillai fed a fleeing king at Mahanaim and asked nothing for himself in return.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 17:27; 2 Samuel 19:32; 2 Samuel 19:34; 2 Samuel 19:37; 1 Kings 2:7; Ezra 2:61; Nehemiah 7:63
