Jahath, Overseer

Name
Jahath, Overseer
Role
Levite Overseer
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Jahath, a Levite of the sons of Merari, was an overseer of the workmen repairing the house of the LORD under Josiah, and the men did the work faithfully.
Key Story
The overseers of the repair were Jahath and Obadiah, Levites of the sons of Merari, and Zechariah and Meshullam of the Kohathites.
