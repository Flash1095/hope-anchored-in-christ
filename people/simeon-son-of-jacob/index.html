Simeon, Son of Jacob

Simeon was the second son of Jacob by Leah, and the father of Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul.

Key Story

Simeon joined Levi in avenging Dinah at Shechem, and his tribe later scattered within Israel, just as Jacob had foretold.

Life Lesson

Anger acted on without God leaves a legacy of scattering — Simeon's story is a warning.
