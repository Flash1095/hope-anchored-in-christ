The Shunammite Woman

Name
The Shunammite Woman
Role
Wealthy Woman of Shunem
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
The Shunammite woman was a hospitable woman who built a room for Elisha and was blessed with a son. When the boy died, she clung to God in famous faith, declaring, It is well.
Key Story
She laid her dead son on Elisha's bed, rode to the prophet, and refused to leave him until he came and raised the boy to life.
Key Verses
2 Kings 4:13; 2 Kings 4:26; 2 Kings 4:35-37
Related Books
2 Kings
Related People
Elisha; Elijah
Life Lesson
Her 'it is well' was spoken while her son lay lifeless — faith anchored in God, not circumstances. That same anchor can hold your soul in the storm.
