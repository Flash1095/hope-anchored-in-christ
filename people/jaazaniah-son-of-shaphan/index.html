Summary
Jaazaniah, son of Shaphan, was one of the seventy elders of Israel whom Ezekiel saw in a vision standing in a dark chamber, each with a censer, burning incense to idols painted on the wall, saying that the Lord did not see them. He is a different man from Jaazaniah son of Azur and Jaazaniah son of Jeremiah.
Key Story
In vision Ezekiel saw Jaazaniah son of Shaphan among seventy elders worshipping idols in the dark, each man with his censer in his hand.
