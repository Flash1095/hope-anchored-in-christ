Anna the Prophetess

Name
Anna the Prophetess
Role
Prophetess; Widow
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Anna was an elderly prophetess and widow who worshipped in the Temple night and day with fasting and prayer for decades. She recognised baby Jesus and spoke of Him to all awaiting redemption.
Key Story
At eighty-four, Anna came up at the very moment Simeon blessed Jesus, gave thanks to God, and told everyone in Jerusalem about the child.
Key Verses
Luke 2:36-38
Related Books
Luke
Related People
Simeon; Jesus
Life Lesson
Anna turned decades of widowhood into decades of worship. Your long, faithful prayers in obscurity are seen by God — and one day you will see what you have prayed for.
