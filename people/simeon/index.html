Simeon

Name
Simeon
Role
Righteous Elder
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Simeon was a righteous and devout man in Jerusalem who had been promised he would not die before seeing the Messiah. Led by the Spirit, he held baby Jesus in the Temple and blessed God.
Key Story
Simeon took Jesus in his arms and sang, My eyes have seen Your salvation, calling Him a light for revelation to the Gentiles.
Key Verses
Luke 2:25-32; Luke 2:34-35
Related Books
Luke
Related People
Jesus; Anna the Prophetess; Mary, Mother of Jesus
Life Lesson
Simeon waited a lifetime and held his promise in his arms. Keep waiting on God in His house — the promise-keeper lets His faithful see His salvation.
