Maachah, Daughter of Absalom

Name
Maachah, Daughter of Absalom
Role
Wife of Rehoboam; Queen Mother
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Maachah, daughter of Absalom, was Rehoboam's favoured wife and mother of Abijah; Asa later removed her from being queen because she had made an idol in a grove. Also known as Maachah, Queen Mother.
Key Story
Rehoboam loved Maachah above all his wives, made her son Abijah chief among his brethren, and years later Asa cut down and burnt her idol at the brook Kidron.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 11:20; 2 Chronicles 11:21; 2 Chronicles 15:16; 1 Kings 15:2; 1 Kings 15:13
Related Books
2 Chronicles; 1 Kings
Related People
Rehoboam; Absalom; Abijah, King of Judah; King Asa; Abijam, King of Judah
Life Lesson
Favour in a palace is no shelter from God's judgment on idolatry, even for a queen mother.
