Cyrus the Great

Name
Cyrus the Great
Role
King of Persia
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Summary
Cyrus was the Persian king who conquered Babylon and decreed that Judah's exiles could return home and rebuild the Temple in Jerusalem. Isaiah had named him God's anointed shepherd generations earlier.
Key Story
In his first year as king, Cyrus issued a proclamation throughout his realm allowing the Jews to return and rebuild the house of the Lord.
Key Verses
Isaiah 45:1; Ezra 1:2-4; 2 Chronicles 36:23
Related Books
Ezra; Isaiah; 2 Chronicles
Related People
Ezra; Zerubbabel
Life Lesson
God can move the heart of a king who does not even know Him to open doors for His people. The hearts of decision-makers are in His hand — pray with hope.

Babylon; Jerusalem
