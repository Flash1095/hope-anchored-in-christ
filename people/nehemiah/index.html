Nehemiah

Name
Nehemiah
Role
Governor of Judah; Cupbearer
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Summary
Nehemiah was cupbearer to Persia's king who wept over Jerusalem's broken walls, obtained leave to rebuild them, and completed the wall in fifty-two days despite fierce opposition. He led both civic and spiritual renewal.
Key Story
With a trowel in one hand and a sword in the other, Nehemiah's builders finished the wall because the people had a mind to work and God frustrated their enemies.
Key Verses
Nehemiah 1:4; Nehemiah 6:16; Nehemiah 8:10
Related Books
Nehemiah; Ezra
Related People
Ezra; Zerubbabel
Life Lesson
Nehemiah began on his knees weeping over broken walls before he ever lifted a stone. Bring God your broken places — prayer, courage, and steady work rebuild what seemed ruined.
