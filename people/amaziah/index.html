Summary
Amaziah was the priest of Bethel under Jeroboam II who tried to silence Amos. He reported Amos to the king as a conspirator, ordered him to flee to Judah and stop prophesying at Bethel, the king's sanctuary — and Amos answered with a prophecy of Amaziah's own ruin.
Key Story
Amaziah, priest of the king's chapel at Bethel, accused Amos of conspiracy and ordered him out of Israel — and heard his own downfall pronounced in reply.
Key Verses
Amos 7:10; Amos 7:12; Amos 7:17
Related Books
Amos
Related People
Amos; Jeroboam II, King of Israel
Life Lesson
Amaziah defended an institution against God's word and lost everything. Never let loyalty to a place or a post overrule loyalty to the truth.
