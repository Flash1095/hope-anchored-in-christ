Summary
Ezekiel was a priest turned prophet among the exiles in Babylon who saw visions of God's glory, a valley of dry bones, and a future restored temple. His dramatic sign-acts made God's messages unforgettable.
Key Story
In a valley full of dry bones, Ezekiel prophesied at God's command, and the bones came together, received breath, and stood as a vast army.
Key Verses
Ezekiel 37:10; Ezekiel 36:26; Ezekiel 1:28
Related Books
Ezekiel
Related People
Jeremiah; Daniel
Life Lesson
Ezekiel proves God can breathe life into what looks utterly dead — a hope, a family, a future. If He can raise an army from bones, He can revive what you have given up on.
