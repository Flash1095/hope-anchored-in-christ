Summary
Irijah, son of Shelemiah, was captain of the ward at the gate of Benjamin who arrested Jeremiah as he tried to leave Jerusalem, charging him with falling away to the Chaldeans, and delivered him to the princes who beat him and put him in prison.
Key Story
As Jeremiah went out toward the land of Benjamin, Irijah the captain laid hold on him, saying he was falling away to the Chaldeans, and would not listen to his denial.
