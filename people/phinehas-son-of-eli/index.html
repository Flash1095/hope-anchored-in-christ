Phinehas, Son of Eli

Name
Phinehas, Son of Eli
Role
Priest; Son of Eli
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Phinehas, son of Eli, served as a priest at Shiloh with his brother Hophni. Both men abused the offerings, and both died when the Philistines captured the ark. His wife died naming her son Ichabod, 'the glory is departed.'
Key Story
Phinehas died on the day the ark was taken, and his son was named for the glory that had left Israel.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 1:3; 1 Samuel 2:12; 1 Samuel 2:34; 1 Samuel 4:11; 1 Samuel 4:19
Related Books
1 Samuel
Related People
Eli; Hophni; Ichabod, Son of Phinehas; Ahitub, Son of Phinehas
Life Lesson
Sin in a priest's house does not stay private; it costs a whole nation its sense of God's presence.
