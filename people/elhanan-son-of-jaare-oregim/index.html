Summary
Elhanan, son of Jaare-oregim of Bethlehem, struck down a Philistine giant in the battle at Gob. The Samuel text names his victim as Goliath the Gittite.
Key Story
Elhanan of Bethlehem struck down a giant at Gob in David's later wars.
