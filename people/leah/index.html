Leah

Role

Testament

Summary
Leah was Jacob's first wife, given to him by her father Laban's deception, and mother of six sons including Judah, through whom Jesus came. Though unloved by Jacob, she was deeply seen by God.
Key Story
When the Lord saw that Leah was not loved, He opened her womb and blessed her with sons, and she learned to praise Him in Judah's birth.
Key Verses
Genesis 29:31; Genesis 29:35; Genesis 49:31
Related Books

Related People

Life Lesson
Leah praised God in the middle of a painful marriage, and from her line came the Messiah. God sees the overlooked, and He writes them into His greatest story.

Haran; Canaan; Hebron
