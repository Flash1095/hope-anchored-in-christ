Summary
Nethaneel, son of Zuar, was the prince of the tribe of Issachar at the census in the wilderness.
Key Story
Nethaneel led Issachar's 54,400 men and brought the tribe's offering for the dedication of the altar.
Key Verses
Numbers 1:8; 7:18-23
