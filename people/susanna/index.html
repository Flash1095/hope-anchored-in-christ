Role

Life of Christ
Summary
Susanna was one of the women who had been healed by Jesus and who travelled with Him through Galilee, ministering to Him from their substance.
Key Story
Susanna is named once, among the women who kept Jesus' travelling ministry going from their own means.
Key Verses
Luke 8:3
Related Books
Luke
Related People
Jesus; Mary Magdalene; Joanna
Life Lesson
A single verse can hold a whole faithfulness. Susanna gave quietly and is remembered for it.
