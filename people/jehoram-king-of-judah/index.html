Jehoram, King of Judah

Jehoram, son of Jehoshaphat and husband of Ahab's daughter Athaliah, walked in the ways of the kings of Israel, killed his brothers, and lost Edom and Libnah to revolt.

Key Story

Jehoram's wicked reign and Edom's revolt.

Life Lesson

Marriage alliances shape a legacy: Jehoram imported Ahab's house into Judah, and Judah paid for it.
