Zohar, Father of Ephron

Zohar was the father of Ephron the Hittite, from whom Abraham bought the cave of Machpelah.

Key Story

Zohar is named only as Ephron's father, in the transaction that gave Abraham his first owned land in Canaan.

Life Lesson

Even a single mention means God did not overlook him.
