Elkanah

Name
Elkanah
Role
Father of Samuel; Husband of Hannah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Elkanah was a Levite from Ramah, husband of Hannah and Peninnah, who went up yearly to worship at Shiloh. His love for Hannah could not heal her barrenness, but he kept bringing her to God.
Key Story
Each year Elkanah gave Hannah a double portion and comforted her tears, asking if he was not better to her than ten sons. When Hannah prayed for a child, he blessed her vow — and Samuel was born.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 1:1-8; 1 Samuel 1:19-20
Related Books
1 Samuel
Related People
Hannah; Samuel; Eli
Life Lesson
Elkanah could not fix Hannah's sorrow, but he stayed, loved her, and worshipped alongside her. Sometimes the most faithful thing we can do is keep showing up and keep pointing to God.
Related Places
Shiloh; Ramah
