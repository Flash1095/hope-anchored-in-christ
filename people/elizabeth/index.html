Elizabeth

Name
Elizabeth
Role
Mother of John the Baptist
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Elizabeth was Zechariah's wife, a descendant of Aaron, righteous yet long barren, who in old age bore John the Baptist. Her joyful greeting confirmed Mary's faith and calling.
Key Story
When Mary visited, the baby leaped in Elizabeth's womb and she cried out, Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the child you bear.
Key Verses
Luke 1:41-45; Luke 1:25
Related Books
Luke
Related People
Zechariah, Father of John the Baptist; Mary, Mother of Jesus; John the Baptist
Life Lesson
Elizabeth's long reproach ended in a testimony that encouraged a younger woman's faith. Your answered prayer may become someone else's confirmation — share it gladly.
