Zimri, Son of Salu

Zimri, son of Salu, was a prince of Simeon who openly brought the Midianite woman Cozbi into the camp at Peor.

Key Story

Zimri's open defiance was stopped by Phinehas, whose zeal turned away God's wrath from Israel.

Life Lesson

Sin flaunted in the open is still sin — and it still costs.
