Johanan, Son of Kareah

Name
Johanan, Son of Kareah
Role
Captain of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Summary
Johanan, son of Kareah, was a captain who warned Gedaliah of Ishmael's plot, pursued Ishmael and recovered the captives after the murder, and then led the remnant to Egypt against Jeremiah's counsel, carrying Jeremiah and Baruch with them.
Key Story
Johanan warned Gedaliah in secret, recovered the captives at Gibeon, then asked Jeremiah to pray — and when the answer was 'stay,' he led the people to Egypt anyway.
Key Verses
Jeremiah 40:8-16; Jeremiah 41:11-18; Jeremiah 42:1-6; Jeremiah 43:1-7
Related Books
Jeremiah
Related People
Gedaliah; Ishmael, Son of Nethaniah; Jeremiah; Azariah, Son of Hoshaiah; Baruch
Life Lesson
Asking God for guidance and then doing the opposite is worse than never asking.
Related Places
Mizpah; Egypt
