Carmi, Son of Zabdi

Carmi, son of Zabdi, was the father of Achan, whose taking of devoted plunder at Jericho cost Israel the battle of Ai.

Key Story

Carmi's son Achan confessed to hiding a garment, silver, and gold, and the whole household paid for the sin.

Life Lesson

A father's name can be remembered through a son's worst day — and still be worth redeeming.
