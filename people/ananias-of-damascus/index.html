Ananias of Damascus

Name
Ananias of Damascus
Role
Disciple at Damascus
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Ananias was a devout disciple at Damascus whom the Lord sent to the blinded Saul after the Damascus road. He laid hands on Saul, who regained his sight and was baptised, and later defended Ananias' character before the Jerusalem crowd.
Key Story
Ananias, who feared Saul's reputation, obeyed and called the persecutor brother, and Saul rose baptised.
Key Verses
Acts 9:10; Acts 22:12
Related Books
Acts
Related People
Paul; Barnabas
Life Lesson
Obedience to a frightening assignment can open a persecutor's eyes. Ananias went, and Paul began.
