Life of Christ
Summary
Malchus was the high priest's servant whose right ear Peter cut off with a sword in Gethsemane. Jesus healed the wound, rebuking the sword and the arrest.
Key Story
Malchus came to arrest Jesus and left healed by Him, the last miracle before the cross.
