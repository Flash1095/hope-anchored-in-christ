Othniel, Son of Kenaz

Name
Othniel, Son of Kenaz
Role
First Judge of Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Othniel, son of Kenaz and Caleb's younger brother, captured Kirjath-sepher and married Achsah, and later became Israel's first judge.
Key Story
Othniel took Kirjath-sepher and won Achsah as his wife; years later the Spirit of the LORD came on him and he delivered Israel from Cushan-rishathaim.
Key Verses
Joshua 15:17; Judges 3:9
Related Books
Joshua
Related People
Caleb; Achsah, Daughter of Caleb; Kenaz
Life Lesson
The courage that takes one city can grow into the courage that delivers a nation.
