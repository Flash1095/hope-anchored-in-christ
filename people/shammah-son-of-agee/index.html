Shammah, Son of Agee

Name
Shammah, Son of Agee
Role
Mighty Man of David
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Shammah, son of Agee the Hararite, was one of David's three mighty men. When the Philistines gathered at a field of lentiles and Israel fled, he stood in the middle of the field, defended it, and the Lord wrought a great victory.
Key Story
Shammah held a lentil field alone against the Philistines — and God gave the victory.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 23:11; 2 Samuel 23:12
Related Books
2 Samuel
Related People
King David; Eleazar, Son of Dodo; Agee, Father of Shammah
Life Lesson
Defending an ordinary field can be the greatest victory of a life.
