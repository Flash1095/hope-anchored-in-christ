Summary
Naomi moved to Moab in famine and returned to Bethlehem widowed and bitter, asking to be called Mara. Through Ruth and Boaz, God restored her joy with a grandson on her knees.
Key Story
The women of Bethlehem celebrated with Naomi as baby Obed was laid on her lap, declaring a son had been born to her in her old age.
Key Verses
Ruth 1:20-21; Ruth 4:14-17
Related Books
Ruth
Related People

Life Lesson
Naomi thought her story had ended in bitterness, but God was holding a baby-sized new beginning. Do not name your life by its hardest season — God is not finished.
