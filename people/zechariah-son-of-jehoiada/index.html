Zechariah, Son of Jehoiada

Zechariah, son of Jehoiada the priest, stood above the people and warned Judah that because they had forsaken the LORD, He had forsaken them; at the king's command they stoned him in the court of the house of the LORD.

Key Story

Zechariah cried, 'Why transgress ye the commandments of the LORD, that ye cannot prosper?' and died saying, 'The LORD look upon it, and require it.'

Life Lesson

Forgetting a father's kindness can lead a king to murder his friend's son.
