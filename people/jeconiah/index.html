Summary
Jeconiah (also called Coniah), son of Jehoiakim, reigned three months in Jerusalem before Nebuchadnezzar carried him to Babylon; after thirty-seven years in prison, Evil-merodach lifted up his head, gave him a seat above the other captive kings, and fed him at the king's table for life.
Key Story
Jeconiah was carried to Babylon with the vessels of the Lord's house, and in the thirty-seventh year of his captivity the new king of Babylon spoke kindly to him and changed his prison garments.
