Summary
Menahem, son of Gadi, slew Shallum, cruelly smote Tiphsah, and bought off Pul king of Assyria with a thousand talents exacted from Israel's wealthy men.
Key Story
Menahem buys off Assyria with Israel's silver.

2 Kings 15:14; 2 Kings 15:19
