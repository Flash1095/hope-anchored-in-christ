Oded, Prophet of Samaria

Name
Oded, Prophet of Samaria
Role
Prophet of the LORD
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Oded, a prophet of the LORD at Samaria, met Israel's returning host and warned that they had slain Judah in a rage reaching to heaven, and that keeping the captives would add to their own sin; the captives were then returned.
Key Story
Oded told the host to deliver the captives again, for the fierce wrath of the LORD was upon them, and the men expressed by name clothed and fed the captives and carried the feeble to Jericho.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 28:9; 2 Chronicles 28:11

Life Lesson
