Ornan the Jebusite

Jebusite whose threshing floor at Mount Moriah David bought for six hundred shekels of gold to build an altar to the LORD, and where Solomon later built the house of the LORD.

Key Story

David bought Ornan’s threshing floor to build an altar to the LORD (1 Chronicles 21); Solomon built the temple at Jerusalem in Mount Moriah, on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite, in the place David had prepared (2 Chronicles 3:1).

Life Lesson

The place of sacrifice and worship often begins in an ordinary person’s field.
