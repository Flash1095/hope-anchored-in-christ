Lysanias

Lysanias was tetrarch of Abilene when John the Baptist began his ministry. Luke names him alongside the other rulers of that year to date the coming of God's word to John.

Key Story

Luke lists Lysanias among the rulers of the year John began preaching in the wilderness.

Life Lesson

Even a ruler known only from one verse has his place in God's dated story. No detail of Luke's history is filler.
