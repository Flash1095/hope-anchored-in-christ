Adoni-zedek, King of Jerusalem

Adoni-zedek was king of Jerusalem when he heard that Gibeon had made peace with Israel, and he led five kings against Gibeon.

Key Story

Adoni-zedek's alliance was shattered by hailstones and a lengthened day at Beth-horon, and he was hanged with the four other kings at Makkedah.

Life Lesson

Five kings against one God is still no contest.
