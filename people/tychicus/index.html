Summary
Tychicus was an Asian companion of Paul who travelled with him toward Jerusalem, and later carried Paul's letters to the churches, a beloved brother and faithful minister.
Key Story
Tychicus travelled with Paul toward Jerusalem and later carried his letters to the churches he loved.
