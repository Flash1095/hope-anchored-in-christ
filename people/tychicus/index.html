Tychicus

Name
Tychicus
Role
Companion and Messenger of Paul
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Tychicus was an Asian companion of Paul who travelled with him toward Jerusalem, and later carried Paul's letters to the churches, a beloved brother and faithful minister.
Key Story
Tychicus travelled with Paul toward Jerusalem and later carried his letters to the churches he loved.
Key Verses
Acts 20:4; Ephesians 6:21-22; Colossians 4:7-8; 2 Timothy 4:12; Titus 3:12
Related Books
Acts; Ephesians; Colossians; 2 Timothy; Titus
Related People
Paul; Timothy; Trophimus; Onesimus
Life Lesson
Messengers carry more than paper. Tychicus carried Paul's heart to churches he could not visit.
