Uzziah, King of Judah

Name
Uzziah, King of Judah
Role
King of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Uzziah, made king at sixteen, reigned fifty-two years; he sought God in the days of Zechariah, warred successfully against Philistines and Arabians, built towers and engines of war, but when strong his heart was lifted up, he burned incense in the temple, and leprosy rose in his forehead, so he lived apart as a leper until death. Also known as Azariah.
Key Story
Azariah the priest withstood Uzziah for burning incense, and while Uzziah was wroth the leprosy rose up in his forehead before the priests.
