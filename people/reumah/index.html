Reumah

Reumah was Nahor's concubine and the mother of Tebah, Gaham, Thahash, and Maachah.

Key Story

Reumah's four sons are listed alongside Milcah's eight, completing the record of Nahor's twelve children.

Life Lesson

God's story keeps moving through families we know almost nothing about — and He knows them all.
