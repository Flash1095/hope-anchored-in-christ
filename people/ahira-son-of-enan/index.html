Ahira, Son of Enan

Name

Role

Summary
Ahira, son of Enan, was the prince of the tribe of Naphtali at the census in the wilderness.
Key Story
Ahira led Naphtali's 53,400 men and brought the tribe's offering for the dedication of the altar.
Key Verses
Numbers 1:15; 7:78-83
Related Books

Related People

Life Lesson
