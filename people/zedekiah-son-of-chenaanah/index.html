Zedekiah, Son of Chenaanah

Name
Zedekiah, Son of Chenaanah
Role
False Prophet
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Zedekiah, son of Chenaanah, made horns of iron and promised Ahab victory at Ramoth-gilead, then smote Micaiah on the cheek when Micaiah foretold disaster.
Key Story
Zedekiah's iron horns and his blow against Micaiah.
Key Verses
1 Kings 22:11; 1 Kings 22:24; 2 Chronicles 18:10; 2 Chronicles 18:23
Related Books
1 Kings; 2 Chronicles
Related People
King Ahab; Micaiah, Son of Imlah; Chenaanah, Father of Zedekiah the Prophet; Micaiah, Son of Imla
Life Lesson
A confident lie with iron horns is still a lie; flattery is not prophecy.
Related Places
Samaria
