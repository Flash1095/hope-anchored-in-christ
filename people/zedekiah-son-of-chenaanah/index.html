Zedekiah, Son of Chenaanah

Zedekiah, son of Chenaanah, made horns of iron and promised Ahab victory at Ramoth-gilead, then smote Micaiah on the cheek when Micaiah foretold disaster.

Key Story

Zedekiah's iron horns and his blow against Micaiah.

Life Lesson

A confident lie with iron horns is still a lie; flattery is not prophecy.
