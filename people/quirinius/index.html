Life of Christ
Summary
Quirinius (spelled Cyrenius in the King James Version) was governor of Syria at the time of the census that brought Joseph and Mary to Bethlehem for Jesus' birth.
Key Story
Luke ties the census of Jesus' birth to the governorship of Cyrenius in Syria.
Key Verses
Luke 2:2
Related Books
Luke
Related People
Jesus; Mary, Mother of Jesus; Joseph, Husband of Mary; Caesar Augustus
Life Lesson
Luke names names and offices because the birth he reports is history. The details invite checking, not blind trust.
