Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego

Name
Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego
Role
Exiles; Daniel's Companions
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Summary
Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego were Daniel's three friends who refused to bow to Nebuchadnezzar's golden image. God walked with them in the fiery furnace and delivered them unharmed.
Key Story
Thrown bound into a furnace heated seven times hotter, they were seen walking free with a fourth figure, and emerged without even the smell of fire.
Key Verses
Daniel 3:17-18; Daniel 3:25-27
Related Books
Daniel
Related People
Daniel; King Nebuchadnezzar
Life Lesson
Their words still steady us: our God is able — but even if He does not, we will not bow. Anchor your hope in who God is, not only in the outcome you want.
