Bela

Bela, the son of Beor, was the first king named as reigning in Edom before any king reigned over Israel.

Key Story

Bela reigned from Dinhabah, opening the list of Edom's kings.

Life Lesson

Kingdoms rise and fall, but God's promise to Israel outlasted every throne of Edom.
