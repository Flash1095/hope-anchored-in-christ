Summary
Reuel was a son of Esau by Bashemath, and the father of Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.
Key Story
Reuel's four sons became dukes in Edom, carrying his mother Bashemath's line forward.
Key Verses
Genesis 36:4-13; Genesis 36:17
