Simon the Zealot

Name
Simon the Zealot
Role
Apostle
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Simon the Zealot was one of the Twelve, likely once part of the movement fiercely resisting Roman rule, whom Jesus transformed into an apostle alongside a former tax collector for Rome. His story shows the gospel uniting former enemies.
Key Story
Simon is listed among the Twelve in every apostolic list and waited in the upper room with the other apostles after Jesus' ascension.
Key Verses
Luke 6:15; Acts 1:13; Matthew 10:4
Related Books
Matthew; Mark; Luke; Acts
Related People
Jesus; Matthew
Life Lesson
Jesus put a Zealot and a tax collector on the same team and made them brothers. The gospel still reconciles people who would never choose each other.

Galilee
