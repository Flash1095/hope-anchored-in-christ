Shobach

Shobach was captain of the host of Hadadezer, king of Zobah. He led the Syrian confederacy against David at Helam and was slain there with the flower of the Syrian army.

Key Story

Shobach led Syria's last great army against David and fell at Helam.

Life Lesson

The captain of a doomed confederacy still dies at his post.
