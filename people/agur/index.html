Agur

Agur, son of Jakeh, was the author of the sayings in Proverbs 30, a humble wisdom teacher who confessed his own ignorance before God.

Key Story

Agur's words begin with weariness and confession — 'I am more brutish than any man' — then gather sharp observations about pride, greed, and the fear of the Lord.

Life Lesson

Agur teaches that wisdom begins with knowing how little we know and fearing the God who knows all.
