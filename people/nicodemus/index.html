Nicodemus

Name
Nicodemus
Role
Pharisee; Member of the Sanhedrin
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Nicodemus was a Pharisee and ruler of the Jews who came to Jesus by night and heard the beloved words of John 3:16. He later defended Jesus in council and helped bury Him with royal honours.
Key Story
Nicodemus brought about seventy-five pounds of myrrh and aloes to anoint Jesus' body and laid Him in the tomb with Joseph of Arimathea.
Key Verses
John 3:1-16; John 7:50-51; John 19:39
Related Books
John
Related People
Jesus; Joseph of Arimathea
Life Lesson
Nicodemus moved from night-time questions to public devotion at the cross. It is never too late to step out of the shadows and stand openly with Jesus.
