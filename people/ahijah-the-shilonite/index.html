Ahijah the Shilonite

Ahijah of Shiloh met Jeroboam in a field, tore his new garment into twelve pieces, and gave ten to Jeroboam, declaring that God would tear the kingdom from Solomon for his idolatry.

Key Story

Ahijah tears his garment and promises Jeroboam ten tribes.

Life Lesson

God's judgment is announced before it falls, and His promises outlive the kings who break them.
