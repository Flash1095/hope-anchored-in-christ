Iddo

Iddo is named as the grandfather of the prophet Zechariah (Zechariah 1:1), and is also called 'Iddo the prophet' among those who encouraged the Temple rebuilding. He stands at the head of Zechariah's line, a prophet himself in the generation of the return.

Key Story

Iddo, grandfather of Zechariah and himself called a prophet, is named among those whose words spurred the returned exiles to rebuild the Temple.

Life Lesson

Iddo's line runs prophet to prophet, grandfather to grandson. Faith handed down is still the surest inheritance.
