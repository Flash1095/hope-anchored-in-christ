Zechariah, Overseer

Zechariah, a Levite of the sons of the Kohathites, was an overseer of the workmen repairing the house of the LORD under Josiah.

Key Story

The overseers included Zechariah and Meshullam, of the sons of the Kohathites, to set the work forward.

Life Lesson

Kohathite overseers set the temple repair forward.
