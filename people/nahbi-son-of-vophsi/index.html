Nahbi, Son of Vophsi

Name
Nahbi, Son of Vophsi
Role
Spy from Naphtali
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Nahbi, Son of Vophsi was one of the twelve spies Moses sent from the tribe of Naphtali to search out the land of Canaan.
Key Story
Nahbi spent forty days spying out Canaan, then joined the ten who brought back a fearful report that turned Israel back into the wilderness.
Key Verses
Numbers 13:14
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Vophsi; Moses; Caleb; Joshua
Life Lesson
Seeing the same land as Caleb, he saw only giants — faith is a choice about what you trust.
