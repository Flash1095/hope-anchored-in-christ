Mephibosheth, Son of Jonathan

Name
Mephibosheth, Son of Jonathan
Role
Son of Jonathan
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Mephibosheth, son of Jonathan, was lame in both feet from a fall when he was five, on the day Saul and Jonathan died. David sought him out for Jonathan's sake, restored Saul's land to him, and set him to eat at the king's table continually. He is also called Merib-baal.
Key Story
Mephibosheth, lame since childhood, ate at David's table for Jonathan's sake.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 4:4; 2 Samuel 9:3; 2 Samuel 9:7; 2 Samuel 9:13; 2 Samuel 19:24

Related People

Life Lesson
Covenant kindness seeks out the lame and forgotten and seats them at the table.
