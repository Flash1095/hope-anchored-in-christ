Queen of Sheba

The queen of Sheba, hearing of Solomon's fame, came to Jerusalem with hard questions and great gifts, and declared that the half of his wisdom had not been told her, before returning to her own land.

Key Story

The queen of Sheba tests Solomon with hard questions.

Life Lesson

True wisdom invites hard questions; it does not fear them.
