Deuel

Deuel is named in Numbers as the father of Eliasaph the prince of Gad; in the camp order he is also called Reuel.

Key Story

Deuel's son Eliasaph led the tribe of Gad through the wilderness.

Life Lesson

God knows His people under every name they are called.
