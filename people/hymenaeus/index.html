Hymenaeus

Name
Hymenaeus
Role
Teacher who erred and was delivered to Satan
Testament
New
Timeline Era

Summary
Hymenaeus was named by Paul in 1 Timothy among those delivered unto Satan that they might learn not to blaspheme, and in 2 Timothy as one whose teaching, with Philetus, said the resurrection was past already and overthrew the faith of some.
