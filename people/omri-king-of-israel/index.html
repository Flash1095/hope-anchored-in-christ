Omri, King of Israel

Name
Omri, King of Israel
Role
King of Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Omri, commander of Israel's army, was made king after Zimri's death, prevailed over the rival Tibni, built Samaria on Shemer's hill, and did worse than all before him, establishing the dynasty of Ahab.
Key Story
Omri prevails over Tibni and builds Samaria.
Key Verses
1 Kings 16:21; 1 Kings 16:28; Micah 6:16; 1 Kings 16:23; 2 Chronicles 22:2
Related Books
1 Kings; Micah; 2 Chronicles
Related People
Zimri, King of Israel; Tibni, King of Israel; Shemer, Owner of Samaria's Hill; King Ahab; Athaliah; Ahaziah, King of Judah
Life Lesson
Political skill without godliness only builds a stronger platform for sin.
