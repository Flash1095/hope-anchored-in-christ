Omri, King of Israel

Omri, commander of Israel's army, was made king after Zimri's death, prevailed over the rival Tibni, built Samaria on Shemer's hill, and did worse than all before him, establishing the dynasty of Ahab.

Key Story

Omri prevails over Tibni and builds Samaria.

Life Lesson

Political skill without godliness only builds a stronger platform for sin.
