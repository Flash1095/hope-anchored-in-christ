Summary
Melzar was the steward whom the prince of the eunuchs had set over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah; Daniel asked him to prove the youths with pulse and water for ten days, and Melzar agreed and took away their portion of the king's meat.
Key Story
Daniel asked Melzar to test them ten days on pulse and water, and at the end of ten days their countenances appeared fairer and fatter than all the children who ate the king's meat.
