Joshua of Beth-shemesh

Joshua of Beth-shemesh owned the field where the Philistines' cart stopped when the ark was returned. The ark was set down on a great stone in his field, and the men of Beth-shemesh offered sacrifices there.

Key Story

The ark's return journey ended in Joshua's field, where Israel worshipped before the tragedy that followed.

Life Lesson

God's return to his people is cause for worship — and for reverence.
