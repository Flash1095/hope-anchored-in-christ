Azariah, the Priest

Name
Azariah, the Priest
Role
Chief Priest
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Azariah the priest went in after Uzziah with eighty valiant priests and withstood the king for burning incense, which belonged to the consecrated sons of Aaron; he then saw the leprosy and thrust Uzziah out.
Key Story
Azariah and the priests told Uzziah to go out of the sanctuary, for he had trespassed, and they thrust him out when leprosy rose in his forehead.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 26:17; 2 Chronicles 26:20
