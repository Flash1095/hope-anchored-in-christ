Summary
Asshur was a son of Shem, and the ancestor linked with the building of Nineveh, Rehoboth, Calah, and Resen.
Key Story
Asshur went out from the land of Shinar and built great cities, including Nineveh, which would later loom large in Israel's story.
Key Verses
Genesis 10:11; Genesis 10:22
