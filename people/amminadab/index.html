Amminadab

Testament

Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Amminadab was the father of Elisheba, Aaron's wife, and of Naashon, the prince of Judah.
Key Story
Amminadab's children tied the priestly line to Judah's royal line: his daughter married Aaron, his son led Judah.

Exodus 6:23
