Jehiel, Ruler of the House of God

Jehiel, a ruler of the house of God with Hilkiah and Zechariah, gave the priests two thousand six hundred small cattle and three hundred oxen for Josiah's Passover offerings.

Key Story

Hilkiah and Zechariah and Jehiel, rulers of the house of God, gave unto the priests for the passover offerings.

Life Lesson

Generous rulers made a Passover like none since Samuel possible.
