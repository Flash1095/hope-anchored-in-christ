Conquest and Judges
Summary
Samson was a Nazirite judge of supernatural strength who fought the Philistines but was undone by compromise and his love for Delilah. In final repentance, God used him once more.
Key Story
Blinded and grinding in prison, Samson prayed for strength one last time and pulled down the temple of Dagon on the Philistine lords.
Key Verses
Judges 13:24; Judges 16:28-30; Hebrews 11:32
Related Books
Judges; Hebrews
Related People
Delilah; Samuel
Life Lesson
Samson's hair grew back in prison — a quiet sign that grace was returning. Even after our worst compromises, a repentant prayer can still move the hand of God.

Samson and Delilah; Samson and the Philistines
