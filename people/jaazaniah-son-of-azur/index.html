Summary
Jaazaniah, son of Azur, was one of the twenty-five princes at the east gate of the temple whom Ezekiel saw in vision devising mischief and giving wicked counsel in Jerusalem; God told Ezekiel to prophesy against them, and Pelatiah son of Benaiah fell dead as he prophesied. He is a different man from Jaazaniah son of Shaphan and Jaazaniah son of Jeremiah.
Key Story
Ezekiel saw Jaazaniah son of Azur among the princes at the east gate, and God commanded him to prophesy against the men who devised mischief in the city.
