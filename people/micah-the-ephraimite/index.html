Micah, the Ephraimite

Name
Micah, the Ephraimite
Role
Man of Mount Ephraim
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Micah, a man of Mount Ephraim, set up a shrine with a graven image and hired a Levite as his priest; the Danites later took his idols and his priest when they moved north. He is distinct from Micah the prophet.
Key Story
Micah restored his mother's silver, made a graven and a molten image with an ephod and teraphim, consecrated one of his sons and then a Levite as priest, and lost gods and priest alike when the Danites passed through to Laish.
Key Verses
Judges 17:1-13; Judges 18:13-27
Related Books
Judges
Related People
Jonathan, Son of Gershom
Life Lesson
A religion a man can make, carry off, and lose is no religion at all.
