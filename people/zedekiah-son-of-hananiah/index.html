Zedekiah, Son of Hananiah

Zedekiah, son of Hananiah, was a prince of Jehoiakim's court who sat in the scribe's chamber when Michaiah reported Jeremiah's roll. He is a different man from Zedekiah the king and from Zedekiah son of Maaseiah.

Key Story

Zedekiah son of Hananiah sat among the princes in Elishama's chamber when Michaiah declared all the words of the roll.

Life Lesson

Hearing God's word in the chamber is only the beginning — what the hearers do next is the story.
