Name

Role

Testament
New
Timeline Era

Summary
Festus succeeded Felix as governor and heard Paul at Caesarea. When Paul appealed to Caesar, Festus agreed, and, lacking a clear charge to send with him, brought Paul before Agrippa II to help frame the case.
Key Story
Festus heard Paul, granted his appeal to Caesar, and asked Agrippa to help him write the charge.
Key Verses
Acts 24:27; Acts 25:1
Related Books
Acts
Related People
Paul; Herod Agrippa II; Bernice
Life Lesson
Festus wanted a charge to send and found none. Paul's innocence puzzled every governor who heard him.
