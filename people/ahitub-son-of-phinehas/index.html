Summary
Ahitub, son of Phinehas and grandson of Eli, is named as the father of Ahiah, who served as priest wearing the ephod in Saul's camp.
Key Story
Ahitub links Eli's fallen house to the priest who served Saul after the ark was lost.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 14:3
Related Books

Related People

Life Lesson
Even a broken priestly line still carried the ephod and the duty of seeking God.
