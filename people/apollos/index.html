Summary
Apollos was an eloquent Jew from Alexandria, mighty in the Scriptures, who preached at Ephesus knowing only the baptism of John until Aquila and Priscilla expounded the way of God more perfectly to him. He then helped the churches in Achaia mightily, showing from the Scriptures that Jesus was Christ.
Key Story
Apollos preached boldly what he knew, learned more perfectly from a tentmaking couple, and proved from Scripture that Jesus was Christ.
