Role
King of Syria
Testament

Summary
Ben-hadad, king of Syria at Damascus, accepted Asa's silver and gold, broke his league with Baasha, and sent his captains against the cities of Israel.
Key Story
Ben-hadad smote Ijon, Dan, Abel-maim and the store cities of Naphtali, and Baasha left off building Ramah.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 16:2; 2 Chronicles 16:4

Life Lesson
