Jonah

Name
Jonah
Role
Prophet; Reluctant Missionary
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Jonah was a prophet who ran from God's call to preach to Nineveh, was swallowed by a great fish, and reluctantly delivered a message that brought a whole city to repentance. Jesus cited Jonah as a sign of His resurrection.
Key Story
From inside the fish Jonah prayed and was vomited onto dry land, then preached in Nineveh, where king and people alike repented in sackcloth.
Key Verses
Jonah 2:2; Jonah 3:5-10; Matthew 12:40-41
Related Books
Jonah; Matthew
Related People
Amos; Jesus
Life Lesson
You can run from God, but you cannot outrun His mercy — for you or for the people you'd rather He skip. The God of second chances gave Jonah one, and He gives them still.
