Life of Christ
Summary
Barabbas was a notorious prisoner, held for insurrection and murder, whom Pilate released at the Passover feast while the crowd demanded Jesus be crucified in his place.
Key Story
The crowd chose Barabbas and sent Jesus to the cross, and the guilty man walked free on the innocent man's sentence.
Key Verses
Matthew 27:16; Mark 15:7; John 18:40

Life Lesson
Barabbas is every one of us: set free because another took his place. His freedom cost Jesus His life.
