Eliasaph, Son of Lael

Eliasaph, son of Lael, was the chief of the Gershonites, the Levites who carried the tabernacle's curtains and coverings.

Key Story

Eliasaph oversaw the Gershonite families in their service of carrying the dwelling place of God.

Life Lesson

Not all service is seen — some of it is carrying the very walls of worship.
