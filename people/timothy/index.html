Timothy

Name
Timothy
Role
Pastor; Paul's Co-worker
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Timothy was Paul's young protégé and true son in the faith, raised on Scripture by his mother Eunice and grandmother Lois. He pastored in Ephesus and received two of Paul's most personal letters.
Key Story
Paul urged Timothy, Don't let anyone look down on you because you are young, but set an example in speech, conduct, love, faith, and purity.
Key Verses
1 Timothy 4:12; 2 Timothy 1:5; 2 Timothy 3:15
Related Books
Acts; 1 Timothy; 2 Timothy
Related People
Paul; Barnabas
Life Lesson
Timothy's sincere faith was planted at a grandmother's and mother's knees. The Scripture and prayers you invest in the young may shepherd churches you will never see.
