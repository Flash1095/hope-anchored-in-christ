Milcah

Name
Milcah
Role
Wife of Nahor
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Milcah was the daughter of Haran and the wife of Nahor, Abraham's brother. She bore him eight children, including Bethuel, the father of Rebekah.
Key Story
Through Milcah's son Bethuel came Rebekah, the wife of Isaac — so the promise line passed through her family too.
Key Verses
Genesis 11:29; Genesis 22:20-23
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Haran; Nahor, Brother of Abraham; Bethuel; Rebekah
Life Lesson
God often works His promises through mothers whose names history nearly forgets.
