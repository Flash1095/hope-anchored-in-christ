Jochebed

Jochebed was a daughter of Levi, the wife of Amram, and the mother of Aaron, Moses, and Miriam.

Key Story

When Pharaoh ordered Hebrew boys drowned, Jochebed hid baby Moses three months, then set him in a basket among the reeds — and later nursed him in Pharaoh's own house.

Life Lesson

A mother's courage can carry a child from a basket of reeds to the palace — and to a calling.
