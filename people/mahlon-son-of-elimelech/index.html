Mahlon, Son of Elimelech

Name
Mahlon, Son of Elimelech
Role
Son of Elimelech
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Mahlon, son of Elimelech and Naomi, died in Moab after marrying Ruth; his widow's marriage to Boaz restored his family's name and land.
Key Story
Mahlon went with his family to Moab in the famine, married Ruth, and died there, leaving his mother a widow and his name to be raised up by Boaz.
Key Verses
Ruth 1:2; Ruth 1:5; Ruth 4:9-10
Related Books
Ruth
Related People
Elimelech; Naomi; Ruth; Chilion, Son of Elimelech
Life Lesson
A man who died young in a foreign field lived on through the kinsman who married his widow.
