Summary
Barak was the Israelite commander Deborah called to lead the fight against Sisera's Canaanite army. Though he hesitated to go without her, he obeyed and won a decisive victory at Mount Tabor.
Key Story
Deborah told Barak that God would deliver Sisera into his hands, but he asked her to go with him. With 10,000 men he routed the iron chariots, and Israel sang Deborah's song of victory.
Key Verses
Judges 4:6-16; Judges 5:1; Hebrews 11:32
Related Books
Judges; Hebrews
Related People
Deborah; Jael
Life Lesson
Barak made it into Hebrews' hall of faith not because he was fearless, but because he went anyway. God honours obedient steps taken with trembling knees — courage grows on the road.
