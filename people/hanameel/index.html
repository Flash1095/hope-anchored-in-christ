Hanameel

Hanameel, son of Shallum and Jeremiah's uncle's son, came to Jeremiah in the prison court during the Babylonian siege and sold him his field at Anathoth, as God had said he would — the signed and sealed deed becoming a sign that houses and fields would again be bought in the land.

Key Story

During the siege, Hanameel sold Jeremiah his field at Anathoth, and Jeremiah sealed the deed in an earthen vessel as a pledge of return.

Life Lesson

Buying land in a besieged city is faith with a signature on it.
