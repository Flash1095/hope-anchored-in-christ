Summary
Eutychus was a young man who sat in a window at Troas during Paul's long preaching and, overcome by sleep, fell from the third loft and was taken up dead. Paul embraced him and announced he was alive, and the company broke bread and talked until morning.
Key Story
Eutychus fell asleep in a window, fell three stories, and was raised in Paul's arms before the bread was broken.

Life Lesson
Long sermons and open windows are a dangerous mix. Eutychus fell, and grace gave him back before dawn.
