Paul

Name
Paul
Role
Apostle; Missionary
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Paul, formerly Saul the persecutor, met the risen Jesus on the Damascus road and became the apostle to the Gentiles. He planted churches across the Roman world, wrote much of the New Testament, and was martyred in Rome.
Key Story
Blinded by light on the Damascus road, Saul heard Jesus ask, Saul, Saul, why do you persecute Me? — and rose a changed man with a world-changing call.
Key Verses
Acts 9:3-6; Galatians 1:15-16; Philippians 3:7-8; 2 Timothy 4:7
Related Books
Acts; Romans; Galatians; Philippians
Related People
Barnabas; Timothy; Stephen; Jesus
Life Lesson
If God could turn the church's fiercest enemy into its greatest missionary, no one you love is beyond His reach — and neither is your own past. Grace really is amazing.
