Jeush

Jeush was a son of Esau by Aholibamah, and one of the dukes of Edom.

Key Story

Jeush is named with his brothers Jaalam and Korah among Aholibamah's sons and among Edom's dukes.

Life Lesson

God's story keeps moving through families we know almost nothing about — and He knows them all.
