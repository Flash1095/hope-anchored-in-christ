Ebed-Melech

Ebed-melech, an Ethiopian eunuch in Zedekiah's house, heard that Jeremiah had been put in the dungeon, pleaded with the king for him, and drew Jeremiah out with old rags under his arms. God promised him deliverance because he had trusted in the Lord.

Key Story

Ebed-melech went to the king's gate, obtained thirty men, and let down old cast clouts and rotten rags to lift Jeremiah out of the miry dungeon.

Life Lesson

One outsider's courage at the king's gate can save a prophet — and God remembers it by name.
