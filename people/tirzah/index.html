Tirzah

Tirzah was one of the five daughters of Zelophehad who asked Moses for their father's inheritance.

Key Story

Tirzah stood with her sisters before Moses and the congregation, and God granted daughters the right to inherit when there is no son.

Life Lesson

Five sisters standing together changed the law of inheritance.
