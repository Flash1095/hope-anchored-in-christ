Summary
Shadrach was the Babylonian name given to Hananiah, one of the four Judean youths taken to Babylon; he was set over the affairs of the province of Babylon and, with Meshach and Abednego, refused to worship Nebuchadnezzar's golden image and was thrown into the burning fiery furnace, from which all three walked out unharmed. ONE profile covers both names; Hananiah is his Hebrew name, not a separate person.
Key Story
Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego told the king they would not serve his gods nor worship the golden image, and the fire that killed the men who threw them in did not even singe their clothes.
