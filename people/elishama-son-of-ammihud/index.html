Elishama, Son of Ammihud

Name
Elishama, Son of Ammihud
Role
Prince of Ephraim
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Elishama, son of Ammihud, was the prince of the tribe of Ephraim at the census in the wilderness, and the grandfather of Joshua.
Key Story
Elishama led Ephraim's 40,500 men and brought the tribe's offering for the dedication of the altar.
Key Verses
Numbers 1:10; 7:48-53
Related Books
Numbers
Related People

Life Lesson
