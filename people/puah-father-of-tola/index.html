Puah, Father of Tola

Puah is named only as the father of Tola the judge, in the line of Dodo. He is distinct from Puah the midwife in Exodus.

Key Story

Puah appears in Judges only as Tola's father.

Life Lesson

Two people can share a name and nothing else — the line, not the name, tells them apart.
