Benaiah, Son of Jehoiada

Name
Benaiah, Son of Jehoiada
Role
Captain of David's Guard
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Benaiah, son of Jehoiada, was a valiant man of Kabzeel who slew two lionlike men of Moab, killed a lion in a pit on a snowy day, and slew a great Egyptian with his own spear. David set him over his guard.
Key Story
Benaiah killed lions in pits, giants with their own spears, and two lionlike men of Moab.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 8:18; 2 Samuel 23:20; 2 Samuel 23:21; 2 Samuel 23:22; 2 Samuel 23:23; 1 Kings 1:8; 1 Kings 2:34; 1 Chronicles 11:22; 1 Chronicles 18:17

Life Lesson
