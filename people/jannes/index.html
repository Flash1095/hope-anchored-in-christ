Summary
Jannes is named in 2 Timothy with Jambres as one who withstood Moses, an example of men of corrupt minds, reprobate concerning the faith. Jewish tradition identifies Jannes and Jambres as magicians in Pharaoh's court, though Exodus itself does not name them.
