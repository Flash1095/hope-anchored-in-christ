Zillah

Zillah was Lamech's other wife and the mother of Tubal-cain and Naamah.

Key Story

Zillah listened as Lamech boasted of his revenge, a dark moment in the early story of humanity.

Life Lesson

Even in a household marked by violence, God still knew Zillah by name.
