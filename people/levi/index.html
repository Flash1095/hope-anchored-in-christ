Levi

Testament

Timeline Era

Summary
Levi was the third son of Jacob by Leah, and the father of Gershon, Kohath, and Merari. His descendants became Israel's priestly tribe.
Key Story
Levi joined Simeon in avenging Dinah, yet God later set his tribe apart for the tabernacle and the priesthood — grace redirecting a violent past.
Key Verses
Genesis 29:34; Genesis 34; Genesis 46:11; Genesis 49:5-7
