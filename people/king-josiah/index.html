King Josiah

King Josiah

Testament

Timeline Era

Summary
Josiah became king of Judah at eight years old and led its greatest reform after the Book of the Law was found in the Temple. He tore down idols and renewed the Passover like no king before him.
Key Story
When the rediscovered Book of the Law was read to him, Josiah tore his robes in grief and humbled himself before God.
