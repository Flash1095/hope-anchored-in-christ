King Josiah

Name
King Josiah
Role
King of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Josiah became king of Judah at eight years old and led its greatest reform after the Book of the Law was found in the Temple. He tore down idols and renewed the Passover like no king before him.
Key Story
When the rediscovered Book of the Law was read to him, Josiah tore his robes in grief and humbled himself before God.
Key Verses
2 Kings 22:11; 2 Kings 23:25; 2 Chronicles 34:27; 2 Kings 22:1; 2 Kings 23:29; Jeremiah 1:2; Jeremiah 3:6; Jeremiah 25:3

Related People

Life Lesson
Josiah let God's Word break his heart and change his nation. Open the Book with a tender heart, whatever your age — revival can begin with one humble reader.
