Jehu, King of Israel

Jehu, son of Jehoshaphat the son of Nimshi, was anointed by Elisha's prophet, rode furiously to Jezreel, slew Jehoram and Jezebel, wiped out Ahab's house and Baal's worshippers, yet kept Jeroboam's calves.

Key Story

Jehu anointed, slays Jehoram and Jezebel, and purges Baal.

Life Lesson

Zeal that purges others' sins but keeps its own is zeal half-finished.
