Laish, Father of Palti

Name
Laish, Father of Palti
Role
Father of Palti
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Laish of Gallim is named as the father of Phalti, to whom Saul gave Michal (called Phaltiel in 2 Samuel 3:15) after taking her from David.
Key Story
Laish's son became a pawn in Saul's struggle against David.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 25:44
Related Books
1 Samuel
Related People
Phalti, Son of Laish; Michal
Life Lesson
A father's name survives in his son's story, even a sorrowful one.
