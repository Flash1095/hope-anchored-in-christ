Saul, King of Edom

Saul of Rehoboth by the river reigned in Edom after Samlah died.

Key Story

Saul is the sixth king named in Edom's king list — a different Saul from Israel's first king centuries later.

Life Lesson

Two men can share a name and walk very different paths; God knows each one.
