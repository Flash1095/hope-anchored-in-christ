Summary
Alphaeus was the father of the apostle James son of Alphaeus, and, by Mark's account, of Levi the tax collector who became the apostle Matthew. He is named only through his sons.
Key Story
Alphaeus is known only as the father of two apostles, James and Levi, called from very different lives.
Key Verses
Mark 2:14; Mark 3:18
Related Books
Mark
Related People
James, Son of Alphaeus; Matthew
Life Lesson
A father can be remembered for the sons God called. Alphaeus raised two men Jesus chose.
