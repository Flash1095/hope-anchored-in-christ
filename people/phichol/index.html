Phichol

Phichol was the commander of Abimelech's army who stood beside the king in his covenants with Abraham and with Isaac.

Key Story

Phichol witnessed Abimelech's treaty with Abraham at Beersheba, and years later the same kind of treaty with Isaac.

Life Lesson

Faithful service is remembered even when it is only a name in someone else's story.
