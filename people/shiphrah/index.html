Testament

Summary
Shiphrah was one of the two Hebrew midwives who feared God and refused Pharaoh's order to kill newborn Hebrew boys.
Key Story
When Pharaoh commanded the midwives to kill every Hebrew son at birth, Shiphrah and Puah let the boys live — and God dealt well with them.
Key Verses
Exodus 1:15-21

Life Lesson
