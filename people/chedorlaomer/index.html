Summary
Chedorlaomer was the king of Elam who led the alliance of eastern kings against Sodom and Gomorrah.
Key Story
After twelve years of rule, Chedorlaomer crushed a rebellion of the plain's cities, took Lot captive, and was himself routed by Abram's night attack.
