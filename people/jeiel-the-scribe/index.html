Jeiel, the Scribe

Name
Jeiel, the Scribe
Role
Scribe of the Army
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Jeiel the scribe, with Maaseiah the ruler under Hananiah, numbered Uzziah's host of fighting men by bands for war.
Key Story
Uzziah's army went out by bands according to their account by the hand of Jeiel the scribe and Maaseiah the ruler.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 26:11
Related Books

Related People
Uzziah, King of Judah; Maaseiah, the Ruler
Life Lesson
Even counting an army is service to the king and the kingdom.
