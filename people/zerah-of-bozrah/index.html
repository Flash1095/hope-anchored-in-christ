Zerah of Bozrah

Name

Role

Testament

Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Zerah of Bozrah was the father of Jobab, who reigned as king in Edom.
Key Story
Zerah is named only as Jobab's father, in the list of Edom's kings — a different Zerah from Judah's and Reuel's sons.
Key Verses
Genesis 36:33
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Jobab, Son of Zerah
Life Lesson
Three men named Zerah appear in Genesis, and God knows each one apart.
