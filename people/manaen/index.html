Manaen

Manaen was among the prophets and teachers at Antioch, and had been brought up with Herod the tetrarch. He shared in the worship from which Barnabas and Saul were sent to the Gentiles.

Key Story

Manaen grew up with Herod and ended up sending missionaries to the nations Herod's family feared.

Life Lesson

Upbringing does not fix a destiny. Manaen left Herod's court for the church's sending.
