Phalti, Son of Laish

Name
Phalti, Son of Laish
Role
Husband of Michal
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Phalti, son of Laish of Gallim, was given Michal as wife after Saul took her from David. When David demanded her back, Phalti followed weeping behind her until Abner sent him home.
Key Story
Palti wept as he followed Michal back to David, a husband caught in a king's bargain.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 25:44; 2 Samuel 3:15; 2 Samuel 3:16
Related Books
1 Samuel; 2 Samuel
Related People
Michal; Laish, Father of Palti
Life Lesson
Even a weeping walk home is part of the record; God notices the collateral damage of power.
