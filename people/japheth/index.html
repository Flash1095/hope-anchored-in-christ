Japheth

Name
Japheth
Role
Son of Noah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Japheth was one of Noah's three sons, saved through the flood. Noah blessed him with enlargement, and his descendants spread across the coastlands and nations of the ancient world.
Key Story
With Shem, Japheth took a garment and walked backward to cover Noah without gazing on his shame. His line represents the widening of humanity's story beyond one family to many nations.
Key Verses
Genesis 9:27; Genesis 10:2-5
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Noah; Shem; Ham
Life Lesson
Japheth's blessing points to a God whose family keeps widening. The story that began with one family was always heading toward every nation — and it still is.
