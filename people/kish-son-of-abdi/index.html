Kish, Son of Abdi

Name
Kish, Son of Abdi
Role
Merarite Levite
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Kish, son of Abdi of the sons of Merari, was one of the Levites who arose to cleanse the house of the LORD under Hezekiah.
Key Story
Of the sons of Merari, Kish the son of Abdi and Azariah the son of Jehalelel arose to cleanse the house.

2 Chronicles 29:12

Life Lesson
