Summary
Meshach was the Babylonian name given to Mishael, one of Daniel's three companions; he was set over the affairs of the province of Babylon and, with Shadrach and Abednego, refused the golden image and survived the fiery furnace. ONE profile covers both names; he is a different man from Mishael, son of Uzziel, who already has a profile.
Key Story
Meshach stood with Shadrach and Abednego before the furnace and answered the king: our God is able to deliver us, but if not, we will not worship your image.
