Alexander the Coppersmith

Alexander the coppersmith did Paul much evil and greatly withstood his words, so that Paul warns Timothy to beware of him. He is listed separately from the Alexander of 1 Timothy, since the letters do not identify them as the same man.

Key Story

Life Lesson

Opposition to the gospel is met with warning, not vengeance; the Lord will reward.
