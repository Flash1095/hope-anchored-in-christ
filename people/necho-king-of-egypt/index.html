Necho, King of Egypt

Name
Necho, King of Egypt
Role
King of Egypt; Pharaoh
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Necho, king of Egypt, came up to fight against Carchemish by Euphrates; he warned Josiah that God had commanded him to make haste and not to meddle, but Josiah disguised himself, fought at Megiddo, was wounded by archers, and died at Jerusalem.
Key Story
Necho sent ambassadors saying, 'What have I to do with thee, thou king of Judah? forbear thee from meddling with God, who is with me,' but Josiah hearkened not.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 35:20; 2 Chronicles 35:22
Related Books
2 Chronicles
Related People
King Josiah; Jehoahaz, Son of Josiah
Life Lesson
Ignoring a warning, even from an enemy king, can end a righteous reign.
