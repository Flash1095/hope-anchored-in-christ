Michal

Name
Michal
Role
Daughter of Saul; Wife of David
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Michal, Saul's younger daughter, loved David and married him, then saved his life by letting him down through a window when Saul sent men to kill him. Saul later gave her to Palti, and David took her back when he became king. She despised David's dancing before the ark and remained childless.
Key Story
Michal loved David, saved him from her father's assassins, and later scorned his worship before the ark.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 14:49; 1 Samuel 18:20; 1 Samuel 19:11; 1 Samuel 19:12; 2 Samuel 3:13; 2 Samuel 6:16; 2 Samuel 6:23
Related Books
1 Samuel; 2 Samuel
Related People
King Saul; King David; Merab; Phalti, Son of Laish
Life Lesson
Love, loyalty, and later contempt can live in one heart — and worship scorned has its own cost.
