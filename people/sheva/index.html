Summary
Sheva was scribe in David's later administration, serving beside Zadok, Abiathar, and Ira the Jairite at the time of Sheba's revolt.
Key Story
Sheva kept the king's records through David's last wars.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 20:25; 1 Chronicles 2:49
Related Books

Related People
King David; Zadok, Son of Ahitub; Ira the Jairite
Life Lesson
