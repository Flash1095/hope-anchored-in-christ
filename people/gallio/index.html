Gallio

Gallio was the proconsul of Achaia before whom the Jews brought Paul at Corinth, accusing him of persuading men to worship contrary to the law. Gallio dismissed the case as a matter of words and names, and cared for none of the beating that followed.

Key Story

Gallio refused to judge Paul's doctrine, calling it a question of words and names, and the case collapsed.

Life Lesson

Gallio's indifference freed Paul and left Sosthenes beaten. A judge who cares for nothing protects no one.
