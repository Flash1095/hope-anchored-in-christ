Joseph, Husband of Mary

Joseph was a righteous carpenter of David's line who took Mary as his wife at the angel's word and protected Jesus through flights and dangers. His quiet obedience sheltered the Saviour's childhood.

Key Story

Warned in dreams, Joseph fled with Mary and Jesus to Egypt by night and later brought the family home to Nazareth.

Life Lesson

Joseph never speaks a recorded word in Scripture, yet his obedience protected God's greatest gift. Faithful, quiet obedience matters enormously in God's story.
