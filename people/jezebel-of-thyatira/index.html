Jezebel of Thyatira

The letter to Thyatira addresses a woman called Jezebel who called herself a prophetess and taught and seduced Christ's servants; whether Jezebel is her name or a symbolic label for her is debated among commentators.

Key Story

The letter to Thyatira addresses a woman called Jezebel who called herself a prophetess and taught and seduced Christ's servants; whether Jezebel is her name or a symbolic label for her is debated among commentators.

Life Lesson

False teaching inside the church is judged as seriously as persecution from outside.
