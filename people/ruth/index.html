Summary
Ruth was a widowed Moabite who clung to her mother-in-law Naomi and to Israel's God. Her loyalty led her to Bethlehem, to Boaz, and into the genealogy of Jesus.
Key Story
Ruth told Naomi, Where you go I will go, and your God will be my God, choosing faithfulness over a safer future in Moab.
Key Verses
Ruth 1:16-17; Ruth 4:13-17; Matthew 1:5

Life Lesson
Ruth gleaned in obscurity before she was honoured in the city gate. Stay faithful in the field you are in — God is writing a redemption story bigger than you can see.
