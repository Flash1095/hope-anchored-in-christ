Kezia

Kezia was Job's second daughter after his restoration, named in Job 42 among the three daughters born when God restored Job's fortunes.

Key Story

With her sisters Jemima and Keren-happuch, Kezia received an inheritance from her father alongside her brothers — an unusual honour in that time.

Life Lesson

Kezia's inheritance shows that restoration in God's hands can include dignity and honour beyond mere replacement.
