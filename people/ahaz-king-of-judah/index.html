Ahaz, King of Judah

Ahaz walked in the ways of Israel's kings, sacrificed his son, copied a Damascus altar for the temple, and bought Assyria's help with temple treasure, inviting the very oppression he feared.

Key Story

Ahaz copies the Damascus altar and appeals to Assyria.

Life Lesson

Fear that copies the world instead of trusting God ends by serving what it feared.
