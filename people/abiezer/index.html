Summary
Abiezer, son of Gilead — called Jeezer in the census list — founded a clan of Manasseh whose families later settled west of the Jordan.
Key Story
Abiezer's clan is named in the second census and again when the daughters of Zelophehad's kinsmen received their inheritance.
