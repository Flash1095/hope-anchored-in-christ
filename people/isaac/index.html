Isaac

Name
Isaac
Role
Patriarch; Son of Promise
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Isaac was the miraculous son of Abraham and Sarah and heir of God's covenant promises. He fathered Jacob and Esau and lived a quiet life of wells and altars.
Key Story
As a young man, Isaac carried the wood up Mount Moriah and willingly submitted as Abraham prepared to offer him to God.
Key Verses
Genesis 22:7-8; Genesis 26:24; Hebrews 11:20
Related Books
Genesis; Hebrews
Related People
Abraham; Sarah; Rebekah; Jacob
Life Lesson
Isaac's life shows that the promise can pass quietly from one generation to the next. Faithfulness in ordinary seasons still carries God's extraordinary purposes.
Related Places
Beersheba; Hebron; Canaan
Related Events
