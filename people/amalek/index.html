Amalek

Name
Amalek
Role
Son of Eliphaz
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Amalek was the son of Eliphaz by his concubine Timna, and the ancestor of the Amalekites.
Key Story
Amalek's descendants became Israel's bitter enemies, attacking them in the wilderness and for generations after.
Key Verses
Genesis 36:12-16; Exodus 17
Related Books

Related People

Life Lesson
