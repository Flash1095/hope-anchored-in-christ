Keren-happuch

Keren-happuch was Job's youngest daughter after his restoration, named in Job 42 among the three daughters born when God restored Job's fortunes.

Key Story

With her sisters, Keren-happuch was counted among the fairest women in the land, and Job gave her an inheritance among her brothers.

Life Lesson

Keren-happuch's story closes Job's book with beauty after ashes — suffering was not the end of Job's story.
