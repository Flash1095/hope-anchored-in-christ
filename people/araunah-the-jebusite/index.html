Araunah the Jebusite

Name
Araunah the Jebusite
Role
Jebusite Owner of the Threshingfloor
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Araunah the Jebusite owned the threshingfloor where the destroying angel stopped during the plague after David's census. David insisted on buying it at full price rather than accepting it as a gift, and built an altar there — the future site of the temple.
Key Story
Araunah offered his threshingfloor free, but David bought it at full price for an altar.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 24:16; 2 Samuel 24:21; 2 Samuel 24:24; 2 Samuel 24:25
