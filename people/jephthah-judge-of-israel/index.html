Summary
Jephthah the Gileadite, son of Gilead, was driven out by his half-brothers, recalled to lead Israel against Ammon, defeated Ammon, and kept the rash vow that cost his only daughter; he judged Israel six years.
Key Story
Cast out as the son of a harlot, Jephthah was fetched back by the elders of Gilead, reasoned with the king of Ammon from Israel's history, vowed whatever came from his house if God gave him victory, and, when his daughter came out dancing, did with her according to his vow; he judged Israel six years.

Judges 11:1-12; Judges 11:29-40; Judges 12:7
