Sethur, Son of Michael

Name
Sethur, Son of Michael
Role
Spy from Asher
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Sethur, Son of Michael was one of the twelve spies Moses sent from the tribe of Asher to search out the land of Canaan.
Key Story
Sethur spent forty days spying out Canaan, then joined the ten who brought back a fearful report that turned Israel back into the wilderness.
Key Verses
Numbers 13:13
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Michael, Father of Sethur; Moses; Caleb; Joshua
Life Lesson
Seeing the same land as Caleb, he saw only giants — faith is a choice about what you trust.
