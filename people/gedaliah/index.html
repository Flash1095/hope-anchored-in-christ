Gedaliah

Gedaliah, son of Ahikam, was made governor over the remnant left in Judah after Jerusalem fell. He urged the people to serve Babylon and live, welcomed the scattered captains at Mizpah, and was murdered by Ishmael son of Nethaniah despite Johanan's warning.

Key Story

Gedaliah told the captains at Mizpah not to fear serving the Chaldeans, but Ishmael came to him at Mizpah and struck him down while eating bread with him.

Life Lesson

Trusting a friendly face over a faithful warning can be fatal — discernment is not unkindness.
