Pharez

Name
Pharez
Role
Son of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Pharez was the twin son of Judah and Tamar, born after his brother Zerah, and the ancestor of David's line.
Key Story
Pharez broke out first at birth despite Zerah's hand appearing first — and through Pharez the promise line ran to David and to Christ.
Key Verses
Genesis 38:27-30; Genesis 46:12; Matthew 1:3
