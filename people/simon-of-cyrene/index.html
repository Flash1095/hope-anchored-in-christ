Simon of Cyrene

Life of Christ
Summary
Simon of Cyrene was a passer-by coming in from the country whom the soldiers compelled to carry Jesus' cross to Golgotha. Mark names his sons Alexander and Rufus, suggesting his family later joined the church.
Key Story
Simon did not volunteer; he was pressed into service, and carried the cross behind Jesus to the place of the skull.
Key Verses
Matthew 27:32; Mark 15:21; Luke 23:26
Related Books
Matthew; Mark; Luke
Related People

Life Lesson
Sometimes the cross finds us on an ordinary errand. Simon's forced steps became his family's doorway into faith.
