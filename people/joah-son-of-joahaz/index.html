Joah, Son of Joahaz

Joah, son of Joahaz the recorder, was sent by Josiah with Shaphan and Maaseiah to repair the house of the LORD.

Key Story

Josiah sent Shaphan, Maaseiah the governor, and Joah the son of Joahaz the recorder, to repair the house.

Life Lesson

A recorder's name is kept in the repair of the temple.
