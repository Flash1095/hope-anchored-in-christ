Aaron

Name
Aaron
Role
First High Priest
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Aaron was Moses' older brother and spokesman, appointed as Israel's first high priest. His descendants served as priests, and his rod that budded confirmed God's choice.
Key Story
Aaron spoke for Moses before Pharaoh and later interceded for Israel, standing between the living and the dead to stop a plague.
Key Verses
Exodus 4:14-16; Numbers 16:48; Hebrews 5:4
Related Books
Exodus; Leviticus; Numbers; Hebrews
Related People
Moses; Miriam; Joshua
Life Lesson
Aaron failed with the golden calf yet was restored to serve as high priest. God restores fallen servants who return to Him — failure need not be the final word.
Related Places
Egypt; Mount Sinai; Kadesh Barnea
Related Events
The Ten Plagues; The Passover; The Exodus and the Red Sea; The Golden Calf; Wilderness Wanderings
