Hoshea, King of Israel

Name
Hoshea, King of Israel
Role
King of Israel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Hoshea, son of Elah, slew Pekah, then conspired with So of Egypt and withheld Assyria's tribute; Shalmaneser besieged Samaria three years, and Israel was carried away to Assyria for its sins.
Key Story
Hoshea's conspiracy and the fall of Samaria.
Key Verses
2 Kings 15:30; 2 Kings 17:4; 2 Kings 17:6
Related Books
2 Kings
Related People
Pekah, King of Israel; So, King of Egypt; Shalmaneser, King of Assyria; Elah, Father of Hoshea
Life Lesson
The last king of Israel shows where a long road of idolatry ends: exile.
