Caleb

Name
Caleb
Role
Spy; Faithful Warrior
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Caleb was one of the twelve spies who urged Israel to trust God and enter Canaan. Because he wholly followed the Lord, he alone of his generation, with Joshua, entered the land.
Key Story
At eighty-five years old, Caleb asked Joshua for the hill country of Hebron, declaring he was still as strong to fight as he had been at forty.
Key Verses
Numbers 14:24; Joshua 14:10-12; Deuteronomy 1:36
Related Books
Numbers; Joshua
Related People
Joshua; Moses
Life Lesson
Caleb waited forty-five years for his promise and never let go of it. It is never too late, and you are never too old, for God to fulfil what He spoke over you.
Related Places
Kadesh Barnea; Hebron; Jordan River
Related Events
The Twelve Spies and Israel's Unbelief; Crossing the Jordan; The Walls of Jericho
