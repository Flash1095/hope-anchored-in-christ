Sarah

Sarah was Abraham's wife and the mother of Isaac, the child of promise. She became a mother in old age, proving that nothing is too hard for the Lord.

Key Story

After decades of waiting and even laughter at the promise, Sarah gave birth to Isaac at ninety years old, just as God had said.

Life Lesson

Sarah waited a lifetime for her promise, and God was not late. The season of waiting is not a season of being forgotten.
