Balaam

Balaam, son of Beor, was a prophet from Pethor whom Balak king of Moab hired to curse Israel — but God put blessings in his mouth instead.

Key Story

On the road to Moab, Balaam's donkey spoke to him and turned from the angel of the LORD; from the heights he blessed Israel four times and foretold a star out of Jacob.

Life Lesson

No payment can buy a curse on those God has blessed.
