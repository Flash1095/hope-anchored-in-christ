Summary
Amraphel was the king of Shinar who joined Chedorlaomer's alliance against the cities of the plain.
Key Story
Amraphel marched with three other kings, defeated Sodom and Gomorrah, and carried off Lot — until Abram rescued him.
Key Verses
Genesis 14:1-16

Related People

Life Lesson
