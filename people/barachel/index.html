Summary
Barachel the Buzite was the father of Elihu, named in Job as Elihu is introduced before his speeches.
Key Story
Barachel himself speaks no words in the book; he is known only as Elihu's father, of the kindred of Ram.
Key Verses
Job 32:2; Job 32:6
Related Books
Job
Related People
Elihu
Life Lesson
Even a one-line mention in Scripture counts: Barachel's son spoke truth, and a father's name is preserved with it.
