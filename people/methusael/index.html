Methusael

Name

Role

Testament
Old
Timeline Era

Summary
Methusael was the son of Mehujael and the father of Lamech, in Cain's line.
Key Story
Methusael's name survives only in the genealogy leading to Lamech, the first man recorded with two wives.
Key Verses
Genesis 4:18
