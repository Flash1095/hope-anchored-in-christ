King Saul

Saul was Israel's first king, tall and impressive, anointed by Samuel, who began humbly but fell through disobedience and jealousy of David. His reign ended in tragedy on Mount Gilboa.

Key Story

Saul impatiently offered a sacrifice that was not his to give, losing God's favour and setting his kingdom on a path of decline.

Life Lesson

Saul shows that starting well is not enough — obedience keeps us anchored. The good news is that God delights to restore those who, unlike Saul, truly turn back to Him.
