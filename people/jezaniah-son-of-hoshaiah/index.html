Jezaniah, Son of Hoshaiah

Timeline Era

Summary
Jezaniah, son of Hoshaiah, was a captain of the remnant who came with Johanan to ask Jeremiah to pray for them and learn God's will about staying or going. He is a different man from Jezaniah the Maachathite.
Key Story
Jezaniah son of Hoshaiah came with all the captains to Jeremiah, asking him to pray that the Lord would show them the way to walk and the thing to do.

Life Lesson
