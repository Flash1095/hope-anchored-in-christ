Jehoiachin, King of Judah

Name
Jehoiachin, King of Judah
Role
King of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Summary
Jehoiachin reigned three months, surrendered to Nebuchadnezzar, and was carried to Babylon with his mother and officers; thirty-seven years later Evil-Merodach released him from prison and set his seat above the other captive kings.
Key Story
Jehoiachin surrenders and is later released in Babylon.
Key Verses
2 Kings 24:8; 2 Kings 25:27; 2 Chronicles 36:8; 2 Chronicles 36:10
Related Books
2 Kings; 2 Chronicles
Related People

Life Lesson
Even in exile, God's mercy finds the prisoner: Jehoiachin's release is a small dawn before the return.
