Toi, King of Hamath

Toi, king of Hamath, had been at war with Hadadezer all his days. When David defeated Hadadezer, Toi sent his son Joram with vessels of silver, gold, and brass to bless David, and David dedicated them to the Lord.

Key Story

Toi turned an enemy's defeat into an offering, sending silver, gold, and brass to David.

Life Lesson

Wisdom knows when a rival's fall is a gift to dedicate, not to hoard.
