Abel

Name
Abel
Role
Shepherd; First Martyr
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Abel was the second son of Adam and Eve, a shepherd whose offering pleased God. He became the first person to die for righteousness.
Key Story
Abel brought God the best of his flock in faith, and his jealous brother Cain murdered him in the field.
Key Verses
Genesis 4:4; Hebrews 11:4; Matthew 23:35
Related Books
Genesis; Hebrews
Related People
Adam; Eve; Cain
Life Lesson
Abel still speaks through his faith, long after his life was cut short. A life offered to God in faith is never wasted and never truly silenced.
