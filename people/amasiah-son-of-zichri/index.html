Amasiah, Son of Zichri

Amasiah, son of Zichri, willingly offered himself unto the LORD and commanded two hundred thousand mighty men of valour under Jehoshaphat.

Key Story

Next to Jehohanan was Amasiah the son of Zichri, who willingly offered himself unto the LORD, with two hundred thousand mighty men.

Life Lesson

God's law taught throughout the land, and willing service under a faithful king, strengthened Judah.
