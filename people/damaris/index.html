Summary
Damaris was a woman at Athens who believed after Paul's address on Mars' Hill, named alongside Dionysius the Areopagite. Acts records nothing further of her.
Key Story
Damaris is named once, among those who clave unto Paul and believed at Athens.
Key Verses
Acts 17:34
Related Books
Acts
Related People
Paul; Dionysius the Areopagite
Life Lesson
One verse holds a whole conversion. Damaris believed at Athens and is remembered for it.
