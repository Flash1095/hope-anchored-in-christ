Shealtiel

Name
Shealtiel
Role
Father of Zerubbabel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Summary
Shealtiel, father of Zerubbabel, is named in Haggai as the father of the governor who led the returned exiles. Through Zerubbabel, Shealtiel stands in the royal line that Matthew's genealogy traces to Christ.
Key Story
Shealtiel's son Zerubbabel governed the returned exiles and, with Joshua the high priest, led the rebuilding of the Temple.
Key Verses
Haggai 1:1; Haggai 2:2; Ezra 3:2; Ezra 3:8; Ezra 5:2; Nehemiah 12:1
Related Books
Haggai; Ezra; Nehemiah
Related People
Zerubbabel; Haggai
Life Lesson
Shealtiel never held office himself, but his son did — and through that son, the royal line ran on to Christ. God builds His purposes through fathers, not only through kings.
Related Places
Jerusalem
Related Events
Rebuilding of the Temple; Nehemiah Rebuilds the Wall
