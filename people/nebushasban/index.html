Nebushasban

Name
Nebushasban
Role
Official of Babylon
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Summary
Nebushasban, titled Rab-saris (chief eunuch), was one of the Babylonian officers sent to take Jeremiah out of the prison court after Jerusalem fell.
Key Story
Nebushasban joined Nebuzaradan's party in sending for Jeremiah and bringing him out of the court of the prison.
Key Verses
Jeremiah 39:13
