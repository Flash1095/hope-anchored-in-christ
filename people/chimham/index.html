Chimham

Name
Chimham
Role
Companion of Barzillai
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Chimham was associated with Barzillai the Gileadite, who asked David to let Chimham cross the Jordan and live at court in his place when Barzillai himself was too old to go to Jerusalem.
Key Story
Chimham crossed over to David's court in the place of the aged Barzillai.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 19:37; 2 Samuel 19:38
Related Books
2 Samuel
Related People
Barzillai the Gileadite; King David
Life Lesson
Going in another's place is its own quiet honour.
