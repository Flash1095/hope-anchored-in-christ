Ahio, Son of Abinadab

Name
Ahio, Son of Abinadab
Role
Son of Abinadab
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Ahio, son of Abinadab, walked before the ark with his brother Uzzah as it was brought up from Kirjath-jearim toward Jerusalem on a new cart.
Key Story
Ahio walked before the ark on the day his brother died beside it.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 6:3; 2 Samuel 6:4
Related Books
2 Samuel
Related People
Abinadab of Kirjath-jearim; Uzzah, Son of Abinadab; King David
Life Lesson
Walking before the ark is honour — and the day can still end in grief.
