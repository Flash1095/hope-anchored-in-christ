Joses, Brother of Jesus

Role
Brother of Jesus
Testament

Summary
Joses was one of the brothers of Jesus named by the people of Nazareth, alongside James, Simon, and Judas. The Gospels record nothing further of him by name.
Key Story
The people of Nazareth named Joses among Jesus' brothers and stumbled that the carpenter's family held no prophet.
Key Verses
Matthew 13:55; Mark 6:3
Related Books
Matthew; Mark
Related People
Jesus; James, Brother of Jesus; Mary, Mother of Jesus
Life Lesson
Familiarity can blind us to glory. Nazareth knew Jesus' brothers and missed its Saviour.
