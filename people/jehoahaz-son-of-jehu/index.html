Jehoahaz, Son of Jehu

Jehoahaz, son of Jehu and king of Israel, was the father of Joash king of Israel, who defeated Amaziah of Judah.

Key Story

Amaziah sent to Joash, the son of Jehoahaz, the son of Jehu, king of Israel, and Joash later took Amaziah at Beth-shemesh.

Life Lesson

A dynasty founded in judgment still passed its throne from father to son.
