Zeeb, Prince of Midian

Name
Zeeb, Prince of Midian
Role
Prince of Midian
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Zeeb, a prince of Midian, was captured and killed by Ephraim at the winepress of Zeeb during Gideon's victory.
Key Story
Ephraim pursued the fleeing Midianites and slew Zeeb at the winepress of Zeeb, bringing the heads of Oreb and Zeeb to Gideon.
Key Verses
Judges 7:25; Judges 8:3; Psalm 83:11
