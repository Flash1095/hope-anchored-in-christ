Huram, the Craftsman

Name
Huram, the Craftsman
Role
Master Craftsman of the Temple
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Huram, a cunning man endued with understanding, was sent by the king of Tyre to Solomon; he made the temple's bronze vessels, including the pots, shovels, basins, pillars and sea.
Key Story
Huram finished the bronze work for the house of God, and made all its instruments of bright brass for King Solomon.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 2:13; 2 Chronicles 4:11; 2 Chronicles 4:16
Related Books

Related People
King Solomon; Hiram, King of Tyre
Life Lesson
God-given skill, used for His house, is itself a form of service.
