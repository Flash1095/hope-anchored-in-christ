Urijah, Priest

Urijah the priest built a new altar in Jerusalem after the pattern Ahaz sent from Damascus, and offered on it instead of the bronze altar, following the king's command.

Key Story

Urijah builds Ahaz's Damascus altar.

Life Lesson

A priest who builds what a king sketches has traded his calling for favour.
