Hobab

Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Hobab, son of Raguel the Midianite, was the brother-in-law of Moses, who asked him to guide Israel through the wilderness he knew.
Key Story
Moses begged Hobab not to leave them, saying he would be as eyes to Israel in the desert.
Key Verses
Numbers 10:29-32
