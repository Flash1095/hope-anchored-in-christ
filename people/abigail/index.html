Abigail

Name
Abigail
Role
Wise Wife; Peacemaker
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Abigail was the wise and beautiful wife of foolish Nabal who prevented David from shedding needless blood. After Nabal's death she became David's wife.
Key Story
Abigail hurried to meet David with provisions, humbly interceded for her household, and turned an angry king from vengeance.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 25:32-33; 1 Samuel 25:23-31
Related Books
1 Samuel
Related People
King David; Nabal; King Saul
Life Lesson
Abigail shows the power of a wise, courageous peacemaker in a volatile moment. God can use your calm words to stop harm and redirect a story toward mercy.
