Judah the Levite

Name
Judah the Levite
Role
Levite
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Summary
Levite named among those who had taken foreign wives (Ezra 10:23), among the Levites who returned with Zerubbabel (Nehemiah 12:8), and among the musicians at the dedication of the wall (Nehemiah 12:33, 12:34, 12:36).
Key Story
Ezra's reform
Key Verses
Ezra 10:23; Nehemiah 12:8; Nehemiah 12:33; Nehemiah 12:34; Nehemiah 12:36
Related Books
Ezra; Nehemiah
Life Lesson
A Levite who fell, returned, and sang: repentance restores service.

Jerusalem
