King David

Name
King David
Role
King of Israel; Psalmist
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
David was the shepherd boy anointed king, slayer of Goliath, and Israel's greatest king, a man after God's own heart. He wrote many psalms and received God's promise of an everlasting kingdom fulfilled in Jesus.
Key Story
As a teenager with a sling and five stones, David faced Goliath declaring that the battle is the Lord's.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 17:45-47; Psalm 23:1; Acts 13:22; 2 Samuel 7:16; 1 Kings 1:1; 1 Kings 2:10; Psalm 18:50; Psalm 78:70
Related Books
1 Samuel; 2 Samuel; Psalms; Acts; 1 Kings; Proverbs; Ecclesiastes
Related People
Samuel; Jonathan; Bathsheba; King Solomon; Nathan; King Saul; Absalom
Life Lesson
David sinned greatly and repented deeply, and God still called him a man after His own heart. Your anchor is not your perfection but a God who forgives and restores.
Related Places
Bethlehem; Hebron; Jerusalem
