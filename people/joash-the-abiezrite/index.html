Joash, the Abiezrite

Joash the Abiezrite, of Ophrah, was Gideon's father; when the men of the city demanded Gideon's death for breaking down Baal's altar, Joash defended him, saying Baal could plead for himself.

Key Story

Joash owned the altar Gideon tore down, and when the townsmen came for his son, he answered that if Baal were a god he could plead his own cause — and Gideon was called Jerubbaal from that day.

Life Lesson

A father's courage can be as simple as refusing to kill his son for a god who cannot defend itself.
