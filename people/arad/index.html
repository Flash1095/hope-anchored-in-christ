Arad

Arad was the Canaanite king of the south who attacked Israel on the march and took some of them prisoner.

Key Story

Israel vowed to destroy the cities of Arad's king, and the LORD delivered the Canaanites into their hand at Hormah.

Life Lesson

A vow made to God in defeat can become a victory in His time.
