Lamech, Father of Noah

Name
Lamech, Father of Noah
Role
Father of Noah
Testament

Timeline Era

Summary
Lamech was the son of Methuselah and the father of Noah. He lived 777 years.
Key Story
When Noah was born, Lamech said this son would bring comfort concerning the ground the LORD had cursed.
Key Verses
Genesis 5:25-31
Related Books
Genesis
Related People

Life Lesson
Even under the curse, Lamech held on to hope — and named his son for comfort.
