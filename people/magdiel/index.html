Magdiel

Magdiel was a duke of Edom, named among the chiefs who ruled by their families and dwelling places.

Key Story

Magdiel appears in the closing list of Edom's dukes, the chiefs descended from Esau.

Life Lesson

God's story keeps moving through families we know almost nothing about — and He knows them all.
