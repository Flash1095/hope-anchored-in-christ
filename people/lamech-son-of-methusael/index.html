Lamech, Son of Methusael

Lamech was the son of Methusael, a descendant of Cain, and the first man recorded as having two wives, Adah and Zillah.

Key Story

Lamech boasted to his wives about killing a man in revenge, showing how violence deepened in Cain's line.

Life Lesson

Lamech's boast shows where unchecked vengeance leads: further from God, not closer.
