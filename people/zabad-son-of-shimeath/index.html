Summary
Zabad, son of Shimeath an Ammonitess, was one of the servants who conspired against Joash for the blood of the sons of Jehoiada and slew him on his bed.
Key Story
Zabad the son of Shimeath and Jehozabad the son of Shimrith conspired against Joash and slew him on his bed.
