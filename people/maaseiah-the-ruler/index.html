Maaseiah, the Ruler

Maaseiah the ruler, with Jeiel the scribe under Hananiah, numbered Uzziah's army for war.

Key Story

The host went out by bands by the hand of Jeiel the scribe and Maaseiah the ruler, under Hananiah.

Life Lesson

Order in an army begins with faithful record-keepers.
