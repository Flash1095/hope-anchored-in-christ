Hiram, Craftsman of Tyre

Name
Hiram, Craftsman of Tyre
Role
Bronze Craftsman
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Hiram, a widow's son of Tyre skilled in bronze, cast the two pillars, the sea, the bases, and all the vessels for Solomon's temple, and finished all the work for the house of the LORD.
Key Story
Hiram casts the temple's bronze works.
Key Verses
1 Kings 7:13; 1 Kings 7:40
Related Books

Related People

Life Lesson
