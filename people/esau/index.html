Esau

Name
Esau
Role
Son of Isaac; Father of Edom
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Esau was Isaac's firstborn twin, a skillful hunter who traded his birthright for a bowl of stew. He became the father of the nation of Edom.
Key Story
Hungry after hunting, Esau sold his birthright to Jacob for bread and lentil stew, despising what was most valuable.
Key Verses
Genesis 25:34; Genesis 27:41; Hebrews 12:16-17
Related Books
Genesis; Hebrews; Obadiah
Related People
Isaac; Rebekah; Jacob
Life Lesson
Esau traded the lasting for the immediate — a warning we all need. Do not let a moment of appetite cost you what God meant for your future.
