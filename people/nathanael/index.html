Summary
Nathanael of Cana in Galilee was brought to Jesus by Philip, confessed him as the Son of God and King of Israel, and was among the seven disciples at the Sea of Tiberias after the resurrection. Tradition identifies Nathanael with the apostle Bartholomew (named in the apostolic lists where Nathanael is not); this profile keeps them separate and notes the tradition without merging them.
