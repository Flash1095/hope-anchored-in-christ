Eliezer, Son of Dodavah

Name
Eliezer, Son of Dodavah
Role
Prophet
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Eliezer, son of Dodavah of Mareshah, prophesied against Jehoshaphat for joining with Ahaziah, and the ships built for Tarshish were broken.
Key Story
Because Jehoshaphat joined himself with Ahaziah, Eliezer declared the LORD had broken his works, and the ships could not go to Tarshish.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 20:37

Life Lesson
An alliance with the wicked can wreck even a well-built enterprise.
