Joseph of Arimathea

Joseph of Arimathea was a rich and honoured council member, a secret disciple waiting for God's kingdom, who boldly asked Pilate for Jesus' body. He laid Jesus in his own new tomb, fulfilling prophecy.

Key Story

Going boldly to Pilate, Joseph requested Jesus' body, bought fine linen, and laid Him in a tomb cut from rock.

Life Lesson

Joseph's courage came at the darkest hour, when following Jesus looked finished. God honours quiet disciples who step forward when it costs the most.
