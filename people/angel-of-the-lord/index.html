Angel of the LORD

Name
Angel of the LORD
Role
Messenger of the LORD
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
The Angel of the LORD appears across the Old Testament — to Hagar in the wilderness, to Abraham on Moriah, to Moses at the burning bush, to Gideon, to Manoah's wife, and to Jerusalem in Hezekiah's day — often speaking in the first person as God himself. Whether these appearances are a theophany, or the pre-incarnate Christ (a Christophany), is debated by scholars and theologians; this profile states the appearances and the debate without settling it.
