Shemaiah, Man of God

Shemaiah the man of God came to Rehoboam as Judah marched to fight Israel and said, You shall not go up nor fight your brethren, for this thing is from me; and the army turned back.

Key Story

Shemaiah stops Rehoboam's war against Israel.

Life Lesson

Sometimes the bravest word a prophet speaks is 'do not fight your brothers.'
