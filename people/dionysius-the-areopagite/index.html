Dionysius the Areopagite

Dionysius was a member of the Areopagus council at Athens who believed after hearing Paul's address on Mars' Hill about the unknown God and the resurrection.

Key Story

Dionysius, a judge of the Areopagus, heard Paul on the unknown God and believed.

Life Lesson

Philosophy heard the resurrection and one judge believed. Dionysius shows the gospel can reach the council chamber.
