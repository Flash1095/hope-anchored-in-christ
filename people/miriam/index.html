Miriam

Miriam was the sister of Moses and Aaron, a prophetess who watched over baby Moses at the Nile and led Israel's women in worship after the Red Sea crossing.

Key Story

After Israel crossed the Red Sea, Miriam took a timbrel and led the women in singing, Greatly to be praised is the Lord.

Life Lesson

Miriam's song was Israel's first worship chorus after deliverance. When God brings you through your Red Sea, let your first response be praise that leads others to sing too.
