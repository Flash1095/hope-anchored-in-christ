Jehozadak

Jehozadak (KJV 'Josedech' in Haggai) is named as the father of Joshua the high priest (Haggai 1:1). He was the high priest carried into Babylonian exile, so his son Joshua served as high priest of the return without his father beside him.

Key Story

Jehozadak, the exiled high priest, is named as the father of Joshua, who served as high priest when the exiles returned and rebuilt.

Life Lesson

Jehozadak went into exile and never came home — but his son rebuilt. Sometimes the greatest service is a son raised for a day the father never sees.
