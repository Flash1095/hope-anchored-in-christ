Adonijah, Son of Haggith

Name
Adonijah, Son of Haggith
Role
Son of David
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Adonijah, David's son by Haggith, exalted himself to be king while David was old, gathered Joab and Abiathar, and after Solomon's anointing asked for Abishag as wife, for which Solomon had him executed.
Key Story
Adonijah's bid for the throne and his execution.
Key Verses
1 Kings 1:5; 1 Kings 2:25
Related Books
1 Kings
Related People
King David; King Solomon; Haggith, Mother of Adonijah; Joab, Son of Zeruiah; Abiathar, Priest; Abishag, Shunammite
Life Lesson
Seizing a crown God has given to another is rebellion, not ambition.
Related Places
Jerusalem
