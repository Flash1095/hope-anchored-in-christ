Summary
Terah was the son of Nahor and the father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran. He lived 205 years.
Key Story
Terah led his family out of Ur toward Canaan, but they settled in Haran, where he died — and Abram's journey continued from there.
Key Verses
Genesis 11:24-32
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Nahor, Father of Terah; Abraham; Nahor, Brother of Abraham; Haran; Lot
Life Lesson
A journey can stall with one generation and resume with the next; God is patient with families.
