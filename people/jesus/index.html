Jesus

Jesus is the Son of God, the promised Messiah, who was born of Mary, taught with authority, died on the cross for our sins, and rose again on the third day. He is the anchor of our hope and the centre of the whole biblical story.

Key Story

Jesus calmed a storm with a word, raised Lazarus from the dead, and after His crucifixion rose bodily from the tomb, appearing to His disciples over forty days.

Life Lesson

We have this hope as an anchor for the soul, firm and secure — and that hope has a name: Jesus. Whatever storm you face, an anchor holds because He holds.
