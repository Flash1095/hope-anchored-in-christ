Hananiah, the False Prophet

Name
Hananiah, the False Prophet
Role
False Prophet
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exile
Summary
Hananiah, son of Azur of Gibeon, was a false prophet who broke the wooden yoke from Jeremiah's neck in the temple and promised that Babylon's yoke would be broken within two years and the captives returned. Jeremiah answered that Hananiah had made the people trust in a lie, and Hananiah died that same year, as Jeremiah foretold. He is a different man from Hananiah, Daniel's companion, whose Babylonian name was Shadrach.
Key Story
Hananiah broke Jeremiah's yoke of wood in the temple, and Jeremiah replaced it with a yoke of iron and told Hananiah he would die that year — and he did, in the seventh month.

Life Lesson
