Abihu

Name
Abihu
Role

Testament

Summary
Abihu was a son of Aaron and Elisheba, and one of the priests of Israel.
Key Story
Abihu went up Mount Sinai with Moses and saw God, but later offered strange fire with his brother Nadab and died.
Key Verses
Exodus 6:23; Exodus 24:1; Exodus 28:1; Leviticus 10:1-2
