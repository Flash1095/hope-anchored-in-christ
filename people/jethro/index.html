Jethro

Jethro was the priest of Midian, the father of Zipporah, and Moses' father-in-law. He is also called Reuel.

Key Story

Jethro sheltered Moses in Midian, gave him Zipporah as wife, and later advised him to share the burden of judging Israel — counsel Moses followed.

Life Lesson

Wise counsel from outside the family can be God's gift: Jethro taught Moses to share the load.
