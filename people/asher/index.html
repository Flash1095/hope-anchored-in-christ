Summary
Asher was the eighth son of Jacob, the second by Zilpah, and the father of Jimnah, Ishuah, Isui, Beriah, and Serah.
Key Story
Asher's tribe later enjoyed rich food and royal delicacies, just as Jacob had blessed him.
Key Verses
Genesis 30:12-13; Genesis 46:17; Genesis 49:20
