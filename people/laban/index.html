Laban

Name
Laban
Role
Brother of Rebekah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Laban was the brother of Rebekah and the son of Bethuel. He later became Jacob's father-in-law, and his dealings with Jacob fill a whole chapter of the patriarchs' story.
Key Story
Laban welcomed Abraham's servant, negotiated Rebekah's marriage to Isaac, and years later outwitted Jacob over wages and wives — until Jacob finally left for home.
Key Verses
Genesis 24; Genesis 29-31
Related Books

Life Lesson
