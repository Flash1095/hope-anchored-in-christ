Erastus

Name
Erastus
Role
Chamberlain and Companion of Paul
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Erastus was one of Paul's companions sent ahead from Ephesus into Macedonia with Timothy while Paul stayed in Asia. He is traditionally identified with the chamberlain of Corinth named in the later epistles, though Acts does not say so.
Key Story
Erastus was sent ahead with Timothy into Macedonia while Paul finished his work in Asia.
Key Verses
Acts 19:22; Romans 16:23; 2 Timothy 4:20
Related Books
Acts; Romans; 2 Timothy
Related People
Paul; Timothy
Life Lesson
Advance parties go first and unseen. Erastus carried the mission into Macedonia ahead of Paul.
Related Places
Ephesus; Corinth
