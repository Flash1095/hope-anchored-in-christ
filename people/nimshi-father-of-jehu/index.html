Nimshi, Father of Jehu

Nimshi was the father of Jehu, whom the LORD anointed to cut off the house of Ahab.

Key Story

Nimshi is named only as the father of Jehu the son of Nimshi.

Life Lesson

An anointed judge came from an otherwise unnamed family.
