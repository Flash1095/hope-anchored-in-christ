Ahitub, Father of Zadok

Ahitub is named as the father of Zadok, the priest who served David and anointed Solomon. He belongs to the Eleazar line, distinct from Ahitub son of Phinehas of Eli's house.

Key Story

Ahitub fathered the priest who would anoint Solomon and carry the priesthood forward.

Life Lesson

Two men can share a name and belong to entirely different stories.
