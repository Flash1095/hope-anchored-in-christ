Tabitha

Tabitha, called Dorcas in Greek, was a disciple at Joppa full of good works and almsdeeds. When she died, the widows showed Peter the coats she had made, and Peter prayed and raised her, saying, Tabitha, arise.

Key Story

Tabitha died, and the widows wept over her coats; Peter prayed, and she opened her eyes and sat up.

Life Lesson

A life of coats and kindness is a sermon the widows preached at her bedside. Tabitha's works outlived her death.
