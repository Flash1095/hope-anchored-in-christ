Summary
Jason was a believer at Thessalonica who hosted Paul and Silas. When the Jews stirred up the city, the mob dragged Jason before the rulers, accusing him of harbouring men who acted contrary to Caesar, saying there was another king, Jesus. Jason was released on security.
Key Story
Jason opened his house to Paul and was dragged before the rulers for harbouring another king's men.
