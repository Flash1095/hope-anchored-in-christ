Amittai

Amittai is named as the father of the prophet Jonah (Jonah 1:1), and Jonah is called 'son of Amittai' again in the history of Jeroboam II's reign, where his prophecy of Israel's restored borders came true.

Key Story

Amittai's son Jonah, called 'son of Amittai,' was sent to Nineveh — and his earlier prophecy of Israel's borders had already proved true under Jeroboam II.

Life Lesson

Amittai's son ran from God and was still used by Him. A parent's legacy is not cancelled by a child's detour.
