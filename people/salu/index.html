Salu

Salu is named in Numbers as the father of Zimri, the Simeonite prince slain at Peor.

Key Story

Salu's son Zimri was killed by Phinehas for openly defying God in the sight of all Israel.

Life Lesson

Even a single mention means God did not overlook him.
