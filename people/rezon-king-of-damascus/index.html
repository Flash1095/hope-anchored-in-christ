Rezon, King of Damascus

Name
Rezon, King of Damascus
Role
King of Damascus
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Rezon, son of Eliadah, fled from his lord Hadadezer of Zobah, gathered men, became captain, and reigned in Damascus as an adversary to Solomon all his days.
Key Story
Rezon becomes Solomon's adversary in Damascus.
Key Verses
1 Kings 11:23; 1 Kings 11:25
Related Books
1 Kings
Related People
King Solomon; Hadadezer, King of Zobah; Eliadah, Father of Rezon
Life Lesson
A runaway servant can become a king, and a king an adversary.
