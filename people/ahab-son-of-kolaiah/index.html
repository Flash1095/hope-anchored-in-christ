Summary
Ahab, son of Kolaiah, was one of the false prophets among the Babylonian exiles whom Jeremiah denounced; he prophesied lies in God's name, and Jeremiah foretold that Nebuchadnezzar would roast him and Zedekiah son of Maaseiah in the fire.
Key Story
Jeremiah named Ahab son of Kolaiah and Zedekiah son of Maaseiah as men who prophesied lies, and said the king of Babylon would slay them before the exiles' eyes.
