Ammihud

Name
Ammihud
Role
Father of Elishama
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Ammihud is named in Numbers as the father of Elishama the prince of Ephraim, and of Shemuel and Pedahel among the men who divided the land.
Key Story
Ammihud's sons served Israel in the wilderness and in the dividing of Canaan.
Key Verses
Numbers 1:10; 34:20; 34:28
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Elishama, Son of Ammihud; Shemuel, Son of Ammihud; Pedahel, Son of Ammihud; Moses
Life Lesson
A father's legacy is measured in the service of his children.
