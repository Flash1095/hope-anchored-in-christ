Lemuel

Lemuel was a king whose words make up Proverbs 31, recording the teaching his mother gave him — including the famous portrait of the virtuous woman.

Key Story

King Lemuel's oracle warns kings against wine and injustice, and praises the woman who fears the Lord, whose worth is far above rubies.

Life Lesson

Lemuel shows that the best royal wisdom is often learned at home — a mother's teaching, kept and passed on.
