Athaliah

Name

Role
Queen of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Athaliah was the daughter of Ahab and Jezebel who seized Judah's throne and tried to destroy the royal family line. God preserved the baby Joash, keeping David's line — and the Messiah's line — alive.
Key Story
Athaliah ruled Judah for six years until the priest Jehoiada crowned young Joash and she was removed from the throne.
Key Verses
2 Kings 11:1-3; 2 Chronicles 22:10-12
Related Books
2 Kings; 2 Chronicles
Related People
Jezebel; King Ahab
Life Lesson
Athaliah nearly erased David's line, but a hidden baby in the temple kept God's promise alive. God's purposes can survive in a single protected life — He guards what He has promised.
