Demetrius, the Silversmith

Name
Demetrius, the Silversmith
Role
Silversmith of Ephesus
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Demetrius was a silversmith at Ephesus who made silver shrines of Diana and stirred up his fellow craftsmen against Paul, fearing their trade and the goddess's honour would fall. The uproar filled the theatre for two hours with cries of Great is Diana. He is distinct from Demetrius of the later epistles.
Key Story
Demetrius counted his profits in shrines and cried that Paul had turned the city from Diana.
