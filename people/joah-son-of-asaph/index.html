Joah, Son of Asaph

Name
Joah, Son of Asaph
Role
Recorder
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Joah, son of Asaph, was Hezekiah's recorder and stood with Eliakim and Shebna before the Rabshakeh, then reported to the king with torn clothes.
Key Story
Joah hears the Rabshakeh and reports to Hezekiah.
Key Verses
2 Kings 18:18; 2 Kings 18:37
Related Books
2 Kings
Related People
King Hezekiah; Eliakim, Son of Hilkiah; Shebna, Scribe; Asaph, Father of Joah
Life Lesson
The recorder's duty is to hear truly and report honestly, even threats.
Related Places
Jerusalem
