Michaiah, Daughter of Uriel

Michaiah, daughter of Uriel of Gibeah, was the mother of Abijah, king of Judah.

Key Story

Abijah reigned three years in Jerusalem, and his mother's name was Michaiah the daughter of Uriel of Gibeah.

Life Lesson

Mothers of kings are named because their sons' reigns are part of God's record.
