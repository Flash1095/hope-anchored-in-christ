Alexander of Ephesus

Name
Alexander of Ephesus
Role
Spokesman at Ephesus
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Alexander was a man put forward by the Jews during the Ephesian riot, who tried to address the theatre crowd in defence of his people, but was shouted down when the crowd recognised him as a Jew. He is distinct from Alexander son of Simon of Cyrene and from Alexander named in the later epistles.
Key Story
Alexander was thrust forward to speak in the theatre and drowned out by two hours of shouting.
Key Verses
Acts 19:33
Related Books
Acts
Related People
Paul; Demetrius, the Silversmith
Life Lesson
A crowd in uproar hears no defence. Alexander's silence warns us against reasoning with a riot.
