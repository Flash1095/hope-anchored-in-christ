Timna

Name
Timna
Role
Sister of Lotan; Concubine of Eliphaz
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Timna was the sister of Lotan, daughter of Seir, and the concubine of Eliphaz, to whom she bore Amalek.
Key Story
Timna links two houses of Edom: Seir's Horite family by birth, and Esau's line by her son Amalek.
Key Verses
Genesis 36:12; Genesis 36:22
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Lotan; Seir; Eliphaz, Son of Esau; Amalek
Life Lesson
God names women in genealogies where the world might forget them.
