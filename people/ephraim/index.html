Ephraim

Name
Ephraim
Role
Son of Joseph
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Ephraim was Joseph's second son by Asenath, born in Egypt before the famine.
Key Story
Joseph named him Ephraim, saying God had made him fruitful in the land of his affliction — and Jacob crossed his hands to bless Ephraim above Manasseh.
Key Verses
Genesis 41:52; Genesis 46:20; Genesis 48
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Joseph, Son of Jacob; Asenath; Manasseh
Life Lesson
God's blessing does not always follow birth order — He chooses as He wills.
