Justus of Corinth

Name
Justus of Corinth
Role
Host of Paul at Corinth
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Justus was a man at Corinth who worshipped God, whose house joined hard to the synagogue. Paul left the synagogue and preached next door at Justus' house after the Jews opposed him. He is distinct from Joseph Barsabbas, also called Justus.
Key Story
Paul moved next door to the synagogue, into the house of Justus, and the church met at the wall between.
Key Verses
Acts 18:7

Life Lesson
When one door closes, the house next door opens. Justus' wall adjoined the synagogue and held the gospel.
