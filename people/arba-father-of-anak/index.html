Arba, Father of Anak

Arba, father of Anak, was the greatest man among the Anakim, and Kirjath-arba — that is, Hebron — was named for him.

Key Story

Arba's name lived on in the city of the Anakim until Caleb drove them out and took Hebron for his inheritance.

Life Lesson

Even giants have fathers, and even their cities change hands.
