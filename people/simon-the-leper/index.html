Life of Christ
Summary
Simon the Leper lived at Bethany, where Jesus dined at his house shortly before the crucifixion. During that meal a woman anointed Jesus' head with costly ointment, which He received as preparation for His burial.
Key Story
At Simon's table in Bethany, a woman poured ointment on Jesus' head, and He called it anointing for His burial.
Key Verses
Matthew 26:6; Mark 14:3

Life Lesson
