Gedaliah, Son of Amariah

Name
Gedaliah, Son of Amariah
Role
Grandfather of Zephaniah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Gedaliah, son of Amariah, is named only in Zephaniah's genealogy (Zephaniah 1:1), as the grandfather of the prophet. He is not the Gedaliah appointed governor after Jerusalem's fall — that Gedaliah was son of Ahikam, a different man.
Key Story
Gedaliah appears only in Zephaniah's genealogy, as the prophet's grandfather between Cushi and Amariah.
Key Verses
Zephaniah 1:1
Related Books
Zephaniah
Related People
Zephaniah; Cushi, Father of Zephaniah; Amariah, Son of Hezekiah
Life Lesson
Two Gedaliahs, two different men — Scripture's genealogies reward careful reading. Never merge namesakes into one person.
