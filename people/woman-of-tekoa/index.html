Summary
The wise woman of Tekoa — unnamed in the text — was sent by Joab to David in mourning dress with a parable about her two sons, one dead and one threatened. Her story moved David to bring Absalom back from exile.
Key Story
The woman of Tekoa's parable of two sons moved David to recall his exiled son.
