Summary
Zophar the Naamathite was the bluntest of Job's three friends, accusing Job of mocking God and deserving even worse than he suffered. He spoke only twice before falling silent.
Key Story
Zophar claimed Job's guilt was obvious and that God's wisdom was far beyond him, so Job should simply repent. Like his friends, he was corrected by God in the end and restored through Job's prayer.
Key Verses
Job 2:11; Job 11:6; Job 42:7-9
Related Books
Job
Related People
Job; Eliphaz; Bildad
Life Lesson
Zophar's bluntness reminds us that certainty is not the same as compassion. When we cannot explain someone's suffering, the faithful move is to stay, listen, and pray — not to accuse.
