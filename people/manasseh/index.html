Summary
Manasseh was Joseph's firstborn son by Asenath, born in Egypt before the famine.
Key Story
Joseph named him Manasseh, saying God had made him forget all his toil and his father's house — yet Jacob later blessed Ephraim above him.
Key Verses
Genesis 41:51; Genesis 46:20; Genesis 48

Life Lesson
