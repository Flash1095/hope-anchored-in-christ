Summary
Tamar was Judah's widowed daughter-in-law who acted boldly to secure the family line after being denied justice. She is named in the genealogy of Jesus.
Key Story
Denied the husband Judah owed her, Tamar disguised herself to conceive by Judah, and he declared her more righteous than himself.
Key Verses
Genesis 38:26; Ruth 4:12; Matthew 1:3
Related Books
Genesis; Matthew
Related People
Judah; Jacob
Life Lesson
Tamar refused to let injustice erase her from God's story, and God honoured her in the Messiah's family tree. Your painful chapter can become part of His redemption story.
