Milcah, Daughter of Zelophehad

Name

Daughter of Zelophehad

Summary
Milcah, daughter of Zelophehad, was one of the five sisters who asked Moses for their father's inheritance.
Key Story
Milcah stood with her sisters before Moses and the congregation, and God granted daughters the right to inherit when there is no son.
Key Verses
Numbers 27:1-11; 36:11
