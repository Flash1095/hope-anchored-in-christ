Zerubbabel

Name
Zerubbabel
Role
Governor of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Return and Second Temple
Summary
Zerubbabel was a descendant of David who governed Judah after the return from exile and led the rebuilding of the Temple. God encouraged him that the work would be finished not by might but by God's Spirit.
Key Story
Zerubbabel laid the Temple's foundation, and despite opposition and discouragement, God's word through Zechariah promised his hands would also finish it.
Key Verses
Zechariah 4:6-9; Ezra 3:8-10; Haggai 2:23
Related Books

Related People

Life Lesson
Do not despise the day of small beginnings. The hands that start God's work in faith, sustained by His Spirit, are the hands He promises will finish it.
