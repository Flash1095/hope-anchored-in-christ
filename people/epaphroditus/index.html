Epaphroditus

Name
Epaphroditus
Role
Messenger of the Philippians to Paul
Testament

Timeline Era

Summary
Epaphroditus was the messenger of the Philippian church who brought Paul their gift and ministered to his wants; he fell sick nigh unto death, and God had mercy on him, before Paul sent him back with honour.
