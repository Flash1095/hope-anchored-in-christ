Mary of Bethany

Life of Christ
Summary
Mary of Bethany was Martha and Lazarus's sister, known for sitting at Jesus' feet to listen and for anointing Him with costly perfume. Jesus defended her devotion and said her act would be told wherever the gospel is preached.
Key Story
Mary poured a pound of pure nard on Jesus' feet and wiped them with her hair, and the house was filled with the fragrance.
Key Verses
John 12:3; Luke 10:39; Matthew 26:13

Life Lesson
