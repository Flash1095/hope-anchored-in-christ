Adah, Wife of Esau

Name
Adah, Wife of Esau
Role
Wife of Esau
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Adah was a wife of Esau, the daughter of Elon the Hittite, and the mother of Eliphaz.
Key Story
Adah's son Eliphaz fathered the dukes of Edom, so her line became the firstborn line of Esau's house.
Key Verses
Genesis 36:2-4; Genesis 36:10-11
Related Books
