Jezaniah the Maachathite

Jezaniah the Maachathite was a captain of the forces who came with his men to Gedaliah at Mizpah after Jerusalem fell. He is a different man from Jezaniah son of Hoshaiah.

Key Story

Jezaniah the Maachathite came with his men to Gedaliah at Mizpah and heard the governor's counsel to serve Babylon and live.

Life Lesson

After ruin, the first faithful step is to gather where God's appointed governor is.
