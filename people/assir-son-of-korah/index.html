Assir, Son of Korah

Assir was a son of Korah, the Levite, named in the genealogy of Moses' tribe.

Key Story

Assir is named with his brothers Elkanah and Abiasaph among Korah's sons.

Life Lesson

God's story keeps moving through families we know almost nothing about — and He knows them all.
