Micah, Father of Abdon

Micah was the father of Abdon, sent by Josiah to enquire of the LORD concerning the found book.

Key Story

Micah is named only as the father of Abdon the son of Micah.

Life Lesson

A messenger's father is named in Josiah's enquiry.
