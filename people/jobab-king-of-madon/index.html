Jobab, King of Madon

Jobab was king of Madon when he joined Jabin of Hazor against Israel at the waters of Merom.

Key Story

Jobab marched with the northern kings and fell with them when Joshua struck the host at Merom.

Life Lesson

Even a single mention means God did not overlook him.
