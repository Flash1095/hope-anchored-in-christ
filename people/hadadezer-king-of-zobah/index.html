Hadadezer, King of Zobah

Hadadezer, king of Zobah, was the lord from whom Rezon fled when David's wars broke his power, and whose servant later troubled Solomon from Damascus.

Key Story

Hadadezer, lord of Rezon.

Life Lesson

Fallen lords leave behind servants who remember the fall.
