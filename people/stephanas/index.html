Stephanas

Stephanas was one of the few Paul baptized at Corinth, the firstfruits of Achaia, whose household devoted itself to the ministry of the saints and who, with Fortunatus and Achaicus, refreshed Paul's spirit.

Key Story

Life Lesson

A household devoted to serving the saints refreshes the whole church.
