Hezekiah, Son of Amariah

Hezekiah, son of Amariah, is the earliest named ancestor in Zephaniah's genealogy (Zephaniah 1:1). He is NOT King Hezekiah of Judah: the genealogy runs Zephaniah–Cushi–Gedaliah–Amariah–Hezekiah, and this Hezekiah lived generations after the king whose name he shared.

Key Story

Zephaniah traced his line back to a Hezekiah who was his great-great-grandfather — a different man from the reforming king of Judah.

Life Lesson

Names repeat; people don't. Zephaniah's Hezekiah is a warning against assuming every familiar name is the famous one.
