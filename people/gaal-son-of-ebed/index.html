Gaal, Son of Ebed

Name
Gaal, Son of Ebed
Role
Leader of Shechem's Revolt
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Gaal, son of Ebed, led a revolt against Abimelech at Shechem, boasting he would remove him, but was defeated and expelled by Zebul.
Key Story
Gaal came to Shechem with his brethren, won the men's confidence at vintage time, and challenged Abimelech; Zebul warned the king, who routed Gaal at the gate and chased him out.
Key Verses
Judges 9:26-29; Judges 9:35-41
Related Books
Judges
Related People
Abimelech, Son of Gideon; Zebul; Ebed, Father of Gaal
Life Lesson
A boast at harvest time withered by the city gate.
