Nahash, King of Ammon

Nahash, king of Ammon, besieged Jabesh-gilead and demanded every man's right eye as terms of surrender. Saul's rescue of the city launched his kingship. Nahash later died, and David showed kindness to his son Hanun for Nahash's earlier kindness to him.

Key Story

Nahash's cruel terms at Jabesh-gilead gave Saul his first victory — and David later repaid an old kindness to his son.

Life Lesson

Cruelty invites judgment, but an old kindness is still remembered and repaid.
