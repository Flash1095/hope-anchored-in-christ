Manasseh, King of Judah

Name
Manasseh, King of Judah
Role
King of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Manasseh reigned fifty-five years, rebuilt the high places his father had removed, set idols in the temple, and shed innocent blood, filling Jerusalem with evil that hastened Judah's judgment.
Key Story
Manasseh's long idolatrous reign.
Key Verses
2 Kings 21:1; 2 Kings 21:18; Jeremiah 15:4; 1 Chronicles 3:13; 2 Chronicles 33:1; 2 Chronicles 33:12; 2 Chronicles 33:15
Related Books
2 Kings; Jeremiah; 2 Chronicles; 1 Chronicles
Related People
King Hezekiah; Hephzibah, Mother of Manasseh; Amon, King of Judah; King Josiah
Life Lesson
A father's faith is not inherited: Hezekiah's son undid his father's reforms in a single reign.
