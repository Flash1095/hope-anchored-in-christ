Onesimus

Onesimus was Philemon's servant who ran away, met Paul in his bonds, and became Paul's son in the faith; Paul sent him back to Philemon no longer as a servant but above a servant, a brother beloved, and names him to the Colossians as one of their own.

Key Story

Life Lesson

The gospel makes the unprofitable profitable; no past bars a person from brotherhood.
