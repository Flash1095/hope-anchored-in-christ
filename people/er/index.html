Er

Er was the firstborn son of Judah by the daughter of Shuah, and the first husband of Tamar.

Key Story

Er was wicked in the sight of the LORD, and the LORD put him to death in Canaan before the family went to Egypt.

Life Lesson

A firstborn's place is no guarantee: what matters is a heart turned toward God.
