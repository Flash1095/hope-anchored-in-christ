Zithri

Zithri was a son of Uzziel and a grandson of Kohath, named in the genealogy of Moses' tribe.

Key Story

Zithri is named with his brothers Mishael and Elzaphan among Uzziel's sons.

Life Lesson

God's story keeps moving through families we know almost nothing about — and He knows them all.
