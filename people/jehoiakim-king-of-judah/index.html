Jehoiakim, King of Judah

Jehoiakim, born Eliakim, was set on the throne by Pharaoh Necho, taxed Judah to pay Egypt, served Nebuchadnezzar three years and then rebelled, and died as Babylon closed in.

Key Story

Jehoiakim taxes Judah, serves Babylon, then rebels.

Life Lesson

A king who taxes God's people to pay foreign masters has already surrendered.
