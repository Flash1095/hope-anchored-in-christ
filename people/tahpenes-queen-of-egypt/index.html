Tahpenes, Queen of Egypt

Queen of Egypt

Summary
Tahpenes, queen of Egypt, was Pharaoh's wife, whose sister was given to Hadad the Edomite, and who weaned Hadad's son Genubath in Pharaoh's house.
Key Story
Tahpenes, queen of Egypt, and Genubath.
Key Verses
1 Kings 11:19
Related Books

Related People
Hadad, Prince of Edom; Genubath, Son of Hadad
Life Lesson
A queen's household sheltered the very child who would trouble Israel.
