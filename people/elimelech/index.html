Summary
Elimelech was a Bethlehemite who moved his family to Moab during a famine in Judah. He died there, leaving Naomi widowed in a foreign land with two sons who also died.
Key Story
Seeking bread in Moab, Elimelech's family found loss instead: his sons married Moabite women, and all three men died. His decision set in motion Ruth's journey back to Bethlehem with Naomi.
Key Verses
Ruth 1:1-5

Life Lesson
Elimelech's story begins with a desperate move for survival and ends in grief — yet God was already preparing Ruth and Boaz. Even our most fearful decisions cannot outrun God's redemption.
