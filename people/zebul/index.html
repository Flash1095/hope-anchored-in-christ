Conquest and Judges
Summary
Zebul, ruler of Shechem under Abimelech, warned Abimelech of Gaal's revolt and drove Gaal from the city.
Key Story
When Gaal boasted in Shechem, Zebul sent word to Abimelech, counselled him to lie in wait, and thrust Gaal out through the gate.
Key Verses
Judges 9:28-41
