Shaphan, Scribe

Shaphan the scribe read the found book of the law before Josiah, and went with the king's messengers to inquire of Huldah.

Key Story

Shaphan reads the book before Josiah.

Life Lesson

The scribe who reads God's word aloud becomes its first hearer.
