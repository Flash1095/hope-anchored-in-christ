Absalom

Name
Absalom
Role
Son of David
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Absalom was David's handsome son whose rebellion seized the throne and drove his father from Jerusalem. His pride and bitterness ended in a tragic death that broke David's heart.
Key Story
Absalom stole the hearts of Israel at the city gate for years before openly rebelling against his father the king.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 15:6; 2 Samuel 18:33
Related Books
2 Samuel
Related People
King David; King Solomon
Life Lesson
Absalom warns us how unchecked bitterness grows into rebellion. Guard your heart, seek reconciliation early, and let God heal wounds before they harden.
Related Places
Jerusalem; Hebron
