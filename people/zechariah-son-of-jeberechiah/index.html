Zechariah, Son of Jeberechiah

Zechariah, son of Jeberechiah, was one of the two faithful witnesses Isaiah took when he recorded the prophecy of Maher-shalal-hash-baz.

Key Story

Isaiah recorded the prophecy on a great roll with Zechariah and Uriah the priest as faithful witnesses.

Life Lesson

A faithful witness does not need a large part — only a true one.
