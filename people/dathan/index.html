Dathan

Name
Dathan
Role
Reubenite Rebel
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Dathan, son of Eliab, joined Korah's rebellion against Moses and Aaron and refused to come up when Moses summoned him.
Key Story
Dathan stood at the door of his tent with his family as the ground opened and swallowed the rebels alive.
Key Verses
Numbers 16:1-33; 26:9-10
Related Books
Numbers
Related People
Abiram; Eliab, Son of Pallu; Korah, Son of Izhar; Moses; Aaron
Life Lesson
Refusing God's appointed leadership is refusing God Himself.
