Summary
Sherezer was sent with Regemmelech from Bethel to Jerusalem to pray before the LORD and ask the priests whether the fasts of the exile should continue (Zechariah 7:2). The question he carried drew from Zechariah a searching answer about empty fasting and true justice.
Key Story
Sherezer, sent from Bethel to ask whether the exile's fasts should go on, carried a question that exposed the difference between ritual and repentance.

Life Lesson
Sherezer asked the right question in the wrong spirit — and God answered deeper than he asked. Bring God your rituals; He will ask for your heart.
