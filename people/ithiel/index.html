Ithiel

Ithiel was one of the two men to whom Agur addressed his words in Proverbs 30, named alongside Ucal in its opening verse.

Key Story

Agur's oracle was spoken to Ithiel and Ucal; nothing more is told of Ithiel, but his name is preserved as a hearer of wisdom.

Life Lesson

Ithiel's place in Scripture honours the hearer of wisdom, not only its speaker.
