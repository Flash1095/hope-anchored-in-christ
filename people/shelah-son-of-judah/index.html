Shelah, Son of Judah

Name
Shelah, Son of Judah
Role
Son of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Shelah was the third son of Judah by the daughter of Shuah, kept back from marrying Tamar after his brothers died.
Key Story
Shelah survived his brothers and fathered a line of his own, though Judah's promise to give him to Tamar was never kept.
Key Verses
Genesis 38:11-14; Genesis 46:12
