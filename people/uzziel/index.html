Summary
Uzziel was a son of Kohath and a grandson of Levi, and the father of Mishael, Elzaphan, and Zithri.
Key Story
Uzziel's sons Mishael and Elzaphan were the ones called to carry Nadab and Abihu out of the sanctuary after their deaths.

Exodus 6:18-22
