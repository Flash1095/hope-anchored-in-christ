Stephen

Name
Stephen
Role
Deacon; First Martyr
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Stephen was one of the first seven deacons, full of faith and the Holy Spirit, who performed great wonders and preached a sweeping sermon before the Sanhedrin. He became the church's first martyr, forgiving his killers as he died.
Key Story
As stones fell, Stephen looked up and saw Jesus standing at the right hand of God, and prayed, Lord, do not hold this sin against them.
Key Verses
Acts 6:5; Acts 7:59-60; Acts 7:55
Related Books
Acts
Related People
Paul; Simon Peter
Life Lesson
Stephen's dying prayer echoed Jesus' own and helped prepare the heart of a watching Pharisee named Saul. Your faithful witness, even in suffering, may change someone you will meet in heaven.
