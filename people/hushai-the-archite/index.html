Hushai the Archite

Name
Hushai the Archite
Role
Friend of David; Double Agent
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Hushai the Archite, David's friend, stayed in Jerusalem when David fled and pretended to serve Absalom. His counsel — to wait and gather all Israel — overruled Ahithophel's plan for a swift strike and saved David's life and kingdom.
Key Story
Hushai's slow counsel defeated Ahithophel's swift counsel and saved David.

2 Samuel 15:32; 2 Samuel 16:16; 2 Samuel 17:14; 2 Samuel 17:15; 1 Chronicles 27:33
