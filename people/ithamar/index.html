Ithamar

Name
Ithamar
Role
Son of Aaron
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Ithamar was the youngest son of Aaron and Elisheba, and one of the priests of Israel.
Key Story
Ithamar oversaw the Levites' work on the tabernacle, keeping account of the gold, silver, and bronze used in its making.
Key Verses
Exodus 6:23; Exodus 28:1; Exodus 38:21
Related Books
Exodus
Related People

Life Lesson
Faithful stewardship of holy things is holy work itself.
