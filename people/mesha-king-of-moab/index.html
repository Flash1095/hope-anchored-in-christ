Mesha, King of Moab

Mesha, king of Moab, a sheepmaster who paid Israel a hundred thousand lambs and as many rams, rebelled after Ahab's death, and in desperation offered his firstborn son on the wall of Kir-hareseth.

Key Story

Mesha rebels and offers his son on the wall.

Life Lesson

Desperation that sacrifices a child shows how far rebellion runs.
