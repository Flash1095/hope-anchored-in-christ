Hoham, King of Hebron

Hoham was king of Hebron and one of the five kings who allied against Gibeon and were defeated by Joshua.

Key Story

Hoham joined Adoni-zedek against Gibeon, fled to the cave at Makkedah, and was hanged with the other four kings.

Life Lesson

Alliances against God end in the same cave.
