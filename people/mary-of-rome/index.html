Mary of Rome

Name
Mary of Rome
Role
Labourer in the Roman church
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Mary was a Christian woman in Rome whom Paul greets in Romans 16:6 for bestowing much labour on the apostles. She is distinct from Mary, the mother of Jesus.
