Amnon

Amnon, David's firstborn by Ahinoam, lusted after his half-sister Tamar, tricked her into his chamber, and assaulted her, then hated her and cast her out. Two years later Absalom had him murdered at a sheep-shearing feast.

Key Story

Amnon's lust for Tamar destroyed his sister, his standing, and finally his life.

Life Lesson

Lust that takes what it wants and then hates what it took destroys everyone it touches.
