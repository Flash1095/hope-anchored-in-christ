Issachar

Name
Issachar
Role
Son of Jacob
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Issachar was the ninth son of Jacob, the fifth by Leah, and the father of Tola, Phuvah, Job, and Shimron.
Key Story
Issachar's tribe later became known for understanding the times and knowing what Israel should do.
Key Verses
Genesis 30:17-18; Genesis 46:13; Genesis 49:14-15
