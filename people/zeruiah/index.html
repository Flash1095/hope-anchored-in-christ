Zeruiah

Name
Zeruiah

Old
Timeline Era

Summary
Zeruiah is named as the mother of Joab, Abishai, and Asahel, David's three nephews and fiercest warriors. Her sister Abigail was the mother of Amasa, whom Joab murdered.
Key Story
Zeruiah mothered the three brothers who commanded David's wars.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 2:13; 2 Samuel 2:18; 2 Samuel 17:25; 1 Chronicles 2:16
Related Books
2 Samuel; 1 Chronicles
Related People
Joab; Abishai; Asahel; Abigail, Daughter of Nahash; Jesse
Life Lesson
A mother's sons can become a king's sword — for good and for ill.
