Amariah, Chief Priest

Amariah the chief priest was set by Jehoshaphat over all matters of the LORD in the Jerusalem court of judges.

Key Story

Jehoshaphat charged that Amariah the chief priest is over you in all matters of the LORD, and Zebadiah for all the king's matters.

Life Lesson

Judgment belongs to the LORD, so judges must act in His fear, faithfully, with a perfect heart.
