Summary
Sihon was king of the Amorites at Heshbon; he refused Israel passage and was defeated, and his land became Israel's first possession east of the Jordan.
Key Story
Sihon gathered his people and fought Israel at Jahaz, and Israel took all his cities from the Arnon to the Jabbok.
