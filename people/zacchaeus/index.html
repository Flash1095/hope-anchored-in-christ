Life of Christ
Summary
Zacchaeus was a wealthy chief tax collector in Jericho, short in stature, who climbed a sycamore tree to see Jesus. Jesus invited Himself to Zacchaeus's house, and salvation transformed his finances and heart that day.
Key Story
Zacchaeus announced he would give half his possessions to the poor and repay anyone he had cheated four times over.
Key Verses
Luke 19:8-10; Luke 19:5
Related Books
Luke
Related People
Jesus
Life Lesson
Jesus said salvation had come to Zacchaeus's house because the Son of Man came to seek and save the lost. If you feel small, hidden, or far off, Jesus is calling you by name to come down and come home.
