Eshcol

Eshcol was a brother of Mamre and a confederate of Abram in the hill country near Hebron.

Key Story

Eshcol joined Abram's rescue of Lot, and Abram insisted Eshcol and his brothers keep their share of the recovered goods.

Life Lesson

Good neighbors matter: Eshcol stood with Abram when it counted.
