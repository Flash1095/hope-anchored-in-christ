Herodias

Life of Christ
Summary
Herodias had left Herod Philip to marry his brother Herod Antipas, a union John the Baptist denounced as unlawful. She nursed a grudge against John and used her daughter's dance to demand his head.
Key Story
Herodias held her grudge until a birthday feast gave her the prophet's head on a charger.
Key Verses
Matthew 14:3; Mark 6:17
Related Books
Matthew; Mark
Related People
John the Baptist; Herod Antipas
Life Lesson
A grudge kept long enough finds its occasion. Herodias shows how resentment, nursed in private, kills in public.
