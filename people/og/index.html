Og

Og was king of Bashan, a giant of the remnant of the Rephaim, who was defeated by Israel at Edrei.

Key Story

Og came out against Israel with all his people, and Israel struck him down until none remained, taking sixty walled cities of Bashan.

Life Lesson

No giant is too big for the God who has already promised the land.
