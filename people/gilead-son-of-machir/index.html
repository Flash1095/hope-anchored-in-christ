Gilead, Son of Machir

Summary
Gilead, son of Machir son of Manasseh, gave his name to the land of Gilead east of the Jordan.
Key Story
Gilead's descendants, including Zelophehad and his daughters, inherited the hills that still bear his name.

Numbers 26:29; 27:1
