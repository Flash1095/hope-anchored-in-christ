Role

Testament

Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Samuel was the last judge and a great prophet who anointed Israel's first two kings, Saul and David. Dedicated before birth, he heard God's voice as a boy and spoke for God all his life.
Key Story
As a child sleeping in the tabernacle, Samuel answered the Lord's call with, Speak, for your servant is listening.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 3:10; 1 Samuel 7:12; Hebrews 11:32
Related Books
1 Samuel; Hebrews
Related People
Hannah; Eli; King Saul; King David
Life Lesson
Samuel set up a stone and called it Ebenezer — thus far the Lord has helped us. Remembering God's past help is how we anchor hope for what lies ahead.
