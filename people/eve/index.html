Eve

Name
Eve
Role
First Woman; Mother of All Living
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Eve was the first woman, created by God as Adam's companion and helper. Scripture calls her the mother of all the living.
Key Story
Deceived by the serpent, Eve ate the forbidden fruit and gave it to Adam, yet she also received the first promise of a Redeemer to come.
Key Verses
Genesis 2:22; Genesis 3:20; Genesis 3:15; 2 Corinthians 11:3
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Adam; Cain; Abel; Seth
Life Lesson
God did not abandon Eve after her failure; He clothed her in grace and spoke hope over her future. Your mistakes are not the end of your story either.
