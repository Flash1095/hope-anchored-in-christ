Tilgath-pilneser, King of Assyria

Name
Tilgath-pilneser, King of Assyria
Role
King of Assyria
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Tilgath-pilneser, king of Assyria, came to Ahaz when Ahaz sent for help, and distressed him but strengthened him not, though Ahaz had stripped the LORD's house and the king's house to pay him.
Key Story
Ahaz gave the king of Assyria a portion out of the house of the LORD and the king's house, but he helped him not.
Key Verses
1 Chronicles 5:26; 1 Chronicles 5:6; 2 Chronicles 28:20; 2 Chronicles 28:21
Related Books
1 Chronicles; 2 Chronicles
Related People
Ahaz, King of Judah
Life Lesson
The mention of Tilgath-pilneser in 1 Chronicles places this person within the story of God's people under David and his kingdom.
