Hamutal, Mother of Jehoahaz and Zedekiah

Hamutal, daughter of Jeremiah of Libnah, was the mother of two kings: Jehoahaz, deposed after three months, and Zedekiah, Judah's last king.

Key Story

Hamutal, mother of two kings.

Life Lesson

One mother bore both a three-month king and the last king of Judah.
