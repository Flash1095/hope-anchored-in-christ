Mamre

Testament

Summary
Mamre was an Amorite chief who lived by the oaks of Mamre near Hebron, and a confederate of Abram.
Key Story
Mamre's oaks gave the place its name, and Mamre himself joined Abram's pursuit to rescue Lot.
Key Verses
Genesis 14:13; Genesis 14:24
