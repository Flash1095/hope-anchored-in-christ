Mamre

Name
Mamre
Role
Ally of Abraham
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Mamre was an Amorite chief who lived by the oaks of Mamre near Hebron, and a confederate of Abram.
Key Story
Mamre's oaks gave the place its name, and Mamre himself joined Abram's pursuit to rescue Lot.
Key Verses
Genesis 14:13; Genesis 14:24
Related Books
Genesis
Related People
Abraham; Eshcol; Aner
Life Lesson
Good neighbors matter: Mamre stood with Abram when it counted.
