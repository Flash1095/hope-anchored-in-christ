Nadab, King of Israel

Nadab, son of Jeroboam, reigned two years and walked in his father's sin, and was slain by Baasha at Gibbethon while Israel besieged the Philistines.

Key Story

Nadab follows Jeroboam's sin and is slain by Baasha.

Life Lesson

A son who inherits only his father's sins inherits his father's judgment too.
