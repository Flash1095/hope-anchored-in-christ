Zechariah, Father of Jahaziel

Key Story
He appears in Jahaziel's genealogy: Jahaziel the son of Zechariah, the son of Benaiah, the son of Jeiel, the son of Mattaniah, a Levite of the sons of Asaph.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 20:14
Life Lesson
Zechariah is named in Scripture only as Jahaziel's father, yet his name stands in the line God used at Judah's crisis. A quiet place in the story is still a place in the story.
