Rehoboam

Name
Rehoboam
Role
King of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Rehoboam was Solomon's son whose harsh answer to the people split the kingdom into Judah in the south and Israel in the north. He reigned seventeen years over Judah in Jerusalem.
Key Story
Rejecting his father's counsellors, Rehoboam promised heavier burdens, and ten tribes broke away under Jeroboam.
Key Verses
1 Kings 12:13-16; 2 Chronicles 10:13-16
Related Books
1 Kings; 2 Chronicles
Related People
King Solomon; Jeroboam
Life Lesson
Rehoboam lost a kingdom by refusing humble counsel. Seeking wise, godly advice — and listening to it — can spare us losses pride would cause.
