Summary
Gideon was an unlikely judge whom God called a mighty warrior while he hid from Midianite raiders. God reduced his army to three hundred men so Israel would know the victory was the Lord's.
Key Story
With torches, jars, and trumpets, Gideon's three hundred men surrounded the Midianite camp at night, and God threw the enemy into confusion.
Key Verses
Judges 6:12; Judges 7:7; Hebrews 11:32
Related Books
Judges; Hebrews
Related People
Deborah; Samuel
Life Lesson
God called Gideon mighty while Gideon felt weakest — God names us by what He will make us, not by what we feel. Your small army is enough when God fights for you.
