Gabriel

Name
Gabriel
Role
Angel; Messenger of God
Testament
Both
Timeline Era
Exile
Summary
Gabriel is the angel who explains Daniel's visions and who announces the births of John the Baptist and of Jesus. Daniel calls him 'the man Gabriel'; Luke calls him the angel who stands in the presence of God. Nothing beyond these biblical appearances is asserted here.
Key Story
Announcing John and Jesus
Key Verses
Daniel 8:16; Daniel 9:21; Luke 1:19; Luke 1:26
Related Books
Daniel; Luke
Related People
Daniel; Zechariah, Father of John the Baptist; Mary, Mother of Jesus
Life Lesson
God's messages come to ordinary people in ordinary places.
