James, Son of Zebedee

James, brother of John and one of the Sons of Thunder, belonged to Jesus' inner circle, witnessing the Transfiguration and Gethsemane. He was the first apostle martyred, killed by Herod's sword.

Key Story

James was present when Jesus was transfigured in glory on the mountain with Moses and Elijah.

Life Lesson

James drank the cup of suffering he had once claimed so confidently. Faithfulness to Jesus may cost us dearly, but those who lose their lives for Him find them forever.
