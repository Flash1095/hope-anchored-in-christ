Sosthenes

Name
Sosthenes
Role
Ruler of the Corinthian Synagogue
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Sosthenes was a ruler of the synagogue at Corinth who was beaten by the Greeks before Gallio's judgment seat after Gallio dismissed the Jews' case against Paul. He is traditionally identified with the Sosthenes who later greets the Corinthians with Paul, though Acts does not say so.
Key Story
Sosthenes was beaten before the judgment seat while Gallio cared for none of these things.
Key Verses
Acts 18:17; 1 Corinthians 1:1
Related Books
Acts; 1 Corinthians
Related People
Paul; Crispus; Gallio
Life Lesson
Gallio's indifference left Sosthenes bleeding at the tribunal. The world often shrugs at the church's wounds.
Related Places
Corinth
