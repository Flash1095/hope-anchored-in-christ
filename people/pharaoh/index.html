Summary
Pharaoh is the title of Egypt's king, not one man. The Bible follows the office across episodes: the pharaoh who took Sarah in Abraham's day, the pharaoh who raised Joseph and then the pharaoh who enslaved Israel, the pharaoh of the Exodus who hardened his heart against Moses, and later pharaohs such as Solomon's father-in-law. Most holders are unnamed in the text. Popular identifications of the Exodus pharaoh (for example Ramesses II or Merneptah) are scholarly proposals, not statements of the text, and are not asserted here. Named Egyptian kings in the text — So, Pharaoh-nechoh, and Hophra — have their own profiles.
