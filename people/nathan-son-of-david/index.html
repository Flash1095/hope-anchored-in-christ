Nathan, Son of David

Name
Nathan, Son of David
Role
Son of David
Testament

Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Nathan, son of David, was born at Jerusalem and named among the sons born to him there. He is distinct from Nathan the prophet who confronted David.
Key Story
Nathan son of David was born at Jerusalem, sharing a name with the prophet who rebuked his father.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 5:14; 1 Chronicles 3:5
Related Books
2 Samuel; 1 Chronicles
Related People
King David
Life Lesson
Sharing a prophet's name does not make a prince a prophet.
