Micaiah, Son of Imla

Name
Micaiah, Son of Imla
Role
Prophet
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Micaiah, son of Imla, was the prophet Jehoshaphat insisted on consulting before Ramoth-gilead; he foretold Israel scattered as sheep without a shepherd and Ahab's fall, and was imprisoned for it.
Key Story
Micaiah saw the LORD on His throne and a lying spirit sent to entice Ahab, and declared that if Ahab returned in peace, the LORD had not spoken by him.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 18:7; 2 Chronicles 18:16; 2 Chronicles 18:27
Related Books
2 Chronicles
Related People
King Jehoshaphat; King Ahab
Life Lesson
A true prophet speaks what God says, even when four hundred others say the opposite.
