Summary
Doeg the Edomite is named in the title of Psalm 52, the psalm David wrote after Doeg told Saul that David had come to the house of Ahimelech.
Key Story
Doeg's report to Saul set in motion the slaughter of the priests of Nob, and Psalm 52 answers his treachery with a warning about the fate of those who trust in riches and lies.
Key Verses
Psalm 52:1

Life Lesson
Doeg is a warning that words can kill — informing on the innocent for favour is a betrayal God judges.
