Abimelech, Son of Gideon

Name
Abimelech, Son of Gideon
Role
King of Shechem
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Abimelech, son of Gideon by his Shechemite concubine, murdered his seventy brothers, was made king by the men of Shechem, and died at Thebez when a woman dropped a millstone on his head. He is distinct from Abimelech, king of Gerar, in Genesis.
Key Story
Abimelech hired vain men with temple silver, slew his brothers on one stone, was crowned at Shechem, burned the tower of Shechem, and was mortally wounded at Thebez by a piece of a millstone, asking his armourbearer to finish him lest men say a woman slew him.
Key Verses
Judges 8:31; Judges 9:1-6; Judges 9:50-55

Life Lesson
