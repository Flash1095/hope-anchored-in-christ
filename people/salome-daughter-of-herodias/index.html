Salome, Daughter of Herodias

Name
Salome, Daughter of Herodias
Role
Daughter of Herodias
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
The daughter of Herodias is unnamed in the Gospels: Matthew and Mark say only that Herodias's daughter danced before Herod and, prompted by her mother, asked for John the Baptist's head on a charger. The name Salome comes from the historian Josephus, not from the Gospel text, and is stated here as tradition, not as a biblical name.
Key Story
John the Baptist's death
Key Verses
Mark 6:22; Mark 6:28; Matthew 14:6; Matthew 14:11

Related People

Life Lesson
A moment's dance can carry a lifetime's guilt — be careful what you ask for.
