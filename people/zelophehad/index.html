Zelophehad

Name
Zelophehad
Role
Father of Five Daughters
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Exodus and Wilderness
Summary
Zelophehad, son of Hepher, died in the wilderness leaving no sons, and his five daughters appealed to Moses for their father's inheritance.
Key Story
The daughters of Zelophehad stood before Moses, Eleazar, and the whole congregation, and God ruled that daughters inherit when there is no son.
Key Verses
Numbers 27:1-11; 36:1-12
