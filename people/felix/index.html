Felix

Name
Felix
Role
Roman Governor of Judea
Testament
New
Timeline Era

Summary
Felix was the Roman governor who kept Paul in custody at Caesarea for two years, hearing him often and hoping for a bribe, and who trembled at Paul's preaching on righteousness, temperance, and judgment to come, yet left Paul bound to please the Jews.
Key Story
Felix heard Paul often, trembled at judgment to come, and left him bound when a successor came.
Key Verses
Acts 23:24; Acts 24:27
Related Books
Acts
Related People
Paul; Drusilla; Tertullus
Life Lesson
Felix trembled and postponed, and postponement became his verdict. A convenient season never came.
