Micaiah, Son of Imlah

Name
Micaiah, Son of Imlah
Role
Prophet
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Micaiah, son of Imlah, was the one prophet Ahab hated because he never prophesied good of him; he saw the LORD send a lying spirit to Ahab's prophets and foretold Ahab's death at Ramoth-gilead, and was imprisoned on bread and water.
Key Story
Micaiah alone foretells Ahab's death at Ramoth-gilead.
Key Verses
1 Kings 22:8; 1 Kings 22:28
Related Books

Related People
King Ahab; King Jehoshaphat; Zedekiah, Son of Chenaanah; Imlah, Father of Micaiah
Life Lesson
One true voice against four hundred is still the majority when God has spoken.
