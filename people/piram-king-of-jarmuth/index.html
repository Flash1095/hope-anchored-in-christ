Piram, King of Jarmuth

King of Jarmuth

Conquest and Judges
Summary
Piram was king of Jarmuth and one of the five kings who allied against Gibeon and were defeated by Joshua.
Key Story
Piram joined Adoni-zedek against Gibeon, fled to the cave at Makkedah, and was hanged with the other four kings.
Key Verses
Joshua 10:3-27
Related Books
Joshua
Related People
Adoni-zedek, King of Jerusalem; Joshua
Life Lesson
Alliances against God end in the same cave.
