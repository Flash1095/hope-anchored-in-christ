Abraham

Abraham is the father of the nation of Israel and of all who believe. Called from Ur, he trusted God's promises of land, descendants, and blessing to all nations.

Key Story

God called Abraham to leave his homeland for an unknown land, and later Abraham offered his promised son Isaac on Mount Moriah in staggering obedience.

Life Lesson

Abraham believed God against all visible evidence, and God counted it as righteousness. Hold on — the God who kept His promise to Abraham will keep His promises to you.
