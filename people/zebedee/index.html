Zebedee

Name
Zebedee
Role
Fisherman; Father of James and John
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Zebedee was a Galilean fisherman and the father of the apostles James and John. He was mending nets with his sons when Jesus called them, and they left the boat and their father to follow Him.
Key Story
Zebedee watched his sons walk away from the family boat to follow a travelling teacher.
Key Verses
Matthew 4:21; Mark 1:19
Related Books
Matthew; Mark
Related People
James, Son of Zebedee; John the Apostle; Jesus
Life Lesson
A father's nets cannot hold sons God has called. Zebedee's loss was the church's gain.
Related Places
Galilee
