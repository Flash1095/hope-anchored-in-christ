Genubath, Son of Hadad

Genubath was the son of Hadad the Edomite by Pharaoh's sister-in-law, weaned by Queen Tahpenes and raised in Pharaoh's household among Pharaoh's sons.

Key Story

Genubath raised in Pharaoh's house.

Life Lesson

A child raised between two kingdoms belongs fully to neither.
