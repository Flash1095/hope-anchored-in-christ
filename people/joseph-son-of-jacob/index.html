Joseph, Son of Jacob

Name
Joseph, Son of Jacob
Role
Dreamer; Governor of Egypt
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Joseph was Jacob's favoured son, sold into slavery by his brothers, who rose to govern Egypt and save nations during famine. His life is a masterpiece of God's providence.
Key Story
Betrayed, imprisoned, and forgotten, Joseph interpreted Pharaoh's dreams and was raised in a single day from prison to palace.
Key Verses
Genesis 41:41-43; Genesis 50:20; Acts 7:9-10
Related Books
Genesis; Acts; Hebrews
Related People
Jacob; Rachel; Judah; Benjamin
Life Lesson
What Joseph's brothers meant for evil, God meant for good. The pit and the prison were not detours — they were the road to the palace and the saving of many lives.
Related Places
Canaan; Egypt; Goshen
