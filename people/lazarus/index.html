Lazarus

Name
Lazarus
Role
Friend of Jesus
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Lazarus was Jesus' beloved friend in Bethany whose death after four days became the stage for one of Jesus' greatest signs. At Jesus' word — Lazarus, come out — he walked from the tomb alive.
Key Story
Jesus wept at Lazarus's tomb, prayed aloud, and cried with a loud voice, Lazarus, come out, and the dead man emerged bound in grave clothes.
Key Verses
John 11:43-44; John 11:35; John 12:10-11
Related Books
John
Related People
Martha; Mary of Bethany; Jesus
Life Lesson
Jesus wept even knowing resurrection was minutes away — He enters our grief before He overturns it. If He called Lazarus out of four days dead, no situation of yours is beyond His voice.
