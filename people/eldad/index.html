Eldad

Eldad was one of the seventy elders on whom the Spirit rested in the wilderness; though he stayed in the camp, he prophesied there.

Key Story

When the Spirit fell on the elders, Eldad prophesied in the camp, and Moses refused to stop him, wishing all the LORD's people were prophets.

Life Lesson

God's Spirit is not confined to the place where we expect Him.
