Benjamin

Benjamin was Jacob's youngest son, born to Rachel as she died on the road to Bethlehem. His tribe later produced King Saul and the apostle Paul, and guarded Jerusalem's gates.

Key Story

Jacob refused to send Benjamin to Egypt, fearing another loss like Joseph's, until Judah pledged his own life for the boy. Joseph's testing of his brothers ended when Benjamin was safe and the family was restored.

Life Lesson

Benjamin was the child of both sorrow and belovedness — his mother died naming him pain, his father renamed him son of my right hand. God can rename what grief first called us.
