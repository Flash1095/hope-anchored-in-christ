Summary
Cornelius was a devout centurion of the Italian band at Caesarea who feared God, gave alms, and prayed continually. An angel sent him to Peter, whose visit to his house opened the church to the Gentiles, as the Holy Spirit fell on his household before baptism.
Key Story
Cornelius sent for Peter, and while Peter was still speaking, the Holy Spirit fell on his Gentile household.
