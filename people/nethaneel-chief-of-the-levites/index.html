Summary
Nethaneel, a chief of the Levites and brother of Cononiah, gave with the other chiefs five thousand small cattle and five hundred oxen for Josiah's Passover.
Key Story
Conaniah, Shemaiah and Nethaneel, his brethren, gave unto the Levites for passover offerings.
