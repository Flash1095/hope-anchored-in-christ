Ahaziah, King of Israel

Ahaziah, son of Ahab, reigned two years, walked in his father's ways and served Baal, and after falling through a lattice in Samaria he sent to inquire of Baal-zebub and died by Elijah's word.

Key Story

Ahaziah falls, inquires of Baal-zebub, and dies.

Life Lesson

When trouble comes, the god a man inquires of reveals the god he serves.
