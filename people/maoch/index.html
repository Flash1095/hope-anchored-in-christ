Maoch

Maoch was king of Gath before his son Achish, in whose reign David twice sought refuge among the Philistines.

Key Story

Maoch's son inherited a throne that would shelter Israel's future king.

Life Lesson

A father's throne can become, unknowingly, a refuge for God's purposes.
