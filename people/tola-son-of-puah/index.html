Tola, Son of Puah

Timeline Era
Conquest and Judges
Summary
Tola, son of Puah, son of Dodo, a man of Issachar, judged Israel twenty-three years and dwelt in Shamir in Mount Ephraim. He is distinct from Tola, son of Issachar, in Genesis.
Key Story
After Abimelech, Tola arose to defend Israel, judged twenty-three years from Shamir in Mount Ephraim, and died and was buried there.
