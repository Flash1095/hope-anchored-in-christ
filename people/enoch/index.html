Enoch

Name
Enoch
Role
Prophet; Man Who Walked With God
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Enoch was a descendant of Adam who walked closely with God for 300 years. He never tasted death, for God took him directly to heaven.
Key Story
After the birth of his son Methuselah, Enoch walked with God in steady fellowship until God took him home.
Key Verses
Genesis 5:24; Hebrews 11:5; Jude 1:14-15
Related Books
Genesis; Hebrews; Jude
Related People
Adam; Methuselah; Noah
Life Lesson
Walking with God is not about one dramatic moment but daily, faithful steps. A quiet life close to God is a powerful witness that endures.
