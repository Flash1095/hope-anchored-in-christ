Simon Peter

Name
Simon Peter
Role
Apostle
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Peter was a Galilean fisherman called by Jesus to become a fisher of men and a rock of the early church. Impulsive and bold, he denied Jesus, was restored by the risen Lord, and preached at Pentecost.
Key Story
After denying Jesus three times, Peter was restored by the risen Christ beside a charcoal fire with three questions: Do you love Me? — then sent to feed His sheep.
Key Verses
Matthew 16:18; John 21:15-17; Acts 2:14; 1 Peter 5:10
Related Books
Matthew; Acts; 1 Peter; 2 Peter
Related People
Jesus; Andrew; John the Apostle
Life Lesson
Peter's worst failure became the doorway to his greatest ministry because Jesus pursued him. The God of all grace Himself will restore you after you have suffered a little while.
