Philip the Tetrarch

Name
Philip the Tetrarch
Role
Tetrarch of Ituraea and Trachonitis
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Life of Christ
Summary
Philip the Tetrarch, a son of Herod the Great, ruled the north-eastern territories when John the Baptist began preaching. He is distinct from the apostle Philip and from Philip the Evangelist.
Key Story
Luke names Philip among the rulers of the year the word of God came to John in the wilderness.
Key Verses
Luke 3:1
Related Books
Luke
Related People
Herod the Great; Herod Antipas
Life Lesson
God's story runs on a dated calendar of real rulers. Luke anchors the gospel in history, not myth.
