King Jehoshaphat

Name
King Jehoshaphat
Role
King of Judah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Divided Monarchy
Summary
Jehoshaphat was a faithful king of Judah who sent teachers of the Law throughout his kingdom and sought God in national crisis. His prayer in battle is one of Scripture's great models of trust.
Key Story
Facing a vast invading army, Jehoshaphat prayed, We do not know what to do, but our eyes are on You — and God fought the battle as singers led the army.
Key Verses
2 Chronicles 20:12; 2 Chronicles 20:15; 2 Chronicles 17:3-6; 1 Kings 22:41; 2 Kings 3:11
Related Books

Related People
King Asa; Elijah; King Ahab; Micaiah, Son of Imlah
Life Lesson
'We do not know what to do, but our eyes are on You' is enough. When you run out of answers, fix your eyes on God and let worship lead the way into battle.
