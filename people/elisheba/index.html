Summary
Elisheba was the daughter of Amminadab and the sister of Naashon, and the wife of Aaron. She bore him Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.
Key Story
Elisheba became the mother of Israel's priestly line, standing at the head of the family that would serve in the tabernacle.

Exodus 6:23
