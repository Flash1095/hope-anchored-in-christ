Eliphaz

Name
Eliphaz
Role
Friend of Job; Temanite
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Eliphaz the Temanite was the first of Job's three friends to speak, and the oldest voice in the debate. His counsel began gently but hardened into accusing Job of hidden sin.
Key Story
Eliphaz argued that suffering must follow sin, urging Job to confess and be restored. At the end, God told Eliphaz he had not spoken rightly about Him, and Job prayed for his friend.
Key Verses
Job 2:11; Job 4:7-8; Job 42:7-9
Related Books
Job
Related People
Job; Bildad; Zophar
Life Lesson
Eliphaz warns us that even true-sounding theology can wound when applied without mercy. Before explaining someone's pain, sit with them — and let God, not a formula, have the last word.
