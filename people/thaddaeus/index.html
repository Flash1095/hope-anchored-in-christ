Summary
Thaddaeus, also called Judas son of James, was one of the Twelve apostles who asked Jesus at the Last Supper why He would reveal Himself to the disciples and not to the world. He shared in the apostles' witness after the resurrection.
Key Story
Thaddaeus asked Jesus, Lord, why do You intend to show Yourself to us and not to the world? — prompting Jesus' promise of the Father and Son making their home with believers.
Key Verses
Jude 1:1; John 14:22-23; Matthew 10:3

Life Lesson
Thaddaeus's single recorded question unlocked a beautiful promise: God makes His home with those who love Him. Ask your questions — Jesus answers seekers generously.
