Shemaiah, Priest of Jeshua's Days

Timeline Era

Summary
Shemaiah was a priestly course head in the days of Jeshua son of Jozadak, listed with Joiarib and Jedaiah. NOTE ON IDENTITY: several men named Shemaiah appear in Nehemiah; the text does not say whether this course head is the Shemaiah of the covenant, the dedication singers, or the first company, so this profile covers only the Nehemiah 12:6 course head and does not merge him with any other Shemaiah.
