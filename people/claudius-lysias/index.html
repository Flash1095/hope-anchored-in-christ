Claudius Lysias

Claudius Lysias was the chief captain of the Roman garrison at Jerusalem who rescued Paul from the temple mob, arrested him, and, learning he was a Roman citizen, sent him under guard to Felix at Caesarea with a letter stating the case.

Key Story

Lysias pulled Paul from the mob, learned he was a Roman, and sent him by night to Caesarea with a letter.

Life Lesson

A Roman's letter carried Paul to his next hearing. Lysias did justice without knowing the gospel he protected.
