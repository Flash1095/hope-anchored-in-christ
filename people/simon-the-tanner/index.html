Simon the Tanner

Name
Simon the Tanner
Role
Host of Peter at Joppa
Testament
New
Timeline Era
Early Church
Summary
Simon the Tanner lived at Joppa by the sea, and Peter lodged at his house while the Lord prepared him to receive Cornelius, the Gentile centurion.
Key Story
Peter stayed with Simon the Tanner while a sheet from heaven taught him to call no man unclean.
Key Verses
Acts 9:43
Related Books
Acts
Related People
Simon Peter; Cornelius
Life Lesson
God prepared Peter for the Gentiles in a tanner's house, a trade the law counted unclean. The lesson began before the vision.
