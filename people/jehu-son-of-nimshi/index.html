Jehu, Son of Nimshi

Jehu, son of Nimshi, was anointed by the LORD to cut off the house of Ahab; executing that judgment, he slew the princes of Judah and the sons of Ahaziah's brethren, and then slew Ahaziah himself.

Key Story

When Jehu executed judgment upon the house of Ahab, he found Judah's princes ministering to Ahaziah and slew them, then sought and slew Ahaziah.

Life Lesson

God's judgment on one house can sweep up its allies as well.
