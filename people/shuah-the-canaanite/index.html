Shuah the Canaanite

Name
Shuah the Canaanite
Role
Father of Judah's Wife
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
Primeval / Patriarchs
Summary
Shuah was a Canaanite whose daughter married Judah, and whose grandsons were Er, Onan, and Shelah.
Key Story
Judah married Shuah's daughter in Canaan, and her sons' story became part of the line leading to David.
Key Verses
Genesis 38:2; Genesis 38:12
