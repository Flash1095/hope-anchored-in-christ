Rebekah

Rebekah was Isaac's wife, chosen at a well in Mesopotamia, and the mother of twin sons Jacob and Esau. She received God's word about her sons before they were born.

Key Story

Rebekah showed generous hospitality by watering Abraham's servant's camels, the sign that she was God's chosen wife for Isaac.

Life Lesson

Rebekah's simple act of kindness at a well changed her whole future. God often opens His biggest doors through ordinary, generous obedience.
