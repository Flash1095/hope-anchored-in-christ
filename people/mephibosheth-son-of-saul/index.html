Mephibosheth, Son of Saul

Name
Mephibosheth, Son of Saul
Role
Son of Saul by Rizpah
Testament
Old
Timeline Era
United Monarchy
Summary
Mephibosheth, son of Saul by Rizpah — distinct from Mephibosheth son of Jonathan — was handed to the Gibeonites with his brother Armoni in atonement for Saul's bloodguilt and was hanged at the beginning of barley harvest.
Key Story
Mephibosheth son of Saul, Rizpah's son, was hanged beside his brother for Saul's broken oath.
Key Verses
2 Samuel 21:8
Related Books
2 Samuel
Related People
King Saul; Rizpah, Daughter of Aiah; Armoni, Son of Saul
Life Lesson
Two men can share a name, a father, and a fate — and still be different men.
