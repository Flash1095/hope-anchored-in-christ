Johanan, Son of Careah

Johanan, son of Careah, was one of the captains who came to Gedaliah at Mizpah and warned him against Ishmael's plot.

Key Story

Johanan warns Gedaliah against Ishmael.

Life Lesson

A warning ignored is still a warning given: Johanan did his duty.
