Jehoash, King of Israel

King of Israel
Testament

Summary
Jehoash, also called Joash, son of Jehoahaz, recovered from Ben-Hadad the cities Syria had taken, visited the dying Elisha, and captured Amaziah of Judah at Beth-shemesh.
Key Story
Jehoash recovers Israel's cities and captures Amaziah.
Key Verses
2 Kings 13:10; 2 Kings 14:13
Related Books

Related People
