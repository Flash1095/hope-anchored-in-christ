Zechariah, Father of John the Baptist

Zechariah was a priest who, while burning incense in the Temple, was promised a son, John, yet was struck silent for his unbelief until the child's birth. His restored voice burst into prophetic praise.

Key Story

At John's circumcision Zechariah wrote, His name is John, and immediately his mouth was opened and he prophesied over his son.

Life Lesson

Zechariah's season of silence ended in song. If God seems silent in your waiting, He is still working — and your voice of praise will return.
