Shammah, Son of Jesse

Name
Shammah, Son of Jesse
Role
Son of Jesse
Testament
Old
Timeline Era

Summary
Shammah, Jesse's third son, was passed over by Samuel at the anointing and served in Saul's army at Elah. He is also called Shimea and Shimeah in the record of David's family.
Key Story
Shammah was the third son presented to Samuel and passed over, then stood with his brothers against Goliath's army.
Key Verses
1 Samuel 16:9; 1 Samuel 17:13
Related Books
