The Ontological Argument

Argument
The Ontological Argument
Category
Philosophy
Summary
Anselm argued that God is the greatest conceivable being - and a being that exists in reality is greater than one that exists only in thought. If God's existence is even possible, then a maximally great being exists in every possible world, including this one. The argument is debated, but it shows belief in God is rationally serious, not a leap in the dark.
Key Points
God is defined as a maximally great being; Existence in reality is greater than existence in thought alone; If possible, such a being must exist
