Argument
Josephus on Jesus
Category
History
Summary
A Jewish historian who wrote in Greek under Roman patronage in the late 1st century. His Antiquities of the Jews retells Jewish history from creation to his own time. Key work: Antiquities of the Jews.
Key Points
Antiquities 18.63-64 (Testimonium Flavianum) describes Jesus as a wise man and doer of startling deeds, crucified under Pontius Pilate, whose followers continued after his death — parts of its wording show later Christian editing, while the core reference to Jesus is widely accepted as authentic; Antiquities 20.200 reports the stoning of James, 'the brother of Jesus, who was called Christ'; Antiquities 18.116-119 describes John the Baptist, his baptism, and his imprisonment and execution by Herod Antipas
