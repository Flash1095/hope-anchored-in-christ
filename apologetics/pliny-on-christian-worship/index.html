Pliny on Christian Worship

Argument
Pliny on Christian Worship
Category
History
Summary
A Roman lawyer and governor of Bithynia-Pontus in the early 2nd century, known from his surviving correspondence with the emperor Trajan. Key work: Letters.
Key Points
Letters 10.96 (about AD 112) reports Christians met before dawn and sang a hymn to Christ 'as to a god'; They bound themselves by oath against theft, robbery, adultery, and fraud, and shared an ordinary meal; The letter confirms early Christian worship of Christ as divine, their moral code, and organised meetings within living memory of the apostolic period
