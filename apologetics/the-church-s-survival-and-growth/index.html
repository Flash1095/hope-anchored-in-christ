Argument
The Church's Survival and Growth
Category
History
Summary
The early church had no armies, no wealth, and no political power - and its leaders were hunted, imprisoned, and killed. Yet within a generation it spread across the Roman Empire, carried by people who had everything to lose. Movements built on a known lie do not grow like that; movements built on witnessed truth do.
Key Points
The church grew under persecution; Its founders gained nothing worldly; Explosive growth fits a true, witnessed message
