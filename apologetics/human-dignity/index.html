Human Dignity

Argument
Human Dignity
Category
Philosophy
Summary
We treat human life as possessing inherent worth - the unborn, the disabled, the dying, the enemy - in ways that go beyond usefulness. If humans are cosmic accidents, dignity is a polite fiction; if we bear God's image, dignity is real and inalienable. The Christian story grounds the worth every conscience already feels.
Key Points
Human worth is treated as inherent, not earned; Usefulness cannot ground dignity; The image of God grounds it
