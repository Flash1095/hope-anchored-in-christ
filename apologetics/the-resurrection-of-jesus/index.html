The Resurrection of Jesus

Argument
The Resurrection of Jesus
Category
History
Summary
The strongest historical case for Christianity is the resurrection. The tomb was empty, followers who had fled in fear suddenly proclaimed a risen Jesus, skeptics like James and Paul were converted, and the movement exploded in Jerusalem itself, where the claim could have been checked. No rival explanation accounts for all these facts as well as the resurrection does.
Key Points
The tomb was empty; Followers reported appearances of the risen Jesus; Skeptics were converted; The church began in Jerusalem
