Changed Lives

Argument
Changed Lives
Category
Experience
Summary
The most personal evidence for Christianity is transformation: addicts freed, enemies reconciled, the proud humbled, the despairing given hope. Skeptics like C. S. Lewis and Lee Strobel set out to refute the faith and were converted by it. Changed lives do not prove every doctrine, but they show the Gospel does what it promises.
Key Points
Conversion transforms character and conduct; Skeptics have been converted by the evidence; The Gospel's power is experiential evidence
