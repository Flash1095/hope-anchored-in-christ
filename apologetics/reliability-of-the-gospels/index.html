Reliability of the Gospels

Argument
Reliability of the Gospels
Category
History
Summary
The Gospels read like reports from people who were there: named eyewitnesses, vivid local detail, Aramaic phrases, and embarrassing admissions the authors would not invent. Early creeds embedded in Paul's letters date to within a few years of the events. The Gospels are historical documents, not myths grown over centuries.
Key Points
Eyewitness detail and local color abound; Early creeds date to within years of the events; Embarrassing details signal honesty
