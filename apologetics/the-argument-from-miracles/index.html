The Argument from Miracles

The Argument from Miracles

Summary

David Hume argued miracles are by definition the least probable explanation, but that assumes what it must prove - that God does not act. If God exists, miracles are possible, and history must weigh the evidence case by case. The well-attested resurrection appearances and the church's explosive birth are exactly the kind of evidence miracles would leave.
