The Moral Argument

The Moral Argument

Summary

Human beings everywhere recognize objective moral truths - that torturing the innocent is wrong, that justice is good - not as mere preferences but as real obligations. Such moral facts need a grounding beyond human opinion, and a good, personal God provides it. If morality is only a human invention, then 'evil' is just a feeling, not a fact.
