The Longing for Justice

Argument
The Longing for Justice
Category
Philosophy
Summary
Every culture protests injustice - the innocent should not suffer, the wicked should not prosper forever - as if the world owes us a moral order it does not currently deliver. That protest only makes sense if justice is real and a final reckoning awaits. Christianity names both: a Judge, and a day.
Key Points
The demand for justice is universal; It assumes a moral order beyond the present; Scripture promises a final judgment
Scripture
Ecclesiastes 3:17; Acts 17:31; Revelation 20:12
