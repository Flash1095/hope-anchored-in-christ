The Origin of Life

Argument
The Origin of Life
Category
Science
Summary
Life's origin remains one of science's deepest puzzles: non-living chemistry must somehow produce self-replicating, information-bearing cells. Decades of experiments have not closed the gap between chemistry and code. The Christian claim is not 'science cannot explain it, therefore God,' but that an intelligent cause is the kind of explanation the evidence points toward.
Key Points
Life requires information and replication together; Experiments have not produced life from non-life; Mind is a known source of information
