The Argument from Contingency

The Argument from Contingency

Summary

Why is there something rather than nothing? Everything we see is contingent — it exists, but it might not have, and it depends on something else for its existence. A chain of dependent things cannot explain itself; it needs a stopping point that depends on nothing at all. That necessary, self-existent Being — who exists by His own nature and not by another's say-so — is what Christians mean by God.
