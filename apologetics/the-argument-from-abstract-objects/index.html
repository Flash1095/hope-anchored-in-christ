The Argument from Abstract Objects

Argument
The Argument from Abstract Objects
Category
Philosophy
Summary
Numbers, logical truths, and propositions are real — 2 + 2 = 4 was true before any mind counted — yet they are not physical things, have no location, and cannot be caused by anything material. Such necessary, non-physical realities need a ground outside the physical world, and a divine mind that eternally knows them is the only adequate ground.
Key Points
Numbers and logical truths are real but not physical; They exist necessarily, not by material causes; A divine mind eternally knowing them is the adequate ground
