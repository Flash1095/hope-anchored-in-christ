Archaeological Confirmation
Category
History
Summary
Again and again, archaeology has caught up with the Bible. Inscriptions name Pontius Pilate and the house of David; the cities, customs, and coins of the biblical world match the text's details. Archaeology cannot prove every miracle, but it keeps confirming that the Bible is rooted in real history, not legend.
Key Points
The Pilate inscription confirms a Gospel figure; The Tel Dan inscription names the house of David; Biblical geography and customs check out
