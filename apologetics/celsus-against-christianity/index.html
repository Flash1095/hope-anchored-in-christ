Celsus Against Christianity

Argument
Celsus Against Christianity
Category
History
Summary
A Platonic philosopher of the late 2nd century who wrote the earliest known sustained pagan attack on Christianity. Key work: The True Word.
Key Points
The True Word (about AD 178) survives only through Origen's extensive quotations in Contra Celsum (about AD 248); Celsus engages Jesus's birth, his mother Mary, his work in Egypt, his disciples, his miracles, his crucifixion, and the claim he rose again, dismissing the resurrection as invention; As a hostile witness he confirms these central Christian claims were already in circulation and under public attack in the 2nd century
