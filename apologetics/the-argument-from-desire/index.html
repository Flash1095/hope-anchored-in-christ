The Argument from Desire

The Argument from Desire

Summary

Every natural desire has a corresponding real object: hunger points to food, thirst to water. Human beings also carry a deeper longing - for lasting joy, perfect love, and a home we have never seen - that nothing in this world fully satisfies. C. S. Lewis argued this longing hints that we were made for another world.
