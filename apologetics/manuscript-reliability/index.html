Manuscript Reliability
Category
History
Summary
The New Testament survives in more than 5,000 Greek manuscripts, some within decades of the originals - far more, and far earlier, than any other ancient work. Textual variants are overwhelmingly trivial, and no core doctrine hangs on a disputed reading. We can be confident the text we hold is the text they wrote.
Key Points
Over 5,000 Greek manuscripts survive; Some fragments date within decades of writing; Variants do not affect core doctrines
