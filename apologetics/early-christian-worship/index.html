Early Christian Worship

Argument
Early Christian Worship
Category
History
Summary
Within years of the crucifixion, Jewish followers of Jesus were worshiping on Sunday, baptizing in His name, and sharing a meal proclaiming His death - practices that broke deeply held Jewish customs. Something enormous must explain why monotheistic Jews began worshiping a crucified man as Lord, and the resurrection is the explanation the early church itself gave.
Key Points
Sunday worship broke Sabbath custom; Baptism and the Lord's Supper centered on Jesus; Jews worshiping Jesus demands an explanation
