The Bible's Unity

The Bible's Unity

Summary

Sixty-six books, written by more than forty authors over some 1,500 years, in three languages and on three continents - yet they tell one coherent story: creation, fall, redemption, and restoration, centered on Jesus. Such unity across such diversity is remarkable on its own; Christians see the hand of one divine Author behind the human ones.
