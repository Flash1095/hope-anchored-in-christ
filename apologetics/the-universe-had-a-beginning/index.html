The Universe Had a Beginning

Argument
The Universe Had a Beginning
Category
Science
Summary
For most of modern history, science assumed an eternal universe. The Big Bang reversed that: the universe is expanding from a beginning, and theorems like Borde-Guth-Vilenkin suggest any expanding universe must have a boundary in the past. A universe with a beginning points beyond itself to a Beginner.
Key Points
Expansion points back to a beginning; The Borde-Guth-Vilenkin theorem supports a past boundary; A beginning needs a cause
