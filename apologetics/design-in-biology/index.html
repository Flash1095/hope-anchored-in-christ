Design in Biology
Category
Science
Summary
Living cells run on information. DNA stores instructions with a four-letter code, copied, proofread, and executed by molecular machines of staggering complexity. Information of this kind always traces back to a mind in our experience, and the cell's information-rich design points to a Designer rather than unguided chance.
Key Points
DNA contains coded information; Codes and machines point to minds; Unguided processes have no observed power to write such code
