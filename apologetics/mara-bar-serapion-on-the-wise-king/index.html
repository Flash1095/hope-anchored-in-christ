Mara bar Serapion on the Wise King

Argument
Mara bar Serapion on the Wise King
Category
History
Summary
A Syriac Stoic writer whose surviving letter to his son, traditionally dated after AD 73, uses historical examples to urge wisdom in suffering. Key work: Letter of Mara bar Serapion.
Key Points
The letter compares Socrates, Pythagoras, and the 'wise king' of the Jews, executed by his own people, whose kingdom was taken away, yet who 'lives on' through the new laws he laid down; The wise king is not named, but identification with Jesus is widely accepted; The letter's exact date is debated, so it is best read as an early non-Christian allusion rather than a precisely dated eyewitness account
