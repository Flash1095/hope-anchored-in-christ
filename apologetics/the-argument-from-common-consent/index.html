The Argument from Common Consent

Argument
The Argument from Common Consent
Category
Philosophy
Summary
In nearly every culture and era, human beings have believed in a God or gods — a universality no other belief about an unseen reality enjoys. Such a widespread, spontaneous conviction is unlikely to be a pure accident of culture; it hints at a real referent behind the belief, a God whom people everywhere have sensed, however imperfectly.
Key Points
Belief in God appears across cultures and eras; Such universality is unlikely to be pure cultural accident; The best explanation is a real God whom people everywhere have sensed
