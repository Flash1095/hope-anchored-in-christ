The Fine-Tuning Argument

Argument
The Fine-Tuning Argument
Category
Science
Summary
The fundamental constants of physics - gravity, the strong nuclear force, the cosmological constant - fall within an astonishingly narrow range that permits life. Had any of them differed by a fraction, no stars, chemistry, or life could exist. Such precision is far better explained by a designing mind than by blind chance.
Key Points
Physical constants are balanced within a razor-thin life-permitting range; Chance alone makes this precision deeply improbable; Design by a wise Creator explains the balance
