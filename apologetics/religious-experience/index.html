Summary
Across cultures and centuries, people report encountering God - in prayer, in worship, in crisis, and in quiet conviction - and emerging changed. While any single experience could be questioned, the sheer breadth and life-transforming fruit of such experiences, consistent with Scripture's picture of God, counts as real evidence, not mere emotion.
Key Points
God-encounters are widely reported across cultures; They often produce lasting moral transformation; Scripture presents a God who meets people
