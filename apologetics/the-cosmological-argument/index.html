The Cosmological Argument

Argument
The Cosmological Argument
Category
Philosophy
Summary
Everything that begins to exist has a cause. The universe began to exist - space, time, matter, and energy all came into being at a finite point in the past - so the universe has a cause beyond itself. That cause must be timeless, spaceless, immaterial, and enormously powerful, which is what Christians mean by God.
Key Points
Whatever begins to exist has a cause; The universe began to exist; Therefore the universe has a cause; A cause outside space and time points to God
