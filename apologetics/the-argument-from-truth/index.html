The Argument from Truth

Argument
The Argument from Truth
Category
Philosophy
Summary
Truth is not a preference; some things are simply true whether we like them or not. But in a universe of only matter and motion, why should there be objective, mind-independent truths at all - including the truth that materialism is true? Truth itself points to a Mind in whom truth is grounded.
Key Points
Objective truth exists independently of opinion; Materialism struggles to ground truth; God as Truth itself explains it
