The Argument from Love

Argument
The Argument from Love
Category
Experience
Summary
Love is the deepest human experience, yet materialism reduces it to chemistry serving reproduction. But self-giving love - a parent's sacrifice, a friend's loyalty, a stranger's mercy - looks like something more: a reflection of a God who is love. If love is real and ultimate, then reality is personal at its root.
Key Points
Self-giving love transcends mere biology; Love is treated as ultimate, not useful; A God who is love explains love's depth
