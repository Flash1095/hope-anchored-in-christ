The Cumulative Case

The Cumulative Case

Summary

No single argument carries the whole weight, and none needs to. Like strands twisted into a rope, cosmology, fine-tuning, morality, history, and experience together make a cord no single objection easily cuts. Christianity's case is cumulative: many independent lines of evidence converging on one conclusion - that the God of the Bible is real.
