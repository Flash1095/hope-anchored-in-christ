The Argument from Reason

Argument
The Argument from Reason
Category
Philosophy
Summary
If our minds are only the product of blind, unguided evolution, why trust them to find truth - including the truth of that theory? Reason, logic, and the reliability of thought itself fit poorly in a universe of mere matter and chance, but they fit perfectly in a universe made by a rational God whose image we bear.
Key Points
Trust in reason needs a foundation; Blind processes aim at survival, not truth; A rational Creator grounds rational minds

Isaiah 1:18; Romans 1:19; Proverbs 1:7
