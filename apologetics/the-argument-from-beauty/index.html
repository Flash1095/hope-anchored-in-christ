The Argument from Beauty

Argument
The Argument from Beauty
Category
Experience
Summary
Beauty moves us in ways usefulness cannot explain. A sunset, a symphony, a mountain valley - these stir awe, longing, and gratitude that point beyond themselves. If the world were only atoms and accident, beauty's power to arrest us would be a strange surplus; in a world made by a beautiful Creator, it is a whisper of home.
Key Points
Beauty is universal and deeply moving; It points beyond mere survival value; A beautiful Creator explains our response to beauty
