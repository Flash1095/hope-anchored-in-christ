The Universe Is Intelligible

Argument
The Universe Is Intelligible
Category
Science
Summary
Not only does the universe permit life - it permits discovery. Its laws are stable, mathematical, and open to minds that can grasp them, and our planet sits in a remarkably well-placed observatory for studying them. A universe made to be understood, by creatures made to understand, fits a Creator who invites us to explore.
Key Points
Nature's laws are stable and mathematical; Humans can grasp them; Discoverability fits a purposeful Creator
