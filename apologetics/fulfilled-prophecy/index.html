Fulfilled Prophecy

Argument
Fulfilled Prophecy
Category
History
Summary
Centuries before Jesus, Hebrew prophets described a Messiah born in Bethlehem, rejected by His own, pierced for others' sins, and silent before His accusers. Jesus fulfilled these specific predictions in ways no one could stage-manage. Prophecy fulfilled is God's signature across history.
Key Points
Micah named Bethlehem seven centuries early; Isaiah 53 describes a suffering servant; Psalm 22 pictures crucifixion before it was invented
Scripture
Micah 5:2; Isaiah 53:5-6; Psalm 22:16-18
