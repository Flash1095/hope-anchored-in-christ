The Argument from Free Will

The Argument from Free Will

Summary

We live as if our choices are real and we are responsible for them - and we hold others responsible too. Strict materialism struggles to ground genuine choice: if every thought is fixed by physics, deliberation is an illusion. Christianity grounds freedom in persons made in God's image, capable of real agency and real responsibility.
