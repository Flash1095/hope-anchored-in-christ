The Argument from Degrees

Argument
The Argument from Degrees
Category
Philosophy
Key Points
Goodness, truth, and beauty exist in degrees; Degrees imply a maximum standard; The maximum is not a thing among things but their source; God is goodness, truth, and beauty itself
Related Books
Psalms; James; 1 John
Related Topics
Apologetics; Creation; Wisdom
Scripture
Psalm 19:1; 1 John 4:8; James 1:17
Summary
We speak of things as more or less good, true, or beautiful — a warmer day, a truer statement, a nobler act. But 'more or less' only means something against a maximum, the way 'hotter' points to a hottest. If goodness, truth, and beauty come in degrees, there must be a greatest Good, Truth, and Beauty itself — not merely a thing that has these qualities, but the source that defines them. That maximum is God.
