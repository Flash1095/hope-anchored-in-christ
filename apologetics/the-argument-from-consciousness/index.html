The Argument from Consciousness

Argument
The Argument from Consciousness
Category
Philosophy
Summary
We do not merely process information; we experience it - colors, pain, joy, and the sense of being a self. Consciousness is stubbornly resistant to purely physical explanation: brain activity correlates with experience but does not obviously produce the inner life we know firsthand. A universe made by a conscious Mind makes consciousness expected rather than miraculous.
Key Points
Conscious experience is real and first-person; Physics describes structure, not subjective experience; A conscious Creator explains conscious creatures
