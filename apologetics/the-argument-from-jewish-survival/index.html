The Argument from Jewish Survival

Argument
The Argument from Jewish Survival
Category
History
Summary
No other people has survived what the Jewish people have survived: exile, dispersion among the nations, centuries of persecution, and repeated attempts at extermination — yet they remain a distinct people, still in their ancient land, still keeping their ancient law. Scripture promised exactly this preservation; history delivered it.
Key Points
The Jewish people survived exile, dispersion, and persecution; They remain distinct, in their land, keeping their law; Scripture promised this preservation in advance

Jeremiah 31:35-37; Leviticus 26:44; Deuteronomy 30:3-5
