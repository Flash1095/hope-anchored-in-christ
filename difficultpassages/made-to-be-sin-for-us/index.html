Made to Be Sin for Us

Passage
Made to Be Sin for Us
Reference
2 Corinthians 5:21
Difficulty
Paul says God 'hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin.' Taken literally, this seems to say Christ became sinful, which clashes with the same verse's claim that he knew no sin and with the rest of the New Testament's picture of a sinless Saviour.
Explanations
The standard explanation is that 'sin' here stands for a sin offering, as the Hebrew word for sin is used in Leviticus, or for Christ being treated as sin's bearer in our place. Either way the point is imputation, not corruption: our sin was reckoned to him and his righteousness to us. The verse's own contrast, between his knowing no sin and our becoming God's righteousness, points against a literal reading.
Site Assessment
The site's position is that 2 Corinthians 5:21 teaches exchange, not transformation of Christ's character: he bore sin's penalty as our representative while remaining sinless.
