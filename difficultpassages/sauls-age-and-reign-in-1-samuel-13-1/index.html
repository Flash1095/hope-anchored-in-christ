Saul's Age and Reign in 1 Samuel 13:1

Passage
Saul's Age and Reign in 1 Samuel 13:1
Reference
1 Samuel 13:1
Difficulty
The KJV reads: 'Saul reigned one year; and when he had reigned two years over Israel.' The verse as it stands is unintelligible as a reign length, since Saul plainly reigns far longer, and the Hebrew text behind it is evidently defective at exactly this point.
Explanations
Textual critics agree the Hebrew numbers here have been lost or corrupted in transmission: the verse lacks the figure for Saul's age at accession and one figure is incomplete. Ancient versions and later manuscripts differ in their attempts to fill the gap, which is itself evidence that no original reading survived intact in the Masoretic tradition. The passage is therefore a copyist problem, not a historical claim that Saul reigned only two years, and no doctrine turns on the missing numbers.
Site Assessment
Site assessment: The honest reading is that 1 Samuel 13:1 is a damaged verse whose numbers cannot be recovered with certainty. That is a fact about transmission, not a contradiction between two complete statements.
