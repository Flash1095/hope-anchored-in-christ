The Johannine Comma: Three That Bear Record in Heaven

Passage
The Johannine Comma: Three That Bear Record in Heaven
Reference
1 John 5:7-8
Difficulty
John writes 'there are three that bear record in heaven, the Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost: and these three are one.' Readers who learn this wording is absent from the earliest Greek manuscripts ask whether the Trinity has lost its proof text, or whether their Bible has been tampered with.
Explanations
The textual facts are broadly settled: the heavenly-witnesses clause appears in no early Greek manuscript and only in much later Latin copies; it entered some printed Greek texts and from there the King James Version. The earthly witnesses of verse 8, the Spirit, the water, and the blood, are in the early manuscripts. Trinitarian doctrine does not rest on this clause: it is taught across passages such as Matthew 28:19 and John 1:1, which are not in dispute.
Site Assessment
The site's position is that the heavenly-witnesses clause is a later addition and should not be used as a proof text, while the doctrine of the Trinity stands on the wider, well-attested witness of Scripture.
