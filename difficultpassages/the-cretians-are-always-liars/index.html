The Cretians Are Always Liars

Passage
The Cretians Are Always Liars
Reference
Titus 1:12-13
Difficulty
Paul quotes a Cretan prophet saying 'The Cretians are alway liars,' then tells Titus to rebuke them sharply. Readers spot a paradox: if all Cretans always lie, then the Cretan prophet lied too, so the statement refutes itself; and the verse also sounds like ethnic stereotyping.
Explanations
The standard resolution is that Paul is quoting a well-known Cretan proverb about a reputation, not stating a literal universal about every Cretan at every moment. A proverbial, hyperbolic saying about a culture's reputation does not create a strict logical paradox, because it is not meant as a formal universal claim. Paul's point is pastoral: false teaching and a lax reputation were real problems in Crete, so Titus must be firm.
Site Assessment
The site's position is that Titus 1:12 is a quoted proverb about reputation, not a literal claim about every Cretan and not a licence for stereotyping any people today.
