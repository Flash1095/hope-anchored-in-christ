God Creates Evil?

Passage
God Creates Evil?
Reference
Isaiah 45:7
Difficulty
If God is good, how can he say, 'I create evil'? The verse seems to make God the author of moral evil, which would contradict his goodness, his holiness, and passages that say God cannot be tempted by evil and tempts no one.
Explanations
Hebrew ra' here is paired with 'peace' and set against 'light' and 'darkness': the contrast is blessing and calamity, prosperity and disaster, not moral goodness and moral wickedness. In context Cyrus is addressed and God claims sovereignty over history's fortunes and judgments, including the disaster that falls on Babylon. Other passages use the same word for calamity, plague, and judgment. The verse therefore claims God ordains calamitous events in judgment and providence; it does not say God commits or approves sin.
Site Assessment
Site assessment: God is sovereign over disaster and judgment, and God is never the author of sin. 'Evil' in the KJV here should be read as calamity, as modern translations render it, and the verse must not be used to blame God for moral evil.
