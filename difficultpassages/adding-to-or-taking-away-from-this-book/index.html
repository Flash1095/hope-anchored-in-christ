Adding to or Taking Away From This Book

Passage
Adding to or Taking Away From This Book
Reference
Revelation 22:18-19
Difficulty
Revelation warns that anyone who adds to 'the words of the prophecy of this book' will receive its plagues, and anyone who takes away will lose his part in the book of life. Readers fear that a misquotation, a printing error, or a doubtful reading could cost them salvation.
Explanations
The widely agreed scope is deliberate tampering with the prophecy of Revelation itself, paralleling the warning at the end of Deuteronomy for the law: it addresses those who would falsify God's message, not accidental slips or textual questions. 'This book' in context means Revelation, though the principle of not falsifying Scripture applies more widely. The warning is aimed at the messenger's integrity, not at a reader's imperfect memory.
Site Assessment
The site's position is that Revelation 22:18-19 warns deliberate falsifiers of the prophecy, not honest readers, translators, or students who wrestle with a difficult text.
