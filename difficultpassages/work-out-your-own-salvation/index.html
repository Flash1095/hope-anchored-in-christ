Work Out Your Own Salvation

Passage
Work Out Your Own Salvation
Reference
Philippians 2:12-13
Difficulty
Paul tells readers to 'work out your own salvation with fear and trembling,' which sounds like earning salvation by effort, especially when set beside his own teaching that salvation is by grace through faith and not of works.
Explanations
The context resolves the tension: verse 13 immediately adds that it is God who works in believers both to will and to do. 'Work out' means live out, carry to completion, what God has already given, not work for a salvation not yet possessed. Fear and trembling here describe serious, reverent obedience, not terror that God may drop the believer.
Site Assessment
The site's position is that Philippians 2:12-13 teaches responsible obedience empowered by God, not self-earned salvation. The two verses must be read together, never verse 12 alone.
