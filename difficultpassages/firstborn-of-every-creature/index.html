Firstborn of Every Creature

Passage
Firstborn of Every Creature
Reference
Colossians 1:15-18
Difficulty
Christ is called 'the firstborn of every creature,' which some readers take to mean he was the first being God created, and therefore not eternal God. That reading has been used to deny his deity.
Explanations
The widely agreed linguistic point is that 'firstborn' in biblical usage often means pre-eminent heir with rights over all, not first in time: Psalm 89 calls David God's firstborn though he was the youngest son. Colossians itself blocks the created reading, saying all things were created by him and he is before all things. The passage is about rank and creatorship, not origin.
Site Assessment
The site's position is that 'firstborn' here means supreme heir and creator, not first creature. The passage affirms Christ's deity rather than denying it.
