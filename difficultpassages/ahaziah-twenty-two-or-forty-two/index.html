Ahaziah: Twenty-Two or Forty-Two?

2 Kings 8:26 says Ahaziah was twenty-two when he began to reign; 2 Chronicles 22:2 says forty-two. Readers ask which age is correct, and notice that forty-two creates a further problem: Ahaziah's father Jehoram died at forty, so a forty-two-year-old son is impossible.

Explanations

The internal evidence settles the direction of the error: twenty-two must be original, and forty-two is a copyist's numeral corruption. The Kings figure also fits Ahaziah's one-year reign and his mother's identity, which both accounts agree on. This is a transmission error with a recoverable original, not two competing histories.

Site Assessment

Site assessment: Ahaziah was twenty-two. The forty-two in Chronicles is a copyist error, demonstrably so from his father's age.
