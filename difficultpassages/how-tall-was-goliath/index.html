How Tall Was Goliath?

The Hebrew text gives Goliath's height as six cubits and a span — roughly nine feet nine inches — while the Septuagint and a Dead Sea Scrolls manuscript give four cubits and a span, roughly six feet nine inches. Readers ask which is right, and whether the difference undermines the story.

Explanations

Textual evidence points to a genuine variant in transmission: the Hebrew tradition and the Greek tradition preserve different figures, and scholars differ on which direction the change went, but agree a variant exists. Either way the narrative point is untouched: Goliath is presented as a giant champion towering over Israel's soldiers, and David's victory depends on God, not on the exact measurement. No theological claim rests on the number of cubits.

Site Assessment

Site assessment: Goliath's exact height cannot be settled from the surviving manuscripts alone, and does not need to be. The variant is a textual footnote, not a crack in the story's meaning.
