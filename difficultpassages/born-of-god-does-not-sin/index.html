Born of God Does Not Sin

Passage
Born of God Does Not Sin
Reference
1 John 3:6-9
Difficulty
John says 'whosoever is born of God doth not commit sin' and 'cannot sin,' yet in the same letter he says 'if we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves.' Readers fear this makes every Christian a fraud, or that 1 John contradicts itself within five chapters.
Explanations
The widely agreed grammatical point is that the verbs in 3:6-9 describe ongoing, habitual practice, not a single act: the born-again person does not live in sin as a settled pattern, because God's seed remains in him. Chapter 1 is about honest confession of actual sins; chapter 3 is about direction of life. A Christian still sins and confesses, but does not make sin his home and trade.
Site Assessment
The site's position is that 1 John 3:9 describes a changed pattern of life, not sinless perfection. Read with 1:8-10, it promises transformation, not a claim no Christian ever sins.
