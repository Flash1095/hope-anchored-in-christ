No Edomite Remaining

Passage
No Edomite Remaining
Reference
Obadiah 1:15-18
Difficulty
Obadiah says of the house of Esau that 'there shall not be any remaining.' Yet Edomites appear later in history, most famously Herod, an Idumean. The prophecy of total extinction appears to have failed.
Explanations
Prophetic judgment language uses totalising, hyperbolic terms for the fall of a nation as a political power, not as a census claim that no individual of that ancestry will ever live again. Edom as a kingdom was destroyed, its territory taken, and its people absorbed and displaced, which is what the oracle announces: the house of Jacob as fire consuming the house of Esau as stubble. Later individuals of Edomite descent, living under other powers centuries later, do not revive Edom as the nation Obadiah judged. The same hyperbolic idiom is used for other nations in the prophets.
Site Assessment
Site assessment: The prophecy announces Edom's end as a nation and power, in the hyperbolic language of judgment oracles. Later Edomite individuals do not contradict that national judgment.
