The Third Year of Jehoiakim

Passage
The Third Year of Jehoiakim
Reference
Daniel 1:1
Difficulty
Daniel says Nebuchadnezzar besieged Jerusalem in the third year of Jehoiakim, but Jeremiah calls the fourth year of Jehoiakim the first year of Nebuchadnezzar. Both cannot be right about the same year, so one of the books appears to misdate the siege.
Explanations
The two books count regnal years differently, as ancient Near Eastern kingdoms did. Judah counted a king's accession year as his first year, while Babylon counted the accession year as year zero and the following year as year one. On Babylonian counting Daniel's 'third year' and on Judean counting Jeremiah's 'fourth year' describe the same period, around 605 BC, when Nebuchadnezzar first asserted control over Jerusalem after Carchemish. The difference is a dating convention, not a contradiction in the event.
Site Assessment
Site assessment: The dates agree once each book's counting convention is recognised. This is a textual and calendrical resolution, not a choice between Daniel and Jeremiah.
