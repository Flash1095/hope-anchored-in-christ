Melchizedek Without Father or Mother

Passage
Melchizedek Without Father or Mother
Reference
Hebrews 7:1-3
Difficulty
Hebrews says Melchizedek was 'without father, without mother, without descent, having neither beginning of days, nor end of life.' Readers wonder whether he was a supernatural being, an appearance of Christ, or a man who was never born and never died.
Explanations
The widely held explanation is literary: Genesis records no genealogy, birth, or death for Melchizedek, and Hebrews builds a comparison on that silence. 'Made like unto the Son of God' signals a likeness used for teaching, not an identity claim. His priesthood, unlike Aaron's, is presented without a recorded beginning or end, which makes him a fitting picture of Christ's permanent priesthood.
Site Assessment
The site's position is that Hebrews 7 argues from what Genesis does not record, not from Melchizedek being uncreated. He is a historical king-priest used as a type of Christ, not Christ himself under another name.
