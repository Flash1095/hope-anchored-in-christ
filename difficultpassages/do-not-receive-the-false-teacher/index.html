Do Not Receive the False Teacher Into Your House

Passage
Do Not Receive the False Teacher Into Your House
Reference
2 John 1:10-11
Difficulty
John tells readers not to receive a false teacher into their house or even wish him well, which sounds like a ban on ordinary hospitality or kindness to anyone who disagrees. Readers wonder whether this forbids housing, feeding, or greeting a neighbour with different beliefs.
Explanations
The agreed context is a house church, not a private home's guest room: receiving a travelling teacher into the house meant giving him a platform and endorsing his teaching to the congregation. Refusing that endorsement is not the same as refusing food to a stranger or civility to a neighbour. The letter's concern is partnership with deceivers who deny Christ come in the flesh, not everyday kindness.
Site Assessment
The site's position is that 2 John 10-11 forbids endorsing and platforming false teachers in the church, not ordinary hospitality or polite conduct toward people of other beliefs.
