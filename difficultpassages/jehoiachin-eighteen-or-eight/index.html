Jehoiachin: Eighteen or Eight?

Passage
Jehoiachin: Eighteen or Eight?
Reference
2 Kings 24:8; 2 Chronicles 36:9
Difficulty
2 Kings 24:8 says Jehoiachin was eighteen when he began to reign in Jerusalem; 2 Chronicles 36:9 says eight. Readers ask how a king's age at accession can differ by ten years between two books.
Explanations
As with Ahaziah, the likeliest explanation is a numeral corruption in transmission, with eighteen the more plausible original: an eighteen-year-old reigning three months before exile fits the political narrative better than an eight-year-old held responsible for doing evil in the sight of the LORD. A secondary suggestion — that eight marks a co-regency beginning in his father's reign — is possible but unnecessary, and scholars generally prefer the copyist explanation. The reign's length (three months) and its end (exile to Babylon) are agreed in both accounts.
Site Assessment
Site assessment: Jehoiachin was eighteen at his sole accession. The eight in Chronicles is best read as a numeral variant, and the history of his brief reign is unaffected.
