Who Killed Goliath — David or Elhanan?

Passage
Who Killed Goliath — David or Elhanan?
Reference
2 Samuel 21:19; 1 Chronicles 20:5
Difficulty
2 Samuel 21:19 says Elhanan 'slew Goliath the Gittite,' although 1 Samuel 17 says David slew Goliath. Readers ask whether Scripture contradicts itself about its most famous duel.
Explanations
1 Chronicles 20:5 resolves the passage: Elhanan slew Lahmi, the brother of Goliath. The Samuel text evidently lost the words 'the brother of' in transmission, and the KJV itself signals this by italicising '[the brother of]' as supplied words. The two Goliaths are not one man killed twice; David killed Goliath of Gath, and Elhanan later killed his brother Lahmi, whose spear is described in the same terms.
Site Assessment
Site assessment: There is no second killing of the same Goliath. Chronicles preserves the fuller reading, and the KJV's brackets honestly show where the Samuel text was supplemented.
