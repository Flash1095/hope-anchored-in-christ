God Tempts No One

Passage
God Tempts No One
Reference
James 1:13-15
Difficulty
James says God 'cannot be tempted with evil, neither tempteth he any man,' yet Genesis says God tested Abraham and the Lord's Prayer asks God not to lead us into temptation. Readers ask whether Scripture contradicts itself on whether God tempts or tests.
Explanations
The agreed distinction is between tempting toward evil and testing to prove or strengthen faith. The Greek word covers both, and James is explicit about his sense: God never entices anyone to sin, and sin comes from a person's own desires. Testing, as with Abraham, is a trial with a good purpose, not an enticement to evil. The two uses of one word describe different actions, not a contradiction.
Site Assessment
The site's position is that James 1:13 denies that God entices anyone to sin. It does not deny that God permits or appoints trials that prove faith.
