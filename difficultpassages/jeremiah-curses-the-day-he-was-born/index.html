Jeremiah Curses the Day He Was Born

A faithful prophet curses the day of his birth and asks why he came from the womb only to see labour and sorrow. If even Jeremiah despairs like this, does faith fail, and is such cursing acceptable speech for a believer?

Explanations

This is lament, in the same tradition as Job's curse on his day of birth. Jeremiah does not curse God, deny God's existence, or abandon his calling; he protests the suffering his calling has brought him, in prayer, to God himself. Scripture preserves such laments precisely because despair and faith can coexist in the same person: the complaint is directed to God rather than away from him, and the book goes on with Jeremiah still speaking God's word.

Site Assessment

Site assessment: Despair expressed honestly to God in lament is not unbelief and not a model to copy as a wish for death. The passage shows a prophet at his lowest, still talking to God, which is itself an act of faith.
