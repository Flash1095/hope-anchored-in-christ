A Day Is as a Thousand Years

Peter says 'one day is with the Lord as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day.' Readers use this as a conversion formula, stretching creation days or prophecy timetables, or they take it to mean God experiences no time at all.

Explanations

The context is patience, not a timetable: Peter is answering scoffers who say Christ's return is delayed, and his point is that God's apparent slowness is longsuffering, giving time for repentance. The day-thousand-years comparison is a simile about God's perspective, drawn from Psalm 90, not an equation to be applied to Genesis days or Revelation's thousand years. It forbids impatience, not careful chronology.

Site Assessment

The site's position is that 2 Peter 3:8 teaches God's patience and perspective, not a day-year conversion chart. It should not be used to re-date creation or the millennium.
