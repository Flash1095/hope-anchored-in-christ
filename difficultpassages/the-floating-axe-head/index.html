The Floating Axe Head

Passage
The Floating Axe Head
Reference
2 Kings 6:1-7
Difficulty
A borrowed iron axe head flies off its handle into the Jordan, and Elisha throws in a stick and the iron floats. Readers ask how iron can float, and whether the story asks them to believe something physics forbids.
Explanations
The passage presents itself as a miracle, not as a claim about the natural buoyancy of iron. Its point is pastoral and small-scale: a borrowed tool mattered to a poor prophet-disciple, and God attended to a small loss. Readers who accept that God can act in nature have no special difficulty here beyond the general question of miracles; readers who reject miracles will reject this one with all the rest, but the text does not pretend the iron floated by nature.
Site Assessment
Site assessment: The axe head floated because, in the story's own terms, God made it float. The passage claims a miracle, and should be judged as one, not as bad physics.
